## 1. Policy framework (per W1729)

### 1.1 Standard template (W1729.2 — every policy below follows it)

Each policy carries: **(a)** purpose & scope · **(b)** definitions (inline) ·
**(c)** policy statement (clear, actionable rules) · **(d)** implementation
procedures (anchored to the owning workflows, not duplicated) · **(e)** roles &
responsibilities (RACI against the [table of
organization](../optimal-table-of-organization.md)) · **(f)** exceptions &
approval process · **(g)** monitoring & enforcement (anchored to the [internal
controls matrix](../internal-controls-matrix.md) CTL references) · **(h)**
related documents · **(i)** version history.

### 1.2 Approval matrix (W1729.4 — binding)

| Policy class | Approver | Examples in this manual |
|---|---|---|
| Governance / critical | **Board** (or Audit Committee) | Code of Conduct, Speak-Up, DOA, Anti-Bribery, Data Breach |
| Financial | **CFO** (Board for thresholds) | Treasury, Capex, Expense, Credit, Tax |
| HR | **CHRO + CEO** | Discipline, Compensation, Drug-Free Workplace |
| Operational | **CEO** (or COO by delegation) | Cash Handling, Returns, Pricing, Inventory Adjustments |
| Privacy | **CEO** (DPO-owned; NPC-facing) | Privacy Notice, Breach Notification |

### 1.3 Lifecycle

- **Review cycle:** every policy reviewed at least every **2 years** (W1729.1);
  review calendar owned by the Corporate Secretary with automated reminders.
- **Change control:** drafts per template → Legal Counsel + CHRO + affected
  departments review (W1729.3) → approval per class → publication on the policy
  portal with **employee acknowledgment** (digital signature, W1729.5) →
  previous version archived (Records Retention, POL-D04).
- **Enforcement:** internal audit includes policy compliance in the audit
  program (W1729 Pain-Point discipline); violations route through Discipline &
  Due Process (POL-H06) for employees and Speak-Up (POL-G03) for reporting.
- **Exceptions:** any exception to a policy statement requires written approval
  by the policy owner **plus** the approver for the policy class; standing
  exceptions are prohibited — they must be re-justified at each review.

### 1.4 How to read the anchors

Each policy cites its **owner** (a real role in the TO), **approver** (per class),
**cadence**, the **workflows that operationalize it** (W-ids), and the **controls
that enforce it** (CTL-ids). The workflow corpus remains the process source of
truth; the policy states the rule, the workflow executes it.

## 3. Policy → control → workflow chain

Policies do not float: every POL-x cites the workflows that execute it and the
CTL-xx controls in the [internal controls
matrix](../internal-controls-matrix.md) that enforce it. The intended audit
chain is: **POL (rule) → W (process) → CTL (check) → audit test**. New workflows
must cite their governing POL in their Controls section; new controls must cite
the POL they enforce.
