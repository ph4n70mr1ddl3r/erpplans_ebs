# Health, Safety & Environment Policies (POL-S)

> Domain manual of the [Corporate Policy Manual](README.md). Approval class:
> **CEO/COO** per policy. Statutory base: **RA 11058** (OSH Act) & DOLE D.O.
> 198-18, fire code, sanitary code, **RA 9003 / RA 6969 / DENR rules** (waste &
> hazardous waste), RA 11285 (energy efficiency). HSE executes through the
> regional safety-officer network (HSE department, 13 FTE) and the VS-69/73/114
> workflow families.

## POL-S01 — Occupational Safety & Health (incl. Driver Safety)

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | HSE Manager / **CEO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (OSH program re-filing) |
| Anchors | RA 11058 / D.O. 198-18 program, per-store safety committees, VS-06 fleet workflows, TESDA-standard equipment certifications |

**Policy statements.**
1. Each store, DC and HQ site maintains a compliant **OSH program**: written
   safety organization, hazard identification and control, safety committee,
   training on engagement, and the mandatory accident/incident reporting and
   investigation ladder (near-misses included).
2. Safety gates before work: new-hire safety orientation (POL-H02), task-
   specific training (material handling, pallet jacks, box cutters, ladder/
   height work, forklift certification per TESDA standards for DC equipment —
   certification tracked with expiry, no valid cert, no machine).
3. **Contractor safety:** third parties (construction, maintenance, armored
   car) follow site-safety rules, inductions and permits-to-work (hot work,
   electrical, height) — vendors' incidents count in site metrics (POL-P04
   linkage).
4. Driver & fleet safety (store delivery + fleet operations): pre-trip
   inspections, no-overtime driving rules, telematics monitoring with the
   coaching ladder (speeding, harsh braking, idle patterns per the telematics
   workflow), and load-securing standards; incidents report within 24 hours
   with drug/alcohol testing per POL-H08 for-cause triggers.
5. Occupational health: company nurse case management, first-aid readiness per
   site, return-to-work plans after injury; reportable incidents file to DOLE
   on calendar.

## POL-S02 — Emergency Preparedness & Disaster Response

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | HSE Manager / **COO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (pre-season, before June) |
| Anchors | [VS-69](../workflows/VS-69-typhoon-disaster-response/) typhoon & disaster response family, VS-26 BCP (POL-B01), PA-184 post-disaster reconstruction |

**Policy statements.**
1. Every site keeps current **emergency action plans** for its risk set:
   fire, earthquake, typhoon/flood, power failure, robbery, medical emergency —
   with floor plans, marshals, assembly points and drill cadence (fire/earthquake
   drills per schedule; typhoon drills pre-season).
2. **Typhoon protocol** (the Philippine operating reality): alerts trigger the
   VS-69 ladder — pre-landfall checklist (outdoor fixtures, generator fuel,
   emergency lighting, cash minimization per POL-R01), personnel safety rules
   (no-travel windows, closure authority with Regional Manager + COO),
   post-event safety assessment BEFORE reopening (structural, electrical,
   sanitation), and damage documentation for insurance (POL-B02) within the
   claims window.
3. Employee protection is the first priority in every emergency decision:
   property protection never overrides personnel safety; closure pay follows
   labor rules (POL-H05).
4. Business recovery activates the BCP (POL-B01): temporary facilities
   (PA-184.3 mobilization playbook), stock transfer from sister stores, and
   communications per the crisis plan.
5. Emergency equipment (extinguishers, exit lights, generators, first-aid,
   comms) inspects on calendar with deficiency remediation tracked to closure.

## POL-S03 — Environmental, Energy & Waste Management

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Sustainability / ESG Lead / **CEO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review |
| Anchors | [VS-73](../workflows/VS-73-store-waste-circular-economy/) (segregation & DENR compliance), VS-108 (renewable/prosumer energy), PA-73.2 (hazardous waste DENR) |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Waste segregation at source** per the store program: recyclables,
   biodegradables, residual, and special/hazardous streams with the labeled
   lanes; haulers are DENR/LGU-accredited with manifest records (waste
   diversion and recycling-revenue tracking per the VS-73 analytics).
2. **Hazardous waste** (batteries, lamps, e-waste, chemicals, used oil) never
   mixes with general waste: stored per the DENR hazardous-waste rules,
   manifested through accredited treaters/transporters (PA-73.2), with
   registration and reporting current.
3. Regulatory: LGU environmental permits and reports (per VS-76 multi-region
   LGU compliance) keep current; penalties from non-compliance are reportable
   events with root-cause review.
4. **Energy management:** sites follow the energy-efficiency program (LED
   retrofits, HVAC setpoints, peak-demand discipline) and the renewable/
   prosumer assets (rooftop solar per VS-108) operate under their safety and
   grid-interconnection rules; energy intensity reports on the ESG dashboard.
5. Product-side: private-brand packaging follows the restricted-substances and
   packaging rules; take-back programs (old-appliance haul-away, battery
   returns) route collected items to the correct waste streams (POL-R02/
   POL-S04 linkage).

## POL-S04 — Dangerous Goods & Hazardous Materials

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | HSE Manager / **COO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | [VS-114](../workflows/VS-114-dangerous-goods-hazmat-transport-ecommerce-regulatory-compliance/) (DG transport & ecommerce regulatory compliance) |

**Policy statements.**
1. The DG program covers the assortment's hazardous classes (flammables/aerosols,
   corrosives, oxidizers, lithium batteries, gas cylinders) across **buy,
   store, sell, ship**: classification, SDS availability, segregated storage
   (compatibility matrix), quantity limits per storage area, and signage.
2. **Ecommerce shipping rules:** DG-restricted items carry the carrier-compliant
   declarations, packaging, labeling and quantity limits per transport regs —
   the DG screening step blocks non-compliant parcel creation; product pages
   carry the shipping restrictions.
3. Stores: no damaged/leaking hazardous product stays on the sales floor;
   customer returns of hazardous items quarantine in the designated area and
   dispose per POL-S03.2 — never restock.
4. Training: staff handling DG (receiving, stockroom, online fulfillment)
   complete the DG awareness/certification track on cycle; incidents (spills,
   exposures, fires involving hazmat) follow the emergency plan with HSE/LGU
   notification as required.
5. Supplier compliance: DG vendors certify classification/packaging compliance
   (POL-P04); non-compliant inbound is refused at receiving.

## POL-S05 — Employee Health, Wellness & Canteen

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Company Nurse / HSE / **CHRO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | HSE wellness program (department canon), sanitary-code permits, PA-19.x sick-leave chain |

**Policy statements.**
1. Sites maintain sanitary-code compliance for canteens/break rooms (permits,
   potable water, sanitation inspections) and the first-aid/medical readiness
   standard (kits, trained first-aiders per shift, nurse coverage per the
   medical program).
2. **Wellness program:** annual health screening offer, APE for managerial/
   covered roles, mental-health access per RA 11036 (counseling hotline,
   manager referral training), and health-promotion campaigns on calendar.
3. Communicable-disease protocol (food-handling roles especially): reporting,
   exclusion periods, return-to-work clearance; outbreak coordination follows
   LGU/DOH directives.
4. Medical records are confidential health data: retained per POL-D04, access
   limited to the medical function; managers receive fitness-for-work outcomes,
   never diagnoses.
5. Workplace accommodations for illness/injury (light-duty, schedule changes)
   coordinate HSE + HRBP with documented medical basis.

## POL-S06 — Live Goods, Plant Health & Agri-Input Compliance

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Garden Operations Lead / **COO** (Quality: co-review) |
| Cadence | 1-year review (pre-wet-season) |
| Anchors | [VS-145](../workflows/VS-145-garden-center-live-goods-and-plant-nursery/) (live goods & plant nursery), CTL-665 (grower qualification & phytosanitary compliance), W4352 (plant guarantee & replacement) |

**Policy statements.**
1. Live goods (plants, nursery stock, garden-adjacent live products) source
   only from qualified, scored growers with **phytosanitary compliance**
   documented per shipment (CTL-665); import shipments clear the customs/
   quarantine gates before receipt (POL-P02/P05 chain).
2. **Pest/disease-introduction prevention** is the controlling risk: incoming
   inspection, quarantine/treatment protocols, biological-preference treatment
   standards in-store, and the disposal rules for infested stock — no
   infested/infected stock sells, transfers or returns to vendors without the
   compliance disposition.
3. Agri-inputs (fertilizers, pesticides, growing media) sell only with the
   required registrations and labeling; restricted products follow the
   customer-advisory and shelf-separation rules; disposal of expired stocks
   follows POL-S03 hazardous streams.
4. Care standards: watering, nutrition and placement protocols per the
   nursery-care standards are the store execution layer; live-goods shrink
   (mortality/wastage) counts through POL-R04 reason codes with the
   care-protocol root-cause loop.
5. Customer guarantees (plant guarantee/replacement, W4352) publish the same
   terms chain-wide; guarantee replacements dispose the returned live goods
   through the treatment protocols, never re-shelf.
