# Human Resources & Employment Policies (POL-H)

> Domain manual of the [Corporate Policy Manual](README.md). Approval class:
> **CHRO + CEO** (W1729.4 HR class) for the conduct-, compensation- and
> labor-relations-bearing policies (H03, H06, H07, H08, H10, H11); **CHRO**
> for the employment-lifecycle policies (H01, H02, H04, H05, H09). Drafted to
> Philippine labor law: Labor Code
> (as renumbered), DOLE implementing rules, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG mandates,
> RA 11036 (mental health), RA 7877 & RA 11313 (safe spaces), RA 9165 (dangerous
> drugs), RA 10173 (employee data). The employment lifecycle executes through
> [PA-19.x (hire-to-retire)](../workflows/VS-19-hire-to-retire/).

## POL-H01 — Recruitment & Selection

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Talent Acquisition Manager / **CHRO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | PA-19.1; the talent-acquisition program ([VS-121](../workflows/VS-121-talent-acquisition-employer-brand-candidate-experience/), PA-121.1–121.3); workforce background screening ([VS-167](../workflows/VS-167-workforce-background-screening-credentialing-and-personnel-vetting/), PA-167.1–167.3); hiring authority per POL-G05 (W1732.1(d)) |

**Policy statements.**
1. Hiring follows an approved headcount (requisition vs TO) — no requisition,
   no hire; the 18-department structure and store/DC staffing standards are the
   supply plan of record.
2. **Equal opportunity:** selection on merit, qualifications and business need —
   no discrimination by age, sex, religion, disability, ethnicity or political
   affiliation; vacancy postings are internal-first per the talent-mobility rule.
3. Verification before offer: credentials, employment history, NBI clearance
   for sensitive roles (finance, LP, IT), and for cashier/LP roles the honesty
   screening panel; offers state rank-and-file vs managerial status, probation
   (≤ 6 months), compensation band and work location. **Screening runs as a
   governed program** (VS-167): vetting depth is proportionate to role risk
   (PA-167.1 tiering), candidate consent and RA 10173 handling apply to every
   check (POL-D02 — screening data is sensitive personal data, retained only
   per the schedule), and adverse findings follow the adverse-action steps
   with candidate explanation before withdrawal.
4. Interview records and applicant data retain per POL-D02/D04 (use limitation:
   recruitment only); referral bonuses disclosure rules apply to relatives of
   incumbents in the same approval chain (POL-G04).

## POL-H02 — Onboarding & Probationary Employment

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | HRBP (Field) / **CHRO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | PA-19.1/19.2; W541 store safe/asset issuance hooks; PA-150.1 drug-test step |

**Policy statements.**
1. Every hire completes: 201-file and government-mandated enrollments
   (SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG) within statutory windows, policy acknowledgments
   (Code of Conduct first), and role equipment issuance recorded in the system.
2. **Probation** (max 6 months, per contract) is governed by written standards
   communicated at hire: KPIs, conduct expectations, and the Regularization
   Board's assessment cadence; conversion, extension only for documented cause,
   or separation happens on the contract date — silence never auto-regularizes
   without the Board-standard assessment.
3. Pre-employment medical exam and (for covered roles) the drug test per
   POL-H08 are onboarding gates; store roles complete the safety orientation
   (POL-S01) before first solo shift.
4. Buddy/peer-trainer assignment and the 30-60-90 check-ins are mandatory for
   store and DC new hires; completion feeds the probation file.

## POL-H03 — Compensation, Benefits & Employee Welfare Programs

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Compensation & Benefits Manager / **CHRO + CEO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (survey cycle + statutory wage orders) |
| Anchors | PA-102.x (comp & ben), VS-144 (housing), VS-169 (uniform/PPE), employee transport & commute ([VS-141](../workflows/VS-141-employee-transport-shuttle-and-daily-commute-management/), PA-141.1–141.3) |

**Policy statements.**
1. Pay is set by the graded structure against published **market benchmarks**
   (survey analysis per the comp workflow): same grade, same band; out-of-band
   rates need CHRO + CFO approval with a documented rationale.
2. Statutory benefits are floor, not ceiling: 13th month pay, service incentive
   leave, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG, maternal/paternity/parental leaves,
   solo-parent and PWD entitlements — administered by Payroll Ops on calendar.
3. **Variable pay** (store incentives, commission plans, annual bonus) requires
   a funded, board-approved plan document per cycle; discretionary "off-plan"
   payouts are prohibited (W1729 anti-exception discipline).
4. **Welfare programs** operate as codified programs: staff housing/dormitory
   (VS-144 allocation policy, occupancy rules, deductions), uniform & PPE
   issuance (VS-169 entitlement matrix per role, replacement cycles, return-on-
   separation), the employee transport/shuttle and commute-allowance program
   (VS-141 — route/vendor safety per POL-S01, allowance framework per
   PA-141.1, never a substitute for wage obligations), HMO and wellness per
   plan rules (POL-S05).
5. Payroll accuracy is a control: variance review vs prior cycle, off-cycle
   payments need CHRO + CFO approval, and dedupe/bank-detail changes follow
   maker-checker (fraud control).

## POL-H04 — Performance Management & Talent Development

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | HRBP (Field) / **CHRO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | PA-19.3 (performance), PA-19.4 (L&D); succession and promotion workflows |

**Policy statements.**
1. Every employee carries written KPIs/OKRs cascaded from the annual plan;
   ratings calibrate in department panels before finalization — no uncalibrated
   distribution.
2. The cycle: goal-setting → mid-year check-in → year-end review → development
   plan; probationers assess at the contract milestones instead.
3. **Promotions and transfers** require: vacancy or new-cap justification,
   merit evidence (two cycles for promotion to manager), receiving-manager
   acceptance, and approval per POL-G05 HR authority; salary changes follow the
   band rules in POL-H03.
4. Training (PA-19.4): mandatory tracks (safety, conduct, data privacy,
   role-certifications incl. TESDA-standard technical tracks) complete on
   calendar; the training-hours target and the L&D budget roll through the
   annual plan; completion records are audit evidence.
5. Succession: each Executive Office role and each department head names
   ready-now/ready-later successors reviewed annually with the CEO.

## POL-H05 — Attendance, Leave & Working Hours

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | HR Shared Services / **CHRO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review (statutory changes immediate) |
| Anchors | PA-19.x payroll chain (timekeeping → payroll), store scheduling workflows |

**Policy statements.**
1. Schedules post in advance per the store labor-standard rules; overtime is
   authorized in advance except documented emergencies; night differential,
   rest-day and holiday premiums compute per statute automatically in payroll.
2. Leaves: statutory leaves ( SIL, VAC/SL credits per policy, maternal/paternity/
   parental, solo-parent, VAWC, gynecological and special leaves) administer on
   the HRIS with filing deadlines and medical documentation rules; leave without
   pay requires approval before absence except documented emergencies.
3. Tardiness/undertime follows the progressive-attendance matrix (POL-H06 for
   due process); no-shock rule: employees see their attendance ledger and can
   dispute within the payroll cycle.
4. Biometric and CCTV timekeeping data is employee personal data — access
   restricted, retained per POL-D04, never used beyond attendance/purpose
   notified (W1735 consent item).
5. **Remote & hybrid work** (where a role permits it): arrangements are
   manager-approved and time-bound in writing (the PA-19.3 workforce-
   management canon), stay governed by the same working-hours, overtime,
   leave and premium rules as on-site work (statements 1–3), and ride the
   endpoint and access rules of POL-I01/POL-I03 — a remote arrangement never
   alters statutory coverage, benefits or premium obligations (POL-H03).

## POL-H06 — Employee Discipline & Due Process

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Labor Relations Director / **CHRO + CEO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | Labor Code due process (twin-notice rule), W43 separation hooks, PA-19.x |

**Policy statements.**
1. Discipline is **corrective, progressive and documented**: verbal coaching →
   written warning → suspension → dismissal, with gravity-of-offense mapping in
   the Employee Handbook's offense table (just causes per Article 297/298).
2. **Twin-notice due process is mandatory:** (1st) written charge with
   opportunity to explain in an administered hearing; (2nd) written decision
   with findings. No dismissal without both notices and a reasonable interval.
3. Gross misconduct categories (fraud, falsification, theft, harassment,
   intoxication/drug positive, gross neglect, disloyalty/competition) route
   immediately to Labor Relations with evidence preservation instructions
   (system logs, CCTV, POS journal) per LP/Legal guidance.
4. Preventive suspension follows statutory limits while investigating; final
   decisions issue only after the investigation report and Legal review.
5. All disciplinary records live in the 201-file with retention per POL-D04;
   labor cases (NLRC/DOLE) manage centrally by Labor Relations with outside
   counsel per Legal — stores never appear or respond directly.

## POL-H07 — Anti-Harassment & Safe Spaces

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | HRBP (Field) / **CHRO + CEO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | RA 7877 (sexual harassment), RA 11313 (safe spaces), RA 11036; W1733 reporting |

**Policy statements.**
1. Zero tolerance for sexual harassment, gender-based harassment, and
   harassment on any protected ground — in stores, DCs, HQ, online, at company
   events, and between any covered persons (staff, vendors, customers).
2. The **Committee on Decorum and Investigation (CODI)** is constituted,
   trained and publicized per statute; complaint channels include HR, the
   speak-up hotline (POL-G03) and direct CODI contact.
3. Complaints investigate within the statutory framework: confidentiality,
   interim measures (schedule/venue separation), evidence handling, and written
   outcome to both parties; retaliation is independently sanctionable.
4. Training: all-staff awareness annually; manager training on receiving and
   escalating complaints is mandatory for supervisory roles.
5. Customer-perpetrated harassment protects the employee: service recovery
   never requires tolerance of harassment; de-escalation and LP involvement
   follow the store playbook.

## POL-H08 — Drug-Free Workplace & Substance Abuse

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | HSE Officer & CHRO / **CHRO + CEO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | RA 9165, DOLE/DDB guidelines; [PA-150.1](../workflows/VS-150-drug-free-workplace-and-substance-abuse-program/) |

**Policy statements.**
1. The company maintains a DOLE-compliant **Drug-Free Workplace Program**:
   written policy, mandatory briefing for all employees, and the designated
   company policy board.
2. Prohibited: manufacture, sale, distribution, possession, use of dangerous
   drugs; reporting to work under the influence of alcohol or drugs; alcohol
   service/consumption on premises except board-approved events.
3. **Testing** per program design: pre-employment for safety-sensitive and
   covered roles, random (neutral selection) and post-incident/for-cause
   testing through DOH-accredited clinics; positives get the statutory
   challenge/rehabilitation procedures before any personnel action.
4. Confidentiality: results are medical-confidential — access limited to the
   policy board and treating professionals; records retain per POL-D04.
5. Assistance: voluntary self-referral for counseling/treatment is supported
   and treated as a health matter when no offense has occurred.

## POL-H09 — Separation, Retirement & Clearance

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | HR Shared Services / **CHRO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | W43 (separation), PA-19.x exit workflows, banking-signatory cascade (W30) |

**Policy statements.**
1. Separations are lawful only for the recognized causes: authorized (retirement
   per plan rules, redundancy, retrenchment with DOLE 30-day notice, closure),
   just cause with POL-H06 due process, or authorized resignation with the
   notice-period contract.
2. **Clearance is a gate to final pay:** company assets (uniform/PPE per
   VS-169, devices, credentials, store keys, petty cash custodianship, company
   cards) return or transfer; system access cuts same-day through the JML
   workflow (POL-I02); banking-signatory and DOA updates cascade per W30/W1732.
3. Final pay, 13th-month proportion, and tax-accommodation (BIR 2316) release
   per statutory timelines; retirement follows the plan's definitions and
   statutory minimums.
4. Exit interviews capture reasons and policy-risk flags (harassment, safety,
   ethics) with read-out to CHRO quarterly; rehire eligibility is recorded and
   honored by TA (POL-H01).
5. GWEP/retirement-benefit administration, and separation-related payroll
   accruals, follow POL-F07 accounting policy.

## POL-H10 — Contingent, Outsourced & Foreign Workforce

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | HRBP (Field) + Labor Relations Director / **CHRO + CEO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (DOLE regulation changes immediate) |
| Anchors | [VS-98](../workflows/VS-98-contingent-contract-outsourced-workforce/) (PA-98.1–98.3, W555), [VS-160](../workflows/VS-160-global-mobility-immigration-and-foreign-worker-compliance/) (PA-160.1–160.3), [VS-183](../workflows/VS-183-dual-training-system-dts-and-tesda-partnership-program/) (DTS/TESDA, PA-183.1–183.3), POL-P03 service contracts, POL-I02 third-party access |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Contracting only, never labor-only:** every engagement of contingent or
   outsourced workers runs through a licensed service provider under a
   service-agreement structure (POL-P03) that preserves the permissible
   contracting arrangement — the company does not engage through
   labor-only contracting structures, and contractual employees perform only
   the usual services of the trade per DO 174-17; assessed labor-only
   findings convert to direct regular employment (labor-case protection:
   POL-H06.5 handling).
2. **Provider gate:** staffing/outsourcing providers verify DOLE registration
   (DO-174 compliance), capitalization, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG remittance
   current-ness and financial capacity before engagement and annually after
   (PA-98.1); providers failing verification exit the panel.
3. **End-to-end record:** every contingent worker has a system record
   (identity, engagement, site, access, time) through the VS-98 lifecycle
   (PA-98.2 onboarding/access/time operations) — no shadow headcount; store
   and DC managers may not source workers outside the panel.
4. **Foreign workers:** no foreign national starts work without the
   compliant work authorization (AEP, 9(g)/other visa, PEZA where
   applicable — PA-160.2); assignments run the mobility framework
   (PA-160.1 charter, tax/payroll/repatriation per PA-160.3); immigration
   compliance is a launch gate for any capability requiring foreign
   expertise, and overstays/underspent visa conditions are reportable
   events.
5. **Trainees & DTS:** student-trainee programs run only under the
   accredited DTS/TESDA framework (PA-183.1 accreditation, PA-183.2
   trainee lifecycle, PA-183.3 tax-incentive compliance) with the statutory
   allowances, hours and mentor ratios — trainees never substitute for
   headcount.
6. **Equal treatment floor:** contingent workers get site safety (POL-S01),
   drug-free-workplace rules (POL-H08), safe-spaces protection (POL-H07),
   and speak-up access (POL-G03); performance/risk/spend analytics
   (PA-98.3) review quarterly with CHRO and COO.

## POL-H11 — Labor Relations & Collective Bargaining

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Labor Relations Director / **CHRO + CEO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (CBAs, docket and jurisprudence changes) |
| Anchors | [VS-84](../workflows/VS-84-labor-relations-collective-bargaining/) (PA-84.1 union recognition & CBA negotiation/administration, PA-84.2 grievance & labor disputes/DOLE conciliation, PA-84.3 employee voice & engagement), Labor Code (Arts. 263–290, union & CBA framework), POL-H06 (discipline interface), POL-D04 (CBA/records retention) |

**Policy statements.**
1. The company deals with unions **lawfully and without interference**:
   certification elections run on employee free choice; no company unionism,
   no interference in organizing, no discrimination against union members —
   and equally, no acceptance of a union demand outside the legal process.
2. **CBA negotiation** (PA-84.1) runs only through the chartered
   negotiating panel (Labor Relations + Legal + affected COO/CHRO): the
   bargaining table is the only place economic terms negotiate — supervisors
   and store management never make or imply commitments to union
   representatives; ratified CBAs administer to the letter, with a compliance
   calendar for every monetary and non-monetary obligation.
3. **Grievance machinery** (PA-84.2): grievances follow the CBA's steps and
   timelines exactly — skipped steps invalidate the process; where no CBA
   applies, the published non-union grievance channel applies; DOLE
   conciliation/NCMB appearances are centralized in Labor Relations with
   outside counsel per Legal — stores never appear, respond or settle
   (POL-H06.5 discipline).
4. **Labor disputes & work stoppages:** pickets, strikes and lockouts are
   managed against the contingency plan — picket conduct rules (no
   obstruction of customers, safety-first, LP documentation), picket-line
   discipline communicated to all employees (crossing is an individual
   right, harassment is not), and business-continuity activation per
   POL-B01 where service threatens.
5. **Employee voice & engagement** (PA-84.3) runs as the standing
   non-union counterpart: labor-management councils, pulse surveys and the
   speak-up channel (POL-G03) — engagement findings route to owners like
   any audit finding; union-busting flavored "voice" programs are
   prohibited.
6. Every CBA, grievance settlement and conciliation agreement files in the
   labor-relations register with obligations calendared and financial
   commitments accrued per POL-F07 — an unrecorded labor commitment is a
   misstatement, not a courtesy.
