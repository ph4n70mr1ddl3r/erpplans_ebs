# Retail Store Operations Policies (POL-R)

> Domain manual of the [Corporate Policy Manual](README.md). Approval class:
> **COO** (operational class, W1729.4). These policies bind all 200 stores, the
> 4 DCs' front-door/store-interface operations, and the ecommerce fulfillment
> touchpoints that handle cash, stock or customers.

---

## POL-R01 — Cash Handling, Store Banking & Custody

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | VP Store Operations / **COO** (Bank-limit details: CFO) |
| Cadence | 1-year review |
| Anchors | W5F (daily cash collection & checklist), W1202 (armored-car pickup & deposit reconciliation), W30 (central matching), W25 (petty cash) |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Till discipline:** cashiers operate assigned drawers (no shared drawers);
   lifts to the safe occur at the float ceiling; till variances log per shift
   with cashier acknowledgment.
2. **Daily banking:** deposits prepare from POS takings per W5F and move via
   armored car or bank-branch deposit; the deposit slip, Z-report and system
   record must agree — same-day unmatched/short deposits investigate centrally
   (W30 step 4; POL-F06).
3. **Safe & dual custody:** the store safe opens only under dual control
   (two authorized keys/codes, never one person); safe counts verify per
   cadence with surprise counts by District Manager/LP; key/custodian changes
   register same-day.
4. **Petty cash** runs as a capped imprest fund (POL-F01.5): custodian named,
   receipts-backed replenishment, no loans/advances, monthly surprise count.
5. Checks accepted only per the check-acceptance rules (verification, limits,
   holding periods); DAIF/DAUD events route to the recovery workflow (W425) —
   stores never negotiate directly.
6. Currency overflows (above the insurance/transfer ceiling) transfer to the
   bank or DC vault per schedule; large-note marking/serial capture follows the
   LP supplemental rules for robbery deterrence.

## POL-R02 — Returns, Refunds, Exchanges & Trade-In / Haul-Away

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | VP Store Operations / **COO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | W1622 returns family (counter returns, exchange/no-refund/quality/refund dispositions), VS-186 haul-away canon, W707 omnichannel returns |

**Policy statements.**
1. The published return policy (window, condition, receipt/proof requirements)
   is customer-facing and uniformly applied; exceptions carry manager reason
   codes — free-text overrides without reason codes are audit findings.
2. **Refunds go back to the original tender** (card reversals to the same card,
   cash against cash with dual authorization above the store threshold);
   cash refunds for card payments are prohibited (fraud canon).
3. Dispositions: return-to-stock (resalable), damaged/packaging-functional
   (per the refurb/liquidation pool rules), quality-fail (vendor claim path,
   POL-P02), and hazardous (never to stock — POL-S04).
4. **Trade-in / old-appliance haul-away** (appliance categories): the program
   runs only with the documented handover form, serial capture, and staging-lane
   rules; old units never re-enter sellable stock without the refurbishment
   disposition.
5. Omnichannel returns (BOPIS buy-online-return-in-store, courier pickups)
   follow W707's orchestration: identity/order verification before payout,
   refund to original payment method, and fraud-flag queue for pattern
   outliers (serial returners — LP analytics).
6. Return-fraud patterns (receipt reuse, wardrobing, price-switch) are LP
   watchlist triggers; loss disposition rules follow POL-R05.

## POL-R03 — Pricing, Price Adjustments & Overrides

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Pricing Manager / **COO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review |
| Anchors | W40 (regular SRP), W13 (promotions), W93 (clearance/markdown), W468 (DTI price freeze), W5B price-type canon |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Price-type precedence** is system-enforced (W5B canon): clearance/markdown
   > promotional > competitor-match > quantity-break > trade/corporate >
   employee discount > regular SRP — cashiers select scenarios, never hand
   prices.
2. Shelf/POS price parity checks run on the store price-change cadence; a
   scanned price higher than the shelf price at the register triggers the
   honored-price rule and a ticketing defect ticket (POL-R05 tracking for
   repeat offenders).
3. **Regulatory freezes:** on a DTI price-freeze declaration (calamity state,
   W468), increases on covered SKUs are blocked in the pricing system; freeze
   compliance reports to Compliance on schedule; erring-store exposure is a
   severe sanction class.
4. Markdowns/clearance follow the markdown calendar with approval per value
   tier (POL-G05); clearance pricing does not re-base on interim regular-price
   changes (the W13 conflict rule); employee discounts follow the published
   entitlement, never stack beyond the system's allowed stack order.
5. Competitor price matches apply only per the published program rules
   (verified current advertisement, identical item, within geography) —
   cashier-judgment matches are not permitted.

## POL-R04 — Inventory Adjustments, Write-offs & Damage

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Inventory Control / **COO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review (thresholds re-based) |
| Anchors | Inventory adjustment workflows, cycle-count program, VS-05 transfers |

**Policy statements.**
1. Stock movements are system-recorded at the moment they happen — receiving,
   transfers (W-series), damages, claims, count adjustments; unrecorded
   "borrowing" between locations is a disciplinary offense even when stock
   returns.
2. **Adjustment authority:** counts/adjustments above store-level tolerance
   require District Manager or Inventory Control approval per the value tiers
   (POL-G05); the person counting never approves their own variance.
3. **Cycle counting** follows the ABC cadence (A-items most frequent);
   full/annual physical inventories run per the calendar with blind-count
   methodology; reconciliation posts within the close cycle (POL-F07).
4. **Write-offs** (damage, expiry, obsolescence, theft-finalized) require
   evidence (photo/inspection), reason-code classification, and dual approval
   at the tier per value; written-off goods are destroyed/disposed per
   POL-S03 — written-off stock re-entering sale is fraud.
5. Damage-prone flows (customer-damage claims, delivery refuses, supplier
   short-ship) have named correction paths; silence-and-scrape is prohibited —
   every known loss classifies to a reason code feeding shrink analytics
   (POL-R05).

## POL-R05 — Shrink Management & Loss Prevention

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Director, Loss Prevention / **COO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | VS-23 loss prevention (regional LP officers, investigations, analytics), POS exception analytics, CCTV estate |

**Policy statements.**
1. Shrink is managed as a measured program: known-loss accounting (damage,
   admin, vendor, cash) vs total shrink so investigations chase the unknown
   residue; store shrink rates publish in the scorecard with district
   benchmarking.
2. **Exception-based reporting** runs on POS data (refunds, voids, discounts,
   no-sales, price overrides by operator) with LP case triage; case evidence
   standards (POS journal, CCTV retention, witness statements) apply before any
   interview.
3. Investigations of employees follow POL-H06 due process and LP/legal
   evidence rules; customer/apprehension actions follow the citizen's-arrest
   safety rules — **employee safety outranks recovery, always** (no-chase
   policy for violent-risk scenarios).
4. CCTV/alarms: coverage standards per store format, retention per schedule
   (POL-D04), access logged; camera data releases only to authorized
   investigations/lawful requests.
5. Insider-risk controls: vendor-master/POS/void rights sit under POL-I02 SoD;
   LP has standing independent access to exception data without store-manager
   mediation; anonymized case learnings feed training quarterly.

## POL-R06 — Product Safety, Standards & Recall

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Regulatory Compliance Officer / **VP Legal & Compliance** (COO for store execution) |
| Cadence | 1-year review |
| Anchors | W29 (Product Recall Execution), W447 (DTI-BPS ICC/SOC certification), W41 (customer complaints), W479 (FDA inspection protocol), RA 7394 Consumer Act arts. 22–30 |

**Policy statements.**
1. Every SKU subject to mandatory **DTI-BPS certification** carries a valid
   ICC/SOC before it sells — certification status is a vendor-compliance gate
   (POL-P02) and a shelf-audit item; the product safety alert registry
   (certification gaps, active safety complaints, recalls, expired shelf life)
   reviews quarterly with Merchandising and Quality.
2. **Stop-sale is immediate:** any product identified as unsafe (regulatory
   notice, complaint cluster, inspection finding) pulls from shelf, ecommerce
   and BOPIS pick paths the same day — commercial impact never delays a
   safety pull; inventory quarantine follows POL-R04 evidence rules.
3. **Recalls** execute through W29: trace the affected lots/SKUs across all
   stores, DCs and the ecommerce catalog, notify customers where the law
   requires (warranty/registration data), process recall returns per POL-R02
   disposition rules, and recover costs from the vendor (claim per POL-P02).
4. Consumer-law integrity: warranty terms (RA 7394 implied warranties plus
   manufacturer warranties) honor per the warranty-management workflows
   (VS-53) — warranty non-compliance is a DTI case class (PA-22.2 triage).
5. Regulated categories with dedicated regimes (dangerous goods POL-S04,
   food-adjacent garden consumables, FDA-covered items per W479) follow their
   stricter standard where regimes overlap.

## POL-R07 — Gift Cards, Store Credit & Promotional Instruments

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | VP Store Operations / **COO** (CFO: financial treatment) |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | W28 (gift card sell/reload/store credit), CTL-49 (unauthorized balance manipulation), LP gift-card fraud analytics (PA-22.1 touchpoint canon) |

**Policy statements.**
1. Gift cards and store credit are company obligations issued only through the
   system (W28): activation, reload, and redemption post in real time — paper
   IOUs, manual ledgers and off-system cards are prohibited.
2. **Balance integrity:** manual balance adjustments require **dual approval**
   (Store Manager + AP Supervisor) with full audit trail (CTL-49); balances
   reconcile to the liability ledger monthly (POL-F07).
3. **Fraud controls:** the exception monitor watches the known patterns —
   brute-force balance inquiries, redemption-away-from-activation inside 24
   hours, return-to-credit-then-cash-out, employee-associated card activity,
   and high-value cash purchases (AML feed to POL-G07); confirmed fraud
   deactivates the card and files the loss report.
4. Terms: expiry, non-redeemability for cash (except statutory remainder
   rules), and lost-card treatment print/publish on the card and at checkout —
   terms changes never apply retroactively to outstanding balances.
5. Promotional instruments (coupons, store-credit promo credits) follow the
   same system-of-record, single-redemption and audit-trail rules; DTI sales-
   promotion permits (W427) precede any public promo instrument launch.
6. Layaway and other customer-deposit instruments run on the same
   system-of-record rules: receipted deposits, published forfeiture terms,
   notice before forfeiture (Consumer Act dispute class, PA-22.2), and
   refund of unforfeited balances on demand.

## POL-R08 — Advertising, Promotions & Promotional Claims

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | VP Marketing / **COO** (Compliance co-review) |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | W427 (DTI sales-promotion permits), RA 7394 truthful-advertising provisions, W13 (promo mechanics), PA-22.2 Consumer Act program |

**Policy statements.**
1. All public advertising and promotions are **truthful, substantiated and not
   misleading** (RA 7394): prices quoted are real, stock is adequate for a
   reasonable promo horizon ("while supplies last" only with a real supply
   plan), and savings claims compute from genuine reference prices.
2. **Permits before launch:** DTI sales-promotion permit (W427) precedes any
   contest, raffle, promo-with-purchase or loyalty multiplier campaign that
   requires one; campaign assets ship with the permit number where mandated.
3. Promo mechanics (eligibility, stacking order, exclusions, redemption
   windows) are approved with the campaign and configured in the pricing/POS
   systems exactly as advertised (POL-R03 precedence) — advertised mechanics
   the systems cannot enforce are blocked at launch review.
4. Comparative advertising names competitors only where lawful and
   substantiated; brand/influencer content follows disclosure rules; no ads
   target protected classes or exploit calamity states (price-freeze discipline
   per POL-R03.3).
5. Post-campaign: promo performance and complaint disposition (W41) review
   with Compliance; recurring complaint classes feed the advertising-standards
   watchlist.

## POL-R09 — Item Identification, Ticketing & Labeling

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Merch Ops & Master Data Manager / **COO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | W311 (Barcode, GTIN & Item Identification Master Governance), W4481 (GS1 standards & item-ID standards), W4484 (EAS/RFID tag selection & SKU-tagging), RA 7394 price-tagging rules |

**Policy statements.**
1. Every sellable item carries a governed identifier: GTIN/barcode per the GS1
   standard (W311/W4481) — no home-grown duplicate barcodes, no un-governed
   local SKU creation; label/tag specifications follow the auto-ID standards
   (W4481) and the EAS/RFID tagging rules (W4484).
2. **Price tags are mandatory and truthful** (RA 7394): every item displays a
   legible price (shelf tag, ticket or POS-displayed price per format);
   ticketing defects (missing/wrong tags) are same-day fixes with the defect
   feed to Pricing (POL-R03.2) and Inventory (POL-R04) for root cause.
3. Labeling content rules: required product information (country of origin,
   warranty statements, hazardous-goods labels per POL-S04, serial numbers for
   serialized appliances) present before the item reaches the floor; private-
   brand labels follow the brand and regulatory checklist.
4. Tag changes ride the price-change cadence (POL-R03) — hand-written or
   improvised tags are prohibited except the approved temporary-out-of-stock/
   system-outage forms.
5. RFID/EAS tagging levels per category follow the shrink strategy (POL-R05)
   and the tagging decision matrix (W4484) — over-tagging and under-tagging
   both deviate with documented approval.
