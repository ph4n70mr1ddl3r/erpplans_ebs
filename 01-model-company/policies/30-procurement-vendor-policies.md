# Procurement & Vendor Management Policies (POL-P)

> Domain manual of the [Corporate Policy Manual](README.md). Approval class:
> **CFO** (POL-P03: VP Legal & Compliance; POL-P04: Board, as a conduct-adjacent
> policy). Spans the VS-03 vendor
> lifecycle, the W2/W632 sourcing mechanics and the VS-131 responsible-sourcing
> program.

## POL-P01 — Competitive Sourcing & Bidding

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Procurement Manager / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | W632 (competitive bidding & tender management, ~15–25 tenders/year), W2 series (merchandise POs) |
| Version | 1.0 — initial issue, 2026-09-25 codification |

**Policy statements.**
1. Spend above the tender threshold is competitively bid: **≥ 3 qualified
   bidders** for tenders and ≥ 2 comparable quotes for sub-threshold buys;
   single-source awards require a documented sole-source justification approved
   one tier above the POL-G05 tier the spend requires.
2. Bids are evaluated on the pre-published criteria (landed cost, quality,
   service/lead time, compliance, vendor risk) — criteria never change after
   opening; the tender file (RFQ, bids, evaluation, award) is retained per
   POL-D04.
3. **Bid integrity:** no bidder receives another's pricing or terms; bid
   periods may not be shortened to favor a bidder; employees connected to a
   bidder (POL-G04) recuse; vendor-hosted hospitality is barred during an
   active bid (POL-G02.4).
4. Merchandise sourcing adds the margin/OTB lens (category strategy, W4
   replenishment economics) but never bypasses the ≥ 2-quote floor for new
   vendors or new categories.
5. Emergency purchases (stores inoperative, safety, regulatory deadlines) may
   direct-buy with COO written approval and retrospective tender within 30 days
   when the category permits.

## POL-P02 — Vendor Lifecycle (Onboarding → AVL → Exit)

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Vendor Management Manager / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | VS-03 vendor-management workflows (sourcing & onboarding, scorecards, development); the procure-to-pay chain ([VS-15](../workflows/VS-15-procure-to-pay/), PA-15.1 invoice processing & matching, PA-15.2 vendor payment & reconciliation); freight procurement ([VS-110](../workflows/VS-110-freight-procurement-carrier-management-and-freight-audit/), PA-110.1–110.3); packaging & RTI pools ([VS-111](../workflows/VS-111-packaging-pallet-and-returnable-transport-item-management/), PA-111.1–111.3); cooperative procurement ([VS-94](../workflows/VS-94-cooperative-community-enterprise-procurement/), PA-94.1–94.3); B2B cooperative partnerships ([VS-185](../workflows/VS-185-b2b-cooperative-credit-and-procurement-partnerships/)) |
| Version | 1.0 — initial issue, 2026-09-25 codification |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Onboarding gate:** no PO issues to a vendor not in the Approved Vendor
   List (AVL); onboarding requires verified registrations (DTI/SEC, BIR TIN,
   mayor's permit), bank details under callback verification (fraud control),
   tax status (VAT/non-VAT, 2307 eligibility), and signed terms per POL-P03.
2. **Scorecards:** vendors are scored on the published dimensions (OTIF,
   quality/rejects, fill rate, compliance docs, cost competitiveness); scorecard
   reviews drive the vendor mix — develop, maintain, conditional, exit.
3. **Development:** conditional vendors get time-boxed corrective plans
   (PA-67.3 vendor development); two failed cycles trigger exit.
4. **Exit & suspension:** quality/regulatory/fraud events suspend a vendor
   immediately (system-blocked at PO and payment); exits settle open POs,
   returns and claims before final payment and remove portal access same day.
5. **Channel folds — one vendor lifecycle, every spend class:** carrier and
   freight procurement (VS-110) onboards, scores, audits and pays carriers
   through the same gates (with the freight-audit step as the PA-15.1
   matching control); packaging/RTI suppliers and pool providers (VS-111)
   carry the same registration and compliance gates with pool-asset
   reconciliation (PA-111.2) as a contract term; cooperative and
   community-enterprise suppliers (VS-94) and cooperative B2B partners
   (VS-185) onboard through the AVL with the impact/fairtrade program
   (PA-94.3) documented in the vendor record — the credit side of coop
   trading follows POL-F05.6; and every payment against a PO flows through
   the three-way match and payment controls of the P2P chain (PA-15.1/15.2)
   — there is no off-channel vendor, no off-channel spend.
6. Vendor master changes (bank details, officers, addresses) follow
   maker-checker with callback verification — the change-audit trail is
   evidence in insider-fraud cases (VS-29 audit-trail canon).
7. **Rebates & vendor funds** (volume rebates, co-op marketing, promotional
   funds) are contracted, accrued and settled only through the rebate
   workflows ([VS-39](../workflows/VS-39-vendor-rebate-incentive/),
   PA-39.1–39.3): no verbal rebate deals, accruals reconciled to agreements
   quarterly, settlements credit-verified before P&L credit.
8. **Damage & freight recovery:** transit damage and carrier/vendor-caused
   losses file claims through the documented chain
   ([VS-90](../workflows/VS-90-damage-claims-freight-recovery/),
   PA-90.1–90.3) — identification & evidence at receipt, claim filing within
   carrier/vendor windows, recovery tracked to settlement and credited per
   POL-F07; silent absorption of recoverable freight damage is a control
   failure (POL-F06 revenue-leakage class).
9. **Global sourcing & agents** (VS-122): overseas buying offices and
   sourcing agents act as procurement intermediaries — agents onboard
   through this lifecycle's gates with the supplier code (POL-P04) and the
   anti-bribery intermediary rules (POL-G02: no success-fee structures that
   reward improper influence; agent commissions documented and market-
   benchmarked), and import buying executes through the customs chain
   (POL-P05) — the agent is a controlled vendor, never an authority
   shadow.

## POL-P03 — Contract Management

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Contracts & Commercial Manager (Senior Counsel) / **VP Legal & Compliance** |
| Cadence | 2-year review (template refresh) |
| Anchors | W230 (legal risk review), W1732 (execution authority), lease & vendor agreement flows |
| Version | 1.0 — initial issue, 2026-09-25 codification |

**Policy statements.**
1. No employee signs for the company outside POL-G05 execution authority;
   contracts use approved templates where they exist, with deviations flagged
   to Legal.
2. **Legal review gates:** all non-template contracts, and template edits
   touching indemnity, liability caps, termination, exclusivity, penalties,
   data sharing or IP assignment, review with Legal (W230) before execution.
3. Standard protections are non-negotiable floors unless VP Legal & Compliance
   approves: governing law (Philippines), dispute venue, data-processing terms
   per POL-D02, audit/inspection rights for supply contracts, anti-bribery
   repurchase/warranty clause per POL-G02.
4. Execution: dual signature follows POL-G05 for amounts > ₱500K; originals
   archive per POL-D04; key dates (renewal windows, escalations, expiries) load
   to the contract calendar with reminders — no evergreen auto-renewals above
   ₱1M without re-approval.
5. Lease administration (rent escalations, CAM, options) runs through the
   property/lease-admin workflows so payment terms never diverge from signed
   terms.

## POL-P04 — Supplier Code of Conduct & Responsible Sourcing

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Procurement Manager / **Board** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | [VS-131](../workflows/VS-131-human-rights-responsible-supply-chain-due-diligence/) (human rights due diligence), vendor scorecard compliance dimension |
| Version | 1.0 — initial issue, 2026-09-25 codification |

**Policy statements.**
1. The **Supplier Code** (appendix to standard terms) binds vendors to: no
   forced/child labor, no unsafe workplaces, freedom of association, legal
   wages and hours, no corruption in dealing with BuildRight, and environmental
   compliance with Philippine law.
2. **Risk-tiered due diligence:** high-risk categories (imports, labor-intensive
   manufacturing, commodities) get salient-risk screening and periodic
   assessments per the VS-131 framework; findings require time-boxed remediation
   or exit.
3. Onboarding requires Code acceptance; renewals re-affirm; material breaches
   (verified forced/child labor, bribery of BuildRight staff) are zero-tolerance
   exit events with LP/Legal referral.
4. Local/sustainable sourcing is preferred at equal cost/quality terms
   (documented in award decisions); restricted substances and packaging rules
   per POL-S03/POL-S04 apply to private-brand specifications.

## POL-P05 — Customs & Trade Compliance

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Imports & Customs Manager / **CFO** (VP Legal & Compliance: enforcement matters) |
| Cadence | 1-year review (tariff & CMTA changes immediate) |
| Anchors | [VS-87](../workflows/VS-87-customs-trade-compliance-tariff/) (PA-87.1–87.3, W2945+), [VS-182](../workflows/VS-182-b2b-bulk-project-custom-import-indent-sourcing-and-brokerage/) (indent/LC/port-to-jobsite, PA-182.1–182.3), POL-P02 onboarding gates, POL-F01 FX/settlement linkage |
| Version | 1.0 — initial issue, 2026-09-25 codification |

**Policy statements.**
1. Every import enters through the trade-compliance workflow with correct
   **tariff classification, origin declaration and customs value**
   (PA-87.1): classification per the governed HS ruling library, valuation
   per the transaction-value rules with additions declared — undervaluation,
   misdeclaration and ghost consignees are fraud, not optimization.
2. **Broker governance:** customs brokers (internal and outsourced) operate
   under written authority with a defined document-checklist discipline;
   broker performance and error rates review quarterly (PA-87.3); the
   company — never the broker — owns the declarant's legal responsibility.
3. **Preference & duty optimization is lawful-only:** FTA preference claims
   require documented origin qualification (certificates of origin, BOM
   evidence) before claim; duty drawbacks, bonded warehouse and exempt
   regimes use only their statutory pathways with complete records
   (PA-87.2) — aggressive schemes without statutory basis are prohibited.
4. **Indent/project imports** (VS-182): LC orchestration and financing ride
   POL-F01/POL-F08 limits; direct-port-to-jobsite deliveries keep the same
   document chain (contractor site rules per POL-S07 where applicable);
   demurrage/detention exposure is planned, not absorbed silently.
5. **Records & audits:** import entries, invoices, packing lists, permits
   and corrigenda retain per POL-D04 (BOC audit window); BOC/BOA audits and
   alert letters route through the PA-22.2 response protocol with Trade
   Compliance and Legal jointly owning the response; brokerage red flags
   (values that don't match payments, third-party payment instructions)
   escalate under POL-G03.
6. Live-goods and DG imports carry the additional quarantine/permit gates
   (POL-S04, POL-S06) — customs clearance never substitutes for those
   permits.
