# Procurement & Vendor Management Policies (POL-P)

> Domain manual of the [Corporate Policy Manual](README.md). Approval class:
> **CFO** (POL-P04: Board, as a conduct-adjacent policy). Spans the VS-03 vendor
> lifecycle, the W2/W632 sourcing mechanics and the VS-131 responsible-sourcing
> program.

---

## POL-P01 — Competitive Sourcing & Bidding

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Procurement Manager / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | W632 (competitive bidding & tender management, ~15–25 tenders/year), W2 series (merchandise POs) |

**Policy statements.**
1. Spend above the tender threshold is competitively bid: **≥ 3 qualified
   bidders** for tenders and ≥ 2 comparable quotes for sub-threshold buys;
   single-source awards require a documented sole-source justification approved
   one tier above the POL-G05 tier the spend requires.
2. Bids are evaluated on the pre-published criteria (landed cost, quality,
   service/lead time, compliance, vendor risk) — criteria never change after
   opening; the tender file (RFQ, bids, evaluation, award) is retained per
   POL-D04.
3. **Bid integrity:** no bidder receives another's pricing or terms; bid
   periods may not be shortened to favor a bidder; employees connected to a
   bidder (POL-G04) recuse; vendor-hosted hospitality is barred during an
   active bid (POL-G02.4).
4. Merchandise sourcing adds the margin/OTB lens (category strategy, W4
   replenishment economics) but never bypasses the ≥ 2-quote floor for new
   vendors or new categories.
5. Emergency purchases (stores inoperative, safety, regulatory deadlines) may
   direct-buy with COO written approval and retrospective tender within 30 days
   when the category permits.

## POL-P02 — Vendor Lifecycle (Onboarding → AVL → Exit)

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Vendor Management Lead / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | VS-03 vendor-management workflows (sourcing & onboarding, scorecards, development) |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Onboarding gate:** no PO issues to a vendor not in the Approved Vendor
   List (AVL); onboarding requires verified registrations (DTI/SEC, BIR TIN,
   mayor's permit), bank details under callback verification (fraud control),
   tax status (VAT/non-VAT, 2307 eligibility), and signed terms per POL-P03.
2. **Scorecards:** vendors are scored on the published dimensions (OTIF,
   quality/rejects, fill rate, compliance docs, cost competitiveness); scorecard
   reviews drive the vendor mix — develop, maintain, conditional, exit.
3. **Development:** conditional vendors get time-boxed corrective plans
   (PA-67.3 vendor development); two failed cycles trigger exit.
4. **Exit & suspension:** quality/regulatory/fraud events suspend a vendor
   immediately (system-blocked at PO and payment); exits settle open POs,
   returns and claims before final payment and remove portal access same day.
5. Vendor master changes (bank details, officers, addresses) follow
   maker-checker with callback verification — the change-audit trail is
   evidence in insider-fraud cases (VS-29 audit-trail canon).

## POL-P03 — Contract Management

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Contracts & Commercial Manager (Senior Counsel) / **VP Legal & Compliance** |
| Cadence | 2-year review (template refresh) |
| Anchors | W230 (legal risk review), W1732 (execution authority), lease & vendor agreement flows |

**Policy statements.**
1. No employee signs for the company outside POL-G05 execution authority;
   contracts use approved templates where they exist, with deviations flagged
   to Legal.
2. **Legal review gates:** all non-template contracts, and template edits
   touching indemnity, liability caps, termination, exclusivity, penalties,
   data sharing or IP assignment, review with Legal (W230) before execution.
3. Standard protections are non-negotiable floors unless VP Legal & Compliance
   approves: governing law (Philippines), dispute venue, data-processing terms
   per POL-D02, audit/inspection rights for supply contracts, anti-bribery
   repurchase/warranty clause per POL-G02.
4. Execution: dual signature follows POL-G05 for amounts > ₱500K; originals
   archive per POL-D04; key dates (renewal windows, escalations, expiries) load
   to the contract calendar with reminders — no evergreen auto-renewals above
   ₱1M without re-approval.
5. Lease administration (rent escalations, CAM, options) runs through the
   property/lease-admin workflows so payment terms never diverge from signed
   terms.

## POL-P04 — Supplier Code of Conduct & Responsible Sourcing

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Procurement Manager / **Board** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | [VS-131](../workflows/VS-131-human-rights-responsible-supply-chain-due-diligence/) (human rights due diligence), vendor scorecard compliance dimension |

**Policy statements.**
1. The **Supplier Code** (appendix to standard terms) binds vendors to: no
   forced/child labor, no unsafe workplaces, freedom of association, legal
   wages and hours, no corruption in dealing with BuildRight, and environmental
   compliance with Philippine law.
2. **Risk-tiered due diligence:** high-risk categories (imports, labor-intensive
   manufacturing, commodities) get salient-risk screening and periodic
   assessments per the VS-131 framework; findings require time-boxed remediation
   or exit.
3. Onboarding requires Code acceptance; renewals re-affirm; material breaches
   (verified forced/child labor, bribery of BuildRight staff) are zero-tolerance
   exit events with LP/Legal referral.
4. Local/sustainable sourcing is preferred at equal cost/quality terms
   (documented in award decisions); restricted substances and packaging rules
   per POL-S03/POL-S04 apply to private-brand specifications.
