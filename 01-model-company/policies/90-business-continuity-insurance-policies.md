## POL-B01 — Business Continuity Management & Crisis Response

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | ERM / Internal Audit Lead / **Board** |
| Cadence | 1-year review + exercise-driven updates |
| Anchors | PA-26.1 (BCP planning & testing), PA-26.2 (crisis management), VS-69 typhoon response, PA-184.3 (temporary facility mobilization) |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Impact analysis first:** every critical business function (store trading,
   POS/payments, DC outbound, ecommerce, payroll, treasury payments, EBS)
   carries a documented BIA with RTO/RPO feeding the recovery strategies — the
   BCP is requirement-driven, not binder-driven.
2. **Continuity plans per site/function:** stores use the store continuity
   playbook (generator, offline POS runbook, cash minimization, stock
   protection); DCs cover power, WMS degradation and outbound alternates; HQ/
   EBS recovery follows the IT resilience rules (POL-I04) and the DR site
   runbooks.
3. **Exercises:** tabletop twice a year, one functional drill (IT failover or
   store offline) and one typhoon-season readiness check annually; every
   exercise produces dated remediations tracked by ERM; the real events (Vinta/
   Odette-class) generate after-action reviews with the same discipline.
4. **Crisis organization:** the crisis team (CEO/COO chair, Comms, HSE, LP,
   IT, Supply Chain, HR) owns major-incident decisions (closure, evacuation,
   external statements); crisis communications release only through the
   designated spokesperson — stores and staff never speak for the company
   (social-media rule in the employee handbook).
5. Continuity of people: employee emergency-contact and muster systems stay
   current; post-disaster employee assistance (advances, housing support per
   VS-144 where affected) activates by standing rule, not ad hoc.
6. Recovery-phase commerce: temporary facility sales mobilization follows the
   PA-184.3 playbook (permits, safety clearance, cash controls) — trading may
   resume only after the safety assessment (POL-S02.2) and with the
   infrastructure-reconstruction program (PA-184.2) tracked to completion.

## POL-B02 — Corporate Insurance & Claims Management

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Facilities & Surety (program) + Finance (recovery) / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (renewal season) |
| Anchors | PA-26.3 (insurance claims & policy management), trade credit insurance (POL-F05), PA-184 insurance-appraisal coordination |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Coverage philosophy:** the board-approved insurance program (property/
   fire with business interruption, liability, group accident, vehicle fleet,
   money/transit/cash-in-vault, surety bonds, trade credit for approved
   accounts) is reviewed annually against asset values, risk assessment output
   and claims experience — under-insurance is a control failure; self-insured
   retentions set with CFO approval.
2. **Insured-asset register:** every insurable asset (sites, stock, fleet,
   equipment) reconciles to the coverage schedule at renewal and on material
   change (new store, remodel, DC expansion) — new sites bind coverage before
   opening.
3. **Claims discipline:** incidents with claim potential notify the insurer/
   broker within the policy window; evidence, documentation and the
   adjuster-coordination file maintain per claim (PA-26.3); disaster claims
   (typhoon damage) follow the PA-184.1 appraisal-coordination workflow with
   independent appraisal where values are material.
4. Recovery integrity: no claim inflates, duplicates or claims uninsured
   losses as insured; recoveries post per POL-F07 accounting policy;
   claim-denial root causes feed the risk-assessment cycle.
5. Surety bonds (performance, permit, lease) track expiry with renewal
   lead-time; bond issuance authority follows POL-G05.
