> Domain manual of the [Corporate Policy Manual](README.md). Approval class:
> **Board** for POL-G policies (W1729.4 — governance/critical class) except as
> stated per policy (POL-G09: CEO).
> Review cycle: 2 years unless stated. Operationalized mainly by

## POL-G01 — Code of Business Conduct & Ethics

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | VP Legal & Compliance / **Board** |
| Cadence | 2-year review; annual employee re-acknowledgment |
| Anchors | W1733 (speak-up), W1734 (COI); CTL register: conduct controls |

**Purpose & scope.** The minimum standard of honest, lawful, respectful conduct
for every director, officer, employee and covered contractor of BuildRight Depot
Corp. and its five subsidiaries, across HQ, all 200 stores, 4 DCs and field
operations.

**Policy statements.**
1. Employees act honestly, comply with all applicable Philippine laws and
   internal policy, and never trade company assets or information for personal
   benefit.
2. Conflicts of interest are disclosed per POL-G04 **before** any decision the
   employee can influence.
3. Company funds, assets and privileged information are used only for company
   purposes; financial records are complete and accurate — falsification of any
   record (expense, timekeeping, inventory, POS) is gross misconduct.
4. Outside employment or directorships that compete with, or supply, BuildRight
   require prior written CHRO + VP Legal & Compliance approval.
5. Confidential information (vendor pricing, margins, customer data, system
   credentials) is never disclosed outside the need-to-know chain.
6. Every covered person acknowledges the Code at hire and annually; the 10–15
   critical policies (this Code included) carry mandatory training per the
   W1729 anti-acknowledgment-fatigue discipline.

**Exceptions.** None to items 1–3. Others: VP Legal & Compliance + CEO in writing.

## POL-G02 — Anti-Bribery, Gifts & Entertainment

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | VP Legal & Compliance / **Board** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | W632 (competitive bidding integrity), P2P workflows; Rev. Proc. / BIR gift rules for deductibility |

**Policy statements.**
1. No employee may offer, give, solicit or accept anything of value to obtain
   or reward an improper advantage — including facilitation payments to
   government officials (LGU permit processing, BIR, customs) and private
   counterparties (vendors, landlords, banks).
2. **Government counterparties:** no gifts, hospitality or sponsorships of any
   value to officials of BIR, DOLE, LGUs, DENR, DTI, BSP or their families;
   lawful, published fees and expedited-program payments only.
3. **Commercial gifts:** nominal items (< ₱2,000 value per occasion, no cash or
   cash equivalents) may be accepted only if unsolicited, infrequent and
   declared in the Gift & Entertainment Register; anything above must be
   declined or surrendered to HR Shared Services for disposition.
4. **Vendor-hosted entertainment** is limited to business-reasonable occasions
   and is prohibited during an active bid, negotiation or vendor dispute with
   that counterparty (W632 integrity rule).
5. Providing gifts follows the mirror rules; marketing sponsorships route
   through POL-G06, never through operational budgets.
6. Violations are gross misconduct; reporting duty per POL-G03.

**Monitoring.** Procurement cross-checks the Gift & Entertainment Register
against the active-bid calendar quarterly; Internal Audit samples it annually.

## POL-G03 — Speak-Up & Whistleblower Protection

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Corporate Secretary / **Board** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | W1733, [PA-119.2](../workflows/VS-119-whistleblower-ethics-and-corporate-integrity-speak-up-program/) (case investigation) |

**Policy statements.**
1. Every employee (and external stakeholder) may report suspected fraud,
   corruption, safety risk, harassment or policy violation — anonymously or
   confidentially — through the speak-up channels (hotline, web form, direct to
   Corporate Secretary or Audit Committee).
2. **Non-retaliation is absolute:** dismissal, demotion, transfer, harassment
   or discrimination against a good-faith reporter (or helper) is itself gross
   misconduct, regardless of whether the underlying report is substantiated.
3. Reports are triaged by the Forensic / Fraud Investigator under Internal
   Audit; the subject of a report and their chain of command are walled off
   from the case until evidence gathering completes.
4. Cases involving Board members, executives or financial reporting go directly
   to the Audit Committee chair.
5. Case records are confidential, retained per POL-D04, and closed with a
   written disposition; substantiated findings feed Discipline (POL-H06) and
   control remediation.

## POL-G04 — Conflict of Interest Disclosure & Management

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Corporate Secretary / **Board** |
| Cadence | 2-year review; annual disclosure campaign |
| Anchors | W1734 |

**Policy statements.**
1. A conflict exists when a personal, family, financial or outside-business
   interest could — or could appear to — influence a company decision.
2. **Annual disclosure:** all managers, department heads, officers and staff in
   sensitive roles (buying, procurement, pricing, vendor management, finance,
   HR, IT, LP) file a COI declaration; all other employees declare at hire and
   on change of circumstances.
3. **Standing conflicts requiring recusal or approval:** hiring or supervising
   a relative (up to 4th civil degree); owning ≥5% of a vendor, customer or
   competitor; accepting loans or personal favors from vendors; side businesses
   selling to or buying from BuildRight.
4. Disclosed conflicts are logged by the Corporate Secretary, assessed, and
   managed by recusal, reassignment or mitigation with a named approver; the
   register is audited annually.
5. Buying and vendor-management staff re-certify their declaration before every
   annual vendor negotiation cycle (W632 season).

## POL-G05 — Delegation of Authority & Signing Authority

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Corporate Secretary / **Board** (thresholds) |
| Cadence | **Annual** review (inflation and org-structure adjustments) |
| Anchors | W1732 (matrix maintenance + ERP enforcement), W1732.3 (system limits) |

**Policy statements.**
1. Authority to commit the company is exercised only within the DOA matrix —
   there is no oral or implied authority.
2. **Financial approval tiers (W1732.2 canon):**
   - < ₱50K — Department Manager
   - ₱50K–200K — Department Head / VP
   - ₱200K–1M — C-Suite member
   - ₱1M–10M — CEO
   - > ₱10M — Board approval
3. **Dual signature** is mandatory for bank transactions > ₱500K; check
   signatories and bank portal rights mirror the matrix per entity (W30
   signatory lifecycle).
4. Contract execution authority follows the same tiers (lease signing, vendor
   agreements) per W1732.1(b); HR authority (hire, salary change, terminate)
   per W1732.1(d); operational authority (inventory adjustments, pricing
   overrides, markdowns) per W1732.1(e).
5. **System enforcement:** approval limits are configured in the ERP per role
   and the system blocks approval beyond authority (W1732.3) — a person may
   never self-approve their own request or expense.
6. Annual re-approval by the Board; signed manager acknowledgments maintained
   by the Corporate Secretary (W1732.5).

## POL-G06 — Political Engagement, Donations & Sponsorships

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | VP Legal & Compliance / **Board** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | [PA-132.1](../workflows/VS-132-corporate-political-engagement-election-compliance/) |

**Policy statements.**
1. The company does not make political contributions in kind or in cash to
   candidates, parties or campaign committees; corporate funds may never be
   used for election purposes (Philippine election-law exposure).
2. **Personal political activity is private:** employees may vote and support
   causes on their own time and resources, never in the company's name, uniform,
   store premises or systems.
3. Trade/industry associations and business chambers: memberships and
   position letters route through VP Legal & Compliance; advocacy positions on
   material issues (retail regulation, labor policy) are approved by the CEO
   and logged.
4. Corporate donations and sponsorships (charitable, civic, community) are
   budgeted, materiality-tested and approved: < ₱100K by the CMO/COO; ≥ ₱100K
   or any politically-adjacent recipient by the CEO; anything touching an
   official or election period by the Board. The donation register is maintained
   by Legal & Finance (corpus rule) and audited annually.
5. Election-period compliance: stores and DCs display no campaign material;
   store premises are never offered as campaign venues.

## POL-G07 — Anti-Money Laundering, Counter-Terrorist Financing & Sanctions

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | MLRO / Compliance Officer / **Board (Audit Committee)** |
| Cadence | 1-year review |
| Anchors | VS-86 (AML compliance program, W2921–W2932), CTL-237, AMLA (RA 9160 as amended); BSP e-money agent obligations for the e-wallet cash-in/out estate (W1301 settlement chain) |

**Policy statements.**
1. The company maintains a Board-approved **AML/CFT program** (MLRO-led):
   risk-based customer due diligence, the compliance manual, training, and the
   independent audit of the program — covering every covered channel: e-wallet
   cash-in/cash-out agency, gift cards/store credit, large cash retail
   transactions, and trade receivables.
2. **KYC/CDD:** covered customers are risk-tiered with due-diligence
   documentation and periodic refresh; sanctions/watchlist screening runs at
   onboarding and on list updates (CTL-237 canon).
3. **Reporting:** covered transactions (≥ ₱500K single-day cash) detect in the
   system with **CTR** filing; suspicious transactions file **STRs with AMLC
   within statutory windows** — tipping-off a subject about a filed report is a
   criminal-offense-class violation.
4. Record-keeping: covered-transaction and KYC records retain per the schedule
   (POL-D04) in the statutorily required form; the MLRO owns regulator
   interface (AMLC/BSP examinations route via Compliance like all PA-22.2
   inspections).
5. Escalation: suspected laundering inside the company (employee complicity)
   reports simultaneously to the MLRO and the speak-up channel (POL-G03);
   substantiated cases go to the Audit Committee.

## POL-G08 — Competition Law Compliance

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | VP Legal & Compliance / **Board** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | W2683 (Philippine Competition Law Compliance, PA-76.2); antitrust in the VS-119 ethics scope (ISO 37301 alignment) |

**Policy statements.**
1. The company complies with **RA 10667** (Philippine Competition Act): no
   price-fixing, bid-rigging, output-restriction or market-allocation
   arrangements with competitors; no abused dominance (predatory pricing below
   cost to exclude, exclusive bundling that forecloses suppliers).
2. **Trade-association discipline:** competitor contacts at association
   meetings avoid price, cost, capacity, customer or territory topics; agendas
   and minutes review with Legal; an employee walks out and reports any
   collusive discussion (protection under POL-G03).
3. **Supplier & customer dealings:** resale-price-maintenance conditions on
   dealers are prohibited; supplier exclusivity arrangements above materiality
   review with Legal (W230); recommended-resale-price language never becomes
   enforcement.
4. **Competitive intelligence** (PA-33.3 canon) collects only lawful public or
   consented information — never misrepresentation, theft of trade secrets, or
   competitor insider solicitation carrying confidentiality duties.
5. Dawn raids/SEC-PCC inquiries follow the Legal-managed response protocol
   (PA-22.2 inspection discipline); antitrust training is mandatory for
   merchandising, pricing, sales and strategy roles.

## POL-G09 — Intellectual Property & Brand Protection

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | IP Counsel / **CEO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | [PA-100.2](../workflows/VS-100-legal-operations-litigation-ip-management/) (IP Portfolio & Brand Protection), CTL-530, VS-71 (anti-counterfeit detection & enforcement) |

**Policy statements.**
1. The IP portfolio (trademarks, private-brand marks, domain names, designs,
   software copyrights) is registered, renewed and defended under the
   IP Counsel's portfolio program (W3265–W3272, W5563); brand usage on signage,
   packaging, marketing and the mobile/app estate follows the brand-usage rules.
2. **Company-created work:** employee-produced work product (code, designs,
   content, private-brand developments) is company property per employment
   terms; contractor engagements assign IP in writing before work starts
   (contract-template floor, POL-P03).
3. **Third-party respect:** no unauthorized use of third-party IP — music/
   ambient-media royalty licensing (in-store audio), fonts, images, software
   licenses, and franchise/vendor IP usage stay within license terms;
   open-source use follows the license-compliance rules in the SDLC standard
   (POL-I06).
4. **Anti-counterfeiting:** counterfeit suspicion (vendor goods, marketplace
   listings, in-store returns) routes through the VS-71 detection and
   investigation workflows; enforcement actions (NBI IPR Division, DTI,
   IPOPHL-recorded disputes) file only through Legal.
5. Employees may not bring counterfeit goods onto company premises or list
   suspect goods on any company channel; takedowns of counterfeit listings
   abusing BuildRight's brand execute through the marketplace-protection
   playbook.

## POL-G10 — Enterprise Risk & Third-Party Risk Management

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | ERM / TPRM Lead (Internal Audit & Risk) / **Board (Audit Committee)** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (appetite re-affirmation) |
| Anchors | W322 (risk appetite framework), PA-26.1 (BCP linkage), POL-P02/P04 (vendor lifecycle & code), POL-I02 (vendor access) |

**Policy statements.**
1. The company runs a standing **enterprise risk register**: strategic,
   operational, financial, compliance and technology risks, each with an owner,
   appetite statement (consistent with the W322 treasury risk appetite), key
   indicators and treatment plan — reviewed quarterly with the Audit Committee.
2. **Third-party risk management:** vendors/partners tier by criticality and
   data/financial exposure; tier-1 relationships carry due diligence (financial,
   security, continuity, integrity per POL-P04), contractual protections
   (POL-P03), and continuity/recovery commitments; concentration limits flag
   single-source dependencies.
3. Risk acceptance above appetite requires the approval tier per POL-G05 and
   is documented with expiry — permanent exceptions are prohibited.
4. Emerging-risk radar (regulatory change, climate/typhoon exposure,
   cyber threats, channel-model changes) feeds the annual planning cycle; risk
   incidents and near-misses (POL-I05, POL-S02, W1733 reports) update the
   register — the register is a living instrument, not an annual document.
5. BCP, insurance, and internal-audit plans derive from the register, closing
   the loop between risk identification and assurance coverage.

## POL-G11 — Corporate Governance, Board Operations & Disclosure

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Corporate Secretary / **Board** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (SEC Code alignment) |
| Anchors | PA-36.1 (board meetings & corporate records), W1730 (annual governance self-assessment), [PA-173.2](../workflows/VS-173-investor-relations-capital-markets-and-securities-disclosure/) (disclosure & securities compliance), SEC Code of Corporate Governance |

**Policy statements.**
1. The Board operates under a **corporate governance charter** with written
   committee charters (Audit, Governance, standing committees — PA-36.1
   register): membership, mandate, quorum, and reporting duties, re-affirmed
   with the annual self-assessment (W1730).
2. Board records — minutes, resolutions, Board Resolution authorizations (bank
   accounts per W30, capex per POL-F03, dividends per W327) — are the
   corporate record of authority; actions outside a recorded Board/C-suite
   resolution are invalid per POL-G05.
3. **Disclosure discipline:** regulatory and securities disclosures (where the
   entity is a reporting issuer — PA-173.2 governance) are accurate, timely and
   approved through the disclosure protocol; insider-list and trading-window
   rules apply to directors, officers and staff holding unpublished material
   information; selective disclosure is prohibited.
4. New-director onboarding (PA-36.1 program: entity structure, governance
   documents, orientation) completes before substantive committee
   participation; directors' independence and COI declarations follow POL-G04
   at the Board tier.
5. The five-entity structure (holding + operating companies) keeps
   documented bylaws, minute books and registrations current per entity;
   annual reportorial requirements (GIS, AFS filing) run on the Compliance
   calendar.
