# Finance, Treasury & Tax Policies (POL-F)

> Domain manual of the [Corporate Policy Manual](README.md). Approval class:
> **CFO** (Board for limits and thresholds, per W1729.4 and POL-G05).
> The Treasury domain codifies what **W322 (Treasury Policy, Governance & Risk

## POL-F01 — Treasury, Cash Management & Banking Authority

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Treasury Manager / **CFO** (Board: account structure, limits) |
| Cadence | 1-year review |
| Anchors | W322, W30 (daily cash position), W323 (ZBA sweeps), W25 (petty cash) |

**Policy statements.**
1. All company cash across the 5 entities is visible daily: bank balances per
   entity, pending inflows and outflows reviewed every business day (W30 step
   3); unbanked cash may not age overnight outside the store safe per POL-R01.
2. **Bank account lifecycle** (W30 touchpoint): accounts are opened only by
   Board-authorized signatories with Board Resolution and closed with CEO +
   CFO approval; the ~210-account register (store deposit, DC operating,
   entity main, USD import, payroll/savings/investment) is reconciled to the
   ledger monthly; no off-register accounts, ever.
3. **Zero-balance sweeps** move store collection accounts to entity main
   accounts daily (W323); idle balances above board-approved floors may be
   placed only in board-approved instruments (time deposits, T-bills, money
   market, SDA) — no speculative instruments, no crypto, no affiliated
   placements outside transfer-pricing rules (POL-F07 intercompany).
4. **Banking authority:** signatory tiers follow POL-G05 (dual signature >
   ₱500K); signatory changes execute within 10 business days of a role change
   or separation (W30) with system staleness alerts (> 12 months).
5. **Petty cash** operates only as board-capped imprest funds per store/entity
   (W25): custodian named, replenishments against receipts only, monthly
   surprise counts by LP or Internal Audit, and no salary advances or loans
   from petty cash.
6. Short-term funding (inter-entity loans, credit lines) uses documented
   agreements at arm's-length rates; the pooling/sweep mechanics live in W323.

## POL-F02 — Foreign Exchange Risk & Hedging

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Treasury Manager / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (policy limits) |
| Anchors | W30 step 10a (FX hedging policy canon) |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Natural hedging first:** match USD payables (import POs per the W2 series)
   with USD receivables where possible and hold USD balances covering 30–60
   days of forward import obligations.
2. **Forward contracts** are permitted only for committed import orders
   (> ₱5M equivalent): notional, rate, maturity and counterparty recorded in
   the system; **no speculative FX positions** — hedging only for committed
   payables.
3. **Exposure limits:** unhedged FX exposure capped at the 30-day rolling
   average of import payments; all forwards require CFO approval.
4. Monthly FX exposure reporting (USD payables, USD balances, forwards,
   unhedged net exposure, unrealized FX gain/loss) feeds the weekly cash-flow
   forecast; settlement gains/losses post to the ledger with realized/unrealized
   split (W30 step 10a(d)–(e)).

## POL-F03 — Capital Expenditure & Investment Approval

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | FP&A Manager / **CFO** (Board: > ₱10M per POL-G05) |
| Cadence | 1-year review (thresholds + hurdle rates) |
| Anchors | W1811 (capex workflow), W1732.3(c) (payment limits) |

**Policy statements.**
1. All capex (new stores, remodels, DC equipment, IT assets, intangibles)
   enters through the capex workflow with a business case: NPV/IRR vs the
   board-approved hurdle rate, payback, and opex impact — no PO against an
   unapproved capex line.
2. **Approval tiers follow POL-G05** (Department Manager → … → Board > ₱10M);
   the ERP capex workflow enforces the tier sequence and blocks out-of-authority
   approval.
3. Budget-year discipline: in-year capex additions above the delegated variance
   band re-approve at the tier the *increment* requires; emergency capex
   (safety, regulatory, business-continuity) may start with CFO written approval
   and Board ratification at the next meeting.
4. Capitalization follows POL-F07 accounting policy; post-completion reviews
   compare actuals vs case for projects > ₱1M, feeding the annual assumptions
   reset.

## POL-F04 — Expense Reimbursement & Business Travel

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Controller / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | VS-34 expense & procurement workflows; DOA tiers for approval |

**Policy statements.**
1. Business expenses are company-purpose, documented (official receipts — BIR
   requirement), and submitted within 30 days of spend; late claims need
   Controller approval and may be denied.
2. **Per-diem and travel classes** follow the board-approved rate card (by
   role band); air travel books economy for domestic and short-haul; hotels at
   negotiated corporate rates; no first class at company expense.
3. Approval follows POL-G05 tiers in the ERP expense module; **no
   self-approval**; approvers attest to business purpose and receipt validity.
4. Company cards (where issued) are for business use only; personal cash
   advances settle against liquidation within the cycle — outstanding advances
   block new advances and deduct from payroll per signed authorization.
5. Alcohol, fines/penalties from personal negligence, and entertainment
   violating POL-G02 are non-reimbursable.

## POL-F05 — Credit & Collections

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | AR / Credit Manager / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review (limits re-tiered) |
| Anchors | PA-16.2 (AR & collections), W5B trade account rules, trade credit insurance canon |

**Policy statements.**
1. Trade credit (B2B/corporate accounts) is granted only against a completed
   credit application, financials/checks, and a board-sanctioned credit limit
   per account; terms are net-30 standard unless CFO approves otherwise.
2. Limits and terms are system-enforced in AR; over-limit or over-term orders
   block at order entry — releases require the tier per the excess amount.
3. Collections follow the aging ladder (statement → call → demand letter →
   hold → legal); accounts > 90 days past due suspend trading; provisioning
   follows the ECL policy in POL-F07.
4. Concentration control: no single trade debtor above the board-approved share
   of receivables; credit insurance (trade credit insurance canon) covers
   approved large accounts, with claims per POL-B02.
5. Post-dated check warehousing/clearing and DAIF/DAUD recovery follow the
   dedicated workflows (W423, W424, W425) — checks are instruments of last
   resort and require credit-manager sign-off.

## POL-F06 — Revenue Assurance

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Revenue Assurance Analyst / **Controller** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | POS audit chain (PA-08.x), W5F store cash chain, VS-23 LP analytics |

**Policy statements.**
1. Every peso of POS takings reconciles to bank: Z-report ↔ deposit ↔ bank
   credit, daily at store level (W5F/W1202) and centrally via auto-matching
   (W30 step 4); unmatched or short deposits are investigated within 24 hours.
2. Promo/markdown integrity: discounts and overrides carry reason codes and
   are analyzed weekly for outlier patterns by cashier/store (LP analytics);
   no-offense-possible configurations (open discounts) are prohibited.
3. Revenue leakage reviews (unbilled B2B, unclaimed vendor funds, marketplace
   settlement completeness) run quarterly under Revenue Assurance with Finance.
4. Findings route to LP (POL-R05) when fraud-indicated, and to control
   remediation otherwise; recurring store-level exceptions escalate to District
   Managers and appear in the store scorecard.

## POL-F07 — Accounting, Financial Reporting & Tax Compliance

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Controller & Tax Manager / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (standards & BIR regulation changes) |
| Anchors | W9/W9A close workflows, PA-17.x (record-to-report), PA-79.x (BIR reporting) |

**Policy statements.**
1. Books follow PFRS for SMEs/large entities as applicable per entity, with a
   documented **accounting policy manual** (position papers for judgment areas:
   revenue recognition, ECL, lease accounting, capex vs opex, inventory NRV)
   maintained by Technical Accounting; the **Accounting Policy Analyst** runs
   the standards radar (W5528) for PFRS/BIR changes.
2. **Month-end close** follows the controlled close calendar (W9): subledger →
   GL reconciliations are mandatory before close sign-off; unposted or held
   entries are reported, not forced.
3. **Intercompany:** transactions follow written intercompany service agreements
   at arm's-length pricing with board authorization (W1731); allocation keys
   are pre-agreed and documented; intercompany profit in inventory eliminates
   at consolidation (W234); transfer pricing documentation (BIR Form 1709 /
   related-party disclosures) is current.
4. **Tax:** BIR filings (withholding 1601/2307, VAT 2550/2551, income tax)
   file on calendar with zero-miss tracking (PA-79.x); tax positions with
   ambiguity require Tax Manager sign-off and documentation; no undocumented
   tax schemes; BIR audits are managed centrally by Tax with Legal support.
5. **Financial statement integrity:** no off-ledger accounts, no round-trip
   revenue, no management overrides of controls — override attempts are
   reportable under POL-G03; external audit findings remediate on timeline
   tracked by the Audit Committee.
