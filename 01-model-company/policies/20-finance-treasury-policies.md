# Finance, Treasury & Tax Policies (POL-F)

> Domain manual of the [Corporate Policy Manual](README.md). Approval class:
> **CFO** (Board for limits and thresholds, per W1729.4 and POL-G05).
> The Treasury domain codifies what **W322 (Treasury Policy, Governance & Risk
> Appetite Framework)** already canonizes; this manual is the corporate-facing
> statement of those rules.

## POL-F01 — Treasury, Cash Management & Banking Authority

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Treasury Manager / **CFO** (Board: account structure, limits) |
| Cadence | 1-year review |
| Anchors | W322, W30 (daily cash position), W323 (ZBA sweeps), W25 (petty cash), supply-chain-finance programs ([VS-105](../workflows/VS-105-supply-chain-finance-working-capital-management/), PA-105.1–105.3) |

**Policy statements.**
1. All company cash across the 5 entities is visible daily: bank balances per
   entity, pending inflows and outflows reviewed every business day (W30 step
   3); unbanked cash may not age overnight outside the store safe per POL-R01.
2. **Bank account lifecycle** (W30 touchpoint): accounts are opened only by
   Board-authorized signatories with Board Resolution and closed with CEO +
   CFO approval; the ~210-account register (store deposit, DC operating,
   entity main, USD import, payroll/savings/investment) is reconciled to the
   ledger monthly; no off-register accounts, ever.
3. **Zero-balance sweeps** move store collection accounts to entity main
   accounts daily (W323); idle balances above board-approved floors may be
   placed only in board-approved instruments (time deposits, T-bills, money
   market, SDA) — no speculative instruments, no crypto, no affiliated
   placements outside transfer-pricing rules (POL-F07 intercompany).
4. **Banking authority:** signatory tiers follow POL-G05 (dual signature >
   ₱500K); signatory changes execute within 10 business days of a role change
   or separation (W30) with system staleness alerts (> 12 months).
5. **Petty cash** operates only as board-capped imprest funds per store/entity
   (W25): custodian named, replenishments against receipts only, monthly
   surprise counts by LP or Internal Audit, and no salary advances or loans
   from petty cash.
6. Short-term funding (inter-entity loans, credit lines) uses documented
   agreements at arm's-length rates; the pooling/sweep mechanics live in W323.
7. **Supplier-finance programs** (reverse factoring, dynamic discounting —
   VS-105) are treasury-governed funding arrangements: program terms
   CFO-approved with the bank/funder, participation is vendor-opt-in and
   payment-term-neutral for the buyer, and the arrangement's accounting
   presentation (financial debt vs trade payable, per PFRS) documents in the
   POL-F07 policy manual — supply-chain finance never disguises borrowing
   or transfers funding cost onto vendors silently.

## POL-F02 — Foreign Exchange Risk & Hedging

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Treasury Manager / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (policy limits) |
| Anchors | W30 step 10a (FX hedging policy canon), commodity input-cost hedging ([VS-106](../workflows/VS-106-commodity-input-cost-risk-management/), PA-106.2) |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Natural hedging first:** match USD payables (import POs per the W2 series)
   with USD receivables where possible and hold USD balances covering 30–60
   days of forward import obligations.
2. **Forward contracts** are permitted only for committed import orders
   (> ₱5M equivalent): notional, rate, maturity and counterparty recorded in
   the system; **no speculative FX positions** — hedging only for committed
   payables.
3. **Exposure limits:** unhedged FX exposure capped at the 30-day rolling
   average of import payments; all forwards require CFO approval.
4. Monthly FX exposure reporting (USD payables, USD balances, forwards,
   unhedged net exposure, unrealized FX gain/loss) feeds the weekly cash-flow
   forecast; settlement gains/losses post to the ledger with realized/unrealized
   split (W30 step 10a(d)–(e)).
5. **Commodity input-cost hedges** (fuel, metals, freight indices — the
   VS-106 exposure classes) follow the same discipline as FX: hedge only
   genuine anticipated purchases, instrument types on the approved list,
   CFO-approved notionals and tenors, no speculative positions — and the
   pass-through/index-pricing decisions (PA-106.3) stay a pricing decision
   (POL-R03), never a hedge rationale.

## POL-F03 — Capital Expenditure & Investment Approval

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | FP&A Manager / **CFO** (Board: > ₱10M per POL-G05) |
| Cadence | 1-year review (thresholds + hurdle rates) |
| Anchors | W1811 (capex workflow), W1732.3(c) (payment limits), M&A/divestiture gates ([VS-130](../workflows/VS-130-corporate-development-ma-divestiture/), PA-130.1–130.3) |

**Policy statements.**
1. All capex (new stores, remodels, DC equipment, IT assets, intangibles)
   enters through the capex workflow with a business case: NPV/IRR vs the
   board-approved hurdle rate, payback, and opex impact — no PO against an
   unapproved capex line.
2. **Approval tiers follow POL-G05** (Department Manager → … → Board > ₱10M);
   the ERP capex workflow enforces the tier sequence and blocks out-of-authority
   approval.
3. Budget-year discipline: in-year capex additions above the delegated variance
   band re-approve at the tier the *increment* requires; emergency capex
   (safety, regulatory, business-continuity) may start with CFO written approval
   and Board ratification at the next meeting.
4. Capitalization follows POL-F07 accounting policy; post-completion reviews
   compare actuals vs case for projects > ₱1M, feeding the annual assumptions
   reset.
5. **Corporate-development transactions** (acquisitions, joint ventures,
   divestitures — VS-130) are investment decisions at the reserved Board tier
   (POL-G05.7): feasibility and pipeline per PA-130.1, due diligence and
   structuring per PA-130.2 with Legal/Tax at the table, integration/
   separation per PA-130.3 — capex-workflow discipline (business case,
   hurdle rates, post-completion review) applies to the program's capital
   components without diluting the reserved authority.

## POL-F04 — Expense Reimbursement & Business Travel

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Controller / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | VS-34 expense & procurement workflows; DOA tiers for approval |

**Policy statements.**
1. Business expenses are company-purpose, documented (official receipts — BIR
   requirement), and submitted within 30 days of spend; late claims need
   Controller approval and may be denied.
2. **Per-diem and travel classes** follow the board-approved rate card (by
   role band); air travel books economy for domestic and short-haul; hotels at
   negotiated corporate rates; no first class at company expense.
3. Approval follows POL-G05 tiers in the ERP expense module; **no
   self-approval**; approvers attest to business purpose and receipt validity.
4. Company cards (where issued) are for business use only; personal cash
   advances settle against liquidation within the cycle — outstanding advances
   block new advances and deduct from payroll per signed authorization.
5. Alcohol, fines/penalties from personal negligence, and entertainment
   violating POL-G02 are non-reimbursable.

## POL-F05 — Credit & Collections

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | AR & Credit Manager / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 2-year review (limits re-tiered) |
| Anchors | PA-16.2 (AR & collections), the trade-credit lifecycle ([VS-68](../workflows/VS-68-trade-credit-risk-management/), PA-68.1 risk assessment, PA-68.2 limits & monitoring, PA-68.3 recovery/write-off), W5B trade account rules, the credit-bearing B2B channels — trade program ([VS-43](../workflows/VS-43-trade-professional-program/)), sari-sari/micro-wholesale ([VS-82](../workflows/VS-82-sari-sari-msme-micro-wholesale/)), strategic key accounts ([VS-107](../workflows/VS-107-strategic-key-account-enterprise-customer-management/)), cooperatives ([VS-185](../workflows/VS-185-b2b-cooperative-credit-and-procurement-partnerships/)) — trade credit insurance canon |

**Policy statements.**
1. Trade credit (B2B/corporate accounts) is granted only against a completed
   credit application, financials/checks, and a board-sanctioned credit limit
   per account; terms are net-30 standard unless CFO approves otherwise.
2. Limits and terms are system-enforced in AR; over-limit or over-term orders
   block at order entry — releases require the tier per the excess amount.
3. Collections follow the aging ladder (statement → call → demand letter →
   hold → legal); accounts > 90 days past due suspend trading; provisioning
   follows the ECL policy in POL-F07.
4. Concentration control: no single trade debtor above the board-approved share
   of receivables; credit insurance (trade credit insurance canon) covers
   approved large accounts, with claims per POL-B02.
5. Post-dated check warehousing/clearing and DAIF/DAUD recovery follow the
   dedicated workflows (W423, W424, W425) — checks are instruments of last
   resort and require credit-manager sign-off.
6. **One credit framework, every channel:** trade-program professionals,
   micro-wholesale (sari-sari) accounts, key accounts and cooperative
   partners all onboard through the VS-68 credit lifecycle — application,
   scored risk assessment (PA-68.1), system-enforced limits and monitoring
   (PA-68.2), and recovery/write-off discipline (PA-68.3) — no channel,
   however small the ticket, grants informal credit outside the framework;
   channel-specific terms (rebates, settlement cycles per PA-185.3) ride on
   top of, never around, the limits.

## POL-F06 — Revenue Assurance

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Revenue Assurance Analyst / **Controller** |
| Cadence | 2-year review |
| Anchors | POS audit chain (PA-08.x), the revenue-assurance program ([VS-118](../workflows/VS-118-revenue-assurance-pricing-integrity-and-leakage-management/), PA-118.1 leak-detection framework, PA-118.2 pricing/promotion/payment integrity monitoring, PA-118.3 recovery & analytics), W5F store cash chain, VS-23 LP analytics |

**Policy statements.**
1. Every peso of POS takings reconciles to bank: Z-report ↔ deposit ↔ bank
   credit, daily at store level (W5F/W1202) and centrally via auto-matching
   (W30 step 4); unmatched or short deposits are investigated within 24 hours.
2. Promo/markdown integrity: discounts and overrides carry reason codes and
   are analyzed weekly for outlier patterns by cashier/store (LP analytics);
   no-offense-possible configurations (open discounts) are prohibited.
3. Revenue leakage reviews run continuously under the VS-118 program:
   unbilled B2B, unclaimed vendor funds, marketplace settlement completeness,
   promotion/loyalty payment integrity (PA-118.2) — quarterly under Revenue
   Assurance with Finance, with the leak-detection framework (PA-118.1) as
   the standing method and recovery tracked to closure (PA-118.3).
4. Findings route to LP (POL-R05) when fraud-indicated, and to control
   remediation otherwise; recurring store-level exceptions escalate to District
   Managers and appear in the store scorecard.

## POL-F07 — Accounting, Financial Reporting & Tax Compliance

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Controller & Tax Manager / **CFO** |
| Cadence | 1-year review (standards & BIR regulation changes) |
| Anchors | W9/W9A close workflows, PA-17.x (record-to-report), PA-79.x (BIR reporting) |

**Policy statements.**
1. Books follow PFRS for SMEs/large entities as applicable per entity, with a
   documented **accounting policy manual** (position papers for judgment areas:
   revenue recognition, ECL, lease accounting, capex vs opex, inventory NRV)
   maintained by Technical Accounting; the **Accounting Policy Analyst** runs
   the standards radar (W5528) for PFRS/BIR changes.
2. **Month-end close** follows the controlled close calendar (W9): subledger →
   GL reconciliations are mandatory before close sign-off; unposted or held
   entries are reported, not forced.
3. **Intercompany:** transactions follow written intercompany service agreements
   at arm's-length pricing with board authorization (W1731); allocation keys
   are pre-agreed and documented; intercompany profit in inventory eliminates
   at consolidation (W234); transfer pricing documentation (BIR Form 1709 /
   related-party disclosures) is current.
4. **Tax:** BIR filings (withholding 1601/2307, VAT 2550/2551, income tax)
   file on calendar with zero-miss tracking (PA-79.x); tax positions with
   ambiguity require Tax Manager sign-off and documentation; no undocumented
   tax schemes; BIR audits are managed centrally by Tax with Legal support.
5. **Financial statement integrity:** no off-ledger accounts, no round-trip
   revenue, no management overrides of controls — override attempts are
   reportable under POL-G03; external audit findings remediate on timeline
   tracked by the Audit Committee.
6. **Standards folds:** lease accounting follows PFRS 16 through the
   lease-accounting workflows ([VS-148](../workflows/VS-148-lease-accounting-pfrs-16-and-right-of-use-asset-management/),
   PA-148.1–148.3 — recognition, ROU measurement, lease administration);
   **revenue recognition** follows PFRS 15 through the
   [VS-157](../workflows/VS-157-revenue-recognition-pfrs-15-and-complex-contract-accounting/)
   workflows (PA-157.1 performance-obligation identification, PA-157.2 SSP
   allocation & deferred revenue, PA-157.3 close/disclosure & new-stream
   onboarding — delivery/installation/extended-warranty bundles never
   recognize before their obligation satisfies);
   **fixed assets** follow the [VS-35](../workflows/VS-35-fixed-asset-management/)
   lifecycle (PA-35.1 registration, PA-35.2 depreciation & reporting,
   PA-35.3 physical verification & disposal — no disposal leaves the books
   without the PA-35.3 gate);
   product costing (standard cost, landed cost, cost rolls, variance
   analysis) follows the costing policy in
   [VS-158](../workflows/VS-158-product-costing-landed-cost-and-cost-accounting/)
   (PA-158.1–158.3) — all are POL-F07 enforcement surfaces, not free-standing
   local practices.

## POL-F08 — Financing & Lending Activities

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Treasury Manager / **CFO** (Board: new programs & limits) |
| Cadence | 1-year review (BSP/SEC regulation changes immediate) |
| Anchors | [VS-154](../workflows/VS-154-home-construction-finance-loan-brokerage-and-mortgage-referral/) (construction-finance referral, PA-154.1–154.3), [VS-188](../workflows/VS-188-trade-reseller-floor-plan-and-dealer-inventory-financing/) (floor-plan financing, PA-188.1–188.3), [VS-189](../workflows/VS-189-trade-receivables-factoring-invoice-discounting-and-securitization/) (receivables financing, PA-189.1–189.3), [VS-170](../workflows/VS-170-inventory-pledge-asset-based-lending-and-trust-receipt-financing/) (inventory pledge/trust receipts, PA-170.1–170.3), [VS-181](../workflows/VS-181-b2b-project-financing-escrow-account-orchestration-and-lien-release/) (escrow/lien, PA-181.x), [VS-96](../workflows/VS-96-equipment-leasing-capital-equipment-finance/) (equipment finance, PA-96.x), consumer installment financing ([VS-38](../workflows/VS-38-consumer-credit-financing/), PA-38.1–38.3), VS-156 (financial-agency products, PA-156.1) |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Charter boundary:** the company operates financing activities only as
   board-approved programs — (a) **referral/brokerage** of third-party
   construction/home loans (VS-154), (b) **floor-plan/dealer inventory
   financing** for trade resellers (VS-188), (c) **receivables financing**
   (factoring/discounting of BuildRight's own or purchased receivables,
   VS-189), (d) **collateralized import/inventory financing** (trust
   receipts, inventory pledges — VS-170), (e) **escrow orchestration** for
   B2B project financing (VS-181), (f) **equipment leasing/finance**
   (VS-96), and (g) **consumer installment financing** at the counter
   (in-house installment sale processing and bank/PF-partner consumer
   finance, VS-38: program design per PA-38.1, installment processing per
   PA-38.2, settlement & reconciliation per PA-38.3). Anything outside this
   charter (deposits-taking, lending to the public at large) is prohibited —
   that is regulated financial-institution territory.
2. **Regulatory perimeter:** every program maps its regulator before launch —
   BSP coverage assessed for credit/lending exposure (RA 3765 Truth in
   Lending, BSP circulars; RA 9474/RA 8556 where lending investors/financing
   companies are involved), SEC registration for financing/factoring
   activity, PD 115 (Investment Contracts Law) screening for
   any investment-soliciting product, RA 11057 for security interests in
   movable property. No program launches without Compliance's written
   perimeter memo (POL-G07 AML obligations assessed at the same gate —
   lending is a covered activity).
3. **Credit discipline:** programs run on a funded credit framework
   (underwriting standards, exposure limits per counterparty, portfolio
   concentration caps, impairment provisioning per POL-F07 ECL policy);
   curtailment/collections follow the program workflows; delinquency and
   portfolio analytics report monthly to CFO and quarterly to the Audit
   Committee.
4. **Customer protection:** truth-in-lending disclosures, published fees,
   no hidden referral commissions (disclosed to the customer per BSP/SEC
   rules), complaint routing through the standard W41/PA-22.2 chain, and
   data handling per POL-D01/D02 (credit applications are sensitive
   personal data).
5. **Segregation:** financing-program cash never commingles with operating
   or store cash (POL-F01/POL-R01); escrow and lien accounts are
   trust-accounted with independent reconciliation (PA-181.x); referral
   commissions accrue only on documented, settled placements.

## POL-F09 — Property Acquisition, Land-Use & Lease Compliance

| | |
|---|---|
| Owner (R) / Approver | Director, Facilities & Real Estate (program) + Controller (accounting) / **CFO** (Board: acquisitions per POL-G05.7) |
| Cadence | 1-year review (statute & valuation changes) |
| Anchors | corporate real-estate portfolio ([VS-97](../workflows/VS-97-corporate-real-estate-property-portfolio/), PA-97.1–97.3), lease administration ([VS-42](../workflows/VS-42-property-lease-admin/), PA-42.1–42.3), real-estate development & construction ([VS-20](../workflows/VS-20-real-estate-construction/), PA-20.1–20.3), landbanking & land-use conversion ([VS-178](../workflows/VS-178-landbanking-site-acquisition-and-agrarian-lgu-zoning-conversion/), PA-178.1–178.3), lease accounting per POL-F07.6 (PFRS 16, VS-148) |

**Policy statements.**
1. **Acquisition gate:** every site acquisition (purchase, JV, long lease)
   enters through the feasibility workflow (PA-178.1/PA-97.1) — title
   verified clean (no adverse claims, correct classification), valuation by
   independent appraisal above materiality, Board approval at the reserved
   tier before any binding instrument (OPT/ejemplo-style option money only
   under written, time-boxed options).
2. **Land-use & agrarian compliance is a closing condition, not a follow-up**
   (PA-178.2): agricultural land completes DAR conversion before development
   commit; ancestral-domain claims screen through the NCIP/FPIC process where
   the land sits inside or adjacent to ancestral domains (RA 8371); zoning
   and reclassification clearances verify with the LGU (PA-178.3) — no
   development funds release against unconverted or unzoned land, and
   landbanking holdings carry documented conversion roadmaps reviewed
   annually.
3. **Lease administration** (PA-42.1–42.3): executed leases live in the
   system of record with critical dates (renewal options, escalation
   windows, exit clauses) calendared; rent escalations and CAM charges
   validate against contract before payment (PA-42.2); property taxes
   reconcile to assessments with exemption checks (PA-42.3) — no lease
   renews silently, no lessor escalation pays unvalidated.
4. **Development & construction execution** (VS-20) follows the capex
   policy (POL-F03) and the contractor-licensing policy (POL-S07): PCAB-
   licensed contractors, permits before mobilization, retention/bonding per
   contract (PA-20.2), and facility maintenance handover (PA-20.3) into the
   asset register (PA-35.1) — a completed building is never an unregistered
   asset.
5. **Accounting & disclosure:** leases recognize per PFRS 16 (POL-F07.6);
   encumbrances (mortgages, liens on acquired land) register in the
   encumbrance ledger (POL-B03.2); disposal of property follows the
   Board-tier reserved authority (POL-G05.7) with gain/loss reviewed by the
   Controller before booking.
