## W5137. Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR) Clearance & Exemption Application

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Title search (W5130) showing CARP/agrarian patents or agrarian reform land cover |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per transaction |
| **Volume** | ~5–8 clearances annually |
| **Owner** | Land Acquisition Manager / Regulatory Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR) Municipal/Provincial offices, Seller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct verification at the provincial DAR office to check if the land is subject to Comprehensive Agrarian Reform Program (CARP) coverage or land retention limits. | Land Acquisition Manager / Regulatory Compliance Officer | Land Acquisition Manager / Regulatory Compliance Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | File application for DAR Clearance (for private agricultural lands) or Exemption Clearance (for lands reclassified prior to June 15, 1988). | Land Acquisition Manager / Regulatory Compliance Officer | Land Acquisition Manager / Regulatory Compliance Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Coordinate provincial DAR inspection to verify no tenant farmers, active cultivation, or CARP coverage violations exist. | Land Acquisition Manager / Regulatory Compliance Officer | Land Acquisition Manager / Regulatory Compliance Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Secure the formal DAR Clearance Certificate / Exemption Order and file in the transaction folder. | Land Acquisition Manager / Regulatory Compliance Officer | Land Acquisition Manager / Regulatory Compliance Officer | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- DAR database, Regulatory compliance manager system (VS-22).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **CARP coverage block**: Land is designated CARP-distributable and cannot be sold; mitigated by pre-acquisition DAR database checks (W5130).

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-765 (ensure controlled execution — Agrarian Reform, NCIP Ancestral Domain & Land Use Conversion Operations (PA-178.2).)
- CTL-765: Exemption Order signed by DAR Regional Director or Secretary; verified by Legal before escrow funds release.

### Time Estimate
- ~30-60 days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-22 (compliance), VS-100 (legal)

## W5138. National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) Ancestral Domain Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved suitability report (W5129) in regions with high IP density (Mindanao, Northern Luzon) |
| **Frequency** | Per site candidate in targeted regions |
| **Volume** | ~10–12 verifications annually |
| **Owner** | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer |
| **Participants** | NCIP Provincial Office, Local Ancestral Domain Office, Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Submit site coordinates and cadastral maps to the NCIP Provincial Office to request Ancestral Domain Verification. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Search NCIP records for Certificate of Ancestral Domain Title (CADT) or Certificate of Ancestral Land Title (CALT) overlays on candidate coordinates. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Obtain official NCIP Geographic Information System (GIS) mapping clearance report. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Secure NCIP initial assessment indicating if the site falls inside, adjacent to, or outside ancestral domains. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- NCIP GIS mapping database, Legal compliance system (VS-100).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Ancestral domain overlap**: Land is protected IP territory and cannot be commercialized; mitigated by mandatory coordinate check before site payment.

### Automation Opportunity
- NCIP database integration for coordinate querying.

### Controls
- CTL-141 (ensure land title & acquisition due diligence.)
- CTL-765: Official NCIP mapping clearance report signed by NCIP Regional Director; documented in site feasibility files.

### Time Estimate
- ~10-15 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal), VS-22 (compliance)

## W5141. Agricultural-to-Commercial Land Reclassification Application (LGU level)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Site has agricultural zoning but is suitable for retail store |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per site reclassification necessity |
| **Volume** | ~5–7 applications annually |
| **Owner** | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer |
| **Participants** | Municipal/City Zoning Board, Sanggunian Bayan/Panglungsod (LGU Council), Municipal/City Planning Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Submit application for Land Reclassification to the Municipal/City Planning and Development Office. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Provide documentation: land title, DAR certification, environmental clearance, and site plan. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Attend public hearings at the Sanggunian Bayan/Panglungsod Committee on Housing and Land Use; present justification for the retail development (jobs, tax revenues, convenient hardware sourcing). | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Secure the Sanggunian Ordinance reclassifying the land from agricultural to commercial use. | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | Regulatory Compliance Officer / Land Liaison Officer | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- LGU ordinance tracking database, GIS land classification maps.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Council rejection**: LGU council denies reclassification due to local political issues; mitigated by demonstrating community benefits and alignment with city CLUP.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-765 (ensure controlled execution — Agrarian Reform, NCIP Ancestral Domain & Land Use Conversion Operations (PA-178.2).)
- CTL-765: Certified true copy of Sanggunian Reclassification Ordinance secured and published as legally mandated.

### Time Estimate
- ~60-120 days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-76 (LGU compliance), VS-104 (government affairs)

## W5142. Land Use Conversion Order Application (DAR Central/Regional level)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | LGU Reclassification Ordinance secured (W5141) for agricultural land |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per site conversion |
| **Volume** | ~5–7 applications annually |
| **Owner** | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Land Legal Specialist |
| **Participants** | Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR) — Center for Land Use Policy, Planning and Implementation (CLUPPI) at the Central Office, or the Regional CLUPPI (RCLUPPI) for smaller tracts |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | File formal Application for Land Use Conversion at the DAR Provincial/Regional Office. | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Land Legal Specialist | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Land Legal Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Submit comprehensive folder: TCT, LGU Reclassification Ordinance, City Zoning locational clearance, DA Certificate of Eligibility, and DENR ECC. | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Land Legal Specialist | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Land Legal Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Arrange joint CLUPPI site inspection and post public notice of conversion on the property for public inspection. | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Land Legal Specialist | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Land Legal Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Pay conversion fees and post the required performance bond to guarantee development (VS-116). | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Land Legal Specialist | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Land Legal Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 5 | Secure the signed Land Use Conversion Order from the DAR Regional Director or Secretary (per DAR AO 1-02, as revised). | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Land Legal Specialist | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Land Legal Specialist | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- DAR conversion database, Performance bond ledger (VS-116), ERP accounts payable (VS-15).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Conversion denial**: DAR rejects conversion due to food security classification of irrigated lands; mitigated by DA (Dept of Agriculture) suitability clearance prior to transaction.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-765 (ensure controlled execution — Agrarian Reform, NCIP Ancestral Domain & Land Use Conversion Operations (PA-178.2).)
- CTL-765: DAR Conversion Order verified by Legal; performance bond posted and audited (VS-116/VS-21).

### Time Estimate
- ~90-180 days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-116 (performance bonds), VS-15 (P2P), VS-21 (internal audit)

## W5143. Tenant Farmer Compensation, Settlement & Relocation Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DAR clearance/conversion process identifying registered or actual tenant farmers on target land |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per site settlement |
| **Volume** | ~3–5 settlements annually |
| **Owner** | Land Acquisition Manager / HR Specialist |
| **Participants** | Tenant farmers, DAR Municipal Agrarian Reform Officer (MARO), Legal (VS-100), CSR Foundation (VS-152) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct census of all actual occupants, tenants, and farmworkers on the target property. | Land Acquisition Manager / HR Specialist | Land Acquisition Manager / HR Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Negotiate tenant disturbance compensation package (under RA 3844 rules) and voluntary surrender of land tenancy rights. | Land Acquisition Manager / HR Specialist | Land Acquisition Manager / HR Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Draft Disturbance Compensation Agreements and secure MARO validation and notarization. | Land Acquisition Manager / HR Specialist | Land Acquisition Manager / HR Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Provide relocation assistance, job retraining programs, and priority employment lists for tenant family members at the new BuildRight store. | Land Acquisition Manager / HR Specialist | Land Acquisition Manager / HR Specialist | 1-2 hours |
| 5 | Disburse settlement payments through audited checks and file quitclaims with the DAR. | Land Acquisition Manager / HR Specialist | Land Acquisition Manager / HR Specialist | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- MARO registration files, CSR project system (VS-152), ERP payroll onboarding (VS-19.2).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Tenancy dispute / Litigation**: Tenants refuse to vacate or file claims at the DAR Adjudication Board (DARAB); mitigated by fair market valuation and MARO-mediated negotiations.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-765 (ensure controlled execution — Agrarian Reform, NCIP Ancestral Domain & Land Use Conversion Operations (PA-178.2).)
- CTL-765: Quitclaims signed by 100% of identified tenants; MARO representative witness signatures secured; payment disbursements audited.

### Time Estimate
- ~30-60 days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal), VS-152 (CSR), VS-19.2 (payroll), VS-121 (TA)

## W5144. DENR Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) / CNC Application for New Sites

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Zoning clearance (W5141) and engineering assessment indicating construction footprint requires environmental clearance |
| **Frequency** | Per new store construction |
| **Volume** | 10–15 applications annually |
| **Owner** | Director of Construction PMO / Environmental Consultant |
| **Participants** | DENR Environmental Management Bureau (EMB), Environmental Compliance Officer (VS-25) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) or Initial Environmental Examination (IEE) Checklist for the proposed store size and location. | Director of Construction PMO / Environmental Consultant | Director of Construction PMO / Environmental Consultant | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Submit ECC or CNC (Certificate of Non-Coverage) application on the DENR-EMB online portal. | Director of Construction PMO / Environmental Consultant | Director of Construction PMO / Environmental Consultant | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Address EMB questions, submit engineering plans for sewage treatment plants (STP), drainage, and waste storage. | Director of Construction PMO / Environmental Consultant | Director of Construction PMO / Environmental Consultant | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Secure signed Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) containing development conditions; assign conditions to Construction PMO (VS-112) and store operations (VS-25). | Director of Construction PMO / Environmental Consultant | Director of Construction PMO / Environmental Consultant | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- DENR EMB online system, ESG compliance tracker (VS-25), Construction project management portal (VS-112).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **ECC delays**: DENR rejects EIA due to local flooding/drainage issues; mitigated by hydrologist surveys prior to site purchase.

### Automation Opportunity
- Online application tracking and condition reminders.

### Controls
- CTL-765 (ensure controlled execution — Agrarian Reform, NCIP Ancestral Domain & Land Use Conversion Operations (PA-178.2).)
- CTL-765: ECC issued before groundbreaking permit application; annual compliance reports filed as required by DENR.

### Time Estimate
- ~30-45 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-25 (ESG), VS-112 (PMO)
