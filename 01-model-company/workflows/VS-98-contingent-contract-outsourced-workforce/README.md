## Overview

Contingent, Contract & Outsourced Workforce Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **non-employee labor** that supports operations across 200 stores, 4 DCs, and HQ:
outsourced **security guards, janitorial/cleaning crews, promodizers, merchandising/service crews,
construction labor (for store build and fit-out per VS-20.2/VS-37), delivery helpers (3PL per
VS-06/VS-56), IT/project contractors, and seasonal/agency workers** beyond BuildRight's 6,925
direct hires. This is the "integrated workforce" component that complements VS-19 (employees) and
VS-34 (service-provider/vendor contracts).

This is distinct from **VS-19 (Hire-to-Retire)** which manages BuildRight's own employees
(including its seasonal W555 employees hired directly), from **VS-34 (Expense & Non-Merchandise
Procurement)** which manages commercial service contracts at the purchase-order/invoice level
(without the human-labor compliance dimension), and from **VS-84 (Labor Relations)** which manages
BuildRight's unionized workforce. This value stream owns the **contingent-workforce operating
model**: sourcing strategy and category planning, agency/service-contractor qualification and
DOLE Department Order No. 174 (Series of 2017) labor-only-contracting compliance and contract
structuring to avoid co-employment/illegal-contracting findings, worker classification and
misclassification-risk control, onboarding/badging/access and permit-to-work safety orientation,
time-and-deliverable tracking with invoice-vs-timesheet reconciliation, SLA/scorecard and
performance management, spend analytics and cost control, incident/injury and claims handling for
non-employees, fraud/theft investigation, conversion-to-regular boundary management, and
outsourced-function transition (insource/outsource) decisions.

DOLE D.O. 174 distinguishes **legitimate job-contracting** (substantial capital, specialized
skills, distinct from the principal's core business) from **labor-only contracting** (deemed
illegal — worker becomes BuildRight's direct employee with full backwages/benefits). With ~10–20%
of in-store and DC labor potentially non-employee at peak, disciplined compliance is essential to
avoid costly regularization claims before the National Labor Relations Commission (NLRC) and
DOLE assessments.
