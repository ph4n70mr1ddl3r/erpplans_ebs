## W3217. Work Order, Requisition & Dispatch Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Operating need (shift coverage, project, event); scheduled or ad-hoc |
| **Frequency** | Daily for recurring services; event-driven for projects |
| **Volume** | ~200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ with recurring crews; ad-hoc project demand |
| **Owner** | Contingent Workforce Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Contingent Workforce Coordinator, User Dept (Store/DC/HQ), Agency, Contingent Workforce Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | User Dept raise work order/requisition: category, headcount, location, date/time window, scope/SOW reference (W3212), special requirements; route through Contingent Workforce Coordinator for approved-supplier dispatch | User Dept / Coordinator | User Dept Head | Per request |
| 2 | Coordinator dispatches to approved agency (W3210) per MSA/SLA (W3213); agency confirms worker assignment and sends worker roster (not individual direction by BuildRight — W3211); coordinator confirms acceptance and pre-engagement screening (W3223) | Coordinator / Agency | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Per request |
| 3 | On completion, User Dept confirms deliverables/coverage against SOW (W3220/W3221); coordinator closes work order and triggers time capture (W3220) and invoice flow (W3221); log exceptions | Coordinator / User Dept | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Per request |

### System Touchpoints
- Contingent-labor PO (EBS Purchasing contingent labor — rate terms, timecard-to-invoice per fit-gap B13; links to VS-34); agency portal; time capture (W3220); invoice (W3221)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Off-contract dispatch risk**: Engagement of non-preferred supplier bypasses compliance; mitigated by PO/work-order gating
- **No-show/coverage risk**: Agency fails to staff; mitigated by SLA penalty (W3212) and multi-supplier pool


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "headcount, location, date/time window, scope/SOW reference" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-send of "sends worker roster" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "On completion, User Dept confirms deliverables/coverage against SOW" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-524 (ensure controlled execution — Contingent Worker Onboarding, Access & Time Operations (PA-98.2).)
- operational: PO/work-order gating; operational: SLA penalty (W3212) and multi-supplier pool

### Time Estimate
- Per request; ongoing coordination

## W3218. Contractor Onboarding, Badging & Site Access

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New contractor worker assigned to a BuildRight site |
| **Frequency** | Per worker assignment |
| **Volume** | Hundreds–thousands of worker assignments/year (security/janitorial turnover) |
| **Owner** | Contingent Workforce Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Coordinator, HR (links to VS-19), Safety (links to VS-24), Security (links to VS-23), IT (links to VS-27 for system access), Agency |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinator registers contractor worker in contingent-workforce system (distinct from BuildRight employee master — links to VS-29 extended masters): name, agency, role/category, assignment location/duration, emergency contact, screening status (W3223), photo for badge; record worker personal data per Data Privacy Act (links to VS-91) with consent | Coordinator / HR | Contingent Workforce Mgr | 15–30 min/worker |
| 2 | Issue site badge (visual + access control): contractor badge visually distinct from employee badge; time-limited to assignment; access scoped to assigned areas only (not back-office systems); enroll in access-control system (links to VS-23.2 physical security); collect and log access | Coordinator / Security | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Per worker |
| 3 | Complete onboarding checklist: safety orientation (W3219), asset/uniform if applicable (W3222), system access if required (minimal — links to VS-27 least-privilege), site orientation; document completion before site entry | Coordinator / Safety / User Dept | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Per worker |

### System Touchpoints
- Contingent-worker records (HR contingent-worker registration, fit-gap B13; links to VS-29); access control (links to VS-23.2); IT identity (links to VS-27); privacy (links to VS-91)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stale-access risk**: Departed workers retain access; mitigated by time-limited badges and access review (W3216)
- **Privacy risk**: Over-collection of contractor personal data; mitigated by purpose-limited collection and consent (links to VS-91)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "record worker personal data per Data Privacy Act" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "Issue site badge" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document completion before site entry" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-524 (ensure controlled execution — Contingent Worker Onboarding, Access & Time Operations (PA-98.2).)
- operational: time-limited badges and access review (W3216); operational: purpose-limited collection and consent (links to VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- 30–60 min/worker

## W3219. Contractor Safety Orientation & Permit-to-Work

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | First site assignment; high-risk task (hot work, working at height, electrical, confined space) |
| **Frequency** | Per worker + per high-risk task |
| **Volume** | All contractor workers + project-specific permits |
| **Owner** | Safety Officer |
| **Participants** | Safety Officer (links to VS-24), User Dept, Contractor Supervisor, Worker |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Safety Officer deliver general contractor safety orientation: site hazards (forklifts, racking, chemicals, electrical, slips/trips/falls), emergency procedures (links to VS-24.2), PPE requirements (links to VS-24.1), incident reporting (W3228), BuildRight HSE rules; quiz/acknowledgement before site entry | Safety Officer | Safety Mgr | 30–60 min/worker |
| 2 | For high-risk tasks, issue permit-to-work (PTW): hot work, working at height, electrical live work, confined space, excavation, lockout-tagout (LOTO); verify competency/certification (e.g., scaffold erector, forklift operator per VS-06.2), isolation, fire watch, rescue plan; PTW time-boxed and signed off | Safety Officer / Contractor Supervisor | Safety Mgr | Per task |
| 3 | Monitor compliance: Safety/Store Ops spot-check PTW and PPE compliance; enforce stop-work authority for unsafe conditions; record violations and feed contractor scorecard (W3225) and HSE metrics (links to VS-24.1) | Safety Officer / User Dept | Safety Mgr | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Permit-to-work/CMMS (links to VS-20.3); HSE (links to VS-24); contractor scorecard (W3225)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Untrained-worker risk**: Contractor worker without orientation/competency causes incident; mitigated by no-orientation-no-entry rule and PTW
- **Compliance-drift risk**: PTW/PPE compliance erodes over time; mitigated by spot-checks and stop-work authority


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-report of "Safety Officer deliver general contractor safety orientation" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "For high-risk tasks, issue permit-to-work" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record violations and feed contractor scorecard" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-121 (ensure lawful contracting & worker classification.)
- operational: no-orientation-no-entry rule and PTW; operational: spot-checks and stop-work authority

### Time Estimate
- 30–60 min orientation/worker; per-task PTW

## W3220. Time Tracking, Attendance & Deliverable Confirmation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily shifts; deliverable completion |
| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | Hundreds–thousands of daily shifts |
| **Owner** | Contingent Workforce Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Coordinator, User Dept, Agency, Contractor Supervisor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Agency/Contractor Supervisor capture worker time-in/time-out per shift (biometric or badge per W3218 access-control) and deliverables completed (cleaning checklist, security log, promodizer report); submit daily timesheet/deliverable record to BuildRight | Agency / Contractor Supervisor | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Daily |
| 2 | User Dept/Coordinator confirm coverage and deliverables against SOW (W3212): required posts/coverage met, deliverables completed to standard, exceptions (absence, shortfall, quality issues) flagged | User Dept / Coordinator | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Daily |
| 3 | System aggregates approved time/deliverables for billing (W3221); reconcile to work orders (W3217) and SLA (W3225); flag anomalies (overtime, coverage gaps) for review | Coordinator / System | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Daily/weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- Time-and-attendance; access-control feed (W3218); work-order (W3217); billing feed (W3221)

- **Coverage-gap risk**: Short-staffing degrades service; mitigated by SLA and daily confirmation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "log, promodizer report" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-post of "posts/coverage met, deliverables completed to standard, exceptions" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "System aggregates approved time/deliverables for billing" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-524 (ensure controlled execution — Contingent Worker Onboarding, Access & Time Operations (PA-98.2).)
- operational: biometric capture, deliverable confirmation, and audit (W3221); operational: SLA and daily confirmation

### Time Estimate
- Daily; ~minutes per crew

## W3222. Asset, Tool & Uniform Issuance & Recovery

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Worker assignment (issue); worker exit (recovery) |
| **Frequency** | Per worker assignment |
| **Volume** | Hundreds–thousands of issue/recovery cycles/year |
| **Owner** | Contingent Workforce Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Coordinator, User Dept, Facilities/Stores (links to VS-07/VS-20.3), Asset Mgmt (links to VS-35), Agency |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinator/Facilities issue BuildRight-provided assets to contractor workers where required (PPE, radio, keys, uniform, badge, mobile device, RF gun per VS-99); record issuance against worker/agency in asset-tracking (links to VS-35/VS-99); agency-provided assets (uniforms, cleaning equipment) remain agency responsibility | Coordinator / Facilities | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Per worker |
| 2 | During assignment, maintain accountability: periodic asset audit, replacement of worn/damaged items, chargeback for loss where contractually permitted; ensure PPE replaced per HSE schedule (links to VS-24.1) | Coordinator / Facilities | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Ongoing |
| 3 | On exit (W3216), recover all BuildRight-issued assets before final access revocation and final invoice settlement (W3221); document recovery or loss; revoke badge/access (W3218) | Coordinator / Security / Facilities | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Per worker exit |

### System Touchpoints
- Asset tracking (links to VS-35/VS-99); access control (links to VS-23.2/W3218); HSE (links to VS-24.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Asset-leakage risk**: Unreturned assets at worker exit; mitigated by recovery-before-exit and chargeback
- **Cross-contamination risk**: Shared tools/uniforms across workers; mitigated by hygiene and assignment discipline


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "record issuance against worker/agency in asset-tracking" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "During assignment, maintain accountability" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document recovery or loss" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-524 (ensure controlled execution — Contingent Worker Onboarding, Access & Time Operations (PA-98.2).)
- operational: recovery-before-exit and chargeback; operational: hygiene and assignment discipline

### Time Estimate
- Per worker issue/recovery

| **Owner** | Contingent Workforce Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Agency conducts and certifies pre-engagement screening per category: NBI clearance (or barangay clearance per W555 provincial context), police clearance, prior-employment/reference checks, drug test where lawful/relevant, court records for cash-handling/security roles; provide certification to BuildRight | Agency / Coordinator | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Per worker |
| 2 | Coordinator/Security verify authenticity (NBI certificate verification, agency credentials); for high-risk roles (security, CIT links to VS-81, cash-handling, IT admin), require enhanced screening and BuildRight Security review; reject where disqualifying findings | Coordinator / Security | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Per worker |

- CTL-524 (ensure controlled execution — Contingent Worker Onboarding, Access & Time Operations (PA-98.2).)

## W3224. Daily Contractor Supervision & Performance Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily operations |
| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | All active contractor crews across 200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ |
| **Owner** | User Department Head |
| **Participants** | User Dept (Store Mgr/DC Mgr/HQ Mgr), Contractor Supervisor, Coordinator, Safety |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | User Dept supervises to the SOW (not as employer — W3211 four-fold test): confirm coverage, conduct quality check (cleaning standard, security post, promodizer activity), document exceptions; coordinate through contractor supervisor for corrective action (not direct worker discipline) | User Dept / Contractor Supervisor | User Dept Head | Daily |
| 2 | User Dept/Safety monitor HSE compliance and conduct (PPE, conduct toward customers/staff, no prohibited activities); apply stop-work for safety issues (W3219); escalate conduct/performance issues to contractor supervisor and coordinator | User Dept / Safety | User Dept Head | Daily |
| 3 | Coordinator aggregates daily observations into periodic performance review (W3225) and incident feed (W3228); route systemic issues to contract management (W3216) | Coordinator | Contingent Workforce Mgr | Daily/weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- Work-order/exception log; HSE (links to VS-24); scorecard feed (W3225)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Boundary-violation risk**: BuildRight supervisor crosses into employer functions (directing discipline/pay); mitigated by supervisor training (W3211) and contractor-supervisor channel
- **Quality-erosion risk**: Unmonitored performance declines; mitigated by daily checks and scorecard (W3225)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "User Dept supervises to the SOW" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-escalate of "escalate conduct/performance issues to contractor supervisor and coordinator" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "Coordinator aggregates daily observations into periodic performance review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-524 (ensure controlled execution — Contingent Worker Onboarding, Access & Time Operations (PA-98.2).)
- operational: supervisor training (W3211) and contractor-supervisor channel; operational: daily checks and scorecard (W3225)

### Time Estimate
- Daily; absorbed in management routine
