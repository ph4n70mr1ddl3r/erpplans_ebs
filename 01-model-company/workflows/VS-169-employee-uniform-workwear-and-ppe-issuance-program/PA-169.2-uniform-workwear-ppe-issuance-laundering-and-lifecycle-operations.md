## W4921. New-Hire Uniform / Workwear Issuance & Onboarding Kit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New-hire onboarding (VS-121/VS-19.1); offer accepted |
| **Frequency** | Per hire (~1,200–1,600 hires/yr) |
| **Volume** | ~1,200–1,600 onboarding kits/yr × role-based kit quantity |
| **Owner** | HR Shared Services (VS-103) / Store Manager / Uniform Program Manager |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19/VS-121), Store Ops (VS-07), Supplier (W4917), HRIS/ESS (VS-29/VS-103) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generate the onboarding kit order from HRIS/ESS on offer-acceptance (links VS-121): apply the role-based standard (W4914) and sizing (W4918) to produce the kit list (e.g., 3 polo/tee, 2 trousers, 1 jacket, branded footwear, name tag, role-PPE per W4915), source from buffer stock (W4932) or supplier call-off (W4917) | HRSS / ESS / Program Manager | Program Manager | Per hire |
| 2 | Issue & fit at onboarding (links VS-19.1): hand over the kit, verify fit/comfort (W4918/W4933), capture issuance/asset record (VS-35/VS-29), brief on care/laundering (W4924) and the appearance/PPE policy, and trigger any PPE fit-test (W4922) | Store Manager / HRSS / HSE | Store Manager | Per hire |
| 3 | Handle exceptions: size exchanges (W4926), delayed supplier delivery (buffer-stock fallback W4932), interim/temporary uniform for day-1, and record any gap; feed onboarding-uniform lead-time to supplier SLA (W4931) | HRSS / Supplier / Store Manager | Program Manager | Per hire |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS/ESS (VS-29/VS-103); recruitment/onboarding (VS-121/VS-19.1); asset record (VS-35); supplier portal (VS-03.4); inventory (W4932)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Day-1-without-uniform risk**: Late supplier/buffer → new hire in non-branded clothes on day 1; mitigated by buffer stock + interim uniform
- **Mis-fit risk**: Wrong size → re-exchange cost; mitigated by sizing at onboarding (W4918)
- **Asset-blindness risk**: Kit not recorded → can't track/recover at separation; mitigated by issuance/asset record (VS-35)

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-kit-order from offer-acceptance; ESS sizing-match; asset auto-register; interim-uniform flag

### Controls
- CTL-738 (ensure controlled execution — Uniform, Workwear & PPE Issuance, Laundering & Lifecycle Operations (PA-169.2).)
- CTL-738: role-based kit per standard; sized/fit-verified; asset-recorded (VS-35); buffer-stock fallback

### Time Estimate
- ~per-hire issuance 20–30 min; ~kit-order lead time absorbed into onboarding (VS-121)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-121/VS-19.1 (recruitment/onboarding), VS-29/VS-103 (HRIS/ESS), VS-35 (asset), VS-03.4 (supplier), W4914/W4918 (standard/sizing), W4922 (PPE), W4932 (inventory)

## W4922. Role-Based PPE Issuance, Fit-Testing & Training

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New-hire (W4921); role/equipment change; PPE replacement (W4925); HSE review (VS-24) |
| **Frequency** | Per employee + per replacement |
| **Volume** | ~6,932 employees + contingent × role-PPE × replacement cycle |
| **Owner** | HSE (VS-24) / Uniform Program Manager / Site Supervisor |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), HR (VS-19), Store Ops (VS-07), DC Ops (VS-04), Occupational Health (VS-83), Supplier (W4917) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Issue role-PPE per the matrix (W4915): hand over certified PPE (hard hat, safety shoes, hi-vis, eye/face, hearing, respiratory, gloves, harness as applicable), verify certification (VS-117), record issuance/asset (VS-35), and source from buffer stock (W4932) | HSE / Site Supervisor / Supplier | HSE Lead | Per employee |
| 2 | Conduct fit-testing & training (links VS-24/VS-19.4): respiratory fit-test, harness/helmet correct-fit verification, instruct on correct use/limitation/care/when-to-replace, and the DOLE-OSH obligation to use; record competency | HSE / Occupational Health / Training | HSE Lead | Per employee |
| 3 | Enforce use & audit compliance (links W4930): supervisors enforce PPE-use on the floor/yard/DC, the PPE-use exception (non-compliance) is a disciplinary/OSH matter (VS-84), and periodic compliance audits verify correct/worn/in-date PPE | Site Supervisor / HSE / LP | HSE Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE (VS-24); HRIS role/competency (VS-19/VS-29); asset record (VS-35); occupational health (VS-83); cert (VS-117); inventory (W4932)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **OSH-injury risk**: Missing/wrong/untrained PPE-use → injury + DOLE liability; mitigated by fit-test + training + enforcement
- **Non-use risk**: Employee not wearing issued PPE; mitigated by supervisor enforcement + disciplinary process (VS-84)
- **Fit-failure risk**: Poorly-fit respiratory/harness → no protection; mitigated by formal fit-testing

### Automation Opportunity
- Role-PPE auto-order from HRIS; fit-test scheduling/tracking; competency auto-record; compliance-audit checklist

### Controls
- CTL-738 (ensure controlled execution — Uniform, Workwear & PPE Issuance, Laundering & Lifecycle Operations (PA-169.2).)
- CTL-738: certified PPE per matrix (W4915); fit-tested & trained; competency recorded; use enforced & audited (W4930)

### Time Estimate
- ~per-employee issuance + fit-test + training 30–60 min; ~enforcement continuous (absorbed into supervision)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-19.4 (training), VS-83 (occupational health), VS-117 (cert), VS-35 (asset), VS-84 (disciplinary), W4915 (matrix), W4925 (replacement), W4930 (audit)

## W4924. Uniform / Workwear Laundering Service — In-House vs Vendor (Industrial Laundry)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Operating-model decision (W4913); laundering need for soiled/contaminated workwear |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (weekly/biweekly cycles) |
| **Volume** | ~6,932 employees × uniform sets × laundering cycle; incl. contaminated (paint/chemical/oil) workwear |
| **Owner** | Uniform Program Manager / Facilities (VS-138) / Procurement / HSE |
| **Participants** | Facilities (VS-138), Laundry Vendor (W4917), HSE (VS-24), Store Ops (VS-07), DC Ops (VS-04) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decide the laundering model per site/role (W4913): employee-self-launder (standard uniform), vendor industrial laundry (managed-uniform/lease programs for high-volume/contaminated), or in-house — factoring contaminated-workwear handling (paint/chemical/oil per VS-114/VS-24) requiring specialist laundering, and cost/quality/logistics | Program Manager / Facilities / HSE / Finance | Program Manager / CHRO | Per program |
| 2 | Operate the laundering cycle: vendor pickup/drop-off schedule, exchange/rotation (clean vs soiled tracking), contamination segregation for chemical-exposed workwear (links VS-114/VS-24 hazardous laundering), and laundry-quality inspection; or manage in-house laundry capacity | Facilities / Vendor / Site Supervisor / HSE | Program Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Govern the laundry vendor (W4917/W4931): SLA (turnaround, lost/damaged-in-launder), hygiene/quality standard, contaminated-workwear compliance, TPRM/cyber for RFID-tracked uniforms, and cost/chargeback (VS-72); track uniform-loss-in-launder | Program Manager / Procurement / Facilities | Program Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Facilities/CAFM (VS-138); vendor/contract (VS-03.3/VS-100.3); HSE/hazmat (VS-24/VS-114); asset/RFID (VS-35/VS-151); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Contamination risk**: Chemical-exposed workwear not properly laundered → residual exposure/cross-contamination (VS-114); mitigated by specialist laundering + segregation
- **Loss-in-launder risk**: Vendor losing/damaging uniforms → re-issue cost; mitigated by SLA + RFID tracking (VS-151)
- **Hygiene risk**: Poor laundry hygiene → skin/health issues; mitigated by the hygiene standard + inspection

### Automation Opportunity
- RFID uniform tracking (VS-151); soiled/clean auto-tracking; contamination-segregation flag; vendor SLA dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-738 (ensure controlled execution — Uniform, Workwear & PPE Issuance, Laundering & Lifecycle Operations (PA-169.2).)
- CTL-738: contaminated-workwear specialist-laundered + segregated; laundry hygiene standard; vendor SLA + loss-tracking

### Time Estimate
- ~model decision per program; ~cycles continuous (absorbed into facilities/vendor); ~governance periodic

### Cross-references
- links to VS-138 (facilities), VS-24/VS-114 (HSE/hazmat laundering), VS-151 (RFID/auto-ID), VS-03.3/VS-100.3 (vendor), VS-72 (chargeback), W4913 (model), W4917/W4931 (vendor), W4926 (loss)

## W4925. PPE Inspection, Maintenance, Replacement & End-of-Life

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Inspection schedule; damage/wear; expiry; incident (VS-24) |
| **Frequency** | Periodic inspection + on-condition + on expiry |
| **Volume** | ~6,932 employees' PPE × inspection/replacement cycle |
| **Owner** | HSE (VS-24) / Site Supervisor / Uniform Program Manager |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), Site Supervisors (VS-07/VS-04), Occupational Health (VS-83), Supplier (W4917), Asset Mgmt (VS-35) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inspect PPE per schedule & standard (links VS-24): harness/helmet/respiratory/eye/gloves/footwear inspection for damage/wear/expiry, in-date certification (VS-117), and pre-use checks for safety-critical PPE (harness, respiratory); record inspection | HSE / Site Supervisor / Employee | HSE Lead | Periodic + pre-use |
| 2 | Maintain & replace: service/repair where serviceable, replace worn/damaged/expired PPE from buffer stock (W4932) or supplier call-off (W4917), fit-test replacements (W4922), and record the asset-lifecycle (VS-35); quarantine failed PPE from use | HSE / Site Supervisor / Supplier / Asset Mgmt | HSE Lead | On-condition |
| 3 | Manage end-of-life & records: retire/destroy end-of-life PPE (avoid reuse of failed PPE), update the asset register (VS-35), retain inspection records for DOLE compliance/audit (W4930), and feed wear-rate/failure-mode data to standards improvement (W4915/W4919) | HSE / Asset Mgmt / Program Manager | HSE Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE (VS-24); asset register (VS-35); cert (VS-117); occupational health (VS-83); inventory (W4932); supplier (VS-03.4); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Failed-PPE-use risk**: Worn/expired PPE still in use → injury + DOLE liability; mitigated by inspection + quarantine of failed PPE
- **Expiry-blindness risk**: PPE past cert/expiry undetected; mitigated by in-date verification + asset register
- **Reuse-of-failed risk**: Retired PPE reused; mitigated by destruction on end-of-life

### Automation Opportunity
- Inspection-scheduling/checklist; expiry auto-alert from asset register (VS-35); buffer-stock auto-trigger; failed-PPE quarantine tracking

### Controls
- CTL-132 (ensure PPE issuance & DOLE compliance.)
- CTL-738: periodic + pre-use inspection; failed/expired PPE quarantined & destroyed; in-date cert verified; records retained (VS-88/W4930)

### Time Estimate
- ~pre-use checks seconds/minutes (absorbed into ops); ~periodic inspection per cycle; ~replacement per occurrence

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-35 (asset), VS-117 (cert), VS-83 (occupational health), VS-88 (records), W4915/W4919 (standards), W4922 (fit-test), W4930 (audit), W4932 (inventory)

## W4926. Uniform Damage, Loss & Replacement Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee damage/loss/theft report; wear-out; laundering loss (W4924) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (~thousands of occurrences/yr) |
| **Volume** | ~6,932 employees × damage/loss/replacement occurrences |
| **Owner** | HR Shared Services (VS-103) / Uniform Program Manager / Site Supervisor |
| **Participants** | HRSS (VS-103), Store Ops (VS-07), Employee, Supplier (W4917), Finance (W4916) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive & validate the damage/loss request via ESS (VS-103): employee/supervisor logs the occurrence (damage description, loss circumstance), apply the policy (W4916 — chargeable vs company-borne), and verify against the asset record (VS-35) to prevent fraud/duplicate | HRSS / Site Supervisor / Program Manager | Program Manager | Per occurrence |
| 2 | Process the replacement: order from buffer stock (W4932) or supplier call-off (W4917), charge the employee if policy-dictated (links VS-19.2 payroll deduction VS-84 due process), issue/fit the replacement (W4918), and update the asset record | HRSS / Supplier / Store Manager / Finance | Program Manager | Per occurrence |
| 3 | Track & analytics: log damage/loss reasons, identify systemic issues (poor durability W4919, site-specific hazards, theft pattern), feed to design/standards improvement and LP if theft/fraud suspected (VS-23) | Program Manager / Analytics / LP | Program Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- ESS/HRSS (VS-103); asset record (VS-35); payroll deduction (VS-19.2); supplier (VS-03.4); inventory (W4932); LP (VS-23); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fraud/abuse risk**: False damage/loss claims for free uniforms; mitigated by asset-record verification + policy
- **Charge-due-process risk**: Improper employee charge → grievance (VS-84); mitigated by clear policy + due process
- **Systemic-durability risk**: High damage rate from poor garment quality; mitigated by wear-rate analytics → design improvement (W4919)

### Automation Opportunity
- ESS damage/loss request; asset-record auto-verify; policy-driven charge auto-calc; wear-rate analytics

### Controls
- CTL-738 (ensure controlled execution — Uniform, Workwear & PPE Issuance, Laundering & Lifecycle Operations (PA-169.2).)
- CTL-738: asset-record verified before replacement; charge per policy + due process (VS-84); wear-rate tracked

### Time Estimate
- ~per-occurrence 10–20 min processing; ~replacement lead time; ~analytics continuous

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103 (HRSS/ESS), VS-35 (asset), VS-19.2 (payroll deduction), VS-84 (grievance/due process), VS-23 (LP/theft), W4916 (policy), W4918 (fit), W4919 (design), W4932 (inventory)
