## W2486. Credit Limit Setting & Approval

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New account approved or existing account review |
| **Frequency** | ~200–300 new + ~1,000 adjustments/month |
| **Volume** | ~1,200–1,300/month |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst |
| **Participants** | Credit Analyst, Finance Mgr, Finance Dir, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Credit Analyst recommends limit based on: risk score, financial capacity, order history, relationship potential, credit insurance coverage; approval per matrix: Analyst ≤PHP 500K, Manager ≤PHP 2M, Director ≤PHP 10M, CFO >PHP 10M | Credit Analyst / Finance Manager / Finance Controller / CFO | CFO | 30 min + approval chain |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Mobile app (links to VS-75 / digital engagement app, VS-10 / ecommerce)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Matrix-bypass risk**: a limit approved above the analyst's authority undercuts the control the matrix exists for; mitigated by the tiered approval matrix (Step 1: Analyst, Manager, Director bands) routing each limit to its right approver
- **Insurance-mismatch risk**: limits set beyond the insured coverage shift default loss onto the balance sheet; mitigated by the credit-insurance coverage input (Step 1) bounded by the W2484 policy assessment

### Automation Opportunity
- Credit-score auto-computed from the financial-data feed; credit-limit auto-suggested from the scoring model; collection-aging auto-tracked with escalation

### Controls
- CTL-212 (ensure trade-credit exposure control.)
- CTL-434 (credit-authorization — links to VS-16 / W24); Operational: Credit Analyst sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~30 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~1,200–1,300/month
- Frequency: ~200–300 new + ~1,000 adjustments/month
- Absorbed into the Credit Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2490. Overdue Payment Monitoring & Escalation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer payment past due |
| **Frequency** | Daily monitoring; ~5–8% of accounts typically past due |
| **Volume** | ~260–416 past-due accounts at any time |
| **Owner** | Collections Specialist |
| **Participants** | Collections Specialist, Credit Analyst, Finance Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates daily past-due report: aging buckets (30/60/90/120+ days); Collections Specialist contacts customers: 30-day (friendly reminder), 60-day (formal demand), 90-day (hold shipments), 120+ day (legal escalation) | System / Collections Specialist | Finance Manager | 2–3 hours/day |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Escalation-skip risk**: accounts jumped straight to hold-shipment without the reminder ladder collect through conflict rather than process; mitigated by the 30/60/90-day ladder (Step 1: friendly reminder, formal demand, hold) applied in sequence
- **Bucket-drift risk**: 60-day balances quietly aging into the 90-day hold band while untouched; mitigated by the daily past-due report (Step 1) with the Collections Specialist owning each bucket movement
- **Escalation-proportionality risk**: collection intensity applied uniformly rather than by balance and behavior, over-collecting good accounts while chronic late-payers get the same call; mitigated by the aging-bucket escalation ladder (Step 1) informed by the W2491 payment-behavior score

### Automation Opportunity
- Process steps auto-sequenced with system controls; data-entry auto-populated from master data; exceptions auto-flagged for human review

### Controls
- CTL-212: duplicate-invoice/payment-application guard; Operational: Collections Specialist sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~260–416 past-due accounts at any time
- Frequency: Daily monitoring; ~5–8% of accounts typically past due
- Absorbed into the Collections Specialist role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2491. Customer Payment Behavior Scoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly payment behavior update |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; ~5,200 active accounts |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 scores/month |
| **Owner** | System / Credit Analyst |
| **Participants** | System, Credit Analyst, Collections Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System calculates payment behavior score: on-time payment %, average days past due, payment trend (improving/deteriorating), promise-to-pay fulfillment rate; feeds into composite risk score | System | Credit Analyst | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- CRM (links to VS-13 / W17 loyalty, W156 CDP, W65 CSAT)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Promise-credit risk**: customers skilled at promise-to-pay commitments score better than payers; mitigated by the promise-fulfillment-rate input (Step 1) discounting broken commitments inside the composite score
- **Trend-blindness risk**: a scoring snapshot that hides an improving-or-deteriorating trajectory misprices the next limit action; mitigated by the payment-trend input (Step 1) feeding the W2480 review cycle
- **Score-distortion risk**: the payment-behavior score is gamed or stale (lump-sum payments inflating on-time %, disputed invoices distorting days-past-due), mispricing credit limits; mitigated by the monthly refresh, trend-based inputs, and Credit Analyst review of composite risk-score outliers

### Automation Opportunity
- Credit-score auto-computed from the financial-data feed; credit-limit auto-suggested from the scoring model; collection-aging auto-tracked with escalation

### Controls
- CTL-153: consented scoring-data handling (data-privacy — links to VS-91 / W53, RA 10173); Operational: System / Credit Analyst sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: ~5,200 scores/month
- Frequency: Monthly; ~5,200 active accounts
- Absorbed into the System / Credit Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | ~100–200 credit hold orders/month |
| **Volume** | ~100–200/month |
| **Owner** | Order Management System / Credit Analyst |
| **Participants** | OMS, Credit Analyst, Trade Account Mgr, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System blocks order; notifies Trade Account Manager and Credit Analyst; Analyst evaluates: partial release (up to available credit), conditional release (with payment commitment), or full hold; overrides logged for audit | System / Credit Analyst | Finance Manager | 15–30 min per hold |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hold-overreach risk**: full holds on partially-covered orders stop revenue the available credit still safely supports; mitigated by the partial and conditional release options (Step 1: up to available credit, with payment commitment) before full hold
- **Release-without-commitment risk**: released orders without a documented payment commitment repeat the over-limit cycle next cycle; mitigated by the Analyst's evaluation record (Step 1) governing each release form

## W2493. Aging Report & Provisioning Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly aging analysis |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; ~5,400 accounts |
| **Volume** | Monthly aging report |
| **Owner** | Finance Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance Mgr, Credit Analyst, Collections Specialist, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates AR aging report: current, 30, 60, 90, 120+ day buckets by account, region, and segment; Finance Manager calculates bad debt provision per PFRS 9 expected credit loss model; adjusts monthly | System / Finance Manager | CFO | 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Provision-mismatch risk**: provisioning on stale aging understates expected loss after a deterioration burst; mitigated by the PFRS 9 expected-credit-loss calculation (Step 1) run on the current aging by account, region and segment
- **Bucket-integrity risk**: mis-aged receivables flatter the 30-day bucket and understate the 120+ tail; mitigated by the system-generated aging across all buckets (Step 1) feeding the Finance Manager's provision

### Automation Opportunity
- Dashboard auto-refreshed from source systems; anomalies auto-flagged; report auto-distributed to stakeholders

### Controls
- CTL-434 (ensure controlled execution — Credit Limit Management & Monitoring (PA-68.2).)
- operational: Finance Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 hours per occurrence
- Volume: Monthly aging report
- Frequency: Monthly; ~5,400 accounts
- Absorbed into the Finance Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
