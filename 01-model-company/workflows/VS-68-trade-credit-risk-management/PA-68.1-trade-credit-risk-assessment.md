## W2478. New Trade Account Credit Application

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New B2B customer applies for trade credit |
| **Frequency** | ~200–300 new credit applications/month |
| **Volume** | ~200–300/month |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst |
| **Participants** | Credit Analyst, Trade Account Mgr, Finance Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Account Manager collects application: business registration (DTI/SEC), financial statements, trade references, banking references; Credit Analyst verifies documents and runs background check | Trade Account Mgr / Credit Analyst | Finance Manager | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Credit Analyst assesses risk: business viability, payment history (via credit bureau), financial ratios (current ratio, debt-to-equity), industry risk, relationship potential; assigns risk score (A–E) | Credit Analyst | Finance Manager | 4–6 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Mobile app (links to VS-75 / digital engagement app, VS-10 / ecommerce)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fraudulent-application risk**: falsified registration papers or fabricated trade references onboard credit the company later chases; mitigated by the document verification and background check (Step 1) before any risk assessment
- **Risk-misgrading risk**: a misread financial ratio grades a risky account as creditworthy and sets its first limit too high; mitigated by the five-factor assessment (Step 2: viability, payment history, financial ratios, industry risk, relationship potential) feeding the W2486 approval matrix

### Automation Opportunity
- Credit-score auto-computed from the financial-data feed; credit-limit auto-suggested from the scoring model; collection-aging auto-tracked with escalation

### Controls
- CTL-212 (ensure trade-credit exposure control.)
- CTL-433 (credit-authorization — links to VS-16 / W24); Operational: Credit Analyst sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~200–300/month
- Frequency: ~200–300 new credit applications/month
- Absorbed into the Credit Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2479. Credit Scoring Model Maintenance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly model recalibration |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly; model serves ~5,200 active accounts |
| **Volume** | Model recalibration quarterly |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst / Data Scientist |
| **Participants** | Credit Analyst, Data Scientist, Finance Dir |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data Scientist recalibrates credit scoring model: updates weightings based on actual default experience, adds new predictive variables (payment trend, order frequency changes), backtests against portfolio performance | Data Scientist / Credit Analyst | Finance Controller | 3–5 days/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Model-drift risk**: weightings calibrated on old default experience misrank today's applicants as the market moves; mitigated by the recalibration against actual default experience (Step 1) with backtesting before deployment
- **Variable-selection risk**: predictors correlated with anything but credit behavior embed distortion into every downstream limit; mitigated by the new-variable review (Step 1: payment trend, order-frequency changes) confined to credit-relevant signals

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days per occurrence
- Volume: Model recalibration quarterly
- Frequency: Quarterly; model serves ~5,200 active accounts
- Absorbed into the Credit Analyst / Data Scientist role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2480. Existing Account Periodic Credit Review

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual review for all accounts; quarterly for high-risk |
| **Frequency** | Annual ~5,200 reviews; quarterly ~500 high-risk |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 annual + ~2,000 quarterly high-risk reviews |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst |
| **Participants** | Credit Analyst, Trade Account Mgr, Finance Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System auto-triggers annual review: refreshes credit bureau data, analyzes 12-month payment behavior, updates financial ratios, recalculates risk score; recommends credit limit adjustment (increase/decrease/maintain) | System / Credit Analyst | Finance Manager | 4–6 hours/account |
| 2 | Quarterly high-risk reviews: accounts with score decline, late payments >15%, or exceeding 80% of credit limit; Credit Analyst conducts deep review; recommends corrective action | Credit Analyst | Finance Controller | 2–3 hours/account |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Review-lag risk**: an annual-only cadence lets a deteriorating account keep its limit for months between reviews; mitigated by the quarterly high-risk deep reviews (Step 2: score decline, >15% late payments, >80% utilization) between the annual refreshes
- **Limit-stickiness risk**: review recommendations arriving without action leave stale limits in force; mitigated by the auto-triggered recalculated score with its limit-adjustment recommendation (Step 1) routed into the W2486 approval matrix

### Automation Opportunity
- Credit-score auto-computed from the financial-data feed; credit-limit auto-suggested from the scoring model; collection-aging auto-tracked with escalation

### Controls
- CTL-212 (ensure trade-credit exposure control.)
- CTL-433 (credit-authorization — links to VS-16 / W24); Operational: Credit Analyst sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~5,200 annual + ~2,000 quarterly high-risk reviews
- Frequency: Annual ~5,200 reviews; quarterly ~500 high-risk
- Absorbed into the Credit Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2481. Credit Bureau & External Data Integration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Monthly; ~5,400 accounts |
| **Volume** | ~5,400 accounts refreshed/month |
| **Owner** | IT Integration Lead |
| **Participants** | IT Integration, Credit Analyst, Credit Bureau |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System pulls monthly credit bureau updates: credit score changes, legal filings, negative events; auto-flags accounts with significant score decline (>20 points) for analyst review | System / IT Integration Lead | Credit Analyst | Automated + 2–3 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-feed gap risk**: a missed monthly bureau pull blinds the portfolio to new legal filings and negative events; mitigated by the scheduled monthly pulls (Step 1) with the >20-point score-decline auto-flag routing to analyst review
- **Signal-overreaction risk**: a single bureau event triggering limit action on an otherwise clean account; mitigated by the flag-for-review design (Step 1) keeping the decision with the Credit Analyst rather than auto-adjusting

### Automation Opportunity
- API-driven real-time sync (catalog, inventory, pricing, orders) eliminating manual data entry; auto-reconciliation of marketplace settlement vs. BuildRight records

### Controls
- CTL-433 (ensure controlled execution — Trade Credit Risk Assessment & Scoring (PA-68.1).)
- CTL-433 (credit-authorization — links to VS-16 / W24); CTL-153 (consented data handling — links to VS-91 / W53, RA 10173); Operational: IT Integration Lead sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: ~5,400 accounts refreshed/month
- Frequency: Monthly; ~5,400 accounts
- Absorbed into the IT Integration Lead role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
