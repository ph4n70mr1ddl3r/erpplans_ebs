## W2494. Pre-Legal Collections & Negotiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Account reaches 90+ days past due |
| **Frequency** | ~50–80 accounts in pre-legal at any time |
| **Volume** | ~50–80 accounts |
| **Owner** | Collections Manager |
| **Participants** | Collections Mgr, Credit Analyst, Legal, Head of Trade / Account Management |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collections Manager conducts intensive collection: phone, email, in-person visit for high-value accounts; negotiates payment plan (structured payments over 3–6 months); documents all collection activity | Collections Manager | Finance Controller | 4–6 hours/account |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Negotiation-creep risk**: structured payment plans extended repeatedly become de facto credit without a limit decision; mitigated by the documented collection activity (Step 1) with plans bounded at 3–6 months
- **Effort-misallocation risk**: intensive in-person collection spent evenly across accounts dilutes recovery on the balances that matter; mitigated by the high-value-account visit prioritization (Step 1) by the Collections Manager

### Automation Opportunity
- Process steps auto-sequenced with system controls; data-entry auto-populated from master data; exceptions auto-flagged for human review

### Controls
- CTL-435 (ensure controlled execution — Bad Debt Recovery & Write-Off Management (PA-68.3).)
- operational: Collections Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~50–80 accounts
- Frequency: ~50–80 accounts in pre-legal at any time
- Absorbed into the Collections Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2496. Bad Debt Write-Off Decision

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Account determined uncollectible after all recovery efforts |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 write-offs/year |
| **Volume** | ~10–15/year; ~PHP 5–15M total write-offs/year |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Dir, Credit Analyst, Legal, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Credit Analyst presents write-off recommendation: collection history, legal status, customer financial situation, recovery probability; Finance Controller reviews; CFO approves; Board approves >PHP 1M per account | Credit Analyst / Finance Controller | CFO | 2–3 days per write-off |
| 2 | Finance processes write-off: removes from AR, adjusts bad debt provision, records in GL, updates customer account status; account flagged for no future credit; COD only | Finance | CFO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Approval-shortcut risk**: write-offs processed below the CFO/Board thresholds erode the provision without governance; mitigated by the tiered review (Step 1: Analyst recommendation, Finance Controller review, CFO approval, Board above threshold)
- **Premature-writeoff risk**: accounts written off while collection avenues remain open abandon recoverable balances; mitigated by the collection-history, legal-status and recovery-probability evidence (Step 1) preceding the recommendation

### Automation Opportunity
- Process steps auto-sequenced with system controls; data-entry auto-populated from master data; exceptions auto-flagged for human review

### Controls
- CTL-435 (ensure controlled execution — Bad Debt Recovery & Write-Off Management (PA-68.3).)
- CTL-435 (credit-authorization — links to VS-16 / W24); Operational: Finance Controller sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~10–15/year; ~PHP 5–15M total write-offs/year
- Frequency: ~10–15 write-offs/year
- Absorbed into the Finance Controller role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; ~5–10 recoveries/year from written-off accounts |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 recoveries/year |
| **Owner** | Collections Specialist |
| **Participants** | Collections Specialist, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collections Specialist receives payment from written-off account; Finance processes recovery: credits bad debt recovery income, updates customer account; evaluates reinstatement only after 12 months of good behavior | Collections Specialist / Finance | Finance Manager | 1–2 hours per recovery |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reinstatement-relapse risk**: a recovered account reinstated to full credit re-defaults within the cycle; mitigated by the reinstatement evaluation only after the sustained-payment condition (Step 1) with the COD-only flag carried from W2496
- **Recovery-misposting risk**: recovered amounts posted as new receipts rather than bad-debt recovery income distort both revenue and the provision; mitigated by the recovery-income credit and account update (Step 1)
