# VS-103: HR Shared Services, Employee Experience & People Analytics

> **People** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

HR Shared Services, Employee Experience (EX) & People Analytics workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **HR service-delivery and insight layer** for 6,911 employees across 5 legal entities
and 205 locations: the employee/manager service center (HR helpdesk), case management and tiered
escalation, employee self-service (ESS) and manager self-service (MSS) portal operations, multi-entity
shared-services delivery and cross-entity case routing, HR transaction processing (verifications,
certifications, employment letters), HR records and e-signature (links to VS-88), employee
onboarding experience and day-1 readiness, engagement pulse and survey operations, internal
communications/intranet and town-hall operations, recognition platform operations, feedback and
idea management, diversity-equity-inclusion (DEI) program operations, workforce planning and
headcount forecasting, recruitment/turnover/diversity analytics, HR dashboards and executive people
reporting, HR data governance and HR technology (HRIS) administration, and HR process excellence.

This is distinct from **VS-19 (Hire-to-Retire)** which owns the *employee lifecycle* (recruitment,
payroll, workforce management, learning, separation); from **VS-84 (Labor Relations)** which owns
union/CBA/grievance and labor-specific advocacy (W2893/W2894); from **VS-102 (Compensation &
Benefits)** which owns pay/benefits design; from **VS-72 (Cross-Entity Shared Services)** which owns
*finance/shared-services chargeback* across entities; and from **VS-29 (Master Data)** which owns the
*data masters*. This value stream owns the *HR service-delivery operations, employee-experience
program, and people-analytics function* — the front door for employee HR requests and the analytics
that inform workforce decisions. At 6,911 employees with 15–20% turnover and a distributed 200-store
footprint, a structured HR shared-services and analytics function is essential to service quality,
self-service adoption, compliance, and data-driven people decisions.

BuildRight's HR shared-services exposure spans 5 payroll entities (multi-entity case routing), 205
locations (store/DC/HQ), ~1,200–1,600 hires/year and ~1,000–1,340 exits (15–20% turnover), and an
HRIS/Core-HR platform integrated with payroll (VS-19.2), master data (VS-29), and BI (VS-28). No
prior value stream owned the HR service-center, EX, or people-analytics discipline.
