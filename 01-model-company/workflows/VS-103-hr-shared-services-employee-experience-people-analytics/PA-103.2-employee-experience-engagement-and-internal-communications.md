## W3337. Employee Onboarding Experience & Day-1 Readiness Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New hire (links to VS-19.1) |
| **Frequency** | ~1,200–1,600 hires/year (per profile §11.4) |
| **Volume** | Per hire |
| **Owner** | Employee Experience Manager |
| **Participants** | EX Manager, HRSS (W3335), Hiring Manager, IT (links to VS-27/VS-99), Training (links to VS-19.4), Buddy/Mentor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EX Manager design/operate the onboarding experience: pre-boarding (welcome pack, e-sign documents links to W3333, system access links to W3335), day-1 readiness (workspace, equipment, uniform, introductions), and first-week program | EX Manager / HRSS | CHRO | Per hire |
| 2 | Hiring Manager/EX Manager deliver role onboarding: team integration, expectations, goals (30/60/90), training (POS/safety/product per VS-19.4), buddy assignment, and store/DC orientation; collect early feedback | Hiring Manager / EX Manager | CHRO | Per hire |
| 3 | EX Manager measure onboarding effectiveness (time-to-productivity, 30/60/90 feedback, early retention links to VS-103.3) and continuously improve the program | EX Manager | CHRO | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Onboarding platform; HRSS (W3335); training (links to VS-19.4); IT/equipment (links to VS-99)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Early-attrition risk**: Poor onboarding loses hires in first 90 days; mitigated by structured experience and feedback
- **Readiness-gap risk**: Day-1 without access/equipment harms experience; mitigated by pre-boarding checklist (W3335)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "EX Manager design/operate the onboarding experience" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-assign of "assignment, and store/DC orientation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-measure of "measure onboarding effectiveness" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-539 (ensure controlled execution — Employee Experience, Engagement & Internal Communications (PA-103.2).)
- operational: structured experience and feedback; operational: pre-boarding checklist (W3335)

### Time Estimate
- 30/60/90 days per hire

## W3338. Employee Engagement Pulse, Survey Operations & Action Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual/pulse cycle; complements VS-84.3 engagement |
| **Frequency** | Annual + quarterly pulse |
| **Volume** | 6,925 employees |
| **Owner** | Employee Experience Manager |
| **Participants** | EX Manager, People Analytics (links to VS-103.3), Managers, Comms (links to VS-103.2), CHRO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EX Manager/People Analytics design and run engagement surveys (annual) and pulses (quarterly): items (engagement drivers, enablement, manager effectiveness, wellbeing), confidentiality, and channel (mobile-friendly for store staff) | EX Manager / People Analytics | CHRO | Per cycle |
| 2 | People Analytics analyze results by segment (store/DC/HQ, tenure, entity), identify drivers and hotspots, and share team-level results with managers for action planning; coordinate with labor-relations engagement (links to VS-84.3 W2889) | People Analytics / Managers | CHRO | Per cycle |
| 3 | Managers/EX Manager build and execute action plans, track progress, communicate "you said / we did" (links to VS-103.2), and measure engagement movement over time (links to VS-103.3) | Managers / EX Manager | CHRO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Survey platform; people analytics (links to VS-103.3); comms (links to VS-103.2); privacy (links to VS-91)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Survey-fatigue risk**: Over-surveying without action erodes trust; mitigated by cadence and visible action
- **Low-response risk**: Store/DC low participation skews results; mitigated by mobile access and manager advocacy


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-analyze of "analyze results by segment" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track progress, communicate" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-539 (ensure controlled execution — Employee Experience, Engagement & Internal Communications (PA-103.2).)
- operational: cadence and visible action; operational: mobile access and manager advocacy

### Time Estimate
- Per cycle + continuous
