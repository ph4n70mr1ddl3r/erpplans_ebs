## W3345. Workforce Planning, Headcount Forecasting & Capacity Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual plan (links to VS-33.1); growth/new stores; restructure |
| **Frequency** | Annual + quarterly |
| **Volume** | 6,932 headcount across 5 entities/205 locations |
| **Owner** | People Analytics Manager |
| **Participants** | People Analytics Manager, HRBP, Store Ops (links to VS-07), Finance (links to VS-17.4), Strategic Planning (links to VS-33.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | People Analytics Manager/HRBP build the workforce plan: current-state headcount by entity/function/location, demand drivers (sales growth, new stores per VS-37, store closures per VS-59, automation), and gap analysis to future-state needs | People Analytics Manager / HRBP | CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | People Analytics Manager/Finance forecast headcount and labor cost under scenarios, reconcile to the labor budget (links to VS-33.1/VS-102 W3327), and identify build/buy/redeploy actions; coordinate store staffing model (29/store per profile §12.1, links to VS-19.3 WFM) | People Analytics Manager / Finance | CHRO | Annual |
| 3 | People Analytics Manager/HRBP track actual vs plan, flag gaps (over/under-staffing by location), and feed to recruitment (links to VS-19.1) and WFM (links to VS-19.3); report to leadership (links to VS-33.2) | People Analytics Manager | CHRO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Workforce-planning tool; HRIS (links to VS-29.2); FP&A (links to VS-17.4); WFM (links to VS-19.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-hire risk**: Forecast too high inflates labor cost; mitigated by scenario planning and budget reconciliation
- **Under-staff risk**: Under-forecast harms service/overtime; mitigated by driver-based demand modeling


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "headcount by entity/function/location, demand drivers" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "People Analytics Manager/Finance forecast headcount and labor cost under" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track actual vs plan, flag gaps" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-540 (ensure controlled execution — People Analytics, Workforce Planning & HR Technology (PA-103.3).)
- operational: scenario planning and budget reconciliation; operational: driver-based demand modeling

### Time Estimate
- Annual + quarterly
