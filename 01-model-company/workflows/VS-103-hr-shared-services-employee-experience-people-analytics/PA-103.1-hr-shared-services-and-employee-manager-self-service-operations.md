## W3329. Employee Service Center (HR Helpdesk) Case Intake, Triage & Resolution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee/manager HR request (any channel) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of cases/month across 6,925 employees |
| **Owner** | HR Shared Services Manager |
| **Participants** | HRSS team, Employees, Managers, HR Business Partners, Subject experts |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HRSS team intake the request via channel (portal links to VS-103.1, phone, email, in-person at store/DC/HQ): capture employee identity, category (pay links to VS-19.2, benefits VS-102, leave, policy, lifecycle), and urgency; create case in case-management system | HRSS team | HR Shared Services Mgr | Per case |
| 2 | HRSS team triage: resolve Tier-1 (policy, status lookups, simple transactions) at first contact; route Tier-2/3 to the right specialist (payroll VS-19.2, C&B VS-102, HRBP, legal VS-100, IT VS-27) with context; set SLA and acknowledge | HRSS team | HR Shared Services Mgr | Per case |
| 3 | HRSS team/specialist resolve, validate, and close the case; update knowledge base (W3331), notify the employee/manager, and capture CSAT (W3336); track reopen and aging | HRSS team / Specialist | HR Shared Services Mgr | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Case management; HRIS/ESS (links to VS-29.2/VS-103.1); knowledge base; payroll (links to VS-19.2); benefits (links to VS-102)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Channel-fragmentation risk**: Requests scattered across channels get lost; mitigated by omnichannel case intake
- **First-contact-resolution risk**: Low FCR inflates cost and frustrates employees; mitigated by Tier-1 empowerment and knowledge base (W3331)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-create of "create case in case-management system" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-route of "route Tier-2/3 to the right specialist" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "validate, and close the case" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-538 (ensure controlled execution — HR Shared Services & Employee/Manager Self-Service Operations (PA-103.1).)
- operational: omnichannel case intake; operational: Tier-1 empowerment and knowledge base (W3331)

### Time Estimate
- Per case (minutes to days)
