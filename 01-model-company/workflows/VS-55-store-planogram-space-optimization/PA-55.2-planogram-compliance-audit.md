## W2174. Planogram Compliance Audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly per store |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~200 audits/month |
| **Owner** | Regional Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Regional Ops Mgr, Store Manager, Merch Planner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Regional Ops Manager walks the store zone-by-zone comparing the actual shelf build to the current planogram (W2170 version); scores compliance on SKU position, facing count, label presence, and display execution per the W86 planogram-compliance standard | Regional Ops Mgr | VP Store Operations | 2–3 hours/store |
| 2 | Photographs the non-compliant zones; uploads to the compliance system (links to VS-177 field-retail audit); Store Manager remediates within 48 hours; the monthly compliance scorecard feeds the Store Manager performance review (target ≥90%) | Regional Ops Mgr / Store Manager | VP Store Operations | 2–4 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/audit system (links to VS-177 field-retail audit, VS-133 OpEx process-mining) — the audit findings register and remediation tracker
- Planogram module (links to VS-29 / W314) — the current planogram the audit compares against
- Store-performance scorecard (links to VS-07 / W67 store performance review) — the compliance score as a Store Manager KPI

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pre-announce-bias risk**: stores know the audit is coming and pre-clean, so the audit doesn't reflect the everyday state; mitigated by the unannounced cadence and the W2180 surprise audit
- **Scorecard-gaming risk**: a Store Manager hits the 90% score by gaming the audited zones while neglecting others; mitigated by the zone-rotation in the audit (different zones sampled each month) and the photo evidence

### Automation Opportunity
- Audit-findings with photos auto-routed to the Store Manager worklist with the 48-hour SLA; compliance-scorecard auto-computed from the findings

### Controls
- CTL-51: planogram-master is the audit baseline; operational: Regional Ops Manager finding-closure sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2.5 hours/store audit × 200 stores + ~3 hours remediation per store = **~900–1,100 hours/month** Regional Ops + Store Managers.

## W2175. Photo-Based Remote Compliance Check

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly photo submission |
| **Frequency** | Weekly from 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~200 submissions/week |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Merch Coordinator, Store Manager, Regional Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store staff photographs each zone per the protocol (angle, lighting, full-zone coverage); uploads via the store-manager app with timestamp and geo-tag (the geo-tag prevents a photo from another store or a stock photo) | Store Staff | Store Manager | 30 min |
| 2 | Coordinator spot-checks ~40 stores/week; scores and flags issues; sends feedback within 48 hours; escalates non-responsive stores (no submission) to the Regional Ops Manager | Merch Coordinator | Merch Planner | 4–6 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-comms/photo app (links to VS-07 / W571, VS-75 digital engagement) — the photo-submission channel
- Compliance system (links to W2174, VS-177) — the photo-scoring and feedback
- Geo-tag/timestamp validation (links to VS-27 IT) — the anti-gaming control

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Photo-quality risk**: a dark/blurry/oblique photo can't be scored, so the check is defeated by poor submission quality; mitigated by the submission-quality auto-check (reject a photo below a brightness/sharpness threshold) and the resubmit prompt
- **Staged-photo risk**: the store stages the zone for the photo then lets it degrade; mitigated by the unannounced weekly cadence and the cross-check against the monthly in-person W2174 audit

### Automation Opportunity
- Photo-quality auto-check at submission; AI-assisted compliance-scoring (image-recognition vs. the planogram) to scale the spot-check beyond 40/week

### Controls
- CTL-195 (ensure planogram & price-tag compliance.)
- Operational: Coordinator weekly review; non-submission auto-flagged at 24 hours past the deadline

### Time Estimate
~30 min/store submission × 200 stores + ~5 hours/week Coordinator = **~105 hours/week ≈ ~5,500 hours/year** Store Staff + Coordinator (Store Staff time absorbed into store-ops).

## W2176. Shelf Label & Price Accuracy Audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly or price change event |
| **Frequency** | Monthly per store + ad-hoc |
| **Volume** | ~200 audits/month + ad-hoc |
| **Owner** | Department Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Dept Supervisor, Store Manager, Pricing Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Supervisor audits the shelf labels against the system SRP using an RF gun: scans the label, the system shows the current SRP, any mismatch is flagged; checks for missing/damaged labels and promo-accuracy (the label matches the active promo per W262); corrects issues on the spot | Department Supervisor | Store Manager | 1–2 hours/section |
| 2 | Store Manager reviews the monthly accuracy score (target ≥99%); below 97% triggers retraining (links to VS-19.4 L&D) and a Pricing Analyst investigation into the price-file root cause | Store Manager | Regional Ops Mgr | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- RF gun / mobile audit (links to VS-27 / W48 IT, VS-151 auto-ID) — the scan-and-compare tool
- Price-tag/labeling (links to VS-08 / W181 price-tag printing, W63 shelf-label generation, W69 price-compliance audit) — the label and SRP masters
- Pricing master (links to VS-29 / W289) — the authoritative SRP the audit validates against

### Pain Points / Risks
- **DTI-exposure risk**: an inaccurate shelf label is a DTI consumer-protection violation (links to W2682) with a price-freeze non-compliance angle; mitigated by the ≥99% target and the on-the-spot correction
- **System-vs-shelf-decoupling risk**: the price file shows the right SRP but the shelf label was never printed/applied (a process gap, not a data error); mitigated by the audit catching the missing label and the W181 print-queue integrity

### Automation Opportunity
- RF-gun scan auto-compares to the SRP master with mismatch-flagging; accuracy-scorecard auto-computed per section/store

### Controls
- CTL-195 (ensure planogram & price-tag compliance.)
- CTL-395 (price-tag accuracy — links to VS-08 / W69, the price-compliance-audit control); operational: Store Manager monthly sign-off on the accuracy score

### Time Estimate
~1.5 hours/section × ~8 sections × 200 stores monthly + ad-hoc on price-change events = **~2,400–3,000 hours/month** Dept Supervisors (absorbed into store-ops).

## W2177. Endcap & Promotional Display Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Promotional period start + mid-period |
| **Frequency** | ~12 checks/year × 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~2,400 compliance checks/year |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Merch Coordinator, Store Manager, Marketing |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinator issues the display brief (the W2168 cross-merch or the W262 promo display spec); Store Manager sets up within 48 hours of the promo start and submits the photos; Coordinator reviews against the brief within 24 hours | Merch Coordinator / Store Manager | Marketing Manager | 4–6 hours/period |
| 2 | Mid-period: verifies the display is maintained (restocked, clean, signage intact, not repurposed for other merchandise); flags depleted displays for replenishment per the promo-stock allocation | Merch Coordinator | VP Store Operations | 2–3 hours/period |

### System Touchpoints
- Promo/display management (links to VS-14 / W262 promotional setup, VS-55 / W2168 cross-merch) — the display brief and the compliance check
- Compliance system (links to W2174, VS-177) — the photo-submission and review
- Promo stock allocation (links to VS-01 / W57 promo-stock allocation tracking) — the restock flag

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Display-repurposing risk**: a Store Manager repurposes the endcap for a different (locally-favored) product mid-promo, undermining the national promo; mitigated by the mid-period check in step 2 and the compliance scorecard
- **Out-of-stock-display risk**: the display runs empty because the promo stock didn't arrive, leaving an empty endcap for the promo period; mitigated by the promo-stock pre-allocation and the restock flag in step 2

### Automation Opportunity
- Photo-vs-brief auto-scoring (image recognition); OOS-display auto-detected from the inventory + display-spec

### Controls
- CTL-395 (ensure controlled execution — Planogram Compliance & Audit (PA-55.2).)
- operational: promo/display spec currency; operational: Coordinator + Marketing joint review of compliance scores per promo

### Time Estimate
~6–9 hours/period Coordinator (per the step durations) + ~30 min/store setup × 200 stores × 12 promos = **~1,270–1,310 hours/year** Coordinator + Store Managers.

## W2178. Store Walk-In Traffic & Heatmap Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly analytics from CCTV/counters |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~200 analyses/month |
| **Owner** | Analytics Manager |
| **Participants** | Analytics Manager, Store Design Mgr, Store Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System compiles the traffic data (visitor count, conversion, dwell time, peak-hour patterns) from the people-counters and CCTV analytics (where equipped); generates the heatmaps showing hot/cold zones and flow patterns | System / Analytics Manager | Store Design Manager | 2–3 hours |
| 2 | Correlates with sales data; identifies high-traffic/low-conversion zones (an opportunity to fix conversion) and low-traffic/high-conversion zones (optimize access to capture more traffic) | Analytics Manager | VP Store Operations | 1–2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- People-counter / CCTV analytics (links to VS-27 / W366 IT infrastructure, VS-23 loss-prevention CCTV) — the traffic and dwell data source
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113, W208 retail analytics) — the heatmap and correlation
- Store-design (links to VS-55 / W2171 layout optimization) — the consumer of the heatmap for layout decisions

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Sensor-coverage-gap risk**: only some stores have people-counters/CCTV analytics, so the heatmap is partial; mitigated by the sensor-equipped-store results generalized as a template for comparable non-sensor stores (links to W2171)
- **Privacy risk**: CCTV-based analytics capture customer images; mitigated by the RA 10173 data-privacy-compliant configuration (anonymized counting, no PII retention per VS-91)

### Automation Opportunity
- Heatmap auto-generated monthly from the sensor feed; anomaly (a suddenly-cold zone) auto-flagged for investigation

### Controls
- CTL-395 (ensure controlled execution — Planogram Compliance & Audit (PA-55.2).)
- CTL-395 (data-privacy — links to VS-91 / W53, RA 10173 compliance for any CCTV-analytics PII); operational: Analytics Manager methodology sign-off

### Time Estimate
~4 hours/month Analytics Manager = **~50 hours/year**, absorbed into the VS-28 cadence.

## W2179. Fixture & Display Equipment Inventory

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly; 200 stores |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~200 fixture inventories/quarter |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Facilities Coord, Merch Planner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store Manager inventories the fixtures (gondolas, pegboards, endcaps, specialty displays); records the condition per fixture; photographs any damage; logs to the facility-asset register | Store Staff | Store Manager | 2–3 hours |
| 2 | Facilities Coordinator reviews the repair/replacement needs by priority and budget; Merch Planner orders the new fixtures needed for the seasonal rotation (links to W2166) | Facilities Coord / Merch Planner | VP Store Operations | 2–3 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Oracle eAM (fit-gap F5; links to VS-138 / W47, VS-35 fixed-asset) — the fixture register and maintenance records
- Procurement (links to VS-34 / W136 indirect procurement) — the new-fixture ordering
- Planogram (links to VS-29 / W314) — the fixture requirement per planogram

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Asset-loss risk**: fixtures aren't tracked as assets, so a discarded gondola or stolen display isn't caught; mitigated by the quarterly inventory and the asset-tag for fixture-grade equipment
- **Repair-vs-replace-misjudgment risk**: a fixture is repeatedly repaired (cumulative cost > replacement) because no one tracks the repair history; mitigated by the eAM repair history per asset driving the replace decision

### Automation Opportunity
- Fixture-condition auto-flagged for repair/replace from the eAM history; new-fixture order auto-derived from the planogram change

### Controls
- CTL-198: asset-grade fixture tracking; operational: Facilities Coordinator review of the quarterly inventory

### Time Estimate
~2.5 hours/store × 200 stores quarterly + ~2.5 hours/review = **~600–800 hours/quarter** Store Managers + Facilities.

## W2180. Store Cleanliness & Visual Standards Audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly self-audit; monthly Regional audit |
| **Frequency** | Weekly + monthly |
| **Volume** | ~800 self + 200 Regional audits/month |
| **Owner** | Store Manager / Regional Ops Mgr |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Regional Ops Mgr, Store Staff |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store Manager conducts the weekly visual walk (cleanliness, obstructions, lighting, signage condition); records issues with photos; resolves within 24 hours — a standing element of the VS-07 daily-ops cycle | Store Manager | VP Store Operations | 45 min |
| 2 | Regional Ops Manager conducts the monthly surprise audit using a 50-point scorecard (the VS-177 field-retail-standards rubric); results factor into the Store Manager performance review | Regional Ops Mgr | VP Store Operations | 45–60 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Store daily-ops (links to VS-07 / W5, W574 daily closing) — the weekly self-audit element
- Field-audit tooling (links to VS-177 / field-retail audit, VS-133 OpEx) — the 50-point scorecard and the Regional surprise audit
- Photo/compliance (links to W2174, W2175) — the issue photos and remediation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Self-audit-complacency risk**: the weekly self-audit becomes a rubber-stamp ("all good") and misses real issues; mitigated by the monthly surprise Regional audit as the ground-truth check
- **Standard-drift risk**: the 50-point scorecard drifts in interpretation across Regional Ops Managers; mitigated by the rubric calibration and the photo evidence per finding

### Automation Opportunity
- Self-audit checklist auto-scheduled in VS-07; surprise-audit cadence auto-randomized per store

### Controls
- CTL-395 (ensure controlled execution — Planogram Compliance & Audit (PA-55.2).)
- Operational: Regional Ops Manager monthly sign-off; scorecard calibration sessions quarterly

### Time Estimate
~45 min/store weekly × 200 stores + ~50 min/store monthly Regional × 200 stores = **~750–800 hours/month** Store Managers + Regional Ops.

## W2181. Planogram Change Implementation Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Every planogram deployment to stores |
| **Frequency** | ~1,500–2,000 implementations/year |
| **Volume** | ~125–170/month |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Merch Coordinator, Store Manager, Regional Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System distributes the change package via the store app (zone maps, labels, instructions, deadline — the output of W2166); Store Manager acknowledges and assigns the stock team | System / Store Manager | Merch Coordinator | 15 min + 4–8 hrs execution |
| 2 | Store Manager submits the completion photos; Coordinator verifies within 48 hours; non-compliant stores receive a remediation request and escalation to Regional Ops if not resolved by the deadline | Store Manager / Merch Coordinator | Regional Ops Mgr | 15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-comms app (links to VS-07 / W571) — the change-package distribution and acknowledgement
- Compliance system (links to W2174, VS-177) — the completion-photo verification
- Planogram version (links to VS-29 / W314, W2170) — the version being implemented

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Deadline-miss risk**: a store misses the implementation deadline (staffing crunch, late label delivery), leaving the shelf in the old planogram past the promo/season start; mitigated by the deadline tracking and the Regional escalation
- **Partial-implementation risk**: the store implements the easy zones and skips the hard ones, reporting "complete"; mitigated by the per-zone completion photos in step 2

### Automation Opportunity
- Change-package auto-distributed to the store app; completion-status auto-tracked with deadline countdown and escalation

### Controls
- CTL-395 (ensure controlled execution — Planogram Compliance & Audit (PA-55.2).)
- operational: planogram-version implementation; operational: Coordinator verification sign-off per implementation

### Time Estimate
~30 min/implementation Coordinator + ~6 hours/implementation store execution × ~150/month = **~1,000 hours/month** Coordinator + Store teams (Store execution absorbed into store-ops).
