## W3689. Revenue Assurance Program Strategy & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual finance planning (links to VS-33.1/VS-17.4); program review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise revenue-assurance program |
| **Owner** | Revenue Assurance Lead / Finance |
| **Participants** | Revenue Assurance Lead, Finance (links to VS-17), FP&A (links to VS-17.4), Treasury (links to VS-18), POS/IT (links to VS-08/VS-27), Internal Audit (links to VS-21.3), Loss Prevention (links to VS-23), Ecommerce (links to VS-10), Loyalty/Gift Card (links to VS-13/VS-54), Promotions (links to VS-58), Payments (links to VS-80), Cross-Entity (links to VS-72) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue Assurance Lead/Finance define the revenue-assurance program strategy and governance: scope (all revenue channels and waterfalls — POS, ecommerce, B2B/AR, marketplace/3P, retail media, loyalty/gift-card, services), the leakage taxonomy (W3690), the operating model (continuous monitoring + periodic audit), and the KPIs (leakage rate by channel, recovery rate, control-effectiveness, exception-cycle-time) | Revenue Assurance Lead / Finance | CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Revenue Assurance Lead establish the governance: Revenue Assurance Lead authority, the cross-functional responsibilities (Finance owns the program, IT/POS enables tooling, Loss Prevention investigates fraud, Audit independently verifies), the revenue-assurance policy, and the integration to FP&A (VS-17.4), treasury (VS-18), audit (VS-21.3), and loss prevention (VS-23) | Revenue Assurance Lead / Finance / Audit | CFO | Annual |
| 3 | CFO ratify the revenue-assurance strategy and policy, approve the program budget (links to VS-17.4), set the leakage-reduction target, and report revenue-assurance posture and leakage exposure to the executive risk committee (links to VS-21.2/VS-36) and board (links to VS-36); communicate the mandate | CFO / Revenue Assurance Lead | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance/FP&A (links to VS-17.4); treasury (links to VS-18); POS/IT (links to VS-08/VS-27); audit (links to VS-21.3); loss prevention (links to VS-23); governance (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-owner risk**: Revenue assurance done only as a monthly audit after-the-fact; mitigated by Revenue Assurance Lead authority and continuous model
- **Scope-blindness risk**: New channels (marketplace, retail media) added without assurance coverage; mitigated by leakage taxonomy (W3690)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitoring + periodic audit" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System continuous audit of "Audit independently verifies" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the program budget" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-108 (ensure revenue leakage detection & recovery.)
- operational: Revenue Assurance Lead authority and continuous model; operational: leakage taxonomy (W3690)

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual

## W3691. Sales-to-Cash Reconciliation & Settlement Integrity

| **Volume** | ~PHP 5.19B monthly revenue; ~2.8M POS transactions; ~515K ecommerce orders/yr |
| **Owner** | Revenue Assurance Lead / Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue Assurance Lead/Finance perform sales-to-cash reconciliation: reconcile POS gross sales (by tender) to bank deposits (links to W215), ecommerce collections to payment-gateway settlement (links to VS-80.2), and AR collections to remittance (links to VS-16.3); identify and investigate unmatched/variance items daily | Revenue Assurance Lead / Finance / Treasury | Revenue Assurance Lead | Daily |
| 2 | Revenue Assurance Lead/Payments verify settlement integrity: confirm acquirer/PSP settlement amounts, MDR/fee deductions, chargebacks and refunds reconcile to expected revenue (links to VS-80.2), and that multi-tender/split-payment transactions captured full value | Revenue Assurance Lead / Payments / Treasury | Revenue Assurance Lead | Daily + monthly |
| 3 | Revenue Assurance Lead drive variance resolution: investigate sales-vs-cash gaps to root cause (system sync failure, offline-mode gap, unbanked cash, settlement timing), recover/correct, and feed findings to leak-detection (W3705) and controls (W3693); report reconciliation integrity to month-end close (links to VS-17.1) | Revenue Assurance Lead / Finance / IT | Revenue Assurance Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Bank reconciliation (links to W215); treasury/cash (links to VS-18.1); payment settlement (links to VS-80.2); POS (links to VS-08); month-end close (links to VS-17.1); ecommerce (links to VS-10)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unreconciled-gap risk**: Sales-to-cash variance left uninvestigated masks leakage; mitigated by daily reconciliation and root-cause
- **Offline-mode risk**: POS offline transactions fail to sync, causing unrecorded sales; mitigated by offline-integrity check (W3692)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile POS gross sales" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "verify settlement integrity" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-sync of "sync failure, offline-mode gap, unbanked cash, settlement timing" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-108 (ensure revenue leakage detection & recovery.)
- operational: daily reconciliation and root-cause; operational: offline-integrity check (W3692)

### Time Estimate
- Daily + monthly

## W3692. POS Transaction Integrity & Data-Flow Assurance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Near-real-time event stream; daily integrity check; offline-mode event |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + daily |
| **Volume** | ~2.8M POS transactions/month; 600 terminals |
| **Owner** | Revenue Assurance Lead / POS-IT |
| **Participants** | Revenue Assurance Lead, POS/IT (links to VS-08/VS-27.2), Finance (links to VS-17.1), Loss Prevention (links to VS-23), BI (links to VS-28.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue Assurance Lead/POS-IT monitor POS transaction integrity in near-real-time: confirm every transaction (and its lines) flows POS → event stream → ERP → GL without loss/duplication, detect sequence gaps, duplicate transaction IDs, failed-sync flags, and offline-mode transactions pending sync | Revenue Assurance Lead / POS-IT / BI | Revenue Assurance Lead | Continuous |
| 2 | Revenue Assurance Lead/POS-IT assure offline-mode revenue capture: when a POS operates offline (network outage, ≥8-hour cache per profile §5), ensure the offline transaction cache syncs fully on reconnect, no transactions are lost/duplicated, and offline sales reconcile to cash; investigate sync failures | Revenue Assurance Lead / POS-IT | Revenue Assurance Lead | Daily |
| 3 | Revenue Assurance Lead trace end-to-end data integrity periodically: select sample transactions and trace from POS scan → price lookup → tender → receipt → event → ERP journal → GL revenue (the W348 step-5 test, now operationalized continuously), and remediate data-flow defects with IT (links to VS-27.1) | Revenue Assurance Lead / IT / Finance | Revenue Assurance Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- POS/event stream (links to VS-08/VS-27.2); ERP GL (links to VS-17.1); offline cache (links to VS-08); IT service mgmt (links to VS-27.1); BI (links to VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Sync-loss risk**: Offline/dropped transactions never reach the GL — direct revenue loss; mitigated by offline-integrity monitoring
- **Duplication risk**: Sync retries create duplicate revenue entries; mitigated by idempotency checks


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitor POS transaction integrity in near-real-time" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "Revenue Assurance Lead/POS-IT assure offline-mode revenue capture" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-scan of "scan → price lookup → tender → receipt → event → ERP journal → GL revenue" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-583 (ensure controlled execution — Revenue Assurance Strategy, Governance & Leak Detection Framework (PA-118.1).)
- operational: offline-integrity monitoring; operational: idempotency checks

### Time Estimate
- Continuous + daily
