## W3705. Leakage Detection, Quantification & Root-Cause Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Exception alert (W3693); reconciliation variance (W3691/W3694); integrity anomaly |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | Exception queue across all channels |
| **Owner** | Revenue Assurance Lead / BI |
| **Participants** | Revenue Assurance Lead, BI (links to VS-28.1), Finance (links to VS-17.4), POS/IT (links to VS-08/VS-27), Channel owners (VS-10/VS-13/VS-54/VS-58/VS-80/VS-95), Loss Prevention (links to VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue Assurance Lead/BI triage and investigate exceptions from the integrity monitors (W3697–W3704): classify each by leakage vector (W3690), determine whether it represents genuine leakage, and prioritize by value/volume/risk | Revenue Assurance Lead / BI | Revenue Assurance Lead | Continuous |
| 2 | Revenue Assurance Lead quantify confirmed leakage: estimate the revenue impact (rate × volume × period), determine root cause (system defect, process gap, master-data error, human error, fraud), and assess the control failure (W3693) | Revenue Assurance Lead / BI / Channel owner | Revenue Assurance Lead | Per event |
| 3 | Revenue Assurance Lead route the leakage: fraud/collusion to investigation (W3708), recoverable to recovery (W3706), system/process root-cause to corrective action, and feed all to analytics (W3707) and the channel owner for fix | Revenue Assurance Lead | Revenue Assurance Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Exception monitors (links to W3693/W3697–W3704); BI/analytics (links to VS-28.1); channel systems (VS-08/VS-10/VS-13/VS-54/VS-58/VS-80/VS-95); loss prevention (links to VS-23)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Detection-latency risk**: Leakage persists undetected for long periods; mitigated by continuous monitoring
- **Quantification-error risk**: Leakage impact mis-estimated; mitigated by structured quantification method


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "Revenue Assurance Lead/BI triage and investigate exceptions from the" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-route of "route the leakage" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-585 (ensure controlled execution — Leakage Recovery, Revenue Analytics & Continuous Assurance (PA-118.3).)
- operational: continuous monitoring; operational: structured quantification method

### Time Estimate
- Continuous

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue Assurance Lead/Finance recover leakage where recoverable: re-charge under-charged transactions where identifiable, recover over-refunded/erroneous value, pursue third-party (acquirer/vendor) recovery for fee/chargeback discrepancies, and correct GL entries (links to VS-17.1) | Revenue Assurance Lead / Finance / Channel owner | Revenue Assurance Lead | Per event |
| 2 | Revenue Assurance Lead drive corrective action on root cause: system fix (with IT links to VS-27.1), process change, master-data correction (W3709), control addition/tuning (W3693), and training to prevent recurrence | Revenue Assurance Lead / IT / Channel owner | Revenue Assurance Lead | Per event |
| 3 | Revenue Assurance Lead/Audit verify recovery and corrective-action effectiveness: confirm leakage stopped, recovery captured, control now detects it, and document the event in the leakage register; report to analytics (W3707) and audit (links to VS-21.3) | Revenue Assurance Lead / Audit | Revenue Assurance Lead | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance/GL (links to VS-17.1); IT service mgmt (links to VS-27.1); master data (links to W3709/VS-29); controls (links to W3693); audit (links to VS-21.3); leakage register (links to W3696)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Non-recovery risk**: Leakage identified but not recoverable (e.g., customer already left); mitigated by prevention focus
