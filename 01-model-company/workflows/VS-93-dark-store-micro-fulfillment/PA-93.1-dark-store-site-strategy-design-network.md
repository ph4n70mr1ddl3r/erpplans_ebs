## W3089. Dark Store Network Strategy & Market Catchment Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Ecommerce penetration target breach, market expansion, or strategic review |
| **Frequency** | Annual strategic review + event-driven |
| **Volume** | Enterprise-wide; ~3–6 candidate dark-store markets |
| **Owner** | VP Supply Chain |
| **Participants** | VP Supply Chain, GM, Digital Commerce Inc., Real Estate Mgr, Finance, IT, Network Planning |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Network Planning aggregates 18-month ecommerce demand by barangay/pincode using order history (links to VS-10), population density, smartphone penetration, and competitor dark-store presence; maps demand heat-zones per Metro Manila / Cebu / Davao cores | Network Planning Analyst | VP Supply Chain | 2–3 weeks |
| 2 | Network Planning runs catchment modeling: for each candidate site, compute 2-hour / 4-hour / same-day / next-day addressable order volume vs. current ship-from-store (VS-10) and DC (VS-04) cost-per-order baselines | Network Planning Analyst | VP Supply Chain | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Steering committee (VP Supply Chain, GM, Digital Commerce Inc., CFO, CIO) selects network plan: number of dark stores, MFC vs. manual format, phasing, and capex envelope; links to VS-33 strategic plan and VS-40 capex | VP Supply Chain | CEO | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce order analytics (links to VS-28); GIS / catchment modeling; capex module (links to VS-40)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Demand-forecast risk**: Ecommerce demand is volatile; mitigated by phased capex, modular MFC format, and 18-month rolling reforecast


### Automation Opportunity
- workflow automation; system-driven processing with exception handling

### Controls
- CTL-508 (ensure controlled execution — Dark Store Site Strategy, Design & Network Planning (PA-93.1).)
- operational: phased capex, modular MFC format, and 18-month rolling reforecast

### Time Estimate
- 6–8 weeks per strategic cycle

## W3090. Dark Store Site Selection, Lease & Build-Out

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved network plan identifies a new site |
| **Frequency** | Per new dark store (~1–2 sites/year at maturity) |
| **Volume** | ~1,500–3,000 sqm per dark store; 300–800 sqm per MFC |
| **Owner** | Real Estate Manager |
| **Participants** | Real Estate Mgr, VP Supply Chain, Facilities, Legal, Finance, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real Estate Manager screens candidate properties against dark-store criteria: catchment fit, last-mile access (rider/driver ingress/egress), ceiling height (≥6 m for mezzanine/automation), floor load, power (esp. for MFC automation), zoning/LGU permits, landlord build-out allowance | Real Estate Manager | COO | 4–8 weeks |
| 2 | Legal/Real Estate negotiate lease: base rent, escalation, CAM, fit-out allowance, exclusivity (no competitor), assignment/sublease, early-termination; Real Estate confirms LGU business permit path per VS-76 | Legal Counsel / Real Estate Manager | COO | 4–6 weeks |
| 3 | Facilities Manager runs build-out: floor reinforcement, racking/mezzanine, electrical/power back-up (generator per VS-20), HVAC, lighting, security (CCTV per VS-23), IT/data; integrate to VS-20 real estate and VS-27 IT | Facilities Manager | VP Supply Chain | 8–16 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease/property master (links to VS-42); capex project accounting (links to VS-40); LGU permits (links to VS-76)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lease/permit delay risk**: LGU permitting in Metro Manila can take 3–6 months; mitigated by early engagement (VS-76) and site criteria that prefer pre-permitted commercial space


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-assign of "Legal/Real Estate negotiate lease" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-508 (ensure controlled execution — Dark Store Site Strategy, Design & Network Planning (PA-93.1).)
- operational: early engagement (VS-76) and site criteria that prefer pre-permitted commercial space

### Time Estimate
- 4–8 months end-to-end per site

## W3091. Dark Store Layout Design, Fixture & Automation Configuration

| **Volume** | Site-specific (1,500–3,000 sqm) |
| **Owner** | Dark Store Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Dark Store Ops Mgr, Engineering, Automation Vendor, IT, Safety, HR |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dark Store Ops Mgr designs functional zones: receiving/staging, reserve storage, forward pick faces (A/B velocity), batch-pick zones, pack stations, dispatch bays, returns zone, rider waiting/loading, amenities; defines flow (one-way, no cross) and slotting logic | Dark Store Operations Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2–3 weeks |
| 2 | Engineering/Automation Vendor configures fixtures and (if MFC) semi-automation: shelving/racking, gravity-feed lanes, put-wall / sortation, goods-to-person shuttle or AutoStore-style cubes, conveyors, auto-baggers; sized to peak-hour throughput | Engineering / Automation Vendor | Dark Store Operations Manager | 4–8 weeks |
| 3 | Safety/HR validate ergonomic and OSH design (lift heights, pack-station ergonomics, fire egress, ventilation per VS-24); HR confirms staffing model feasibility | Safety Officer / HR | Dark Store Operations Manager | 1–2 weeks |

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Dark Store Ops Mgr designs functional zones" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validate ergonomic and OSH design" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-508 (ensure controlled execution — Dark Store Site Strategy, Design & Network Planning (PA-93.1).)
- operational: modular design, vendor neutrality, and phased automation (manual→MFC)

## W3094. Dark Store Go-Live, Pilot & Ramp-Up

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Site commissioned and staff trained |
| **Frequency** | Per new site |
| **Volume** | Phased: pilot store catchment → metro → multi-metro |
| **Owner** | Dark Store Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Dark Store Ops Mgr, GM, Digital Commerce Inc., IT, Quality, CS, Marketing |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ecommerce/ORM team (links to VS-60) begins routing a controlled % of eligible orders to the new dark store (e.g., 5% → 25% → 50% → 100% over 4–6 weeks), geofenced to the catchment area, with daily SLA and accuracy review | Ecommerce Ops Lead | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | 4–6 weeks ramp |
| 2 | Dark Store Ops Mgr runs daily war-room during hyper-care: review order cycle time, pick accuracy, pack quality, dispatch-on-time, rider wait time, exceptions; resolve systemic issues with IT/Vendor | Dark Store Operations Manager | VP Supply Chain | Daily during ramp |
| 3 | At end of ramp: ops review against business case (cost-per-order, orders/hour, SLA); decide steady-state ownership transfer, hand back to standard daily ops (PA-93.2), and publish post-implementation report | Dark Store Operations Manager / Finance | VP Supply Chain | 1 week wrap-up |

### System Touchpoints
- OMS routing rules (links to VS-60); dark-store ops dashboard; hyper-care issue log

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Ramp disruption risk**: Over-routing to an unstable dark store damages customer SLA; mitigated by capped ramp % and daily KPI gates


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-dispatch of "dispatch-on-time, rider wait time, exceptions" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-post of "At end of ramp" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-508 (ensure controlled execution — Dark Store Site Strategy, Design & Network Planning (PA-93.1).)
- operational: capped ramp % and daily KPI gates

## W3095. Dark Store Capacity Planning & Expansion Triggers

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly capacity review or SLA degradation |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + event-driven |
| **Volume** | Per dark store |
| **Owner** | Dark Store Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Dark Store Ops Mgr, Network Planning, Finance, HR, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dark Store Ops Mgr reviews rolling 13-week capacity: forecasted orders/day vs. demonstrated peak-hour throughput, labor availability, automation capacity, dispatch-bay/rider staging capacity; flags bottlenecks | Dark Store Operations Manager | VP Supply Chain | Monthly |
| 2 | When utilization ≥ 80% sustained or SLA dips below target: trigger expansion menu — add shifts, extend cut-off, re-slot velocity, add pack stations, MFC automation bolt-on, or open a new dark store (links to W3090) | Network Planning / Dark Store Ops Mgr | VP Supply Chain | Per trigger |
| 3 | Finance/SC validate capex/ROI for any automation or new-site trigger; submit via VS-40 capex workflow; HR pre-recruits against expansion plan | Finance Analyst / HR | CFO / VP Supply Chain | Per trigger |

### System Touchpoints

- **Capacity surprise**: Demand surges (sale events, typhoon rebuild — links to VS-69) overwhelm dark store; mitigated by 13-week rolling forecast and pre-approved surge labor pool


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews rolling 13-week capacity" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validate capex/ROI for any automation or new-site trigger" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-508 (ensure controlled execution — Dark Store Site Strategy, Design & Network Planning (PA-93.1).)

| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Per dark store |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
