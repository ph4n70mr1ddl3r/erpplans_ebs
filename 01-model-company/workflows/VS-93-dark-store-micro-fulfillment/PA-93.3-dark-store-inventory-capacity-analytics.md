## W3105. Dark Store Inventory Positioning, Safety Stock & Replenishment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly cycle + daily min/max triggers |
| **Frequency** | Daily/weekly |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–8,000 active SKUs per dark store (curated for catchment) |
| **Owner** | Dark Store Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Dark Store Ops Mgr, Inventory Planning, DC Ops (links to VS-04), Category (links to VS-01) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inventory Planning sets per-SKU safety stock and reorder point for the dark store based on catchment velocity, lead time from feeder DC (links to VS-04), and target service level (98%+); distinguishes A/B/C velocity | Inventory Planner | Dark Store Operations Manager | Weekly |
| 2 | System auto-generates replenishment transfer requests to feeder DC when min breached; DC schedules transfer (links to VS-05/VS-04); inbound received and putaway to forward pick faces | WMS / DC Ops | Dark Store Operations Manager | Daily |
| 3 | Dark Store Ops Mgr reviews slow-movers (links to VS-05 aging) and obsolete candidates; returns to DC or removes from dark-store range (links to W3110); confirms physical-to-book accuracy via cycle count | Dark Store Operations Manager / Inventory | VP Supply Chain | Weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory/safety stock engine (links to VS-05); inter-site transfer (links to VS-04/VS-05); cycle count

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stockout risk**: Dark store stockouts cause substitute/short-ship and lost sale; mitigated by velocity-based safety stock and same-day DC replenishment


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "generates replenishment transfer requests to feeder DC when min breached" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "Dark Store Ops Mgr reviews slow-movers" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-510 (ensure controlled execution — Dark Store Inventory, Capacity & Performance Analytics (PA-93.3).)
- operational: velocity-based safety stock and same-day DC replenishment

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 hr/week per dark store

## W3106. Dark Store Slotting, Velocity Analysis & Re-Slotting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly slotting review; seasonal/sale velocity shift |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + event-driven |
| **Volume** | All pick faces |
| **Owner** | Dark Store Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Dark Store Ops Mgr, Industrial Engineer, Pickers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Industrial Engineer computes SKU velocity (lines/day, picks/day) and current slot location; identifies A-velocity SKUs in non-golden-zone slots and C-velocity SKUs consuming golden-zone space | Industrial Engineer | Dark Store Operations Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | Engineer proposes re-slot plan: A-velocity → eye-level/golden zone nearest pack stations; C-velocity → reserve/upper; bundle/kit components co-located; automation (MFC) bins re-balanced | Industrial Engineer | Dark Store Operations Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | Picker execute re-slot during low-volume window (overnight/early AM); WMS updates bin assignments; productivity uplift measured post-implementation vs. baseline lines/picker-hour (links to W3107) | Picker / WMS | Dark Store Operations Manager | Per re-slot event |

### System Touchpoints
- WMS slotting module; velocity analytics (links to PA-93.3/VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Re-slot disruption risk**: Re-slot during peak hurts throughput; mitigated by overnight execution and phased moves


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-assign of "execute re-slot during low-volume window" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-510 (ensure controlled execution — Dark Store Inventory, Capacity & Performance Analytics (PA-93.3).)
- operational: overnight execution and phased moves

### Time Estimate
- 1 day/month per dark store

## W3108. Dark Store Order Cycle Time, Cut-Off Adherence & SLA Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily SLA cycle; SLA breach |
| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | All orders |
| **Owner** | Dark Store Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Dark Store Ops Mgr, Ecommerce Ops (links to VS-60), CS (links to VS-13), BI |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System tracks end-to-end order cycle time: order received → wave release → pick complete → pack complete → dispatch → delivery/handover; segments by promise tier (same-day, next-day, BOPIS) | WMS / BI | Dark Store Operations Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Dark Store Ops Mgr reports cut-off adherence (orders accepted before cut-off delivered same-day %), promise-breach rate, and breach root-cause (pick delay, pack delay, dispatch delay, carrier delay) | Dark Store Operations Manager | VP Supply Chain | Daily |

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-dispatch of "dispatch → delivery/handover" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-report of "reports cut-off adherence" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-510 (ensure controlled execution — Dark Store Inventory, Capacity & Performance Analytics (PA-93.3).)
- operational: segmenting internal vs. external SLA

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 hr/day per dark store

| **Frequency** | Daily + weekly |
| **Volume** | All orders + audit samples |
| **Owner** | Dark Store Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Quality, Finance, CS (links to VS-13), Loss Prevention (links to VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

- System auto-scan of "scanner calibration, fraud-control" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-77 (ensure dark-store order accuracy & dispatch control.)
- operational: automation bolt-on (links to W3095) and productivity program

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 hr/week per dark store
