## Overview

Dark Store & Micro-Fulfillment Operations workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — governing the
dedicated ecommerce-fulfillment facilities (dark stores and micro-fulfillment centers / MFCs)
that serve high-density urban demand as ecommerce grows from ~3% to ~7% of revenue over three
years (model company §8.5), with ~42,900 ecommerce orders/month scaling toward ~100,000/month.

A **dark store** is a closed-to-public, staff-picked retail-format facility located in dense
urban markets (Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao cores) optimized exclusively for ecommerce/BOPIS/
quick-commerce order fulfillment at sub-2-hour pick cycles. A **micro-fulfillment center (MFC)**
is a smaller, often semi-automated (goods-to-person, auto-store, shuttle) fulfillment node
embedded in or alongside existing stores or DCs to compress last-mile distance and cost. Both
are distinct from full DCs (VS-04 — bulk store replenishment, pallet-in/pallet-out) and from
store-level ship-from-store (VS-10 PA-10.2 — opportunistic fulfillment from selling floor).

While **VS-04** owns DC & warehouse operations and **VS-10** owns the ecommerce platform and
ship-from-store fulfillment, and **VS-60** owns omnichannel order routing decisions, this value
stream owns the **dark-store/MFC operating model**: network strategy and site selection, layout
and (optional) automation commissioning, dedicated pick/pack/dispatch operations at speed, dark
store inventory positioning and slotting, and the throughput/SLA/cost-per-order analytics
specific to micro-fulfillment — across BOPIS click-and-collect, same-day, next-day, and
quick-commerce delivery promises.
