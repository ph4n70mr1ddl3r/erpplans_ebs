## W2190. Delivery Partner Identification & Screening

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Need for new delivery capacity or partner replacement |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; typically 2–4 new partners/year |
| **Volume** | ~2–4 evaluations/year; ~10–15 active partners |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement, VP Supply Chain, Legal, Finance, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics Manager identifies potential partners based on geographic coverage, vehicle fleet, pricing, and reputation; Procurement conducts due diligence (DTI/SEC registration, insurance, safety record, references) | Logistics Manager / Procurement | VP Supply Chain | 3–5 days |
| 2 | Legal reviews contract: SLA terms, liability, insurance, data privacy (RA 10173), rate card, payment terms, termination; Finance evaluates pricing and sets up vendor master | Legal / Finance | CFO | 2–3 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor/partner master (links to VS-03 / W36, VS-62B 3PL onboarding) — the partner record and due-diligence documentation
- Contract repository (links to VS-100 / W230, VS-88) — the 3PL service agreement
- TPRM (links to VS-161 / W4721 third-party risk tiering) — the partner risk assessment

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-vetted-partner risk**: insufficient due diligence lets a partner with a poor safety/financial record through; mitigated by the documented due-diligence checklist (DTI/SEC, insurance, references) and the TPRM risk-tiering
- **Over-concentration risk**: too much volume with one partner creates leverage and continuity risk; mitigated by the no-single-partner->50% rule (W2195)

### Automation Opportunity
- Due-diligence checklist auto-assembled per partner; risk-tier auto-suggested from the screening data

### Controls
- CTL-06: 3PL vendor-master onboarding; CTL-167 (TPRM risk-tiering — links to VS-161); operational: VP Supply Chain + CFO sign-off

### Time Estimate
~4 days/evaluation × ~3/year = **~96 hours/year** Logistics Manager + Procurement + Legal.

## W2191. 3PL System Integration & Onboarding

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contract signed with new partner |
| **Frequency** | ~2–4 integrations/year |
| **Volume** | ~2–4/year |
| **Owner** | IT Integration Lead |
| **Participants** | IT, Logistics Manager, CIO, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT configures API integration: order push, delivery status, proof of delivery, driver tracking; tests in sandbox; Logistics Manager trains partner on BuildRight processes | IT / Logistics Manager | CIO | 5–10 days |
| 2 | Pilot period: 2-week controlled rollout with 1 DC; monitors performance vs. SLA; full rollout upon meeting pilot criteria | Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- API/integration (links to VS-27 / W257 enterprise API lifecycle, VS-56 / W2198 tracking) — the 3PL integration
- ERP/order management (links to VS-60 / W536 unified order management, VS-04 / W106 dispatch) — the order-push and status
- IT change management (links to VS-27 / W132) — the integration release

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Integration-failure risk**: the API integration fails in production (the sandbox didn't catch a real-world condition); mitigated by the 2-week pilot with one DC before full rollout
- **Partner-system-limitation risk**: the partner's system can't support the required integration (no API, manual-only); mitigated by the integration-capability screening at W2190

### Automation Opportunity
- Integration test-harness auto-run; pilot-criteria auto-graded

### Controls
- CTL-397 (ensure controlled execution — 3PL Partner Onboarding & Qualification (PA-56.1).)
- CTL-397: API/integration integrity — links to VS-27; operational: CIO sign-off on go-live after pilot

### Time Estimate
~7.5 days/integration × ~3/year = **~180 hours/year** IT Integration Lead.

## W2192. Driver & Vehicle Compliance Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Partner onboarding or annual re-certification |
| **Frequency** | Annual; onboarding as needed |
| **Volume** | ~200–300 drivers across all 3PL partners |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinator verifies driver credentials: professional license, drug test, background check, BuildRight safety training; verifies vehicle compliance: registration, insurance, roadworthiness | Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 2–3 days |
| 2 | Annual re-certification: partners submit updated documents; non-compliant drivers/vehicles suspended until cleared | Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Driver/vehicle compliance (links to VS-167 / W167 workforce vetting, VS-06 / W197 driver safety) — the credential verification
- TPRM (links to VS-161 / W4721) — the compliance as a third-party-risk dimension
- RA 10173 (links to VS-91 / W53) — the driver personal-data privacy

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lapsed-credential risk**: a driver's license or drug test expires between annual re-certifications, leaving a non-compliant driver delivering for BuildRight; mitigated by the expiry-tracking with auto-suspension on lapse
- **Privacy risk**: the driver background check and drug test involve personal data; mitigated by the RA 10173-compliant handling (consent, purpose limitation, VS-91)

### Automation Opportunity
- Credential-expiry auto-tracked; auto-suspension on lapse; re-certification auto-requested 30 days before expiry

### Controls
- CTL-180 (ensure 3PL delivery-partner compliance & performance.)
- CTL-397: driver/vehicle compliance — links to VS-167, VS-06; CTL-153 (driver personal-data privacy — links to VS-91); operational: Logistics Manager sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/onboarding + ~1.5 weeks annual re-cert = **~300 hours/year** Logistics Coordinator.

## W2193. 3PL Rate Card Negotiation & Renewal

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual rate card review or significant cost change |
| **Frequency** | Annual; ad-hoc for fuel price changes |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 rate cards |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement, Finance, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics Manager analyzes spend, volume, and market rates; benchmarks vs. spot rates; negotiates: base per-km rate, weight surcharges, waiting time, multi-drop discounts, peak surcharges | Logistics Manager | Procurement | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Finance validates cost impact; approves; updated rate card configured in ERP | Finance | CFO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Rate card / pricing (links to VS-03 / W62 contract, VS-29 / W304 routing & transit-time master) — the negotiated rates
- Cost analytics (links to VS-61 / W2325 fleet TCO, VS-27 / W113) — the spend and benchmark data
- Fuel-adjustment (links to VS-61 / W2316 fuel pass-through) — the ad-hoc fuel-trigger

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Above-market-rate risk**: a partner's rate drifts above market without BuildRight noticing; mitigated by the annual benchmark and the spot-rate comparison
- **Rate-card-complexity risk**: an overly complex rate card (many surcharges, exceptions) makes billing opaque and error-prone; mitigated by the simplified rate-card preference (base + standard surcharges)

### Automation Opportunity
- Spend/benchmark auto-assembled; rate-card-delta auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-397: 3PL-contract accuracy — links to VS-03; CTL-48 (rate-dispute resolution); operational: CFO sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2 days/partner × ~12 = **~190 hours/year** Logistics Manager + Finance.

## W2194. 3PL Insurance & Liability Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Insurance renewal or delivery incident |
| **Frequency** | Annual renewal; ad-hoc for incidents |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 partner policies |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | Legal, Finance, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manager ensures all partners maintain required insurance (vehicle, cargo, third-party liability); tracks expiry dates; files claims for incidents (damage, loss, accident) | Logistics Manager | Legal | 2–3 hours/quarter |
| 2 | Annual: reviews insurance adequacy; adjusts coverage based on claims history and delivery volume growth | Logistics Manager / Finance | CFO | 1 day/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance (links to VS-26 / W59 insurance policy, W610 claims) — the 3PL insurance tracking
- Claims (links to VS-90 / W90 damage claims, VS-53 / W2140 performance guarantee) — the delivery-incident claims
- Partner master (links to VS-03 / W36) — the insurance-expiry tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lapsed-insurance risk**: a partner's insurance lapses between annual reviews, leaving BuildRight exposed on a delivery incident; mitigated by the expiry-tracking with auto-suspension on lapse
- **Underinsurance risk**: the partner's coverage is below the cargo value, leaving a gap on a total-loss; mitigated by the coverage-vs-cargo-value check

### Automation Opportunity
- Insurance-expiry auto-tracked; auto-suspension on lapse; claim auto-filed on incident

### Controls
- CTL-397: 3PL insurance adequacy — links to VS-26; CTL-42 (partner-bank-detail guard on settlements); operational: CFO sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2.5 hours/quarter + ~1 day/year ≈ 10 + 8 hours = **~18–20 hours/year** Logistics Manager + Finance.

## W2195. 3PL Capacity Planning & Allocation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Seasonal capacity review or new store openings |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly; ad-hoc for openings |
| **Volume** | 4 DCs; ~17,200 deliveries/month (the W19 home-delivery canon) |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | VP Supply Chain, DC Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manager forecasts capacity needs: seasonal peaks, new stores (~10–15/year), replenishment volume; allocates across partners by capacity, performance, cost; ensures no single partner >50% | Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2–3 days/quarter |
| 2 | Coordinates surge capacity for peaks: pre-books vehicles, temporary agreements, adjusts DC scheduling | Logistics Manager | DC Operations | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Capacity forecasting (links to VS-02 / W31 demand forecasting, VS-60 / W2307 fulfillment capacity) — the volume forecast
- Allocation (links to VS-56 / W2196 scorecard) — the performance-based allocation
- DC scheduling (links to VS-04 / W585 dock scheduling) — the surge coordination

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Capacity-shortfall risk**: a peak exceeds the 3PL capacity, causing delivery delays; mitigated by the quarterly forecast and the surge-capacity pre-booking
- **Over-allocation-to-one risk**: >50% with one partner creates leverage/continuity risk; mitigated by the diversification rule

### Automation Opportunity
- Capacity-need auto-forecast; allocation auto-suggested per performance/cost

### Controls
- CTL-397 (ensure controlled execution — 3PL Partner Onboarding & Qualification (PA-56.1).)
- CTL-397: capacity-plan accuracy; operational: VP Supply Chain sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/quarter + ~1 week surge = **~120 hours/year** Logistics Manager.

## W2196. 3PL Partner Performance Scorecard

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly review cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 scorecards/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | VP Supply Chain, COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates scorecard: on-time delivery rate (≥95%), damage rate (<0.5%), order acceptance rate, avg delivery time, complaint rate, driver professionalism; Manager reviews with partner | System / Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1–2 hours/partner |
| 2 | Below-target partners get 30-day corrective action plans; quarterly: VP reviews portfolio for retention/reallocation/new sourcing decisions | Logistics Manager | COO | 2–3 hours/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Scorecard (links to VS-67 / W2458 vendor scorecard, VS-56 / W2199 SLA monitoring) — the 3PL performance data
- Analytics (links to VS-27 / W113) — the scorecard visualization
- Corrective action (links to VS-67 / W2465 PIP pattern) — the below-target CAP

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scorecard-without-consequence risk**: below-target partners don't face consequences (the CAP isn't enforced); mitigated by the retention/reallocation decision at the quarterly review
- **Metric-gaming risk**: a partner games a metric (marks "delivered" before actual delivery to hit OTD); mitigated by the POD-verified delivery (W2202) as the ground truth

### Automation Opportunity
- Scorecard auto-generated; below-target auto-flagged with CAP

### Controls
- CTL-397: 3PL performance accuracy; CTL-24 (delivery-fraud detection); operational: COO quarterly portfolio review

### Time Estimate
~1.5 hours/partner × 12 + ~2.5 hours/quarter = **~90 hours/year** Logistics Manager.

## W2197. 3PL Contract Termination & Transition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Partner underperformance or strategic decision |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; ~1–2/year |
| **Volume** | ~1–2 transitions/year |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement, IT, VP Supply Chain, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manager initiates termination per contract terms (60–90 days notice); documents justification; Procurement sources replacement; IT begins integration | Logistics Manager / Procurement / IT | VP Supply Chain | 30–60 days |
| 2 | Executes volume transition over 2–4 weeks; closes financial settlement with departing partner | Logistics Manager | Finance | 2–4 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract termination (links to VS-03 / W62B 3PL offboarding, VS-56 / W2190) — the termination process
- Volume transition (links to VS-56 / W2195 capacity allocation) — the reallocation
- Financial settlement (links to VS-15 / W7, VS-56 / W2206 invoice) — the final settlement

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transition-disruption risk**: the volume shift causes delivery gaps during the handover; mitigated by the overlap period (new partner ramped up before old fully offboarded)
- **Final-settlement-dispute risk**: the departing partner disputes the final invoice or claims unpaid deliveries; mitigated by the POD-verified settlement (W2202) and the CTL-48 dispute workflow

### Automation Opportunity
- Termination-checklist auto-sequenced; volume-transition auto-coordinated; settlement auto-reconciled

### Controls
- CTL-397 (ensure controlled execution — 3PL Partner Onboarding & Qualification (PA-56.1).)
- operational: partner-master status update; final-settlement bank-detail guard; operational: VP Supply Chain sign-off

### Time Estimate
~45 days/transition × ~1.5/year = **~200 hours/year** Logistics Manager + Procurement + IT + Finance.
