## W2206. Monthly 3PL Invoice Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | 3PL partner submits monthly invoice |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; ~10–15 invoices |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 30–50M/month total logistics |
| **Owner** | Logistics Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analyst matches invoice vs. system delivery records: validates count, distances, surcharges, SLA penalties; investigates discrepancies with partner; approves adjusted invoice; payment Net 30 | Logistics Finance Analyst | Finance Manager | 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- AP/invoice (links to VS-15 / W7, W7C, VS-56 / W2202 POD) — the invoice matching
- Delivery records (links to VS-56 / W2198 tracking, W2202 POD) — the system-record baseline
- Settlement (links to VS-15 / W7) — the Net-30 payment

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Invoice-vs-system-mismatch risk**: the partner's invoice doesn't match the system delivery count (over-billing); mitigated by the POD-matched reconciliation (no POD, no payment)
- **SLA-penalty-omission risk**: the partner's invoice doesn't deduct the SLA-penalty credits; mitigated by the SLA-penalty auto-applied from the W2199 monitoring

### Automation Opportunity
- Invoice auto-matched to delivery records; discrepancies auto-flagged; SLA-penalties auto-deducted

### Controls
- CTL-399 (ensure controlled execution — 3PL Settlement & Cost Optimization (PA-56.3).)
- operational: invoice/POD-match integrity; CTL-178 (3PL SLA reviews documented with penalties applied at billing); operational: Finance Manager sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month × 12 partners = **~60 hours/month** Logistics Finance Analyst.

- Route data (links to VS-06 / W196, VS-56 / W2204) — the route attribution
- Partner scorecard (links to VS-56 / W2196) — the cost alongside performance

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cost-without-context risk**: a high per-delivery cost on a route reflects its difficulty (remote, bulky items), not partner inefficiency; mitigated by the like-for-like comparison (same route, same delivery type)
- **Optimization-vs-service-trade-off risk**: cost-optimization routes to the cheapest partner, ignoring service quality; mitigated by the cost-and-performance joint view

### Automation Opportunity
- Per-delivery cost auto-computed; high-cost auto-flagged with context

### Controls
- CTL-399 (ensure controlled execution — 3PL Settlement & Cost Optimization (PA-56.3).)

### System Touchpoints
- Contract/fuel clause (links to VS-61 / W2316 fuel pass-through, VS-56 / W2193 rate card) — the fuel-adjustment clause
- DOE price (links to VS-61 / W2311) — the >5%-change trigger
- Rate/ERP (links to VS-29 / W304, VS-15 / W7) — the rate adjustment implementation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Clause-ambiguity-dispute risk**: the fuel clause is ambiguous (which baseline, what lag); mitigated by the clear clause terms (DOE weekly, 1-month lag, ±5% band)

### Automation Opportunity
- DOE-price-change auto-monitored; clause-trigger auto-detected; rate auto-adjusted on validation

### Controls
- CTL-399 (contract/fuel-clause accuracy — links to VS-61); CTL-44 (rate-adjustment integrity); operational: CFO sign-off
