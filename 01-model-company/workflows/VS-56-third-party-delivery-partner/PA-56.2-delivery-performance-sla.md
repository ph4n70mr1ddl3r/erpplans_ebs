## W2198. Real-Time Delivery Tracking & Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Active deliveries in progress |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~17,200 deliveries/month |
| **Volume** | ~573 deliveries/day (~17,200/month) |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Manager, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System tracks all 3PL deliveries: driver location, ETA, status; Coordinator monitors exceptions (delays >30 min, no-show, breakdown, route deviation); auto-alerts store of ETA | System / Logistics Coord | Logistics Manager | Real-time |
| 2 | Store confirms receipt; exceptions escalated to Logistics Manager for corrective action | Store Manager / System | Logistics Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Telematics/tracking (links to VS-06 / W199 fleet telematics, VS-56 / W2191 integration) — the real-time GPS tracking
- Alerting (links to VS-27 / W380) — the exception auto-alert
- Store comms (links to VS-07 / W571) — the ETA notification to stores

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Tracking-blind-spot risk**: a delivery enters a GPS dead-zone (tunnel, remote province) and tracking goes dark; mitigated by the last-known-position + ETA-estimate fallback
- **Exception-overload risk**: too many minor exceptions (5-min delays) desensitize the Coordinator; mitigated by the >30-min threshold for exception-flagging

### Automation Opportunity
- Tracking auto-monitored; exceptions auto-flagged at threshold; ETA auto-pushed to store

### Controls
- CTL-180 (ensure 3PL delivery-partner compliance & performance.)
- CTL-398 (delivery-tracking integrity — links to VS-06); operational: Logistics Manager escalation on exceptions

### Time Estimate
Real-time monitoring absorbed into Logistics Coordinator role (~4 hours/day).

## W2199. SLA Compliance Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily SLA report |
| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | ~17,200 deliveries/month; 1–3 day SLA |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System calculates daily SLA compliance by partner, DC, route, store; Coordinator investigates breaches (traffic, weather, capacity, dispatch delay); implements corrections | System / Logistics Coord | Logistics Manager | 1–2 hours/day |

### System Touchpoints
- SLA engine (links to VS-56 / W2196 scorecard, VS-60 / W2292 fulfillment SLA) — the daily SLA computation
- Analytics (links to VS-27 / W113) — the SLA-breach dataset
- Partner scorecard (links to VS-56 / W2196) — the SLA feeds the scorecard

### Pain Points / Risks
- **SLA-target-unrealistic risk**: the SLA is set tighter than the logistics reality (Philippine traffic, archipelago), causing chronic breaches that demoralize; mitigated by the SLA calibrated to the actual logistics capability
- **Breach-without-correction risk**: breaches are reported but the root cause isn't fixed; mitigated by the root-cause analysis and the partner-CAP (W2196)

### Automation Opportunity
- SLA auto-computed daily; breaches auto-flagged with root-cause-suggestion

### Controls
- CTL-180 (ensure 3PL delivery-partner compliance & performance.)
- CTL-398 (SLA-accuracy — links to VS-56); operational: Logistics Manager daily sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1.5 hours/day = **~400 hours/year** Logistics Coordinator.

## W2201. Delivery Quality Inspection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store receives delivery from 3PL |
| **Frequency** | ~1,800–2,400 store delivery drops/month (2–3 per store per week) |
| **Volume** | ~60–80 drops/day chain-wide (the consolidation canon per data-volumes §1.1) |
| **Owner** | Store Receiving Clerk |
| **Participants** | Receiving Clerk, Store Manager, Logistics Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clerk inspects: damage, count vs. manifest, item condition; records GR; damaged items photographed, noted on POD, damage record created and routed to damage-claim handling process | Receiving Clerk | Store Manager | 10–15 min/delivery |
| 2 | Weekly: Store Manager reports quality issues; patterns by partner or route investigated | Store Manager | Logistics Manager | 15 min/week |

### System Touchpoints
- GR/inspection (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, W109 store-level receiving) — the delivery inspection and GR
- Damage claims (links to VS-90 / W90 damage claims, VS-56 / W2196 scorecard) — the damage-record routing
- POD (links to VS-56 / W2202) — the damage notation on POD

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Damage-not-detected risk**: the Clerk accepts a delivery without noticing hidden damage, losing the claim window; mitigated by the inspection-standard and the damage-photo evidence
- **Chronic-damage-partner risk**: a partner consistently damages deliveries, but the pattern isn't seen because each delivery's damage is handled as a one-off; mitigated by the weekly quality-issue report aggregating per-partner damage rates

### Automation Opportunity
- Damage-rate-by-partner auto-aggregated; chronic-damage partner auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-24: receiving-fraud detection; CTL-75 (damage-claim detection & recovery — links to VS-90); operational: Store Manager weekly sign-off

### Time Estimate
~12 min/delivery × ~450/day = **~90 hours/day** Receiving Clerks (distributed across stores, absorbed into receiving).

## W2202. Proof of Delivery & Documentation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Every delivery completion |
| **Frequency** | Every delivery (~10,000/month) |
| **Volume** | ~10,000 PODs/month |
| **Owner** | 3PL Driver / Receiving Clerk |
| **Participants** | Logistics Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Driver obtains POD: digital signature + GPS + timestamp; system stores with delivery record; auto-matches for settlement | 3PL Driver / System | Logistics Coordinator | 2 min/delivery |

### System Touchpoints
- POD system (links to VS-56 / W2191 integration, VS-06 / W199 telematics) — the digital POD capture
- Settlement (links to VS-56 / W2206 invoice) — the POD auto-matched for settlement
- GR (links to VS-04 / W3) — the POD reconciled to the GR

### Pain Points / Risks
- **POD-fraud risk**: a driver forges a POD (delivers to the wrong location, signs for an undelivered item); mitigated by the GPS + timestamp + digital-signature triangulation
- **POD-lost risk**: a POD isn't captured (system failure, driver forgot), leaving the delivery unverifiable for settlement; mitigated by the no-POD-no-settlement rule (the partner can't bill without a POD)

### Automation Opportunity
- POD fully digital (signature + GPS + timestamp); auto-matched to settlement

### Controls
- CTL-398 (ensure controlled execution — Delivery Performance & SLA Management (PA-56.2).)
- operational: POD integrity; delivery-fraud detection; operational: Logistics Coordinator settlement-match review

### Time Estimate
~2 min/delivery × ~10,000/month = **~330 hours/month** 3PL Drivers + system (auto-captured).

| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

- DC coordination (links to VS-04 / W3, W106 dispatch) — the re-delivery coordination
- Customer comms (links to VS-13 / W56) — the exception-customer notification

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Exception-cascade risk**: an exception triggers a re-delivery that also fails (the root cause persists); mitigated by the exception-depth-limit and the root-cause fix

### Automation Opportunity
- Exception auto-categorized; resolution auto-suggested; customer auto-notified

### Controls
- CTL-398 (exception-management accuracy); CTL-24 (exception-pattern fraud detection); operational: Logistics Manager exception-rate review

### Time Estimate
~15 min/exception × ~650/month = **~160 hours/month** Logistics Coordinator.

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily delivery planning |
| **Frequency** | Daily; 4 DCs |
| **Volume** | ~4 DCs × 5–8 routes × 2–3 stops |
| **Owner** | DC Dispatch Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Logistics Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates optimized routes: groups by geography, minimizes distance, balances capacity; Supervisor reviews and adjusts for priorities, last-minute orders, road closures | System / DC Dispatch Supervisor | Logistics Manager | 1–2 hours/day |

### System Touchpoints
- Route optimization (links to VS-06 / W196 route planning, W199 telematics) — the route engine
- DC dispatch (links to VS-04 / W106 dispatch, W585 dock scheduling) — the dispatch coordination
- Order management (links to VS-60 / W536) — the delivery-order source

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Optimization-vs-reality-divergence risk**: the optimized route doesn't account for real-time road conditions (traffic, construction); mitigated by the Supervisor's manual adjustment and the real-time traffic feed
- **Over-optimization risk**: too many stops per route saves cost but delays the last-stop customer; mitigated by the stops-per-route cap

### Automation Opportunity
- Routes auto-generated; real-time-traffic-adjusted; Supervisor exceptions only

### Controls
- CTL-398 (ensure controlled execution — Delivery Performance & SLA Management (PA-56.2).)
- CTL-398 (route-optimization accuracy); operational: Logistics Manager daily sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1.5 hours/day × 4 DCs = **~1,500 hours/year** DC Dispatch Supervisors.

## W2205. Seasonal Delivery Surge Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Peak season (Christmas, summer) |
| **Frequency** | 2–3 major peaks/year |
| **Volume** | ~30–50% volume increase |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manager forecasts peak volume; pre-books 3PL capacity 4–6 weeks ahead; implements surge plan: extended windows, additional routes, temporary drivers, DC overtime | Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1–3 weeks |
| 2 | Post-peak: analyzes vs. plan; adjusts for next peak; updates contracts with peak rate agreements | Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2–3 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Capacity forecasting (links to VS-56 / W2195 capacity planning, VS-60 / W2307) — the peak-volume forecast
- 3PL capacity (links to VS-56 / W2190 partner onboarding, W2193 rate card) — the surge capacity pre-booking
- DC scheduling (links to VS-04 / W585) — the extended windows

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Capacity-shortfall risk**: the peak exceeds the pre-booked capacity; mitigated by the 4–6 week pre-booking and the temporary-driver/DC-overtime surge plan
- **Peak-rate-cost risk**: surge rates (peak premium) inflate the delivery cost; mitigated by the annual peak-rate agreement (locked-in rate vs. spot premium)

### Automation Opportunity
- Peak-volume auto-forecasted; capacity-need auto-computed; peak-rate-agreement auto-referenced

### Controls
- CTL-398 (ensure controlled execution — Delivery Performance & SLA Management (PA-56.2).)
- CTL-398 (capacity-plan accuracy); operational: VP Supply Chain sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2 weeks/peak × 2.5 + ~2.5 days post-peak = **~200 hours/year** Logistics Manager.
