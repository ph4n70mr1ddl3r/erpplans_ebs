## W3561. Project Benefits Realization Tracking & Validation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Business case (W3551); post-go-live; quarterly cycle |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + post-implementation |
| **Volume** | All completed/in-flight projects |
| **Owner** | PMO / FP&A |
| **Participants** | PMO, FP&A (links to VS-17.4/VS-33.2), Sponsor, Business Owner, BI (links to VS-28.1), Strategy (links to VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PMO/Sponsor define the benefits plan per project: benefit types (financial — revenue/cost/cash; non-financial — customer/NPS links to VS-13, operational, regulatory, ESG links to VS-25), baselines, targets, measurement method, owner, and realization timeline; align to the business case (W3551) | PMO / Sponsor / FP&A | Sponsor | Per project |
| 2 | PMO/FP&A track and validate benefits realization post-go-live: measure actual vs target (links to domain analytics, e.g., store sales lift links to VS-109/W3491, capex ROI links to VS-40/W2748, ESG links to VS-108/W3468), validate attribution, and identify shortfalls/root-cause | PMO / FP&A / Business Owner | Sponsor | Quarterly |
| 3 | PMO/Strategy report benefits realization at portfolio level to executives (links to VS-33.2) and governance (W3552), manage shortfalls (corrective action, re-baseline), and feed outcomes to portfolio prioritization (W3547) and lessons (W3560) | PMO / Strategy | COO / CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A/performance (links to VS-17.4/VS-33.2); BI (links to VS-28.1); domain analytics (links to VS-109/W3491/VS-108/W3468/VS-40/W2748); PMIS (links to W3562)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Benefit-overstatement risk**: Projected benefits not realized; mitigated by disciplined baselines and validation
- **Attribution-ambiguity risk**: Hard to isolate project benefit from macro; mitigated by control-group/methodology


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-measure of "measurement method, owner, and realization timeline" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validate benefits realization post-go-live" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "report benefits realization at portfolio level to executives" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-567 (ensure controlled execution — Project Benefits Realization, PMIS & PMO Analytics (PA-112.3).)
- operational: disciplined baselines and validation; operational: control-group/methodology

### Time Estimate
- Quarterly + post-implementation
