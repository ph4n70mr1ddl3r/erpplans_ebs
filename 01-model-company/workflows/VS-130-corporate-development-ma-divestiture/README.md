# VS-130: Corporate Development, Mergers, Acquisitions, Divestiture & Strategic Transactions

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Corporate Development, M&A & Strategic Transactions workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning
the end-to-end lifecycle of **inorganic growth and corporate transactions** (acquisitions,
mergers, joint ventures, strategic alliances/partnerships, divestitures, and carve-outs) for a
PHP 62.3B-revenue, 5-entity, 200-store hardware/home-improvement retailer growing 10–15 new
stores/year and operating across multiple Philippine regions. Beyond organic store openings
(VS-37), BuildRight's growth path may include acquiring competitor chains or regional banners,
acquiring/merging with specialty retailers or service businesses (installation, design, B2B),
forming joint ventures (e.g., property development with BuildRight Property Management Inc.,
marketplace or fintech JVs), and divesting or carving out non-strategic assets, stores,
categories, or entities.

Today this discipline is **entirely unowned as a program**: VS-33 sets corporate strategy and
the organic growth plan, VS-37 executes new-store opening, VS-97 manages the existing property
portfolio, VS-40 performs the *financial accounting* of capital projects, VS-100.3 provides
*corporate legal advisory* on contracts, and VS-112 governs *project/program* delivery — but no
value stream owns the **corporate-development / M&A transaction lifecycle**: growth-strategy and
build-vs-buy analysis, target sourcing/screening, valuation and deal structuring, due diligence
(commercial, financial, legal, operational, HR, ESG), SPA negotiation, regulatory clearance
(including PCC merger notification via VS-129's W3967), Day-1 readiness and cutover, post-merger
integration (PMI) and synergy realization, carve-out/separation and transitional services, and
divestiture execution and value-realization tracking. 'merger and acquisition', 'divestiture'
(as a corporate transaction), and 'acquisition integration' each appeared in **zero** PA files
(the only 'divestiture' references were conflict-of-interest divestiture, not corporate M&A).

This is distinct from **VS-33 (Strategic Planning)** which sets strategy and the organic plan
(this value stream executes *inorganic* transactions that implement that strategy). It is distinct
from **VS-37 (Store Opening)** which opens company-owned stores organically (this value stream
acquires whole businesses/store portfolios). It is distinct from **VS-40 (Capex & Project
Accounting)** which accounts for capital-project spend (this value stream owns the *transaction*
and *integration*, with financial accounting flowing to VS-40). It is distinct from **VS-112
(PMO)** which governs project delivery (this value stream owns the *deal* and *PMI*, which uses
PMO discipline but adds transaction-specific governance). It is distinct from **VS-100 (Legal
Operations)** which manages legal matters/contracts (this value stream drives the *business*
transaction with legal as a key participant). It is distinct from **VS-129 (Competition &
Antitrust)** which owns merger notification/clearance (this value stream owns the broader M&A
lifecycle, calling on W3967 as a gate). Corporate development & M&A is the *inorganic-growth and
transaction* discipline — distinct from strategy, store opening, capex accounting, PMO, legal,
and competition compliance.

BuildRight's exposure is strategic: a growth-stage, family-owned corporation pursuing scale and
capability will inevitably evaluate acquisitions, JVs, and divestitures, and the value at stake
(PHP hundreds of millions to low billions per major transaction) and the integration-failure rate
(60–70% of M&A deals fail to realize targeted value) make disciplined corporate development a
board-level priority. Without a dedicated owner, BuildRight risks ad-hoc deals, weak due
diligence, integration failures, and divestiture value leakage — risks that no existing value
stream owns end-to-end.
