## W3993. Post-Merger Integration (PMI) Strategy, Planning & Synergy Realization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Signing; pre-close integration planning |
| **Frequency** | Per transaction |
| **Volume** | Per transaction |
| **Owner** | Integration Lead / Head of Corporate Development |
| **Participants** | Integration Lead, Corp Dev, all workstreams, COO, CFO, affected business unit, PMO (links to VS-112), Strategy (links to VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Integration Lead define the PMI strategy and guiding principles: value-creation thesis, integration intensity (full absorption vs preservation), integration operating model and governance, synergy targets (revenue/cost/financial), and the integration roadmap with value-capture milestones | Integration Lead / Corp Dev / Strategy | CEO / Integration Steering | Per transaction |
| 2 | Integration Lead build the integration plan: workstream plans (commercial, operations, finance, HR, IT, legal), synergy-tracking (baseline, target, owner, date), dependencies, risks, and budget — leveraging PMO discipline (links to VS-112) and the Day-1 plan (links to W3994) | Integration Lead / workstream leads | Integration Steering | Per transaction |
| 3 | Integration Lead execute synergy realization, track value capture against baseline, manage integration risks, and report progress to the steering committee and Board (links to VS-36) | Integration Lead / workstreams | CEO | Per transaction + ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- PMO (links to VS-112); strategy (links to VS-33); finance (links to VS-17.4); governance (links to VS-36); analytics (links to VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Synergy-shortfall risk**: Targeted synergies not realized; mitigated by bottom-up tracking and accountability
- **Integration-overreach risk**: Over-integration destroying acquired value; mitigated by intensity-tailored approach


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "Integration Lead define the PMI strategy and guiding principles" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-track of "Integration Lead build the integration plan" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "report progress to the steering committee and Board" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-622 (ensure controlled execution — Integration, Carve-Out, Divestiture & Post-Merger Performance (PA-130.3).)
- operational: bottom-up tracking and accountability; operational: intensity-tailored approach

### Time Estimate
- 6–24 months per transaction
