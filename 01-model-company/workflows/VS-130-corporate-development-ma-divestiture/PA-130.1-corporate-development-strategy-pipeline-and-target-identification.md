## W3977. Corporate Development & Growth Strategy, Portfolio & Capital Allocation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual strategy (links to VS-33.1); board direction; capital plan |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on direction |
| **Volume** | Enterprise corporate-development strategy |
| **Owner** | Head of Corporate Development / CEO |
| **Participants** | Corp Dev, Strategy (links to VS-33), Finance/Treasury (links to VS-17.4/VS-18), Board (links to VS-36), business-unit leaders, Legal (links to VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Corp Dev/Strategy define the corporate-development strategy aligned to enterprise strategy (links to VS-33.1): growth objectives (geographic — new Philippine regions; capability — installation/design/B2B/digital/fintech; category — adjacencies), portfolio lens (which businesses/categories to grow/hold/divest), and the role of M&A/JV vs organic (links to VS-37) | Corp Dev / Strategy | CEO / Board | Annual |
| 2 | Corp Dev/Finance establish the capital-allocation framework for transactions: capex vs M&A vs shareholder returns, financing capacity (cash, debt links to VS-18, equity), risk appetite, and the impact on the 5-entity structure and consolidation (links to VS-72) | Corp Dev / Finance / Treasury | CFO / Board | Annual |
| 3 | CEO/Board ratify the strategy, approve the corporate-development mandate and budget, and set decision rights/governance (links to W3983) | CEO / Board | Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy (links to VS-33); finance/treasury (links to VS-17.4/VS-18); governance (links to VS-36); consolidation (links to VS-72)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Strategy-misalignment risk**: Deals pursued opportunistically without strategic anchor; mitigated by strategy-first mandate
- **Capital-overcommitment risk**: M&A crowding out operations; mitigated by capital-allocation framework


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "Corp Dev/Strategy define the corporate-development strategy aligned to enterprise" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the corporate-development mandate and budget, and set decision…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-620 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Development Strategy, Pipeline & Target Identification (PA-130.1).)
- operational: strategy-first mandate; operational: capital-allocation framework

### Time Estimate
- 3–4 weeks annual

## W3980. Target Screening, Strategic Fit & Preliminary Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Prioritized target |
| **Frequency** | Per target |
| **Volume** | Per prioritized target |
| **Owner** | Head of Corporate Development |
| **Participants** | Corp Dev, Strategy, Insights (links to VS-44), Finance (links to VS-17.4), affected business unit, Legal (links to VS-100), HR (links to VS-19/VS-84), Tax (links to VS-79) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Corp Dev conduct the preliminary strategic-fit and business assessment using public/authorized information: market position, store/portfolio overlap, assortment and channel fit, customer base, brand, operations, and high-level synergies | Corp Dev / Strategy / affected BU | Head of Corp Dev | Per target |
| 2 | Corp Dev/Finance conduct a preliminary financial screen: revenue, EBITDA/margin, growth, capital structure, and a preliminary value range; Corp Dev/Legal/HR screen red flags (legal, labor/union, tax, ABC, ESG) | Corp Dev / Finance / Legal / HR / Tax | Head of Corp Dev | Per target |
| 3 | Corp Dev recommend go/no-go to the stage-gate (links to W3983), and if go, prepare the LOI/term-sheet groundwork (links to W3982) | Corp Dev | CEO | Per target |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy/insights (links to VS-33.3/VS-44); finance (links to VS-17.4); legal (links to VS-100); HR (links to VS-19/VS-84); tax (links to VS-79)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Overpayment-setup risk**: Thin screen leading to inflated price; mitigated by disciplined screen and value range
- **Hidden risk**: Material issues missed pre-LOI; mitigated by red-flag screen


### Automation Opportunity
- System rule-based authorization of "Corp Dev conduct the preliminary strategic-fit and business assessment" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-flag of "Corp Dev/Finance conduct a preliminary financial screen" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-generate of "prepare the LOI/term-sheet groundwork" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-620 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Development Strategy, Pipeline & Target Identification (PA-130.1).)
- operational: disciplined screen and value range; operational: red-flag screen

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 weeks per target

## W3981. Financial Modeling, Valuation & Investment Sizing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-screen go decision; offer preparation |
| **Frequency** | Per target |
| **Volume** | Per target |
| **Owner** | Head of Corporate Development / CFO |
| **Participants** | Corp Dev, Finance (links to VS-17.4), Treasury (links to VS-18), Tax (links to VS-79), Strategy, external valuation advisors, affected business unit |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Corp Dev/Finance build the deal financial model: standalone financials (revenue, EBITDA, FCF), synergy cases (revenue/cost/financial), integration cost, financing structure, and returns (NPV/IRR/payback/ROIC) consistent with BuildRight's standards (links to VS-40/VS-17.4) | Corp Dev / Finance | CFO | Per target |
| 2 | Corp Dev/external-advisors determine valuation using multiple methods (DCF, trading comps, transaction comps, asset-based) and establish the offer range and walk-away price; assess financing mix (cash, debt links to VS-18, equity) and covenant/liquidity impact | Corp Dev / Treasury / external advisors | CFO | Per target |
| 3 | CFO/Corp-Dev present the valuation and financing plan to the approval authority (links to W3983), and set the negotiation envelope (links to W3982) | CFO / Corp Dev | CEO / Board | Per target |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance (links to VS-17.4); treasury (links to VS-18); tax (links to VS-79); capex accounting (links to VS-40); analytics (links to VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-optimism risk**: Synergies/returns overstated; mitigated by conservative base case and sensitivity analysis
- **Financing risk**: Deal un-fundable or covenant-breaching; mitigated by treasury involvement


### Automation Opportunity
- workflow automation; system-driven processing with exception handling

### Controls
- CTL-620 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Development Strategy, Pipeline & Target Identification (PA-130.1).)
- operational: conservative base case and sensitivity analysis; operational: treasury involvement

## W3983. M&A Governance, Approval Authority & Stage-Gate Process

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Stage-gate review; approval request |
| **Frequency** | Per stage-gate |
| **Volume** | Per transaction |
| **Owner** | CEO / Board |
| **Participants** | CEO, Board/Audit Committee (links to VS-36), CFO, Head of Corp Dev, VP Legal, affected business unit |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Corp Dev/CEO operate the stage-gate process: defined gates (strategy, screen, valuation, LOI, DD, SPA, close, integration), decision criteria, documentation, and the approval-authority matrix by deal size (CEO → Board → shareholders) tied to BuildRight's authority matrix (links to VS-36 governance) | Corp Dev / CEO | Board | Per gate |
| 2 | Board/Audit-Committee review material transactions (strategic fit, valuation, risks, financing, competition clearance links to W3967, integration plan links to PA-130.3) and approve/decline at each binding gate | Board / Audit Committee | Board / Shareholders | Per gate |
| 3 | Corp Dev/Legal document decisions, maintain the deal file and audit trail (links to VS-88/VS-21), and ensure disclosure to debt-holders/lenders where covenants require (links to VS-18) | Corp Dev / Legal | CEO | Per gate |

### System Touchpoints
- Governance (links to VS-36); legal/records (links to VS-100/VS-88); audit (links to VS-21); treasury/covenants (links to VS-18)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Approval-bypass risk**: Binding commitments made without authority; mitigated by stage-gate and authority matrix
- **Covenant-breach risk**: Deal not disclosed to lenders; mitigated by covenant review


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "documentation, and the approval-authority matrix by deal size" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review material transactions" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-file of "file and audit trail" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-160 (ensure M&A stage-gate & regulatory clearance.)
- operational: stage-gate and authority matrix; operational: covenant review

### Time Estimate
- Per gate
