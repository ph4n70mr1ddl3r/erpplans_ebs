## W3985. Commercial & Strategic Due Diligence

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | LOI/exclusivity (links to W3982) |
| **Frequency** | Per target |
| **Volume** | Per target |
| **Owner** | Head of Corporate Development / Strategy |
| **Participants** | Corp Dev, Strategy (links to VS-33.3), Insights (links to VS-44), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Trade/B2B Sales (links to VS-11), Marketing (links to VS-14), affected business unit, external commercial advisors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Corp Dev/Strategy conduct commercial DD: market size/growth, competitive position, customer base and concentration (trade/B2B/B2G mix), store portfolio performance, assortment/channel/digital maturity, brand equity, and revenue quality (links to VS-44/VS-57 for market intelligence) | Corp Dev / Strategy / Insights | Head of Corp Dev | Per target |
| 2 | Corp Dev/Merchandising assess strategic fit and synergy potential: category/assortment overlap and gaps, store/network overlap and cannibalization, channel complementarity, vendor overlap, and revenue/cost synergy hypotheses to refine the model (links to W3981) | Corp Dev / Merchandising / Trade Sales | Head of Corp Dev | Per target |
| 3 | Corp Dev compile findings into the commercial DD report, flag value-killers/risk, and update the deal model and SPA price/terms (links to W3991) | Corp Dev / Strategy | Head of Corp Dev | Per target |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy/insights (links to VS-33.3/VS-44); merchandising (links to VS-01); competitive intel (links to VS-57); analytics (links to VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Synergy-overstatement risk**: Commercial DD overstating synergies; mitigated by bottom-up hypotheses
- **Missed-cannibalization risk**: Network overlap understated; mitigated by store-level overlap analysis


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-compile of "compile findings into the commercial DD report, flag value-killers/risk, and…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-621 (ensure controlled execution — Transaction Due Diligence, Valuation & Deal Structuring (PA-130.2).)
- operational: bottom-up hypotheses; operational: store-level overlap analysis

### Time Estimate
- 3–6 weeks per target

| **Volume** | Per transaction |

| **Participants** | Legal (links to VS-100), external counsel, Corp Dev, Finance/Tax, HR, CEO, sellers/sellers' counsel |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal/external-counsel draft the SPA (asset or share purchase agreement) reflecting structure (links to W3982): purchase price and mechanism, working-capital adjustment, escrow/retention/earn-out, reps & warranties, indemnities and caps/baskets, specific indemnities for DD findings, conditions precedent (incl. PCC clearance links to W3992), termination rights, and restrictive covenants | Legal / External Counsel | VP Legal | Per transaction |

- **Inadequate-protection risk**: DD risks not reflected in SPA; mitigated by specific indemnities and W&I
- **Negotiation-creep risk**: Price/terms eroding returns; mitigated by walk-away discipline and stage-gate


### Automation Opportunity
- workflow automation; system-driven processing with exception handling

### Controls
- CTL-621 (ensure controlled execution — Transaction Due Diligence, Valuation & Deal Structuring (PA-130.2).)
- operational: specific indemnities and W&I; operational: walk-away discipline and stage-gate
