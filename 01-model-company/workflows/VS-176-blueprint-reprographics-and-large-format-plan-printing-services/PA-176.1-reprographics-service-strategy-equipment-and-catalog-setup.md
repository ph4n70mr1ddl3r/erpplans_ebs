## W5081. Reprographics Service Strategy, Catalog & Business-Model Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy refresh (VS-33); B2B/trade-pro program (VS-43); new service launch |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | 1 program across selected high-trade-density stores |
| **Owner** | Director, Store Services / Trade Pro & Services (VS-43) |
| **Participants** | Strategy (VS-33), Trade Pro (VS-43), Merchandising (VS-01), Marketing (VS-14), FP&A (VS-17.4), Legal/Compliance (VS-100/VS-91), IT (VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the reprographics strategy & catalog: the service catalog (large-format plan print/scan/copy, bid-set/plan-room, binding/folding, archiving), the rollout (which high-trade-density stores), the make-vs-partner/3PL model, and the KPIs (job volume, margin, attach, uptime) | Director Store Services / Trade Pro / Strategy | Director Store Services | Annual |
| 2 | Build the business case: equipment capex, consumables cost, breakeven job volume, payback, and the trade-pro-traffic/attach linkage to B2B revenue (links VS-11/VS-43) with FP&A | Director Store Services / FP&A | Director Store Services / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Govern & approve: leadership sign-off (VS-36), the legal/compliance posture (copyright/IP, RA 10173 document confidentiality — W5087), and the entity placement with Legal/Compliance | Director Store Services / Legal / Compliance | Director Store Services / CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A/strategy (VS-17.4/VS-33); trade-pro/B2B (VS-43/VS-11); merchandising (VS-01); legal (VS-100); privacy (VS-91); print-management system (new — W5086); IT (VS-27)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Demand-misread risk**: Deploying equipment where B2B trade volume won't sustain it; mitigated by trade-density-based rollout
- **Legal/compliance risk**: Copyright/IP and RA 10173 exposure mishandled; mitigated by Legal/Compliance posture at step 3 (W5087)
- **Margin-illusion risk**: Margin eroded by hidden consumables/equipment cost; mitigated by fully-burdened unit-economics (W5083)

### Automation Opportunity
- Trade-density-based site-suitability scoring; business-case template tied to B2B actuals

### Controls
- CTL-758 (ensure controlled execution — Reprographics Service Strategy, Equipment & Catalog Setup (PA-176.1).)
- CTL-758: business case leadership-approved (VS-36); legal/privacy posture defined (VS-100/VS-91); economics FP&A-validated

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual strategy + per-store business case

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-43/VS-11 (trade/B2B), VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-100/VS-91 (legal/privacy); W5082 (equipment), W5083 (pricing), W5087 (IP/confidentiality)
