> **Sell & Serve** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Trade, project & wholesale: trade account management, project sales & B2B, and wholesale operations (~40% of revenue).

## Why it matters

~40% of revenue concentrated in ~5,400 B2B accounts; project/contractor/wholesale and government bidding drive large-ticket, lower-frequency, higher-margin business with credit and bond requirements.

## Owner & participants

- **Owner**: VP Trade Sales / B2B Sales Managers
