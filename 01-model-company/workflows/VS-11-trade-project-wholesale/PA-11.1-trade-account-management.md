| Field | Detail |

| **Volume** | Government tenders range PHP 500K–20M; institutional RFQs PHP 200K–10M |
| **Owner** | Bid & Tender Manager |
| **Participants** | Bid Manager, Head of Trade / Account Management, Legal Counsel, CFO, Category Manager, Sales Rep |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bid Manager monitors PhilGEPS/Tender boards; downloads RFP | Bid Manager | — | Daily |
| 2 | Initial Go/No-Go: Evaluate capability, capacity, and margin potential | Bid Manager | Head of Trade / Account Management | 1 day |
| 3 | Coordinate with Legal (Tender Bonds, JV agreements) and Finance (Performance Bonds, Cash Flow) | Bid Manager | Legal / CFO | 3–5 days |
| 4 | Compile Bid Package: Pricing (W162), Technical Specs (W50), Corporate Docs | Bid Manager | — | 1–2 weeks |

| 6 | If won: Transition to W163 (Contract Pricing) and W164 (Fulfillment) | Bid Manager | — | — |

### System Touchpoints
- PhilGEPS integration or monitoring dashboard for automated RFP/RFQ alert and download (W166.1)
- Bid Package compilation module: pricing from W162, technical specs from W50, corporate document library (W166.4)
- Bid tracking register with lifecycle status (Identified → Go/No-Go → In Progress → Submitted → Under Evaluation → Won/Lost) (W166.5)
- Integration with W163 (contract pricing for won bids), W164 (staged delivery), and W165 (milestone billing)

### Pain Points / Risks
- Government procurement in the Philippines is heavily bureaucratic — PhilGEPS compliance requirements, notarization, and documentary submissions are rigid; a single missing document disqualifies the entire bid regardless of commercial competitiveness.
- Performance bond and tender bond requirements tie up bank credit facilities; with ~5–10 active bids, PHP 5M–50M in bank guarantees may be encumbered simultaneously, limiting available credit for operations.
- Government payment terms are notoriously long (60–120 days post-delivery for some agencies), creating severe cash flow strain on projects that require upfront material procurement.
- Win rates for government tenders are often low (< 30%) due to lowest-bid-wins evaluation criteria; significant bid preparation costs (staff time, bond fees, document preparation) are sunk regardless of outcome.

## W1011. Customer Trade Account Credit Insurance & Bad Debt Protection Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New trade account credit approval, annual credit insurance renewal, or trade credit insurance claim event |
| **Frequency** | ~500 new credit insurance enrollments/year; ~5,200 annual renewals; ~50–80 claims/year |
| **Volume** | 1 policy per trade account; claims per default event |
| **Owner** | Credit Manager |
| **Participants** | Credit Analyst, Finance Manager, Insurance Broker, Credit Insurance Provider, CFO |

### Background

BuildRight Depot extends trade credit to ~5,200 trade accounts and ~200 corporate accounts (Section 9.2). The combined AR exposure at any time is ~PHP 1.5–2.5 Billion (based on ~3,500 AR invoices/month across ~5,400 accounts, with Net 30–90 terms). Bad debt write-offs average ~0.5–1.0% of AR annually (PHP 7.5–25M/year). Trade credit insurance protects BuildRight against customer insolvency, protracted default (>90 days), and catastrophic concentration risk (single large corporate account default could be PHP 10–50M). In the Philippine market, trade credit insurance is offered by a limited number of providers (Philippine Export-Import Credit Agency Philguarantee, Coface, Euler Hermes, Atradius). BuildRight's Credit Manager maintains a portfolio-level policy covering ~70% of trade account exposure (top 500 accounts by outstanding balance, representing ~80% of AR value). This workflow manages the credit insurance lifecycle: new account enrollment, annual renewal, coverage limit management, claim filing, and recovery.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **New Account Credit Insurance Assessment**: When Credit Analyst approves a new trade account per W24 (Credit Application): (a) assess whether account qualifies for credit insurance — minimum exposure threshold PHP 100,000/year; (b) submit account to credit insurer for coverage approval — insurer runs independent credit assessment using SEC/DTI records, financial statements, and trade references; (c) insurer assigns coverage limit (typically 80–90% of approved credit limit); (d) premium rate quoted (0.15–0.50% of covered sales annually, based on risk tier); (e) Credit Analyst reviews insurer's assessment vs. BuildRight's internal scoring — if insurer declines coverage, Credit Analyst may apply stricter terms (lower limit, shorter terms, COD) per W572; (f) approved coverage loaded into credit management system | Credit Analyst / Credit Insurance Provider | Credit Manager | 30–60 min per new account |
| 2 | **Annual Policy Renewal & Portfolio Review**: Annual: (a) Credit Manager reviews portfolio performance with insurer: claim rate, recovery rate, covered vs. uncovered exposure; (b) insurer adjusts premium rates based on portfolio loss experience; (c) Credit Manager negotiates: aggregate coverage limit (total portfolio cap), per-account sub-limits, deductible per claim, and waiting period; (d) accounts with adverse changes (payment deterioration, industry downturn, negative SEC filings) flagged for coverage reduction or exclusion; (e) new accounts added to policy; closed/inactive accounts removed; (f) premium payment scheduled per policy terms; (g) coverage documentation filed with Treasury per FIN-023 (insurance policy lifecycle) | Credit Manager / Insurance Broker | CFO | 4–8 hours/year |
| 3 | **Ongoing Coverage Monitoring**: Continuous: (a) credit insurer monitors all covered accounts and issues alerts: downgrade notifications (insurer reduces or withdraws coverage for specific accounts due to adverse information); (b) Credit Analyst receives insurer alerts in real-time; (c) on insurer downgrade: Credit Analyst immediately reviews account status — if internal assessment also negative, reduce credit limit or place on hold per W572; (d) if internal assessment remains positive, document disagreement with insurer and maintain current terms (BuildRight bears uninsured risk for the difference); (e) monthly: reconcile covered exposure vs. actual AR outstanding per covered account | Credit Analyst / System | Credit Manager | 15–30 min/week |
| 4 | **Claim Filing & Recovery**: When a covered account defaults (payment > 90 days overdue per W108): (a) Credit Analyst initiates claim with insurer; (b) documentation package: credit agreement, delivery receipts, invoices, statement of account, demand letters, evidence of collection efforts per W108, and any legal filings per W812; (c) insurer reviews claim within 30–60 days; (d) if approved: insurer pays coverage percentage (80–90%) of outstanding amount minus deductible; (e) insurer subrogates rights and pursues recovery from debtor; (f) BuildRight assigns receivable to insurer and cooperates in legal proceedings; (g) if insurer recovers more than claim payment, BuildRight receives proportional share of recovery; (h) claim payment posted to GL: insurance recovery offset against bad debt provision per W81 | Credit Analyst / Credit Manager | CFO | 2–4 hours per claim |
| 5 | **Coverage Gap Analysis & Self-Insurance Decision**: Quarterly: (a) Credit Manager analyzes uncovered exposure: accounts below insurance threshold, accounts declined by insurer, coverage gaps (limits lower than actual exposure); (b) calculate self-insurance reserve requirement: uncovered exposure × expected default rate (historical 0.5–1.0%); (c) recommend to CFO: increase coverage limits (higher premium), tighten credit terms for uncovered accounts, or increase bad debt provision per W81; (d) report to CFO in monthly financial review per W35 | Credit Manager | CFO | 2–3 hours/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Credit management system with insurance coverage tracking per account
- Credit insurer API integration for real-time account status monitoring
- AR aging system (W108, W889) with insurance flag per account
- Insurance claim documentation module (FIN-023, W610)
- GL bad debt provision module (W81)
- Credit scoring engine (W572) with insurer assessment data
- Financial reporting module (W35) for coverage gap analysis

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Insurer coverage withdrawal**: Credit insurers may suddenly withdraw coverage for entire industry sectors (e.g., construction companies during a building downturn) or geographic regions (e.g., typhoon-devastated areas); BuildRight must have contingency plans for rapid credit tightening when insurer coverage is withdrawn
- **Claim documentation burden**: Insurers require extensive documentation (delivery receipts, demand letters, legal filings) to process claims; AP/AR Clerks must maintain meticulous records for covered accounts; incomplete documentation delays or jeopardizes claim payment
- **Premium cost vs. bad debt cost**: Trade credit insurance premiums (0.15–0.50% of covered sales) must be weighed against historical bad debt rates (0.5–1.0% of AR); the ROI depends on coverage percentage and claim recovery efficiency; CFO should review annually
- **Subrogation conflicts**: When insurer pursues legal recovery against a BuildRight customer, the legal action may damage the customer relationship beyond repair; Credit Manager should coordinate with Sales Manager on accounts where both recovery and relationship preservation are priorities


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "records, financial statements, and trade references" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documentation filed with Treasury per FIN-023" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "reviews account status — if internal assessment also negative, reduce credit…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-272 (ensure controlled execution — Trade Account Management (PA-11.1).)
- operational: Credit Manager review and approval gate; operational: periodic reconciliation against source documents

### Staffing Implication
- **Credit Analyst**: Insurance monitoring and claim processing adds ~30–60 min/week; absorbed by existing Credit Analyst team
- **Credit Manager**: Annual renewal and quarterly analysis adds ~15–20 hours/year; absorbed by existing role
- **No incremental headcount**

### Time Estimate
- New account assessment: 30–60 min per account
- Annual renewal: 4–8 hours/year
- Ongoing monitoring: 15–30 min/week
- Claim filing: 2–4 hours per claim
- Quarterly gap analysis: 2–3 hours/quarter

## W1022. Customer Trade Account Statement Dispute & Resolution Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Trade account customer disputes line items on their AR statement or consolidated invoice |
| **Frequency** | ~400–600 disputes/month across ~5,200 trade accounts (~8–10% dispute rate) |
| **Volume** | 1–5 disputed line items per dispute event |
| **Owner** | AR Analyst |
| **Participants** | Customer (B2B), Sales Associate/Store, AR Analyst, Credit Manager, AP Analyst (for vendor-side resolution) |

### Background

BuildRight Depot's ~5,200 trade account customers (Section 9.2) receive monthly statements (W892) or consolidated invoices (W1014) listing all transactions for the billing period. Approximately 8–10% of statements generate disputes — common reasons include: (a) **pricing disputes** (customer believes trade discount was not applied, or promotional price differs from invoiced price); (b) **quantity disputes** (customer claims they received fewer items than invoiced — receiving discrepancy or delivery shortage); (c) **unrecognized transactions** (customer doesn't recognize a charge — possible mis-posting to wrong account or fraudulent use of trade account); (d) **duplicate billing** (same transaction appears twice — system error or re-invoicing); (e) **credit memo not reflected** (customer returned items per W12 but credit memo not yet applied); (f) **payment not credited** (customer paid but payment not yet posted to their account — timing difference or misallocation). Timely dispute resolution is critical for customer satisfaction and cash collection — disputed amounts are typically withheld from payment, reducing BuildRight's cash position. SLA: initial response within 48 hours, resolution within 10 business days for standard disputes, 20 business days for complex disputes requiring vendor or third-party involvement.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dispute Receipt & Logging**: Customer submits dispute: (a) via B2B self-service portal (W936) — customer selects disputed line items and enters dispute reason and supporting documentation; (b) via email to AR team — AR Analyst manually logs dispute in system; (c) via phone to Customer Service — agent logs dispute and assigns to AR Analyst; (d) system generates dispute ticket with unique tracking number; (e) customer receives acknowledgment within 4 hours with expected resolution timeline; (f) disputed amounts flagged in AR aging — excluded from collection activity per W108 while dispute is open (prevents over-aggressive collection on disputed amounts) | Customer / AR Analyst / Customer Service Agent | AR Analyst | 5–10 min per dispute |
| 2 | **Initial Investigation & Classification**: AR Analyst investigates: (a) **Pricing dispute**: verify POS transaction price vs. trade price agreement (W163) and any active promotions (W13); check if cashier applied correct trade discount at POS; (b) **Quantity dispute**: verify delivery receipt (DR) quantities vs. invoice quantities; check Goods Receipt record at customer site (if available) or store delivery confirmation (W592); (c) **Unrecognized transaction**: verify customer account assignment — was transaction posted to correct account? Check loyalty card number used at POS vs. trade account number; (d) **Duplicate billing**: system auto-checks for duplicate invoice numbers, same amount/same date/same items; (e) **Missing credit memo**: check if return was processed per W12 and credit memo generated per W101; verify credit memo posting to account; (f) **Payment not credited**: check bank statement for customer payment per W89; verify payment allocation to correct account | AR Analyst | Credit Manager | 15–45 min per dispute |
| 3 | **Internal Resolution & Documentation**: Based on investigation: (a) **Dispute valid — BuildRight error**: (i) pricing error: credit memo issued for difference per W101; (ii) quantity error: if customer was overbilled, credit memo for excess quantity; if underbilled, customer notified and additional invoice issued; (iii) duplicate billing: duplicate invoice reversed; (iv) missing credit memo: credit memo re-issued; (v) payment misallocated: payment re-posted to correct account; (b) **Dispute valid — third-party error** (e.g., delivery partner lost goods): AR Analyst coordinates with logistics team; claim filed with carrier per W610; customer issued credit memo pending carrier recovery; (c) **Dispute invalid**: AR Analyst documents evidence (delivery receipt signed by customer, POS price file showing correct trade price, Goods Receipt confirmation) and responds to customer with supporting documentation; (d) **Partially valid**: partial credit memo for agreed portion; remaining disputed amount escalated to Credit Manager for judgment call | AR Analyst | Credit Manager | 15–30 min per dispute |
| 4 | **Customer Communication & Resolution Confirmation**: (a) AR Analyst communicates resolution to customer: email with dispute tracking number, investigation summary, and resolution action taken; (b) if credit memo issued: amount and credit memo number specified; customer sees credit on next statement; (c) if additional invoice issued: customer notified with explanation and payment terms; (d) if dispute rejected: evidence provided; customer may escalate to Credit Manager; (e) customer confirms resolution by acknowledging in B2B portal, email response, or phone confirmation; (f) disputed amounts unblocked in AR aging; undisputed portion of withheld payment becomes due per original terms | AR Analyst | Credit Manager | 10–15 min per dispute |
| 5 | **Dispute Analytics & Systemic Issue Identification**: Monthly: (a) AR Analyst compiles dispute analytics: dispute volume by type, by store, by customer; resolution time by type; dispute rate trend; credit memo value from disputes; (b) identify systemic issues: (i) specific store with high pricing dispute rate → retraining per W518 (Cashier Training); (ii) specific product category with high quantity disputes → receiving process review per W666; (iii) specific customer with recurring disputes → account review per W328 (Credit Limit Review); (c) systemic issues reported to: Store Manager (store-level issues), Category Manager (pricing issues), Supply Chain Manager (delivery issues); (d) quarterly: dispute analytics included in credit portfolio review per W663 | AR Analyst / Credit Manager | Credit Manager | 2–3 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Dispute management module with ticket tracking and SLA monitoring
- B2B self-service portal (W936) for customer dispute submission
- AR aging system with dispute flag and payment hold functionality
- POS transaction lookup with price audit trail
- Delivery receipt and Goods Receipt verification (W109, W3)
- Credit memo module (W101, W766)
- Bank reconciliation (W89) for payment verification
- Duplicate invoice detection (FIN-020)
- Dispute analytics dashboard with root cause categorization

### Pain Points / Risks
- **High dispute volume on aging AR**: Disputed amounts sitting in AR aging distort the true aging profile; credit collection team must distinguish disputed vs. genuinely overdue amounts; AR aging reports should have a "disputed" column separate from "current", "30-day", "60-day", "90-day+" buckets
- **Evidence availability**: Dispute resolution depends on documentation availability; if delivery receipts were not collected or Goods Receipt was not recorded at the customer site, BuildRight has limited evidence to defend its position; digitizing all delivery documentation per W255 and W592 is critical
- **Customer relationship balance**: Aggressive dispute rejection damages customer relationships; AR Analyst should approach disputes with a "customer-first" mindset — if evidence is ambiguous, lean toward customer-favorable resolution for trade accounts with good payment history and high lifetime value
- **Timeliness**: Philippine B2B customers expect rapid dispute resolution; if BuildRight takes > 10 business days, customers may withhold future payments or switch to competitors; SLA monitoring (step 4) and escalation for overdue disputes are essential


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "enters dispute reason and supporting documentation" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "record at customer site" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "Internal Resolution & Documentation" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-185 (ensure B2B billing & receivable milestone integrity.)
- operational: AR Analyst review and approval gate

### Staffing Implication
- **AR Analyst**: Dispute resolution adds ~45–100 min per dispute × 400–600 disputes/month = ~300–1,000 hours/month; distributed across existing AR team (46 Finance & Accounting staff per profile §3.3, including AR Analysts)
- **Credit Manager**: Escalation review adds ~5–10 hours/month
- **No incremental headcount**

### Time Estimate
- Dispute logging: 5–10 min
- Investigation: 15–45 min
- Resolution & documentation: 15–30 min
- Customer communication: 10–15 min
- **Total per dispute**: 45–100 min
- Monthly analytics: 2–3 hours

## W1117. Customer Trade Account Statement of Account Auto-Generation & Email Delivery

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly statement generation schedule (1st business day of the month), or customer request for on-demand statement |
| **Frequency** | Monthly (scheduled); on-demand (customer request via portal, phone, or email) |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 trade accounts × 1 statement/month = ~5,200 scheduled statements; ~500 on-demand requests/month |
| **Owner** | AR Analyst (Finance) |
| **Participants** | AR Analyst, Customer (Trade Account Holder), IT (system configuration) |

### Background

BuildRight Depot maintains ~5,400 active trade and corporate accounts with AR balances managed per W8 (accounts receivable). Philippine B2B practice requires a monthly Statement of Account (SOA) — an itemized document showing: opening balance, all transactions during the period (invoices, payments, credit notes, debit notes), and closing balance due. Currently, SOA generation and delivery is a manual process: AR staff generate statements from the ERP, export to PDF, and email individually — consuming significant staff time for 5,400 accounts. Additionally, trade customers frequently request on-demand SOAs for: (a) their own internal approval processes before releasing payment; (b) dispute resolution when they believe a payment was not credited; (c) reconciliation with their own AP records; (d) bank loan requirements (showing trade references and payment behavior). An automated SOA generation and delivery system eliminates manual effort, ensures consistency, and provides self-service access. This complements W892 (customer statement generation & distribution) which covers all customer types; this workflow specifically addresses the trade account SOA with B2B-specific features.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Monthly Scheduled SOA Generation**: (a) on the 1st business day of each month, system automatically generates SOAs for all active trade accounts with non-zero balances: (i) pulls all AR transactions for the prior month: invoices (W8), payments received (W556), credit notes (W101), debit notes, adjustments; (ii) calculates: opening balance (as of 1st of prior month), total invoices, total payments, total credits, total debits, closing balance (as of last day of prior month), current portion (0–30 days), past due portions (31–60, 61–90, 90+ days); (iii) includes BIR-compliant information: BuildRight TIN, trade customer TIN, registered business names and addresses; (b) system formats SOA as PDF: (i) BuildRight branded header; (ii) trade account name, address, and account number; (iii) itemized transaction table (date, reference, description, debit, credit, running balance); (iv) aging summary; (v) payment instructions (bank details: BDO/BPI/Metrobank account numbers); (vi) AR contact information for inquiries; (vii) "Questions about your statement? Contact [email] or [phone]" | System | AR Analyst | automated |
| 2 | **Automated Email Delivery & Portal Publishing**: (a) system emails SOA to the trade account's designated billing contact email: (i) email subject: "BuildRight Depot — Statement of Account for [Account Name] — [Month Year]"; (ii) body: brief summary (total due, due date, link to self-service portal); (iii) PDF attached; (iv) BCC to AR team email for tracking; (b) system simultaneously publishes SOA to the trade account's self-service portal (W936): (i) customer logs into portal to view, download, or print SOA; (ii) portal retains 24-month SOA history; (iii) portal shows real-time AR balance (not just month-end); (c) system logs delivery status: sent, delivered, bounced, opened (if email tracking enabled); (d) bounced emails flagged for AR Analyst review: contact may have changed — update customer master (W508) | System | AR Analyst | automated |
| 3 | **On-Demand SOA Request Handling**: (a) trade customer requests statement via: (i) self-service portal (W936) — instant download of most recent SOA or custom date range statement; (ii) email to AR team — auto-generated response with portal link, or manual email within 4 hours; (iii) phone call to customer service (W259) — agent generates and emails SOA during the call; (b) for on-demand requests specifying a custom date range (e.g., "last 6 months consolidated"), system generates an ad-hoc SOA with the same format but for the specified period; (c) all on-demand requests logged for tracking (frequency, reason, channel) | System / Customer Service | AR Analyst | 1–5 min (self-service); 10–15 min (manual) |
| 4 | **SOA Dispute Handling & Reconciliation Support**: (a) if trade customer disputes their SOA: (i) customer submits dispute via portal (W869 — vendor dispute resolution adapted for customer disputes) or email; (ii) dispute categorized: missing payment (customer paid but not credited), unrecognized invoice, incorrect amount, duplicate charge, credit note not reflected; (iii) AR Analyst investigates: (a) checks bank statement for missing payment — if received but unapplied, applies to customer account; (b) pulls invoice copy from document management (W255) for unrecognized charge; (c) verifies credit note posting for missing credits; (iv) resolution communicated within 5 business days; (v) if SOA was incorrect, corrected SOA generated and re-sent; (vi) dispute resolution tracked for aging (W889) and root cause analysis (W507) | AR Analyst | AR Manager | 15–30 min per dispute |
| 5 | **Monthly SOA Delivery Effectiveness Reporting**: (a) system generates monthly SOA delivery report: (i) total SOAs generated; (ii) delivery success rate (target: ≥ 99%); (iii) bounce rate and reasons; (iv) portal self-service access rate (% of customers who viewed SOA on portal vs. email only); (v) on-demand request volume and average response time; (vi) dispute rate (% of SOAs disputed) and resolution time; (vii) correlation between SOA delivery and payment speed (accounts that view SOA within 3 days pay 5 days faster on average); (b) report reviewed by AR Manager for process improvement | System / AR Analyst | AR Manager | 30 min/month |

### System Touchpoints
- AR module (W8) — transaction data, balance calculation, aging
- Customer master data (W508) — billing contact email, TIN, address
- AR Balance Forward Billing statement cycle + BI Publisher format (fit-gap A14) — PDF formatting with brand template
- Email gateway — automated delivery with tracking
- Customer self-service portal (W936) — SOA access and download
- Payment processing module (W556) — payment application tracking
- Credit/debit note module (W101) — credit tracking
- Document management (W255) — invoice copy retrieval for disputes
- Dispute resolution module — tracking and resolution
- BI analytics (W880) — delivery effectiveness dashboard

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Email delivery reliability**: Philippine email providers may flag bulk SOA emails as spam; SPF/DKIM/DMARC configuration and dedicated email sending domain are essential
- **Contact data decay**: Trade account billing contacts change frequently (employee turnover, role changes); regular contact validation during order interactions is needed
- **Dispute volume**: If invoice/payment processing has errors, dispute volume can spike, overwhelming AR Analyst capacity; accurate 3-way match (W7) and payment application are preventive
- **Portal adoption**: If trade customers do not adopt the self-service portal, they continue requesting manual SOAs, negating the automation benefit; portal training and onboarding are needed
- **Confidentiality**: SOAs contain sensitive financial information; email delivery must use encrypted attachments or secure portal links to comply with RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "accounts with non-zero balances" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "Automated Email Delivery & Portal Publishing" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "logged for tracking" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-185 (ensure B2B billing & receivable milestone integrity.)
- operational: AR Analyst (Finance) review and approval gate; operational: periodic reconciliation against source documents

### Staffing Implication
- **AR Analyst**: Monthly reconciliation and exception handling adds ~10–15 hours/month (down from ~40–60 hours with manual process)
- **No permanent incremental headcount** — automation significantly reduces existing workload

### Time Estimate
- Monthly generation: automated
- Email delivery: automated
- On-demand handling: 1–5 min (self-service); 10–15 min (manual)
- Dispute handling: 15–30 min per dispute
- Monthly reporting: 30 min (automated report + review)
- **Total staff time saved**: ~25–40 hours/month vs. manual process

## W1269. Customer Trade Account Application, Credit Assessment & Onboarding

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer (contractor, builder, interior designer, architect, or business) applies for a BuildRight trade account with credit terms |
| **Frequency** | ~40–60 new trade account applications/month across 200 stores; ~500–700/year |
| **Volume** | Each application processed individually; total active trade accounts: ~5,200 |
| **Owner** | Trade Account Manager (HQ) |
| **Participants** | Sales Associate (store-level intake), Trade Account Manager, Credit Analyst, Finance Manager, Customer |

### Background

BuildRight Depot's customer base includes ~5,200 trade account customers (contractors, builders, interior designers, architects) and ~200 corporate account customers, collectively representing 40% of revenue (30% trade + 10% corporate). These customers purchase on credit terms — Net 30 for trade accounts, Net 60–90 for corporate accounts — with per-account credit limits. Despite having workflows for corporate tendering (W166), credit insurance (W1011), statement disputes (W1022), and statement generation (W1117), there is no workflow covering the front-end application, credit assessment, and onboarding process for new trade accounts. The gap is significant: at ~50 new applications/month, each requiring credit verification, limit setting, and system setup, a structured workflow ensures consistent risk assessment and rapid onboarding. Philippine trade credit carries elevated risk due to informal business practices among small contractors, limited credit bureau data availability, and higher default rates compared to developed markets.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

## W1507. Customer Trade Account Mobile QR-Onboarding & Instant Activation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contractor, builder, interior designer, or trade professional visits store and expresses interest in trade pricing; or existing walk-in customer's purchase pattern triggers trade account eligibility detection at POS |
| **Frequency** | ~5–10 new trade account activations per store per year (≈1,000–2,000/year chain-wide per the Volume field) |
| **Volume** | ~1,000–2,000 new trade accounts/year chain-wide; target: grow from 5,000 to 7,500 active trade accounts within 2 years |
| **Owner** | Sales Associate (in-store); Category Manager (HQ credit approval) |
| **Participants** | Sales Associate, Department Supervisor, Category Manager, Credit Analyst (HQ), Customer |

### Background

Trade customers (contractors, builders, interior designers, architects) represent 30% of BuildRight Depot's revenue (~PHP 18.7B/year). The existing trade account onboarding process per W1269 requires a multi-day credit assessment, documentation submission, and HQ approval cycle — appropriate for accounts needing credit limits but overly burdensome for trade professionals who simply want discounted pricing without credit terms. Many contractors walk into a BuildRight store, learn about trade pricing, but never complete the paper-based application process because they need to return another day with documents. This workflow introduces a mobile QR-based instant activation pathway for trade customers seeking cash-and-carry trade pricing (no credit), reducing onboarding from days to minutes. The full credit-bearing trade account process remains governed by W1269.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Trade eligibility detection**: Two triggers: (a) Sales Associate identifies repeat professional customer (contractor badge, bulk purchase, project-specific buying pattern); (b) POS system auto-detects customer purchasing pattern consistent with trade buyers (≥3 visits/month, average transaction ≥PHP 5,000, purchasing in trade categories like lumber, cement, plumbing, electrical) — system prompts cashier: "Customer may qualify for Trade Pricing — ask if they are a trade professional?" | Sales Associate / System | Dept Supervisor | 2 min |
| 2 | **QR-based application**: Sales Associate presents QR code on customer-facing display; customer scans with phone camera; mobile web form opens with pre-populated fields: (a) store location, (b) date/time, (c) form captures: full name, trade type (contractor/electrician/plumber/tile setter/interior designer/architect/other), business name (optional), TIN (required), mobile number, email (optional); (d) customer uploads: government ID photo (driver's license, passport, or PRC license — required), selfie with ID for verification; (e) customer agrees to trade account terms and conditions (cash-and-carry pricing, no credit, 5–15% discount on eligible items); (f) total form completion: 3–5 minutes on mobile phone | Customer / Sales Associate | Dept Supervisor | 5 min |
| 3 | **Automated verification**: System performs real-time verification: (a) TIN format validation per BIR requirements, (b) government ID OCR extraction — verify name matches TIN registration, (c) duplicate check against existing trade account database (same TIN or mobile number), (d) check against negative list (accounts suspended per W1395 for payment defaults or policy violations); (e) verification result: Green (auto-approve) or Yellow (manual review required — ID unclear, TIN mismatch, borderline case) | System | — | Automated (30–60 sec) |
| 4 | **Instant activation (Green path)**: For auto-approved applications: (a) system creates Trade Account with "Cash-and-Carry" type, (b) generates Trade Account number and QR code card (digital, sent via SMS/email; optional physical card printed at store), (c) account immediately active with standard trade discount tier (5% base discount on eligible items), (d) customer receives SMS confirmation: "Welcome to BuildRight Trade Program! Your account [number] is active. Show your QR code at checkout for trade pricing."; (e) Sales Associate demonstrates QR code scan at POS — customer's first trade-priced transaction can happen immediately | System / Sales Associate | Dept Supervisor | 2 min |
| 5 | **Manual review (Yellow path)**: For applications requiring manual review: (a) application routed to Category Manager notification queue, (b) Category Manager reviews within 4 business hours: verify ID quality, assess trade legitimacy, approve or reject with reason, (c) if approved: auto-activation per Step 4; (d) if rejected: customer receives SMS with reason and invitation to re-apply with corrected documents; (e) manual review rate expected at ~15–20% of applications (unclear IDs, unusual trade types, TIN discrepancies) | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 5–10 min per review |
| 6 | **Post-activation upsell to credit account**: Within 30 days of activation, if trade customer's monthly purchases exceed PHP 50,000: (a) system flags account for credit upgrade eligibility, (b) Sales Associate or Category Manager contacts customer to offer credit terms per W1269 (Net 30, credit limit based on purchase history), (c) if customer accepts: initiate full credit assessment per W1269; existing trade account converted to credit-bearing account with higher discount tier (10–15%), (d) conversion rate target: 20% of instant-activation accounts upgrade to credit within 12 months | Sales Associate / Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 15 min per outreach |
| 7 | **Monthly activation analytics**: Category Manager reviews instant activation program metrics: (a) activation rate by store and region, (b) Green vs. Yellow path ratio, (c) time from scan to first trade purchase, (d) 30-day and 90-day trade customer retention, (e) credit upgrade conversion rate, (f) average monthly spend of instant-activation vs. traditional trade accounts | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 2 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints

- Mobile web form with QR code generation for in-store scanning
- Government ID OCR and TIN verification API (BIR integration or third-party identity verification service)
- Trade account creation module linked to customer master per MDM-003
- POS trade pricing engine with QR code scan for instant discount application
- Duplicate detection and negative list screening per W1395
- Customer notification engine (SMS + email) for activation confirmation
- Trade account analytics dashboard for Category Manager review
- Credit upgrade eligibility auto-flag linked to W1269 credit assessment pipeline

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Identity verification fraud**: uploaded government IDs may be photos of photos or digitally altered; OCR alone cannot detect sophisticated ID fraud; mitigated by selfie-with-ID comparison and manual review for flagged cases
- **TIN validation limitations**: BIR does not offer real-time TIN-to-name verification API for private entities; system can only validate TIN format, not authenticity; mitigated by requiring government ID cross-reference
- **Instant pricing without credit discipline**: trade accounts with cash-and-carry terms bypass the credit discipline of W1269; these customers may never upgrade to credit accounts, limiting their lifetime value to BuildRight
- **Store Associate adoption resistance**: Associates may not proactively offer instant trade activation if they perceive the QR process as adding complexity to their customer interaction; mitigated by making the process self-service (customer scans QR and fills form on their phone, Associate just verifies completion)
- **Trade discount leakage**: providing trade pricing to marginally qualifying customers (DIYers claiming to be contractors) erodes margin on customers who would have paid full retail; mitigated by requiring government ID and TIN, and monitoring trade account purchase patterns for retail-like buying behavior


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-detect of "Trade eligibility detection" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-scan of "scans with phone camera" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review required — ID unclear, TIN mismatch, borderline case" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-272 (ensure controlled execution — Trade Account Management (PA-11.1).)
- operational: selfie-with-ID comparison and manual review for flagged cases; operational: requiring government ID cross-reference; operational: can only validate TIN format, not authenticity

### Staffing Implication

- **Sales Associate**: ~5 min per activation × 5–10/month = ~25–50 min/month; minimal impact
- **Category Manager**: ~10 min per Yellow-path review × 2–4/month per region + 2 hours/month analytics = ~3–4 hours/month; absorbed
- **No incremental headcount**

### Time Estimate

- Per activation (Green path): ~7 min total (2 min eligibility + 5 min form + automated verification + 2 min POS demo)
- Per activation (Yellow path): ~7 min customer time + 5–10 min Category Manager review = ~15 min total
- Monthly chain-wide: ~1,000–2,000 activations × 10 min average = ~170–330 hours/month distributed across 200 stores
