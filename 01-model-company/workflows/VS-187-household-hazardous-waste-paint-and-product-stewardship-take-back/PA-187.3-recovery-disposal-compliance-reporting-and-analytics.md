## W5361. TSD Facility Processing, Recovery, Recycling & Energy-Recovery Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Manifest arrives at TSD (W5351/W5360); periodic recovery verification |
| **Frequency** | Per manifest batch; quarterly recovery audit |
| **Volume** | ~5,000–15,000 manifest batches/yr; ~4 quarterly audits |
| **Owner** | Compliance Specialist (Environmental) |
| **Participants** | TSD Facility, Sustainability/ESG Manager, Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive TSD processing confirmation per manifest: stream, quantity, processing method (recycle/recovery/disposal). | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | per manifest |
| 2 | Verify the processing method matches the agreed recovery path (e.g. oil re-refined, batteries recycled, lamps mercury-recovered). | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | per manifest |
| 3 | Capture recovery certificates/yields per stream and partner; reconcile to the manifest (cradle-to-grave close). | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | per manifest |
| 4 | Audit a sample of TSD records quarterly (internal audit cross-link VS-21). | Internal Audit | Compliance Specialist | quarterly |
| 5 | Flag any discrepancy (short recovery, unexplained disposal) for TPRM follow-up (VS-161). | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | per discrepancy |

### System Touchpoints
- Hazardous-waste manifest/chain-of-custody close-out; TSD recovery-certificate register; audit-trail (VS-21/VS-88).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **False-recovery risk**: a TSD reports recycling that was actually landfill disposal, undermining ESG claims and cradle-to-grave compliance; mitigated by recovery-certificate verification and quarterly sampling audits.
- **Discrepancy risk**: manifest-out vs. TSD-received quantity gaps indicate leakage/loss; mitigated by per-manifest reconciliation and discrepancy investigation.

### Automation Opportunity
- TSD processing confirmation auto-reconciles to the manifest and auto-requests recovery certificates.

### Controls
- CTL-793 (ensure controlled execution — Recovery, Disposal, Compliance Reporting & Stewardship Analytics (PA-187.3).)
- operational: cradle-to-grave TSD verification; CTL-21 (quantity reconciliation).

### Time Estimate
- ~20 min per manifest close-out; ~10,000/yr → ~3,300 person-hours/yr group-wide.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-161 (TPRM), VS-21 (audit), VS-88 (records), VS-25 (ESG recovery reporting)

## W5362. Latex Paint Reblending, Donation-to-Community & Reuse-Product Sales Channel

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Usable latex paint identified at take-back (W5353) |
| **Frequency** | ~20–40% of latex take-back is reusable |
| **Volume** | ~60k–200k liters reblended/yr |
| **Owner** | Sustainability/ESG Manager |
| **Participants** | Reblending Partner, CSR/Foundation (VS-152), Paint Dept, Pricing (VS-57) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Consolidate usable latex paint at the reblending partner; blend, filter, and standardize into a limited color range. | Reblending Partner | Sustainability/ESG Manager | per batch |
| 2 | QA the reblended paint (coverage, adhesion, VOC); label as reblended/recycled product. | Reblending Partner | Sustainability/ESG Manager | per batch |
| 3 | Route to the preferred channel: donation to community housing/disaster-recovery (VS-152/VS-180) or sale as a value recycled product. | Sustainability/ESG Manager | Sustainability/ESG Manager | per batch |
| 4 | For sale: price and merchandise as a recycled/reblended SKU (VS-01/VS-57); recognize recovery revenue. | Paint Dept | Sustainability/ESG Manager | per batch |
| 5 | Track recovery revenue and donation value for the program business case (W5368). | Finance Business Partner | CFO | monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- New recycled-SKU setup (VS-01/VS-29); pricing/merchandising (VS-57); CSR donation records (VS-152); recovery-revenue GL.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **QA risk**: reblended paint of inconsistent quality (coverage/odor) erodes brand trust; mitigated by QA before re-sale and clear recycled-product labeling.
- **Mis-pricing risk**: pricing reblended paint above its perceived value leaves it unsold; mitigated by competitive positioning and a donation outlet as alternative.

### Automation Opportunity
- Reblended-batch auto-creates a recycled SKU and routes by channel decision rules.

### Controls
- CTL-31: NRV/quality provision on recycled stock; CTL-114 (recovery-revenue recognition accuracy).

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 weeks elapsed per batch cycle; recovery yield ~PHP 50–150/liter vs. disposal cost.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-09 (paint), VS-01 (assortment/SKU), VS-57 (pricing), VS-152 (CSR donation), VS-180 (disaster-relief donation)

## W5363. Hazardous Waste Disposal, Incineration/Cement-Kiln & Landfill Diversion Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Non-recoverable hazardous waste (oil-based paint, contaminated oil, restricted pesticides, etc.) |
| **Frequency** | Per disposal batch |
| **Volume** | ~3,000–8,000 disposal batches/yr |
| **Owner** | Compliance Specialist (Environmental) |
| **Participants** | TSD Facility, HSE Officer, Sustainability/ESG Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm the stream is non-recoverable per the recovery hierarchy (reuse > recycle > energy-recovery > disposal). | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | per batch |
| 2 | Route to the licensed disposal method: cement-kiln co-processing (energy recovery), licensed incineration, or permitted hazardous-waste landfill. | TSD Facility | Compliance Specialist | per batch |
| 3 | Capture the disposal method and destination for the manifest close-out and diversion tracking. | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | per batch |
| 4 | Track the landfill-diversion rate (diverted vs. landfilled) as a program KPI (W5368). | Sustainability/ESG Manager | Compliance Specialist | monthly |
| 5 | Minimize disposal over time by improving recovery (W5361/W5362) and assortment/substitution. | Sustainability/ESG Manager | Compliance Specialist | quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Manifest close-out with disposal-method/destination; diversion-rate dashboard (VS-25/VS-28).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Diversion-target risk**: excessive landfill disposal undermines the program's ESG value and diversion targets; mitigated by the recovery hierarchy and diversion-rate tracking.
- **Illegal-disposal risk**: disposal to an unpermitted landfill/incinerator is a RA 6969/RA 9003 violation and cradle-to-grave liability; mitigated by licensed-TSD-only routing and recovery certificates.

### Automation Opportunity
- Disposal-method auto-tagged on manifest close-out; diversion-rate auto-computed.

### Controls
- CTL-793 (ensure controlled execution — Recovery, Disposal, Compliance Reporting & Stewardship Analytics (PA-187.3).)
- operational: licensed-disposal compliance; CTL-34 (diversion-target tracking).

### Time Estimate
- ~20 min per batch close-out; ~5,000/yr → ~1,700 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-179 (EPR/cement-kiln co-processing), VS-25 (ESG), VS-114 (DG disposal), VS-73 (store waste)

## W5364. Take-Back Volume Tracking, DENR-EMB Annual Hazardous Waste Report & NSWMC Filing

| **Frequency** | Monthly internal; annual statutory |
| **Volume** | 1 annual report/entity + monthly internal closes |
| **Owner** | Compliance Specialist (Environmental) |
| **Participants** | Sustainability/ESG Manager, Finance, Legal (VS-100), DENR-EMB/NSWMC |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aggregate the hazardous-waste ledger by stream, store, and entity for the reporting period. | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | 1 week |
| 2 | Reconcile volumes to manifests and TSD recovery certificates (W5361/W5363). | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | 3 days |
| 3 | Prepare the DENR-EMB hazardous-waste generator report (annual) and NSWMC/EPR-aligned filings (cross-link VS-179). | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | 1 week |
| 4 | Legal/Finance review; submit via the DENR/NSWMC channels; retain per records-retention (VS-88). | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | 3 days |
| 5 | Feed the volumes into ESG/sustainability reporting (VS-25) and program analytics (W5368). | Sustainability/ESG Manager | VP Legal & Compliance | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Hazardous-waste ledger aggregation; DENR-EMB report format; NSWMC/EPR filing (VS-179); records retention (VS-88); ESG reporting (VS-25).

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Deadline-miss risk**: late DENR-EMB/NSWMC filing triggers penalties; mitigated by the permit-portfolio reporting calendar (VS-166) and lead-time.

### Automation Opportunity
- DENR-EMB report auto-generated from the reconciled ledger; filing-calendar alerts from the permit portfolio.

### Controls
- CTL-793 (ensure controlled execution — Recovery, Disposal, Compliance Reporting & Stewardship Analytics (PA-187.3).)
- operational: statutory environmental reporting; reporting accuracy/reconciliation.

### Time Estimate
- ~3 weeks elapsed per annual cycle; ~1 week per monthly close.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-22 (compliance), VS-179 (EPR/NSWMC), VS-88 (records), VS-25 (ESG), VS-166 (filing calendar)

| **Owner** | Internal Audit (Environmental compliance) |
| **Participants** | Compliance Specialist, TPRM (VS-161), Legal (VS-100), HSE Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## W5366. Take-Back Program Incident, Spill/Leak & Exposure Response

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Spill/leak (oil/paint/acid/mercury), battery thermal event, or customer/staff exposure at a take-back point |
| **Frequency** | Uncommon (~20–80 incidents/yr group-wide, per the Volume register); high severity |
| **Volume** | ~20–80 incidents/yr group-wide |
| **Owner** | HSE Officer |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Compliance Specialist, BFP, DENR-EMB (if reportable), Medical (VS-83) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Secure the area, contain the spill with the spill kit, and evacuate/exclude customers; render first aid/exposure care. | HSE Officer | Store Manager | immediate |
| 2 | Identify the substance (mercury, acid, oil, solvent) and apply the substance-specific response (e.g. mercury vapor procedure for broken lamps — W5356). | HSE Officer | HSE Officer | immediate |
| 3 | Notify BFP/DENR-EMB if reportable (significant spill, mercury release, fire); notify insurance (VS-26). | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | within SLA |
| 4 | Clean up, dispose of contaminated material via the licensed TSD (W5360), and decontaminate the area. | HSE Officer | HSE Officer | per incident |
| 5 | Root-cause and corrective action (containment design, staff training, policy); capture lessons-learned (VS-133). | HSE Officer | Compliance Specialist | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Incident/case module (VS-24/VS-23); spill-response substance library; insurance/DENR-reportable filing.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Exposure/escalation risk**: a mishandled mercury or acid spill exposes staff/customers and escalates to a reportable environmental incident; mitigated by substance-specific response procedures and spill kits at every point.
- **Notice-window risk**: late DENR-EMB/BFP/insurance notification compounds the incident; mitigated by an incident-response SLA with defined notice windows.

### Automation Opportunity
- Incident-report app with substance-specific response checklists and auto-started notice clocks.

### Controls
- CTL-793 (ensure controlled execution — Recovery, Disposal, Compliance Reporting & Stewardship Analytics (PA-187.3).)
- operational: spill kits + substance procedures at every point.

### Time Estimate
- ~8–40 person-hours per incident; ~40/yr → ~1,000 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-114 (DG incident), VS-26 (insurance), VS-83 (medical/first aid), VS-133 (lessons-learned)

## W5367. Stewardship Program Cost Recovery, Co-Funding Reconciliation & Vendor Settlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly co-funding cycle; vendor invoicing for TSD services |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~1 reconciliation/entity/month; ~5–15 vendor invoices/month |
| **Owner** | Finance Business Partner (ESG) |
| **Participants** | Accounts Payable (VS-15), Merchandising (VS-39), Sustainability/ESG Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reconcile actual take-back volumes (by brand/SKU) against manufacturer co-funding terms (W5348). | Finance Business Partner | Finance Business Partner | 1 week |
| 2 | Compute co-funding receivable per brand and recovery revenue (reblended paint, scrap metal, battery lead — W5362/W5363). | Finance Business Partner | Finance Business Partner | 3 days |
| 3 | Process and 2-way-match TSD/transporter vendor invoices against manifests (VS-15). | Accounts Payable | Finance Business Partner | 1 week |
| 4 | Settle co-funding receivables with brands and TSD payables; net into the program P&L. | Accounts Payable | CFO | 1 week |
| 5 | Report program cost-recovery vs. gross cost to the ESG/steering committee (W5368). | Finance Business Partner | CFO | monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- AP/2-way-match (VS-15); co-funding/rebate module (VS-39); recovery-revenue GL; program P&L.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Co-funding-leakage risk**: failing to claim brand co-funding on eligible returns leaves recovery revenue on the table; mitigated by per-brand/SKU reconciliation against returns.
- **Invoice-accuracy risk**: TSD invoices not matched to manifests over-state cost; mitigated by 2-way match (manifest→invoice).

### Automation Opportunity
- Co-funding receivable auto-computed from scanned returns; TSD invoices auto-2-way-matched to manifests.

### Controls
- CTL-39: co-funding/rebate accuracy; CTL-14/CTL-44 (invoice match); CTL-21 (recovery-revenue accuracy).

### Time Estimate
- ~2 weeks elapsed per monthly cycle; ~20 person-hours.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-15 (P2P/AP), VS-39 (rebates/co-funding), VS-179 (EPR/PRO settlement), VS-17 (GL)

## W5368. Diversion Rate, Recycling Yield, Customer Participation & Program Impact Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly program-performance review; ESG reporting cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | 1 program dashboard/month; ~4 ESG reporting cycles/yr |
| **Owner** | Sustainability/ESG Manager |
| **Participants** | BI/Analytics (VS-28), Finance, Marketing, Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compute diversion rate (diverted from landfill ÷ total), recycling yield per stream, and recovery revenue vs. cost. | BI Analyst | Sustainability/ESG Manager | 2 days |
| 2 | Compute customer participation (unique participants, repeat rate, per-store penetration) and footfall/attach lift. | BI Analyst | Marketing Manager | 2 days |
| 3 | Compare to diversion targets (W5345) and benchmark; identify under-performing stores/streams. | Sustainability/ESG Manager | Sustainability/ESG Manager | 1 day |
| 4 | Feed the metrics into ESG/sustainability reporting and board ESG reporting (VS-25/VS-36). | Sustainability/ESG Manager | VP Legal & Compliance | per cycle |
| 5 | Recommend program adjustments (scope, incentives, partner mix) to the steering committee (W5345). | Sustainability/ESG Manager | COO | quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- BI/analytics on the take-back ledger + recovery + customer data (VS-28); ESG reporting module (VS-25).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-performance-blindness risk**: a stream/store under-performing without measurement leaves the program below target and business case; mitigated by monthly metrics with explicit adjust decisions.
- **ESG-claim risk**: reporting diversion/yield metrics not supported by reconciled data (greenwashing); mitigated by ledger→manifest→TSD-reconciled metrics (W5361/W5364).

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-refreshing diversion/yield/participation dashboard from the reconciled ledger; target-vs-actual auto-flag.

### Controls
- CTL-793 (ensure controlled execution — Recovery, Disposal, Compliance Reporting & Stewardship Analytics (PA-187.3).)
- operational: ESG-claim substantiation; metric-data accuracy.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 week per monthly review; ~2 person-hours/week ongoing.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-25 (ESG reporting), VS-28 (BI), VS-118 (revenue assurance on recovery), VS-36 (board ESG reporting), VS-152 (CSR impact)
