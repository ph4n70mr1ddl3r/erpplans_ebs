## W5345. Product Stewardship & Take-Back Program Strategy, Scope & Regulatory Mapping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Executive/ESG decision to launch a structured customer take-back program |
| **Frequency** | Annual strategy refresh; ad-hoc scope expansion |
| **Volume** | 1 program strategy/yr |
| **Owner** | Sustainability/ESG Manager |
| **Participants** | VP Legal & Compliance, HSE Officer, Merchandising, Store Operations, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the take-back scope by stream (paint, used oil, batteries, lamps, pesticides/solvents, e-waste, cylinders) and map each to the product assortment BuildRight sells. | Sustainability/ESG Manager | Sustainability/ESG Manager | 1 week |
| 2 | Map each stream to its regulatory regime: RA 6969 (Toxic Substances), RA 8749 (Clean Air), RA 9275 (Clean Water), RA 9003 (solid waste), RA 6969 hazardous-waste rules, DENR-EMB DAOs, and RA 11898 EPR where overlapping. | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | 1 week |
| 3 | Set diversion targets, program KPIs, and the business case (footfall lift, ESG/CSR value, recovery revenue, cost of compliance). | Finance Business Partner | CFO | 1 week |
| 4 | Define phasing (pilot stores → regional → national) and the stream rollout sequence. | Sustainability/ESG Manager | COO | 3 days |
| 5 | Approve the strategy at the ESG/steering committee and board ESG reporting (links VS-25/VS-36). | Sustainability/ESG Manager | VP Legal & Compliance | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- ESG/sustainability program module (VS-25); product-assortment cross-reference (VS-01); compliance mapping (VS-22).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scope-creep risk**: accepting streams BuildRight is not permitted/ equipped to handle (e.g. medical waste) creates regulatory and safety exposure; mitigated by an explicit scope list tied to the assortment and DENR-EMB permits.
- **Business-case risk**: a program run purely as cost without recovery revenue or footfall/ESG value is defunded; mitigated by quantifying recovery revenue (W5362) and ESG/CSR value in the business case.

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-map take-back streams to the active assortment so the scope stays current as SKUs change.

### Controls
- CTL-791 (ensure controlled execution — Program Strategy, Regulatory Setup & Partner Network (PA-187.1).)
- operational: regulatory compliance scope; program budget adherence.

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks elapsed per annual refresh; ~100 person-hours.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-25 (ESG), VS-22 (compliance), VS-179 (EPR/plastic), VS-152 (CSR)

## W5346. DENR-EMB Hazardous Waste Generator/Treater Registration & Permitting (RA 6969)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch (W5345); new store added to the program; permit renewal |
| **Frequency** | Initial registration per store/entity; periodic renewal (per DENR-EMB cycle) |
| **Volume** | ~200 store-level hazardous-waste generator registrations; ~5 entity-level permits |
| **Owner** | Compliance Specialist (Environmental) |
| **Participants** | DENR-EMB liaison, HSE Officer, Facilities, Permit Portfolio (VS-166) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Register each participating store as a hazardous-waste generator with DENR-EMB (RA 6969 / DAO permits) for the streams it will accept. | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | per store |
| 2 | Secure the entity-level permits (generator, and transporter/treater arrangements via accredited TSD — W5347). | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | 4–8 weeks |
| 3 | Establish the per-store hazardous-waste storage area/containment to DENR spec (links W5349). | HSE Officer | Facilities Manager | 2–4 weeks |
| 4 | Record permits in the license/permit portfolio with renewal alerts (VS-166); gate take-back availability on valid permit. | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | per permit |
| 5 | Maintain the required hazardous-waste register/ledger and reporting workflow (W5364). | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Permit/portfolio register (VS-166) with DENR-EMB expiry alerts; hazardous-waste ledger module; availability gate on permit validity.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Operating-without-permit risk**: accepting hazardous waste at a store that lacks DENR-EMB generator registration is itself a violation (RA 6969 penalties, store closure order); mitigated by gating take-back availability on a valid permit per store.
- **Permit-lapse risk**: an expired generator registration unnoticed takes the whole store's take-back offline; mitigated by 60–90-day renewal alerts in the permit portfolio.

### Automation Opportunity
- Permit-expiry alerts auto-gate per-store take-back; renewal applications pre-filled from the store profile.

### Controls
- CTL-41: DENR/agency regulatory compliance; CTL-34 (permit-validity availability gate).

### Time Estimate
- ~6–12 weeks elapsed initial; ~2–4 person-hours per renewal.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-166 (permit portfolio), VS-24 (HSE), VS-22 (compliance), VS-76 (LGU)

## W5347. Take-Back Partner, Transporter & TSD (Treatment-Storage-Disposal) Facility Accreditation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program setup (W5345); partner renewal/performance review |
| **Frequency** | Initial accreditation + periodic re-qualification |
| **Volume** | ~5–15 accredited partners (transporters + TSD facilities) |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager (Services) |
| **Participants** | Compliance Specialist, HSE Officer, TPRM (VS-161), Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify DENR-accredited hazardous-waste transporters and TSD facilities per stream (paint recycler, oil re-refiner, battery recycler, lamp/hg handler, e-waste processor). | Procurement Manager | Compliance Specialist | 2 weeks |
| 2 | Qualify each on DENR accreditation, capacity, coverage, pricing, and HSSE record (TPRM — VS-161). | TPRM Analyst | Compliance Specialist | 2 weeks |
| 3 | Negotiate service agreements with manifest/handling fees, SLAs, and reporting obligations. | Procurement Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2 weeks |
| 4 | Record partners in the vendor/TSD master; link to the manifest chain-of-custody (W5351). | Procurement Manager | Compliance Specialist | 1 week |
| 5 | Monitor partner performance, accreditation validity, and incidents (W5365); re-qualify periodically. | TPRM Analyst | Compliance Specialist | quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor master (VS-03) flagged "DENR-accredited TSD"; TPRM inventory (VS-161); manifest/chain-of-custody link (W5351).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unaccredited-partner risk**: handing waste to a transporter/TSD whose DENR accreditation lapsed shifts liability for the waste back to BuildRight (cradle-to-grave under RA 6969); mitigated by accreditation verification at qualification and periodic re-check, and manifest cradle-to-grave tracking (W5351).
- **Concentration risk**: a single TSD failure halts a stream's disposal and stockpiles hazardous waste at stores; mitigated by at least two accredited partners per major stream per region.

### Automation Opportunity
- DENR-accreditation status re-checked automatically from the DENR registry at each re-qualification cycle.

### Controls
- CTL-06: vendor/TSD onboarding with DENR check; CTL-41 (regulatory partner compliance).

### Time Estimate
- ~6–8 weeks elapsed initial; ~1 week per periodic re-qualification.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-03 (vendor mgmt), VS-161 (TPRM), VS-179 (EPR/PRO partners)

## W5349. In-Store Take-Back Collection Point Design, Containment & Safety Infrastructure

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New store added to the program (W5345/W5346) |
| **Frequency** | Per store build/retrofit |
| **Volume** | ~200 collection points at full rollout |
| **Owner** | Facilities & Real Estate Manager |
| **Participants** | HSE Officer, BFP Fire Safety Inspector, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design the collection point: ventilated/curtained area, separated from stock and customer flow, with spill containment. | Facilities Manager | HSE Officer | 1 week |
| 2 | Install stream-specific containment: flame cabinets (paint/solvent), acid/lithium-rated bins with fire separation (batteries), mercury-tight containers (lamps), oil-rated bunded drums (used oil). | Facilities Manager | HSE Officer | 2 weeks |
| 3 | Equip with spill kits, PPE, fire suppression rated to the streams (BFP Fire Code FSIC), and emergency eyewash. | Facilities Manager | BFP Inspector | 1 week |
| 4 | Signage, customer instructions, and max-quantity limits (W5350); staff training on handling. | HSE Officer | Store Manager | 1 week |
| 5 | Commission with HSE + BFP sign-off before first acceptance; gate acceptance on passed commissioning. | HSE Officer | Store Manager | 3 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Facilities/CAPEX project module; BFP FSIC and DENR containment records; availability gate on commissioning.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fire/explosion risk**: a lithium-battery or solvent fire from improper containment in a retail store is catastrophic (BFP Fire Code, life safety); mitigated by fire-separated, stream-rated containment and BFP FSIC commissioning.
- **Customer-exposure risk**: leaking/fuming hazardous waste in customer-accessible area causes exposure complaints; mitigated by separated, ventilated containment outside customer flow.

### Automation Opportunity
- Standard collection-point design template and BOM auto-generated per stream set and store tier.

### Controls
- CTL-171: take-back containment & asset safeguarding; operational: BFP/DENR commissioning gate.

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks elapsed per store; capital ~PHP 150k–500k per point.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-138 (facilities), VS-24 (HSE), VS-114 (DG containment), VS-166 (BFP FSIC permits)

## W5350. Take-Back Acceptance Policy, Eligibility, Quantity Limits & Customer Incentive Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch (W5345); periodic policy review |
| **Frequency** | Annual policy refresh |
| **Volume** | 1 acceptance policy/program; ~150,000–300,000 take-back transactions/yr |
| **Owner** | Program Operations Lead |
| **Participants** | Compliance Specialist, Marketing (VS-58), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define eligibility (residential vs. commercial), acceptable condition, and per-visit/per-stream quantity limits per DENR-EMB small-quantity-generator rules. | Compliance Specialist | Program Operations Lead | 1 week |
| 2 | Define refusal conditions (unidentified/unlabeled containers, commercial-scale volumes, excluded streams) and the staff script. | Program Operations Lead | Program Operations Lead | 3 days |
| 3 | Design the customer incentive (loyalty points, discount coupon, or "drop a quart, get a brush" — links VS-58/VS-13). | Marketing Manager | Program Operations Lead | 1 week |
| 4 | Publish the policy in-store, online (W5352), and to staff; train counter staff on acceptance/refusal. | Program Operations Lead | Store Manager | ongoing |
| 5 | Monitor acceptance denials and customer complaints to tune the policy. | Program Operations Lead | Program Operations Lead | monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Loyalty/promotions engines (VS-13/VS-58) for incentive issuance; take-back transaction record per customer.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-acceptance risk**: accepting commercial-scale volumes under a residential policy turns the store into an unpermitted commercial hazardous-waste site; mitigated by quantity limits and a documented refusal script.
- **Incentive-abuse risk**: customers gaming a points-for-waste incentive (returning water-filled containers); mitigated by inspection at acceptance and per-customer/per-period caps.

### Automation Opportunity
- Online eligibility checker and appointment booking; loyalty points auto-issued on a validated acceptance scan.

### Controls
- CTL-32: loyalty-points integrity on take-back incentives; CTL-46 (incentive-gaming fraud detection).

### Time Estimate
- ~3 weeks per annual refresh; ~3 min per acceptance transaction at the counter.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-58 (promotions), VS-13 (loyalty), VS-22 (compliance)

## W5351. Hazardous Waste Manifest, Transporter Accreditation & Chain-of-Custody System Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program setup (W5345); consolidation pickup (W5360) |
| **Frequency** | Per pickup batch; system maintained ongoing |
| **Volume** | ~6,000–12,000 manifest movements/yr |
| **Owner** | Compliance Specialist (Environmental) |
| **Participants** | HSE Officer, TSD Transporter, Store Staff |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Configure the hazardous-waste manifest module per DENR-EMB format (generator, transporter, TSD, stream, quantity, date). | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | setup |
| 2 | At each pickup, generate the manifest from the store's hazardous-waste ledger (per-stream accumulation since last pickup). | Store Staff | HSE Officer | per pickup |
| 3 | Capture transporter accreditation, signature, and timestamp; hand over with spill-response acknowledgment. | TSD Transporter | HSE Officer | per pickup |
| 4 | Track the movement to TSD receipt and processing confirmation (cradle-to-grave). | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | per movement |
| 5 | Reconcile manifest-out vs. TSD-received; investigate any quantity discrepancy. | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | per movement |

### System Touchpoints
- Hazardous-waste manifest/chain-of-custody module; DENR-EMB format; TSD transporter accreditation link (W5347); store hazardous-waste ledger.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cradle-to-grave liability risk**: a manifest gap (waste leaves the store but isn't confirmed at TSD) leaves BuildRight liable for the waste indefinitely under RA 6969; mitigated by mandatory TSD-receipt confirmation and discrepancy investigation.
- **Manifest-error risk**: an inaccurate manifest (wrong stream/quantity) is itself a DENR-EMB violation; mitigated by manifest auto-generated from the weighed/counted ledger rather than hand-keyed.

### Automation Opportunity
- Manifest auto-generated from the weighed/counted store ledger; TSD-receipt confirmation auto-reconciles and flags gaps.

### Controls
- CTL-791 (ensure controlled execution — Program Strategy, Regulatory Setup & Partner Network (PA-187.1).)
- operational: hazardous-waste manifest/DENR compliance; manifest quantity accuracy.

### Time Estimate
- ~30–60 min per pickup batch at store; ongoing reconciliation.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-114 (DG transport manifest), VS-161 (TPRM transporter), VS-88 (records retention of manifests)
