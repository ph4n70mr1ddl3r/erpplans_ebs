## W4617. Principal Settlement, Float & Commission Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Settlement cycle (daily/weekly); commission accrual |
| **Frequency** | Per cycle (frequent) |
| **Volume** | Settlement/float/commission across principals |
| **Owner** | Finance (VS-17) / Treasury (VS-18) |
| **Participants** | Principals (W4602–W4604), Compliance (VS-22), Cash Mgmt (VS-08.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance/Principals reconcile VAS transactions to principal statements (bills/remittance/e-money/load), and settle collected cash vs principal (net commission) per cycle | Finance / Principals | CFO | Per cycle |
| 2 | Treasury/Finance manage the float/settlement account (VS-18), ensure cash is banked/settled timely (VS-81), and monitor float aging | Treasury / Finance | CFO | Per cycle |
| 3 | Finance/Tax book commission revenue (VS-79 VAT/withholding), manage intercompany where applicable (VS-72), and report settlement/commission to the board (VS-36) | Finance / Tax | CFO | Per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance/GL (VS-17); treasury/float (VS-18); CIT/banking (VS-81); tax (VS-79); intercompany (VS-72); principals (W4602–W4604)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Settlement-mismatch risk**: BuildRight cash ≠ principal statement → dispute/loss; mitigated by reconciliation & SoD
- **Float-risk**: BuildRight carries float between collection and principal settlement; mitigated by timely settlement & float monitoring

### Automation Opportunity
- Principal-statement reconciliation; float/settlement automation; commission-revenue booking

### Controls
- CTL-93 (ensure VAS agency cash & BSP compliance.)
- CTL-700: transactions reconciled to principal; float monitored/timely-settled (VS-18); commission tax-treated (VS-79); SoD

### Time Estimate
- Per cycle days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17/VS-18 (finance/treasury), VS-81 (CIT), VS-79 (tax), VS-72 (intercompany), W4602–W4604 (principals), W4613 (cash reconciliation)

### Controls
- CTL-93 (ensure VAS agency cash & BSP compliance.)
