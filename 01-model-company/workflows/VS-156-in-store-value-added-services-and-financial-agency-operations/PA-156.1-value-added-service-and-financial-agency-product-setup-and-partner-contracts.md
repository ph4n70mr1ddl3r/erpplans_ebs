## W4601. VAS/Financial-Agency Strategy, Portfolio & Footfall Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (VS-33); footfall/revenue goal; community need |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | One VAS/financial-agency strategy & portfolio |
| **Owner** | Commercial / Store Ops (VS-07) / Finance Strategy |
| **Participants** | Insights (VS-44), Marketing (VS-14), Compliance (VS-22), Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Commercial/Store Ops set the VAS strategy & portfolio (bills payment, remittance, e-money cash-in/out, mobile load), target stores (provincial/community), and the footfall/basket/commission model | Commercial / Store Ops / Finance Strategy | COO / CMO / CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Commercial/Insights size the opportunity (footfall lift, basket-linkage, commission revenue, cost-to-serve) using VS-44/VS-13, and define success metrics | Commercial / Insights / Finance Strategy | COO / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Commercial/Legal/Compliance validate the agency model (BuildRight as agent, not principal) within retail charter/BSP rules, and obtain board approval (VS-36) | Commercial / Legal / Compliance | COO / Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy (VS-33); store ops (VS-07); insights (VS-44); marketing (VS-14); compliance/legal (VS-22/VS-100.3); board (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Charter-overstep risk**: BuildRight acting as principal (lender/remitter) not agent → BSP exposure; mitigated by strict agency model & legal validation
- **Mis-sized-portfolio risk**: Services customers don't want/costly to serve; mitigated by insight-driven sizing

### Automation Opportunity
- Opportunity/footfall modeling; portfolio-economics analytics; charter-compliance check

### Controls
- CTL-698 (ensure controlled execution — Value-Added Service & Financial Agency Product Setup & Partner Contracts (PA-156.1).)
- CTL-698: strategy agency-only (legal-validated VS-100.3); insight-sized; board-approved (VS-36)

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual strategy

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-07 (stores), VS-44 (insights), VS-22/VS-100.3 (compliance/legal), W4602–W4604 (partners)
