## W4169. Facilities Management Strategy & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy refresh; growth (10–15 stores/yr); cost/service pressure |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on major change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise footprint (~205 sites; ~1.6–3.0M sqm selling + ~130,000 sqm DC + ~3,000 sqm HQ) |
| **Owner** | Head of Facilities / Store Operations Support |
| **Participants** | COO, Store Ops (VS-07), Real Estate (VS-97/VS-20), Procurement (VS-34), Finance (VS-17.4), Facilities Coordination Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities define the IFM operating model — in-house vs outsourced per service line (cleaning, pest, guarding, HVAC, grounds), single-source IFM vs best-of-breed, and the site-cluster structure across the archipelago | Head of Facilities / Procurement | COO | Annual |
| 2 | Facilities set the facilities strategy and standards (service levels, response times, sustainability, safety, customer-experience impact), aligned to corporate strategy (VS-33) and growth (VS-37) | Head of Facilities | COO | Annual |
| 3 | Facilities document the model, obtain executive approval, sequence rollout with OCM (VS-134), and resource the facilities org | Head of Facilities / Finance | COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- IFM/CAFM platform strategy; real estate portfolio (VS-97/VS-20); store ops (VS-07); procurement (VS-34); strategy (VS-33)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fragmentation risk**: Each site/service contracted separately → overlap, gaps, leakage; mitigated by integrated IFM operating model
- **Provincial-capacity risk**: Uneven local contractor quality outside metro hubs; mitigated by cluster/national provider strategy

### Automation Opportunity
- Service-line standardization templates; site-cluster optimization

### Controls
- CTL-644 (ensure controlled execution — Facilities Management Strategy, IFM Provider & SLA Governance (PA-138.1).)
- CTL-644: IFM operating model approved by COO; strategy aligned to corporate plan (VS-33)

### Time Estimate
- 3–4 weeks annual; ongoing operating-model governance

### Cross-references
- links to VS-97/VS-20 (real estate), VS-07 (store ops), VS-34 (procurement), VS-33 (strategy), VS-37 (growth), VS-134 (OCM)

## W4170. IFM Provider Strategy, Sourcing & Contract Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contract renewal; new service line; new-store cluster |
| **Frequency** | Per contract + annual review |
| **Volume** | ~10–30 facilities-service contracts (national/regional providers) |
| **Owner** | Procurement (VS-34) / Head of Facilities |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), Finance (VS-15/VS-17.4), Store Ops (VS-07), Risk (VS-21.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities/Procurement run RFP/sourcing for IFM service lines (cleaning, pest, guarding, HVAC, grounds, cafeteria), evaluating capability, coverage, price, safety/ESG, and references | Procurement / Facilities | Head of Facilities | Per RFP |
| 2 | Facilities/Procurement/Legal structure contracts (SLA, scope, pricing, performance penalties, termination, insurance, ABC compliance links to VS-86.3), and onboard the provider (links to VS-98 contingent workforce) | Procurement / Legal / Facilities | Head of Facilities | Per contract |
| 3 | Facilities manage the contract lifecycle (renewal, scope change, performance), and consolidate providers for leverage where sensible | Facilities / Procurement | Head of Facilities | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract repository (VS-34.2/VS-100.3); procurement (VS-34); contingent workforce (VS-98); risk (VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-644 (ensure controlled execution — Facilities Management Strategy, IFM Provider & SLA Governance (PA-138.1).)
- CTL-644: facilities contracts with SLA/penalty/exit clauses; ABC due diligence (VS-86.3); insurance verified

### Time Estimate
- ~4–8 weeks/RFP; ongoing contract mgmt ~0.5 FTE

### Cross-references
- links to VS-34 (procurement), VS-34.2 (service-provider mgmt), VS-100.3 (legal/contracts), VS-98 (contingent workforce), VS-86.3 (ABC)

## W4171. Facilities Service Catalog & SLA Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Operating-model setup; service review; site-type change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Service catalog across ~205 sites × service lines |
| **Owner** | Head of Facilities |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), DC Ops (VS-04), HQ Admin, Providers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities define the service catalog — hard services (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire systems, elevators, BMS) and soft services (cleaning, pest, guarding, grounds, waste, cafeteria, mailroom) — per site type (store/DC/HQ) | Facilities / Store Ops | Head of Facilities | Annual |
| 2 | Facilities set SLAs per service (frequency, response/resolution times, quality standards) differentiated by site criticality (24/7 store vs DC vs HQ) and codify in provider contracts (W4170) | Facilities | Head of Facilities | Annual |
| 3 | Facilities publish the catalog/SLA to sites and providers, train site staff on how to request/report, and govern SLA changes | Facilities / Store Ops | Head of Facilities | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- CAFM/IFM service catalog; provider contracts (W4170); store ops comms (VS-07 W1775)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Undefined-scope risk**: Overlap/gap between equipment maintenance (VS-20.3) and building services; mitigated by clear catalog boundaries
- **One-size risk**: Same SLA for a 24/7 store and an office; mitigated by criticality-differentiated SLAs

### Automation Opportunity
- Service-catalog templating per site type; SLA-differentiation rules

### Controls
- CTL-644 (ensure controlled execution — Facilities Management Strategy, IFM Provider & SLA Governance (PA-138.1).)
- CTL-644: service catalog boundary vs equipment maintenance (VS-20.3) documented; SLAs in contracts

### Time Estimate
- ~3 weeks setup; ongoing governance

### Cross-references
- links to VS-20.3 (equipment maintenance — boundary), VS-07 (store ops), VS-04 (DC ops), VS-73 (waste), VS-23.2 (security)

| **Volume** | ~205 buildings × fabric/systems (roof, structure, HVAC, fire, electrical, plumbing, elevators) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities maintain the building/fabric asset register (building systems, age, condition, warranty, replacement cost) distinct from FF&E/equipment (VS-35/VS-20.3), integrated with the real-estate portfolio (VS-97) | Facilities Asset Mgr / Real Estate | Head of Facilities | Annual |
| 2 | Facilities/Engineering conduct periodic building-condition surveys (roof, structure, HVAC, fire/life-safety, elevators, facade), rate condition, and identify capex/refurbishment triggers (links to VS-109 remodel, VS-40 capex) | Facilities Asset Mgr / Engineering | Head of Facilities | Annual |
| 3 | Facilities feed condition data into capex planning (VS-40) and the remodel/refurbishment program (VS-109), and track landlord-vs-tenant repair responsibility (links to VS-42 lease admin, VS-97.2) | Facilities Asset Mgr / Capex / Real Estate | Head of Facilities | Continuous |

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-644 (ensure controlled execution — Facilities Management Strategy, IFM Provider & SLA Governance (PA-138.1).)
- CTL-644: register boundaries vs VS-35/VS-20.3/VS-97 documented; landlord-tenant responsibility mapped (VS-42)

### Time Estimate
- Annual survey ~0.5 FTE; ongoing register maintenance

### Cross-references
- links to VS-97 (real estate), VS-35 (fixed assets), VS-20.3 (equipment — boundary), VS-40 (capex), VS-109 (remodel), VS-42/VS-97.2 (lease responsibility)

## W4173. Preventive Maintenance Planning & Scheduling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | PM schedule cycle; OEM interval; compliance requirement |
| **Frequency** | Continuous scheduling (daily/weekly/monthly/annual cycles) |
| **Volume** | ~205 sites × building systems (HVAC filters/coils, fire-system tests, elevator certs, generator, BMS calibration) |
| **Owner** | Facilities Maintenance Lead |
| **Participants** | Providers/contractors, Store Ops (VS-07), DC Ops (VS-04), HSE (VS-24), Calibration (VS-115), Maintenance & Projects Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities build the preventive-maintenance (PM) schedule per asset/system from OEM intervals, warranty, and compliance (fire-system, elevator, generator), and resource it across providers/sites | Facilities Maintenance Lead / Provider | Head of Facilities | Per cycle |
| 2 | Facilities execute PM (HVAC service, fire-system test, elevator annual cert, generator load-test, BMS check), documenting completion and findings in the work-order system (W4174) | Provider / Facilities Maintenance Lead | Facilities Maintenance Lead | Per schedule |
| 3 | Facilities feed PM findings into condition/capex (W4172), coordinate with calibration where relevant (VS-115), and monitor PM-vs-reactive ratio (W4192) | Facilities Maintenance Lead | Head of Facilities | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- CAFM PM scheduling; work-order system (W4174); HSE (VS-24); calibration (VS-115); condition/capex (W4172/VS-40)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance-gap risk**: Missed fire-system/elevator/generator cert → regulatory/safety exposure; mitigated by scheduled PM with completion tracking
- **Reactive-creep risk**: Deferred PM → more breakdowns; mitigated by PM-vs-reactive ratio monitoring (W4192)

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto PM scheduling from OEM intervals; completion/compliance auto-tracking; certificate-expiry alerts

### Controls
- CTL-644 (ensure controlled execution — Facilities Management Strategy, IFM Provider & SLA Governance (PA-138.1).)
- CTL-644: PM completion tracked; statutory systems (fire/elevator/generator) cert-current (VS-24/VS-22)

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; PM labor ~national provider force

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-22 (permits/fire), VS-115 (calibration), VS-40 (capex), W4174 (work-orders), VS-20.3 (equipment PM)

## W4174. Facilities Helpdesk, Work-Order & Reactive Maintenance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Site-reported issue (broken AC, leak, light out, pest sighting); emergency |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (high volume across 205 sites) |
| **Volume** | Thousands of work-orders/yr across ~205 sites |
| **Owner** | Facilities Helpdesk / Facilities Maintenance Lead |
| **Participants** | Site staff (VS-07/VS-04), Providers, HSE (VS-24), Store Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Site staff log the issue via the facilities helpdesk (channel: app/phone/portal), triaged by criticality (safety/operations-impacting vs routine) and service line (W4171) | Site Staff / Helpdesk | Facilities Helpdesk Lead | Per ticket, ~5 min log |
| 2 | Facilities dispatch to the right provider/technician, track to SLA resolution (W4171), and escalate on breach; emergencies (fire/flood/power) trigger emergency response (VS-24.2/VS-69) | Helpdesk / Provider | Facilities Maintenance Lead | Per ticket |
| 3 | Facilities verify completion/quality with the site, close the work-order, capture cost, and analyze recurring issues (W4192) | Helpdesk / Site Staff | Facilities Maintenance Lead | Per ticket |

### System Touchpoints
- CAFM helpdesk/work-order system; provider dispatch; emergency response (VS-24.2/VS-69); SLA framework (W4171)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Response-breach risk**: Slow resolution closes a store/aisle; mitigated by SLA + escalation
- **Recurring-issue risk**: Same fault repeatedly logged → root cause unaddressed; mitigated by recurring-issue analytics (W4192)

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile work-order logging; auto-dispatch by service line/location; SLA-breach auto-escalation; photo/iot evidence

### Controls
- CTL-644 (ensure controlled execution — Facilities Management Strategy, IFM Provider & SLA Governance (PA-138.1).)
- CTL-644: SLA adherence tracked; emergency path to VS-24.2/VS-69; completion verified with site

### Time Estimate
- ~5 min log + dispatch; resolution per SLA; helpdesk tier-1 staffed by the IFM provider (VS-138.2) with ~1–2 in-house FTE for escalation and vendor management (within the 8-person Facilities & Real Estate team, profile §3.3)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07 (store staff), VS-24.2 (emergency response), VS-69 (typhoon/disaster), VS-23.2 (security incidents), W4171 (SLA), W4178 (analytics)
