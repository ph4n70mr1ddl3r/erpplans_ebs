## W4177. Janitorial, Cleaning & Hygiene Service Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily/periodic schedule; special clean (post-typhoon, spill, event) |
| **Frequency** | Daily (stores/DC/HQ) + periodic deep clean |
| **Volume** | ~205 sites × daily cleaning (selling floor, restrooms, backroom, yards, HQ offices) |
| **Owner** | Facilities Soft-Services Lead / Cleaning Provider |
| **Participants** | Site staff (VS-07/VS-04), Provider workforce (VS-98), HSE (VS-24), Store Ops, Facilities Coordination Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facilities/Provider execute the daily cleaning schedule per site-type standard (floor, restrooms, glass, backroom, yard) with checklists and photo evidence, resourced by the provider workforce (VS-98) | Provider / Facilities Soft-Services Lead | Facilities Soft-Services Lead | Daily |
| 2 | Facilities schedule periodic deep cleans (floor stripping/waxing, high-level dusting, facade) and special cleans (post-typhoon VS-69, spill/biohazard, post-event), coordinating with site operations | Facilities Soft-Services Lead / Provider | Facilities Soft-Services Lead | Periodic |
| 3 | Facilities/Provider stock cleaning consumables, manage hygiene supplies (restroom, sanitizing stations), verify quality with the site, and report cleaning SLA (W4171/W4176) | Provider / Facilities Soft-Services Lead | Facilities Soft-Services Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- CAFM cleaning schedule/checklist; consumables inventory (VS-07.2); provider workforce (VS-98); work-orders (W4174)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Customer-experience risk**: Dirty store/restroom harms experience (links VS-13); mitigated by checklist + verification
- **Chemical-safety risk**: Cleaning-chemical handling (some DG) → health/exposure; mitigated by SDS (VS-114/VS-24.3) and PPE

### Automation Opportunity
- Checklist/photo-evidence mobile app; consumables auto-replenishment alerts

### Controls
- CTL-645 (ensure controlled execution — Hard & Soft FM Service Operations (Cleaning, Pest, Security, Grounds, Workplace) (PA-138.2).)
- CTL-645 (cleaning checklist with verification; cleaning-chemical SDS/PPE (VS-24.3/VS-114))

### Time Estimate
- Daily per site; cleaning labor ~national provider force

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07.2 (consumables/store equipment), VS-98 (provider workforce), VS-24.3 (chemical safety), VS-114 (SDS), VS-13 (customer experience), VS-69 (post-typhoon clean)

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

### Time Estimate

### Cross-references
