## W3753. ECM Project Delivery, Commissioning & Handover

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved ECM project (W3749); capex release |
| **Frequency** | Per project |
| **Volume** | ECM project portfolio |
| **Owner** | EEO / Facilities / PMO |
| **Participants** | EEO, Facilities (links to VS-20.3), PMO (links to VS-112), Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34), Finance/Capex (links to VS-40), Store Ops (links to VS-07), DC Ops (links to VS-04), Contractors, Commissioning Agent |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EEO/Facilities/PMO deliver the ECM project: design, procurement (links to VS-34), construction/installation, schedule/cost/quality management (links to VS-112), and coordination with store/DC operations to minimize disruption (links to VS-07/VS-04) | EEO / Facilities / PMO / Contractor | EEO | Per project |
| 2 | EEO/Facilities/Commissioning Agent commission the ECM: functional performance testing, BMS/control verification, balancing, and confirmation that the system meets the design/energy-intent before handover | EEO / Facilities / Commissioning Agent | EEO | Per project |
| 3 | EEO/Facilities/Store Ops hand over to operations: train facility/store staff on the new system/SOPs (links to VS-19.4), update the BMS and asset register (links to VS-35), transfer to maintenance (links to VS-20.3), and initiate M&V (W3747) | EEO / Facilities / Store Ops / HR | EEO | Per project |

### System Touchpoints
- PMO (links to VS-112); facilities (links to VS-20.3); procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34); capex (links to VS-40); store ops (links to VS-07); fixed assets (links to VS-35); M&V (links to W3747)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Commissioning-skip risk**: ECM installed but not commissioned → savings not realized; mitigated by commissioning
- **Disruption risk**: ECM work disrupts trading; mitigated by phasing around store hours


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-schedule of "EEO/Facilities/PMO deliver the ECM project" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-test of "testing, BMS/control verification, balancing, and confirmation that the system…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-update of "update the BMS and asset register" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-591 (ensure controlled execution — ECM Delivery, Performance Optimization & Compliance Analytics (PA-120.3).)
- operational: commissioning; operational: phasing around store hours

### Time Estimate
- Per project
