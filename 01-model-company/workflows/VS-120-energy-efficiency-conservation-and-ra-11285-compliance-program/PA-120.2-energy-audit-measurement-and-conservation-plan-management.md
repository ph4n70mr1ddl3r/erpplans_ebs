## W3745. Mandatory Energy Audit & Audit-Firm Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | RA 11285 audit cycle (Type-1 every 3 yrs; Type-2 annually for largest consumers); designation (W3738) |
| **Frequency** | Per audit cycle |
| **Volume** | Per designated site/entity |
| **Owner** | EEO / Facilities |
| **Participants** | EEO, Facilities (links to VS-20.3), DOE-accredited Energy Auditor/Firm, Compliance (links to VS-22), Sustainability (links to VS-25), Procurement (links to VS-34), Records (links to VS-88) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EEO/Facilities/Procurement plan and source the mandatory energy audit: schedule per RA 11285 cycle per designated site/entity, select a DOE-accredited energy auditor/firm, scope the audit (Type-1 walk-through / Type-2 detailed), and contract (links to VS-34) | EEO / Facilities / Procurement | EEO | Per cycle |
| 2 | EEO/Facilities support the energy audit: provide consumption data (links to W3741), facility access, system documentation, and operational context; coordinate with store/DC teams (links to VS-07/VS-04); manage audit findings | EEO / Facilities / Store Ops | EEO | Per audit |
| 3 | EEO/Compliance submit the audit report and certification to DOE per the IRR, manage any DOE follow-up, archive to records (links to VS-88/W3744), and feed findings/ECMs to the conservation plan (W3748) and ECM pipeline (W3749) | EEO / Compliance / Records | EEO | Per audit |

### System Touchpoints
- Facilities (links to VS-20.3); procurement (links to VS-34); compliance (links to VS-22); energy data (links to W3741); records (links to VS-88); conservation plan (links to W3748)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Audit-overdue risk**: Mandatory audit not conducted within the cycle → DOE non-compliance; mitigated by cycle tracking (W3738)
- **Auditor-quality risk**: Weak audit misses ECMs; mitigated by DOE-accreditation and scope


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-schedule of "schedule per RA 11285 cycle per designated site/entity, select a DOE-accredited…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documentation, and operational context" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "EEO/Compliance submit the audit report and certification to DOE" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-138 (ensure RA 11285 energy compliance & M&V integrity.)
- operational: cycle tracking (W3738); operational: audit not conducted within the cycle → DOE non-compliance; operational: DOE-accreditation and scope

### Time Estimate
- Per audit (weeks)

| **Frequency** | Per major site/project |
| **Volume** | Investment-grade audits per priority site |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EEO/Facilities/Energy Consultant conduct investment-grade energy audits (IGA) beyond the statutory minimum: detailed analysis of significant energy uses (HVAC, refrigeration, lighting, process), identify and quantify ECMs (savings, cost, payback, GHG), and develop the ECM register per site | EEO / Facilities / Energy Consultant | EEO | Per site |
| 2 | EEO/Facilities evaluate ECMs: technical feasibility, interaction/sequence, risk, and the prioritized ECM pipeline; integrate with facility-renewal/store-remodel plans (links to VS-20.3/VS-109) to bundle energy work into capital projects | EEO / Facilities / Store Ops | EEO | Per site |

### System Touchpoints

### Pain Points / Risks

- System auto-validate of "feed the ECM pipeline to business-case/investment governance" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-138 (ensure RA 11285 energy compliance & M&V integrity.)
- operational: investment-grade depth; operational: remodel integration

### Time Estimate
