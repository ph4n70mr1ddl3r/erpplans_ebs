## W2969. Enterprise Document Taxonomy & Classification Scheme Maintenance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual review or new document type |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~200–300 document types |
| **Owner** | Records Manager |
| **Participants** | Records Mgr, Compliance, IT, Department Owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Records Manager maintains enterprise taxonomy: document class (financial, HR, legal, tax, contract, operational), subtype, confidentiality, retention class | Records Manager | Compliance Officer | Annual |
| 2 | Department Owners validate taxonomy covers their records; Records Manager maps each class to retention rule and authoritative system | Department Owners | Records Manager | Annual |
| 3 | IT configures taxonomy in document management system; enforces classification at capture | IT | Records Manager | Per change |

### System Touchpoints
- Document management system taxonomy; classification rules

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Findability risk**: Poor taxonomy hampers retrieval and audit response; mitigated by structured classification scheme


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "Records Manager maintains enterprise taxonomy" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "Department Owners validate taxonomy covers their records" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document management system" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-493 (ensure controlled execution — Document Classification, Versioning & Taxonomy (PA-88.1).)
- operational: structured classification scheme

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks annual

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
