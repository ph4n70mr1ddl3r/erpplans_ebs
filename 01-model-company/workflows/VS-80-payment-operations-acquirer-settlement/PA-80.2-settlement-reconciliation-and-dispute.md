## W2785. Daily Card/E-Wallet Settlement File Ingestion & GL Posting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily settlement file from acquirer/e-wallet/gateway (T+1) |
| **Frequency** | Daily per partner |
| **Volume** | ~10 settlement files/day; ~PHP 2.9B/month non-cash tender |
| **Owner** | AR Settlement Accountant |
| **Participants** | AR Settlement Accountant, IT, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT ingests daily settlement files (EDI/CSV/API) from each acquirer and e-wallet into ERP settlement staging; validates file integrity and partner authentication | IT | AR Settlement Accountant | Daily |
| 2 | AR Settlement Accountant posts settlement to clearing GL: gross captured, fees deducted, net bank credit; maps each batch by MID/entity/store | AR Settlement Accountant | Finance Controller | Daily |
| 3 | Finance validates net settlement matches bank statement credit; investigates timing differences (T+1 vs. T+2) and currency (USD card settlements) | Finance | Finance Controller | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Settlement file ingestion; clearing GL; bank statement import; multi-currency module

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reconciliation risk**: File format/schema changes break ingestion; mitigated by schema validation and partner pre-notification


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "validates file integrity and partner authentication" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Accountant posts settlement to clearing GL" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-match of "matches bank statement credit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-215 (ensure card/e-wallet settlement & chargeback control.)
- operational: schema validation and partner pre-notification

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 hours/day

## W2786. Settlement-to-POS Capture Reconciliation & Exception Triage

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily settlement posting |
| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | ~2.8M POS txns/month; ~1.4M non-cash lines/month |
| **Owner** | AR Settlement Accountant |
| **Participants** | AR Settlement Accountant, Store Ops, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ERP auto-matches settlement records to POS capture by reference (RRN/auth code/QR ref); flags unmatched captures and unmatched settlements | ERP automation | AR Settlement Accountant | Daily |
| 2 | AR Settlement Accountant triages exceptions: terminal batch not closed, declined-but-captured, duplicate capture, missing settlement; assigns owner | AR Settlement Accountant | Finance Controller | Daily |
| 3 | Store Ops/IT resolves terminal-level issues (resend batch, manual settlement, RFI to acquirer); AR Settlement Accountant clears exception queue | Store Ops / IT / AR Settlement Accountant | Finance Controller | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Auto-match engine; exception queue; terminal monitoring

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Leakage risk**: Unreconciled exceptions over-state receivables; mitigated by daily SLA-bound triage


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "records to POS capture by reference" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Accountant triages exceptions" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-send of "resend batch, manual settlement, RFI to acquirer" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-215 (ensure card/e-wallet settlement & chargeback control.)
- operational: daily SLA-bound triage

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 hours/day

## W2788. Chargeback Notification, Evidence Compilation & Representment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Chargeback notification from acquirer (issuer dispute) |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~300–500 chargebacks/year (~0.003% of card txns, per the ~30–40/month Volume) |
| **Volume** | ~30–40/month |
| **Owner** | Chargeback Analyst |
| **Participants** | Chargeback Analyst, Store Ops, LP, AR Settlement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chargeback Analyst receives chargeback (fraud, friendly fraud, product not received, duplicate); logs in dispute tracker; pulls POS transaction evidence | Chargeback Analyst | Payment Operations Manager | Per dispute |
| 2 | Store Ops/LP compiles compendium: signed charge slip, ID presented, CCTV timestamp, delivery proof/POD, BIR invoice, customer communication; assembles representment package | Store Ops / LP | Chargeback Analyst | 1–3 days |
| 3 | Chargeback Analyst submits representment via acquirer portal within scheme deadline (typically 7–45 days); tracks outcome; books chargeback loss or reversal | Chargeback Analyst | Finance Controller | Per dispute |

### System Touchpoints
- Acquirer dispute portal; dispute tracker; evidence repository; loss-recovery GL

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Financial risk**: Missed representment deadline forfeits right to recover; mitigated by SLA-bounded tracker and reminder workflow


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs in dispute tracker" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-compile of "Store Ops/LP compiles compendium" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-submit of "submits representment via acquirer portal within scheme deadline" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-215 (ensure card/e-wallet settlement & chargeback control.)
- operational: SLA-bounded tracker and reminder workflow

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 hours per chargeback

## W2790. Refund, Void & Partial-Reversal Settlement Matching

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer refund or transaction void |
| **Frequency** | ~0.04% of transactions (returns — the ~40/day AR-credit-memo returns canon); ~1,200/month |
| **Volume** | ~1,200 refund/void events/month (~14,500/year at the ~1.05-lines/doc measured profile) |
| **Owner** | AR Settlement Accountant |
| **Participants** | AR Settlement Accountant, Customer Service, Store Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Service processes refund to original tender in POS/ecommerce; generates reversal reference; ensures within scheme refund window | Customer Service | Store Manager | Per refund |

- System auto-match of "matches reversal to original capture" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "Accountant reconciles reversal settlement to bank debit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-470 (ensure controlled execution — Settlement Reconciliation & Chargeback/Dispute Management (PA-80.2).)
- operational: auto-match and daily reconciliation

## W2792. Aged Unreconciled Settlement & Suspense GL Clearing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Items aged > 30 days in settlement suspense |
| **Frequency** | Weekly review + monthly close |
| **Volume** | ~200–400 aged items/month |
| **Owner** | AR Settlement Accountant |
| **Participants** | AR Settlement Accountant, Finance, Payment Ops Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AR Settlement Accountant reviews aged settlement suspense weekly; classifies (acquirer error, timing, write-off candidate); assigns resolution owner | AR Settlement Accountant | Finance Controller | Weekly |
| 2 | Finance investigates root cause with acquirer/partner; recovers where possible; provisions write-off for unrecoverable items > 90 days | Finance | Finance Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Payment Ops Manager closes suspense items; reports aged suspense trend and root-cause themes to management for systemic remediation | Payment Ops Manager | Finance Controller | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Aged suspense report; write-off provisioning; root-cause tracker

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Financial risk**: Unmanaged suspense distorts financials and signals control weakness; mitigated by 30/60/90 aging discipline and write-off governance


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews aged settlement suspense weekly" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-flag for investigation of "investigates root cause with acquirer/partner" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "reports aged suspense trend and root-cause themes to management for systemic…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-470 (ensure controlled execution — Settlement Reconciliation & Chargeback/Dispute Management (PA-80.2).)
- operational: 30/60/90 aging discipline and write-off governance

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 hours/week
