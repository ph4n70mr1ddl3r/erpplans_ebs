## W2793. PCI-DSS SAQ Scope Management & Annual Self-Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual PCI-DSS attestation; scope change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | 1 enterprise SAQ-D (or P2PE SAQ) covering 600 terminals + ecommerce |
| **Owner** | Payment Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Payment Ops Mgr, IT Security, QSA (if applicable), Compliance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Security maps PCI-DSS scope (CDE, connected systems, people); selects appropriate SAQ (P2PE for stores with validated terminals; SAQ-D for ecommerce) | IT Security | Payment Operations Manager | Annual |
| 2 | Compliance validates control evidence (network segmentation, access controls, encryption, logging); runs internal ASV vulnerability scan quarterly | Compliance / IT Security | Payment Operations Manager | Quarterly + Annual |
| 3 | Payment Ops Manager completes SAQ and Attestation of Compliance (AOC); submits to acquirer/scheme; archives for 12 months | Payment Ops Manager / QSA | CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- PCI-DSS scope register; ASV scan platform; AOC repository

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Scope creep or missed controls trigger fines/termination; mitigated by annual scope review and segmentation validation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "IT Security maps PCI-DSS scope" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-scan of "Compliance validates control evidence" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-submit of "submits to acquirer/scheme" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-471 (ensure controlled execution — Payment Cost, Fraud & Tokenization Governance (PA-80.3).)
- operational: annual scope review and segmentation validation

### Time Estimate
- 4–8 weeks per annual cycle

- System auto-log of "IT Security maintains Hardware Security Module" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "validates terminal encryption" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-471 (ensure controlled execution — Payment Cost, Fraud & Tokenization Governance (PA-80.3).)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Suspected breach, terminal compromise, or acquirer incident notice |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; rare |
| **Volume** | ~1–5 incidents/year |
| **Owner** | Payment Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Payment Ops Mgr, IT Security, IT Incident, Legal, BSP/NPC (if breach), Acquirer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Incident contains incident (isolate terminal/system); IT Security starts forensic capture; Payment Ops Manager notifies acquirer/scheme per agreement | IT Incident / IT Security | Payment Operations Manager | 1–4 hours |
| 2 | Payment Ops Manager coordinates PCI-PFI (PCI Forensic Investigator) engagement if breach suspected; notifies BSP and NPC where cardholder/personal data involved | Payment Ops Manager / Legal | CFO | 24–72 hours |

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "IT Incident contains incident" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System workflow orchestration of "Payment Ops Manager coordinates PCI-PFI" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "Legal manages regulatory notification and customer notification per Data" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-471 (ensure controlled execution — Payment Cost, Fraud & Tokenization Governance (PA-80.3).)
- operational: rehearsed runbook and pre-engaged PFI

### Time Estimate
- Hours to weeks depending on severity
