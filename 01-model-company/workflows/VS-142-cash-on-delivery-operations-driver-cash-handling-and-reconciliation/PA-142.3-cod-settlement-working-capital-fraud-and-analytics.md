## W4281. COD Settlement Cycle, Clearing & Inter-Entity Settlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Settlement cycle; monthly close (VS-17.1); intercompany (VS-72) |
| **Frequency** | Daily clearing + monthly settlement |
| **Volume** | COD settlement across 5 entities (Digital Commerce Inc. collects, Depot Inc. recognizes revenue) |
| **Owner** | Finance / GL (VS-17.1) / Intercompany (VS-72) |
| **Participants** | Treasury (VS-18), GL (VS-17.1), Intercompany (VS-72), Digital Commerce (VS-10), Payment Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance/GL run the COD settlement cycle: clear the COD cash-collected against the order receivable/float, recognize revenue at the right entity (Depot Inc. per profile §2), and post the Digital Commerce fulfillment fee (per profile §2) | Finance / GL | Finance Controller | Daily/Monthly |
| 2 | Intercompany/Finance settle inter-entity (Digital Commerce Inc. collected cash → remit to Depot Inc. per the per-order fee model), at arm's-length transfer pricing (VS-72) | Intercompany / Finance | CFO | Monthly |
| 3 | Finance reconcile settlement to bank (VS-18) and GL (VS-17.1), eliminate intercompany on consolidation (VS-17), and report (W4287) | Finance / GL | CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- GL/consolidation (VS-17/VS-17.1); intercompany (VS-72); treasury/bank (VS-18); ecommerce entity (VS-10)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Entity-misallocation risk**: COD revenue booked to wrong entity → consolidation error; mitigated by entity-recognition rule
- **Intercompany-dispute risk**: Fee/cash settlement between entities; mitigated by clear per-order fee model (profile §2)

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto settlement/clearing with entity coding; intercompany auto-settle; consolidation elimination

### Controls
- CTL-658 (ensure controlled execution — COD Settlement, Working-Capital, Fraud & Analytics (PA-142.3).)
- CTL-658: revenue recognized at correct entity (profile §2); intercompany arm's-length (VS-72); settlement reconciled to bank/GL

### Time Estimate
- Automated daily clearing; ~2–3 days monthly settlement

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17/VS-17.1 (GL/consolidation), VS-72 (intercompany), VS-18 (bank), VS-10 (Digital Commerce), W4279 (posting), W4287 (reporting)

|---|---|

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/Finance measure COD float (cash-in-motion from delivery to bank deposit, unremitted aging W4275), the cost-of-capital, and the COD contribution to the cash-conversion-cycle (links VS-105) | Treasury / FP&A | CFO | Monthly |

### Automation Opportunity

- CTL-658 (ensure controlled execution — COD Settlement, Working-Capital, Fraud & Analytics (PA-142.3).)
- CTL-658: COD float measured & aged; remittance SLA; float in cash forecast & CCC (VS-105)

### Time Estimate
