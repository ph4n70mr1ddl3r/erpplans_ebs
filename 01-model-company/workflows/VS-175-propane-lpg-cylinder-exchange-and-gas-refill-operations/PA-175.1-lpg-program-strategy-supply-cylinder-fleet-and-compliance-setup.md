## W5057. Propane/LPG Cylinder Exchange & Refill Program Strategy & Business-Model Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy refresh (VS-33); category strategy for Seasonal/Outdoor, Plumbing, Material Handling; new program |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | 1 program across 200 stores + bulk accounts |
| **Owner** | Category Manager (Seasonal/Outdoor/Plumbing) / Director Store Services |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the LPG program strategy & model: exchange (full-for-empty pool) vs refill (customer-owned) vs both, the cylinder-size portfolio (11kg/5kg/50kg/forklift), the attach-to-appliance-sale linkage (grill/heater/torch), and the recurring-revenue/deposit-float economics | Category Mgr / Director Store Services | Director Store Services / Category Director | Annual |
| 2 | Build the safety & regulatory posture: confirm BFP Fire Code storage feasibility per store, DOE retailer-accreditation scope (W5063), and the HAZMAT/OSH operating model with HSE/Legal (links VS-24/VS-114/VS-100) | HSE / Legal / Category Mgr | Director Store Services / HSE Manager | Annual |
| 3 | Govern & approve: leadership/HSE sign-off (VS-36), the entity/scope (Depot Inc.), and the KPIs (exchange volume, attach rate, deposit-float, safety/incident rate, margin) with FP&A | Category Mgr / FP&A / HSE | Director Store Services / CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A/strategy (VS-17.4/VS-33); merchandising/category (VS-01); supplier mgmt (VS-03); HSE (VS-24); compliance/permits (VS-166); cylinder-asset/deposit module (new — W5060/W5074)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Safety-acceptance risk**: BFP/OSH gating some stores out of LPG storage; mitigated by per-store feasibility at step 2
- **Model-choice risk**: Wrong exchange-vs-refill mix for the local market; mitigated by pilot & demand analysis
- **Margin-illusion risk**: Exchange margin eroded by hidden cylinder-fleet/refill costs; mitigated by fully-burdened unit-economics (W5062)

### Automation Opportunity
- Per-store BFP-feasibility scoring; attach-rate analytics from appliance vs cylinder sales linkage

### Controls
- CTL-755 (ensure controlled execution — LPG Program Strategy, Bulk Supply, Cylinder Fleet & Regulatory Setup (PA-175.1).)
- CTL-755: program HSE/leadership-approved (VS-36/VS-24); BFP feasibility per store; economics FP&A-validated

### Time Estimate
- ~6–8 weeks annual strategy + per-store feasibility

## W5061. LPG Storage Cage & Yard Design, BFP Fire-Code Infrastructure & Safety Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New store / new program; BFP compliance review; capacity expansion |
| **Frequency** | Per store setup + periodic review |
| **Volume** | ~200 stores × cage/yard setup |
| **Owner** | Store Ops/Facilities (VS-07/VS-138) / HSE (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | Facilities (VS-138), HSE (VS-24), BFP/fire consultant, Construction (VS-20/VS-109), LP/Security (VS-23), Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design the LPG cage/yard: outdoor/ventilated cage location (away from ignition/egress/customer flow), capacity (max cylinders per BFP/DOE), fire-rated construction, ventilation, signage, and segregation from other DG (links VS-114/VS-147) | Facilities / HSE / Fire consultant | HSE Manager / VP Store Operations | Per store |
| 2 | Install fire/safety infrastructure: detection/gas-leak alarm, fire extinguisher/suppression suited to gas, bonding/grounding, spill containment, lighting, and CCTV coverage (VS-23) | Facilities / HSE / Security | HSE Manager | Per store |
| 3 | Commission & certify: BFP Fire Safety Inspection/FSIC (links VS-166), LGU/DOE retailer permit (W5063), and HSE acceptance before stocking cylinders | HSE / Facilities / Legal | HSE Manager / VP Store Operations | Per store |

### System Touchpoints
- Facility/IFM (VS-138); BFP FSIC/permit (VS-166); HSE (VS-24); security/CCTV (VS-23); construction (VS-20/VS-109); DG storage (VS-114)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fire/explosion risk**: Improperly located/vented cage → fire/explosion exposure (links VS-147); mitigated by BFP-compliant design + FSIC
- **Capacity-exceedance risk**: Storing beyond BFP/DOE max-cylinder limit → violation; mitigated by capacity signage + control
- **Customer-proximity risk**: Cage too close to customer/ignition; mitigated by siting away from sales floor

### Automation Opportunity

### Cross-references
