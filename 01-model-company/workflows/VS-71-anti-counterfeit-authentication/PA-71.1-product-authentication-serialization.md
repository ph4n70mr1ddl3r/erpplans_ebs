## W2550. High-Risk Product Serialization & Registration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Inbound receipt of serialized products (power tools, high-value electrical, premium fixtures) |
| **Frequency** | Per goods receipt; ~2,000–3,000 serialized units/month |
| **Volume** | ~500 SKUs requiring serialization across power tools, premium electrical, appliances |
| **Owner** | Receiving Clerk / DC Warehouse Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Receiving Clerk, Quality Inspector, LP Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receiving Clerk scans vendor serial numbers/QR codes during goods receipt; system validates against vendor master serialization file (ASN); flags units with missing, duplicate, or non-matching serial numbers for investigation | Receiving Clerk | DC Ops Manager | 5–10 min per shipment |
| 2 | System registers each serial number in authentication database: vendor, model, manufacturing date, batch/lot, import documentation reference; generates BuildRight authentication QR label applied to packaging | System / Receiving Clerk | LP Manager | 2–3 min per unit |
| 3 | For products without vendor serialization (local brands): system generates BuildRight-unique serial at GR; records origin vendor, PO reference, and receipt date; enables full chain-of-custody tracking from receipt to sale | System / Receiving Clerk | LP Manager | 1–2 min per unit |

### System Touchpoints
- Serial number scanning at goods receipt
- Authentication database with vendor ASN matching
- QR label printing system

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vendor non-compliance**: Some vendors don't provide advance serialization data
- **Manual entry errors**: Typing serial numbers manually introduces data quality issues


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-scan of "scans vendor serial numbers/QR codes during goods receipt" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "System registers each serial number in authentication database" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "records origin vendor, PO reference, and receipt date" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-233 (ensure anti-counterfeit program integrity.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

## W2551. QR Code Authentication System Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer or staff scans product QR code |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~5,000–10,000 scans/month |
| **Volume** | ~500,000+ active QR codes in system |
| **Owner** | IT Application Manager |
| **Participants** | IT App Manager, LP Manager, Vendors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT maintains authentication platform: QR code generation engine, scan validation API, mobile-responsive result page, analytics backend; ensures 99.9% uptime for customer-facing verification; manages vendor API integrations for real-time validation | IT Application Manager | CIO | Ongoing |
| 2 | When QR scanned: system validates code authenticity, returns product information (brand, model, manufacturing date, authorized retailer confirmation, warranty status); flags previously scanned codes (potential counterfeiting indicator) for LP investigation | System | LP Manager | <3 seconds per scan |
| 3 | IT generates weekly authentication analytics: scan volume by product category and region, authentication failure rate, geographic scan patterns, repeat-scan alerts (same code scanned at different locations = counterfeiting indicator) | IT / LP Analyst | LP Manager | 2–3 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints
- QR code management platform
- Mobile-responsive customer verification portal
- Authentication analytics dashboard

### Pain Points / Risks
- **QR cloning**: Sophisticated counterfeiters may copy valid QR codes; mitigated by unique per-unit codes and geographic scan anomaly detection
- **System downtime**: Authentication platform unavailability prevents customer verification


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-scan of "scan validation API, mobile-responsive result page, analytics backend" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validates code authenticity, returns product information" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "IT generates weekly authentication analytics" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-442 (ensure controlled execution — Product Authentication & Serialization (PA-71.1).)
- operational: unique per-unit codes and geographic scan anomaly detection; authentication-platform uptime SLA monitored (99.9% target) with offline-verification fallback and vendor-API failover tested quarterly

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 hours/week of analytics and platform-maintenance events (per step 3); platform uptime is a continuous SLA, not per-occurrence effort

## W2552. Customer Product Verification Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer reports suspected counterfeit product or requests verification |
| **Frequency** | ~50–100 inquiries/month |
| **Volume** | ~50–100/month via customer service, in-store, online |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Representative |
| **Participants** | Customer Service Rep, LP Officer, Category Manager, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer submits verification request: product photos, QR code scan result, purchase receipt, reason for suspicion (poor quality, packaging differences, performance issues); Customer Service logs in authentication case system | Customer Service Rep | LP Manager | 15–20 min |
| 2 | LP Officer reviews case: compares product images against authentic reference library, validates serial number in database, checks purchase history for source store verification; if counterfeit confirmed: escalates to LP Manager and initiates investigation (W2559) | LP Officer | LP Manager | 30–60 min |
| 3 | Customer Service communicates result: if authentic — reassures customer and explains verification; if counterfeit — offers replacement with genuine product, issues refund, requests customer cooperation in investigation (source information); documents all interactions | Customer Service Rep | LP Manager | 15–30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Customer service authentication case management
- Product image comparison against reference library
- Serial number validation lookup

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Customer frustration**: Genuine product quality issues misidentified as counterfeiting by customer
- **Evidence collection**: Customer reluctance to provide detailed information or return suspected item


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-scan of "scan result, purchase receipt, reason for suspicion" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "LP Officer reviews case" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "documents all interactions" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-442 (ensure controlled execution — Product Authentication & Serialization (PA-71.1).)
- operational: Customer Service Representative review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

| **Volume** | ~50–80 vendors with serialization-required products |
| **Owner** | LP Manager |
| **Participants** | LP Manager, Procurement, Quality, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## W2555. Gray Market & Unauthorized Channel Detection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Anomalous scan patterns or market monitoring alerts |
| **Frequency** | Weekly monitoring; ad-hoc investigations |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 investigations/month |
| **Owner** | LP Analyst |
| **Participants** | LP Analyst, LP Manager, Category Manager, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LP Analyst monitors for gray market indicators: BuildRight-serialized products found in unauthorized retail channels (online marketplaces, competitor stores), geographic scan anomalies (products scanning far from authorized store), bulk scan patterns (reseller activity detection) | LP Analyst | LP Manager | 2–3 hours/week |
| 2 | Analyst investigates suspicious patterns: traces product origin through serial chain-of-custody, identifies purchase point (store/POS transaction), determines if diversion occurred at vendor, DC, or store level; compiles evidence dossier | LP Analyst | LP Manager | 1–2 days per case |
| 3 | LP Manager escalates confirmed gray market cases: to Category Manager for vendor discussion (if vendor-side diversion), to Legal for contractual enforcement, to LP team for store-level investigation (if internal diversion); implements controls: quantity limits for high-risk products, enhanced tracking for repeat offenders | LP Manager | COO | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Scan analytics with geographic anomaly detection
- Serial chain-of-custody traceability
- Online marketplace monitoring (Shopee, Lazada, FB Marketplace)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Online marketplace proliferation**: Difficult to monitor all online channels
- **Internal collusion**: Store staff purchasing discounted products for resale


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-scan of "LP Analyst monitors for gray market indicators" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-compile of "compiles evidence dossier" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-escalate of "escalates confirmed gray market cases" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-442 (ensure controlled execution — Product Authentication & Serialization (PA-71.1).)

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New product introduction or packaging redesign |
| **Frequency** | 5–10 updates/month |
| **Volume** | ~500 serialized SKUs |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Category Mgr, Vendor, LP Manager, Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager defines serialization requirements per product category: power tools (mandatory vendor serialization + BuildRight QR), electrical components (mandatory for circuit breakers, wiring), appliances (mandatory serial + extended warranty QR), accessories (optional based on price point) | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 day |
