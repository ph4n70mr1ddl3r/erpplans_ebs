## W2566. Vendor Anti-Counterfeit Compliance Program Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual vendor program cycle or new vendor onboarding |
| **Frequency** | Annual per vendor; per onboarding |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 vendors |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement Mgr, LP Manager, Legal, Quality |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement incorporates anti-counterfeit requirements into vendor onboarding: product authentication capabilities, serialization standards, packaging security features, supply chain traceability, brand protection cooperation clause; vendors of high-risk categories (power tools, electrical) must demonstrate compliance before activation | Procurement / LP Manager | VP Merchandising | Per onboarding (1–2 days) |
| 2 | LP Manager defines vendor anti-counterfeit tier: Tier 1 (mandatory serialization, quarterly audit) for power tools and premium electrical brands; Tier 2 (serialization recommended, annual audit) for mid-range tools and fixtures; Tier 3 (awareness only) for low-risk categories; updates tier classification annually | LP Manager | COO | 1 week/year |
| 3 | Procurement monitors vendor compliance: tracks serialization rate, audit findings, counterfeit incident history; non-compliant vendors receive improvement plan with 90-day deadline; persistent non-compliance affects vendor scorecard and contract renewal | Procurement / LP Manager | VP Merchandising | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor master with anti-counterfeit tier classification
- Vendor scorecard with compliance metrics
- Audit finding tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vendor pushback**: Small vendors lack resources for serialization compliance
- **Competitive pressure**: Stricter requirements may reduce vendor pool


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "anti-counterfeit requirements into vendor onboarding" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-update of "updates tier classification annually" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "tracks serialization rate, audit findings, counterfeit incident history" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-444 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor Compliance & Anti-Counterfeit Analytics (PA-71.3).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per onboarding; tier classification ~1 week/year; compliance monitoring ongoing

## W2567. Supply Chain Integrity Audit & Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual audit calendar or incident-triggered audit |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly for Tier 1 vendors; annual for Tier 2 |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 audits/year |
| **Owner** | Quality Assurance Manager |
| **Participants** | QA Mgr, LP Manager, Procurement, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | QA Manager prepares audit scope: review vendor manufacturing and packaging controls, serialization processes, raw material traceability, quality testing documentation, distribution chain integrity; for import vendors: include customs documentation verification and origin certification review | QA Manager | COO | 1–2 days prep |
| 2 | QA conducts on-site or remote audit: physical inspection of manufacturing facility, review of quality control records, verification of serialization infrastructure, packaging line observation, sample product testing against BuildRight specifications | QA Manager / LP Officer | COO | 1–2 days on-site |
| 3 | QA issues audit report: findings (critical/major/minor), corrective action requests with timeline, re-audit schedule for non-compliant vendors; LP Manager incorporates findings into vendor anti-counterfeit scorecard; Procurement uses findings in contract negotiations | QA Manager / LP Manager | COO | 3–5 days |

### System Touchpoints
- the in-house Audit & GRC platform (BUILD — fit-gap H11) audit findings and CAP tracking
- Vendor scorecard integration
- Corrective action tracking with deadline alerts

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Audit scope limitation**: Remote audits cannot verify physical controls adequately
- **Vendor deception**: Staged factory conditions during audit visits


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "documentation, distribution chain integrity" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "records, verification of serialization infrastructure, packaging line…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "QA issues audit report" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-444 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor Compliance & Anti-Counterfeit Analytics (PA-71.3).)
- operational: Quality Assurance Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

## W2568. Anti-Counterfeit Technology Evaluation & Deployment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Technology refresh cycle or emerging counterfeiting technique |
| **Frequency** | Annual evaluation; deployment as needed |
| **Volume** | 1–2 technology evaluations/year |
| **Owner** | IT Innovation Lead |
| **Participants** | IT Innovation, LP Manager, CIO, Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Innovation evaluates anti-counterfeit technologies: NFC tags (vs. QR codes), blockchain-based provenance tracking, AI-powered visual authentication (photo comparison), RFID for bulk scanning, invisible ink/taggant marking; assesses cost, accuracy, scalability, and vendor ecosystem compatibility | IT Innovation Lead | CIO | 2–3 weeks |
| 2 | LP Manager and Category Manager assess business case: counterfeit loss reduction potential, customer trust improvement, vendor adoption feasibility; prioritizes technologies by ROI and risk severity | LP Manager / Category Manager | COO | 1 week |
| 3 | IT deploys approved technologies: pilot at 10 stores for 1 category, measure authentication accuracy and operational impact, iterate, then rollout to full network; trains staff on new technology; integrates with existing authentication platform | IT Innovation / Training | CIO | 1–3 months |

### System Touchpoints
- Technology evaluation framework
- Pilot tracking with success metrics
- Integration architecture for new authentication methods

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Technology fragmentation**: Multiple authentication methods create operational complexity
- **Cost vs. benefit**: Advanced technologies (NFC, blockchain) expensive for low-margin categories


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-scan of "scanning, invisible ink/taggant marking" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "technologies by ROI and risk severity" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "IT deploys approved technologies" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-444 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor Compliance & Anti-Counterfeit Analytics (PA-71.3).)
- operational: IT Innovation Lead review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

## W2569. Counterfeit Financial Impact & Loss Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly financial reporting cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | 1 monthly report |
| **Owner** | LP Analyst |
| **Participants** | LP Analyst, LP Manager, Finance, COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LP Analyst compiles counterfeit financial impact: direct losses (counterfeit products sold = margin loss + customer restitution cost), investigation costs (staff time, lab testing, legal fees), prevention costs (serialization infrastructure, training, technology), recovery (vendor credits, insurance claims, legal settlements) | LP Analyst | LP Manager | 2–3 days |
| 2 | LP Manager reviews financial impact report: total cost of counterfeiting vs. prevention investment budget, cost per incident by category, ROI on anti-counterfeit program; compares against industry benchmarks (retail counterfeit loss typically 1–3% of category sales) | LP Manager | COO | 1–2 hours |
| 3 | Finance incorporates counterfeit loss into category P&L: adjusts gross margin for affected categories, tracks recovery income, reports to CFO for quarterly executive review; LP Manager proposes budget adjustments based on financial impact trend | Finance / LP Manager | CFO | 1–2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- LP financial tracking with GL coding
- Category P&L integration
- Executive dashboard with counterfeit loss KPI

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden costs**: Brand reputation damage and customer trust erosion difficult to quantify
- **Underestimation**: Only detected counterfeits are counted; true loss is higher


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "LP Analyst compiles counterfeit financial impact" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews financial impact report" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "counterfeit loss into category P&L" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-444 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor Compliance & Anti-Counterfeit Analytics (PA-71.3).)
- operational: LP Analyst review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

| **Volume** | 4 quarterly meetings; 6–12 intelligence exchanges/year |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
