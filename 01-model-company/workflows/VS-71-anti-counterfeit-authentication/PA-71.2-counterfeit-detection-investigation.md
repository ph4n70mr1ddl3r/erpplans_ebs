## W2558. In-Store Counterfeit Product Detection & Seizure

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Staff identifies suspected counterfeit during receiving, stocking, or customer interaction |
| **Frequency** | ~20–30 detections/month across all stores |
| **Volume** | ~20–30/month |
| **Owner** | Department Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Dept Supervisor, LP Officer, Store Manager, Receiving Clerk |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Staff member identifies suspected counterfeit: packaging quality deviation (printing, color, material), product weight/feel difference, missing security features (hologram, serialization QR), price discrepancy with market; escalates to Department Supervisor | Sales Associate / Receiving Clerk | Department Supervisor | 10–15 min |
| 2 | Supervisor conducts initial assessment: compares against authentic reference sample, scans QR code (if present), checks serial number in system; if suspected counterfeit confirmed: segregates product in LP quarantine area, documents with photos, logs in LP case system | Department Supervisor | Store Manager | 20–30 min |
| 3 | LP Officer conducts on-site investigation within 48 hours: collects evidence (product, packaging, labels), traces product origin (PO → GR → shelf), determines if breach occurred at vendor, DC, or store level; escalates confirmed cases to LP Manager for full investigation (W2559) | LP Officer | LP Manager | 2–4 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- LP case management system with photo evidence upload
- Product authentication scan via mobile app
- Inventory quarantine with LP hold status

### Pain Points / Risks
- **False positives**: Genuine products with manufacturing variations flagged incorrectly
- **Evidence contamination**: Staff handling may compromise forensic evidence


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "printing, color, material" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-scan of "Supervisor conducts initial assessment" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-escalate of "escalates confirmed cases to LP Manager for full investigation" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-233 (ensure anti-counterfeit program integrity.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

## W2559. Counterfeit Investigation & Evidence Collection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Confirmed counterfeit detection requiring full investigation |
| **Frequency** | ~5–10 full investigations/month |
| **Volume** | ~5–10/month |
| **Owner** | LP Manager |
| **Participants** | LP Manager, LP Officer, Legal, Procurement, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LP Manager initiates formal investigation: assigns case number, assigns LP Officer as lead investigator; determines scope (single product, vendor, store, category); sets investigation timeline (target: 30-day resolution) | LP Manager | COO | 30 min |
| 2 | LP Officer collects evidence: product samples, packaging, labeling; traces supply chain (vendor PO → shipment → GR → putaway → shelf); interviews relevant staff (receiving clerk, stock associate, department supervisor); reviews CCTV footage of receiving and stocking areas | LP Officer | LP Manager | 3–5 days |
| 3 | LP Officer compiles investigation report: root cause determination (vendor-supplied counterfeit, supply chain diversion, internal substitution), evidence chain of custody, financial impact assessment; LP Manager reviews and determines action: vendor notification, contract action, staff discipline, law enforcement referral | LP Officer / LP Manager | COO | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- LP case management with investigation workflow
- Supply chain traceability (PO → GR → shelf tracking)
- CCTV review system with transaction timestamp correlation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Investigation delays**: Staff interviews and CCTV review are time-consuming
- **Legal exposure**: Incorrect accusation of vendor or staff creates liability


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-assign of "assigns case number, assigns LP Officer as lead investigator" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews CCTV footage of receiving and stocking areas" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "counterfeit, supply chain diversion, internal substitution" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-443 (ensure controlled execution — Counterfeit Detection & Investigation (PA-71.2).)
- operational: LP Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## W2561. Law Enforcement & Regulatory Reporting for Counterfeiting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Large-scale counterfeiting case or organized counterfeiting ring identified |
| **Frequency** | 1–3 cases/year |
| **Volume** | 1–3/year |
| **Owner** | VP Legal |
| **Participants** | VP Legal, LP Manager, NBI/DTI, Law Enforcement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Legal evaluates case for law enforcement referral: severity (value, volume, organized ring indicator), strength of evidence, jurisdictional considerations; for qualifying cases (typically >PHP 200K value or organized operation): prepares formal complaint | VP Legal | CEO | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Legal files complaint with appropriate agency: NBI Intellectual Property Rights Division for organized counterfeiting, DTI for consumer protection violations, local PNP for criminal cases; provides evidence package, sworn statements, product samples | VP Legal | CEO | 3–5 days |
| 3 | Legal coordinates with law enforcement: provides testimony, expert witnesses, additional evidence as requested; tracks case progress; protects BuildRight interests and reputation throughout proceedings | VP Legal | CEO | 1–12 months |

### System Touchpoints
- LP case management with law enforcement referral tracking
- Document management for legal filings
- Evidence chain of custody tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Prosecution difficulty**: Philippine IP enforcement is slow and resource-intensive
- **Retaliation risk**: Organized counterfeiters may retaliate against stores or staff; mitigated by standard operating procedures and system-enforced validation rules


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "prepares formal complaint" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "counterfeiting, DTI for consumer protection violations, local PNP for criminal…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "tracks case progress" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-233 (ensure anti-counterfeit program integrity.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

| **Frequency** | ~10–20 cases/month |
| **Volume** | ~10–20/month |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Manager |
| **Participants** | Customer Service Mgr, LP Officer, Store Manager, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer reports suspected counterfeit: presents product and receipt; Customer Service verifies purchase history, checks product against authentication database; if confirmed or highly suspected counterfeit: immediately offers resolution | Customer Service Rep / LP Officer | Store Manager | 15–30 min |

## W2563. Online Marketplace Counterfeit Monitoring & Takedown

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly monitoring scan or vendor brand alert |
| **Frequency** | Weekly monitoring; 5–10 takedown requests/month |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 suspicious listings identified/month |
| **Owner** | LP Analyst |
| **Participants** | LP Analyst, LP Manager, Legal, Marketplace Platforms |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LP Analyst monitors online marketplaces (Shopee, Lazada, FB Marketplace, Carousell): searches for BuildRight-exclusive brands, private label products, high-risk categories (power tools, electrical); identifies suspicious listings based on pricing (significantly below SRP), product imagery (BuildRight packaging used without authorization), seller location patterns | LP Analyst | LP Manager | 4–6 hours/week |
| 2 | Analyst validates suspicious listings: purchases sample for authentication (if warranted), cross-references with BuildRight serial numbers, checks if seller is authorized dealer; for confirmed counterfeits: files takedown request with marketplace platform using brand registry tools | LP Analyst | LP Manager | 2–3 hours/case |
| 3 | Legal follows up on takedown requests: tracks platform response timeline (typically 3–7 days), escalates non-responsive platforms, coordinates with vendor brand protection teams for joint takedown actions; maintains marketplace enforcement log | Legal / LP Analyst | VP Legal | 1–2 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints
- Online marketplace monitoring tools
- Brand registry portals (Shopee Brand Protection, Lazada IP Protection)
- Takedown request tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Whack-a-mole**: Counterfeit sellers relist under new accounts after takedown
- **Platform cooperation**: Some platforms slow to act on takedown requests


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitors online marketplaces" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "checks if seller is authorized dealer" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "Legal follows up on takedown requests" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-233 (ensure anti-counterfeit program integrity.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate

## W2564. Counterfeit Trend Analysis & Threat Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly LP review cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | 1 monthly report |
| **Owner** | LP Analyst |
| **Participants** | LP Analyst, LP Manager, Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LP Analyst compiles counterfeit trend data: incidents by category (power tools highest risk), by brand, by source (vendor, DC, store, external), by geographic region, by detection method (QR scan, visual, customer report); calculates counterfeit rate per 10,000 units sold | LP Analyst | LP Manager | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Analyst performs threat assessment: identifies emerging counterfeiting patterns (new categories, improved fake quality), seasonal trends (holiday season spike), high-risk vendors or stores; produces risk heat map by category and region | LP Analyst | LP Manager | 1 day |
| 3 | LP Manager presents findings to Category Manager and COO: recommends prevention investments (additional serialization categories, enhanced inspection procedures), enforcement priorities (focus on highest-loss categories), vendor engagement actions; updates annual anti-counterfeit strategy | LP Manager | COO | 2–3 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- LP analytics dashboard with counterfeit incident tracking
- Trend analysis and heat map visualization
- Category risk scoring

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Underreporting**: Many counterfeits go undetected; actual counterfeit rate likely higher than reported
- **Resource constraint**: the 20-officer regional LP field corps (27-person department incl. investigations & analytics) across 200 stores limits investigation capacity
