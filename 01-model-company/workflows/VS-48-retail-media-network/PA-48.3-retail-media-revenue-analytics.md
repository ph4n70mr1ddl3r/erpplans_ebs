## W2014. Retail Media Revenue Recognition & Billing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Media campaign completed per booked schedule |
| **Frequency** | Monthly billing cycle |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 media invoices/month |
| **Owner** | AR Clerk |
| **Participants** | AR Clerk, Marketing Manager, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates media invoice upon campaign completion: (a) campaign details: vendor, placement, duration, stores, (b) billing: media rate × placement × duration, (c) less: co-op fund credit (if applicable), (d) less: credits for quality issues or downtime, (e) net amount due, (f) BIR-compliant invoice per DOC-002 | System | AR Clerk | Automated |
| 2 | AR Clerk reviews invoice: (a) verify campaign was executed per booking (compliance rate ≥ 90%), (b) verify pricing matches rate card, (c) apply any agreed discounts or credits, (d) submit invoice to vendor via portal or email | AR Clerk | Accounting Manager | 15–30 min/invoice |
| 3 | Revenue recognition per PFRS 15: (a) in-store/print: recognize revenue when campaign executed (materials installed, catalog published), (b) ecommerce: recognize as advertising services rendered (impressions delivered), (c) GL posting: debit AR, credit media revenue + output VAT | Accounting Manager | CFO | Automated after review |

### System Touchpoints
- Automated media invoicing (W2014.1)
- Revenue recognition per PFRS 15 (W2014.3)
- Integration with W8 (AR processing) for billing

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Recognition-timing risk**: media revenue recognized on booking rather than execution misstates PFRS 15 periods; mitigated by recognizing in-store/print on campaign execution and ecommerce as impressions serve, verified against ≥90% compliance (steps 2–3)
- **Billing-accuracy risk**: invoices deviate from the rate card or omit co-op credits, triggering vendor disputes; mitigated by AR verification of campaign execution, rate-card pricing, and credits before invoice submission (step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "generates media invoice upon campaign completion" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "AR Clerk reviews invoice" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "Revenue recognition per PFRS 15" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-387 (ensure controlled execution — Retail Media Revenue & Analytics (PA-48.3).)
- operational: AR Clerk review and approval gate

---

### Time Estimate
- 15–30 min/invoice; automated for high-volume campaigns
## W2015. Retail Media Vendor Collection & AR Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly collection cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 active media AR accounts |
| **Owner** | AR Clerk |
| **Participants** | AR Clerk, Account Manager, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly AR aging for media accounts: (a) separate aging from merchandise AP vendors (media vendors may owe BuildRight, not the reverse), (b) terms: Net 30 standard; Net 15 for new vendors, (c) some vendors may offset media costs against merchandise rebates per W27 | AR Clerk | Accounting Manager | Per monthly cycle |
| 2 | Collection follow-up: (a) 30+ days: Account Manager contacts vendor marketing department, (b) 60+ days: escalate to VP Merchandising for leverage (BuildRight is also a customer of this vendor for merchandise), (c) 90+ days: offset against merchandise AP per mutual agreement | AR Clerk / Account Manager | Accounting Manager | Per aging |
| 3 | Vendor credit management: (a) set credit limit per vendor for media spend based on merchandise relationship value, (b) prepayment required for vendors with no existing merchandise relationship | Credit Analyst | Accounting Manager | Per vendor |

### System Touchpoints
- Media AR aging report (W2015.1)
- AP/AR offset capability (W2015.2)
- Integration with W108 (collection escalation)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Netting-confusion risk**: media AR and merchandise AP with the same vendor are offset incorrectly, understating both balances; mitigated by separate media AR aging with credit limits set on the merchandise relationship value (steps 1, 3)
- **Leverage-overuse risk**: aggressive collections via the merchandise relationship poison the trade relationship; mitigated by tiered escalation (Account Manager → VP Merchandising) and prepayment terms for vendors with no merchandise relationship (steps 2–3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Monthly AR aging for media accounts" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-escalate of "escalate to VP Merchandising for leverage" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-193 (ensure retail-media billing & placement delivery.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

---

### Time Estimate
- Per monthly collection cycle
## W2016. Retail Media Network Profitability Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly profitability review |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | Consolidated media P&L |
| **Owner** | CFO |
| **Participants** | CFO, CMO, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accounting Manager compiles media P&L: (a) revenue: media sales (in-store + ecommerce + print), (b) cost of media: (i) production costs (printing, creative), (ii) installation labor (store staff time), (iii) digital platform costs (ad serving technology), (iv) account management labor, (c) gross margin target: 50–70% (media is high-margin), (d) net media contribution after allocated overhead | Accounting Manager | CFO | 1 day/quarter |
| 2 | CFO and CMO review profitability: (a) by media type: which channels are most profitable?, (b) by vendor: which vendors generate most media revenue?, (c) by store: which stores have highest media fill rate?, (d) optimization: shift resources toward highest-margin media types | CFO / CMO | COO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Media P&L dashboard (W2016.1)
- Integration with W35 (FP&A reporting) for consolidated view

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden-cost risk**: production costs and store installation labor under-allocated make media look more profitable than it is; mitigated by full-cost media P&L compilation including production, installation labor, and platform costs (step 1)
- **Cross-subsidy risk**: one profitable channel masks money-losing placements or stores; mitigated by profitability review sliced by media type, vendor, and store with action on losers (step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "Accounting Manager compiles media P&L" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "CFO and CMO review profitability" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-387 (ensure controlled execution — Retail Media Revenue & Analytics (PA-48.3).)
- operational: CFO review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 1 day/quarter
## W2017. Retail Media Sales Lift Attribution & Vendor ROI Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Campaign completion; quarterly attribution analysis |
| **Frequency** | Per campaign; quarterly aggregated analysis |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 campaigns/quarter |
| **Owner** | Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Manager, Digital Marketing Manager, Category Manager, Retail Media Operations Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales lift calculation per campaign: (a) compare product sales during campaign vs. pre-campaign baseline (4-week average), (b) control group: compare sales at stores with campaign vs. stores without, (c) ecommerce: attributed revenue from ad clicks (last-click attribution), (d) calculate incremental sales: sales lift − organic sales growth | Marketing Manager | CMO | 1–2 days/quarter |
| 2 | Vendor ROI: (a) vendor investment: total media spend on BuildRight campaign, (b) vendor return: incremental gross margin from sales lift (using vendor's margin, not BuildRight's), (c) ROI = vendor return ÷ vendor investment, (d) target: ROI ≥ 5x for vendor (industry benchmark for retail media) | Marketing Manager | CMO | 1 day/quarter |
| 3 | Present ROI analysis to vendor: (a) demonstrate value of BuildRight media investment, (b) use data to justify rate increases or upsell additional placements, (c) share anonymized industry benchmarks for context | Account Manager | CMO | Per vendor |

### System Touchpoints
- Sales lift analytics with control group comparison (W2017.1)
- Vendor ROI calculation module (W2017.2)
- Integration with W35 (analytics) and W28 (BI) for data

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-attribution risk**: claiming total sales lift without controls inflates vendor ROI and future rate expectations; mitigated by the 4-week pre-campaign baseline plus control-store comparison methodology (step 1)
- **Value-invisibility risk**: vendors unable to see ROI decline to rebook; mitigated by ROI presentation using the vendor's own margin, with anonymized benchmarks to justify rate increases (steps 2–3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-calculate of "calculate incremental sales" (replaces manual Step 1).

### Controls
- CTL-193 (ensure retail-media billing & placement delivery.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

---

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 days/quarter for analysis
## W2018. Retail Media Inventory Yield & Utilization Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly inventory utilization review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~3,050 media positions |
| **Owner** | Marketing Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Manager, CMO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly utilization report: (a) fill rate: % of available inventory sold by media type, (b) revenue per placement: average revenue by media type, (c) unsold inventory: which placements and periods are unsold?, (d) revenue per store: identify stores with low media revenue (may need more sales effort or lower pricing) | Marketing Manager | CMO | 1 day/month |
| 2 | Optimization actions: (a) high fill rate (>80%): consider rate increase, (b) low fill rate (<50%): promotional pricing, package deals, or internal use, (c) seasonal patterns: pre-sell high-demand periods at premium rates, (d) new inventory: introduce new placement types based on vendor demand | Marketing Manager | CMO | Ongoing |
| 3 | Annual: comprehensive yield optimization review: (a) total media revenue per sqm of selling area, (b) benchmark against international retail media networks, (c) set Year 2 revenue target and inventory plan | Marketing Manager / CMO | COO | 2–3 days/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Media utilization dashboard with fill rate tracking (W2018.1)
- Integration with W35 (management reporting) for KPI tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Idle-inventory risk**: unsold positions across ~3,050 media placements earn nothing; mitigated by monthly fill-rate monitoring with rate increases above 80% fill and promotional packaging below 50% (step 2)
- **Cannibalization risk**: internal-use placement fills sellable inventory at peak periods; mitigated by tracking internal-use allocation and pre-selling high-demand seasonal periods (steps 1–2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-report of "Monthly utilization report" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-387 (ensure controlled execution — Retail Media Revenue & Analytics (PA-48.3).)
- operational: Marketing Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate

| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | Cross-entity media transactions |
| **Owner** | Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Accounting Manager, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accounting Manager allocates media revenue across entities: (a) BuildRight Depot, Inc.: in-store media revenue at stores, (b) BuildRight Digital Commerce, Inc.: ecommerce media revenue, (c) revenue shared per IC agreement: Digital Commerce retains media revenue as incremental income (not subject to per-order fulfillment fee) | Accounting Manager | CFO | 1 day/month |

- **Entity mis-statement risk**: media revenue booked to the wrong entity (BuildRight Depot in-store vs. Digital Commerce ecommerce) misstates statutory books across 5 entities; mitigated by allocation rules tied to the originating entity with costs following the revenue (step 1)
- **Elimination-error risk**: intercompany media transactions survive consolidation and overstate group revenue; mitigated by IC elimination at settlement per W9A before consolidation (step 2)

## W2021. Retail Media Network Audit & Compliance Review

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual audit; quarterly compliance review |
| **Frequency** | Annual external audit; quarterly internal review |
| **Volume** | All media transactions |
| **Owner** | Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Accounting Manager, Internal Auditor, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quarterly: Internal Auditor reviews media compliance: (a) rate card compliance: are vendors charged per approved rates?, (b) placement verification: were booked placements actually installed/active?, (c) vendor credit accuracy: are quality issue credits applied correctly?, (d) co-op fund compliance: are co-op reimbursements per agreement terms? | Internal Auditor | CFO | 2–3 days/quarter |
| 2 | Annual: External Auditor reviews media revenue: (a) revenue recognition per PFRS 15, (b) sample campaigns: verify execution and invoicing accuracy, (c) IC allocation accuracy | External Auditor | CFO | Per annual audit |
| 3 | Annual: BIR compliance review: (a) media revenue is subject to 12% output VAT, (b) media services may be subject to percentage tax for some entities (verify per entity registration), (c) ensure all media revenue properly declared in VAT returns | Tax Accountant | CFO | 1 day/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Media transaction audit trail (W2021.1)
- Integration with W21 (internal audit) for audit coverage

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rate-integrity risk**: off-rate-card deals to favored vendors create revenue leakage and favoritism exposure; mitigated by the quarterly rate-card compliance audit sampling live campaigns (step 1)
- **Tax-misclassification risk**: media revenue given the wrong VAT/percentage-tax treatment per entity registration; mitigated by the annual BIR compliance review verifying 12% output VAT and entity-specific tax treatment (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews media compliance" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "verify execution and invoicing accuracy" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-387 (ensure controlled execution — Retail Media Revenue & Analytics (PA-48.3).)
- operational: Accounting Manager review and approval gate

---

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 days/quarter internal; per external audit cycle
