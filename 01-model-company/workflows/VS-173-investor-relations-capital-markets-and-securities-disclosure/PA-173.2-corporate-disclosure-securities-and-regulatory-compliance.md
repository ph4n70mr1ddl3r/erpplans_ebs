## W5017. Corporate Disclosure Controls, Materiality Assessment & Selective-Disclosure / Fair-Disclosure Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Any potential external statement/disclosure; program setup; regulatory change (RA 8799/SEC rules) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + per-disclosure |
| **Volume** | ~every investor-facing statement × disclosure events |
| **Owner** | VP Investor Relations / VP Legal & Compliance (VS-100) / CFO |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100), Compliance (VS-22), CFO/CEO, Disclosure Committee, Internal Audit (VS-21), Corporate Secretary Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Operate the disclosure-controls framework: the disclosure committee, the material-information register, the who-can-speak-to-investors policy, the pre-clearance of external statements (earnings W5011, events W5012, ESG W5016), and the evidence/audit trail that every disclosure was reviewed and approved | VP IR / Legal / Compliance | CFO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 2 | Run materiality assessment: for any development (operational, financial, legal, ESG, transaction), assess materiality to investors/lenders under SEC/securities standards, decide disclose/withhold, and time the disclosure (links W5019 ad-hoc) | VP IR / Legal / CFO | Disclosure Committee | Per event |
| 3 | Enforce fair-disclosure (Reg-FD-equivalent / RA 8799): ensure material info is disclosed publicly and simultaneously (no selective disclosure to favored analysts/investors), manage the public-release channel (W5014), and train spokespeople | VP IR / Legal / Compliance | CFO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Disclosure-committee workflow; material-information register; IR portal/publish (W5014); legal (VS-100); compliance (VS-22); audit trail (VS-88/VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Selective-disclosure risk**: Material info given to one party before public release (RA 8799/Reg FD); mitigated by simultaneous-public-release rule & pre-clearance
- **Materiality-miss risk**: Failing to disclose a material event on time (W5019); mitigated by materiality assessment & escalation
- **Uncontrolled-statement risk**: Spokesperson deviating from approved script; mitigated by pre-clearance & training

### Automation Opportunity
- Disclosure-checklist/pre-clearance workflow; material-info register; simultaneous-publish orchestration; spokespeople registry

### Controls
- CTL-117 (ensure disclosure & insider-trading compliance.)
- CTL-750: disclosure committee; material-info register; pre-clearance; simultaneous public release; spokespeople trained

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous; ~per-disclosure hours/days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal/RA 8799), VS-22 (compliance), VS-21 (audit assurance), W5011/W5012/W5016 (statements), W5014 (publish), W5019 (ad-hoc), W5022 (insider trading), W5024 (governance)

| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~5–15 material events per year + rumor incidents |
| **Owner** | VP Investor Relations / VP Legal & Compliance (VS-100) / CFO |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100), Compliance (VS-22), CFO/CEO, Crisis Comms (VS-14), Corporate Security (VS-159), HSE (VS-24), Strategy (VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## W5022. Insider List, Trading-Window & Securities-Trading Compliance (RA 8799 SRC)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program setup; insider-list change; trading-window open/close; trade request; closed period |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + per-window + per-trade |
| **Volume** | ~30–50 insiders in the population × trades × closed/open windows |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance (VS-100) / Compliance (VS-22) / VP Investor Relations |
| **Participants** | Compliance (VS-22), Legal (VS-100), HR (VS-19), Finance (VS-17/VS-18), Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the insider list & policy: the insiders register (directors/officers/key employees with material non-public info — links VS-36 board, VS-19 HR), the securities-trading policy, blackout/closed periods (pre-earnings), and pre-clearance of trades | Compliance / Legal / VP IR | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 2 | Operate the trading window & pre-clearance: open/close the window around earnings (links W5011) and material events (W5019), pre-clear & log every insider trade, and enforce the ban on trading on material non-public info (RA 8799 Securities Regulation Code — insider trading is a criminal offense) | Compliance / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per window/trade |
| 3 | Report & monitor: insider-trade reporting/filing (links VS-100, VS-166), monitor for suspicious/Rule-10b5-equivalent patterns, and audit insider-list/trade logs (VS-21); coordinate investigations (VS-119/VS-86) | Compliance / Internal Audit / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Insider-list/trading platform; HR (VS-19); board/governance (VS-36); disclosure-controls (W5017); legal (VS-100); audit (VS-21); ethics/AML (VS-119/VS-86)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Insider-trading risk**: Insider trading on material non-public info (RA 8799 — civil/criminal liability); mitigated by window, pre-clearance & insider list
- **Closed-period-breach risk**: Trading during blackout; mitigated by hard window controls
- **Reporting-failure risk**: Late/missing insider-trade filing; mitigated by filing calendar

### Automation Opportunity
- Insider-list/trading-window platform; pre-clearance workflow; trade-reporting automation; anomaly detection

### Controls
- CTL-117 (ensure disclosure & insider-trading compliance.)
- CTL-750: insider list maintained; window/pre-clearance enforced; RA 8799 insider-trading ban; trades reported/audited

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous; ~per-trade pre-clearance minutes

### Cross-references
