## W2510. Store Emergency Closure Decision & Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | PAGASA Signal No. 3+ in store area, local government evacuation order, or facility safety assessment |
| **Frequency** | 2–5 closures per store per year; region-wide events affect 20–60 stores simultaneously |
| **Volume** | Up to 200 stores for nationwide super typhoons |
| **Owner** | Regional Manager |
| **Participants** | Regional Manager, Store Manager, VP Store Operations, HR, Security |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Regional Manager assesses conditions per decision matrix: PAGASA signal, local government advisory, road accessibility, staff safety, facility integrity; authorizes closure with VP Store Operations approval for multi-store events | Regional Manager | VP Store Operations | 30–60 min |
| 2 | Store Manager executes closure: announce to customers via PA system, complete current transactions, secure cash in safe, power down non-essential systems, engage alarm, lock facility, escort remaining customers out safely | Store Manager | Regional Manager | 1–2 hours |
| 3 | System automatically updates store status to "Temporarily Closed" on ecommerce, BOPIS, Google Business, and customer-facing channels; pauses all pending BOPIS orders with automated customer notification | System / Store Ops | VP Store Operations | 15–30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Store status management with automated channel propagation
- POS Z-report for mid-day closure
- Ecommerce/BOPIS order suspension per store
- Google Business Profile API for hours update
- **Custody & precedence** (per the [Event Custody & Precedence Register](../event-custody-and-precedence-register.md), E-02): this workflow is the closure-*decision* canon (Regional Manager decides; VP Store Operations approves multi-store events; COO chain-wide); closure/reopening *mechanics* and reporting run VS-26 W850, protocol context VS-26 W848; frequency canon: 2–5/store/yr typhoon-signal closures vs W850's 5–10/store/yr all-cause closures

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Liability**: Customer or employee injury during closure process
- **Revenue loss**: PHP 840K+ per store per day of closure (avg PHP 25M/month ÷ 30)


### Automation Opportunity
- System rule-based authorization of "authorizes closure with VP Store Operations approval for multi-store events" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-update of store status to "Temporarily Closed" on the store locator (replaces manual Step 3 posting).

### Controls
- CTL-437 (ensure controlled execution — Active Disaster Response & Emergency Operations (PA-69.2).)
- operational: Regional Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 hours per store closure

## W2511. DC Emergency Shutdown & Asset Protection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | PAGASA Signal No. 3+ in DC area or facility flooding risk assessment |
| **Frequency** | 2–4 shutdowns per DC per year |
| **Volume** | 4 DCs; typically 1–2 affected per event |
| **Owner** | DC Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | DC Ops Manager, Warehouse Supervisor, Safety Officer, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DC Ops Manager initiates emergency shutdown: halt all inbound/outbound operations, complete in-process picking/packing or secure at last checkpoint, stage all MHE (forklifts, reach trucks) at high ground, secure dock doors with flood barriers | DC Ops Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2–3 hours |
| 2 | Warehouse Supervisors execute critical asset protection: move high-value inventory (power tools, appliances) above flood line, seal chemical storage areas, secure server room with waterproof covers, activate UPS for graceful system shutdown | Warehouse Supervisor / IT | DC Ops Manager | 2–3 hours |
| 3 | Safety Officer confirms all personnel evacuated; DC Ops Manager reports final status to Operations Center; maintains remote monitoring via security cameras where possible | Safety Officer / DC Ops Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- WMS emergency shutdown mode with transaction freeze
- Warehouse inventory location tracking for flood-risk assessment
- Security camera remote access

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inventory loss**: Flooding at DC3 (Laguna — low-lying area) could damage PHP 500M+ in inventory
- **Recovery time**: DC may be non-operational for 3–7 days post-flooding for cleanup and stock verification


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "checkpoint, stage all MHE" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-capture of "Safety Officer confirms all personnel evacuated" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-437 (ensure controlled execution — Active Disaster Response & Emergency Operations (PA-69.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 hours per DC shutdown

## W2512. Emergency Merchandise Rapid Replenishment Dispatch

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-typhoon needs assessment or government/NGO emergency order |
| **Frequency** | 6–10 activations per year |
| **Volume** | 10–50 emergency deliveries per activation |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | Logistics Mgr, DC Ops, Procurement, Store Ops, Government/NGO Liaison |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Supply Chain receives post-disaster needs assessment from affected stores and regional government; prioritizes dispatch: medical facility orders first, then essential supply stores, then general replenishment | Supply Planning / Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2–4 hours |
| 2 | DC Ops picks emergency orders with dedicated priority wave; Logistics Manager dispatches available trucks (owned fleet first, then 3PL surge); coordinates road access with local government | DC Ops / Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 4–8 hours |
| 3 | Store Ops receives emergency delivery with expedited GR process (visual count only, detailed matching deferred 48 hrs); updates inventory; begins emergency sales operations | Store Manager / Receiving Clerk | Regional Manager | 2–3 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Emergency PO and transfer order creation with fast-track approval
- WMS priority wave management

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Security**: Emergency deliveries in disaster zones face looting risk


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-dispatch of "Supply Chain receives post-disaster needs assessment from affected stores" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "Logistics Manager dispatches available trucks" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "count only, detailed matching deferred 48 hrs" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-437 (ensure controlled execution — Active Disaster Response & Emergency Operations (PA-69.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate

## W2513. Real-Time Disaster Operations Center Activation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Multi-store/multi-DC typhoon impact event (Signal No. 3+ across 10+ stores) |
| **Frequency** | 3–5 activations per year |
| **Volume** | 1 operations center per event |
| **Owner** | COO |
| **Participants** | COO, VP Store Operations, VP Supply Chain, CIO, CHRO, CFO, CMO, VP Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | COO activates Disaster Operations Center at HQ (Davao) or virtual: establishes command structure, communication channels, situation reporting cadence (every 2 hours during active event); assigns functional leads | COO | CEO | 1 hour |
| 2 | Functional leads provide situation reports: Store Ops (store status, damage, employee safety), Supply Chain (DC status, inventory, logistics), IT (system status, communication channels), HR (employee welfare), Finance (financial exposure), Legal (regulatory/liability) | Functional VPs | COO | Ongoing (every 2 hours) |
| 3 | COO makes operational decisions: store closure/reopening, emergency procurement authorization (up to PHP 200M), staff redeployment, customer communication, insurance notification; documents all decisions for post-event review | COO | CEO | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Real-time dashboard: store/DC status, employee check-in, inventory levels, financial exposure
- Emergency communication platform with redundancy (internet + SMS + radio)
- Insurance claim notification system

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Information gaps**: Communication blackouts in severely affected areas delay damage assessment
- **Decision fatigue**: Extended events (48+ hours) strain leadership capacity


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "Center at HQ" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "Functional leads provide situation reports" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "documents all decisions for post-event review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-437 (ensure controlled execution — Active Disaster Response & Emergency Operations (PA-69.2).)
- operational: COO review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- Continuous during active event (typically 24–72 hours)

## W2514. Emergency Generator & Power Contingency Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Commercial power outage at store or DC during/after typhoon |
| **Frequency** | 15–30 power outage activations per year across network |
| **Volume** | 10–50 simultaneous locations per major typhoon |
| **Owner** | Maintenance/Utility Staff |
| **Participants** | Maintenance Staff, Store Manager, DC Ops Manager, Facilities |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintenance activates backup generator: verifies fuel level (minimum 8-hour runtime), starts generator, transfers load to backup, prioritizes circuits (POS, security, lighting, refrigeration for DC); logs activation time | Maintenance / Utility Staff | Store Manager / DC Ops Manager | 15–30 min |
| 2 | Store Manager confirms POS operation in offline mode; switches to cash-first tender policy if connectivity lost; monitors generator fuel consumption and arranges refueling logistics | Store Manager | Regional Manager | Ongoing |
| 3 | Facilities tracks generator runtime, fuel consumption, and maintenance status across affected locations; coordinates fuel resupply with suppliers; escalates if runtime < 4 hours remaining | Facilities Manager | COO | Ongoing (every 4 hours) |

### System Touchpoints
- IoT generator monitoring (fuel level, runtime, load status)
- POS offline mode activation
- Facilities management system for generator fleet tracking
- **Custody & precedence** (per the [Event Custody & Precedence Register](../event-custody-and-precedence-register.md), E-04): this workflow owns DC-level power contingency and typhoon-specific fuel logistics; store-level generator operations (any cause) run VS-24 W1223, the store canon

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fuel scarcity**: Regional fuel shortages post-typhoon limit generator runtime
- **Generator failure**: Aging units at older stores may fail during extended runs


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs activation time" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-monitor of "monitors generator fuel consumption and arranges refueling logistics" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-escalate of "escalates if runtime < 4 hours remaining" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-437 (ensure controlled execution — Active Disaster Response & Emergency Operations (PA-69.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 30 min activation; ongoing monitoring every 4 hours during outage

## W2515. Disaster-Affected Employee Welfare & Deployment Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-typhoon employee safety check process |
| **Frequency** | 3–5 events per year requiring welfare tracking |
| **Volume** | 50–2,000 employees per event |
| **Owner** | HR Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | HR Ops Mgr, Store Managers, DC Ops, CHRO, Employee Relations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR Ops activates welfare check: automated SMS/IVR check-in to all employees in affected region; tracks response status (safe, affected, unreachable); Store/DC Managers supplement with direct calls to non-responders within 12 hours | HR Operations Manager | CHRO | 12–24 hours |
| 2 | For affected employees: HR assesses needs (housing, medical, financial); activates emergency salary advance (up to PHP 20,000 tax-free); coordinates relief goods distribution from store inventory per company policy | HR Ops / Employee Relations | CHRO | 24–48 hours |
| 3 | HR deploys replacement staff from unaffected stores to maintain minimum operations at reopened locations; tracks overtime and hazard pay eligibility per DOLE advisory | HR Operations Manager | COO | 1–3 days |

### System Touchpoints
- HR emergency notification with two-way status tracking
- Payroll emergency advance module
- Workforce scheduling with disaster redeployment

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Employee trauma**: Psychological impact on staff in severely affected areas; requires counseling support
- **Staffing gaps**: Inability to reopen stores promptly due to staff displacement


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "HR Ops activates welfare check" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System workflow orchestration of "coordinates relief goods distribution from store inventory per company policy" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "tracks overtime and hazard pay eligibility per DOLE advisory" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-232 (ensure typhoon-response protocol execution.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- Initial welfare check: 12–24 hours; ongoing support: 1–4 weeks

## W2517. Active Typhoon Customer Safety & In-Store Emergency Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Sudden weather deterioration while store is open with customers present |
| **Frequency** | 2–5 activations per store per year |
| **Volume** | Up to 200 stores; varies by typhoon track |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, All Staff, Customers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store Manager monitors weather conditions; at sudden deterioration: activates customer safety protocol via PA announcement; directs customers away from outdoor lumber yard, loading dock, and exterior areas | Store Manager | Store Manager | Immediate |
| 2 | If shelter-in-place required: guide customers to interior secure area (away from large windows, overhead fixtures); provide water, first aid; maintain calm with regular PA updates every 15 minutes | All Staff | Store Manager | Duration of event |
| 3 | When safe to do so: gradually resume operations or execute orderly closure (W2510); document any customer incidents for insurance and liability purposes | Store Manager | Regional Manager | 1–2 hours post-event |

### System Touchpoints
- PA system with pre-recorded emergency announcements
- First aid station inventory tracking
- Incident reporting module for customer injury documentation
- **Custody & precedence** (per the [Event Custody & Precedence Register](../event-custody-and-precedence-register.md), E-03): the Store Manager holds site-level incident command at all times; the closure decision (Signal 3+/LGU order) follows W2510; evacuation protocol upkeep per VS-24 W695

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Customer panic**: Large crowds during sudden severe weather create stampede risk
- **Liability exposure**: Customer injury on premises during weather emergency


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitors weather conditions" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-update of "updates every 15 minutes" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document any customer incidents for insurance and liability purposes" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-437 (ensure controlled execution — Active Disaster Response & Emergency Operations (PA-69.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- Duration of event (typically 1–6 hours for shelter-in-place scenarios)
