## W2518. Post-Typhoon Store Damage Assessment & Rapid Reopening

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weather conditions improve and local government lifts evacuation order |
| **Frequency** | 2–5 per store per year |
| **Volume** | Up to 200 stores per major event; typically 20–60 stores |
| **Owner** | Regional Manager |
| **Participants** | Regional Manager, Store Manager, Facilities, Safety, LP, Insurance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Regional Manager authorizes Store Manager to conduct initial assessment: structural integrity (roof, walls, doors), electrical safety, water intrusion, inventory damage, security status; documents with photos/video via mobile app | Store Manager | Regional Manager | 2–3 hours |
| 2 | Facilities and Safety conduct secondary assessment for: electrical system safety check, mold/water damage remediation needs, structural engineering review for severe damage, fire system verification | Facilities / Safety Officer | COO | 4–8 hours |
| 3 | Based on assessment: classify store as Green (safe to open), Yellow (partial operations), Red (closed for repairs); Green/Yellow stores execute rapid reopening: clean-up, inventory count, system boot, cash float load, resume operations within 24 hours where possible | Store Manager / Regional Manager | VP Store Operations | 12–24 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile damage assessment app with photo documentation
- Store status dashboard for regional/national visibility
- Insurance documentation module with photo evidence upload
- **Custody & precedence** (per the [Event Custody & Precedence Register](../event-custody-and-precedence-register.md), E-06): this workflow is the single-store rapid damage-triage & reopening canon; multi-store/major-event phased reopening governance runs VS-26 W1552 (chained from W848 Step 8); reopening mechanics per VS-26 W850

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Secondary damage**: Water intrusion causing mold growth if not addressed within 48 hours
- **Safety liability**: Premature reopening before electrical safety verification


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "documents with photos/video via mobile app" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review for severe damage, fire system verification" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "count, system boot, cash float load, resume operations within 24 hours where…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-438 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Disaster Recovery & Community Support (PA-69.3).)
- operational: Regional Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 12–24 hours from assessment start to reopening for Green stores; 2–14 days for Yellow/Red

## W2519. Post-Disaster Inventory Loss Quantification & Insurance Claim

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store/DC damage assessment reveals inventory loss |
| **Frequency** | 2–5 significant claims per year |
| **Volume** | PHP 5M–200M per event; typically 1–10 locations per claim |
| **Owner** | Finance Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance Mgr, Store/DC Ops, LP, Insurance Broker, External Adjuster |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store/DC Ops conducts emergency inventory count: categorizes stock as undamaged, damaged-but-salvageable, total loss; records per SKU with quantity and WAC value; documents with photos/video | Store/DC Ops / LP | Regional Manager | 1–3 days |
| 2 | Finance compiles loss claim: extracts pre-disaster inventory valuation from ERP, compares with post-disaster count, calculates loss at WAC and SRP; prepares insurance claim with adjuster coordination | Finance Manager / Insurance Broker | CFO | 3–5 days |
| 3 | System processes inventory write-off: journals loss to insurance claim receivable account, removes damaged stock from perpetual inventory, updates aging and NRV for salvageable items; tracks claim through settlement | Finance / Accounting | CFO | 1–2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory valuation report by location
- Emergency physical count module with damage categorization
- Insurance claim tracking with GL integration
- Inventory write-off workflow with approval tiers
- **Custody & precedence** (per the [Event Custody & Precedence Register](../event-custody-and-precedence-register.md), E-07): this workflow quantifies the loss and initiates the claim; claim *processing and settlement* always run the VS-26 PA-26.3 canon (W876), triggered per W848 Step 7

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Claim underpayment**: Insurance adjuster disputes loss valuation; mitigated by thorough photo documentation and pre-disaster inventory records
- **ERP data loss**: If on-premise server damaged, backup recovery needed before loss quantification


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "records per SKU with quantity and WAC value" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "count, calculates loss at WAC and SRP" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "tracks claim through settlement" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-438 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Disaster Recovery & Community Support (PA-69.3).)
- operational: thorough photo documentation and pre-disaster inventory records

### Time Estimate
- 5–10 business days from event to claim submission; 30–90 days to settlement

## W2520. Community Building Materials Donation & Relief Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Major disaster event with significant community impact in store area |
| **Frequency** | 2–4 activations per year |
| **Volume** | PHP 5M–20M in donated materials per event |
| **Owner** | CSR Manager / Store Manager |
| **Participants** | CSR Mgr, Store Manager, LGU, DSWD/Red Cross, Logistics, Finance, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CSR/Store Manager identifies community needs in coordination with LGU and DSWD: temporary shelter materials (tarps, plywood, nails), water containers, tools for debris clearing; proposes donation list with estimated value | CSR Manager / Store Manager | CEO | 1–2 days |
| 2 | CEO approves donation budget; Finance processes zero-price goods issue from inventory with donation GL coding; Legal ensures BIR-deductible donation documentation (certification from DSWD/LGU, official receipts) | Finance / Legal | CEO | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Logistics arranges delivery to LGU distribution points or directly to affected communities; Store Manager coordinates volunteer staff for on-site assistance; PR team documents for corporate communications | Logistics / Store Manager / Marketing | CMO | 2–3 days |

- Donation GL coding for BIR tax deduction documentation
- Logistics delivery scheduling for relief operations

- **Inventory distortion**: Large donations deplete stock for regular customers; requires emergency replenishment coordination


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "CEO approves donation budget" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "Logistics arranges delivery to LGU distribution points or directly to affected…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-438 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Disaster Recovery & Community Support (PA-69.3).)

## W2521. Post-Disaster Emergency Product Pricing & Availability Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-disaster reopening or DTI price monitoring activation |
| **Frequency** | 6–10 activations per year |
| **Volume** | 500–2,000 SKUs across 20–100 stores |
| **Owner** | Pricing Manager |
| **Participants** | Pricing Mgr, Legal, Merchandising, Store Ops, DTI Liaison |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing Manager reviews DTI price freeze status for disaster-affected areas; confirms which SKUs remain under price control and which have been released; updates system pricing rules accordingly | Pricing Manager | VP Merchandising | 2–4 hours |
| 2 | Merchandising manages availability communication: updates ecommerce stock levels (may show limited availability), coordinates with Marketing on "rebuilding supplies available" messaging, ensures BOPIS availability reflects actual post-disaster stock | Merchandising / Marketing | CMO | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Store Ops monitors for stockouts on emergency items; escalates to Supply Chain for emergency replenishment; implements purchase limits per customer on scarce items (e.g., max 10 plywood sheets) to prevent hoarding | Store Manager / Supply Planning | VP Supply Chain | Ongoing (1–2 weeks post-event) |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing engine with DTI price freeze compliance tracking
- Ecommerce inventory availability with disaster override
- POS purchase limit enforcement per SKU

### Pain Points / Risks
- **DTI enforcement**: Aggressive monitoring during disasters; violations result in public naming and fines
- **Customer frustration**: Purchase limits anger contractors buying for multiple projects


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews DTI price freeze status for disaster-affected areas" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-post of "Merchandising manages availability communication" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-escalate of "escalates to Supply Chain for emergency replenishment" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-438 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Disaster Recovery & Community Support (PA-69.3).)
- operational: Pricing Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 hours for initial setup; ongoing monitoring for 1–2 weeks

## W2522. Typhoon Event After-Action Review & Lessons Learned

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-disaster recovery stabilized (typically 2–4 weeks after major event) |
| **Frequency** | After every significant typhoon event (3–5 per year) |
| **Volume** | 1 AAR per major event |
| **Owner** | COO |
| **Participants** | All functional VPs, Regional Managers, Store/DC Managers, HR, Safety |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | COO compiles event timeline: PAGASA warnings vs. company response, store/DC closure and reopening times, inventory loss, employee safety incidents, customer complaints, financial impact, supply chain disruption duration | COO | COO | 3–5 days |
| 2 | Cross-functional review meeting: each VP presents functional performance; identifies gaps in preparedness, response, and recovery; documents lessons learned and action items with owners and deadlines | All VPs | COO | 4–8 hours |
| 3 | COO updates typhoon preparedness protocols, checklists, and training materials based on lessons learned; HR incorporates into annual training; Facilities updates capital improvement plan for identified structural weaknesses | COO / HR / Facilities | COO | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Event documentation repository
- Action item tracking with accountability
- Training content management system for protocol updates

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lessons not implemented**: Without accountability tracking, AAR improvements may not be executed before next typhoon season
- **AAR fatigue**: Multiple typhoons per season can overwhelm improvement capacity


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-compile of "compiles event timeline" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documents lessons learned and action items with owners and deadlines" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "checklists, and training materials based on lessons learned" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-438 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Disaster Recovery & Community Support (PA-69.3).)
- operational: COO review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per major event AAR cycle

## W2523. Disaster Recovery Financial Impact Reporting & Budget Rebalancing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-disaster financial impact exceeds PHP 10M or affects >10 stores |
| **Frequency** | 2–4 significant events per year |
| **Volume** | 1 financial impact report per event |
| **Owner** | FP&A Manager |
| **Participants** | FP&A Mgr, Finance, Treasury, Insurance, COO, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A compiles disaster financial impact: revenue loss (closed days × daily revenue per store), inventory write-offs, facility repair costs, emergency procurement premiums, employee assistance costs, donation costs, insurance recoveries | FP&A Manager | CFO | 3–5 days |
| 2 | CFO reviews impact vs. insurance coverage and retained risk; updates annual P&L forecast; proposes budget reallocation if net impact > PHP 50M (defer non-critical capex, adjust marketing spend, reduce discretionary expenses) | CFO | CEO / Board | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Treasury manages cash flow impact: coordinates with insurance for advance payments, accelerates AR collections in unaffected regions, manages vendor payment prioritization, adjusts capital expenditure timing | Treasury Manager | CFO | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Financial reporting with disaster cost center coding
- Budget management with reallocation workflow
- Treasury cash flow forecasting with disaster scenario modeling

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cascading impact**: Multiple typhoons in one season can exceed insurance coverage and reserve budget
- **Audit complexity**: Disaster-related transactions require detailed documentation for BIR and external audit


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-compile of "compiles disaster financial impact" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews impact vs." (replaces manual Step 2).
- System workflow orchestration of "coordinates with insurance for advance payments, accelerates AR collections in…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-438 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Disaster Recovery & Community Support (PA-69.3).)
- operational: FP&A Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 5–10 business days per event for full financial assessment

## W2524. Post-Disaster Vendor Recovery & Supply Chain Re-Establishment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-disaster supply chain disruption assessment |
| **Frequency** | 3–5 activations per year |
| **Volume** | 50–200 vendors potentially affected per major event |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement, Logistics, Merchandising, Finance, Vendors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement contacts affected vendors: assesses their operational status (facility damage, workforce availability, production capacity); classifies as fully operational, partially operational, or offline; identifies critical supply gaps | Procurement / Buyers | VP Merchandising | 2–3 days |
| 2 | For offline/critical vendors: activates alternative sourcing from unaffected regions or substitute vendors; negotiates temporary supply agreements with expedited onboarding; Merchandising approves SKU substitutions where necessary | Procurement / Merchandising | VP Supply Chain | 3–5 days |
| 3 | Logistics re-establishes transport routes: identifies road closures, port disruptions, bridge damage; works with 3PL partners on alternative routing; adjusts lead times in system for affected vendor-origin shipments | Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor status tracking with disaster impact flagging
- Alternative vendor fast-track onboarding
- Route Optimization Engine with dynamic route planning for road closures (in-house build — fit-gap C14)
- Procurement lead time adjustment per vendor

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Extended disruption**: Major port damage (e.g., Cebu port) can disrupt Visayas supply chain for weeks
- **Quality substitution**: Alternative vendors may not meet BuildRight quality standards


### Automation Opportunity
- System workflow notification of "contacts affected vendors" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves SKU substitutions where necessary" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "Logistics re-establishes transport routes" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-438 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Disaster Recovery & Community Support (PA-69.3).)
- operational: Procurement Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 weeks for full supply chain re-establishment

## W2525. Annual Typhoon Season Readiness Program & Drill Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual pre-typhoon season (May) |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | 200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ |
| **Owner** | COO |
| **Participants** | All functional leaders, Store/DC Managers, Safety, HR |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | COO reviews and updates typhoon preparedness protocols based on prior season lessons learned; updates store/DC checklists, emergency contact lists, generator maintenance schedules; reviews insurance coverage adequacy with Finance | COO | COO | 1 week |
| 2 | HR coordinates annual typhoon preparedness training for all 6,925 employees (online module + in-store tabletop exercise); ensures new hires receive orientation; certifies first aid responders per store (minimum 2 per store); conducts evacuation drill at each location | HR / Safety Officer | CHRO | 2–3 weeks |
| 3 | IT tests disaster communication systems: emergency notification platform, backup internet at all locations, POS offline mode, data backup integrity; Facilities completes generator preventive maintenance and fuel top-up at all 205 locations | IT / Facilities | CIO / COO | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Learning management system for annual training tracking
- Oracle eAM for generator PM scheduling (fit-gap F5)
- IT disaster recovery testing documentation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Complacency**: Quiet typhoon seasons reduce urgency for preparedness training
- **Resource constraint**: May–June is also peak pre-rainy-season sales period, competing for staff attention


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews and updates typhoon preparedness protocols based on prior season…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "preparedness training for all 6,925 employees" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-test of "tests disaster communication systems" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-438 (ensure controlled execution — Post-Disaster Recovery & Community Support (PA-69.3).)
- operational: COO review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
