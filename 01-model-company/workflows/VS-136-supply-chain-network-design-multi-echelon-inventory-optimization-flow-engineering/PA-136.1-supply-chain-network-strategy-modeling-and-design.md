## W4121. Supply Chain Network Strategy & Design Principles

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy refresh; growth; major cost/service pressure |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on strategic change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise network (4 DCs, 200 stores, 205+ locations) |
| **Owner** | VP Supply Chain / Network Strategy Lead |
| **Participants** | COO, Strategy (VS-33), Real Estate (VS-97/VS-20), Finance (VS-17.4), Merchandising (VS-01) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Network Strategy define the network strategy and design principles (cost-to-serve, service-level, resilience, scalability to 300+ stores, sustainability), aligned to corporate strategy (VS-33) and the growth plan (10–15 stores/yr links to VS-37) | Network Strategy Lead / Strategy | VP Supply Chain / COO | Annual |
| 2 | Network Strategy document the current-state network (DCs, cross-dock, DSD, store-receiving, lane map), service levels, and cost-to-serve baseline | Network Strategy Lead / Finance | VP Supply Chain | Annual |
| 3 | Network Strategy set design targets and trade-off guardrails (cost vs service vs working-capital vs capex), and present to the executive team | Network Strategy Lead / Finance | COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy (VS-33); network modeling (W4122); finance/cost-to-serve (VS-17.4/VS-110); real estate (VS-97/VS-20)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Drift risk**: Network evolves ad-hoc without strategy; mitigated by documented principles and annual review
- **Sub-optimization risk**: Optimizing one dimension (e.g., cost) harms another (service); mitigated by explicit trade-off guardrails

### Automation Opportunity
- Strategy-to-design-principle cascading; cost-to-serve baseline automation

### Controls
- CTL-638 (ensure controlled execution — Supply Chain Network Strategy, Modeling & Design (PA-136.1).)
- CTL-638: network strategy approved by COO/Board; trade-off guardrails documented

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-37 (growth), VS-97/VS-20 (real estate), VS-110 (freight cost)
