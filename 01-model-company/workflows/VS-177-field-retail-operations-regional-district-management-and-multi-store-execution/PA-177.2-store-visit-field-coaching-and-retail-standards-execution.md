## W5113. Regional/District Store-Visit Cadence, Routing & Itinerary Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Cadence policy; territory assignment (W5106); monthly/quarterly plan |
| **Frequency** | Monthly planning + continuous |
| **Volume** | ~33 stores per Regional Manager / ~15 per District Manager (profile §12.1) × tier-based cadence (DM ~monthly; RM ~quarterly+) → ~180–360 visits/yr per DM, ~130–200 per RM |
| **Owner** | Regional/District Manager |
| **Participants** | Director Field Retail Operations, Store Managers (VS-07), Analytics (VS-28), Travel/Fleet (VS-06/VS-61), Operations Compliance Lead, Field Compliance Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the visit cadence: standard visit frequency by store tier (A/B/C/D from W5109), the visit-type mix (full audit, coaching, drop-in, targeted), and the calendar build across the territory | Regional/District Manager | Regional/District Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | Optimize routing & logistics: route/travel plan (island geography), the unannounced-visit ratio for objectivity, and the pre-visit prep (performance review, prior action-items W5118) | Regional/District Manager | Regional/District Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Communicate & coordinate: share the cadence with Store Managers (the announced portion), coordinate with LP/operations-standards specialists, and log the plan in the field system | Regional/District Manager / Store Ops | Regional/District Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Field-visit calendar (new/VS-63 task system); store P&L (W5109); action-items (W5118); analytics (VS-28); travel/fleet (VS-06/VS-61)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cadence-failure risk**: Manager can't fit visits into the month (documented pain — "Regional Managers with ~33 stores per RM (District Managers ~15) struggle to fit monthly reviews into a 20-working-day month"); mitigated by tier-based cadence + routing optimization
- **Predictability risk**: All visits announced → stores prepare/gaming; mitigated by unannounced-visit ratio
- **Travel-cost risk**: Inefficient routing → cost/time waste; mitigated by route optimization

### Automation Opportunity
- Cadence calendar auto-generation by tier; route optimization; pre-visit brief auto-population

### Controls
- CTL-762 (ensure controlled execution — Store Visit, Field Coaching & Retail Standards Execution (PA-177.2).)
- CTL-762: cadence per policy (tier-based); unannounced-visit ratio enforced; visit log complete

### Time Estimate
- ~monthly planning ~1–2 days/manager; ~continuous routing

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07 (store ops), VS-06/VS-61 (travel/fleet); W5106 (territory), W5109 (tiering), W5114 (audit), W5118 (action-items)

## W5114. Store-Visit Execution, Retail-Standards Audit & Compliance Scoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Scheduled visit (W5113); targeted follow-up; incident |
| **Frequency** | Per the cadence |
| **Volume** | ~2,400–4,800 visits/yr chain-wide (200 stores × 12–24 tier-based visits/store/yr) across ~19 field managers (~6 RMs + ~13 DMs) |
| **Owner** | Regional/District Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager (VS-07), Department Supervisors, LP (VS-23), CX (VS-13), Facilities (VS-138), Field Compliance Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Execute the store walk & standards audit: opening/closing, salesfloor, lumber yard, receiving/backroom, customer service, restrooms, safety/HSE (links VS-24), and the structured retail-standards checklist scoring | Regional/District Manager / Store Manager | Regional/District Manager | Per visit (3–5 hrs) |
| 2 | Review operating performance with the Store Manager: P&L (W5109), CX, shrink, staff, prior action-items (W5118), and the coaching conversation (W5115) | Regional/District Manager / Store Manager | Regional/District Manager | Per visit |
| 3 | Document & log the visit: the standards score, findings, action-items assigned (W5118), and the system entry for analytics (W5120) | Regional/District Manager | Regional/District Manager | Per visit |

### System Touchpoints
- Field-audit/standards app (new); store P&L (W5109); CX (VS-13); shrink (VS-23); HSE (VS-24); action-items (W5118); analytics (W5120)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Shallow-audit risk**: Rushed visit → missed standards; mitigated by structured checklist + time allocation
- **Scoring-inconsistency risk**: Different managers score differently → unreliable benchmark; mitigated by certification (W5108) + W5120 consistency analytics
- **Gaming risk**: Store prepares for announced visits; mitigated by unannounced-visit ratio (W5113) + independent assurance (VS-146)

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile standards-audit app with auto-scoring; photo-evidence capture; auto-action-item creation

### Controls
- CTL-102 (ensure field retail-standards execution.)
- CTL-762: structured checklist used; audit competency certified (W5108); independent assurance cross-check (VS-146)

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 hrs per store visit + documentation

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07 (store ops), VS-24 (HSE), VS-23 (shrink), VS-146 (assurance); W5113 (cadence), W5115 (coaching), W5118 (action-items), W5120 (analytics)

## W5116. Retail-Execution Field Audit (Planogram/Price/Signage/Merchandising Standards)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Visit (W5114); new-planogram rollout (VS-55); promotion/reset; complaint |
| **Frequency** | Per visit + campaign-based |
| **Volume** | ~per-visit audit across the standards domains |
| **Owner** | Regional/District Manager / Operations-Standards Specialist |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), Merchandising (VS-01/VS-55), Pricing (VS-01.2/VS-118), LP (VS-23), Planogram (VS-55), Labeling (VS-151) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audit the retail-execution standards: planogram compliance (VS-55), price/shelf-label accuracy & price-tag presence (VS-151/RA 7394), promotional-signage compliance (VS-14), merchandising standards, and end-cap/display execution | Regional/District Manager / Ops-Standards | Regional/District Manager | Per visit |
| 2 | Reconcile to source-of-truth: spot-check shelf price vs POS/price-file (VS-118 revenue assurance), planogram vs space plan (VS-55), and signage vs promo calendar (VS-14) | Regional/District Manager / Pricing / Merch | Regional/District Manager | Per visit |
| 3 | Log discrepancies & assign action: price-tag reprint (VS-151), planogram reset (VS-55), promo-correction (VS-14), the action-items (W5118), and the pricing-integrity escalation if systemic (VS-118) | Regional/District Manager / Store Manager | Regional/District Manager | Per visit |

### System Touchpoints
- Planogram/space (VS-55); price-file/pricing (VS-01.2/VS-118); labeling (VS-151); promo/signage (VS-14); action-items (W5118)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Price-integrity risk**: Shelf price ≠ system price → RA 7394 exposure + revenue leakage; mitigated by reconciliation + VS-118 escalation
- **Planogram-drift risk**: Non-compliant planogram → lost sales/space productivity; mitigated by audit + reset (VS-55)
- **Audit-fatigue risk**: Too many audits → shallow checks; mitigated by focused campaign-based audits

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile planogram/price-audit app; shelf-photo vs planogram image-match; price-file auto-reconcile

### Controls
- CTL-102 (ensure field retail-standards execution.)
- CTL-762: shelf price reconciled to price-file (VS-118); planogram compliance (VS-55); price-tag presence (RA 7394/VS-151)

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 hrs per visit on retail-execution standards

### Cross-references
- links to VS-55 (planogram), VS-118 (price integrity), VS-151 (labeling), VS-14 (promo); W5114 (visit), W5118 (action-items)

## W5118. Field Action-Item Tracking, Follow-Up & CAP Closure

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Visit findings (W5114/W5116); coaching actions (W5115); escalation (W5122) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~10–20 open action-items per store × 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Regional/District Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Managers (VS-07), functional leads (Merch/IT/HR/Facilities/LP), Director Field Retail Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create & assign action-items: from each visit/audit/coaching, assign owner, due-date, priority, and the corrective-action plan (CAP) | Regional/District Manager | Regional/District Manager | Per visit |
| 2 | Track & follow-up: monitor open items, chase overdue, re-visit verification, and escalate stalled items (W5122) | Regional/District Manager / Store Manager | Regional/District Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Close & verify effectiveness: verify the fix is effective (not just done), close the CAP, and feed lessons to analytics (W5120) & CI (VS-133) | Regional/District Manager | Regional/District Manager | Per item |

### System Touchpoints
- Action-item/CAP tracker (links VS-63 task system); field dashboard (W5127); escalation (W5122); analytics (W5120); CI (VS-133)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Drop-through risk**: Action-items created but not closed → no improvement; mitigated by tracking + overdue chase
- **Closure-without-effectiveness risk**: Marked done but not effective → recurrence; mitigated by effectiveness verification
- **System-fragmentation risk**: Action-items in email/spreadsheets, not a system; mitigated by a single tracker

### Automation Opportunity
- Single action-item/CAP tracker; overdue auto-alert; effectiveness-verification workflow

### Controls
- CTL-102 (ensure field retail-standards execution.)
- CTL-762: every finding has an assigned action-item; overdue escalated; effectiveness verified before closure

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous; ~minutes per item

### Cross-references
- links to VS-63 (task system), VS-133 (CI); W5114/W5115/W5116 (sources), W5122 (escalation), W5120 (analytics)

## W5120. Field Coaching Quality, Visit Effectiveness & Standards-Compliance Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly review; continuous monitoring |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + continuous |
| **Volume** | ~all visits/coaches/stores across the 200-store chain |
| **Owner** | Director Field Retail Operations / Analytics (VS-28) |
| **Participants** | Analytics (VS-28), Store Ops (VS-07), Strategy (VS-33), HR (VS-103), LP (VS-23), CX (VS-13) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Measure coaching & visit effectiveness: visit cadence adherence, action-item closure rate/effectiveness, coaching-quality scores, and the visit-to-performance-improvement linkage | Analytics / Director Field Retail Operations | Director Field Retail Operations | Monthly |
| 2 | Measure standards-compliance consistency: audit-score consistency across managers (W5114), the gaming-detection signal, and the independent-assurance reconciliation (VS-146) | Analytics / Director Field Retail Operations / LP | Director Field Retail Operations | Monthly |

- Field dashboard (W5127); analytics (VS-28); standards scores (W5114); action-items (W5118); assurance (VS-146); CI (VS-133)

- **Inaction risk**: Analytics without improvement action; mitigated by step-3 review & CI (VS-133)

### Automation Opportunity
- Field-effectiveness dashboard; gaming-detection analytics; outcome-linkage modeling

### Controls
- CTL-762 (ensure controlled execution — Store Visit, Field Coaching & Retail Standards Execution (PA-177.2).)

### Time Estimate
