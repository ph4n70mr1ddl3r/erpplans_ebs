## W5105. Field Retail Operations Strategy, Charter & Operating-Model Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy refresh (VS-33); new CEO/COO; format evolution; major footprint change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | 1 program covering the 200-store chain |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations / Director Field Retail Operations |
| **Participants** | Strategy (VS-33), Store Ops (VS-07), HR (VS-103/VS-102), LP (VS-23), Finance FP&A (VS-17.4), IT (VS-27), CX (VS-13), Facilities (VS-138) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the field-operating model: span-of-control philosophy (stores-per-manager), the regional/district/area hierarchy, the visit cadence standard, the decision-rights matrix (store-vs-field-vs-HQ), and the field KPIs (comp sales, standards-compliance %, turnaround success, store-hotline resolution time) | Director Field Retail Operations / VP Store Operations | VP Store Operations | Annual |
| 2 | Align the field model to strategy & growth: coverage of the 200-store six-region footprint, the 10–15 new-store/year ramp (links VS-37), remodel-program field support (VS-109), and the omnichannel/marketplace store role (VS-10/VS-60) | Director Field Retail Operations / Strategy | VP Store Operations / COO | Annual |
| 3 | Govern & approve: leadership sign-off (VS-36), the field org-cost envelope with FP&A, and the entity placement with HR/Legal | VP Store Operations / FP&A / HR | VP Store Operations / COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy/FP&A (VS-33/VS-17.4); store ops (VS-07); workforce planning (VS-103.3); field analytics dashboard (W5127); governance (VS-36); IT (VS-27)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Span-of-control risk**: Too many stores per manager → shallow visits, missed signals; mitigated by data-driven span modeling and the W5106 territory design
- **Disconnect risk**: Field model mis-aligned to strategy/growth (new-format stores unsupported); mitigated by step-2 strategy linkage
- **Accountability risk**: Ambiguous decision rights store-vs-field-vs-HQ → slow issues; mitigated by the decision-rights matrix

### Automation Opportunity
- Span-of-control / workload modeling from store count, sales, complexity; decision-rights-matrix templating

### Controls
- CTL-761 (ensure controlled execution — Field Organization, Territory Design & Regional/District Store Management (PA-177.1).)
- CTL-761: operating model leadership-approved (VS-36); cost envelope FP&A-validated; KPIs tied to VS-33 corporate scorecard

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual strategy + org-model design

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-07 (store ops), VS-103.3 (workforce planning), VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-36 (governance); W5106 (territory), W5107 (staffing), W5127 (analytics)

## W5106. Regional/District Territory Design, Alignment & Store-Group Assignment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual review; new-store openings (VS-37); store closures (VS-59); workload imbalance |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~6 regions → ~10–15 districts → 200 store-group assignments |
| **Owner** | Director Field Retail Operations |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), Strategy (VS-33), HR (VS-103), Supply Chain/DC (VS-02/VS-04 — DC catchment), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design territories by workload & geography: stores-per-manager target, geographic clustering (island/region), DC catchment alignment (links VS-04 4-DC footprint), drive-time/travel-load balancing, and the regional/district naming convention | Director Field Retail Operations / Strategy | Director Field Retail Operations | Annual |
| 2 | Assign stores to territories: per-store group assignment, manager allocation, the transition plan for re-alignments, and the system master update (location hierarchy) | Director Field Retail Operations / Store Ops / IT | Director Field Retail Operations | Annual + event |
| 3 | Validate balance & re-balance triggers: workload equity check (sales, store count, complexity, new-store load), the re-alignment threshold policy, and the quarterly balance review | Director Field Retail Operations / Analytics | Director Field Retail Operations | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Location hierarchy / org master (VS-29); DC catchment (VS-04/VS-02); analytics (VS-28); workforce planning (VS-103.3); field dashboard (W5127)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Workload-imbalance risk**: One manager over-loaded → missed visits/turnarounds; mitigated by workload-based design + quarterly re-balance
- **Travel-load risk**: Island geography → excessive travel time/cost; mitigated by geographic clustering & DC-catchment alignment
- **Master-data risk**: Store→manager assignment stale in systems → reporting/visit errors; mitigated by step-2 system master update

### Automation Opportunity
- Workload-based territory optimization (sales/store-count/complexity/travel); auto-flag of imbalance

### Controls
- CTL-761 (ensure controlled execution — Field Organization, Territory Design & Regional/District Store Management (PA-177.1).)
- CTL-761: territory design data-driven & balanced; assignments reflected in location-hierarchy master (VS-29); re-balance threshold governed

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual territory design + per-event re-alignment

### Cross-references
- links to VS-29 (location master), VS-04/VS-02 (DC catchment), VS-103.3 (workforce planning); W5105 (strategy), W5107 (staffing), W5109 (P&L/grading)

## W5109. Multi-Store P&L Governance, Store Grading & Performance Tiering

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly close (VS-17); performance review cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly review + quarterly tiering |
| **Volume** | 200 stores graded/tiered each cycle |
| **Owner** | Director Field Retail Operations / FP&A (VS-17.4) |
| **Participants** | FP&A (VS-17.4), Store Ops (VS-07), Regional/District Managers, Strategy (VS-33), CX (VS-13) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compile multi-store performance: per-store P&L contribution, comp sales, margin, shrink (VS-23), inventory turn, standards-compliance score (W5114), CX score (VS-13), and the field-manager portfolio roll-up | FP&A / Analytics / Director Field Retail Operations | Director Field Retail Operations | Monthly |
| 2 | Grade & tier stores: A/B/C/D tiering (or equivalent), the watch-list flagging of underperformers, and the regional/peer benchmark ranking | Director Field Retail Operations / FP&A | Director Field Retail Operations | Quarterly |
| 3 | Drive action: tier-based action plan (turnaround W5111 for D-tier, growth plan for A-tier), accountability to the field manager, and the executive review | Director Field Retail Operations / Regional Managers | VP Store Operations | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A/store P&L (VS-17.4 — W1405 store P&L); analytics (VS-28); standards score (W5114); CX (VS-13); shrink (VS-23); field dashboard (W5127)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-tiering risk**: Stale/inaccurate data → wrong tiering → mis-allocated attention; mitigated by monthly data refresh & multi-metric grading
- **Gaming risk**: Field managers game the standards score; mitigated by independent assurance (VS-146 mystery shopping)
- **Inaction risk**: Tiering without follow-through → no improvement; mitigated by tier-based action plans + executive review

### Automation Opportunity
- Multi-store performance dashboard with auto-tiering; benchmark ranking; watch-list auto-flag

### Controls
- CTL-102 (ensure field retail-standards execution.)
- CTL-761: P&L data reconciled to VS-17.4 close; multi-metric grading; independent assurance cross-check (VS-146); action-plan accountability

### Time Estimate
- ~monthly compile + quarterly tiering review

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.4 (FP&A/store P&L), VS-33 (strategy), VS-13 (CX), VS-23 (shrink); W5114 (standards), W5111 (turnaround), W5127 (dashboard)

## W5110. New-Store Stabilization, Maturity Ramp & Field Handover Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New-store opening (VS-37); acquisition integration (VS-130) |
| **Frequency** | Per new store (~10–15/yr) + post-acquisition |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 new stores/yr |
| **Owner** | Director Field Retail Operations / Store Opening (VS-37) |
| **Participants** | Store Opening (VS-37), Store Ops (VS-07), HR/Training (VS-19.4/VS-37.2), Merchandising (VS-01), IT (VS-27), Facilities (VS-138) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plan field stabilization: assign a field sponsor, set the 30/60/90-day stabilization milestones (sales ramp, standards compliance, CX, shrink, staff retention), and the intensive visit cadence for the ramp period | Director Field Retail Operations / Store Opening | Director Field Retail Operations | Per opening |
| 2 | Execute stabilization support: intensive field visits, hands-on standards/merchandising help, staff coaching, escalation of issues to HQ support center (W5122), and weekly stabilization reporting | Regional/District Manager / Director Field Retail Operations | Director Field Retail Operations | 90-day ramp |
| 3 | Hand over to steady-state: maturity-ramp sign-off (meets steady-state KPIs), transition from intensive to standard cadence, and the post-stabilization lessons-learned | Director Field Retail Operations / Store Ops | Director Field Retail Operations / VP Store Operations | 90–180 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Store opening (VS-37); store ops (VS-07); field dashboard (W5127); training (VS-19.4); escalation (W5122); analytics (VS-28)

- **Handover-gap risk**: Premature handover to steady-state → regression; mitigated by KPI-gated sign-off
- **Knowledge-loss risk**: Lessons not captured → repeat mistakes at next opening; mitigated by step-3 lessons-learned

### Automation Opportunity
- 30/60/90 milestone tracker; maturity-ramp benchmark vs peer openings; auto-flag of off-track ramp

### Controls
- CTL-761 (ensure controlled execution — Field Organization, Territory Design & Regional/District Store Management (PA-177.1).)
- CTL-761: stabilization plan documented; KPI-gated steady-state sign-off; lessons-learned captured (VS-133)

### Time Estimate

## W5111. Underperforming/Distressed-Store Diagnosis & Turnaround Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | D-tier grading (W5109); comp-sales decline; shrink spike; CX collapse; incident |
| **Frequency** | Per flagged store (typically ~5–15 active turnarounds) |
| **Volume** | ~5–15 turnaround engagements/yr |
| **Owner** | Director Field Retail Operations |
| **Participants** | Regional/District Manager, Store Manager (VS-07), FP&A (VS-17.4), LP (VS-23), CX (VS-13), HR (VS-103/VS-84), Merchandising (VS-01) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diagnose root cause: structured diagnosis across sales/traffic/conversion, merchandising/assortment, operations/standards, people/leadership, shrink/loss, CX, and local market/competition | Regional/District Manager / Director Field Retail Operations / FP&A | Director Field Retail Operations | Per case (1–2 weeks) |
| 2 | Build the turnaround plan: 90-day action plan with owners, milestones, KPI targets, the resource asks (HQ support, capital, staffing), and the governance cadence | Director Field Retail Operations / Store Manager / Functional Leads | Director Field Retail Operations / VP Store Operations | Per case |
| 3 | Execute, monitor & decide: intensive execution + weekly review, escalation of blockers (W5122), and the continue/extend/close decision vs the closure option (VS-59) | Director Field Retail Operations / Store Manager | VP Store Operations | 90–180 days |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A (VS-17.4); analytics (VS-28); standards (W5114); CX (VS-13); shrink (VS-23); HR (VS-103/VS-84); field dashboard (W5127); escalation (W5122)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-diagnosis risk**: Treating symptoms not root cause → wasted effort; mitigated by structured multi-dimensional diagnosis
- **Drift risk**: Turnaround plan not executed → no improvement; mitigated by weekly governance + escalation
- **Closure-delay risk**: Holding a non-viable store too long → losses compound; mitigated by the explicit closure option (VS-59)

### Automation Opportunity
- Distressed-store early-warning (auto-flag on multi-metric decline); turnaround-plan template; milestone tracker

### Controls
- CTL-761 (ensure controlled execution — Field Organization, Territory Design & Regional/District Store Management (PA-177.1).)
- CTL-761: structured diagnosis documented; turnaround plan leadership-approved; weekly governance; closure decision per VS-59 criteria

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 weeks diagnosis + 90–180 days execution per turnaround

### Cross-references
- links to VS-59 (store closure), VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-23 (shrink), VS-13 (CX), VS-84 (labor relations); W5109 (grading), W5113 (visits), W5122 (escalation)
