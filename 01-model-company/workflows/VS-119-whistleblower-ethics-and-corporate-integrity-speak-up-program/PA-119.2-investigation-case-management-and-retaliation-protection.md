## W3721. Investigation Planning, Scoping & Evidence Collection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Triaged case (W3717) |
| **Frequency** | Per case |
| **Volume** | Investigation caseload |
| **Owner** | Ethics & Compliance Officer / Lead Investigator |
| **Participants** | Lead Investigator, Ethics & Compliance Officer, Legal (links to VS-100.3), LP (links to VS-23), HR (links to VS-19), IT/Security (links to VS-27.3), ABC (links to VS-86), Audit (links to VS-21), Records (links to VS-88), Forensic / Fraud Investigator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lead Investigator plan and scope the investigation: define the allegation, the investigation objectives, scope/limits, the investigation team (and independence/conflict-recusal), the evidence sources, and the investigation plan/SLA | Lead Investigator / Ethics & Compliance Officer / Legal, Forensic / Fraud Investigator | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Per case |
| 2 | Lead Investigator collect and preserve evidence: documents/records, system/transaction data, email/communications, CCTV (links to VS-23.2), access/system logs, and physical evidence; preserve chain-of-custody and apply legal-hold (links to VS-88.2) | Lead Investigator / IT / LP / Records | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Per case |
| 3 | Lead Investigator manage investigation confidentiality and need-to-know, coordinate with IT for data extraction (links to VS-27.3), and document the evidence record in case-management (links to W3720); flag any need for forensic specialists | Lead Investigator / IT / Legal | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Case-management (links to W3720); CCTV (links to VS-23.2); IT/system logs (links to VS-27.3); legal-hold (links to VS-88.2); records (links to VS-88); audit (links to VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Evidence-spoliation risk**: Evidence lost/altered before collection; mitigated by prompt preservation and legal-hold
- **Scope-creep risk**: Investigation expands beyond the allegation; mitigated by clear scoping


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs, and physical evidence" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record in case-management" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-587 (ensure controlled execution — Investigation, Case Management & Retaliation Protection (PA-119.2).)
- operational: prompt preservation and legal-hold; operational: clear scoping

### Time Estimate
- Per case (days to weeks)

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Adjudication-bias risk**: Senior subjects shielded from consequence; mitigated by Ethics Committee independence

### Controls

## W3725. Retaliation Monitoring & Protection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Reporter/witness/investigator involved in a case; retaliation report |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + on report |
| **Volume** | Reporters/witnesses across cases |
| **Owner** | Ethics & Compliance Officer / HR |
| **Participants** | Ethics & Compliance Officer, HR (links to VS-19/VS-84), Legal (links to VS-100.3), the reporter/witness's management, Risk (links to VS-21.2), Board Audit Committee (links to VS-36) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ethics & Compliance Officer/HR monitor reporters/witnesses/investigators for retaliation: track adverse actions (discipline, schedule/perf-change, transfer, exclusion) post-report for a defined period, and investigate any pattern suggestive of retaliation | Ethics & Compliance Officer / HR | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Continuous |
| 2 | Ethics & Compliance Officer/HR/Legal investigate any retaliation allegation: substantiate, take corrective action against the retaliator (links to W3724), restore/protect the reporter, and document; treat retaliation as serious misconduct itself | Ethics & Compliance Officer / HR / Legal | Ethics Committee | On allegation |
| 3 | Ethics & Compliance Officer report retaliation incidents and protection posture to the Ethics Committee (W3719) and board audit committee (links to VS-36); reinforce anti-retaliation culture (links to W3718) | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Ethics Committee | Quarterly + on incident |

### System Touchpoints
- HR (links to VS-19/VS-84); legal (links to VS-100.3); case-management (links to W3720); risk (links to VS-21.2); governance (links to VS-36); culture/training (links to W3718)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Subtle-retaliation risk**: Retaliation via schedule/perf/exclusion is hard to detect; mitigated by pattern monitoring
- **Under-reporting risk**: Retaliation goes unreported; mitigated by independent channel and culture


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-schedule of "schedule/perf-change, transfer, exclusion" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "Ethics & Compliance Officer/HR/Legal investigate any retaliation allegation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-post of "posture to the Ethics Committee" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-158 (ensure whistleblower confidentiality & non-retaliation.)
- operational: pattern monitoring; operational: independent channel and culture

### Time Estimate
- Continuous + on allegation
