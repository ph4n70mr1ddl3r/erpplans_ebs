# VS-119: Whistleblower, Ethics & Corporate Integrity (Speak-Up) Program

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Whistleblower, Ethics & Corporate Integrity (Speak-Up) Program workflows for BuildRight Depot
Corp. — governing the **enterprise-wide speak-up and business-ethics program** that gives every
employee, contractor, vendor, and stakeholder a confidential channel to raise concerns about
misconduct, and that investigates, resolves, and learns from those concerns. BuildRight operates
6,918 employees across 200 stores, 4 DCs, and 5 legal entities (profile §4), uses ~10–20%
contingent/outsourced labor (VS-98), runs ~800–1,000 vendors and ~40% import spend, earns ~10%
B2G revenue (RA 9184 procurement) and ~30% B2B/project revenue, and handles cash, payments, and
consumer data at scale — an exposure surface that spans anti-bribery/corruption (ABC), anti-
financial-crime (AML), fraud, theft, harassment, discrimination, workplace-safety, environmental,
consumer-protection, antitrust, conflicts of interest, and vendor/supply-chain integrity. A
functioning speak-up program is also a governance expectation: the board's audit/risk committee
periodically reviews ethics-program posture, and Philippine and cross-border frameworks (the SEC
Code of Corporate Governance for Public Companies — relevant to group financing — and ISO
37001/37301) increasingly expect a confidential reporting channel and whistleblower protection.

Today the program is reduced to a single workflow — **W2943 (ABC Investigation, Whistleblower
Coordination & Remediation)** in VS-86.3 (anti-bribery) — which coordinates only the anti-bribery-
specific slice triggered by a whistleblower report or detected ABC violation. The enterprise
speak-up program is unowned: multi-channel intake (hotline, web-portal, email, mobile, in-person)
with anonymity/confidentiality; triage and classification across **all** violation types (not only
ABC); case management and investigation lifecycle; retaliation monitoring and protection; ethics
committee and case governance; awareness/training and culture; conflict-of-interest and gifts/
integrity disclosure management; third-party/supply-chain integrity due diligence; analytics and
heat-mapping; and multi-entity (5 legal entities) ethics coordination. This value stream
consolidates the discipline: ethics governance and speak-up channel/intake; investigation, case
management and retaliation protection; and ethics analytics, culture and program assurance — with
W2943 (ABC) becoming one specialized investigation track (W3726) within the broader program.

This is distinct from **VS-86 (Anti-Financial Crime, AML/KYC/ABC)** which owns the AML/ABC
*compliance regime* (this value stream owns the *speak-up channel and ethics-investigation
program* — of which the ABC-specific W2943 becomes one investigation track). It is distinct from
**VS-21 (Internal Audit & Risk)** which independently *audits* controls (this value stream owns
the *report-and-investigate* function for misconduct allegations that often originate outside the
audit cycle). It is distinct from **VS-100 (Legal Operations)** which manages *litigation and
legal matters* (this value stream manages the *internal ethics investigation* that may, if
substantiated and serious, escalate to legal/law-enforcement). It is distinct from **VS-23 (Loss
Prevention)** which investigates *theft/shrink* (this value stream investigates *misconduct* —
including but beyond theft: harassment, discrimination, safety, environmental, ABC, fraud,
conflict). It is distinct from **VS-84 (Labor Relations)** which handles *grievances/CBA* (this
value stream handles *ethics/misconduct reports* that may involve but go beyond individual
grievances). It is distinct from **VS-19/VS-103 (HR)** which manages *employee conduct/HR case
management* (this value stream owns the *independent, confidential, retaliation-protected*
speak-up channel and the cross-functional ethics investigation — deliberately outside the line/
HR chain to preserve independence). The whistleblower/ethics/integrity program is the *speak-up,
independent-investigation, and corporate-integrity* discipline — distinct from ABC compliance,
audit, legal, loss prevention, labor relations, and HR.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: a large distributed workforce, significant contingent labor,
B2G/ABC exposure, vendor/supply-chain integrity risk, cash-and-data handling, and 5 legal
entities. Without a dedicated independent owner, BuildRight risks undetected misconduct (theft,
fraud, bribery, harassment, safety/environmental violations), regulatory/legal exposure, culture
erosion, and retaliation against those who speak up — risks that no existing value stream owns
end-to-end.
