## W3713. Ethics & Integrity Program Strategy & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual governance planning (links to VS-36/VS-33.1); program review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise ethics & integrity program |
| **Owner** | Ethics & Compliance Officer / Chief Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Ethics & Compliance Officer, CEO/Board Audit & Risk Committee (links to VS-36/VS-21), Legal (links to VS-100.3), HR (links to VS-19/VS-103), Internal Audit (links to VS-21), ABC/AML (links to VS-86), Loss Prevention (links to VS-23), Risk (links to VS-21.2), Cross-Entity (links to VS-72) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ethics & Compliance Officer define the ethics & integrity program strategy and governance: scope (all violation types — ABC/AML, fraud/theft, harassment/discrimination, safety/environmental, consumer-protection, antitrust, conflicts, vendor/supply-chain integrity), the framework (ISO 37001 ABMS / 37301 CMS aligned), the speak-up channel strategy (W3715), the operating model (independent of line/HR where needed), and KPIs (report volume, substantiation rate, cycle-time, retaliation incidents, culture-trust score) | Ethics & Compliance Officer | CEO / Board Audit Committee | Annual |
| 2 | Ethics & Compliance Officer establish the governance: Ethics & Compliance Officer authority and independence (reports to the board audit/risk committee per links to VS-36), the Ethics Committee (W3719), the cross-functional investigation model (HR, Legal, LP, ABC, Audit), and integration to ABC (VS-86), audit (VS-21), legal (VS-100), and risk (VS-21.2) | Ethics & Compliance Officer / Legal / Board | CEO / Board Audit Committee | Annual |
| 3 | Board Audit & Risk Committee ratify the ethics strategy, policy, and authority, approve the program budget (links to VS-17.4), and set the integrity-objective; communicate the mandate enterprise-wide (links to W3718) | Board Audit Committee / Ethics & Compliance Officer | Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Governance/board (links to VS-36); audit/risk (links to VS-21.2); legal (links to VS-100.3); HR (links to VS-19/VS-103); ABC/AML (links to VS-86); loss prevention (links to VS-23); cross-entity (links to VS-72)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lack-of-independence risk**: Program reports through line management, suppressing reports; mitigated by board reporting line
- **Scope-narrowness risk**: Program covers only ABC, missing other misconduct; mitigated by all-violation scope


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-report of "report volume, substantiation rate, cycle-time, retaliation incidents,…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System continuous audit of "Ethics & Compliance Officer establish the governance" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-capture of "Board Audit & Risk Committee ratify the ethics strategy" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-158 (ensure whistleblower confidentiality & non-retaliation.)
- operational: board reporting line; operational: all-violation scope

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual

## W3715. Speak-Up Channel Operations & Multi-Channel Intake

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Reporter contacts a channel |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (intake as reports arrive) |
| **Volume** | Report volume across the workforce (~6,932 + contingent + vendors) |
| **Owner** | Ethics & Compliance Officer / Speak-Up Intake |
| **Participants** | Ethics & Compliance Officer, Speak-Up Intake team, third-party hotline vendor (links to W3720), HR (links to VS-103.1), IT (links to VS-27), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Cross-Entity (links to VS-72) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ethics & Compliance Officer operate multi-channel intake: 24/7 third-party hotline (phone/SMS), web-portal, email, mobile-app form, and in-person to HR/Ethics/LP — available to employees, contractors, vendors, customers, and the public; ensure language accessibility (Filipino/English) and channel awareness (links to W3718) | Ethics & Compliance Officer / Intake / Vendor | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Continuous |
| 2 | Ethics & Compliance Officer/Intake capture each report: the allegation (who/what/when/where), category, evidence, reporter preference (named/anonymous/confidential), and risk/urgency; create the case record (links to W3717) with strict need-to-know access | Ethics & Compliance Officer / Intake | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Per report |
| 3 | Ethics & Compliance Officer acknowledge the report to the reporter (where possible) within SLA, confirm protection (W3716), and route to triage (W3717); manage intake quality and channel-performance with the vendor (links to W3720) | Ethics & Compliance Officer / Intake | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Per report |

### System Touchpoints
- Case-management system (links to W3720); third-party hotline/intake vendor (links to W3720); HR case mgmt (links to VS-103.1); IT/security (links to VS-27.3); legal (links to VS-100.3); cross-entity (links to VS-72)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Channel-trust risk**: Reporters distrust the channel (fear of exposure/retaliation); mitigated by anonymity (W3716) and independence
- **Accessibility-gap risk**: Channel not available in Filipino/after-hours to front-line staff; mitigated by multi-channel + language


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "Ethics & Compliance Officer/Intake capture each report" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-route of "route to triage" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-586 (ensure controlled execution — Ethics Governance, Speak-Up Channel & Intake Operations (PA-119.1).)
- operational: anonymity (W3716) and independence; operational: multi-channel + language

### Time Estimate
- Continuous + per report

| 2 | Ethics & Compliance Officer/IT ensure technical confidentiality: secure case-management access controls, anonymization of reporter identity where requested, audit trail, and data-protection (links to VS-91/VS-27.3) so reporter identity cannot be exposed | Ethics & Compliance Officer / IT / Security | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Maintained + per report |

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Retaliation risk**: Reporter punished for speaking up; mitigated by anti-retaliation policy and monitoring (W3725)


### Automation Opportunity

- System auto-report of "reporter identity where requested, audit trail, and data-protection" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "monitor for retaliation" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-586 (ensure controlled execution — Ethics Governance, Speak-Up Channel & Intake Operations (PA-119.1).)

- Maintained + continuous

## W3717. Report Triage, Classification & Routing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New report (W3715) |
| **Frequency** | Per report |
| **Volume** | All reports |
| **Owner** | Ethics & Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Ethics & Compliance Officer, Legal (links to VS-100.3), HR (links to VS-19/VS-103), ABC/AML (links to VS-86), LP (links to VS-23), Audit (links to VS-21), Risk (links to VS-21.2), IT/Security (links to VS-27.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ethics & Compliance Officer triage each report: classify by violation type (ABC, fraud/theft, harassment/discrimination, safety/environmental, consumer/data, conflict, vendor-integrity, other), assess credibility, severity, urgency, and risk; determine the investigation track and the independent investigator | Ethics & Compliance Officer / Legal | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Per report |
| 2 | Ethics & Compliance Officer route the case: ABC → ABC track (links to W2943/VS-86) within the program; fraud/theft → LP/audit (links to VS-23/VS-21) with ethics oversight; harassment/HR-conduct → HR-investigation (links to VS-19/VS-84) with ethics oversight where independence needed; safety/environmental → HSE (links to VS-24); serious matters → Legal/Ethics-led (links to W3721) | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Per report |
| 3 | Ethics & Compliance Officer manage conflicts of interest in routing (recuse where the accused is in the reporting chain), assign case ownership, set the investigation SLA, and open the case in case-management (links to W3720); notify the Ethics Committee of high-severity cases (links to W3719) | Ethics & Compliance Officer / Legal | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Per report |

### System Touchpoints
- Case-management (links to W3720); ABC (links to VS-86/W2943); LP (links to VS-23); audit (links to VS-21); HR (links to VS-19/VS-84); HSE (links to VS-24); legal (links to VS-100.3); risk (links to VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-routing risk**: Report routed to the accused's chain, suppressing it; mitigated by conflict-recusal and independence
- **Triage-delay risk**: Reports sit untriaged, delaying action on urgent matters; mitigated by triage SLA


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-track of "track and the independent investigator" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-route of "route the case" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-notify of "notify the Ethics Committee of high-severity cases" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-158 (ensure whistleblower confidentiality & non-retaliation.)
- operational: conflict-recusal and independence; operational: triage SLA

### Time Estimate
- Per report
