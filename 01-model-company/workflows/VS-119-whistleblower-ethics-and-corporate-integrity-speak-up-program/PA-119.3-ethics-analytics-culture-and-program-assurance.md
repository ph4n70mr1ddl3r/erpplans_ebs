## W3729. Ethics & Speak-Up Analytics & KPI Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Reporting cycle; committee review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Program KPIs and analytics |
| **Owner** | Ethics & Compliance Officer / BI |
| **Participants** | Ethics & Compliance Officer, BI (links to VS-28.1/VS-103.3), Audit (links to VS-21), Risk (links to VS-21.2), Ethics Committee (links to W3719), Board Audit Committee (links to VS-36) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ethics & Compliance Officer/BI maintain the ethics & speak-up KPI dashboard: report volume by channel/category/location, substantiation rate, case cycle-time, retaliation incidents, repeat-offender/repeat-issue patterns, training completion, and culture-trust score | Ethics & Compliance Officer / BI | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Monthly |
| 2 | Ethics & Compliance Officer analyze trends: by category (ABC, fraud, harassment, safety, etc.), by location/entity, over time; identify hotspots, emerging risks, and control-effectiveness decay; benchmark against program maturity and external norms | Ethics & Compliance Officer / BI / Risk | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Quarterly |
| 3 | Ethics & Compliance Officer publish analytics to the Ethics Committee (W3719), board audit committee (links to VS-36), and executives; drive prioritized action on hotspots and feed to risk assessment (W3730) and continuous improvement (W3736) | Ethics & Compliance Officer / BI | Ethics & Compliance Officer | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- BI/analytics (links to VS-28.1/VS-103.3); case-management (links to W3720); audit/risk (links to VS-21.2); governance (links to VS-36); risk assessment (links to W3730)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric-blindness risk**: No visibility into report patterns and hotspots; mitigated by KPI dashboard
- **Misleading-metric risk**: Low report volume misread as "no problem" rather than "no trust"; mitigated by culture measurement (W3731)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-report of "report volume by channel/category/location, substantiation rate, case…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-analyze of "Ethics & Compliance Officer analyze trends" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System continuous audit of "Ethics & Compliance Officer publish analytics to the Ethics" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-588 (ensure controlled execution — Ethics Analytics, Culture & Program Assurance (PA-119.3).)
- operational: KPI dashboard; operational: culture measurement (W3731)

### Time Estimate
- Monthly + quarterly
