## W4969. Forklift & Loading Equipment Operations in the Customer Vehicle Area

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Bulky pickup requiring mechanical loading (W4970) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous during trading hours × 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~5–15 bulky mechanical-loading events per store per day × 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Loading-Zone Supervisor / Yard Lead (VS-07) / HSE (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | Forklift Operators, Spotters (W4971), Store Ops (VS-07), HSE (VS-24), Customer Safety (VS-147) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Authorize & dispatch the forklift/loading equipment to the customer area: only qualified operators (W4973), only in the designated envelope (W4964), with a spotter (W4971), horn/lights/blue-light, and pedestrian-vehicle separation active (links VS-147) | Yard Lead / Operator / Spotter | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per event |
| 2 | Operate the equipment safely in the customer area: controlled speed, clear travel path, load lowered except lifting, no passengers, horn at intersections, and immediate stop on pedestrian incursion; coordinate with the spotter (W4971) | Operator | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per event |
| 3 | Return, inspect & log: return equipment to designated parking/charging, log usage/hours, note defects (W4973), and report any near-miss/incident to HSE (VS-24) and customer-safety (VS-147 CAPA) | Operator / Yard Lead / HSE | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Equipment/asset & maintenance (VS-35/VS-138); HSE (VS-24); incident reporting (VS-147/VS-24); CMMS (VS-138)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Contact-injury risk**: Forklift contact with customer/pedestrian/vehicle (VS-147); mitigated by spotter, demarcation, and speed/horn controls
- **Unauthorized-use risk**: Unqualified operator using equipment → accident; mitigated by qualification gate (W4973)
- **Equipment-failure risk**: Defective forklift in use; mitigated by pre-use inspection (W4973)

### Automation Opportunity
- Operator-qualification-interlock on equipment; telematics/geofence (operator-zone); near-miss capture

### Controls
- CTL-744 (ensure controlled execution — Bulky-Goods Loading, Forklift-in-Customer-Area & Vehicle Coordination (PA-171.2).)
- CTL-744: qualified operator only; spotter present; designated envelope; pre-use inspection; incident reported (VS-24/VS-147)

### Time Estimate
- ~per-event 5–20 min (load + travel + return)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE/forklift), VS-147 (premises safety), VS-35/VS-138 (equipment/maintenance), W4964 (layout), W4970 (loading), W4971 (spotter), W4973 (qualification)

## W4970. Bulky-Goods Loading into Customer Vehicles (Lumber, Tile, Cement, Appliance)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Counter release (W4962); appointment (W4963); customer at loading bay |
| **Frequency** | Continuous × 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~7–10 bulky loads per store per day × 200 stores (per W950) |
| **Owner** | Loading-Zone Supervisor / Loaders |
| **Participants** | Loaders, Forklift Operators (W4969), Spotters (W4971), Store Ops (VS-07), CX (VS-13) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare the load & vehicle: confirm the order/items (from W4962), position the customer vehicle in the bay, verify vehicle suitability (W4968), and plan the load (order, weight distribution W4972, securement points) | Loader / Supervisor / Customer | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per load |
| 2 | Load the bulky goods: load lumber (length/cantilever), tile (pallet/crate), cement (bags/pallet), appliances (W4976 fragile/hazardous), using the forklift (W4969) or manual handling (W4975), with the spotter (W4971) guiding; protect the vehicle and goods from damage | Loaders / Operator / Spotter | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per load |
| 3 | Secure & hand off: secure the load (W4972), confirm completeness/condition with the customer, capture the load/release confirmation, advise the customer on safe transport, and clear the bay for the next pickup | Loaders / Supervisor / Customer | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per load |

### System Touchpoints
- Order mgmt (VS-10/VS-16); WMS/staging (VS-04); calibration (VS-115 — scales); CX (VS-13); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Damage risk**: Goods or vehicle damaged during loading → return/complaint/claim (VS-32/VS-13/VS-90); mitigated by trained loading and protection
- **Incomplete-load risk**: Item left behind → customer return/extra trip; mitigated by completeness check
- **Overload risk**: Vehicle overloaded → unsafe/illegal (LTFRB/LTO); mitigated by W4972

### Automation Opportunity
- Load-plan from order dimensions; completeness scan; damage-photo capture at bay; CX feedback prompt

### Controls
- CTL-744 (ensure controlled execution — Bulky-Goods Loading, Forklift-in-Customer-Area & Vehicle Coordination (PA-171.2).)
- CTL-744: order verified; vehicle suitability checked; load secured (W4972); completeness/condition confirmed with customer

### Time Estimate
- ~per-load 10–30 min depending on bulk

### Cross-references
- links to VS-10/VS-16 (order), VS-04 (staging), VS-115 (scales), VS-32/VS-13/VS-90 (damage/return/claim), W4962 (counter), W4968 (vehicle), W4969 (forklift), W4971 (spotter), W4972 (securement)

## W4971. Spotter, Banksperson & Pedestrian-Safety Protocol for Customer-Area Loading

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Any mechanical loading in the customer area (W4969/W4970) |
| **Frequency** | Per loading event × 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~10–20 spotter-required events per store per day × 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Loading-Zone Supervisor / HSE (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | Spotters, Forklift Operators (W4969), Store Ops (VS-07), HSE (VS-24), Customer Safety (VS-147) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Position & brief the spotter: for every mechanical load in the customer area, assign a spotter/banksperson, brief on the path/load/blind-spots, and establish hand-signal/verbal/radio communication with the operator | Supervisor / Spotter / Operator | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per event |
| 2 | Control pedestrians & vehicles during loading: the spotter keeps customers/vehicles/pedestrians clear of the swing/fall/travel envelope, stops the operator on any incursion, and manages the bay/queue (links VS-147 premises safety) | Spotter / Operator | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per event |
| 3 | Confirm completion & safety: signal all-clear on completion, confirm load securement (W4972), release the customer vehicle, and report any near-miss/incident (VS-24/VS-147) | Spotter / Operator / Supervisor | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE (VS-24); incident reporting (VS-147/VS-24); store-comms (VS-63); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pedestrian-incursion risk**: Customer walks into the travel/fall envelope → injury (VS-147); mitigated by mandatory spotter and stop-on-incursion
- **Communication-failure risk**: Operator and spotter out of sync → accident; mitigated by established signals and briefing
- **Spotter-absent risk**: Loading without a spotter → uncontrolled hazard; mitigated by the no-spotter-no-load control

### Automation Opportunity
- Spotter-required gate (no mechanical load without spotter logged); pedestrian-detection sensors; near-miss capture

### Controls
- CTL-744 (ensure controlled execution — Bulky-Goods Loading, Forklift-in-Customer-Area & Vehicle Coordination (PA-171.2).)
- CTL-744: spotter mandatory for mechanical loading; signals briefed; stop-on-incursion; incident reported

### Time Estimate
- ~per-event spotter duty 10–30 min

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-147 (premises safety), W4969 (forklift), W4970 (loading), W4972 (securement), W4964 (layout)

## W4972. Load Securement, Weight Distribution & Vehicle Overload Prevention

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | During/after loading (W4970); vehicle suitability check (W4968) |
| **Frequency** | Per bulky load × 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~7–10 bulky loads requiring securement per store per day × 200 stores (all bulky loads are secured per W4970) |
| **Owner** | Loading-Zone Supervisor / Loaders |
| **Participants** | Loaders, Forklift Operators (W4969), Store Ops (VS-07), Customer Safety (VS-147), Calibration (VS-115) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plan weight distribution & load order: distribute weight across the vehicle axles, place heavy items low/forward, balance side-to-side, and stay within the vehicle's payload/load rating (links W4968 suitability) | Loader / Supervisor | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per load |
| 2 | Secure the load: strap/tie-down/chock/block the cargo (lumber/tile/cement/appliance), verify securement points and strap rating, and prevent shift/rollover in transit (links VS-147 — load-shift injury/liability) | Loaders / Supervisor | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per load |
| 3 | Prevent overload & advise the customer: weigh/estimate the load (links VS-115 scales for catch-weight/bulk), refuse unsafe overload (offer delivery VS-06/VS-143 or larger rental VS-162), advise the customer on safe transport/securement, and document | Loaders / Supervisor / CX | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per load |

### System Touchpoints
- Calibration/scales (VS-115); CX (VS-13); delivery alternatives (VS-06/VS-143/VS-162); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Load-shift risk**: Unsecured cargo shifts/rolls in transit → road incident/liability (VS-147); mitigated by securement protocol
- **Overload risk**: Vehicle overloaded → unsafe, illegal, damage, LTFRB/LTO exposure; mitigated by weight check and refusal of unsafe load
- **Liability risk**: Customer injury/damage from a load BuildRight loaded → premises/product-liability (VS-147/VS-100); mitigated by securement and advice

### Automation Opportunity
- Weight/dimension auto-estimate from order; securement checklist; overload alert; advice template

### Controls
- CTL-744 (ensure controlled execution — Bulky-Goods Loading, Forklift-in-Customer-Area & Vehicle Coordination (PA-171.2).)
- CTL-744: weight distribution planned; load secured to rating; overload refused; customer advised; documented

### Time Estimate
- ~per-load securement 5–15 min

### Cross-references
- links to VS-147 (premises/liability), VS-115 (scales), VS-06/VS-143/VS-162 (alternatives), VS-100 (liability), W4968 (vehicle suitability), W4970 (loading)

## W4973. Forklift Operator Qualification, Equipment Inspection & DOLE OSH Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Operator onboarding; equipment use (W4969); periodic compliance; incident |
| **Frequency** | Per operator + daily pre-use + periodic audit |
| **Volume** | ~2–3 trained operators per store × 200 stores; ~1–2 units of loading equipment per store |
| **Owner** | HSE (VS-24) / Yard Lead / Store Ops |
| **Participants** | HSE (VS-24), Forklift Operators, Training (VS-19.4/VS-169 PPE), Store Ops (VS-07), Occupational Health (VS-83) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Qualify operators per DOLE OSH (forklift/reach-truck): training, certification/licensing, competency assessment, medical/fitness (links VS-83), PPE issuance (VS-169), and the operator register; only qualified operators use equipment in the customer area (W4969) | HSE / Training / Occupational Health | HSE Lead | Per operator |
| 2 | Inspect equipment pre-use: daily pre-use inspection (forks, brakes, hydraulics, tires, lights/horn, seatbelt), defect tag-out, and maintenance/refuel/charge per the schedule (links VS-138/VS-35); no defective equipment in service | Operator / Yard Lead / Facilities | HSE Lead / Supervisor | Daily |
| 3 | Audit & comply: periodic DOLE-OSH forklift compliance audit, operator re-certification, equipment maintenance records, incident investigation (VS-24), and feed the customer-safety program (VS-147) | HSE / Internal Audit (VS-21) / Facilities | HSE Lead | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE/compliance (VS-24); training/OLA (VS-19.4); equipment/CMMS (VS-35/VS-138); occupational health (VS-83); PPE (VS-169); audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unqualified-operator risk**: Uncertified operator → accident + DOLE liability; mitigated by qualification gate
- **Equipment-defect risk**: Defective forklift in use → accident; mitigated by pre-use inspection and tag-out
- **Compliance-failure risk**: DOLE-OSH non-compliance → penalty/officer liability; mitigated by audit and records

### Automation Opportunity
- Operator-qualification-interlock on equipment; pre-use checklist app; defect tag-out workflow; re-cert scheduling

### Controls
- CTL-97 (ensure will-call release integrity & loading safety.)
- CTL-744: qualified/certified operators only; pre-use inspection logged; defective equipment tagged out; DOLE-OSH audited

### Time Estimate
- ~per-operator qualification days/weeks; ~daily pre-use 10–15 min/equipment; ~periodic audit per cycle

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-19.4 (training), VS-83 (occupational health), VS-169 (PPE), VS-35/VS-138 (equipment/CMMS), VS-21 (audit), W4969 (operations), W4971 (spotter)

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Continuous × 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 bulk-breaking events per store per week × 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Yard Lead / Loading-Zone Supervisor / Store Ops |
| **Participants** | Yard Staff, Loaders, Forklift Operators (W4969), Calibration (VS-115), Inventory (VS-05) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Break bulk & measure: break bulk stock (cement bags from pallet, lumber board-foot cut, catch-weight nails/wire — links VS-09.1 W951 bulk breaking, VS-115 scales), measure/weigh accurately, and stage for the customer | Yard Staff / Loaders | Yard Lead | Per event |
| 2 | Transfer yard-to-vehicle: move the broken/measured bulk to the customer vehicle (manual or forklift W4969), with the spotter (W4971) and securement (W4972), and update inventory on the break (links VS-05) | Yard Staff / Loaders / Operator | Yard Lead | Per event |

### Controls
- CTL-744 (ensure controlled execution — Bulky-Goods Loading, Forklift-in-Customer-Area & Vehicle Coordination (PA-171.2).)

## W4976. Hazardous / Fragile / High-Value Goods Loading Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Pickup of DG/hazmat, fragile, or high-value goods |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven × 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 DG/fragile/high-value pickups per store per week × 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Loading-Zone Supervisor / HSE (VS-24) / LP (VS-23) |
| **Participants** | Loaders, HSE (VS-24), LP (VS-23), DG Compliance (VS-114), Store Ops (VS-07) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Apply the goods-specific loading protocol: DG/hazmat (paint/solvent/gas cylinder/LPG — links VS-114 DG compliance, segregation/securement/ventilation); fragile (glass, ceramic, appliance — protection/orientation); high-value (power tools, copper — LP oversight VS-23) | Loaders / HSE / LP | Loading-Zone Supervisor | Per event |

### Time Estimate
- ~per-event 10–25 min depending on goods

### Cross-references
