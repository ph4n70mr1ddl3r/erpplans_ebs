## W4025. Corporate Political Engagement Policy, Governance & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program stand-up; annual review; regulatory/election cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise program (5 entities) |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance / Chief Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Legal (links to VS-100), Compliance (links to VS-119), Govt Affairs (links to VS-104), Finance (links to VS-17.4), Corporate Communications (links to VS-14.3), Board (links to VS-36), ABC (links to VS-86) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Legal & Compliance define the political-engagement policy: principles (lawful, accountable, non-partisan-by-default, transparent), the relationship to ABC (VS-86), government affairs (VS-104), ethics (VS-119), and B2G sales (VS-46); and the prohibitions (corporate contributions to candidates/parties largely prohibited under the Omnibus Election Code and RA 9006) | VP Legal & Compliance / Govt Affairs | CEO / Board Audit Committee | Annual |
| 2 | VP Legal establish governance: a political-engagement oversight (Board Audit/Risk Committee), an approval authority for any political-adjacent spend/activity, decision rights, and integration with records (VS-88), disclosure (W4032), and speak-up (VS-119) | VP Legal / Compliance | CEO | Annual |
| 3 | CEO/Board ratify the policy, approve budget/resourcing, and cascade as a board-level risk (links to VS-21.2 ERM and VS-36 governance) | CEO / Board | Board Audit Committee | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Governance (links to VS-36); legal (links to VS-100); govt affairs (links to VS-104); ABC (links to VS-86); ethics (links to VS-119); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-owner risk**: Political activity governed ad-hoc; mitigated by dedicated program and policy
- **Policy-ambiguity risk**: Unclear what's permitted; mitigated by clear principles and prohibitions


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "accountable, non-partisan-by-default, transparent" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "VP Legal establish governance" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve budget/resourcing, and cascade as a board-level risk" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-161 (ensure election-period & political-contribution compliance.)
- operational: dedicated program and policy; operational: clear principles and prohibitions

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual

## W4027. Political Contribution & Donation Policy, Approval & Disclosure

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contribution/donation request; spend decision; disclosure cycle |
| **Frequency** | On request + maintained policy |
| **Volume** | Occasional (heavily restricted) |
| **Owner** | VP Legal / CFO |
| **Participants** | Legal, Finance (links to VS-17.4), Compliance (links to VS-119), ABC (links to VS-86), Govt Affairs (links to VS-104), CEO, Board |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal/Finance maintain the political-contribution/donation policy: the general prohibition on corporate contributions to candidates/parties/election campaigns under the Omnibus Election Code and RA 9006; the narrow permitted categories (lawful CSR/community donations links to VS-25.2 with no electoral nexus); approval authority (Board for any political-adjacent spend); and documentation/disclosure requirements | Legal / Finance / Compliance | VP Legal / CFO | Maintained |
| 2 | Legal/Compliance review every request or spend that could be construed as political (sponsorships of political events, donations to political foundations, in-kind support, candidate-linked charity), apply the ABC controls (links to VS-86) and anti-graft controls (links to W4037), and route to approval or decline | Legal / Compliance | CEO / Board | Per request |
| 3 | Finance/Legal record and disclose any permitted political-adjacent spend (links to W4032 and VS-17.4), and report to the Board Audit Committee (links to VS-36) | Finance / Legal / Compliance | Board Audit Committee | Per event + periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance (links to VS-17.4); ABC (links to VS-86); ethics (links to VS-119); records (links to VS-88); governance (links to VS-36); CSR (links to VS-25.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Prohibited-contribution risk**: Corporate funds reaching a candidate/party (criminal liability); mitigated by prohibition, review, and Board authority
- **Disguised-contribution risk**: CSR/sponsorship with electoral nexus; mitigated by nexus review and anti-graft controls


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "Legal/Finance maintain the political-contribution/donation policy" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review every request or spend that could be construed as political" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record and disclose any permitted political-adjacent spend" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-161 (ensure election-period & political-contribution compliance.)
- operational: prohibition, review, and Board authority; operational: nexus review and anti-graft controls

### Time Estimate
- Maintained + per request
