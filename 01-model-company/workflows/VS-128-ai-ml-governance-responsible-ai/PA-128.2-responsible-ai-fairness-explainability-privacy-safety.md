## W3937. AI Fairness, Bias Testing & Non-Discrimination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Model build/validation (links to W3932); protected-class exposure |
| **Frequency** | Per model + periodic |
| **Volume** | Fairness testing of all rights/customer-affecting models |
| **Owner** | AI Governance / Responsible AI |
| **Participants** | AI Governance, Data Science (links to VS-30.2), Model Risk (links to VS-21), Legal (links to VS-100), Privacy (links to VS-91), business AI owners (fraud VS-125, credit/financing VS-38, HR-adjacent VS-23/VS-19) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI Governance/Data-Science define fairness metrics and protected attributes/proxies relevant to BuildRight (gender, age, region/income-proxy, mandatory-discount populations links to VS-85) per use-case: fraud-decline parity (links to VS-125), credit decision fairness (links to VS-38), pricing/promo fairness, LP/HR-adjacent model fairness (links to VS-23/VS-19) | AI Governance / Data Science / Legal | AI Governance Lead | Per model class |
| 2 | AI Governance/Data-Science run bias testing pre-deployment and in production (links to W3947), document disparities, and require mitigation (reweighting, threshold adjustment, feature removal) before approval | AI Governance / Data Science / Model Risk | AI Governance Lead | Per model |
| 3 | AI Governance/Legal document fairness outcomes for RA 10173/non-discrimination defense (links to VS-91/VS-22) and report fairness pass-rates to the AI steering and AI ethics review board (links to W3952) | AI Governance / Legal | AI Governance Lead | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- ML platform (links to VS-30.2); model registry (links to W3931); fraud (links to VS-125); credit (links to VS-38); LP/HR (links to VS-23/VS-19); privacy (links to VS-91); mandatory discount (links to VS-85)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Disparate-impact risk**: Model declines/charges customers disparately by protected group (links to VS-91/VS-85); mitigated by bias testing and mitigation
- **Proxy-discrimination risk**: Non-protected features act as proxies; mitigated by proxy analysis


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "AI Governance/Data-Science define fairness metrics and protected attributes/proxies relevant" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "document disparities, and require mitigation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "AI Governance/Legal document fairness outcomes for RA 10173/non-discrimination defense" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-615 (ensure controlled execution — Responsible AI — Fairness, Explainability, Privacy & Safety (PA-128.2).)
- operational: bias testing and mitigation; operational: proxy analysis

### Time Estimate
- ~3–10 days per model fairness cycle + periodic retest

| **Volume** | Safety/robustness for tier-1 models |

| **Participants** | AI Governance, Data Science (links to VS-30.2), Model Risk (links to VS-21), Security (links to VS-27.3), business AI owners, IT (links to VS-27) |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | AI Governance/Security govern adversarial-AI threats: prompt injection/jailbreak (GenAI), data poisoning (training), model evasion (fraud links to VS-125), model theft/extraction, and membership inference (privacy links to VS-91) | AI Governance / Security / Data Science | AI Governance Lead | Continuous |
| 3 | AI Governance define and test failover/fallback (model down → revert to deterministic rules/human), and integrate with incident management (links to W3943) and monitoring (links to W3947) | AI Governance / IT / Data Science | AI Governance Lead | Per model |

### System Touchpoints

- **Silent-failure risk**: Model degrades without detection, causing wrong decisions; mitigated by drift monitoring (links to W3947) and fail-safe
