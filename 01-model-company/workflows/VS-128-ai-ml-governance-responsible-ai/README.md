## Overview

AI/ML Governance & Responsible AI workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **enterprise
AI/ML governance and responsible-AI discipline** that controls the model risk, fairness,
explainability, privacy, safety, and human-oversight of the rapidly expanding AI/ML footprint:
fraud detection (links to VS-125 W3859), demand forecasting (links to VS-127 W3906),
personalization/recommendation (links to VS-126 W3892), pricing/markdown, inventory optimization,
loss-prevention analytics (links to VS-23), customer-service chatbots, document/OCR automation
(links to VS-30.2), computer-vision/CCTV analytics (links to VS-23), and generative-AI assistants
across the ~6,932-employee, PHP 62.3B-revenue, 5-entity operation.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**: 'AI governance' is referenced in only ~3 PA
files, 'responsible AI' in 1, 'AI ethics' in 2, and the defining terms 'model risk management'
and 'algorithmic fairness' in **zero** PA files each. AI/ML is built and operated inside VS-30.2
(AI/ML & Automation — the *engineering* of models), VS-125 (fraud models), VS-126 (CDP
personalization), VS-127 (forecasting), VS-28 (analytics), and VS-113 (architecture) — but no
value stream owns the **AI governance and responsible-AI discipline**: AI strategy and policy, the
model inventory/registry and tiering, model-risk management and independent validation, fairness/
bias testing and non-discrimination, explainability and customer disclosure, AI data-privacy and
RA 10173 profiling consent, AI safety and robustness, human oversight and intervention, generative-
AI content governance, AI incident management, ISO 42001/NIST-AI-RMF alignment and future PH AI-
regulation readiness, the AI development lifecycle with responsible-by-design controls, pre-
deployment testing/evaluation, production monitoring and drift detection, AI change/retrain/retire,
AI security/adversarial threats, and an AI ethics review board. Since the 2026-09-03 agentic
extension (sourcing model §12), PA-128.3 also owns the **agent lifecycle operations** for the
AI & Agent Platform — candidate intake & registry registration, shadow/canary evaluation with
autonomy-tier ratification, and runtime/guardrail telemetry with quarterly re-registration —
extending the model discipline to governed agents.

This is distinct from **VS-30.2 (AI/ML & Automation)** which engineers and deploys AI/ML solutions
(this value stream owns the *governance and controls* layered on top of those solutions). It is
distinct from **VS-27.3 (Cybersecurity)** which protects systems and data (this value stream owns
*AI-specific security and adversarial threats* — prompt injection, model theft, data poisoning —
working alongside security). It is distinct from **VS-91 (Consumer Data Privacy)** which owns
personal-data protection (this value stream owns the *AI-specific profiling/consent and
automated-decision* obligations under RA 10173, working alongside privacy). It is distinct from
**VS-113 (Enterprise Architecture)** which governs the application/integration landscape (this
value stream owns the *model landscape and AI-technology risk*). It is distinct from **VS-21
(Internal Audit & Risk)** which audits controls enterprise-wide (this value stream owns the *AI
risk taxonomy and model-risk framework* that audit tests against). AI/ML governance & responsible
AI is the *AI-risk and responsible-AI* discipline — distinct from AI engineering, cybersecurity,
privacy, architecture, and enterprise audit.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: AI/ML now touches revenue (pricing, promo, personalization,
forecasting), customer decisions (clienteling, credit, fraud flags), employment-adjacent decisions
(loss-prevention, scheduling), and personal data at scale (~600K loyalty members), where an
uncontrolled model can cause direct revenue loss (bad forecast → stockout/overstock), customer
harm (unfair bias, privacy breach, wrongful fraud decline), regulatory exposure (RA 10173 profiling
consent, future PH AI regulation, sector expectations), and reputational damage. Without a dedicated
owner, BuildRight risks ungoverned model sprawl, unmeasured bias/fairness, unexplainable adverse
decisions, no drift detection, no incident response, and no audit-ready model inventory — risks
that no existing value stream owns end-to-end.
