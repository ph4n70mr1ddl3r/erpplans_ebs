### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

| 1 | Global Sourcing/Procurement source and select sourcing agents: RFP, capability/country-coverage assessment, references, commercial model (commission, mark-up, fee), financial/compliance due diligence (links to VS-86.3), and shortlist/recommend per the sourcing-model decision (W3787) | Global Sourcing / Procurement / Legal | VP Merchandising | Per selection |
| 2 | Global Sourcing/Legal contract the agent: scope of authority (sourcing, vendor management, consolidation, QC, logistics), commission/fee, exclusivity/territory, IP and BuildRight-owned specifications (links to VS-100.2), anti-bribery (links to VS-86.3), data protection (links to VS-91), audit rights, termination, and KPIs | Global Sourcing / Legal | VP Merchandising / CFO | Per contract |
| 3 | Global Sourcing manage agent performance: KPI scorecard (savings, quality, on-time, vendor-base management, compliance), periodic business review, fee reconciliation, and corrective action / replacement when underperforming | Global Sourcing / Quality / Finance | VP Merchandising | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34); legal/contracts (links to VS-100.3/W688); ABC (links to VS-86.3); data privacy (links to VS-91); quality (links to VS-31); finance (links to VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Agent-capture risk**: Agent controls vendor/price visibility; mitigated by audit rights and direct vendor visibility (links to W3795/W3796)

- System auto-count of "country-coverage assessment, references, commercial model" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "Global Sourcing/Legal contract the agent" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review, fee reconciliation, and corrective action / replacement when…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-596 (ensure controlled execution — Sourcing Agent, Overseas Buying Office & Import Vendor Management (PA-122.2).)
