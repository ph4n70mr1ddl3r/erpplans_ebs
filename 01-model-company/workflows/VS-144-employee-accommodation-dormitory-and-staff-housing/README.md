## Overview

Employee Accommodation, Dormitory & Staff Housing workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning
the **workforce-housing discipline** that provides accommodation to employees at BuildRight's
~205 distributed sites (200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ) where provincial/island locations, transferees,
and rotating shift staff (including the 10 PM–6 AM night-differential window) make commute-only
staffing infeasible or a documented attrition driver. With ~6,911 employees across Mindanao,
Visayas, Luzon-outside-NCR, Metro Manila, and North/Central Luzon — and a 15–20% frontline
turnover rate — staff housing is a frontline-attrition and site-staffing lever comparable to the
daily-transport discipline now owned by VS-141.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. "Staff dormitory", "staff house",
"bunkhouse", "employee housing", and "company barracks" each appear in **zero** PA files as
dedicated workflow headers (only 5 incidental mentions across VS-07/VS-19/VS-25/VS-141). Only
adjacent coverage exists — VS-141 owns daily **commute/transport**, VS-138 owns **building**
services, VS-20.3/VS-97 own **facilities**, VS-34 buys **transactionally** — none owns the
**employee-housing program**: housing strategy/eligibility, lease-vs-own portfolio, allocation &
occupancy, resident welfare & grievance, housekeeping/security/utilities, dormitory safety
(BFP/OSH), cost recovery & salary-deduction, vendor/property-manager governance, and housing
portfolio analytics tied to retention.

This is distinct from **VS-141 (Employee Transport, Shuttle & Daily Commute)** which owns daily
**people movement** — this value stream owns the **stationary residence** where staff live. It is
distinct from **VS-138 (Integrated Facilities Management)** which owns commercial building services
for trading sites — this value stream owns **residential** operations with welfare, 24/7 occupancy,
and a resident-landlord duty of care. It is distinct from **VS-20.3/VS-97 (Facility/Real Estate)**
which own commercial buildings — this value stream owns the **employee-welfare housing portfolio**
and its cost-recovery/allocation economics. It is distinct from **VS-34 (Expense & Procurement)**
which buys transactionally — this value stream owns the structured housing program with assignment,
subsidy policy, and retention linkage. It is distinct from **VS-19 (Hire-to-Retire)** which owns the
employment relationship — this value stream owns the **physical accommodation** delivered as a
retention/staffing benefit.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: provincial/island sites (Mindanao 60 stores, Visayas 40) have
thin rental markets and limited after-hours transport; transferees, store-opening teams (VS-37),
new-province expansion, and night-shift DC/store staff need accommodation; and housing quality,
safety (BFP Fire Code, DOLE OSH on employer-provided housing), and welfare directly drive the
15–20% frontline attrition rate and the ~10–15 new-store/year expansion. An unowned housing
program leaks cost (untracked subsidy, idle units), creates welfare/safety/liability exposure, and
forfeits a primary site-staffing and retention lever.
