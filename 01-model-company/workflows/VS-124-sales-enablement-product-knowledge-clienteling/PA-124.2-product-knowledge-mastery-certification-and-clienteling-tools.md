## W3841. Product Knowledge Mastery Program & Role-Based Knowledge Standards

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W3833); capability review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + maintained |
| **Volume** | Role/department knowledge standards |
| **Owner** | Sales Enablement / Merchandising |
| **Participants** | Sales Enablement, Merchandising/Category (links to VS-01), Store Ops (links to VS-07), L&D (links to VS-19.4), Product Info (links to VS-01.3), Trade Capability (links to VS-123), Master Associates |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Enablement/Merchandising define role-based product-knowledge mastery standards per department/role (lumber, tiles, plumbing, electrical, paint, tools, appliances, hardware; cashier, sales associate, department supervisor, Pro-Desk): the must-know product attributes, applications, compatibility, common customer jobs, and selling points | Sales Enablement / Merchandising / Master Associates | Sales Enablement Lead | Annual |
| 2 | Sales Enablement/Product-Info build the knowledge base from product information (links to VS-01.3 PIM/W50, product content, specifications, SDS links to W1465), category expertise, and master-associate input, organized for quick floor reference (links to W3839) | Sales Enablement / Merchandising / Product Info | Sales Enablement Lead | Maintained |
| 3 | Sales Enablement/L&D integrate the mastery standards into the competency framework (links to VS-19.4/W683/W1127) and the apprenticeship curriculum where relevant (links to VS-123/W3810), and communicate expectations to associates | Sales Enablement / L&D | Sales Enablement Lead | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Merchandising/PIM (links to VS-01/VS-01.3); L&D/competency (links to VS-19.4/W683); enablement platform (links to W3839); apprenticeship (links to VS-123)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inconsistent-knowledge risk**: Associates know products unevenly; mitigated by role-based standards
- **Stale-knowledge risk**: Standards not updated for new SKUs; mitigated by maintenance cycle


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-communication of "communicate expectations to associates" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-602 (ensure controlled execution — Product Knowledge Mastery, Certification & Clienteling Tools (PA-124.2).)
- operational: role-based standards; operational: maintenance cycle

### Time Estimate
- Annual + maintained
