## W3833. Sales Enablement Strategy, Operating Model & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual strategy (links to VS-33.1); capability review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise sales-enablement strategy |
| **Owner** | Sales Enablement / Store Ops |
| **Participants** | Sales Enablement, Store Ops (links to VS-07), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Marketing (links to VS-14), L&D (links to VS-19.4), HR (links to VS-19/VS-103), In-Store Services (links to VS-09), Finance (links to VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Enablement/Store-Ops define the sales-enablement strategy: target selling outcomes (basket size, conversion, attach rate, trade-pro capture, service attach), the capability model for associates (product knowledge + selling skills + clienteling + tools), the operating model (central enablement team + store-level coaches), and KPIs aligned to merchandise/commercial plan (links to VS-101/VS-33.2) | Sales Enablement / Store Ops | COO / CMO | Annual |
| 2 | Sales Enablement establish governance: enablement steering (Store Ops, Merchandising, Marketing, L&D, HR), cadence, integration to L&D (links to VS-19.4), merchandising (links to VS-01.1 assortment/pricing), and marketing campaigns (links to VS-14.1), and the link to incentive/compensation (links to VS-102/W3838) | Sales Enablement / Store Ops / HR | COO | Annual |
| 3 | COO/Executive ratify the strategy, approve the enablement budget (links to W3840), and cascade to store/category leadership as a commercial priority | COO / Sales Enablement | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Store ops (links to VS-07); merchandising (links to VS-01); marketing (links to VS-14); L&D (links to VS-19.4); finance (links to VS-17.4); strategy (links to VS-33)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-owner risk**: Selling quality varies by store with no program; mitigated by enablement strategy
- **Misaligned-incentive risk**: Enablement not tied to commercial outcomes; mitigated by KPI linkage


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "capture, service attach" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the enablement budget" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-88 (ensure selling-quality & training integrity.)
- operational: enablement strategy; operational: KPI linkage

### Time Estimate
- 3–4 weeks annual

| **Volume** | Selling-skills curriculum & playbooks |
| **Owner** | Sales Enablement / L&D |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Enablement/L&D design the selling-skills curriculum: greeting/needs-discovery, consultative/needs-based selling, objection handling, closing, multi-tender/loyalty enrollment, cross-sell/upsell, and customer-follow-up — distinct from (and complementary to) the trade-product knowledge (links to W3842) and general L&D (links to VS-19.4) | Sales Enablement / L&D / Master Associates | Sales Enablement Lead | Annual |
| 2 | Sales Enablement build consultative selling playbooks for BuildRight's context: project-based selling (a customer building a bathroom/kitchen/extension needs a solution basket, not a single item), problem-solving selling (the customer describes a problem, the associate specifies the solution), and value/quality selling vs price-only — leveraging estimation (links to VS-09.2) and design (links to VS-66) | Sales Enablement / In-Store Services / Merchandising | Sales Enablement Lead | Annual |
| 3 | Sales Enablement/L&D publish the curriculum and playbooks to the enablement/OLA platform (links to W3839/VS-19.4), and refresh for new categories/services/promotions (links to VS-01.1/VS-14) | Sales Enablement / L&D | Sales Enablement Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- L&D/LMS (links to VS-19.4); enablement platform (links to W3839); in-store services (links to VS-09); merchandising (links to VS-01); design (links to VS-66)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Generic-curriculum risk**: Selling skills not tailored to BuildRight's project/category reality; mitigated by tailored playbooks
- **Adoption-gap risk**: Playbooks published but not used on the floor; mitigated by coaching (links to W3836)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-publish of "publish the curriculum and playbooks to the enablement/OLA platform" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-601 (ensure controlled execution — Sales Enablement Strategy, Content & Coaching Framework (PA-124.1).)
- operational: tailored playbooks; operational: coaching (links to W3836)

### Time Estimate
- Annual + continuous

## W3838. Sales Contest, Incentive & Motivation Program Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Commercial cycle; incentive review |
| **Frequency** | Per campaign + annual |
| **Volume** | Enterprise sales-incentive program |
| **Owner** | Sales Enablement / HR / Finance |
| **Participants** | Sales Enablement, HR/Compensation (links to VS-102), Finance (links to VS-17.4), Store Ops (links to VS-07), Marketing (links to VS-14), Payroll (links to VS-19.2), Recognition (links to W630) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Enablement/HR/Finance design sales contests and incentives aligned to commercial priorities (attach, basket size, trade-pro capture, category push, slow-mover clearance links to VS-64), with equitable access across stores/roles, lawful and within budget (links to VS-102/VS-17.4) | Sales Enablement / HR / Finance | COO / CFO | Per campaign |
| 2 | Sales Enablement/Marketing launch and communicate contests (store comms links to VS-63, recognition links to W630), track leaderboards transparently, and sustain motivation | Sales Enablement / Marketing / Store Ops | Sales Enablement Lead | Per campaign |
| 3 | Sales Enablement/Payroll administer incentive payout (links to VS-19.2/W1001 profit-sharing where applicable), measure contest ROI (sales lift vs incentive cost — links to W3849), and iterate the incentive design | Sales Enablement / Finance / Payroll | Sales Enablement Lead | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Compensation (links to VS-102); payroll (links to VS-19.2); finance (links to VS-17.4); store comms (links to VS-63); recognition (links to W630); marketing (links to VS-14)

- **Gaming risk**: Incentives drive undesirable behavior (e.g., over-selling, discount abuse); mitigated by design and controls (links to VS-23/W1475)

- System auto-track of "track leaderboards transparently, and sustain motivation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-measure of "measure contest ROI" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-601 (ensure controlled execution — Sales Enablement Strategy, Content & Coaching Framework (PA-124.1).)
- operational: contest rules pre-cleared against discount-abuse controls (W1475); normalized scoring for store-size equity
