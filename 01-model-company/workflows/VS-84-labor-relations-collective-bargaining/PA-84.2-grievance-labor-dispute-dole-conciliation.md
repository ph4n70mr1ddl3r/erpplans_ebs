## W2881. Grievance Intake, Classification & Step-1 Conference

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee or union files grievance |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~5–20 grievances/month |
| **Volume** | All employees |
| **Owner** | HR Manager |
| **Participants** | HR, Employee, Union Steward, Supervisor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR receives grievance (written/verbal via union or individual); logs in grievance case system; classifies (wage, discipline, working condition, interpretation) | HR | HR Manager | Per grievance |
| 2 | HR convenes Step-1 conference with employee, union steward, and immediate supervisor within CBA-prescribed timeline (often 5–7 days) | HR | HR Manager | Per grievance |
| 3 | HR documents Step-1 decision; communicates to employee/union; escalates to Step-2 if unresolved | HR | HR Manager | Per grievance |

### System Touchpoints
- Grievance case system; CBA-prescribed timelines; documentation repository

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Process risk**: Missed CBA timeline forfeits management defense; mitigated by SLA-bounded case system


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs in grievance case system" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documents Step-1 decision" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-217 (ensure labor-relations statutory compliance.)
- operational: SLA-bounded case system

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 hours per grievance

## W5497. POSH / Safe Spaces Compliance Program — CODI Committee, Statutory Investigation & Reporting (RA 7877 / RA 11313)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | (a) Report/complaint of sexual harassment or gender-based sexual harassment (GBSH) — work-related superior/subordinate, peer-to-peer, client/customer-to-employee, or online conduct against an employee; (b) annual CODI constitution/reconstitution cycle; (c) policy or regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Continuous intake; annual committee constitution and code re-publication; case-driven investigations. Formal cases: ~10–30/year chain-wide (benchmark range for a 6,911-employee multi-site retailer, understated by underreporting without a trusted channel) |
| **Volume** | All 6,911 employees across 205 sites + ~10–20% contingent workers (VS-98) are in scope; both laws apply to every BuildRight entity with 10+ employees (all 205 sites qualify). One CODI per legal entity (5), each supported by trained case handlers; awareness training reaches 100% of employees annually (~6,911 completions) |
| **Owner** | Labor Relations Director |
| **Participants** | CODI Chair & Members (HR, supervisory, rank-and-file, union representative where a CBA unit exists), HR Manager, CHRO, Legal Counsel (VS-100), EAP Manager/OH Nurse (VS-83), IT Security (online-conduct evidence, VS-27), DOLE (reporting/jurisdictional interfaces) |

### Background

RA 7877 (Anti-Sexual Harassment Act, 1995) obliges employers to promulgate rules and constitute a **Committee on Decorum and Investigation (CODI)** for sexual-harassment cases involving authority/influence relationships. RA 11313 (Safe Spaces Act, 2019) expanded coverage to **peer, client/customer-to-employee, and online gender-based sexual harassment** and requires employers with 10+ employees to adopt a code of conduct and investigation machinery — retail is acutely exposed because ~5,800 frontline staff serve 2.8M monthly customer interactions. Prior coverage was step-level only: the annual anti-harassment refresher inside W719 (VS-19.1 DEI) and generic grievance intake in W2881 — no workflow owned the statutory CODI machinery. Distinct from: W2881/W2882 (CBA grievance steps), W2884 (general disciplinary investigation — this workflow's statutory sibling with its own committee and confidentiality regime), W2943/VS-119 (ethics/ABC speak-up), W1733 (channel administration), VS-159.3 (security/workplace-violence investigations), VS-100 (litigation once cases escalate).

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Annual CODI constitution per entity: chair + members representing management, supervisory, and rank-and-file levels (plus the union representative where a bargaining unit exists — links to VS-84.1), with at least one member of each gender; publish committee membership, reporting channels, and the anti-harassment code on notice boards, ESS portal, and store huddle boards (links to VS-103.2 W3339) | Labor Relations Director / HR Manager | CHRO | 3–5 days/year |
| 2 | Code of conduct maintenance: definitions under RA 7877 (authority/influence SH) and RA 11313 (peer, client-to-employee, online GBSH — including customer conduct toward cashiers, promodisers, and service-desk staff), examples, sanctions matrix, confidentiality/gag-order obligations, and whistleblower protection; annual employee acknowledgment captured in HR records (per W51 pattern) | Labor Relations Director / Legal | CHRO | 2–3 days/year |
| 3 | Multi-channel intake: in-person (any manager, HR, or CODI member), ESS confidential form, hotline, or sealed written report; acknowledge within 24 hours; apply interim measures (schedule/location adjustment of the *respondent*, not the complainant; preventive suspension per W2883 where warranted); enforce gag order on disclosure of identities | HR Manager / CODI Chair | Labor Relations Director | 1–2 days |
| 4 | Jurisdiction & classification: determine RA 7877 vs RA 11313 track; route adjacent matters — general grievances (W2881), ethics/corruption (VS-119), criminal conduct to Legal (VS-100) and PNP, customer-safety violence (VS-159.3); confirm CODI jurisdiction and statutory investigation clock | CODI Chair / Legal | Labor Relations Director | 1–3 days |
| 5 | Investigation: documentary and interview evidence (complainant, respondent, witnesses) with RA 10173 safeguards (VS-91); written interview summaries; preservation of online-conduct evidence (messages, screenshots, posts) with IT Security chain-of-custody (VS-27); target completion within the statutory period (≤30 days from formal complaint) | CODI Members / Legal | CODI Chair | 15–25 days/case |
| 6 | Hearing & determination: both parties heard separately; CODI deliberates on preponderance of evidence; written report with findings, aggravating/mitigating factors, and recommended sanction aligned to the sanctions matrix; served with due-process notices (2-notice rule per W2883/W2884) | CODI Chair / Members | CHRO | 3–5 days |
| 7 | Decision implementation & appeal: management implements sanction (reprimand to dismissal), records in the disciplinary file; internal appeal per policy; Legal explains criminal/civil options to the complainant (VS-100); SENA referral if the matter ripens into a labor dispute (W2885) | HR Manager / Legal | CHRO | 2–5 days |
| 8 | Remediation & non-retaliation monitoring: workplace restoration (transfers, team reintegration), 6–12-month retaliation watch (assignments, ratings, schedules), EAP counseling for the complainant and affected witnesses (W2865), warning/supervision for substantiated-but-minor findings | HR Manager / CODI Chair | Labor Relations Director | 6–12 months/case |
| 9 | Statutory reporting & records: DOLE reportorial/submission requirements as they apply to harassment cases and committee operations; anonymized quarterly case statistics to the Audit Committee (W1721); confidential case files retained per VS-88 retention schedule in a restricted-access class separate from ordinary personnel files | Labor Relations Director / Corporate Secretary | CHRO | 2–3 days/quarter |
| 10 | Annual program review: case analytics (types, locations, customer-vs-employee origin, cycle time), root-cause and hot-spot analysis with LP/VS-23 where patterns indicate risk, refresher-training refresh with W719, policy/code update, and awareness campaign reinforcement at town halls (W3340/W2890) | Labor Relations Director / HR Manager | CHRO | 3–5 days/year |

### System Touchpoints
- HR case management: confidential POSH/GBSH case type with restricted-access role (complainant/respondent identities masked in general case lists), statutory-clock SLA timer, and evidence attachments with chain-of-custody metadata
- ESS confidential reporting form (anonymous option for initial intake) routed only to CODI chair + Labor Relations Director
- Policy-acknowledgment tracking object for the annual code re-publication (W51-pattern completion records)
- Disciplinary-action object for sanction posting (integration with W2884 due-process notices)
- Anonymized case-statistics extract to the board-committee reporting pack (W1721)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Retaliation risk**: complainants transferred, downgraded, or frozen out after filing — defeats the statute's purpose and creates NLRC exposure; mitigated by 6–12-month retaliation watch tied to the non-retaliation audit (W356) and manager accountability
- **Confidentiality/gag-order breach**: identities leaked through case files or gossip — re-traumatizes complainants and deters reporting; mitigated by restricted-access case class, masked case lists, and sanctioned breach handling
- **Misclassification risk**: treating an RA 11313 customer-to-employee GBSH complaint as an ordinary customer-complaint (VS-13) or a W2881 grievance forfeits statutory protection; mitigated by the step-4 jurisdiction gate and CODI training
- **Underreporting**: benchmark incidence suggests retail harassment reports understate reality; mitigated by trusted multi-channel intake, visible CODI membership at store level, and annual awareness campaigns
- **Evidence spoliation**: online-conduct evidence (deleted chats/posts) lost before preservation; mitigated by immediate IT Security preservation request with chain-of-custody logging
- **CODI capacity/conflict**: committee members investigating their own chain of command; mitigated by cross-entity member pools and external counsel support for senior-respondent cases

### Automation Opportunity
- Case-intake form auto-creates the confidential case record with the statutory clock started and restricted-access flag applied (replaces manual Step 3 registration).
- SLA timer escalates to the CODI chair and CHRO when an investigation approaches the statutory deadline (supports Step 5).
- Anonymized case-statistics dashboard auto-assembles the quarterly board-committee extract (replaces manual Step 9 compilation).

### Controls
- CTL-482 (ensure controlled execution — Grievance Handling, Labor Dispute & DOLE Conciliation (PA-84.2).)
