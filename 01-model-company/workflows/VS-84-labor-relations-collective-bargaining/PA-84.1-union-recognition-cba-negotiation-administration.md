## W2873. Labor Organization Certification, Recognition & Verification (DOLE)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Petition for certification election or voluntary recognition request |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 events/decade |
| **Owner** | Labor Relations Director |
| **Participants** | LR Director, Legal, HR, DOLE, Union |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LR Director receives petition for certification election or voluntary recognition request; validates the petitioner's legitimate-labor-organization status and bargaining-unit definition (a petition by an LLO is automatically ordered conducted per RA 9481 — no minimum support showing) | LR Director | VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |
| 2 | LR Director/Legal files with the DOLE Regional Office (Med-Arbiter, per D.O. 40-03) for certification election; participates in pre-election conference; mediates bargaining-unit definition | LR Director / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Weeks |
| 3 | HR conducts certification election (with DOLE); notifies results; recognizes certified union as exclusive bargaining representative upon majority vote | HR / DOLE | VP Legal & Compliance | Per election |

### System Touchpoints
- Labor relations case file; DOLE Regional Office / Bureau of Labor Relations records; bargaining-unit roster

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Unfair labor practice (ULP) charge for interference; mitigated by strict legal process and neutrality


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "validates the petitioner's legitimate-labor-organization status" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "files with the DOLE Regional Office (Med-Arbiter, per D.O. 40-03)" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-481 (ensure controlled execution — Union Recognition, CBA Negotiation & Administration (PA-84.1).)
- operational: strict legal process and neutrality

### Time Estimate
- Weeks to months per certification

## W2874. CBA Negotiation Preparation, Data Assembly & Bargaining Strategy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | CBA expiry (60-day pre-expiry notice) |
| **Frequency** | Every 5 years (CBA term) + 3-year wage reopening |
| **Volume** | Per recognized bargaining unit |
| **Owner** | Labor Relations Director |
| **Participants** | LR Director, Finance, HR, Legal, Management Panel |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LR Director assembles negotiation team (management panel); reviews prior CBA, grievances, and labor market benchmarks | LR Director | VP Legal & Compliance | Pre-negotiation |
| 2 | Finance prepares costing model for union proposals: wage increase, benefits, allowances, incentive plan; estimates 5-year financial impact | Finance | CFO | Pre-negotiation |
| 3 | Management approves bargaining mandate, reservation points, and strike contingency plan; LR Director finalizes strategy | Management / LR Director | CEO | Pre-negotiation |

### System Touchpoints
- CBA costing model; labor-market benchmark; bargaining mandate record

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Negotiation risk**: Inadequate preparation concedes value; mitigated by rigorous costing and mandate clarity


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews prior CBA, grievances, and labor market benchmarks" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "prepares costing model for union proposals" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves bargaining mandate, reservation points, and strike contingency plan" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-217 (ensure labor-relations statutory compliance.)
- operational: rigorous costing and mandate clarity

### Time Estimate
- 4–8 weeks preparation

## W2875. CBA Negotiation Sessions, Deadlock Management & DOLE Conciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | CBA negotiation cycle |
| **Frequency** | Per CBA cycle (5-year + reopeners) |
| **Volume** | Multiple sessions |
| **Owner** | Labor Relations Director |
| **Participants** | LR Director, Management Panel, Union Panel, DOLE Conciliator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LR Director conducts bargaining sessions per Labor Code (meets within 30 days of notice); bargains in good faith; documents positions | LR Director | VP Legal & Compliance | Per session |
| 2 | On deadlock, LR Director files notice of strike/lockout or submits to DOLE conciliation; participates in preventive mediation | LR Director / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per deadlock |
| 3 | Management executes contingency plan (operations continuity, temporary staffing, security) during impasse; seeks resolution before strike deadline | Management / BCM | CEO | Per deadlock |

### System Touchpoints
- Negotiation session record; DOLE conciliation case file; BCM continuity plan

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Operational risk**: Strike disrupts retail operations; mitigated by good-faith bargaining and contingency planning


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "conducts bargaining sessions per Labor Code" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "files notice of strike/lockout or submits to DOLE conciliation" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-481 (ensure controlled execution — Union Recognition, CBA Negotiation & Administration (PA-84.1).)
- operational: good-faith bargaining and contingency planning

### Time Estimate
- Weeks to months per negotiation

## W2876. CBA Drafting, Ratification, Registration & Publication

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Negotiation agreement (Terms of Settlement) |
| **Frequency** | Per CBA cycle |
| **Volume** | Per bargaining unit |
| **Owner** | Labor Relations Director |
| **Participants** | LR Director, Legal, Union, DOLE |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal/LR Director drafts CBA text from agreed terms; reviews for legal compliance and consistency with company policy and Labor Code | Legal / LR Director | VP Legal & Compliance | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | Union ratifies CBA with membership vote; LR Director verifies ratification; both parties sign | Union / LR Director | VP Legal & Compliance | Per ratification |
| 3 | LR Director registers CBA with DOLE Regional Office within 5 days of signing; publishes for employees; archives executed copy | LR Director | VP Legal & Compliance | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- CBA repository; DOLE CBA registration portal; employee communication

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Unregistered CBA lacks enforceability; mitigated by 5-day registration discipline


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews for legal compliance and consistency with company policy and Labor" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-register of "registers CBA with DOLE Regional Office within 5 days of signing" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-217 (ensure labor-relations statutory compliance.)
- operational: 5-day registration discipline

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 weeks per cycle

## W2877. CBA Implementation, Wage/Benefit Administration & Compliance Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | CBA effective date; ongoing compliance |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + per CBA provision |
| **Volume** | All CBA-covered employees |
| **Owner** | HR Manager |
| **Participants** | HR, Payroll, Finance, LR Director |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR/Payroll implements CBA provisions: wage scales, step increases, allowances, incentives, leave, benefits in payroll and HR systems | HR / Payroll | HR Manager | Per effective date |
| 2 | HR tracks compliance with each CBA article (grievance procedure, union rights, no-retaliation, security of tenure); audits implementation | HR | HR Manager | Quarterly |
| 3 | LR Director monitors compliance exceptions; resolves disputes over interpretation; documents agreed interpretations as CBA side-agreements | LR Director | VP Legal & Compliance | Per dispute |

### System Touchpoints
- Payroll/HR CBA configuration; compliance tracker; side-agreement repository

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Non-implementation invites ULP/grievance; mitigated by implementation audit


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-track of "tracks compliance with each CBA article" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "documents agreed interpretations as CBA side-agreements" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-217 (ensure labor-relations statutory compliance.)
- operational: implementation audit

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; 1–2 weeks per major provision

## W2879. Union Dues Check-Off, Agency Fee & Authorization Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee authorization / payroll cycle |
| **Frequency** | Per payroll cycle |
| **Volume** | All union members |
| **Owner** | Payroll Manager |
| **Participants** | Payroll, HR, Union, LR Director, VP Legal & Compliance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR captures employee check-off authorization (written, revocable); maintains authorization records per Labor Code requirements | HR | HR Manager | Per authorization |
| 2 | Payroll deducts union dues/agency fee per authorization; remits to union within timeframe prescribed by law/CBA; reports remittance | Payroll | Payroll Manager | Per payroll |
| 3 | LR Director reconciles remittance with union; resolves disputes; audits authorization currency annually | LR Director | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Check-off authorization records; payroll deduction; remittance reconciliation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Non-remission or unauthorized deduction; mitigated by authorization discipline and reconciliation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "records per Labor Code requirements" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-report of "Payroll deducts union dues/agency fee per authorization" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconciles remittance with union" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-217 (ensure labor-relations statutory compliance.)
- operational: authorization discipline and reconciliation

### Time Estimate
- Per payroll cycle; 1–2 days/year audit
