## W2889. Employee Engagement Survey, Pulse & Sentiment Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual + quarterly pulse |
| **Frequency** | Annual + quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~6,911 employees |
| **Owner** | HR Analytics Lead |
| **Participants** | HR Analytics, LR Director, Communications |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR Analytics designs engagement survey and quarterly pulse; ensures anonymity and Data Privacy Act compliance | HR Analytics Lead | CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | HR Analytics deploys survey across all locations (mobile/kiosk for non-desk workers); tracks participation; analyzes results by segment | HR Analytics | CHRO | Per cycle |
| 3 | LR Director/Communications shares results with management and LMC; co-designs action plans with employees/union; tracks improvement | LR Director / Communications | VP Legal & Compliance | Per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Survey platform; sentiment analytics; action-plan tracker

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Engagement risk**: Low participation / no action erodes trust; mitigated by anonymity and visible follow-through


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-track of "HR Analytics deploys survey across all locations" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-483 (ensure controlled execution — Employee Voice, Engagement & Partnership Management (PA-84.3).)
- operational: anonymity and visible follow-through

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 weeks per cycle

- System auto-monitor of "monitors for retaliation claims" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-483 (ensure controlled execution — Employee Voice, Engagement & Partnership Management (PA-84.3).)
