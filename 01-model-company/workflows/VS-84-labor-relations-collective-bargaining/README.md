## Overview

Labor Relations & Collective Bargaining Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
covering the full labor-relations lifecycle across 6,932 employees, including union recognition
and certification, Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA) negotiation and administration,
grievance handling, DOLE conciliation/mediation/arbitration, labor dispute avoidance, and
constructive employee-partner (union/labor coalition) engagement under the Philippine Labor Code.

While Hire-to-Retire (VS-19) owns day-to-day HR operations and W79 handles the basic
whistleblower/grievance intake, this value stream owns the **labor relations program**:
collective representation, CBA economics and compliance, formal grievance arbitration, and
partnership governance with recognized labor organizations.
