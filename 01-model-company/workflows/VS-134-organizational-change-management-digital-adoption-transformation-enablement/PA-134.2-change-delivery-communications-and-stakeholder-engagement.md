## W4081. Change Communications Strategy, Messaging & Channel Plan

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Initiative charter; major milestone |
| **Frequency** | Per initiative + ongoing |
| **Volume** | Per major initiative (audiences: ~6,911 employees, vendors, B2B customers, board) |
| **Owner** | OCM Lead / Corporate Comms (links to VS-14.3) |
| **Participants** | Comms (VS-14.3/VS-103.1), Internal Comms, sponsor, HR (VS-103) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OCM/Comms build the comms strategy: the change story/vision, key messages by audience and phase (awareness→understanding→adoption→sustainment), channels (town hall, huddle, app, email, video, store signage), and cadence | OCM / Internal Comms | OCM Lead | Per initiative |
| 2 | OCM/Comms align messaging with corporate comms (VS-14.3) and store comms/task (VS-63), ensure two-way channels (Q&A, feedback), and pre-clear regulatory/legal-sensitive messaging (links to VS-22/VS-100) | OCM / Comms / Legal | OCM Lead | Per initiative |
| 3 | OCM/Comms execute, measure reach/understanding, and adjust | OCM / Comms | Sponsor | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Comms platforms (links to VS-14.3/VS-103.1/VS-63); intranet/app (VS-75); engagement analytics (W4091)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Noise risk**: Comms lost in clutter; mitigated by channel discipline and manager cascade (W4083)
- **Spin risk**: Over-promising; mitigated by honest messaging and Legal/Compliance pre-clearance

### Automation Opportunity
- Multi-channel comms orchestration; reach/understanding analytics

### Controls
- CTL-633 (ensure controlled execution — Change Delivery, Communications & Stakeholder Engagement (PA-134.2).)
- CTL-633: legal/compliance pre-clearance for sensitive messaging

### Time Estimate
- Per initiative + ongoing execution

### Cross-references
- links to VS-14.3 (comms), VS-63 (store comms), VS-75 (app), VS-22/VS-100 (legal/compliance)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## W4085. Resistance Management & Change Intervention

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Resistance signals (survey, adoption data, manager feedback) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | Per initiative / per audience |
| **Owner** | OCM Lead |
| **Participants** | Sponsor, HR (VS-103), managers, Labor Relations (VS-84 where relevant) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OCM detect resistance (adoption analytics W4091, survey W4077, manager/super-user feedback), diagnose root (fear, capability, process disagreement, trust, overload), and segment | OCM / HR | OCM Lead | Continuous |
| 2 | OCM design interventions (targeted comms/training, manager conversation, process adjustment, quick wins, address legitimate concerns), engage Labor Relations for collective-bargaining-impacting change (links to VS-84) | OCM / HR / Labor Relations | Sponsor | Per signal |
| 3 | OCM monitor intervention effectiveness, escalate persistent/blocking resistance to sponsor (W4079), and capture lessons (W4096) | OCM | Sponsor | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Adoption analytics (W4091); survey (W4077); HR case mgmt (VS-103); labor relations (VS-84)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Ignored-resistance risk**: Signals missed until adoption fails; mitigated by continuous detection
- **Coercion risk**: Forcing change without addressing legitimate concerns; mitigated by root-cause diagnosis and good-faith response

### Automation Opportunity
- Resistance-signal detection from adoption/feedback data; intervention tracking

### Controls
- CTL-633 (ensure controlled execution — Change Delivery, Communications & Stakeholder Engagement (PA-134.2).)
- CTL-633: legitimate concerns addressed; labor-relations engagement for CBA-impacting change

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; per intervention

### Cross-references
- links to VS-84 (labor relations), VS-103 (HR cases), W4091 (adoption)

| **Participants** | IT (VS-27), PMO (VS-112), service desk, champions (W4084), process owners, BCP (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | OCM/IT execute cutover with on-site/floor-walking support, super-users (W4084), expanded service desk, and real-time issue triage; protect frontline trading (links to VS-07/VS-08) | OCM / IT / Champions | Sponsor | Per go-live |

- Readiness-checklist automation; real-time adoption/issue dashboard during hypercare

### Controls
- CTL-633 (ensure controlled execution — Change Delivery, Communications & Stakeholder Engagement (PA-134.2).)

| **Frequency** | Per initiative + ongoing |
| **Volume** | Per major cross-functional transformation |
| **Owner** | CHRO / PMO (VS-112) |
| **Participants** | All affected function VPs, OpEx (VS-133), IT (VS-27/VS-113), HR (VS-103) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CHRO/PMO establish the cross-functional governance (workstream leads, RACI, integration points) for transformations spanning functions (e.g., omnichannel = stores+supply+ecommerce+IT+finance) | CHRO / PMO | Transformation Board | Per initiative |

- **Handoff risk**: Gaps at function boundaries; mitigated by RACI and integration points

### Automation Opportunity
- Cross-functional dependency mapping; integrated status dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-633 (ensure controlled execution — Change Delivery, Communications & Stakeholder Engagement (PA-134.2).)

### Time Estimate
