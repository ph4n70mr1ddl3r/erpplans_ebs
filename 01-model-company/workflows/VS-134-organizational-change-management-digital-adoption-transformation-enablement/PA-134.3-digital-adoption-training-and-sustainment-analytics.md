## W4089. Digital Adoption Strategy & In-App Guidance (Digital Adoption Platform)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Major system deployment (ERP, POS, ecommerce, WMS, CRM) |
| **Frequency** | Per deployment + maintained |
| **Volume** | Enterprise systems (ERP for ~6,925 users; POS for cashiers; CRM/app for staff) |
| **Owner** | OCM Lead / IT Adoption Lead (links to VS-27) |
| **Participants** | IT (VS-27), Enterprise Architecture (VS-113), L&D (VS-19.4), process owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OCM/IT define the digital-adoption strategy: a Digital Adoption Platform (DAP) for in-app walkthroughs/contextual help, self-service enablement, and usage analytics across the ERP, POS, ecommerce, and WMS | OCM / IT / EA | CHRO / CIO | Per deployment |
| 2 | OCM/IT build role/role-based in-app guidance, smart tips, and onboarding tours, and integrate with training (W4090) and the service desk (VS-27.1) | OCM / IT / L&D | OCM Lead | Per deployment |
| 3 | OCM/IT measure adoption lift from DAP, optimize content, and sustain as systems evolve | OCM / IT | OCM Lead | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Digital Adoption Platform (DAP) over ERP/POS/WMS/ecommerce; LMS (VS-19.4); service desk (VS-27.1); analytics (W4091)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Tool-sprawl risk**: DAP adds complexity; mitigated by EA governance (VS-113) and integration
- **Generic-help risk**: Help not tailored to role/task; mitigated by role-based guidance

### Automation Opportunity
- Context-aware in-app guidance; usage-driven content optimization

### Controls
- CTL-634 (ensure controlled execution — Digital Adoption, Training & Sustainment Analytics (PA-134.3).)
- CTL-634: DAP content governance; data-privacy for usage analytics (links to VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- Per deployment; ongoing optimization

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27 (IT), VS-113 (EA), VS-91 (privacy), VS-19.4 (L&D)

## W4091. User Adoption Measurement, System Usage & Adoption Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Go-live; continuous |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (daily/weekly) |
| **Volume** | Enterprise systems (~6,925 users across ERP/POS/WMS/CRM/app) |
| **Owner** | OCM Data Lead / BI (links to VS-28) |
| **Participants** | BI (VS-28), IT (VS-27), HR analytics (VS-103.3), process owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OCM/BI define adoption metrics (active users, feature usage, process compliance, time-on-system, error/exception rates, support-ticket volume) and baselines, by role/site | OCM / BI | CHRO | Per system |
| 2 | OCM/BI build the adoption dashboard, detect laggards/exceptions, and correlate adoption to benefit (W4095) and to resistance signals (W4085) | OCM / BI | OCM Lead | Continuous |
| 3 | OCM report adoption to sponsors/leadership, trigger interventions where adoption lags, and track to sustainment targets | OCM | Sponsor / COO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- BI/analytics (VS-28); system usage logs (VS-27); DAP analytics (W4089); process mining (VS-133 W4058)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Activity-vs-outcome risk**: High usage but poor outcomes; mitigated by correlation to benefit/process KPIs
- **Privacy risk**: User-level monitoring perceived as surveillance; mitigated by aggregation and policy (links to VS-91)

### Automation Opportunity
- Real-time adoption analytics; exception-driven intervention triggers

### Controls
- CTL-634 (ensure controlled execution — Digital Adoption, Training & Sustainment Analytics (PA-134.3).)
- CTL-634: adoption analytics aggregated/role-level with privacy policy (VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; dashboard build ~4–8 weeks

### Cross-references
- links to VS-28 (BI), VS-27 (IT), VS-91 (privacy), VS-133 W4058 (mining)

## W4092. Behavioral Change & Process Compliance Sustainment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-go-live; compliance drift detected |
| **Frequency** | Continuous post-go-live |
| **Volume** | Per changed process across 200 stores/4 DCs/HQ |
| **Owner** | Process Owner / OCM Lead |
| **Participants** | Process owners, OpEx (VS-133), Quality (VS-31), managers, Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OCM/process owner sustain the change: monitor process compliance (via process mining VS-133 W4058 and adoption W4091), and detect reversion to old ways | OCM / Process Owner / OpEx | Process Owner | Continuous |
| 2 | OCM/OpEx intervene on non-compliance (coaching, re-training, barrier removal, control reinforcement W4063), and address systemic causes with OpEx (VS-133) | OCM / OpEx / Managers | Process Owner | Per signal |
| 3 | OCM/process owner embed the change into standard work/SOPs (VS-30.3), performance expectations, and onboarding, then transition fully to BAU | OCM / Process Owner / HR | Process Owner | Post-go-live |

### System Touchpoints
- Process mining (VS-133 W4058); SOP library (VS-30.3); adoption analytics (W4091); quality (VS-31)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reversion risk**: Old behaviors return post-go-live; mitigated by compliance monitoring and controls
- **Policing risk**: Sustainment becomes punitive; mitigated by coaching-first and barrier removal

### Automation Opportunity
- Compliance drift detection; automated coaching triggers

### Controls
- CTL-126 (ensure change adoption & sustainment.)
- CTL-634: process compliance monitored; control reinforcement (VS-133 W4063)

### Time Estimate
- Continuous for 60–180 days post-go-live, then BAU

### Cross-references
- links to VS-133 (OpEx), VS-30.3 (SOP), VS-31 (quality), VS-21 (audit)

## W4093. Post-Implementation Reinforcement & Continuous Improvement Loop

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-go-live stabilization |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | Per deployed change |
| **Owner** | OCM Lead / OpEx (VS-133) |
| **Participants** | OpEx (VS-133), process owners, IT (VS-27), users, champions (W4084) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OCM/OpEx run a structured post-implementation review (what worked, adoption gaps, unintended consequences, fix backlog) at +30/+60/+90 days | OCM / OpEx / Process Owner | CHRO / COO | Post-go-live |
| 2 | OCM/OpEx feed fixes/improvements into the OpEx pipeline (VS-133 W4052) and the system backlog (VS-27/VS-113), and close the loop with users | OCM / OpEx / IT | Process Owner | Continuous |
| 3 | OCM/OpEx ensure the change is continuously improved (not frozen), and hand sustained improvement to OpEx (VS-133) | OCM / OpEx | COO | Post-go-live |

### System Touchpoints
- OpEx pipeline (VS-133); IT backlog (VS-27/VS-113); feedback channels (W4091)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Frozen-change risk**: Deployed change never improved; mitigated by the improvement loop
- **Loop-not-closed risk**: Feedback collected but not acted on; mitigated by backlog accountability

### Automation Opportunity
- Post-implementation review templating; feedback-to-backlog automation

### Controls
- CTL-634 (ensure controlled execution — Digital Adoption, Training & Sustainment Analytics (PA-134.3).)
- CTL-634: +30/+60/+90 reviews documented; fix-backlog tracked

### Time Estimate
- Post-go-live reviews; ongoing

### Cross-references
- links to VS-133 (OpEx), VS-27/VS-113 (IT backlog)

## W4095. Change Benefit Realization & Adoption ROI Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Initiative benefit case; post-go-live |
| **Frequency** | Per initiative + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Enterprise transformation portfolio |
| **Owner** | CHRO / Finance (links to VS-17.4) |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17.4), PMO (VS-112), sponsors, OpEx (VS-133), IT/TBM (VS-135) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CHRO/Finance define the benefit case per initiative (incl. the adoption-dependency: what benefit requires what adoption level), and baseline | CHRO / Finance | Sponsor / CFO | Per initiative |

### System Touchpoints
- Benefit tracking (VS-133 W4065 / VS-135); FP&A (VS-17.4); adoption analytics (W4091); governance (VS-36)

- **Unattributed-benefit risk**: Benefit claimed without adoption causation; mitigated by adoption-dependency modeling
- **Decay risk**: Benefit decays as adoption slips; mitigated by sustainment (W4092)

### Automation Opportunity
- Benefit-adoption correlation analytics; ROI dashboards

### Controls
- CTL-126 (ensure change adoption & sustainment.)
- CTL-634: benefit reconciled by Finance; adoption-dependency documented

### Time Estimate
- Per initiative + quarterly

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-133 W4065 (benefit), VS-135 (TBM), VS-36 (governance)
