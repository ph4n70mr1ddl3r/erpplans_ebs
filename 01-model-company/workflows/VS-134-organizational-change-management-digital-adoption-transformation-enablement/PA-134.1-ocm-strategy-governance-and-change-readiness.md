## W4073. OCM Strategy, Operating Model & Transformation Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program stand-up; transformation portfolio established; annual review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on major transformation |
| **Volume** | Enterprise OCM function (5 entities, ~6,911 employees, 200 stores) |
| **Owner** | CHRO |
| **Participants** | CEO, CHRO (links to VS-103), PMO (VS-112), Innovation/Digital (VS-30), OpEx (VS-133), Communications (VS-14.3/VS-103.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CHRO define the OCM operating model: a central CoE with embedded change leads on major programs; the relationship to the PMO (VS-112), OpEx (VS-133), HR (VS-103), and Comms (VS-14.3); and the enterprise change taxonomy | CHRO | CEO | Annual |
| 2 | CHRO author the change governance: the transformation board, decision rights, stage-gates that include change-readiness criteria, and benefit-realization linkage (VS-112/VS-133) | CHRO / PMO | CEO | Annual |
| 3 | CEO/Board ratify and cascade; integrate change-readiness reporting into governance (VS-36) | CEO / Board | Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Transformation/PMO platform (links to VS-112); governance (VS-36); HR (VS-103); comms (VS-103.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-owner risk**: Change happens *to* people with no owner of the people-side; mitigated by chartered CoE
- **Fragmentation risk**: Every project invents its own change approach; mitigated by enterprise taxonomy and governance

### Automation Opportunity
- Change-readiness criteria embedded in project stage-gates; transformation-portfolio dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-126 (ensure change adoption & sustainment.)
- CTL-632: change-readiness gate before go-live; transformation-board oversight

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual

### Cross-references
- links to VS-112 (PMO), VS-133 (OpEx), VS-30 (digital), VS-103 (HR), VS-36 (governance)

## W4074. Change Portfolio Prioritization & Capacity Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Portfolio intake; capacity strain; quarterly planning |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Enterprise change portfolio (ERP, omnichannel, remodels VS-109, net-zero VS-108, statutory, process improvements VS-133) |
| **Owner** | CHRO / Transformation Board |
| **Participants** | PMO (VS-112), function VPs, HR workforce planning (VS-103.3), OpEx (VS-133) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CHRO/PMO maintain the enterprise change portfolio (every initiative changing how people work), assess cumulative change load by audience (store/DC/HQ/function), and identify saturation/overload | CHRO / PMO / HR | Transformation Board | Continuous |
| 2 | CHRO lead prioritization and sequencing: sequence to absorb capacity, de-conflict simultaneous impacts on the same audience, and protect frontline trading (peak seasons links to VS-07) | CHRO / PMO | CEO / COO | Quarterly |
| 3 | CHRO report change load and capacity to the transformation board, and recommend defer/accelerate/combine decisions | CHRO | Transformation Board | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Portfolio/PMO platform (VS-112); workforce planning (VS-103.3); audience-impact map (W4076)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Saturation risk**: Too many changes at once → adoption collapse; mitigated by capacity planning and sequencing
- **Trade-disruption risk**: Changes deployed during peak harm sales; mitigated by change-windows aligned to retail calendar (VS-07)

### Automation Opportunity
- Audience-level change-load analytics; auto-detection of overlapping impacts

### Controls
- CTL-632 (ensure controlled execution — OCM Strategy, Governance & Change Readiness Assessment (PA-134.1).)
- CTL-632: change-window policy; transformation-board sequencing approval

### Time Estimate
- Continuous + quarterly review

### Cross-references
- links to VS-112 (PMO), VS-103.3 (workforce), VS-07 (store ops/seasons), VS-109 (remodels)

## W4075. Stakeholder Mapping, Analysis & Engagement Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Initiative charter; major change |
| **Frequency** | Per initiative + maintained |
| **Volume** | Per major initiative (audiences: executives, store managers, associates, DC staff, vendors, B2B customers) |
| **Owner** | OCM Lead |
| **Participants** | Sponsor, initiative team, HR (VS-103), Comms (VS-14.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OCM map stakeholders (influence × interest), identify champions/resistors/key-influencers, and segment audiences by impact and readiness | OCM Lead / Initiative Team | OCM Lead | Per initiative |
| 2 | OCM build the engagement plan per segment (what they need to know/feel/do, channel, cadence, owner) and align to comms (VS-14.3) | OCM / Comms / HR | Sponsor | Per initiative |
| 3 | OCM validate with sponsors/managers, refine through the change, and feed insights to resistance management (W4085) | OCM Lead | Sponsor | Per initiative |

### System Touchpoints
- Stakeholder/engagement tool; comms platform (VS-14.3/VS-103.1); HR (VS-103)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Blind-spot risk**: Missing a key influencer; mitigated by structured mapping and manager input
- **One-size risk**: Generic comms that miss segment needs; mitigated by segmentation

### Automation Opportunity
- Stakeholder map templating; engagement-tracking automation

### Controls
- CTL-632 (ensure controlled execution — OCM Strategy, Governance & Change Readiness Assessment (PA-134.1).)
- CTL-632: engagement plan approved by sponsor; segment coverage validated

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per major initiative

### Cross-references
- links to VS-14.3 (comms), VS-103 (HR), VS-107 (key accounts for B2B stakeholders)

## W4076. Change Impact Assessment & Affected-Person Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Initiative scope/design defined |
| **Frequency** | Per initiative + at design changes |
| **Volume** | Per major initiative (process, role, system, KPI, location impacts) |
| **Owner** | OCM Lead / Process Owner |
| **Participants** | Initiative team, affected process owners, HR role design (VS-102.1), OpEx (VS-133) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OCM/process owner assess impacts: which processes (W4050), roles, systems, KPIs, locations, and behaviors change, and the magnitude (incremental vs transformational) | OCM / Process Owner / Initiative Team | OCM Lead | Per initiative |
| 2 | OCM/HR quantify affected people by role/location (e.g., 200 store managers, ~5,800 associates, 600 DC staff), and identify role/job-design changes (links to VS-102.1) | OCM / HR | OCM Lead / CHRO | Per initiative |
| 3 | OCM translate impacts into the training/enablement plan (W4090), comms plan (W4081), and resistance-focus (W4085) | OCM / HR / Comms | Sponsor | Per initiative |

### System Touchpoints
- Process architecture (links to VS-133 W4050); job architecture (VS-102.1); HR (VS-103); training/LMS (VS-19.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Underestimate risk**: Impact seen as small when it's big for affected roles; mitigated by role-level analysis
- **Role-ambiguity risk**: New process without clear role design; mitigated by job-architecture linkage (VS-102.1)

### Automation Opportunity
- Impact-assessment templating; affected-people auto-count from HR master (VS-29 MDM-011)

### Controls
- CTL-632 (ensure controlled execution — OCM Strategy, Governance & Change Readiness Assessment (PA-134.1).)
- CTL-632: impact assessment reviewed by HR and process owner; role changes via job architecture

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per major initiative

### Cross-references
- links to VS-102.1 (job architecture), VS-133 W4050 (process), VS-19.4 (training)

## W4078. Change Risk Assessment & Mitigation Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Initiative risk identification; go-live approach |
| **Frequency** | Per initiative + maintained |
| **Volume** | Per major initiative |
| **Owner** | OCM Lead / Risk (links to VS-21.2) |
| **Participants** | Risk (VS-21.2), sponsor, process owner, BCP (VS-26), Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OCM/Risk identify change-specific risks: adoption failure, productivity dip, customer/service disruption, compliance lapse during transition, key-person dependency, and reversal/reputational risk | OCM / Risk / Process Owner | OCM Lead | Per initiative |
| 2 | OCM build mitigation: phasing, rollback plan, hypercare (W4087), backup/BCP (VS-26), dual-running where critical, and contingency comms | OCM / Risk / BCP | Sponsor | Per initiative |
| 3 | OCM monitor risks through go-live, escalate per the risk framework (VS-21.2), and capture lessons (W4096) | OCM / Risk | Sponsor / Transformation Board | Per initiative |

### System Touchpoints
- Risk register (links to VS-21.2); BCP (VS-26); change portfolio (W4074); audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **No-rollback risk**: Change that can't be reversed if it fails; mitigated by rollback design and dual-running
- **Productivity-dip risk**: Post-go-live performance drop; mitigated by hypercare (W4087) and staffing buffer

### Automation Opportunity
- Risk-register integration; go-live readiness checklist automation

### Controls
- CTL-126 (ensure change adoption & sustainment.)
- CTL-632: rollback plan for critical changes; BCP alignment (VS-26)

### Time Estimate
- Per initiative; 3–7 days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2 (ERM), VS-26 (BCP), VS-21 (audit)

## W4079. Executive Sponsorship Activation & Alignment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Initiative charter; sponsor assignment |
| **Frequency** | Per initiative + ongoing |
| **Volume** | Per major initiative (executive sponsor per transformation) |
| **Owner** | CHRO |
| **Participants** | Executive sponsors, CEO, CHRO, Transformation Board |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CHRO ensure every major initiative has an accountable, visible executive sponsor, and brief the sponsor on their role (vision, remove barriers, visible commitment, model the change) | CHRO | CEO | Per initiative |

- Sponsor-action tracker; visibility pulse

### Controls
- CTL-632 (ensure controlled execution — OCM Strategy, Governance & Change Readiness Assessment (PA-134.1).)
- CTL-632: named, accountable sponsor; sponsor actions logged

### Time Estimate
- Ongoing; per-initiative activation ~1 week
