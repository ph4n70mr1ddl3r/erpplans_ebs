## Overview

Organizational Change Management (OCM), Digital Adoption & Transformation Enablement workflows for
BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the enterprise discipline of **leading the people side of change**
so that transformation initiatives actually land and stick across a 5-entity, 200-store, 4-DC,
~6,925-employee operation distributed over 13+ Philippine regions. BuildRight runs a continuous
pipeline of major change: the unified ERP program, omnichannel/marketplace/retail-media
build-out, 10–15 new-store openings/year (VS-37), the store-remodel program (VS-109), the net-zero/
renewable program (VS-108), BIR e-invoicing and statutory digitization, and ongoing process
improvement (VS-133) — yet the 60–70% transformation-failure benchmark is driven overwhelmingly by
people-side failure (resistance, low adoption, change saturation), not technology failure.

Today this discipline is **entirely unowned as a program**: VS-30.1 owns the *digital-transformation
initiative portfolio*; VS-112 (PMO) owns *project delivery*; VS-19.4 / VS-124 own *training content*
delivery; VS-103.1 owns *HR communications*; VS-27.1 owns the *IT service desk / go-live hypercare*
— but no value stream owns the **OCM discipline**: change portfolio & capacity planning, stakeholder
mapping & analysis, change-impact & readiness assessment, sponsor activation, the equip-the-manager
cascade, the super-user/champion network, resistance management, change communications & saturation
management, go-live readiness, digital-adoption-platform (DAP) operations, adoption measurement &
sustainment, and benefit realization attributable to adoption. The defining terms 'change
management', 'OCM', 'change enablement', 'adoption management', and 'organizational change' each
appeared in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers (only incidental mentions across ~45
PA files where a project notes "communicate the change"); the discipline is genuinely unowned.

This is distinct from **VS-30 (Innovation & Digital)** which owns the *technology transformation
portfolio* (this value stream owns the *people/adoption* layer that makes it succeed). It is
distinct from **VS-112 (Corporate PMO)** which owns *project delivery & benefits realization*
(this value stream owns the *change-management workstream* within and across projects). It is
distinct from **VS-19.4 / VS-124** which deliver *training* (this value stream owns the *change
strategy, stakeholder engagement, resistance, and adoption sustainment* that training alone does
not achieve). It is distinct from **VS-103 (HR Shared Services/EX)** which owns *employee
experience operations* (this value stream owns *transformation-specific change*). Organizational
change management is the *people-side-of-change* discipline — distinct from technology, project
delivery, training, and HR operations.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: a distributed, ~6,925-person workforce with varying tech
literacy, high frontline turnover (15–20%), and simultaneous large transformations will stall or
fail without a dedicated OCM capability — every failed/slow adoption directly delays benefit
realization, sustains shadow ways-of-working, and erodes the return on PHP-hundreds-of-millions
of program investment.
