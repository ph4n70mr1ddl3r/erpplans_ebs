## W3313. Employee Benefits Program Design, Enrollment & Annual Renewal (HMO/Medical)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual renewal; program redesign; enrollment window |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | 6,932 employees + dependents |
| **Owner** | Compensation & Benefits Manager |
| **Participants** | C&B Manager, HMO providers, Broker, Payroll (links to VS-19.2), HRBP, Employees |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C&B Manager design/maintain the HMO/medical benefits program: plan tiers (employee/dependent/level), coverage (inpatient/outpatient/ER), provider network, co-pay, and employee cost-share; benchmark competitiveness (W3308) and cost (W3320) | C&B Manager / Broker | CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | C&B Manager conduct annual renewal: collect utilization data (W3320), negotiate rates/terms with HMO and broker (links to VS-34.2), secure budget approval (W3327), and execute renewal; run open-enrollment for employee elections | C&B Manager / Broker | CHRO | Annual |
| 3 | C&B Manager/Payroll process enrollment changes, dependent adds/drops, and premium deductions via payroll (links to VS-19.2); handle eligibility/life-event changes (W3318) and maintain the benefits master | C&B Manager / Payroll | CHRO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Advanced Benefits (OAB): benefits enrollment, eligibility and life-event processing; deduction envelopes to Payroll PH (links to VS-19.2); vendor (links to VS-34.2); HRIS (links to VS-29.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cost-spike risk**: Double-digit renewal increases breach budget; mitigated by plan redesign, negotiation, and utilization management
- **Enrollment-error risk**: Wrong elections/deductions create employee disputes; mitigated by controls and self-service (links to VS-103.1)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-collect of "collect utilization data" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-122 (ensure pay equity & statutory benefits compliance.)
- operational: plan redesign, negotiation, and utilization management; operational: controls and self-service (links to VS-103)

### Time Estimate
- Annual + continuous
