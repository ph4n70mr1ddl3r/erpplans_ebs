## W3305. Job Analysis, Documentation & Job Description Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New role creation; role change; annual job-architecture refresh |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + annual |
| **Volume** | ~250–350 distinct jobs across 6,918 employees |
| **Owner** | Compensation & Benefits Manager |
| **Participants** | C&B Manager, HR Business Partners, Hiring Managers, Master Data (links to VS-29) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C&B Manager conduct job analysis with hiring managers: capture purpose, key accountabilities, decision rights, scope (people/budget/revenue), required competencies, qualifications, and working conditions (store/DC/HQ, shift) | C&B Manager / HRBP | CHRO | Per job |
| 2 | C&B Manager document the standardized job description (job code, title, family, level, FLSA/exempt status, physical/safety requirements); store in the HR master (links to VS-29.2) and link to recruitment (links to VS-19.1) and payroll (links to VS-19.2) | C&B Manager | CHRO | Per job |
| 3 | C&B Manager maintain job-description version control, periodic review cadence, and change governance; retire obsolete jobs and update incumbents/payroll | C&B Manager / HRBP | CHRO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS job master (links to VS-29.2); recruitment (links to VS-19.1); payroll (links to VS-19.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Job-creep risk**: Outdated descriptions cause wrong grading/pay; mitigated by annual refresh
- **Inconsistency risk**: Non-standard descriptions break benchmarking; mitigated by templates and governance


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "accountabilities, decision rights, scope" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "document the standardized job description" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review cadence, and change governance" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-535 (ensure controlled execution — Job Architecture, Pay Structure & Market Benchmarking (PA-102.1).)
- operational: annual refresh; operational: templates and governance

### Time Estimate
- Per job; annual refresh

## W3306. Job Evaluation, Grading & Job Architecture (Career Framework) Maintenance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New/changed job; periodic architecture review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | ~250–350 jobs into the grading structure |
| **Owner** | Compensation & Benefits Manager |
| **Participants** | C&B Manager, HRBP, CHRO, Executives, Job-evaluation committee |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C&B Manager evaluate each job against the job-evaluation methodology (e.g., point-factor: know-how, problem-solving, accountability, scope) and assign a grade/band; build/maintain the career framework (job family, levels, progression criteria) | C&B Manager | CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | Job-evaluation committee (HR + business + finance) review borderline/contested grades for consistency and equity; approve the architecture and grade assignments | Committee | CHRO | Annual |
| 3 | C&B Manager publish the career framework, map incumbents to grades, and link to succession/development (links to VS-19.1/VS-19.4) and pay structure (W3307); update HR master (links to VS-29.2) | C&B Manager | CHRO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Framework-staleness risk**: Outdated framework blocks career mobility; mitigated by annual refresh


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "C&B Manager evaluate each job against the job-evaluation methodology" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review borderline/contested grades for consistency and equity" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-update of "update HR master" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls

## W3307. Annual Salary Structure Design, Range Setting & Range Penetration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C&B Manager designs the annual salary structure: per grade, minimum / midpoint / maximum (range spread typically 30–50%), informed by market data (W3308), pay positioning (e.g., 50th/75th percentile), internal equity, and budget (W3327) | C&B Manager | CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | CHRO/CFO review structure cost impact, affordability vs labor-budget (links to VS-33.1), and market competitiveness; approve structure and effective date; set range-penetration targets by grade/tenure | CHRO / CFO | CEO | Annual |
| 3 | C&B Manager loads the structure into HRIS/payroll (links to VS-19.2), publish to HRBP/managers, and monitor range penetration and compliance (W3311) | C&B Manager | CHRO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Oracle Compensation Workbench (fit-gap E10) — salary budgets, merit distributions, pay-range administration; HRIS/payroll (links to VS-19.2); FP&A (links to VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unaffordable-structure risk**: Over-positioned structure breaches labor budget; mitigated by cost modeling (W3327) and CFO review
- **Uncompetitive-structure risk**: Under-positioned structure loses talent; mitigated by market benchmarking (W3308)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "review structure cost impact, affordability vs labor-budget" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "monitor range penetration and compliance" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-535 (ensure controlled execution — Job Architecture, Pay Structure & Market Benchmarking (PA-102.1).)
- operational: cost modeling (W3327) and CFO review; operational: market benchmarking (W3308)

## W3309. Pay Equity, Equal-Pay-for-Equal-Work Analysis & Gender/Wage-Gap Remediation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual; legal/compliance trigger; merger/acquisition |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on trigger |
| **Volume** | All 6,918 employees |
| **Owner** | Compensation & Benefits Manager |
| **Participants** | C&B Manager, Legal (links to VS-100), CHRO, Internal Audit (links to VS-21.1), Data Privacy (links to VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C&B Manager conduct pay-equity analysis: regression of pay on legitimate pay factors (grade, tenure, performance, location) controlling for gender/other protected status; identify unexplained pay gaps by grade/job | C&B Manager / Analytics | CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | C&B Manager/Legal review flagged gaps for root cause (historical, hire-in, compression); design remediation (adjustments within budget W3327), assess legal exposure (Labor Code, links to VS-84/VS-100), and execute adjustments via payroll (links to VS-19.2) | C&B Manager / Legal | CHRO | Annual |
| 3 | C&B Manager monitor remediation outcomes, report equity metrics to executives and audit (links to VS-21.1), and maintain analysis under privacy/privilege (links to VS-91/VS-100) | C&B Manager | CHRO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Pay-equity analytics (links to VS-28.1/VS-103.3); payroll (links to VS-19.2); audit (links to VS-21.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Legal-exposure risk**: Unremediated pay gaps create DOLE/NLRC and reputational risk (links to VS-84/VS-100); mitigated by annual analysis and remediation
- **Privacy risk**: Sensitive pay/demographic data mishandled; mitigated by privacy controls (links to VS-91)

- System auto-review of "review flagged gaps for root cause" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "monitor remediation outcomes, report equity metrics to executives and audit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
