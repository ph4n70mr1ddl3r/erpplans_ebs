# VS-102: Compensation, Benefits & Total Rewards Strategy

> **People** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Compensation, Benefits & Total Rewards Strategy workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — governing
the **strategic pay and rewards discipline** for 6,925 employees across 5 legal entities and 205
locations: job architecture (job analysis, job evaluation/grading, career framework), salary
structure design and range administration, market compensation benchmarking and pay positioning,
pay-equity and equal-pay analysis (gender/wage-gap), statutory pay compliance (regional
minimum-wage per DOLE), benefits program design and administration (HMO/medical, retirement,
allowances, wellness links to VS-83), dependent and life-event benefits, benefits vendor and broker
management, short-term/long-term incentive (STI/LTI) plan design and administration, sales
commission and store/team performance bonus, recognition programs, total-rewards statements, and
C&B budget planning and effectiveness analytics.

This is distinct from **VS-19.2 (Payroll & Compensation)** which executes *payroll processing* —
semi-monthly pay runs, statutory remittance (SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG per W1527), 13th-month
computation (W1416), final pay (W643), garnishments (W280), and off-cycle payments (W641). This
value stream owns the *design and governance* of what people are paid and the rewards programs they
participate in — the upstream decisions (salary structure, pay equity, benefits design, incentive
plans) that payroll then executes. It is also distinct from **VS-19.1 (Recruitment & Onboarding)**
which sets hiring ranges, from **VS-19.5 (Separation & Benefits)** which administers final
benefits, from **VS-83 (Occupational Health & Wellness)** which delivers wellness programs, and
from **VS-19.3 (Workforce Management)** which schedules labor. Pay equity, market competitiveness,
benefits cost (~PHP-scale medical/retirement for 6,925 employees), and incentive-effectiveness are
direct drivers of BuildRight's ability to attract and retain talent against 15–20% annual turnover
(per profile §11.4) and DOLE/BIR/Labor-Code compliance.

BuildRight's exposure spans 5 payroll entities (per profile §2), region-varying minimum wages (per
profile §11.2), mandatory 13th-month and statutory benefits, a large store-level workforce
(5,800 across 200 stores) with shift/holiday/night differentials, a trade/incentive-driven sales
culture (links to VS-11/VS-43), and executive pay governance before the board (links to VS-36). No
prior value stream owned the *compensation-strategy / total-rewards* discipline.
