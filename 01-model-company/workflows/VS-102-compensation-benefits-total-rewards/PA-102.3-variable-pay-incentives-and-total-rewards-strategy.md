## W3321. Short-Term Incentive (STI) / Bonus Plan Design & Administration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual STI cycle; plan redesign |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | ~HQ/management population (5 entities) |
| **Owner** | Compensation & Benefits Manager |
| **Participants** | C&B Manager, CHRO, Finance (links to VS-17.4), Payroll (links to VS-19.2), Managers, Compensation Committee (links to VS-36) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C&B Manager/CHRO design the STI/bonus plan: eligibility, performance measures (corporate, business-unit, individual links to VS-19.4 performance), weighting, targets/funding formula, cap, and payout curve; align to corporate performance (links to VS-33.2) | C&B Manager / CHRO | Compensation Committee | Annual |
| 2 | Finance/C&B Manager compute plan funding against actual performance (links to VS-33.2 CPM), validate affordability vs budget (W3327), and obtain committee/CEO approval (links to VS-36); model payout scenarios | Finance / C&B Manager | CEO | Annual |
| 3 | Managers assign individual performance ratings (links to VS-19.4); C&B Manager calculate payouts, Payroll (links to VS-19.2) executes with tax (links to VS-79), communicate to employees, and audit for governance (links to VS-21.1) | C&B Manager / Payroll | CHRO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Incentive planning; CPM (links to VS-33.2); payroll (links to VS-19.2); tax (links to VS-79); governance (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over/under-funding risk**: Payouts misaligned to performance/budget; mitigated by funding formula and approval
- **Pay-for-performance-dilution risk**: Weak linkage rewards low performers; mitigated by clear measures and calibration


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-measure of "C&B Manager/CHRO design the STI/bonus plan" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validate affordability vs budget" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-calculate of "calculate payouts, Payroll" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-537 (ensure controlled execution — Variable Pay, Incentives & Total Rewards Strategy (PA-102.3).)
- operational: funding formula and approval; operational: clear measures and calibration

### Time Estimate
- Annual cycle

## W3322. Sales Commission & Trade-Incentive Plan Design, Calculation & Payout

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual plan; new sales motion (trade per VS-43, project per VS-11) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly/quarterly payout |
| **Volume** | Sales/trade/account population |
| **Owner** | Compensation & Benefits Manager |
| **Participants** | C&B Manager, Sales/Trade leadership (links to VS-11/VS-43), Finance (links to VS-17.4), Payroll (links to VS-19.2), Sales Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C&B Manager/Sales leadership design the commission/trade-incentive plan: quota setting, rate structures (tiered, accelerator), SPIFs, credit/attribution rules (per account/order links to VS-11.2/VS-16), clawback, and cap; align to margin not just revenue (links to VS-101 margin) | C&B Manager / Sales leadership | CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | Sales Ops/Finance compute commissions from sales results (links to VS-16 O2C/VS-08 POS), validate against quotas/credit rules, handle disputes, and reconcile to revenue/margin; obtain sales-leadership approval | Sales Ops / Finance | Sales leadership | Monthly |
| 3 | Payroll (links to VS-19.2) executes commission payout with tax (links to VS-79); C&B Manager audit plan cost vs budget (W3327) and effectiveness (W3328) and adjust the plan | C&B Manager / Payroll | CHRO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- **Oracle Incentive Compensation** — compensation plans, quotas and rate structures, resources and the compensation-group hierarchy, transaction collection and crediting rules, the calculation batch with its calculated/posted phases, and payment approval into payroll; O2C (links to VS-16); POS/analytics (links to VS-08); payroll (links to VS-19.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Dispute-heavy risk**: Unclear credit rules cause disputes; mitigated by documented attribution rules
- **Margin-blind incentive risk**: Revenue-only commission erodes margin; mitigated by margin-weighted design (links to VS-101)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "C&B Manager/Sales leadership design the commission/trade-incentive plan" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validate against quotas/credit rules, handle disputes, and reconcile to…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System continuous audit of "audit plan cost vs budget" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-537 (ensure controlled execution — Variable Pay, Incentives & Total Rewards Strategy (PA-102.3).)
- operational: documented attribution rules; operational: margin-weighted design (links to VS-101)

### Time Estimate
- Monthly/quarterly

| 2 | Store Ops/Finance compute store/team performance vs targets (links to VS-33.2), allocate bonus pool by store ranking, and approve payouts within budget (W3327); manage fairness across store formats/clusters (links to VS-101.2 W3302) | Store Ops / Finance | COO | Monthly |
