## W2382. Seasonal Assortment Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Pre-season planning (3 months before season) |
| **Frequency** | 6 seasons/year; 13 categories reviewed |
| **Volume** | ~6 seasonal assortment plans/year |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Category Mgr, Merch Planner, Buyer, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager identifies seasonal opportunities per Philippine calendar: summer (garden, aircon, outdoor), rainy (waterproofing, flood control), ber-months (holiday décor, gifts, appliances); selects seasonal SKUs | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 3–5 days |
| 2 | Merch Planner determines store-level assortment by format (flagship/standard/compact); Buyer places seasonal POs with vendors; confirms delivery timeline 6–8 weeks before season start | Merch Planner / Buyer | Category Manager | 5–10 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Assortment/item master (links to VS-01 / W1 SKU planning, VS-29 / W299 assortment & store-cluster master) — the seasonal SKU set per format
- Seasonal calendar (links to VS-29 / W306 seasonal-calendar master) — the 6-season Philippine retail calendar driving the planning trigger
- Purchase orders (links to VS-03 / W2B import POs, VS-15 P2P) — the seasonal PO placement

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Import-lead-time risk**: a seasonal SKU sourced via import (40% of COGS) has a 10–12 week lead time, so the PO must place before sell-through signal confirms demand; mitigated by the 3-month-pre-season trigger and the W2399 post-season forecast-accuracy feedback loop
- **Format-mismatch risk**: the compact format can't carry the full seasonal assortment, and over-assortment creates fixture crowding; mitigated by the format-tiered assortment per W299 store-cluster master

### Automation Opportunity
- Assortment auto-suggested from the prior-year same-season sell-through by format; seasonal-PO auto-generated from the approved assortment

### Controls
- CTL-39: rebate/co-op terms on seasonal POs validated; operational: VP Merchandising sign-off on the seasonal assortment plan

### Time Estimate
~4 days Category Manager + ~7.5 days Merch Planner/Buyer × 6 seasons = **~690–840 hours/year**.

## W2383. Seasonal Inventory Positioning at DCs

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Seasonal inventory arriving at DCs |
| **Frequency** | Pre-season; 6 cycles/year |
| **Volume** | ~4 DCs receiving seasonal inventory |
| **Owner** | DC Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | DC Ops Mgr, Merch Planner, Logistics Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merch Planner provides DC with seasonal allocation plan: quantity per SKU per store group; DC receives seasonal inventory; stages for store allocation; schedules outbound shipments aligned with store display readiness | Merch Planner / DC Ops Mgr | VP Supply Chain | 1–2 weeks/season |

### System Touchpoints
- WMS (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving/putaway, W106 dispatch) — seasonal-inventory receiving, staging, and outbound
- Allocation (links to VS-02 / W4 replenishment, W105 multi-channel allocation) — the SKU-per-store-group allocation
- Inventory (links to VS-05 / W220 slow-moving tracking) — seasonal inventory flagged as time-bound

### Pain Points / Risks
- **DC-congestion risk**: seasonal inventory arrives in bulk and competes for DC staging space with regular replenishment; mitigated by the pre-season staging reservation and cross-dock-fast-forward for high-volume seasonal SKUs
- **Mis-timed-outbound risk**: DC ships to stores before the display zone is ready, leaving seasonal inventory in the backroom; mitigated by the outbound shipment aligned with the W2384 display-setup readiness signal

### Automation Opportunity
- Allocation auto-generated from the assortment plan × store-cluster demand; outbound auto-scheduled against the store display-readiness flag

### Controls
- CTL-21: seasonal inventory tracked separately to prevent commingling with sellable stock; operational: DC Ops Manager staging-plan sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1.5 weeks/season × 6 seasons across 4 DCs = **~350–500 hours/year** DC Ops + Merch Planner.

## W2384. Seasonal Planogram & Display Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Seasonal display zone activation |
| **Frequency** | 6/year; 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~1,200 display setups/year |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Merch Coord, Store Managers, Regional Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinator distributes seasonal display plan: zone assignment, product list, signage, visual merchandising guide; stores set up within 1-week window; compliance photos submitted and verified | Merch Coord / Store Manager | Merchandising Planner | 1 week per season |

### System Touchpoints
- Planogram (links to VS-55 / W2166 seasonal refresh, W314 planogram template) — the seasonal display spec
- Store comms (links to VS-07 / W571) — the display-plan distribution and compliance-photo return
- VS-55 / W2181 planogram-change-implementation tracking — the compliance-verification pattern

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance-variance risk**: stores interpret the display plan differently, producing inconsistent execution across the chain; mitigated by the detailed visual-merchandising guide and the compliance-photo verification
- **Window-pressure risk**: the 1-week window collides with regular store ops, producing rushed execution; mitigated by the W2389 transition planning that sequences the outgoing-season teardown with the incoming-season setup

### Automation Opportunity
- Display-plan auto-distributed per format; compliance-photo auto-scored against the spec (links to VS-55 W2175 pattern)

### Controls
- CTL-421 (ensure controlled execution — Seasonal Planning & Phase-In Management (PA-64.1).)
- operational: planogram currency; operational: Merchandising Planner compliance-photo sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1 week/season Coordinator + ~4 hours/store setup × 200 stores × 6 seasons = **~5,000–6,000 hours/year** Coordinator + Store teams (setup absorbed into store-ops).

### Controls
- CTL-421 (ensure controlled execution — Seasonal Planning & Phase-In Management (PA-64.1).)

### Time Estimate
~1.5 days/season × 6 seasons = **~70–90 hours/year** Pricing Analyst.

## W2387. Seasonal Launch Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | First 2 weeks of seasonal selling |
| **Frequency** | 6/year; first 2 weeks of each season |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × 6 seasons |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Planner |
| **Participants** | Merch Planner, Category Mgr, Analytics Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System monitors daily sell-through: compares vs. plan; identifies slow starters (may need markdown acceleration) and fast sellers (may need reorder); daily flash report to Category Manager | System / Merch Planner | Category Manager | 30 min/day during launch |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113, W208 retail analytics) — the daily sell-through dashboard
- Replenishment (links to VS-02 / W4, W56 backorder) — the fast-seller reorder trigger
- Markdown (links to VS-64 / W2390) — the slow-starter markdown trigger

### Pain Points / Risks
- **False-alarm risk**: a slow first 3 days triggers a premature markdown on a SKU that was just waiting for the weekend traffic; mitigated by the 2-week observation window before action and the weekday-vs-weekend normalization
- **Reorder-too-late risk**: a fast seller is identified but the import reorder lead time (10–12 weeks) means it won't arrive in-season; mitigated by the W2388 vendor coordination for expedite/transfer options

### Automation Opportunity
- Sell-through-vs-plan auto-flagged daily; slow-starter/fast-seller auto-categorized with suggested action

### Controls
- CTL-421 (ensure controlled execution — Seasonal Planning & Phase-In Management (PA-64.1).)
- Operational: Category Manager daily-flash review sign-off during the launch window

### Time Estimate
~30 min/day × ~10 launch days/season × 6 seasons = **~30 hours/year** Planner + system automated.

### Time Estimate
~1.5 weeks/season × 6 seasons across ~75 vendors = **~250–350 hours/year** Buyer.

## W2389. Multi-Season Inventory Transition Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Transition between seasons |
| **Frequency** | 6/year; overlap period ~2–3 weeks |
| **Volume** | ~2–3 week transition per season |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Planner |
| **Participants** | Merch Planner, Category Mgr, DC Ops, Store Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Planner manages transition: outgoing seasonal markdown (clear remaining), incoming seasonal setup (stage new display); coordinates display zone handover; adjusts replenishment from outgoing to incoming | Merchandising Planner | Category Manager | 1–2 weeks/transition |

### System Touchpoints
- Seasonal calendar (links to VS-29 / W306) — the season-overlap window
- Planogram transition (links to VS-55 / W2166, W264 seasonal transition) — the zone handover
- Replenishment (links to VS-02 / W4) — the outgoing→incoming replenishment switch

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Zone-handover-collision risk**: the outgoing season's clearance event and the incoming season's setup collide in the same zone and the same week, confusing customers and overwhelming staff; mitigated by the sequenced handover (clearance completes, then teardown, then setup) within the transition window
- **Outgoing-season-remainder risk**: the outgoing season's inventory isn't fully cleared before the incoming setup, forcing it into the backroom (dead stock); mitigated by the W2393 clearance-inventory consolidation before the handover

### Automation Opportunity
- Transition-window timeline auto-generated per zone; outgoing-remainder auto-flagged for consolidation

### Controls
- CTL-421 (ensure controlled execution — Seasonal Planning & Phase-In Management (PA-64.1).)
- operational: planogram transition currency; operational: Merchandising Planner transition-completion sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1.5 weeks/transition × 6 transitions + DC/Store coordination absorbed = **~300–400 hours/year** Planner + coordination.
