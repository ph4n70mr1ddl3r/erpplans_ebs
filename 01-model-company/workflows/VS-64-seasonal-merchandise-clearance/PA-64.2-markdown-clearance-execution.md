## W2390. Sell-Through Based Markdown Trigger

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Seasonal SKU sell-through falls below plan threshold |
| **Frequency** | Continuous during season; ~200–300 markdown decisions/season |
| **Volume** | ~200–300 decisions/season |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Category Mgr, Pricing Analyst, Merch Planner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System monitors sell-through vs. plan; auto-flags SKUs below threshold: <40% sold at mid-season, <70% at 75% of season; Pricing Analyst recommends markdown level based on residual inventory and weeks remaining | System / Pricing Analyst | Category Manager | Automated + 30 min/decision |
| 2 | Category Manager approves markdown; system updates price; POS reflects new price; shelf labels updated; customer communication if significant price reduction | Category Manager / IT | VP Merchandising | 1–2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing master (links to VS-29 / W289, VS-57 / W107, VS-64 / W2385 markdown triggers) — the threshold-armed markdown
- Analytics (links to VS-27 / W113, VS-64 / W2387 sell-through monitoring) — the sell-through signal
- POS/label (links to VS-08 / W5B, W181 price-tag) — the price update and label

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Threshold-gaming risk**: a SKU hovers just above the threshold to avoid markdown, accumulating dead stock; mitigated by a trend-based trigger (3 weeks of sub-plan sell-through) alongside the point threshold
- **Markdown-spiral risk**: repeated markdowns erode margin below the vendor-allowance recovery, netting a loss; mitigated by the W2397 reconciliation and the stop-loss floor (clearance-at-cost trigger)

### Automation Opportunity
- Threshold-trigger auto-surfaced as a worklist; markdown-recommendation auto-computed from residual inventory × weeks-remaining

### Controls
- CTL-176 (ensure markdown & clearance pricing compliance.)
- CTL-422 (price-change authorization — links to VS-08 / W40 price-change execution); operational: Category Manager markdown approval

### Time Estimate
~30 min/decision × ~250 decisions/season × 6 seasons (~750 hours) + ~1–2 hours/decision approval on the ~25% escalated subset per the W93 authorization matrix (~375–750 hours) = **~1,125–1,500 hours/year** Pricing Analyst + Category Manager.

## W2391. Progressive Markdown Strategy Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Markdown schedule execution |
| **Frequency** | Per season; typically 3 markdown phases |
| **Volume** | ~3 phases × 200 stores × ~100 SKUs |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Planner |
| **Participants** | Merch Planner, Pricing Analyst, Store Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phase 1 (mid-season): 10–15% off slow movers. Phase 2 (late season): 25–40% off remaining. Phase 3 (final 2 weeks): 50–75% off clearance. Planner defines phases; system executes per schedule | Merchandising Planner | Category Manager | 1 day/phase |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing master (links to VS-29 / W289, VS-64 / W2390) — the phased markdown schedule
- Promo/label (links to VS-14 / W262, VS-08 / W181) — the clearance signage and labels per phase
- Analytics (links to VS-27 / W113, W2387) — the sell-through that confirms phase progression

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Phase-too-aggressive risk**: phase 2's 25–40% off triggers before phase 1 has cleared its target, compressing margin; mitigated by the phase-gate on sell-through (advance to phase 2 only when phase 1 cleared ≥60%)
- **Phase-too-conservative risk**: phases are too shallow to clear inventory before season end, leaving dead stock for W2393 consolidation; mitigated by the W2397 post-clearance reconciliation feeding the next season's phase-depth model

### Automation Opportunity
- Phase auto-executed on the schedule OR on the sell-through gate (whichever triggers first); phase-effectiveness auto-measured for W2403

### Controls
- CTL-176 (ensure markdown & clearance pricing compliance.)
- CTL-422 (price-change authorization); operational: Category Manager phase-definition sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1 day/phase × 3 phases × 6 seasons = **~140–180 hours/year** Merch Planner.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing promotes clearance event (social media, email, in-store signage); Store Manager sets up clearance zone; staff applies clearance labels; manages customer flow during event | Marketing / Store Manager | Regional Ops Mgr | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Marketing/promo (links to VS-14 / W83 campaign, W262 promotional setup) — the clearance-event promotion
- Clearance zone/label (links to VS-55 / W2168, VS-08 / W181) — the clearance-zone setup and labels
- POS (links to VS-08 / W5B, W93 clearance-markdown price) — the clearance-price transaction

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Crowd-safety risk**: a heavily-promoted clearance event draws a crowd that exceeds the zone's safe capacity; mitigated by the customer-flow management and the staffing surge for the event
- **Mis-labeled-item risk**: a non-clearance item is marked clearance (or vice versa), causing a customer dispute or margin leakage; mitigated by the W2395 compliance audit during the event

### Automation Opportunity
- Clearance-label auto-generated per the W2390/2391 markdown; clearance-zone plan auto-distributed

### Controls
- CTL-422 (ensure controlled execution — Markdown & Clearance Execution (PA-64.2).)
- CTL-422 (price-tag accuracy — links to W69); operational: Store Manager clearance-zone setup sign-off

### Time Estimate
(~6 h execution + ~1–2 h setup) per store per event × 200 stores × 6 events/year ≈ **~8,400–9,600 hours/year** Store teams (absorbed into store-ops surge; × 1.5-week elapsed cycles).

## W2393. Clearance Inventory Consolidation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-clearance remaining inventory |
| **Frequency** | Per season; after clearance event |
| **Volume** | ~5–15% of seasonal inventory unsold after clearance |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Planner |
| **Participants** | Merch Planner, DC Ops, Store Managers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Planner identifies remaining clearance inventory across stores; determines disposition: (a) transfer to stores still selling (regional variation), (b) consolidate at DC for bulk sale to discount buyers, (c) return to vendor per markdown agreement, (d) donate for tax benefit | Merchandising Planner | Category Manager | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory/disposition (links to VS-05 / W91 damaged/obsolete, W220 slow-moving, VS-32 returns) — the remaining-inventory identification and disposition
- Transfers (links to VS-05 / W22 store transfers, W154 rebalancing) — the cross-store consolidation
- Vendor return (links to VS-03 / W88 RTV, VS-64 / W2388 markdown agreement) — the vendor return/swap

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Dead-stock-blindness risk**: the remaining inventory isn't identified and sits in stores/backrooms, aging into obsolescence; mitigated by the post-clearance remaining-inventory report auto-generated from the inventory system
- **Disposition-suboptimization risk**: inventory that could be donated (tax benefit) is instead scrapped (no benefit); mitigated by the disposition-decision framework ranking options by net recovery

### Automation Opportunity
- Remaining inventory auto-flagged post-clearance; disposition auto-suggested per the net-recovery ranking

### Controls
- CTL-422 (ensure controlled execution — Markdown & Clearance Execution (PA-64.2).)
- operational: clearance-disposition approval with separate tracking; Category Manager disposition sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1.5 weeks/season × 6 seasons = **~350–500 hours/year** Merch Planner + DC Ops.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per season; ~50–100 seasonal vendors |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 vendors/season |
| **Owner** | Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Analyst, Category Mgr, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance calculates vendor markdown allowance per agreement: unsold inventory × agreed support %; submits claim to vendor; vendor validates and credits; Finance records in AP | Finance Analyst | Finance Manager | 2–3 days/season |

### System Touchpoints
- Co-op/rebate (links to VS-39 / W27, VS-64 / W2388 markdown-support term) — the allowance agreement
- AP (links to VS-15 / W7, W7C) — the vendor credit recording
- Inventory (links to VS-05 / W91, VS-64 / W2393) — the unsold-quantity basis

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Quantity-dispute risk**: the vendor disputes the unsold-quantity claim (argues BuildRight over-ordered); mitigated by the documented PO-vs-sell-through evidence and the W2388 agreement term
- **Allowance-non-recovery risk**: the vendor delays or denies the credit and BuildRight writes off the markdown without recovery; mitigated by the CTL-48 dispute-resolution workflow and the allowance-aging tracker

### Automation Opportunity
- Allowance auto-calculated from the unsold quantity × agreement rate; vendor-claim auto-generated with evidence pack

### Controls
- CTL-39: rebate/allowance accuracy; CTL-44 (duplicate-claim guard); operational: Finance Manager sign-off on the claim

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/season × 6 seasons across ~75 vendors = **~150–200 hours/year** Finance Analyst.

### Time Estimate

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-clearance financial analysis |
| **Frequency** | Per season; after clearance complete |
| **Volume** | 6/year |
| **Owner** | Finance Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance Mgr, Category Mgr, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance reconciles: seasonal inventory cost, markdown cost, vendor allowance, clearance revenue, net margin on seasonal category; calculates seasonal category profitability vs. plan | Finance Manager | CFO | 2–3 days/season |

### System Touchpoints
- Cost accounting (links to VS-158 / W85, VS-17 / W26 budget) — the seasonal-category P&L
- Co-op/allowance (links to VS-39 / W27, VS-64 / W2394) — the vendor-allowance recovery
- Inventory write-off (links to VS-05 / W91, VS-64 / W2393) — the unrecovered remainder

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-distortion risk**: the reconciliation conflates seasonal margin with regular-category margin, hiding a seasonal-category loss; mitigated by the seasonal-category P&L isolated per W2404
- **Allowance-timing risk**: the vendor allowance is recorded in a different period than the markdown, distorting the period margin; mitigated by the allowance-accrual at markdown time (not waiting for the vendor credit)

### Automation Opportunity
- Seasonal P&L auto-assembled from the cost/markdown/allowance/revenue datasets; variance-vs-plan auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-422 (ensure controlled execution — Markdown & Clearance Execution (PA-64.2).)
- operational: allowance accuracy; CTL-176 (clearance events reconciled financially post-event — COGS accuracy owned with VS-158); operational: CFO sign-off on the seasonal P&L

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/season × 6 seasons = **~120–150 hours/year** Finance Manager.
