## W5177. Disaster Relief Supply Forecasting & Category Inventory Staging Plan

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Start of typhoon season (Q2); weather bureau PAGASA tracking major storm warning |
| **Frequency** | Seasonal + storm event-driven |
| **Volume** | Staging plan covering 4 regional DCs and 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Director of Supply Chain Planning / S&OP Manager (VS-127) |
| **Participants** | Category Managers (VS-01), Logistics Manager (VS-06), DC Operations (VS-04) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analyze historic storm tracks and geographic store vulnerabilities (e.g. East Coast Luzon, Visayas). | Director of Supply Chain Planning / S&OP Manager (VS-127) | Director of Supply Chain Planning / S&OP Manager (VS-127) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Model relief demand: forecast quantity spikes for galvanized iron (GI) roof sheets, plywood, nails, cement, flashlights, batteries, and portable generators. | Director of Supply Chain Planning / S&OP Manager (VS-127) | Director of Supply Chain Planning / S&OP Manager (VS-127) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Draft the Disaster Staging Plan: define inventory reserve targets for safety stock levels at DC hubs (Mindanao, Visayas, Luzon). | Director of Supply Chain Planning / S&OP Manager (VS-127) | Director of Supply Chain Planning / S&OP Manager (VS-127) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Execute inventory stock transfers to pre-position forecasted SKUs at regional DCs prior to landfall. | Director of Supply Chain Planning / S&OP Manager (VS-127) | Director of Supply Chain Planning / S&OP Manager (VS-127) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Replenishment module (VS-02), S&OP database (VS-127), weather data interface.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-stocking / Dead stock**: Staged inventory unsold if storm shifts; mitigated by focusing staging on high-velocity standard SKUs (e.g. cement, nails) that sell under normal operations.

### Automation Opportunity
- Storm-path mapping overlay on ERP warehouse inventory maps.

### Controls
- CTL-770 (ensure controlled execution — Emergency Material Allocation, Disaster Kit Assembly & Staging Operations (PA-180.1).)
- CTL-770: Staging Plan approved by VP Supply Chain; safety stock thresholds configured in ERP.

### Time Estimate
- ~2-3 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-02 (supply planning), VS-127 (S&OP), VS-06 (logistics), VS-04 (DC ops)

## W5179. Disaster Relief Home Rebuilding Kit Kitting & Staging

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Disaster Staging Plan activation (W5177); NGO partner request |
| **Frequency** | Seasonal + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~5,000–10,000 kits assembled annually |
| **Owner** | DC Operations Manager (VS-04) |
| **Participants** | Kitting Associates, Packaging team (VS-111), Procurement, CSR (VS-152) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define Bill of Materials (BOM) for the 'BuildRight Home Rebuilding Kit' (e.g. 10 GI sheets, 3 pcs lumber, 5kg nails, hammer, handsaw, tarpaulin). | DC Operations Manager (VS-04) | DC Operations Manager (VS-04) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Set up the kitting line in DC staging zones: configure workspaces, arrange packaging crates, and stage components. | DC Operations Manager (VS-04) | DC Operations Manager (VS-04) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Execute kit assembly: pick components, bundle lumber/sheets, pack hand tools, and shrink-wrap the consolidated kit (VS-92). | DC Operations Manager (VS-04) | DC Operations Manager (VS-04) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Label kits with barcodes, technical descriptions, and safety instructions; stage in outbound lane for dispatch. | DC Operations Manager (VS-04) | DC Operations Manager (VS-04) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- WMS kitting module (VS-04), Bill of Materials database (VS-92), Packaging standards database (VS-111).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Kit component bottlenecks**: Missing single tool delaying entire kit assembly; mitigated by substituting equivalent brands or tools from retail stock.

### Automation Opportunity
- Semi-automated strapping and shrink-wrapping machinery.

### Controls
- CTL-770 (ensure controlled execution — Emergency Material Allocation, Disaster Kit Assembly & Staging Operations (PA-180.1).)
- CTL-770: Kit weight checked on exit scale; completed kits inspected by DC QA supervisor.

### Time Estimate
- ~5-10 mins assembly time per kit

### Cross-references
- links to VS-92 (kitting), VS-111 (packaging), VS-04 (DC ops), VS-152 (CSR)

## W5180. Emergency Power Generator & Solar Portable Kit Pre-Positioning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Typhoon track warning indicating category 3+ storm heading to grid-vulnerable areas |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per warning |
| **Volume** | ~3–5 pre-positioning events annually |
| **Owner** | Solar & Renewable Product Manager (VS-70) / Category Buyer |
| **Participants** | DC Logistics, Store Managers, LP (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify power-vulnerable stores in the storm path: check historical power outage logs and flood zones. | Solar & Renewable Product Manager (VS-70) / Category Buyer | Solar & Renewable Product Manager (VS-70) / Category Buyer | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Calculate emergency power stock: allocate 5kW-10kW generators for store backup power, and portable 1kW solar generator/battery kits for retail sale. | Solar & Renewable Product Manager (VS-70) / Category Buyer | Solar & Renewable Product Manager (VS-70) / Category Buyer | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Coordinate fast-track transfer from primary DC to store backrooms; verify pre-positioning is complete 24 hours before landfall. | Solar & Renewable Product Manager (VS-70) / Category Buyer | Solar & Renewable Product Manager (VS-70) / Category Buyer | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Instruct store LP on security protocols for high-value generator staging zones (VS-23). | Solar & Renewable Product Manager (VS-70) / Category Buyer | Solar & Renewable Product Manager (VS-70) / Category Buyer | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- WMS transfer system (VS-04), Store LP system (VS-23), ESG energy tracker (VS-120).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stolen / Missing generators**: High theft risk post-storm; mitigated by locking generators in store cash vaults or secure cages.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-770 (ensure controlled execution — Emergency Material Allocation, Disaster Kit Assembly & Staging Operations (PA-180.1).)
- CTL-770: Serial numbers of pre-positioned units recorded in transfer documents; receipts signed by Store Manager and LP officer.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 days logistics window

### Cross-references
- links to VS-70 (solar energy), VS-23 (loss prevention), VS-04 (DC ops), VS-120 (energy compliance)

## W5181. DC Emergency Staging Zone Allocation & Fast-Track Pick Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Landfall + storm damage reports; urgent store replenishment requests (W5178) |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven post-landfall |
| **Volume** | Continuous post-disaster (typically 7–14 days) |
| **Owner** | DC Warehouse Manager (VS-04) |
| **Participants** | Shift Supervisors, Forklift Operators, Inventory Clerk |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clear designated Outbound Emergency Staging Zones near DC shipping docks. | DC Warehouse Manager (VS-04) | DC Warehouse Manager (VS-04) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Release emergency wave picking: prioritize picking of relief kits (W5179), structural timber, cement, and water containers. | DC Warehouse Manager (VS-04) | DC Warehouse Manager (VS-04) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Configure warehouse routes: assign dedicated forklift drivers to transport relief materials to the emergency staging zone. | DC Warehouse Manager (VS-04) | DC Warehouse Manager (VS-04) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Scan and verify loaded pallets against dispatch documents; coordinate driver signature and vehicle gate-pass. | DC Warehouse Manager (VS-04) | DC Warehouse Manager (VS-04) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- WMS staging screen (VS-04), ERP logistics dispatch (VS-06).

- WMS voice-directed picking priority allocation.

### Controls
- CTL-83 (ensure calamity pricing & relief-channel compliance.)
- CTL-770: Gate-pass security check matches loaded items to emergency manifest; deviations flagged.

### Time Estimate
- ~30-45 mins per truckload pick and stage

### Cross-references
- links to VS-04 (DC ops), VS-06 (logistics)

## W5182. Emergency Vendor Direct-to-Store Delivery (DSD) Fast-Track PO Issuance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DC stockouts of critical relief items; direct shipping viable from local vendor plant to store |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per vendor availability |
| **Volume** | ~30–40 emergency POs per major disaster |
| **Owner** | Procurement Operations Manager (VS-03) |
| **Participants** | Local Vendors (cement, roofing, water), Store Managers, AP Finance (VS-15) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify local vendors with active plants near affected stores (avoiding DC transit). | Procurement Operations Manager (VS-03) | Procurement Operations Manager (VS-03) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Draft emergency DSD PO: define quantities, delivery store, and agreed emergency pricing (complying with price freezes). | Procurement Operations Manager (VS-03) | Procurement Operations Manager (VS-03) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Route PO through the ERP fast-track approval workflow: bypass standard category manager queue if emergency limits apply. | Procurement Operations Manager (VS-03) | Procurement Operations Manager (VS-03) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Coordinate delivery slot with store manager; receive goods at store, verify counts, and scan delivery receipts into ERP. | Procurement Operations Manager (VS-03) | Procurement Operations Manager (VS-03) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Purchase Order module (VS-03.2), Vendor portal (VS-03.4), Store receiving (VS-07.3).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vendor charging inflated prices**: Violation of price freezes; mitigated by pre-negotiated emergency price sheets stored in the ERP vendor master.

### Automation Opportunity
- Supplier portal direct notification of emergency POs.

### Controls
- CTL-83 (ensure calamity pricing & relief-channel compliance.)
- CTL-770: PO matched to store-receiving logs; variance audit on billing price before payment release.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 hours PO creation to approval

### Cross-references
- links to VS-03 (procurement), VS-15 (P2P), VS-03.2 (PO cycle), VS-03.4 (vendor portal), VS-07.3 (store receiving)

## W5184. DC-to-Store Emergency Transport Route Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-landfall road clearance reports; relief dispatch preparation |
| **Frequency** | Continuous post-disaster |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 dispatch routes managed per storm |
| **Owner** | Fleet Planner / Logistics Control Tower Lead (VS-06) |
| **Participants** | Drivers, LGU Road Officials, DPWH (Public Works) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monitor road closures: collect reports from DPWH, local news, and regional store managers. | Fleet Planner / Logistics Control Tower Lead (VS-06) | Fleet Planner / Logistics Control Tower Lead (VS-06) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Determine optimized route: use navigation tools to bypass flooded roads, collapsed bridges, and landslide zones. | Fleet Planner / Logistics Control Tower Lead (VS-06) | Fleet Planner / Logistics Control Tower Lead (VS-06) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Brief drivers: provide route maps, emergency LGU contacts, satellite phones, and road-clearance credentials. | Fleet Planner / Logistics Control Tower Lead (VS-06) | Fleet Planner / Logistics Control Tower Lead (VS-06) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Monitor truck GPS in real-time; coordinate backup routes if drivers report unexpected blockages. | Fleet Planner / Logistics Control Tower Lead (VS-06) | Fleet Planner / Logistics Control Tower Lead (VS-06) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Logistics control tower (W250), GPS tracking system, in-house Route Optimization Engine (fit-gap C14).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Truck stuck in flood / Hijack**: Delivery stranded or looted in remote areas; mitigated by coordinating police/military escorts for high-value relief loads.

### Automation Opportunity
- Real-time mapping overlay of road blocks on fleet routing software.

### Controls
- CTL-770 (ensure controlled execution — Emergency Material Allocation, Disaster Kit Assembly & Staging Operations (PA-180.1).)
- CTL-770: Driver route plans approved by Fleet Supervisor; satellite check-in schedules established.

### Time Estimate
- ~30-60 mins per dispatch routing

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (logistics), VS-110 (freight procurement), VS-159 (corporate security)
