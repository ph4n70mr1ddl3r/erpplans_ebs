## W2913. SC/PWD/Solo Parent Tax Credit Computation & Monthly Claim Buildup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~140K–225K transactions/month |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | Tax Accountant, Finance, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ERP aggregates SC/PWD/Solo Parent discount amount and associated VAT component from mandatory book per entity/month | ERP automation | Tax Accountant | Monthly |
| 2 | Tax Accountant computes tax-credit-claimable amount (discount treated as deductible from gross sales or VAT output per BIR rules); validates computation methodology | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | Tax Manager approves monthly claim buildup; books tax-credit receivable; integrates into VAT return computation | Tax Manager | CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Mandatory book aggregation; tax-credit receivable GL; VAT return integration

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Recovery risk**: Under-claim forfeits rightful credit; mitigated by ERP-aggregated computation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "discount amount and associated VAT component from mandatory book per entity/month" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "Tax Accountant computes tax-credit-claimable amount" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves monthly claim buildup" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-486 (ensure controlled execution — Tax Credit Recovery, Registry Reporting & Audit Defense (PA-85.3).)
- operational: ERP-aggregated computation

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 days/month
