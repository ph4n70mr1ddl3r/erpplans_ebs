## W3513. Freight Invoice Audit & Match

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Carrier freight invoice (links to VS-15); monthly cycle |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + monthly |
| **Volume** | High-volume across all carriers/legs |
| **Owner** | Logistics / AP (Freight Audit) |
| **Participants** | Logistics (links to VS-06), AP (links to VS-15.1/W7), Finance (links to VS-17), Carrier, IT (links to VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AP/Logistics receive the carrier freight invoice and perform automated freight audit (links to W1166): match the invoice line to the shipment/load and contracted rate/accessorial (W3499), validate mode/lane/weight/cube/distance, apply fuel surcharge (W3519) and accessorial rules, and flag exceptions (rate variance, duplicate, unauthorized accessorial, off-guide W3500) | AP / Logistics | Logistics Manager / AP Manager | Per invoice |
| 2 | AP/Logistics resolve exceptions: validate with the carrier/Logistics, approve/deduct/dispute per contract (W3499), and manage short-paid/disputed invoices and credits; capture dispute root-cause for the carrier scorecard (W3512) and reduction program (W3518) | AP / Logistics / Carrier | Logistics Manager / Finance | Per exception |
| 3 | AP post the approved freight invoice to GL (links to VS-17.1/W7), allocate to cost object/entity (W3514), and pay per terms (links to VS-15.2); report audit-accuracy, exception rate, and savings-from-audit to the freight analytics (W3516) and review (W3520) | AP / Finance | Finance Controller | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Freight-audit/Oracle Transportation Execution (links to W1166/VS-27); AP/GL (links to VS-15.1/VS-17.1/W7); carrier contract/rate (links to W3499); cost allocation (links to W3514/VS-72)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-payment risk**: Unaudited invoices overcharge (rate/accessorial errors); mitigated by automated audit and rate match
- **Dispute-backlog risk**: Disputes stall AP and carrier relations; mitigated by disciplined resolution


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics receive the carrier freight invoice and perform automated freight…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validate with the carrier/Logistics, approve/deduct/dispute per contract" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "AP post the approved freight invoice to GL" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-78 (ensure freight invoice accuracy & carrier compliance.)
- operational: automated audit and rate match; operational: disciplined resolution

### Time Estimate
- Per invoice; high-volume batch

| Field | Detail |

| **Trigger** | Audited freight invoice (W3513); intercompany need |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | Group freight across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Finance / Logistics |
| **Participants** | Finance (links to VS-17/VS-72), Logistics (links to VS-06), AP (links to VS-15), Treasury (links to VS-18), Intercompany (links to VS-72) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance/Logistics allocate freight cost to the correct cost object: by entity (Depot/Logistics/Digital Commerce/Property links to VS-72), by site/DC/store (links to VS-29 location), by category/SKU (for landed cost W3515), by channel (store/ecommerce links to VS-10), and by customer/order (cost-to-serve W3516); apply driver-based allocation keys | Finance / Logistics | Finance Controller | Monthly |
| 2 | Finance/Intercompany manage the intercompany freight: BuildRight Logistics Inc. warehousing/distribution fees (profile §2), Digital Commerce per-order fulfillment fee, and cross-entity shared-services chargeback (links to VS-72); settle at arm's-length transfer pricing and eliminate on consolidation (links to VS-17.2) | Finance / Intercompany | Finance Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Treasury/AP pay the carrier per terms (links to VS-15.2/VS-18.2), manage freight float/cash impact, and report the allocated freight cost by entity/site/category to FP&A (links to VS-17.4/W3516) and intercompany (links to VS-72); reconcile allocations to GL (links to VS-17.1) | Treasury / AP / Finance | Finance Controller / CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- AP/GL (links to VS-15/VS-17.1); intercompany (links to VS-72); consolidation (links to VS-17.2); treasury/bank (links to VS-18.2); location/cost-object (links to VS-29); FP&A (links to VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Intercompany-dispute risk**: Cross-entity chargebacks contested; mitigated by transfer-pricing policy (links to VS-72)

- System auto-file of IC freight-settlement evidence at arm's-length transfer pricing (replaces manual Step 2 filing).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile allocations to GL" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-561 (ensure controlled execution — Freight Audit, Payment & Freight Cost Analytics (PA-110.3).)
- operational: driver-based allocation; operational: transfer-pricing policy (links to VS-72)

### Time Estimate
- Monthly
