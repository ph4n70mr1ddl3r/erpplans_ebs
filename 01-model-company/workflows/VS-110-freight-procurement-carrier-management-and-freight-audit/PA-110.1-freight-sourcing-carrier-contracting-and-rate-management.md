## W3497. Freight Category Strategy & Spend Baseline

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual procurement/SCM planning (links to VS-33.1); spend-review cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual + periodic refresh |
| **Volume** | Group freight spend (inbound/import/line-haul/last-mile) |
| **Owner** | Logistics / Procurement (Category) |
| **Participants** | Logistics (links to VS-06), Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34), FP&A (links to VS-17.4/VS-33.1), Treasury (links to VS-18), Supply Planning (links to VS-02) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics/Procurement build the freight-spend baseline and category strategy: spend by leg (import ocean, drayage, inbound vendor→DC/DSD, inter-DC/inter-island, DC→store, last-mile), by carrier, by lane/region, by mode, and by cost element (line-haul, fuel surcharge, accessorial); identify consolidation, mode-shift, and carrier-rationalization opportunities | Logistics / Procurement / FP&A | COO | Annual |
| 2 | Logistics/Supply Planning set the freight-service requirements and lane network: required transit/lead times (links to VS-02 lead times), service frequencies, capacity needs (TEUs/month, replenishment orders/month links to profile §7), network design (DC catchments links to VS-04), and island/region constraints (links to W66); align to the S&OP (links to VS-02.1) and demand (links to VS-02) | Logistics / Supply Planning | COO | Annual |
| 3 | Logistics/FP&A set the freight cost-reduction targets and the carrier-base strategy (core carriers, capacity tiers, framework vs spot), align to the budget (links to VS-33.1/VS-17.4) and commodity/fuel outlook (links to VS-106/VS-61), and approve the sourcing plan (W3498); report to governance (W3520/W110 review) | Logistics / FP&A | COO / CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Freight-spend/BI (links to VS-28.1); S&OP (links to VS-02.1); DC network (links to VS-04); budget (links to VS-33.1/VS-17.4); commodity/fuel (links to VS-106/VS-61); procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Spend-opacity risk**: Fragmented freight spend across legs/POs obscures the total; mitigated by consolidated spend baseline
- **Target-misalignment risk**: Cost targets conflict with service requirements; mitigated by joint logistics/SCM planning


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics/Procurement build the freight-spend baseline and category strategy" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "Logistics/Supply Planning set the freight-service requirements and lane network" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "Logistics/FP&A set the freight cost-reduction targets and the carrier-base" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-559 (ensure controlled execution — Freight Sourcing, Carrier Contracting & Rate Management (PA-110.1).)
- operational: consolidated spend baseline; operational: joint logistics/SCM planning

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 weeks annual

## W3498. Carrier & 3PL Sourcing, RFQ & Qualification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Sourcing plan (W3497); contract-expiry; new-lane/capacity need |
| **Frequency** | Annual RFQ + event-driven |
| **Volume** | Carrier base across all legs (~dozens of carriers/3PLs) |
| **Owner** | Procurement (Category) / Logistics |
| **Participants** | Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34), Logistics (links to VS-06), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Insurance/Risk (links to VS-26), HSE (links to VS-24), Carrier/3PL |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement/Logistics run the carrier/3PL RFQ: define lane/volume/service specs per leg, issue RFP to qualified carriers and 3PLs (links to VS-03 methodology), pre-qualify on capability (equipment, coverage, capacity), financial stability, insurance, safety/regulatory record (links to VS-26/VS-24), and references | Procurement / Logistics | COO | Per RFQ |
| 2 | Procurement/Logistics evaluate bids: rate per lane/mode, fuel/accessorial structure, transit/lead time, capacity commitment, SLA, technology (track-and-trace, EDI/API links to VS-27), and total cost; score and short-list; manage a balanced core-carrier panel per lane | Procurement / Logistics / FP&A | COO | Per RFQ |
| 3 | Procurement/Legal recommend award per authority (links to VS-36), manage framework/master agreements where volume warrants, and route selected carriers to contracting (W3499) and onboarding (W3504); log the award and feed the routing guide (W3500) | Procurement / Legal | COO | Per RFQ |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/vendor (links to VS-03/VS-34); legal (links to VS-100.3); insurance (links to VS-26); HSE (links to VS-24); IT/EDI (links to VS-27)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low-bid risk**: Lowest rate masks poor service/hidden accessorials; mitigated by total-cost and SLA scoring
- **Carrier-concentration risk**: Over-reliance on one carrier per lane; mitigated by dual-sourcing core carriers


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics run the carrier/3PL RFQ" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-track of "Procurement/Logistics evaluate bids" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-route of "route selected carriers to contracting" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-559 (ensure controlled execution — Freight Sourcing, Carrier Contracting & Rate Management (PA-110.1).)
- operational: total-cost and SLA scoring; operational: dual-sourcing core carriers

### Time Estimate
- 4–8 weeks per RFQ cycle

## W3499. Carrier Contracting, Rate Negotiation & Tariff Structures

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Award (W3498); contract cycle |
| **Frequency** | Per carrier + annual rate review |
| **Volume** | Carrier base across all legs |
| **Owner** | Procurement (Category) / Legal |
| **Participants** | Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Logistics (links to VS-06), Finance (links to VS-40/VS-17), Carrier/3PL |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement/Logistics negotiate the rate structure per carrier/leg: point-to-point rates, zone/mileage-based rates (per-km by vehicle type links to W1166), minimum charge, FAK (freight-all-kind) vs commodity rate, accessorial schedule (detention, demurrage, layover, loading/unloading, fuel surcharge formula), and volume rebates; benchmark vs market (links to VS-57 methodology) | Procurement / Logistics | COO | Per carrier |
| 2 | Legal/Procurement structure and execute the contract: term, capacity commitment and volume tier, SLA (links to VS-56/W1371), liability/cargo insurance limits (links to VS-26), claims process (W3511), fuel/index mechanism (W3519), termination, and dispute resolution (links to VS-100.3); ensure multi-entity applicability (links to VS-72) | Legal / Procurement | COO / CFO | Per carrier |
| 3 | Procurement/Logistics load the contracted rates/tariff into the Oracle Transportation Execution/ERP rate master (links to VS-27/W1166), publish the rate-effective date, and integrate to the routing guide (W3500) and freight-audit baseline (W3513); track contract compliance and savings vs baseline (W3518) | Procurement / Logistics / IT | COO | Per carrier |

### System Touchpoints
- Oracle Transportation Execution/ERP rate master (links to VS-27/W1166); legal/contract (links to VS-100.3/W402); insurance (links to VS-26); multi-entity (links to VS-72); freight audit (links to W3513)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rate-opaqueness risk**: Complex accessorial structures obscure true cost; mitigated by total-cost modeling and audit (W3513)
- **Fuel-clause risk**: Poor fuel/index mechanism passes volatility to BuildRight; mitigated by balanced formula (W3519)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics negotiate the rate structure per carrier/leg" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-track of "track contract compliance and savings vs baseline" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-559 (ensure controlled execution — Freight Sourcing, Carrier Contracting & Rate Management (PA-110.1).)
- operational: total-cost modeling and audit (W3513); operational: balanced formula (W3519)

### Time Estimate
- 2–6 weeks per carrier

| **Trigger** | Contracted rates (W3499); network/lanes |
| **Frequency** | Maintained + on change |
| **Volume** | All origin→destination lanes |
| **Owner** | Logistics / Transportation |
| **Participants** | Logistics (links to VS-06), Supply Planning (links to VS-02), Procurement (links to VS-03), DC Operations (links to VS-04), Carrier |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics publish the routing guide: preferred carrier(s) and mode per origin→destination lane (vendor→DC, vendor→store DSD, DC→store, DC→DC, DC/store→customer, port→DC drayage), rate reference, packaging/handling requirements, appointment/dock rules (links to VS-04/W585), and documentation requirements; cascade to vendors (W3502) and internal teams | Logistics / Transportation | COO | Maintained |
| 2 | Logistics/IT enforce the routing guide at tender (W3501): system-suggested carrier per lane, compliance flags for off-guide routing, approval workflow for exceptions, and capture of routing-deviation reason and cost impact; integrate to PO (links to VS-03.2) and SO (links to VS-11/VS-10) | Logistics / IT | COO | Continuous |
| 3 | Logistics/Procurement monitor routing-guide compliance, carrier share per lane, off-guide spend, and capacity/utilization; rebalance carrier assignments and update the guide as rates/lanes change (W3499); report compliance and off-guide cost to the carrier scorecard (W3512) and review (W3520) | Logistics / Procurement | COO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Oracle Transportation Execution/routing guide (links to VS-27); PO/SO (links to VS-03.2/VS-11/VS-10); dock scheduling (links to VS-04/W585); carrier scorecard (links to VS-67/W3512)

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Guide-staleness risk**: Outdated guide routes to expired rates/lanes; mitigated by maintenance discipline

- System auto-log of "Logistics publish the routing guide" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-flag of "flags for off-guide routing, approval workflow for exceptions, and capture of…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "Logistics/Procurement monitor routing-guide compliance, carrier share per lane, off-guide" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-78 (ensure freight invoice accuracy & carrier compliance.)
- operational: system enforcement and exception approval; operational: enforcement and exception approval; operational: maintenance discipline

### Time Estimate
- Maintained continuously + monthly review

## W3501. Freight Tendering, Spot Quote & Capacity Booking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Shipment ready (links to VS-04/VS-06); routing guide (W3500) |
| **Frequency** | Per shipment + daily/weekly batches |
| **Volume** | ~5,000 replenishment + ~42.9K ecommerce + imports/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics / Transportation |
| **Participants** | Logistics (links to VS-06), DC Operations (links to VS-04), Carrier/3PL, Procurement (links to VS-03) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics/DC tender the shipment to the routing-guide carrier (W3500): automated tender via Oracle Transportation Execution/EDI/API (links to VS-27), shipment details (origin/destination, weight/cube, commodity, required pickup/delivery), rate confirmation, and capacity booking; manage accept/decline and fallback to next-preferred carrier | Logistics / DC / Carrier | Logistics Manager | Per shipment |
| 2 | For off-guide, overflow, or peak capacity, Logistics procure spot/spot-quote capacity: solicit spot quotes from approved carriers/brokers, evaluate rate/transit, book per authority, and document the spot exception and reason; manage peak-season (Ber-months, typhoon recovery links to VS-69) capacity | Logistics / Procurement | Logistics Manager | Per event |
| 3 | Logistics confirm booking, dispatch (links to W1165 last-mile/W3505 outbound), track the shipment (W3508), and capture the rate basis (contract vs spot) for freight audit (W3513); manage load consolidation (W3507) and tender efficiency | Logistics / DC | Logistics Manager | Per shipment |

### System Touchpoints
- Oracle Transportation Execution/tender (links to VS-27); carrier EDI/API; dock scheduling (links to VS-04/W585); track-and-trace (links to W3508); freight audit (links to W3513)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Capacity-shortfall risk**: Peak/typhoon demand exceeds contracted capacity; mitigated by spot process and capacity planning
- **Tender-inefficiency risk**: Manual tendering delays shipment and inflates cost; mitigated by automated Oracle Transportation Execution tendering


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics/DC tender the shipment to the routing-guide carrier" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "document the spot exception and reason" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-dispatch of "Logistics confirm booking, dispatch" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-559 (ensure controlled execution — Freight Sourcing, Carrier Contracting & Rate Management (PA-110.1).)
- operational: routing-guide authority documented; spot exceptions approved and logged per lane

### Time Estimate
- Per shipment; high-volume batch daily
