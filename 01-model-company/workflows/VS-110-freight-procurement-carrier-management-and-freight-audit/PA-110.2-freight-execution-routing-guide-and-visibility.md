## W3505. Outbound DC-to-Store Freight Planning & Load Tendering

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store replenishment wave (links to VS-02/VS-04/W1368); routing guide (W3500) |
| **Frequency** | Daily waves; 2–3 deliveries/store/week |
| **Volume** | ~50,000 replenishment orders/month (production-calibrated per data-volumes §1.1; consolidated into ~60–80 store delivery drops/day chain-wide) |
| **Owner** | Logistics / DC Operations |
| **Participants** | Logistics (links to VS-06.1), DC Operations (links to VS-04.2), Supply Planning (links to VS-02), Carrier/3PL, Store (links to VS-07.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics/DC plan the outbound freight from the replenishment wave (links to VS-04.2/W1368): consolidate orders into loads by store/region/priority, select vehicle type (10-wheeler/6-wheeler per profile §7.2), apply the routing guide carrier (W3500), and sequence multi-stop routes (W3507); balance cost vs service | Logistics / DC / Supply Planning | Logistics Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Logistics tender the load to the routing-guide carrier (W3501), confirm capacity/rate, schedule pickup and dock door (links to VS-04/W1353), and dispatch; manage owned-fleet (20%) vs third-party (80%) assignment per profile §7.2 and capacity | Logistics / Carrier / DC | Logistics Manager | Daily |
| 3 | DC/Logistics load and ship (links to VS-04.2), capture shipment/manifest, transmit ASN to store (links to VS-07.3), track in-transit (W3508), and confirm delivery/receipt (links to VS-07.3); capture the rate/shipment for freight audit (W3513) and analytics (W3516) | DC / Logistics / Store | Logistics Manager | Daily |

### System Touchpoints
- WMS/outbound (links to VS-04.2/W1368); Oracle Transportation Execution routing (links to VS-27/W3500); dock scheduling (links to VS-04/W1353); track-and-trace (links to W3508); store receiving (links to VS-07.3); freight audit (links to W3513)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-utilization risk**: Partially-full trucks waste capacity; mitigated by consolidation (W3507)
- **Service-miss risk**: Late replenishment causes stockouts; mitigated by SLA and wave planning


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics/DC plan the outbound freight from the replenishment wave" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "Logistics tender the load to the routing-guide carrier" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "DC/Logistics load and ship" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-560 (ensure controlled execution — Freight Execution, Routing Guide & Visibility (PA-110.2).)
- operational: consolidation (W3507); operational: SLA and wave planning

### Time Estimate
- Daily; high-volume

## W3507. Multi-Stop Route Optimization & Load Consolidation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Outbound wave (W3505); last-mile batch (W3506) |
| **Frequency** | Daily + per batch |
| **Volume** | All DC→store and last-mile loads |
| **Owner** | Logistics / Transportation |
| **Participants** | Logistics (links to VS-06), DC Operations (links to VS-04), IT (links to VS-27/VS-30.2), Supply Planning (links to VS-02) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics optimize multi-stop routes in the Route Optimization Engine (in-house build — fit-gap C14) for DC→store delivery (links to W1369/W3505) and last-mile (W3506): sequence stops, apply road/constraint data (Philippine road constraints links to W1369), vehicle capacity, time windows, and driver-hours; minimize distance/cost while meeting service | Logistics | Logistics Manager | Daily |
| 2 | Logistics/DC consolidate loads: combine replenishment orders by store/region/priority into full-truck loads (profile §7.2: each truck consolidates a store's ~20–30 transfer orders since its previous drop), co-load inbound/outbound where backhaul opportunities exist, and manage cube/weight utilization; balance consolidation vs service/urgency | Logistics / DC | Logistics Manager | Daily |
| 3 | Logistics/Finance measure route efficiency (cost/km, cost/stop, stops/route, load factor, empty miles/backhaul %), validate savings vs baseline (W3497), and feed continuous improvement (W3518); tune the optimization as the network/demand changes | Logistics / Finance | Logistics Manager | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Route Optimization Engine multi-stop routing (in-house build — fit-gap C14; links to VS-27/W1369); WMS/load (links to VS-04.2); analytics (links to VS-28.1); automation/AI (links to VS-30.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Suboptimal-routing risk**: Manual routing wastes miles; mitigated by the Route Optimization Engine (in-house build — fit-gap C14)
- **Service-vs-cost tension**: Over-consolidation delays urgent orders; mitigated by priority rules


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics optimize multi-stop routes in the Route Optimization Engine (in-house build — fit-gap C14) for DC→store delivery" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "Logistics/DC consolidate loads" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "validate savings vs baseline" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-560 (ensure controlled execution — Freight Execution, Routing Guide & Visibility (PA-110.2).)
- operational: Route Optimization Engine tuning (in-house build — fit-gap C14); operational: priority rules

### Time Estimate
- Daily + monthly analytics
