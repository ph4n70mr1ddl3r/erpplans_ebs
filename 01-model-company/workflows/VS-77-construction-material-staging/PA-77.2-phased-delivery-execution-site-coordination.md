## W2702. Phase Delivery Trigger & Material Staging at DC

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Construction phase ready for material |
| **Frequency** | Per phase; ~80–120 phase deliveries/month |
| **Volume** | ~80–120/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Coord, DC Ops, Trade Sales Rep, Contractor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contractor notifies phase readiness (3–5 days advance); Rep triggers phase delivery order; Logistics verifies availability in project staging area at DC | Contractor / Trade Sales Rep / Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 15–30 min |
| 2 | DC Ops picks and stages: verifies quantities against phase list, stages at loading dock, creates manifest; Quality inspects outbound materials | DC Ops / Quality | DC Ops Manager | 2–4 hours |
| 3 | Logistics Coordinator schedules delivery: assigns truck, coordinates time with contractor, arranges special equipment; sends confirmation with ETA | Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 30–60 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Premature-trigger risk**: phases shipped on early notification arrive to an unready site and weather in the mud; mitigated by the 3–5-day advance notice (Step 1) with the scheduled ETA confirmation (Step 3)
- **Outbound-quality risk**: a phase picked short or damaged discovered at site restarts the whole delivery cycle; mitigated by the quantity verification against the phase list with Quality's outbound inspection (Step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- Delivery scheduling auto-optimized from the route/dock/traffic data; real-time tracking auto-pushed to customer and store; POD auto-captured digitally

### Controls
- CTL-461 (ensure controlled execution — Phased Delivery Execution & Site Coordination (PA-77.2).)
- operational: Logistics Coordinator sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~80–120/month
- Frequency: Per phase; ~80–120 phase deliveries/month
- Absorbed into the Logistics Coordinator role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2703. On-Site Material Verification & Receipt

| **Volume** | ~80–120/month |
| **Owner** | Contractor Site Representative |
| **Participants** | Contractor Rep, Driver, Trade Sales Rep |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Driver arrives: contacts site rep, directs to unloading area; rep verifies manifest against phase list item by item; checks for transit damage | Driver / Contractor Representative | Logistics Coordinator | 30–60 min |
| 2 | Site rep accepts: signs receipt noting exceptions; photographs materials; Driver photographs signed receipt and uploads via app | Contractor Representative / Driver | Logistics Coordinator | 15–20 min |
| 3 | Trade Sales Rep reviews: processes accepted delivery, triggers billing milestone, addresses exceptions (replacement, shortage investigation, reservation adjustment) | Trade Sales Rep | Trade Sales Manager | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Blanket-signature risk**: receipts signed unverified convert shortages into disputed billing milestones; mitigated by the item-by-item manifest check with photographed exceptions (Steps 1–2) before the Rep's billing trigger (Step 3)
- **Exception-drift risk**: noted exceptions logged but never resolved hang over the project file; mitigated by the exception processing (Step 3: replacement, shortage investigation, reservation adjustment)

| **Participants** | Contractor Site Mgr, Trade Sales Rep, Logistics |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Rep advises on storage: cement off-ground under cover, tiles flat on pallets, lumber with spacers for air circulation, paint in cool dry area | Trade Sales Rep / Contractor | Trade Sales Manager | Per phase delivery |
| 2 | Rep conducts periodic site storage assessment: verifies proper storage, identifies issues, advises corrective action; documents for project file | Trade Sales Rep | Trade Sales Manager | Per site visit |
| 3 | If material damaged from improper storage: coordinates replacement at contractor's expense (not BuildRight liability post-delivery); documents for insurance | Trade Sales Rep / Contractor | Trade Sales Manager | Per incident |

### System Touchpoints

- **Liability-blur risk**: post-delivery damage blamed on product quality instead of storage; mitigated by the documented assessments (Step 2) and the contractor-expense replacement rule post-delivery (Step 3)

## W2705. Cross-Phase Material Transfer & Reallocation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Schedule change requiring reallocation |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; 5–10 per project |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 per project |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Representative |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Rep, Supply Planning, Contractor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contractor requests reallocation: construction ahead/behind schedule; wants to move materials between phases; Rep documents request | Contractor / Trade Sales Rep | Trade Sales Manager | 15–30 min |
| 2 | Supply Planning evaluates impact: checks if reallocating causes future stockout, identifies if additional procurement needed, adjusts reservation | Supply Planning | VP Supply Chain | 1 day |
| 3 | Trade Sales Rep confirms reallocation: adjusted quantities, cost implications, updated schedule; updates ERP; monitors for cascading impacts | Trade Sales Rep / Contractor | Trade Sales Manager | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory/ATP (links to VS-05 / W105 multi-channel allocation, VS-29 / W252 item master)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transfer-cost opacity risk**: reallocations treated as free moves ignore the double-handling and re-staging cost; mitigated by the cost implications stated in the confirmation (Step 3)
- **Cascading-shortage risk**: reallocating materials to an ahead-of-schedule phase stockouts the later phase it fed; mitigated by the future-stockout check with additional procurement (Step 2) and the cascading-impact monitoring (Step 3)
- **Verbal-reallocation risk**: site-level agreement to move materials without the ERP update corrupts every downstream reservation; mitigated by the documented request (Step 1) and the ERP update (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-demand auto-forecast from the project schedule; delivery-phasing auto-sequenced; site-reconciliation auto-tracked from the delivery-vs-installed delta

### Controls
- CTL-461 (ensure controlled execution — Phased Delivery Execution & Site Coordination (PA-77.2).)
- operational: inventory-count integrity; Operational: Trade Sales Representative sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~5–10 per project
- Frequency: Ad-hoc; 5–10 per project
- Absorbed into the Trade Sales Representative role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

| **Participants** | Trade Sales Rep, Receiving Clerk, Finance, Contractor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contractor identifies surplus: unused tiles, excess cement, leftover lumber, extra fixtures; contacts Rep; verifies eligibility (unused condition, within return window) | Contractor / Trade Sales Rep | Trade Sales Manager | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Logistics arranges pickup; Receiving Clerk processes return: inspects condition, accepts if resaleable, routes to inventory | Logistics / Receiving Clerk | Logistics Manager | 3–5 days |
| 3 | Finance processes credit: at original price minus restocking fee (5–10%); adjusts billing; Trade Sales Rep conducts project close-out review | Finance / Trade Sales Rep | Finance Controller | 1 week |

- **Surplus-eligibility risk**: used or weathered materials collected as returns fail inspection at cost to both sides; mitigated by the eligibility verification (Step 1: unused condition, return window) before pickup
- **Credit-friction risk**: restocking-fee surprises at close-out poison an otherwise clean project; mitigated by the disclosed 5–10% restocking fee credited against billing (Step 3)

## W2708. Project Delivery SLA Compliance & Penalty Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Delivery milestone missed |
| **Frequency** | Per delayed delivery; ~10–15/year |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 per year |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Mgr, Logistics Manager, Finance, Contractor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics Manager reports delay: root cause (DC error, vendor late, truck breakdown, weather, access), revised ETA; Rep communicates to contractor | Logistics Manager / Trade Sales Rep | VP Supply Chain | Within 4 hours |
| 2 | Trade Sales Rep manages SLA penalty: if contract includes penalty (1%/day late), Finance calculates; Rep negotiates resolution (waiver, expedited replacement) | Trade Sales Rep / Finance | Trade Sales Manager | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Logistics Manager conducts root cause analysis: implements prevention (earlier staging, backup trucks, weather planning); tracks on-time rate (target: ≥95%) | Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | Per incident |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Penalty-spiral risk**: 1%/day penalties accruing on an unmanaged delay compound past the project's margin; mitigated by the early delay report with revised ETA (Step 1) and the negotiated resolution (Step 2: waiver, expedited replacement)
- **Root-cause recurrence risk**: delays root-caused to weather repeatedly without prevention; mitigated by the prevention implementation (Step 3: earlier staging, backup trucks, weather planning) against the ≥95% on-time target

### Automation Opportunity
- Delivery scheduling auto-optimized from the route/dock/traffic data; real-time tracking auto-pushed to customer and store; POD auto-captured digitally

### Controls
- CTL-182 (ensure jobsite delivery accuracy & site reconciliation.)
- CTL-461 (compliance-monitoring — links to VS-22); Operational: Trade Sales Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~Within 4 per occurrence
- Volume: ~10–15 per year
- Frequency: Per delayed delivery; ~10–15/year
- Absorbed into the Trade Sales Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2709. Project Completion Material Reconciliation & Close-Out

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project construction completed |
| **Frequency** | Per project; ~20–30/year |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 per year |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Mgr, Finance, Supply Planning, Contractor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Manager initiates reconciliation: compares ordered vs. delivered vs. returned, reconciles all phases, verifies no outstanding requests | Trade Sales Manager | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Finance completes billing: all payments received, retention released, credits settled, project account closed; produces final P&L | Finance | CFO | 1 week |
| 3 | Supply Planning releases unused reservations: returns inventory to general stock, closes staging areas, cancels remaining POs; Trade Sales Manager conducts lessons-learned review | Supply Planning / Trade Sales Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Site-readiness risk**: delivery arrives before the site can receive (no crane slot, access blocked, phase incomplete), forcing re-delivery and demurrage; mitigated by the site-readiness confirmation gate before dispatch
- **Reconciliation-gap risk**: projects closing with ordered-vs-delivered-vs-returned unreconciled strand open reservations and unbilled items; mitigated by the three-way reconciliation (Step 1) before Finance completes billing and closes the account (Step 2)
- **Reservation-remnant risk**: unused reservations left open after close-out ghost-lock inventory for the next project; mitigated by the reservation release with staging-area closure and PO cancellation (Step 3)
