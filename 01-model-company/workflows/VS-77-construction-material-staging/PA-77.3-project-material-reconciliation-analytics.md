## W2710. Project Material Consumption vs. Estimate Variance Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project completion or quarterly review |
| **Frequency** | Per project; quarterly portfolio review |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 projects |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Mgr, Category Manager, FP&A |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Manager compares estimated vs. actual: per-item variance, variance % per category, total project variance in PHP; identifies consistent over/under-estimation | Trade Sales Manager | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Category Manager investigates significant variances: over-estimation (surplus/returns) vs. under-estimation (rush orders); develops improved estimation templates | Category Manager / Trade Sales Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 day |
| 3 | FP&A incorporates into pricing model: adjusts estimation factors, improves margin prediction, develops project-type benchmarks; presents quarterly to CFO | FP&A / Trade Sales Manager | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Estimate-drift risk**: the same over-estimation pattern repeating across projects because nobody closes the loop; mitigated by the improved estimation templates (Step 2) feeding the pricing-model factors (Step 3)
- **Variance-noise risk**: per-item noise burying the category-level biases worth managing; mitigated by the per-category and total-PHP variance view (Step 1) with significant-variance investigation

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls
- CTL-462 (ensure controlled execution — Project Material Reconciliation & Analytics (PA-77.3).)
- operational: Trade Sales Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~20–30 projects
- Frequency: Per project; quarterly portfolio review
- Absorbed into the Trade Sales Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2713. Project Inventory Carrying Cost Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly analysis cycle — Project Inventory Carrying Cost |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 projects with reserved inventory |
| **Owner** | Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Analyst, Supply Planning Manager, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Analyst calculates carrying cost: average reserved inventory value (PHP 200M–500M), carrying rate (~15–20% annually), cost per project per month | Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Analyst identifies high cost projects: long duration, early reservation, slow phase delivery; recommends optimization (reduce lead time, consignment, just-in-phase) | Finance Analyst / Supply Planning Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1 day |
| 3 | Finance Controller incorporates into pricing: adjusts quotes for carrying cost; Trade Sales Manager uses to negotiate better payment terms | Finance Controller / Trade Sales Manager | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory/ATP (links to VS-05 / W105 multi-channel allocation, VS-29 / W252 item master)
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Snapshot-staleness risk**: carrying cost computed on a stock snapshot misses the intra-month reservation swings; mitigated by the average-reserved-inventory basis (Step 1) over the PHP 200M–500M range
- **Carrying-cost invisibility risk**: quotes priced without the 15–20% annual carrying rate on PHP 200M–500M reserved stock subsidize long projects; mitigated by the carrying-cost calculation (Step 1) incorporated into pricing (Step 3)
- **Early-reservation incentive risk**: contractors reserving whole-project quantities at award because waiting costs them nothing; mitigated by the high-cost project identification (Step 2: long duration, early reservation, slow phases) with just-in-phase recommendations

### Automation Opportunity
- API-driven real-time sync (catalog, inventory, pricing, orders) eliminating manual data entry; auto-reconciliation of marketplace settlement vs. BuildRight records

### Controls
- CTL-462 (ensure controlled execution — Project Material Reconciliation & Analytics (PA-77.3).)
- operational: inventory-count integrity; Operational: Finance Analyst sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~20–30 projects with reserved inventory
- Frequency: Quarterly
- Absorbed into the Finance Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2716. Project Supply Chain Risk & Resilience Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly risk review |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 active projects |
| **Owner** | Supply Planning Manager |
| **Participants** | Supply Planning Mgr, Procurement, Trade Sales Manager, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Supply Planning Manager assesses risks: vendor concentration, import dependency, DC capacity, weather, geopolitical; per project | Supply Planning Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Manager develops mitigation: alternative vendors, safety stock, flexible scheduling, strategic pre-positioning; quantifies risk exposure in PHP per project | Supply Planning Manager / Procurement | VP Supply Chain | 1 day |
| 3 | VP Supply Chain reviews risk portfolio: total exposure, concentration, insurance adequacy, contingency budget; approves mitigation investments | VP Supply Chain | COO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113 BI platform, W231 QBR dashboard)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Contingency-gap risk**: risk registers listing exposures without the contingency budget line to fund responses; mitigated by the VP-reviewed contingency budget and insurance adequacy (Step 3)
- **Concentration-blindspot risk**: vendor or import dependency assessed chain-wide but not per project; mitigated by the per-project risk assessment (Step 1: vendor concentration, import dependency, DC capacity, weather, geopolitical)
- **Unquantified-mitigation risk**: resilience investments approved without the exposure they protect; mitigated by the PHP-quantified risk exposure per project (Step 2) with the VP-reviewed mitigation investments (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Credit-score auto-computed from the financial-data feed; credit-limit auto-suggested from the scoring model; collection-aging auto-tracked with escalation

### Controls

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~20–30 active projects
- Frequency: Quarterly
- Absorbed into the Supply Planning Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
