## W2694. Construction Project Material Requirements Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contractor engages BuildRight for project supply |
| **Frequency** | Per project; ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects) |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 projects; PHP 2M–100M each |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Mgr, Category Managers, Supply Planning, Procurement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Manager works with contractor: reviews blueprint and bill of quantities, identifies BuildRight-supplied categories, estimates quantities per phase | Trade Sales Manager / Contractor | VP Merchandising | 2–5 days |
| 2 | Supply Planning validates availability: checks inventory vs. requirements, identifies long-lead items (imported tiles: 60–90 days), raises advance POs | Supply Planning | VP Supply Chain | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Trade Sales Manager produces phased delivery schedule: divides project into phases, allocates quantities, aligns delivery dates with timeline; obtains contractor sign-off | Trade Sales Manager | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Long-lead blindside risk**: imported tiles needed at 60–90 days discovered after the construction clock starts; mitigated by the long-lead identification with advance POs (Step 2) before the phased schedule locks (Step 3)
- **Quantity-basis risk**: quantities estimated off the blueprint without the bill-of-quantities cross-check over- and under-buy whole phases; mitigated by the blueprint review with per-phase estimates (Step 1) and the contractor sign-off (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls
- CTL-460 (ensure controlled execution — Project Material Planning & Phasing (PA-77.1).)
- operational: Trade Sales Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~2–5 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~20–30 projects; PHP 2M–100M each
- Frequency: Per project; ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects)
- Absorbed into the Trade Sales Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2695. Project Material Reservation & Inventory Allocation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project material plan approved |
| **Frequency** | Per project; ongoing monitoring |
| **Volume** | ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects) |
| **Owner** | Supply Planner |
| **Participants** | Supply Planner, Category Manager, DC Ops, Trade Sales Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Supply Planner creates inventory reservations: allocates stock against project PO, prevents ROP from consuming project stock; configures ATP for uncommitted availability | Supply Planner | VP Supply Chain | 1 day |
| 2 | Category Manager monitors reservations: ensures no stockouts for regular customers, adjusts allocation, authorizes substitutions when unavailable | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | Ongoing |
| 3 | DC Ops segregates project inventory: dedicated staging area per project, labels with project reference, coordinates delivery scheduling per phase | DC Ops | DC Ops Manager | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory/ATP (links to VS-05 / W105 multi-channel allocation, VS-29 / W252 item master)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inventory-desync risk**: reservation/ATP desync between project stock and ROP-driven replenishment causing stock diversion; mitigated by real-time ATP validation and buffer-stock reservation
- **Reservation-cannibalization risk**: ROP replenishment consuming stock a project PO already reserved strands the phase; mitigated by the reservation logic preventing ROP consumption (Step 1) with ATP showing uncommitted availability only
- **Retail-starvation risk**: project reservations sized without the retail floor's needs turn one big project into chain stockouts; mitigated by the Category Manager's stockout monitoring and allocation adjustments (Step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- Reservation auto-created against ATP with automatic release/expiry; project-vs-retail allocation auto-validated

### Controls
- CTL-460 (ensure controlled execution — Project Material Planning & Phasing (PA-77.1).)
- operational: inventory-count integrity; Operational: Supply Planner sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1 day per occurrence
- Volume: ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects)
- Frequency: Per project; ongoing monitoring
- Absorbed into the Supply Planner role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2697. Project Material Price Lock & Escalation Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project contract negotiation or price change |
| **Frequency** | Per project; monthly monitoring |
| **Volume** | ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects) |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Mgr, Category Manager, Pricing Analyst, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Manager negotiates price lock: fixed price for project duration (3–12 months) or escalation formula tied to published index, with maximum cap | Trade Sales Manager | VP Merchandising | Per project negotiation |
| 2 | Pricing Analyst monitors market movements vs. locked prices: identifies margin erosion risk; quantifies impact; escalates if margin falls below 15% | Pricing Analyst | VP Merchandising | Monthly |
| 3 | Finance tracks project margin: revenue vs. actual cost including price lock impact, holding cost, delivery cost; reports monthly to VP Merchandising | Finance / Trade Sales Manager | CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Escalation-cap breach risk**: market movements past the formula's cap turn the locked price into BuildRight's loss; mitigated by the maximum-cap term (Step 1) with the Pricing Analyst's margin-erosion escalation below 15% (Step 2)
- **Lock-duration risk**: a 3–12 month lock set against a project that runs longer reprices the tail at market; mitigated by the duration-matched lock terms (Step 1) and the Finance monthly margin tracking including holding cost (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Pricing auto-computed from the cost + margin target; settlement auto-reconciled with discrepancy-flagging; invoice auto-generated from the POD/delivery record

### Controls
- CTL-204 (ensure project staging & price-lock integrity.)
- CTL-460 (price-change authorization — links to VS-08 / W40); Operational: Trade Sales Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects)
- Frequency: Per project; monthly monitoring
- Absorbed into the Trade Sales Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2701. Project Account Credit & Payment Terms Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project PO creation or contract negotiation |
| **Frequency** | Per project |
| **Volume** | ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects) |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Mgr, Credit Manager, Finance, Contractor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Manager negotiates payment terms: milestone-based, progress billing, retention (5–10%), credit terms (Net 30/60); Credit Manager approval per authorization matrix | Trade Sales Manager / Credit Manager | VP Merchandising / CFO | Per project |
| 2 | Finance sets up project billing: creates project account, links phase delivery to billing milestones, configures retention tracking, establishes credit limit | Finance / Credit Manager | CFO | Per project |
| 3 | Trade Sales Manager monitors payment health: tracks billed vs. collected, escalates overdue payments, ensures retention release upon completion | Trade Sales Manager / Finance | VP Merchandising | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Project-credit concentration risk**: one project account's default exposure sized by milestone-billing timing rather than a limit decision; mitigated by the Credit Manager's approval per the authorization matrix (Step 1) with the configured credit limit (Step 2)
- **Phase-mismatch risk**: material arrives ahead of the construction phase needing it (or late), inflating staging cost and delaying the project; mitigated by the phasing plan tied to the construction schedule

### Automation Opportunity
- Credit-score auto-computed from the financial-data feed; credit-limit auto-suggested from the scoring model; collection-aging auto-tracked with escalation

### Controls
- CTL-212: trade-credit authorization & exposure guard — links to VS-16 / W24; Operational: Trade Sales Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: ~80–120 active B2B projects (of which ~20–30 are large staged/phased material projects)
- Frequency: Per project
- Absorbed into the Trade Sales Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
