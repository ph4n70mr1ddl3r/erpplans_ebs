## W1894. Trade Professional Product Training Program Planning & Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New product launch; annual training calendar; trade account request |
| **Frequency** | Monthly training sessions across regions |
| **Volume** | ~500–1,000 contractors trained/year across all sessions |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Category Manager, Account Manager, Product Trainers, Trade Professionals |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Annual: Category Manager develops trade training calendar: (a) align with product launch schedule: new power tool line → power tool training, new tile collection → tile installation training, (b) align with seasonal demand: waterproofing training before rainy season, aircon installation training before summer, (c) regional scheduling: rotate training across Mindanao, Visayas, Luzon to maximize accessibility, (d) training formats: in-store demo (1–2 hours), regional seminar (half-day), online webinar (1 hour) | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 week/year |
| 2 | Monthly: execute scheduled training sessions: (a) Category Manager or Product Trainer conducts session: product features, installation techniques, common mistakes, safety considerations, BuildRight product advantages vs. competitor products, (b) Account Manager invites trade accounts by tier: Gold/Platinum get priority; Bronze/Silver on waitlist, (c) training materials: product samples for hands-on practice, installation guides, specification sheets, (d) attendance tracked in CRM for account engagement scoring | Category Manager / Product Trainer | VP Merchandising | 2–4 hours/session |
| 3 | Post-training: Account Manager follows up with attendees: (a) provide product samples or trial pricing for newly trained products, (b) track post-training purchase behavior: did attendees increase purchases of trained products?, (c) gather feedback for training improvement | Account Manager | VP Store Operations | 15 min/attendee |

### System Touchpoints
- Training calendar management with regional scheduling (W1894.1)
- Attendance tracking in CRM linked to trade account engagement scoring (W1894.2d)
- Post-training purchase analytics (W1894.3b)
- Integration with W41 (learning & development), W14 (marketing campaigns)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low attendance**: Trade professionals are busy during work hours; schedule training on Saturday mornings or evenings; offer incentives (loyalty points, free materials) for attendance


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-schedule of "schedule" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Account Manager invites trade accounts by tier" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track post-training purchase behavior" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-372 (ensure controlled execution — Trade Training, Certification & Community Engagement (PA-43.3).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Staffing Implication
- **Product Trainer**: ~12 sessions/month across regions; if using Category Managers, absorbed; if dedicated trainer, 1 FTE

### Time Estimate
- Calendar planning: 1 week/year
- Per session: 2–4 hours delivery + 1–2 hours preparation
- Aggregate: ~430–860 hours/year (12 sessions/month × 2–4 h delivery + 1–2 h prep)

### Time Estimate
- Planning: 2–3 weeks/event
- Execution: half-day/event
- Post-event: 1 week
- Aggregate: ~4 events × 4 weeks = ~16 weeks/year

## W1896. Trade Community Online Forum & Knowledge Base Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Ongoing community management |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; daily moderation |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 trade account members |
| **Owner** | Marketing Team |
| **Participants** | Marketing Team, Category Managers, Trade Professionals |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketing Team manages trade professional online community: (a) platform: dedicated section on buildright.com.ph or private Facebook group for trade accounts, (b) content: product tips, installation guides, specification sheets, project photos, industry news, (c) discussion forums: contractors share project experiences, ask technical questions, recommend products, (d) Category Managers participate as subject matter experts: answer technical questions, provide product guidance | Marketing Team | CMO | 1–2 hours/day |
| 2 | Marketing Team moderates community: (a) ensure discussions are professional and on-topic, (b) remove spam, competitor promotions, or inappropriate content, (c) escalate technical questions to Category Managers if needed, (d) encourage active participation through featured contractor spotlights, project of the month | Marketing Team | CMO | 30 min/day |
| 3 | Monthly: Marketing Team reports community engagement metrics: (a) active users, (b) posts and comments, (c) most-discussed topics, (d) product questions (feed into training calendar W1894), (e) community growth | Marketing Team | CMO | 2 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Online community platform with trade account authentication (W1896.1a)
- Content management system for knowledge base articles (W1896.1b)
- Engagement analytics dashboard (W1896.3)
- Integration with W10.1 (ecommerce platform), W30.3 (document & knowledge management)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low engagement**: Online communities require critical mass; if < 10% of trade accounts are active, the platform appears abandoned; seed discussions and incentivize early participation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Marketing Team manages trade professional online community" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-escalate of "escalate technical questions to Category Managers if needed" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-post of "posts and comments" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-372 (ensure controlled execution — Trade Training, Certification & Community Engagement (PA-43.3).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Staffing Implication
- **Marketing Team (Community Manager)**: ~1–2 hours/day = ~250–500 hours/year — absorbed by existing digital marketing role

### Time Estimate
- Daily management: 1–2 hours/day
- Monthly reporting: 2 hours/month
- Total: ~300–550 hours/year

| **Participants** | Category Manager, Vendor Representatives, Account Manager, Trade Professionals |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Account Manager invites relevant trade accounts: (a) target trade type matching seminar topic (plumbers for pipe fitting demo, electricians for wiring demo), (b) Gold/Platinum priority, (c) track RSVP in CRM | Account Manager | VP Store Operations | 1 week |

| 4 | Post-seminar: (a) track purchase impact: did attendees buy the demonstrated products?, (b) vendor provides co-op marketing cost contribution per W39 (vendor rebate), (c) Category Manager evaluates vendor partnership for future seminars | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor seminar calendar and coordination module (W1897.1)

- Post-seminar purchase analytics (W1897.4a)

- **Vendor self-promotion**: Vendors may use seminars to promote their products at the expense of competing BuildRight offerings; Category Manager must review content before approval


### Automation Opportunity
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves seminar content to ensure it adds value" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Account Manager invites relevant trade accounts" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "tracked" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-372 (ensure controlled execution — Trade Training, Certification & Community Engagement (PA-43.3).)
- operational: Category Manager review and approval gate

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by Category Manager and AM

### Time Estimate
- Per seminar: ~2–3 weeks (planning + execution + follow-up)
- Aggregate: ~12 seminars × 2.5 weeks = ~30 weeks/year

## W1900. Contractor Safety Training & Compliance Certification Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DOLE safety training requirements; contractor request for safety certification support |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly safety training sessions |
| **Volume** | ~200–400 contractors trained/year |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, Safety Officer, DOLE-Accredited Trainer, Trade Professionals |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Store Operations plans quarterly safety training: (a) partner with DOLE-accredited safety training organization, (b) topics: construction safety (working at heights, electrical safety, excavation safety), PPE selection and usage (BuildRight PPE products featured), hazard identification, (c) offer as value-added benefit to trade accounts: free or subsidized training for Gold/Platinum members | VP Store Operations | COO | 1 week |
| 2 | Execute safety training: (a) DOLE-accredited trainer conducts session, (b) BuildRight Safety Officer presents PPE product knowledge, (c) contractors receive attendance certificate (usable for DOLE compliance requirements), (d) loyalty points awarded for attendance | Safety Officer / Trainer | VP Store Operations | Half-day |
| 3 | Post-training: (a) track PPE sales to attendees — did training increase PPE purchases from BuildRight?, (b) safety training as differentiator: contractors appreciate value-added service that helps their business compliance | AM | VP Store Operations | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Safety training calendar and registration (W1900.1)
- Attendance certification generation (W1900.2c)
- Post-training purchase analytics (W1900.3a)
- Integration with W24 (HSE — health, safety & environment), W41 (training & development)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Liability**: BuildRight is providing safety awareness training, not certifying contractor competence; clear disclaimer that training does not replace contractor's own safety responsibility


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "VP Store Operations plans quarterly safety training" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-track of "track PPE sales to attendees — did training increase PPE purchases from…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-189 (ensure trade-program benefit & master-data integrity.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by VP Store Operations and Safety Officer
- **Training cost**: DOLE-accredited trainer fee ~PHP 30K–50K per session

### Time Estimate
- Planning: 1 week/quarter
- Execution: half-day/quarter
- Follow-up: 1 day/quarter
- Total: ~15 days/year
