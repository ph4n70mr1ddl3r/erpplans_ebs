## W4689. Corporate Investigations: Fraud, Theft, Embezzlement & IP Misappropriation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Allegation/tip (whistleblower VS-119, audit VS-21, LP VS-23, anomaly) |
| **Frequency** | Per case |
| **Volume** | ~dozens of significant corporate investigations/yr |
| **Owner** | CSO / Corporate Investigations Manager |
| **Participants** | Internal Audit (VS-21), LP (VS-23), Fraud (VS-125), Legal (VS-100.3), HR (VS-19/VS-84), Ethics (VS-119), law enforcement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake and triage allegations of corporate crime (executive/employee fraud, embezzlement, IP theft/trade-secret misappropriation, vendor collusion, bribery VS-86); assess credibility and scope; preserve evidence immediately | Investigations Manager / CSO | CSO / VP Legal | Per case |
| 2 | Conduct the investigation: interviews, digital forensics (with cyber VS-27.3), document review, surveillance (W4693) within legal limits; coordinate with Legal (privilege VS-100.3) and HR (discipline/termination VS-19) | Investigations Manager / Legal / HR | CSO / VP Legal | Per case |
| 3 | Conclude with findings, recovery/restitution, discipline/termination (VS-19), insurance claim (VS-26 crime policy), and law-enforcement referral/prosecution (VS-100) where warranted; capture lessons (W4696) | CSO / Legal / HR / Insurance | CSO / VP Legal / CFO | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Case management (security ops); whistleblower (VS-119); audit (VS-21); LP/fraud (VS-23/VS-125); digital forensics (VS-27.3); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Evidence-spoliation risk**: Evidence not preserved → inadmissible / failed recovery; mitigated by immediate preservation and chain-of-custody (W4693)
- **Privilege/legal-misstep risk**: Investigation conducted outside Legal privilege or violating labor/privacy rights (VS-91/VS-84) → exposure; mitigated by Legal coordination

### Automation Opportunity
- Case-management workflow; digital-evidence preservation tools; cross-function allegation intake

### Controls
- CTL-709 (ensure controlled execution — Investigations, Insider Threat, Workplace Violence & Crisis Response (PA-159.3).)
- CTL-709: evidence preserved with chain-of-custody; Legal-privileged and rights-compliant (VS-91/VS-84); recovery/insurance pursued

### Time Estimate
- ~per significant investigation 1–6 weeks

### Cross-references
- distinct from VS-23 (retail shrink); links to VS-21/VS-119/VS-125 (audit/ethics/fraud), VS-100.3/VS-100 (legal/prosecution), VS-26 (crime insurance), W4693 (evidence)

## W4690. Insider-Threat Detection, Background Re-Check & Offboarding Risk

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Insider-threat indicator; role change (to sensitive role); offboarding (VS-19) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + per indicator |
| **Volume** | ~ indicators across ~6,911 employees + contingent (VS-98) + sensitive-role population |
| **Owner** | CSO / Insider-Threat Program Manager |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19/VS-103), IT/cyber (VS-27.3 — user-behavior analytics), Ethics (VS-119), Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Operate insider-threat detection: indicators from HR (performance/behavior/financial-stress VS-103), cyber (data-exfiltration/user-behavior analytics VS-27.3), ethics (VS-119), and access patterns; assess for espionage, sabotage, fraud, or workplace-violence potential | Insider-Threat Program Manager / CSO | CSO / VP Legal | Continuous |
| 2 | Conduct background re-checks for sensitive roles (finance, treasury VS-18, data/privacy, security, executive support) and on indicators; manage offboarding-risk (W43 separation) — access revocation, exit interviews, data-handling review | CSO / HR / IT Security | CSO / CHRO | Per case |
| 3 | Intervene proportionately (support, monitoring, role change, discipline, law-enforcement referral) with Legal/HR/privacy safeguards (VS-91/VS-84); document and review | CSO / Legal / HR | CSO / VP Legal | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- HR/people analytics (VS-103); cyber UBA (VS-27.3); ethics (VS-119); access management (VS-27.3); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missed-indicator risk**: Insider-threat indicators in disparate systems not correlated → undetected risk; mitigated by cross-function intake and UBA
- **Privacy/labor-rights risk**: Insider-threat monitoring exceeding lawful bounds → VS-91/VS-84 exposure; mitigated by Legal/HR governance and proportionality

### Automation Opportunity
- UBA + cross-function indicator correlation; background-re-check workflow; offboarding-access-revocation automation (VS-27)

### Controls
- CTL-709 (ensure controlled execution — Investigations, Insider Threat, Workplace Violence & Crisis Response (PA-159.3).)
- CTL-709: monitoring lawful and proportionate (VS-91/VS-84); sensitive-role re-checks; offboarding access revoked

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous monitoring; per significant case 1–3 weeks

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103 (people analytics), VS-27.3 (cyber/UBA/access), VS-119 (ethics), VS-19 W43 (offboarding), VS-91/VS-84 (privacy/labor)

## W4691. Workplace-Violence Threat Assessment, Intervention & De-escalation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Threat indicator (disgruntled employee, domestic-violence spillover, harassment VS-84, terminations) |
| **Frequency** | Per indicator |
| **Volume** | ~20–40 threat indicators/year across the workforce |
| **Owner** | CSO / Threat Assessment Team Lead |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19/VS-84), OH/EAP (VS-83), Legal (VS-100.3), management, law enforcement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Operate a multi-disciplinary Threat Assessment Team (security + HR + OH/EAP + Legal) for workplace-violence indicators; assess threat level using a structured methodology (proximity, intent, capability, stressors) | Threat Assessment Team / CSO | CSO / CHRO | Per indicator |
| 2 | Intervene proportionately: EAP/counseling (VS-83), mediation, administrative leave, restraining order, enhanced security, law-enforcement referral; manage high-risk terminations (VS-19 W43) with security presence | Threat Assessment Team / HR / Legal | CSO / CHRO / VP Legal | Per case |
| 3 | De-escalate and monitor post-intervention; brief affected managers/workplaces discreetly; review and adjust on new information | CSO / HR | CSO | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Case management (security ops); HR/Labor (VS-19/VS-84); EAP/OH (VS-83); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-responded-threat risk**: A credible violence indicator not intervened → harm; mitigated by the structured methodology and multi-disciplinary team
- **Privacy/stigma risk**: Disclosure of an individual's mental-health/domestic situation breaching privacy (VS-91); mitigated by discreet, need-to-know handling

### Automation Opportunity
- Threat-assessment methodology workflow; cross-function indicator intake; discreet-manager-briefing tool

### Controls
- CTL-709 (ensure controlled execution — Investigations, Insider Threat, Workplace Violence & Crisis Response (PA-159.3).)
- CTL-709: structured threat assessment by multi-disciplinary team; proportionate intervention; discreet/privacy-compliant handling (VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- ~per case 1–5 days assessment + ongoing monitoring

### Cross-references
- links to VS-19/VS-84 (HR/labor), VS-83 (OH/EAP), VS-19 W43 (termination), VS-91 (privacy), VS-150 (drug-free intersect)

| **Frequency** | Rare (preparedness continuous) |

| **Owner** | CSO / VP Legal (joint) |

|---|---|---|---|---|

## W4696. Corporate Security Metrics, Assurance & Post-Incident Review

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Periodic review; post-incident; assurance (VS-21) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly/quarterly + post-incident |
| **Volume** | 1 corporate-security KPI/KRI set |
| **Owner** | CSO |
| **Participants** | Internal Audit (VS-21), Head of Internal Audit & Risk (VS-21.2), Board Risk Committee (VS-36), Op Excellence (VS-133) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain corporate-security metrics & KRIs (threat-profile trends, incident frequency/severity, travel-risk exposure, investigation outcomes, EP/GSOC readiness, vendor performance); report to the Head of Internal Audit & Risk and board risk committee (VS-36) | CSO / Analytics | CSO / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Monthly/quarterly |
| 2 | Conduct post-incident reviews (after every significant incident) capturing root cause, response effectiveness, and corrective actions; feed corrective actions into the program and into VS-21/VS-133 | CSO / Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Op Excellence | CSO | Per incident |
| 3 | Assure the program via Internal Audit (VS-21) and the physical-security audit (VS-21.3 W358); continuously improve (VS-133) and refresh the strategy (W4673) | CSO / Internal Audit | CSO / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Security metrics/analytics; ERM/KRIs (VS-21.2); audit (VS-21); board reporting (VS-36); op excellence (VS-133)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric-vanity risk**: Metrics that don't predict/effect outcomes → false assurance; mitigated by outcome-oriented KRIs tied to risk appetite (W4673)
- **Lessons-not-learned risk**: Post-incident reviews without corrective-action closure → recurrence; mitigated by tracked corrective actions (VS-133)

### Automation Opportunity
- Security-metrics dashboards; post-incident-review workflow with corrective-action tracking

### Controls
- CTL-709 (ensure controlled execution — Investigations, Insider Threat, Workplace Violence & Crisis Response (PA-159.3).)
- CTL-709: outcome-oriented KRIs reported to board; post-incident corrective actions tracked; program assured via VS-21

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days/quarter metrics + reporting; per-incident review absorbed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21/VS-21.2/VS-21.3 W358 (audit/ERM), VS-36 (board), VS-133 (op excellence), W4673 (strategy)
