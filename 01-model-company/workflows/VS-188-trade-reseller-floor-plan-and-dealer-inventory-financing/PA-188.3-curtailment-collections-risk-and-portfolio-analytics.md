## W5385. Dealer Aging, Curtailment Default & Out-of-Trust (OOT) Detection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily aging run; curtailment-period expiry; sold-but-not-curtailed flag (W5382) |
| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | ~1,000 active dealers monitored daily |
| **Owner** | Credit Manager (Dealer Finance) |
| **Participants** | Credit Analyst, Collections, Field Examiner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Run daily aging on curtailment and interest balances by dealer and bucket (current/30/60/90+). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | daily |
| 2 | Flag curtailment defaults (units sold but unpaid past period) and OOT exposure per dealer. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | daily |
| 3 | Triage: contact dealer, confirm cause, and set a cure timeline (pay-down or collateral substitution). | Collections | Credit Manager | per flag |
| 4 | Escalate repeat/serious OOT to workout (W5386) and risk-grade downgrade (W5388). | Credit Manager | Credit Manager | per escalation |
| 5 | Report portfolio aging and OOT exposure to the credit committee (W5391). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- Floor-plan aging/OOT engine; collections/case module; risk-grade update (VS-68).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **OOT-accumulation risk**: unflagged OOT grows into large unsecured exposure before detection; mitigated by daily sold-vs-curtailed matching and aging.
- **Slow-cure risk**: weak cure follow-up lets a curable default become a loss; mitigated by defined cure timelines and escalation to workout.

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-aging and OOT-flagging with rule-based collections worklists and escalation.

### Controls
- CTL-796: OOT/fraud detection; CTL-796 (aging accuracy); CTL-796 (cure-authority separation).

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 person-hours/day aging/triage; ~10 person-hours/week reporting.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (credit risk/aging), VS-23 (LP/fraud), VS-16 (AR aging)

## W5386. Dealer Collection, Workout, Restructuring & Skip-Tracing for Missing Collateral

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Default escalated from W5385; missed cure |
| **Frequency** | ~2–6% of active dealers/yr |
| **Volume** | ~30–120 workout cases/yr |
| **Owner** | Workout/Recovery Manager |
| **Participants** | Credit Manager, Legal (VS-100), Collections, Field Examiner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assess the default: cause, dealer financial condition, and collateral status (field exam W5381 if stale). | Workout Manager | Credit Manager | per case |
| 2 | Pursue collections: demand curtailment, negotiate a restructuring/extended cure where viable, or move to recovery. | Workout Manager | Credit Manager | per case |
| 3 | For missing collateral, skip-trace the units (serial-level) to locate them for repossession (W5387). | Field Examiner | Workout Manager | per case |
| 4 | Engage Legal for demand/default notice and enforcement of the security agreement (W5371). | Legal Counsel | Workout Manager | per case |
| 5 | Document the workout outcome (cured/restructured/recovery) and update the dealer risk grade/limit. | Workout Manager | Credit Manager | per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Workout/case module; collateral location/skip-trace; legal/enforcement records (VS-100); risk-grade update.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Loss-escalation risk**: weak workout lets a recoverable default become a write-off; mitigated by defined workout timelines and restructuring options.
- **Skip-trace failure risk**: unlocatable collateral (dealer concealed/sold) becomes unsecured loss; mitigated by serial-level tracking (W5379) and field-exam triggers.

### Automation Opportunity
- Default-driven case auto-creation with restructuring-calc and skip-trace worklist.

### Controls
- CTL-109: collections fraud detection & case governance; CTL-796 (ensure controlled execution — Curtailment, Collections, Risk & Portfolio Analytics (PA-188.3).) — restructuring approval authority.

### Time Estimate
- ~20–80 person-hours per case; ~70/yr → ~3,000 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (credit), VS-100 (legal/enforcement), VS-23 (LP), VS-159 (security/skip-trace)

## W5387. Collateral Repossession, Redemption & Disposition of Defaulted Floor-Plan Units

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Failed workout/cure (W5386); security-agreement enforcement |
| **Frequency** | Rare (~1–3% of default cases reach repo); high impact |
| **Volume** | ~10–40 repossession events/yr |
| **Owner** | Workout/Recovery Manager |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100), Corporate Security (VS-159), Repo Agent, Sales (disposition) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm the right to repossess under the security agreement (W5371) and applicable law; obtain legal sign-off. | Legal Counsel | Workout Manager | per event |
| 2 | Engage a licensed repo agent / Corporate Security to recover the units (serial-level) from the dealer. | Corporate Security | Workout Manager | per event |
| 3 | Provide the dealer a redemption window to cure and reclaim before disposition. | Workout Manager | Workout Manager | per event |
| 4 | Dispose of unredeemed collateral: re-sell into BuildRight's channels (VS-11/VS-32/VS-155) or wholesale to recover principal. | Sales | Workout Manager | per event |
| 5 | Account for recovery vs. outstanding balance; post any deficiency to the dealer and any surplus per law. | Finance | CFO | per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Collateral/case module; disposition order (VS-11/VS-32); GL recovery/deficiency posting (VS-17); records (VS-88).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Wrongful-repo risk**: repossessing without proper legal basis exposes BuildRight to damages; mitigated by legal sign-off and documented default/security-agreement basis.
- **Recovery-shortfall risk**: disposing collateral below the outstanding balance leaves a deficiency to chase; mitigated by re-sale into BuildRight's channels at market and deficiency accounting.

### Automation Opportunity
- Repo-case workflow with redemption-clock and disposition options.

### Controls
- CTL-796: ensure controlled execution — Curtailment, Collections, Risk & Portfolio Analytics (PA-188.3).) — legal authorization; CTL-26 (asset recovery; CTL-21 (recovery/deficiency accounting).

### Time Estimate
- ~40–120 person-hours per repossession event; ~25/yr → ~1,500 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal), VS-159 (security), VS-32/VS-155 (disposition/resale), VS-17 (GL), VS-23 (LP)

| **Owner** | Credit Manager (Dealer Finance) |
| **Participants** | Risk (VS-21/VS-68), Treasury, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

## W5389. Loss Provisioning, Charge-off & Recovery Accounting for Floor-Plan Losses

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close; default/loss event (W5386/W5387) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly provisioning; per charge-off event |
| **Volume** | ~1 monthly provision; ~20–60 charge-offs/yr |
| **Owner** | Finance (Dealer Finance) |
| **Participants** | Credit Manager, CFO, Internal Audit (VS-21), Tax (VS-79) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compute the expected credit loss (ECL) provision under PFRS 9 from aging/grade/default data (W5385/W5388). | Finance | CFO | per close |
| 2 | Post the provision to the GL and provision contra-asset (VS-17); document methodology. | Finance | CFO | per close |
| 3 | On a confirmed loss (unrecoverable): charge-off the principal net of provision and recovery (W5387). | Finance | CFO | per event |
| 4 | Track recoveries post charge-off and recognize recovery income. | Finance | CFO | ongoing |
| 5 | Reconcile provisions/charge-offs to the credit-risk and tax records (VS-79 BIR deductibility). | Finance | CFO | per close |

### System Touchpoints
- ECL/provisioning engine (PFRS 9 — VS-157/VS-17); GL; recovery-income posting; tax reconciliation (VS-79).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-provisioning risk**: an inadequate ECL distorts the audited PFRS financials and over-states income; mitigated by a documented PFRS 9 methodology and periodic audit.
- **Deductibility risk**: charge-offs not properly documented/deductible for BIR lose tax benefit; mitigated by documentation and tax review (VS-79).

### Automation Opportunity
- ECL auto-computed from aging/grade data with documented methodology; charge-off workflow.

### Controls
- CTL-31: adequate provisions; CTL-30 (month-end close); CTL-34 (tax-compliant charge-off); CTL-21 (accuracy).

### Time Estimate
- ~1 week per monthly provisioning cycle; ~2 days per charge-off batch.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (GL), VS-157 (revenue/loss), VS-79 (tax), VS-21 (audit), VS-68 (credit)
