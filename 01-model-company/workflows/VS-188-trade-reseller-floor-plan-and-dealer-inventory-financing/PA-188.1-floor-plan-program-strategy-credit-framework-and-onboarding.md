## W5369. Floor-Plan & Dealer Financing Program Strategy, Product Design & Risk Appetite

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Executive decision to launch/expand dealer inventory financing |
| **Frequency** | Annual strategy refresh |
| **Volume** | 1 program strategy/yr |
| **Owner** | Treasury/Working-Capital Lead |
| **Participants** | CFO, VP Legal & Compliance, Credit Manager (VS-68), Merchandising/Trade (VS-11) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the eligible reseller segments (sub-distributors, larger sari-sari/MSME per VS-82, cooperatives per VS-94/VS-185) and eligible product categories (high-value, resalable, identifiable — appliances, power tools, equipment). | Treasury Lead | CFO | 1 week |
| 2 | Set the risk appetite: max program exposure, per-dealer limit ceilings, advance rates, and target NPL/loss rate. | Credit Manager | CFO | 1 week |
| 3 | Build the business case: financing margin vs. funding cost, wholesale-volume lift, default/loss assumption, and capital allocation. | Finance Business Partner | CFO | 1 week |
| 4 | Confirm the legal/regulatory vehicle (in-house AR/lending vs. lending-company structure under RA 9474 / BSP or financing-company structure under RA 8556 / SEC) and BSP/SEC implications. | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | 1 week |
| 5 | Approve at the capital/credit committee and board reporting (VS-36/VS-173). | CFO | CEO | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/funding module (VS-18); credit-risk appetite (VS-68); capex/capital allocation (VS-40); board reporting (VS-36).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Regulatory-structure risk**: conducting a lending business without the right SEC/lending-company registration or license triggers regulatory action (SEC/BSP); mitigated by confirming the legal vehicle before launch.
- **Over-exposure risk**: an uncapped program grows exposure beyond the risk appetite and capital; mitigated by program/ceiling limits in the risk appetite.

### Automation Opportunity
- Wholesale-volume/at-risk data from trade accounts (VS-11/VS-82) feeds the demand model and limit envelope.

### Controls
- CTL-794 (ensure controlled execution — Program Strategy, Credit Framework & Dealer Onboarding (PA-188.1).)
- operational: capital/program approval with exposure-limit and budget adherence at Board/ExComm gates.

### Time Estimate
- ~5–6 weeks elapsed per annual refresh; ~120 person-hours.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury), VS-68 (credit risk), VS-40 (capital), VS-36/VS-173 (board), VS-82/VS-94/VS-185 (reseller base)

## W5370. Dealer Eligibility, Credit Underwriting & Floor-Plan Limit Setting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Dealer applies for floor-plan; annual limit review |
| **Frequency** | Per dealer onboarding; annual review |
| **Volume** | ~1,000 active floor-plan dealers (the program register); ~50 new onboardings/yr replacing churned dealers |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst (Dealer Finance) |
| **Participants** | Credit Manager, Trade Account Manager (VS-107), Compliance/AML (VS-86) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Collect dealer KYB: business registration (SEC/DTI/CDA), financials, trade references, and existing trade-account history (VS-11/VS-68). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | 1 day |
| 2 | Run credit underwriting: financial ratios, payment history, sales velocity, and KYC/AML/sanctions (VS-86). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Set the floor-plan limit per dealer based on stocking need, sales velocity, and risk grade; apply program ceilings (W5369). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | 1 day |
| 4 | Document the credit decision and approval per the credit-authority matrix (separation CTL-12). | Credit Manager | Credit Manager | 0.5 day |
| 5 | Communicate the limit and terms to the dealer; set up in the floor-plan system (W5374). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | 0.5 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Credit-risk module (VS-68); trade-account history (VS-11/VS-16); AML/KYC (VS-86); floor-plan dealer master + limit.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-classification risk**: onboarding a dealer whose business is not genuinely reseller (e.g. an end-user seeking credit) defeats the collateral model; mitigated by verifying reseller status and trade references.
- **Over-limit risk**: a limit set above the dealer's demonstrable sales velocity funds unsellable stock and default; mitigated by sales-velocity-based limit setting and annual review.

### Automation Opportunity
- Trade-account payment history and sales velocity auto-pulled into the underwriting scorecard.

### Controls
- CTL-794 (ensure controlled execution — Program Strategy, Credit Framework & Dealer Onboarding (PA-188.1).)
- operational: credit approval separated from sales execution (authority matrix); dealer/KYB onboarding records complete before limit setting.

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 person-days per dealer onboarding; ~50 new dealers/yr → ~200 person-days/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (credit), VS-11 (trade), VS-16 (O2C), VS-86 (AML/KYB), VS-107 (key accounts), VS-167 (screening)

## W5371. Dealer Floor-Plan Agreement, Security Agreement & Collateral Perfection (Civil Code Pledge)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved dealer credit decision (W5370) |
| **Frequency** | Per dealer onboarding; renewal |
| **Volume** | ~300–800 agreements; ~1,000 active |
| **Owner** | Legal Counsel |
| **Participants** | Credit Manager, Dealer, Notary, Collateral Registry |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Draft the floor-plan/master agreement + security/pledge agreement defining the inventory collateral, BuildRight's security interest, and default/remedy rights. | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | 2 days |
| 2 | Document the perfection requirements: possession/registry of the pledge (Civil Code pledge rules), and any third-party-claim notice. | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | 1 day |
| 3 | Execute and notarize; capture dealer signatures and authorized signatories (VS-88). | Legal Counsel | Legal Counsel | 1 day |
| 4 | Perfect the security interest per applicable rules; record in the collateral/lien register (W5379). | Credit Analyst | Legal Counsel | 1 day |
| 5 | Set up the dealer's floor-plan account with limit, advance rate, and curtailment terms (W5374). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | 0.5 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract management (VS-100); document vault (VS-88); collateral/lien register (W5379); floor-plan account master.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unenforceable-security risk**: a security interest not properly perfected (wrong form, unregistered) is unenforceable against the dealer's other creditors in default; mitigated by documented perfection requirements and legal sign-off.
- **Signatory risk**: an agreement signed by an unauthorized dealer officer is unenforceable; mitigated by KYB signatory verification (VS-86/W5370).

### Automation Opportunity
- Standard agreement templates with e-signature and perfection-checklist workflow.

### Controls
- CTL-794 (ensure controlled execution — Program Strategy, Credit Framework & Dealer Onboarding (PA-188.1).)
- operational: legal authorization on the agreement stack; perfection-checklist sign-off before funding.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 weeks elapsed per onboarding at ~5.5 person-days of effort (2+1+1+1+0.5); ~50 new dealers/yr → ~275 person-days/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal/contracts), VS-88 (documents), VS-68 (credit), VS-86 (KYB)

## W5372. Dealer Inventory Collateral Verification, Floor-Plan Audit & Stocking-List Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Dealer onboarding; periodic field examination |
| **Frequency** | Onboarding exam; ~quarterly field exams for larger dealers |
| **Volume** | ~50 onboarding exams/yr (per new-dealer intake); ~2,000–4,000 field exams/yr (the ~1,000 active dealers on the risk-tiered cycle) |
| **Owner** | Field Examiner / Credit Analyst |
| **Participants** | Dealer, Internal Audit (VS-21), TPRM (VS-161) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify the dealer's physical inventory against the floor-plan stocking list (serial/VIN/lot per funded unit). | Field Examiner | Credit Manager | per exam |
| 2 | Confirm BuildRight's collateral is identifiable, present, and not commingled/sold-out-of-trust. | Field Examiner | Credit Manager | per exam |
| 3 | Reconcile stocking list ↔ floor-plan ledger ↔ dealer's own inventory system. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per exam |
| 4 | Document findings, exceptions (missing/sold units), and required cures; rate the collateral coverage. | Field Examiner | Credit Manager | per exam |
| 5 | Feed exceptions to curtailment/collections (W5382/W5385) and adjust the dealer risk grade. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per exam |

### System Touchpoints
- Floor-plan ledger (unit-level); field-exam findings register; risk-grade update (VS-68).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Out-of-trust (OOT) risk**: the dealer sells funded units without paying down the floor-plan, leaving BuildRight unsecured — the core floor-plan fraud risk; mitigated by periodic field exams with serial-level reconciliation and exception cures.
- **Phantom-collateral risk**: the stocking list lists units not actually present; mitigated by physical verification and dealer inventory-system access (W5374).

### Automation Opportunity
- Dealer inventory-feed (W5374) auto-reconciled to the floor-plan ledger with exception alerts on sold-but-not-curtailed units.

### Controls
- CTL-794 (ensure controlled execution — Program Strategy, Credit Framework & Dealer Onboarding (PA-188.1).)
- operational: collateral-accuracy; OOT/fraud detection; operational: serial-level reconciliation.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 person-days per field exam; ~3,000/yr → ~6,000 person-days/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (credit risk), VS-21 (audit), VS-161 (TPRM), VS-05 (inventory), VS-23 (loss prevention)

## W5373. Floor-Plan Financing Funding Source, Bank Credit Line & Capital Allocation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch (W5369); funding-line renewal |
| **Frequency** | Annual funding-line cycle; ongoing drawdown |
| **Volume** | ~1–3 funding lines; program exposure PHP hundreds of millions |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | CFO, Bank/Lenders, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Determine the program's funding need from the dealer-limit roll-up (W5370) and drawdown forecast. | Treasury Manager | CFO | 1 week |
| 2 | Source funding: own balance sheet, a dedicated bank credit line, or an ABL facility against the floor-plan receivable/collateral (cross-link VS-170). | Treasury Manager | CFO | 2 weeks |
| 3 | Negotiate facility terms (rate, covenants, advance rate against the floor-plan portfolio); execute. | Treasury Manager | CFO | 2 weeks |
| 4 | Allocate capital to the program; monitor covenant compliance (links VS-18 W319). | Finance Business Partner | CFO | ongoing |
| 5 | Manage the funding-cost vs. financing-margin spread to protect program NIM. | Treasury Manager | CFO | monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/funding module (VS-18); debt facility & covenant compliance (VS-18 W319); ABL/collateral (VS-170).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Funding-cost risk**: a rise in funding cost compresses the program NIM below breakeven; mitigated by matching funding tenor to the floor-plan duration and pricing pass-through (W5375).
- **Covenant-breach risk**: breaching a facility covenant (e.g. portfolio NPL ratio) triggers acceleration; mitigated by covenant monitoring and early curtailment/workout (W5385).

### Automation Opportunity
- Drawdown forecast auto-computed from the dealer-limit roll-up; covenant dashboard.

### Controls
- CTL-794 (ensure controlled execution — Program Strategy, Credit Framework & Dealer Onboarding (PA-188.1).)
- operational: facility authorization with covenant-compliance monitoring and treasury reconciliation of advances.

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks elapsed per funding cycle; ~1 day/month ongoing.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury), VS-170 (ABL/collateral), VS-105 (SCF/working capital)

## W5374. Dealer Onboarding, System Integration & Inventory Reporting Feed Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Executed agreement (W5371) |
| **Frequency** | Per dealer onboarding |
| **Volume** | ~300–800 dealers in the floor-plan portfolio; ~50 new onboardings/year |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst (Dealer Finance) |
| **Participants** | IT (VS-27), Dealer, Trade Account Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Create the dealer's floor-plan account linked to its trade/customer account (VS-11/VS-16/VS-29). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | 0.5 day |
| 2 | Establish the inventory-reporting feed: the dealer's sold-unit and stock reports flow into the floor-plan ledger (for curtailment and audit W5372). | IT Engineer | Credit Analyst | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Configure curtailment schedule, advance rate, and billing terms in the floor-plan system. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | 0.5 day |
| 4 | Train the dealer on reporting cadence, curtailment process, and portal access. | Trade Account Manager | Credit Analyst | 0.5 day |
| 5 | Validate the feed end-to-end before the first funding (W5377). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Floor-plan account master; dealer inventory-reporting feed/API; trade/customer link (VS-11/VS-16); customer master (VS-29).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Feed-failure risk**: a broken reporting feed leaves curtailment (W5382) and OOT detection (W5385) blind; mitigated by feed-health monitoring and a fallback manual-reporting SOP.
- **Account-linkage risk**: a floor-plan account not linked to the trade account misses cross-default signals; mitigated by mandatory linkage at onboarding.

### Automation Opportunity
- Standard dealer-portal onboarding with API key provisioning and feed-health dashboard.

### Controls
- CTL-794 (ensure controlled execution — Program Strategy, Credit Framework & Dealer Onboarding (PA-188.1).)
- operational: account/feed accuracy; dealer data safeguards.

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 person-days per onboarding; ~50/yr → ~200 person-days/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27 (IT/integration), VS-11/VS-16 (trade/O2C), VS-29 (master data)

## W5375. Floor-Plan Pricing, Interest/Markup Rate Structure & Subvention/Manufacturer Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program pricing review (W5369); manufacturer subvention opportunity |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly pricing review |
| **Volume** | 1 rate structure/quarter |
| **Owner** | Treasury/Pricing Lead |
| **Participants** | Credit Manager, Merchandising (VS-39), Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build the floor-plan rate (interest/markup) covering funding cost (W5373), credit-loss assumption, opex, and target margin. | Treasury/Pricing Lead | CFO | 1 week |
| 2 | Define curtailment periods, advance rates, and any volume/loyalty tiering by dealer risk grade. | Credit Manager | CFO | 3 days |
| 3 | Negotiate manufacturer/brand subvention (rate buy-down) on eligible categories to lower the dealer's effective rate (links VS-39). | VP Merchandising | CFO | 2 weeks |
| 4 | Configure the rate structure and subvention in the floor-plan/billing system. | Treasury/Pricing Lead | Credit Manager | 1 week |
| 5 | Monitor realized rate vs. structure and subvention recovery (W5391). | Finance Business Partner | CFO | monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Floor-plan rate engine; billing (W5383); subvention/rebate module (VS-39); treasury/funding (VS-18).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-pricing risk**: a rate below funding + loss + opex erodes program margin; mitigated by the rate build and quarterly realized-rate monitoring.
- **Subvention-leakage risk**: manufacturer subvention not claimed/recovered against eligible units; mitigated by per-unit subvention tracking (W5391).

### Automation Opportunity
- Rate structure auto-applied by dealer risk grade; subvention auto-reconciled per funded unit.

### Controls
- CTL-39: subvention/rebate accuracy; CTL-22 (correct tax on interest/markup); CTL-35 (margin adherence).

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks elapsed per quarterly review.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-39 (vendor rebates/subvention), VS-18 (funding cost), VS-22 (tax on financing income)

## W5376. BSP/SEC Lending-Company Compliance, Disclosure & Truth-in-Lending (RA 3765) Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch (W5369); regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Initial setup; ongoing compliance monitoring |
| **Volume** | 1 compliance framework; ongoing |
| **Owner** | Compliance Specialist |
| **Participants** | Legal Counsel (VS-100), Credit Manager, Finance, BSP/SEC liaison |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm the regulatory vehicle and any lending-company/financing-company registration/reporting under the applicable vehicle: RA 9474 (Lending Company Regulation Act, BSP) or RA 8556 (Financing Company Act, SEC). | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | setup |
| 2 | Establish Truth-in-Lending (RA 3765) disclosure: effective interest rate, total cost, and a standard disclosure to every dealer. | Legal Counsel | Compliance Specialist | setup |
| 3 | Configure the disclosure generation into the agreement/origination workflow (W5377). | Compliance Specialist | Credit Manager | setup |
| 4 | Set up regulatory reporting (SEC/BSP) and record retention (VS-88). | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | ongoing |
| 5 | Monitor regulatory changes and audit compliance periodically (VS-22/VS-21). | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Disclosure-generation in origination (W5377); regulatory reporting; records retention (VS-88); compliance monitoring (VS-22).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unregistered-lending risk**: conducting a lending/financing business without required SEC/BSP registration triggers penalties/cease-and-desist; mitigated by confirming the regulatory vehicle (W5369) and registrations.
- **Disclosure-violation risk**: non-compliant Truth-in-Lending disclosure (RA 3765) voids finance charges and triggers penalties; mitigated by standard auto-generated disclosure at origination.

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-generated RA 3765 disclosure at origination with effective-rate computation.

### Controls
- CTL-794 (ensure controlled execution — Program Strategy, Credit Framework & Dealer Onboarding (PA-188.1).)
- operational: statutory lending-compliance calendar (SEC/BSP filings per the registered vehicle); tax on financing income accrued and remitted.

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks elapsed initial setup; ongoing monitoring.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-22 (compliance), VS-100 (legal), VS-88 (records), VS-79 (tax), VS-86 (AML on lending)
