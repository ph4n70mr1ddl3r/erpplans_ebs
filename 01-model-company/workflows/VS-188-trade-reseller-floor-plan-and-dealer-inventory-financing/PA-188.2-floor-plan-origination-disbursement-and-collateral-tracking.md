## W5377. Dealer Purchase/Floor-Plan Funding Request & Loan Origination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Dealer requests funding for a purchase/stocking order |
| **Frequency** | ~1–10 funding requests/dealer/month |
| **Volume** | ~10,000–40,000 originations/yr |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst (Dealer Finance) |
| **Participants** | Dealer, Trade Account Manager, Credit Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive the dealer's funding request with the unit-level stocking list (SKU, serial/VIN/lot, cost). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per request |
| 2 | Verify against the dealer's floor-plan limit and available headroom (W5370); check eligibility of categories. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per request |
| 3 | Originate the floor-plan loan: principal, advance rate, curtailment schedule, rate (W5375), and RA 3765 disclosure (W5376). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per request |
| 4 | Credit-decision per the authority matrix (CTL-12 separation); approve/decline with documented reason. | Credit Manager | Credit Manager | per request |
| 5 | Release to disbursement (W5378) and post the loan to the floor-plan ledger (W5379). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per request |

### System Touchpoints
- Floor-plan origination; limit/headroom check (W5370); rate engine (W5375); RA 3765 disclosure (W5376); GL posting (VS-17).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-limit risk**: funding beyond the dealer's headroom breaches the credit decision; mitigated by a hard limit-check gate in origination.
- **Ineligible-category risk**: funding non-eligible/non-resalable categories weakens collateral; mitigated by category-eligibility check at origination.

### Automation Opportunity
- Online funding request with auto-limit-check, rate-engine, and disclosure generation.

### Controls
- CTL-12: separate credit approval from sales; CTL-05 (prevent unauthorized credit-limit overrides); CTL-21 (origination accuracy).

### Time Estimate
- Originations are portal-received and system score-decisioned per the W5370 framework: the Credit Analyst's ~1–2-hour underwriting touch applies to the ~15–25% exception share (new-dealer, over-headroom, ineligible-category, and limit-review requests); ~25,000/yr → ~3,750–12,500 person-hours/yr (~8–25 hours/week), inside the dedicated Dealer Finance pod within the Finance & Accounting department envelope (§11.1's 53 HC covers Finance & Accounting + Internal Audit & Risk): Credit Manager 1 + Credit Analysts (Dealer Finance) 6–8 + Field Examiners 4–6 + program ops 1–2, ≈12–15 FTE of the AR/credit function; routine field exams beyond the in-house risk tier are contracted to third-party field-exam firms under VS-161, and disbursement processing rides shared VS-15 AP capacity.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (credit), VS-11 (trade/orders), VS-16 (O2C), VS-17 (GL)

## W5378. Floor-Plan Disbursement to Vendor, Dual-Payee Check & Supplier Settlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved origination (W5377) |
| **Frequency** | Per origination |
| **Volume** | ~10,000–40,000 disbursements/yr |
| **Owner** | Treasury/AP (Dealer Finance) |
| **Participants** | Supplier/Vendor, AP (VS-15), Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generate the disbursement as a dual-payee instrument (BuildRight + dealer, or BuildRight + supplier) to ensure funds reach the supplier/stock. | Treasury/AP | Credit Manager | per disbursement |
| 2 | Pay the supplier directly (or the dealer against proof of purchase) per the floor-plan structure. | AP | Credit Manager | per disbursement |
| 3 | Settle the supplier invoice (2-way/3-way match where applicable — VS-15) and clear the payable. | AP | Credit Manager | per disbursement |
| 4 | Post the disbursement to the floor-plan loan principal and the cash GL (VS-17/VS-18). | Finance | Credit Manager | per disbursement |
| 5 | Confirm unit receipt by the dealer (triggering collateral registration W5379). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per disbursement |

### System Touchpoints
- Disbursement/payment (VS-18/VS-80); AP (VS-15); floor-plan ledger principal posting; GL (VS-17).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Diversion risk**: funds paid to the dealer (not the supplier) are diverted and not used to acquire collateral, leaving BuildRight unsecured; mitigated by dual-payee/direct-to-supplier disbursement.
- **Duplicate-payment risk**: paying a supplier invoice twice (origination + dealer's own payment); mitigated by 2-way/3-way match and clear-floor-plan-only payment marking.

### Automation Opportunity
- Disbursement auto-routed dual-payee; supplier invoice auto-marked floor-plan-paid.

### Controls
- CTL-14/CTL-44 (duplicate-invoice/payment guard); CTL-10 (separate payment initiation from approval); CTL-21 (posting accuracy).

### Time Estimate
- Dual-payee generation, supplier settlement, and floor-plan-ledger posting are system-automated; the ~30–60-min manual touch (exception re-issuance, match breaks, proof-of purchase checks) applies to ~10–20% of ~25,000/yr → ~1,250–5,000 person-hours/yr (~3–10 hours/week), riding shared VS-15 AP capacity rather than dedicated headcount.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-15 (P2P/AP), VS-18 (treasury/disbursement), VS-80 (payments), VS-17 (GL)

## W5379. Unit-Level Collateral Registration, Serial/VIN Tracking & Floor-Plan Ledger Posting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Dealer confirms unit receipt (W5378) |
| **Frequency** | Per funded unit/batch |
| **Volume** | ~50,000–200,000 funded units/yr |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst (Dealer Finance) |
| **Participants** | Dealer, Collateral Registry, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture unit-level collateral identity: SKU, serial/VIN/lot, cost, dealer, and loan reference. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per unit |
| 2 | Register the unit in the floor-plan/collateral ledger with BuildRight's security interest (W5371). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per unit |
| 3 | Post the funded unit to the dealer's floor-plan loan balance (principal). | Finance | Credit Manager | per unit |
| 4 | Reconcile units funded ↔ disbursement ↔ supplier invoice ↔ dealer receipt. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per batch |
| 5 | Enable unit-level curtailment tracking and OOT detection (W5382/W5385). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per unit |

### System Touchpoints
- Floor-plan/collateral ledger (unit-level serial/VIN); loan principal posting; reconciliation vs. disbursement/invoice/receipt.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Untracked-collateral risk**: a funded unit not registered at serial level cannot be curtailed or verified, enabling OOT; mitigated by mandatory serial-level registration before principal posting.
- **Reconciliation-gap risk**: funded units ≠ disbursement/invoice indicates leakage/fraud; mitigated by batch reconciliation.

### Automation Opportunity
- Serial-level capture at dealer receipt auto-registers the collateral and posts the principal.

### Controls
- CTL-795 (ensure controlled execution — Loan Origination, Disbursement & Inventory Collateral Tracking (PA-188.2).)
- operational: collateral-ledger accuracy; CTL-23 (OOT/fraud detection); CTL-26 (collateral registration).

### Time Estimate
- ~10–15 min per unit batch; ongoing.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-05 (inventory), VS-35 (assets), VS-17 (GL), VS-23 (loss prevention)

| **Frequency** | Per movement; ad-hoc |
| **Volume** | ~5,000–20,000 movements/yr |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst (Dealer Finance) |
| **Participants** | Dealer, Legal (VS-100), other creditors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive dealer notification of an inter-branch/unit transfer of floor-planned inventory. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per movement |
| 2 | Update the unit's location in the collateral ledger; preserve BuildRight's security interest across the move. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per movement |
| 3 | On any third-party (subordinate/junior) lien claim on a unit, assess and document priority / subordination agreement. | Legal Counsel | Credit Manager | per claim |
| 4 | Confirm BuildRight's first-priority security interest is maintained or formally subordinated only with approval. | Credit Manager | Credit Manager | per claim |
| 5 | Update the collateral/lien register and dealer risk grade if subordination occurred. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Collateral/lien register (unit-level); location update; legal/subordination records (VS-100).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lost-priority risk**: an undocumented transfer or unmanaged subordination subordinates BuildRight's security interest to another creditor; mitigated by movement-notification and lien-priority review.
- **Hidden-transfer risk**: a dealer moving units off-premises to evade audit/recovery; mitigated by movement-notification policy and field-exam verification (W5381).

### Automation Opportunity
- Dealer-portal movement notification auto-updates the collateral ledger and flags off-premises moves.

### Controls
- CTL-795 (ensure controlled execution — Loan Origination, Disbursement & Inventory Collateral Tracking (PA-188.2).)
- operational: movement-notification policy.

### Time Estimate
- ~30 min per movement; ~10,000/yr → ~5,000 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100 (legal/liens), VS-68 (credit risk), VS-05 (inventory movement)

## W5381. Dealer Stocking-List Audit, Physical Inventory Verification & Field Examination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Onboarding (W5372); periodic exam cycle; risk-triggered exam |
| **Frequency** | ~quarterly for larger dealers; risk-triggered ad-hoc |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–4,000 exams/yr |
| **Owner** | Field Examiner |
| **Participants** | Dealer, Credit Analyst, Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Schedule the exam (cycle or risk-triggered by aging/OOT flags from W5385). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per exam |
| 2 | Physically verify funded units on the stocking list (serial-level) against the floor-plan ledger. | Field Examiner | Credit Manager | per exam |
| 3 | Identify exceptions: missing units (sold OOT), damaged, or commingled with non-funded stock. | Field Examiner | Credit Manager | per exam |
| 4 | Require cure for exceptions (curtailment W5382, repayment, or collateral substitution); document. | Credit Manager | Credit Manager | per exam |
| 5 | Update the dealer risk grade and exam findings register; escalate repeat/serious exceptions. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per exam |

### System Touchpoints
- Floor-plan/collateral ledger; field-exam findings register; risk-grade update; OOT-exception link (W5385).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **OOT-undetected risk**: infrequent or shallow exams let OOT accumulate into large unsecured exposure; mitigated by risk-based exam frequency and serial-level verification.
- **Dealer-obstruction risk**: a dealer obstructing the exam signals distress; mitigated by a right-to-examine clause and escalation to workout (W5386).

### Automation Opportunity
- Risk-based exam scheduling from aging/OOT signals; mobile serial-scan app for field verification.

### Controls
- CTL-795 (ensure controlled execution — Loan Origination, Disbursement & Inventory Collateral Tracking (PA-188.2).)
- operational: collateral-accuracy; CTL-23 (OOT/fraud detection); CTL-11 (separate counting from approval).

### Time Estimate
- ~1–3 person-days per exam, risk-tiered: the in-house Field Examiners (4–6) cover the high-risk tier (~600–900 exams/yr → ~900–2,700 person-days/yr); the routine quarterly tier of the ~3,000/yr cycle is contracted to third-party field-exam firms under VS-161 governance (dealer-charged or program-costed, not BuildRight headcount).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (credit), VS-21 (audit), VS-23 (LP), VS-161 (TPRM)

## W5382. Sold-Unit Reporting, Curtailment Schedule & Floor-Plan Paydown Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Dealer reports a unit sold (or curtailment period expires) |
| **Frequency** | Per unit sold; curtailment schedule |
| **Volume** | ~50,000–200,000 curtailments/yr |
| **Owner** | Credit Analyst (Dealer Finance) |
| **Participants** | Dealer, Cash Application, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive dealer sold-unit report (from the inventory feed W5374 or manual) with the unit serial and sale date. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per unit |
| 2 | Match the sold unit to the funded collateral (W5379); compute the curtailment (principal + accrued interest) due. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per unit |
| 3 | Apply the dealer's curtailment payment to the specific unit's loan balance; release the collateral. | Cash Application | Credit Manager | per unit |
| 4 | Flag units sold but not curtailed within the curtailment period → OOT exception (W5385). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | daily |
| 5 | Update the floor-plan ledger and dealer available headroom. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per unit |

### System Touchpoints
- Dealer inventory feed (W5374); floor-plan curtailment engine; cash application (VS-16); collateral release.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **OOT risk**: a unit sold without curtailment within the period is the core floor-plan default vector; mitigated by sold-vs-curtailed matching and OOT-exception flagging.
- **Mis-application risk**: a curtailment payment applied to the wrong unit distorts the collateral ledger; mitigated by serial-level payment matching.

### Automation Opportunity
- Dealer sold-unit feed auto-triggers curtailment and collateral release; auto-flag OOT exceptions.

### Controls
- CTL-795 (ensure controlled execution — Loan Origination, Disbursement & Inventory Collateral Tracking (PA-188.2).)
- operational: curtailment accuracy; CTL-23 (OOT detection); CTL-20 (cash accountability).

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 min per curtailment; ~125,000/yr → ~15,000 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (O2C/cash application), VS-68 (credit), VS-05 (inventory)

## W5383. Floor-Plan Interest/Markup Accrual, Billing & Dealer Statement Generation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly billing cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly per active floor-plan dealer |
| **Volume** | ~1,000 dealer statements/month |
| **Owner** | Finance (Dealer Finance) |
| **Participants** | Credit Manager, Dealer, AR (VS-16) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Accrue interest/markup on each dealer's outstanding floor-plan balance at the contractual rate (W5375). | Finance | Finance | per cycle |
| 2 | Compute subvention/manufacturer buy-down on eligible units (W5375) and net the dealer bill. | Finance | Finance | per cycle |
| 3 | Generate the dealer statement: opening balance, fundings, curtailments, interest, subvention, and closing balance. | Finance | Finance | per cycle |
| 4 | Post interest income and subvention to GL (VS-17/VS-157); issue the statement to the dealer. | Finance | Finance | per cycle |
| 5 | Monitor collections against the statement (W5384) and aging (W5385). | Credit Manager | Finance | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Floor-plan accrual/billing engine; AR (VS-16); GL posting (VS-17); revenue recognition (VS-157).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Accrual-error risk**: mis-keyed rates or balances produce incorrect statements and disputes; mitigated by automated accrual from the rate engine and ledger.
- **Revenue-misclassification risk**: floor-plan interest income vs. subvention vs. late fees must be correctly classified for tax (VAT/percentage tax) and PFRS; mitigated by defined GL mapping (VS-157).

### Automation Opportunity
- Fully automated accrual, subvention netting, statement generation, and GL posting.

### Controls
- CTL-34: correct tax treatment on financing income; CTL-30 (month-end close — interest/markup accrual completeness).

### Time Estimate
- ~2 days elapsed per monthly cycle; ~1,000 statements.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (AR), VS-17 (GL), VS-157 (revenue recognition), VS-39 (subvention)

## W5384. Dealer Payment Processing, Cash Application & Loan Account Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Dealer makes curtailment/interest payment |
| **Frequency** | Per payment |
| **Volume** | ~50,000–200,000 payments/yr |
| **Owner** | Cash Application Lead |
| **Participants** | Dealer, AR (VS-16), Treasury (VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive the dealer payment (bank transfer/check/e-wallet) and match to the dealer/statement. | Cash Application Lead | Credit Manager | per payment |
| 2 | Apply the payment: curtailment to specific units (W5382) or interest/statement (W5383) per remittance detail. | Cash Application Lead | Credit Manager | per payment |
| 3 | Reconcile the floor-plan loan account: payments vs. statement vs. ledger. | Cash Application Lead | Credit Manager | daily |
| 4 | Investigate and resolve unapplied/short/over-payments and dealer disputes. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | per exception |
| 5 | Update dealer available headroom and aging (W5385). | Cash Application Lead | Credit Manager | per payment |

### System Touchpoints
- Cash application/lockbox (VS-16/VS-18); floor-plan ledger reconciliation; bank reconciliation (VS-17 CTL-19).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unapplied-cash risk**: payments not applied to the correct unit/statement distort curtailment and OOT detection; mitigated by remittance-detail matching and daily reconciliation.
- **Short-pay/dispute risk**: unresolved short-pays accumulate into delinquency; mitigated by timely dispute resolution and aging follow-up (W5385).

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-matching of bank remittance to units/statement; auto-update headroom.

### Controls
- CTL-19: bank reconciliation; CTL-20 (cash accountability); CTL-21 (application accuracy).

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 min per payment; ~125,000/yr → ~15,000 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (O2C/AR), VS-18 (treasury/cash), VS-17 (GL), VS-80 (payments)
