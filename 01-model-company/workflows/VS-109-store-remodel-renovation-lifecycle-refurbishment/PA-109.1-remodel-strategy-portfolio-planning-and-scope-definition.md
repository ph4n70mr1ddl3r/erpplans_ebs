## W3473. Store Lifecycle & Remodel Portfolio Strategy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual capital & store-lifecycle planning (links to VS-33.1/W26, VS-40) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + multi-year roadmap |
| **Volume** | ~200 stores in the lifecycle portfolio |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations / Real Estate |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, Real Estate (links to VS-20/VS-97), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Finance/FP&A (links to VS-17.4/VS-40), Strategy (links to VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Store Operations/Strategy set the store-lifecycle and remodel strategy: target refresh cadence (5–7 yrs by format/age), format tiers (full remodel vs refresh vs re-fixture), category-expansion priorities (links to VS-01.1), market-repositioning rationale, and capex envelope vs the corporate capital plan (links to VS-40/VS-33.1); align to store-growth (links to VS-37) and closure (links to VS-59) plans | VP Store Operations / Strategy | COO | Annual |
| 2 | Real Estate/FP&A build the multi-year remodel roadmap from the store-lifecycle score (W3474): store age, sales trend, comp gap to market, fixture condition, customer-experience score (links to VS-13/VS-44), and capex affordability; sequence the ~30–40/year program against capacity, seasonality, and cash | Real Estate / FP&A | VP Store Operations | Annual |
| 3 | COO/CFO approve the remodel portfolio strategy and roadmap, confirm the capex allocation (links to VS-40) and the program governance (links to W3496/W112 PMO), and integrate to the annual plan (links to VS-33.1) and store-operations strategy | COO / CFO | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Capex/portfolio (links to VS-40/VS-112); store-lifecycle/asset master (links to VS-35/VS-97); strategy/performance (links to VS-33); merchandising (links to VS-01)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cadence-misjudgment risk**: Remodeling too late erodes comp sales; too early wastes capital; mitigated by lifecycle scoring (W3474)
- **Capex-conflict risk**: Remodel capex crowds out new-store growth (links to VS-37); mitigated by portfolio governance and phasing


### Automation Opportunity
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the remodel portfolio strategy and roadmap, confirm the capex allocation" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-556 (ensure controlled execution — Remodel Strategy, Portfolio Planning & Scope Definition (PA-109.1).)
- operational: lifecycle scoring (W3474); operational: portfolio governance and phasing

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 weeks annual

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~200 stores scored; ~30–40 qualified/year |

| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, Regional Managers (links to VS-07), Real Estate (links to VS-20), Facilities (links to VS-20.3), Merchandising (links to VS-01), FP&A (links to VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Regional Managers/FP&A score each store on the lifecycle matrix: sales trend & comp vs market (links to VS-33.3), gross-margin & inventory turn (links to VS-101), customer-satisfaction/NPS (links to VS-13), store age & fixture/asset condition (links to VS-35/VS-20.3), maintenance-spend trend, and trade-area growth/competitor pressure | Regional Mgrs / FP&A | VP Store Operations | Annual |
| 2 | Real Estate/Facilities validate physical condition: roof/structural, MEP/HVAC, floor/walls, fixtures, signage, technology/POS currency (links to VS-99), compliance gaps (links to VS-22/VS-76), and accessibility; flag safety/structural priorities | Real Estate / Facilities | VP Store Operations | Annual |
| 3 | VP Store Operations qualify stores into tiers (full remodel / refresh / re-fixture / defer) and feed the roadmap (W3473); for full remodels, commission a concept/scope brief (W3475) and business case (W3476); document the qualification decision for governance (W3496) | VP Store Operations / Real Estate | COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-performance/BI (links to VS-28.1/VS-33.2); asset/facility (links to VS-35/VS-20.3); compliance (links to VS-22); customer feedback (links to VS-13/VS-44)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Score-distortion risk**: One bad year triggers a needless remodel; mitigated by multi-year trend and condition validation
- **Condition-blindspot risk**: Latent structural/MEP defects missed until construction; mitigated by facility survey (links to VS-20.3)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Real Estate/Facilities validate physical condition" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document the qualification decision for governance" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-556 (ensure controlled execution — Remodel Strategy, Portfolio Planning & Scope Definition (PA-109.1).)
- operational: multi-year trend and condition validation; operational: facility survey (links to VS-20)

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual scoring

| **Volume** | ~30–40 remodels/year |
| **Owner** | Store Design / Concept Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store Design/Merchandising define the remodel concept and format: department adjacencies and space reallocation (links to VS-55/W2160), category expansion/contraction (links to VS-01.1), new zones (e.g., appliance gallery, project-design center links to VS-66, solar/renewable bay links to VS-70), customer-flow and queue/checkout layout (links to VS-08), and fixture/finish standard | Store Design / Merchandising | VP Store Operations | Per remodel |
| 2 | Store Design/Real Estate scope the works: structural (walls/openings), MEP/HVAC (links to VS-20.3), lighting/electrical, finishes, fixtures/FF&E (W3481), signage/graphics (W3485), technology/POS refresh (W3486), and exterior/storefront; distinguish capital vs expense and phasing (W3480) | Store Design / Real Estate | VP Store Operations | Per remodel |
| 3 | Store Design publish the concept brief, scope-of-works, target schedule, and Class-3 cost estimate (W3476); obtain Merchandising/Store Ops/Marketing sign-off and the Store Manager's operational input; proceed to business case | Store Design / Merchandising | COO | Per remodel |

- **Scope-creep risk**: Undefined concept expands mid-build; mitigated by clear scope-of-works and change control (W3556)
- **Format-misfit risk**: Concept doesn't fit the trade area; mitigated by merchandising/market input (links to VS-01/VS-33.3)

- System auto-log of "Store Design/Real Estate scope the works" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-schedule of "schedule, and Class-3 cost estimate" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-556 (ensure controlled execution — Remodel Strategy, Portfolio Planning & Scope Definition (PA-109.1).)
- operational: clear scope-of-works and change control (W3556); operational: merchandising/market input (links to VS-01/VS-33)

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 weeks per remodel
