- System auto-document of "documented decision rights" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "report governance effectiveness and material decisions to the Board Audit/Risk…" (replaces manual Step 3).

- CTL-625 (ensure controlled execution — Responsible Sourcing Governance, Reporting & Stakeholder Engagement (PA-131.3).)
