## W4401. Customer Incident & Accident Response (First Aid, Stabilize, Secure)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer injury/near-miss/property-damage on premises |
| **Frequency** | Per incident (~daily across 200 stores for minor; severe less frequent) |
| **Volume** | Minor incidents ~ hundreds/yr; recordable/severe fewer; near-misses (if captured) thousands |
| **Owner** | Store Manager / First Aider (VS-83) / Customer Safety |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), First Aider/Clinic (VS-83), HSE (VS-24), Security (VS-23), Customer Safety, Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store/First Aider secure the scene (stop hazard, cone-off, evacuate if needed), provide first aid/clinic referral, call emergency services for serious injury, and care for the customer | First Aider / Store Ops / Security | Store Manager | Immediate (minutes) |
| 2 | Store/Customer Safety capture the incident in the system (W4392) — what/when/where, customer details (PII per VS-91), witnesses, photos/CCTV, equipment/merchandise involved | Store Manager / Customer Safety | Store Manager | Within hours |
| 3 | Store/Customer Safety preserve evidence (CCTV clip, damaged item, spill sample), notify insurance (W4387/W4405), and avoid admission of liability (per legal W4387) | Store Manager / Customer Safety / Insurance | Store Manager / Head of Customer Safety | Within 24 hrs |

### System Touchpoints
- Incident system (W4392); first-aid/clinic (VS-83); CCTV/evidence (VS-23.2/W4392); insurance (VS-26); legal (VS-100); privacy (VS-91)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Response-delay risk**: slow first aid worsens injury + liability; mitigated by trained first-aiders on every shift (VS-83) & emergency protocol
- **Evidence-loss risk**: scene cleaned before documentation → claim defenseless; mitigated by mandatory photo/CCTV/witness capture before clean-up
- **Admission-of-liability risk**: store staff apologizes/admits → prejudices defense; mitigated by no-admission protocol (W4387)

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile incident app with CCTV auto-clip; witness/statement template; auto-notify insurance/legal; no-admission prompt

### Controls
- CTL-673 (ensure controlled execution — Customer Incident Response, Claims & Safety Analytics (PA-147.3).)
- CTL-673: trained first-aider every shift; scene secured; evidence captured (photo/CCTV/witness); no admission of liability; insurance notified within 24 hrs

### Time Estimate
- Immediate response minutes; ~30–60 min documentation per incident

### Cross-references
- links to VS-83 (clinic), VS-23.2 (CCTV), VS-26 (insurance), VS-100 (legal), VS-91 (privacy), W4387 (framework), W4392 (system), W4405 (notification)

## W5555. Customer or Visitor Death on Premises — Scene Protocol, Family Liaison & Trading-Continuity Decision

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | A customer or visitor dies on premises — sudden natural death (cardiac, stroke), fatal accident (fall, struck-by), or unattended death discovered on site — before any police determination of cause |
| **Frequency** | The rare fatality tail of the ~daily chain-wide customer-incident population (W4401): a small number of events per year across ~205 sites serving ~99,000 people on premises per day (customers + staff, the W330 population canon) |
| **Volume** | 1 event × the site's trading surface (partial closure with a screened area, or full closure, for 2–12 hours) × responders (Store Manager, security, first aider, PNP/SOCO) × the notification set (family, insurer per W4405, DOLE only where an employee is involved, comms only if public) |
| **Owner** | Store Manager / Head of HSE |
| **Participants** | Security Guard, First Aider (VS-83), Regional Manager, Head of Customer Safety, HSE Manager (VS-24), Legal Counsel (VS-100), HR Business Partner (employee-involved cases; W1325 death-in-service benefits), Insurance Coordinator (W4403/W4405), EAP Counselor (W2867), Corporate Communications (W134/W855 if the event becomes public), PNP Scene of the Crime Operations (SOCO) team, Barangay officials |

### Background

W4401 owns first aid/stabilize/secure and names fatality only as W4405's notification trigger — 'death on premises'/'died in store'/'natural death' appear in zero PA files as protocol content. A death on premises is not an injury claim with worse stakes: until PNP/SOCO document the scene, it is a potential crime scene, so the do-not-move/no-cleaning discipline is a legal duty, not delicacy; the family may arrive before the police do; the trading-continuity decision (close, screen, or continue) is a dignity and reputation decision no daily-ops workflow governs; and witnessing staff need structured stand-down. This workflow is the single canon for that event class — the W5536/W5537/W5538 life-safety family's fatality counterpart, gating scene release on documented external clearance. If the death follows a crime, during-event response rode W717/E-21 and this workflow takes over at scene control; if an employee died on duty, the DOLE 24-hour report and W1325 benefits chain rides W4405/W4403.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Response until pronouncement**: first aid and resuscitation attempts continue per the First Aider protocol until medical personnel formally pronounce — abandonment before pronouncement is both a welfare and a legal failure; emergency services called immediately | First Aider / Security Guard | Store Manager | Immediate |
| 2 | **Scene control — nothing moves**: screen or cordon the area; halt all cleaning; hold CCTV footage (W838 preservation request); the decedent's belongings are not touched or photographed by staff; access limited to responders | Store Manager / Security Guard | Store Manager | Immediate |
| 3 | **Police handoff & scene release**: PNP/SOCO take custody and conduct their on-site processing; the scene is released to BuildRight only on their documented clearance; Barangay assists family tracing where identity or next-of-kin is unresolved | Store Manager / Security Guard | Regional Manager | 2–8 hours |
| 4 | **Family care & liaison**: a private space away from the scene; factual briefing only with the W4401 no-admission-of-liability rule; written contact log; religious/cultural needs accommodated; family never learns of the death from social media (Step 3/4 sequencing rule) | Store Manager / HR Business Partner | Head of Customer Safety | Same day + aftercare |
| 5 | **Trading-continuity decision**: Regional Manager with the Head of Customer Safety decides and documents — full closure, partial closure with a screened area, or continued trading; where the scene is visible to customers, the dignity-first default is closure until screened; the decision and its basis are recorded either way | Regional Manager / Store Manager | VP Store Operations | Within 2 hours |
| 6 | **Employee care**: witnessing staff stood down for the shift where feasible; critical-incident stress debriefing per W2867; no same-day formal statements (memory-contamination rule) | HR Business Partner / EAP Counselor | Store Manager | Same day |
| 7 | **Documentation & statutory notification**: incident file per W4402 with restricted visibility; insurer notification per W4405 within 24 hours; employee-involved deaths add the DOLE 24-hour report and the W1325 benefits chain; privacy handling of the decedent's data per VS-91 | Store Manager / Customer Safety | Head of Customer Safety | Within 24–48 hours |
| 8 | **Aftercare & CAPA**: structured family follow-up; claim handling per W4403 under an empathy protocol (claim defense never argued at the scene); hazard-CAPA per W4404 where the death followed an accident; protocol learnings folded into the W4406 drill rotation | Head of Customer Safety / Insurance Coordinator | Head of Customer Safety | 2–6 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Incident case records with restricted visibility and post-24-hour edit lock (W4392/W4402-class)
- CCTV preservation-hold workflow before any footage rotation (links to W838)
- Insurer notification and premises-liability claim objects (links to W4405/W4403)
- DOLE 24-hour reporting objects for employee-involved deaths (links to W4405)
- EAP/critical-incident case tracking (links to W2867); crisis-communications tree (links to W855/W134)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scene-contamination risk**: staff moving the body or cleaning before SOCO clearance destroys the investigation and the company's legal position; mitigated by Step 2's nothing-moves rule and Step 3's documented-release gate
- **Dignity risk**: trading continuing in view of a covered body becomes the viral photograph that defines the brand; mitigated by Step 5's dignity-first default with a documented override basis
- **Liability-admission risk**: apologies framed as acceptance of fault at the worst possible moment; mitigated by the W4401 no-admission rule and factual-briefing-only discipline in Step 4
- **Family-distress risk**: next-of-kin discovering the death from social media or from a customer video; mitigated by the Step 3/4 sequencing rule and Barangay-assisted tracing
- **Witness-trauma risk**: unaddressed exposure of the staff who performed resuscitation; mitigated by Step 6's stand-down and W2867 debriefing

### Automation Opportunity
- Incident-case template whose fatality flag auto-attaches the scene-release checklist and blocks case closure without documented police clearance (replaces manual Step 3/7 verification)
- Auto-notification pack on the fatality flag (insurer, DOLE-if-employee, EAP, Regional Manager) with a 24-hour follow-up task (replaces manual Step 7 dispatch)

### Controls
- CTL-673 (ensure controlled execution — Customer Incident Response, Claims & Safety Analytics (PA-147.3).)
- operational: scene released only on documented PNP/SOCO clearance filed in the incident record; trading-continuity decision documented with its basis before either closing or continuing; decedent and family data handled per the restricted-visibility privacy rule

### Time Estimate
Per event: ≈ 24–40 staff-hours across the first 48 hours (store management, Customer Safety, HR, security) plus aftercare contacts over 2–6 weeks. Protocol upkeep ≈ 2–3 hours/year inside the W4406 drill rotation — no incremental headcount.

### Staffing Implication
- Absorbed by the store management bench, the Customer Safety and HRBP rosters, and the existing EAP contract; no incremental headcount.
