## W4393. Daily Store Safety Inspection, Hazard Hunt & Housekeeping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store open; shift change; scheduled cycle |
| **Frequency** | Daily (open + mid-day + close) per site |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × ~3 inspections/day × ~360 days ≈ ~216,000 inspections/yr |
| **Owner** | Store Manager (VS-07) / Customer Safety |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), HSE (VS-24), Stock Associates, IFM (VS-138), Customer Safety |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store team run the documented safety walk per zone (sales floor, lumber/tile yard, parking) against the hazard register (W4386) — spills, obstacles, damaged racking, blocked egress, exposed sharps, unstable stacks | Store Manager / Stock Associates | Store Manager | ~20–30 min/inspection |
| 2 | Store team correct immediate hazards (spill clean-up, cone-off, restack, remove) or escalate to IFM (VS-138)/HSE, and log every finding into the incident system (W4392) including near-misses | Store Ops / IFM / HSE | Store Manager | Per finding |
| 3 | Customer Safety/HSE trend findings, identify repeat hazards, and trigger corrective action (W4404) or standard change (W4386) | Customer Safety / HSE | Head of Customer Safety | Weekly/monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile inspection app vs hazard register (W4392); store-ops task (VS-63); IFM CMMS (VS-138); HSE (VS-24); BI (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Checklist-compliance risk**: walk done but hazards missed (tick-box); mitigated by hazard-register-driven checklist + photo evidence
- **Spill-response risk**: wet floor unattended → slip injury (most common claim); mitigated by immediate cone/spill-kit protocol + "wet floor" signage (W4398)

### Automation Opportunity
- Hazard-driven mobile checklist; photo capture; auto-task to IFM/HSE; repeat-hazard analytics

### Controls
- CTL-164 (ensure customer safety & premises-liability control.)
- CTL-672: documented daily walk vs hazard register; immediate-hazard correction; findings logged incl. near-miss; trends reviewed (W4404)

### Time Estimate
- ~20–30 min/inspection × ~216,000/yr ≈ ~72,000–108,000 staff-hrs/yr (built into existing store-staff model)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07 (store), VS-138 (IFM), VS-24 (HSE), VS-63 (task), W4386 (standards), W4392 (system), W4404 (CAPA)

## W5562. Vehicle-Impact & Storefront-Crash Response Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | A motor vehicle strikes a BuildRight building — a storefront or entrance impact, a parking-lot collision with the building face or support columns, a loading-dock/delivery-vehicle impact, or a boom/crane crash against the structure while customers or staff are on site |
| **Frequency** | Rare but chain-real event class: ~2–4 vehicle-into-building strikes per year across ~205 roadside sites with parking lots and dock aprons (big-box formats sit behind driving surfaces); most are low-speed parking-lot contacts, the tail case is a high-speed storefront intrusion during trading |
| **Volume** | 1 event × 1 site × the crash-surface scope (glazing, canopy, columns, racking near the impact line) × the trading-impact tier (partial-zone closure to full closure); each event also opens the driver/third-party liability track and the structural-clearance gate |
| **Owner** | Store Manager (site command); Head of Customer Safety (protocol & reopen gate) |
| **Participants** | Regional Manager, Security Guard / Loss Prevention, Facilities & Maintenance (W650 call-out), Structural Engineer (retainer), PNP traffic investigator, third-party insurer (driver's liability desk), VS-26 (property/BI claim, W876), VS-147.3 (injury/claims chain where injuries occur), Store Communications (VS-63), Legal (VS-100 demand & subrogation posture) |

### Background

'Vehicle into building'/'storefront crash' appear in zero PA files — W330's in-store emergency trigger names structural failure generically but defers event-specific execution, W5555 owns the fatality scene protocol, W1450's structural assessment is quake-specific, and W653 owns our fleet crashing elsewhere — nobody owned the survivable-crash class: the crash-scene safety law (an unstable vehicle, a fuel leak and compromised glazing are hazards before they are evidence), the liability ordering (the driver's compulsory third-party liability and own-damage cover is the primary claim — BuildRight's property loss runs as subrogation, not the other way round), the red-tag structural discipline before any zone reopens (the W5537 BFP-clearance analog for impact damage), and the bollard/barrier retrofit feedback into the site-standards register.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Crash-scene safety & command**: hold-back cordon (vehicle instability, fuel leak, glass); isolate power/fuel at the meter where the impact struck utilities; first aid per W501 with injuries routed via W140/VS-147.3; request PNP traffic investigation | Store Manager / Security Guard | Store Manager | 5–15 min |
| 2 | **Evidence preservation**: CCTV preservation hold (W838); scene photos before any cleanup; witness details; driver/vehicle/insurer details on the incident record; no fault statements by anyone | Store Manager | Store Manager | 30–60 min |
| 3 | **Structural triage**: Facilities + structural-engineer call-out assess the impact zone — glazing, canopy, columns, the racking line behind the face; red-tag zones cordoned; occupancy of unaffected zones documented | Facilities / Structural Engineer | Head of Customer Safety | 2–6 hours |
| 4 | **Liability posture**: the driver's CTPL and own-damage insurance is the primary claim track; BuildRight's property loss runs as subrogation; Legal issues the demand/notice letter to driver, owner and insurer | Legal / Insurance | VP Legal & Compliance | 1–2 days elapsed |
| 5 | **Claim initiation**: property-claim pack (photos, engineer's report, repair quotes) to the third-party insurer; own-policy engagement (VS-26 W876) for the uninsured gap and business interruption | Insurance / VS-26 Claims | CFO | 2–5 days elapsed |
| 6 | **Trading-continuity decision**: partial trade behind the cordon is the default; full closure rides the Regional Manager closure authority (the E-02 custody — W2510 decides, W850 executes) when the impact zone touches exits, utilities or occupied selling space; documented either way | Store Manager / Regional Manager | Regional Manager | On assessment |
| 7 | **Repair & reopen gate**: repairs under permit where structure is touched; no reopen of red-tagged zones until the structural engineer's written clearance and — where load-bearing elements were struck — the LGU building official's clearance (the W5537 BFP-gate analog) | Facilities / Head of Customer Safety | Head of Customer Safety | Per repair scope |
| 8 | **After-action & retrofit**: the incident feeds the site-standards register — bollard/barrier adequacy at the struck face reviewed per this PA and VS-138 PM; claims outcome reconciles to the safety-investment backlog | Head of Customer Safety / Facilities | Head of Customer Safety | 1–2 weeks post-event |

### System Touchpoints
- Incident-case record with a vehicle-impact type and mandatory scene-photo attachments
- CCTV preservation hold (links to W838)
- Facility work orders with a structural flag (links to W650/W47)
- Red-tag/reopen checklist states on the site file
- Claim records to VS-26 (W876) and the third-party subrogation file
- Demand-letter/outside-counsel matter in the legal case system (links to VS-100)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Structural-understatement risk**: a struck column or canopy beam reads as cosmetic glazing damage; mitigated by Step 3's engineer red-tag before any zone reopens
- **Fault-admission risk**: on-site fault talk by staff becomes the insurer's defense exhibit; mitigated by the no-statement rule and Step 1's PNP investigation ownership
- **Evidence-decay risk**: the vehicle is removed and the scene cleaned before documentation; mitigated by Step 2's preservation hold before any cleanup
- **Recovery-gap risk**: the store sits closed waiting on the third-party insurer; mitigated by Step 5's own-policy BI engagement for the gap period
- **Repeat-impact risk**: the same face is struck again; mitigated by Step 8's bollard/barrier review feeding the retrofit backlog

### Automation Opportunity
- Vehicle-impact incident type with a mandatory photo checklist (replaces manual evidence capture)
- Red-tag zone flags that block POS reopening checklists until engineer clearance is recorded
- Claim-pack auto-assembly from the incident record + engineer's report + repair quotes
- Bollard/barrier audit triggers on the site-standards register from the incident record

### Controls
- CTL-672 (ensure controlled execution — In-Store Customer Safety Operations & Hazard Control (PA-147.2).)
- operational: crash-scene cordons and no-statement rules briefed at every store; red-tag/reopen decisions documented with the engineer's written clearance; subrogation files reconciled to claim outcomes

### Time Estimate
Per event ≈ 8–20 staff-hours on day one (scene command, evidence, triage) plus 4–8 hours of engineer/insurer follow-through spread over the recovery weeks; at the ~2–4-events-per-year class the chain-wide figure stays under ~100 staff-hours per year, absorbed within store management, facilities and the claims desk.

### Staffing Implication
- Absorbed by store management, facilities and the VS-26 claims desk with counsel support; no incremental headcount.
