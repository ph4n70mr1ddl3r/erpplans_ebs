## Overview

Green fleet transition, electric vehicle (EV) fleet operations and sustainable transportation
for BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **multi-year decarbonization of BuildRight's own
goods-moving and service fleet** (the ~20% owned share of the outbound-distribution fleet run by
VS-06, plus the customer self-haul rental fleet VS-162, the heavy/compact equipment rental fleet
VS-186, the contractor jobsite-delivery fleet VS-74, and the employee shuttle fleet VS-141), and
the **charging / alternative-fuel infrastructure, route-network redesign, and EVIDA/DOE/LTO
compliance** that the transition requires. The program covers green-fleet strategy and emissions
baseline; asset inventory and electrification-readiness assessment; the EV/alt-fuel (battery-electric,
plug-in hybrid, biodiesel/B20, LNG/CNG) procurement, OEM selection, and homologation; depot/DC
charging and alt-fuel infrastructure siting and grid/utility coordination; total-cost-of-ownership
and lifecycle analysis; RA 11697 (EVIDA, 2022) incentive, registration, and toll/lane-exemption
compliance; daily EV charging operations and smart-load scheduling; battery state-of-health and
lifecycle management; green-fleet telematics, eco-driving, and driver behavior; range-aware route
dispatch and payload optimization; charging–renewable integration with the rooftop-solar prosumer
plant (VS-108) and demand response; EV-specific maintenance, diagnostics, and roadside/towing;
fleet GHG measurement, MRV, and Scope 1 reduction reporting (feeds VS-25); DOE/LTO/LTFRB
green-fleet compliance and inspection; high-voltage and lithium-battery-fire safety under DOLE-OSH
and BFP; driver training and certification; third-party/3PL carrier green-fleet requirements and
contract clauses (the ~80% outsourced share); charging billing and electricity cost allocation;
battery second-life and end-of-life recycling (RA 11898 EPR linkage); and green-fleet performance
and decarbonization analytics.

This matters at BuildRight's scale. The Philippines' **Electric Vehicle Industry Development Act
(RA 11697 / EVIDA, 2022)** and its DOE IRR establish a national EV roadmap that
explicitly includes **commercial and fleet adoption**, corporate fleet EV-share expectations, and
building/Parking-Space EV-readiness provisions that interact directly with BuildRight's existing
EV-charging host network (VS-163) and rooftop-solar prosumer program (VS-108). At ~50,000 monthly
store-replenishment orders (production-calibrated per data-volumes §1.1), ~42,900 ecommerce orders/month, ~10–15 new stores/yr, and ~6,925
employees across a 200-store archipelago, transportation is a material Scope 1 emissions line and
operating-cost line; a structured green-fleet transition is the primary lever on both the ESG
decarbonization commitment (VS-25) and the 12–14% EBITDA target. It is distinct from VS-06
(operates the current diesel fleet — this transitions it), VS-61 (diesel fuel & cost — this owns
electricity/alt-fuel energy), VS-163 (hosts EV charging for *customers* — this owns charging for
BuildRight's *own* fleet, though the two share infrastructure), VS-108 (own-generation solar — this
matches fleet charging load to it), VS-141 (employee shuttles — this decarbonizes them), and
VS-25 (reports the footprint — this reduces it). The capability was genuinely uncovered: the
defining terms 'fleet electrification', 'electric fleet', 'EV fleet', 'green fleet', and 'fleet
decarbonization' each appeared in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers, and the single
existing reference is a forward-pointer inside VS-163 PA-163.1 step 1 — *"BuildRight's own EV-fleet
charging need (VS-06/VS-61 green-fleet transition)"* — explicitly acknowledging the gap and the
owner that did not yet exist.
