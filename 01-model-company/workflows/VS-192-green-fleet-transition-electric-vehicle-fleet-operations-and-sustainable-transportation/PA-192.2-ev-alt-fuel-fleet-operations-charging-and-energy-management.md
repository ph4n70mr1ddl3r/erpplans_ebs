## W5473. EV Fleet Daily Charging Operations, Load Scheduling & Smart-Charge Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vehicles in service; nightly/peak charging cycles |
| **Frequency** | Daily, per vehicle and per depot |
| **Volume** | Per EV in the owned fleet, daily; scales with conversion (~early tens → larger) |
| **Owner** | Fleet Charging Operations Supervisor (VS-06) |
| **Participants** | Drivers, Facilities/Energy (VS-138/VS-120), Depot/DC Ops (VS-04), EV-host ops (VS-163) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Each evening, the charge-management system schedules each returning EV against its next-day dispatch (W5477), state-of-charge, time-of-departure, and the site's transformer/charger capacity (W5468). | Charge Ops Supervisor / System | Fleet Charging Operations Supervisor | nightly |
| 2 | Apply smart-charging rules: prioritize low-tariff / high-renewable hours (W5479), cap simultaneous draw to the site limit, defer non-urgent charging, and sequence to avoid peak-demand charges. | Charge Ops Supervisor / System | Fleet Charging Operations Supervisor | nightly |
| 3 | Reconcile fleet-charging priority vs the shared customer-host network (VS-163) under a documented priority policy (fleet-priority off-hours, customer-priority during trading). | Charge Ops Supervisor | Fleet Charging Operations Supervisor | ongoing |
| 4 | Log every charging session (vehicle, charger, kWh, start/stop, tariff, renewable-share) for billing (W5486), MRV (W5481), and analytics (W5488). | System | Fleet Charging Operations Supervisor | per session |
| 5 | Exception-handle charging failures (faulty charger, vehicle not accepting charge, interrupted session) and trigger maintenance (W5474) or diesel/hybrid fallback (W5477). | Charge Ops Supervisor | Fleet Charging Operations Supervisor | per exception |

### System Touchpoints
- Charge-management/CPMS (links VS-163 EV-host platform); telematics/SoC (VS-06/W5475); energy/tariff (VS-120); Route Optimization Engine dispatch (in-house build — fit-gap C14; W5477); billing (W5486); MRV (W5481).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **UndersoC-at-departure risk**: a scheduling fault leaves a vehicle under-charged for its dispatch, forcing diesel fallback or a missed delivery; mitigated by SoC-at-departure rules and pre-departure checks.
- **Peak-demand-charge risk**: unmanaged simultaneous charging trips a demand-charge band and erodes the electricity-cost case; mitigated by smart-load scheduling and solar peak-shaving (W5479).
- **Fleet-vs-customer contention risk**: fleet charging starves the customer-host revenue stream (VS-163) at peak; mitigated by the documented priority policy and off-hours fleet charging.

### Automation Opportunity
- SoC-vs-dispatch-aware overnight scheduling; tariff-aware and renewable-aware smart-charging; automatic session logging and exception alerting; demand-charge avoidance logic.

### Controls
- CTL-807 (ensure controlled execution — EV / Alt-Fuel Fleet Operations, Charging & Energy Management (PA-192.2).)
- operational: documented fleet-vs-customer priority policy; SoC-at-departure minimum enforced.

### Time Estimate
- Automated once configured; ~15–30 min/supervisor daily exception-handling + review.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-163 (EV-host shared), VS-120 (energy/tariff), VS-138 (facilities), VS-06 (fleet/telematics), W5468 (infrastructure), W5474 (charger maintenance), W5477 (dispatch), W5479 (renewable matching), W5481 (MRV), W5486 (billing)

| **Owner** | Facilities & Energy Manager (VS-138) |
| **Participants** | Charger vendor/EPC, Fleet (VS-06), HSE (VS-24), EV-host ops (VS-163) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

| 2 | Execute preventive maintenance per OEM schedule (connector/cable inspection, cooling, firmware updates, ground-fault/earth-leakage tests) and maintain a charger asset register with uptime SLA. | Facilities / Charger Vendor | Facilities & Energy Manager | per schedule |
| 3 | Triage and resolve faults (error codes, communication faults, payment/OCPP issues, hardware faults) under a vendor SLA; escalate to OEM field service; provide a backup charger or diesel fallback. | Facilities / Charger Vendor | Facilities & Energy Manager | per fault |
| 4 | Track and report charger uptime/availability (target e.g., ≥98%), MTBF/MTTR, and session success-rate per site; drive vendor performance against SLA. | Facilities Analyst | Facilities & Energy Manager | monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Charger/CPMS (VS-163 shared); facilities/CMMS (VS-138); vendor/SLA (VS-161/VS-34); HSE (VS-24); asset register (VS-35).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Single-point-charger-failure risk**: a depot with one charger that fails halts the wave; mitigated by redundancy/N+1 sizing (W5468) and SLA-backed field service.
- **Connector/cable-abuse risk**: driver mishandling damages connectors, driving downtime and cost; mitigated by driver training (W5484) and inspection.
- **Firmware/homologation-drift risk**: an OEM firmware push introduces a fault or breaks OCPP interop; mitigated by staged firmware rollouts and a rollback plan.

### Automation Opportunity
- Uptime/availability monitoring with auto-fault tickets; OEM SLA scorecard; predictive maintenance from usage/fault telemetry; firmware-rollout management.

### Controls
- CTL-807 (ensure controlled execution — EV / Alt-Fuel Fleet Operations, Charging & Energy Management (PA-192.2).)
- operational: charger-vendor TPRM/SLA; BFP/electrical-permit verification; uptime evidence; N+1 redundancy policy for depot fleet chargers; staged firmware rollout.

### Time Estimate
- Automated monitoring; ~hours/week site PM per site batch; fault-response per SLA.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-163 (EV-host shared), VS-138 (facilities/CMMS), VS-34/VS-161 (vendor/SLA), VS-24 (HSE), VS-35 (assets), W5468 (infrastructure), W5483 (battery-fire/HV safety)

## W5475. EV Battery State-of-Health, Range Assurance & Battery Lifecycle Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vehicles in service; periodic SoH review; warranty/degradation thresholds |
| **Frequency** | Continuous SoH telemetry; quarterly SoH review per vehicle |
| **Volume** | Per EV in the fleet |
| **Owner** | Fleet Maintenance Manager (VS-06.2) |
| **Participants** | OEM/battery vendor, Telematics (VS-06), Maintenance (VS-06.2), Treasury/warranty (W5467) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture battery SoH telemetry (capacity fade, internal resistance, cell-balance, temperature excursions, charge-cycle count) from the vehicle BMS via telematics (VS-06). | Telematics / Maintenance Analyst | Fleet Maintenance Manager | continuous |
| 2 | Track each vehicle's usable range vs its dispatch requirement (W5477); flag vehicles approaching range-insufficiency for route reassignment or remediation. | Maintenance Analyst | Fleet Maintenance Manager | quarterly |
| 3 | Detect degradation against the OEM warranty threshold (e.g., <70% at 5-yr/300k-km per W5467) and prepare warranty claims / battery-module replacement cases. | Maintenance / Warranty Admin | Fleet Maintenance Manager | per threshold |
| 4 | Manage the battery lifecycle plan: in-service → degradation monitoring → warranty/replacement → second-life (W5487) → recycling (W5487), maintaining a battery passport/traceability record. | Maintenance Analyst | Fleet Maintenance Manager | ongoing |
| 5 | Maintain battery thermal-management discipline (avoid chronic high-temp operation that accelerates degradation in the PH tropical climate) and feed any thermal event into the safety program (W5483). | Maintenance / Driver | Fleet Maintenance Manager | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Telematics/BMS feed (VS-06); fleet asset register (VS-06); warranty tracker (links W5467); maintenance/CMMS (VS-06.2/VS-138); battery lifecycle/second-life (W5487).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Accelerated-degradation risk**: PH tropical heat + heavy-payload duty cycles degrade batteries faster than OEM temperate-climate baselines, eroding the TCO case; mitigated by thermal management, conservative warranty terms (W5467), and SoH-based route assignment.
- **Warranty-evidence risk**: inadequate SoH telemetry forfeits a valid warranty claim; mitigated by continuous BMS data capture and documented thresholds.
- **Range-misjudgment risk**: dispatching a degraded-range vehicle to a long lane strands the route; mitigated by SoH-aware dispatch (W5477).

### Automation Opportunity
- BMS→Oracle eAM SoH feed (fit-gap F5) with degradation-trend alerting; warranty-threshold auto-detection and claim drafting; battery-passport/traceability ledger.

### Controls
- CTL-807 (ensure controlled execution — EV / Alt-Fuel Fleet Operations, Charging & Energy Management (PA-192.2).)
- operational: documented thermal-management discipline; SoH-based route-assignment rule.

### Time Estimate
- Automated telemetry; ~hours/quarter per vehicle for SoH review; per-claim case work.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (fleet/telematics), VS-06.2/VS-138 (maintenance/CMMS), W5467 (warranty), W5477 (SoH-aware dispatch), W5483 (thermal/fire safety), W5487 (second-life/recycling)

## W5477. Range-Aware Route Dispatch, Payload Optimization & Charging-Stop Planning

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily dispatch; network re-design (W5472); SoH updates (W5475) |
| **Frequency** | Daily, per vehicle and per route |
| **Volume** | Per EV/alt-fuel dispatch across 4 DCs + last-mile + jobsite |
| **Owner** | Dispatch Supervisor (VS-06.1/VS-06.3) |
| **Participants** | Drivers, Network Planning (W5472), Charging Ops (W5473), Store Ops (VS-177) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assign vehicles to dispatches matching usable range (from SoH W5475) to the route's energy requirement (distance, payload, grade, climate/A/C load from W5472). | Dispatcher | Dispatch Supervisor | per dispatch |
| 2 | Optimize payload/cube within the EV's reduced-payload envelope; split/combine orders or adjust replenishment frequency (VS-02) to maintain coverage. | Dispatcher / Supply Planning | Dispatch Supervisor | per dispatch |
| 3 | Plan any en-route/ opportunity charging stops (links W5474) into the schedule with dwell, and confirm charger availability at the stop. | Dispatcher / Charging Ops | Dispatch Supervisor | per dispatch |
| 4 | Pre-condition the cabin (while plugged in) to preserve range and confirm SoC-at-departure meets the route requirement plus margin. | Driver / Charging Ops | Dispatch Supervisor | per dispatch |
| 5 | Define and trigger fallback coverage (diesel/hybrid vehicle or 3PL with green clause W5485) for EV-unavailable, range-insufficient, or peak-day events; track fallback-usage (W5488). | Dispatcher / Fleet | Dispatch Supervisor | per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Oracle Transportation Execution/dispatch (VS-06); network/energy model (W5472); SoH (W5475); charging availability (W5473/W5474); supply planning (VS-02); telematics (VS-06).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Range-miscalculation risk**: under-estimating energy need (e.g., heavy payload + A/C + climb) strands a vehicle mid-route; mitigated by conservative energy-buffer margins and telematics-based re-planning.
- **Fallback-creep risk**: over-reliance on diesel fallback defeats decarbonization; mitigated by the fallback-usage KPI ceiling (W5488) and root-cause on each fallback event.
- **Charger-contention risk**: multiple dispatched vehicles target the same opportunity charger; mitigated by charger-aware dispatch and reservation.

### Automation Opportunity
- SoH/route-aware vehicle-assignment; payload/cube optimization within the EV envelope; charger-aware stop planning with reservation; automated fallback trigger and logging.

### Controls
- CTL-807 (ensure controlled execution — EV / Alt-Fuel Fleet Operations, Charging & Energy Management (PA-192.2).)
- operational: documented energy-buffer margin; fallback-usage ceiling and root-cause policy.

### Time Estimate
- Automated dispatch once configured; ~minutes/dispatcher daily exception-handling.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (Transportation Execution/dispatch), VS-02 (supply planning), W5472 (network/route), W5473/W5474 (charging), W5475 (SoH), W5485 (3PL fallback), W5488 (fallback-usage analytics)

| 2 | Receive, store, and dispense alt-fuel compliantly (storage tanks, BFP Fire Code for LNG/CNG, DENR where applicable — links VS-114); maintain fuel-quality testing (water, sediment, blend ratio, cetane) on receipt. | Facilities / Fuel Manager | Fuel & Fleet Cost Manager | per delivery |
| 3 | Monitor alt-fuel vehicle performance (fuel economy, cold-start, filter-clogging from biodiesel, component compatibility) and adjust maintenance intervals (VS-06.2) accordingly. | Maintenance / Fleet | Fuel & Fleet Cost Manager | monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Fuel/fleet cost (VS-61); procurement (VS-03/VS-34); facilities/storage (VS-138); DG/hazmat if applicable (VS-114); maintenance (VS-06.2); ESG/MRV (W5481/VS-25).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fuel-quality risk**: off-spec biodiesel (water/microbial contamination, wrong blend) clogs filters and strands vehicles; mitigated by receipt quality testing and accredited suppliers.
- **Greenwashing-claim risk**: claiming a GHG reduction without feedstock/certification evidence breaches ESG disclosure integrity (VS-25); mitigated by ISCC/RSB-certified supply and documented lifecycle factors.
- **LNG/CNG-safety risk**: high-pressure gaseous fuel storage carries BFP/DENR and fire risk beyond diesel; mitigated by compliant storage, HSE review (VS-24), and trained operators.

### Automation Opportunity
- Fuel-quality test-result logging with auto-hold on off-spec; alt-fuel GHG auto-calculation from certified lifecycle factors; supplier continuity/supply-risk alerting.

### Controls
- CTL-807 (ensure controlled execution — EV / Alt-Fuel Fleet Operations, Charging & Energy Management (PA-192.2).)
- operational: fuel-supplier TPRM/accreditation; BFP/DENR storage compliance; fuel-quality and sustainability-claim evidence; receipt quality-test mandatory before dispensing.

### Time Estimate
- Per delivery receipt/quality test ~1–2 hours; ongoing monitoring absorbed.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-61 (fuel/fleet cost), VS-03/VS-34 (procurement), VS-138 (storage), VS-114 (DG if applicable), VS-06.2 (maintenance), VS-24 (HSE), VS-25 (ESG claim), W5481 (MRV)

| 3 | Track and verify the renewable-share of fleet charging (kWh-renewable / kWh-charged) to support a credible "renewable-charged fleet" ESG claim (VS-25) and avoid double-counting against the REC accounting (VS-108 W3465). | ESG / Energy Analyst | Facilities & Energy Manager | monthly |
| 4 | Coordinate battery-storage dispatch (charge from solar surplus, discharge to cover charging peak) and manage battery cycle-life economics. | Facilities / Solar O&M | Facilities & Energy Manager | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Solar/BMS/SCADA (VS-108); charge-management (W5473/W5474); energy/tariff/RCOA/WESM (VS-120); REC registry (VS-108 W3465); ESG data (VS-25).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Double-counting risk**: claiming the same renewable kWh for both REC trading/sale (VS-108) and fleet "renewable-charged" marketing breaches integrity; mitigated by clear REC retirement-vs-sale accounting and conservative claims.
- **Seasonal-shortfall risk**: monsoon-season solar shortfall shifts charging to grid/peak tariff, eroding the cost case; mitigated by seasonal planning and grid/fallback.
- **DR-participation risk**: a DR event curtails charging and disrupts dispatch; mitigated by evaluating DR economics and an opt-out/compensation policy.

### Automation Opportunity
- Solar-forecast-aware charging scheduling; automated REC-retirement accounting; tariff/DR-aware load shaping; renewable-share auto-verification for ESG.

### Controls
- CTL-807 (ensure controlled execution — EV / Alt-Fuel Fleet Operations, Charging & Energy Management (PA-192.2).)
- operational: documented REC retirement-vs-sale policy; conservative "renewable-charged" claim threshold.

### Time Estimate
- Automated optimization once configured; ~hours/week for review and seasonal planning.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-108 (solar/REC W3465), VS-120 (energy/RCOA/WESM), VS-138 (facilities), W5473/W5474 (charging), VS-25 (ESG claim), W5481 (MRV)

### Steps

| 2 | Diagnose faults via OBD/EV telematics (fault codes, HV-system warnings, thermal events) and triage to in-house (light), OEM authorized (HV/battery), or roadside/tow. | Maintenance Technician / OEM | Fleet Maintenance Manager | per fault |

- Maintenance/CMMS (VS-06.2/VS-138); EV telematics/OBD (VS-06); OEM service portal; towing/dispatch (VS-56); HSE (VS-24); safety program (W5483).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unqualified-HV-work risk**: untrained technicians working on HV systems face lethal shock and breach DOLE-OSH; mitigated by HV-qualification gating and outsourcing to authorized service.
- **Stranded-vehicle-recovery risk**: improper towing (flat-towing the driven axle) damages an EV drivetrain; mitigated by HV-safe towing protocols and trained towing vendors.
- **Thermal-event risk**: a battery thermal-runaway event is a fire/toxic-gas hazard requiring specialist response; mitigated by the W5483 program, trained responders, and isolation.

### Automation Opportunity
- OBD/fault-code → Oracle eAM auto-ticketing (fit-gap F5) and OEM-authorization routing; HV-qualification/authorization enforcement in work-order assignment; thermal-event auto-alerting to HSE.

### Controls
- CTL-807 (ensure controlled execution — EV / Alt-Fuel Fleet Operations, Charging & Energy Management (PA-192.2).)
- operational: HV-qualification gating before HV work; HV-safe towing protocol mandatory; thermal-event response per W5483.

### Time Estimate
- Per OEM PM schedule; fault diagnostics ~hours/event; roadside/tow per event.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06.2/VS-138 (maintenance/CMMS), VS-19.4 (technician qualification), VS-56/VS-74 (towing/roadside), VS-24 (HSE), W5475 (battery SoH), W5483 (battery-fire/HV safety), W5484 (technician training)
