| Field | Detail |

| **Owner** | ESG/Sustainability Manager (VS-25) |
| **Participants** | Fleet (VS-06), Fuel (VS-61), Charging Ops (W5473), Finance (VS-17.4), External assurance (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aggregate activity data per GHG Protocol Scope 1: diesel/gasoline litres (VS-61), biodiesel/LNG/CNG (W5478), and the residual liquid-fuel share of the owned fleet; capture electricity kWh for EV charging separately (Scope 2, per W5479 renewable-share). | ESG Analyst | ESG/Sustainability Manager | monthly |
| 2 | Apply emission factors (Philippine/IEA grid EF for electricity; fuel-specific EF for liquids; certified lifecycle EF for biodiesel per W5478) consistently with the corporate GHG inventory methodology. | ESG Analyst | ESG/Sustainability Manager | per cycle |
| 3 | Compute actual tCO₂e vs the decarbonization target (W5465) and the ex-ante baseline (W5471); attribute reductions to electrification, alt-fuel, eco-driving (W5476), and route redesign (W5472). | ESG Analyst | ESG/Sustainability Manager | per cycle |
| 4 | Report into the corporate ESG/Scope-1 disclosure (VS-25 W694/W1334) and any voluntary framework (GHG Protocol, TCFD/ISSB, CDP); support external assurance (VS-21). | ESG / Finance | ESG/Sustainability Manager | annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ESG data platform (VS-25); fuel/fleet cost (VS-61); charging/billing (W5473/W5486); carbon-accounting/MRV module; financial records (VS-17); assurance (VS-21).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-gap risk**: incomplete 3PL or telematics data understates Scope 1 and overstates reductions; mitigated by the 3PL green-data clause (W5485) and conservative top-down estimation until complete.
- **Greenwashing-disclosure risk**: overstated reductions in assured ESG disclosure create regulatory/reputational exposure (SEC/VS-173, VS-25); mitigated by conservative EFs, documented methodology, and external assurance.
- **EF-staleness risk**: outdated grid EFs mis-state the electricity Scope 2 benefit; mitigated by periodic EF refresh to current PH/IEA values.

### Automation Opportunity
- Fuel-card/charging → carbon-accounting auto-posting; EF library with auto-refresh; reduction-attribution analytics; audit-ready evidence pack generation.

### Controls
- CTL-808 (ensure controlled execution — Green Fleet Performance, Compliance, Safety & Analytics (PA-192.3).)
- operational: documented GHG Protocol methodology; conservative-EF policy; external assurance for public disclosure.

### Time Estimate
- Automated data aggregation monthly; ~1–2 weeks/annual assured reporting cycle.

## W5483. EV & Alt-Fuel Fleet Safety — High-Voltage, Battery Fire & DOLE-OSH Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | EV/alt-fuel vehicles in service; safety-program establishment; any HV/thermal/fire event |
| **Frequency** | Continuous program; per event |
| **Volume** | Whole green fleet + charging infrastructure + drivers/technicians/first-responders |
| **Owner** | HSE Manager (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | Fleet (VS-06), Facilities (VS-138), Maintenance (W5480), Drivers/technicians, Fire/BFP, emergency responders |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish the EV/alt-fuel fleet safety program under DOLE-OSH (RA 11058) and BFP Fire Code: HV-work permits, LOTO/energy-isolation for HV systems, battery-fire response, and first-responder protocols. | HSE Manager / Fleet | COO (HSE Lead) | per program |
| 2 | Site the charging/alt-fuel infrastructure with BFP-compliant separation, fire-detection/suppression appropriate to lithium-battery fires (not water), ventilation, and emergency shut-off (links W5468). | HSE / Facilities / BFP | COO (HSE Lead) | per site |
| 3 | Train drivers, technicians, and first-responders (links W5484) on HV safety, battery-fire response, and towing/recovery (W5480); maintain competency records. | HSE / L&D | COO (HSE Lead) | per role |
| 4 | Investigate every HV/thermal/fire event to root cause (links VS-24 incident investigation), with corrective/preventive action; report serious incidents to DOLE/BFP per OSH/Fire-Code requirements. | HSE | COO (HSE Lead) | per event |
| 5 | Maintain emergency-response readiness (drills, equipment, mutual-aid with BFP/LGU) and feed systemic issues into the recall path and the OEM/battery vendor (W5467/W5480). | HSE / Fleet | COO (HSE Lead) | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE/incident management (VS-24); facilities/Fire Code (VS-138); maintenance (W5480); L&D/competency (VS-19.4); BFP/DOLE reporting (VS-22).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lithium-battery-fire risk**: a thermal-runaway fire is intense, toxic, and re-igniting, and water suppression is contraindicated; mitigated by appropriate suppression (e.g., specialized agents, containment), separation, and trained response.
- **HV-shock risk**: untrained contact with HV systems is lethal; mitigated by HV-work-permit and LOTO discipline, qualified-only work (W5480), and training.
- **Unprepared-first-responder risk**: municipal responders unfamiliar with EV incidents respond unsafely; mitigated by coordination/drills and OEM emergency-response guides onboard vehicles.

### Automation Opportunity
- Incident-reporting workflow with auto-routing to DOLE/BFP where reportable; HV-work-permit/LOTO enforcement in the CMMS; thermal-event telemetry auto-alerting (W5475/W5480).

### Controls
- CTL-808 (ensure controlled execution — Green Fleet Performance, Compliance, Safety & Analytics (PA-192.3).)
- operational: HV-work-permit/LOTO mandatory; lithium-fire-appropriate suppression; qualified-only HV work; reportable-incident DOLE/BFP notification.

### Time Estimate
- Program establishment ~weeks; per-event investigation ~hours–days; drills absorbed into VS-24.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE/incident), VS-138 (facilities/BFP), VS-19.4 (training), VS-22 (DOLE/BFP reporting), W5468 (BFP-compliant siting), W5480 (HV-safe PM/towing), W5484 (HV-qualification training)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Improper-disposal risk**: landfilling or informal recycling of lithium batteries breaches RA 6969/RA 11898 and creates fire/environmental liability; mitigated by accredited-TSD routing and chain-of-custody.

### Time Estimate
- Per battery disposition decision ~hours–days; chain-of-custody absorbed.

### Cross-references
