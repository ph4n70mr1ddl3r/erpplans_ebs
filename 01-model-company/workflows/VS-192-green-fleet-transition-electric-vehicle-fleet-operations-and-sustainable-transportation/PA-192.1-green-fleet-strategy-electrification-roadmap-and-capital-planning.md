## W5465. Green Fleet Strategy, Emissions Baseline & Decarbonization Target Setting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Owner** | Head of Supply Chain & Logistics (COO org) |
| **Participants** | ESG/Sustainability (VS-25), Fleet Ops (VS-06), Treasury (VS-18), Facilities/Energy (VS-120/VS-138), Real Estate (VS-97) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish the Scope 1 fleet emissions baseline from fuel-consumption records (VS-61), telematics (VS-06), and 3PL carrier data: diesel/gasoline litres, kilometres, load factors, by vehicle class and lane. | Fleet/ESG Analyst | Head of Supply Chain & Logistics | 4–6 weeks/baseline |
| 2 | Set the multi-year decarbonization target (absolute tCO₂e and intensity gCO₂e/tonne-km), aligned to any corporate SBTi/net-zero ambition and the EVIDA fleet-EV-share expectation; define the milestone years (e.g., −X% by 2030). | ESG/Sustainability | Head of Supply Chain & Logistics | per cycle |
| 3 | Define the green-fleet strategy mix: battery-electric (where range/payload fit), plug-in hybrid (transition), biodiesel/B20 (drop-in for existing diesel), LNG/CNG (heavy line-haul if viable), and route/modal shifts; document the "no-regret" early lanes (urban last-mile, store-replenishment shuttles, intra-DC). | Head of Supply Chain & Logistics | COO | per cycle |
| 4 | Identify dependencies and interlocks: charging/alt-fuel infrastructure (W5468), grid/utility capacity (VS-120), own-solar generation (VS-108), EVIDA incentives (W5470), and the 3PL-carrier green clause path (W5485). | Fleet/ESG Analyst | Head of Supply Chain & Logistics | per cycle |
| 5 | Publish the strategy to the board (links VS-25 ESG governance and VS-33 strategy) and feed it into the capital plan (W5471) and the transition roadmap (W5466). | ESG/Sustainability / COO | CEO | per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Fleet/telematics data (VS-06); fuel & cost records (VS-61); ESG data platform (VS-25); carbon-accounting/MRV module (feeds W5481); capital-planning (VS-40/VS-112); strategy/CPM (VS-33).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Baseline-integrity risk**: incomplete 3PL fuel data (80% outsourced fleet) understates Scope 1 and sets a weak target; mitigated by carrier green-data clauses (W5485) and conservative top-down estimation until bottom-up coverage matures.
- **Technology-bet risk**: committing early to a single drivetrain (e.g., full BEV) before PH charging/vehicle supply matures strands capital; mitigated by a hedged mix (hybrid + biodiesel drop-in + targeted BEV) and stage-gated capital (W5471).

### Automation Opportunity
- Fuel-card → ERP → carbon-accounting auto-posting (eliminates manual litre-to-tCO₂e conversion); telematics-driven emissions calculation per trip; baseline-vs-actual dashboard refresh.

### Controls
- CTL-806: baseline data evidence; CTL-02 (capital approval gate); operational: documented methodology consistent with GHG Protocol Corporate Standard, reviewed by ESG/Finance before board submission.

### Time Estimate
- ~6–8 weeks/annual strategy refresh; ~4–6 weeks for the one-time baseline establishment.

### Cross-references

- **Duty-cycle mismatch risk**: a BEV spec'd on average daily-km fails on peak days (peak-season double-runs) and strands a route; mitigated by designing to the p95 duty-cycle, not the mean, and retaining hybrid/fallback vehicles.

### Automation Opportunity
- Telematics-driven readiness scoring (auto-classify each vehicle's daily-km/payload distribution against BEV envelopes); wave-scheduling tooling with infrastructure-readiness interlocks.

### Controls
- CTL-806: asset-register and readiness evidence; CTL-02 (roadmap/capital approval); operational: p95 duty-cycle design rule documented per vehicle class.

### Time Estimate

## W5469. Green Fleet Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), Lifecycle & Capital Investment Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Procurement decision (W5467); annual capital plan; strategy refresh |
| **Frequency** | Per vehicle-class decision; annual model refresh |
| **Volume** | Per decision (vehicle class × ownership mode); ~few-to-tens of decisions/yr |
| **Owner** | Logistics Finance Analyst (VS-17.4/VS-158) |
| **Participants** | Fleet (VS-06), Treasury (VS-18), ESG (VS-25), Procurement (VS-03) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build the TCO model per vehicle class and ownership mode (outright / finance lease / full-service lease): capex, electricity vs diesel cost (VS-61/W5486), maintenance (EV lower, battery replacement reserve), insurance (VS-26), tyres, residual value, and EVIDA incentives (W5470). | Logistics Finance Analyst | CFO / Head of Supply Chain | per decision |
| 2 | Quantify the carbon-cost benefit (tCO₂e avoided × internal/external carbon price) and any ESG-cost-of-capital benefit; include the social cost where the board adopts it. | ESG / Finance | CFO | per decision |
| 3 | Sensitivity-test the model on the high-uncertainty variables (electricity tariff, battery replacement cost, residual value, diesel-price path, incentive availability) and identify break-even vs diesel. | Logistics Finance Analyst | CFO | per decision |
| 4 | Compare ownership modes (own vs lease vs full-service) and make/buy (owned fleet vs 3PL with green clause W5485); recommend the value-maximizing structure. | Finance / Fleet | CFO / COO | per decision |
| 5 | Document the business case for capital governance (W5471) and post-investment benefit tracking (W5488). | Logistics Finance Analyst | CFO | per decision |

### System Touchpoints
- Cost accounting (VS-158); treasury/financing (VS-18); fuel/electricity cost (VS-61/W5486); ESG/carbon data (VS-25); capital planning (VS-40/VS-112).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Residual-value risk**: EV residuals are volatile in a nascent PH market; an optimistic residual understates TCO; mitigated by conservative residuals, buy-back guarantees (W5467), and sensitivity bands.
- **Incentive-dependence risk**: the case relies on EVIDA incentives that may be delayed or curtailed; mitigated by a no-incentive break-even test and treating incentives as upside.
- **Benefit-realization risk**: the modeled savings fail to materialize (e.g., electricity pricier than modeled); mitigated by post-investment tracking (W5488) and corrective action.

### Automation Opportunity
- TCO model auto-fed from actual electricity/fuel/maintenance cost data; sensitivity automation on tariff/battery/residual variables; benefit-realization tracker vs ex-ante case (W5488).

### Controls
- CTL-806: TCO evidence/assumptions documented; CTL-02 (capital approval gate); CTL-33 (post-investment review evidence); operational: no-incentive break-even test mandatory; conservative residual policy.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 weeks/vehicle-class TCO model (first instance); ~2–3 days/refresh thereafter.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-158 (cost), VS-18 (treasury), VS-61 (fuel), VS-25 (ESG/carbon), VS-40/VS-112 (capital/PMO), W5470 (EVIDA incentives), W5471 (capital governance), W5488 (benefit tracking)

| 5 | Re-baseline route KPIs (km/run, on-time, cost/run, gCO₂e/tonne-km), publish the re-designed network, and feed dispatch (W5477) and analytics (W5488). | Network Planner | Network & Transportation Planning Lead | per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Network design/MEIO (VS-136); route optimization in the Route Optimization Engine (in-house build — fit-gap C14; VS-06); supply planning (VS-02); telematics (VS-06); last-mile (VS-06.3/VS-56); store ops (VS-177/VS-07).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Range-anxiety-induced over-provisioning risk**: over-segmenting routes to be range-safe under-utilizes vehicles and erodes the TCO case; mitigated by p95-duty-cycle design and telematics feedback once live.
- **Payload-coverage risk**: reduced EV payload leaves some store-replenishment orders under-covered on a given run; mitigated by frequency/allocations adjustment (VS-02) and fallback coverage.
- **Fallback-creep risk**: routine reliance on diesel fallback defeats the decarbonization goal; mitigated by tracking fallback-usage as a KPI (W5488) with a target ceiling.

### Automation Opportunity
