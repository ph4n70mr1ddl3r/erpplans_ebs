## W5257. Student Biometric Time and Attendance Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily shift schedule starting |
| **Frequency** | Daily per trainee |
| **Volume** | ~400–600 punches/day (in/out for ~200–300 trainees) |
| **Owner** | Store HR Administrator |
| **Participants** | Student Trainee, Department Supervisor, TVI Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scan fingerprint/biometrics at store entrance terminal at shift start/end. | Student Trainee | Store HR Administrator | 10 sec |
| 2 | Review daily attendance exception report (tardiness, absences). | Store HR Administrator | Store HR Administrator | 30 min/day |
| 3 | Verify trainee absence reasons and check doctor notes/excuses. | Department Supervisor | Store HR Administrator | 10 min/incident |
| 4 | Transmit verified attendance logs to TVI Coordinator weekly. | Store HR Administrator | Store HR Administrator | 30 min/week |

### System Touchpoints
- Time & Attendance Terminal, HRIS Module (VS-19).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Biometric bypass fraud risk**: Trainees buddy-punching or manipulating manual time card entries; mitigated by enforcing strict biometric fingerprint/facial scan terminals.

### Automation Opportunity
- Biometric scanner auto-matching shift schedules; push notification alerts for deviations.

### Controls
- CTL-13: payroll/time verification controls; SM review on attendance reports.

### Time Estimate
- ~1.5 hours daily store-level administration

### Cross-references
- links to VS-19 (Hire to Retire), VS-07.4 (store staffing)

## W5258. In-Store Skills Mentoring Trainee Logbook and Output Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily practical session completion |
| **Frequency** | Daily per trainee |
| **Volume** | ~200-300 logs verified daily |
| **Owner** | Store Department Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Student Trainee, Department Mentor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Execute assigned training task (e.g., custom tinting matching, bulk tile receipt checking). | Student Trainee | Store Department Supervisor | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Record completed tasks, learnings, and duration in paper/digital logbook. | Student Trainee | Store Department Supervisor | 15 min |
| 3 | Review trainee output quality and verify logbook entries. | Department Mentor | Store Department Supervisor | 10 min |
| 4 | Sign off logbook sheet confirming competency development. | Store Department Supervisor | Store Department Supervisor | 5 min |

### System Touchpoints
- OLA (VS-19.4), Store Execution module.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Logbook falsification risk**: Trainees log hours/tasks not physically performed; mitigated by random spot checks of signed outputs by store managers.

### Automation Opportunity
- Digital logbook scanning system with photo attachments of completed tasks.

### Controls
- CTL-133 (ensure DTS/TESDA training compliance.)
- operational: supervisor verification control; periodic spot checks by Store Manager.

### Time Estimate
- ~30 min daily per trainee

### Cross-references
- links to VS-19.4 (L&D), VS-07 (store operations)

## W5259. Store Department Rotation and Skills Diversification Scheduling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Completion of current department block (per 30-day block schedule) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly per trainee |
| **Volume** | ~200-300 rotation transitions monthly |
| **Owner** | Store HR Administrator |
| **Participants** | Student Trainee, Store Manager, Department Mentor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review rotation matrix mapping trainees to open department slots (lumber yard, tools, paint). | Store HR Administrator | Store HR Administrator | 1 hour |
| 2 | Assess trainee competency readiness for transition (verified W5258). | Store HR Administrator | Store HR Administrator | 1 hour |
| 3 | Update department assignment flags in HRIS schedule builder. | Store HR Administrator | Store HR Administrator | 30 min |
| 4 | Conduct handover meeting between outgoing and incoming department mentors. | Store HR Administrator | Store HR Administrator | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS Rotation Engine, Scheduling Module (VS-07.4).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Skill gap over-concentration risk**: Trainees spend entire rotation in a single department, missing core skills; mitigated by hard-coded system flags limiting single department stays to max 45 days.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated schedule alerts flagging rotation schedules violating curriculum plans.

### Controls
- CTL-780 (ensure controlled execution — Student-Trainee Lifecycle & Store Rotation (PA-183.2).)
- operational: SM sign-off on rotation lists

### Time Estimate
- ~3 hours monthly per rotation planning

### Cross-references
- links to VS-07.4 (store staffing), VS-19.4 (L&D)

## W5260. Trainee Incident Management OHS Violations and Safety Escalation

| Field | Detail |

| **Trigger** | Workplace safety violation or OHS incident involving trainee |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc per incident |
| **Volume** | ~5-10 escalations annually |
| **Owner** | Store Safety Officer |
| **Participants** | Student Trainee, Department Supervisor, HR-L&D Manager, TVI Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify and contain safety hazard; administer immediate first aid if required per W501. | Store Safety Officer | Store Safety Officer | 30 min |
| 2 | Document safety violation / incident: capture photos, witness statements, and OHS logs. | Store Safety Officer | Store Safety Officer | 2-3 hours |
| 3 | Report incident details to TVI Coordinator and HR-L&D Manager within 24 hours. | Store Safety Officer | Store Safety Officer | 1 hour |
| 4 | Conduct post-incident investigation and draft OHS remediation plan. | Store Safety Officer | Store Safety Officer | 2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Safety Incident Database (VS-24.1), Compliance System.

- **Accident liability exposure risk**: Trainee injuries lead to DOLE OHS citations due to poor documentation; mitigated by logging full incident packages on mobile form within 24 hours.

### Automation Opportunity
- Safety incident notification engine with automatic reporting to TVI partners.

### Controls
- CTL-780: ensure controlled execution — Student-Trainee Lifecycle & Store Rotation (PA-183.2).; OHS safety audit board sign-off.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 days for full report closure

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24.1 (HSE), VS-76 (LGU compliance)

## W5262. Trainee Grievance Handling Harassment Guard and Well-being Check

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Trainee submits grievance request or monthly wellness checkpoint |
| **Frequency** | Monthly check; ad-hoc grievance |
| **Volume** | ~200-300 monthly wellness checks; ~5-10 grievances annually |
| **Owner** | HR Employee Relations Officer |
| **Participants** | Student Trainee, Store HR Coordinator, TVI Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct anonymous monthly well-being check surveys via mobile portal. | HR Employee Relations Officer | HR Employee Relations Officer | 30 min |
| 2 | Review flagged scores (stress, safety concerns, harassment indicators). | Store HR Coordinator | HR Employee Relations Officer | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Conduct confidential investigation interviews if grievance received. | HR Employee Relations Officer | HR Employee Relations Officer | 3-5 hours |
| 4 | Deploy corrective actions and report findings to TVI Coordinator. | HR Employee Relations Officer | HR Employee Relations Officer | 2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Grievance portal (VS-103), HR ER database (VS-19.1).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Retaliation risk**: Trainees fear reporting abuse due to supervisor backlash; mitigated by providing third-party hosted anonymous reporting hotline.

### Automation Opportunity
- Anonymous SMS/web chatbot grievance collection and ticket routing portal.

### Controls
- CTL-153: personnel-data privacy controls per the RA 10173 program (links to VS-91 / W53); HR ER Director formal sign-off.

### Time Estimate
- ~3-5 working days per grievance investigation

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103 (HR shared services), VS-119 (ethics)

### Controls
- CTL-780: ensure controlled execution — Student-Trainee Lifecycle & Store Rotation (PA-183.2).; CHRO sign-off on graduating rosters.

### Time Estimate
