## W5265. DTS Allowance Stipend Calculation Payroll Matching and Disbursement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved attendance sheets (W5257) |
| **Frequency** | Semi-monthly payroll cycle |
| **Volume** | ~400-600 stipend calculations monthly |
| **Owner** | Payroll Analyst |
| **Participants** | Store HR Administrator, Treasury specialist, Bank Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compile approved attendance records and calculate student stipends (typically 75% of minimum wage). | Store HR Administrator | Payroll Analyst | 2-3 hours |
| 2 | Match stipend calculations with non-employee profiles to confirm active program status. | Payroll Analyst | Payroll Analyst | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Generate direct-deposit bank upload file and submit to Treasury. | Payroll Analyst | Payroll Analyst | 1 hour |
| 4 | Approve bank transfer and reconcile stipend disbursement. | Treasury Specialist | Payroll Analyst | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- Payroll Module (VS-19.2), Bank Portal (VS-18).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Overpayment risk**: Stipends paid to terminated/absent students; mitigated by system validation block checking biometric absence records before invoice release.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated time-to-stipend payroll matching tool.

### Controls
- CTL-13: Separate payroll computation from approval; CFO payment approval.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 day per payroll cycle

### Cross-references
- links to VS-19.2 (payroll), VS-18 (treasury)

## W5266. DTS Training Expense Accounting and BIR Deduction Ledgers Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved stipend payroll disbursements (W5265) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly accounting close |
| **Volume** | ~200-300 journal entries monthly |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | Cost Accountant, Chief Accountant (VS-17.1), HR Compliance Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compile all monthly DTS program expenses: stipends, school fees, uniforms, materials. | Cost Accountant | Tax Accountant | 2-3 hours |
| 2 | Classify and post eligible training expenses to dedicated DTS tax ledger accounts. | Tax Accountant | Tax Accountant | 2 hours |
| 3 | Perform double-entry ledger check ensuring no mixing of standard opex and DTS accounts. | Chief Accountant | Tax Accountant | 2 hours |
| 4 | Verify tax deductions tracking matches BIR rules for 50% additional deduction under RA 7686. | Tax Accountant | Tax Accountant | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Tax Ledger (VS-79), GL Module (VS-17.1).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Ineligible expense entry risk**: Mixed ledger accounts trigger BIR tax audit disallowances; mitigated by separate chart of accounts for DTS-eligible expenses.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated tax-credit expense allocation rules in ERP accounting module.

### Controls
- CTL-34: complete tax-compliance controls; Chief Accountant sign-off required.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 days per monthly close

### Cross-references
- links to VS-79 (tax), VS-17 (finance)

## W5269. BIR Certificate of DTS Training Expense Audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual tax close (typically March/April) |
| **Frequency** | Annually during tax season |
| **Volume** | ~5-8 corporate BIR filings audited annually |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | External Tax Auditor, Finance Controller (VS-17.4), Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compile annual DTS tax audit package: ledger receipts, MOA agreements, TESDA accreditations. | Tax Accountant | Tax Accountant | 1-2 days |
| 2 | Audit records against the RA 7686 additional-deduction requirements (50% of DTS system expenses, capped at 5% of direct labor cost) claimed in the annual income tax return. | External Tax Auditor | Tax Accountant | 2-3 days |
| 3 | Submit audited package to BIR during annual income tax return filing. | Tax Accountant | Tax Accountant | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Track BIR clearance verification and save final assessment certificate. | Tax Accountant | Tax Accountant | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Tax module (VS-79), Document System (VS-88).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Deduction rejection risk**: BIR disallows DTS deductions due to missing documentation; mitigated by storing all compliance records in secure vault with direct audit access.

### Automation Opportunity
- Direct export file creation for BIR audit package generation.

### Controls
- CTL-34: Complete tax compliance controls; CFO approval required on returns.

### Time Estimate
- ~30-45 days audit duration

### Cross-references
- links to VS-79 (tax), VS-88 (document control)
