## W5249. TESDA DTS Dual Training Accreditation and Site Inspection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategic L&D plan and TVI alignment target (VS-19.4) |
| **Frequency** | Annually per store cluster |
| **Volume** | ~10-15 accreditation inspections annually |
| **Owner** | HR-L&D Manager |
| **Participants** | TVI Coordinator, TESDA Regional Inspector, Facilities Standards Manager (VS-138) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Submit DTS accreditation application to local TESDA Provincial Office. | HR-L&D Manager | HR-L&D Manager | 2-3 hours |
| 2 | Coordinate TESDA physical site inspection of store/DC training facilities. | Facilities Standards Manager | HR-L&D Manager | 2-4 hours |
| 3 | Verify OHS standards, training tools, and supervisor qualifications against TESDA guidelines. | TESDA Regional Inspector | HR-L&D Manager | 2-3 hours |
| 4 | Secure DTS Certificate of Accreditation and upload to compliance library. | HR-L&D Manager | HR-L&D Manager | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- HR Compliance Tracker, TESDA Document Library.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Accreditation lapse risk**: Delayed accreditation blocks student deployment and halts tax incentives; mitigated by initiating renewals 90 days before expiry.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-41: LGU/agency permit renewal alert engine; HR-L&D Manager formal sign-off.

### Time Estimate
- ~15-30 days for inspection and certification issuance

### Cross-references
- links to VS-19.4 (L&D), VS-138 (facilities)

| **Owner** | HR Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | TVI Director, VP Legal (VS-100), Chief Accountant (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vet candidate TVIs (technical-vocational schools) for curriculum alignment and TESDA status. | HR Compliance Manager | HR Compliance Manager | 2-3 hours |
