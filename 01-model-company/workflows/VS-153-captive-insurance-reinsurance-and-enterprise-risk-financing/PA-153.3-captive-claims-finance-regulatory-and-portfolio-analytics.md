## W4545. Captive Claims Administration & TPA Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Claim event (VS-26.3); TPA cycle |
| **Frequency** | Per claim + ongoing |
| **Volume** | Claims across in-scope captive programs (property/cat, liability, fleet, cyber) |
| **Owner** | Insurance (VS-26.3) / Risk / TPA (W4534) |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), Finance (VS-17), Claimants, Fronting carrier |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TPA/Insurance administer captive claims (FNOL, investigation, adjustment, settlement) per the commercial claims process (VS-26.3), routing captive-layer claims to the captive | TPA / Insurance | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Insurance Manager | Per claim |
| 2 | Risk/Legal manage large/complex captive claims (cat, liability litigation VS-100, cyber VS-27.3), and approve settlements within authority | Risk / Legal | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Captive Board | Per claim |
| 3 | Finance/TPA pay claims from captive funds, record loss data (W4551), update reserves (W4542), and trigger reinsurance recovery (W4546) above retention | Finance / TPA / Actuarial | CFO / Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Per claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Claims (VS-26.3); legal (VS-100.3); finance/GL (VS-17); cyber (VS-27.3); reserves (W4542); loss data (W4551)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Claims-quality risk**: Poor TPA handling → claimant harm/litigation; mitigated by TPA SLA & oversight (W4534)
- **Authority-breach risk**: Settlement beyond authority; mitigated by authority schedule & escalation

### Automation Opportunity
- Claims-administration system; TPA SLA dashboards; loss-data capture automation

### Controls
- CTL-691 (ensure controlled execution — Captive Claims, Finance, Regulatory & Portfolio Analytics (PA-153.3).)
- CTL-691: claims handled per VS-26.3 standard; TPA SLA-monitored (W4534); settlements within authority; reserves updated (W4542)

### Time Estimate
- Per claim lifecycle; large claims months+

### Cross-references
- links to VS-26.3 (claims), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-17 (finance), W4534 (TPA), W4542 (reserves), W4546 (reinsurance recovery)

## W4546. Captive Reinsurance Recovery & Collections

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Loss above retention (W4545); treaty bound (W4540) |
| **Frequency** | Per recoverable event + ongoing |
| **Volume** | Reinsurance recoveries/collectibles across treaties |
| **Owner** | Risk / Treasury (VS-18) / Finance (VS-17) |
| **Participants** | Broker, Reinsurers, Actuarial, Captive Board |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk/Broker report losses/bordereaux to reinsurers per treaty (W4540), and submit reinsurance recovery claims above the captive retention | Risk / Broker | Head of Internal Audit & Risk | Per event |
| 2 | Treasury/Finance collect reinsurance recoveries from reinsurers, reconcile to submissions, and pursue overdue/aged balances | Treasury / Finance | CFO | Per event |
| 3 | Actuarial/Risk monitor reinsurance recoverable on the balance sheet, assess reinsurer default risk, and report collection health to the captive board | Actuarial / Risk / Treasury | Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Captive Board | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Reinsurance (W4540); treasury (VS-18); finance/GL (VS-17); claims (W4545); broker (W4534)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reinsurer-default risk**: Reinsurer fails to pay a cat recovery; mitigated by security-approved reinsurers (W4540) & monitoring
- **Reconcile-error risk**: Recoveries ≠ submissions; mitigated by bordereaux reconciliation

### Automation Opportunity
- Bordereaux/recovery automation; reinsurer-collectible aging; recoverable-on-balance dashboards

### Controls
- CTL-691 (ensure controlled execution — Captive Claims, Finance, Regulatory & Portfolio Analytics (PA-153.3).)
- CTL-691: recoveries submitted per treaty (W4540); reinsurers security-monitored; collectibles reconciled & aged

### Time Estimate
- Per event + ongoing reconciliation

### Cross-references
- links to W4540 (reinsurance), VS-18 (treasury), VS-17 (finance), W4545 (claims), W4534 (broker)

| **Frequency** | Annual |

| **Owner** | Audit (VS-21) / Captive Board Audit Committee |
| **Participants** | External auditor, Appointed actuary, Finance (VS-17), Compliance (VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audit/Finance run the captive external audit (statutory financial statements, controls), integrated to the group audit (VS-21) | Audit / Finance | Captive Board Audit Committee | Annual |
| 2 | Appointed Actuary issues the actuarial opinion on reserve adequacy (W4542) per domicile requirement, with the statement of actuarial opinion | Actuary / Actuarial | Captive Board | Annual |
| 3 | Captive Board/Head of Internal Audit & Risk act on audit/actuarial findings, report to the group board/audit committee (VS-36), and remediate issues | Captive Board / Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Audit | Captive Board Audit Committee / Group Board | Annual |

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Adverse-opinion risk**: Actuarial/audit adverse finding → regulatory/reputational impact; mitigated by prior remediation & reserve adequacy (W4542)
- **Finding-backlog risk**: audit and actuarial findings accepted at the board but remediating on no clock; mitigated by the Captive Board and Head of Internal Audit & Risk remediation ownership with group-board reporting (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Audit-evidence/finding management; reserve-adequacy support; actuarial-opinion document workflow

### Controls
- CTL-691 (ensure controlled execution — Captive Claims, Finance, Regulatory & Portfolio Analytics (PA-153.3).)
- CTL-691: captive externally audited (VS-21); actuarial opinion obtained; findings remediated & board-reported (VS-36)

### Time Estimate

## W4552. Captive Performance Review, Run-Off & Strategic Options

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual review; strategic shift; underperformance |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on trigger |
| **Volume** | Captive performance review & strategic-options assessment |
| **Owner** | CFO / Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Captive Board / Strategy (VS-33) |
| **Participants** | Risk (VS-21.2), Treasury (VS-18), Legal (VS-100.3), M&A (VS-130) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CFO/Head of Internal Audit & Risk/Strategy review captive performance vs the W4529 business-case success metrics (cost-of-risk reduction, combined ratio, capital efficiency), and the strategic rationale | CFO / Head of Internal Audit & Risk / Strategy | Board | Annual |
| 2 | CFO/Strategy/Captive Board evaluate strategic options (continue/expand, restructure scope, run-off/commute, redomesticate, divest) if performance/rationale changes | CFO / Strategy / Captive Board / Legal | Board | On trigger |
| 3 | Captive Board/CFO execute the chosen path (expand W4530, run-off W4541 commutation, or wind-down via VS-130/M&A-type transaction), reporting to the board (VS-36) | Captive Board / CFO / Legal | Board | Per decision |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy (VS-33); risk (VS-21.2); treasury (VS-18); legal (VS-100.3); M&A (VS-130); captive governance (W4533)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Sunk-cost bias**: Persisting with an underperforming captive; mitigated by success-metric review & options assessment
- **Run-off-complexity risk**: Wind-down leaves long-tail liabilities; mitigated by commutation/run-off planning (W4541) & legal rigor

### Automation Opportunity
- Performance-vs-baseline dashboards; strategic-options modeling; run-off/commutation valuation

### Controls
- CTL-691 (ensure controlled execution — Captive Claims, Finance, Regulatory & Portfolio Analytics (PA-153.3).)
- CTL-691: captive performance reviewed vs success metrics (W4529); strategic-options assessed; wind-down via VS-130/M&A discipline if chosen

### Time Estimate
- ~annual review + options-assessment weeks

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-21.2 (risk), VS-18 (treasury), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-130 (M&A), W4529 (business case), W4541 (commutation)
