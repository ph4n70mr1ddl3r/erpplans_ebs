## W4633. Standalone Selling Price (SSP) Methodology & Estimation Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New multi-element arrangement; annual SSP refresh; market-price shift |
| **Frequency** | Per new obligation + annual/bi-annual refresh |
| **Volume** | SSP for ~ installation service, design service, warranty, loyalty points, subscription, bundle components, retail-media placement |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Pricing (VS-01.2), Merchandising (VS-01), Services (VS-12/VS-66), Analytics (VS-28), Controller, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the SSP hierarchy/methodology per obligation type: (a) observable standalone price first (when the obligation is also sold separately — e.g., extended warranty, some installation); (b) market-adjusted assessment; (c) residual approach only when permitted; (d) expected-cost-plus-margin for obligations never sold separately (installation bundled only) | Revenue Accounting / Pricing / Services | Controller | Per methodology |
| 2 | Maintain the SSP register with per-obligation SSP, data source, refresh date, and estimation variables (e.g., installation labor rate, warranty expected cost + margin); refresh on the cadence and on material market shifts (commodity/labor — links VS-106/VS-102) | Revenue Accounting / Analytics | Controller | Annual + on change |
| 3 | Submit the SSP methodology and any judgment-heavy estimates to the External Auditor for review at year-end (and pre-launch of new arrangements); document conclusions and retain evidence | Revenue Accounting / Controller / External Auditor | CFO | Annual + per launch |

### System Touchpoints
- SSP register (AR Revenue Management — fit-gap A11); pricing/cost masters (VS-01.2/VS-158); analytics (VS-28); audit evidence repository (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Judgment-heavy SSP risk**: Obligations never sold separately (installation, design) require expected-cost-plus-margin estimates the auditor may challenge → prior-period adjustment; mitigated by documented methodology and early auditor engagement
- **Stale-SSP risk**: An SSP not refreshed after a labor/commodity shift mis-allocates revenue; mitigated by the refresh cadence and market-shift trigger

### Automation Opportunity
- SSP register auto-fed from observable separate-sales data; market/labor-cost shift alerts triggering review; allocation-engine consumption of SSP

### Controls
- CTL-702 (ensure controlled execution — Standalone Selling Price, Allocation & Deferred-Revenue Measurement (PA-157.2).)
- CTL-702: documented SSP hierarchy; SSP register versioned and Controller-approved; judgment estimates auditor-reviewed

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 days annual SSP refresh; ~1–2 days per new-obligation SSP determination

### Cross-references
- links to VS-01.2/VS-158 (pricing/cost), VS-12/VS-66 (services), VS-106/VS-102 (cost inputs), W4634 (allocation), W4644 (audit)

## W4634. Transaction-Price Allocation Across Performance Obligations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Each multi-element sale (bundle/kit, product+install, product+warranty+points); SSP refresh |
| **Frequency** | Per transaction (system) + periodic review |
| **Volume** | Every bundle/kit sale (VS-92); every product+installation sale (VS-12); points earned on every POS sale |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Merchandising (VS-92), Services (VS-12), IT (VS-27/VS-113), Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Configure the allocation engine: distribute the transaction price across each performance obligation based on relative SSP (W4633); apply any allocation-specific discount to the obligations to which it relates | Revenue Accounting / IT | Controller / CIO | Per setup |
| 2 | Validate allocation on sample transactions (bundle, product+install, product+warranty+points) — confirm the sum of allocations equals transaction price and each obligation's allocation is within SSP range; investigate out-of-range allocations | Revenue Accounting / Analytics | Controller | Quarterly sample |
| 3 | Post each allocation to the correct revenue/deferred-revenue GL by obligation and period; route exceptions (manual override, non-standard contract) to Revenue Accounting review | Revenue Accounting / IT | Controller | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Allocation engine (AR Revenue Management — fit-gap A11); kit/BOM (VS-92.1); service work order (W544); loyalty engine (VS-13); GL (VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-allocation risk**: A bug or stale SSP in the allocation engine mis-states revenue across obligations and periods at scale (every POS/bundle transaction); mitigated by sample validation and SSP freshness
- **Discount-allocation error risk**: A bundle discount not allocated to the obligations to which it relates distorts per-obligation revenue; mitigated by the relative-SSP rule and exception review

### Automation Opportunity
- Relative-SSP auto-allocation at POS/order; out-of-range exception report; allocation audit trail for every transaction

### Controls
- CTL-702 (ensure controlled execution — Standalone Selling Price, Allocation & Deferred-Revenue Measurement (PA-157.2).)
- CTL-702: allocation engine Controller-approved; sample-tested quarterly; SSP within range; exceptions reviewed

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 days/quarter allocation validation + sample testing; per-setup ~2–4 days

### Cross-references
- links to W4633 (SSP), VS-92 (kit), VS-12 (install), VS-13 (points), W544 (work order), W4648 (controls)

## W4635. Gift-Card Breakage Estimation, Recognition & Remote-Redemption Testing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly gift-card liability review (VS-54); annual breakage recalc |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + annual comprehensive |
| **Volume** | Outstanding gift-card liability (VS-54 W28); ~ gift cards sold/redeemed monthly |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Gift-card ops (VS-54), Analytics (VS-28), Controller, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compute the historical redemption pattern (aging of unredeemed balances, redemption curve) from gift-card transaction history; estimate the breakage rate (portion expected never to be redeemed) | Revenue Accounting / Analytics | Controller | Annual + quarterly |
| 2 | Recognize breakage revenue ONLY when the likelihood of redemption becomes remote (per PFRS 15 ¶B43) — proportionally based on the redemption pattern, not on a fixed time; document the remote-redemption determination | Revenue Accounting | Controller / CFO | Monthly |
| 3 | Reconcile the gift-card liability GL (W28) to the gift-card outstanding-balance feed; post breakage revenue journal during month-end close (W9A); re-assess the estimate with full-year data at year-end | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Monthly + annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Gift-card module (VS-54 W28); GL contract-liability (VS-17.1); analytics (VS-28); month-end close (W9A)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Premature-breakage risk**: Recognizing breakage before redemption is remote overstates revenue (aggressive accounting) — a classic audit finding; mitigated by the remote-redemption test and proportionate recognition
- **Limited-history risk**: BuildRight's gift-card program is relatively new → breakage estimate rests on thin data; mitigated by conservative estimation and auditor review

### Automation Opportunity
- Redemption-curve auto-computation; breakage candidate-list with remote-redemption flag; auto-reconcile liability GL to feed

### Controls
- CTL-702 (ensure controlled execution — Standalone Selling Price, Allocation & Deferred-Revenue Measurement (PA-157.2).)
- CTL-702: breakage recognized only at remote-redemption (proportionate); methodology auditor-reviewed; liability GL reconciled monthly

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 days/month gift-card liability + breakage accounting; ~3–5 days annual recalc

### Cross-references
- links to VS-54 (gift card), W28 (liability), W4636 (analogous loyalty breakage), W4641 (close), W4643 (disclosure)

## W4636. Loyalty-Points Liability Measurement, Fair-Value & Forfeiture Adjustment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly points-accounting cycle; redemption-rate drift |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly estimate + monthly release |
| **Volume** | Points earned on 2.8M monthly POS transactions (600K members); points liability GL |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Loyalty (VS-13), Analytics (VS-28), Actuarial support, Controller, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Determine the per-point fair value (standalone selling price of points) and the redemption-rate/forfeiture estimate; refresh quarterly using actual redemption behavior and tier mix | Revenue Accounting / Analytics | Controller / CFO | Quarterly |
| 2 | Recognize deferred revenue to revenue as points are redeemed; for points expected to forfeit, recognize the breakage-style portion when redemption is remote (mirroring W4635); true-up cumulative catch-up each period | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Reconcile the points-deferred-revenue GL to the loyalty-engine liability (W104/W17) monthly; investigate redemption-rate drift > threshold and re-estimate | Revenue Accounting / Loyalty ops | Controller | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Loyalty engine (VS-13 W17/W104); POS earning feed (VS-08); deferred-revenue GL (VS-17.1); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Estimation-volatility risk**: Redemption-rate shifts swing the liability and period revenue; mitigated by quarterly true-up and documented methodology
- **Engine-to-GL drift risk**: (as W4631) mitigated by monthly reconcile

### Automation Opportunity
- Redemption-triggered auto-release; engine-to-GL auto-reconcile; drift-alert triggering re-estimation

### Controls
- CTL-702 (ensure controlled execution — Standalone Selling Price, Allocation & Deferred-Revenue Measurement (PA-157.2).)
- CTL-702: documented fair-value/redemption methodology (auditor-reviewed); monthly engine-to-GL reconcile; quarterly true-up

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days/quarter estimate + ~1 day/month release/reconcile

### Cross-references

## W4637. Subscription & Extended-Warranty Deferred-Revenue Amortization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Subscription/warranty sale (VS-47/VS-53); monthly amortization cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly amortization + per sale |
| **Volume** | Subscription contracts (VS-47); extended-warranty contracts (VS-53); growing base |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Subscription/Warranty ops (VS-47/VS-53), Controller, Tax (VS-79) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For subscription services (VS-47) and extended warranties (VS-53), recognize revenue over the coverage/service period (straight-line unless usage-based is more faithful); defer the full consideration at sale | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Per sale + monthly |
| 2 | Run the monthly deferred-revenue amortization schedule (per contract, by period); post the amortization journal during close (W9A); handle renewals, cancellations, and refunds as estimate updates (W4638) | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Reconcile the subscription/warranty deferred-revenue GL to the active-contract schedule; investigate contracts with no amortization or abnormal patterns | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Subscription module (VS-47); Install Base + Service Contracts (VS-53 W33, fit-gap D16); deferred-revenue schedule (AR Revenue Management — fit-gap A11); GL (VS-17.1); month-end close (W9A)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Period-mismatch risk**: Recognizing subscription/warranty revenue up-front (rather than over the period) overstates period revenue; mitigated by the deferred-amortization rule
- **Renewal/cancellation handling risk**: Mis-handling mid-period cancellations/renewals distorts the schedule; mitigated by estimate-update routing (W4638)

### Automation Opportunity
- Per-contract amortization schedule auto-generated; auto-post during close; exception report for abnormal patterns

### Controls
- CTL-702 (ensure controlled execution — Standalone Selling Price, Allocation & Deferred-Revenue Measurement (PA-157.2).)
- CTL-702: revenue deferred and amortized over the coverage period; schedule reconciled to GL monthly; cancellations routed to estimate-update

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 days/month amortization + reconcile

### Cross-references
- links to VS-47 (subscription), VS-53 (warranty), W33 (warranty liability), W4638 (variable consideration), W4641 (close)

## W4638. Variable Consideration, Constraint & Estimate-Update (Rebates, Refunds, SC/PWD, Price-Freeze)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Variable consideration in a contract (rebates, refunds, volume discounts, SC/PWD mandatory discounts, price-freeze, trade-in top-up); quarterly estimate update |
| **Frequency** | Per contract + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Vendor rebates (VS-39), SC/PWD discounts (VS-85), price-freeze (DTI), refunds/returns (~0.04% return rate — the ~40/day AR-credit-memo canon), trade-in variance (VS-155) |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade/Government ops (VS-11/VS-46), Rebate ops (VS-39), Tax (VS-79/VS-85), Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify variable consideration in each contract type; estimate the amount using the expected-value or most-likely-amount method; apply the constraint (recognize only to the extent a significant revenue reversal is NOT highly probable when the uncertainty resolves) | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Per contract type |
| 2 | Update estimates each reporting period; record cumulative catch-up adjustments in the period of estimate change; coordinate with the relevant operational flow (rebate accrual VS-39, SC/PWD VS-85, refund liability VS-32) | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Quarterly |
| 3 | Document the constraint assessment for judgment-heavy variable consideration (e.g., large project volume rebates, trade-in buy-back variance VS-155); retain evidence for audit | Revenue Accounting / Controller | CFO | Quarterly + annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Rebate module (VS-39); returns/refund liability (VS-32); SC/PWD discount (VS-85); trade-in (VS-155); AR Revenue Management variable-consideration ledger (fit-gap A11); GL (VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reversal risk**: Recognizing variable consideration too aggressively → significant revenue reversal when the uncertainty resolves (audit finding); mitigated by the constraint test
- **Estimate-staleness risk**: Out-of-date estimates mis-state period revenue; mitigated by quarterly update and cumulative catch-up

### Automation Opportunity
- Expected-value estimation from historical data; constraint-test checklist; cumulative catch-up auto-posting

### Controls
- CTL-702 (ensure controlled execution — Standalone Selling Price, Allocation & Deferred-Revenue Measurement (PA-157.2).)
- CTL-702: constraint applied (no highly-probable reversal); estimates updated quarterly; judgment-heavy items Controller/CFO-reviewed

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days/quarter variable-consideration update across contract types

### Cross-references
- links to VS-39 (rebate), VS-85 (SC/PWD), VS-32 (refunds), VS-155 (trade-in), W4641 (close), W4643 (disclosure)

## W4639. Significant-Financing-Component & Advance/Prepayment Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Long-term project with deferred payment (VS-11/VS-46); large advance/prepayment (VS-15 W498); annual review |
| **Frequency** | Per significant contract + annual |
| **Volume** | Project/G2G deferred-payment contracts; vendor advance payments (W498); subscription prepayments |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade/Government ops (VS-11/VS-46), Treasury (VS-18), AP (VS-15), Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For contracts where payment timing differs significantly from delivery (e.g., project retainage over 12+ months, large customer deposits, long prepayment cycles), assess whether a significant financing component exists (>12 months and material) | Revenue Accounting / Treasury | Controller | Per contract |
| 2 | Where a significant financing component exists, adjust the transaction price for the time value of money (discount); separate interest revenue/expense; document the discount-rate rationale | Revenue Accounting / Treasury | Controller / CFO | Per contract |
| 3 | For the practical expedients (no financing component if ≤12 months, or if consideration is variable and the financing element is indeterminate), document the basis; align with Treasury's discount-rate policy (VS-18) | Revenue Accounting / Treasury | Controller | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Project module (VS-11.2); AP prepayment (W498); Treasury discount-rate policy (VS-18); AR Revenue Management (fit-gap A11); GL (VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missed-financing-component risk**: Failing to discount a long deferred-payment contract mis-states revenue and omits interest — audit finding; mitigated by the >12-month/materiality screen
- **Discount-rate judgment risk**: Inappropriate discount rate distorts the adjustment; mitigated by Treasury alignment and documented rationale

### Automation Opportunity
- Financing-component screen on contracts with payment-delivery gap >12 months; discount-rate feed from Treasury policy

### Controls
- CTL-702 (ensure controlled execution — Standalone Selling Price, Allocation & Deferred-Revenue Measurement (PA-157.2).)
- CTL-702: >12-month/materiality screen applied; discount rate Treasury-aligned and documented; practical expedients basis recorded

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days/quarter assessment; per-contract ~2–4 hours when triggered

### Cross-references
- links to VS-11.2/VS-46.2 (project/G2G), W498 (prepayment), VS-18 (treasury/discount), W4643 (disclosure)

## W4640. Contract Costs — Cost-to-Obtain & Cost-to-Fulfill (Capitalization, Amortization & Impairment)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Incremental contract-acquisition cost (sales commission); cost-to-fulfill (setup); annual impairment review |
| **Frequency** | Per contract + annual impairment |
| **Volume** | Sales commissions (VS-11/VS-107 trade & key-account); fulfillment setup costs (subscription/marketplace onboarding) |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Sales (VS-11/VS-107), HR/Comp (VS-102), Controller, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify incremental costs to obtain a contract (e.g., sales commissions tied to winning a trade/key-account/G2G contract) and capitalize; expense other acquisition costs as incurred | Revenue Accounting / Sales / Comp | Controller | Per contract |
| 2 | Identify costs to fulfill that generate/enhance resources used to satisfy obligations (e.g., subscription/marketplace onboarding setup) and capitalize; amortize both cost categories in proportion to revenue recognition (or straight-line if more faithful); apply the ≤12-month practical expedient for commissions where eligible | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Per period |
| 3 | Assess impairment of capitalized contract costs each period (expected economic benefits no longer expected); coordinate amortization with the sales-comp accrual (W228 trade commissions) and reconcile the asset GL | Revenue Accounting / Comp | Controller | Quarterly + annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Commission module (W228); contract-cost asset GL (VS-17.1); amortization schedule; impairment review (AR Revenue Management — fit-gap A11)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-capitalization risk**: Capitalizing costs that don't meet the incremental/enhancing test inflates the asset and defers expense; mitigated by the cost-classification criteria
- **Impairment miss risk**: Failing to impair a capitalized cost whose contract is lost/curtailed → overstated asset; mitigated by the periodic impairment review tied to contract status

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-amortization in proportion to revenue recognition; impairment trigger on contract-status change (lost/curtailed); commission-to-contract-cost linking

### Controls
- CTL-702 (ensure controlled execution — Standalone Selling Price, Allocation & Deferred-Revenue Measurement (PA-157.2).)
- CTL-702: capitalization criteria applied; amortization aligned to revenue pattern; impairment reviewed quarterly; asset GL reconciled

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 days/quarter contract-cost amortization + impairment review

### Cross-references
- links to VS-11/VS-107 (sales), W228 (commissions), VS-102 (comp), VS-47/VS-95 (subscription/marketplace), W4643 (disclosure)
