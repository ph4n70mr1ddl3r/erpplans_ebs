## W4641. Monthly/Quarterly Revenue Close, Reassessment & Journal-Entry Cycle

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Month-end close (W9A); quarter-end; revenue-estimate update |
| **Frequency** | Monthly (close) + quarterly (comprehensive reassessment) |
| **Volume** | 5 entities × 12 months = 60 monthly closes; ~10–15 complex scenarios/quarter |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Controller, GL/Close (VS-17.1), channel owners, Tax (VS-79) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | During month-end close (W9A), post the recurring PFRS 15 journals: gift-card breakage, points deferred-revenue release, subscription/warranty amortization, contract-cost amortization, variable-consideration estimate updates, principal-vs-agent net/gross postings | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Per close |
| 2 | Reassess estimates quarterly (SSP, breakage, redemption, variable consideration); post cumulative catch-ups; verify the allocation engine and deferral schedules across all entities | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Quarterly |
| 3 | Tie total recognized revenue to the GL revenue accounts and to the channel subledgers; investigate any unreconciled variance before close sign-off | Revenue Accounting / GL | Controller | Per close |

### System Touchpoints
- Month-end close (W9A); RM&I revenue journals (fit-gap A11); GL (VS-17.1); channel subledgers; allocation engine (W4634)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cut-off risk**: PFRS 15 journals missed or posted in the wrong period distort the close; mitigated by the close checklist and tie-out to channel subledgers
- **Variance-hiding risk**: A GL-subledger variance closed without investigation embeds an error; mitigated by mandatory variance clearance before sign-off

### Automation Opportunity
- Recurring PFRS 15 journal template with auto-post during close; variance dashboard blocking close sign-off

### Controls
- CTL-703 (ensure controlled execution — Period Close, Disclosure, Audit & New-Revenue-Stream Onboarding (PA-157.3).)
- CTL-703: close checklist; cumulative catch-ups posted quarterly; GL-subledger variance cleared before sign-off

### Time Estimate
- ~4–7 days/quarter (close + reassessment), absorbed in Revenue Accounting + Controller

### Cross-references
- extends W487 (VS-15.1) steps 3–6; links to W9A (close), W4642 (year-end), W4647 (reconcile)

## W4642. Year-End Comprehensive PFRS 15 Review, Policy Refresh & Recalculation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Year-end close (W9B); annual audit |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | 1 enterprise review across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager / Controller |
| **Participants** | CFO, channel owners, Tax (VS-79), External Auditor, Legal (VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review all PFRS 15 accounting policies for continued appropriateness (principal-vs-agent, over-time/point-in-time, SSP methodology, breakage, variable consideration, financing component, contract costs); document any policy refresh | Controller / Revenue Accounting | CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Recalculate breakage rates, SSP allocations, redemption estimates, and deferred-revenue balances using full-year data; assess any new streams launched during the year (W4645) for PFRS 15 treatment | Revenue Accounting / Analytics | Controller | Annual |
| 3 | Coordinate with the External Auditor on the PFRS 15 audit approach and sampling (W4644); finalize the AFS disclosure note (W4643) and obtain CFO sign-off | Controller / Revenue Accounting / External Auditor | CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Year-end close (W9B); analytics (VS-28); RM&I (fit-gap A11); disclosure schedule (W4643); audit evidence (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Policy-drift risk**: A policy not refreshed after a business change becomes stale; mitigated by the annual review
- **Prior-period adjustment risk**: A recalculation that materially differs from prior quarters triggers a prior-period adjustment; mitigated by early auditor engagement

### Automation Opportunity
- Full-year recalculation of breakage/SSP/redemption from historical data; policy-refresh checklist

### Controls
- CTL-703 (ensure controlled execution — Period Close, Disclosure, Audit & New-Revenue-Stream Onboarding (PA-157.3).)
- CTL-703: policies reviewed annually; recalculations Controller/CFO-approved; disclosure note CFO-signed

### Time Estimate
- ~5–7 days/year comprehensive review (extends W487 step 7)

### Cross-references
- extends W487 (VS-15.1) step 7; links to W9B (year-end), W4643 (disclosure), W4644 (audit)

## W4643. Revenue Disaggregation, Disclosure Schedule & AFS Note Preparation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarter-end disclosure pack; year-end AFS |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly (interim) + annual (AFS) |
| **Volume** | 1 disclosure schedule; multiple disaggregation dimensions |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Controller, CFO, External Auditor, Financial Reporting (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare the revenue disaggregation schedule: revenue by (a) nature/timing (goods, services, over-time, point-in-time), (b) channel (POS, ecommerce, trade, project, G2G, retail media, marketplace, VAS, subscription), (c) geography/region, (d) entity — per PFRS 15 ¶114 and SEC/PFRS presentation | Revenue Accounting / Financial Reporting | Controller | Per period |
| 2 | Prepare the contract-balance disclosure: contract assets (recognized-but-unbilled), contract liabilities (deferred revenue, customer deposits), and significant judgments (SSP, breakage, variable-consideration constraint, financing component); include the revenue-reconciliation (opening/closing deferred revenue, recognized from prior-period deferred) | Revenue Accounting | Controller / CFO | Per period |
| 3 | Roll the schedule into the AFS revenue note (year-end) and interim disclosure pack; obtain External Auditor review and CFO sign-off; retain evidence (VS-88) | Revenue Accounting / Financial Reporting / External Auditor | CFO | Annual + quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Financial reporting (VS-17.4); RM&I revenue disaggregation (fit-gap A11); GL contract balances (VS-17.1); records retention (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Disaggregation-error risk**: Inconsistent channel/geography mapping distorts the disclosure; mitigated by the register-to-GL mapping (W4625)
- **Judgment-disclosure risk**: Omitting a significant judgment (e.g., financing component) draws an audit/SEC finding; mitigated by the judgment checklist

### Automation Opportunity
- Disaggregation auto-roll from the revenue-stream register; contract-balance disclosure auto-template; judgment checklist

### Controls
- CTL-703 (ensure controlled execution — Period Close, Disclosure, Audit & New-Revenue-Stream Onboarding (PA-157.3).)
- CTL-703: disaggregation reconciled to GL/register; judgment checklist complete; note CFO-signed and auditor-reviewed

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 days/quarter interim pack; ~5–8 days annual AFS note

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.4 (financial reporting), W4625 (register), W4647 (contract balances), VS-88 (records)

| **Participants** | External Auditor, CFO, channel owners, Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engage the External Auditor early on the PFRS 15 audit plan, risk areas (judgment-heavy: SSP, breakage, over-time measure, principal-vs-agent), and the sampling approach; agree the population (revenue-stream register W4625) | Controller / Revenue Accounting / External Auditor | CFO | Audit planning |
| 2 | Provide auditor-requested evidence: contracts, SSP methodology and data, allocation samples, deferral/breakage calculations, principal-vs-agent analyses, disclosure support; respond to queries | Revenue Accounting / Channel Owners | Controller | During audit |

### Controls

## W4645. New-Revenue-Stream / New-Channel Pre-Launch PFRS 15 Assessment & Accounting Decision

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Proposed new revenue stream / new channel (e.g., a new Pass-17-style service, new marketplace, new subscription) |
| **Frequency** | Per new stream |
| **Volume** | ~2–5 new streams/yr (recent growth: retail media, marketplace, VAS, captive, construction finance, trade-in) |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | Channel owner, Strategy (VS-33), Legal (VS-100.3), Tax (VS-79), IT (VS-27/VS-113), Controller, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each proposed new stream, conduct the pre-launch PFRS 15 assessment BEFORE go-live: contract identification (W4626), performance obligations (W4627), principal-vs-agent (W4628), SSP/allocation (W4633/W4634), variable consideration (W4638), financing component (W4639), contract costs (W4640) — produce a documented accounting decision | Revenue Accounting / Channel / Legal / Tax | Controller / CFO | Per stream |
| 2 | Configure the IT/ERP posting to match the decision (W4646); add the stream to the revenue-stream register (W4625) and disclosure schedule (W4643); brief the External Auditor on the new stream's treatment | Revenue Accounting / IT / Controller | CFO | Per stream |
| 3 | Gate go-live on the documented decision, IT config, and register entry; monitor the first close cycle for the new stream and true-up estimates (W4641) | Revenue Accounting / Controller | CFO | Per launch |

### System Touchpoints
- Revenue-stream register (W4625); IT config (W4646); disclosure schedule (W4643); audit evidence (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Premature-launch risk**: A new stream going live without a PFRS 15 decision accumulates mis-recognized revenue; mitigated by the go-live gate
- **Config-mismatch risk**: IT posting not matching the accounting decision → books diverge; mitigated by config sign-off and first-close monitoring

### Automation Opportunity
- New-stream assessment checklist/template auto-creating register + disclosure entries; go-live gate enforced in the launch workflow

### Controls
- CTL-703 (ensure controlled execution — Period Close, Disclosure, Audit & New-Revenue-Stream Onboarding (PA-157.3).)
- CTL-703: no new stream goes live without a documented PFRS 15 decision and matching IT config (go-live gate); External Auditor briefed

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 days per new-stream assessment (parallel to the channel's own launch work)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-79 (tax), W4625 (register), W4646 (config); pre-empts the "shadow-revenue risk" in W4625

| **Frequency** | Per change + periodic config review |
| **Volume** | 1 revenue-IT config landscape across ERP + channel subledgers |

| **Participants** | IT (VS-27), EA (VS-113), Controller, channel owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Configure and maintain the revenue-IT landscape: SSP/allocation engine (W4633/W4634), deferred-revenue schedules (gift card, points, subscription, warranty), gross-vs-net routing (W4628), and GL account mapping per stream/obligation/entity | IT Finance Lead / Revenue Accounting | CIO / Controller | Per setup/change |
| 2 | Govern changes through change-control (VS-27.1): test config in non-prod, document the change, obtain Controller sign-off, deploy; maintain the config-to-accounting-decision traceability matrix | IT / Revenue Accounting | CIO / Controller | Per change |

### Time Estimate

## W4647. Contract-Liability, Contract-Asset & AR-Receivable Reconciliation & Cut-off

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Month-end close (W9A); quarter/year-end |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | Contract-liability balances (deferred revenue, deposits); contract-asset balances (unbilled project revenue); AR (VS-16) |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager |
| **Participants** | AR (VS-16), Project Finance (VS-11), Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reconcile the contract-liability GL (deferred revenue — gift card, points, subscription, warranty, deposits) to the underlying schedules each month; investigate unmatched items | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Monthly |
| 2 | Reconcile the contract-asset GL (recognized-but-unbilled project/government revenue) to billings (W165/W1288) and the project module; reconcile AR (VS-16) to revenue cut-off (revenue recognized in the period the customer is billed or delivery occurs) | Revenue Accounting / Project Finance / AR | Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Perform cut-off testing at period-end (shipments/deliveries/services near the period boundary posted to the correct period); clear reconciling items before close sign-off | Revenue Accounting | Controller | Per close |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract-liability/asset GL (VS-17.1); AR (VS-16); project billing (W165/W1288); deferred schedules (W4635–W4637)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cut-off error risk**: Period-boundary transactions in the wrong period mis-state revenue (classic audit area); mitigated by cut-off testing
- **Unbilled/deferred drift risk**: Contract asset/liability balances not reconciled to schedules hide errors; mitigated by monthly reconcile

### Automation Opportunity
- GL-to-schedule auto-reconcile with exception routing; cut-off-boundary transaction flag for testing

### Controls
- CTL-114 (ensure PFRS 15 recognition & cut-off control.)
- CTL-703: contract balances reconciled monthly; cut-off tested each period; reconciling items cleared before sign-off

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 days/month reconciliation + cut-off

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (AR), VS-11.2 (project), W165/W1288 (billing), W4635–W4637 (deferred), W4641 (close)

## W4648. Revenue-Recognition Internal Controls, SoD & Audit-Trail Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Risk assessment; audit; control remediation; annual review |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + annual review |
| **Volume** | 1 PFRS 15 control set across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Revenue Accounting Manager / Controller |
| **Participants** | Internal Audit (VS-21), Revenue Assurance (VS-118), IT Security (VS-27.3), Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the PFRS 15 internal-control set: preventive (go-live gate W4645, config sign-off W4646, allocation-engine validation W4634) and detective (monthly reconcile W4647, variance clearance W4641, policy review W4642); map each to the internal-controls matrix | Revenue Accounting / Internal Audit | Controller | Annual + continuous |
| 2 | Enforce segregation of duties: those who configure the engine (W4646), those who post journals (W4641), and those who reconcile (W4647) are separate; restrict manual-override and manual-journal authority | Controller / IT Security | CFO | Continuous |
| 3 | Preserve a complete audit trail (every allocation, deferral release, override, estimate change) for 10 years (BIR/audit); coordinate with Revenue Assurance (VS-118) on transaction-level leakage vs. recognition-level error | Revenue Accounting / IT / Internal Audit | Controller | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Internal-controls matrix (VS-21); audit trail (VS-88 records); SoD roles (VS-27.3); Revenue Assurance (VS-118); ERP journal history

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Override-abuse risk**: Manual journal/override authority mis-used to manage revenue; mitigated by SoD, restricted authority, and audit trail
- **Control-coverage gap risk**: A control missing for a new stream/judgment; mitigated by the annual control review tied to the register

### Automation Opportunity
- SoD-conflict detection; manual-override log analytics; control-coverage report tied to the register

### Controls
- CTL-114 (ensure PFRS 15 recognition & cut-off control.)
- CTL-703: documented control set mapped to internal-controls matrix; SoD enforced; audit trail retained 10 years; controls reviewed annually

### Time Estimate
- ~5–8 days/year control-set maintenance; continuous SoD/audit-trail operation

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21 (internal audit), VS-118 (revenue assurance), VS-27.3/VS-88 (security/records), W4641/W4646/W4647 (operating controls)
