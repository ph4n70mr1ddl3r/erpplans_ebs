## W2526. Solar Product Assortment Planning & Vendor Selection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual assortment review or new solar technology market entry |
| **Frequency** | Annual with quarterly updates |
| **Volume** | ~200–300 solar SKUs across panels, inverters, batteries, mounting, wiring |
| **Owner** | Category Manager — Electrical |
| **Participants** | Category Mgr, Procurement, Technical Specialist, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager reviews solar market trends: panel technology (mono PERC, bifacial, half-cut), inverter types (string vs. micro), battery storage, EV charging integration; identifies 20–30 new SKUs for introduction; evaluates vendor certifications (TÜV, IEC, UL) | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | Procurement sources and qualifies solar vendors: Philippines product certification (BPS/DTI), ERC registration, warranty terms, technical support capability, lead time for panels (import: 45–60 days); negotiates volume rebates and co-marketing funds | Procurement | VP Merchandising | 2–3 weeks |
| 3 | Merchandising finalizes assortment matrix: Tier 1 stores (Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao) carry full solar range; Tier 2 stores carry core packages; online carries complete catalog; creates planogram for dedicated solar display area (minimum 16 sqm) | Category Manager / Merchandising | VP Merchandising | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Item master with solar-specific attributes (wattage, efficiency rating, certifications)
- Vendor qualification workflow with technical certification tracking
- Assortment planning module with store-tier matrix

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rapid technology evolution**: Solar panel efficiency improves 1–2% annually; SKU obsolescence risk
- **Import dependency**: 90%+ solar products imported; supply chain disruption risk


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Category Manager reviews solar market trends" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-import of "Procurement sources and qualifies solar vendors" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-create of "creates planogram for dedicated solar display area" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-439 (ensure controlled execution — Solar Product Merchandising & Consultation (PA-70.1).)
- operational: Category Manager — Electrical review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

## W2527. In-Store Solar Consultation & System Sizing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer inquiry about solar installation at store or via ecommerce |
| **Frequency** | ~200–300 consultations/month across all stores |
| **Volume** | ~1–2 per store per week at Tier 1 stores; ~2–4 per month at Tier 2 |
| **Owner** | Department Supervisor — Electrical |
| **Participants** | Dept Supervisor, Solar-Trained Sales Associate, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Associate conducts needs assessment: customer monthly electricity bill (kWh), roof type and orientation, available roof area, current electrical panel capacity, shading analysis; enters data into ERP solar sizing tool | Sales Associate | Department Supervisor | 30–45 min |
| 2 | System generates recommended solar system size: calculates panel count, inverter sizing, battery backup requirement; produces estimated ROI (payback period typically 3–5 years in Philippines); generates preliminary quotation | System / Sales Associate | Department Supervisor | 15–20 min |
| 3 | Customer selects configuration; Associate creates sales order with installation option; if installation requested, schedules site survey with certified solar installer partner; captures lead in CRM for follow-up | Sales Associate | Department Supervisor | 20–30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Solar sizing calculator integrated with ERP product catalog and pricing
- Quotation generation with bundle pricing for solar kits
- CRM lead capture with solar pipeline stage
- Installation service scheduling integration

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Incorrect sizing**: Undersized system fails to deliver promised savings; mitigated by site survey verification
- **Customer expectation management**: ROI estimates depend on future electricity rates and actual solar irradiance


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "enters data into ERP solar sizing tool" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "count, inverter sizing, battery backup requirement" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-create of "creates sales order with installation option" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-439 (ensure controlled execution — Solar Product Merchandising & Consultation (PA-70.1).)
- operational: site survey verification; operational: fails to deliver promised savings

### Time Estimate
- 60–90 minutes per consultation

## W2528. Solar Package & Bundle Configuration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New product introduction or seasonal promotion |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly; 4–6 new packages/year |
| **Volume** | ~15–20 active solar bundles |
| **Owner** | Category Manager — Electrical |
| **Participants** | Category Mgr, Pricing Analyst, Merchandising, Procurement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager designs solar packages by system size: 1kW (basic household), 3kW (standard), 5kW (medium), 10kW (large household/small business); each bundle includes panels, inverter, mounting kit, cables, breakers, and installation labor SKU | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 2–3 days |
| 2 | Pricing Analyst calculates bundle pricing: component cost + 5–10% bundle discount vs. individual items; verifies margin targets (25–30%); compares against competitor solar package pricing in market | Pricing Analyst | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |
| 3 | System configures kit/bundle in ERP: links component SKUs to parent bundle, defines BOM, sets assembly lead time (if pre-assembled), configures POS bundle scanning; tests at pilot store before rollout | IT / Category Manager | CIO / VP Merchandising | 2–3 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Kit/bundle master with BOM component linking
- Pricing engine with bundle discount rules
- POS bundle barcode configuration

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Component mismatch**: Kit components from different vendors may have compatibility issues
- **Inventory synchronization**: Kit availability depends on all component availability


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Pricing Analyst calculates bundle pricing" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-scan of "System configures kit/bundle in ERP" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-202 (ensure solar program compliance & delivery.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

## W2529. Solar Product Pricing & Net Metering ROI Calculator

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly pricing review or Meralco/DU rate change |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~200–300 solar SKUs |
| **Owner** | Pricing Analyst |
| **Participants** | Pricing Analyst, Category Manager, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing Analyst updates ROI calculator parameters: current utility rates per DU (Meralco, Davao Light, VECO, etc.), ERC-approved net metering rates, average solar irradiance per region (5–5.5 kWh/m²/day in Philippines), panel degradation rate (0.5%/year), system cost trends | Pricing Analyst | Category Manager | 1–2 hours |
| 2 | Analyst reviews solar product pricing vs. competitors and online market; adjusts pricing to maintain target margin while ensuring ROI calculator shows <5 year payback for standard systems; flags SKUs where cost increase threatens payback threshold | Pricing Analyst | VP Merchandising | 2–3 hours |
| 3 | System updates pricing and ROI calculator across all channels (in-store kiosk, ecommerce, mobile app); generates updated sales collateral with revised savings estimates | Pricing Analyst / IT | Category Manager | 2–4 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ROI calculator engine with DU rate database
- Multi-channel price sync
- Pricing analytics with competitive benchmarking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rate volatility**: DU rate changes affect ROI calculations; stale data misleads customers
- **Regulatory changes**: Net metering rules may change under ERC


### Automation Opportunity
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approved net metering rates, average solar irradiance per region" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews solar product pricing vs." (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-generate of "generates updated sales collateral with revised savings estimates" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-439 (ensure controlled execution — Solar Product Merchandising & Consultation (PA-70.1).)
- operational: Pricing Analyst review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 30–120 min per occurrence

## W2531. Solar Product Inventory & Lead Time Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly inventory review or stockout incident |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~200–300 solar SKUs |
| **Owner** | Supply Planner |
| **Participants** | Supply Planner, Procurement, DC Ops, Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Supply Planner reviews solar inventory levels vs. demand forecast: panels (high volume, import, 45–60 day lead time), inverters (medium volume, import, 30–45 days), mounting kits (local, 14 days), batteries (growing demand, import, 30–60 days); adjusts safety stock for A-items at 3 weeks vs. standard 1–2 weeks | Supply Planner | VP Supply Chain | 3–4 hours |
| 2 | For stockout or low-stock critical items: Procurement expedites with vendor; explores air freight for high-margin items; identifies substitute products; Category Manager approves SKU substitution at store level | Procurement / Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |
| 3 | DC Ops ensures special handling for solar panels: vertical storage racks, fragile handling protocols, dedicated staging area for large orders; coordinates consolidated delivery with customer installation schedule | DC Ops / Logistics | VP Supply Chain | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory planning with extended lead time parameters for solar imports
- ATP check considering import vessel schedules
- WMS special handling instructions for fragile/highbay items

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Long lead times**: Solar panel imports from China/Taiwan; seasonal demand spikes exceed buffer stock
- **Product damage**: Solar panels are fragile; transport damage rate ~2–3% requires vendor claims process


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews solar inventory levels vs." (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves SKU substitution at store level" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-schedule of "DC Ops ensures special handling for solar panels" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-439 (ensure controlled execution — Solar Product Merchandising & Consultation (PA-70.1).)

## W2532. Solar Lead Capture & CRM Funnel Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer expresses interest in solar system (in-store, online, phone) |
| **Frequency** | ~200–300 new leads/month |
| **Volume** | ~600–900 active leads in pipeline at any time |
| **Owner** | Solar Sales Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Solar Sales Coord, Sales Associates, Installation Partners, Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Associate captures lead in CRM: customer contact, electricity consumption data, property details, system interest level, budget range; assigns lead source (in-store, online, referral, trade account) | Sales Associate | Solar Sales Coordinator | 10–15 min |
| 2 | Solar Sales Coordinator qualifies lead: contacts customer within 24 hours, confirms requirements, schedules site survey with installation partner, sends preliminary quotation; updates CRM pipeline stage (New → Qualified → Site Survey → Quoted) | Solar Sales Coordinator | Category Manager | 30–45 min per lead |
| 3 | Coordinator manages pipeline: follows up quoted leads within 7 days, nurtures cold leads with monthly solar education content, reports conversion metrics (lead-to-sale target: 15–20%), escalates large commercial leads (>20kW) to Category Manager | Solar Sales Coordinator | VP Merchandising | 2–3 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM with solar-specific pipeline stages and lead scoring
- Automated lead assignment and follow-up reminders
- Solar lead analytics dashboard

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lead leakage**: In-store associates fail to capture leads in CRM; mitigated by POS solar inquiry prompt
- **Long sales cycle**: 30–90 days from lead to sale; requires persistent follow-up


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Sales Associate captures lead in CRM" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-send of "sends preliminary quotation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-escalate of "escalates large commercial leads" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-439 (ensure controlled execution — Solar Product Merchandising & Consultation (PA-70.1).)
