## W3681. In-Market Compliance Monitoring & Store Shelf Audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monitoring cycle; risk-based audit; new product on shelf |
| **Frequency** | Periodic + risk-based |
| **Volume** | 200 stores; regulated-SKU subset |
| **Owner** | Product Compliance Manager / Store Operations |
| **Participants** | Product Compliance Manager, Store Ops (links to VS-07), LP (links to VS-23), Quality (links to VS-31), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Audit (links to VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Compliance Manager/Store Ops conduct periodic in-market certification compliance audits across stores and DCs: verify PS Mark/ICC mark presence on regulated products on shelf, match to current certificates/licenses (links to W3666/W3667), and flag missing/expired marks for action | Product Compliance Manager / Store Ops / LP | Product Compliance Manager | Periodic |
| 2 | Product Compliance Manager manage exceptions: investigate a regulated product found on shelf without valid certification, determine root cause (PO/GR gating failure, license lapse, sticker not applied), and trigger stop-sell (links to W3684) and corrective action | Product Compliance Manager / Quality / Store Ops | Product Compliance Manager | On finding |
| 3 | Product Compliance Manager/Audit publish compliance-monitoring results: certification-conformance rate by category/store/vendor, recurring failure patterns, and the corrective-action backlog; report to program governance (links to W3665) and audit (links to VS-21) | Product Compliance Manager / Audit | Product Compliance Manager | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Store operations (links to VS-07); loss prevention (links to VS-23); quality (links to VS-31); item master/regulated flag (links to W3666); stop-sell (links to W3684); audit (links to VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Shelf-presence risk**: Regulated product on shelf without visible/current mark — illegal sale; mitigated by periodic audit
- **Gating-bypass risk**: Product reached shelf despite cert gate; mitigated by exception root-cause and gating fix


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "verify PS Mark/ICC mark presence on regulated products on shelf, match to…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-flag for investigation of "investigate a regulated product found on shelf without valid certification,…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "Product Compliance Manager/Audit publish compliance-monitoring results" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-582 (ensure controlled execution — Market Surveillance, Compliance Monitoring & Certification Analytics (PA-117.3).)
- operational: periodic audit; operational: exception root-cause and gating fix

### Time Estimate
- Periodic + risk-based
