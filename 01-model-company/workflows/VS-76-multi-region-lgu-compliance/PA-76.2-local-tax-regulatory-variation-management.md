## W2678. Local Business Tax Computation & Payment Across LGUs

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly payment cycle |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly (or per LGU schedule) |
| **Volume** | ~205 locations across ~150 LGUs (per W54) |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | Tax Accountant, Finance Analyst, Store Managers, LGU Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Accountant compiles the local business tax (LBT) liability per location per RA 7160: gross receipts per store per LGU (from the W2676 reconciled figure), the applicable rate per the LGU tax ordinance (0.5–2% varying by business activity and LGU), tax-base adjustments (exempt sales, intercompany elimination), quarterly installment computation | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 3–5 days per quarter |
| 2 | Accountant prepares the LGU-format return per LGU (each LGU's return template differs); Store Manager submits to the LGU Treasury; pays via cashier or LGU-accredited bank; obtains the official receipt and uploads it | Tax Accountant / Store Manager | Tax Manager | 5–10 days per quarter |
| 3 | Finance reconciles actual vs. budgeted LBT, variance by LGU; identifies rate increases or newly-enacted fees (links to W2680); Tax Manager incorporates the variance into the quarterly forecast and the next W2676 accrual true-up | Finance / Tax Manager | CFO | Per quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Tax master (links to VS-29 / W293 tax & regulatory master, VS-79 / W90 indirect-tax) — LGU LBT rates and bases per location
- GL / AP (links to VS-17 / W7C recurring-invoice, VS-15 P2P) — LBT payment posted against the location's LBT accrual
- Store-P&L (links to VS-17 / W26 budget) — LBT as a store-level local-tax line

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Tax-base-mapping risk**: the LBT base (gross receipts) is defined per LGU and may include/exclude items differently from BuildRight's revenue recognition (VAT treatment, intercompany, consignment sell-through); mitigated by the documented per-LGU gross-receipts reconciliation issued to Store Managers before submission
- **Multi-LGU-format risk**: ~150 LGUs (per W54) each with a different return template and submission quirk (some require in-person, some online); mitigated by the per-LGU submission checklist maintained in the VS-166 license-portfolio and a rolling calendar of LGU-specific deadlines

### Automation Opportunity
- LBT liability auto-computed from the GL gross-receipts extract × the LGU rate master; per-LGU return template auto-populated for Store-Manager submission

### Controls
- CTL-458 (tax-payment accuracy and timeliness — the 2%/month surcharge on late LBT is the penalty control); CTL-44 (duplicate-payment guard on the quarterly installments)

### Time Estimate
~4 days/quarter × 4 + ~7 days/quarter Store-Manager submission × ~205 locations (distributed ~10 min each) = **~700–900 hours/year** Tax Accountant + Store Managers, concentrated at quarter-end.

## W2679. Real Property Tax Assessment & Payment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual assessment cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual assessment; quarterly payments |
| **Volume** | 209 properties |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | Tax Accountant, Property Manager, LGU Assessor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Accountant reviews the real property tax (RPT) assessment per location: assessed value, the LGU tax rate (1–3% depending on province/city/municipality and use class), the Special Education Fund add-on (additional 1% per RA 7160), exemptions (where the property qualifies); compares vs. prior year to catch unexpected re-assessments/increases | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 1–2 months after assessment |
| 2 | For disputes (over-assessment): Property Manager files a protest with the Local Board of Assessment Appeals within 60 days of receipt; provides evidence of overvaluation (recent market comparables, condition evidence); Tax Accountant pays the undisputed amount to avoid penalty while the protest is pending | Property Manager / Tax Accountant / Legal | VP Legal | Within 60 days |
| 3 | Tax Accountant schedules the quarterly RPT payments and captures the early-payment discount (LGUs offer 10–20% for advance annual payment per RA 7160); ensures timely payment to avoid the 2%/month surcharge and the 2-year lien-risk on delinquent property | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Fixed-asset / property master (links to VS-35 / W399 fixed-asset master, VS-97 real estate) — owned-property register with assessed values
- Tax master (links to VS-29 / W293, VS-79 / W90) — RPT rates per LGU and use class
- GL / AP (links to VS-17 / W7C) — RPT accrual and payment

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-assessment risk**: LGU assessors re-value aggressively after a renovation or area-gentrification, inflating RPT on a property whose market value hasn't moved commensurately; mitigated by the 60-day protest window in step 2 and the comparables evidence base
- **Lien risk**: 2+ years of delinquent RPT allows the LGU to auction the property (a catastrophic loss for an owned store site); mitigated by the W2677 expiry-alert pattern applied to RPT due dates and the early-payment discipline in step 3

### Automation Opportunity
- Assessment-vs-prior-year delta auto-flagged for review; early-payment-discount opportunity auto-surfaced at the start of each LGU's discount window

### Controls
- CTL-458 (ensure controlled execution — Local Tax & Regulatory Variation Management (PA-76.2).)
- CTL-458 (fixed-asset/property-record accuracy — links to VS-35 / W184 fixed-asset audit); operational: Tax Manager sign-off on any protest filed (legal-materiality control)

### Time Estimate
~1.5 months Tax Accountant annual review (distributed) + ~1 day/quarter payment processing × 209 properties + protest work ad-hoc = **~400–600 hours/year** Tax Accountant + Property Manager.

## W2680. LGU Regulatory Change Monitoring & Impact Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | LGU ordinance or regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Continuous monitoring; ~20–30 changes/year |
| **Volume** | ~150 LGUs (per W54) |
| **Owner** | Legal Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Legal Compliance Officer, Legal, Tax Manager, VP Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal Compliance Officer monitors regulatory changes across the ~150 LGUs (per W54) where BuildRight operates: new ordinances (single-use-plastic bans, store-hour limits, signage rules), tax-rate changes (links to W2676/W2678), zoning amendments, environmental regulations; sources: LGU websites, Sangguniang session minutes, retail-association bulletins, VS-166 regulatory-intelligence feed | Legal Compliance Officer | VP Legal | Continuous (~6 hours/week) |
| 2 | Officer assesses each change's impact on BuildRight: new costs, required operational changes, compliance deadlines, the affected stores; prepares an impact memo for VP Legal and the affected departments (Tax, HR, Facilities, Store Ops) | Legal Compliance Officer / Legal | VP Legal | Per change event (~4–8 hours) |
| 3 | Affected departments implement the changes; Officer monitors compliance across stores; documents the change-and-response for inspection readiness (links to VS-22 / W95 external-audit coordination) | All Affected / Legal Compliance Officer | COO / VP Legal | Per change timeline |

### System Touchpoints
- Regulatory-intelligence feed (links to VS-166 license-portfolio, VS-22 / W22.3 regulatory-change-management) — the canonical monitoring source and impact-assessment record
- Document/policy repository (links to VS-88) — versioned impact memos and the updated internal SOPs
- Communication/distribution (links to VS-07 / W571 HQ→store communication) — change notices pushed to affected stores

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missed-change risk**: an LGU amends an ordinance and BuildRight doesn't learn of it until an inspection or penalty; mitigated by the multi-source monitoring in step 1 (not relying on a single channel) and the retail-association membership that surfaces sector-relevant changes
- **Implementation-gap risk**: the change is detected and assessed but not implemented at the store level (the memo is issued, no one acts); mitigated by the compliance-monitoring in step 3 and the W2677 dashboard surfacing un-acted changes

### Automation Opportunity
- Regulatory-feed auto-ingest with keyword classification (plastic, tax, signage, hours) routing to the right department's impact-assessment queue

### Controls
- CTL-235 (ensure multi-LGU license compliance.)
- Operational: VP Legal quarterly review of the change log + implementation status (audit trail for the Board Audit Committee)

### Time Estimate
~6 hours/week monitoring + ~6 hours/change × 25 changes/year + implementation tracking absorbed = **~500–700 hours/year** Legal Compliance Officer + affected departments.

## W2681. DOLE Regional Labor Standard Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DOLE inspection or regional regulation change |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc inspections; annual review |
| **Volume** | ~205 locations across 6 regions |
| **Owner** | HR Compliance Manager |
| **Participants** | HR Compliance Mgr, Regional HR, Store Managers, DOLE |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR Compliance Manager monitors the regional labor-standard variations: minimum wage rates per Regional Tripartite Wages and Productivity Board (NCR ~PHP 610/day; BARMM ~PHP 361/day — large spread), 13th-month pay (prorated, per PD 851), holiday pay, overtime, night-shift differential, 5/6-day-workweek rules per DOLE advisory | HR Compliance Mgr | CHRO | Ongoing |
| 2 | Regional HR ensures compliance per the applicable regional rate: DOLE-required workplace postings, written contracts, the correct wage per the regional rate, mandatory-benefits enrollment (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG per the relevant laws) | Regional HR / Store Manager | CHRO | Ongoing |
| 3 | HR Compliance Manager prepares the documentation package for DOLE inspections (under RA 11058 OSH law and DO 183-18 for general labor standards): employment records, payroll registers, benefits enrollment proof, working-hours/attendance logs, leave records, safety-committee minutes | HR Compliance Manager | CHRO | Ongoing + pre-inspection |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS / payroll (links to VS-19 / W10 payroll, VS-29 / W292 employee master) — the source of all DOLE-evidence records
- OSH/safety module (links to VS-24 / W140 OHS, W141 workplace-safety inspection) — safety-committee minutes and OSH-compliance records
- Location master (VS-29 / W254) — each location tagged with its DOLE region for rate lookup

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rate-misapplication risk**: an employee transferred across regions (NCR→Visayas) stays on the wrong regional wage rate, creating an underpayment liability; mitigated by the location-master region tag driving the payroll rate and a transfer-triggered rate review
- **Documentation-gap risk**: a DOLE inspection cites a missing posting or an incomplete payroll register even when underlying compliance is solid; mitigated by the pre-inspection self-audit checklist in step 3 and the centralized HRIS as the single evidence source

### Automation Opportunity
- Regional-rate master auto-applied to payroll by the employee's location; DOLE-evidence-pack auto-assembled from HRIS on inspection notice

### Controls
- CTL-458 (ensure controlled execution — Local Tax & Regulatory Variation Management (PA-76.2).)
- CTL-458 (payroll-statutory accuracy — links to VS-19 / W10, W337 payroll & statutory compliance audit); operational: HR Compliance Manager pre-inspection self-audit sign-off

### Time Estimate
~4 hours/week monitoring + ~2 hours/location annual review × ~205 locations + inspection prep ad-hoc = **~600–800 hours/year** HR Compliance Manager + Regional HR.

## W2682. Consumer Protection Compliance (RA 7394)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DTI complaint or compliance review |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ad-hoc for complaints |
| **Volume** | 200 stores + ecommerce |
| **Owner** | Legal Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Legal Compliance Officer, Store Manager, Customer Service, DTI |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal Compliance Officer ensures ongoing compliance with the Consumer Act (RA 7394): accurate price tags (links to VS-08 / W69 price-compliance audit), clearly-posted return policy, no deceptive practices, honest advertising (the "Per DTI Permit No." requirement on promos per W868), proper product labeling | Legal Compliance Officer / Store Manager | VP Legal | Ongoing |
| 2 | Customer Service handles DTI-filed complaints: investigates, proposes a resolution (refund, replacement), represents BuildRight at DTI mediation; tracks the resolution-rate target (within 15 days per DTI's standard expectation) | Customer Service / Legal Compliance Officer | COO | Per complaint |
| 3 | Officer analyzes complaint trends: by store, category, complaint type; identifies high-complaint stores for improvement; reports quarterly to VP Legal and links repeat issues to the VS-23 loss-prevention or VS-118 pricing-integrity workflows where relevant | Legal Compliance Officer | VP Legal | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM / complaint module (links to VS-13 / W41 complaint management, W469 DTI-customer-complaint-escalation) — the canonical complaint record and DTI-mediation trail
- Price-tag/labeling (links to VS-08 / W181 price-tag printing, VS-118 pricing integrity) — the price-tag accuracy that pre-empts DTI price complaints
- Advertising/promotion review (links to VS-14 marketing, VS-22 / W22.1) — the "Per DTI Permit No." compliance on every promo

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Viral-complaint risk**: a single DTI complaint that escalates to social media damages the brand disproportionately to the underlying issue; mitigated by the 15-day resolution target and Customer Service authority to resolve at the lowest possible level (links to POS-075 on-the-spot resolution)
- **Systemic-pricing-error risk**: a price-tag error that affects many customers generates many DTI complaints at once; mitigated by the VS-118 pricing-integrity monitoring that catches the error before customers do

### Automation Opportunity
- Complaint-trend dashboard auto-generated by store/category/type; DTI-mediation deadline tracker with alerts

### Controls
- CTL-37: warranty claim validity — DTI often surfaces warranty disputes; operational: Legal Compliance Officer sign-off on any DTI-mediated settlement

### Time Estimate
~4 hours/week compliance monitoring + ~4 hours/complaint × ~100 complaints/year + quarterly analysis = **~500–700 hours/year** Legal Compliance Officer + Customer Service.

## W2683. Philippine Competition Law Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New business practice or regulatory inquiry |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; periodic training |
| **Volume** | 5 legal entities |
| **Owner** | VP Legal |
| **Participants** | VP Legal, Compliance Team, Category Manager, Procurement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Legal ensures compliance with the Philippine Competition Act (RA 10667): reviews vendor agreements for anti-competitive clauses (resale-price-maintenance, exclusive-dealing), reviews BuildRight's pricing practices, evaluates market concentration in categories where BuildRight may hold a dominant position | VP Legal / Legal | CEO | Ongoing |
| 2 | Legal trains Category Managers and Procurement annually: no price-fixing discussions with competitors, no market-allocation agreements, no abuse-of-dominant-position conduct, proper conduct at industry-association meetings (a high-risk venue for inadvertent coordination); refresher delivered via OLA | Legal | VP Legal | Annual |
| 3 | VP Legal monitors Philippine Competition Commission (PCC) enforcement actions and guidance; reviews BuildRight practices against current PCC guidance; maintains the compliance documentation (links to VS-129 competition & antitrust compliance, the dedicated VS for this domain) | VP Legal | CEO | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract repository (links to VS-100 / W230 legal contract review) — vendor agreements reviewed for anti-competitive clauses
- OLA (links to VS-19.4 learning & development) — annual competition-law training tracked per Category Manager / Procurement staff
- VS-129 (the dedicated competition-compliance value stream) — W2683 is the LGU/RA-10667-flavored view; VS-129 owns the deeper program

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inadvertent-coordination risk**: a Category Manager discusses pricing with a competitor at an industry event, creating per-se illegal conduct without intent; mitigated by the annual training in step 2 and a written "contact-with-competitors" protocol
- **Dominance-threshold risk**: in a narrow local market (a provincial town where BuildRight is the only big-box), conduct that's benign elsewhere can be abuse-of-dominance; mitigated by the market-concentration review in step 1 flagging such local dominance

### Automation Opportunity
- Vendor-agreement clause scanner (flags RPM, exclusivity, MFN clauses for Legal review); training-completion tracker with auto-reminders

### Controls
- CTL-159: rebate-agreement review — rebate terms can have anti-competitive effect; operational: CEO sign-off on any practice flagged as dominant-position-sensitive

### Time Estimate
~4 hours/week review + annual training delivery absorbed into L&D + PCC monitoring ~2 hours/week = **~400–500 hours/year** VP Legal + Legal team.

## W2684. LGU Disaster & Emergency Response Regulatory Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | LGU emergency preparedness requirement |
| **Frequency** | Annual; event-driven during typhoon season |
| **Volume** | ~205 locations |
| **Owner** | Safety Officer |
| **Participants** | Safety Officer, Legal Compliance Officer, Store Manager, LGU DRRMO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Safety Officer ensures each location meets the LGU disaster-preparedness requirements under RA 10121 (DRRM Act): an emergency action plan registered with the LGU Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (DRRMO), a designated evacuation area, Go Bag supplies, a trained disaster-response team per store | Safety Officer | COO | Annual review |
| 2 | Store Manager participates in LGU-conducted disaster drills (earthquake, fire, typhoon); coordinates with the DRRMO on evacuation procedures; aligns the store plan with the barangay disaster plan (links to W2673 community relations) | Store Manager / Safety Officer | Regional Manager | Per drill schedule |
| 3 | Legal Compliance Officer tracks DRRM compliance per LGU: updated plans filed, drill-participation certificates, equipment-inspection records; identifies high-risk LGUs (typhoon-prone Visayas, flood-prone Metro Manila) for enhanced requirements | Legal Compliance Officer / Safety Officer | VP Legal | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE/safety module (links to VS-24 / W140 OHS, W141 safety inspection, W330 emergency-response) — emergency-action plans and drill records
- Typhoon/disaster response (links to VS-69 typhoon & disaster response, the dedicated VS) — the operational counterpart to this regulatory-compliance view
- Regulatory license/permit repository (VS-166) — DRRM plan-filing records and drill certificates

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Plan-on-paper risk**: the emergency action plan is filed but the store team doesn't know it (drills not actually conducted, just documented); mitigated by the LGU drill-participation requirement in step 2 and the VS-24 fire-drill execution records
- **High-risk-location-underpreparation risk**: a Visayas typhoon-path store has the same baseline plan as a low-risk Luzon store; mitigated by the risk-tiered enhancement in step 3 (Go Bag inventory levels, drill frequency, structural reinforcement tied to typhoon exposure)

### Automation Opportunity
- Risk-tier map (typhoon/flood/earthquake exposure per location) auto-applied to set location-specific preparedness requirements; drill-schedule auto-generated per LGU requirement

### Controls
- CTL-458 (ensure controlled execution — Local Tax & Regulatory Variation Management (PA-76.2).)
- CTL-458 (emergency-preparedness — links to VS-26 / W158 BCP drill & DR testing); operational: Safety Officer annual sign-off on each location's plan-filing + drill completion

### Time Estimate
~3–4 hours/location annual review × ~205 locations + drill participation ~2 hours/location/event + Officer tracking absorbed = **~700–900 hours/year** Safety Officers + Store Managers.

## W2685. BIR CAS Registration Per Location

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New store opening or re-registration |
| **Frequency** | Per new location; updates per system change |
| **Volume** | ~205 locations across ~30 RDOs |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, IT, Legal Compliance Officer, BIR RDO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Manager ensures the Computerized Accounting System (CAS) registration for each location: registers the ERP with the BIR Revenue District Office (RDO) having jurisdiction over the location; submits the system documentation per BIR Revenue Memorandum; obtains the BIR CAS Certificate (a precondition for issuing BIR-compliant invoices from that location) | Tax Manager / IT | CFO | Per new location |
| 2 | IT provides the documentation: system flowchart, data-security description, 10-year record-retention capability (per TRAIN law NIRC Sec. 235), audit-trail features, sample invoices/receipts with all BIR-required information (TIN, serial number, "VAT Registered" marker, QR code per the e-invoicing requirement) | IT / Tax Manager | CIO | Per registration/update |
| 3 | Tax Manager maintains the CAS records: RDO assignments per location, the CAS certificate renewal/re-validation dates, system-change notifications to BIR (any ERP upgrade affecting invoicing must be reported to the relevant RDOs) | Tax Manager | CFO | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- POS / e-invoicing (links to VS-08 / W473 BIR EIS e-invoicing compliance, POS-013) — the invoicing system the CAS registration authorizes
- Tax master (links to VS-29 / W293, VS-79 / W90) — RDO assignment per location; CAS certificate reference
- IT change management (links to VS-27 / W132) — ERP upgrades that affect invoicing trigger the BIR-notification step

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Trade-without-CAS risk**: a new store opens before the CAS certificate issues, issuing invoices the BIR won't recognize (a penalizable violation and a VAT-input problem for customers); mitigated by the CAS registration being a hard gate in the VS-37 opening critical path, sequenced 60+ days out
- **Upgrade-notification-lapse risk**: an ERP upgrade changes the invoice format and isn't reported to the RDO, invalidating the CAS registration retroactively; mitigated by the IT-change-management → Tax-Manager notification link in step 3 (any invoicing-affecting change flagged for BIR notification)

### Automation Opportunity
- RDO-assignment auto-derived from the location master; CAS-certificate-expiry/re-validation tracker in the W2677 dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-458 (ensure controlled execution — Local Tax & Regulatory Variation Management (PA-76.2).)
- operational: the CAS certificate as a permit-validity control — no CAS, no active-invoicing status; operational: CFO sign-off on the per-location CAS registration before opening

### Time Estimate
~20–30 hours per new-location CAS registration × ~12 openings/year + ongoing RDO maintenance ~2 hours/month = **~300–400 hours/year** Tax Manager + IT.
