## W4697. Global Mobility Strategy, Policy & Assignment-Tax Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Talent/business strategy (VS-33); new foreign-hire need; annual review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | 1 global-mobility strategy & policy |
| **Owner** | Head of Global Mobility / HR Strategy |
| **Participants** | CHRO (VS-19), CFO/Controller (VS-17), Tax (VS-79), Legal (VS-100.3), Chief Security Officer (VS-159), Strategy (VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define the global-mobility strategy and policy aligned to talent and business strategy (VS-33): when to deploy foreign expertise vs. develop local talent (VS-123 apprenticeship), the assignment-tax framework, and the governance with Legal/Tax/Chief Security Officer (VS-159) | Head of Global Mobility / CHRO / Tax | CHRO / CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Set assignment-tax principles: residency determination (resident vs. non-resident), tax-treaty application, 19-series withholding for non-residents, tax-equalization vs. tax-protection, and home/host split (W4714) | Head of Global Mobility / Tax / Legal | CHRO / CFO / VP Tax | Annual |
| 3 | Obtain leadership endorsement; publish the mobility policy and assignment handbook; train HR/business leaders on when/how to initiate an assignment (intake to W4702) | Head of Global Mobility / CHRO | CHRO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- HR/HRIS (VS-19/VS-103); tax (VS-79); strategy (VS-33); legal (VS-100.3); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Ad-hoc-assignment risk**: Assignments handled case-by-case without policy → inconsistent packages, tax errors, and inequity; mitigated by the documented policy
- **Tax-treaty-misapplication risk**: Mis-applied treaty/19-series withholding → tax exposure and employee cost; mitigated by Tax-led framework (VS-79)

### Automation Opportunity
- Policy/handbook portal; assignment-intake workflow; tax-treaty decision support

### Controls
- CTL-710 (ensure controlled execution — Mobility Strategy, Policy & Assignment Framework (PA-160.1).)
- CTL-710: mobility policy CHRO/CFO-endorsed; assignment-tax framework Tax-led (VS-79); assignments initiated via policy intake

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks annual strategy + policy

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-79 (tax), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-123 (local-talent development), VS-159 (security), W4714 (assignment tax)

## W4698. Assignment Types: Long-Term, Short-Term, Commuter, Localization & Business-Visitor Policy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Assignment need; policy application |
| **Frequency** | Per assignment |
| **Volume** | ~20–40 assignments/year across types (foreign executives, seconded experts, provincial transfers) |
| **Owner** | Head of Global Mobility |
| **Participants** | HR Business Partners (VS-19/VS-103), Tax (VS-79), Immigration (PA-160.2), hiring managers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Classify each assignment by type — long-term (LT, typically >12 months, with relocation/family), short-term (ST, <12 months, no family relocation), commuter (regular cross-border without relocation), localization (permanent host-country conversion), and business-visitor (short meetings, no work-permit) — each with distinct immigration/tax/payroll treatment | Global Mobility / HRBP / Tax | Head of Global Mobility | Per assignment |
| 2 | Apply the per-type package and compliance (immigration W4705–W4707, payroll/tax W4713/W4714, housing W4716, duty-of-care W4704) and confirm the assignment meets the policy test (genuine business need, not labor-only-contracting avoidance VS-98) | Global Mobility / HRBP | Head of Global Mobility / CHRO | Per assignment |
| 3 | Document the assignment decision and terms in the assignment letter; ensure the business-visitor vs. work-permit line is not crossed (visitor doing productive work = immigration violation) | Global Mobility / Legal | Head of Global Mobility / VP Legal | Per assignment |

### System Touchpoints
- Assignment-record system (W4702); HR (VS-19/VS-103); immigration (PA-160.2); tax (VS-79)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-classification risk**: A long-term assignment run as a business-visitor/commuter → immigration/tax violation; mitigated by the classification framework and Legal review
- **Labor-only-contracting-confusion risk**: A foreign "assignee" structure that masks labor-only-contracting (VS-98/DOLE D.O. 174); mitigated by the genuine-business-need test

### Automation Opportunity
- Assignment-classification decision tool; per-type package templates; assignment-letter generation

### Controls
- CTL-710 (ensure controlled execution — Mobility Strategy, Policy & Assignment Framework (PA-160.1).)
- CTL-710: classification framework applied; business-visitor vs. work-permit line enforced; genuine-business-need documented

### Time Estimate
- ~per assignment 2–5 days classification + setup

### Cross-references
- links to W4702 (records), PA-160.2 (immigration), W4713/W4714 (payroll/tax), W4716 (housing), VS-98 (contingent-workforce distinction)

## W4699. Mobility Cost-Budget, Package Design & Balance-Sheet vs. Lump-Sum

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Assignment costing; annual mobility budget |
| **Frequency** | Per assignment + annual budget |
| **Volume** | ~20–40 assignments/year; 1 mobility budget |
| **Owner** | Head of Global Mobility / Finance Partner |
| **Participants** | Finance/FP&A (VS-17.4), Comp & Benefits (VS-102), Tax (VS-79), Procurement (VS-34) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cost each assignment: base salary, assignment allowances (premium, hardship, cost-of-living differential), housing (VS-144/W4716), relocation, tax-equalization cost, schooling (dependents), and return trips; choose balance-sheet (maintain home-country spend + host differentials) vs. lump-sum (VS-102) | Global Mobility / Finance / Comp | Head of Global Mobility / CFO | Per assignment |
| 2 | Build the annual mobility budget (by entity VS-72); approve assignments per the cost-approval matrix (tiered by cost); track actual vs. budget | Global Mobility / FP&A | CHRO / CFO | Annual + per assignment |
| 3 | Identify cost-optimization (localization W4718 vs. continued expatriation, package benchmarking VS-102) and report mobility spend analytics (W4720) | Global Mobility / Finance | CHRO / CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance/FP&A (VS-17.4); comp (VS-102); HR (VS-19); procurement (VS-34); mobility analytics (W4720)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cost-creep risk**: Uncapped assignment allowances inflate cost; mitigated by the package policy and approval matrix
- **Over-expatriation risk**: Expatriate assignments continued when localization would be cheaper/better (VS-123); mitigated by localization review (W4718)

### Automation Opportunity
- Assignment-costing calculator; package templates; actual-vs-budget dashboards

### Controls
- CTL-710 (ensure controlled execution — Mobility Strategy, Policy & Assignment Framework (PA-160.1).)
- CTL-710: assignment cost per policy and approval matrix; actual-vs-budget tracked; localization reviewed

### Time Estimate
- ~per assignment costing 2–3 days; ~annual budget absorbed

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-102 (comp), VS-72 (entity), W4716 (housing), W4718 (localization), W4720 (analytics)

| **Owner** | Head of Global Mobility / Director, Regional Loss Prevention |
| **Participants** | Chief Security Officer (VS-159), Travel Risk (VS-159 W4679), OH (VS-83), HR (VS-19) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each cross-border assignee, coordinate with Corporate Security (VS-159) on travel-risk briefing, destination-risk intelligence (VS-159 W4679), and duty-of-care during assignment travel | Global Mobility / Chief Security Officer / Travel Risk | Head of Global Mobility / Chief Security Officer | Per assignment |

- Assignee-traveler-tracking enrollment; joint mobility-security workflow; event-triggered activation

### Controls
- CTL-130 (ensure immigration & foreign-worker compliance.)
