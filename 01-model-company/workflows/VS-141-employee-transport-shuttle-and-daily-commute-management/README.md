# VS-141: Employee Transport, Shuttle & Daily Commute Management

> **People** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Employee Transport, Shuttle & Daily Commute Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
owning the enterprise **daily people-movement discipline** that gets **~6,918 employees** to and
from **205 operating sites** (200 stores + 4 DCs + Davao HQ) across the Philippine archipelago,
safely, on time, and at controlled cost. BuildRight runs **2–3 shifts per store** (early-open,
mid, and late/close shifts including the 10 PM–6 AM night-differential window) and **24-hour DC
operations**, so a large share of frontline staff start or end outside safe public-transport
hours — and provincial/island sites (Mindanao, Visayas, provincial Luzon) have thin, unreliable,
or unsafe after-hours public transport. Without a structured transport program, attendance,
attrition, safety, and payroll are all directly exposed.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**. The defining terms — "employee shuttle",
"staff shuttle", "company bus", "shuttle route", "transport allowance", and "daily commute" —
appear in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers. What exists is only adjacent:
**VS-19.1** owns *business travel* (occasional inter-island flights/hotels for managers, buyers,
and auditors — ~600–960 trips/yr, W74 travel-lifecycle), **VS-18.2** reimburses *ad-hoc errand*
transport from petty cash ("employee transportation for urgent store errands"), **VS-19.3**
notes "commute challenges" as a transfer check-in topic, and **VS-138** owns *building* services
(HVAC, cleaning, cafeteria) — not people movement. No value stream owns the **daily commute
operating model**: transport-need assessment and policy, the owned/leased shuttle fleet, route
and shift-schedule design, contracted shuttle/charter vendors, the transport-allowance and
service-vehicle program, ride-share/van-pool partnerships, driver and vehicle safety (DOLE OSH,
LTFRB franchise), late-night and women-safe transport, transport cost allocation, and commute
experience/analytics.

This is distinct from **VS-19.1 (Business Travel)** which moves *managers/HQ staff occasionally*
for business — this value stream moves the *frontline workforce daily* to/from their scheduled
shifts. It is distinct from **VS-06 (Logistics & Fleet)** which moves *goods* in commercial
trucks — this value stream moves *people* in passenger vans/buses (separate vehicles, separate
licensing, separate safety regime). It is distinct from **VS-138 (Integrated Facilities
Management)** which owns the *building/workplace environment* — this value stream owns the *journey
to and from* the workplace. It is distinct from **VS-102 (Compensation/Benefits)** which *designs*
pay/benefits (including a transport allowance line) — this value stream *operates* the transport
services and the allowance program day-to-day. Employee transport is the *daily people-movement*
discipline — distinct from business travel, goods fleet, facilities, and benefits design.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: ~6,918 employees at 205 distributed sites, a 15–20% annual
turnover rate in which commute difficulty is a documented frontline attrition driver, 2–3 daily
shifts with after-hours start/end times, provincial and island geography with poor transit, DOLE
OSH and LTFRB franchise obligations for any operated/contracted passenger service, and a direct
link to attendance, shrink (unsupervised late close/open), and payroll cost (transport allowance
is a recurring line). A failed or unsafe shuttle is also a single-point people-safety and
reputational event.
