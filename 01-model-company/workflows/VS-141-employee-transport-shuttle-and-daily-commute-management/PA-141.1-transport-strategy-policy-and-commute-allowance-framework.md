## W4241. Employee Commute-Need Assessment & Transport Strategy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual workforce/transport planning; new-store opening (VS-37); shift-pattern change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on major footprint/shift change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise: ~6,932 employees across 205 sites; ~5,800 store staff on 2–3 shifts |
| **Owner** | Head of HR Shared Services / Transport Lead (VS-103) |
| **Participants** | Store/DC Ops (VS-07/VS-04), Workforce Planning (VS-103.3), Finance (VS-17.4), Strategy (VS-33), Real Estate (VS-97) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR/Ops run an annual commute-need assessment per site: employee home-location distribution (geocoded), shift roster start/end times vs local public-transport availability, after-dark safety exposure, and ride-share/jeepney/tricycle feasibility | Transport Lead / Workforce Planning / Site Mgrs | Head of HR Shared Services | Annual |
| 2 | HR/Ops quantify the transport gap (employees per site needing assisted commute, by shift), size the service need (routes, seats, trips/shift), and prioritize sites (after-hours shifts, unsafe corridors, high-attrition stores) | Transport Lead / Analytics (VS-28) | Head of HR Shared Services | Annual |
| 3 | HR/Strategy set the transport strategy (service tiers, owned vs contracted mix, allowance vs shuttle, ESG/rideshare goals), obtain approval, and cascade to operating-model (W4244) and route design (W4250) | Transport Lead / Strategy / Finance | CHRO / COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Workforce planning/roster (VS-103.3 / VS-19.3 shift data); HRIS employee address & shift (VS-19); analytics (VS-28); store/DC locations (VS-97/VS-07); strategy (VS-33)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Coverage-blind risk**: Need unquantified → shuttle/allowance mis-sized (over-spend or stranded staff); mitigated by geocoded home-distribution + shift-mapped gap analysis
- **Equity risk**: Some sites/shifts favored; mitigated by transparent service-tier criteria

### Automation Opportunity
- Geocoded home-distribution mapping; shift-roster → transport-need auto-derivation; gap-heat-map dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-653 (ensure controlled execution — Transport Strategy, Policy & Commute-Allowance Framework (PA-141.1).)
- CTL-653: need-assessment evidence-based (geocoded); strategy approved (CHRO/COO); aligned to workforce plan (VS-103.3)

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual assessment; site add/change triggers a 1-week refresh

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103.3 (workforce planning), VS-19.3 (shifts), VS-28 (analytics), VS-37 (new-store trigger), VS-97/VS-07 (sites), W4244 (operating model), W4250 (routes)

## W4242. Transport Policy, Eligibility & Shift-Coverage Rules

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4241); policy review; DOLE/labor change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | One enterprise transport policy governing ~6,932 employees |
| **Owner** | Head of HR Shared Services / HR Policy (VS-103.1) |
| **Participants** | Labor Relations (VS-84), Legal (VS-100.3), Comp (VS-102), Contingent Workforce (VS-98) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR Policy author the transport policy: who is eligible (shift, role, site, distance, contingent-worker parity per VS-98), service vs allowance rules, late/early-shift coverage windows, no-show/cancellation rules, and guest/visitor rules | HR Policy / Transport Lead | Head of HR Shared Services | Annual |
| 2 | HR Policy/Legal align the policy to DOLE Labor Code (not a wage/benefit guarantee unless CBA — coordinate VS-84), BIR tax treatment of transport allowance (de minimis / non-taxable limits), and contingent-worker co-employment guardrails (VS-98) | HR Policy / Legal / Comp | CHRO / VP Legal | Annual |
| 3 | HR Policy publish the policy (employee handbook / ESS VS-103.1), train site managers on enforcement, and establish the exception/approval path | HR Policy / Site Mgrs | Head of HR Shared Services | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- HR policy/ESS (VS-103.1); HRIS (VS-19); labor relations (VS-84); legal (VS-100.3); comp (VS-102); contingent workforce (VS-98)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-classification risk**: Allowance construed as taxable wage or CBA benefit; mitigated by Legal/Comp tax-treatment review (W4243) and CBA alignment (VS-84)
- **Co-employment risk**: Contingent staff transported without guardrail; mitigated by parity rules aligned to VS-98

### Automation Opportunity
- Policy-in-system eligibility rules (shift/role/site) driving auto-entitlement; ESS policy acknowledgement tracking

### Controls
- CTL-653 (ensure controlled execution — Transport Strategy, Policy & Commute-Allowance Framework (PA-141.1).)
- CTL-653: policy Legal/Comp-reviewed; tax treatment documented (W4243); CBA-consistent (VS-84); published & acknowledged

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks annual policy cycle

### Cross-references
- links to VS-103.1 (policy/ESS), VS-84 (labor/CBA), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-102 (comp), VS-98 (contingent), W4243 (tax), W4251 (entitlement)

## W4243. Transport-Allowance Design, Rate Setting & Tax Treatment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Policy (W4242); annual comp cycle; fuel/price change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on material cost change |
| **Volume** | Allowance rates per site-zone × shift-type, paid to eligible employees |
| **Owner** | Compensation (VS-102) / Finance (VS-17.4) / Payroll (VS-19.2) |
| **Participants** | HR Shared Services (VS-103), Payroll (VS-19.2), Legal/Tax (VS-100.3/VS-79), Finance (VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Comp/Finance set allowance rates by site-zone (distance band), shift type, and mode, benchmarked to local transport cost (fuel, jeepney/tricycle fare, ride-share), and modeled vs the cost of operating a shuttle (W4244) | Comp / Finance | CHRO / CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Tax/Legal determine BIR treatment (de minimis/non-taxable transport allowance limits under TRAIN, or taxable if exceeding), coordinate with VS-79 (EWT/compensation) and VS-19.2 payroll coding | Tax (VS-79) / Legal | VP Legal / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Payroll/Comp configure the allowance in the payroll engine (rate table by zone×shift, eligibility from W4242), test, and launch; monitor cost and tax impact | Payroll (VS-19.2) / Comp | CHRO | Per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Payroll/HRIS (VS-19.2); comp (VS-102); tax (VS-79); finance/FP&A (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Tax-leak risk**: Mis-coded allowance → undisclosed taxable benefit / BIR exposure; mitigated by BIR-limit-aligned rates and Payroll coding
- **Cost-creep risk**: Allowance drifts above shuttle cost; mitigated by modeled shuttle-vs-allowance comparison (W4244) and annual review

### Automation Opportunity
- Rate-table auto-application by zone×shift; payroll-vs-shuttle cost decision-support; tax-limit auto-flagging

### Controls
- CTL-653 (ensure controlled execution — Transport Strategy, Policy & Commute-Allowance Framework (PA-141.1).)
- CTL-653: rates Finance-justified; BIR tax treatment documented (VS-79); payroll-coded & tested (VS-19.2)

### Time Estimate
- ~2 weeks annual design; payroll config ~1 week

### Cross-references
- links to VS-102 (comp), VS-19.2 (payroll), VS-79 (tax), VS-17.4 (finance), W4242 (eligibility), W4244 (model comparison)

## W4244. Transport Operating-Model Decision (Owned vs Leased vs Contracted vs Allowance)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4241); cost review; vendor-market change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on material change |
| **Volume** | Operating-model decision per site-cluster (owned shuttle, leased, contracted charter, rideshare/vanpool, or allowance-only) |
| **Owner** | Transport Lead / Procurement (VS-34) / Fleet (VS-06) / Finance (VS-17.4) |
| **Participants** | Fleet (VS-06), Procurement (VS-34), Finance (VS-17.4), Legal (VS-100.3), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Transport/Finance build the TCO comparison per option (owned fleet incl. driver/depreciation/maintenance/insurance per VS-06/VS-35; leased; contracted charter per-trip; rideshare/vanpool per-employee; vs pure allowance per W4243) | Transport Lead / Finance | COO / CFO | Annual |
| 2 | Procurement/Fleet assess supply market (qualified shuttle/charter vendors per region, LTFRB-franchised), and Legal assess liability/insurance structure for each model (VS-26, VS-100.3) | Procurement / Fleet / Legal | COO | Annual |
| 3 | Transport/Leadership decide the model per cluster, obtain approval, and trigger fleet plan (W4245), vendor sourcing (W4257), or allowance rollout (W4243) | Transport Lead / Finance | COO / CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Fleet/asset (VS-06/VS-35); procurement (VS-34); finance/FP&A (VS-17.4); legal (VS-100.3); insurance (VS-26)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Model-mismatch risk**: Owned fleet where vendor is cheaper (or vice versa); mitigated by per-cluster TCO
- **Liability risk**: Contracted/rideshare shifts liability unclearly; mitigated by Legal/insurance structuring (VS-26/VS-100.3)

### Automation Opportunity
- TCO scenario modeling (owned/leased/contracted/allowance); supply-market cost benchmarking

### Controls
- CTL-653 (ensure controlled execution — Transport Strategy, Policy & Commute-Allowance Framework (PA-141.1).)
- CTL-653: model Finance-TCO-justified per cluster; liability/insurance structured (VS-26); approved (COO/CFO)

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual model decision

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06/VS-35 (fleet/asset), VS-34 (procurement), VS-17.4 (finance), VS-26 (insurance), VS-100.3 (legal), W4243 (allowance), W4245 (fleet), W4257 (vendor)

## W4247. Transport Governance, DOLE/LTFRB Compliance Framework & Insurance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Operating model (W4244); regulatory change; annual review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on regulatory change |
| **Volume** | One transport governance/compliance framework + insurance program |
| **Owner** | Transport Lead / Legal (VS-100.3) / Compliance (VS-22) / HSE (VS-24) |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), Compliance (VS-22), HSE (VS-24), Insurance (VS-26), Fleet (VS-06) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal/Transport map the regulatory regime: LTFRB franchise for any operated/contracted common-carrier passenger service, LTO vehicle registration/inspection, DOLE OSH duty of care for employee transport, and driver licensing (professional license, PNP/LTO clearances) | Legal / Transport Lead | VP Legal / COO | Annual |
| 2 | Insurance/HSE structure the coverage (passenger- liability / vehicle insurance via VS-26, own-damage, acts-of-nature for typhoon-prone routes) and the HSE transport-safety standard (pre-trip inspection, speed, fatigue, no-phone) | Insurance / HSE | CFO / COO | Annual |
| 3 | Compliance document the framework, lodge any required permits/franchise filings (VS-22), train drivers/managers, and schedule periodic compliance review (W4260) | Compliance / Transport Lead | VP Legal | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal/compliance (VS-100.3/VS-22); HSE (VS-24); insurance (VS-26); fleet (VS-06)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Franchise risk**: Operating passenger service without LTFRB franchise → fines/impound; mitigated by franchise mapping & filings
- **Liability gap risk**: Under-insured passenger accident; mitigated by passenger-liability coverage (VS-26) and HSE standard

### Automation Opportunity
- Franchise/insurance/driver-license expiry registry with auto-alerts; digital pre-trip inspection checklist

### Controls
- CTL-127 (ensure employee-transport compliance & vendor settlement.)
- CTL-653: LTFRB franchise/LTO registration confirmed; passenger liability insured (VS-26); HSE transport standard documented (VS-24)

### Time Estimate
- ~3 weeks annual framework; permit filings ongoing

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100.3 (legal), VS-22 (permits/compliance), VS-24 (HSE), VS-26 (insurance), VS-06 (fleet/driver), W4260 (compliance review)
