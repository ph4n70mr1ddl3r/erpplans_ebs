### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Transport/Analytics design routes balancing employee home-cluster coverage, pickup-point safety/accessibility, travel time, shift start/end alignment, and vehicle capacity — using the geocoded need (W4241) and traffic/route data | Transport Lead / Analytics | Head of HR Shared Services | Per review |

## W4250. Daily Shuttle Dispatch, Manifest & Passenger Check-In

| **Volume** | ~6,911 employees; majority of store/DC shift staff boarding daily |
| **Owner** | Transport Dispatcher / Site Transport Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Drivers, Site Managers (VS-07/VS-04), Security (VS-23), HR (VS-103) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dispatcher generates the daily trip manifest (route, driver, vehicle, expected riders from W4251), confirms vehicle/driver readiness (pre-trip W4252), and dispatches on schedule | Dispatcher / Driver | Transport Lead | Per trip |
| 2 | Driver/Coordinator board employees against the manifest (check-in — employee ID/ESS QR), enforce seat-belt/no-overload, and log actual riders/departure/arrival times | Driver / Coordinator | Transport Lead | Per trip |
| 3 | Coordinator closes the trip (actual manifest, no-shows, exceptions), flag issues (overcapacity, refused boarding), and feed ridership data to analytics (W4264) and payroll/allowance (W4253) | Coordinator / Dispatcher | Transport Lead | Per trip |

### Controls

## W5569. Mass-Commute Disruption & Transport-Strike Continuity Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | A citywide or regional commute interruption threatening mass absenteeism on a trading day — a PUV/jeepney strike or transport-group protest, major-flood or landslide road closures on primary commute corridors, or a fuel-supply disruption idling commuter transport across a metro where BuildRight concentrates stores and a DC |
| **Frequency** | ~2–5 disruption events per year across the operating regions (transport-group strikes recur with modernization policy cycles; flood-season road closures are annual); most resolve within one day, the rare multi-day event is the tail case |
| **Volume** | 1 event × the affected metro's sites (30–80 stores + 1 DC) × the affected sites' crews (typically 30–60% of store headcount commutes by public transport) × the trading day's staffing requirement |
| **Owner** | Transport Lead / Fleet Planning (VS-06) (continuity protocol); Site Managers (attendance execution) |
| **Participants** | Store/DC Managers (site staffing decisions), HR Shared Services (VS-103.1 attendance & leave placement), Regional Managers (multi-site calls), Fleet Dispatch (shuttle surge per W4250), Payroll (VS-19.2, attendance coding), VS-19.3 (scheduling), Communications/VS-63 (employee & customer messaging), Regional Manager closure authority (the E-02 custody where trading cannot be sustained) |

### Background

The corpus owns the site and the vehicle: typhoon/flood protocols (W576/W848/W1387) protect *stores*, W850/W2510 decide whether a *site* opens, W4255 responds to a *shuttle* incident, and W484/W854 handled pandemic attendance — but nobody owned the commute-interruption event, where every site is open and reachable-yet-unstaffed: the crews cannot get in. A PUV-modernization-class strike strands 30–60% of a metro store's crew with zero damage to the building; the flood-closure variant strands crews on the far side of impassable corridors. The event needs an owner for the attendance-forecast call, the skeleton-crew and critical-role matrix (cashiers, keyholders, receiving), the shuttle-surge and pickup-consolidation plan, the ride-share/allowance activation under W4253, the attendance coding that keeps payroll honest, and the customer-impact decision riding the existing closure custody.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Disruptor & scope call**: classify the event (strike vs flood-closure vs fuel) from news/authoritative feeds; map affected corridors against the site roster; forecast attendance impact per site from commute-mode data (W4251/W4253 profiles) | Transport Lead | HR Shared Services | 1–2 hours (day before or 5–6 AM) |
| 2 | **Attendance directive**: site-level staffing directive — report-if-you-can with no-penalty absence coding for stranded crews; critical-role matrix activates (keyholders, cashiers, receiving; department minimums) | HR Shared Services / Site Managers | Regional Manager | Same morning |
| 3 | **Continuity transport**: shuttle surge on consolidated pickup points (W4250 manifest discipline), ride-share/van-pool allowance activation per W4253 for critical roles, carpool coordination via the site channels | Fleet Dispatch / Transport Lead | Transport Lead | Event-long |
| 4 | **Trading decision**: open-with-skeleton-crew is the default with service-level expectations set (lane count, services suspended); where staffing or safety cannot sustain trading, closure rides the E-02 custody — W2510's decision matrix, W850's mechanics; documented either way | Regional Manager | VP Store Operations | On threshold |
| 5 | **Customer & employee messaging**: opening status and service levels pushed to store channels, app/store locator and local social; employees get the transport options and the no-penalty confirmation in one communication | VS-63 / Communications | VP Store Operations | Same morning |
| 6 | **Payroll & leave integrity**: attendance coding per the directive (transport-disruption code — no absence-hit, no unverified absent); partial-day attendance tracked; the coding rides W10-class payroll rails | Payroll (VS-19.2) | CHRO | Per cycle |
| 7 | **After-action**: attendance-vs-forecast reconciliation, shuttle-surge utilization, corridor vulnerability register update (chronic corridors pre-plan consolidated pickups), and the multi-day playbook refresh | Transport Lead / HR | Head of HR Shared Services | Post-event |

### System Touchpoints

- Workforce-management attendance codes with the transport-disruption category and no-penalty flag (links to W561/W10)
- Shuttle dispatch console with surge manifests and consolidated pickup points (links to W4250)
- Ride-share/allowance activation record under the W4253 entitlement rules
- Store-communication cascade and app/store-locator status flags (links to VS-63, VS-75)
- Closure-decision record where trading cannot be sustained (links to the E-02 custody, W2510/W850)
- Corridor-vulnerability register keyed to the site roster for pre-planning

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Under-staffed-trading risk**: opening on a skeleton crew without setting service expectations produces queue failures and safety issues at lumber-yard loading; mitigated by Step 4's explicit service-level matrix
- **Penalty-absence risk**: stranded crews charged with unexcused absences, driving grievance and attrition; mitigated by Step 2's no-penalty coding directive and Step 6's payroll integrity
- **Keyholder single-point risk**: the one keyholder stranded closes the store regardless of crew; mitigated by Step 2's critical-role matrix with cross-trained backups
- **Ad-hoc-transport risk**: managers improvising unreimbursed pickups; mitigated by Step 3's W4253-gated allowance activation with manifest discipline
- **Silent-closure risk**: a store silently unopened while signage says open; mitigated by Step 5's status push and the E-02 custody documentation

### Automation Opportunity

- Attendance-impact forecast model keyed to commute-mode profiles and corridor status feeds (replaces manual Step 1 estimation)
- One-tap transport-disruption attendance code in the WFM app with payroll-rule auto-apply (replaces manual Step 6 coding)
- Shuttle-surge manifest template with consolidated pickup points pre-computed per corridor scenario (replaces ad-hoc dispatch)
- Store-locator/app status flags driven from the event record (replaces phone-tree status calls)

### Controls

- CTL-654 (ensure controlled execution — Shuttle Service, Route & Daily Commute Operations (PA-141.2).)
- operational: attendance coding under a declared commute disruption uses the no-penalty category verified against the event record; surge transport runs on W4250 manifests with W4253-gated allowances; any trading-day closure rides the E-02 custody and is documented either way

### Time Estimate

Per event day ≈ 30–60 minutes per affected site for the staffing call and messaging (≈ 15–30 staff-hours per 30-site metro) plus 4–8 hours of transport-coordination and payroll coding centrally; at the 2–5-events-per-year cadence this stays absorbed within site management, HR Shared Services and the transport program — no incremental headcount.

### Staffing Implication

- Absorbed by site management, HR Shared Services, Payroll and the transport program; no incremental headcount.

### Cross-references
- links to W4250 (shuttle surge manifests), W4251/W4253 (commute profiles & allowance activation), W4255 (vehicle-level incident boundary), W561/W10 (attendance & payroll coding), VS-63/VS-75 (status messaging), VS-103.1 (leave placement), W2510/W850 (E-02 closure custody), W576/W848/W1387 (site-protection boundary — the event targets the commute, not the site)
