## W4257. Shuttle/Charter Vendor Sourcing, Qualification & Contracting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Operating model (W4244) selecting contracted service; vendor-market review; expansion |
| **Frequency** | Per sourcing cycle + onboarding |
| **Volume** | Regional shuttle/charter vendors across Mindanao/Visayas/Luzon/NCR |
| **Owner** | Procurement (VS-34) / Transport Lead |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), Insurance (VS-26), HSE (VS-24), Finance (VS-17.4), Contingent Workforce (VS-98) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement/Transport run a sourcing event (RFP/RFQ) for shuttle/charter vendors per region, defining service specs (routes, shifts, vehicle standard, driver standard, safety/insurance) | Procurement / Transport Lead | COO | Per cycle |
| 2 | Procurement/Legal/HSE qualify bidders (LTFRB franchise, LTO-registered vehicles, driver licensing/clearance, passenger-liability insurance, HSE/DOLE record, financial standing) | Procurement / Legal / HSE | COO | Per cycle |
| 3 | Procurement/Legal negotiate and execute the master service agreement (rates, SLA, insurance indemnity, termination), onboard the vendor (coordinate VS-98 for embedded drivers), and load to vendor master (VS-03) | Procurement / Legal | COO / CFO | Per vendor |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/sourcing (VS-34); vendor master (VS-03); legal/contract (VS-100.3); insurance (VS-26); HSE (VS-24); contingent workforce (VS-98)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unqualified-carrier risk**: Vendor without franchise/insurance → impound/liability; mitigated by qualification gate (LTFRB/insurance)
- **Co-employment risk**: Embedded drivers misclassified; mitigated by VS-98 guardrails

### Automation Opportunity
- Vendor qualification checklist with document expiry tracking; e-sourcing; contract repository

### Controls
- CTL-655 (ensure controlled execution — Transport Vendor, Safety, Cost & Commute Analytics (PA-141.3).)
- CTL-655: vendor LTFRB-franchised & insured (VS-26); HSE/DOLE record checked; contract Legal-reviewed (VS-100.3)

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks per sourcing cycle; onboarding ~1–2 weeks/vendor

### Cross-references
- links to VS-34 (procurement), VS-03 (vendor master), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-26 (insurance), VS-24 (HSE), VS-98 (contingent drivers), W4258 (SLA)

## W4258. Transport Vendor SLA, Performance & Rate Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contract start (W4257); monthly QBR; SLA breach |
| **Frequency** | Monthly review + continuous monitoring |
| **Volume** | SLA performance per vendor × route × month |
| **Owner** | Transport Lead / Procurement (VS-34) |
| **Participants** | Site Managers, Finance (VS-17.4), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Transport define and track the SLA (on-time pickup/arrival, vehicle/driver standard adherence, safety incidents, no-show response time, complaint resolution), and capture data from dispatch (W4250) | Transport Lead / Dispatcher | COO | Monthly |
| 2 | Transport/Procurement run the monthly/quarterly business review with vendors (performance scorecard, breach penalties/incentives, capacity changes), and manage rate changes (fuel-index clauses) | Transport Lead / Procurement | COO | Monthly/Qtrly |
| 3 | Transport manage corrective action/penalty enforcement, vendor development, and exit/replacement on sustained underperformance (coordinate VS-03/VS-34) | Transport Lead / Procurement / Legal | COO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Dispatch/ridership (W4250); vendor scorecard (VS-67); procurement/contract (VS-34); HSE (VS-24); finance (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **SLA-drift risk**: Late pickups unchecked → attendance impact; mitigated by on-time SLA tracking + penalties
- **Cost-creep risk**: Fuel-index/auto rate hikes unmanaged; mitigated by rate governance + benchmarking

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto SLA computation from GPS/dispatch data; vendor scorecard dashboard; penalty auto-trigger

### Controls
- CTL-655 (ensure controlled execution — Transport Vendor, Safety, Cost & Commute Analytics (PA-141.3).)
- CTL-655: SLA tracked & enforced; rate changes governed/justified; underperformance acted on

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days/month review

### Cross-references
- links to VS-34 (procurement), VS-67 (scorecard), VS-03 (vendor), VS-24 (HSE), VS-17.4 (finance), W4250 (data), W4259 (invoice)

## W4259. Transport Vendor Invoice, Reconciliation & Settlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly vendor billing cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | Invoices per vendor (per-trip or monthly charter billing) |
| **Owner** | Accounts Payable (VS-15) / Finance (VS-17.4) / Transport Lead |
| **Participants** | AP (VS-15), Finance (VS-17.4), Procurement (VS-34), Transport |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AP/Transport receive the vendor invoice (per-trip manifest count or fixed monthly charter), match to contracted rates (W4257) and actual trip/ridership records (W4250) | AP / Transport Lead | Finance Controller | Monthly |
| 2 | AP/Finance reconcile discrepancies (unrun trips, SLA penalties W4258, fuel-index adjustments), apply holdback/penalties, and resolve with the vendor | AP / Finance / Procurement | Finance Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | AP process payment per terms (VS-15 P2P), post to GL/allocation (W4246), and retain audit evidence (BIR-compliant OR/invoice per VS-79) | AP / GL (VS-17.1) | CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- AP/P2P (VS-15); GL/allocation (VS-17.1/W4246); contract/rate (VS-34); manifest (W4250); BIR invoicing (VS-79)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-billing risk**: Invoiced trips not run; mitigated by manifest-vs-invoice match before payment
- **Tax-compliance risk**: Non-BIR-compliant vendor invoices → disallowed expense; mitigated by OR/invoice validation (VS-79)

### Automation Opportunity
- Manifest→invoice auto-match; penalty auto-deduction; 3-way-style reconciliation; BIR-invoice validation

### Controls
- CTL-127 (ensure employee-transport compliance & vendor settlement.)
- CTL-655: invoice matched to manifest/rate before payment; penalties applied; BIR-compliant invoice retained (VS-79)

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days/month reconciliation

### Cross-references
- links to VS-15 (P2P/AP), VS-17.1 (GL), VS-17.4 (finance), VS-34 (contract/rate), VS-79 (BIR), W4250 (manifest), W4258 (penalties), W4246 (allocation)

## W4264. Transport Program Performance Review & Reporting

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly/annual reporting cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + annual |
| **Volume** | One transport program scorecard for leadership |
| **Owner** | Transport Lead / Finance (VS-17.4) / HR (VS-103) |
| **Participants** | Analytics (VS-28), HSE (VS-24), EX (VS-103.2), Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Transport/Analytics assemble the program scorecard (cost-per-employee W4261, on-time SLA W4258, safety incidents W4255, ridership/utilization W4250, commute-EX W4248, attrition correlation W4262) | Transport Lead / Analytics | COO / CHRO | Monthly |
| 2 | Transport present the review to leadership (COO/CHRO/CFO), flag risks (safety, cost, EX, compliance W4260), and secure decisions/investment | Transport Lead | COO / CHRO / CFO | Monthly/Qtrly |

- Balanced-scorecard dashboard; auto KPI rollup; audit-evidence pack generation

### Controls
- CTL-127 (ensure employee-transport compliance & vendor settlement.)
- CTL-655: balanced scorecard (cost/safety/EX/compliance); controls audit-assured (VS-21); feeds strategy/budget (VS-33)

### Time Estimate
- ~2 days/month; ~1 week annual review
