## W2825. Sari-Sari Store & MSME Prospecting, Onboarding & KYC

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Field acquisition campaign; in-store sign-up |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | Target ~50,000 active MSME partners within 3 years |
| **Owner** | MSME Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | MSME Sales, Field Sales, Compliance, AR |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Field Sales prospects sari-sari stores, hardware mini-marts, small contractors in store catchment; registers via app with store photo, location, owner ID | Field Sales | MSME Sales Manager | Per account |
| 2 | Compliance performs KYC: DTI/SEC/Mayor's permit, owner valid ID, BIR TIN; validates business legitimacy; screens for sanctions/AML (W2921 linkage) | Compliance / AR | MSME Sales Manager | 1–2 days |
| 3 | AR creates MSME account in customer master with tier, credit terms, default reorder list; activates in loyalty-as-reseller program | AR | AR Manager | Per account |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile sign-up app; customer master; KYC workflow; sanctions screening

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Fake/unverified MSME accounts enable fraud; mitigated by ID/permit validation and sanctions screening


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-register of "registers via app with store photo, location, owner ID" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validates business legitimacy" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "account in customer master with tier, credit terms, default reorder list" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-206 (ensure MSME micro-wholesale credit & order integrity.)
- operational: ID/permit validation and sanctions screening

### Time Estimate
- 15–30 min per account

## W2832. MSME Account Health, Churn Prevention & Win-Back

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Account inactivity alert (no order > 30 days) |
| **Frequency** | Weekly review + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~50,000 active accounts |
| **Owner** | MSME Sales Manager |
| **Participants** | MSME Sales, Field Sales, Loyalty |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Loyalty/MSME Sales monitors account health score (recency, frequency, basket trend); flags at-risk accounts for intervention | Loyalty / MSME Sales | MSME Sales Manager | Weekly |
| 2 | Field Sales contacts at-risk MSME; diagnoses reason (price, competition, stockout, closure); offers targeted win-back incentive | Field Sales | MSME Sales Manager | Per account |

- System auto-report of "reports churn rate and win-back conversion" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-475 (ensure controlled execution — MSME/Sari-Sari Account Acquisition & Micro-Distribution (PA-82.1).)
- operational: health-score monitoring and proactive outreach

### Time Estimate
