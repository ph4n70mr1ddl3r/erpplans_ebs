# VS-19: Hire-to-Retire

> **People** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

## Overview

Hire-to-retire: recruitment, payroll & compensation, workforce management, learning, and separation for 6,925 employees across 5 entities.

## Why it matters

6,925 employees, ~1,200–1,600 hires/yr, 15–20% turnover; payroll accuracy, statutory benefits (SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG/13th-month), and DOLE labor-code compliance are mandatory and high-volume.
