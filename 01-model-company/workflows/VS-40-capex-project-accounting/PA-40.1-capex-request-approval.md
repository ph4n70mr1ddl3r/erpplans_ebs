## W1811. Capital Expenditure Request Submission

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Identified need for capital investment (new store, renovation, equipment, IT project) |
| **Frequency** | ~30–50 capex requests/year |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 new stores + ~20–30 renovation/IT projects = ~30–50 requests |
| **Owner** | Requesting Department Head |
| **Participants** | Requesting Department Head, VP Store Operations, COO, CIO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Requesting Department Head submits capex request in ERP: (a) project name and description, (b) business justification: why needed, what problem solved, what opportunity captured, (c) capex type: new store construction, store renovation, DC expansion, IT infrastructure, vehicle/fleet, equipment replacement, (d) estimated total cost (breakdown: materials, labor, equipment, services, contingency 10–15%), (e) expected timeline: start date, milestones, completion date, (f) expected benefits: revenue increase, cost reduction, risk mitigation, compliance requirement, (g) priority: mandatory (regulatory/safety), growth (new revenue), maintenance (renewal/replacement), discretionary | Department Head | VP / C-Suite sponsor | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Supporting documentation attached: (a) for new stores: site plan, lease summary, financial model per W1652, (b) for IT projects: vendor proposals, technical specifications, implementation plan, (c) for renovations: scope of work, contractor quotes, before/after photos, (d) for equipment: vendor quotes (minimum 2 for items > PHP 500K), technical specifications, installation requirements | Department Head | — | 1–2 days |
| 3 | System validates completeness: (a) all required fields populated, (b) supporting documents attached, (c) cost estimate within reasonable range (system flags outliers vs. historical benchmarks), (d) request routed to financial evaluation per W1812 | System | — | Automated |


### System Touchpoints
- Capex request form in ERP — project description, justification, cost estimate, category (step 1)
- Supporting documentation attachment — site plans, lease summaries, financial models per W1652 (step 2)
- System completeness validation before routing (step 3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Weak-justification risk**: requests approved on narrative enthusiasm without quantified benefit; mitigated by mandatory business-case fields and documentation (steps 1–2)
- **Cost-underestimate risk**: estimates anchored optimistically to ease approval; mitigated by reasonableness validation and the W1812 financial evaluation (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-submit of "submits capex request in ERP" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "Supporting documentation attached" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "System validates completeness" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-361 (ensure controlled execution — Capital Expenditure Request & Approval (PA-40.1).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
2–4 days elapsed per occurrence (1–2 days + 1–2 days) — at ~30–50 capex requests/year ≈ 30–50 occurrences/year.

## W1812. Capex Financial Evaluation & Business Case Review

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Capex request submitted and validated (W1811) |
| **Frequency** | ~30–50 evaluations/year |
| **Volume** | 1 evaluation per request |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, Requesting Department Head, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A performs financial evaluation: (a) **total investment cost**: all capitalized costs including materials, labor, equipment, installation, testing, project management, (b) **operating cash flows**: incremental revenue, cost savings, or avoided costs per year for project life, (c) **net present value (NPV)**: discount at company's weighted average cost of capital (WACC ~10–12% for Philippine retail), (d) **internal rate of return (IRR)**: target > 15% for growth projects; no minimum for mandatory/regulatory, (e) **payback period**: target < 3 years for stores; < 2 years for IT/equipment, (f) **sensitivity analysis**: NPV at -10% revenue, +10% cost, 1-year delay | FP&A | CFO | 4–8 hours/request |
| 2 | FP&A assesses strategic alignment: (a) maps to Board-approved strategic priorities per W1645, (b) evaluates against alternative uses of capital (opportunity cost), (c) considers timing: can this be deferred without significant impact? (d) reviews against annual capex budget affordability (total requested vs. available capital) | FP&A | CFO | 2 hours/request |
| 3 | FP&A prepares financial evaluation summary: (a) recommendation: approve, approve with conditions, defer, reject, (b) key assumptions and risks, (c) comparison to similar completed projects (actual vs. projected returns) | FP&A | CFO | 1 day |


### System Touchpoints
- Financial evaluation model — total investment cost, NPV, IRR, payback per project (step 1)
- Strategic alignment assessment vs. Board priorities and alternatives (step 2)
- Evaluation summary with recommendation and key assumptions (step 3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Assumption-optimism risk**: rosier assumptions than the W1645 corporate baseline inflate returns; mitigated by assumption disclosure in the summary (step 3)
- **Alternative-blindness risk**: a project approved when a cheaper alternative (lease vs. build) serves better; mitigated by explicit alternatives evaluation (step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "discount at company's weighted average cost of capital" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews against annual capex budget affordability" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-generate of "prepares financial evaluation summary" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-210 (ensure capex authorization & commitment control.)
- operational: FP&A review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
1 day elapsed per occurrence (1 day) — at ~30–50 evaluations/year ≈ 30–50 occurrences/year.

## W1813. Capex Approval Workflow & Authorization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Financial evaluation completed (W1812) |
| **Frequency** | ~30–50 approvals/year |
| **Volume** | 1 approval per request |
| **Owner** | Approver per DOA matrix |
| **Participants** | Department Head, FP&A, CFO, CEO, Board |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tiered approval per Delegation of Authority per W1732: (a) < PHP 1M: CFO approval (based on FP&A recommendation), (b) PHP 1M–10M: CEO approval (CFO endorses), (c) PHP 10M–50M: Board committee approval (Executive Committee), (d) > PHP 50M: Full Board approval (presented at board meeting per W1714) | Approver per tier | Per DOA | 1–5 days |
| 2 | Approval workflow in system: (a) request routed sequentially: FP&A → CFO → CEO → Board committee → Board (depending on amount), (b) each approver reviews financial evaluation and supporting documents in system, (c) approver records decision: approved, approved with conditions, deferred, rejected — with comments, (d) system enforces approval limits (approver cannot exceed DOA threshold) | System | — | 1–5 days |
| 3 | Conditions of approval (if any): (a) reduce scope to fit budget, (b) obtain additional vendor quotes, (c) revise timeline, (d) phase the project over 2+ years, (e) funding source reallocation (defer another project) | CFO / CEO | Department Head | 1–3 days |
| 4 | Approved request creates capex project in system: (a) unique project number (format: CPX-YYYY-NNN), (b) approved budget, (c) project timeline and milestones, (d) cost center and entity assignment, (e) budget reserved against annual capex plan per W1648 | System | FP&A | Automated |


### System Touchpoints
- Tiered approval routing per DOA (W1732) — CFO < PHP 1M; CEO PHP 1–10M; Board committee/Board above (step 1)
- Sequential system workflow — FP&A → CFO → CEO → Board with audit trail (step 2)
- Conditions-of-approval capture (scope reduction, quotes, revised timeline) (step 3)
- Capex project creation — CPX-YYYY-NNN number, budget, timeline (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Approval-bypass risk**: spending begins before authorization; mitigated by system gating — no project number, no PO (steps 2, 4)
- **Ambiguous-conditions risk**: verbal conditions never enforced; mitigated by documented conditions tracked to satisfaction before commitment (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "Approval workflow in system" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-capture of "center and entity assignment" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-210 (ensure capex authorization & commitment control.)
- operational: Approver per DOA matrix review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
3–13 days elapsed per occurrence (1–5 days + 1–5 days + 1–3 days) — at ~30–50 approvals/year ≈ 30–50 occurrences/year.

## W1814. Capex Budget Allocation & Commitment Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Capex request approved (W1813) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~30–50 active capex projects at any time |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, CFO, Project Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A allocates approved budget to capex project: (a) breaks down total budget by cost category: construction, equipment, materials, services, contingency, (b) assigns entity: which of the 5 legal entities bears the cost, (c) links to funding source: retained earnings, bank loan, lease financing | FP&A | CFO | 1 day |
| 2 | System tracks committed costs: (a) **committed**: POs issued for the project (not yet received/invoiced), (b) **incurred**: invoices received and matched to PO/GR, (c) **paid**: payments made to vendors/contractors, (d) **remaining budget**: approved budget − committed − incurred, (e) **forecast to complete**: Project Manager estimate of remaining costs | System | FP&A | Continuous |
| 3 | Monthly: FP&A reviews all active capex projects: (a) budget utilization (% spent vs. % complete), (b) cost variance (actual vs. budget at current completion), (c) schedule variance (actual vs. planned milestones), (d) forecast to complete vs. remaining budget, (e) flag: projects trending > 110% of budget | FP&A | CFO | 1–2 days/month |
| 4 | Quarterly: CFO presents capex portfolio status to Board: (a) total capex spent vs. annual budget, (b) major project updates (new stores, DC, IT), (c) budget overruns and corrective actions, (d) revised full-year capex forecast | CFO | Board | Quarterly |


### System Touchpoints
- GL budget allocation by cost category — construction, equipment, materials, professional fees, contingency (step 1)
- Budgetary-control funds checking with committed vs. incurred cost tracking — POs issued vs. invoiced (step 2 — fit-gap F6)
- Monthly portfolio review — utilization % vs. completion %, variance (step 3)
- Quarterly Board capex portfolio report (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Commitment-blindness risk**: overruns hidden in issued-but-uninvoiced POs; mitigated by committed + incurred tracking (step 2)
- **Portfolio-skew risk**: early projects consume the annual envelope, starving later priorities; mitigated by quarterly portfolio review (step 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-assign of "allocates approved budget to capex project" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews all active capex projects" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-210 (ensure capex authorization & commitment control.)
- operational: FP&A review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
1 day elapsed per occurrence (1 day); cadence-carried steps as authored (Continuous, 1–2 days/month, Quarterly) — cadence: Continuous; annual effort is event-driven (annualize at realized volume).

## W1815. Capex Request Revision & Scope Change Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project scope change, cost increase, or timeline extension |
| **Frequency** | ~10–20 revisions/year |
| **Volume** | ~20–30% of projects require revision |
| **Owner** | Project Manager |
| **Participants** | Project Manager, FP&A, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project Manager identifies need for scope change: (a) construction discovers unforeseen site conditions (common in Philippine construction — soil issues, utility relocation), (b) vendor price increase since original quote, (c) design change requested by Store Operations, (d) regulatory requirement not initially anticipated, (e) scope reduction opportunity (value engineering) | Project Manager | Department Head | Event-driven |
| 2 | Project Manager submits change order request: (a) description of change, (b) reason for change, (c) cost impact (increase or decrease), (d) schedule impact, (e) revised total project cost and timeline | Project Manager | FP&A | 1–2 days |
| 3 | FP&A evaluates change order: (a) revised NPV/IRR if revenue impact, (b) revised budget vs. available contingency, (c) if within original budget + contingency (< 10% increase): CFO approves, (d) if exceeding contingency or > 10% budget increase: requires approval per W1813 tiers | FP&A | CFO | 1–2 days |
| 4 | System updates project budget with approved change order; revised budget tracked going forward | System | FP&A | Automated |


### System Touchpoints
- Change order request workflow — description, reason, cost impact (step 2)
- FP&A evaluation — revised returns, contingency availability (step 3)
- Revised budget tracking post-approval (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scope-creep risk**: change orders approved piecemeal until economics invert; mitigated by revised NPV/IRR evaluation per change (step 3)
- **Contingency-exhaustion risk**: contingency silently consumed by routine changes; mitigated by contingency visibility in every evaluation (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-request of "requested by Store Operations" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-submit of "submits change order request" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "FP&A evaluates change order" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-210 (ensure capex authorization & commitment control.)
- operational: Project Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
2–4 days elapsed per occurrence (1–2 days + 1–2 days); cadence-carried steps as authored (Event-driven) — at ~10–20 revisions/year ≈ 10–20 occurrences/year.

## W1816. Emergency Capex Request Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Urgent capital need (equipment failure, safety issue, storm damage, regulatory order) |
| **Frequency** | ~5–10 emergency requests/year |
| **Volume** | Low volume but time-critical |
| **Owner** | Department Head |
| **Participants** | Department Head, CEO, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emergency triggers: (a) store HVAC failure in summer (cannot operate without cooling in Philippine heat), (b) typhoon damage to store structure, (c) forklift failure at DC (halts operations), (d) BIR order to upgrade POS system (compliance deadline), (e) fire damage to store or DC | Department Head | CEO | Immediate |
| 2 | Department Head contacts CEO/CFO directly (phone/email): (a) describes emergency, (b) estimated cost, (c) business impact of delay, (d) proposed solution and timeline | Department Head | — | 1–2 hours |
| 3 | CEO provides verbal approval; CFO authorizes emergency procurement: (a) procurement can proceed without full financial evaluation (W1812 bypassed), (b) PO issued with emergency flag, (c) retroactive documentation completed within 5 business days (full capex request, financial evaluation, formal approval) | CEO / CFO | — | Same day |
| 4 | Retroactive formal approval completed; project entered in system with "Emergency" designation; Board informed at next meeting if amount > PHP 10M | Department Head / FP&A | CFO | Within 5 days |


### System Touchpoints
- Emergency triggers and direct CEO/CFO contact path (steps 1–2)
- Verbal approval with emergency procurement authorization (step 3)
- Retroactive formalization with "Emergency" designation and Board notification (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Emergency-label-abuse risk**: non-urgent requests routed as emergencies to skip evaluation; mitigated by retroactive review and Board visibility (step 4)
- **Paper-trail-gap risk**: verbal approvals never documented; mitigated by mandatory retroactive system entry (step 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System workflow notification of "contacts CEO/CFO directly" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "documentation completed within 5 business days" (replaces manual Step 3).
- System auto-capture of "entered in system" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-361 (ensure controlled execution — Capital Expenditure Request & Approval (PA-40.1).)
- operational: Department Head review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
~3–6 hours per emergency capex event (direct CEO/CFO contact 1–2 hours + same-day verbal approval and procurement authorization + retroactive formal approval within 5 days) × ~5–10 events/year ≈ 15–60 hours/year.

## W1817. Capex Requisition to Purchase Order Conversion

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Capex request approved (W1813); procurement ready to proceed |
| **Frequency** | ~30–50 PO conversions/year (multiple POs per project) |
| **Volume** | ~100–200 capex POs/year |
| **Owner** | Procurement |
| **Participants** | Procurement, Project Manager, Department Head |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement converts approved capex request to purchase requisition: (a) breaks project into procurement packages (construction, equipment, services), (b) each package becomes a separate PO with vendor, items, quantities, prices, (c) PO references capex project number for cost tracking | Procurement | Department Head | 1–3 days |
| 2 | Procurement sources vendors per standard procurement process: (a) competitive bid for items > PHP 500K (minimum 3 quotes), (b) negotiated contract for construction (per VS-03), (c) select best value (not just lowest cost — consider quality, timeline, warranty), (d) PO issued with delivery terms aligned to project timeline | Procurement | Department Head | 1–4 weeks |
| 3 | PO linked to capex project: (a) system auto-commits PO amount against project budget, (b) project budget report shows: committed (POs), incurred (invoices), remaining, (c) PO approval per standard DOA (not capex DOA — PO approver verifies that capex project exists and has budget) | System / Procurement | FP&A | Per PO |


### System Touchpoints
- Requisition conversion with procurement packaging (construction, equipment, services) (step 1)
- Vendor sourcing — competitive bid > PHP 500K, minimum 3 quotes (step 2)
- PO-to-project linkage with automatic budget commitment (step 3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unlinked-PO risk**: project POs booked outside the capex project, understating spend; mitigated by PO referencing the CPX project number with auto-commit (step 3)
- **Bid-threshold-splitting risk**: packages carved below the PHP 500K bid threshold; mitigated by package-level review in sourcing (steps 1–2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-track of "Procurement converts approved capex request to purchase requisition" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-issue of "issued with delivery terms aligned to project timeline" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approver verifies that capex project exists and has budget" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-361 (ensure controlled execution — Capital Expenditure Request & Approval (PA-40.1).)
- operational: Procurement review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
Per capex project ~3–5 days of procurement effort (request-to-requisition conversion 1–3 days + vendor sourcing over 1–4 weeks elapsed; PO-to-project budget commitment automated) × ~30–50 projects/year ≈ 90–250 days/year at ~100–200 capex POs.

## W1818. Capex Approval Documentation & Audit Trail

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Capex request created; maintained throughout project lifecycle |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | All capex projects (~30–50/year) |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, Internal Audit, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Required documentation per capex project: (a) original request with business case and financial evaluation, (b) approval documentation (system approval trail or board resolution), (c) vendor quotes and selection rationale, (d) POs linked to project, (e) change orders with approval, (f) progress reports and milestone confirmations, (g) completion certificate and asset turnover documentation per W1827, (h) post-implementation review per W2748 | FP&A / Project Manager | CFO | Continuous |
| 2 | System maintains complete audit trail: (a) who submitted, reviewed, approved at each stage, (b) dates and timestamps, (c) version history for revised requests, (d) linked documents accessible from project record | System | — | Automated |
| 3 | External auditor reviews capex documentation during annual audit: (a) sample of capex projects: verify approval per DOA, verify cost capitalization per PFRS, verify project completion and asset turnover, (b) assess internal controls over capex process | External Auditor | CFO | During annual audit |


### System Touchpoints
- Project documentation set — request, business case, evaluation, approvals, change orders (step 1)
- System audit trail — submitter/reviewer/approver with timestamps (step 2)
- External auditor sample review against DOA (step 3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Audit-trail-break risk**: approvals happen outside the system (email/verbal) leaving gaps; mitigated by system-workflow routing as the approval channel (step 2)
- **Document-scatter risk**: approvals and evaluations filed in personal drives; mitigated by the project documentation set retention (step 1)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of the "Required documentation per capex project" checklist (replaces manual Step 1 assembly).
- System auto-refresh of the CIP project record from posted transactions (maintains the automated Step 2 feed).
- System auto-review of "reviews capex documentation during annual audit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-361 (ensure controlled execution — Capital Expenditure Request & Approval (PA-40.1).)
- operational: FP&A review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
Capex documentation assembly continuous within project administration across ~30–50 projects/year (approval audit trail automated); external-audit sample support ≈ 2–3 days/year at the annual audit.
