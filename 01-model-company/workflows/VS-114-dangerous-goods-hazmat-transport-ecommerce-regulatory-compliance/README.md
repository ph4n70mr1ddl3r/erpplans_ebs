# VS-114: Dangerous Goods (DG) & Hazmat Transport, Ecommerce & Regulatory Compliance

> **Governance & Assurance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Dangerous Goods (DG) & Hazardous Materials Transport, Ecommerce & Regulatory Compliance workflows for
BuildRight Depot Corp. — governing the **enterprise compliance program for the movement and shipment
of hazardous products** across the supply chain and customer channels. The assortment is
DG-intensive: Paint & Finishes (~2,800 SKUs — flammable liquids/solvents, aerosols), adhesives and
thinners, garden/agro chemicals (fertilizers, pesticides), cleaning chemicals, fuels/lubricants,
aerosol-powered products, gas cylinders, lithium-battery products (power tools, appliances), and
cement/silica (respirable hazard) — collectively 8–10%+ of the 35,000-SKU assortment. The model
company profile (§6.2, §7) shows these goods moving by import ocean freight (~40% import, ~400–600
TEUs/month), inter-island domestic sea/land, DC-to-store distribution, and ecommerce last-mile
(~42,900 ecommerce orders/month), and the DCs maintain "special handling areas: paint/chemicals"
(§3.2). VS-24.3 (HSE) covers **fixed-site** storage/handling safety at DCs (segregation, GHS
labels, spill containment, ventilation, fire suppression, SDS, DENR ECC) and VS-87 covers customs
**import** compliance; but no value stream owns the **transport, ecommerce-shipment, and enterprise
regulatory lifecycle** of dangerous goods: DG classification (UN number, hazard class, packing
group), transport-documentation and carrier-qualification for sea (IMDG)/air (IATA)/road (ADR/DOT),
ecommerce ship-eligibility and channel rules (the model-company reality that "hazmat shipping
restrictions for paint, solvents, and chemicals" limit courier options), DG packaging/marking/
labeling for transport (distinct from VS-111 consumer packaging), DENR-EMB hazardous-waste
transport manifest (RA 6969) for off-site disposal, DG worker/handler training, DG transport
incident/spill response, DG site permitting (BFP Fire Code for hazmat storage), and DG insurance/
liability. This value stream owns the enterprise DG/hazmat compliance program: classification and
inventory, training, packaging/labeling, procurement/import compliance, and program governance;
transport carrier qualification, documentation, modal compliance (IMDG/IATA/ADR), ecommerce
ship-eligibility, last-mile/3PL DG management, transport incident response, and transport analytics;
and fixed-site storage compliance (in partnership with VS-24.3), worker handling safety, spill
response/remediation, hazardous-waste transport/disposal, incident investigation, site permitting,
insurance/liability, and compliance analytics.

This is distinct from **VS-24.3 (HSE — DC/Store Hazmat)** which manages **fixed-site** worker and
facility safety for hazmat *storage* (this value stream owns the *transport, ecommerce-shipment, and
enterprise-regulatory* lifecycle, and partners with VS-24.3 on storage-compliance consistency). It
is distinct from **VS-87 (Customs Trade Compliance)** which handles **import** duty/tariff/customs
(this value stream handles DG transport regulations across all legs, including imported DG). It is
distinct from **VS-89 (Product Recall)** which manages **defective-product recall** (this value
stream manages the compliant movement and shipment of hazmat in normal operations; a hazmat product
recall would be led by VS-89 with DG-compliant reverse transport from this value stream). It is
distinct from **VS-111 (Packaging)** which engineers **product/transport packaging** generally
(this value stream specifies DG-compliant UN-certified packaging, marking, and labeling for
hazardous goods). It is distinct from **VS-06/VS-110/VS-56 (Logistics/Freight/3PL)** which *execute*
transport (this value stream governs the DG-compliance requirements carriers must meet). It is
distinct from **VS-73 (Waste)** which manages general store waste (this value stream manages the
DG-regulated hazardous-waste transport and disposal manifest). DG & hazmat compliance is the
*enterprise regulatory program for the movement and shipment of hazardous goods* — distinct from
fixed-site HSE, customs, recall, packaging, logistics execution, and waste management.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: an 8–10%+ DG-intensive assortment moving by ocean, inter-island,
road, and ecommerce last-mile across 200 stores and 4 DCs, under multiple Philippine regulators
(DENR-EMB RA 6969, BFP Fire Code, DOLE OSH, MARINA/Coast Guard for sea, CAB for air, LTFRB/DOTr for
road) and international modal rules (IMDG/IATA/ADR). Non-compliance causes carrier refusal and
shipment detention, ecommerce channel blocking, port/airport seizure, fines/penalties, license/
permit loss, and — in the worst case — fire, spill, injury, and environmental damage. No existing
value stream owns this end-to-end discipline.
