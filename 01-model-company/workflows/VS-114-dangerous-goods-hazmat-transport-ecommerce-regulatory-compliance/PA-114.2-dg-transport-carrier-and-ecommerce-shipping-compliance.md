## W3601. DG Transport Carrier Qualification & Contracting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Carrier sourcing (links to VS-110); DG carrier need; carrier performance review |
| **Frequency** | Per carrier + ongoing |
| **Volume** | DG-capable carriers (sea, air, road, last-mile) |
| **Owner** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Logistics |
| **Participants** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer, Logistics/Freight (links to VS-06/VS-110), Procurement (links to VS-34), HSE (links to VS-24), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Insurance (links to VS-26), Vendor Mgmt (links to VS-03) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/Logistics define DG carrier qualification criteria: modal DG authorization (MARINA/Coast Guard sea, CAB air, LTFRB/DOTr road hazmat permit links to W3607), DG driver training/cert (W3597), DG equipment (placards, spill kits, PPE), DG insurance (links to VS-26), incident history, and capacity for BuildRight's DG classes | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Logistics | VP Supply Chain / VP Legal | Per carrier |
| 2 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/Logistics/Procurement qualify and contract DG carriers: due diligence, DG capability assessment, contract terms (DG scope, liability, insurance, documentation obligations, incident notification, sub-contracting rules), rate/surcharges (DG premium), and the DG service-level agreement; integrate to the carrier scorecard (links to VS-110/VS-67) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Logistics / Procurement / Legal | VP Supply Chain / VP Legal | Per carrier |
| 3 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/Logistics manage DG carrier performance: monitor compliance/incidents/documentation quality, conduct periodic re-qualification, manage the approved DG carrier list, and remove/replace non-compliant carriers; report DG carrier performance to governance (W3608) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Logistics | VP Supply Chain / VP Legal | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Carrier contracting (links to VS-110); procurement (links to VS-34); legal (links to VS-100.3); insurance (links to VS-26); carrier scorecard (links to VS-67/VS-110); HSE (links to VS-24)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unauthorized-carrier risk**: Non-DG-authorized carrier ships DG illegally; mitigated by approved-list gating
- **Liability-gap risk**: Carrier contract doesn't allocate DG liability; mitigated by legal-reviewed DG terms


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics define DG carrier qualification criteria" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documentation obligations, incident notification, sub-contracting rules" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "monitor compliance/incidents/documentation quality, conduct periodic…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-572 (ensure controlled execution — DG Transport, Carrier & Ecommerce Shipping Compliance (PA-114.2).)
- operational: approved-list gating; operational: legal-reviewed DG terms

### Time Estimate
- Per carrier + ongoing

## W3602. DG Transport Documentation & Manifest Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DG shipment (inbound/inter-DC/store-replenishment/ecommerce/customer-delivery) |
| **Frequency** | Per DG shipment |
| **Volume** | All DG shipments (subset of ~6.5K receipts/month (~72K/year), ~5K replenishment orders/month, ~42.9K ecommerce orders/month) |
| **Owner** | DG Shipper / Logistics |
| **Participants** | DG Shipper (certified W3597), Logistics (links to VS-06/VS-110), DC Operations (links to VS-04), Ecommerce (links to VS-10), Import (links to VS-02.2), DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer, Carrier |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DG Shipper/Logistics prepare the DG transport documentation per shipment: Shipper's Declaration for Dangerous Goods (IATA air), DG manifest/multimodal DG form (IMDG sea), transport document (ADR road), the consignment detail (UN number, proper shipping name, class, packing group, quantity, packing instruction), and emergency-response telephone/reference; verify classification (W3594) and packaging/marking (W3598) | DG Shipper / Logistics | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Per shipment |
| 2 | DG Shipper/Logistics generate the load/vehicle manifest, placarding plan (placards per transport unit per class), segregation/compatibility check (incompatible DG separated), and the carrier handoff with documentation; ensure the driver/crew is DG-briefed and equipped (spill kit, PPE) | DG Shipper / Logistics / Carrier | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Per shipment |
| 3 | DG Shipper/Logistics retain the DG transport documents (retention per W3600/VS-88), transmit electronic manifests where required, and feed DG shipment data to analytics (W3608) and incident readiness (W3606); manage document exceptions and rejections | DG Shipper / Logistics | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Per shipment |

### System Touchpoints
- DG documentation system; hazmat master (links to W3594/VS-29); transport/Oracle Transportation Execution (links to VS-110); records (links to VS-88/W3600); ecommerce (links to VS-10); analytics (links to W3608)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Documentation-error risk**: Wrong/missing DG declaration causes carrier refusal, detention, or incident; mitigated by certified shipper and verification
- **Segregation-error risk**: Incompatible DG co-loaded causes reaction; mitigated by segregation check


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics prepare the DG transport documentation per shipment" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "DG Shipper/Logistics generate the load/vehicle manifest, placarding plan" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reject outside tolerance of "DG Shipper/Logistics retain the DG transport documents" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-572 (ensure controlled execution — DG Transport, Carrier & Ecommerce Shipping Compliance (PA-114.2).)
- operational: certified shipper and verification; operational: segregation check

### Time Estimate
- Per shipment

## W3603. DG Ocean / Air / Road Modal Transport Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DG shipment by mode (import ocean, inter-island sea, air, road) |
| **Frequency** | Per mode + per shipment |
| **Volume** | DG shipments across modes |
| **Owner** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Logistics |
| **Participants** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer, Logistics/Freight (links to VS-06/VS-110), Import (links to VS-02.2/VS-87), Carrier, HSE (links to VS-24), DG Shipper |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/Logistics apply the modal transport regulations per shipment: IMDG Code for sea (import ocean links to VS-02.2, inter-island domestic sea), IATA DGR for air, ADR/DOT/LTFRB for road; verify modal-specific packing instructions, quantity limits, and prohibitions (some DG prohibited by air, e.g., certain flammables/lithium-battery limits) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / DG Shipper / Logistics | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Per shipment |
| 2 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/Logistics ensure modal carrier/terminal compliance: sea-terminal DG acceptance and stowage segregation, air-carrier operator variations and acceptance, road route/tunnel restrictions and hazmat-route permits; coordinate port/airport handling (W3607) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Logistics / Carrier | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Per shipment |
| 3 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer manage modal-specific incidents and regulatory engagement (links to W3606/W3596), track modal compliance KPIs, and feed learnings to classification (W3594) and carrier management (W3601); report modal compliance to governance (W3608) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Logistics | VP Supply Chain / VP Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Oracle Transportation Execution/carrier (links to VS-110); import (links to VS-02.2/VS-87); hazmat master (links to W3594); port/airport handling (links to W3607); records (links to W3600); analytics (links to W3608)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Air-prohibited-DG risk**: DG shipped by air that's prohibited/over-limited causes seizure; mitigated by modal eligibility check
- **Stowage-segregation risk**: Wrong stowage on vessel/aircraft causes incident; mitigated by IMDG/IATA stowage rules


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Logistics apply the modal transport regulations per shipment" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-route of "route/tunnel restrictions and hazmat-route permits" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track modal compliance KPIs, and feed learnings to classification" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-156 (ensure dangerous-goods transport & training compliance.)
- operational: modal eligibility check; operational: IMDG/IATA stowage rules

### Time Estimate
- Per shipment + continuous

## W3606. DG Transport Incident, Spill & Emergency Response

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DG transport incident (spill, leak, fire, accident, exposure) |
| **Frequency** | Rare but high-impact |
| **Volume** | DG transport incidents |
| **Owner** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Logistics Emergency Response |
| **Participants** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer, Logistics (links to VS-06), HSE (links to VS-24/VS-26.2), Carrier, Emergency Responders, Legal (links to VS-100), Insurance (links to VS-26), Compliance (links to VS-22), Communications (links to VS-14.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Carrier/Logistics respond to the DG transport incident: immediate safety (isolate, evacuate, PPE), first response per the SDS (W3595) and emergency-response guide, notification to BuildRight DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer and HSE, and engagement of emergency responders (fire/BFP, medical, hazmat contractor); secure the scene and prevent spread | Carrier / Logistics / DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | VP Supply Chain / VP Legal | Immediate |
| 2 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE manage the response and notification: regulatory notification (DENR-EMB for environmental release, DOLE for worker, MARINA/Coast Guard/CAB/LTFRB for transport, LGU), carrier/3PL coordination, containment/clean-up/remediation (hazmat contractor), and customer/public communication (links to VS-14.3); engage legal (links to VS-100) and insurance (links to VS-26) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE / Legal / Insurance | VP Legal / COO | Incident life |
| 3 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE conduct incident investigation and root-cause (links to VS-24/VS-21), document the incident (W3613/W3600), manage claims/insurance recovery (links to VS-26/VS-90), and feed corrective action to classification/packaging/training/carrier (W3594/W3598/W3597/W3601); report to governance (W3616) and risk (links to VS-21.2) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE / Audit | VP Legal | Post-incident |

### System Touchpoints
