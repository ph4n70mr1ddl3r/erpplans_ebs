## W3609. DG Storage Compliance & Fixed-Site Risk Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DG storage design/change (DC, store); inspection cycle; incident |
| **Frequency** | Per site + ongoing inspection |
| **Volume** | 200 stores + 4 DCs with DG storage areas (paint/chemical cages) |
| **Owner** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE |
| **Participants** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer, HSE (links to VS-24.3), Facilities (links to VS-20.3/VS-07.2), DC Operations (links to VS-04), Store Operations (links to VS-07), BFP/Fire Safety, Quality (links to VS-31) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE define DG fixed-site storage compliance standards (in partnership with VS-24.3): segregated storage (incompatible separation), ventilation, spill containment, fire suppression (links to VS-24.3 BFP), temperature control, quantity limits per area, signage, and emergency equipment (eyewash, spill kit); align to DENR ECC and BFP Fire Code | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE | VP Legal / COO | Maintained |
| 2 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE/Facilities implement and maintain DG storage compliance across DCs and stores: storage layout, ventilation/suppression integrity, signage/equipment, quantity monitoring, and reconciliation to the hazmat inventory (W3595); resolve deficiencies | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE / Facilities | COO | Per site + ongoing |
| 3 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE inspect DG storage compliance on a schedule (DC monthly, store quarterly per VS-24.3 cadence), manage findings to closure, and report fixed-site DG compliance and risk to governance (W3616); coordinate the interface with VS-24.3 (worker-safety storage) to avoid duplication | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE | VP Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE/DC/store facility (links to VS-24.3/VS-04/VS-07/VS-20.3); hazmat inventory (links to W3595); permits (links to W3614); findings/analytics (links to W3616)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Storage-non-compliance risk**: Improper segregation/containment causes fire/spill; mitigated by standards and inspection
- **Quantity-excess risk**: Storing beyond permit/area limits breaches Fire Code; mitigated by quantity monitoring


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitoring, and reconciliation to the hazmat inventory" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-inspect of "inspect DG storage compliance on a schedule" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-573 (ensure controlled execution — DG Storage/Handling Safety, Incident Response & Regulatory Compliance (PA-114.3).)
- operational: standards and inspection; operational: quantity monitoring

### Time Estimate
- Per site + ongoing

## W3611. DG Spill Response, Containment & Remediation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DG spill/leak at a fixed site (DC, store, ecommerce pack station) |
| **Frequency** | Occasional |
| **Volume** | DG spill events |
| **Owner** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE |
| **Participants** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer, HSE (links to VS-24/VS-26.2), Site Operations, Hazmat Contractor, Occupational Health (links to VS-83), Facilities (links to VS-20.3), Environmental/Sustainability (links to VS-25), DENR-EMB |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Site staff/HSE respond to the spill: immediate safety (isolate, evacuate if needed, PPE), first response per SDS (W3595) and spill kit, containment (booms/pads/drain cover to prevent environmental release), and notification to DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE; engage hazmat contractor for significant spills | Site Staff / HSE / DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Site Manager / VP Legal | Immediate |
| 2 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE/Hazmat Contractor manage clean-up and remediation: recover/absorb the spilled material, decontaminate the area, manage the waste as hazardous (W3612), restore storage/handling, and conduct environmental assessment if release occurred; coordinate with DENR-EMB for environmental release (links to VS-25) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE / Hazmat Contractor | VP Legal / COO | Per event |
| 3 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE investigate the spill root-cause (links to VS-24/VS-21), document (W3613/W3600), and implement corrective/preventive action (training W3597, packaging W3598, storage W3609, procedures W3610); report to governance (W3616) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE / Audit | VP Legal | Post-event |

### System Touchpoints
- SDS/spill kit (links to W3595); HSE/incident (links to VS-24/VS-26.2); hazardous waste (links to W3612); environmental (links to VS-25); records (links to W3613/W3600)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Environmental-release risk**: Spill reaching drain/soil causes DENR liability; mitigated by containment and drain protection
- **Slow-clean-up risk**: Delayed clean-up spreads contamination; mitigated by spill plan and hazmat contractor on retainer


### Automation Opportunity
- System workflow orchestration of "coordinate with DENR-EMB for environmental release" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE investigate the spill root-cause" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-573 (ensure controlled execution — DG Storage/Handling Safety, Incident Response & Regulatory Compliance (PA-114.3).)
- operational: containment and drain protection; operational: spill plan and hazmat contractor on retainer

### Time Estimate
- Per event
