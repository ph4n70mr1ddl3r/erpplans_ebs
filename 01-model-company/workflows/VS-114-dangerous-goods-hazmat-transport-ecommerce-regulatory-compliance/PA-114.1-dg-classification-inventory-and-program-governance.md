## W3593. DG/Hazmat Compliance Program Strategy & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual compliance/HSE planning (links to VS-24/VS-22); regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise DG/hazmat program |
| **Owner** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer, HSE (links to VS-24), Logistics (links to VS-06/VS-110), Ecommerce (links to VS-10), DC Operations (links to VS-04), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Compliance/Regulatory (links to VS-22), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Sustainability (links to VS-25) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE define the DG compliance program strategy and governance: scope (the DG-intensive categories — paint/solvents, adhesives/thinners, aerosols, garden chemicals, cleaning chemicals, fuels/lubricants, gas cylinders, lithium batteries, cement/silica), the regulatory map (DENR-EMB RA 6969, BFP Fire Code, DOLE OSH, MARINA/Coast Guard, CAB, LTFRB/DOTr, IMDG/IATA/ADR), the program operating model, and the KPIs (compliance %, incidents, carrier refusals, audit findings) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE | VP Legal / COO | Annual |
| 2 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer establish the DG governance: DG Compliance Officer role/authority, the DG committee, cross-functional responsibilities (Merchandising classifies, Logistics ships, Ecommerce lists, DC/Store stores, HSE protects workers), the policy/standards, and the integration to HSE (VS-24.3), quality (VS-31), and recall (VS-89) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Compliance | VP Legal | Annual |
| 3 | VP Legal/COO approve the DG program strategy and policy, ratify governance/authorities, allocate the program budget (links to VS-34/VS-17.4), and report DG compliance posture to the executive risk committee (links to VS-21.2/VS-36) and board (links to VS-36); communicate the mandate | VP Legal / COO | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- HSE (links to VS-24); compliance/regulatory (links to VS-22); legal (links to VS-100.3); risk (links to VS-21.2); governance (links to VS-36); finance (links to VS-34/VS-17.4); sustainability (links to VS-25)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fragmented-ownership risk**: DG compliance split across HSE/Logistics/Ecommerce with no single owner; mitigated by DG Compliance Officer authority
- **Under-prioritization risk**: DG risk under-resourced until an incident; mitigated by risk-committee reporting and quantified exposure


### Automation Opportunity
- System continuous audit of "DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE define the DG compliance program strategy" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "Logistics ships, Ecommerce lists, DC/Store stores, HSE protects workers" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the DG program strategy and policy, ratify governance/authorities,…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-156 (ensure dangerous-goods transport & training compliance.)
- operational: DG Compliance Officer authority; operational: risk-committee reporting and quantified exposure

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 weeks annual

## W3594. DG Product Classification (UN/IMDG/IATA/ADR) & Master Data

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New SKU/NPI (links to VS-01.1); vendor/private-label onboarding; regulatory update |
| **Frequency** | Per SKU + on update |
| **Volume** | ~2,800–3,500 DG-intensive SKUs (8–10% of 35K assortment) |
| **Owner** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Merchandising |
| **Participants** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer, Merchandising/Category (links to VS-01), Private Label (links to VS-41), Master Data (links to VS-29), Quality (links to VS-31), Import (links to VS-87), Supplier, Vendor Mgmt (links to VS-03) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/Merchandising classify each DG SKU against the modal regulations: UN number, proper shipping name, hazard class/division (1–9), packing group (I/II/III), subsidiary risks, special provisions, and the modal-specific classification (IMDG sea, IATA air, ADR/DOT road); obtain vendor/manufacturer classification or commission expert classification | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Merchandising / Supplier | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Per SKU |
| 2 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/Master Data capture the DG classification in the item/hazmat master (links to VS-29): UN number, class, packing group, DG flag, transport-eligibility by mode (air/sea/road), ecommerce ship-eligibility flag (W3604), restricted-quantity/limited-quantity thresholds, and the linked SDS (W3595); maintain through the product lifecycle (links to VS-01.1) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Master Data | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Per SKU |
| 3 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/Quality validate classification for private-label and high-risk SKUs (links to VS-41/VS-31), manage classification disputes/updates on regulatory change (W3596), and feed classification to transport (W3602–W3605), ecommerce (W3604), and storage (W3609) compliance; audit classification accuracy | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Quality | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Item/hazmat master (links to VS-29); NPI/lifecycle (links to VS-01.1); private label (links to VS-41); quality (links to VS-31); SDS/chemical info (links to W3595); ecommerce listing (links to VS-10/W3604); transport (links to W3602)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-classification risk**: Wrong UN/class causes non-compliant shipment and incident; mitigated by expert classification and validation
- **Master-data gap risk**: DG attributes not on the item master block automated compliance checks; mitigated by mandatory DG attributes at SKU setup


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-classify of "classify each DG SKU against the modal regulations" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-flag of "flag, transport-eligibility by mode" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "validate classification for private-label and high-risk SKUs" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-571 (ensure controlled execution — DG Classification, Inventory & Program Governance (PA-114.1).)
- operational: expert classification and validation; operational: mandatory DG attributes at SKU setup; operational: DG attributes at SKU setup

### Time Estimate
- Per SKU

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per SKU + maintained |
| **Volume** | ~3,000+ chemical/DG SKUs with SDS |
| **Owner** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Quality |
| **Participants** | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer, Quality (links to VS-31), HSE (links to VS-24.3), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Master Data (links to VS-29), Supplier, Store/DC Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/Quality obtain and maintain the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for each chemical/DG SKU: GHS-compliant SDS (16-section), hazard identification, composition, first-aid, fire-fighting, accidental-release, handling/storage, exposure/PPE, stability, toxicology, ecological, disposal, transport (links to W3594), regulatory; multi-language where imported; supplier-sourced | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Quality / Supplier | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Per SKU + maintained |
| 2 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE maintain the chemical inventory and register (DENR-EMB Chemical Inventory Report under RA 6969, Priority Chemical List where applicable), the SDS library (accessible to DC/store/workers links to VS-24.3), and the chemical information feed to transport/storage/ecommerce compliance; reconcile to the hazmat master (W3594) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Maintained |
| 3 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/Quality manage SDS updates on formulation/regulatory change, supplier SDS accuracy audit (links to VS-31/VS-67), and SDS availability/acknowledgement by workers (links to VS-24/W3597); report SDS coverage and gaps to governance (W3616) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / Quality | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- SDS/chemical library; hazmat master (links to W3594/VS-29); DENR chemical inventory (links to VS-24.3/VS-22); quality (links to VS-31); worker access (links to VS-24/W3597); vendor scorecard (links to VS-67)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **SDS-gap risk**: Missing/outdated SDS blocks safe handling and compliance; mitigated by coverage KPIs and supplier obligation
- **Chemical-inventory-reporting risk**: Failure to report DENR-EMB inventory attracts penalty; mitigated by register and reporting discipline


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "toxicology, ecological, disposal, transport" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile to the hazmat master" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-update of "updates on formulation/regulatory change, supplier SDS accuracy audit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-571 (ensure controlled execution — DG Classification, Inventory & Program Governance (PA-114.1).)
- operational: coverage KPIs and supplier obligation; operational: register and reporting discipline

### Time Estimate
- Per SKU + maintained

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE define DG training and certification requirements by role: hazmat handler, DG driver (hazmat endorsement), DG shipper/consignor (IATA/IMDG certified), ecommerce DG packer, supervisor, and emergency responder; align to DOLE OSH and modal rules (IATA requires trained shippers) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE | VP Legal | Annual |
| 2 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/L&D deliver the DG training: classroom/e-learning, hands-on handling/PPE (links to VS-24), spill response (W3611), documentation (W3602), and modal-specific (IMDG/IATA/ADR); certify and record completion; manage external certifications (IATA shipper, DENR-accredited training) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / L&D | VP Legal | Per role + recurring |
| 3 | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/HSE maintain the DG training/competency register, track certifications/expiry, manage refresher/re-certification, and audit competency coverage; feed gaps to L&D (links to VS-19.3) and HR (links to VS-103); report to governance (W3616) and audit (links to VS-88) | DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer / HSE / HR | VP Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- HR/L&D (links to VS-19.3/VS-103); HSE/PPE (links to VS-24); records (links to VS-88); competency register; logistics/DC/store/ecommerce operations

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Untrained-handler risk**: Uncertified staff shipping/handling DG breaches regulation and risks incident; mitigated by certification gating and register
- **Certification-lapse risk**: Expired IATA/IMDG certification invalidates shipments; mitigated by expiry tracking and refresher


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "DG/Hazmat Compliance Officer/L&D deliver the DG training" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track certifications/expiry, manage refresher/re-certification, and audit…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls

### Pain Points / Risks
