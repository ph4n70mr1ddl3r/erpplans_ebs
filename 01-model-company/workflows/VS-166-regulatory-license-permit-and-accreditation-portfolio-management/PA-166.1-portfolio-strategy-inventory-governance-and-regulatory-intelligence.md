## W4841. Regulatory License/Permit Portfolio Strategy & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch; annual compliance strategy review; entity/site expansion; major regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on trigger |
| **Volume** | 1 group portfolio strategy; ~thousands of authorizations across ~205 sites |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance / Chief Compliance Officer / Compliance Portfolio Manager |
| **Participants** | COO, Store Ops (VS-07), Real Estate (VS-97), Tax (VS-79), HSE (VS-24), HR (VS-19), Government Affairs (VS-104), Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the portfolio strategy & operating model: centralized compliance center-of-excellence vs distributed domain-ownership with central oversight (the federated model), the single-source-of-truth register, governance/RACI, the renewal engine, and the compliance dashboard; decide make-vs-buy for permit services | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Legal / Strategy | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 2 | Define the portfolio scope & taxonomy: all license/permit/certification/accreditation classes (LGU, BFP, DENR, BIR/CAS, DTI-BPS, PCAB, PhilGEPS, FDA/FPA/BPI, PEZA/BOI, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG, DOLE, LTO/LTFRB, BSP, customs, NPC, energy, signage/occupancy) and the per-class data model (entity, site, regulator, expiry, owner, fee, evidence, conditions, risk) | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 3 | Approve the strategy, integrate into the enterprise compliance plan (VS-22) and ERM (VS-21.2), resource the function, and set KPIs (coverage %, on-time-renewal %, lapse incidents, penalty $, inspection findings); report to the board/audit & risk committee (VS-36) | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Strategy / Risk | VP Legal & Compliance / CFO / Board Audit & Risk Committee | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Compliance/GRC register on Oracle Internal Controls Manager (ICM — fit-gap H11); ERM (VS-21.2); legal (VS-100.3); analytics (VS-28); board governance (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fragmentation risk**: Permits staying siloed in domain teams → lapses unnoticed; mitigated by the central register & dashboard
- **Operating-model risk**: Central team too thin vs distributed too loose; mitigated by the federated model with clear RACI
- **Strategic-invisibility risk**: Compliance cut as overhead; mitigated by lapse/penalty/revenue-at-risk KPIs

### Automation Opportunity
- Oracle Internal Controls Manager (ICM) GRC register platform (fit-gap H11); federated-ownership workflow; KPI dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-169 (ensure enterprise license/permit register integrity.)
- CTL-728: single-source-of-truth register; ownership matrix; board-reported KPIs

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 weeks annual strategy; continuous operating-model tuning

### Cross-references
- links to VS-22 (compliance), VS-21.2 (ERM), VS-36 (board), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-28 (analytics); sets inputs for W4842–W4848

## W4842. Unified License/Permit/Accreditation Inventory & Register Build

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch (W4841); new site/entity; discovery of an un-inventoried authorization |
| **Frequency** | Initial build + continuous maintenance |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of authorizations; ~205 sites × multiple classes |
| **Owner** | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Compliance Operations |
| **Participants** | All domain compliance owners (VS-22/76/79/114/117/138/145/165/46/06), Master Data (VS-29), Records (VS-88) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build the unified register: harvest every existing authorization from domain owners (LGU business permits, BFP FSIC, DENR ECC, BIR/CAS/ATP, DTI-BPS, PCAB, PhilGEPS, SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG, DOLE, LTO/LTFRB, BSP, NPC, etc.), normalize to the taxonomy (W4841), and load with full metadata (entity, site, regulator, license #, issue/expiry, owner, fee, evidence-link, conditions, renewal-rule) | Compliance Operations / Domain Owners / Master Data | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Initial build (weeks–months) |
| 2 | Reconcile & de-duplicate: cross-check the register against finance (fee payments VS-17), records (VS-88), and regulator portals; resolve duplicates, gaps, and stale entries; link each to the site/entity master (VS-29) and the domain compliance VS | Compliance Operations / Master Data / Finance / Records | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Initial + continuous |
| 3 | Maintain continuously: register is the single source of truth, updated on every issuance/renewal/lapse/variation (W4849–W4856); enforce the data-quality standard and the update SLA from domain owners; report register-health to governance (W4864) | Compliance Operations / Domain Owners | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Oracle Internal Controls Manager (ICM) GRC register platform (fit-gap H11); master data (VS-29); records/DMS (VS-88); finance (VS-17); domain compliance VSs

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Coverage-gap risk**: An un-inventoried permit lapsing unnoticed; mitigated by the harvest + regulator-portal reconciliation
- **Data-quality risk**: Stale/duplicate records undermining trust; mitigated by the data-quality standard and reconciliation
- **Update-lag risk**: Domain owners not updating the register; mitigated by the update SLA and ownership matrix (W4844)

### Automation Opportunity
- Register-platform ingestion; regulator-portal reconciliation; master-data auto-link (VS-29); duplicate-detection

### Controls
- CTL-169 (ensure enterprise license/permit register integrity.)
- CTL-728: single-source-of-truth register; reconciled to finance/records/portals; data-quality standard enforced

### Time Estimate
- ~initial build 4–8 weeks; maintenance continuous

### Cross-references
- links to VS-29 (master data), VS-88 (records), VS-17 (finance), all domain compliance VSs, W4841 (strategy), W4844 (ownership), W4861 (data governance)

## W4843. Regulatory Intelligence — Obligation Mapping by Site/Entity/Activity

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New site/activity (store VS-37, DC, locker VS-164, EV charger VS-163, new service VS-156); regulator change (W4847); annual refresh |
| **Frequency** | Per new site/activity + annual |
| **Volume** | ~205 sites × activity-mix; new-site onboarding (~10–15/yr) |
| **Owner** | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Regulatory Intelligence Analyst |
| **Participants** | Government Affairs (VS-104), domain compliance owners, Legal (VS-100.3), Real Estate (VS-97) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the regulatory-obligation map: for each site type (store/DC/HQ/locker/EV charger) and activity (retail, installation, B2G, hazmat, VAS/bills-payment, renewable generation), the full set of required authorizations (LGU, BFP, DENR, BIR, DTI, PCAB, FDA/FPA, BSP, NPC, energy, etc.) by geography (region/LGU-specific differences); source from Government Affairs (VS-104) and regulators | Regulatory Intelligence / Government Affairs / Domain Owners | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Continuous + annual |
| 2 | Apply on new site/activity (VS-37 store open, VS-109 remodel, VS-163/VS-164 new asset, VS-156 new service): generate the required-permit checklist for that site/activity/geography, assign owners (W4844), and seed the acquisition campaign (W4851); verify no activity starts before authorizations are in place | Regulatory Intelligence / Store Ops / Real Estate | Compliance Portfolio Manager / VP Legal & Compliance | Per new site/activity |
| 3 | Maintain obligation-coverage gaps: detect activities/sites missing a required authorization (vs the obligation map), prioritize remediation (W4853), and feed obligation changes (W4847) to the map and register | Regulatory Intelligence / Domain Owners | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- ICM obligation map (GRC, fit-gap H11); government affairs (VS-104); store lifecycle (VS-37/109/163/164); compliance register (W4842); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Obligation-blindspot risk**: An activity run without a required authorization (e.g., VAS/bills-payment without BSP accreditation); mitigated by the obligation map applied at onboarding
- **Geographic-variation risk**: LGU/region-specific permit differences missed; mitigated by the per-geography map
- **Map-staleness risk**: Obligation map not updated for regulatory change; mitigated by W4847 link

### Automation Opportunity
- Site/activity → required-permit checklist engine; obligation-map vs register coverage-gap query; LGU-rule library

### Controls
- CTL-728 (ensure controlled execution — Portfolio Strategy, Inventory, Governance & Regulatory Intelligence (PA-166.1).); CTL-729 (ensure controlled execution — Renewal Execution, Inspection Coordination & Multi-Site Campaigns (PA-166.2).)
- CTL-728: obligation map applied at every new site/activity; no activity before authorizations in place; coverage gaps remediated

### Time Estimate
- ~per new site/activity checklist 1–3 days; map maintenance continuous

### Cross-references
- links to VS-104 (government affairs), VS-37/109/163/164 (site/asset lifecycle), VS-156 (VAS), W4842 (register), W4847 (change), W4851 (acquisition), W4853 (remediation)

## W4844. License/Permit Governance, Ownership Matrix & Accountability

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4841); org change; lapse incident (accountability review) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | 1 ownership matrix; ~all authorizations assigned |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance / Chief Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Domain compliance owners, Store Ops (VS-07), DC Ops (VS-04), HR, Tax, HSE |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the ownership/RACI matrix: for every authorization class, the Accountable executive (single throat-to-choke), the Responsible owner (domain team / site), Consulted (Legal/Finance), and Informed; assign each of the thousands of authorizations to an owner in the register (W4842) | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual + continuous |
| 2 | Enforce accountability via the renewal workflow (W4845/W4849): owner is notified on the tiered alerts, owns the renewal filing, and is accountable for lapse; escalate un-owned or orphaned authorizations; integrate with performance objectives | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Domain Owners | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 3 | Review accountability after any lapse/penalty incident (W4853/W4858): root-cause the ownership failure, adjust the matrix, and reinforce; report accountability-health to governance (W4864) | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Internal Audit (VS-21) | VP Legal & Compliance | Per incident + annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ICM ownership matrix (GRC, fit-gap H11); register (W4842); HR/performance (VS-19); audit (VS-21); governance (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Orphaned-permit risk**: An authorization with no clear owner lapsing; mitigated by full ownership assignment
- **Diffused-accountability risk**: "Everyone's responsibility" = no one's; mitigated by single Accountable per authorization
- **Ownership-drift risk**: Owners changing role without handover; mitigated by the matrix + HR coordination

### Automation Opportunity
- Ownership-matrix workflow; owner-notification routing; orphaned-authorization detection; HR-role-change handover trigger

### Controls
- CTL-169 (ensure enterprise license/permit register integrity.)
- CTL-728: every authorization has a single Accountable owner; renewals owner-routed; lapse incidents root-caused

### Time Estimate
- ~annual matrix review 1–2 weeks; continuous assignment

### Cross-references
- links to VS-19 (HR/performance), VS-21 (audit), VS-36 (governance), W4842 (register), W4845 (renewal), W4853/W4858 (incidents)

## W4845. Renewal Calendar, Expiry-Risk Engine & Compliance Dashboard

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous calendar; tiered expiry alerts (e.g., 90/60/30/7 days) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | All renewable authorizations (~thousands) |
| **Owner** | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Compliance Operations |
| **Participants** | Domain owners (renewal filers), Finance (fees), Real Estate/Store Ops (site context) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Operate the renewal calendar from the register (W4842): every authorization's expiry, renewal lead-time, required documents, fee, and owner; tiered alerts (90/60/30/7 days) routed to the owner (W4844); reconcile expiries monthly | Compliance Operations | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Run the expiry-risk engine: prioritize by business impact (store-closing LGU/BFP/BIR, bid-blocking PhilGEPS/PCAB, service-blocking BSP/PCAB, fleet-blocking LTO/LTFRB), flag at-risk renewals (owner non-response, document gaps, regulator backlog), and escalate critical-risk items to the VP Legal & Compliance / COO | Compliance Operations / Domain Owners | Compliance Portfolio Manager / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 3 | Maintain the compliance dashboard: coverage %, on-time-renewal %, expiring-soon, at-risk, lapse/penalty incidents, inspection findings, by entity/site/domain; distribute to owners, executives, and the board (VS-36) | Compliance Operations / Analytics | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- GRC calendar/engine; register (W4842); analytics/dashboard (VS-28); finance fees (VS-17); board governance (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lapse risk**: A renewal missed despite alerts (owner non-response); mitigated by tiered alerts + escalation
- **Prioritization risk**: Equal treatment of low/high-impact permits diluting focus; mitigated by the risk engine
- **Dashboard-adoption risk**: Dashboard ignored; mitigated by executive/board distribution

### Automation Opportunity
- Calendar with tiered auto-alerts; risk-scoring engine; executive dashboard; owner non-response escalation

### Controls
- CTL-169 (ensure enterprise license/permit register integrity.)
- CTL-728: every authorization on the calendar; tiered alerts; critical-risk escalated; board-reported dashboard

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous operation; monthly reconciliation ~1–2 days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-28 (analytics), VS-36 (board), W4842 (register), W4844 (ownership), W4849 (renewal execution), W4853 (remediation)

| 2 | Maintain the audit trail: every authorization lifecycle event (issue/renew/lapse/vary/inspect/penalty) timestamped with owner and evidence; reconcile to the register (W4842) and finance fee payments (VS-17) | Compliance Operations / Records / Finance | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Continuous |

| **Frequency** | Continuous + on change |
| **Volume** | ~60–100 regulatory changes tracked per year across all regulators (BIR/DOLE/DENR/DTI/LGU) |
| **Owner** | Regulatory Intelligence Analyst / Government Affairs (VS-104) |
| **Participants** | Domain compliance owners, Legal (VS-100.3), Strategy (VS-33) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monitor regulatory change across all relevant regulators (BIR, DTI/BPS, DENR, DOLE, BFP, LGU, BSP, FDA/FPA/BPI, NPC, PCAB/CIAP, PEZA/BOI, LTO/LTFRB, BOI/PEZA, energy) via Government Affairs (VS-104), subscriptions, and associations; log each change | Regulatory Intelligence / Government Affairs | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Assess impact to the portfolio: which authorizations/sites/activities are affected, the required action (new permit, renewal-rule change, condition change, fee change, decommission), the deadline, and the owner (W4844); prioritize by impact and feed to the obligation map (W4843) and register (W4842) | Regulatory Intelligence / Domain Owners / Legal | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Per change |
| 3 | Track change-adoption to closure: confirm the required action is executed by the owner (W4849/W4851/W4856), update the register/obligation map, and report change-adoption to governance (W4864); capture emerging regulatory risk for ERM (VS-21.2) | Regulatory Intelligence / Domain Owners / Risk | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Continuous |

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Change-blindspot risk**: A regulatory change missed → non-compliance; mitigated by multi-source monitoring
- **Slow-adoption risk**: Change identified but action delayed past deadline; mitigated by adoption tracking
- **Impact-misjudgment risk**: Under/over-stating impact; mitigated by Legal/owner review

### Automation Opportunity
- Regulatory-change feed; impact-to-portfolio mapping engine; adoption tracker; ERM sync

### Controls
- CTL-169 (ensure enterprise license/permit register integrity.)
- CTL-728: regulatory changes logged & impact-assessed; adoption tracked to closure; emerging risk fed to ERM

### Time Estimate

## W4848. Portfolio Risk Tiering, Single-Points-of-Failure & Resilience

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual ERM cycle (VS-21.2); lapse incident (W4853); concentration review |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on incident |
| **Volume** | 1 portfolio risk view; ~all authorizations tiered |
| **Owner** | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Risk (VS-21.2) |
| **Participants** | Domain owners, Legal (VS-100.3), BCP (VS-26), Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tier the portfolio by risk: business impact (store-closing, bid-blocking, service-blocking, fleet-blocking, fines), regulator scrutiny, and historical lapse/penalty rate; identify high-risk authorizations for heightened oversight (more frequent review, backup owner) | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Risk / Domain Owners | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 2 | Identify single-points-of-failure: a sole owner, a sole regulator-relationship, a sole third-party permit-service provider (W4854), a site with a sole critical permit, or a personnel credential with no backup (links to VS-165 W4835); build redundancy/continuity for each | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Risk / BCP | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 3 | Build portfolio resilience: BCP for regulator-outage/portal-down (VS-26), continuity workarounds for lapsed permits (W4853), alternative permit-service providers (W4854), and cross-training of owners; feed resilience gaps to ERM (VS-21.2) and BCP (VS-26) | Compliance Portfolio Manager / BCP / Domain Owners | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual + continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- ERM (VS-21.2); BCP (VS-26); register (W4842); risk tiering; audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Concentration risk**: A sole owner/provider/relationship failing; mitigated by redundancy
- **High-impact-lapse risk**: A tier-1 permit lapsing; mitigated by heightened oversight
- **Resilience-gap risk**: No continuity for regulator/service-provider failure; mitigated by BCP links

### Automation Opportunity
- Risk-tiering engine; single-point-of-failure detection; resilience-gap dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-728 (ensure controlled execution — Portfolio Strategy, Inventory, Governance & Regulatory Intelligence (PA-166.1).)
- CTL-728: portfolio risk-tiered; single-points-of-failure addressed; resilience linked to BCP (VS-26)

### Time Estimate
- ~annual risk tiering 1–2 weeks; resilience continuous

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2 (ERM), VS-26 (BCP), W4842 (register), W4853 (remediation), W4854 (providers), VS-165 W4835 (credential backup)
