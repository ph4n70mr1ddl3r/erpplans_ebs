## W4857. Executive Compliance Dashboard, Board Reporting & KPI Suite

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly executive & board cycle (VS-36); KPI refresh |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly board |
| **Volume** | 1 executive dashboard; ~board reporting cycles |
| **Owner** | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Analytics (VS-28) |
| **Participants** | VP Legal & Compliance, CCO, domain owners, Board/Audit & Risk Committee |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the KPI suite: coverage %, on-time-renewal %, expiring-soon, at-risk, lapse incidents, penalty $, inspection findings, regulator-change adoption %, provider performance, cost; slice by entity/site/domain | Analytics / Compliance Operations | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Maintain the executive dashboard (W4845): real-time portfolio health, heat-map by site/domain, top risks, trend; distribute monthly to executives and domain owners | Analytics / Compliance Operations | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | Report to the board/audit & risk committee (VS-36.1) quarterly: portfolio status, material risks (store-closing/bid-blocking), lapse/penalty/inspection events, emerging regulatory risk (W4847), and program maturity (W4864); ensure officer-personal-liability exposure is disclosed | VP Legal & Compliance / Compliance Portfolio Manager | VP Legal & Compliance / Board Audit & Risk Committee | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI dashboard (VS-28); register (W4842); calendar (W4845); board governance (VS-36); ERM (VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **KPI-gaming risk**: Owners manipulating metrics (e.g., hiding at-risk renewals); mitigated by source-data reconciliation
- **Board-disconnect risk**: Board not seeing material exposure; mitigated by quarterly reporting + officer-liability disclosure
- **Dashboard-irrelevance risk**: Dashboard not actionable; mitigated by owner/executive distribution + action-tracking

### Automation Opportunity
- Real-time dashboard; KPI auto-calculation from register; board-report auto-assembly

### Controls
- CTL-730 (ensure controlled execution — Compliance Reporting, Analytics, Cost & Program Assurance (PA-166.3).)
- CTL-730: KPIs reconciled to source data; quarterly board reporting; officer-liability disclosed

### Time Estimate
- ~monthly dashboard maintenance; quarterly board reporting 1–2 weeks

### Cross-references
- links to VS-28 (analytics), VS-36 (board), VS-21.2 (ERM), W4842/W4845 (register/calendar), W4864 (maturity)

## W4858. Penalty/Fine Management, Dispute Resolution & Regulatory-Debt Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Penalty/fine notice (from any regulator); inspection finding (W4852); dispute |
| **Frequency** | Ad hoc |
| **Volume** | ~10–20 penalty events per year (mostly minor LGU/administrative) |
| **Owner** | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Legal (VS-100.3) / Finance |
| **Participants** | Domain owners, Finance (VS-17 payment), Government Affairs (VS-104), External counsel |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive & log the penalty/fine notice, assess validity & exposure (fine amount, recurring nature, license/operational impact), decide pay-vs-dispute, and assign the owner; record in the regulatory-debt tracker | Compliance Operations / Legal / Domain Owner | Compliance Portfolio Manager / VP Legal & Compliance | Per event (immediate) |
| 2 | Execute: pay the fine (VS-17) or file the dispute/appeal within the deadline (with Legal/Government Affairs/external counsel), negotiate mitigation, and manage any license/operational consequence (W4853) during the dispute | Legal / Finance / Government Affairs / Domain Owner | VP Legal & Compliance / CFO | Per event |
| 3 | Close & learn: record the outcome in the audit trail (W4846) and legal matter (VS-100.3), conduct root-cause (ownership failure, process failure, provider failure W4854), strengthen controls, and feed penalty-trend to risk tiering (W4848), governance (W4864), and analytics (W4862) | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Internal Audit | VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal matter (VS-100.3); finance penalty payment (VS-17); audit trail (W4846); register (W4842); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missed-deadline risk**: Late dispute/payment → default/enhanced penalty; mitigated by deadline tracking
- **Regulatory-debt-accumulation risk**: Untracked recurring fines; mitigated by the debt tracker
- **Recurrence risk**: Same penalty recurring; mitigated by root-cause review

### Automation Opportunity
- Penalty-notice → deadline tracker; regulatory-debt dashboard; root-cause workflow

### Controls
- CTL-730 (ensure controlled execution — Compliance Reporting, Analytics, Cost & Program Assurance (PA-166.3).)
- CTL-730: penalty disputes/payments within deadline; debt tracked; root-cause reviewed

### Time Estimate
- ~per event days–weeks (dispute-dependent)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100.3 (legal), VS-17 (finance), VS-104 (government affairs), W4846 (audit trail), W4848 (risk tiering), W4862/W4864 (analytics/governance)

## W4859. Permit-Portfolio Cost Management & Compliance-Spend Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual budget cycle; quarterly cost review; provider/fee benchmarking |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly + annual budget |
| **Volume** | Group compliance-spend (fees, providers W4854, internal cost) |
| **Owner** | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Finance (VS-17.4 FP&A) |
| **Participants** | Domain owners, Procurement (VS-03/VS-34), Treasury (VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the compliance-spend ledger: permit/license fees, renewal fees, third-party provider fees (W4854), internal staffing, penalty/fine payments (W4858), by entity/site/domain/regulator; reconcile to finance (VS-17) | Compliance Operations / Finance | Compliance Portfolio Manager / CFO | Continuous |
| 2 | Analyze cost: trend, cost-per-site, benchmark fees/providers, identify cost-out (provider consolidation, fee negotiation, in-sourcing vs out-sourcing), and forecast the renewal-fee burden; integrate into the annual budget (VS-17.4) | Finance / Compliance Portfolio / Procurement | Compliance Portfolio Manager / CFO | Quarterly + annual |
| 3 | Drive cost optimization: consolidate providers (W4854), negotiate fee schedules (with Government Affairs VS-104 where regulator-set), eliminate duplicate/unused permits, and report cost-out to governance (W4864) and analytics (W4862) | Compliance Portfolio / Procurement / Finance | VP Legal & Compliance / CFO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance FP&A (VS-17.4); procurement (VS-03/VS-34); treasury (VS-18); register (W4842); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cost-leakage risk**: Duplicate/unused permits or over-paying providers; mitigated by the cost ledger + reconciliation
- **Budget-surprise risk**: Unforecasted renewal-fee burden; mitigated by the forecast
- **Over-optimization risk**: Cost-cutting that increases lapse risk; mitigated by risk-aware optimization

### Automation Opportunity
- Compliance-spend ledger auto-feed from finance; cost-per-site analytics; provider-fee benchmarking

### Controls
- CTL-730 (ensure controlled execution — Compliance Reporting, Analytics, Cost & Program Assurance (PA-166.3).)
- CTL-730: compliance-spend reconciled to finance; cost-out risk-aware; budgeted annually

### Time Estimate
- ~quarterly review 1 week; annual budget 1–2 weeks

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-03/34 (procurement), VS-18 (treasury), VS-104 (government affairs), W4854 (providers), W4862/W4864 (analytics/governance)

| **Frequency** | Ad hoc |

## W4861. License/Permit Data Governance, Master-Data Sync & Duplicates Cleanup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Data-quality review; master-data change (VS-29); duplicate detection; analytics anomaly |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + periodic cleanup |
| **Volume** | All register records (~thousands) |
| **Owner** | Compliance Portfolio Manager / Master Data (VS-29) |
| **Participants** | Domain owners, Analytics (VS-28), Records (VS-88) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Govern the register's data quality: the data model (W4841), mandatory fields, validation rules, and the update SLA from domain owners; designate the register as the single source of truth synced to/from the site/entity master (VS-29) | Compliance Operations / Master Data | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Detect & resolve data issues: duplicates (same permit entered twice), gaps (missing expiry/owner/evidence), stale entries (expired not updated), and mismatches (register vs finance/records/portals); run periodic cleanup campaigns | Master Data / Compliance Operations / Analytics | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Periodic |
| 3 | Enforce data-quality KPIs (completeness, accuracy, timeliness), report register-health to governance (W4864), and continuously improve the data model and integrations (e.g., auto-sync from regulator portals, finance, store lifecycle) | Master Data / Analytics | Compliance Portfolio Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- GRC/register; master data (VS-29); analytics (VS-28); records (VS-88); finance (VS-17); store lifecycle (VS-37/59)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Duplicate risk**: Duplicate records causing double-renewal/double-fee or false alerts; mitigated by duplicate-detection
- **Stale-data risk**: Expired records not updated undermining the calendar; mitigated by the update SLA
- **Integration-gap risk**: Register drifting from master data/portals; mitigated by auto-sync

### Automation Opportunity
- Duplicate-detection; data-quality KPI dashboard; auto-sync from master data/portals/finance/store lifecycle

### Controls
- CTL-730 (ensure controlled execution — Compliance Reporting, Analytics, Cost & Program Assurance (PA-166.3).)
- CTL-730: register is single source of truth; data-quality KPIs enforced; duplicates/gaps resolved

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous governance; periodic cleanup campaigns 1–2 weeks

### Cross-references
- links to VS-29 (master data), VS-28 (analytics), VS-88 (records), VS-17 (finance), W4842 (register), W4864 (governance)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

| 2 | Support control testing: walkthroughs, sample testing of renewals/on-time/evidence/ABC (W4854), SOX-style key-control testing where compliance is a financial-reporting control (e.g., license-to-operate affecting revenue recognition), and remediation of findings | Internal Audit / Compliance Portfolio / Domain Owners | VP Legal & Compliance | Per audit cycle |

- CTL-730 (ensure controlled execution — Compliance Reporting, Analytics, Cost & Program Assurance (PA-166.3).)
