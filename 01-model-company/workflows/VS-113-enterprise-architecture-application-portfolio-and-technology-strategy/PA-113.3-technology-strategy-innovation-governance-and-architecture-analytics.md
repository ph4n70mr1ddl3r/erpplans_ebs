## W5515. Sourcing Decision Gate Operation & Capability Sourcing Register

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Capability sourcing proposal (new capability, replacement, renewal-with-change) or a registered re-evaluation trigger firing (e.g. "ERP vendor ships native WMS at parity", "260 stores") |
| **Frequency** | Monthly SIB + on demand; annual register reaffirmation at QBR |
| **Volume** | Every capability decision across the 188-VS estate routes through one gate — ~30–45 SIB assessments/year (new decisions, renewals-with-change, fired re-evaluation triggers) plus ~9 annual register reaffirmations |
| **Owner** | Head of Enterprise Architecture (SIB assessment lead) |
| **Participants** | Head of Enterprise Architecture, SIB (CIO chair; affected IT PO/PM & BPO; FinOps/TBM analyst links to VS-135; CFO delegate; SEC lead — TPRM links to VS-161; Head of Engineering for build cases; AAP Platform Lead for agentic decisions), Product Council (ratifies 3-year-TCO > PHP 25M), CEO (in-suite-tier removal waiver, rare), ARB (links to W3571), vendor-portfolio analyst (Register secretariat), Procurement/Legal (links to VS-34/VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Head of EA run the scored use-EBS → build assessment with the affected PO/PM and BPO: strategic differentiation, EBS fit-to-standard gap, de-unification integration cost, data gravity, PH regulatory fit, 3-year TCO, talent/key-person risk, and exit strategy — ordered test in-EBS → build: if an EBS module ships the function (or can be configured to), the answer is use it; build is admitted only when the EBS-standard answer demonstrably does not exist | Head of EA / Owning PO & BPO | CIO (SIB chair) | 6–10 hours/decision |
| 2 | Assemble the five mandatory appendices — no sourcing decision is valid without them: IAP integration estimate (flows, contracts, latency budget, run cost — links to W3579); control-mapping appendix naming every touched control of the 808-control register with its post-change evidence source; FinOps-verified TCO sheet (links to W4100); run-cost & talent plan for build decisions (the buy exit does not exist under the two-tier doctrine); re-evaluation trigger | Head of EA / FinOps / SEC / IAP | CIO (SIB chair) | Included in Step-1 pack |
| 3 | ARB record the architecture opinion (integration pattern, data ownership, retirement of affected capabilities — links to W3571); SEC pre-read the TPRM tier and Philippine-localization posture for any sourcing proposal (links to VS-161) | ARB / SEC | Head of EA | Per decision |
| 4 | SIB decide at the monthly board; vendor-portfolio analyst record the decision in the Capability Sourcing Register — capability, value streams, posture, product/system, owning team, re-evaluation trigger — the single source of truth for what is sourced how | SIB / Vendor-portfolio analyst | CIO (SIB chair) | At monthly SIB |
| 5 | Route decisions exceeding SIB decision rights: 3-year TCO > PHP 25M → Product Council ratification; removal of a capability from the in-suite tier → CEO-noted waiver (rare; same weight as the v1.x single-vendor waiver) | SIB / Product Council / CEO | CIO | Per qualifying decision |
| 6 | Maintain the Register lifecycle: annual reaffirmation of every standing decision at QBR; re-evaluation triggers monitored (vendor roadmap, scale milestones, regulatory change); fired triggers re-enter the gate at Step 1; register feed to portfolio reporting (W3592) and the sourcing reserve/exit-reserve disclosure (links to VS-135) | Vendor-portfolio analyst / Head of EA | CIO | Annual + continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Capability Sourcing Register (CIO Office secretariat — decision records, appendices, re-evaluation triggers, reaffirmation state)
- SIB/ARB/Product-Council decision log & meeting packs
- TBM/FinOps cost model (links to VS-135/W4100) — 3-year TCO verification
- TPRM register & risk tiering (links to VS-161) — sourcing-proposal pre-reads
- IAP contract catalog & integration estimates (links to W3579)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Gate-bypass risk**: a team contracts an unregistered capability product or service without an SIB decision, recreating siloed spend and the integration sprawl the single-path rule prevents; mitigated by the Register as the single decision record and the annual QBR reaffirmation exposing unregistered products
- **Appendix-degradation risk**: control-mapping or IAP estimates skipped under schedule pressure, leaving control-evidence gaps at vendor boundaries; mitigated by the no-decision-without-appendices rule and the SEC enabling cell's review of the control-mapping appendix

### Automation Opportunity
- Register record auto-drafted from the SIB decision pack and appendices (replaces manual Step 4 keying).
- Re-evaluation-trigger watchlist auto-monitors vendor-roadmap and scale milestones against the Register (replaces manual Step 6 tracking).

### Controls
- CTL-570 (ensure controlled execution — Technology Strategy, Innovation Governance & Architecture Analytics (PA-113.3).)
- operational: no sourcing decision without the five mandatory appendices (IAP estimate, control-mapping, TCO, exit/run plan, re-evaluation trigger)
- operational: every capability decision recorded in the Capability Sourcing Register; in-suite-tier changes require a CEO-noted waiver

### Time Estimate
Assessment packs ~6–10 hours/decision × ~30–45 decisions/year ≈ 180–450 hours/year; SIB secretariat ~2 hours/month × 12 months ≈ 24 hours/year. Total **≈ 204–474 hours/year** (≈0.10–0.23 FTE), absorbed within the CIO Office.

### Staffing Implication
- Absorbed within the sized CIO Office (Head of EA + vendor-portfolio analyst, OM §5.3/§9.1); the gate formalizes what the SIB already does monthly — no incremental headcount.
