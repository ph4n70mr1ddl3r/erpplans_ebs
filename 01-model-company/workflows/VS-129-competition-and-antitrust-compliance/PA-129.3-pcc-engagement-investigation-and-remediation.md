## W3969. Philippine Competition Commission (PCC) Relationship & Engagement Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Maintained relationship; PCC inquiry/guidance; policy consultation |
| **Frequency** | Ongoing |
| **Volume** | Enterprise PCC relationship |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance |
| **Participants** | Legal (links to VS-100), Govt Affairs (links to VS-104), CEO/CFO, affected business leads, external counsel |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Legal own the PCC relationship: a single accountable point of contact, awareness of current PCC priorities/guidelines/enforcement trends, participation in PCC public consultations (links to VS-104), and engagement of qualified external competition counsel on retainer | VP Legal / Legal / Govt Affairs | CEO | Maintained |
| 2 | VP Legal maintain the inquiry-response playbook and dawn-raid plan (links to W3970), the records/legal-hold interface (links to VS-88/W3970), and the privilege protocol for competition-sensitive advice | VP Legal / Legal | CEO | Maintained |
| 3 | VP Legal/CEO escalate any PCC inquiry/investigation to the Board Audit/Risk Committee (links to VS-36) and activate matter management (links to VS-100) | VP Legal / CEO | Board Audit Committee | On event |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal matter mgmt (links to VS-100); govt affairs (links to VS-104); records/legal hold (links to VS-88); governance (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Uncoordinated-response risk**: Multiple parties engaging PCC inconsistently; mitigated by single accountable contact
- **Privilege risk**: Sensitive advice not protected; mitigated by privilege protocol


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "accountable point of contact, awareness of current PCC…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "VP Legal maintain the inquiry-response playbook and dawn-raid plan" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-escalate of "escalate any PCC inquiry/investigation to the Board Audit/Risk Committee" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-619 (ensure controlled execution — Competition Authority (PCC) Engagement, Investigation & Remediation (PA-129.3).)
- operational: single accountable contact; operational: privilege protocol

### Time Estimate
- Maintained + on event

## W3970. Competition Inquiry, Dawn Raid & Information Request Response

| **Volume** | Rare but high-impact |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance |
| **Participants** | Legal, Compliance, IT (links to VS-27), Records (links to VS-88), Security (links to VS-23), affected site leadership, external counsel, executive team |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Legal execute the dawn-raid plan on unannounced inspection: verify PCC authority and scope, contact external counsel, manage staff conduct (courtesy, no obstruction, no destruction of documents), and document everything seized/requested | VP Legal / Legal / Site Leadership | CEO | On event (immediate) |
| 2 | VP Legal manage information requests/summonses: scope negotiation, privilege/log review, legal hold (links to VS-88), controlled production, and tracking | VP Legal / Legal / Records / IT | CEO | On event |
| 3 | VP Legal debrief, preserve lessons, brief the Board Audit Committee (links to VS-36), and adjust controls/training (links to W3960/W3968) | VP Legal / Legal | CEO | Post-event |

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Over-production risk**: Privileged or out-of-scope material produced; mitigated by scope negotiation and log review


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "VP Legal execute the dawn-raid plan on unannounced inspection" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "log review, legal hold" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System continuous audit of "VP Legal debrief, preserve lessons, brief the Board Audit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-619 (ensure controlled execution — Competition Authority (PCC) Engagement, Investigation & Remediation (PA-129.3).)
- operational: plan, training, and on-site leadership; operational: scope negotiation and log review

## W3972. Leniency, Settlement & Commitment (Whistleblower) Program Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Detected cartel/serious conduct; PCC offer of settlement/commitment; strategic leniency application |
| **Frequency** | On event |
| **Volume** | Rare |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance |
| **Participants** | VP Legal, external competition counsel, CEO, Board Audit Committee, Compliance (links to VS-119), affected executives/staff |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Legal/external-counsel assess leniency/marker eligibility for any detected cartel conduct (price-fixing, bid-rigging, market/customer allocation) and decide whether to file a leniency/marker application with the PCC for immunity/reduced fines, coordinating with speak-up (links to VS-119) | VP Legal / External Counsel | CEO / Board Audit Committee | On event |
| 2 | VP Legal assess settlement vs full-defense vs commitment options during a PCC matter, weighing fine reduction, conduct-change obligations, admission scope, and downstream private-action exposure | VP Legal / External Counsel | CEO | Per matter |
| 3 | VP Legal execute the chosen path (leniency cooperation, settlement agreement, or binding commitments), manage the obligations, and document the decision basis (links to records VS-88) | VP Legal / External Counsel | CEO | Per matter + ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal (links to VS-100); ethics/speak-up (links to VS-119); records (links to VS-88); governance (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missed-leniency risk**: Late application loses immunity; mitigated by rapid assessment on detection
- **Over-admission risk**: Settlement admissions widen private-action exposure; mitigated by careful scoping


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-file of "file a leniency/marker application with the PCC for immunity/reduced fines,…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "VP Legal execute the chosen path" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-619 (ensure controlled execution — Competition Authority (PCC) Engagement, Investigation & Remediation (PA-129.3).)
- operational: rapid assessment on detection; operational: careful scoping

### Time Estimate
- Per matter
