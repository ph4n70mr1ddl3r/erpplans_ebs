## W3961. Pricing Decision & Competitor-Information-Exchange Controls

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Pricing decision; competitive-intel activity; info-sharing request |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~Thousands of pricing decisions/yr; ongoing competitive monitoring |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising / VP Legal |
| **Participants** | Merchandising/Pricing (links to VS-01.2), Competitive Intel (links to VS-57), Marketing (links to VS-14), Revenue Assurance (links to VS-118), Store Ops (links to VS-07) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandising/Pricing document every significant pricing decision (SRP, trade price, promo, markdown) as a *unilateral* BuildRight decision with internal-cost/margin basis, and route competitively sensitive inputs only through controlled channels (public sources, legitimate VS-57 intelligence) | Merchandising / Pricing | VP Merchandising | Per decision |
| 2 | Legal maintain the competitor-information-exchange control: no exchanges with competitors of future prices, output, margins, costs, territories, customers, or bidding plans; a "clean team" protocol for any legitimately acquired third-party data; and immediate-escalation guidance if a competitor raises a prohibited topic | Legal / Pricing / Competitive Intel | VP Legal | Maintained |
| 3 | Merchandising/Legal log the decision record and basis (links to records VS-88), and feed anomalies to monitoring/audit (W3968) and speak-up (links to VS-119) | Merchandising / Legal | VP Merchandising | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing (links to VS-01.2); competitive intel (links to VS-57); records (links to VS-88); audit (links to VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unilateral-action risk**: Decision looks coordinated without documented basis; mitigated by decision record and clean-team protocol
- **Inbound-breach risk**: Competitor raises prohibited topic; mitigated by escalation protocol and documentation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "document every significant pricing decision" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "log the decision record and basis" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-618 (ensure controlled execution — Anti-Competitive Conduct Prevention & Compliance Controls (PA-129.2).)
- operational: decision record and clean-team protocol; operational: escalation protocol and documentation

### Time Estimate
- Continuous

## W3962. Vendor Agreement, RPM & Exclusivity Clause Review & Contract Controls

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New/renewed vendor or trade agreement; clause-library change |
| **Frequency** | Per contract + maintained clause library |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 vendors; ~1,400–1,600 POs/month (links to VS-03) |
| **Owner** | VP Legal / VP Supply Chain |
| **Participants** | Legal (links to VS-100.3), Procurement (links to VS-03), Merchandising (links to VS-01), Contract Management (links to VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal maintain the antitrust clause library and prohibited-clause list: RPM (mandatory resale price, max/min resale price, MAP enforced as price), naked exclusivity, most-favored-nation/parity where foreclosure-prone, territorial/customer restraints outside selective distribution, and tying of unrelated goods | Legal | VP Legal | Maintained |
| 2 | Legal/Procurement review every vendor and trade agreement against the clause library before execution (gate into VS-100.3 contract review and VS-03 vendor onboarding), flag and remediate non-compliant clauses, and document effect-based justification for any arguably-restrictive but pro-competitive clause | Legal / Procurement | VP Legal | Per contract |
| 3 | Legal monitor executed agreements for drift (amendments, side letters, rebate/MAP enforcement that functions as RPM), and refresh the clause library on PCC guidance change | Legal / Contract Mgmt | VP Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract mgmt (links to VS-100.3); vendor mgmt (links to VS-03); records (links to VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **RPM risk**: Clauses or rebate/MAP enforcement that fix resale price; mitigated by clause library and review gate
- **Drift risk**: Side arrangements recreating restraints; mitigated by monitoring and audit


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "document effect-based justification for any arguably-restrictive but…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "monitor executed agreements for drift" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-618 (ensure controlled execution — Anti-Competitive Conduct Prevention & Compliance Controls (PA-129.2).)
- operational: clause library and review gate; operational: monitoring and audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Proposed M&A/JV meeting PCC notification thresholds (links to VS-130) |
| **Frequency** | Per transaction |
| **Volume** | Occasional (growth-stage company) |
| **Owner** | VP Legal / Corp Dev |
| **Participants** | Legal, Corp Dev (links to VS-130), Finance (links to VS-17.4/VS-40), Tax (links to VS-79), Strategy (links to VS-33), affected business unit |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal/Corp-Dev assess notifiability of every proposed acquisition, merger, JV, or asset purchase against PCC thresholds (transaction-value and turnover thresholds in the PCC Rules for Mergers and Acquisitions, as updated), and determine the notification timeline (mandatory pre-notification within 30 days of agreement) | Legal / Corp Dev | VP Legal | Per transaction |
| 2 | Legal prepare and file the PCC notification (Form 1 and supporting documents — parties, transaction, market definition, overlaps, competitive effects, efficiencies), manage the review period (Phase 1 / Phase 2), and respond to PCC requests for information | Legal / Corp Dev / Strategy | VP Legal / CEO | Per transaction |
| 3 | Legal manage gun-jumping risk (no closing/coordination before clearance), conditions/remedies if offered, and post-clearance compliance; coordinate with foreign filings where the target/BuildRight's B2B customers trigger extraterritorial review | Legal / Corp Dev | VP Legal | Per transaction + ongoing |

### System Touchpoints

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Gun-jumping risk**: Closing or coordinating before PCC clearance; mitigated by staging and clean-team
- **Incomplete-filing risk**: Notification rejected or Phase 2 triggered by weak competitive analysis; mitigated by thorough market definition

- System auto-update of "Legal/Corp-Dev assess notifiability of every proposed acquisition, merger, JV" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documents — parties, transaction, market definition, overlaps, competitive…" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-618 (ensure controlled execution — Anti-Competitive Conduct Prevention & Compliance Controls (PA-129.2).)
