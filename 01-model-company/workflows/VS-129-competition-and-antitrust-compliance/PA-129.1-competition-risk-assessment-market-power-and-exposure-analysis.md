| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual strategy (links to VS-33.1); program stand-up; regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise competition-compliance program (5 entities) |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance / Chief Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Legal (links to VS-100), Compliance (links to VS-22/VS-119), Merchandising/Pricing (links to VS-01.2/VS-57), Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34), Trade/B2B Sales (links to VS-11/VS-43/VS-82), Corp Dev (links to VS-130), Govt Affairs (links to VS-104), Internal Audit (links to VS-21), HR/L&D (links to VS-19.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Legal & Compliance define the competition-compliance program: scope (all 5 entities, all product categories, all channels — POS, ecommerce, marketplace, B2B/trade, B2G), framework (RA 10667 + IRR + PCC guidelines + foreign regimes where BuildRight's B2B customers/export-import touchpoints apply), governance (Board Audit/Risk Committee oversight, Compliance-led operating model, business-unit compliance partners), and KPIs (risk-coverage %, training completion, control-testing pass rate, incidents, PCC engagements) | VP Legal & Compliance | CEO / Board Audit Committee | Annual |
| 2 | VP Legal & Compliance map the program to BuildRight's risk surface and assign accountability: pricing (Merchandising), vendor/channel contracts (Procurement + Legal), associations (Govt Affairs), procurement/buyer-power (Procurement), digital/marketplace (Ecommerce/Digital Commerce Inc.), M&A (Corp Dev); integrate with ethics/speak-up (links to VS-119), audit (links to VS-21), records (links to VS-88), and the existing single workflow W2683 (now superseded as the program summary) | VP Legal & Compliance | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal/compliance matter management (links to VS-100); ERM/risk register (links to VS-21.2); governance/board reporting (links to VS-36); strategy (links to VS-33); records (links to VS-88)

- **Fragmented-ownership risk**: Pricing, procurement, and sales each optimize locally without antitrust lens; mitigated by program-level ownership and embedded compliance partners
- **No-owner risk**: Today a single workflow (W2683) covers all of competition law; mitigated by elevation to a dedicated program

- System auto-record of "VP Legal & Compliance map the program to BuildRight's risk surface" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-capture of "CEO/Board ratify the strategy, approve the compliance budget and" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-617 (ensure controlled execution — Competition Risk Assessment, Market Power & Exposure Analysis (PA-129.1).)

- operational: program-level ownership and embedded compliance partners; operational: elevation to a dedicated program

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal/Strategy define the relevant product and geographic markets per PCC guidelines (substitutability, SSNIP/hypothetical-monopolist reasoning) for each BuildRight category (lumber/building materials, tiles, plumbing, electrical, paint, tools, hardware, appliances, garden) and channel (big-box retail, B2B/trade, ecommerce, marketplace) — noting the Philippines big-box hardware/DIY market and provincial/regional sub-markets | Legal / Strategy / Insights | VP Legal | Annual |
| 2 | Strategy/Finance estimate BuildRight's market share and position in each relevant market (revenue-based, with caveats), identify whether BuildRight holds a *dominant position* (RA 10667 §15 presumption thresholds: ≥50% individual share, or the top two entities with ≥60% combined), and flag markets where dominance triggers special-conduct obligations | Strategy / Finance / Legal | VP Legal | Annual |

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Evidence risk**: Share estimates undocumented; mitigated by retained analysis and refresh cycle
