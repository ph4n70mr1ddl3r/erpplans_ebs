# VS-16: Order-to-Cash

> **Finance** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Order-to-cash: credit application & scoring, AR & collections, and customer payment & settlement for ~5,200 trade accounts.

## Why it matters

~40% of revenue is B2B credit; credit-risk discipline, collections, and payment integrity govern DSO, bad debt, and working capital. PDC and CWT (2307) are PH-specific.

## Owner & participants

- **Owner**: Credit Manager / Controller
