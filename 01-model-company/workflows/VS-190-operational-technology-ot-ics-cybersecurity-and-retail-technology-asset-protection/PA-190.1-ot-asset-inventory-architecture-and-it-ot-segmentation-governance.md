## W5417. OT/ICS Asset Discovery, Inventory & Cyber Risk Taxonomy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program stand-up; new site/store/DC commissioning (VS-37); new connected-device channel go-live (VS-163/VS-164/VS-149) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous discovery; full re-inventory quarterly |
| **Volume** | 1 OT estate across ~205 sites + 4 DCs + HQ — ~25,000–40,000 OT endpoints (600 POS, ~3–5 BMS/fire panels/site, access-control controllers, ~200+ generators, SCADA on solar plants, EV/locker/SCO controllers, weighbridges, IoT sensors) |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead (IT Security, VS-27.3) |
| **Participants** | Facilities/IFM (VS-138), DC Ops (VS-04), Store Ops (VS-07), Energy/Solar Ops (VS-108), EV/Locker Ops (VS-163/VS-164), IT Asset Mgmt (VS-99) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Passive/active-discover OT endpoints per site (ARP/LLDP sweeps, asset tags, vendor manifests from IFM/DC) — never active-scan a safety PLC without ops sign-off. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | per site |
| 2 | Classify each asset into the OT taxonomy: BMS/HVAC, fire/life-safety, access control, CCTV/EAS (VS-23), generator/ATS, fuel monitoring, paint/cutting PLC, lumber-yard automation, SCADA solar, EV controller, locker/SCO controller, POS, RF/WMS infra, weighbridge, IoT sensor. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | per asset |
| 3 | Capture the cyber attributes IT CMDBs miss: firmware/PLC revision, engineering-workstation exposure, default-credential status, serial/fieldbus (Modbus/BACnet/OPC-UA), vendor, safety impact level. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | per asset |
| 4 | Assign OT criticality tier (Safety-of-life / Store-closing / Revenue / Operational) driving segmentation and monitoring priority. | OT Security Lead | OT Security Lead | per asset |
| 5 | Load into the OT asset register (separate view from IT CMDB per VS-99) and reconcile against IFM/DC/EV/locker asset lists. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- OT asset register / OT-CMDB module (distinct from IT CMDB in VS-99); IFM BMS asset list (VS-138); DC WMS device list (VS-04); solar SCADA tag list (VS-108); EV/locker controller inventory (VS-163/VS-164); POS estate register (VS-08).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Shadow-OT risk**: connected devices (smart lockers VS-164, EV chargers VS-163, SCO VS-149, IoT flood sensors VS-69) procured by business units without security review ship with vendor cloud links and default creds — mitigated by the W5421 onboarding gate and quarterly reconciliation.
- **Discovery-induced-outage risk**: aggressive active scanning crashes a fragile PLC/BMS or trips a fire panel; mitigated by passive-first discovery and ops sign-off before any active probe of a safety system.

### Automation Opportunity
- Passive ARP/LLDP/MAC-vendor fingerprinting auto-populating the OT register; fieldbus protocol discovery (BACnet/Modbus/OPC-UA); drift-detection vs IFM/DC asset lists.

### Controls
- CTL-800 (ensure controlled execution — OT Asset Inventory, Architecture & IT/OT Segmentation Governance (PA-190.1).)
- operational: asset-inventory completeness extended to OT; segregation of duties on OT criticality assignment; no active scan of a safety PLC without facilities/ops sign-off.

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 person-days/site initial inventory (~600–800 person-days one-time across 205 sites, phased over 18–24 months); ~0.5 day/site quarterly refresh.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-99 (IT asset lifecycle), VS-138 (IFM/BMS), VS-04 (DC WMS devices), VS-108 (solar SCADA), VS-163/VS-164/VS-149 (connected-device channels), VS-08 (POS estate), VS-23 (CCTV/EAS)

## W5418. OT Security Architecture, Purdue-Model Zone Design & IT/OT Segmentation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program stand-up; new site/remodel network design (VS-109); new connected-device channel; recurring architecture review |
| **Frequency** | Architecture refresh annual; per major site project |
| **Volume** | 1 reference OT architecture + per-site zone design across ~205 sites |
| **Owner** | OT Security Architect (Enterprise Architecture, VS-113) |
| **Participants** | Network Engineering (VS-27.2), IFM (VS-138), DC Ops (VS-04), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Adopt a Purdue Enterprise Reference Architecture / IEC 62443 zone-and-conduit model adapted for retail: Level 0–3 OT (safety/field/control/operations) vs Level 4–5 IT (corporate/internet), with a demilitarized zone (DMZ) between. | OT Security Architect | OT Security Lead | setup |
| 2 | Define zone groupings by safety/operational impact: a fire/life-safety zone, a BMS/HVAC zone, a payment zone (POS/SCO/lockers), an energy zone (solar SCADA/EV), a logistics zone (WMS/RF/weighbridge). | OT Security Architect | OT Security Lead | setup |
| 3 | Specify the IT/OT boundary controls: one-way data diodes or read-only historians where feasible, application-layer proxies in the DMZ, deny-by-default firewall policy between zones. | Network Engineer | OT Security Lead | setup |
| 4 | Document allowed conduits (which OT system legitimately talks to which IT/cloud system — e.g. BMS→IFM cloud, SCADA→utility) and forbid everything else. | OT Security Architect | OT Security Lead | setup |
| 5 | Review at the enterprise-architecture forum (VS-113) and align to the cloud/integration perimeter. | OT Security Architect | CIO | annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Network segmentation/firewall config (VS-27.2); enterprise architecture repository (VS-113); IFM BMS cloud (VS-138); solar SCADA historian (VS-108); POS payment network (VS-08/VS-80).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Convergence-outage risk**: flat networks where a compromised corporate laptop can reach a fire panel or SCADA controller; mitigated by enforced IT/OT segmentation with deny-by-default policy.
- **Legitimate-conduit-strangulation risk**: over-restrictive segmentation breaks BMS-to-cloud or SCADA-to-utility telemetry and degrades energy/facilities ops; mitigated by documented allowed-conduits and proxy/diode design.

### Automation Opportunity
- Firewall-rule / conduit drift detection against the documented allowed-conduit list; segmentation-test automation (controlled reachability probes from IT→OT zones).

### Controls
- CTL-800 (ensure controlled execution — OT Asset Inventory, Architecture & IT/OT Segmentation Governance (PA-190.1).)
- operational: IT/OT segregation; architecture documentation; deny-by-default firewall policy with documented exception conduits.

### Time Estimate
- ~6–8 weeks elapsed reference-architecture setup; ~2–4 weeks per major site/remodel zone design; ~1 week annual review.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-113 (enterprise architecture), VS-27.2 (network/platform), VS-138 (BMS), VS-108 (SCADA), VS-80 (payment network)

## W5419. OT Identity, Credential & Role-Based Access Control Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program stand-up; device onboarding (W5421); vendor/staff access provisioning |
| **Frequency** | Setup; periodic access recertification quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~25,000–40,000 OT endpoints + ~300–500 engineering/vendor accounts |
| **Owner** | OT Identity Lead (IT Security, VS-27.3) |
| **Participants** | IFM (VS-138), vendor service teams, DC/Store ops, HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eliminate shared/default credentials on OT devices — per-device unique creds, a privileged-access-management (PAM) vault for the engineering-workstation and admin accounts. | OT Identity Lead | OT Security Lead | setup |
| 2 | Define OT RBAC roles (read-only operator, maintenance, engineering, vendor-remote) mapped to the zone model (W5418). | OT Identity Lead | OT Security Lead | setup |
| 3 | Enforce MFA on all interactive OT/admin access; require jump-host (bastion) for any vendor/remote OT session (W5433). | OT Identity Lead | OT Security Lead | setup |
| 4 | Quarterly recertify OT access — confirm each engineering/vendor account still has a live business need (links to VS-167 vetting and VS-161 TPRM). | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | quarterly |
| 5 | Rotate credentials on vendor/contractor offboarding and on role change; revoke standing access in favor of just-in-time. | OT Identity Lead | OT Security Lead | per event |

### System Touchpoints
- PAM/privileged-access vault (VS-27.3 extended to OT); identity provider (VS-27); OT RBAC policy on controllers; vendor-management records (VS-161 TPRM).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Default-credential risk**: BMS/fire/PLC devices shipped with vendor-default passwords that are publicly documented — the single most common OT compromise vector; mitigated by mandatory per-device unique creds + PAM vault before commissioning.
- **Standing-vendor-access risk**: a vendor with always-on remote access to a fire panel or SCADA controller is a persistent intrusion path; mitigated by just-in-time, jump-hosted, session-recorded access (W5433).

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-flag of "Eliminate shared/default credentials on OT devices" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-flag of "Quarterly recertify OT access" (replaces manual Step 4).
- System auto-revoke of "Rotate credentials on vendor/contractor offboarding" (replaces manual Step 5).

### Controls
- CTL-800 (ensure controlled execution — OT Asset Inventory, Architecture & IT/OT Segmentation Governance (PA-190.1).)
- operational: access control / least privilege extended to OT; privileged-account management; no default credentials on any commissioned OT device.

### Time Estimate
- ~8–10 weeks elapsed setup (largely the default-cred remediation sweep across ~205 sites); ~2 weeks per quarterly recert.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27.3 (IT identity/PAM), VS-167 (workforce vetting), VS-161 (vendor/TPRM), VS-138 (IFM access)

## W5420. OT Device Hardening, Secure Configuration Baseline & Change Control

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program stand-up; device onboarding (W5421); vulnerability/findings remediation |
| **Frequency** | Baseline refresh annual; per-device hardening at onboarding |
| **Volume** | 1 baseline per OT device class (BMS, fire, PLC, SCADA, POS, SCO, locker, EV, RF, weighbridge) |
| **Owner** | OT Security Engineer |
| **Participants** | IFM (VS-138), DC Ops (VS-04), vendor service, HSE (VS-24), Change Advisory (VS-27.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Author a secure-configuration baseline per OT device class (disable unused services/ports, enforce logging, disable vendor cloud links unless approved, lock engineering-mode). | OT Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | per class |
| 2 | Validate each baseline against a non-production instance before deployment (never harden a live safety system untested). | OT Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | per class |
| 3 | Submit hardening changes through the OT-aware change-control process (VS-27.1) with explicit safety/ops impact assessment and rollback. | OT Security Engineer | Change Advisory Board | per change |
| 4 | Apply the baseline at onboarding (W5421) and on a remediation sweep for in-life devices, in a maintenance window with ops standby. | OT Security Engineer / Vendor | OT Security Lead | per device |
| 5 | Re-baseline when a vendor firmware/PLC revision changes; track config drift between audits. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Secure-configuration baseline library; change-management system (VS-27.1); OT-CMDB config store (W5417); vendor firmware/PLC revision data.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hardening-induced-outage risk**: disabling a "legacy" service on a BMS or fire panel can break a safety function or vendor remote support; mitigated by non-prod validation and ops/vendor sign-off.
- **Config-drift risk**: vendors silently re-enable cloud links or default settings during a service visit; mitigated by drift detection and post-visit configuration verification.

### Automation Opportunity
- Configuration drift detection (current config vs baseline); automated baseline-compliance reporting; vendor-visit change detection.

### Controls
- CTL-800 (ensure controlled execution — OT Asset Inventory, Architecture & IT/OT Segmentation Governance (PA-190.1).)
- operational: least privilege / hardening; change management; secure configuration; non-prod validation before any live safety-system hardening.

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 weeks per device-class baseline; ~10–30 min/device hardening in a window; ~1 week annual re-baseline sweep.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27.1 (change/release mgmt), VS-138 (IFM config), VS-115 (calibration — secure the calibrated device's connected layer), VS-99 (asset config baseline)

## W5421. Connected-Retail-Device Onboarding Security Gate (POS, SCO, RF, Locker, EV, BMS)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New connected-device procurement/deployment (new POS model, SCO rollout VS-149, smart-locker VS-164, EV host VS-163, BMS upgrade VS-138, solar SCADA VS-108, IoT sensor VS-69) |
| **Frequency** | Per new device/channel/model — ~10–30 onboarding events/yr across channels + new-store/DC commissioning |
| **Volume** | ~600 POS + ~per-store SCO/locker/EV/BMS at ~10–15 new stores/yr + refresh cycles |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead |
| **Participants** | Procurement (VS-34), IT Asset Mgmt (VS-99), Store Opening (VS-37), IFM (VS-138), VS-163/VS-164/VS-149 channel owners, VS-161 TPRM |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Require a pre-procurement OT security review of any new connected device — vendor SBOM, firmware update model, cloud-dependency, default-credential posture, disclosed vulnerabilities/CVE history. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | per model |
| 2 | Verify the device passes its class secure-configuration baseline (W5420) and IT/OT segmentation placement (W5418) before PO release. | OT Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | per model |
| 3 | Confirm default credentials replaced, vendor cloud links explicitly approved or disabled, and logging/monitoring onboarded (W5425) at install. | OT Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | per site |
| 4 | Register the device in the OT asset register (W5417) with criticality tier and onboarding evidence; block go-live until registered. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | per device |
| 5 | Feed device vendor risk into the TPRM tiering (VS-161) and contractual security requirements (SBOM, vuln disclosure, support lifecycle). | OT Security Lead | VP Legal & Compliance | per model |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/PO gate (VS-34); OT asset register (W5417); TPRM vendor records (VS-161); secure-config baseline (W5420); monitoring onboarding (W5425); store-commissioning checklist (VS-37).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Procurement-end-run risk**: a business unit buys smart-lockers/EV chargers/IoT sensors direct-to-store bypassing the security gate — mitigated by a hard PO-gate block and new-store-commissioning checklist item.
- **EOL-firmware risk**: a low-cost device ships on an unsupported firmware/OS with no vendor patch path; mitigated by the support-lifecycle check in step 1 and TPRM tiering.

### Automation Opportunity
- PO-gate integration blocking release until the OT security review is recorded; auto-population of the OT register from commissioning data; SBOM ingestion.

### Controls
- CTL-800 (ensure controlled execution — OT Asset Inventory, Architecture & IT/OT Segmentation Governance (PA-190.1).)
- operational: asset inventory/onboarding gate; secure configuration; TPRM/vendor security; operational: no connected retail device commissioned without a recorded OT security review.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 weeks per new device-model review; ~15–30 min/device onboarding verification at install.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-34 (procurement gate), VS-99 (IT asset onboarding), VS-37 (store commissioning), VS-149/VS-164/VS-163 (connected-device channels), VS-161 (TPRM)

## W5422. OT Network Architecture Review, Firewall Rule & Zone-Boundary Lifecycle

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly lifecycle review; post-incident (W5430); major change (new channel/site) |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly rule/boundary review; per change |
| **Volume** | 1 OT firewall/segmentation rulebase per site × ~205 locations (200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ) |
| **Owner** | OT Network Security Engineer |
| **Participants** | Network Engineering (VS-27.2), IFM (VS-138), DC Ops (VS-04), Internal Audit (VS-21.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pull the current OT/zone-boundary firewall and ACL rulebase per site and reconcile to the documented allowed-conduit list (W5418). | OT Network Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | quarterly |
| 2 | Flag "any-any" / overly-broad / stale / unused rules and orphaned conduits for cleanup. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | quarterly |
| 3 | Validate segmentation effectiveness with controlled reachability testing (IT→OT, internet→OT, zone→zone). | OT Network Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | quarterly |
| 4 | Approve and apply rule changes through change control (VS-27.1) with documented business justification. | OT Network Security Engineer | Change Advisory Board | per change |
| 5 | Report zone-boundary hygiene metrics to the architecture forum (VS-113) and audit (VS-21.3). | OT Security Lead | CIO | quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Firewall/ACL management (VS-27.2 extended to OT); change management (VS-27.1); allowed-conduit register (W5418); architecture repository (VS-113).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rule-creep risk**: emergency/temporary firewall rules accumulate and silently widen the IT/OT attack surface; mitigated by quarterly stale-rule cleanup and expiry dates on every exception.
- **Segmentation-bypass risk**: an undocumented modem, vendor VPN, or IoT cellular backhaul (VS-164/VS-163) bypasses the controlled boundary; mitigated by reachability testing and the W5421 onboarding gate.

### Automation Opportunity
- Rulebase analytics (stale/unused/over-broad rule detection); automated reachability testing; rule-expiry enforcement.

### Controls
- CTL-800: IT/OT zone-segmentation enforcement evidence; CTL-800 (OT asset/segmentation rulebase change control); CTL-800 (rulebase documentation retained for audit); operational: every exception rule carries an expiry date and business owner.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days/site per review (~205–410 person-days/yr at a rotating ~25%-per-quarter sample with annual full coverage of ~205 sites, field-team assisted); ~1–3 days per change.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27.2 (network/firewall ops), VS-27.1 (change mgmt), VS-113 (architecture review), VS-21.3 (audit)

## W5423. OT Asset End-of-Life Security, Secure Decommissioning & Cryptographic Erase

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Device refresh/replacement (VS-99 lifecycle), store closure (VS-59), remodel (VS-109), vendor swap |
| **Frequency** | Per device retirement — ~1,000–3,000 OT device retirements/yr across refresh cycles |
| **Volume** | POS (600 on a refresh), BMS/fire/access panels, SCADA/PLC, SCO/locker/EV controllers, RF/weighbridge |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead |
| **Participants** | IT Asset Mgmt (VS-99), IFM (VS-138), Store Ops (VS-07), records/retention (VS-88) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before physical removal: revoke credentials, close vendor remote access (W5433), and disconnect from the network/zone. | OT Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | per device |
| 2 | For devices holding data (POS, SCO, BMS historian, SCADA logger, access-control DB): perform cryptographic/logical erase per VS-99 W3254 and verify. | OT Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | per device |
| 3 | For controller/PLC devices: reset to factory, remove engineering programs, and confirm no residual site-specific logic/config. | OT Security Engineer / Vendor | OT Security Lead | per device |
| 4 | Update the OT asset register (W5417) to retired; retain decommissioning evidence per records retention (VS-88, BIR 10-yr). | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | per device |
| 5 | Coordinate secure disposal/recycling (VS-73/VS-99 W3254 e-waste) so no controller with site logic or credentials is resold or landfilled. | IT Asset Mgmt | OT Security Lead | per device |

### System Touchpoints
- OT asset register retirement (W5417); ITAM disposal (VS-99 W3254); records/retention (VS-88); e-waste/recycling (VS-73).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Residual-credential risk**: a retired POS/BMS/SCADA device resold with cached credentials or site logic gives an attacker a blueprint of BuildRight's environment; mitigated by mandatory cryptographic/factory erase with verified evidence.
- **Premature-disconnect risk**: disconnecting a controller before ops has cutover to its replacement can drop a safety/building function; mitigated by sequencing with the refresh/commissioning plan (VS-99/VS-109).

### Automation Opportunity
- Erase-evidence capture into the asset register; auto-revocation of credentials/access on retirement flag; certificate/key revocation automation.

### Controls
- CTL-800 (ensure controlled execution — OT Asset Inventory, Architecture & IT/OT Segmentation Governance (PA-190.1).)
- operational: asset register retirement; secure disposal/erase; data retention — extended to OT; operational: verified erase evidence before any device leaves BuildRight control.

### Time Estimate
- ~20–45 min/device decommissioning (longer for SCADA/BMS historians with data-erase verification); batched at refresh/remodel/closure.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-99 (IT asset lifecycle/disposal), VS-59 (store closure), VS-109 (remodel), VS-88 (records), VS-73 (e-waste)

## W5424. OT Security Posture Dashboard, Criticality Tiering & Risk Acceptance Register

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly governance cycle; board/audit risk reporting (VS-21.2/VS-36); new finding |
| **Frequency** | Monthly dashboard; quarterly tiering/risk-acceptance review |
| **Volume** | 1 OT security posture dashboard + ~1 risk register |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead |
| **Participants** | CIO, IT Security Manager/IT Security (VS-27.3), Internal Audit (VS-21), ERM (VS-21.2), HSE (VS-24), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aggregate OT posture: asset coverage (W5417), segmentation hygiene (W5422), default-cred/baseline compliance (W5419/W5420), open vulnerabilities (W5427), incidents (W5430), third-party access (W5433). | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | monthly |
| 2 | Maintain the OT criticality tiering (Safety-of-life / Store-closing / Revenue / Operational) driving prioritization. | OT Security Lead | OT Security Lead | quarterly |
| 3 | Log accepted OT risks (where remediation is deferred for ops/safety/cost reasons) with owner, expiry, and compensating control. | OT Security Lead | CIO | per risk |
| 4 | Feed OT cyber risk into enterprise risk management (VS-21.2), cyber-insurance underwriting (VS-26), and board reporting (VS-36). | OT Security Lead | CIO | quarterly |
| 5 | Review risk-acceptance expiries and escalate overdue remediation to the architecture/exec forum. | OT Security Lead | CIO | quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- ICM risk library (the in-suite GRC register, fit-gap H11; VS-21.2 extended to OT); OT posture dashboard (W5417–W5433 feeds); board reporting (VS-36); cyber-insurance records (VS-26).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Silent-acceptance risk**: OT risks deferred indefinitely without an owner/expiry accumulate into an unmanaged exposure; mitigated by mandatory owner + expiry on every accepted risk and quarterly review.
- **Underinsurance risk**: cyber/property insurance under-priced for the OT exposure because underwriter wasn't told the SCADA/BMS/fire-panel attack surface; mitigated by feeding OT posture to underwriting (VS-26).

### Automation Opportunity
- Posture-metric auto-aggregation from W5417–W5433 feeds; risk-acceptance expiry alerts; ICM (GRC) integration.

### Controls
- CTL-800: ensure controlled execution — OT Asset Inventory, Architecture & IT/OT Segmentation Governance (PA-190.1).) — risk acceptance governance; CTL-46 (posture/coverage evidence; CTL-37 (insurance/underwriter disclosure); operational: every accepted OT cyber risk has a named owner and expiry.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 week/monthly dashboard cycle; ~1 week/quarterly tiering & risk-acceptance review.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2 (ERM), VS-21 (audit), VS-36 (board reporting), VS-26 (cyber-insurance), VS-27.3 (IT security posture)
