## W5433. OT-Aware Third-Party / Vendor Remote-Access Governance & Session Recording

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor service contract setup; remote-support request; periodic recertification |
| **Frequency** | Setup per vendor; per remote session; quarterly recertification |
| **Volume** | ~50–150 vendor/contractor organizations with potential OT remote access (BMS, fire, SCADA, EV, locker, POS service, IFM providers); ~100s–1,000s sessions/yr |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead |
| **Participants** | Vendor service teams, IFM (VS-138), Procurement (VS-34), VS-161 TPRM, Legal (VS-100), HSE (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Require every vendor OT remote session to traverse a BuildRight-controlled jump-host/bastion with MFA, just-in-time approval, and full session recording — never a vendor VPN/modem direct to the controller. | OT Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | setup |
| 2 | Contractually bind vendors (via Procurement/VS-34 and TPRM/VS-161) to cyber terms: no standing access, notification of compromise, SBOM/vuln disclosure, personnel vetting (VS-167). | Procurement / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | per contract |
| 3 | Approve each session against a ticket with business justification + ops sign-off (especially for safety-system access); time-box the access. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | per session |
| 4 | Record and review sessions (sampling + 100% for safety-system access); alert on policy violation (off-hours, out-of-scope device, file transfer). | OT SOC Analyst | OT SOC Lead | per session |
| 5 | Revoke and offboard vendor access at contract end or on TPRM trigger (VS-161) — confirm zero residual paths (W5423). | OT Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | per offboard |

### System Touchpoints
- PAM/bastion/jump-host (VS-27.3 extended to OT); session-recording store; ticket/approval workflow; TPRM vendor records (VS-161); contract management (VS-100/VS-34).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vendor-backdoor risk**: a vendor-installed out-of-band modem or "support" cloud link is the single most common OT breach path (Target-style); mitigated by mandatory bastion routing and the W5421 onboarding gate detecting rogue links.
- **Off-hours-misuse risk**: a vendor session at 2 AM to a fire panel or POS estate is a red flag; mitigated by session-recording review and off-hours alerting.

### Automation Opportunity
- Just-in-time access auto-provisioning from approved tickets; off-hours/out-of-scope auto-alert; session-recording anomaly detection.

### Controls
- CTL-802 (ensure controlled execution — OT Compliance, Third-Party Access & Cyber Resilience Analytics (PA-190.3).)
- operational: least privilege / JIT vendor access; session recording via bastion host; no vendor direct network path to any OT controller.

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks bastion/contract setup; ~5–10 min/session approval; ~sampling review weekly.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-161 (TPRM), VS-167 (vendor personnel vetting), VS-34 (procurement), VS-100 (contracts), VS-138 (IFM vendor access)

## W5434. OT Cyber Supply-Chain & SBOM Risk Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New connected-device procurement (W5421); vendor acquisition/EOL announcement; supply-chain incident |
| **Frequency** | Per device model; continuous monitoring |
| **Volume** | ~25,000–40,000 OT endpoints across ~10–20 device classes and ~50–150 vendors |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead |
| **Participants** | VS-161 TPRM, Procurement (VS-34/VS-03), VS-99 ITAM, channel owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Require a software bill of materials (SBOM) and firmware/support-lifecycle commitment from every OT device vendor at procurement (W5421). | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | per model |
| 2 | Maintain the OT SBOM repository and map disclosed component vulnerabilities (e.g., a vulnerable openssl/log4j equivalent in a controller firmware) to BuildRight devices. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | ongoing |
| 3 | Assess fourth-party risk — a sub-component or cloud dependency inside a BMS/SCADA/locker/EV controller that itself has a risky supplier; concentrate with VS-161. | OT Security Lead | OT Security Lead | per model |
| 4 | Monitor vendor lifecycle (acquisition, end-of-support, insolvency) and trigger replacement (VS-99/W5423) before a device class goes unpatched. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | ongoing |
| 5 | Coordinate OT supply-chain risk into enterprise TPRM reporting (VS-161) and the risk register (W5424). | OT Security Lead | CIO | quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- OT SBOM repository; TPRM platform (VS-161); OT asset register (W5417); procurement/contract records (VS-34).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Opaque-firmware risk**: vendors refuse or cannot produce an SBOM, hiding vulnerable components; mitigated by making SBOM a procurement gate (W5421) and deprioritizing non-compliant vendors.
- **Silent-EOL risk**: a vendor end-of-lives a controller firmware with no migration path, leaving BuildRight running unpatchable devices; mitigated by lifecycle monitoring and proactive replacement planning (VS-99).

### Automation Opportunity
- SBOM-to-CVE auto-mapping; vendor-lifecycle news monitoring; fourth-party concentration analytics.

### Controls
- CTL-802 (ensure controlled execution — OT Compliance, Third-Party Access & Cyber Resilience Analytics (PA-190.3).)
- operational: TPRM/vendor risk; SBOM/inventory; operational: SBOM required before any new OT device model is approved.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days/model SBOM ingestion + risk assessment; ~1 week/quarter TPRM consolidation.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-161 (TPRM), VS-34/VS-03 (procurement/vendor), VS-99 (asset lifecycle), W5421 (onboarding gate)

## W5435. OT Cybersecurity Compliance (IEC 62443 / NIST SP 800-82) & Audit Evidence

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual compliance cycle; audit (VS-21.3); customer/partner due-diligence (B2B/VS-107); regulatory expectation |
| **Frequency** | Annual self-assessment; per audit/due-diligence |
| **Volume** | 1 OT compliance program (IEC 62443-2-1 / 3-3 control set; NIST SP 800-82r3) |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead |
| **Participants** | Compliance (VS-22), Internal Audit (VS-21), Legal (VS-100), channel owners, external auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Map the OT security program (W5417–W5434) to a recognized control framework — IEC 62443 (SL selection per zone) and NIST SP 800-82r3 — and identify gaps. | OT Security Lead | OT Security Lead | annual |
| 2 | Maintain evidence (asset inventory, segmentation, baselines, vuln mgmt, IR drills, access records) audit-ready in the in-house Audit & GRC platform (BUILD — fit-gap H11). | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | ongoing |
| 3 | Support internal audit's OT-cyber review (VS-21.3) and any BSP cyber-resilience / payment-infrastructure expectation (VS-80/VS-142). | OT Security Lead | Head of Internal Audit | per audit |
| 4 | Respond to B2B/customer (VS-107) and insurer (VS-26) due-diligence questionnaires on OT posture without over-disclosing sensitive architecture. | OT Security Lead / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | per request |
| 5 | Track gap remediation to the risk register (W5424) and report maturity to the board/audit committee (VS-36/VS-21). | OT Security Lead | CIO | annual |

### System Touchpoints
- the in-house Audit & GRC platform (BUILD — fit-gap H11; VS-22/VS-21 extended to OT); evidence repository; BSP cyber-resilience expectations (VS-80); board reporting (VS-36).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Evidence-scatter risk**: controls are operating but evidence is dispersed across IT/IFM/DC, failing an audit on paper; mitigated by platform consolidation and the W5417–W5434 evidence feeds.
- **Over-disclosure risk**: handing a customer/insurer the full OT architecture diagram creates its own risk; mitigated by Legal review and tiered/controlled disclosure.

### Automation Opportunity
- Control-to-evidence auto-mapping; platform evidence auto-ingest from W5417–W5434; questionnaire-response library.

### Controls
- CTL-151: OT statutory/framework compliance — IEC 62443 / NIST SP 800-82 mapping maintained continuously; CTL-802 (ensure controlled execution — OT Compliance, Third-Party Access & Cyber Resilience Analytics (PA-190.3).) — governance & evidence completeness; operational: annual compliance-mapping attestation.

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks/annual self-assessment; ~1–2 weeks per audit/due-diligence response.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-22 (compliance), VS-21.3 (audit), VS-100 (legal/disclosure), VS-80/VS-142 (BSP payment infra), VS-26 (insurer DD), VS-107 (B2B DD)

## W5436. OT Penetration Testing, Red-Team & Cyber-Physical Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual test cycle; major architecture change (W5418); new connected channel; post-incident (W5430) |
| **Frequency** | ~1–2 OT-focused tests/yr; periodic purple-team exercises (W5429) |
| **Volume** | 1 OT test program per cycle |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead |
| **Participants** | External pen-test firm, OT SOC (W5425/W5432), IFM (VS-138), HSE (VS-24), DC Ops (VS-04), Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scope the test with explicit safety constraints — non-prod OT range (W5429) for technical exploitation; production limited to non-intrusive validation, with HSE sign-off on anything touching a safety system. | OT Security Lead / HSE | OT Security Lead | per test |
| 2 | Execute (external firm): IT→OT pivot attempts, OT-IDS evasion, controller/default-credential checks, payment-zone (POS/SCO) testing, cyber-physical (does a compromise open a locked door or suppress an alarm). | External Pen-Tester | OT Security Lead | per test |
| 3 | Validate findings exploitability in BuildRight context and route to vuln mgmt (W5427); never auto-trust scanner output on OT. | OT Vulnerability Manager | OT Security Lead | per finding |
| 4 | Retest after remediation and close with verified evidence. | External Pen-Tester / OT Security | OT Security Lead | per cycle |
| 5 | Feed lessons into detection (W5432), architecture (W5418), and the risk register (W5424); brief leadership. | OT Security Lead | CIO | per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Non-prod OT range (W5429); OT-IDS/detection (W5425); vuln register (W5427); physical access control (VS-23/VS-138).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Test-induced-outage risk**: aggressive pen-testing crashes a fragile PLC or trips a safety system; mitigated by non-prod-first scoping and HSE sign-off on any production probe.
- **Scanner-false-confidence risk**: a vulnerability scanner green-lights a controller that is in fact exploitable via a vendor-specific vector; mitigated by expert manual validation and OT-aware testers.

### Automation Opportunity
- Safe/non-intrusive validation automation; retest-evidence capture; finding-to-control mapping.

### Controls
- CTL-802 (ensure controlled execution — OT Compliance, Third-Party Access & Cyber Resilience Analytics (PA-190.3).)
- operational: test governance with safety sign-off; remediation evidence retained; HSE sign-off on any production-touching test activity.

### Time Estimate
- ~4–8 weeks elapsed per test cycle (scope→execute→remediate→retest).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27.3 (IT pen-test), VS-21.3 (audit/assurance), VS-24 (HSE safety scoping), VS-23 (physical access/evasion), VS-138 (BMS/door controllers)

## W5437. OT-Aware Business Continuity, Recovery & Fail-Secure Operating Mode

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | BCP planning/testing (VS-26.1); OT incident (W5430); major dependency failure (cloud/vendor) |
| **Frequency** | BCP plan maintenance annual; test per W5429 cycle |
| **Volume** | 1 OT-aware BCP/recovery capability per site-class |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead |
| **Participants** | BCP/DR (VS-26), HSE (VS-24), Store/DC Ops (VS-07/VS-04), IFM (VS-138), channel owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define fail-secure operating modes per OT class: POS/SCO offline mode (continue selling, VS-08 NFR-013), BMS manual-override, fire-system fail-safe-to-alarm, SCADA local-control fallback, EV/locker offline grace. | OT Security Lead / HSE | OT Security Lead | per class |
| 2 | Quantify recovery objectives (RTO/RPO) per OT criticality tier — Safety-of-life near-zero RTO, POS/SCO hours, BMS hours-to-days. | OT Security Lead | BCP Lead | per tier |
| 3 | Maintain offline/cached capability: POS offline ≥8h (VS-08), BMS local override, comms/voice fallback for store/DC teams. | OT Security Engineer | BCP Lead | per site |
| 4 | Coordinate OT-cyber-driven closure/recovery with enterprise BCP (VS-26) and typhoon/disaster response (VS-69) so a cyber event during a typhoon is rehearsed. | OT Security Lead | COO | annual |
| 5 | Test recovery rebuild from known-good images (W5428) within the RTO/RPO targets; report gaps to the risk register (W5424). | OT Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | per test |

### System Touchpoints
- BCP/DR platform (VS-26); known-good image repository (W5428); POS offline capability (VS-08); BMS local override (VS-138); generator/fuel (VS-61/VS-07).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cloud-dependency risk**: a BMS/locker/EV/SCADA controller that bricks when its vendor cloud is unreachable takes the function down with the cloud; mitigated by documented offline/fail-secure modes and cloud-link risk assessment (W5421).
- **Recovery-exceeds-RTO risk**: rebuilding a mass-compromised POS/SCO or BMS estate takes longer than the business can absorb, closing stores for days; mitigated by image-based rebuild and the W5429 drill.

### Automation Opportunity
- Image-based rebuild automation; RTO/RPO compliance monitoring; fail-secure-mode self-test.

### Controls
- CTL-802 (ensure controlled execution — OT Compliance, Third-Party Access & Cyber Resilience Analytics (PA-190.3).)
- operational: BCP governance with recovery evidence retained; documented fail-secure mode per OT device class.

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks/annual plan refresh; ~1–2 weeks per recovery test cycle.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-26 (BCP/DR), VS-08 (POS offline), VS-69 (typhoon/disaster), VS-138 (BMS override), VS-61 (generator fuel), W5429 (drills)

## W5438. OT Cyber Awareness, Engineering-Workstation Hygiene & Insider-Threat Controls

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Onboarding; recurring awareness cycle; insider-threat signal (VS-125/VS-119) |
| **Frequency** | Annual training + targeted; continuous hygiene monitoring |
| **Volume** | ~300–500 OT engineering/maintenance users + vendor personnel (incl. contingent per VS-98) |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead |
| **Participants** | HR/L&D (VS-19.4), VS-167 vetting, IFM (VS-138), VS-98 contingent workforce, VS-125 fraud/insider |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Run OT-specific cyber awareness for engineering/maintenance staff and vendors — why a USB in a controller engineering workstation or a default password is catastrophic, not just an IT issue. | OT Security Analyst / L&D | OT Security Lead | annual + onboarding |
| 2 | Lock down OT engineering workstations — no general internet/email, host-based protection, removable-media control, and clean-before-connect for any field laptop. | OT Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | continuous |
| 3 | Apply insider-threat controls to OT-privileged users (detect mass config export, off-hours controller access, credential abuse) coordinated with VS-125/VS-119. | OT SOC Analyst | OT Security Lead | continuous |
| 4 | Ensure contingent/vendor personnel (VS-98/VS-167) get OT-aware vetting and access commensurate with the OT-criticality tier they touch. | OT Security Lead / VS-167 | OT Security Lead | per onboarding |
| 5 | Reinforce with documented sanctions/escalation paths (HR VS-84/VS-103, Legal VS-100) for OT security policy violations. | OT Security Lead | VP Legal & Compliance | per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Engineering-workstation hardening (host protection); removable-media control; insider-threat detection (VS-125); L&D platform (VS-19.4); vetting records (VS-167).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Engineering-workstation-bridge risk**: a maintenance laptop that moves between the corporate network and OT zones is the classic IT→OT bridge; mitigated by clean-before-connect and dedicated hardened workstations.
- **Trusted-insider risk**: an engineer or vendor with privileged OT access and intent can cause direct physical/financial harm (suppress alarms, divert payment, exfiltrate config); mitigated by insider-threat detection and least-privilege/JIT access (W5419/W5433).

### Automation Opportunity
- Just-in-time engineering-workstation provisioning; removable-media/auto-isolation; insider-threat detection rules.

### Controls
- CTL-802 (ensure controlled execution — OT Compliance, Third-Party Access & Cyber Resilience Analytics (PA-190.3).)
- operational: least privilege / workstation hygiene; awareness and insider-risk controls; no general internet/email on OT engineering workstations.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 hours/person annual training; workstation hardening continuous; insider-threat monitoring continuous.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-19.4 (L&D), VS-167 (vetting), VS-98 (contingent workforce), VS-125 (fraud/insider), VS-119 (ethics), VS-84/VS-103 (HR conduct)

## W5439. Connected-Channel (POS/SCO/EV/Locker/BMS) Cyber Coordination & Incident Liaison

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Channel-specific cyber event; recurring coordination; new channel onboarding (W5421) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly coordination forum; per incident |
| **Volume** | ~8–10 connected-device channels (POS/SCO/RF/locker/EV/BMS/SCADA/fire/access/weighbridge) |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead |
| **Participants** | VS-08/VS-149 (POS/SCO), VS-164 (lockers), VS-163 (EV), VS-138 (BMS), VS-108 (SCADA), VS-04 (WMS/RF), VS-23 (CCTV/EAS) channel owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Run a monthly OT-cyber coordination forum across every connected-device channel — share posture, incidents, advisories, and onboarding pipeline. | OT Security Lead | OT Security Lead | monthly |
| 2 | Maintain a per-channel cyber liaison (named owner in each channel VS) empowered to act on OT security direction during an incident (W5430). | OT Security Lead | CIO | per channel |
| 3 | Coordinate cross-channel incidents (e.g., a vendor common to BMS+SCADA+lockers breached simultaneously) with one incident commander and consistent comms. | OT IR Lead | OT Security Lead | per incident |
| 4 | Align channel-specific security config (POS PIN pad, SCO weight-security, locker access-auth, EV OCPP/OCPI, BMS BACnet) to the OT baseline (W5420). | OT Security Engineer | OT Security Lead | per channel |
| 5 | Feed channel cyber metrics into the posture dashboard (W5424) and channel governance (each channel VS). | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- OT posture dashboard (W5424); channel-specific controllers (VS-08/VS-149/VS-164/VS-163/VS-138/VS-108); IR case management (W5430).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Siloed-response risk**: a single incident spanning BMS + lockers + POS gets handled as three uncoordinated channel events, losing the common root cause; mitigated by the cross-channel forum and single-incident-commander model.
- **Channel-config-drift risk**: each channel VS hardens its own device differently from the OT baseline; mitigated by W5420 baseline alignment and the monthly forum.

### Automation Opportunity
- Cross-channel posture rollup; common-vendor/common-CVE correlation across channels.

### Controls
- CTL-802 (ensure controlled execution — OT Compliance, Third-Party Access & Cyber Resilience Analytics (PA-190.3).)
- operational: cross-channel segregation consistency; coordination governance; channel posture evidence.

### Time Estimate
- ~0.5 day/monthly forum; per-incident coordination ad-hoc.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-08/VS-149 (POS/SCO), VS-164 (lockers), VS-163 (EV), VS-138 (BMS), VS-108 (SCADA), VS-04 (WMS/RF), VS-23 (CCTV/EAS)

## W5440. OT Cybersecurity Performance, Maturity & Investment Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly/annual governance cycle; budget/investment (VS-135); board reporting (VS-36) |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly performance; annual maturity + investment |
| **Volume** | 1 OT performance/maturity/investment view |
| **Owner** | OT Security Lead |
| **Participants** | CIO, CFO, IT Security Manager/IT Security (VS-27.3), TBM/FinOps (VS-135), Internal Audit (VS-21), Insurance (VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Report OT performance KPIs: asset coverage %, segmentation hygiene, default-cred/baseline compliance, MTTC/MTTR for OT vulns, incident counts, drill/exercise currency. | OT Security Analyst | OT Security Lead | quarterly |
| 2 | Assess OT security maturity against a model (IEC 62443 SL / NIST 800-82 / CMMI-style) and track year-over-year progression. | OT Security Lead | CIO | annual |
| 3 | Quantify OT cyber risk in financial terms (loss exposure from a store-closing POS/BMS outage, a safety-system compromise) for VS-21.2 ERM and VS-26 insurance. | OT Security Lead / ERM | CFO | annual |
| 4 | Build the OT security investment case (tools, staffing, remediation, device replacement) into the technology-business-management plan (VS-135). | OT Security Lead / TBM | CIO/CFO | annual |
| 5 | Brief the board/audit committee (VS-36/VS-21) on OT cyber posture, material risks, and investment; align disclosure with VS-173 investor relations where material. | CIO / OT Security Lead | CEO | annual |

### System Touchpoints
- OT posture dashboard (W5424); Audit & GRC/ERM (VS-21.2); TBM/FinOps (VS-135); board reporting (VS-36); investor disclosure (VS-173).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Underinvestment risk**: OT security competes for budget against visible IT/digital initiatives and is chronically under-funded until an incident; mitigated by quantified financial-loss exposure and maturity tracking.
- **Metric-gaming risk**: KPIs (coverage %, MTTR) drift into optimism if measured leniently; mitigated by audit-validated evidence (VS-21.3) and honest baselines.

### Automation Opportunity
- KPI auto-aggregation from W5417–W5438; maturity-model self-scoring with evidence links; loss-exposure modeling.

### Controls
- CTL-802 (ensure controlled execution — OT Compliance, Third-Party Access & Cyber Resilience Analytics (PA-190.3).)
- operational: performance/investment governance on evidence-backed KPIs; OT cyber posture reported to the board/audit committee at least annually.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 week/quarterly performance; ~3–4 weeks/annual maturity + investment cycle.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21.2 (ERM), VS-21.3 (audit), VS-135 (TBM/FinOps), VS-26 (insurance), VS-36 (board), VS-173 (investor disclosure)
