## W5393. Receivables Financing Strategy, Instrument Selection & Working-Capital Plan

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Working-capital need; treasury/funding strategy review |
| **Frequency** | Annual strategy refresh |
| **Volume** | 1 strategy/yr |
| **Owner** | Treasury/Working-Capital Lead |
| **Participants** | CFO, Credit Manager (VS-68), Finance, Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quantify the trade-receivables balance and cash-conversion cycle from AR aging (VS-16/VS-68) and the funding gap. | Treasury Lead | CFO | 1 week |
| 2 | Evaluate instruments: recourse factoring, non-recourse factoring (credit-insured), invoice discounting (confidential), and securitization — on cost, risk transfer, and customer-impact. | Treasury Lead | CFO | 1 week |
| 3 | Select the target funding mix (e.g. factoring for high-risk segments, discounting for investment-grade accounts) and target advance rates. | Treasury Lead | CFO | 3 days |
| 4 | Build the business case: funding cost vs. DSO/cash-release benefit vs. risk transfer vs. relationship cost. | Finance Business Partner | CFO | 1 week |
| 5 | Approve at the treasury/credit committee and align to the group funding plan (VS-18) and board reporting (VS-36). | CFO | CEO | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/funding module (VS-18); AR aging (VS-16/VS-68); working-capital analytics (VS-105/VS-170).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Instrument-mismatch risk**: choosing recourse factoring when the goal was risk transfer leaves BuildRight holding the credit risk and paying factor fees; mitigated by clear objective-setting in the strategy.
- **Over-reliance risk**: depending solely on factoring for working capital concentrates funding risk on the factor; mitigated by a diversified funding mix (VS-18/VS-170).

### Automation Opportunity
- AR-aging and DSO analytics auto-fed into the funding-gap and instrument-evaluation model.

### Controls
- CTL-797: ensure controlled execution — Receivables Financing Strategy, Funder Relationships & Program Setup (PA-189.1).) — strategy/facility approval; CTL-21 (working-capital-data accuracy; CTL-35 (budget/funding-plan adherence).

### Time Estimate
- ~5 weeks elapsed per annual refresh; ~100–200 person-hours of named-owner effort within that window (step durations are elapsed windows, not contiguous effort).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury), VS-16/VS-68 (AR/credit), VS-105 (SCF), VS-170 (ABL), VS-36 (board)

## W5394. Factor/Funder Selection, Facility Negotiation & Master Factoring Agreement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved strategy (W5393); facility renewal |
| **Frequency** | Initial + periodic renewal (~1–3 yr terms) |
| **Volume** | ~1–3 factor/funder facilities |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | CFO, Legal (VS-100), Credit Manager, Factors/Banks |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | RFP/qualify factors/funders (banks, specialized AR financiers) on pricing, advance rate, recourse terms, and service. | Treasury Manager | CFO | 2 weeks |
| 2 | Negotiate facility terms: discount/fee rate, advance rate, recourse period, concentration limits, and termination. | Treasury Manager | CFO | 2 weeks |
| 3 | Legal review and execute the master factoring/discounting agreement (assignment, representations, covenants). | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | 2 weeks |
| 4 | Configure the facility in the treasury/factoring system with limits, rates, and recourse terms. | Treasury Manager | CFO | 1 week |
| 5 | Onboard to drawdown (W5401) and monitor covenant compliance (VS-18). | Treasury Manager | CFO | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/funding module (VS-18); factoring facility master; covenant compliance (VS-18 W319); contract management (VS-100).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Covenant-breach risk**: breaching a facility covenant (dilution ratio, concentration) triggers termination/recourse; mitigated by covenant monitoring and early corrective action.
- **Funder-concentration risk**: a single funder withdrawing leaves the program unfunded; mitigated by multiple funder relationships where scale allows.

### Automation Opportunity
- Covenant dashboard with early-warning on dilution/concentration ratios.

### Controls
- CTL-797: ensure controlled execution — Receivables Financing Strategy, Funder Relationships & Program Setup (PA-189.1).) — facility authorization; CTL-21 (covenant compliance; CTL-19 (treasury reconciliation).

### Time Estimate
- ~6–8 weeks elapsed per facility negotiation; ~1 day/month ongoing.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury/facility), VS-100 (legal/contracts), VS-170 (ABL alternatives)

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
- CTL-797 (ensure controlled execution — Receivables Financing Strategy, Funder Relationships & Program Setup (PA-189.1).)

### Time Estimate
- ~2 weeks elapsed setup; ~1 week per quarterly review.

### Cross-references

## W5396. Customer Notification Strategy (Notification vs. Silent/Confidential Factoring)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Facility setup (W5394); instrument selection (W5393) |
| **Frequency** | Setup; per newly enrolled customer segment |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 trade customers potentially affected |
| **Owner** | Treasury Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Account Manager (VS-107), Legal (VS-100), Credit Manager, Marketing/Comms (VS-14) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decide per segment: notification factoring (customer pays the factor) vs. silent/confidential discounting (customer pays BuildRight, which remits). | Treasury Manager | CFO | setup |
| 2 | For notification factoring: draft customer notification and remit-to change; legal review for assignment validity (W5400). | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | setup |
| 3 | Coordinate customer communication with Trade Account Management to manage relationship impact (VS-107/VS-14). | Trade Account Manager | Treasury Manager | per segment |
| 4 | Set up the remit-to instruction and lockbox (W5398) for notified customers. | Treasury Manager | Credit Manager | per segment |
| 5 | Monitor customer payment behavior post-notification (W5411). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Customer master remit-to (VS-29); factoring notification records; lockbox (W5398); AR (VS-16).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Relationship-damage risk**: notifying customers of factoring can signal financial distress and harm relationships; mitigated by silent/confidential discounting for sensitive segments and careful communication.
- **Invalid-assignment risk**: a notification that fails legal assignment requirements leaves the sale unenforceable; mitigated by legal review of notification/assignment language (W5400).

### Automation Opportunity
- Per-segment notification templates and remit-to auto-update.

### Controls
- CTL-27: customer data on notifications; CTL-797 (ensure controlled execution — Receivables Financing Strategy, Funder Relationships & Program Setup (PA-189.1).) — assignment legal sign-off; CTL-21 (remit-to accuracy).

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks elapsed setup; per-segment communication ongoing.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-107 (key accounts), VS-14 (comms), VS-100 (legal/assignment), VS-29 (master data)

## W5397. Receivables Sale Accounting Design, True-Sale/Off-Balance-Sheet Opinion & Derecognition (PFRS 9)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Facility setup (W5394); accounting policy/audit cycle |
| **Frequency** | Setup; annual audit confirmation |
| **Volume** | 1 accounting design/facility |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | External Auditor, Legal (VS-100), CFO, Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design the receivables-sale accounting: derecognition (PFRS 9) vs. secured borrowing, based on whether control/risks transferred. | Finance Controller | CFO | setup |
| 2 | Obtain a true-sale / off-balance-sheet legal opinion (recourse structure permitting). | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | setup |
| 3 | Configure GL/accounting for the sale, recourse reserve, and factor-fee/interest treatment (VS-17/VS-157). | Finance Controller | CFO | setup |
| 4 | Document the policy and obtain external-auditor agreement at the annual audit. | Finance Controller | CFO | annual |
| 5 | Monitor for changes in substance (recourse activity, W5407) that could re-characterize the sale (W5415). | Finance Controller | CFO | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- GL/revenue-recognition (VS-17/VS-157); factoring accounting module; auditor workpapers (VS-21).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Re-characterization risk**: a "sale" that is in substance a secured borrowing (excessive recourse, no true transfer) stays on-balance-sheet and distorts leverage/EBITDA and the audited PFRS financials; mitigated by true-sale opinion and substance monitoring.
- **Audit-qualification risk**: an unsupported derecognition policy risks audit qualification; mitigated by external-auditor agreement and documented policy.

### Automation Opportunity
- Recourse/substance analytics flag changes that could re-characterize the sale (W5415).

### Controls
- CTL-31: adequate provisions/recourse reserve; CTL-29 (consolidated reporting); CTL-21 (accounting accuracy).

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks elapsed setup; ~1 week per annual audit confirmation.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-157 (revenue/contract accounting), VS-17 (GL), VS-21 (audit), VS-100 (legal opinion)

### Steps

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set up the factoring/securitization platform: invoice submission, drawdown, reconciliation, and reporting modules. | IT/Treasury Systems Lead | Treasury Manager | 3–4 weeks |
| 2 | Establish the lockbox/bank-account infrastructure for customer remittance to the factor (notification) or BuildRight (silent). | Treasury Manager | Treasury Manager | 2 weeks |

| 4 | Configure remit-to instructions and customer notifications per W5396. | AR Lead | Treasury Manager | per customer |

### System Touchpoints
- Factoring platform; lockbox/bank (VS-18); AR integration (VS-16); customer master remit-to (VS-29); IT integration (VS-27).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Integration-failure risk**: a broken AR↔factoring↔lockbox link causes missed drawdowns and mis-applied cash; mitigated by end-to-end testing and reconciliation (W5404).
- **Mis-directed-payment risk**: a customer paying BuildRight instead of the factor (or vice-versa) under a notification setup; mitigated by clear remit-to instructions and lockbox controls.

### Automation Opportunity
- Full straight-through processing: eligible invoice → auto-submit → drawdown → lockbox → reconciliation.

### Controls
- CTL-19: bank/lockbox reconciliation; CTL-21 (integration accuracy); CTL-27 (customer remit data).

### Time Estimate
- ~8–10 weeks elapsed setup; ongoing maintenance.

### Cross-references

## W5399. Dilution, Reserve & Holdback Modeling, and Credit-Protection/Insurance Sourcing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Facility setup (W5394); non-recourse instrument selection (W5393) |
| **Frequency** | Setup; periodic review |
| **Volume** | 1 model/facility |
| **Owner** | Credit Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury, Trade Credit Insurer (VS-68), Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Model portfolio dilution (credits/disputes/short-pays as % of sales) to size the reserve/holdback. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | setup |
| 2 | Structure the factor reserve/holdback (a % of advance held back against dilution/default). | Treasury Manager | Credit Manager | setup |
| 3 | For non-recourse: source trade-credit insurance/credit-protection (VS-68) covering the factored receivables. | Credit Manager | CFO | setup |
| 4 | Configure the reserve/holdback and insurance into the facility and accounting (W5397). | Finance | Credit Manager | setup |
| 5 | Review dilution/loss experience vs. model and adjust reserve/insurance periodically (W5410). | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Credit-risk/dilution analytics (VS-68); trade-credit insurance (VS-68/VS-26); factoring reserve accounting (VS-17).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-reserved risk**: a reserve too small for actual dilution leaves BuildRight covering shortfalls from recourse/holdback; mitigated by dilution modeling and periodic review.
- **Uninsured non-recourse risk**: non-recourse factoring without credit insurance transfers risk back on funder default; mitigated by credit-protection sourcing.

### Automation Opportunity
- Dilution auto-computed from AR credits/disputes; reserve auto-sized.

### Controls
- CTL-797 (ensure controlled execution — Receivables Financing Strategy, Funder Relationships & Program Setup (PA-189.1).)
- operational: dilution accuracy; adequate reserve; coverage.

### Time Estimate
- ~3 weeks elapsed setup; ~1 week per quarterly review.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-68 (trade credit risk/insurance), VS-26 (insurance), VS-17 (GL)

### Steps

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm any regulatory registration/reporting for the receivables-financing activity (SEC/BSP where applicable). | Compliance Specialist | VP Legal & Compliance | setup |

### System Touchpoints
- Regulatory reporting; assignment/notification records (VS-88); AML (VS-86); compliance monitoring (VS-22).

### Automation Opportunity
- Assignment-notification templates and AML screening integrated into submission.

### Controls
- CTL-208: BSP/SEC & truth-in-lending statutory compliance (regulatory reporting and registration); CTL-797 (ensure controlled execution — Receivables Financing Strategy, Funder Relationships & Program Setup (PA-189.1).) — assignment legal sign-off; CTL-27 (customer data).
