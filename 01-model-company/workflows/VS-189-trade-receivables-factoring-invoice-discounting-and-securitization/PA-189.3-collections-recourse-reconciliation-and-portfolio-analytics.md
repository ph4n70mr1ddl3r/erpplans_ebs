## W5409. Factor/Funder Reconciliation, Fee/Interest Settlement & Monthly Account Statement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close; factor statement received |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~1 reconciliation/facility/month |
| **Owner** | Finance (Receivables Finance) |
| **Participants** | Treasury, Factor/Funder, Finance Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive the factor's monthly statement (advances, collections, fees, interest, reserve, recourse). | Finance | Finance Controller | per close |
| 2 | Reconcile the statement to BuildRight's records (submissions W5401, drawdowns W5402, collections W5404). | Finance | Finance Controller | per close |
| 3 | Settle fees/interest and reserve per the facility terms; post to GL (VS-17/VS-157). | Finance | Finance Controller | per close |
| 4 | Investigate and resolve discrepancies with the factor (timing, fees, reserve). | Treasury Manager | Finance Controller | per exception |
| 5 | Report reconciliation status and any open items to the treasury committee (W5416). | Finance | CFO | per close |

### System Touchpoints
- Factoring reconciliation module; GL (VS-17); treasury/funding (VS-18); factor statement ingestion.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unreconciled-difference risk**: unreconciled differences distort cash/fee/interest and the financials; mitigated by monthly reconciliation and discrepancy resolution.
- **Fee-leakage risk**: unchallenged incorrect factor fees/interest erode margin; mitigated by statement reconciliation and challenge of exceptions.

### Automation Opportunity
- Factor statement auto-ingested and reconciled to BuildRight's records.

### Controls
- CTL-19: funder-statement reconciliation; CTL-120 (fee/interest settlement accuracy); CTL-30 (close).

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 days elapsed per monthly close.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury/fees), VS-17 (GL), VS-157 (financing income/expense)

## W5410. Receivables Aging, Sub-Limits, Concentration & Pool Health Monitoring

|---|---|

| **Volume** | 1 portfolio review/month |
| **Owner** | Credit Manager |
| **Participants** | Credit Analyst, Treasury, Risk (VS-21/VS-68) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monitor factored-receivables aging and delinquency by customer/segment. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | monthly |
| 2 | Check concentration vs. sub-limits (W5395) and facility thresholds; flag breaches. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | monthly |
| 3 | Monitor pool health (collections rate, dilution, recourse rate) vs. facility covenants/triggers. | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | monthly |

### Controls

## W5412. Recourse Exposure, Bad-Debt Buyback Reserve & ECL Provisioning (PFRS 9)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close; recourse/buyback activity (W5407); credit deterioration |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | 1 provision/month |
| **Owner** | Finance (Receivables Finance) |
| **Participants** | Credit Manager, Finance Controller, External Auditor, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compute recourse exposure and expected credit loss (ECL) on recourse/bought-back receivables under PFRS 9. | Finance | Finance Controller | per close |
| 2 | Size the bad-debt/buyback reserve from recourse history and portfolio risk (W5399). | Credit Manager | CFO | per close |
| 3 | Post the ECL provision and reserve to GL (VS-17); document methodology. | Finance | Finance Controller | per close |
| 4 | Reconcile provisions to credit-risk and recourse activity (W5407). | Finance | Finance Controller | per close |
| 5 | Confirm methodology with the external auditor annually (VS-21). | Finance Controller | CFO | annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ECL/provisioning engine (PFRS 9 — VS-157/VS-17); credit-risk (VS-68); GL.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-provisioning risk**: an inadequate ECL/reserve on recourse exposure distorts the audited PFRS financials; mitigated by documented methodology and audit confirmation.
- **Recourse-loss risk**: recourse losses above reserve erode program economics; mitigated by reserve sizing and portfolio risk management (W5410).

### Automation Opportunity
- ECL auto-computed from recourse/aging/risk data with documented methodology.

### Controls
- CTL-31: adequate provisions; CTL-30 (close); CTL-21 (accuracy); CTL-29 (consolidated reporting).

### Time Estimate
- ~1 week per monthly close.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-157 (revenue/loss), VS-17 (GL), VS-68 (credit), VS-21 (audit)

## W5414. Receivables-Sale P&L, Cost-of-Funds vs. Discount Analytics & Funding-Mix Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly program-performance review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | 1 program dashboard/month |
| **Owner** | Finance Business Partner |
| **Participants** | CFO, Treasury, CEO, Treasury, Credit Manager, CFO, BI (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compute program P&L: financing income net of discount/fees, recourse losses, and operating cost. | Finance Business Partner | CFO | monthly |
| 2 | Analyze cost-of-funds vs. discount across instruments (factoring vs. discounting vs. ABL — VS-170). | Treasury | CFO | quarterly |
| 3 | Optimize the funding mix (shift to lower-cost instruments where risk permits). | Treasury | CFO | quarterly |
| 4 | Compare to the business case (W5393) and risk-appetite targets; flag erosion. | Finance Business Partner | CFO | monthly |
| 5 | Report program profitability to the treasury/finance committee and board (VS-36/VS-173). | CFO | CEO | quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/funding analytics (VS-18); profitability dashboard (VS-28/VS-17); board reporting (VS-36/VS-173).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-erosion risk**: rising recourse losses or fees compress program margin below target; mitigated by P&L monitoring and funding-mix optimization.
- **Sub-optimal-mix risk**: using high-cost factoring when lower-cost discounting/ABL would suffice; mitigated by cost-of-funds analytics.

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-refreshing program P&L and cost-of-funds dashboard.

### Controls
- CTL-799 (ensure controlled execution — Collections, Recourse Management, Reconciliation & Portfolio Analytics (PA-189.3).)
- operational: profitability-data accuracy; CTL-35 (vs. business case); CTL-19 (treasury reconciliation).

### Time Estimate
- ~1 week per monthly review.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-18 (treasury), VS-17 (P&L), VS-170 (ABL/mix), VS-105 (SCF), VS-28 (BI), VS-36/VS-173 (board)

## W5416. Portfolio Cash-Conversion-Cycle, DSO Impact & Treasury Integration Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly working-capital/treasury review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly (steps 1–2) + quarterly (steps 3–5) |
| **Volume** | 1 working-capital dashboard/month |
| **Owner** | Treasury/Working-Capital Lead |
| **Participants** | Treasury Lead, CFO, Finance Business Partner, CEO, Credit Manager, BI (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compute the program's cash-conversion-cycle and DSO impact (cash released vs. un-financed baseline). | Treasury Lead | CFO | monthly |
| 2 | Integrate the factoring program into the group cash-flow forecast and funding plan (VS-18). | Treasury Lead | CFO | monthly |
| 3 | Analyze the working-capital benefit vs. program cost (W5414) and risk transfer. | Finance Business Partner | CFO | quarterly |
| 4 | Identify opportunities to expand/contract the program based on cash need and cost (W5393). | Treasury Lead | CFO | quarterly |

- Treasury/cash-flow forecast (VS-18); working-capital analytics (VS-105/VS-170); BI (VS-28); board reporting (VS-36/VS-173).
