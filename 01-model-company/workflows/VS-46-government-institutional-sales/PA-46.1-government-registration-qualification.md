## W1950. PHILGEPS Registration & Renewal Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Initial registration; annual renewal cycle |
| **Frequency** | Initial: once per entity; Renewal: annual for 5 entities |
| **Volume** | 5 entity registrations maintained |
| **Owner** | Legal & Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Legal & Compliance Officer, CFO, VP Legal, Procurement Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal & Compliance Officer manages PHILGEPS registration for each entity: (a) BuildRight Depot, Inc. (primary — retail operations), (b) BuildRight Logistics, Inc. (if supplying logistics services to government), (c) register at philgeps.gov.ph with: SEC registration, mayor's permit, BIR registration, audited financial statements, PCAB license (if applicable for construction supply), (d) obtain "Red" or "Platinum" membership (Platinum required to download bidding documents and submit bids; web-verified — PhilGEPS tiers are Red/Platinum only) | Legal & Compliance Officer | VP Legal | 2–3 days/entity initial |
| 2 | Annual renewal: (a) update financial statements, (b) renew mayor's permit and business registration, (c) update contact information and authorized representatives, (d) verify PCAB license currency, (e) submit renewal 30 days before expiration | Legal & Compliance Officer | VP Legal | 1 day/entity/year |
| 3 | System integration: (a) PHILGEPS registration status tracked per entity in vendor master, (b) renewal calendar alerts 60 days before expiration, (c) registration certificate stored in document management per DOC-001 | System / Legal & Compliance Officer | CFO | Automated monitoring |

### System Touchpoints
- PHILGEPS registration tracking in compliance module (W1950.3)
- Document storage for registration certificates (W1950.3)
- Integration with W22.1 (regulatory permits) for consolidated compliance view

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Registration lapse**: Expired PHILGEPS registration disqualifies BuildRight from all government bidding; automated renewal alerts critical


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Legal & Compliance Officer manages PHILGEPS registration for" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "verify PCAB license currency" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document management per DOC-001" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-191 (ensure B2G procurement compliance & collection.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
- Initial: 2–3 days/entity; Renewal: 1 day/entity/year

## W1951. Government Entity Account Creation & Credit Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Government agency or LGU first purchase request |
| **Frequency** | ~20–30 new government accounts/year |
| **Volume** | ~150–200 active government/institutional accounts |
| **Owner** | Trade Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Account Manager, Credit Analyst, Sales Team, VP Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Account Manager creates government account: (a) entity type classification: national government agency, GOCC, LGU, state university, government hospital, public school, (b) collect: official designation, head of procuring entity (HOPE) name, procurement officer contact, billing address, TIN, (c) government accounts exempt from standard credit scoring — credit worthiness based on government entity status and approved budget | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | 1–2 days/account |
| 2 | Credit Analyst sets government account terms: (a) credit limit: based on entity's annual procurement budget (if disclosed) or historical purchase pattern, (b) payment terms: government accounts typically Net 30–60 per government procurement law, (c) mandatory: government purchase order or purchase request as billing basis, (d) BIR withholding tax handling: government entities withhold 1% EWT per revenue regulation | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | 1 day/account |
| 3 | System setup: (a) government account flag in customer master, (b) VAT treatment: government purchases are VAT-inclusive (BuildRight charges 12% VAT; government entity may be VAT-exempt per specific law — verify per entity), (c) EWT handling: system configured for 1% EWT on government sales, (d) payment terms and credit limit configured | System / Trade Account Manager | Credit Manager | 30 min/account |

### System Touchpoints
- Government customer master with entity classification (W1951.3)
- EWT configuration for government sales (W1951.3)
- Integration with W24 (credit application) for account setup process

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Payment delays**: Government entities may pay beyond terms due to bureaucratic processes; factor longer collection cycle into cash flow planning


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Account Manager creates government account" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-request of "request as billing basis" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-validate of "verify per entity" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-379 (ensure controlled execution — Government Registration & Qualification (PA-46.1).)
- operational: Trade Account Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 days per new government account

## W1953. Government Procurement Calendar Monitoring & Opportunity Identification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily PHILGEPS and government procurement portal monitoring |
| **Frequency** | Daily monitoring; weekly pipeline review |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 government procurement notices reviewed/week; ~5–10 opportunities pursued |
| **Owner** | Trade Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Account Manager, Category Manager, VP Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daily: Trade Account Manager monitors procurement opportunities: (a) PHILGEPS portal for new bidding opportunities in hardware/building materials categories, (b) LGU procurement portals for local government opportunities, (c) PhilGEPS shopping/small-value procurement modes (same portal as (a); 'G-EPS' is its legacy name), (d) DPWH, DepEd, DOH, DND procurement notices for infrastructure, school, hospital, and military construction projects | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | 30–60 min/day |
| 2 | Weekly: Trade Account Manager reviews opportunity pipeline: (a) filter by: (i) product relevance (does BuildRight carry the requested items?), (ii) geographic coverage (can BuildRight deliver to project sites?), (iii) value threshold (pursue opportunities > PHP 500K), (iv) competitiveness (does BuildRight have pricing advantage?), (b) categorize: hot (bidding deadline within 2 weeks), warm (bidding in 2–8 weeks), cold (advance notice only) | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | 1–2 hours/week |
| 3 | For hot opportunities: (a) notify Category Manager for pricing preparation, (b) coordinate with VP Store Operations for bid/no-bid decision, (c) begin bid preparation per W1959 | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | Per opportunity |

### System Touchpoints
- PHILGEPS integration for automated opportunity alerts (W1953.1)
- Government procurement pipeline tracking (W1953.2)
- Integration with W130 (competitive intelligence) for market pricing

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Volume of notices**: Thousands of procurement notices posted daily; automated filtering by category keywords and geography essential


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "monitors procurement opportunities" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews opportunity pipeline" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-notify of "notify Category Manager for pricing preparation" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-379 (ensure controlled execution — Government Registration & Qualification (PA-46.1).)
- operational: Trade Account Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Institutional/NGO purchase inquiry |
| **Frequency** | ~10–20 new institutional accounts/year |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 active institutional accounts (schools, hospitals, churches, NGOs) |
| **Owner** | Trade Account Manager |
| **Participants** | Trade Account Manager, Credit Analyst, Sales Associate |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Account Manager qualifies institutional account: (a) entity type: private school, private hospital, church/religious organization, NGO, foundation, cooperative, (b) collect: SEC/DSWD registration, TIN, authorized representative, billing address, (c) assess purchase potential: annual building material spend estimate | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | 1–2 days/account |
| 2 | Credit assessment for institutional accounts: (a) unlike government accounts, institutional accounts require standard credit scoring per W24, (b) credit check: financial statements, trade references, bank references, (c) credit limit and terms set per standard trade account policy: Net 30 for established institutions, COD for new/risky accounts | Credit Analyst | Credit Manager | 1–2 days/account |
| 3 | Onboarding: (a) create institutional account in customer master, (b) assign pricing tier: institutional pricing (typically 5–10% below SRP for bulk purchases), (c) provide account credentials for BuildRight institutional ordering (if available), (d) assign dedicated Sales Associate or Trade Account Manager | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | 1 day/account |

### System Touchpoints
- Institutional customer master with entity type classification (W1956.3)
- Integration with W24 (credit application) for credit assessment
- Integration with W460 (trade account onboarding) for onboarding process

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Credit risk**: Institutional accounts (especially NGOs and smaller schools) may have higher default risk than government accounts; stricter credit limits recommended; mitigated by CTL-379 (controlled execution — Government Registration & Qualification (PA-46.1))


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Account Manager qualifies institutional account" (replaces manual Step 1).

### Controls
- CTL-379 (ensure controlled execution — Government Registration & Qualification (PA-46.1).)

## W1957. Government Supplier Performance Scorecard & Annual Review

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual government sales review |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | Consolidated government sales performance |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, Trade Account Manager, CFO, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Account Manager compiles annual government sales scorecard: (a) total government revenue by entity type (national govt, LGU, GOCC, institutional), (b) number of bids submitted, won, and lost with win rate, (c) average deal size, (d) collection performance: DSO for government accounts vs. trade accounts, (e) top 10 government accounts by revenue, (f) government sales as % of total revenue (target: 3–5%) | Trade Account Manager | VP Store Operations | 2–3 days/year |
| 2 | VP Store Operations presents government sales review to executive team: (a) performance vs. prior year, (b) pipeline for next year (known upcoming government projects), (c) competitive landscape: which competitors are winning government bids?, (d) strategic recommendations: expand/contract government sales focus | VP Store Operations | COO | 1 day/year |
| 3 | CFO reviews government sales profitability: (a) gross margin on government sales vs. retail/trade, (b) collection cost: government accounts may require more collection effort, (c) working capital impact: longer payment terms tie up cash, (d) EWT recovery: ensure 1% EWT is properly credited against income tax | CFO | CEO | 1 day/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Government sales analytics dashboard (W1957.1)
- Integration with W35 (management reporting) for consolidated view

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin pressure**: Government competitive bidding drives prices down; ensure minimum margin thresholds are maintained in bid pricing


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Account Manager compiles annual government sales scorecard" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review to executive team" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-collect of "CFO reviews government sales profitability" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-379 (ensure controlled execution — Government Registration & Qualification (PA-46.1).)
- operational: VP Store Operations review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- 3–4 days/year total
