## Overview

Technology Business Management (TBM), IT Financial Management & Cloud FinOps workflows for
BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the enterprise discipline of **managing the financials of
technology** across a 5-entity, 200-store, PHP 62.3B-revenue retailer running a unified ERP
with ~10+ active integration touchpoints (POS, ecommerce, payments, banks, BIR eFPS, statutory,
delivery, loyalty, WMS, supplier portal) and an expanding digital perimeter (ecommerce,
marketplace, retail media, mobile app, BIR e-invoicing, AI/ML). Technology spend at this scale
typically runs 1–3% of revenue (≈ **PHP 0.6B–1.9B/yr**) spanning the cloud/SaaS estate,
software licenses, integration, telco/network across 205+ sites, and the IT org — yet without a
dedicated TBM/FinOps discipline, that spend is governed only by annual budget and reactive
vendor invoices, leaking value through under-used licenses, idle cloud resources, shadow IT, and
un-optimised commitments.

Today this discipline is **entirely unowned as a program**: VS-99 (IT Asset & Technology
Lifecycle) tracks hardware/software *inventory and disposal*; VS-113 (Enterprise Architecture)
designs the *application landscape*; VS-27 (IT Operations) runs *platforms*; VS-17.4 / VS-33 do
*corporate FP&A and budget*; VS-34 buys *transactionally* — but no value stream owns the
**technology-financial discipline**: the TBM cost taxonomy, zero-based IT budgeting, technology
investment portfolio & business-case/TCO modeling, demand management & intake prioritization,
showback/chargeback to business units, application run-cost & rationalization, **cloud FinOps**
(inform/optimize/operate — visibility, rightsizing, reservations/commitments, waste elimination,
anomaly detection), SaaS license utilization & renewal optimization, technology value realization,
unit-economics (cost-per-transaction, cost-per-store), and IT financial close/accruals. The
defining terms 'finops', 'cloud cost', 'technology business management', 'IT financial
management', and 'TBM' appeared in **zero** PA files as dedicated workflow headers; the discipline
is genuinely unowned.

This is distinct from **VS-99 (IT Asset & Technology Lifecycle)** which owns hardware/software
*discovery and asset lifecycle* (this value stream owns the *financial* view of that estate —
cost, value, optimization). It is distinct from **VS-113 (Enterprise Architecture)** which owns
the *design* of the application landscape (this value stream owns the *run-cost, rationalization
business case, and financial governance* of it). It is distinct from **VS-27 (IT Operations)**
which *operates* platforms (this value stream owns the *FinOps cost-optimization loop* over those
platforms). It is distinct from **VS-17.4 / VS-33 (FP&A)** which own *enterprise finance*
(this value stream owns the *technology-domain financial* discipline — TBM taxonomy, cloud unit
economics, showback/chargeback). Technology business management is the *financial-management-of-
technology* discipline — distinct from asset lifecycle, architecture, operations, and corporate
finance.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: a cloud-first, integration-heavy, rapidly digitizing retailer
with an expanding SaaS/perimeter estate has compounding, usage-based spend that, ungoverned, drifts
to 2–3% efficiency loss annually on a PHP-billion base — and every un-tracked technology dollar is
one not reinvested in the customer-facing differentiation the strategy depends on.
