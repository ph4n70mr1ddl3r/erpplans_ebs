## W4097. Technology Business Management (TBM) Framework & Cost Taxonomy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program stand-up; annual framework refresh |
| **Frequency** | Maintained + annual |
| **Volume** | Enterprise technology spend (≈ PHP 0.6B–1.9B/yr across 5 entities) |
| **Owner** | FinOps Lead / CIO Office |
| **Participants** | CFO (links to VS-17.4), CIO (VS-27), EA (VS-113), ITAM (VS-99), Finance (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FinOps Lead establish the TBM cost taxonomy (infrastructure, application, cloud/SaaS, telco/network, vendor services, people) mapped to business units, capabilities, and the application portfolio (VS-113) | FinOps Lead / EA / ITAM | CIO / CFO | Annual |
| 2 | FinOps Lead map all technology spend into the taxonomy (incl. interentity allocation links to VS-72), define the cost-to-serve metrics, and align to the GL/COA (links to VS-17/VS-29) | FinOps Lead / Finance | CIO | Annual |
| 3 | FinOps Lead publish the TBM framework, onboard new spend, and review annually with Finance and the executive team | FinOps Lead | CFO / CIO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- TBM platform/ITFM tool; GL/COA (VS-17/VS-29); application portfolio (VS-113); ITAM (VS-99); interentity (VS-72)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Invisible-spend risk**: Technology spend scattered, un-tracked; mitigated by single taxonomy and complete mapping
- **Taxonomy-drift risk**: Categories inconsistent over time; mitigated by governance and annual review

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated spend ingestion from vendors/bank/GL into the TBM taxonomy

### Controls
- CTL-635 (ensure controlled execution — Technology Investment Planning, Budgeting & TBM Framework (PA-135.1).)
- CTL-635: TBM taxonomy approved by CFO/CIO; reconciliation to GL monthly

### Time Estimate
- 2–3 weeks annual; maintained

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (finance), VS-113 (EA), VS-99 (ITAM), VS-72 (interentity)

## W4098. Annual IT Budget Planning, Zero-Based & Capacity-Based Budgeting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual budget cycle; reforecast |
| **Frequency** | Annual + quarterly reforecast |
| **Volume** | Enterprise technology budget (run vs grow vs transform) |
| **Owner** | FinOps Lead / CIO |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17.4), CIO office, function sponsors, EA (VS-113), OpEx (VS-133) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FinOps Lead/CIO build the annual IT budget (run-the-business, grow, transform), with zero-based review of discretionary lines and capacity-based modeling for usage-driven cloud/SaaS/telco | FinOps Lead / CIO Office / EA | CIO / CFO | Annual |
| 2 | FinOps Lead align the budget to the investment portfolio (W4099) and corporate FP&A (VS-17.4/VS-33), and reforecast quarterly against actuals (W4117) | FinOps Lead / Finance | CIO | Quarterly |
| 3 | FinOps Lead present the budget to the executive/Board (VS-36), manage trade-offs, and cascade accountability to budget owners | FinOps Lead / CIO | CFO / Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- TBM (W4097); FP&A (VS-17.4); corporate performance (VS-33); cloud/SaaS billing (W4106)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-provision risk**: Usage-driven spend under-budgeted; mitigated by capacity modeling and FinOps (W4106)
- **Run-grows-out-of-control risk**: Run-cost crowds out grow/transform; mitigated by zero-based review and rationalization (W4103)

### Automation Opportunity
- Capacity-based budget modeling from usage trends; forecast-vs-actual automation

### Controls
- CTL-635 (ensure controlled execution — Technology Investment Planning, Budgeting & TBM Framework (PA-135.1).)
- CTL-635: budget approved by CFO/Board; variance reviewed quarterly

### Time Estimate
- 3–4 weeks annual + quarterly reforecast

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-33 (performance), VS-36 (governance)

## W4100. Business Case, TCO & ROI Modeling for Technology Initiatives

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Investment proposal; major initiative |
| **Frequency** | Per initiative |
| **Volume** | Per technology initiative |
| **Owner** | FinOps Lead / Sponsor |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17.4), sponsor, EA (VS-113), ITAM (VS-99), OpEx benefit (VS-133 W4065) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FinOps Lead build the TCO (license/subscription, cloud, integration, implementation, support, telco, end-of-life) and the benefit case (revenue, cost, risk, productivity) with Finance sign-off | FinOps Lead / Finance | Sponsor / CFO | Per initiative |
| 2 | FinOps Lead model ROI/NPV/payback, test sensitivity, and document assumptions/risks; align benefit tracking to VS-133 W4065 | FinOps Lead / OpEx | Sponsor | Per initiative |
| 3 | FinOps Lead track realized TCO/ROI against the case post-implementation (links to W4113), and feed lessons into future cases | FinOps Lead / Finance | CFO | Post-implementation |

### System Touchpoints
- TBM (W4097); benefit tracking (VS-133 W4065); capex (VS-40); ITAM lifecycle (VS-99)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **TCO-blind risk**: Upfront cost only, ignoring run/hidden costs; mitigated by full TCO discipline
- **Overstate-benefit risk**: Rosy benefit case; mitigated by Finance sign-off and realization tracking

### Automation Opportunity
- TCO templates with industry benchmarks; sensitivity modeling

### Controls
- CTL-635 (ensure controlled execution — Technology Investment Planning, Budgeting & TBM Framework (PA-135.1).)
- CTL-635: business case approved by Finance; benefit reconciled post-implementation

### Time Estimate
- Per initiative; 1–2 weeks

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.4 (finance), VS-133 W4065 (benefit), VS-40 (capex), VS-99 (ITAM)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | TBM framework; monthly close; allocation review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + periodic review |
| **Volume** | Enterprise technology cost allocation (5 entities, business units, capabilities) |
| **Owner** | FinOps Lead |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17), business unit leaders, intercompany (VS-72) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FinOps Lead define allocation rules (headcount, revenue, usage, transaction volume) per cost pool, aligned to TBM (W4097) and interentity rules (VS-72) | FinOps Lead / Finance | CIO / CFO | Periodic |
| 2 | FinOps Lead run showback/chargeback monthly (showback by default; chargeback where agreed), publish unit-cost metrics to business units, and reconcile to GL (VS-17/W4117) | FinOps Lead / Finance | FinOps Lead | Monthly |
| 3 | FinOps Lead review allocation fairness with business units, refine drivers, and use insights to drive demand/cost behavior | FinOps Lead | CIO / CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- TBM (W4097); GL/intercompany (VS-17/VS-72); usage telemetry (W4106); FP&A (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Allocation-war risk**: Business units dispute allocations; mitigated by transparent drivers and showback-first
- **Gaming risk**: Allocation drivers distort behavior; mitigated by balanced, reviewed drivers

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated allocation engine; unit-cost dashboards for business units

### Controls
- CTL-635 (ensure controlled execution — Technology Investment Planning, Budgeting & TBM Framework (PA-135.1).)
- CTL-635: allocation rules approved by CFO; reconciled to GL

### Time Estimate
- Monthly close + quarterly review

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (finance), VS-72 (interentity), VS-17.4 (FP&A)

## W4103. Application Portfolio Rationalization & Run-Cost Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual portfolio review; redundancy/duplication; high run-cost |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on trigger |
| **Volume** | Enterprise application portfolio (ERP core + ~10+ integrations + perimeter apps) |
| **Owner** | FinOps Lead / Enterprise Architect (VS-113) |
| **Participants** | EA (VS-113), IT (VS-27), ITAM (VS-99), business owners, OpEx (VS-133) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EA/IT Finance assess the application portfolio (business value, technical fit, run-cost, usage, redundancy, risk) using TIME (Tolerate/Invest/Migrate/Eliminate) framing | EA / FinOps Lead / ITAM | CIO | Annual |
| 2 | EA/IT Finance identify rationalization actions (consolidate, retire, replace, renegotiate), build the business case (W4100), and sequence with OCM (VS-134) | EA / FinOps Lead / OCM | CIO | Annual |
| 3 | EA/IT Finance execute retire/consolidate, capture run-cost savings (W4113), and update the architecture (VS-113) and ITAM (VS-99) | EA / IT / ITAM | CIO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Application portfolio (VS-113); ITAM (VS-99); TBM run-cost (W4097); benefit (W4113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Sentimental-app risk**: Low-value apps kept alive; mitigated by objective TIME scoring
- **Retire risk**: Retiring an app with hidden dependencies; mitigated by dependency analysis (VS-113)

### Automation Opportunity
- Usage/value auto-scoring from telemetry; dependency mapping

### Controls
- CTL-635 (ensure controlled execution — Technology Investment Planning, Budgeting & TBM Framework (PA-135.1).)
- CTL-635: retire decisions via architecture governance; data-retention before decommission (VS-88/VS-91)

### Time Estimate
- Annual + ongoing execution

### Cross-references
- links to VS-113 (EA), VS-99 (ITAM), VS-88/VS-91 (records/privacy), VS-134 (OCM)

## W4104. Software License & Subscription Entitlement Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Renewal cycle; new project; usage review |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + renewal-driven |
| **Volume** | Enterprise software/SaaS estate (~6,918 potential users + devices) |
| **Owner** | FinOps Lead / ITAM (VS-99) |
| **Participants** | ITAM (VS-99), IT (VS-27), Procurement (VS-34), business owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ITAM/IT Finance maintain the entitlement inventory (licenses, subscriptions, seats, true-ups, co-terms), map to actual usage (W4110), and forecast demand | ITAM / FinOps Lead | CIO | Continuous |
| 2 | ITAM/Procurement optimize entitlement (right-size seats, tier optimization, co-term/renewal consolidation, negotiate) before renewal, with procurement controls (VS-34) | ITAM / Procurement / FinOps Lead | CIO | Per renewal |
| 3 | ITAM/IT Finance manage compliance (avoid under-licensing/audit exposure links to VS-99/VS-21), capture savings (W4113), and update ITAM | ITAM / Audit | CIO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- ITAM/SAM tool (VS-99); usage telemetry (W4110); procurement (VS-34); TBM (W4097)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-buy risk**: Paying for unused licenses; mitigated by usage-based right-sizing (W4110)
- **Audit-exposure risk**: Under-licensing triggers vendor audit/penalty; mitigated by entitlement governance and true-ups

### Automation Opportunity
- Usage-to-entitlement auto-reconciliation; renewal-optimization recommendations

### Controls
- CTL-148 (ensure technology spend & license optimization.)
- CTL-635: entitlement inventory maintained; compliance verified before audit

### Time Estimate
- Continuous + per renewal

### Cross-references
- links to VS-99 (ITAM/SAM), VS-34 (procurement), VS-21 (audit), W4110 (SaaS utilization)
