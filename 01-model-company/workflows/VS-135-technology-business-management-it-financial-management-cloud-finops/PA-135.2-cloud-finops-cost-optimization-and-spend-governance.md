## W4105. Cloud FinOps Operating Model & FinOps Culture (Inform / Optimize / Operate)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program stand-up; cloud/SaaS maturity |
| **Frequency** | Maintained + maturity evolution |
| **Volume** | Enterprise FinOps function (ERP core, SaaS, integration, perimeter) |
| **Owner** | FinOps Lead |
| **Participants** | CIO (VS-27), Engineering (VS-27/VS-113), Finance (VS-17.4), business owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FinOps Lead establish the FinOps operating model per the FinOps Framework phases: Inform (visibility/allocations W4106), Optimize (right-sizing W4107, waste W4108), Operate (anomaly W4109, governance W4112, culture) | FinOps Lead / FinOps Lead | CIO / CFO | Maintained |
| 2 | FinOps Lead build a cross-functional FinOps team (finance + engineering + business), define unit economics (cost-per-transaction/store/order), and embed cost-awareness into engineering (links to VS-113) | FinOps Lead / Engineering | CIO | Maintained |
| 3 | FinOps Lead mature the practice (crawl→walk→run), benchmark, and report culture/maturity to the executive team | FinOps Lead | CIO / CFO | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Cloud cost management platform; TBM (W4097); EA/engineering (VS-113); finance (VS-17.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Engineering-alienation risk**: FinOps seen as a finance tax on engineering; mitigated by shared unit-economics and enablement
- **Tool-heavy risk**: Too many tools, too little outcome; mitigated by outcome-focused maturing

### Automation Opportunity
- FinOps platform consolidation; unit-economics auto-computation

### Controls
- CTL-636 (ensure controlled execution — Cloud FinOps, Cost Optimization & Spend Governance (PA-135.2).)
- CTL-636: FinOps accountability shared (engineering + finance); unit-economics targets

### Time Estimate
- Maintained; maturing over 12–24 months

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27 (IT ops), VS-113 (EA), VS-17.4 (finance)

## W4107. Cloud Resource Rightsizing, Reservation & Commitment Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Usage data; reservation/commitment cycle |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + commitment cycle |
| **Volume** | Enterprise cloud resource estate |
| **Owner** | FinOps Lead / Cloud Engineering |
| **Participants** | Engineering (VS-27/VS-113), Finance (VS-17.4), business owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FinOps/engineering analyze usage vs provisioned capacity, identify rightsizing opportunities (over-provisioned compute/storage/services), and resize | FinOps Lead / Engineering | FinOps Lead | Continuous |

### Automation Opportunity
- Rightsizing recommendations; commitment-portfolio optimization modeling

### Controls
- CTL-636 (ensure controlled execution — Cloud FinOps, Cost Optimization & Spend Governance (PA-135.2).)
- CTL-636: commitment decisions with risk/Finance review; performance guardrails on rightsizing

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; commitment cycles

## W4109. Cloud Cost Anomaly Detection & Budget Alerting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Spend spike; budget threshold |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (real-time) |
| **Volume** | Enterprise cloud/SaaS spend |
| **Owner** | FinOps Lead |
| **Participants** | Engineering (VS-27), Finance (VS-17.4), budget owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FinOps configure anomaly detection (unexpected spikes, trend breaks, new services) and budget alerts (threshold/forecast) per service/business unit, mapped to TBM (W4097) | FinOps Lead / BI | FinOps Lead | Continuous |
| 2 | FinOps/engineering triage alerts (legitimate growth vs leak/waste/attack), act (contain, rightsize, investigate security links to VS-27.3), and document | FinOps Lead / Engineering / Security | FinOps Lead | Per alert |
| 3 | FinOps tune thresholds (reduce noise), report anomaly metrics, and feed findings to optimization (W4107/W4108) and forecasting (W4106) | FinOps Lead | CIO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Cloud cost platform; security/SIEM (VS-27.3); BI (VS-28); TBM (W4097)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Spike-blind risk**: Cost spike unnoticed until month-end; mitigated by real-time anomaly detection
- **Alert-fatigue risk**: Too many false alerts; mitigated by threshold tuning and baselining

### Automation Opportunity
- - System auto-flag of "auto-flags line items exceeding 80% of monthly allocation"
- System auto-route of anomalies to the owning cost-center lead for escalation

### Controls
- CTL-636 (ensure controlled execution — Cloud FinOps, Cost Optimization & Spend Governance (PA-135.2).)
- CTL-636: budget alerts with owner accountability; security-investigation linkage for suspicious spikes

### Time Estimate
- Continuous

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27.3 (security), VS-28 (BI), W4106 (visibility)
