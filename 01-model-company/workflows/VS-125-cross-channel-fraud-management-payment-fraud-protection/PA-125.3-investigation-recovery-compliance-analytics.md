## W3873. Fraud Investigation, Evidence & Case Adjudication

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Confirmed-fraud case (links to W3860); escalated alert |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~3,000–8,000 confirmed cases/yr |
| **Owner** | Fraud Management |
| **Participants** | Fraud Investigators, Legal (links to VS-100), LP (links to VS-23), Channels (links to VS-07/VS-10/VS-80/VS-13), Privacy (links to VS-91), HR for employee cases (links to VS-19) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Investigators conduct structured investigation per typology (links to W3858): transaction reconstruction, CCTV/POS-journal correlation (links to VS-08/VS-23), digital forensics for ATO/ecommerce (links to VS-10/VS-27.3), interview of staff/customers, and structured evidence pack with chain-of-custody | Fraud Investigators / LP / IT Security | Fraud Management Lead | Per case |
| 2 | Investigators adjudicate against the evidence standard (confirmed / probable / dismissed), determine loss quantum and recovery potential (links to W3874), and decide referral path (recovery, law-enforcement links to W3877, employment action links to W3876, insurance links to VS-26) | Fraud Management / Legal | Fraud Management Lead | Per case |
| 3 | Fraud Management close cases with root-cause, control-gap identification, and learning fed back to rules/models/policy (links to W3862) and to the typology catalog (links to W3858) | Fraud Management | Fraud Management Lead | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Case management; POS journal (links to VS-08); CCTV (links to VS-23); digital forensics (links to VS-27.3); ecommerce (links to VS-10); legal matters (links to VS-100); HR case (links to VS-19); insurance (links to VS-26)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Evidence-admissibility risk**: Evidence not preserved to a legal/hireable standard; mitigated by chain-of-custody discipline and Legal review
- **Learning-loss risk**: Case outcomes not fed back to detection; mitigated by closed-loop feedback to W3862


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Investigators conduct structured investigation per typology" (replaces manual Step 1).

### Controls
- CTL-606 (ensure controlled execution — Investigation, Recovery, Compliance & Analytics (PA-125.3).)
- operational: chain-of-custody discipline and Legal review; operational: closed-loop feedback to W3862

### Time Estimate
- ~1–8 hrs/case; ~3,000–8,000 cases/yr

## W3874. Fraud Recovery, Restitution & Asset Recovery

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Confirmed loss case (links to W3873) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | Recovery target ~20–50% of confirmed fraud loss |
| **Owner** | Fraud Management / Finance / Legal |
| **Participants** | Fraud Management, Finance/AR (links to VS-16/VS-17), Legal (links to VS-100), Collections (links to VS-16), LP (links to VS-23), Treasury (links to VS-18), Insurance (links to VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fraud Management/Finance quantify recoverable loss (principal + cost), identify recovery sources (customer account balance/points/gift balance offset links to VS-13/VS-54, restitution agreement, civil/criminal recovery links to VS-100, insurance claim links to VS-26, vendor/3P recovery), and prioritize by quantum | Fraud Management / Finance / Legal | Fraud Management Lead | Per case |
| 2 | Fraud Management/Legal/Collections pursue recovery: demand letters, restitution agreements, account-offset, small-claims/civil action where material, and coordinate with law-enforcement asset recovery (links to W3877) | Fraud Management / Legal / Collections | Fraud Management Lead | Per case |
| 3 | Fraud Management/Finance/Treasury record recoveries against the original loss (links to VS-17 GL), report net loss-rate (links to W3880), and recover via insurance where applicable (links to VS-26/W26.3) | Fraud Management / Finance / Treasury | Fraud Management Lead | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- AR/collections (links to VS-16); GL (links to VS-17); loyalty/gift balance offset (links to VS-13/VS-54); legal matters (links to VS-100); insurance claims (links to VS-26); treasury (links to VS-18)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low-recovery risk**: Customer-side fraud losses hard to recover at PH scale; mitigated by account-offset and prioritized civil action
- **Double-recovery risk**: Same loss recovered from insurance and customer; mitigated by subrogation coordination (links to VS-26)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Fraud Management/Finance quantify recoverable loss" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System workflow orchestration of "coordinate with law-enforcement asset recovery" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record recoveries against the original loss" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-606 (ensure controlled execution — Investigation, Recovery, Compliance & Analytics (PA-125.3).)
- operational: account-offset and prioritized civil action; operational: subrogation coordination (links to VS-26)

### Time Estimate
- ~1–6 hrs/case recovery effort; recovery cycle 30–180 days

| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of chargebacks/yr; representment win-rate target ≥65% |
| **Owner** | Fraud Management / Payments |
| **Participants** | Fraud Management, Payments (links to VS-80), Ecommerce (links to VS-10), Logistics (links to VS-06/VS-56), Legal (links to VS-100), Finance (links to VS-17), CX (links to VS-13), Insights (links to VS-28.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Fraud Management/Logistics assemble and submit representment within scheme SLA (Visa/Mastercard/QRPh): order details, delivery POD/photo/signature (links to VS-06.3), IP/device match, auth/AVS results, customer comms, prior history | Fraud Management / Logistics / Payments | Fraud Management Lead | Per chargeback |
| 3 | Fraud Management/Insights track representment outcomes (win-rate, pre-arb, fee leakage links to VS-118), identify serial chargeback customers (links to VS-126), and feed learnings to ecommerce scoring (links to W3866) and acquirer relationships (links to VS-80) | Fraud Management / Insights / Payments | Fraud Management Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Chargeback/acquirer platform (links to VS-80); ecommerce (links to VS-10); delivery POD (links to VS-06.3/VS-56); CX/quality (links to VS-13/VS-31); identity (links to VS-126); revenue assurance (links to VS-118); analytics (links to VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **SLA-miss risk**: Late representment auto-loses the dispute; mitigated by SLA tracker and pre-assembled evidence templates
- **Fee-leakage risk**: Chargeback fees and pre-arb costs erode recovery; mitigated by analytics (links to VS-118)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-route of "route service-disputes to CX/quality" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "assemble and submit representment within scheme SLA" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track representment outcomes" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-606 (ensure controlled execution — Investigation, Recovery, Compliance & Analytics (PA-125.3).)
- operational: SLA tracker and pre-assembled evidence templates; operational: analytics (links to VS-118)

### Time Estimate
- ~20–60 min/representment; ~thousands/yr
