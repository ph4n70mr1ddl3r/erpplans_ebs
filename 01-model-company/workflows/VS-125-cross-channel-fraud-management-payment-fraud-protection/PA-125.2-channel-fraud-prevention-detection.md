## W3865. POS / Store Transaction Fraud Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | POS transaction stream (links to VS-08); exception alert |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (~2.8M POS txns/month across 600 terminals) |
| **Volume** | ~33.6M POS txns/yr; ~0.05–0.2% flagged for review |
| **Owner** | Fraud Management / Store Ops |
| **Participants** | Fraud Management, Store Ops (links to VS-07), Cashiers (links to VS-08), Loss Prevention (links to VS-23), POS/IT (links to VS-08/VS-27), Payments (links to VS-80), HR (links to VS-19) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fraud Management/Store-Ops monitor POS for in-store fraud patterns: cashier sweethearting/price-override, no-sale/cash-drawer anomalies, manual-price and markdown abuse (links to VS-01.2/W36), barcode-switch, false refunds, fictitious voids, loyalty-point farming at POS, and EAS-bypass on high-value items (links to VS-23) — using exception reports and scoring (links to W3859) | Fraud Management / LP / Store Ops | Fraud Management Lead | Daily |
| 2 | Fraud Management/LP investigate flagged cashiers/transactions (till audit, CCTV sync links to VS-23, journal review links to VS-08), confirm or dismiss, and route employee fraud to HR/Internal (links to W3876) | Fraud Management / LP / Store Ops | Fraud Management Lead | Per alert |
| 3 | Fraud Management/Payments handle card-present fraud (skimming/clone, stolen-card use), coordinate with acquirer (links to VS-80) and law enforcement for organized rings (links to W3877), and tighten store controls (manager override thresholds links to VS-08) | Fraud Management / Payments / LP | Fraud Management Lead | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- POS journal/exception reports (links to VS-08); CCTV/EAS (links to VS-23); loyalty at POS (links to VS-13); payments/acquirer (links to VS-80); manager override (links to VS-08); HR case (links to VS-19/VS-119)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Insider-collusion risk**: Cashier-customer collusion at POS hard to detect from journals alone; mitigated by CCTV sync and exception analytics
- **Override-abuse risk**: Manager price-override abused for discounts to associates; mitigated by override thresholds and audit (links to VS-08/W1475)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitor POS for in-store fraud patterns" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "Fraud Management/LP investigate flagged cashiers/transactions" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System workflow orchestration of "coordinate with acquirer" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-605 (ensure controlled execution — Channel-Specific Fraud Prevention & Detection (PA-125.2).)
- operational: CCTV sync and exception analytics; operational: override thresholds and audit (links to VS-08/W1475)

### Time Estimate
- ~10–30 min/alert; ~7–28 alerts/store/month (per the 0.05–0.2% flag rate) → ~85–340 alerts/yr/store

## W3866. Ecommerce & Digital Channel Fraud Prevention

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Ecommerce checkout (links to VS-10); order placement |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (~42,900 ecommerce orders/month) |
| **Volume** | ~515K ecommerce orders/yr; ~2–5% held for review; COD high-risk subset |
| **Owner** | Fraud Management / Ecommerce |
| **Participants** | Fraud Management, Ecommerce Ops (links to VS-10), Payments (links to VS-80), Logistics (links to VS-06/VS-56), Loyalty (links to VS-13), IT (links to VS-27/VS-113), Privacy (links to VS-91), Marketplace (links to VS-95) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fraud Management/Ecommerce score every card-not-present order at checkout (links to VS-10) using device intelligence, 3-D Secure/Strong-Customer-Auth (BSP/QRPh), velocity, address/courier risk, and behavioral signals (links to W3859/W3861), with synchronous approve/step-up/decline within checkout flow | Fraud Management / Ecommerce / Payments | Fraud Management Lead | Real-time |
| 2 | Fraud Management manage COD fraud (the dominant PH ecommerce risk): non-receipt claims, fake-address/undeliverable, courier collusion, return-fraud on delivery; pre-screen COD orders (links to VS-06.3/VS-56) and apply holds/deposits for high-risk customers (links to VS-126 identity) | Fraud Management / Ecommerce / Logistics | Fraud Management Lead | Daily |
| 3 | Fraud Management handle order holds, manual review queue, customer communication (links to VS-13 CX), and false-positive recovery (approve good orders) with feedback to model tuning (links to W3862) | Fraud Management / Ecommerce | Fraud Management Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce checkout/3-DS (links to VS-10); payments gateway (links to VS-80); device/identity (links to VS-126/W3861); logistics/COD (links to VS-06.3/VS-56); loyalty (links to VS-13); CX (links to VS-13); marketplace (links to VS-95)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **COD-leakage risk**: COD non-receipt claims and courier collusion are the largest PH ecommerce loss vector; mitigated by COD scoring and delivery-confirmation controls
- **Conversion risk**: Over-tight fraud blocks hurt ecommerce conversion (~3% revenue channel); mitigated by tuned models and step-up auth


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "Fraud Management/Ecommerce score every card-not-present order at checkout" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review queue, customer communication" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-605 (ensure controlled execution — Channel-Specific Fraud Prevention & Detection (PA-125.2).)
- operational: COD scoring and delivery-confirmation controls; operational: tuned models and step-up auth

### Time Estimate
- ~5–15 min/review; ~10,000–25,000 reviews/yr

## W3867. Returns & Refund Abuse Prevention

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Return/refund event (links to VS-32.1); refund request |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (~0.04% return rate on 2.8M monthly POS txns — the ~40/day AR-credit-memo returns canon — plus ecommerce returns) |
| **Volume** | ~14,500 returns/yr (the AR-credit-memo canon); ~1–3% flagged as abuse |
| **Owner** | Fraud Management / Returns |
| **Participants** | Fraud Management, Returns/Customer Service (links to VS-32.1/VS-09), Store Ops (links to VS-07), Ecommerce (links to VS-10), Payments (links to VS-80), Loyalty (links to VS-13), LP (links to VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fraud Management/Returns score returns for abuse patterns: return-without-receipt, wardrobbing/return-after-use, receipt-reuse/duplicate-receipt, refund-to-different-tender, empty-box/switch, stolen-then-returned, receipt-fraud via fake invoices, and returns to abuse promo/loyalty (links to VS-32.1) | Fraud Management / Returns / LP | Fraud Management Lead | Per return |
| 2 | Fraud Management apply controls: refund authorization thresholds, store credit vs cash for flagged returns, blacklist/return-ban for serial abusers (links to VS-126), and restock-damage recovery (links to VS-90 damage claims) | Fraud Management / Returns / Store Ops | Fraud Management Lead | Per case |
| 3 | Fraud Management/Payments reconcile refund-to-card and original-tender matching (links to VS-80), detect refund-fraud rings across stores, and feed cases (links to W3860) | Fraud Management / Payments | Fraud Management Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Returns processing (links to VS-32.1); POS refund (links to VS-08); ecommerce returns (links to VS-10); payments refund (links to VS-80); loyalty (links to VS-13); damage claims (links to VS-90); identity/blacklist (links to VS-126)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Refund-leakage risk**: Cash refunds for stolen/wardrobed goods; mitigated by tender-match and store-credit routing
- **CX risk**: Tight return controls anger good customers (Consumer Act links to VS-22); mitigated by targeted scoring and policy transparency


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-flag of "flagged returns, blacklist/return-ban for serial abusers" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile refund-to-card and original-tender matching" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-605 (ensure controlled execution — Channel-Specific Fraud Prevention & Detection (PA-125.2).)
- operational: tender-match and store-credit routing; operational: targeted scoring and policy transparency

### Time Estimate
- ~5–20 min/flagged return; ~7,000–20,000 flagged returns/yr
