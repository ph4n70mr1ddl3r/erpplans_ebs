## W3657. Bond Release, Retrieval & Closeout

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contract completion/milestone; warranty expiry; bond expiry (links to W3654) |
| **Frequency** | Per bond closeout |
| **Volume** | Released bonds across the portfolio |
| **Owner** | Surety Program Manager / Finance |
| **Participants** | Surety Program Manager, Sales (links to VS-46/VS-11), Legal (links to VS-100.3), PMO (links to VS-112), Surety/Bank, Finance (links to VS-17), Warranty Mgmt (links to VS-53) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surety Program Manager/PMO identify release-eligible bonds: contract/substantial-completion milestone, customer acceptance, warranty expiry, or bond expiry; verify the release conditions in the contract/bond and obtain the beneficiary release/clearance | Surety Program Manager / PMO / Sales | CFO | Per bond |
| 2 | Surety Program Manager/Treasury execute the release: request the surety/bank to release/cancel the bond and return the collateral/cash margin (W3644/W3653), release the credit-line encumbrance (links to VS-18.2), and confirm the closure in writing; manage beneficiary non-response/obstruction | Surety Program Manager / Treasury / Surety/Bank | CFO | Per bond |
| 3 | Surety Program Manager/Finance update the encumbrance register (W3654) and records (W3648), release the restricted cash/collateral in accounting (links to VS-17), and report release cycle-time and trapped-bond risk to governance (W3661); drive release discipline to minimize trapped capital | Surety Program Manager / Finance | CFO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract/PMO (links to VS-46/VS-11/VS-112); warranty (links to VS-53); treasury/banking (links to VS-18.2); finance/accounting (links to VS-17); encumbrance register (links to W3654); records (links to W3648)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Trapped-capital risk**: Bonds not released after completion trap collateral/cash/credit; mitigated by release tracking and active closeout
- **Beneficiary-obstruction risk**: Beneficiary won't release without dispute; mitigated by acceptance evidence and legal (W3660)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "verify the release conditions in the contract/bond and obtain the beneficiary…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-request of "request the surety/bank to release/cancel the bond and return the…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "Surety Program Manager/Finance update the encumbrance register" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-579 (ensure controlled execution — Bond Release, Claims, Recovery & Surety Analytics (PA-116.3).)
- operational: release tracking and active closeout; operational: acceptance evidence and legal (W3660)

### Time Estimate
- Per bond + continuous

## W3658. Bond Claim, Demand & Default Response Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Bond call/demand by beneficiary; alleged default; performance dispute |
| **Frequency** | Rare but high-impact |
| **Volume** | Bond claims/demands |
| **Owner** | Surety Program Manager / Legal / CFO |
| **Participants** | Surety Program Manager, Legal (links to VS-100), CFO/CEO, Treasury (links to VS-18), PMO (links to VS-112), B2G/B2B Sales (links to VS-46/VS-11), Surety/Bank, Risk (links to VS-21.2), Insurance (links to VS-26), Communications (links to VS-14.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surety Program Manager/Legal receive and triage the bond call/demand: the beneficiary's demand, the alleged default/breach, the bond/contract basis, the demand validity (compliance with bond terms), and the amount; immediate notification to CFO/CEO and the surety/bank; assess exposure | Surety Program Manager / Legal / CFO | CEO | Immediate |
| 2 | Legal/Surety Program Manager respond to the demand: where the demand is wrongful/invalid, contest it (injunction, legal notice); where valid, manage the resolution (cure the default, negotiate settlement, or accept the bond payment); engage the surety/bank on their obligations and coordinate the counter-indemnity (W3659) | Legal / Surety Program Manager / Surety/Bank | CEO / Board | Claim life |
| 3 | Surety Program Manager/Legal manage the aftermath: bond payment and recovery/subrogation (W3659), dispute/litigation (W3660), insurance/recovery (links to VS-26), regulatory notification (government-beneficiary cases links to VS-22), communications (links to VS-14.3), and capture the claim record/lessons (W3648/W3661); report to the board (links to VS-36) | Surety Program Manager / Legal / CFO | CEO | Post-claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal (links to VS-100); treasury/banking (links to VS-18); surety/bank; risk (links to VS-21.2); insurance (links to VS-26); governance (links to VS-36); records (links to W3648/W3661)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Wrongful-call risk**: Beneficiary calls the bond unfairly; mitigated by contest/legal response
- **Default-exposure risk**: Valid default exposes BuildRight to payment and reputational damage; mitigated by delivery and early dispute resolution


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Legal/Surety Program Manager respond to the demand" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "Surety Program Manager/Legal manage the aftermath" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-579 (ensure controlled execution — Bond Release, Claims, Recovery & Surety Analytics (PA-116.3).)
- operational: contest/legal response; operational: delivery and early dispute resolution

### Time Estimate
- Per claim

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surety Program Manager/Treasury/Legal manage the counter-indemnity call: the surety/bank's demand for indemnity after paying the bond, the indemnity amount (bond paid + surety costs/fees), and BuildRight's obligation under the GAI (W3644); assess the validity and amount | Surety Program Manager / Legal / Treasury | CFO / CEO | Per call |
| 2 | Treasury/Finance settle the indemnity obligation (cash/collateral call W3644, credit draw), account for the loss/recoverable (links to VS-17), and pursue recovery/subrogation: recover from the responsible party (subcontractor/defaulting JV partner), insurer (professional indemnity/contractor insurance links to VS-26), or via contract remedies | Treasury / Finance / Legal | CFO | Per call |

### Pain Points / Risks

### Controls
- CTL-579 (ensure controlled execution — Bond Release, Claims, Recovery & Surety Analytics (PA-116.3).)
