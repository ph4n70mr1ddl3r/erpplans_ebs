## W3649. Bid Bond Application & Issuance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | B2G bid/tender decision (links to VS-46/W1759/W1899); B2B tender requiring bid security |
| **Frequency** | Per bid (~5–10 concurrent bids) |
| **Volume** | Bid bonds per tender cycle |
| **Owner** | Surety Program Manager / B2G Sales |
| **Participants** | Surety Program Manager, B2G/B2B Sales (links to VS-46/VS-11), Treasury (links to VS-18), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Surety/Bank, Finance (links to VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | B2G/B2B Sales/Surety Program Manager initiate the bid-bond request upon Go/No-Go decision (links to VS-46): tender/bid value, required bid security % (typically 1–2% per RA 9184, or per tender), acceptable instrument (surety bond/bank guarantee/cash), beneficiary (government agency/customer), validity period, and the bid deadline; verify authority (W3647) and capacity (W3646) | B2G/B2B Sales / Surety Program Manager | CFO | Per bid |
| 2 | Surety Program Manager/Treasury arrange the bid bond: apply to the surety company or bank (W3642/W3643), provide the bid/tender detail and corporate authorization, pay the premium/fee (W3645), secure the counter-indemnity/collateral (W3644), and obtain the issued instrument in the required format/beneficiary/validity | Surety Program Manager / Treasury / Surety/Bank | CFO | Per bid (days) |
| 3 | Surety Program Manager record the bid bond in the encumbrance register (W3654), deliver to Sales for bid submission, manage the bid outcome (release W3657 if not won, convert to performance bond W3650 if won), and capture the record (W3648); report bid-bond activity to governance (W3661) | Surety Program Manager / Sales | CFO | Per bid |

### System Touchpoints
- B2G/B2B bid (links to VS-46/VS-11); treasury/surety/bank (links to VS-18/W3642); authority (links to W3647); encumbrance register (links to W3654); records (links to W3648)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Bid-deadline-miss risk**: Bond not issued by tender deadline causes bid rejection; mitigated by lead-time and pre-arranged facility
- **Wrong-format risk**: Bond not in the beneficiary's required format is rejected; mitigated by format review


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "B2G/B2B Sales/Surety Program Manager initiate the bid-bond request upon" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Surety Program Manager/Treasury arrange the bid bond" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record the bid bond in the encumbrance register" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-578 (ensure controlled execution — Bond/Guarantee Application, Issuance, Tracking & Encumbrance Management (PA-116.2).)
- operational: lead-time and pre-arranged facility; operational: format review

### Time Estimate
- Per bid (days)

## W3650. Performance & Payment Bond Application & Issuance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contract award (links to VS-46/VS-11); performance-security requirement |
| **Frequency** | Per awarded contract |
| **Volume** | Performance/payment bonds for awarded B2G/B2B contracts |
| **Owner** | Surety Program Manager / Finance |
| **Participants** | Surety Program Manager, B2G/B2B Sales (links to VS-46/VS-11), Treasury (links to VS-18), Legal (links to VS-100.3), PMO/Project Delivery (links to VS-112), Surety/Bank, Finance (links to VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surety Program Manager/Sales initiate the performance/payment bond on contract award: contract value, required performance security % (typically 5% goods / 10% infrastructure in cash or bank guarantee per the RA 9184 IRR (higher percentages apply to surety-bond forms)), the contract term and milestones, beneficiary, validity (often 10% beyond contract completion), and the contract-risk assessment (links to VS-21.2); verify authority (W3647) and capacity (W3646) | Surety Program Manager / Sales / Legal | CFO | Per award |
| 2 | Surety Program Manager/Treasury/Legal arrange the performance and payment bonds: apply to surety/bank, provide the contract detail and corporate authorization, complete the contract-risk underwriting with the surety, pay premium/fee (W3645), secure counter-indemnity/collateral (W3644), and obtain the issued instruments; link to the contract/PO | Surety Program Manager / Treasury / Legal / Surety/Bank | CFO | Per award (days–weeks) |
| 3 | Surety Program Manager record the bonds in the encumbrance register (W3654), link to the contract and PMO delivery (links to VS-112), manage the bond through delivery (amendments W3656, milestone-linked reductions), and plan the release at completion (W3657); report to governance (W3661) | Surety Program Manager / PMO | CFO | Per contract |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract/award (links to VS-46/VS-11); PMO delivery (links to VS-112); treasury/surety/bank (links to VS-18/W3642); authority (links to W3647); encumbrance register (links to W3654); records (links to W3648); risk (links to VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-bonding risk**: Bond below contract requirement breaches the contract; mitigated by requirement verification
- **Over-bonding risk**: Bond above requirement wastes premium/encumbrance; mitigated by accurate requirement


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "Surety Program Manager/Sales initiate the performance/payment bond on contract" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Surety Program Manager/Treasury/Legal arrange the performance and payment bonds" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record the bonds in the encumbrance register" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-578 (ensure controlled execution — Bond/Guarantee Application, Issuance, Tracking & Encumbrance Management (PA-116.2).)
- operational: requirement verification; operational: accurate requirement

### Time Estimate
- Per award (days–weeks)

| **Volume** | Warranty/retention bonds for completed contracts |
| **Owner** | Surety Program Manager / Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Surety Program Manager/Treasury arrange the warranty/retention bond: apply to surety/bank, provide the completion/warranty detail, pay premium/fee (W3645), secure counter-indemnity (W3644), and obtain the instrument; substitute cash retention with bond where economic (W3653/W3645) | Surety Program Manager / Treasury / Surety/Bank | CFO | Per completion |
| 3 | Surety Program Manager manage the warranty bond through the warranty period (link to VS-53 warranty), reduce/release on warranty milestones (W3657), manage warranty claims (links to VS-53/W3658), and capture records (W3648); report to governance (W3661) | Surety Program Manager / Warranty Mgmt | CFO | Warranty period |

### System Touchpoints

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cash-trapped risk**: Cash retention held when a bond would free the cash; mitigated by bond-substitution analysis
- **Warranty-claim-exposure risk**: Warranty bond called for a defect claim; mitigated by warranty delivery (links to VS-53)

- System auto-count of "Surety Program Manager/Treasury arrange the warranty/retention bond" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "Surety Program Manager manage the warranty bond through the" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-578 (ensure controlled execution — Bond/Guarantee Application, Issuance, Tracking & Encumbrance Management (PA-116.2).)
- operational: bond-substitution analysis; operational: warranty delivery (links to VS-53)

### Time Estimate
- Per completion + warranty period

## W3652. Bank Guarantee Issuance & LC-for-Guarantee Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Bank-guarantee or LC requirement (customer/regulatory); instrument-mix decision (W3641) |
| **Frequency** | Per instrument |
| **Volume** | Bank guarantees and standby LCs |
| **Owner** | Treasury / Surety Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Treasury (links to VS-18), Surety Program Manager, Finance (links to VS-17), Legal (links to VS-100.3), Bank, B2G/B2B Sales (links to VS-46/VS-11) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury/Surety Program Manager arrange the bank guarantee or standby LC: confirm the instrument-mix decision (bank guarantee where the beneficiary requires it or surety is unavailable), the facility/line encumbrance (W3642/W3646), the margin/collateral (W3644), and the beneficiary/validity/terms | Treasury / Surety Program Manager | CFO | Per instrument |
| 2 | Treasury/Bank issue the bank guarantee / standby LC: application, the facility utilization (encumbering the credit line links to VS-18.2), fee/margin (W3645), the SWIFT/instrument issuance, and the beneficiary delivery; manage the BSP/banking compliance (links to VS-80) | Treasury / Bank | CFO | Per instrument (days) |
| 3 | Treasury/Surety Program Manager record the bank guarantee in the encumbrance register (W3654) with the credit-line encumbrance, manage through the contract term (amendments W3656, release W3657), and report credit-line encumbrance to treasury/cash planning (links to VS-18.1) and governance (W3661) | Treasury / Surety Program Manager | CFO | Per instrument + ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/banking (links to VS-18.2); credit facility (links to VS-18); finance (links to VS-17); legal (links to VS-100.3); encumbrance register (links to W3654); analytics (links to W3661)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Credit-encumbrance risk**: Bank guarantee encumbers credit line that supports operations; mitigated by instrument-mix (surety bond frees credit) and capacity planning (W3646)
- **Margin-tie-up risk**: Cash margin/collateral ties up liquidity; mitigated by collateral optimization (W3645)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-issue of "issue the bank guarantee / standby LC" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record the bank guarantee in the encumbrance register" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-578 (ensure controlled execution — Bond/Guarantee Application, Issuance, Tracking & Encumbrance Management (PA-116.2).)
- operational: instrument-mix (surety bond frees credit) and capacity planning (W3646); operational: collateral optimization (W3645)

### Time Estimate
- Per instrument + ongoing

| 2 | Finance/Surety Program Manager evaluate and execute cash-retention substitution: replace cash retention with a retention bond (W3651) where economic (frees cash, costs premium W3645), per contract terms and customer acceptance; manage the substitution accounting | Finance / Surety Program Manager / Sales | CFO | Per contract |
| 3 | Finance manage retention/margin release on contract milestone/completion/warranty expiry (links to W3657), reconcile and recover the cash, and report cash-retention exposure and release cycle-time to governance (W3661) and treasury/cash (links to VS-18.1) | Finance / Surety Program Manager | CFO | Continuous |

## W3654. Bond/Guarantee Tracking, Encumbrance & Register Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Bond issuance (W3649–W3652); monthly cycle; reporting |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + monthly |
| **Volume** | Outstanding bond/guarantee portfolio |
| **Owner** | Surety Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Surety Program Manager, Treasury (links to VS-18), Finance (links to VS-17), Legal (links to VS-100.3), PMO (links to VS-112), BI (links to VS-28.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surety Program Manager maintain the bond/guarantee encumbrance register: each instrument's type, beneficiary, contract, value, term, validity/expiry, provider (surety/bank), counter-indemnity/collateral, credit-line encumbrance (links to VS-18.2), status (active/released/claimed), and the linked contract/project | Surety Program Manager | CFO | Continuous |
| 2 | Surety Program Manager/Treasury track facility and credit-line utilization against limits (W3642/W3646), aggregate the contingent-liability exposure, and reconcile to the accounting records (restricted cash, contingent liability disclosure links to VS-17) and provider records | Surety Program Manager / Treasury / Finance | CFO | Monthly |
| 3 | Surety Program Manager/BI produce the encumbrance dashboards: outstanding exposure by type/entity/provider/contract, facility utilization, expiring-soon bonds (links to W3656), and release-eligible bonds (links to W3657); report to governance (W3661) and treasury/risk (links to VS-18/VS-21.2) | Surety Program Manager / BI | CFO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Treasury/banking (links to VS-18.2); finance/accounting (links to VS-17); encumbrance register; BI (links to VS-28.1); analytics (links to W3661); risk (links to VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Register-inaccuracy risk**: Encumbrance not tracked causes capacity mis-planning and unreleased bonds; mitigated by disciplined register and reconciliation

- System auto-record of "Surety Program Manager/Treasury track facility and credit-line utilization against" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "report to governance" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-107 (ensure bond encumbrance & renewal control.)
- operational: disciplined register and reconciliation; operational: expiry tracking (W3656)

| **Frequency** | Per multi-entity engagement |
| **Volume** | Multi-entity bonds (5 entities) |
| **Owner** | Surety Program Manager / Cross-Entity Shared Services |
| **Participants** | Surety Program Manager, Cross-Entity Shared Services (links to VS-72), Treasury (links to VS-18), Legal/Corporate Secretary (links to VS-100.3/VS-36), Entity Finance, Sales (links to VS-46/VS-11) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surety Program Manager/Legal determine the bonding entity for each engagement: which BuildRight entity is the contracting party (Depot Inc. retail, Logistics Inc. distribution, Digital Commerce Inc., or a JV), the corporate authorization for that entity, and any joint-and-several liability across entities | Surety Program Manager / Legal / Corporate Secretary | CFO / CEO | Per engagement |
| 2 | Surety Program Manager/Treasury arrange the bond in the correct entity name with the entity-level facility (W3642) and counter-indemnity (W3644), manage JV-partner bonding (where BuildRight's share requires a proportional bond), and coordinate intercompany allocation of the premium/cost (links to VS-72) | Surety Program Manager / Treasury / Cross-Entity | CFO | Per engagement |
| 3 | Surety Program Manager/Cross-Entity track the bond by entity in the encumbrance register (W3654), allocate premium/collateral/encumbrance to the correct entity for accounting (links to VS-17/VS-72), and report multi-entity surety exposure to governance (W3661) | Surety Program Manager / Cross-Entity / Finance | CFO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Cross-entity/intercompany (links to VS-72); treasury/banking (links to VS-18); legal/corporate secretary (links to VS-100.3/VS-36); finance (links to VS-17); encumbrance register (links to W3654)

- **Wrong-entity risk**: Bond in the wrong entity name may be rejected or create intercompany/contingent-liability mis-mapping; mitigated by entity determination discipline
- **JV-liability risk**: Joint-and-several liability exposes BuildRight to the JV partner's share; mitigated by JV agreement and proportional bonding

- System auto-count of "Surety Program Manager/Treasury arrange the bond in the correct" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track the bond by entity in the encumbrance register" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-578 (ensure controlled execution — Bond/Guarantee Application, Issuance, Tracking & Encumbrance Management (PA-116.2).)
- operational: entity determination discipline; operational: JV agreement and proportional bonding

### Time Estimate
- Per engagement
