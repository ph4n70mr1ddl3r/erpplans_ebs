## W2574. Shared Services Cost Pool Identification & Classification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual classification cycle or new shared service establishment |
| **Frequency** | Annual; event-driven for new services |
| **Volume** | 5 legal entities; ~15 shared service categories |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, Entity CFOs, COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Controller identifies shared service cost pools: IT infrastructure and applications, HR services (payroll, recruitment), finance shared services (AP, AR, GL), procurement operations, real estate and facilities management, logistics planning; classifies each as direct-chargeable or allocated | Finance Controller | CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | For each cost pool: defines allocation methodology (headcount for HR, transaction volume for AP/AR, revenue for IT, square footage for facilities, FTE for procurement); documents methodology in transfer pricing policy; obtains CFO and external advisor sign-off for arm's-length compliance | Finance Controller / Transfer Pricing Advisor | CFO | 1 week |
| 3 | System configures cost pool allocation rules: sets allocation keys per entity, establishes monthly run schedule, defines GL posting rules per entity; Finance Controller validates with test run; documents for audit trail | Finance Controller / IT | CFO | 3–5 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Cost-center / allocation engine (links to VS-29 / W288 financial master data, VS-17 / W14 intercompany) — the allocation-rule configuration and the monthly run
- Transfer-pricing documentation (links to VS-72 / W2594 TP documentation & BIR compliance) — the methodology record for the BIR TP file
- GL (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close) — the per-entity posting destination

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Arm's-length-challenge risk**: the allocation methodology (e.g., headcount for HR) doesn't reflect actual benefit received, exposing BuildRight to a BIR transfer-pricing adjustment; mitigated by the external-TP-advisor sign-off and the benchmarking study in W2594
- **Allocation-key-drift risk**: an allocation key set when Depot Inc. had 90% of headcount becomes unfair as Digital Commerce grows, but is never refreshed; mitigated by the annual classification review trigger

### Automation Opportunity
- Allocation rules configurable per cost pool with versioned methodology; test-run auto-compared to prior-period for reasonableness before go-live

### Controls
- CTL-213 (ensure shared-services chargeback integrity.)
- CTL-445 (transfer-pricing arm's-length — links to VS-17 / W235 transfer-pricing tax compliance); operational: CFO + external-advisor sign-off on the methodology

### Time Estimate
~2.5 weeks Finance Controller + ~1 week methodology + ~4 days config × annual cycle = **~180–220 hours/year** Finance Controller + Transfer Pricing Advisor.

## W2575. Monthly Shared Services Cost Pool Compilation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~15 cost pools across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Analyst, Cost Center Managers, Finance Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Analyst compiles monthly shared services costs: extracts actual costs from each shared service cost center (IT: PHP ~5M/month, HR: PHP ~2M/month, Finance: PHP ~3M/month, Procurement: PHP ~1.5M/month, Facilities: PHP ~8M/month); validates against budget and prior month | Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 2–3 days |
| 2 | Analyst processes cost adjustments: accrues for unbilled services, corrects misposted costs, allocates shared costs that are directly chargeable (e.g., IT project specific to one entity); prepares allocation summary for controller review | Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1 day |
| 3 | Finance Controller reviews and approves compiled cost pool; signs off on allocation run; system posts allocation entries to each entity's GL; generates allocation report for intercompany reconciliation | Finance Controller | CFO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Cost-center compilation (links to VS-17 / W9A month-end close, W14 intercompany) — the monthly cost-pool extract and allocation run
- Accruals (links to VS-17 / W9A) — the unbilled-service accrual
- IC reconciliation (links to VS-72 / W2583 monthly IC invoice) — the allocation report reconciling to the IC billing

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Misposted-cost risk**: a cost lands in the shared pool that should be entity-direct (an entity-specific IT project), inflating the pool and mis-allocating; mitigated by the direct-chargeable separation in step 2 and the cost-center-manager review
- **Timing-mismatch risk**: the allocation run posts before all month-end costs are final, requiring reversal/re-post; mitigated by the allocation run sequenced after the W9A close cutoff

### Automation Opportunity
- Cost-pool extract auto-generated from the GL; allocation run auto-posted on Controller sign-off

### Controls
- CTL-213: duplicate-allocation guard & chargeback cost-accuracy — links to VS-17; operational: Finance Controller sign-off on the allocation run

### Time Estimate
~3 days Analyst compilation + ~1 day adjustments + ~1 day Controller review = **~5 days/month ≈ ~600 hours/year** Finance Analyst + Controller.

## W2576. IT Shared Services Chargeback Calculation & Allocation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly IT cost allocation cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | IT budget ~PHP 60M/year across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | IT Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | IT Finance Analyst, CIO, Entity CFOs |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Finance Analyst compiles IT shared costs: infrastructure (servers, network, cloud), applications (ERP licenses, POS system), helpdesk (staff costs), security (cybersecurity tools), project costs (new implementations); separates direct-chargeable projects from shared pool | IT Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 2 days |
| 2 | Analyst calculates entity allocation: infrastructure by user count (Depot Inc. ~5,000 users = 75%, Logistics ~600 = 9%, Digital ~50 = 1%, Property ~100 = 1.5%, Holdings ~315 = 5%), applications by transaction volume, helpdesk by ticket count, security equally; validates allocation reasonableness vs. entity revenue contribution | IT Finance Analyst | CIO / Finance Controller | 1 day |
| 3 | System generates IT chargeback entries per entity; posts to IC AP/AR accounts; produces entity-level IT cost report for management review; CIO reviews entity IT spending vs. budget in monthly IT governance meeting | System / IT Finance Analyst | CIO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- IT cost-center / chargeback (links to VS-27 / W373 IT FinOps & cloud cost management, VS-135 TBM) — the IT cost compilation and allocation
- IC AP/AR (links to VS-17 / W14, VS-72 / W2583) — the chargeback posting
- User/ticket master (links to VS-27 / W371 mobile device, W48 helpdesk) — the allocation-key data (user count, ticket count)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Shadow-IT risk**: an entity incurs IT cost outside the shared pool (a SaaS subscription bought directly), understating its IT chargeback; mitigated by the VS-99 software-asset-management control surfacing unmanaged subscriptions
- **User-count-staleness risk**: the allocation uses a stale user count (post-reorg headcount change), mis-allocating; mitigated by the user-count refreshed monthly from the HRIS (W292 employee master)

### Automation Opportunity
- User/ticket counts auto-pulled from the IT systems; chargeback auto-calculated and posted on CIO sign-off

### Controls
- CTL-213 (ensure shared-services chargeback integrity.)
- CTL-445 (IT-asset/license accuracy — links to VS-99); operational: CIO monthly IT-governance sign-off

### Time Estimate
~4 days/month IT Finance Analyst + CIO review absorbed = **~380–450 hours/year**.

## W2577. HR Shared Services Cost Allocation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly HR cost allocation cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | HR budget ~PHP 24M/year shared services |
| **Owner** | HR Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | HR Finance Analyst, CHRO, Entity CFOs |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR Finance Analyst compiles shared HR costs: payroll processing (semi-monthly runs for 6,925 employees across 5 entities), recruitment shared services, training and development, benefits administration, HRIS system costs; separates entity-specific costs (entity-specific hiring, entity-specific training) | HR Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Allocates shared HR costs: payroll processing by headcount per entity, recruitment by number of hires per entity, training by training hours consumed per entity, benefits administration by enrollment count per entity, HRIS by headcount; Depot Inc. bears ~86% (store + HQ headcount) | HR Finance Analyst | CHRO / Finance Controller | 1 day |
| 3 | System posts HR chargeback entries; generates entity HR cost reports; CHRO reviews HR cost per employee by entity in quarterly HR metrics review | System / HR Finance Analyst | CHRO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- HR cost-center / chargeback (links to VS-19 / W10 payroll, VS-17 / W14 intercompany) — the HR cost compilation and allocation
- HRIS (links to VS-19 / W292 employee master) — the headcount/hires/training-hours allocation keys
- IC AP/AR (links to VS-72 / W2583) — the chargeback posting

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Entity-specific-vs-shared misclassification risk**: an entity-specific training program is booked to the shared pool, spreading its cost to entities that didn't benefit; mitigated by the entity-specific separation in step 1
- **Cost-per-employee-distortion risk**: a startup entity (Digital Commerce, ~50 staff) shows a high cost-per-employee because fixed HRIS costs spread over a small base; mitigated by the CHRO review contextualizing the metric and the startup-phase allocation adjustment

### Automation Opportunity
- Headcount/hires/training-hours auto-pulled from HRIS; chargeback auto-posted on CHRO sign-off

### Controls
- CTL-445 (ensure controlled execution — Shared Services Cost Pool Management (PA-72.1).)
- CTL-445 (payroll/HR-cost accuracy — links to VS-19 / W10, W337 payroll audit); operational: CHRO quarterly HR-metrics sign-off

### Time Estimate
~3 days/month HR Finance Analyst + CHRO review absorbed = **~290–340 hours/year**.

## W2578. New Shared Service Establishment & Cost Pool Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New shared service identified or entity structure change |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; 2–3 per year |
| **Volume** | 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, VP of affected function, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Controller evaluates new shared service request: cost pool definition, affected entities, allocation methodology proposal, transfer pricing implications; prepares business case with 3-year cost projection | Finance Controller | CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | CFO and affected entity heads review proposal; negotiate allocation methodology; agree on initial budget and annual review mechanism; external advisor confirms arm's-length compliance | CFO / Entity CFOs / Transfer Pricing Advisor | CEO | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Finance Controller configures new cost pool in ERP: creates shared service cost center, defines allocation keys, sets up IC posting rules, establishes monthly reporting; trains cost center managers on timekeeping and cost attribution; first allocation run with parallel posting for validation | Finance Controller / IT | CFO | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Cost-center / allocation config (links to VS-29 / W288, VS-17 / W14) — the new cost-pool setup
- Transfer-pricing documentation (links to VS-72 / W2594) — the new-service methodology added to the TP file
- ERP config / change management (links to VS-27 / W132) — the new cost center and posting rules released via change management

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Methodology-dispute risk**: the affected entities dispute the proposed allocation methodology, delaying establishment; mitigated by the external-advisor arm's-length confirmation in step 2
- **First-run-error risk**: the new allocation's first live run mis-posts (wrong key, wrong entity mapping); mitigated by the parallel-posting validation in step 3 before the run goes live

### Automation Opportunity
- Cost-pool config template (reused from W2574 established pools); parallel-run auto-reconciled to the validation baseline

### Controls
- CTL-445 (ensure controlled execution — Shared Services Cost Pool Management (PA-72.1).)
- CTL-445 (transfer-pricing arm's-length — links to W235); operational: CEO sign-off on the new-service agreement; CFO sign-off on the go-live after parallel-run validation

### Time Estimate
~4 weeks per new-service establishment × ~2.5/year = **~400–500 hours/year** Finance Controller + affected function.

## W2579. Shared Service Cost Budget & Variance Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual budget cycle; monthly variance review |
| **Frequency** | Annual budget; monthly review |
| **Volume** | ~15 cost pools |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, CFO, Cost Center Managers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Controller establishes annual shared services budget per cost pool: top-down from enterprise budget, bottom-up from cost center manager inputs; sets monthly phasing; obtains CFO approval | Finance Controller / Cost Center Managers | CFO | 2–3 weeks (during budget cycle) |
| 2 | Monthly: Finance Analyst compares actual shared services costs vs. budget; investigates variances >5%; identifies cost overruns (especially IT projects and facilities maintenance); prepares variance commentary for CFO review | Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1–2 days/month |
| 3 | Quarterly: Finance Controller presents shared services cost trend to CFO and COO; proposes corrective actions for over-budget categories; adjusts allocation rates if significant permanent cost changes; updates annual forecast | Finance Controller | CFO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Budgeting (links to VS-17 / W26 budget, VS-101 merchandise financial planning) — the annual budget and monthly phasing
- Variance analytics (links to VS-27 / W113, VS-17 / W9A) — the actual-vs-budget comparison
- Allocation-rate adjustment (links to VS-72 / W2574) — the rate change when permanent cost shifts occur

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Variance-fatigue risk**: every month has variances and the >5% investigation becomes routine rubber-stamping; mitigated by the variance categorized (volume-driven vs. efficiency-driven) and the quarterly trend review focusing on systemic issues
- **Rate-volatility risk**: allocation rates change monthly chasing variance, destabilizing entity P&L comparability; mitigated by the rate-change only for *permanent* cost shifts (documented), not transient ones

### Automation Opportunity
- Variance auto-flagged >5% with category auto-suggested; rate-change-impact auto-modeled before approval

### Controls
- CTL-213 (ensure shared-services chargeback integrity.)
- CTL-445 (budget-vs-actual accuracy — links to VS-17 / W26); operational: CFO quarterly variance-review sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2.5 weeks annual budget + ~1.5 days/month variance + ~1 day/quarter trend = **~250–300 hours/year** Finance Controller + Analyst.

## W2580. Shared Services Cost Pool Audit & Documentation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Internal audit cycle or external audit request |
| **Frequency** | Annual internal; annual external |
| **Volume** | ~15 cost pools |
| **Owner** | Internal Auditor |
| **Participants** | Internal Auditor, Finance Controller, External Auditors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Internal Auditor reviews shared services allocation methodology: verifies arm's-length pricing, tests allocation calculation accuracy, reviews cost pool classification (direct vs. allocated), assesses completeness of cost capture; produces audit findings report | Internal Auditor | Head of Internal Audit | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | Finance Controller addresses audit findings: corrects methodology issues, adjusts misallocated costs, strengthens documentation for transfer pricing file; implements controls recommended by auditor | Finance Controller | CFO | 2–4 weeks |
| 3 | External auditors review shared services allocation as part of intercompany transactions audit: verify transfer pricing documentation, confirm allocation mathematical accuracy, assess management judgment reasonableness; Finance Controller provides supporting documentation | Finance Controller / External Auditors | CFO | During annual audit (1–2 weeks) |

### System Touchpoints
- Audit module (links to VS-21 / W120 audit planning, W349 corporate tax & statutory reporting audit) — the audit findings register
- Transfer-pricing documentation (links to VS-72 / W2594) — the TP file audited
- Cost-center / allocation (links to VS-72 / W2574–W2577) — the allocation engine audited for accuracy

### Pain Points / Risks
- **TP-documentation-gap risk**: the audit finds the TP documentation doesn't match the actual allocation methodology (documentation stale); mitigated by the annual TP-refresh in W2594 synchronized with the allocation review
- **Management-judgment-challenge risk**: the external auditor challenges the reasonableness of an allocation key (e.g., "headcount doesn't reflect HR benefit"); mitigated by the benchmarking study and the documented rationale

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-test of "tests allocation calculation accuracy" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-flag of "verify transfer pricing documentation" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-445 (ensure controlled execution — Shared Services Cost Pool Management (PA-72.1).)
- operational: auditor independent of the Controller (segregation of duties); Head of Internal Audit sign-off on the findings; CFO sign-off on remediation

### Time Estimate
~1.5 weeks internal audit + ~3 weeks remediation + ~1.5 weeks external audit = **~350–450 hours/year** Internal Audit + Finance Controller.

## W2581. Shared Service Provider Recharge to External Third Parties

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Service provided to external party by shared service function |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; 5–10 per year |
| **Volume** | 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, Legal, Entity CFOs |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Controller identifies external recharge opportunity: shared service provider (e.g., BuildRight Logistics) provides warehousing to external party, or IT provides system access to joint venture partner; calculates recharge cost including direct cost + reasonable margin (5–10%) | Finance Controller | CFO | 2–3 days |
| 2 | Legal prepares service agreement: scope, pricing, payment terms, data privacy provisions (RA 10173), liability limitations; CFO approves pricing; entity head signs agreement | Legal / Finance Controller | CEO | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Finance generates monthly recharge invoice; posts revenue to shared service entity; ensures VAT and withholding tax compliance on recharge revenue; tracks accounts receivable from external party | Finance / Billing | Finance Controller | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Billing / AR (links to VS-16 / W8 AR, VS-15 / W7) — the recharge invoicing
- Tax (links to VS-79 / W90 VAT & withholding on the recharge revenue) — the tax compliance
- Contract repository (links to VS-100 / W230) — the service agreement

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-below-arm's-length risk**: the recharge margin (5–10%) is below what an independent party would charge, creating a transfer-pricing concern even on an external transaction (related-party JV); mitigated by the benchmarking and the external-advisor review
- **Tax-non-compliance risk**: the recharge revenue isn't correctly VAT-rated or WHT-processed, creating a BIR exposure; mitigated by the tax-compliance step in step 3 and the VS-79 oversight

### Automation Opportunity
- Recharge invoice auto-generated per the agreement; VAT/WHT auto-determined from the service type

### Controls
- CTL-445 (tax compliance — links to VS-79 / W90); CTL-44 (duplicate-invoice guard); operational: CEO sign-off on the agreement

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/opportunity + ~1.5 weeks agreement + monthly billing absorbed × ~7/year = **~180–250 hours/year** Finance Controller + Legal.
