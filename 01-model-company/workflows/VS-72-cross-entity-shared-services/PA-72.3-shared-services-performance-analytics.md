## W2590. Shared Services Efficiency & Cost-per-Transaction Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly analytics cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~15 shared service categories |
| **Owner** | Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Analyst, Finance Controller, COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Analyst calculates cost-per-transaction for each shared service: AP cost per invoice processed (target: PHP 150–200), AR cost per invoice, payroll cost per payslip, IT cost per user, procurement cost per PO; compares against industry benchmarks | Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Analyst identifies efficiency improvement opportunities: automation potential (AP OCR, payroll automation), volume consolidation across entities, shared service center staffing optimization, technology upgrade ROI | Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1 day |
| 3 | Finance Controller presents efficiency analytics to COO and CFO: cost trend by shared service, benchmark comparison, improvement recommendations with projected savings; proposes annual efficiency targets | Finance Controller | CFO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Cost accounting (links to VS-158 / W85, VS-72 / W2575) — the cost-per-transaction numerator
- Volume masters (links to VS-15 / W7 invoice count, VS-19 / W10 payslip count, VS-27 / W373 IT user count) — the denominator
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113) — the benchmark comparison and trend

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Benchmark-staleness risk**: the industry benchmark is years old and BuildRight's "above-benchmark" result is actually competitive; mitigated by the annual benchmark refresh in W2591
- **Cost-per-transaction-gaming risk**: a function reclassifies transactions to lower its cost-per-transaction (e.g., bundles two invoices as one); mitigated by the transaction-count integrity from the source system

### Automation Opportunity
- Cost-per-transaction auto-computed from cost ÷ volume; benchmark-delta auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-447 (ensure controlled execution — Shared Services Performance & Analytics (PA-72.3).)
- operational: transaction-count integrity; operational: Finance Controller quarterly efficiency-review sign-off

### Time Estimate
~3 days/month Analyst + ~1 day/quarter Controller = **~300–350 hours/year**.

## W2591. Cross-Entity Shared Services Benchmarking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual benchmarking cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | 5 entities; ~15 categories |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, CFO, External Benchmarking Firm |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Controller engages benchmarking firm: compares BuildRight shared services cost and efficiency against Philippine retail sector peers, ASEAN retail benchmarks, global best practices; covers IT, HR, Finance, Procurement, Facilities functions | Finance Controller | CFO | 4–6 weeks |
| 2 | Controller analyzes benchmarking results: identifies above-benchmark costs (opportunity for reduction), below-benchmark costs (potential underinvestment risk), best practice adoption gaps; produces gap analysis with quantified improvement potential | Finance Controller | CFO | 2 weeks |
| 3 | CFO and COO review benchmarking findings: set 3-year improvement targets, approve transformation initiatives (shared service automation, process re-engineering), allocate investment budget; Finance Controller tracks progress quarterly | CFO / COO | CEO | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Benchmarking dataset (links to VS-27 / W113, VS-72 / W2590 efficiency) — the external benchmark merged with internal metrics
- Transformation portfolio (links to VS-112 / PMO, VS-133 OpEx continuous improvement) — the approved initiatives
- Investment budget (links to VS-40 / W21 capex, VS-135 TBM/FinOps) — the transformation funding

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Apples-to-oranges risk**: the benchmark defines "IT cost" differently than BuildRight, making the comparison misleading; mitigated by the definition-mapping before the benchmark and the footnoted comparison
- **Benchmark-driven-overcut risk**: a below-benchmark result triggers cost-cutting that's actually underinvestment (the function is lean but fragile); mitigated by the below-benchmark-as-underinvestment-risk lens in step 2

### Automation Opportunity
- Benchmark-dataset auto-merged with internal metrics with definition-mapping; gap-analysis auto-quantified

### Controls
- CTL-447 (ensure controlled execution — Shared Services Performance & Analytics (PA-72.3).)
- Operational: CFO + COO sign-off on the benchmarking findings and targets; CEO sign-off on major transformation investment

### Time Estimate
~7 weeks/year Finance Controller + external firm = **~250–300 hours/year** Finance Controller.

## W2592. IC Profit Elimination & Consolidation Impact Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly consolidation cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | 5 entities |
| **Owner** | Consolidation Accountant |
| **Participants** | Consolidation Accountant, Finance Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Consolidation Accountant calculates IC profit elimination: identifies IC transactions with unrealized profit (inventory transfers between entities at above-cost, IC service margins), calculates elimination amounts, posts consolidation adjustment entries | Consolidation Accountant | Finance Controller | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Accountant analyzes IC profit elimination trend: tracks IC margin levels by service type, identifies margin creep (IC pricing gradually increasing above arm's-length), flags high-margin IC transactions for transfer pricing review | Consolidation Accountant | Finance Controller | 1 day |
| 3 | Finance Controller reviews elimination impact on consolidated financials: ensures eliminations are complete and accurate, verifies no material IC balances remain uneliminated, confirms consolidated tax positions reflect proper elimination | Finance Controller | CFO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Consolidation (links to VS-17 / W9A month-end close, W234 consolidated profit elimination) — the elimination engine
- IC pricing (links to VS-72 / W2582 agreements) — the margin source for the elimination
- TP documentation (links to VS-72 / W2594) — the margin-creep flag feeding the TP review

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-creep risk**: IC pricing drifts above arm's-length over time (cumulative small increases), inflating entity profit and creating a TP exposure; mitigated by the margin-trend monitoring in step 2
- **Incomplete-elimination risk**: an IC balance isn't eliminated in consolidation, distorting the consolidated financials; mitigated by the no-material-residual check in step 3

### Automation Opportunity
- Elimination auto-computed from the IC transactions; margin-creep auto-flagged from the trend

### Controls
- CTL-447 (ensure controlled execution — Shared Services Performance & Analytics (PA-72.3).)
- CTL-447 (consolidation accuracy — links to VS-17 / W9A, W234); CTL-18 (TP arm's-length — links to W235); operational: Finance Controller elimination sign-off

### Time Estimate
~3 days/month Consolidation Accountant + Controller = **~290 hours/year**.

### Steps

|---|---|---|---|---|

### System Touchpoints

- **Methodology-vs-actual-divergence risk**: the documented methodology doesn't match the actual allocation (the documentation says "headcount" but the actual uses "revenue"); mitigated by the W2580 audit cross-check and the synchronization of the TP refresh with the allocation review

### Automation Opportunity

## W2595. Shared Services Technology Investment ROI Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-implementation review of shared service automation |
| **Frequency** | Post-implementation; quarterly review |
| **Volume** | 5–10 active automation projects |
| **Owner** | IT Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | IT Finance Analyst, CIO, Finance Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Finance Analyst tracks shared service automation project ROI: compares projected vs. actual cost savings (AP automation: 30% cost reduction target, payroll automation: 20% error reduction), measures adoption rates and usage metrics, calculates payback period vs. business case | IT Finance Analyst | CIO / Finance Controller | 1–2 days/quarter |
| 2 | Analyst identifies underperforming investments: projects not delivering projected savings, low adoption requiring change management intervention, scope creep increasing cost beyond business case; recommends corrective actions | IT Finance Analyst | CIO | 1 day |
| 3 | Finance Controller incorporates automation savings into shared services cost budget: adjusts allocation rates downward for functions with realized savings, reallocates saved capacity to growth activities, updates 3-year cost forecast | Finance Controller | CFO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Automation portfolio (links to VS-30 / W398 emerging-tech PoC, VS-133 OpEx, VS-112 PMO) — the automation projects
- Cost savings (links to VS-72 / W2590 efficiency, VS-158 / W85) — the realized savings
- Allocation rates (links to VS-72 / W2574) — the rate adjustment for realized savings

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Savings-non-realization risk**: the automation delivers but the savings are absorbed into other cost growth (no net reduction), eroding the ROI; mitigated by the allocation-rate-adjustment in step 3 locking in the savings
- **Adoption-risk**: the automation is delivered but not adopted (staff keep the manual process); mitigated by the adoption-metric tracking in step 1 and the change-management intervention

### Automation Opportunity
- ROI auto-tracked from cost-savings vs. business-case; adoption auto-measured from system-usage telemetry

### Controls
- CTL-447 (ensure controlled execution — Shared Services Performance & Analytics (PA-72.3).)
- operational: capex-approval for automation investment; CTL-637 (technology value realization — realized savings finance-validated and allocation rate adjusted); operational: CIO + Finance Controller quarterly ROI sign-off

### Time Estimate
~3 days/quarter IT Finance Analyst + Controller = **~180–220 hours/year**.

## W2596. Shared Services Entity Profitability Contribution Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly profitability review |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | 5 entities |
| **Owner** | FP&A Manager |
| **Participants** | FP&A Mgr, Finance Controller, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A Manager analyzes entity profitability after IC allocations: revenue per entity, cost of services including IC charges, operating margin per entity; compares entity standalone profit vs. allocated shared services cost impact | FP&A Manager | Finance Controller | 2–3 days |
| 2 | FP&A identifies cross-subsidization concerns: entities with disproportionate shared service cost allocation relative to benefit received, entities subsidizing others' growth, transfer pricing margins distorting entity profitability | FP&A Manager | CFO | 1–2 days |
| 3 | CFO reviews entity profitability with CEO: discusses allocation equity, strategic investment justification (e.g., Digital Commerce Inc. startup losses funded by mature Depot Inc.), performance incentive implications; adjusts allocation methodology if significant inequity identified | CFO | CEO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Entity P&L (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-72 / W2575 allocation) — the per-entity profitability
- IC charges (links to VS-72 / W2583) — the allocated cost
- FP&A (links to VS-17 / W26 budget, VS-101 merchandise financial planning) — the entity-profitability model

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cross-subsidization-distortion risk**: a mature entity (Depot Inc.) subsidizes a startup entity (Digital Commerce) via allocation, making the mature entity look less profitable and the startup look worse; mitigated by the strategic-investment-justification lens in step 3 (the subsidy is intentional, documented)
- **Allocation-equity-dispute risk**: an entity CFO argues the allocation is unfair (benefit ≠ cost share); mitigated by the periodic allocation-methodology review (W2574) and the external-advisor arm's-length confirmation

### Automation Opportunity
- Entity P&L-after-IC auto-computed; cross-subsidization auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-447 (ensure controlled execution — Shared Services Performance & Analytics (PA-72.3).)
- CTL-447 (TP arm's-length — links to W235); CTL-213 (entity-profitability & chargeback accuracy); operational: CFO + CEO review of allocation equity

### Time Estimate
~4 days/quarter FP&A + CFO review = **~180–220 hours/year**.

## W2597. Shared Services Annual Performance Report & Board Presentation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual reporting cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | 5 entities; ~15 shared service categories |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, CFO, COO, Board |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Controller compiles annual shared services performance report: total shared services cost (PHP ~150M/year), cost per entity, efficiency trend (3-year), automation progress, SLA compliance, transfer pricing compliance, benchmarking results, 3-year outlook | Finance Controller | CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | CFO reviews and approves report; highlights key findings and strategic recommendations for Board presentation; includes shared services transformation roadmap and investment requests | CFO | CEO | 1 week |
| 3 | CFO presents to Board Audit Committee: shared services governance, IC transaction compliance, transfer pricing status, cost efficiency achievements, investment proposals; Board provides oversight feedback and approves major initiatives | CFO | Board Audit Committee | 1–2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Performance-report dataset (links to VS-27 / W113, VS-72 / W2590–W2596) — the year-round analytics rolled up
- Board reporting (links to VS-21 / W120 audit, VS-36 / W124 corporate secretary) — the Board Audit Committee artifact
- Transformation roadmap (links to VS-112 / PMO, VS-133 OpEx) — the investment proposals

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-stitching risk**: the annual report draws from many workflows and hand-stitching introduces error; mitigated by the year-round W2590 dashboard as the canonical source (the report is a rollup, not a re-collection)
- **Board-overload risk**: the report is too detailed for a Board audience; mitigated by the CFO strategic-summary layer (step 2) translating detail into decisions

### Automation Opportunity
- Annual report auto-rolled-up from the W2590 dashboard with year-over-year delta; Board-deck auto-generated from the strategic summary

### Controls
- CTL-349: board & committee governance — annual report receipt minuted (links to VS-21 / VS-36); operational: Board Audit Committee receipt minuted

### Time Estimate
~1.5 weeks Controller compilation + ~1 week CFO review + Board presentation = **~120–150 hours/year**.
