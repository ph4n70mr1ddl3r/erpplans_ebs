## W5337. Heavy Equipment Preventive Maintenance Schedule, Service Bay & Mechanic Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Telematics hour-meter threshold (W5324); calendar PM; return inspection flagged (W5334) |
| **Frequency** | ~1 PM per unit per ~250 operating hours; ~1,500–2,500 PM events/yr |
| **Volume** | 1 work-order per PM event |
| **Owner** | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead |
| **Participants** | Mechanics, Parts Clerk, OEM Service Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Auto-generate PM work-order from the hour-meter threshold; pull the unit from availability. | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | 15 min |
| 2 | Execute class-specific PM (engine oil, hydraulics, filters, tracks/tires, safety devices, electrics) in the service bay. | Mechanic | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | 2–6 hours |
| 3 | Replace wear parts from the on-hand kit (W5322); raise parts-replenishment if below min. | Parts Clerk | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | 30 min |
| 4 | Record PM completion, hour-meter-in, parts used, and mechanic hours in the asset history. | Mechanic | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | 20 min |
| 5 | Return unit to Available; notify any waitlist (VS-12.2 W1318). | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | 10 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Oracle eAM maintenance work orders (per-asset, fit-gap F5); parts inventory (VS-05); rental availability status; telematics hour-meter trigger.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **In-field-breakdown risk**: PM deferred past the threshold causes a failure on a customer's jobsite (lost rental day + recovery cost + reputational hit); mitigated by auto-generated PM work-orders and a hard availability-hold until PM closes.
- **Parts-stockout risk**: a unit grounded for weeks waiting for a hydraulic seal not in the kit; mitigated by the W5322 minimum spares kit and min-max parts replenishment.

### Automation Opportunity
- PM auto-scheduled from telematics; parts min-max auto-reorder; availability auto-restored on PM close.

### Controls
- CTL-104: rental-fleet PM & availability integrity; operational: no-unit-to-Available-without-PM-close.

### Time Estimate
- ~3–7 hours per PM; ~2,000/yr → ~10,000 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-12.2 (W1554 power-tool PM, W1172 fleet lifecycle), VS-20.3 (facility/bay), VS-05 (parts inventory)

## W5338. Equipment Breakdown, Field Service Dispatch & Substitute Unit Provisioning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | In-rental breakdown reported by customer or telematics fault code |
| **Frequency** | ~1–3% of rentals |
| **Volume** | ~50–150 breakdowns/yr |
| **Owner** | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead |
| **Participants** | Field Service Tech/OEM, Logistics Dispatcher, Customer, Credit/Billing |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Triage the reported fault (telematics code + customer description); decide field-repair vs. swap-out. | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | 30 min |
| 2 | Dispatch a field service tech or OEM partner to the jobsite (links VS-12/VS-06 dispatch). | Logistics Dispatcher | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | 1 hour |
| 3 | If swap-out: source a substitute unit, deliver (W5331), and hand over (W5330) to keep the customer's job running (VS-12.2 W1374 pattern). | Logistics Dispatcher | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | 0.5–1 day |
| 4 | Recover the broken unit to the bay; diagnose, repair, and route to PM (W5337). | Mechanic | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | varies |
| 5 | Adjust billing (no-charge for downtime), and capture root-cause for fleet-quality feedback to procurement (W5322). | Credit/Billing | Rental Fleet Mechanic Lead | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Field-service/dispatch (VS-06/VS-12); substitute-unit availability; rental billing adjustment; asset repair history.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Customer-loss risk**: a breakdown without a substitute unit loses the rental and the customer to a competitor; mitigated by a documented substitute-unit SLA and a small float reserve at each hub.
- **Mis-charging risk**: charging the customer for BuildRight's equipment failure erodes trust; mitigated by a downtime no-charge rule for non-customer-caused failures.

### Automation Opportunity
- Telematics fault code auto-opens a case and suggests field-repair vs. swap-out; auto-source nearest substitute unit.

### Controls
- CTL-104: breakdown-repair accountability & substitute-unit recovery; operational: downtime no-charge rule for non-customer-caused failures.

### Time Estimate
- ~3–6 person-hours dispatcher/mechanic per breakdown; ~100/yr → ~500 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-12 (installation/service dispatch), VS-06 (logistics), VS-31 (quality/root-cause)

## W5339. Equipment LTO/LTFRB Registration, Crane/Rigging Permit & Roadworthiness Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Unit intake (registered/titled equipment); annual registration renewal; permit expiry |
| **Frequency** | Annual per registered unit; ad-hoc for crane/rigging permits |
| **Volume** | ~60–120 registered units; ~20–40 crane/rigging permits |
| **Owner** | Compliance Specialist (Fleet) |
| **Participants** | LTO/LTFRB liaison, Rental Fleet Manager, Permit Portfolio (VS-166) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify which units require LTO/LTFRB registration (self-propelled/road-going) vs. plant-only equipment. | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | per intake |
| 2 | File/renew registration, emission, and roadworthiness (annual); obtain plates/decals. | LTO/LTFRB Liaison | Compliance Specialist | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | For crane/aerial/rigging classes, secure and renew the specific operating permits (links VS-165 PCAB/contractor). | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | 2–4 weeks |
| 4 | Record permits in the license/permit portfolio with expiry alerts (VS-166). | Compliance Specialist | Compliance Specialist | 30 min |
| 5 | Gate rental availability on valid registration/permit; block rental of expired units. | Rental Fleet Manager | Compliance Specialist | ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Permit/portfolio register (VS-166) with expiry alerts; rental availability gate on permit validity.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Operating-illegal risk**: renting out an unregistered or expired-permit unit (e.g. an overdue crane permit) exposes BuildRight to LTFRB/LGU fines, impound, and operator personal liability; mitigated by an availability gate that blocks rental of expired units.
- **Lapse risk**: a permit that lapses unnoticed takes a unit (and its revenue) out of availability; mitigated by 60-day expiry alerts in the permit portfolio.

### Automation Opportunity
- Permit-expiry alerts auto-gate availability; renewal applications pre-filled from the asset register.

### Controls
- CTL-41: LGU/agency regulatory compliance; CTL-26 (permit-validity availability gate).

### Time Estimate
- ~2–6 person-hours per renewal; ~100 renewals/yr → ~400 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-166 (permit portfolio), VS-165 (PCAB contractor licensing), VS-76 (LGU compliance), VS-06 (fleet registration)

## W5340. Operator Safety Compliance, DOLE-OSH Equipment Certification & Plant-Equipment Logbook

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual DOLE equipment certification; per-use logbook; regulatory inspection |
| **Frequency** | Annual certification per certified unit; per-shift logbook |
| **Volume** | ~60–120 annual certifications; ~10,000+ logbook entries/yr |
| **Owner** | HSE Officer |
| **Participants** | DOLE-accredited inspector, Mechanics, Rental Yard Lead |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Schedule DOLE-accredited third-party certification for pressure/lift/earthmoving equipment (per DOLE OSH standards). | HSE Officer | HSE Officer | annual |
| 2 | Maintain the per-unit plant-equipment logbook: pre-use checks, faults, and rectifications (DOLE requirement). | Rental Yard Lead | HSE Officer | per shift |
| 3 | Verify safety devices (emergency stop, overload alarm, fall protection anchors) at each PM (W5337) and handover (W5330). | Mechanic | HSE Officer | per event |
| 4 | Record certifications in the asset history and permit portfolio (VS-166); gate availability on valid certification. | HSE Officer | HSE Officer | per certification |
| 5 | Respond to DOLE inspection findings and document corrective action (links VS-24/VS-22). | HSE Officer | HSE Officer | per finding |

### System Touchpoints
- Asset certification register (VS-166); digital logbook capturing pre-use checks; availability gate on certification validity.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **DOLE-finding risk**: an uncertified or expired piece of lifting/pressure equipment operated at a customer site triggers a DOLE OSH finding and officer personal liability (RA 11058); mitigated by the availability gate and annual certification scheduling.
- **Logbook-gap risk**: a missing pre-use logbook entry undermines incident defense (W5341); mitigated by a digital logbook with a mandatory pre-use check before handover.

### Automation Opportunity
- Digital pre-use check app feeding the logbook; certification-expiry alerts auto-gate availability.

### Controls
- CTL-790 (ensure controlled execution — Equipment Maintenance, Safety, Compliance & Fleet Utilization Analytics (PA-186.3).)
- operational: DOLE/OSH compliance; operational: no-handover-without-completed-pre-use-check.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 weeks elapsed per annual certification cycle fleet-wide; logbook entries ~5 min each.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-166 (permits), VS-22 (compliance), VS-115 (calibration of safety devices)

## W5341. Equipment Rental Accident, Injury & Third-Party Damage Incident Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Accident/injury/third-party damage involving rented equipment (at jobsite or in transit) |
| **Frequency** | Rare (~0.5–2% of rentals); high severity |
| **Volume** | ~30–100 incidents/yr |
| **Owner** | HSE Officer |
| **Participants** | Rental Fleet Manager, Legal (VS-100), Insurance (VS-26), Corporate Security (VS-159), Customer, Police |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Secure the scene and ensure medical aid; preserve the unit and evidence (DOLE reportable threshold). | HSE Officer | HSE Officer | immediate |
| 2 | Capture incident facts: telematics (W5332), logbook (W5340), operator competency record (W5326), and witness statements. | HSE Officer | HSE Officer | 1 day |
| 3 | Notify insurance/captive within the policy notice window (W5327/VS-26); file DOLE reportable if applicable (VS-24). | Risk & Insurance Manager | HSE Officer | 1 day |
| 4 | Coordinate legal defense and third-party claim handling (VS-100/VS-26); engage Corporate Security for fraud/foul-play (VS-159). | Legal Counsel | HSE Officer | varies |
| 5 | Root-cause analysis; corrective action to fleet/PM/training (W5337/W5326); capture lessons-learned (VS-133). | HSE Officer | HSE Officer | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Incident/case module (VS-24/VS-23); telematics replay; insurance claim register (VS-26); DOLE-reportable filing.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Liability-exposure risk**: a serious injury (e.g. aerial-lift fall) without documented competency/safety-briefing evidence exposes BuildRight to large damages; mitigated by the W5326/W5330 documentation chain.
- **Notice-window risk**: late insurance/DOLE notification forfeits coverage or compounds the regulatory finding; mitigated by a 24–48h notice SLA embedded in incident response.

### Automation Opportunity
- Incident-report app that auto-pulls telematics/logbook/competency evidence and starts the insurance/DOLE notice clock.

### Controls
- CTL-104: rental-incident recovery — fraud detection in claims; CTL-219 (asset-register evidence); operational: 48h insurance/DOLE notice SLA.

### Time Estimate
- ~40–120 person-hours per serious incident; ~60/yr → ~4,000 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-24 (HSE), VS-26 (insurance), VS-100 (legal), VS-159 (security), VS-147 (customer/premises safety), VS-133 (lessons-learned)

## W5342. Fleet Utilization, Idle-Time, Rate-Realization & Yield Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly/monthly fleet-performance review |
| **Frequency** | Weekly |
| **Volume** | 1 review per hub weekly; 1 group review monthly |
| **Owner** | Rental Fleet Manager |
| **Participants** | COO, Finance Business Partner, BI/Analytics (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compute utilization (rented-hours ÷ available-hours) and idle-time by class, hub, and region from telematics. | Rental Fleet Manager | Rental Fleet Manager | 3 hours |
| 2 | Compute realized rate vs. rate card (W5325) and yield (revenue ÷ capex) per class. | Finance Business Partner | COO | 2 hours |
| 3 | Identify under-utilized classes/hubs → rebalancing (W5344), repricing (W5325), or disposition (W5343). | Rental Fleet Manager | COO | 2 hours |
| 4 | Benchmark attach-to-material-sales lift (does renting a mixer lift a cement/aggregate basket?). | BI Analyst | COO | 2 hours |
| 5 | Publish the fleet-performance dashboard to the Services leadership. | BI Analyst | COO | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- BI/analytics on the telematics + billing + rental-lifecycle data (VS-28); fleet-performance dashboard.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stranded-asset risk**: a class sitting at 30% utilization erodes the capex hurdle silently; mitigated by weekly utilization review with explicit rebalance/reprice/disposition decisions.
- **Attach-blindness risk**: renting equipment that doesn't lift material sales under-cuts the business case (W5321); mitigated by attach-lift measurement in the dashboard.

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-refreshing utilization/yield dashboard from telematics + billing; auto-flag classes below the utilization hurdle.

### Controls
- CTL-790 (ensure controlled execution — Equipment Maintenance, Safety, Compliance & Fleet Utilization Analytics (PA-186.3).)
- operational: performance vs. business-case hurdle; utilization-data accuracy; .

### Time Estimate
- ~10 person-hours/week fleet-wide review.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-28 (BI/analytics), VS-118 (revenue assurance), VS-12.2 (W1376 rental analytics)

## W5343. Fleet Age, Lifecycle Replacement, Disposition & Trade-In Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Unit hits lifecycle age/hours; chronic high-repair-cost flag; or category exit |
| **Frequency** | ~10–20% of fleet/yr replaced |
| **Volume** | ~60–120 disposition decisions/yr |
| **Owner** | Rental Fleet Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance (Asset Accountant), Procurement (W5322), VS-35, Used-equipment buyer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify lifecycle candidates from age, operating hours, and repair-cost-trend (W5337). | Rental Fleet Manager | Rental Fleet Manager | 1 week |
| 2 | Decide refurbish vs. replace vs. dispose based on remaining economic life and the replacement capex (W5321/VS-40). | Finance Business Partner | COO | 1 week |
| 3 | For replacement: order the successor unit (W5322) and plan the swap to minimize availability gap. | Procurement Manager | COO | per W5322 |
| 4 | Dispose via trade-in to OEM, sale to used-equipment buyer, or VS-155 trade-in/B2B sale; ensure data/device wipe on telematics. | Asset Accountant | CFO | 2 weeks |
| 5 | Retire the asset from the register, recognize gain/loss, and update the fleet plan (VS-35). | Asset Accountant | CFO | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Fixed Asset retirement/disposal (VS-35/CTL-26); replacement capex (VS-40); trade-in/B2B sale order (VS-11/VS-155).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Run-to-failure risk**: keeping units past economic life inflates repair cost and breakdown frequency (W5338); mitigated by lifecycle-decision triggers on age/hours/repair-cost.
- **Disposal-leakage risk**: disposing of a PHP 3M unit below market or without telematics wipe; mitigated by a disposal valuation and mandatory device-data wipe (links VS-99/VS-91).

### Automation Opportunity
- Lifecycle-candidate auto-flag from age/hours/repair-cost; disposal valuation from used-equipment-market data.

### Controls
- CTL-26: fixed asset disposal; CTL-27 (telematics/customer-data wipe before disposal).

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 weeks elapsed per disposition batch; ~80/yr → ~600 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-35 (fixed assets), VS-40 (replacement capex), VS-155 (trade-in), VS-12.2 (W1172 fleet lifecycle), VS-99 (IT/asset disposal)

## W5344. Cross-Store Equipment Rebalancing, Inter-DC Transport & Demand Seasonality Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Utilization imbalance (W5342); seasonal demand shift; new hub ramp |
| **Frequency** | Monthly rebalancing review; ad-hoc transfers |
| **Volume** | ~200–400 inter-store/inter-DC transfers/yr |
| **Owner** | Rental Fleet Manager |
| **Participants** | Supply Planning (VS-02/VS-127), Logistics Dispatcher, Store Managers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Map demand vs. fleet by hub/region and season (rainy-season dewatering pumps, summer concrete equipment, etc.). | Rental Fleet Manager | Rental Fleet Manager | 1 week |
| 2 | Identify rebalancing moves from low- to high-demand hubs using utilization (W5342) and forecast (VS-127). | Supply Planner | Rental Fleet Manager | 3 days |
| 3 | Execute inter-DC/store transfers via logistics (W5331/VS-06) with in-transit insurance (VS-25/VS-26). | Logistics Dispatcher | Rental Fleet Manager | per transfer |
| 4 | Update the rental-asset home-location and availability ledger; confirm telematics re-home. | Rental Fleet Manager | Rental Fleet Manager | per transfer |
| 5 | Review post-season rebalancing effectiveness and feed the next season's fleet plan (W5321). | Rental Fleet Manager | COO | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental-asset home-location + transfer orders (VS-05/VS-06); demand forecast (VS-02/VS-127); telematics re-home confirmation.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-positioning risk**: a high-demand unit stuck at a low-demand hub while another hub stock-outs loses the rental and ships a substitute long-distance; mitigated by monthly utilization-driven rebalancing.
- **In-transit risk**: a PHP 5M unit damaged/lost in an inter-hub transfer; mitigated by in-transit insurance and telematics tracking (links VS-25/CTL-25).

### Automation Opportunity
- Utilization-imbalance auto-suggests rebalancing moves; transfer order auto-created and telematics re-homes on arrival.

### Controls
- CTL-25: safeguard in transit; CTL-21 (transfer-accuracy/reconciliation).

### Time Estimate
- ~2 person-hours per transfer + dispatcher effort; ~300/yr → ~1,000 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (logistics/transfers), VS-02/VS-127 (planning/forecast), VS-12.2 (W1553 cross-store rebalancing)
