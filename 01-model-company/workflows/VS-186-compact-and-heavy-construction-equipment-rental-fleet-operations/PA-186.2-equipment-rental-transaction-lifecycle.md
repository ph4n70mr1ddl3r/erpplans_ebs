## W5329. Heavy Equipment Reservation, Quoting & Rental Agreement Generation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer booking (online W5328 or in-store) |
| **Frequency** | ~3,000–6,000 rentals/yr |
| **Volume** | 1 reservation per rental |
| **Owner** | Rental Counter Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Credit Analyst, Customer, Store Manager (override approvals) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm unit availability for the requested window at the chosen hub (single rental-asset ledger). | Rental Counter Staff | Rental Counter Supervisor | 10 min |
| 2 | Generate quote: base rate (day/week), delivery (W5331), CDW (W5327), deposit hold, and estimated fuel/wear. | Rental Counter Staff | Rental Counter Supervisor | 10 min |
| 3 | Confirm pre-qualification standing (W5326); place deposit pre-auth on card/trade account. | Credit Analyst | Rental Counter Supervisor | 15 min |
| 4 | Generate the rental agreement: serial, hour-meter-out, rate, period, CDW election, and operator acknowledgment. | Rental Counter Staff | Rental Counter Supervisor | 15 min |
| 5 | Reserve the unit (status: Reserved) and freeze availability for the window. | Rental Counter Staff | Rental Counter Supervisor | 5 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease & Finance Management lease contracts with rental-period billing (fit-gap A13 — per-unit serial + window freeze, eAM maintenance holds); quote/rate engine (W5325); credit pre-auth (VS-68); agreement e-sign + vault (VS-88).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-promise risk**: a unit promised that is actually in maintenance or already reserved; mitigated by reservation drawn from a single real-time availability ledger that includes maintenance holds.
- **Deposit-shortfall risk**: a deposit hold below the first-loss/deductible leaves BuildRight exposed if the renter damages and walks; mitigated by a minimum deposit sized to the unit class deductible.

### Automation Opportunity
- Online booking generates the agreement and pre-auth automatically; counter staff only confirm ID and hand over.

### Controls
- CTL-05: prevent unauthorized credit-limit overrides on the deposit hold; CTL-12 (credit approval separated from the counter).

### Time Estimate
- ~55 min per reservation; ~4,500/yr → ~4,100 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-12.2 (tool rental agreement W139.2), VS-68 (credit), VS-88 (documents)

## W5330. Equipment Pre-Rental Inspection, Safety Briefing & Operator Handover

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer collects at counter, or unit about to be delivered to jobsite (W5331) |
| **Frequency** | Every rental out |
| **Volume** | ~3,000–6,000 handovers/yr |
| **Owner** | Rental Yard Lead |
| **Participants** | Mechanic, HSE Officer, Customer/Operator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct pre-rental inspection with the customer: condition photos, hour-meter-out, fluids, tires/tracks, safety devices, and damage log. | Rental Yard Lead | Rental Yard Lead | 20 min |
| 2 | Deliver class-specific safety briefing: controls, emergency stop, PPE required, load/capacity limits, refueling/charging, and emergency contact. | HSE Officer | Rental Yard Lead | 15 min |
| 3 | Verify operator competency acknowledgment (W5326) is signed; re-confirm for high-risk classes (aerial lifts, earthmoving). | HSE Officer | Rental Yard Lead | 5 min |
| 4 | Confirm telematics (W5324) is live and geofence set; hand over keys/controls. | Mechanic | Rental Yard Lead | 5 min |
| 5 | Capture customer signature on condition + briefing acknowledgment; post to the agreement (VS-88). | Rental Yard Lead | Rental Yard Lead | 5 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental inspection app (condition photos + hour-meter-out) feeding the damage-on-return comparison (W5334); telematics handover confirmation; document vault.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Dispute-on-return risk**: no documented pre-rental condition means the customer disputes damage charged on return (the VS-12.2 W1555 pattern, amplified by unit value); mitigated by mandatory photo + signed condition log at handover.
- **Unsafe-use risk**: an operator who skips the briefing injures themselves or a third party; mitigated by a signed operator acknowledgment and a refusal-to-handover gate if the briefing is not completed.

### Automation Opportunity
- Inspection app auto-generates the condition baseline and compares it on return; safety briefing delivered via required video on the customer's phone before handover.

### Controls
- CTL-104: rental-equipment condition & safety baselining; operational: no-handover-without-signed-acknowledgment gate.

### Time Estimate
- ~50 min per handover; ~4,500/yr → ~3,750 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-12.2 (W1319 safety briefing), VS-24 (HSE), VS-147 (customer/premises safety)

## W5331. Customer Equipment Transport, Delivery-to-Jobsite & Pickup Logistics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Rental with delivery option (vs. customer self-haul) |
| **Frequency** | ~40–60% of rentals include delivery (~1,500–3,000 deliveries/yr) |
| **Volume** | 1 delivery leg per delivered rental (out + return) |
| **Owner** | Logistics Dispatcher |
| **Participants** | 3PL/Hauler, Store Receiving (at jobsite return), Insurance (W5327) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plan transport: equipment weight/dimensions, tilt-bed/lowboy/trailer requirement, and route permits for oversized loads (links VS-74). | Logistics Dispatcher | Logistics Dispatcher | 30 min |
| 2 | Book qualified hauler (3PL or BuildRight tow vehicle) with cargo/CTR insurance (VS-25/VS-26). | Logistics Dispatcher | Logistics Dispatcher | 30 min |
| 3 | Load, secure, and chain per equipment class; document load-down photos. | 3PL Driver | Logistics Dispatcher | 45 min |
| 4 | Deliver to jobsite; obtain site sign-off and handover to the customer's operator (W5330). | 3PL Driver | Logistics Dispatcher | varies |
| 5 | Schedule and execute return pickup; confirm telematics (W5324) re-enters the yard geofence. | Logistics Dispatcher | Logistics Dispatcher | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Logistics/dispatch (VS-06); 3PL portal (VS-56); telematics geofence in/out; oversized-load permit records (LGU/LTFRB — VS-76/VS-166).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transit-damage risk**: an unchained mini-excavator shifts on a tilt-bed and is damaged or causes a road incident; mitigated by class-specific chain-down SOP and load-down photo evidence.
- **Permit-default risk**: moving an oversize/overweight unit without the required LGU/LTFRB permit causes fines/impound; mitigated by a permit-checklist gate before dispatch.

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-select qualified hauler and trailer type from equipment weight/dimensions; telematics auto-confirms jobsite arrival.

### Controls
- CTL-25: safeguard inventory in transit; operational: permit-checklist gate before dispatch.

### Time Estimate
- ~2 hours dispatcher effort per delivered rental; hauler transit varies (0.5–1 day).

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (logistics fleet), VS-56 (3PL delivery), VS-74 (jobsite delivery), VS-110 (freight)

## W5332. In-Period Equipment Monitoring, Telematics Tracking & Misuse Detection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Unit is out on rental (post W5330) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous while rented |
| **Volume** | ~40–90 units out at any time (at 3–7-day average rentals on the ~3,000–6,000 rentals/year volume) |
| **Owner** | Rental Fleet Manager |
| **Participants** | IT/IoT Engineer, HSE Officer, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monitor telematics feed: location vs. agreed jobsite geofence, hour-meter accumulation, and idle/duty patterns. | Rental Fleet Manager | Rental Fleet Manager | daily |
| 2 | Alert on anomalies: geofence breach (unit leaving the agreed area), excessive hours vs. booked period, or kill-switch/ignition tamper. | IT/IoT Engineer | Rental Fleet Manager | real-time |
| 3 | Triage alerts: contact renter for legitimate job-site moves vs. escalate to recovery (W5336) for a suspected theft/breach. | Rental Fleet Manager | Rental Fleet Manager | per alert |
| 4 | Reconcile accumulated hours to the booked period; flag likely overage → extension billing (W5333). | Rental Fleet Manager | Rental Fleet Manager | daily |

### System Touchpoints
- Telematics/IoT platform; ERP rental-lifecycle event stream; alerting/case module.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Theft/abscond risk**: a geofence breach ignored for days becomes an unrecoverable loss on a PHP 5M unit; mitigated by a same-day triage SLA on geofence-breach alerts and a kill-switch on high-value units.
- **Revenue-leakage risk**: a unit run 14 hours/day on a 1-day booking without an extension call loses billing; mitigated by hour-meter-vs-period auto-flag that prompts extension billing (W5333).

### Automation Opportunity
- Rule-based alerting (geofence/hour/tamper) that auto-opens a case and, for high-value units, auto-triggers a kill-switch after an unresolved breach window.

### Controls
- CTL-23: detect theft/fraud via telematics exception; operational: same-day geofence-breach triage SLA.

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 person-hours/day fleet-wide monitoring; triage per alert ~15 min.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-27 (IT/IoT ops), VS-23 (loss prevention/asset protection), VS-125 (fraud)

## W5333. Equipment Extension, Rollover, Early Return & Re-rental Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Renter requests extension; telematics shows overage (W5332); or early return |
| **Frequency** | ~20–30% of rentals have a change |
| **Volume** | ~1,000–1,800 changes/yr |
| **Owner** | Rental Counter Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Credit Analyst, Customer, Next-in-queue renter |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Validate the change request against the unit's next reservation (no conflict) and renter's credit standing. | Rental Counter Staff | Rental Counter Supervisor | 10 min |
| 2 | For extension: re-rate to the appropriate daily/weekly tier and re-confirm deposit/CDW coverage (W5327). | Rental Counter Staff | Rental Counter Supervisor | 10 min |
| 3 | For early return: stop billing at actual return, release the remaining window to availability, and notify any waitlist (links VS-149/W139 booking). | Rental Counter Staff | Rental Counter Supervisor | 10 min |
| 4 | Amend the agreement and capture customer acknowledgment; re-freeze availability. | Rental Counter Staff | Rental Counter Supervisor | 10 min |
| 5 | Re-invoice/reverse per the change; post to billing (W5335). | Rental Counter Staff | Rental Counter Supervisor | 10 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Rental reservation amendment; rate-engine tier re-rating; availability re-freeze/waitlist (VS-12.2 W1318); billing link.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Double-booking risk**: an extension that conflicts with the next confirmed reservation; mitigated by a reservation-conflict check that blocks the extension and offers a substitute unit (VS-12.2 W1374 pattern).
- **Revenue-leakage risk**: early returns not re-credited to availability leave the unit idle and unbookable; mitigated by automatic availability release on early-return confirmation.

### Automation Opportunity
- Self-service extension/return in the app that auto-checks conflicts, re-rates, and re-freezes availability.

### Controls
- CTL-05: deposit/credit re-check on extension; operational: reservation-conflict block.

### Time Estimate
- ~50 min per change; ~1,400/yr → ~1,150 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-12.2 (W1318 reservation, W1375 overdue escalation), VS-60 (omnichannel availability)

## W5334. Heavy Equipment Return Inspection, Damage Assessment & Repair Cost Recovery

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Unit returns to yard (post W5331 pickup or self-return) |
| **Frequency** | Every rental return |
| **Volume** | ~3,000–6,000 returns/yr |
| **Owner** | Rental Yard Lead |
| **Participants** | Mechanic, Customer, Credit/Billing, Insurance (W5327) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inspect against the pre-rental baseline (W5330): compare condition photos, hour-meter-in, fluids, tracks/tires, glass, and structure. | Rental Yard Lead | Rental Yard Lead | 25 min |
| 2 | Log any new damage with photos and a damage code; classify fair-wear vs. chargeable. | Mechanic | Rental Yard Lead | 15 min |
| 3 | Quote repair cost (parts + labour + downtime) for chargeable damage; confirm with customer. | Mechanic | Rental Yard Lead | 20 min |
| 4 | Recover from deposit/CDW/insurance per the agreement (W5327); raise a damage charge invoice if deposit/CDW is insufficient. | Credit/Billing | Rental Yard Lead | 20 min |
| 5 | Route unit to maintenance (W5337) or back to available; release deposit balance. | Rental Yard Lead | Rental Yard Lead | 10 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Inspection app with baseline-comparison; damage-case module; deposit/CDW settlement link (W5335); maintenance work-order creation.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Disputed-damage risk**: damage charged without baseline evidence is disputed and erodes customer trust; mitigated by photo-vs-photo baseline comparison at return (the VS-12.2 W139.3 pattern, extended to high-value units).
- **Lost-recovery risk**: chargeable damage not quoted/recovered at return is absorbed; mitigated by a no-unit-back-to-available gate until damage disposition is closed.

### Automation Opportunity
- AI image-difference between baseline and return photos auto-flags new damage; repair-cost auto-quote from a parts/labour catalogue.

### Controls
- CTL-104: asset recovery/damage control — inflated or fabricated damage-claim detection.

### Time Estimate
- ~90 min per return with damage; ~30 min clean return; ~4,500/yr → ~2,700 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-12.2 (W139.3 inspection, W1555 damage recovery), VS-26 (insurance claim), VS-89 (recall if damage reveals a defect)

## W5335. Equipment Rental Billing, Deposit/Hold Management & Settlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Rental closes (return W5334) or periodic billing for long rentals |
| **Frequency** | Every rental close + weekly billing for open rentals |
| **Volume** | ~3,000–6,000 invoices/yr |
| **Owner** | Rental Billing Lead |
| **Participants** | Credit/Billing, Customer, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compute final charges: actual hours/days from telematics (W5332), delivery, fuel/wear top-up, CDW, and damage (W5334). | Rental Billing Lead | Rental Billing Lead | 15 min |
| 2 | Apply deposit hold and CDW; calculate net receivable or refund. | Credit/Billing | Rental Billing Lead | 10 min |
| 3 | Generate invoice; post to AR (trade account) or settle against the card pre-auth (links VS-16). | Rental Billing Lead | Rental Billing Lead | 10 min |
| 4 | Release/refund the deposit balance per the agreement; record revenue split (rental vs. CDW vs. delivery). | Credit/Billing | Rental Billing Lead | 10 min |
| 5 | Reconcile the rental-day revenue to the fleet utilization analytics (W5342). | Finance Business Partner | CFO | weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- Billing engine reading telematics hours; AR/card settlement (VS-16/VS-80); revenue split to rental/CDW/delivery GL accounts (VS-17/VS-157).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Billing-accuracy risk**: manual hour entry vs. telematics causes under/over-billing and disputes; mitigated by telematics-sourced actuals as the single billing source.
- **Revenue-misclassification risk**: CDW (insurance-like) revenue booked as rental revenue distorts margin and tax (percentage tax/VAT) treatment; mitigated by a defined revenue-split GL mapping (links VS-157).

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-invoice from telematics hours + signed return inspection; auto-release of deposit balance on clean return.

### Controls
- CTL-20: cash/deposit accountability; CTL-22 (correct VAT on rental vs. CDW vs. delivery).

### Time Estimate
- ~45 min per close; ~4,500/yr → ~3,400 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (order-to-cash), VS-80 (payments), VS-17 (GL), VS-157 (revenue recognition)

## W5336. Lost, Stolen or Abandoned Equipment Recovery, Recovery Agent & Police Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Geofence breach unresolved (W5332), overdue non-return (VS-12.2 W1375 escalation), or unit abandoned |
| **Frequency** | Rare (~0.3–1% of rentals); high impact |
| **Volume** | ~15–60 incidents/yr |
| **Owner** | Loss Prevention Officer |
| **Participants** | Corporate Security (VS-159), Legal (VS-100), Police, Insurance (VS-26), Recovery Agent |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm the loss: telematics last-known location, kill-switch activation, and contact attempts to the renter. | Loss Prevention Officer | Loss Prevention Officer | 2 hours |
| 2 | Escalate to Corporate Security and engage a licensed recovery agent; file a police report (theft/embezzlement under RPC). | Corporate Security | Loss Prevention Officer | 1 day |
| 3 | Notify insurance/captive (W5327/VS-26) within the policy notice window; preserve evidence for the claim. | Risk & Insurance Manager | Loss Prevention Officer | 1 day |
| 4 | Coordinate recovery/repo where located; if unrecoverable, process the claim and charge-off (VS-35). | Corporate Security | Loss Prevention Officer | varies |
| 5 | Update the renter record (blacklist), file legal recovery of the unit value (VS-100), and capture lessons-learned. | Legal Counsel | Loss Prevention Officer | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- Telematics recovery/kill-switch; LP case module (VS-23); insurance claim register (VS-26); police-blotter reference store.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unrecoverable-loss risk**: delayed escalation past the insurance notice window forfeits the claim on a PHP 5M unit; mitigated by a defined escalation timeline (24–48h) and the same-day triage SLA from W5332.
- **Safety/repo risk**: a recovery agent confronting a hostile renter creates personal-safety and legal exposure; mitigated by licensed-recovery-agent-only engagement and legal coordination (VS-100).

### Automation Opportunity
- Kill-switch auto-triggers after an unresolved geofence breach window; auto-opens an LP case and insurance-notice clock.

### Controls
- CTL-23: theft/fraud detection; CTL-26 (asset loss/charge-off); operational: 48-hour insurance-notice SLA.

### Time Estimate
- ~40–80 person-hours per major incident; ~30/yr → ~1,800 person-hours/yr.

### Cross-references
- links to VS-23 (loss prevention), VS-159 (corporate security/investigations), VS-26 (insurance), VS-100 (legal), VS-12.2 (W1375 overdue)
