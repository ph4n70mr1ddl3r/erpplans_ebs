# VS-111: Packaging, Pallet & Returnable Transport Item (RTI) Management

## Overview

Packaging, Pallet & Returnable Transport Item (RTI) Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
governing the **packaging-asset and transport-packaging discipline** that flows invisibly alongside
the ~35,000-SKU merchandise across the supply chain: product/transport packaging (cartons,
corrugate, stretch wrap, dunnage, protective packaging), pallets (wooden and plastic, owned and
pooled e.g. CHEP/LOSAL), and returnable transport items / RTIs (totes, cages, stillages, IBCs,
returnable containers, roll cages). At BuildRight's volume (~72K inbound receipts/year, ~400–600
import TEUs/month, ~50,000 replenishment orders/month, ~134M POS line items/year), packaging and
pallets are a material cost line, a real source of damage/shrink, a freight-cost driver (cube/weight
utilization), and a sustainability/waste/EPR exposure — yet no existing value stream owns the
packaging-engineering, pallet/RTI pool, or packaging-compliance/cost discipline end-to-end; it is
referenced incidentally across VS-04 (DC receiving), VS-05 (inventory), VS-06 (logistics), VS-32
(reverse), VS-73 (waste), VS-41 (private-label packaging), VS-87 (import packaging), and VS-24.3
(hazmat). This value stream consolidates the discipline: packaging engineering/specification and
procurement, transport/protective packaging design, private-label/vendor packaging compliance,
sustainable packaging and EPR, packaging labeling/hazmat compliance; pallet/RTI pool strategy,
procurement and inventory, RTI tracking/reconciliation, deposit/exchange, loss/damage/write-off,
inbound vendor compliance/chargeback, reverse-logistics/retrieval, and pool settlement; and
packaging sustainability, compliance/audit/EPR reporting, damage analytics, cube/freight
optimization, total cost of packaging, single-use-plastic reduction, and program governance.

This is distinct from **VS-04 (DC & Warehouse)** which *receives and stores* packaged goods and
*uses* pallets in putaway/pick, from **VS-05 (Inventory Lifecycle)** which manages *merchandise*
inventory (not the packaging/pallet asset), and from **VS-06 (Logistics & Fleet)** which *moves*
packaged/palletized freight. It is distinct from **VS-32 (Returns & Reverse Logistics)** which
handles *returned merchandise* (RTI reverse retrieval is a packaging-asset flow, distinct from
customer returns), from **VS-73 (Store Waste & Circular Economy)** which manages *waste* (this
value stream reduces packaging-at-source and tracks RTI to prevent waste), and from **VS-41 (Private
Label)** which *develops private-label products* (this value stream engineers their packaging).
It is distinct from **VS-03/VS-34 (Procurement)** which *transacts* packaging/pallet purchases
(this value stream specifies, engineers, and governs them), from **VS-87 (Customs)** which handles
*import packaging/dunnage compliance*, and from **VS-24.3 (Hazmat)** which manages *hazardous
goods* (this value stream engineers compliant hazmat packaging). Packaging, pallet & RTI management
is the *packaging-asset and transport-packaging engineering* discipline — distinct from DC
operations, merchandise inventory, logistics, returns, waste, private-label development,
procurement, customs, and hazmat handling.

BuildRight's exposure is material and unmanaged: packaging/pallet spend on the order of
PHP 200–500M/year across product/transport packaging, pallets, dunnage, and RTI; damage losses
linked to inadequate packaging; freight-cost leakage from poor cube/pallet utilization; growing
EPR/plastic-regulation exposure (Philippine EPR Act RA 11898, single-use-plastic LGU ordinances);
and pallet/RTI loss (a perennial shrink source at scale). No existing value stream owns the
engineering, pool, tracking, compliance, and cost-analytics discipline for these assets.
