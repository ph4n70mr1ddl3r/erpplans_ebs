## W3529. Pallet & RTI Pool Strategy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual SCM planning (links to VS-33.1); network change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Group pallet/RTI estate |
| **Owner** | SCM / Packaging Engineering |
| **Participants** | SCM, Packaging Eng, Logistics (links to VS-06), DC Operations (links to VS-04), Finance (links to VS-17/VS-35), Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34), Sustainability (links to VS-25) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SCM/Packaging Eng set the pallet/RTI pool strategy: owned vs rented/pooled (e.g., CHEP/LOSAL) vs exchange, pallet type/standard (EUR/ISO/stringer/plastic), RTI types (totes, roll cages, stillages, IBCs) by use-case, pool sizing and safety float, and the build-vs-rent economics; align to network volume (links to VS-04/VS-06) and sustainability (links to VS-25) | SCM / Packaging Eng / Logistics | COO | Annual |
| 2 | SCM/Finance model the total cost of ownership (TCO) per strategy: owned-pallet capex/depreciation (links to VS-35), storage/handling, loss/write-off (W3533), retrieval/reverse (W3535), and rented-pool per-trip fees/deposits; select the optimal mix by lane/volume | SCM / Finance | CFO | Annual |
| 3 | SCM/Procurement establish the pool program: pool-provider contracts (links to VS-03/VS-34), exchange agreements with vendors/carriers (W3532), deposit accounting (links to VS-17/W3514), and the asset-master/RTI-tracking setup (links to VS-35/VS-29/W3531); report to governance (W3544) | SCM / Procurement / Finance | COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Asset/pool master (links to VS-35/VS-29); logistics/DC (links to VS-06/VS-04); finance/TCO (links to VS-17/VS-35); procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34); sustainability (links to VS-25)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Strategy-mismatch risk**: Owning where renting is cheaper (or vice-versa); mitigated by TCO modeling
- **Standard-fragmentation risk**: Mixed pallet types disrupt handling/automation; mitigated by standardization


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "SCM/Procurement establish the pool program" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-563 (ensure controlled execution — Pallet & RTI Pool, Tracking & Reconciliation (PA-111.2).)
- operational: TCO modeling; operational: standardization

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 weeks annual

| **Volume** | RTI estate across DCs/stores/carriers |
| **Owner** | SCM / DC Operations |
| **Participants** | SCM, DC Operations (links to VS-04), Store (links to VS-07), Logistics (links to VS-06), Master Data (links to VS-29), IT (links to VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SCM/IT establish RTI tracking: barcode/RFID tagging of totes/cages/stillages/IBCs (links to VS-29/VS-23), the RTI master and lifecycle states (in-DC, in-transit, at-store, at-customer, at-carrier, in-repair), and the tracking system (Oracle Transportation Execution/WMS links to VS-27); define the reconciliation cadence | SCM / IT / Master Data | SCM Manager | Setup + maintained |
| 2 | DC/Logistics/Store track RTI movement on every shipment/receipt (links to VS-04/VS-06/VS-07.3): scan in/out, capture custody (DC, carrier, store, customer), and update state; manage exceptions (unscanned, wrong-location, overdue) | DC / Logistics / Store | SCM Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | SCM conduct cycle reconciliation of RTI by type/location/custody vs the master (links to VS-05.1 cycle-count methodology), investigate variances (loss W3533, mis-scan, mis-placement), and recover mis-placed RTI (W3535); report RTI availability, utilization, and loss to analytics (W3540) and governance (W3544) | SCM / DC / Finance | SCM Manager | Weekly/monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- RTI master/RFID (links to VS-29/VS-23/VS-27); WMS (links to VS-04); Oracle Transportation Execution (links to VS-27); store/DC (links to VS-07.3/VS-04); cycle count (links to VS-05.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Tracking-blindspot risk**: Untracked RTI leads to loss and stockouts; mitigated by RFID/barcode and reconciliation
- **Custody-dispute risk**: Ambiguous custody causes loss disputes; mitigated by scan-in/out discipline


### Automation Opportunity

- System auto-scan of "scan in/out, capture custody" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "SCM conduct cycle reconciliation of RTI by type/location/custody vs" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-79 (ensure RTI & pallet asset reconciliation.)
- operational: RFID/barcode and reconciliation; operational: scan-in/out discipline

### Time Estimate
- Continuous + weekly/monthly
