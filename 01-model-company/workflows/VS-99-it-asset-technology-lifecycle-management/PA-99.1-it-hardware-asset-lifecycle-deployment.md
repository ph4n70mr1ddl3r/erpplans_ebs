## W3233. IT Asset Discovery, Inventory & CMDB Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous discovery; periodic reconciliation; audit |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + quarterly reconciliation |
| **Volume** | ~2,500+ endpoints + 600 POS + network/storage/peripherals |
| **Owner** | IT Asset Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Asset Mgr, IT Ops (links to VS-27), Field IT, Finance (links to VS-35), Security (links to VS-27.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Asset Mgr/IT Ops (links to VS-27.2) maintain discovery tools (agent-based and network scan) that automatically detect hardware/software on the network; capture device identity (hostname, serial, MAC), specs, location, user, status; load into CMDB as the single source of truth | IT Asset Mgr / IT Ops | IT Asset Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | Reconcile CMDB to procurement (links to VS-34), fixed-asset register (links to VS-35), and finance; identify discrepancies (ghost assets on books but missing, shadow/rogue devices on network but unbought/unknown); investigate and correct | IT Asset Mgr / Finance | IT Asset Manager | Quarterly |
| 3 | Audit physical existence at sample locations (stores/DCs/HQ) vs CMDB (links to VS-35.3 physical verification methodology); address rogue/unmanaged devices (quarantine per VS-27.3); report CMDB accuracy KPI to IT governance | IT Asset Mgr / Internal Audit (links to VS-21) / Field IT | IT Asset Manager | Annual + spot |

### System Touchpoints
- Discovery/CMDB (links to VS-27); fixed assets (links to VS-35); security (links to VS-27.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Shadow-IT risk**: Unmanaged devices bypass security and support; mitigated by network discovery and quarantine
- **Ghost-asset risk**: Paying maintenance/insurance on retired assets still on books; mitigated by CMDB–fixed-asset reconciliation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-scan of "IT Asset Mgr/IT Ops" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-reconcile of "Reconcile CMDB to procurement" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "Audit physical existence at sample locations" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-526 (ensure controlled execution — IT Hardware Asset Lifecycle & Deployment (PA-99.1).); CTL-143 (ensure IT asset & license entitlement integrity — cross-reference.)
- operational: network discovery and quarantine; operational: CMDB–fixed-asset reconciliation

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; 1–2 weeks/quarterly reconciliation

## W3234. Hardware Procurement, Standardization & Catalog Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New-store opening (links to VS-37); refresh (W3238); ad-hoc demand |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + project-based |
| **Volume** | ~600 POS + ~2,500 endpoints + peripherals; ~10–15 new stores/year |
| **Owner** | IT Asset Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Asset Mgr, Procurement (links to VS-34), IT Ops (links to VS-27), Finance, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Asset Mgr/IT Ops maintain the standard hardware catalog: approved makes/models per device class (POS terminal, scanner, mobile, laptop, network, server, printer), standard configurations, lifecycle/roadmap, total-cost-of- ownership basis; standardization reduces support complexity and spare-pool breadth | IT Asset Mgr / IT Ops | IT Asset Manager | Ongoing |
| 2 | Procurement/IT (links to VS-34) source via framework agreements / preferred vendors: negotiate price, warranty, lead time, RMA process, trade-in; consolidate demand for new-store openings (VS-37) and refresh waves (W3238); enforce catalog compliance (reject non-standard buys) | Procurement / IT Asset Mgr | IT Asset Manager | Per cycle |
| 3 | Receive, asset-tag, and register: receive per the PO (three-way match for capitalizable lines); capitalizable purchases flow through the mass additions queue for Fixed Asset review before posting to the register (links to VS-35 W1690); assign unique asset tag/serial, record in CMDB (W3233) and fixed-asset master (links to VS-35), capture warranty and PO reference; assign the custodian of record into the Assigned-To field — a named individual for IT equipment per VS-35 custody policy; the tag-scan confirmation doubles as that custodian's custody acceptance, so no device goes productive untagged or unowned; stage for provisioning (W3235) | IT Asset Mgr / Receiving | IT Asset Manager | Per delivery |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/PO (links to VS-34); CMDB (W3233); fixed assets (links to VS-35); receiving

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Non-standard-buy risk**: Off-catalog devices inflate support cost and break standardization; mitigated by catalog enforcement and approval gates
- **Lead-time risk**: Import lead time for hardware delays store openings (VS-37); mitigated by framework agreements and safety stock


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-scan of "scanner, mobile, laptop, network, server, printer" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "catalog compliance" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record in CMDB" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-526 (ensure controlled execution — IT Hardware Asset Lifecycle & Deployment (PA-99.1).)
- operational: catalog enforcement and approval gates; operational: framework agreements and safety stock

### Time Estimate
- Per procurement cycle; ongoing catalog maintenance

## W3235. Device Provisioning, Imaging & Deployment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New device (W3234); new store (VS-37); refresh (W3238) |
| **Frequency** | Project + ad-hoc |
| **Volume** | ~600 POS + ~2,500 endpoints deployed; ~10–15 new stores/year |
| **Owner** | IT Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Ops Mgr (links to VS-27), Field IT, Store Ops (links to VS-07), IT Asset Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Ops provision and image devices centrally: standard OS image, security baseline (links to VS-27.3), required applications (POS software, WMS, MDM enrollment, antivirus, monitoring agents), configuration per device role (cashier vs back-office vs DC RF); automate via imaging/zero-touch deployment | IT Ops / Desktop Support | IT Ops Manager | Per device |
| 2 | Deploy to site: ship to store/DC, Field IT or store staff install and connect (POS, scanner, printer, network), verify connectivity to ERP (links to VS-27.2), test offline POS capability (per POS-013 ≥8 hours offline); custody transfers as a release-and-accept event — the staging custodian releases the device and the site custodian of record accepts, and the deployment confirmation updates the asset register's Assigned-To field so custody is never blank in transit; capture deployment confirmation in CMDB | Field IT / Store Ops | IT Ops Manager | Per site |
| 3 | Onboard to support: register in monitoring/MDM, assign support owner, document site configuration; for new stores, integrate with VS-37 store-opening commissioning (W227 handover); confirm user acceptance — the site custodian of record's acceptance scan is the custody acceptance on the register (links to VS-35 W1692) | IT Ops / Store Ops | IT Ops Manager | Per deployment |

### System Touchpoints
- Imaging/MDM (links to VS-27); monitoring (links to VS-27.2); store opening (links to VS-37); CMDB (W3233)
- Asset register Assigned-To custodian field updates at each deployment — the release-and-accept custody chain riding the VS-35 register (links to VS-35 W1693)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Misconfiguration risk**: Inconsistent images cause support incidents; mitigated by standardized imaging and automation
- **Site-readiness risk**: Store not ready (network/power) delays deployment; mitigated by VS-37 site-readiness checklist
- **Custody-gap risk**: devices in transit between staging and site drift unowned; mitigated by the release-and-accept custody event updating Assigned-To at each deployment


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "IT Ops provision and image devices centrally" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-scan of "scanner, printer, network" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document site configuration" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-526 (ensure controlled execution — IT Hardware Asset Lifecycle & Deployment (PA-99.1).)
- operational: standardized imaging and automation; operational: VS-37 site-readiness checklist

### Time Estimate
- 1–4 hours per device; 1–3 days per new-store deployment

## W3237. Asset Maintenance, Break/Fix & Spare Pool Management

| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~thousands of break/fix tickets/year |
| **Owner** | IT Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Ops Mgr, Service Desk (links to VS-27.1), Field IT, Vendor/Warranty, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Service Desk (links to VS-27.1) triage break/fix: diagnose (remote first), attempt repair, escalate to Field IT or vendor where needed; for store/DC critical devices (POS, RF), prioritize and swap from spare pool to restore service rapidly | Service Desk / Field IT | IT Ops Manager | Per ticket |
| 2 | Manage vendor warranty/RMA: log warranty status per asset (W3233), initiate RMA for covered failures, track turnaround, manage out-of-warranty repair/replacement decisions (repair vs replace); manage extended-warranty contracts | IT Asset Mgr / Vendor | IT Asset Manager | Per ticket |

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Warranty-leakage risk**: Paying for repairs covered by warranty; mitigated by warranty tracking and RMA discipline

## W3238. Technology Refresh, Replacement Cycle & Rollout

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Lifecycle/end-of-support; performance/obsolescence; security/vulnerability (W3250); new capability |
| **Frequency** | Annual plan + project waves |
| **Volume** | ~3–5 year refresh cycles; ~600 POS + ~2,500 endpoints |
| **Owner** | IT Asset Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Asset Mgr, IT Ops (links to VS-27), Finance (links to VS-40/VS-35), Procurement (links to VS-34), Store Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Asset Mgr/Finance build refresh plan: device lifecycle policy by class (POS 5–7 yr, laptop 3–4 yr, server 4–5 yr, network 5–7 yr), vendor end-of-support dates (W3250), performance/TCO analysis (W3251), capex budget (links to VS-40); prioritize by risk and ROI | IT Asset Mgr / Finance | IT Asset Manager | Annual |
| 2 | Execute refresh wave: procure (W3234), provision/deploy (W3235) with minimal operational disruption (after-hours, phased by region), recover and retire old devices (W3239) — recovered devices re-custody to the IT Asset Manager's spare pool as a release-and-accept event on the asset register's Assigned-To field (links to VS-35 W1693), update CMDB and fixed assets (links to VS-35); manage trade-in/resale value | IT Ops / Field IT / Procurement | IT Ops Manager | Per wave |
| 3 | Manage disposal accounting (links to VS-35.3): derecognize retired assets, gain/loss on disposal, transfer to new assets; capture lessons and update refresh policy; report refresh KPI (cycle adherence, residual value) | IT Asset Mgr / Finance | IT Asset Manager | Per wave |

### System Touchpoints
- Capex/project (links to VS-40); fixed assets (links to VS-35); CMDB (W3233); procurement (links to VS-34)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Refresh-deferral risk**: Running unsupported/vulnerable devices (links to W3250); mitigated by lifecycle policy and budget
- **Disruption risk**: Refresh during peak disrupts operations; mitigated by after-hours/phased rollout

### Controls
- CTL-526 (ensure controlled execution — IT Hardware Asset Lifecycle & Deployment (PA-99.1).)

## W3239. Asset Retirement, Data Sanitization & E-Waste Disposal

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Refresh (W3238); damage beyond repair; lease return; site closure (links to VS-59) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + project waves |
| **Volume** | Hundreds–thousands of retirements/year |
| **Owner** | IT Asset Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Asset Mgr, Security (links to VS-27.3/VS-91), Finance (links to VS-35), Sustainability (links to VS-73/VS-25), Disposal Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Asset Mgr/Security retire devices: revoke access/deactivate (MDM, AD, apps per VS-27.3), perform certified data sanitization (NIST 800-88 / cryptographic erase for SSDs) to ensure no residual BuildRight or customer data (Data Privacy Act RA 10173 compliance, links to VS-91); document sanitization certificate per asset | IT Asset Mgr / Security | IT Asset Manager | Per asset |
| 2 | Decide disposition: redeploy internally (cascade to lower-tier use), resell (trade-in, secondary market — recover value), donate (CSR per VS-25, with sanitization), recycle/dispose as e-waste (DENR-accredited treatment/storage/disposal — TSD facility per VS-73.2 hazardous/e-waste compliance); ensure no data-bearing media leaves unsanitized | IT Asset Mgr / Sustainability | IT Asset Manager | Per asset |
| 3 | Reconcile retirement: derecognize from CMDB (W3233) and fixed assets (links to VS-35.3), record gain/loss/disposal cost, retain sanitization/disposal certificates (links to VS-88) for audit and privacy compliance (links to VS-91); report e-waste and recovery metrics to sustainability (links to VS-25/VS-73) | IT Asset Mgr / Finance | IT Asset Manager | Per asset |

### System Touchpoints
- Security/identity (links to VS-27.3); privacy (links to VS-91); fixed assets (links to VS-35); records (links to VS-88); sustainability (links to VS-73/VS-25)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-breach risk**: Residual data on retired devices (links to VS-91 NPC breach notification); mitigated by certified sanitization and certificates
- **Environmental-compliance risk**: Improper e-waste disposal violates DENR rules (links to VS-73.2); mitigated by accredited TSD vendor and documentation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "document sanitization certificate per asset" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "record gain/loss/disposal cost, retain sanitization/disposal certificates" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-526 (ensure controlled execution — IT Hardware Asset Lifecycle & Deployment (PA-99.1).)
- operational: certified sanitization and certificates; operational: accredited TSD vendor and documentation

### Time Estimate
- Per asset; 30 min–2 hours including documentation

## W3240. Lost / Stolen Asset Recovery & Loss Investigation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Loss report; inventory discrepancy (W3233); theft/anomaly |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~50–200 loss events/year |
| **Owner** | IT Asset Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Asset Mgr, Loss Prevention (links to VS-23), Security (links to VS-27.3), Legal, Insurance (links to VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On loss/theft: immediate containment — remote-wipe/lock via MDM (W3236), deactivate credentials and access (links to VS-27.3), report to local police (for theft) and Loss Prevention (links to VS-23); preserve evidence (last-known-location, access logs, CCTV) | IT Ops / Loss Prevention / Security | IT Asset Manager | Per event (immediate) |
| 2 | Investigate: Loss Prevention (links to VS-23.1) determines cause/theft/fraud, interviews, reviews CCTV and access data; for high-value or data-bearing devices, assess data-breach risk (links to VS-91) and notify NPC if personal data compromised within 72 hours | Loss Prevention / Security / Legal | IT Asset Manager | Per event |
| 3 | Recover/resolve: recover device where possible, file insurance claim (links to VS-26.3) for unrecovered/insured assets, derecognize from CMDB/fixed assets (links to VS-35.3), replace (W3234/W3237); tighten controls (accountability, tracking, physical security per VS-23.2); capture lessons to risk register (links to VS-21.2) | IT Asset Mgr / Loss Prevention / Finance | IT Asset Manager | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- MDM (links to VS-27); loss prevention (links to VS-23); security (links to VS-27.3); privacy (links to VS-91); insurance (links to VS-26)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data-compromise risk**: Lost device with customer/employee data triggers RA 10173 breach notification (links to VS-91); mitigated by encryption, remote-wipe, and breach playbook
- **Asset-leakage risk**: Chronic untracked loss; mitigated by accountability and analytics (W3251)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "preserve evidence" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews CCTV and access data" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-143 (ensure IT asset & license entitlement integrity.)
- operational: encryption, remote-wipe, and breach playbook; operational: accountability and analytics (W3251)

### Time Estimate
- Per event; days for investigation
