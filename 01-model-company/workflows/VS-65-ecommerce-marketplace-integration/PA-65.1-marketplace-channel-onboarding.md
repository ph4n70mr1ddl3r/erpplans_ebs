## W2406. Marketplace Platform Selection & Registration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategic decision to add marketplace channel |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; 1–2 new platforms/year |
| **Volume** | 1–2 platforms/year |
| **Owner** | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. |
| **Participants** | Ecommerce Dir, Legal, Finance, VP Marketing |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. evaluates marketplace: audience fit (home improvement/DIY buyers), commission structure (5–15%), logistics integration, competition, brand control; presents recommendation to VP Marketing | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | VP Marketing | 5–10 days |
| 2 | Legal reviews marketplace seller agreement: terms, IP protection, dispute resolution, data sharing; Finance sets up financial controls; IT begins integration planning | Legal / Finance / IT | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | 5–10 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Commission-drift risk**: the 5–15% commission evaluated at platform selection is re-tierable by the marketplace after onboarding, silently degrading channel margin; mitigated by the seller-agreement change-notice terms secured in the legal review (Step 2) and the W2423 monthly effective-commission recomputation
- **Channel-exit risk**: seller-agreement terms on data sharing, IP and dispute resolution accepted at registration constrain a later sunset; mitigated by the legal and finance review gating signature (Step 2) and the W2429 annual review's expand/maintain/sunset decision

### Automation Opportunity
- Process steps auto-sequenced with system controls; data-entry auto-populated from master data; exceptions auto-flagged for human review

### Controls
- CTL-424 (ensure controlled execution — Marketplace Channel Onboarding & Configuration (PA-65.1).)
- operational: GM, Digital Commerce Inc. sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 days per occurrence
- Volume: 1–2 platforms/year
- Frequency: Ad-hoc; 1–2 new platforms/year
- Absorbed into the GM, Digital Commerce Inc. role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2408. Marketplace Catalog & Pricing Configuration

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store setup complete; catalog upload |
| **Frequency** | Initial setup + ongoing catalog updates |
| **Volume** | ~5,000–10,000 SKUs per marketplace |
| **Owner** | Ecommerce Catalog Manager |
| **Participants** | Catalog Mgr, Pricing Analyst, IT Integration |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Catalog Manager selects SKUs for marketplace: excludes consignment, heavy/bulky items (unless marketplace supports), low-margin items; creates marketplace-specific listings with optimized titles, descriptions, images | Catalog Manager | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | 5–10 days initial |
| 2 | Pricing Analyst sets marketplace prices: BuildRight price + marketplace commission adjustment; competitive pricing vs. marketplace competitors; configures shipping fee structure | Pricing Analyst | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | 2–3 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
- Inventory/ATP (links to VS-05 / W105 multi-channel allocation, VS-29 / W252 item master)
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Commission-math risk**: marketplace prices must carry the commission adjustment on top of BuildRight price; publishing the unadjusted price shifts the commission onto margin on every order; mitigated by the commission-adjusted price build (Step 2) and the CTL-200 marketplace order & settlement reconciliation flagging net-realized-vs-listed gaps
- **Assortment-leakage risk**: excluded categories (consignment, heavy/bulky, low-margin) re-enter the marketplace assortment through bulk listing uploads; mitigated by the SKU-selection exclusion filters (Step 1) and the W2419 listing-quality audit

### Automation Opportunity
- API-driven real-time sync (catalog, inventory, pricing, orders) eliminating manual data entry; auto-reconciliation of marketplace settlement vs. BuildRight records

### Controls
- CTL-200 (ensure marketplace order & settlement reconciliation.)
- CTL-424 (price-change authorization — links to VS-08 / W40); Operational: Ecommerce Catalog Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~5–10 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~5,000–10,000 SKUs per marketplace
- Frequency: Initial setup + ongoing catalog updates
- Absorbed into the Ecommerce Catalog Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2412. Marketplace Inventory Allocation Strategy

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store live with orders flowing |
| **Frequency** | Monthly review; weekly adjustments |
| **Volume** | ~5,000–10,000 SKUs per marketplace |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Planner |
| **Participants** | Merch Planner, Ecommerce Dir, Inventory Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merch Planner determines inventory allocation: how much stock reserved for marketplace vs. own ecommerce vs. stores; prevents stock-outs across channels; adjusts weekly based on sell-through | Merchandising Planner | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | 2–3 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
- Inventory/ATP (links to VS-05 / W105 multi-channel allocation, VS-29 / W252 item master)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Allocation-starvation risk**: marketplace share set too low strands sellable stock in stores while the listing stocks out; set too high it starves own-channel availability; mitigated by the weekly sell-through rebalance (Step 1) and W2420's delist-before-stock-out trigger
- **Double-promise risk**: an allocation reservation not honored by the fulfilling source promises the same unit to two channels; mitigated by the W2414 routing reserving inventory at order download and the weekly allocation review

### Automation Opportunity
- API-driven real-time sync (catalog, inventory, pricing, orders) eliminating manual data entry; auto-reconciliation of marketplace settlement vs. BuildRight records

### Controls
- CTL-424 (ensure controlled execution — Marketplace Channel Onboarding & Configuration (PA-65.1).)
- operational: inventory-count integrity; Operational: Merchandising Planner sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~5,000–10,000 SKUs per marketplace
- Frequency: Monthly review; weekly adjustments
- Absorbed into the Merchandising Planner role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
