## W2422. Marketplace Financial Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly settlement cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; 1–2 platforms |
| **Volume** | ~1–2 reconciliations/month |
| **Owner** | Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Analyst, Ecommerce Dir, Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Analyst reconciles: marketplace settlement report vs. BuildRight order records; verifies: gross sales, marketplace commission, shipping fee deductions, promotional subsidies, refunds, net settlement | Finance Analyst | Finance Manager | 4–6 hours/month |
| 2 | Disputes discrepancies with marketplace (missing orders, incorrect commissions, unauthorized refunds); resolves within marketplace dispute window (typically 30 days) | Finance Analyst | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | 2–3 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Deduction-acceptance risk**: settlement deductions (commissions, subsidies, refunds) accepted without a line-level match quietly become the channel's standing cost; mitigated by the line-level verification (Step 1: gross sales, commission, shipping, subsidies, refunds, net) and Step 2's dispute inside the 30-day window
- **Dispute-expiry risk**: discrepancies raised late miss the marketplace dispute window and are written off; mitigated by the monthly reconciliation cadence (Step 1) keeping every dispute inside the window

### Automation Opportunity
- Process steps auto-sequenced with system controls; data-entry auto-populated from master data; exceptions auto-flagged for human review

### Controls
- CTL-426 (ensure controlled execution — Marketplace Performance & Settlement (PA-65.3).)
- operational: Finance Analyst sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~1–2 reconciliations/month
- Frequency: Monthly; 1–2 platforms
- Absorbed into the Finance Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dashboard: orders, revenue, AOV, conversion rate, top SKUs, customer ratings, return rate, stock-out rate; daily review for anomalies; monthly deep analysis with optimization recommendations | System / GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | VP Marketing | 30 min/day + 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113 BI platform, W231 QBR dashboard)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric-normalization risk**: a dashboard consumed as daily routine stops flagging anomalies once they normalize into the numbers; mitigated by the daily anomaly review plus the monthly deep analysis with optimization recommendations (Step 1)
- **Definition-drift risk**: AOV or conversion computed differently from the W2413 baseline makes trends unreadable; mitigated by the dashboard carrying the baseline's own metric set (orders, AOV, conversion, ratings, return rate, stock-out rate)

### Automation Opportunity
- Dashboard auto-refreshed from source systems; anomalies auto-flagged; report auto-distributed to stakeholders

### Controls
- CTL-426 (ensure controlled execution — Marketplace Performance & Settlement (PA-65.3).)
- operational: GM, Digital Commerce Inc. sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~30 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~5K–10K orders/month
- Frequency: Daily; 1–2 platforms
- Absorbed into the GM, Digital Commerce Inc. role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

| 1 | Compares: revenue, margin, customer acquisition cost, AOV, conversion rate, return rate by channel; identifies which products/categories perform better on marketplace vs. own site; recommends channel-specific assortment | Analytics Manager | VP Marketing | 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
