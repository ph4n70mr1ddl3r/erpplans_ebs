## W3633. DTI Weights & Measures Compliance & Inspection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Statutory verification cycle (W3625/W3626/W3629); DTI inspection; new legal-for-trade device |
| **Frequency** | Statutory cycle + on inspection |
| **Volume** | Legal-for-trade measuring devices across 200 stores + 4 DCs |
| **Owner** | Metrology Manager / Compliance |
| **Participants** | Metrology Manager, Compliance/Regulatory (links to VS-22), Quality (links to VS-31), Store/DC Operations, DTI-BPS Inspector/Authorized Verifier, Legal (links to VS-100.3) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Metrology Manager/Compliance maintain the legal-for-trade device register and statutory calendar: every POS scale, catch-weight scale, and weighbridge used in commerce, the verification/seal expiry, the DTI RDO jurisdiction, and the authorized verifier; ensure all commerce devices are verified/sealed (Consumer Act RA 7394, Fair Trade Law) | Metrology Manager / Compliance | Quality Manager / VP Legal | Maintained |
| 2 | Metrology Manager/Compliance coordinate statutory verification by DTI or an authorized verifier: schedule, witness/perform verification, secure the seal/sticker and certificate, record in the register (links to W3620/W3624), and manage re-verification on device change/move/repair; manage DTI inspections and findings | Metrology Manager / Compliance / Verifier | Quality Manager / VP Legal | Per cycle + inspection |
| 3 | Metrology Manager/Compliance manage DTI inspection response and findings (links to VS-22/W77-style regulatory response): non-compliance (unsealed/expired device, tampered seal), corrective action, penalty management, and consumer-complaint defense; report DTI weights & measures compliance to governance (W3640) and audit (links to VS-21) | Metrology Manager / Compliance / Legal | Quality Manager / VP Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Device register (links to W3620); records (links to W3624); compliance (links to VS-22); legal (links to VS-100.3); audit (links to VS-21); analytics (links to W3640)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unsealed-device risk**: Operating a commerce device without a valid DTI seal attracts penalty and consumer complaint; mitigated by statutory calendar
- **Inspection-finding risk**: DTI findings not remediated escalate; mitigated by closure tracking


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Metrology Manager/Compliance maintain the legal-for-trade device register and" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "record in the register" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-inspect of "inspection response and findings" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-145 (ensure measurement traceability & weights-and-measures compliance.)
- operational: statutory calendar; operational: closure tracking

### Time Estimate
- Per cycle + continuous

## W3634. Out-of-Tolerance Investigation & Measurement Correction

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | OOT detected during calibration (W3622/W3625–W3632); customer/inventory discrepancy |
| **Frequency** | Per OOT event |
| **Volume** | OOT events across the device estate |
| **Owner** | Metrology Manager |
| **Participants** | Metrology Manager, Quality (links to VS-31), Device Owners (Stores/DC/Services), Finance (links to VS-17), Compliance (links to VS-22), Audit (links to VS-21), IT (links to VS-27) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Metrology Manager quarantine the OOT device: remove from legal-for-trade/commerce service, label out-of-service, swap with a calibrated spare (links to W3636), and prevent further use until corrected; preserve as-found state and notify the device owner and quality | Metrology Manager / Device Owner | Quality Manager | Immediate |
| 2 | Metrology Manager investigate the OOT root cause: device failure, drift, damage, environmental, misuse, or standard error (links to W3618); assess the measurement impact window (since last-known-good calibration) and the population of affected transactions/records (revenue, inventory, quality acceptances links to W3635) | Metrology Manager / Quality / Audit | Quality Manager | Per OOT |
| 3 | Metrology Manager/Finance manage the measurement correction: correct/recalibrate or repair the device (W3636), assess and process revenue/AR corrections for under/over-charge (links to VS-16/VS-17), inventory corrections (links to VS-05), and quality re-inspection; document the OOT and correction (W3624), and feed prevention to intervals/training/device design (W3619/W3623); report to governance (W3640) | Metrology Manager / Finance / Quality | Quality Manager / CFO | Per OOT |

### System Touchpoints
- Device registry (links to W3620); records (links to W3624); AR/revenue (links to VS-16/VS-17); inventory (links to VS-05); quality (links to VS-31); analytics (links to W3635/W3640)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Silent-OOT risk**: OOT device stays in service leaking revenue/causing errors; mitigated by calibration cadence and variance monitoring (W3635)
- **Under-correction risk**: OOT impact on past transactions not assessed/remediated; mitigated by impact-window analysis


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Metrology Manager quarantine the OOT device" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "Metrology Manager investigate the OOT root cause" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document the OOT and correction" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-576 (ensure controlled execution — Weights & Measures Compliance, MRM & Measurement Analytics (PA-115.3).)
- operational: calibration cadence and variance monitoring (W3635); operational: impact-window analysis

### Time Estimate
- Per OOT

## W3635. Measurement Variance, Revenue Impact & Loss Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly analytics cycle; OOT (W3634); variance threshold |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Measurement-variance and revenue-impact data |
| **Owner** | Metrology Manager / BI-Finance |
| **Participants** | Metrology Manager, BI (links to VS-28.1), Finance/FP&A (links to VS-17.4), Quality (links to VS-31), Loss Prevention (links to VS-23), Store/DC Operations, ExCo (links to VS-33.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Metrology Manager/BI instrument measurement-variance metrics: scale-transaction variance (expected vs. actual weight/charge on catch-weight), cut-length variance/yield, paint-dose variance, weighbridge inbound/outbound variance, fuel variance (links to VS-61), and inventory-count variance attributable to measurement; identify outliers and drift | Metrology Manager / BI | Quality Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | Metrology Manager/Finance quantify the revenue/cost impact: under-charge revenue leakage on catch-weight (POS scale reading low), over-issue material/yield loss (cutting/paint/dispensing), inventory inaccuracy cost (links to VS-05), and quality cost (false accept/reject); model the value-at-risk by device class | Metrology Manager / Finance / BI | Quality Manager / CFO | Quarterly |
| 3 | Metrology Manager publish the measurement-variance and impact analytics to executives (links to VS-33.2) and governance (W3640), feed high-impact devices to calibration priority and OOT investigation (W3634), and track reduction over time; coordinate with Loss Prevention on measurement-driven shrink (links to VS-23) | Metrology Manager / BI / Finance | Quality Manager / COO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- BI (links to VS-28.1); POS/transaction (links to VS-08); inventory (links to VS-05); finance (links to VS-17.4); LP (links to VS-23); analytics (links to W3640)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden-leakage risk**: Revenue leakage from measurement drift hidden in margins; mitigated by variance analytics and impact modeling
- **Analytics-inaction risk**: Variance known but not prioritized; mitigated by value-at-risk ranking


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Metrology Manager/BI instrument measurement-variance metrics" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-issue of "issue material/yield loss" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track reduction over time" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-576 (ensure controlled execution — Weights & Measures Compliance, MRM & Measurement Analytics (PA-115.3).)
- operational: variance analytics and impact modeling; operational: value-at-risk ranking

### Time Estimate
- Monthly + quarterly

## W3638. Measurement System Analysis (MSA) & Gage R&R

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New/critical measurement process; quality/acceptance concern; OOT trend |
| **Frequency** | Per measurement process + periodic |
| **Volume** | Critical measurement systems (quality, acceptance, cut-length, dispensing) |
| **Owner** | Metrology Manager / Quality |
| **Participants** | Metrology Manager, Quality (links to VS-31), Calibration Technicians (W3622), Device Operators, Process Owners, Statistics/BI (links to VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Metrology Manager/Quality conduct Measurement System Analysis (MSA) on critical measurement systems: bias, linearity, stability, and repeatability & reproducibility (Gage R&R — the variation from the measurement system vs. the process); identify whether the measurement system is fit for acceptance decisions (% study variation, ndc) | Metrology Manager / Quality / Statistics | Quality Manager | Per process |
| 2 | Metrology Manager/Quality act on MSA findings: if the measurement system contributes excessive variation, address device (calibration/repair W3634), method (procedure), operator (training W3623), environment, or standard; re-run MSA to confirm improvement | Metrology Manager / Quality / Technicians | Quality Manager | Per process |
| 3 | Metrology Manager integrate MSA into the quality/acceptance design (links to VS-31): ensure measurement systems underlying product acceptance, cut-length charge, and dispensing are validated for their decision; maintain MSA records (W3624) and report to governance (W3640) and quality audit (links to VS-31/VS-21) | Metrology Manager / Quality | Quality Manager | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Quality (links to VS-31); calibration (links to W3622); training (links to W3623); records (links to W3624); analytics (links to VS-28/W3640)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unfit-measurement-system risk**: Measurement variation larger than tolerance produces wrong decisions; mitigated by MSA and Gage R&R
- **MSA-neglect risk**: MSA done once and never refreshed; mitigated by periodic re-validation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "conduct Measurement System Analysis" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-measure of "measurement system contributes excessive variation, address device" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "integrate MSA into the quality/acceptance design" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-576 (ensure controlled execution — Weights & Measures Compliance, MRM & Measurement Analytics (PA-115.3).)
- operational: MSA and Gage R&R; operational: periodic re-validation

### Time Estimate
- Per process + periodic

| **Volume** | Metrology program and landscape |
| **Owner** | Metrology Manager |
| **Participants** | Metrology Manager, Quality (links to VS-31), Audit (links to VS-21), Innovation (links to VS-30), IT (links to VS-27), Finance (links to VS-17.4), ExCo (links to VS-33.2), Compliance (links to VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Metrology Manager/Audit assess metrology maturity: program governance (W3617), traceability integrity (W3618), interval optimization (W3619), registry completeness (W3620), calibration currency/OOT rate (W3622/W3634), MSA coverage (W3638), regulatory compliance (W3633), cost-effectiveness (W3637), and analytics maturity (W3635); benchmark to good practice | Metrology Manager / Audit | Quality Manager | Annual |

### Controls
- CTL-576 (ensure controlled execution — Weights & Measures Compliance, MRM & Measurement Analytics (PA-115.3).)
