## W3617. Calibration & Metrology Program Strategy & Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual quality/compliance planning (links to VS-31/VS-22); regulatory cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | Enterprise measurement-device estate |
| **Owner** | Metrology Manager / Quality |
| **Participants** | Metrology Manager, Quality (links to VS-31), Compliance (links to VS-22), IT (links to VS-27), Store Ops (links to VS-07), DC Operations (links to VS-04), Finance (links to VS-17.4), HSE (links to VS-24), Audit (links to VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Metrology Manager/Quality define the calibration program strategy and governance: scope (POS/catch-weight scales, cutting equipment, paint dispensers, DC weighbridges/truck scales, fuel/logistics meters, environmental/process instruments, test & measurement tools), the regulatory map (DTI-BPS weights & measures, Consumer Act RA 7394, ISO 17025 for labs), the operating model (central standards lab + field/in-house calibration + external labs), and the KPIs (calibration currency, OOT rate, measurement variance, audit findings) | Metrology Manager / Quality | Quality Manager / COO | Annual |
| 2 | Metrology Manager establish the metrology governance: Metrology Manager role/authority, the cross-functional responsibilities (Stores/DC own the device, Metrology owns calibration, Quality owns compliance), the calibration policy, the device-classification scheme (legal-for-trade vs. process-control), and integration to quality (VS-31), facilities (VS-07.2/VS-20.3), and master data (VS-29) | Metrology Manager / Quality / Compliance | Quality Manager | Annual |
| 3 | Quality Manager/COO approve the calibration program strategy and policy, ratify governance/authorities, allocate the program budget (links to VS-34/VS-17.4), and report metrology posture to the quality/compliance governance (links to VS-31/VS-22) and audit (links to VS-21); communicate the mandate | Quality Manager / COO | CEO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Quality (links to VS-31); compliance (links to VS-22); facilities (links to VS-07.2/VS-20.3); master data (links to VS-29); finance (links to VS-34/VS-17.4); audit (links to VS-21); IT/CMMS (links to VS-27/VS-20.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fragmented-ownership risk**: Calibration split across stores/DC/facilities/IT with no single owner; mitigated by Metrology authority
- **Under-resourcing risk**: Calibration under-funded until an audit finding or revenue loss; mitigated by quantified exposure and governance reporting


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Metrology Manager/Quality define the calibration program strategy and governance" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approve the calibration program strategy and policy, ratify…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-145 (ensure measurement traceability & weights-and-measures compliance.)
- operational: Metrology authority; operational: quantified exposure and governance reporting

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 weeks annual

## W3618. Measurement Standards, Traceability & ISO 17025 Alignment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program strategy (W3617); standards/traceability requirement; audit |
| **Frequency** | Maintained + per standard cycle |
| **Volume** | Enterprise measurement standards chain |
| **Owner** | Metrology Manager |
| **Participants** | Metrology Manager, Quality (links to VS-31), External Calibration Labs (W3621), National Metrology Laboratory (NML-ITDI, DOST), Compliance (links to VS-22), Standards Lab (W3622) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Metrology Manager establish the measurement-traceability chain: each working standard and field device traces through reference/working standards in the standards lab (W3622) to a national standard (the National Metrology Laboratory — NML under DOST-ITDI — or another recognized National Metrology Institute), with documented uncertainty per the GUM (Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement); maintain the traceability hierarchy | Metrology Manager / Standards Lab | Quality Manager | Maintained |
| 2 | Metrology Manager/Quality align the in-house calibration function to ISO/IEC 17025 (general requirements for the competence of calibration/testing laboratories) where BuildRight operates an in-house standards lab (W3622): quality system, documented procedures, uncertainty budgets, environmental control, equipment, and personnel competency; pursue accreditation where it adds value | Metrology Manager / Quality | Quality Manager | Maintained |
| 3 | Metrology Manager maintain the reference/working standards (calibrated against national/recognized standards on cycle), manage standards custody/handling, and reconcile the traceability chain for audit (links to VS-31/VS-21/W3624); report traceability integrity to governance (W3640) | Metrology Manager / Standards Lab | Quality Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Standards lab (links to W3622); quality/ISO (links to VS-31); external labs (links to W3621); compliance (links to VS-22); records (links to W3624)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Broken-traceability risk**: A standard calibrated to an un-traceable source invalidates all downstream calibrations; mitigated by traceability chain and accredited labs
- **Uncertainty-omission risk**: Calibration without uncertainty is incomplete; mitigated by GUM-compliant uncertainty budgets


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "Metrology Manager establish the measurement-traceability chain" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documented procedures, uncertainty budgets, environmental control, equipment,…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile the traceability chain for audit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-574 (ensure controlled execution — Calibration Program, Standards & Measurement Traceability (PA-115.1).)
- operational: traceability chain and accredited labs; operational: GUM-compliant uncertainty budgets

### Time Estimate
- Maintained + continuous

## W3619. Calibration Intervals, Tolerance & MRA Methodology

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program strategy (W3617); device-class definition; OOT trend (W3634) |
| **Frequency** | Maintained + periodic optimization |
| **Volume** | Calibration intervals and tolerances by device class |
| **Owner** | Metrology Manager |
| **Participants** | Metrology Manager, Quality (links to VS-31), Device Owners (Stores/DC/Services/Facilities), Compliance (links to VS-22), Standards Lab (W3622) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Metrology Manager define calibration intervals by device class and criticality: legal-for-trade POS scales (statutory interval — typically annual DTI verification), cutting/paint dispensers (usage-based or time-based), DC weighbridges (statutory + usage), fuel meters (statutory), environmental instruments (time-based), test & measurement tools (usage-based); align to regulation, manufacturer, and usage | Metrology Manager / Quality | Quality Manager | Maintained |
| 2 | Metrology Manager define tolerances (acceptance criteria) and maximum-permissible-error (MPE) by device class and measurement: align to legal-for-trade MPE (DTI), process-control need (revenue/quality impact), and the OOT-investigation thresholds (W3634); document the tolerance methodology | Metrology Manager / Quality | Quality Manager | Maintained |
| 3 | Metrology Manager optimize intervals using calibration history and OOT data (W3634/W3635): extend intervals for stable devices, shorten for drift-prone ones (reliability-centered calibration / OOT-based interval adjustment); apply measurement-risk-assessment (MRA) to set economically-optimal intervals; report optimization to governance (W3640) | Metrology Manager / Quality | Quality Manager | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Device registry (links to W3620/VS-29); calibration history (links to W3624/W3635); OOT (links to W3634); quality (links to VS-31); analytics (links to W3640)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fixed-interval-inefficiency risk**: Over- or under-calibrating due to static intervals; mitigated by OOT-based optimization
- **Tolerance-mis-set risk**: Tolerances too tight (waste) or too loose (revenue/quality loss); mitigated by MPE/impact-based methodology


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "define calibration intervals by device class and criticality" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "document the tolerance methodology" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-capture of "OOT-based interval adjustment" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-574 (ensure controlled execution — Calibration Program, Standards & Measurement Traceability (PA-115.1).)
- operational: OOT-based optimization; operational: MPE/impact-based methodology

### Time Estimate
- Maintained + periodic

| 2 | Metrology Manager/Master Data maintain the calibration attributes per device: interval (W3619), tolerance/MPE (W3619), last/next calibration date, calibration method (in-house W3622 vs. external W3621), standards used (traceability W3618), status (due/overdue/OOT/in-service/out-of-service), and the calibration history link (W3624) | Metrology Manager / Master Data | Metrology Manager | Per device + maintained |

### System Touchpoints
- Item/device master (links to VS-29); fixed asset/ITAM (links to VS-35/VS-99); Oracle eAM as the CMMS-of-record — device asset register, PM/calibration scheduling and due-date alerts (links to VS-20.3/VS-27; fit-gap F5/G9); records (links to W3624)

- System auto-record of "record, and report calibration" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-145 (ensure measurement traceability & weights-and-measures compliance.)
- operational: acquisition gate and discovery; operational: system-driven scheduling

## W3621. External Calibration Lab Qualification & Vendor Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Calibration outsourcing decision; lab sourcing; lab performance review |
| **Frequency** | Per lab + ongoing |
| **Volume** | External calibration labs (accredited) |
| **Owner** | Metrology Manager / Procurement |
| **Participants** | Metrology Manager, Procurement (links to VS-34), Quality (links to VS-31), Compliance (links to VS-22), Vendor Mgmt (links to VS-03), Finance (links to VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Metrology Manager/Procurement define external-lab qualification criteria: ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation scope (the lab must be accredited for the specific calibration), traceability to national/international standards (W3618), uncertainty capability, turnaround, geographic coverage (on-site for weighbridges/large devices), and accreditation-body recognition (PAB or ILAC-MRA signatory) | Metrology Manager / Procurement / Quality | Quality Manager | Per sourcing |
| 2 | Metrology Manager/Procurement qualify and contract external labs: due diligence (accreditation certificate/scope verification), contract (scope, turnaround, uncertainty, recall/OOT notification, liability), rate/SLA, and the approved-lab list; integrate to vendor management (links to VS-03) and the scorecard (links to VS-67) | Metrology Manager / Procurement / Vendor Mgmt | Quality Manager / VP Supply Chain | Per lab |
| 3 | Metrology Manager manage external-lab performance: monitor turnaround, quality (calibration acceptance, OOT notification), accreditation currency, and cost; re-qualify periodically, manage the approved lab list, and route devices to the optimal lab (in-house vs. external); report to governance (W3640) | Metrology Manager | Quality Manager | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/vendor (links to VS-34/VS-03); quality/ISO (links to VS-31); vendor scorecard (links to VS-67); device registry (links to W3620); records (links to W3624)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Non-accredited-lab risk**: Lab without proper ISO 17025 scope invalidates calibration; mitigated by accreditation-scope verification
- **Turnaround risk**: Lab backlog takes devices out of service; mitigated by SLA and dual-sourcing

- System rule-based auto-approval of "Metrology Manager/Procurement qualify and contract external labs" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-route of "route devices to the optimal lab" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-574 (ensure controlled execution — Calibration Program, Standards & Measurement Traceability (PA-115.1).)
- operational: accreditation-scope verification; operational: SLA and dual-sourcing

### Time Estimate
- Per lab + ongoing

| 2 | Calibration Technicians perform calibration per documented procedure and tolerance (W3619): pre-check, as-found verification, adjustment, as-left verification, uncertainty estimation, pass/fail/OOT determination (links to W3634), labeling/sealing (legal-for-trade seals), and the calibration certificate/record (W3624); return to service or quarantine if OOT | Calibration Technicians | Metrology Manager | Per device |
