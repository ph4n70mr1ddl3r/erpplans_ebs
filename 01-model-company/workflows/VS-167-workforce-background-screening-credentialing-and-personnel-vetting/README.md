# VS-167: Workforce Background Screening, Credentialing & Personnel Vetting

> **People** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Unified workforce background-screening, credentialing & personnel-vetting workflows for
BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **enterprise-wide cross-category discipline** that screens,
verifies, and continuously monitors the trustworthiness and credentials of every individual
granted access to BuildRight's people, assets, cash, data, and customers — across **employees**
(~6,932 + ~1,200–1,600 hires/yr), **contingent/outsourced workers** (~10–20% of store/DC labor),
**vendor/3PL personnel with site access** (delivery drivers, service technicians, promodizers),
and **executives/sensitive roles** — through a centralized screening program, role-based
screening packages, screening-vendor governance, consent/privacy (RA 10173) compliance,
adverse-action/fair-chance due process, and ongoing re-screening.

This matters at BuildRight's scale and risk profile. The group onboards ~1,200–1,600 hires/yr at
15–20% turnover, runs ~10–20% contingent labor (security guards, janitorial, promodizers,
agency labor per VS-98), has ~800–1,000 vendors plus 3PL/delivery partners (VS-56/VS-110) whose
personnel enter sites and handle goods, and operates in a cash-and-inventory-rich, customer-
facing, safety-sensitive environment (forklift/reach operators, delivery drivers, paint/chemical
handlers, cashiers) — the insider-threat, theft/fraud, workplace-violence, customer-harm,
negligent-hiring, and brand-reputation exposure is material. A single negligent-hiring incident
(an unscreened worker committing theft, fraud, assault, or customer harm) carries legal liability
(quasi-delict/Civil Code, DOLE), regulatory exposure (DOLE OSH, NPC privacy), and reputational
damage. Background screening is a recognized control for insider risk (VS-23 LP), payment fraud
(VS-125), corporate security (VS-159), and contingent-workforce governance (VS-98).

Today this discipline is **unowned as a unified program**. The defining terms — "background
screening program", "pre-employment screening", "criminal background check", "credentialing",
"workforce vetting", and "screening vendor management" — appear in **zero** PA files as
dedicated workflow headers for a cross-category program. The only existing screen is the single
**contingent-worker** workflow W3223 (Background Check, NBI & Pre-Engagement Screening in
VS-98.2) — a slice analogous to how VS-161 TPRM unified the scattered supplier-risk slices.
Employees are screened as a single onboarding step within VS-121/VS-19 (the "background check"
header), vendor/3PL drivers have license-only checks (W1400), and executive vetting is ad hoc —
but no value stream owns the **unified screening operating model**: centralized screening-vendor
management, role-based screening packages, consent/RA 10173 governance, adverse-action/fair-
chance due process, ongoing re-screening, cross-entity consistency, and insider-risk linkage. The
capability is **not** covered by **VS-121 (Talent Acquisition)**, which owns the *candidate
transaction* (a background check as one onboarding step) — this owns the *screening program and
vendor/consent/analytics discipline*. It is **not** covered by **VS-98 (Contingent Workforce)**,
which owns *W3223 the contingent slice* — this unifies employees + contingent + vendor personnel
+ executives. It is **not** covered by **VS-159 (Corporate Security)**, which owns physical
security/executive protection — this owns the *pre-employment & ongoing trust-vetting* that feeds
it. It is **not** covered by **VS-150 (Drug-Free Workplace)**, which owns substance screening —
this owns the broader background/credential screening (with drug-test integration). And it is
**not** covered by **VS-91 (Data Privacy)**, which owns privacy governance — this executes
consent and data-minimization *within the screening context*.

This value stream owns the end-to-end screening discipline: program strategy & operating model;
screening policy & role-based matrix; screening-vendor selection & governance (PNP-SOSIA-licensed
providers, TPRM VS-161); consent/RA 10173 privacy governance; screening-package design (NBI/
police/court, prior-employment, education, credit-for-finance-roles, sanctions/PEP); quality/
SLA/evidence chain-of-custody; governance/adverse-action review board; screening data
governance & retention; **employee pre-hire screening integration** (VS-121 offer-conditional);
**contingent/outsourced screening governance** (VS-98/W3223); **vendor/3PL site-access
screening** (VS-56/VS-110); **executive/sensitive-role enhanced vetting** (VS-159); periodic
re-screening & continuous monitoring; cross-entity/regional consistency; international/foreign-
worker screening (VS-160); drug-test/health/license integration (VS-150/VS-83/W1400);
adverse-action decisioning & NLRC due process (VS-84); screening analytics & insider-risk
linkage (VS-23/VS-125/VS-159); false-document detection; vendor TPRM; cost/ROI analytics; and
program assurance. It integrates tightly with VS-121 (recruiting), VS-98 (contingent), VS-56/
VS-110 (vendor/3PL personnel), VS-159 (security), VS-150 (drug), VS-91 (privacy), VS-84 (labor/
NLRC), VS-161 (TPRM), and VS-23/VS-125 (LP/fraud).
