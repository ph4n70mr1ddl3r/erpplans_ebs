## W4881. Screening Operations Analytics — Volume, TAT, Hit-Rate, Vendor Performance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly analytics cycle; vendor performance review (W4867); governance reporting |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly |
| **Volume** | Group screening operations |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Analytics (VS-28) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19/103), Procurement (VS-03), Contingent Coordinator (VS-98) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build the screening-operations analytics suite: screening volume by category (employee/contingent/vendor/executive) and role-tier, TAT by check-type/vendor, hit-rate (adverse findings) by check-type/role, re-screening currency, vendor performance (W4867), and cost (W4885) | Analytics / Screening Program Manager | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Quarterly |
| 2 | Analyze & report: TAT vs SLA, bottleneck check-types, hit-rate trends (rising criminal/sanctions hits?), vendor comparison, coverage gaps (unscreened categories/roles), and the adverse-action outcome mix (clear/conditional/disqualify) | Analytics / Screening Program Manager / Procurement | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Quarterly |
| 3 | Feed insights to vendor governance (W4867/W4884), policy (W4866), resourcing, and continuous improvement (W4888); track action closure | Analytics / Screening Program Manager | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (VS-28); screening platform; HRIS (VS-19/103); procurement (VS-03); contingent workforce (VS-98)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Insight-inaction risk**: Analytics not acted on; mitigated by action-tracking
- **Coverage-blindspot risk**: An unscreened category/role undetected; mitigated by coverage-gap analytics
- **Vendor-underperformance risk**: A slow/inaccurate vendor not addressed; mitigated by vendor comparison

### Automation Opportunity
- Screening-operations dashboard; TAT/coverage auto-monitor; vendor-performance scorecard

### Controls
- CTL-733 (ensure controlled execution — Screening Analytics, Risk & Program Assurance (PA-167.3).)
- CTL-733: analytics reviewed quarterly; actions tracked to closure

### Time Estimate
- ~quarterly cycle 1 week; continuous insight

### Cross-references
- links to VS-28 (analytics), W4867/W4884 (vendor), W4866 (policy), W4885 (cost), W4888 (improvement)

## W4882. Insider-Risk Linkage — Screening to LP/Fraud/Security Event Correlation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Insider event (theft VS-23, fraud VS-125, security VS-159, workplace-violence); periodic correlation |
| **Frequency** | Per event + periodic correlation |
| **Volume** | ~10–20 insider events per year |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / LP (VS-23) / Corporate Security (VS-159) |
| **Participants** | Fraud (VS-125), HR, Legal (VS-100.3), Analytics (VS-28) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On an insider event (theft/fraud/security/violence by an employee/contingent/vendor person), correlate to the individual's screening record (was the event foreseeable from screening? a gap? a missed check? a stale screen?) — a post-incident screening-review | LP / Corporate Security / Screening Program Manager | VP Legal & Compliance / Chief Compliance & Security Officer | Per event |
| 2 | Identify screening-program improvements: role-package gaps (W4869), re-screening gaps (W4877), source-quality issues (W4883), vendor failures (W4884), or consistency gaps (W4878); strengthen controls accordingly | Screening Program Manager / LP / Corporate Security | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |
| 3 | Maintain the insider-risk-screening feed (W4877 triggers): insider-event learnings feed continuous monitoring and triggered re-vet; report correlation findings/trends to governance (W4888), ERM (VS-21.2), and analytics (W4881); support negligent-hiring defense with screening evidence (W4870) | Screening Program Manager / Risk / LP / Legal | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- LP/fraud (VS-23/VS-125); corporate security (VS-159); ERM (VS-21.2); screening evidence (W4870); analytics (VS-28); legal (VS-100.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Negligent-hiring liability risk**: An incident revealing a screening gap → civil liability; mitigated by post-incident review + evidence (W4870)
- **Correlation-blindspot risk**: Insider events not feeding back to screening improvements; mitigated by the linkage
- **Privacy risk**: Over-mining screening data post-incident; mitigated by lawful basis + minimization (W4868)

### Automation Opportunity
- Event → screening-record correlation; insider-risk dashboard (VS-23/VS-159); control-improvement tracker

### Controls
- CTL-733 (ensure controlled execution — Screening Analytics, Risk & Program Assurance (PA-167.3).)
- CTL-733 (every insider event screened-correlated; gaps remediated; evidence preserved (W4870))

### Time Estimate
- ~per event review days; correlation periodic

### Cross-references
- links to VS-23 (LP), VS-125 (fraud), VS-159 (security), VS-21.2 (ERM), W4870 (evidence), W4877 (triggers), W4888 (governance)

## W4883. Screening Fraud & False-Document Detection (Forged Clearances/Credentials)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Suspect document; authenticity-check failure; fraud pattern; vendor source-quality issue |
| **Frequency** | Ad hoc + periodic |
| **Volume** | ~30–60 suspect documents per year (subset of screens) |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / LP (VS-23) / Vendor (W4867) |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), Security (VS-159), HR, Government Affairs (VS-104) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Detect false/forged documents: forged NBI clearances, fake police clearances, fabricated employment/education credentials, altered licenses (W1400/W4754/W600); use source-authentication (NBI portal verification, school/employer direct verification, PRC portal), document-forensics, and pattern-detection | Screening Program Manager / Vendor / LP | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 2 | Investigate & decide: verify via the issuing source, document the fraud, route to the Adverse-Action Board (W4880 — disqualification), and where material, escalate to LP (VS-23) and Legal (VS-100.3) for possible legal action (falsification of documents) | LP / Legal / Screening Program Manager | VP Legal & Compliance | Per case |
| 3 | Manage vendor source-quality (W4867/W4884) and report fraud-trends (rising forgery?) to governance (W4888), analytics (W4881), and Government Affairs (VS-104) where systemic; strengthen verification controls | Screening Program Manager / LP / Analytics | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Screening platform (verification); LP (VS-23); legal (VS-100.3); government portals (NBI/PRC/TESDA); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Forged-document risk**: A forged clearance letting an unfit person in → incident + negligent-hiring; mitigated by source-authentication
- **Verification-cost risk**: Verifying every document is costly; mitigated by risk-based verification + automation
- **False-allegation risk**: Wrongly flagging a genuine document; mitigated by source-confirmation

### Automation Opportunity
- Source-authentication (NBI/PRC/school portals); document-forensics; forgery-pattern detection

### Controls
- CTL-733 (ensure controlled execution — Screening Analytics, Risk & Program Assurance (PA-167.3).)
- CTL-733: source-authentication for high-risk documents; fraud investigated; disqualification/legal action where warranted

### Time Estimate
- ~per case hours–days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-23 (LP), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-104 (government affairs/systemic), W4867 (vendor), W4880 (adverse-action), W4881 (analytics)

| **Participants** | HR Policy, Labor Relations (VS-84), Compliance Portfolio (VS-166) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## W4888. Program Maturity, Benchmarking & Continuous Improvement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual program review; maturity assessment; major incident (W4882); strategy refresh (W4865) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on incident |
| **Volume** | 1 program |
| **Owner** | CHRO / Chief Compliance & Security Officer / Screening Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Strategy (VS-33), Internal Audit (VS-21), OpEx (VS-133), HR, Security (VS-159) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assess program maturity across dimensions (coverage, package-design, consent/privacy, chain-of-custody/evidence, re-screening/continuous-monitoring, vendor-governance, analytics/automation, governance) using a maturity model; benchmark vs industry/peer | Screening Program Manager / Strategy / Internal Audit | CHRO / Chief Compliance & Security Officer | Annual |

### Controls
- CTL-733 (ensure controlled execution — Screening Analytics, Risk & Program Assurance (PA-167.3).)

### Time Estimate
- ~annual assessment 1–2 weeks; improvement continuous
