## W4865. Workforce Vetting & Background-Screening Program Strategy & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch; annual HR/security strategy review; major insider-risk event; regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on trigger |
| **Volume** | 1 group program; ~6,932 employees + contingent + vendor personnel + executives |
| **Owner** | CHRO / Chief Compliance & Security Officer / Screening Program Manager |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-19/121), Security (VS-159), LP (VS-23), Legal (VS-100.3), Privacy (VS-91), Contingent Workforce (VS-98) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the program strategy & operating model: the screening scope (employees, contingent, vendor/3PL personnel, executives), the centralized screening function (shared service) with role-based packages, make-vs-buy (screening vendor W4867), and integration with recruiting (VS-121), contingent (VS-98), security (VS-159), and LP (VS-23) | Screening Program Manager / HR / Security | CHRO / Chief Compliance & Security Officer | Annual |
| 2 | Define the risk-based screening taxonomy: role risk tiers (cash-handling/finance, security/CIT, IT-admin/data, child-adjacent, forklift/driver safety-sensitive, executive) mapped to screening-package depth; align to negligent-hiring liability, DOLE OSH, and fair-chance principles | Screening Program Manager / Security / Legal / HR | CHRO / Chief Compliance & Security Officer | Annual |
| 3 | Approve the strategy, resource the function, integrate into ERM (VS-21.2) and the insider-risk program (VS-23/VS-159), and set KPIs (screening coverage %, TAT, hit-rate, adverse-action %, re-screening currency, insider events linked to screening gaps); report to the board/audit & risk committee (VS-36) | Screening Program Manager / Risk / Strategy | CHRO / Chief Compliance & Security Officer / Board | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS (VS-19/103); security/insider-risk (VS-159/VS-23); ERM (VS-21.2); legal (VS-100.3); analytics (VS-28); board governance (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fragmentation risk**: Screening siloed per workforce category → inconsistent coverage; mitigated by the centralized program
- **Over/under-screening risk**: Wrong package depth for the role risk; mitigated by the role-risk taxonomy
- **Strategic-invisibility risk**: Program cut as overhead; mitigated by insider-event/loss-avoided KPIs

### Automation Opportunity
- Role-to-package mapping engine; centralized screening-workflow platform; KPI dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-731 (ensure controlled execution — Screening Program Strategy, Policy & Governance (PA-167.1).)
- CTL-731: centralized screening function; role-risk taxonomy; board-reported KPIs

### Time Estimate
- ~3–5 weeks annual strategy; continuous operating-model tuning

### Cross-references
- links to VS-121 (recruiting), VS-98 (contingent), VS-159 (security), VS-23 (LP), VS-21.2 (ERM), VS-36 (board); sets inputs for W4866–W4872

## W4866. Screening Policy, Role-Based Screening Matrix & Adverse-Action/Fair-Chance Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4865); regulatory change (DOLE/NPC); legal/HR-policy refresh; adverse-action dispute |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | 1 policy; 1 role-based matrix; 1 adverse-action framework |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Legal (VS-100.3) / HR Policy |
| **Participants** | CHRO, Security (VS-159), Labor Relations (VS-84), Privacy (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain the screening policy: when screening occurs (pre-offer/offer-conditional, periodic re-screen, trigger-based), what is screened per role tier (W4865), consent requirements (W4868), data minimization, retention (W4872), and the lawful basis (RA 10173, DOLE, role-related/legitimate interest) | Screening Program Manager / Legal / HR Policy | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 2 | Maintain the role-based screening matrix: every role (cashier, finance, security guard, CIT, IT-admin, child-adjacent, forklift/driver, executive) mapped to required checks (NBI, police, court, prior-employment, education, credit, sanctions/PEP, drug W150, license W1400); review with Security and Legal | Screening Program Manager / Security / Legal / HR | CHRO / Chief Compliance & Security Officer | Annual |
| 3 | Maintain the adverse-action/fair-chance framework: individualized assessment of criminal history vs role, the pre-adverse-action notice, the candidate's right to respond/dispute, the decision and notice, and appeal; align to DOLE/NLRC due process (VS-84) and avoid blanket bans (fair-chance/bias risk) | Legal / HR Policy / Screening Program Manager | VP Legal & Compliance / CHRO | Annual + on dispute |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS/policy (VS-19/103); legal (VS-100.3); labor relations (VS-84); privacy (VS-91); security (VS-159)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Negligent-hiring risk**: Under-screening a high-risk role → incident + liability; mitigated by the role-risk matrix
- **Fair-chance/discrimination risk**: Blanket bans or disparate impact → NLRC/NPC/human-rights exposure; mitigated by individualized assessment
- **Policy-drift risk**: Policy not updated for regulatory/legal change; mitigated by annual review + W4868 link

### Automation Opportunity
- Role-to-package policy engine; individualized-assessment decision tool; adverse-action notice automation

### Controls
- CTL-731 (ensure controlled execution — Screening Program Strategy, Policy & Governance (PA-167.1).)
- CTL-731: policy lawful & current; role-based matrix applied; adverse-action due process followed

### Time Estimate
- ~annual policy/matrix review 2–3 weeks

### Cross-references
- links to VS-84 (labor/NLRC), VS-91 (privacy), VS-159 (security), VS-100.3 (legal), W4865 (strategy), W4868 (consent), W4871 (adverse-action)

| **Frequency** | Per sourcing event + multi-year contract + ongoing governance |
| **Volume** | ~1–3 screening vendors/providers group-wide |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Procurement (VS-03/VS-34) / Legal (VS-100.3) |
| **Participants** | TPRM (VS-161), Privacy (VS-91), Security (VS-159), Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Define vendor requirements: licensing (PNP-SOSIA under RA 5487 for investigation agencies; PBSA/BSAAP accreditation where held), coverage (NBI/police/court/education/credit/sanctions access), TAT, data security, RA 10173 compliance, integrations (HRIS/ATS), and pricing model | Screening Program Manager / Procurement / Legal / Privacy | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |
| 2 | Run sourcing & contract: RFP, evaluate (capability, TPRM cyber & financial DD VS-161, privacy/DPA VS-91, references), negotiate the agreement (SLA, data-handling, DPA, breach notice, audit rights, term/exit), and contract via Legal | Procurement / Legal / TPRM / Privacy | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Per event |
| 3 | Onboard & govern: integrate to HRIS/ATS (VS-121), set SLA monitoring, conduct periodic performance/quality review (W4870), manage re-compete at term, and feed vendor performance to TPRM (VS-161) and analytics (W4885) | Screening Program Manager / Procurement / TPRM | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### Controls
- CTL-731 (ensure controlled execution — Screening Program Strategy, Policy & Governance (PA-167.1).)

### Time Estimate
- ~per sourcing event 6–10 weeks

## W4868. Consent, RA 10173 Privacy & Data-Minimization Governance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Screening initiation; privacy-program cycle (VS-91); DSAR; regulatory change |
| **Frequency** | Per screen + continuous governance |
| **Volume** | All screened individuals (~thousands/yr) |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Privacy Officer (DPO) (VS-91) |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), HR, Screening vendor, Labor Relations (VS-84) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Govern consent: each screened individual provides informed, specific, RA 10173-compliant consent before screening (purpose, scope, data, recipient, retention, rights); capture and retain the consent record; ensure the lawful basis (consent and/or legitimate-interest/role-related per policy W4866) | Screening Program Manager / DPO / HR | VP Legal & Compliance / DPO | Per screen |
| 2 | Enforce data minimization: collect only the screening data the role-package (W4866/W4869) requires, no more; limit access (need-to-know), secure storage/transfer, and the screening-vendor data-handling (DPA W4867); conduct a screening-specific PIA/DPIA | DPO / Screening Program Manager / Legal | DPO | Continuous |
| 3 | Honor data-subject rights: access, rectification, erasure (where applicable), objection, and DSAR (links to VS-91); manage the 72-hour breach-notification obligation for any screening-data breach (vendor or internal) | DPO / Legal / Screening Program Manager | DPO | Per request/event |

### System Touchpoints
- Privacy/DPO platform (VS-91); consent management; HRIS (VS-19/103); legal (VS-100.3); breach response (VS-91/VS-27.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unlawful-processing risk**: Screening without valid consent/lawful basis → NPC exposure (RA 10173); mitigated by consent governance + PIA
- **Over-collection risk**: Collecting more than the role requires; mitigated by data minimization + role-package
- **Breach risk**: Screening-data breach (sensitive criminal/credit data); mitigated by security + vendor DPA + 72-hour notice

### Automation Opportunity
- Consent-management workflow; role-package data-minimization enforcement; DSAR workflow (VS-91)

### Controls
- CTL-131 (ensure lawful & consented personnel vetting.)
- CTL-731: RA 10173 consent before screening; data minimization by role-package; DSAR honored; breach notified

### Time Estimate
- ~per consent minutes; PIA days; governance continuous

### Cross-references
- links to VS-91 (privacy/DPO/DSAR/breach), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-84 (labor), W4866 (policy), W4867 (vendor/DPA), W4872 (retention)

## W4869. Screening-Package Design — NBI/Police/Court, Employment, Education, Credit, Sanctions

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Role-package design (W4866); new role/source-country; source/quality issue |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on change |
| **Volume** | ~role-tier packages (5–7) × check-types |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Security (VS-159) / HR |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), Finance (credit-check roles), Privacy (VS-91), Screening vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design/maintain the check-type catalog: NBI clearance (national criminal), police/barangay clearance (local), court-records check (regional trial courts), prior-employment verification (W3223-style reference checks), education/credential verification (links to PRC/TESDA W600/W558), credit check (finance/cash roles only, lawful), sanctions/PEP watchlist (links to VS-86), and drug test (VS-150) | Screening Program Manager / Security / Legal / HR | CHRO / Chief Compliance & Security Officer | Annual |
| 2 | Map check-types to role-tiers (W4866): e.g., cashier = NBI + employment + drug; finance = NBI + employment + credit + sanctions; security/CIT = enhanced NBI + court + sanctions; IT-admin = NBI + employment + sanctions; executive = full enhanced; ensuring proportionality and fairness | Screening Program Manager / Security / Legal | CHRO / Chief Compliance & Security Officer | Annual |
| 3 | Maintain source-quality & coverage: each check-type's source (NBI, court, school, employer, credit bureau), reliability, TAT, and gaps (e.g., court-records coverage varies by region); manage source-quality issues and alternative verification (links to W590 trade-pro verification) | Screening Program Manager / Vendor / Legal | CHRO / Chief Compliance & Security Officer | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Screening platform; HRIS (VS-19/103); sanctions/AML (VS-86); PRC/TESDA (W600/W558); drug (VS-150); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Source-coverage risk**: Court-records/NBI gaps producing false-negatives; mitigated by multi-source + alternative verification
- **Disproportionality risk**: Over-screening (e.g., credit for non-finance roles) → privacy/fairness exposure; mitigated by role-tier proportionality
- **False-document risk**: Forged clearances/credentials; mitigated by verification (W4883) and source-authentication

### Automation Opportunity
- Role → package → check-types engine; multi-source verification; source-quality dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-731 (ensure controlled execution — Screening Program Strategy, Policy & Governance (PA-167.1).)
- CTL-731: check-types mapped to role-tiers; proportionality enforced; source-quality monitored

### Time Estimate
- ~annual design 1–2 weeks; source-quality continuous

### Cross-references
- links to VS-86 (sanctions/PEP), VS-150 (drug), W600/W558 (PRC/TESDA), W4866 (policy/matrix), W4883 (false-document)

## W4870. Screening Quality Standards, SLA & Evidence Chain-of-Custody

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Screening operations; SLA review; audit; quality issue |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + periodic review |
| **Volume** | All screens (~thousands/yr) |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Quality / Vendor (W4867) |
| **Participants** | HR, Security, Legal (VS-100.3), Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintain screening quality standards: completeness (all package checks done), accuracy (correct individual, no false-merge), verification (source-authenticated), TAT (SLA per check-type), and the evidence standard (what record proves the screen was done) | Screening Program Manager / Quality / Vendor | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 2 | Maintain the evidence chain-of-custody: from consent → screening-order → vendor-result → verification → decision → retention, tamper-evident and audit-ready; reconcile to the HRIS and the legal/regulatory record (VS-88) | Screening Program Manager / Records / Legal | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 3 | Monitor SLA/quality: track TAT, accuracy, completeness, vendor performance (W4867), exceptions, and audit findings; remediate quality issues; report quality-health to governance (W4888) and analytics (W4881) | Screening Program Manager / Quality / Internal Audit | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Screening platform (chain-of-custody); HRIS (VS-19/103); records (VS-88); audit (VS-21); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **False-merge risk**: A screening result matched to the wrong individual → wrong decision; mitigated by identity verification
- **Evidence-gap risk**: A screen done but not provable (no chain-of-custody) → negligent-hiring liability if challenged; mitigated by the evidence standard
- **SLA-breach risk**: Slow screening delaying onboarding/assignment; mitigated by SLA monitoring

### Automation Opportunity
- Chain-of-custody platform; SLA/quality dashboard; exception alerting

### Controls
- CTL-731 (ensure controlled execution — Screening Program Strategy, Policy & Governance (PA-167.1).)
- CTL-731: quality standards enforced; tamper-evident chain-of-custody; SLA monitored

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous; periodic review monthly

### Cross-references

## W4871. Program Governance, Separation-of-Duties, Adverse-Action Review Board & Appeals

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Adverse finding requiring decision; governance cycle; appeal/dispute |
| **Frequency** | Per adverse finding + continuous governance |
| **Volume** | ~80–150 adverse findings/year (subset of screens) |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Adverse-Action Review Board / Legal (VS-100.3) |
| **Participants** | HR, Security (VS-159), Labor Relations (VS-84), Privacy (VS-91) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Govern the program with separation-of-duties: the screening function (orders/checks) is separate from the adverse-action decision (review board) and from the hiring/assignment decision — preventing conflict-of-interest and bias; document the SoD in the governance framework | Screening Program Manager / Legal / HR | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |
| 2 | Operate the Adverse-Action Review Board: on an adverse finding, conduct the individualized assessment (W4866 — nature/gravity/time-since/relevance to role), issue the pre-adverse-action notice, allow the individual to respond/dispute/evidence rehabilitation, decide, and notify; ensure NLRC/due-process alignment (VS-84) | Review Board / Legal / HR / Security | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Per finding |
| 3 | Manage appeals & disputes: a defined appeal path, escalation to HR/Legal, and where unresolved, NLRC/labor-relations handling (VS-84); document all decisions in the chain-of-custody (W4870) and report adverse-action outcomes/bias indicators to governance (W4888) and analytics (W4881) | Review Board / Legal / Labor Relations | CHRO / VP Legal & Compliance | Per appeal |

### System Touchpoints
- Screening/HRIS platform; legal (VS-100.3); labor relations (VS-84); privacy (VS-91); analytics (VS-28)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Conflict-of-interest risk**: Same person screening and deciding → bias; mitigated by SoD
- **Inconsistent-decision risk**: Similar findings decided differently → fairness/NLRC risk; mitigated by the review board + individualized assessment
- **Bias/disparate-impact risk**: Decisions disparately affecting a protected group; mitigated by bias-indicator monitoring

### Automation Opportunity
- Adverse-finding → review-board workflow; individualized-assessment decision tool; bias-indicator dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-131 (ensure lawful & consented personnel vetting.)
- CTL-731: SoD between screen/decide/hire; individualized assessment; appeal path; NLRC-aligned

### Time Estimate
- ~per finding days; governance continuous

### Cross-references
- links to VS-84 (labor/NLRC), VS-91 (privacy), VS-159 (security), VS-100.3 (legal), W4866 (fair-chance framework), W4870 (chain-of-custody), W4888 (governance)

## W4872. Screening Data Governance, Retention, DSAR & Records Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Screen completion; retention-cycle; DSAR; audit; records-policy refresh |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + on retention/DSAR |
| **Volume** | All screening records (~thousands) |
| **Owner** | Screening Program Manager / Records (VS-88) / DPO (VS-91) |
| **Participants** | Legal (VS-100.3), HR, Internal Audit (VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Govern screening-data retention: per RA 10173 data-minimization and the records policy (VS-88), retain screening records only as long as necessary for the lawful purpose (employment/assignment duration + any statutory/defensive period), then securely dispose/sanitize; define retention by record-type | Screening Program Manager / Records / DPO / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance / DPO | Continuous |
| 2 | Manage DSAR (links to VS-91): an individual's access/rectification/erasure request on their screening data, fulfilled within the statutory period, with legal/HR review where erasure conflicts with defensive-retention (negligent-hiring defense) | DPO / Legal / HR / Screening Program Manager | DPO | Per request |
| 3 | Maintain records & audit trail: secure storage, access controls (need-to-know), the chain-of-custody (W4870), and audit-ready production for regulator/internal audit (VS-21); reconcile to HRIS and legal-hold (VS-88); report records-health to governance (W4888) | Records / Screening Program Manager / Internal Audit | VP Legal & Compliance | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Records/DMS (VS-88); privacy/DPO (VS-91); HRIS (VS-19/103); legal (VS-100.3); audit (VS-21)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-retention risk**: Holding screening data longer than necessary → RA 10173 exposure; mitigated by retention rules
- **Under-retention risk**: Disposing of records needed for negligent-hiring defense; mitigated by defensive-retention rules
- **DSAR-deadline risk**: Late DSAR response → NPC exposure; mitigated by SLA

### Automation Opportunity
- Retention-rule engine; DSAR workflow (VS-91); legal-hold-aware disposal

### Controls
- CTL-131 (ensure lawful & consented personnel vetting.)
- CTL-731: retention per RA 10173/records policy; DSAR honored; chain-of-custody & audit-ready

### Time Estimate
- ~continuous governance; per DSAR days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-88 (records), VS-91 (privacy/DSAR), VS-100.3 (legal), VS-21 (audit), W4868 (consent), W4870 (chain-of-custody), W4888 (governance)
