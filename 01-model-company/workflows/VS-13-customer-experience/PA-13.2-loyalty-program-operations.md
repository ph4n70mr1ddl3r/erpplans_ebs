## W263. Loyalty Member Enrollment & Onboarding Journey

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Workflow ID** | W263 |
| **Name** | Loyalty Member Enrollment & Onboarding Journey |
| **Trigger** | Customer requests loyalty membership (in-store or online) |
| **Frequency** | ~170–270 new enrollments/day (one register with W735/W822: ~5,000–8,000/month; target: grow from 600K to 800K members over the ramp) |
| **Volume** | ~5,000–8,000 new enrollments/month (per W735/W822) |
| **Owner** | CS Agent / Sales Associate |
| **Participants** | Sales Associate (store), CS Agent (online/call center), CS Supervisor (1), Marketing Team (data quality) |
| **Time Estimate** | 3–5 min per enrollment |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Enrollment Trigger**: Customer initiates enrollment via: (a) **in-store**: Sales Associate or Customer Service Rep offers enrollment during checkout or at dedicated enrollment kiosk; (b) **online**: customer self-registers on ecommerce website or mobile app during account creation; (c) **call center**: CS Agent assists enrollment during inbound call; (d) **event**: DIY workshop (W147) or store event registration includes loyalty opt-in | Sales Associate / CS Agent / Customer | CS Supervisor | 1 min |
| 2 | **Data Capture**: Customer provides enrollment data: (a) full name (first, last), (b) mobile number (primary identifier for PH consumers), (c) email address, (d) birthday (optional, for birthday promo targeting), (e) home store preference (optional); for in-store: Sales Associate enters data into POS enrollment screen; for online: customer fills form on website/app; system validates: mobile number format (+63), email format, minimum age (18+) | Sales Associate / Customer | CS Supervisor | 2 min |
| 3 | **Deduplication Check**: System performs real-time deduplication: (a) matches against existing loyalty database by mobile number (primary key), email, or name + birthday combination; (b) if existing member found: system retrieves existing account, alerts associate/customer, and offers account recovery (reset PIN, update email) instead of new enrollment; (c) if duplicate prevented, logs attempt for fraud monitoring (W37); (d) deduplication prevents inflated member counts and ensures CRM-002 data quality per W253 | System | CS Supervisor | Automated |
| 4 | **Consent Management**: System presents Data Privacy consent per RA 10173: (a) customer reads privacy notice (digital on screen or printed copy in-store); (b) customer explicitly consents to: (i) collection and processing of personal data for loyalty program, (ii) receiving promotional communications (SMS, email, push — with granular opt-in per channel), (iii) sharing data with partnered merchants (if applicable); (c) consent recorded with timestamp, consent version, and channel; (d) customer can modify consent at any time via app, website, or in-store | System / Customer | CS Supervisor / DPO | 1 min |
| 5 | **Account Creation**: System creates loyalty account: (a) assigns unique loyalty ID (numeric or alphanumeric); (b) generates digital loyalty card in mobile app (QR code + barcode for POS scanning per POS-005); (c) sets initial tier to Bronze per CRM-005; (d) opens points balance at 0; (e) creates customer master record per MDM-003; (f) for in-store: prints temporary physical card or provides QR code printout until app download | System | CS Supervisor | Automated |
| 6 | **Welcome Communication**: System triggers automated welcome sequence: (a) immediate: SMS confirmation with loyalty ID, welcome message, and app download link; (b) within 1 hour: welcome email with program overview (tier benefits, points earning rates, redemption options, partner discounts); (c) within 24 hours: push notification (if app downloaded) with first-purchase incentive coupon (e.g., "Earn 2x points on your first purchase within 7 days!"); (d) welcome communications tracked in customer 360 profile (W156) | System / Marketing Team | CS Supervisor | Automated |
| 7 | **First Purchase Incentive Tracking**: System tracks enrollment-to-first-purchase conversion: (a) monitors whether new member makes first qualifying purchase within 7 days (welcome coupon validity); (b) if no purchase within 7 days: triggers reminder SMS/push on day 5; (c) if no purchase within 14 days: triggers re-engagement email with curated product recommendations; (d) first-purchase conversion rate tracked as enrollment KPI for Marketing and Store Ops | System / Marketing Team | CS Manager | Automated |
| 8 | **Monthly Enrollment Quality Review**: CS Supervisor and Marketing review enrollment metrics: (a) total new enrollments per channel (in-store vs. online vs. call center vs. event); (b) enrollment-to-first-purchase conversion rate (target: 70% within 14 days); (c) data quality metrics: % complete profiles (all fields filled), % email verified, % app downloaded; (d) deduplication rate (% of enrollment attempts blocked as duplicates — target: < 5%); (e) consent rate (% opting into all communication channels); (f) per-store enrollment leaderboard for Store Manager incentives | CS Supervisor / Marketing | CS Manager | 2 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- POS enrollment screen with real-time deduplication (CRM-002, MDM-003)
- Ecommerce / mobile app self-registration flow with RA 10173 consent management
- Loyalty ID generation and digital card creation (CRM-001, CRM-005)
- Automated welcome communication engine (SMS, email, push) integrated with CDP (W156)
- First-purchase incentive coupon system with expiration tracking
- Enrollment analytics dashboard per channel and per store
- Deduplication engine (W253) with real-time matching

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data quality at store level**: Sales Associates may rush enrollment, entering incomplete or incorrect data; degrades CRM-002 quality and impacts personalization effectiveness
- **Duplicate suppression resistance**: Some customers deliberately create multiple accounts for welcome bonuses; need fraud detection logic (same mobile, same device)
- **Low app download conversion**: In-store customers may enroll but not download app, reducing engagement channel; need in-store app download prompts (QR codes at checkout, store WiFi landing page)
- **Consent fatigue**: Lengthy consent forms reduce enrollment completion; balance RA 10173 compliance with conversion-friendly UX
- **Enrollment staff incentives**: If Sales Associates are incentivized per enrollment, risk of fake or low-quality enrollments; quality metrics must accompany volume targets


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "center" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validates" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "logs attempt for fraud monitoring" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-279 (ensure controlled execution — Loyalty Program Operations (PA-13.2).)
- operational: CS Agent / Sales Associate review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
- Enrollment trigger and data capture: 3 min/enrollment (in-store); Automated (online)
- Deduplication check: Automated (real-time)
- Consent management: 1 min/enrollment
- Account creation and digital card generation: Automated
- Welcome communication dispatch: Automated
- First purchase incentive tracking: Automated (ongoing per cohort)
- Monthly enrollment quality review: 2 hours/month (CS Supervisor + Marketing)
- **Total in-store enrollment time**: ~3–5 min per enrollment (Sales Associate)
- **Total online enrollment time**: ~2 min customer self-service (Automated backend)
- **Monthly enrollment processing**: ~250–670 in-store hours/month (~5,000–8,000 enrollments × 3–5 min, per the Volume) distributed across 200 stores (~1–3.5 hours/store/month)

### Staffing Implication
- **Sales Associates (in-store)**: ~4–6 hours/store/month on enrollment activities. Absorbed within existing Sales Associate duties across 200 stores.
- **CS Agents (call center/online)**: a portion of the ~30-agent call center team handles phone-assisted enrollments; estimated ~50–100 enrollments/day via call center = ~3–8 hours/day total. Absorbed.
- **CS Supervisor (1)**: 2 hours/month for enrollment quality review plus daily oversight. Absorbed within existing supervisory role.
- **Marketing Team**: 2 hours/month for enrollment quality review + ongoing welcome journey optimization. Absorbed.
- **No incremental headcount.**

## W735. Customer Onboarding Journey Management & First-90-Day Engagement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New loyalty member enrollment per W263; or trade/corporate account activation per W460 |
| **Frequency** | ~5,000–8,000 new loyalty enrollments/month; ~50–100 new trade/corporate accounts/month |
| **Volume** | ~60,000–96,000 new customer onboarding journeys/year |
| **Owner** | Customer Experience Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Automation Specialist, Loyalty Program Manager, Trade Sales Manager, Customer Service |

### Background

W263 covers the loyalty enrollment process and W460 covers trade/corporate account onboarding. This workflow covers what happens AFTER enrollment — the critical first 90 days when new customers form their impression of BuildRight and decide whether to become repeat customers or lapse. Research shows that customers who engage within the first 90 days have 3-4x higher long-term retention. This workflow manages the structured onboarding journey including welcome communications, first-purchase incentives, product education, feedback collection, and early churn intervention.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Welcome communication (Day 0-1)**: upon enrollment, system triggers automated welcome sequence: (a) email/SMS: welcome to BuildRight loyalty program, explain tier benefits, current point balance, how to earn/redeem; (b) digital loyalty card via mobile app per W263; (c) first-purchase incentive: welcome coupon (e.g., 10% off next purchase within 30 days) with unique barcode tracked per W539; (d) for trade/corporate accounts: personal welcome call from Trade Sales Manager within 2 business days per W617 | System / Trade Sales Mgr | Customer Experience Mgr | Automated |
| 2 | **First-purchase engagement (Day 7-14)**: if customer has not made first purchase within 14 days: (a) system triggers re-engagement email: highlight personalized product recommendations based on enrollment preferences (e.g., "Just joined as a contractor? Check out our Pro Desk"); (b) include extended welcome coupon (if original expired); (c) for trade accounts: Trade Sales Manager follow-up call to schedule first store visit or pro desk introduction per W112 | System / Trade Sales Mgr | Customer Experience Mgr | Automated + 10-15 min per trade account |
| 3 | **First-purchase celebration (at first transaction)**: when new loyalty member completes first purchase: (a) system triggers: (i) bonus points award (e.g., 2x points on first purchase); (ii) push notification: "Great first purchase! You earned [X] bonus points"; (iii) transaction receipt includes loyalty points summary; (b) for first B2B purchase: Trade Sales Manager sends personalized thank-you and introduces account manager for ongoing relationship per W617 | System / Trade Sales Mgr | Customer Experience Mgr | Automated |
| 4 | **30-day check-in**: at 30 days post-enrollment: (a) system sends: (i) engagement summary: points earned, stores visited, categories purchased; (ii) educational content: "Did you know?" features (loyalty redemption, BOPIS, trade pricing if applicable); (iii) feedback request: quick 3-question survey on onboarding experience per W65; (b) for customers with zero purchases at 30 days: (i) escalate to re-engagement campaign per W618 churn prediction; (ii) offer re-activation incentive per W84; (c) for trade accounts: quarterly business review scheduling per W617 | System / Customer Experience Mgr | — | 10-15 min/day reviewing non-responders |
| 5 | **60-day product education**: at 60 days: (a) system sends personalized product recommendations based on purchase history: (i) related categories to explore; (ii) seasonal promotions relevant to customer segment; (iii) new product launches per W564 in purchased categories; (b) invite to store events per W147 or DIY workshops; (c) for trade accounts: invite to contractor networking events or exclusive trade nights | System / Marketing | Customer Experience Mgr | Automated |
| 6 | **90-day graduation assessment**: at 90 days: (a) system classifies onboarding outcome: (i) Engaged (3+ purchases, active app usage): successful onboarding → transition to regular customer lifecycle management; (ii) Casual (1-2 purchases, minimal engagement): trigger second-chance engagement per W618 with enhanced incentive; (iii) Lapsed (0 purchases since enrollment): activate dormant account recovery per W560 and W84; (b) Customer Experience Manager reviews 90-day cohort data monthly: (i) onboarding conversion rate by channel (in-store vs. online vs. app); (ii) average time to first purchase; (iii) 90-day retention rate; (c) insights feed into enrollment process optimization per W263 and loyalty program design per W104 | System / Customer Experience Mgr | CMO | 2-3 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints

- Marketing automation platform for triggered email/SMS sequences
- Loyalty engine per CRM-001 for points tracking and bonus award triggers
- Customer data platform per W156 for behavioral data and segmentation
- CRM per CRM-001/003 for customer profile and purchase history
- Mobile app per W615 for push notifications and digital loyalty card
- W263 enrollment data as onboarding journey trigger
- W618 churn prediction for early intervention
- W84 account reactivation for lapsed customers
- Analytics dashboard for 90-day cohort reporting

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Low welcome email engagement**: welcome emails have ~30–40% open rate in Philippine retail; many customers enroll but never read onboarding communications, reducing program effectiveness
- **First-purchase incentive abuse**: welcome coupons may be used by existing customers who re-enroll with different information to obtain multiple discounts
- **Trade account onboarding gap**: trade accounts require human touch (personal call, store visit) that does not scale well when onboarding 50-100 new accounts/month
- **Mobile app download barrier**: the onboarding journey assumes mobile app engagement, but many new loyalty members never download the app, limiting communication channels to email/SMS
- **Cohort analysis complexity**: accurately attributing customer behavior to onboarding interventions vs. organic purchase patterns requires careful analytics


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "accounts" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "Check out our Pro Desk" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-send of "sends personalized thank-you and introduces account manager for ongoing…" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-279 (ensure controlled execution — Loyalty Program Operations (PA-13.2).)
- operational: Customer Experience Manager review and approval gate

### Staffing Implication

Customer Experience Manager: ~10–15 hours/month reviewing 90-day cohorts and intervention queues. Trade Sales Managers: ~10–15 min per new trade account for personal welcome calls. Marketing Automation Specialist: ~4–6 hours/month configuring and optimizing triggered campaigns. No incremental headcount.

### Time Estimate

**Per loyalty enrollment**: automated journey with minimal manual intervention (~10–15 min/day reviewing exceptions). **Per trade account**: ~20–30 min for personal onboarding touchpoints. **Monthly cohort review**: 2-3 hours.

## W757. Customer On-the-Spot Loyalty Tier Upgrade Offer Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Loyalty member checkout (W5B) where system identifies member is within threshold of next tier |
| **Frequency** | Daily; triggered by qualifying loyalty transactions |
| **Volume** | ~200–300 upgrade opportunities/day chain-wide |
| **Owner** | Cashier |
| **Participants** | Cashier, Customer |

### Background
BuildRight Depot's tiered loyalty program (CRM-005: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) rewards higher-spending customers with better benefits. When a customer is within a spending threshold of the next tier (e.g., PHP 2,000 away from Gold), a real-time POS prompt can motivate incremental spending to reach the tier, driving both immediate revenue and long-term customer loyalty.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tier proximity detection — During checkout (W5B), system calculates customer's rolling 12-month spend and distance to next tier threshold; if within 150% of average basket value from next tier, triggers upgrade prompt | System | — | Instant |
| 2 | POS prompt display — Customer-facing screen displays personalized upgrade offer: "You're only PHP [X] away from [Tier Name]! Spend [X] today to unlock [benefit description]. Would you like to see items that can help you reach [Tier Name]?" | System | — | Instant |
| 3 | Customer interest check — Cashier verbally reinforces: "Just so you know, you're close to our next loyalty tier — would you like me to suggest a couple of items that could get you there today?" | Cashier | — | 30 sec |
| 4 | Targeted suggestion — If customer agrees, system displays 2–3 personalized product suggestions based on: (a) customer's purchase history category affinity, (b) items frequently bought together with current basket, (c) current promotional items | System | — | Automated |
| 5 | Upgrade transaction — If customer adds suggested items: Cashier scans items; system calculates new projected 12-month spend; confirms tier upgrade will be achieved | Cashier | — | 2 min |
| 6 | Tier upgrade confirmation — If threshold met: system processes tier upgrade effective immediately; customer receives SMS confirmation: "Congratulations! You've been upgraded to [Tier Name]. Your new benefits include: [list]. Enjoy!" | System | — | Automated |
| 7 | Upgrade analytics — System tracks: upgrade offer display rate, acceptance rate, incremental revenue per upgrade, upgrade retention rate after 12 months; feeds to W565 marketing ROI and W104 loyalty financial governance | System | — | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- W313 loyalty program configuration — tier thresholds and benefit descriptions
- W5B POS checkout integration — real-time tier proximity calculation
- W156 CDP — customer purchase history for personalized suggestions
- W13 promotional rules — eligible promotional items for suggestions
- W17 loyalty operations — tier upgrade processing
- W565 marketing ROI — upgrade revenue attribution
- W104 loyalty financial governance — tier migration liability impact

### Pain Points / Risks
- Customer annoyance — if upgrade prompts appear on every transaction, customers may perceive as pushy selling; limit to once per customer per month
- Threshold gaming — customers may accelerate spending to reach tier then reduce spending; tier re-evaluation (W515) mitigates with 12-month rolling calculation
- Tier devaluation — if too many customers upgrade rapidly, tier benefits become costly; W104 financial governance reviews tier economics quarterly
- Personalization relevance — irrelevant product suggestions undermine the upgrade offer's credibility; suggestion engine requires ongoing optimization


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "Tier proximity detection — During checkout" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-scan of "Upgrade transaction — If customer adds suggested items" (replaces manual Step 5).
- System auto-track of "Upgrade analytics — System tracks" (replaces manual Step 7).

### Controls
- CTL-279 (ensure controlled execution — Loyalty Program Operations (PA-13.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
Per offer: 30 sec prompt + 2 min if accepted. System processes: automated. Average impact: ~1 minute added per qualifying transaction.

### Staffing Implication
No incremental headcount. Upgrade offers are delivered within existing cashier checkout flow. No additional training beyond standard loyalty program training (W518).

## W822. Customer Loyalty Tier Benefit Fulfillment & Welcome Package Processing

|---|---|

BuildRight's loyalty program has 4 tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) with ~600,000 members per CRM-001 through CRM-005. W104 covers financial governance of the loyalty program, but not the operational fulfillment.

| 7 | **Monthly benefit fulfillment analytics**: (a) upgrade conversion rate, (b) physical card delivery SLA compliance, (c) benefit activation rate by tier, (d) upgrade-induced incremental revenue per W565 | Loyalty Program Manager | Marketing Manager | 3 hours/month |

- W263 enrollment — new member trigger
- W104 financial governance — benefit cost management
- W570 points expiry — expiry management coordination
- W17 loyalty operations — loyalty system operations
- W708 customer communication — welcome package delivery
- W674 partner management — partner benefit activation

- Benefit awareness gap — customers don't know their benefits; communication must be clear, accessible, and repeated across channels
- Partner benefit integration failures — partner systems may not reflect tier benefits in real-time; reconciliation needed

- Benefit cost escalation — generous tier benefits can erode program profitability; quarterly review per W104 is essential

- System auto-generate of "generates benefit activation package" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-dispatch of "dispatched via courier or available for store pickup" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-279 (ensure controlled execution — Loyalty Program Operations (PA-13.2).)
- operational: Loyalty Program Manager review and approval gate

### Time Estimate

Per event: mostly automated. Physical card production: batch process 2 hours/week. Monthly analytics: 3 hours. Quarterly benefit review: 4-6 hours.

### Staffing Implication

Absorbed by Loyalty Program Manager and Marketing Coordinator; ~10–15 hours/week. No incremental headcount.

## W1036. Customer Digital Product Passport & Sustainability Information Access

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer scans product QR code in-store or accesses product sustainability information on buildright.com.ph |
| **Frequency** | ~20,000–30,000 scans/month chain-wide; growing with sustainability awareness |
| **Volume** | 1 scan per customer interaction; ~35,000 SKUs with digital passports |
| **Owner** | Category Manager (content); ESG Officer (compliance) |
| **Participants** | Customer, Product Content Manager, Vendor, ESG Data Analyst, Marketing Coordinator |

### Background

Sustainability transparency is increasingly important to Philippine consumers, especially among younger demographics and professional trade customers who must comply with green building certification requirements (BERDE/LEED per W800). BuildRight Depot's product categories have significant sustainability dimensions: lumber (sustainable forestry certification — FSC/PEFC), paint (VOC content per PNS paint standards, lead-free certification per DENR CCO 2013-24), cement (carbon footprint per ton, blended vs. ordinary Portland cement), electrical products (energy efficiency rating per DOE Philippine Energy Label), plumbing fixtures (water efficiency), and insulation materials (recycled content, formaldehyde-free). The Digital Product Passport (DPP) is a QR code printed on shelf labels (W181) and displayed on product pages that, when scanned, shows the customer: product environmental certifications, carbon footprint data, material composition, end-of-life disposal guidance, and manufacturer sustainability practices. This workflow manages the creation, maintenance, and customer access of DPP content. While the EU is mandating DPPs for certain product categories by 2027, the Philippines has no current mandate — but BuildRight implements DPPs proactively as a differentiator and ESG commitment (per W192, W194, W694). This is distinct from W1019 (SDS customer access for hazmat) and W50 (PIM); it specifically focuses on sustainability information access.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

### Background

BuildRight Depot's loyalty program awards 1 point per PHP 100 spent (1 point = PHP 1.00 redemption value) to ~600,000 registered members across Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers. With ~55% of transactions carrying loyalty members and ~PHP 62.3B in annual revenue, approximately PHP 34.3B in annual revenue generates loyalty points — yielding ~343 million points earned per year (PHP 343M in potential redemption value). Under Philippine Financial Reporting Standards (PFRS 15 — Revenue from Contracts with Customers), loyalty points represent a **performance obligation** — BuildRight has received payment for goods AND a promise to deliver future value (redeemable points). The accounting treatment is: (a) at point of sale: defer a portion of revenue equal to the stand-alone selling price of the loyalty points (estimated redemption value), recording the deferral as a liability (deferred revenue — loyalty points), (b) as points are redeemed: recognize deferred revenue as actual revenue (offset by the cost of goods or services provided at redemption), (c) for unredeemed points (breakage): estimate breakage rate (% of points that will never be redeemed) and recognize breakage as revenue over time per PFRS 15. The loyalty points liability on BuildRight's balance sheet is estimated at PHP 150–250M — a material amount that requires systematic tracking, accurate forecasting, and proper financial reporting. This workflow manages the financial management of the loyalty program, complementing W17 (loyalty program operations) which handles the operational aspects.

BuildRight Depot's loyalty program ("BuildRight Rewards") with ~600,000 members partners with 15–20 external companies to enhance member benefits: (a) partner banks (credit card earn rates: 2× points on BuildRight purchases with partner cards), (b) fuel companies (points earned on fuel purchases, redeemable at BuildRight), (c) home insurance providers (discounted premiums for Gold/Platinum members), (d) home service providers (free installation assessment for Silver+ members), (e) partner retailers (cross-shopping earn opportunities). These partnerships create financial obligations: (a) when a member earns points at a partner, BuildRight must reimburse the partner for the points cost (partner pays BuildRight a per-point fee for redemption at BuildRight; BuildRight pays partner a per-point fee for points earned at partner), (b) tier benefits (free delivery for Gold members, exclusive pricing for Platinum) have costs that must be allocated between BuildRight and the benefit provider, (c) partner promotions (double points weekends, member-only discounts) require cost-sharing agreements and settlement, (d) the total loyalty program cost (points liability, tier benefits, partner fees) must be tracked and compared to program revenue impact (increased basket size, higher visit frequency, reduced churn) to justify continued investment. This workflow manages the quarterly partner rebate settlement, tier benefit cost allocation, and annual ROI analysis.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Quarterly partner transaction reconciliation**: (a) Loyalty Program Manager reconciles partner transactions for the quarter: (i) total points earned at partner locations by BuildRight members (BuildRight owes partner for these points — typically PHP 0.50–1.00 per point earned), (ii) total points redeemed at BuildRight by partner program members (partner owes BuildRight — typically PHP 0.80–1.20 per point redeemed), (iii) net settlement = (points earned at partner × BuildRight cost/point) − (points redeemed at BuildRight × partner reimbursement/point), (b) verify transaction counts against partner reporting: resolve discrepancies (missing transactions, incorrect point values, system integration errors) | Loyalty Program Manager / Finance Analyst | Controller | 2–3 days/quarter |

| 4 | **Annual loyalty program ROI analysis**: (a) Finance Analyst prepares annual loyalty program ROI: (i) total program costs: (A) points liability increase (W1274), (B) partner settlements, (C) tier benefit costs (delivery, discounts, services), (D) program operations (staff, technology, marketing), (ii) total program revenue impact: (E) incremental revenue from loyalty members vs. non-members (loyalty members have ~20% higher ATV and ~15% higher visit frequency per industry benchmarks), (F) reduced churn value (retained members × annual revenue per member), (G) partner referral revenue, (iii) ROI = (E + F + G − A − B − C − D) ÷ (A + B + C + D), (b) CMO presents ROI to CEO/CFO: is the loyalty program generating positive returns? should tier structure be adjusted? should partner portfolio be expanded or reduced? | Finance Analyst / Loyalty Program Manager | CMO / CFO | 1–2 weeks/year |

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Revenue attribution difficulty**: it is challenging to attribute incremental revenue specifically to the loyalty program vs. other factors (marketing, seasonal demand, store openings); the ROI analysis depends on assumptions about counterfactual behavior (what would members have spent without the loyalty program?)
- **Partner data quality**: partner transaction reporting may be delayed, incomplete, or in a different format than BuildRight's system; reconciliation discrepancies are common and time-consuming to resolve
- **Points liability growth**: as the loyalty program grows to 600,000+ members with increasing point balances, the financial liability per W1274 grows; partner settlements add to program cost without a guaranteed proportional revenue increase
- **Partner dependency risk**: if a key partner (e.g., a major bank providing credit card earn) terminates the agreement, the loyalty program's value proposition may be significantly weakened for card-linked members; mitigated by the Loyalty Program Manager review and approval gate and operational: periodic reconciliation against source documents

## W1499. Customer Loyalty Program Tier Qualification Period Reset, Points Expiration Management & Downgrade Communication

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual tier qualification period ends (calendar year); also monthly points expiration sweep for points older than 24 months |
| **Frequency** | Annual tier reset (January); monthly points expiration sweep; quarterly downgrade prevention campaign |
| **Volume** | ~600,000 loyalty program members; ~30% qualify for Silver or higher tier; ~7–10% of members downgraded each year (~40,000–60,000, per W1217); ~5–10% of outstanding points expire annually |
| **Owner** | Loyalty Program Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Loyalty Program Coordinator, Marketing Associate, Customer Service Representative, BI Analyst |

### Background

BuildRight's loyalty program "BuildRight Rewards" has a tiered structure: Bronze (entry level, all enrolled members), Silver (PHP 50,000+ annual spend), Gold (PHP 150,000+ annual spend), and Platinum (PHP 500,000+ annual spend or verified trade professional). Tiers are re-evaluated annually based on the preceding calendar year's purchases. Points accumulated through purchases expire 24 months after earning if unused. This creates two operational challenges: (a) tier downgrades — customers who don't maintain their spending level lose tier benefits, which can trigger dissatisfaction and defection; and (b) points expiration — customers who lose accumulated points feel penalized, creating negative sentiment. Both require proactive communication and targeted retention campaigns to minimize churn while maintaining the economic integrity of the program.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Annual tier recalculation** (December): (a) BI Analyst runs year-to-date spend aggregation for all loyalty members; (b) system compares each member's 12-month spend to tier thresholds; (c) members who achieved a higher tier: upgraded effective January 1; (d) members who fall below their current tier threshold: flagged for downgrade review; (e) edge cases reviewed: members within 10% of threshold (e.g., Silver member at PHP 47,000 spend — should they be "rounded up"?); (f) final tier assignments approved by Loyalty Program Coordinator with VP Merchandising sign-off for any exception overrides | BI Analyst / System | Loyalty Program Coordinator | 2–3 days in December |
| 2 | **Downgrade prevention campaign** (November): Before tier reset: (a) Loyalty Program Coordinator identifies members at risk of downgrade: (i) currently Silver/Gold/Platinum but projected to fall below threshold based on year-to-date spend and typical December purchasing pattern; (ii) count by tier: ~25,000–45,000 Silver at risk, ~10,000–15,000 Gold at risk, ~2,500–4,000 Platinum at risk (consistent with the ~40,000–60,000 annual downgrades); (b) targeted retention campaign launched: (i) Silver at risk: "You're PHP X away from keeping Silver — shop now to maintain your benefits"; personalized SMS/email with amount needed; (ii) Gold at risk: same messaging + exclusive December in-store event invitation; (iii) Platinum at risk: personal call from Loyalty Program Coordinator offering one-time tier extension for members within 15% of threshold (discretionary override); (c) campaign runs November 15 – December 31; daily monitoring of qualifying purchases | Loyalty Program Coordinator / Marketing Associate | VP Merchandising | 3–4 weeks |
| 3 | **Tier downgrade communication** (January): For members who do downgrade: (a) personalized email sent January 2 explaining: (i) new tier effective immediately; (ii) specific benefits lost; (iii) how to requalify in the current year; (iv) any grace period benefits (e.g., Silver benefits extended through January as courtesy); (b) SMS notification for members without email on file; (c) for Platinum-to-Gold downgrades: personal call from Loyalty Program Coordinator acknowledging the change and reiterating value of continued membership; (d) all downgrade communications reviewed by Marketing Associate for tone sensitivity | Loyalty Program Coordinator / Marketing Associate | VP Merchandising | 1 week |
| 4 | **Monthly points expiration sweep**: Monthly: (a) system identifies points earned > 24 months ago that have not been redeemed; (b) 60-day warning: members with points expiring in 60 days receive email/SMS: "Your PHP X worth of points expire on [date] — redeem now!"; (c) 30-day warning: final reminder with specific redemption suggestions (product recommendations based on purchase history); (d) on expiration date: unredeemed points zeroed out; (e) expiration value tracked for program liability accounting (points liability reduction = PHP value of expired points); (f) monthly expiration report: total points expired, number of members affected, average value per member | System / Loyalty Program Coordinator | CFO (for liability impact) | 2–3 hours/month |
| 5 | **Points expiration redemption campaign**: For members in the 60-day warning window: (a) Marketing Associate creates targeted promotions: (i) "Double Points Weekend" for purchases that earn points (not relevant for expiring points but drives engagement); (ii) curated "Redeem Your Points" product selections in the loyalty app; (iii) limited-time bonus: "Redeem expiring points for 1.5x value on select items this weekend"; (b) campaign effectiveness tracked: redemption rate for expiring-point members vs. general member base; (c) Loyalty Program Coordinator adjusts campaign tactics based on monthly results | Marketing Associate / Loyalty Program Coordinator | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Loyalty member spend aggregation engine with annual tier calculation (W1499.1)
- Tier risk projection model based on year-to-date spend and historical December patterns (W1499.2)
- Automated tier downgrade communication engine with personalized messaging (W1499.3)
- Points aging tracker with 24-month expiration sweep and tiered warning notifications (W1499.4)
- Points liability accounting integration for expired points write-off (W1499.4)
- Targeted redemption campaign module with product recommendation engine (W1499.5)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Downgrade churn**: data shows ~25% of downgraded members do not make any purchase in the following 6 months, vs. 10% churn for members who maintain their tier; each downgrade represents potential revenue loss
- **Customer hostility to points expiration**: customers who lose PHP 500+ worth of points feel "robbed"; negative social media posts and NPS score drops are common in the month after expiration sweeps
- **Grace period consistency**: if BuildRight extends grace periods for some members (Platinum within 15% of threshold) but not others, perceived unfairness damages the program's reputation
- **Accounting complexity**: points liability must be estimated and accrued monthly; expired points reduce the liability but require careful revenue recognition treatment

- System auto-count of "count by tier" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-file of "Tier downgrade communication" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls

- operational: Loyalty Program Coordinator review and approval gate

- BI Analyst: ~1 day on tier calculation query. Absorbed.
- Marketing Associate: ~1–2 days/month on expiration campaigns. Absorbed.
- No incremental headcount.

- Prevention campaign: ~20 hours over Nov–Dec
- Monthly expiration: 2–3 hours + 1–2 days campaign
- **Annual: ~200–250 hours total across involved roles**
