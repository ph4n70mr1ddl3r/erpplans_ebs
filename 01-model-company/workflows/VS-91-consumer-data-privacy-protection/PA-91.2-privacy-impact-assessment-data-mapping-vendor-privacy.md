## W3049. Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) & DPIA for New Initiatives

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New initiative/system processing personal data, or material change |
| **Frequency** | Per initiative |
| **Volume** | New product launches, app features, analytics/AI use cases |
| **Owner** | Data Protection Officer (DPO) |
| **Participants** | DPO, Project Sponsor, IT, Legal, Security |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project Sponsor completes PIA screening for new initiative (app feature, loyalty analytics, AI/profiling, biometrics); DPO determines if full DPIA required (high-risk) | Project Sponsor / DPO | DPO | Per initiative |
| 2 | DPO leads DPIA: necessity/proportionality, risks to subjects, mitigations, residual risk, and go/no-go recommendation; consults subjects where appropriate | DPO | DPO | Per initiative |
| 3 | DPO/Steering Committee decide on processing; mandate mitigations; document decision; re-review on material change | DPO / Steering Committee | VP Legal & Compliance | Per initiative |

### System Touchpoints
- PIA/DPIA register; project intake gate (links to VS-30); decision log

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Late-gate risk**: Privacy considered after launch; mitigated by PIA gate in project intake


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "DPO/Steering Committee decide on processing" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-503 (ensure controlled execution — Privacy Impact Assessment, Data Mapping & Vendor Privacy Due Diligence (PA-91.2).)
- operational: PIA gate in project intake

### Time Estimate
- 1–4 weeks per initiative

## W3051. Data Processing Agreement (DPA) & Vendor Privacy Due Diligence

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Onboarding processor handling personal data |
| **Frequency** | Per vendor + renewal |
| **Volume** | ~100–200 processors among ~800–1,000 vendors + SaaS/IT providers |
| **Owner** | Vendor Management Manager |
| **Participants** | Vendor Mgmt, DPO, Legal, IT Security |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vendor Mgmt/DPO screen vendors processing personal data; assess privacy posture (certifications, security, sub-processors, breach history) | Vendor Mgmt / DPO | Vendor Management Manager | Per vendor |
| 2 | Legal/DPO execute Data Processing Agreement (DPA) defining purpose, data, sub-processors, security, breach notice, audit rights, deletion on exit | Legal / DPO | VP Legal & Compliance | Per vendor |
| 3 | Vendor Mgmt integrate DPA requirement into vendor onboarding (links to VS-03) and contract lifecycle; block non-compliant processors | Vendor Mgmt | Vendor Management Manager | Per vendor |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor onboarding (links to VS-03); contract/DPA repository (links to VS-88); sub-processor register

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Processor-risk**: Vendor mishandles data exposing BuildRight; mitigated by DPA and due diligence


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-post of "Vendor Mgmt/DPO screen vendors processing personal data" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System continuous audit of "audit rights, deletion on exit" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-503 (ensure controlled execution — Privacy Impact Assessment, Data Mapping & Vendor Privacy Due Diligence (PA-91.2).)
- operational: DPA and due diligence

### Time Estimate
- Per vendor

| **Frequency** | Per design + periodic review |
| **Volume** | Enterprise systems |
| **Owner** | Data Protection Officer (DPO) |
| **Participants** | DPO, IT, Data Governance, Records, Business Process Owners |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DPO/Data Governance apply data minimization: collect only necessary personal data; challenge purpose vs. fields collected at each touchpoint | DPO / Data Governance | DPO | Per design |

- System auto-record of "Records/DPO define retention schedule per data category aligned to…" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "IT embeds privacy-by-design defaults" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-503 (ensure controlled execution — Privacy Impact Assessment, Data Mapping & Vendor Privacy Due Diligence (PA-91.2).)

## W3055. CCTV / Surveillance Privacy Impact & Signage Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | CCTV deployment or review |
| **Frequency** | Per site + annual review |
| **Volume** | 200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ |
| **Owner** | Data Protection Officer (DPO) |
| **Participants** | DPO, LP, Store Ops, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DPO/LP assess CCTV coverage for necessity and proportionality (security vs. privacy); restrict cameras from private/excess areas; document lawful basis (legitimate security interest) | DPO / LP | DPO | Per site + annual |
| 2 | Store Ops/LP install compliant privacy signage at all monitored entrances/areas; inform visitors of surveillance and purpose | Store Ops / LP | Store Operations Manager | Per site |
| 3 | LP/DPO enforce retention and access controls on CCTV footage; restrict viewing to authorized security purposes; delete per retention schedule | LP / DPO | LP Manager | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- CCTV system (links to VS-23); retention/access control (links to VS-27/VS-88); signage compliance

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Excess-surveillance risk**: Over-broad CCTV captures private behavior; mitigated by proportionality assessment and signage


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "document lawful basis" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-monitor of "Store Ops/LP install compliant privacy signage at all monitored" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-schedule of "LP/DPO enforce retention and access controls on CCTV footage" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-503 (ensure controlled execution — Privacy Impact Assessment, Data Mapping & Vendor Privacy Due Diligence (PA-91.2).)
