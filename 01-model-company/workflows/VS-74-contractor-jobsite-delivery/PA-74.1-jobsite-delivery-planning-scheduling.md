## W2622. Contractor Job Site Delivery Order Capture & Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contractor places bulk order for job site delivery |
| **Frequency** | ~300–500 deliveries/month |
| **Volume** | ~300–500/month |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Representative |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Rep, Logistics Coordinator, Contractor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Rep captures order: delivery address, site contact, access restrictions, required delivery window, special equipment needed (crane, boom truck) | Trade Sales Rep | Regional Sales Manager | 20–30 min |
| 2 | Logistics Coordinator plans delivery: maps route to job site, verifies truck accessibility, coordinates with contractor for site preparation, schedules delivery within agreed window | Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 30–45 min |
| 3 | System generates delivery order with job site details: GPS coordinates, site contact, special handling instructions; assigns to appropriate vehicle type; contractor receives SMS confirmation with ETA | System / Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Site-access surprise risk**: a truck dispatched to a job site it cannot enter (weight limits, no turning room) returns full and re-delivers another day; mitigated by the access-restriction capture and truck-accessibility verification (Steps 1–2) before dispatch
- **Window-miss risk**: deliveries arriving outside the agreed window find no receiving crew and idle the vehicle; mitigated by the delivery-window agreement and the GPS-tagged delivery order with SMS confirmation (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Delivery scheduling auto-optimized from the route/dock/traffic data; real-time tracking auto-pushed to customer and store; POD auto-captured digitally

### Controls
- CTL-451 (ensure controlled execution — Job Site Delivery Planning & Scheduling (PA-74.1).)
- operational: Trade Sales Representative sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~20–30 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~300–500/month
- Frequency: ~300–500 deliveries/month
- Absorbed into the Trade Sales Representative role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2623. Multi-Drop Job Site Delivery Route Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Multiple contractor deliveries in same region |
| **Frequency** | Daily per DC |
| **Volume** | 4 DCs; ~300–500 deliveries/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Coord, Drivers, 3PL Partners, DC Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics Coordinator consolidates orders by region: groups 2–4 job site deliveries per route; sequences by proximity and time window; ensures total weight within vehicle capacity | Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 1–2 hours |
| 2 | System optimizes route considering: road restrictions, job site access times, unloading duration per site, driver hours of service compliance | System / Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 15–30 min |
| 3 | DC Ops picks and loads multi-drop delivery: organizes by drop sequence; includes delivery manifest per drop; driver receives route plan via mobile app with GPS navigation | DC Ops / Driver | DC Ops Manager | 2–3 hours loading |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Capacity-overflow risk**: a multi-drop route loaded past vehicle capacity fails mid-sequence and re-sequences the day; mitigated by the total-weight check within vehicle capacity (Step 1) before the route plan issues
- **Hours-of-service breach risk**: a route whose drive-plus-unload time exceeds driver hours stalls at the farthest drop; mitigated by the optimizer's hours-of-service compliance (Step 2) with the drop-sequence manifest loading (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Delivery scheduling auto-optimized from the route/dock/traffic data; real-time tracking auto-pushed to customer and store; POD auto-captured digitally

### Controls
- CTL-451 (ensure controlled execution — Job Site Delivery Planning & Scheduling (PA-74.1).)
- operational: Logistics Coordinator sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 hours per occurrence
- Volume: 4 DCs; ~300–500 deliveries/month
- Frequency: Daily per DC
- Absorbed into the Logistics Coordinator role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

- **Receiving-crew gap risk**: materials lifted to placement with no contractor personnel to receive them travel back down; mitigated by the receiving-personnel confirmation (Step 2) and the signed delivery receipt at execution (Step 3)

## W2625. Phased Construction Delivery Schedule Management

| **Frequency** | Per major project; ~20–30 active projects |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 active projects |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Representative |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Rep, Logistics, Supply Planning, Contractor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Rep develops phased delivery schedule with contractor: project phases (foundation → framing → electrical → finishing), material quantities per phase, delivery dates aligned with construction timeline | Trade Sales Rep / Contractor | Regional Sales Manager | 2–3 hours |
| 2 | Supply Planning reserves inventory for phased deliveries: sets aside materials for future phases; adjusts ROP and safety stock for high-demand project SKUs | Supply Planning | VP Supply Chain | 1 day |
| 3 | Logistics Coordinator manages ongoing phased delivery calendar: confirms dates 3 days before each phase, adjusts for construction delays; system tracks completion against plan | Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Phase-mismatch risk**: materials delivered for a phase the construction schedule has not reached sit exposed on site and degrade; mitigated by the phase-aligned delivery dates (Step 1) with the 3-day confirmation (Step 3) before each phase ships
- **Reservation-strand risk**: inventory reserved for future phases starves near-term demand as schedules slip; mitigated by the Supply Planning reservation with ROP/safety-stock adjustment (Step 2) and the delay-adjustable calendar (Step 3)

## W2627. Job Site Delivery Access & Permit Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Delivery requires access planning |
| **Frequency** | Per delivery requiring coordination |
| **Volume** | ~100–150/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Coord, Contractor, LGU Traffic Office |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics Coordinator verifies job site access: gate location, security clearance, vehicle restrictions, unloading area; for Metro Manila: checks LGU truck ban hours (6–9AM and 4–8PM) | Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 15–30 min |
| 2 | Coordinator arranges special access if needed: LGU delivery permit, barangay clearance, temporary road closure coordination, security guard notification | Logistics Coordinator / Contractor | Logistics Manager | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Driver confirms site access upon arrival; if denied: contacts Logistics Coordinator for alternative; documents access issues for future planning | Driver / Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 15 min per delivery |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Truck-ban risk**: Metro Manila deliveries scheduled inside the 6–9AM/4–8PM truck-ban windows are stopped at the city edge; mitigated by the LGU truck-ban hours check (Step 1) before scheduling and the special-access arrangements (Step 2)
- **Turned-away blindness risk**: access denials resolved case-by-case repeat on the next delivery to the same site; mitigated by the denied-access documentation (Step 3) feeding future planning and the LGU permit/barangay clearance path (Step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
