## W2638. Contractor Delivery On-Time Performance Measurement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly reporting cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~300–500 deliveries/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Analyst |
| **Participants** | Logistics Analyst, Logistics Manager, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics Analyst compiles contractor delivery OTIF metrics: On-Time, In-Full, Damage-Free; calculates score per DC and contractor segment | Logistics Analyst | Logistics Manager | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Analyst identifies trends: compares vs. standard replenishment, identifies chronic late routes, analyzes root causes; produces Pareto analysis | Logistics Analyst | Logistics Manager | 1 day |
| 3 | Logistics Manager reviews with VP Supply Chain: proposes corrective actions, evaluates 3PL performance, sets improvement targets | Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1 day/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric-gaming risk**: OTIF scored before site verification flatters the metric the customer already disputes; mitigated by the Damage-Free third leg (Step 1) and the trend/root-cause analysis (Step 2) over chronic late routes
- **Segment-blindness risk**: a fleet-average score hiding one contractor segment's chronic failures; mitigated by the per-DC and per-segment scoring (Step 1) reviewed to VP Supply Chain (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Root-cause clustering auto-run on emergency-delivery and failed-delivery events; OTIF trend dashboard auto-refreshed from POD data; carrier/scorecard metrics auto-updated monthly

### Controls
- CTL-453 (ensure controlled execution — Job Site Delivery Performance & Analytics (PA-74.3).)
- operational: Logistics Analyst sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~300–500 deliveries/month
- Frequency: Monthly
- Absorbed into the Logistics Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2639. Contractor Delivery Cost per Order Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly cost analysis |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~300–500 deliveries/month |
| **Owner** | Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Analyst, Logistics Manager, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance Analyst calculates delivery cost per contractor order: direct costs (fuel, driver, 3PL), indirect costs, special equipment surcharges; compares against fee charged | Finance Analyst | Finance Controller | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Analyst identifies cost optimization: consolidate routes, negotiate better 3PL rates, reduce crane frequency, encourage self-pickup for smaller orders | Finance Analyst / Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1 day |
| 3 | Finance Controller reviews contractor delivery profitability: revenue vs. cost; identifies money-losing routes; proposes fee adjustments or minimum orders for free delivery | Finance Controller | CFO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fee-cost inversion risk**: delivery fees set below true direct-plus-indirect cost quietly sell every contractor delivery at a loss; mitigated by the cost-vs-fee comparison (Step 1) with the Finance Controller profitability review and fee adjustments (Step 3)
- **Optimization-churn risk**: consolidation changes made without modeling disrupt the service the fees fund; mitigated by the modeled levers (Step 2: route consolidation, 3PL rates, crane frequency, self-pickup) quantified before implementation

### Automation Opportunity
- Pricing auto-computed from the cost + margin target; settlement auto-reconciled with discrepancy-flagging; invoice auto-generated from the POD/delivery record

### Controls
- CTL-453 (ensure controlled execution — Job Site Delivery Performance & Analytics (PA-74.3).)
- operational: Finance Analyst sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 days per occurrence (per the step durations)
- Volume: ~300–500 deliveries/month
- Frequency: Monthly
- Absorbed into the Finance Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Experience sends delivery satisfaction survey post-delivery: timeliness, driver professionalism, material condition, scheduling ease; target >30% response | Customer Experience | COO | Automated |

## W2641. Contractor Delivery Route Optimization Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly review cycle — Contractor Delivery Route |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | 4 DCs; ~50–100 routes |
| **Owner** | Logistics Analyst |
| **Participants** | Logistics Analyst, Logistics Manager, DC Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics Analyst maps all contractor routes: identifies overlapping routes, underutilized trucks, excessive empty return miles; uses GIS analysis | Logistics Analyst | Logistics Manager | 1 week |
| 2 | Analyst models optimization: combine stores + contractor deliveries, dedicated contractor delivery days, zone-based scheduling; quantifies savings | Logistics Analyst | Logistics Manager | 1 week |
| 3 | Logistics Manager implements optimizations: adjusts schedules, communicates to contractors, monitors 4 weeks; reports savings quarterly | Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2–4 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113 BI platform, W231 QBR dashboard)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Empty-miles blindness risk**: overlapping routes and empty returns invisible without the GIS view keep paying for dead kilometers; mitigated by the GIS route mapping (Step 1) quantifying overlap, underutilization and empty return miles
- **Change-shock risk**: schedule optimizations imposed without contractor communication read as service degradation; mitigated by the contractor communication and the 4-week monitored rollout (Step 3) with quarterly savings reporting

### Automation Opportunity
- Root-cause clustering auto-run on emergency-delivery and failed-delivery events; OTIF trend dashboard auto-refreshed from POD data; carrier/scorecard metrics auto-updated monthly

### Controls
- CTL-453 (ensure controlled execution — Job Site Delivery Performance & Analytics (PA-74.3).)
