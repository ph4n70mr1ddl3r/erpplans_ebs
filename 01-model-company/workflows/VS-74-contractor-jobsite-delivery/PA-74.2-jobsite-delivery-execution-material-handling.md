## W2630. Job Site Delivery Unloading & Material Placement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Truck arrives at construction site |
| **Frequency** | ~300–500 deliveries/month |
| **Volume** | ~300–500/month |
| **Owner** | Driver / Delivery Crew |
| **Participants** | Driver, Delivery Crew, Contractor Site Foreman |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Driver arrives at site: contacts foreman, confirms unloading location, verifies site access and safety (hard hat area, PPE required); positions truck | Driver | Logistics Coordinator | 15–20 min |
| 2 | Delivery crew and contractor personnel unload: hand-bomb materials if no forklift/crane; place in designated staging area per contractor direction; separate by type and phase | Delivery Crew / Contractor Personnel | Driver | 30–90 min |
| 3 | Site foreman verifies delivery against manifest: counts items, notes damage or shortage, signs receipt; Driver photographs delivered materials as proof | Site Foreman / Driver | Logistics Coordinator | 15–20 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Placement-chaos risk**: materials dumped unlabeled across the site bury the framing stock under finishing materials; mitigated by the designated staging area with separation by type and phase (Step 2) per contractor direction
- **Shortage-at-site risk**: counts disputed after the truck departs have no evidence trail; mitigated by the foreman's manifest verification with damage/shortage notes and the driver's placement photographs (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Delivery scheduling auto-optimized from the route/dock/traffic data; real-time tracking auto-pushed to customer and store; POD auto-captured digitally

### Controls
- CTL-452 (ensure controlled execution — Job Site Delivery Execution & Material Handling (PA-74.2).)
- operational: Driver / Delivery Crew sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~15–20 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~300–500/month
- Frequency: ~300–500 deliveries/month
- Absorbed into the Driver / Delivery Crew role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contractor reports damage or shortage with photos; Trade Sales Rep logs incident in ERP; severity classification: minor (<PHP 5K), moderate, major (>PHP 50K) | Contractor / Trade Sales Rep | Regional Sales Manager | 15–30 min |

- **Pattern-blindness risk**: repeated minor claims absorbed individually never surface the theft-or-abuse pattern; mitigated by the severity classification (Step 1: <PHP 5K, moderate, >PHP 50K) with LP investigation of patterns (Step 3)

## W2632. Delivery Return & Excess Material Pickup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contractor has excess materials for return |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; ~50–100 returns/month |
| **Volume** | ~50–100/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Coord, Trade Sales Rep, Contractor, Receiving Clerk |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contractor requests return: provides PO reference, item list; Rep verifies eligibility (within 30 days, unused condition); creates return authorization | Contractor / Trade Sales Rep | Regional Sales Manager | 15–20 min |
| 2 | Logistics Coordinator schedules pickup: combines with next delivery to same area; driver collects with return manifest | Logistics Coordinator / Driver | Logistics Manager | 1–3 days |
| 3 | Receiving Clerk processes return: inspects condition, verifies against authorization, processes goods receipt; Finance issues credit note | Receiving Clerk / Finance | Store Manager | 1–2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Ineligible-return risk**: used or stale materials collected on site fail inspection and re-deliver at full logistics cost; mitigated by the eligibility verification (Step 1: within 30 days, unused condition) before authorization
- **Dedicated-pickup cost risk**: return pickups dispatched as standalone trips carry full cost for one pallet; mitigated by the pickup combined with the next same-area delivery (Step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "verifies eligibility (within 30 days, unused condition)" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "processes goods receipt; Finance issues credit note" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-452 (ensure controlled execution — Job Site Delivery Execution & Material Handling (PA-74.2).)
- operational: Logistics Coordinator sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~15–20 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~50–100/month
- Frequency: Ad-hoc; ~50–100 returns/month
- Absorbed into the Logistics Coordinator role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2633. Job Site Delivery Proof of Delivery & Documentation

| **Frequency** | Per delivery; ~300–500/month |
| **Volume** | ~300–500/month |
| **Owner** | Driver |
| **Participants** | Driver, Logistics Coordinator, Trade Sales Rep, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Driver collects POD: signed receipt from foreman (paper or electronic), photographs of materials at placement, GPS coordinates, delivery timestamp | Driver | Logistics Coordinator | 10 min per delivery |
| 2 | System uploads POD data: matches to delivery order, records GPS and timestamp, stores photos; auto-matches to sales order for billing | System / Driver | Logistics Coordinator | Automated |
| 3 | Finance uses POD for billing: triggers invoice for trade accounts; settles 3PL payment upon confirmed delivery; archives for 10 years per BIR | Finance | Finance Controller | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **POD-gap risk**: deliveries without complete proof of delivery (signature, photos, GPS, timestamp) unwind into billing disputes and BIR exposure; mitigated by the four-part POD capture (Step 1) matched to the delivery order in the system (Step 2)
- **Billing-trigger failure risk**: invoicing triggered without confirmed delivery bills ahead of receipt or not at all; mitigated by the POD-gated invoicing and 3PL settlement (Step 3) with 10-year BIR archiving

### Automation Opportunity
- Delivery scheduling auto-optimized from the route/dock/traffic data; real-time tracking auto-pushed to customer and store; POD auto-captured digitally

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per delivery to active sites |
| **Volume** | ~200–300/month |
| **Owner** | Driver |
| **Participants** | Driver, Safety Officer, Contractor Site Safety Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Driver dons required PPE: hard hat, safety vest, safety shoes; attends site safety briefing; identifies hazards (excavations, overhead work, equipment) | Driver | Logistics Coordinator | 10–15 min |
| 2 | Driver follows site-specific safety rules: speed limit, designated routes, prohibited areas, emergency assembly point; reports unsafe conditions | Driver / Safety Officer | Logistics Manager | During delivery |
| 3 | Safety Officer conducts random compliance checks; tracks safety incident rate (target: zero); incorporates into driver training | Safety Officer | COO | Monthly review |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Compliance/risk (links to VS-22 / regulatory, VS-86 / AML, VS-91 / privacy)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **PPE-noncompliance risk**: a driver refused at the gate for missing PPE re-delivers another day; mitigated by the full PPE and site safety-briefing requirement (Step 1) before site entry
- **Hazard-blindness risk**: drivers operating near excavations or overhead work without the site's hazard picture create the incident the rules exist to prevent; mitigated by the site-specific rules and hazard identification (Steps 1–2) with random Safety Officer compliance checks (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity

| **Volume** | ~50–80/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Coord, Driver, Contractor, 3PL Dump Truck Partner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Logistics Coordinator arranges bulk delivery (sand, gravel, cement): requires dump trucks; coordinates with DSD vendor or 3PL; confirms contractor has designated dump area | Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | 1 day |
| 2 | Driver delivers: positions dump truck, tips load carefully; contractor foreman visually estimates quantity; photographs tipped load | Driver / Contractor Foreman | Logistics Coordinator | 30–60 min |
| 3 | System processes bulk delivery: catch-weight recorded with estimated weight; Finance bills based on delivered weight; Logistics tracks cost per cubic meter | System / Finance / Logistics | Finance Controller | Per delivery |

### System Touchpoints
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Catch-weight dispute risk**: visually estimated bulk quantities billed on estimated weight invite short-load claims; mitigated by the catch-weight recording with tipped-load photographs (Step 2) and delivered-weight billing (Step 3)
- **Dump-area failure risk**: a tip executed without a designated dump area blocks the site or buries equipment; mitigated by the dump-area confirmation with the contractor (Step 1) before dispatch

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-demand auto-forecast from the project schedule; delivery-phasing auto-sequenced; site-reconciliation auto-tracked from the delivery-vs-installed delta

### Controls
- CTL-452 (ensure controlled execution — Job Site Delivery Execution & Material Handling (PA-74.2).)

## W2637. Job Site Delivery Real-Time Tracking & ETA Communication

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Delivery dispatched from DC or store |
| **Frequency** | Per delivery |
| **Volume** | ~300–500/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Coord, Driver, Contractor, Trade Sales Rep |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System sends automated ETA notification to contractor when dispatched: estimated arrival, driver name and contact, vehicle plate, delivery contents summary | System | Logistics Coordinator | Automated |
| 2 | Driver updates status in mobile app: departed, en route (GPS tracking), arrived, unloading started, complete; contractor tracks via app | Driver / System | Logistics Coordinator | Per status update |
| 3 | Logistics Coordinator manages ETA exceptions: if delayed >30 min, notifies contractor with updated ETA; tracks on-time delivery rate (target: ≥90%) | Logistics Coordinator | Logistics Manager | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **ETA-silence risk**: a delay communicated only on arrival strands the contractor's receiving crew; mitigated by the automated ETA notification at dispatch (Step 1) and the >30-minute exception notification (Step 3) against the ≥90% on-time target
- **Status-gap risk**: tracking that drops mid-route hides the truck's true position from a waiting site; mitigated by the driver's in-app status updates (Step 2: departed, en route, arrived, unloading, complete)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-notify of "sends automated ETA notification to contractor when dispatched" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-track of "updates status in mobile app" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-alert of "notifies contractor with updated ETA" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-452 (ensure controlled execution — Job Site Delivery Execution & Material Handling (PA-74.2).)
- operational: Logistics Coordinator sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: ~300–500/month
- Frequency: Per delivery
- Absorbed into the Logistics Coordinator role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
