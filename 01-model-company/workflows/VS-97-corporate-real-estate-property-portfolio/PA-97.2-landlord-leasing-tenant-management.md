## W3193. Master Lease & Intercompany Lease Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New property added (W3187/W3188); intercompany framework refresh; PFRS 16 adoption/maintenance |
| **Frequency** | Per property + periodic framework review |
| **Volume** | ~205+ properties; intercompany lease to BuildRight Depot Inc. on every owned site |
| **Owner** | Lease Administration Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Admin Mgr, Director, Facilities & Real Estate, Finance/Transfer Pricing (links to VS-72), Legal, Tax (links to VS-79) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Admin/Tax (links to VS-79/VS-72) establish the intercompany lease framework: arm's-length rent per transfer-pricing rules (links to VS-72), base-rent methodology (per-sqm market rent benchmarked to comparable third-party leases — W3207), CAM pass-through, escalation formula, party roles (Property Mgmt Inc. as lessor; Depot Inc. as lessee); document arm's-length support per BIR transfer-pricing documentation (RR 02-2013) | Lease Admin Mgr / Tax | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | 4–8 weeks |
| 2 | Legal/Lease Admin create per-property intercompany lease (or master intercompany lease with schedules): premises description, term, rent, CAM, utilities, maintenance responsibilities, insurance (links to W3204), restoration/make-good, dispute resolution; classify lease under PFRS 16 for lessor accounting (operating vs finance lease) | Legal / Lease Admin Mgr | VP Legal & Compliance | 2–4 weeks |
| 3 | Finance (links to VS-17.2/VS-72) book lessor lease and set up straight-line rent recognition (operating lease) or finance-lease receivable (finance lease); configure intercompany rent billing (W3195) and elimination on consolidation (links to VS-17.2) | Finance Analyst | Controller | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease/contract module; transfer pricing (links to VS-72); tax (links to VS-79); GL/consolidation (links to VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Transfer-pricing risk**: Non-arm's-length intercompany rent attracts BIR adjustment and penalties; mitigated by W3207 benchmarking and TP documentation
- **Mis-classification risk**: Wrong PFRS 16 lease classification distorts revenue/finance income; mitigated by lease-accounting policy and review


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "methodology" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Legal/Lease Admin create per-property intercompany lease" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-521 (ensure controlled execution — Landlord Leasing Operations & Tenant Management (PA-97.2).)
- operational: W3207 benchmarking and TP documentation; operational: lease-accounting policy and review

### Time Estimate
- 8–12 weeks initial framework; 1–2 weeks per new property schedule

## W3194. Commercial Tenant Acquisition & Lease Origination

|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Per vacancy |
| **Volume** | ~1–3 third-party tenants per store/property; ~200–600 third-party agreements chain-wide (overlaps W177 concessionaire scope for in-store) |
| **Owner** | Leasing Manager |
| **Participants** | Leasing Mgr, Director, Facilities & Real Estate, Legal, Finance, Store Ops (links to VS-07), Tenant |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leasing Mgr source and qualify tenants for outlot/pad/anchor space: tenant mix strategy (banks, ATMs, food/beverage, courier drop-points, pharmacies — complementary, non-competing with BuildRight); tenant creditworthiness (links to VS-86 KYB/financial review), trade-mix fit, fit-out capability; align with Store Ops (links to VS-07) for site compatibility | Leasing Mgr | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | 4–12 weeks |
| 2 | Negotiate commercial terms: base rent (fixed + percentage rent), CAM/utilities recovery, term and options, tenant improvement allowance, co-tenancy/exclusivity clauses, use clause, assignment/subletting, insurance/indemnity (links to W3204); Legal drafts lease (links to VS-22 contract review) | Leasing Mgr / Legal | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | 3–8 weeks |

- System auto-assign of "Negotiate commercial terms" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-521 (ensure controlled execution — Landlord Leasing Operations & Tenant Management (PA-97.2).)
- operational: credit screening and security deposits (links to VS-86); operational: clear use clause and audit (W3196)

### Time Estimate

## W3195. Rent Billing, CAM Recovery & Tenant Receivables

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly billing cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~205+ intercompany leases + ~200–600 third-party tenant leases |
| **Owner** | Property AR Manager |
| **Participants** | Property AR Mgr, Lease Admin, Finance (links to VS-16/VS-17), Facilities (links to VS-20.3/W3205) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Admin/AR generate monthly rent roll billing: base rent (per lease/escalation schedule W3197), CAM recovery (actual vs estimated — reconcile annually), utility recharges (sub-metered electricity/water per W1563/W701 where applicable), percentage rent (sales-based where applicable, requires tenant sales reporting); issue invoices (e-invoicing/BIR compliance links to VS-79) | Lease Admin / AR Clerk | Property AR Manager | 2–3 days/cycle |
| 2 | Finance post lessor revenue (operating-lease income straight-lined per PFRS 16; intercompany rent eliminated on consolidation per VS-17.2); manage tenant receivables aging, dunning, and allocation (links to VS-16.2 AR/collections methodology); segregate intercompany from third-party for consolidation | Finance Analyst / AR Clerk | Controller | Monthly |
| 3 | Annually reconcile estimated vs actual CAM/utilities: compute each tenant's pro-rata share of recoverable operating expenses (excluding landlord-responsibility items), issue CAM reconciliation statement, true-up via credit/charge (mirrors BuildRight-as-tenant CAM reconciliation in VS-20.1 W1183 but from the landlord side); document dispute resolution | Lease Admin / Finance | Property AR Manager | 6–10 weeks/annual cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease/billing engine; e-invoicing/BIR (links to VS-79); AR (links to VS-16); GL/consolidation (links to VS-17); utility sub-meter (links to VS-20.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **CAM dispute risk**: Tenants challenge recoverable-expense classification and pro-rata share; mitigated by transparent CAM ledger and lease-defined recoveries
- **Rent-collection risk**: Third-party tenant delinquency; mitigated by deposits, dunning (links to VS-16.2), and default process (W3199)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "generate monthly rent roll billing" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "post lessor revenue" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document dispute resolution" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-134 (ensure property income & LGU tax compliance (as owner).)
- operational: transparent CAM ledger and lease-defined recoveries; operational: deposits, dunning (links to VS-16)

### Time Estimate
- Billing: 2–3 days/month; CAM reconciliation: 6–10 weeks/year

## W3199. Tenant Default, Eviction & Recovery

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Rent delinquency; lease breach; abandonment; insolvency |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~1–3% of third-party tenants/year |
| **Owner** | Property Manager |
| **Participants** | Property Mgr, Legal, Finance/AR (links to VS-16.2), Director, Facilities & Real Estate, Sheriff/LGU, Recovery (links to VS-21/VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AR/Property Mgr detect default: rent aging (links to VS-16.2), breach (use/insurance/maintenance), abandonment indicators; issue demand letter and cure-period notice per lease and Philippine civil law; document all steps for evidentiary use | Property Mgr / Legal | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | 1–4 weeks |
| 2 | Legal pursue remedies per lease and law: ejectment filing (Regional Trial Court for forcible entry/unlawful detainer under Rules of Court), provisional remedies (preliminary attachment), apply security deposit to arrears, claim damages and unpaid rent through lease term or reletting; coordinate with LGU/sheriff for peaceful execution | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | 3–12 months |
| 3 | Recovery and reletting: recover possession, secure and clean premises, assess damages vs deposit, pursue deficiency claim (links to VS-16.3/VS-21), relet space (W3198), update tenant and receivable records; capture lessons for tenant screening (W3194) | Property Mgr / Legal / AR | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | 4–12 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- AR/collections (links to VS-16.2); legal case management (links to VS-100); records/evidence (links to VS-88); tenant master (links to VS-29)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Protracted-ejectment risk**: Philippine ejectment cases can take 6–18 months; mitigated by watertight lease, demand-letter discipline, and security deposits
- **Asset-recovery risk**: Tenant removes fixtures/damages premises; mitigated by inventory-at-commencement, deposit, and restitution claims

- System workflow orchestration of "coordinate with LGU/sheriff for peaceful execution" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "Recovery and reletting" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-521 (ensure controlled execution — Landlord Leasing Operations & Tenant Management (PA-97.2).)
- operational: watertight lease, demand-letter discipline, and security deposits; operational: inventory-at-commencement, deposit, and restitution claims

### Time Estimate
- 6–18 months per contested default
