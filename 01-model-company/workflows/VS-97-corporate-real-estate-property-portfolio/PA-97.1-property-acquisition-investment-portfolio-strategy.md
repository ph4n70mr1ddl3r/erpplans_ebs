## W3185. Property Portfolio Strategy & Investment Plan

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategic cycle (links to VS-33); store-growth pipeline (10–15 new stores/year per profile); financing-window opportunity |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~205+ owned/controlled properties; ~10–15 acquisitions/developments/year |
| **Owner** | Director, Facilities & Real Estate |
| **Participants** | Director, Facilities & Real Estate, Real Estate Investment Mgr, Finance/Treasury (links to VS-18), Strategy (links to VS-33), Store Ops (links to VS-37), Legal, Real-Estate & Site-Selection Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real Estate Investment Mgr maintain portfolio strategy: own-vs-lease decision framework per site (links to VS-42 lease/buy analysis), target ownership mix, geographic concentration limits (Mindanao/Visayas/Luzon/NCR), yield/NOI targets, capital deployment envelope; align to VS-33 strategic plan and VS-37 store-opening pipeline | Real Estate Investment Mgr / Strategy | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | 4–6 weeks/cycle |
| 2 | Finance/Treasury (links to VS-18/VS-40) model portfolio capital plan: acquisition capex, development capex, recurring capex (TI/RC), debt capacity, cash-flow waterfall, target leverage and coverage ratios; integrate with group capex envelope (links to VS-40) and VS-72 intercompany framework | Finance Analyst / Treasury | CFO | 4–6 weeks/cycle |
| 3 | Investment committee (CEO/CFO/COO/Board where required by VS-36 governance authority matrix) approves strategy, ownership thresholds, and delegation of authority; feed approved pipeline to VS-37 store-opening and VS-20 site-selection processes | Investment Committee / Board | CEO | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategic plan/portfolio (links to VS-33); capex (links to VS-40); treasury/funding (links to VS-18); governance/authority (links to VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Concentration risk**: Over-weighting one region/island exposes portfolio to typhoon and seismic clustering; mitigated by geographic diversification and VS-69 disaster-resilience standards
- **Own-vs-lease mis-judgment**: Acquiring sites that later underperform locks capital and creates stranded assets; mitigated by disciplined underwriting (W3186) and exit-option analysis (W3192)


### Automation Opportunity
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves strategy, ownership thresholds, and delegation of authority" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-520 (ensure controlled execution — Property Acquisition, Investment & Portfolio Strategy (PA-97.1).)
- operational: geographic diversification and VS-69 disaster-resilience standards; operational: disciplined underwriting (W3186) and exit-option analysis (W3192)

### Time Estimate
- 8–12 weeks per strategic cycle

| Field | Detail |

| **Frequency** | Per transaction |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 transactions/year |
| **Owner** | Real Estate Investment Manager |
| **Participants** | Real Estate Investment Mgr, Legal, Finance/Treasury (links to VS-18), Tax (links to VS-79/VS-87), Broker, Seller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real Estate Investment Mgr structure the deal: outright purchase (land/building), sale-and-leaseback (acquire from owner-operator and lease back — links to VS-96 equipment leasing for asset-finance parallel), joint venture, or ground lease; price per appraisal (W3189) and underwriting (W3186); negotiate price, deposit, conditions, and timeline | Real Estate Investment Mgr / Broker | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | 2–6 weeks |
| 2 | Legal/Tax (links to VS-79/VS-87) structure for tax efficiency: DST (documentary stamp tax on real-estate conveyance), VAT on sale (if seller is VAT-registered — pass-through analysis), capital-gains tax borne by seller (verify), CGT/estate/transfer-tax exposure, holding-entity selection (Property Mgmt Inc. vs Holdings); draft/review Deed of Absolute Sale, sale-and-leaseback lease, and conditions | Legal Counsel / Tax | VP Legal & Compliance | 2–4 weeks |

- **Tax-leakage risk**: Poor structuring triggers uncapped VAT/DST; mitigated by upfront tax planning (links to VS-79)

- System auto-register of "Register of Deeds" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-520 (ensure controlled execution — Property Acquisition, Investment & Portfolio Strategy (PA-97.1).)
- operational: W3191 title insurance and ENC verification; operational: upfront tax planning (links to VS-79)

### Time Estimate

## W3189. Investment Property Valuation & Fair-Value Measurement (PFRS 40)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual reporting cycle; acquisition/disposition (W3187/W3192); impairment indicator; financing/refinance event |
| **Frequency** | Annual (fair-value measurement) + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~205+ investment properties in portfolio |
| **Owner** | Valuation Manager |
| **Participants** | Valuation Mgr, External Valuer, Finance (links to VS-17), Director, Facilities & Real Estate, Auditor (links to VS-22.2) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Valuation Mgr engage independent, PRC-accredited appraiser (independence per audit requirements, links to VS-22.2) to value each investment property at fair value under PFRS 40; provide lease, rent-roll, NOI, cap-rate comps, and market data; specify valuation date and basis (highest-and-best-use, existing-use) | Valuation Mgr | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | 4–8 weeks |
| 2 | Finance/Valuation (links to VS-17) record fair-value movement: gain/loss to P&L (PFRS 40 fair-value model) or disclosure under cost model; reconcile to prior period; perform impairment review where indicators exist (vacancy, seismic/typhoon damage per VS-69, market decline) | Finance Analyst / Valuation Mgr | Controller | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Auditor (links to VS-22.2) reviews valuation methodology, appraiser credentials, and key assumptions (cap rate, discount rate, terminal value); finance responds to audit findings; final fair values incorporated into consolidated FS (links to VS-17.2) | External Auditor / Finance | CFO | At audit |

### System Touchpoints

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Valuation-bias risk**: Aggressive cap rates inflate carrying value and equity; mitigated by independent appraiser, market-data benchmarking, and audit challenge
- **P&L volatility**: Fair-value swings flow through P&L; mitigated by transparent disclosure and sensitivity analysis

- System auto-record of "record fair-value movement" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "methodology, appraiser credentials, and key assumptions" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-520 (ensure controlled execution — Property Acquisition, Investment & Portfolio Strategy (PA-97.1).)

### Time Estimate

## W3191. Property Due Diligence, Title Transfer & ENC Acquisition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | LOI acceptance / pre-closing (W3187) |
| **Frequency** | Per transaction |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 transactions/year |
| **Owner** | Real Estate Investment Manager |
| **Participants** | Real Estate Investment Mgr, Legal, Title Officer, Surveyor, Environmental Engr (links to VS-24/VS-73), Finance, Broker |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal/Title conduct title due diligence: Certified True Copy of Original/Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT/OCT) from Register of Deeds, trace title chain, identify encumbrances (mortgages, liens, easements, adverse claims, annotations), verify tax declarations and real-property-tax clearance (links to W3202), confirm lot boundaries via geodetic surveyor, check DENR/ LGU land-use and zoning (links to W3206) | Legal / Title Officer / Surveyor | VP Legal & Compliance | 3–6 weeks |
| 2 | Environmental Engineer (links to VS-24/VS-73) conduct Phase I environmental site assessment: historical land use (contamination risk — paints, chemicals, fuel), wetland/floodplain, asbestos/lead in existing structures; escalate to Phase II sampling if warranted; engineering due diligence (structural, geotechnical, utilities) | Environmental Engineer | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | 2–4 weeks |
| 3 | Acquire clean title and ENC: clear identified encumbrances prior to close, obtain title insurance where available, execute Deed of Sale, register transfer at Register of Deeds, secure new TCT in Property Mgmt Inc. name; archive all DD documentation (links to VS-88) | Legal / Title Officer | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | 3–6 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Title/land registry; environmental (links to VS-24/VS-73); records (links to VS-88); property master (links to VS-29)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hidden-encumbrance risk**: Undisclosed liens/easements impair use and resale; mitigated by title search, insurance, and indemnities from seller
- **Environmental-liability risk**: Contaminated site transfers liability to buyer under Philippine environmental law; mitigated by Phase I/II ESA and contractual indemnity

- System auto-validate of "check DENR/ LGU land-use and zoning" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-escalate of "escalate to Phase II sampling if warranted" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "Acquire clean title and ENC" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-520 (ensure controlled execution — Property Acquisition, Investment & Portfolio Strategy (PA-97.1).)
- operational: title search, insurance, and indemnities from seller; operational: Phase I/II ESA and contractual indemnity

### Time Estimate
