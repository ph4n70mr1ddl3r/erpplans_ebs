## W3201. Property-Level P&L, NOI & Yield Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly close; quarterly portfolio review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly + quarterly |
| **Volume** | ~205+ properties |
| **Owner** | Property Controller |
| **Participants** | Property Controller, Finance (links to VS-17), Director, Facilities & Real Estate, Treasury (links to VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Property Controller close each property's P&L monthly: rental revenue (intercompany + third-party per W3195), CAM recovery net of recoverable expenses, other income (parking, signage), operating expenses (repairs/maintenance, utilities common area, security, property mgmt fee), property tax (W3202), insurance (W3204); compute NOI, cap rate on fair value (W3189), yield-on-cost | Property Controller / Finance Analyst | Property Controller | 3–5 days/close |
| 2 | Finance (links to VS-17.4) consolidate property P&L into Property Mgmt Inc. entity reporting and group consolidation (links to VS-17.2); eliminate intercompany rent and intercompany profit on services; report NOI by property, by cluster, and portfolio | Finance Analyst / Property Controller | CFO | Monthly |
| 3 | Director, Facilities & Real Estate/Finance review portfolio performance vs budget (links to VS-33) and prior year: occupancy, NOI growth, yield vs hurdle, variance drivers, watchlist properties; feed into strategy (W3185) and asset-plan decisions (hold/sell/redevelop — W3192) | Director, Facilities & Real Estate / Finance | CEO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Property accounting/GL (links to VS-17); consolidation (links to VS-17.2); FP&A (links to VS-17.4); valuation (links to W3189)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **NOI-distortion risk**: Mis-classified capex vs opex inflates NOI; mitigated by capitalization policy (W3203/links to VS-35) and review
- **Intercompany-leak risk**: Incomplete elimination double-counts rent; mitigated by automated IC matching (links to VS-17.2/VS-72)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-report of "reporting and group consolidation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review portfolio performance vs budget" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-134 (ensure property income & LGU tax compliance (as owner).)
- operational: capitalization policy (W3203/links to VS-35) and review; operational: automated IC matching (links to VS-17)

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 days/month close; 2–3 days/quarter review

## W3202. Real Property Tax, Assessment & LGU Compliance (as Owner)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual RPT cycle; LGU assessment notice; transfer of ownership (W3187) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~205+ titled properties across ~150 LGUs |
| **Owner** | Property Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Property Tax Mgr, Finance (links to VS-79), Legal, LGU Assessor/Collector |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Property Tax Mgr maintain property tax declaration master: Tax Declaration No., classification (residential/commercial/industrial/ agricultural), assessed value, LGU/municipality, barangay, annual RPT due; reconcile to title/property master (links to VS-29/W3191) and to BuildRight-as-tenant RPT pass-through analysis (links to VS-42.3) | Property Tax Mgr | Property Controller | Annual |
| 2 | Pay annual RPT (on or before Jan 31, or quarterly per LGU schedule) to claim early-payment discount (typically 10–20%); for new acquisitions, transfer tax declaration to Property Mgmt Inc. name and update Assessor's Office; ensure no delinquency that could trigger auction/lien | Property Tax Mgr / Finance | Property Controller | Annual |
| 3 | Contest unfair assessments: file protest with Local Board of Assessment Appeals (LBAA) within statutory period where assessed value exceeds fair market value; document evidence (W3189 appraisal, sales comps); coordinate with VS-79 tax and VS-76 LGU compliance | Property Tax Mgr / Legal | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | Event-driven |

### System Touchpoints
- Property tax module; tax compliance (links to VS-79); LGU compliance (links to VS-76); property master (links to VS-29)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Delinquency-auction risk**: Unpaid RPT can trigger LGU auction of the property; mitigated by master calendar and early-payment discipline
- **Over-assessment risk**: LGUs over-assess commercial property; mitigated by systematic protest (LBAA) and appraisal evidence


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile to title/property master" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Pay annual RPT" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "Contest unfair assessments" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-134 (ensure property income & LGU tax compliance (as owner).)
- operational: master calendar and early-payment discipline; operational: systematic protest (LBAA) and appraisal evidence

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks/annual cycle per cluster; event-driven for protests

## W3207. Intercompany Rent Benchmarking & Transfer Pricing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual TP cycle (links to VS-72); new intercompany lease (W3193); renewal (W3197) |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~50 intercompany leases (the owned-site portfolio per W119; the ~205 total lease portfolio is predominantly third-party per W1183) |
| **Owner** | Transfer Pricing Manager |
| **Participants** | Transfer Pricing Mgr, Tax (links to VS-79), Finance (links to VS-72), Director, Facilities & Real Estate, External TP Advisor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Transfer Pricing Mgr/TP Advisor conduct annual arm's-length benchmarking for intercompany rent: comparable uncontrolled price (CUP) using third-party lease comps (W3194 market deals) and market data, build per-sqm market rent range by location class (NCR/Visayas/Mindanao, mall vs standalone, store format); document methodology per BIR RR 02-2013 and OECD guidelines | TP Mgr / External TP Advisor | Tax Manager | 4–8 weeks |
| 2 | Compare intercompany rent charged (W3193/W3195) to arm's-length range; adjust where outside range; document in TP documentation (master file, local file, country-by-country where applicable); ensure consistency between rent charged, RPT basis (W3202), and valuation inputs (W3189) | TP Mgr / Finance | CFO | 2–4 weeks |
| 3 | Defend on BIR audit (links to VS-79.3/VS-22.2): produce TP documentation, benchmarking, comparables, and contemporaneous evidence; manage any BIR adjustment, interest, and penalty; update framework (W3193) | TP Mgr / Legal / Tax | CFO | Event-driven |

### System Touchpoints
- TP documentation (links to VS-72); tax (links to VS-79); billing (W3195); valuation (W3189)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **TP-adjustment risk**: BIR challenges intercompany rent as non-arm's-length; mitigated by CUP benchmarking and contemporaneous docs
- **Inconsistency risk**: Rent, RPT basis, and valuation inputs must reconcile; mitigated by single source of truth per property


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "methodology per BIR RR 02-2013 and OECD guidelines" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "document in TP documentation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-update of "Defend on BIR audit" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-522 (ensure controlled execution — Property Accounting, Compliance & Portfolio Analytics (PA-97.3).)
- operational: CUP benchmarking and contemporaneous docs; operational: single source of truth per property

### Time Estimate
- 6–12 weeks/annual cycle
