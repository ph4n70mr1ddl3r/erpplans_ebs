## W180. E-commerce Marketplace Integration (Lazada/Shopee)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Order placed on external marketplace (Lazada/Shopee) |
| **Frequency** | High; ~9,000–13,000 orders/month (one register with W724/W1470's ~30% share) |
| **Volume** | Avg 2–3 items per order; avg order value PHP 1,500–3,000 |
| **Owner** | Ecommerce Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | System (Middleware), DC Picker, Marketplace Courier |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Order Sync**: Middleware pulls orders from Lazada/Shopee APIs; creates Sales Orders in ERP | System | — | Automated |
| 2 | **ATP Verification**: System checks DC stock; if unavailable, system auto-notifies marketplace to cancel or delay | System | — | Automated |
| 3 | **Picking**: DC Picker receives marketplace pick task; scans each item | DC Picker | DC Supervisor | 10 min |
| 4 | **Packaging**: Items packed in marketplace-specific packaging; marketplace shipping labels printed from middleware | DC Packer | — | 5 min |
| 5 | **Handover**: Marketplace Courier (Lex/Shopee Xpress) picks up from DC; pickup confirmation synced to marketplace | DC Dispatch | — | 5 min |
| 6 | **Settlement**: Bi-weekly: Marketplace remits payment (net of commission/fees); Finance reconciles with ERP Sales Orders | Finance Clerk | Controller | 4 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- API integration (Middleware) for Order, Inventory, and Status sync
- Marketplace-specific shipping label generation
- Automated commission/fee deduction accounting

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Commission and fee opacity**: Lazada and Shopee deduct platform commission (2–5%), payment gateway fees, promotional contributions, and penalty fees (late shipping, order cancellation) from gross settlement before remitting net payment; reconciling net settlement to individual orders is extremely complex, and BuildRight typically cannot identify overcharged fees until the monthly deep reconciliation
- **Inventory sync lag causing overselling**: marketplace inventory sync via middleware has inherent latency (5–15 minutes); during flash sales or high-traffic events, marketplace sells items that BuildRight's DC has already allocated to other channels (POS, own website), resulting in forced cancellations, marketplace penalty fees, and seller rating damage
- **Marketplace seller rating sensitivity**: Lazada and Shopee algorithms penalize sellers with cancellation rates > 2% or late shipment rates > 5% by reducing search ranking and removing eligibility for flash sale events; a single inventory sync failure can cascade into weeks of reduced marketplace visibility and lost sales
- **Returns and dispute asymmetry**: marketplace platforms overwhelmingly side with the buyer in return disputes; BuildRight has limited ability to reject returns or contest "item not as described" claims, resulting in higher return rates on marketplace orders (~8–12%) compared to own-website orders (~3–5%)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-create of "creates Sales Orders in ERP" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "checks DC stock" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-scan of "scans each item" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-271 (ensure controlled execution — Marketplace & Social Commerce (PA-10.3).)
- operational: periodic reconciliation against source documents

### Time Estimate
- Order sync and ATP verification: automated (real-time)
- Picking and packaging: ~15 min/order
- Handover to marketplace courier: ~5 min/batch
- Bi-weekly settlement reconciliation: 4 hours per cycle
- Monthly commission/fee audit: 2 hours/month

### Staffing Implication
- **DC Pickers/Packers**: ~9,000–13,000 marketplace orders/month (per the Frequency) ÷ 30 days = ~300–430 orders/day; at ~15 min pick+pack per order, that's ~42–67 labor-hours/day across all DCs; absorbed within the existing DC pick/pack team that also handles W19 home delivery orders, as marketplace orders use the same pick/pack infrastructure
- **Finance Clerk (settlement reconciliation)**: bi-weekly settlement reconciliation (4 hours/cycle) + monthly commission/fee audit (2 hours/month) = ~10 hours/month; absorbed by existing Finance Clerk within W7 AP processing duties
- **Ecommerce Operations Manager**: oversees marketplace channel health, seller rating, and inventory sync; approximately 5–10 hours/week dedicated to marketplace operations including exception handling and promotional coordination; this is a shared responsibility within the existing Ecommerce Operations Manager role

## W724. Marketplace Channel Daily Operations & Order Management (Lazada/Shopee)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Daily marketplace order processing cycle; or real-time order notification from marketplace platform |
| **Frequency** | Daily; real-time order notifications throughout the day |
| **Volume** | ~300–430 marketplace orders/day (~9,000–13,000/month); ~30% of total ecommerce volume (per W1470) |
| **Owner** | Ecommerce Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketplace Specialist, Warehouse Picker, DC Dispatcher, Finance (reconciliation), Customer Service |

### Background

W180 covers the initial integration setup with marketplace platforms (Lazada, Shopee). This workflow covers the daily operational management of marketplace orders — the highest-volume daily ecommerce activity that the integration alone does not address. Marketplace channels have unique requirements: platform-specific fulfillment SLAs (Lazada requires ship-within-24h for standard items), platform commission structures (2–5% for BuildRight's home-improvement categories, per W180), platform-specific promotional mechanics (Shopee 9.9 sale, Lazada Mid-Year Sale), and platform-managed logistics options that differ from BuildRight's own fulfillment. With marketplace channels representing ~30% of ecommerce volume (per W1470 and the Volume), dedicated daily operations are essential.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Morning order sync and prioritization**: at start of business day: (a) system syncs overnight marketplace orders from Lazada and Shopee via API integration per W180; (b) Marketplace Specialist reviews order queue: (i) orders requiring ship-today (received before cutoff, platform SLA requires same-day ship); (ii) orders pending inventory confirmation (ATP check against ERP); (iii) orders with address issues or customer notes requiring clarification; (iv) promotional orders with special fulfillment requirements; (c) prioritize ship-today orders for immediate DC pick | Marketplace Specialist | Ecommerce Ops Mgr | 30-45 min/day |
| 2 | **Inventory and price synchronization**: throughout the day, system maintains real-time sync: (a) inventory levels: ERP inventory changes (POS sales per W533, DC shipments) pushed to marketplace platforms within 5 minutes to prevent overselling; (b) price sync: promotional pricing per W13 activated simultaneously on marketplace and own channels per W678; (c) listing management: new product listings activated per W569 pushed to marketplace; discontinued items delisted; (d) Marketplace Specialist monitors for sync errors and resolves within 1 hour | System / Marketplace Specialist | Ecommerce Ops Mgr | Ongoing |
| 3 | **Fulfillment routing**: for each marketplace order: (a) system determines fulfillment method: (i) DC fulfillment: item picked from nearest DC per W19; (ii) drop-ship: routed to vendor for direct shipment per W246; (iii) ship-from-store: routed to store with inventory per W19B (if marketplace allows); (b) platform-specific logistics: (i) Lazada: choose between BuildRight own logistics or Lazada Logistics (LEX); (ii) Shopee: choose between own logistics or Shopee Xpress (SPX); (c) Marketplace Specialist reviews system routing decisions and adjusts if needed for SLA compliance | System / Marketplace Specialist | Ecommerce Ops Mgr | 15-20 min/day |
| 4 | **Order fulfillment and shipment tracking**: marketplace orders enter standard fulfillment pipeline: (a) DC pick and pack per W19 (home delivery) or W11 (BOPIS equivalent); (b) marketplace-specific label generation: platform-required shipping labels with tracking barcodes; (c) handoff to carrier: for platform logistics, schedule pickup per carrier SLA; for own logistics, dispatch per W106; (d) upload tracking number to marketplace platform via API; (e) monitor delivery status: system tracks marketplace-specific milestones (Ready to Ship → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered) | DC Picker / Dispatcher | Ecommerce Ops Mgr | Per W19/W246 |
| 5 | **Customer service coordination**: for marketplace-specific customer issues: (a) respond to marketplace chat/inquiries within platform SLA (Lazada: 1 hour, Shopee: 30 min); (b) handle order cancellation requests: cancel in marketplace platform and ERP simultaneously; (c) process marketplace-initiated returns per W215 with platform-specific return procedures; (d) manage marketplace seller ratings and dispute resolution; (e) escalate platform account issues (listing takedowns, policy violations) to Ecommerce Operations Manager | Marketplace Specialist / Customer Service | Ecommerce Ops Mgr | 1-2 hours/day |
| 6 | **Daily settlement reconciliation**: at end of day: (a) reconcile marketplace orders shipped vs. orders in ERP; (b) verify tracking number upload completion for all shipped orders; (c) review platform commission calculations: order value × commission rate per category; (d) flag discrepancies: orders in platform but not in ERP (sync failure) or vice versa; (e) Finance receives daily marketplace settlement summary for monthly reconciliation per W99 | Marketplace Specialist / Finance | Ecommerce Ops Mgr | 30-45 min/day |

### System Touchpoints

- Marketplace API integration per W180 (Lazada Seller Center API, Shopee Open Platform API)
- ERP order management for unified order processing
- Real-time inventory sync per ECOM-001
- Price sync engine per W678 (multi-channel pricing consistency)
- Fulfillment routing engine per W536 (unified order management)
- Carrier integration for platform-specific logistics
- Settlement reconciliation per W99 (payment settlement)
- Customer service platform integration per W258

### Pain Points / Risks

- **Platform SLA pressure**: Lazada and Shopee impose strict ship-within-24h requirements; failure results in order cancellation, seller penalty points, and reduced search ranking; mitigated by early morning order processing and DC priority for marketplace orders
- **Commission impact on margins**: marketplace commissions (2–5% for BuildRight's categories) plus payment processing fees reduce gross margin significantly compared to own-channel sales; requires careful SKU selection for marketplace — avoid listing low-margin items where commission eliminates profit
- **Inventory overselling risk**: if inventory sync fails or is delayed, marketplace may sell items already committed to in-store or ecommerce orders; mitigated by safety stock buffer dedicated to marketplace channel per W105
- **Platform policy volatility**: Lazada and Shopee frequently change seller policies, commission rates, and promotional requirements; requires constant monitoring and rapid adaptation
- **Account suspension risk**: marketplace platforms can suspend seller accounts for policy violations (late shipping, customer complaints, counterfeit claims); suspension halts all marketplace revenue immediately; mitigated by CTL-271 (controlled execution — Marketplace & Social Commerce (PA-10.3))

- System auto-monitor of "monitors for sync errors and resolves within 1 hour" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "logistics" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls

Marketplace Specialist: 1 FTE dedicated to marketplace daily operations (order processing, customer service, platform management). Ecommerce Operations Manager: ~1–2 hours/day oversight. Customer Service: ~1 hour/day on marketplace inquiries. No incremental headcount beyond existing ecommerce team.

**Total daily**: 4-6 hours (30-45 min order sync + ongoing inventory/price management + 15-20 min routing + fulfillment per W19 + 1-2 hours customer service + 30-45 min reconciliation).

## W917. Ecommerce Live Commerce & Social Selling Operations

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Scheduled live commerce session on social media platform (Facebook Live, TikTok Live); flash product launch; seasonal campaign event |
| **Frequency** | 4–6 live sessions/week chain-wide (2–3 on Facebook Live, 1–2 on TikTok Live, 1 on buildright.com.ph); ~20–24 sessions/month |
| **Volume** | Average 500–2,000 live viewers per session; ~200–500 orders per session; average order value PHP 2,500–4,000 |
| **Owner** | Digital Commerce Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketing Host/Presenter, Category Manager (product expert), Ecommerce Fulfillment Team (W19, W11), Social Media Manager (W142), Customer Service (W258) |

### Background

Live commerce (selling products through live video streaming on social media) has exploded in the Philippines — the country ranks among the highest in Southeast Asia for social media engagement and live commerce adoption. Facebook Live and TikTok Live are dominant platforms, with Filipino consumers actively purchasing during live streams through integrated checkout (TikTok Shop) or comment-to-order (Facebook Live). For BuildRight, live commerce presents a unique opportunity: (a) demonstrate products in real-time — show how a power drill handles concrete, how paint colors look on actual walls, how tiles appear under different lighting; (b) answer customer questions live — technical advice on product selection for specific projects; (c) drive urgency through limited-time live-only promotions and flash discounts; (d) reach customers in areas without a nearby BuildRight store; (e) build community and trust through authentic, unscripted product demonstrations. Home improvement is particularly suited to live commerce because products are tactile and benefit from visual demonstration. This workflow covers the full live commerce cycle from session planning to order fulfillment and analytics.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Live Commerce Session Planning**: (a) weekly: Digital Commerce Manager and Category Manager select products for upcoming live sessions based on: seasonal calendar per W264 (rainy season = waterproofing products; ber months = holiday décor), trending products from POS data per W522, new product launches per W569, and vendor co-sponsorship availability per W513; (b) session format selection: (i) product demo + Q&A (30–45 min), (ii) flash sale with countdown (15–20 min), (iii) DIY tutorial + product selling (45–60 min), (iv) contractor pro-tips session (30 min, targeting B2B); (c) product selection criteria: visually demonstrable, available ATP at fulfillment DC per W536, live-only promotional pricing approved per W300, and inventory depth sufficient for expected demand (minimum 200 units per featured SKU); (d) promotional pricing: live-only discount (10–20% off SRP) or bundle offer per W910; discount approved by Category Manager per discount authority matrix; (e) session logistics: host/presenter assigned, product samples prepared for studio/staging area, backdrop and lighting set up in ecommerce studio at HQ | Digital Commerce Manager / Category Manager | VP Marketing | 4–6 hours/week |
| 2 | **Pre-Live Promotion & Audience Building**: (a) 48 hours before session: promotional posts on Facebook page, Instagram stories, and TikTok teaser video per W142 announcing date/time, featured products, and live-only discount; (b) email notification to loyalty members in relevant segment per W673 (e.g., DIY enthusiasts for tool demos, contractors for pro-tips sessions); (c) push notification via BuildRight mobile app per W615; (d) session event page created on Facebook with RSVP; (e) product page on buildright.com.ph updated with "Watch Live" badge linking to stream; (f) inventory pre-reservation: system reserves minimum expected quantity for live session orders (released after session if unsold) | Social Media Manager / Digital Commerce Manager | — | 2–3 hours/session |
| 3 | **Live Session Execution**: (a) host goes live on scheduled platform; (b) session flow: introduction and agenda (2 min), product demonstration with close-up camera work (15–20 min), live Q&A from viewer comments (10 min), flash sale announcement with countdown timer (5 min), closing with next session teaser (2 min); (c) purchasing mechanism: (i) TikTok Shop: direct purchase via TikTok Shop integration with real-time inventory check; (ii) Facebook Live: comment-to-order (customer comments "ORDER [product code] [quantity]") → system captures order from comment feed and creates ecommerce order → customer receives DM with payment link; (iii) buildright.com.ph: embedded live stream with "Buy Now" button synced to ecommerce checkout; (d) Category Manager monitors comments and provides technical answers to host via earpiece; (e) Customer Service team monitors order queue and handles payment failures or customer inquiries in real-time; (f) system enforces live-only pricing with automatic price revert when session ends | Marketing Host / Category Manager / Customer Service / System | Digital Commerce Manager | 30–60 min/session |
| 4 | **Post-Session Order Processing & Fulfillment**: (a) within 1 hour of session end: all live commerce orders compiled and batch-processed in unified order management per W536; (b) orders routed to fulfillment: DC pick/pack/ship per W19 for home delivery, or BOPIS per W11; (c) fulfillment SLA: live commerce orders shipped within 24 hours (priority flag over standard ecommerce orders); (d) customer notifications: order confirmation (within 1 hour), shipping confirmation with tracking (within 24 hours), delivery notification per W592; (e) payment reconciliation: all live session payments reconciled against order records per W611; (f) unfilled orders (stock-out from higher-than-expected demand): customer notified with apology and offered: (i) backorder with expected ship date, (ii) substitute product per W279, or (iii) refund per W101 | Ecommerce Fulfillment Team / System / Customer Service | Digital Commerce Manager | 2–3 hours post-session |
| 5 | **Live Commerce Analytics & Optimization**: (a) per session: viewership metrics (peak concurrent viewers, average view duration, total unique viewers), engagement rate (comments, reactions, shares), conversion rate (viewers → orders), average order value, total GMV, top-selling products, cart abandonment rate during session; (b) weekly: session comparison — which format, time slot, and product category performs best; audience growth trend; repeat live-commerce shopper rate; (c) monthly: live commerce as % of total ecommerce revenue, customer acquisition cost via live commerce vs. other channels, return rate for live commerce orders (indicative of product expectation vs. reality), and host/presenter performance rating; (d) quarterly: platform mix optimization (Facebook vs. TikTok vs. own site), vendor co-sponsorship ROI per W513, and live commerce content calendar for next quarter aligned with seasonal calendar per W306; (e) annual: live commerce program ROI, investment in studio equipment and talent, and expansion plan (new platforms, new session formats, regional-language sessions for VisMin markets) | Digital Commerce Manager / Social Media Manager / System | VP Marketing | 4–6 hours/month |

- **Technical failure during live stream**: internet outage, camera malfunction, or platform crash disrupts session; mitigated by backup internet (mobile hotspot), backup camera, and platform-specific contingency (switch to alternate platform mid-stream); session rescheduled if unrecoverable
- **Higher-than-expected demand**: stock-outs during live session create customer disappointment visible to all viewers; mitigated by inventory pre-reservation, real-time inventory display on product overlay during stream, and immediate backorder communication

- **Host performance inconsistency**: not all hosts are equally engaging or knowledgeable; mitigated by host training, Category Manager earpiece support, and monthly host performance rating per W31

- **Regulatory compliance**: DTI Sales Promotion Permit required for live-only promotions per W427; prices displayed must comply with Consumer Act per W731; "live-only" claims must be genuine (price available only during live session, not simultaneously on website)

## W947. Ecommerce Live Video Shopping & Virtual Store Walkthrough

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer schedules or initiates a live video shopping session via BuildRight mobile app or website |
| **Frequency** | ~2,000–3,000 sessions/month |
| **Volume** | 1 customer per session; average session duration 15–30 min; average order value PHP 4,500 (higher than standard ecommerce AOV of PHP 3,500) |
| **Owner** | Ecommerce Manager |
| **Participants** | Customer, Video Shopping Associate, Category Specialist (on-call), Ecommerce Operations, Payment Gateway |

### Background

Live video commerce is a rapidly growing channel in Southeast Asian retail, driven by social media platforms and the desire for interactive, trust-building shopping experiences. For BuildRight's home improvement customers — especially those undertaking first-time renovation projects — seeing and discussing products in real-time with a knowledgeable associate builds confidence and reduces purchase hesitation. Live video shopping bridges the gap between the tactile in-store experience and the convenience of ecommerce. Customers can request to see specific products, compare items side-by-side, ask questions about specifications and compatibility, and receive real-time recommendations. This service is particularly valuable for: (a) provincial customers who live far from BuildRight stores; (b) overseas Filipino workers (OFWs) purchasing materials for home construction projects in the Philippines; (c) B2B trade customers who want to verify product specifications before placing bulk orders; and (d) elderly or PWD customers who find in-store shopping challenging.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Session Scheduling & Preparation**: Customer books live video session via: (a) mobile app — selects date/time slot, product category of interest, and specific items or project description; (b) website — same booking flow; (c) walk-in scheduling at customer service counter (for future session); system confirms availability of Video Shopping Associate and sends calendar invite with video link; for project-specific sessions (e.g., "complete bathroom renovation"): system pre-loads relevant product catalog pages, current promotions, and inventory availability for customer's preferred store; customer receives pre-session email with: video link, session agenda, and project checklist to prepare dimensions/requirements | System / Ecommerce Ops | Ecommerce Manager | 5 min (automated) |
| 2 | **Session Initiation & Customer Need Assessment**: Video Shopping Associate joins video call at scheduled time; conducts brief needs assessment: (a) project type (new build, renovation, repair, DIY); (b) budget range; (c) timeline; (d) product categories of interest; (e) store preference for BOPIS pickup or home delivery; associate uses mobile device with gimbal/stabilizer to walk store aisles, showing products in real-time; customer can direct associate to specific departments or products | Video Shopping Associate | Ecommerce Manager | 5–10 min |
| 3 | **Live Product Demonstration & Consultation**: Associate presents products per customer interest: (a) physical product demonstration — showing dimensions, material quality, color options, weight, packaging; (b) side-by-side product comparison — placing items next to each other for visual comparison; (c) specification explanation — reading labels, showing technical specs, explaining compatibility; (d) project advice — recommending complementary products, suggesting alternatives when preferred item is out of stock, providing installation tips; for technical questions beyond associate's expertise, system enables instant escalation to Category Specialist (via 3-way video call or chat relay); associate checks real-time inventory per W533 during session | Video Shopping Associate / Category Specialist | Ecommerce Manager | 10–20 min |
| 4 | **Real-Time Order Creation & Payment**: Associate adds selected items to shared digital cart visible to customer on screen; customer reviews cart — quantities, prices, product specifications; system applies: (a) current promotional pricing; (b) loyalty member discounts; (c) trade account pricing (if verified per W897); customer confirms order and pays via: (a) in-app payment (credit card, GCash, Maya); (b) payment link sent to customer's email/SMS; order created in ERP with "Live Video" channel tag; fulfillment routed per W536 (BOPIS or home delivery) | Video Shopping Associate / System / Customer | Ecommerce Manager | 5–10 min |
| 5 | **Post-Session Follow-Up**: System sends post-session communication within 2 hours: (a) order confirmation with delivery/pickup details; (b) session recording link (with customer consent per RA 10173) for customer reference; (c) related product recommendations based on items viewed during session; (d) satisfaction survey (3 questions); (e) loyalty points credited for session attendance (50 points); for sessions that did not result in immediate purchase: abandoned cart reminder sent within 24 hours per W557; Video Shopping Associate makes personal follow-up call within 48 hours for high-value sessions (order value > PHP 10,000 or project-based inquiries) | System / Video Shopping Associate | Ecommerce Manager | 10 min |

### System Touchpoints

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Store floor disruption**: Video Shopping Associate walking store aisles with camera may disrupt other customers and staff; scheduling sessions during lower-traffic periods (weekday mornings) minimizes disruption; dedicated "video shopping zone" in top stores for popular categories
- **Internet connectivity**: Store WiFi must support stable live video streaming; Philippine internet reliability varies; backup cellular connectivity (4G/5G hotspot) required per store; session quality monitoring with automatic bandwidth adjustment
- **Associate product knowledge**: Associates must have broad product knowledge across multiple categories; structured training program per W51 with category rotation; escalation to Category Specialist for deep technical questions
- **Customer no-show**: Booked sessions with no customer attendance waste associate time; SMS reminder 1 hour before session; no-show rate estimated at 15–20%; overbooking by 10% during peak periods
- **Session recording privacy**: Recording customer faces and voices requires explicit consent per RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act); consent captured at booking; recordings stored securely with 30-day auto-deletion; used only for customer reference and quality training
- **Conversion measurement**: Attributing purchases to live video sessions requires channel tagging; offline purchases influenced by video sessions (customer visits store after video session) are harder to track; loyalty card linkage provides partial attribution

## W1470. Marketplace Customer Review Management, Seller Rating Optimization & Dispute Resolution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer posts product review on Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop; or BuildRight's seller rating drops below 4.5 stars; or marketplace dispute/return request received |
| **Frequency** | ~500–800 new reviews/month across all marketplace channels; ~50–80 marketplace disputes/month |
| **Volume** | ~42,900 ecommerce orders/month (per model company profile), of which ~30% fulfilled via marketplace = ~12,800 marketplace orders/month generating reviews |
| **Owner** | Ecommerce Manager |
| **Participants** | Ecommerce Associate, Customer Service Representative, Store Manager (for fulfillment issues), Category Manager (for product quality issues), Vendor (for product defects) |

### Background

Marketplace seller ratings on Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop directly impact product visibility, search ranking, and eligibility for platform promotional events (9.9, 11.11, 12.12 mega sales per W1209). A rating below 4.5 stars can result in: reduced search placement, exclusion from platform campaigns, and potential account suspension. Negative reviews — especially those citing fulfillment failures (wrong item, damaged product, late delivery) or product quality issues — must be addressed promptly to protect BuildRight's marketplace reputation. With ~12,800 marketplace orders/month, even a 2% negative review rate generates ~250 negative reviews/month that require response. This workflow manages the end-to-end marketplace review response, rating optimization, and dispute resolution process.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Review monitoring & triage**: (a) system aggregates customer reviews from all marketplace channels (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop) via API integration; (b) Ecommerce Associate reviews incoming reviews daily: (i) **Positive (4–5 stars)**: auto-thank response with loyalty program invitation per W17; (ii) **Neutral (3 stars)**: personalized response acknowledging feedback, offering assistance; (iii) **Negative (1–2 stars)**: escalation to resolution workflow — prioritize by severity (product safety > fulfillment failure > late delivery > minor complaint); (c) weekly: Ecommerce Manager reviews rating trend across all platforms; if any platform rating drops below 4.5 stars, initiate root cause analysis | System / Ecommerce Associate | Ecommerce Manager | 1 hour/day |
| 2 | **Negative review response & resolution**: For each negative review: (a) Ecommerce Associate investigates: (i) order lookup in ERP — what was ordered, what was shipped, delivery timeline; (ii) root cause categorization: fulfillment error (wrong item, missing parts), product quality (defective, not as described), delivery issue (late, damaged in transit), customer service issue (slow response, unhelpful); (b) Ecommerce Associate responds to customer via marketplace chat within 24 hours: (i) acknowledge the issue, apologize, offer resolution (replacement, refund, store credit), (ii) request customer to update review if issue resolved; (c) for product quality issues: notify Category Manager — if defective product from specific batch, trigger W1347 recall assessment | Ecommerce Associate / Category Manager | Ecommerce Manager | 15 min/review |
| 3 | **Marketplace dispute & return management**: (a) when customer initiates marketplace return/dispute: system receives dispute notification via API; (b) Ecommerce Associate reviews: (i) is the return valid (product genuinely defective/wrong)? (ii) is the customer a serial returner? (iii) what is the marketplace platform's dispute resolution timeline? (c) process return per marketplace rules: (i) approve return and issue refund via marketplace, (ii) customer ships item back or drops at BuildRight store, (iii) Stock Associate inspects returned item and records disposition per W12; (d) dispute resolution within platform SLA (typically 3–5 days) to avoid automatic refund in customer's favor | Ecommerce Associate / CSR | Ecommerce Manager | 20 min/dispute |
| 4 | **Rating optimization actions**: (a) Ecommerce Manager identifies systemic issues from review patterns: (i) specific store with high fulfillment error rate → Store Manager coaching per W67, (ii) specific product category with quality complaints → Category Manager review vendor quality per W688, (iii) delivery partner with high damage rate → renegotiate or switch partner per W668, (iv) listing accuracy issues (product not as described) → Content Manager update per W50; (b) monthly: Ecommerce Manager compiles marketplace performance report: rating by platform, review response rate and time, dispute resolution rate, NPS by channel | Ecommerce Manager | VP Marketing | 4 hours/month |
| 5 | **Seller metrics compliance**: (a) each marketplace platform has seller performance metrics: response rate (> 90%), ship-on-time rate (> 95%), order cancellation rate (< 5%), dispute rate (< 2%); (b) Ecommerce Associate monitors daily: any metric approaching threshold triggers preventive action; (c) if metric breaches threshold: Ecommerce Manager escalates to VP Marketing for corrective action plan to avoid platform penalties | Ecommerce Associate / Ecommerce Manager | VP Marketing | 30 min/day |

### System Touchpoints
- Marketplace review aggregation — API-based review collection from Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop (W1470.1)
- Review response management system — templated and personalized responses with SLA tracking (W1470.2)
- Marketplace dispute API integration — return/dispute notification, status tracking, and resolution (W1470.3)
- Marketplace seller performance dashboard — rating, response rate, ship-on-time, cancellation rate by platform (W1470.5)
- Integration with W180 (marketplace integration), W1209 (mega sale events), W12 (returns), W50 (product content), W17 (loyalty), W67 (store review), W1464 (marketplace listing governance)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Volume overwhelm** — with ~12,800 marketplace orders/month and a typical 4–6% review rate, ~500–800 reviews/month (per the Frequency) require monitoring; a viral negative review can attract dozens of follow-up complaints
- **Platform bias against sellers** — marketplace platforms (especially Shopee) tend to favor buyers in dispute resolution; sellers must provide extensive documentation to win disputes
- **Serial returners** — some customers exploit marketplace return policies to use products and return them used; pattern detection needed per W841 return fraud workflow
- **Cross-platform consistency** — different marketplace platforms have different dispute processes, review formats, and seller requirements; managing 3+ platforms with different rules is operationally complex

- operational: Ecommerce Manager review and approval gate

- **Ecommerce Manager**: ~4 hours/month on analytics and optimization; absorbed
- **No incremental headcount**

## W5550. Marketplace Account Suspension, Enforcement Freeze & Appeal Recovery Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | A marketplace platform (Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop) issues an account-level enforcement action against BuildRight's seller account: seller-metric failure or penalty-point breach, listing-violation strike, IP/counterfeit complaint takedown, fraud-suspicion payout freeze, or full selling-privilege suspension/account deactivation |
| **Frequency** | Full or partial suspensions: rare, ~1–2 events/year across all platforms; lower-tier enforcement (mass listing takedowns, campaign exclusion, settlement holds): ~10–20 events/year |
| **Volume** | 1 event × the affected platform's share of ~12,800 marketplace orders/month (≈30% of ~42,900 ecommerce orders/month per W1470) — a full Shopee-class suspension removes ~40–50% of marketplace demand overnight; settlement freezes hold PHP 15–40M of platform receivables |
| **Owner** | Ecommerce Manager / GM, Digital Commerce Inc. |
| **Participants** | Ecommerce Associate, Content Manager, Category Manager (IP authorization letters), Finance Controller (settlement recovery), Customer Service Representative (order comms), VP Legal (IP/policy appeals), VP Marketing, IT (channel integration flags) |

### Background

W1470 owns the preventive layer — seller-metrics compliance with escalation *to avoid* platform penalties — and W659 owns incidents on BuildRight's own platform; 'account suspension', 'account health' and 'delisting' appear in zero PA files with no owner of the enforcement event itself. A platform suspension is the marketplace analog of the W5547/W5548 platform-outage class — same channel, different cause (enforcement, not downtime): the platform can switch the channel off unilaterally and instantly, the appeal runs on the platform's own clock, and the settlement freeze strands receivables while it runs. This protocol owns detection, containment, the appeal, the payout-freeze accounting, and the reinstatement gate; the steady-state metric discipline stays in W1470 and the listing governance in W1464.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Detection & classification**: Seller Center / Seller Console notice, account-health dashboard alert, or platform partner-manager call; classify severity: (a) listing-level takedown (SKU strikes), (b) feature suspension (campaign exclusion), (c) settlement/payout freeze, (d) selling-privilege suspension, (e) account deactivation; capture the cited policy clause, evidence links, and the appeal window (typically 3–14 days); open the event record | Ecommerce Associate / Ecommerce Manager | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | < 4 h |
| 2 | **Freeze-scope containment**: pause marketplace ad spend; stop new listing pushes (W1464) and scheduled campaign participation (W1209); decide the order-acceptance posture (auto-pause storefront vs. honor in-flight orders); flag the channel integration so inventory allocation stops honoring the suspended storefront (W2412) | Ecommerce Manager / IT | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | < 8 h |
| 3 | **Demand & inventory re-routing**: redirect demand to the own webstore/app (W569-class own-channel listing) for marketplace-fulfillable SKUs; release stranded allocation; customer comms for in-flight orders, cancellation handling per W98 where needed | Ecommerce Manager / Supply Planning | VP Marketing | 1–2 days |
| 4 | **Evidence assembly & appeal**: build the appeal pack — order logs and fulfillment proof (POD, chat records), brand-authorization letters from the Category Manager for IP complaints, the corrective-action statement citing the W1470 metric-remediation plan; file within the platform deadline; engage the platform partner manager at severity (c)+; track appeal status daily | Ecommerce Manager / VP Legal / Category Manager | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | 1–3 days (+ appeal window) |
| 5 | **Settlement-freeze accounting**: recognize frozen platform receivables; reconcile platform settlement statements against ERP records per the W2414-class settlement chain; dispute improperly deducted penalties; re-forecast cash timing with Treasury when a freeze exceeds 30 days | Finance Controller / Ecommerce Associate | CFO | Per event |
| 6 | **Root-cause remediation**: fix the underlying defect before re-enablement — seller-metric corrective plan (W1470), listing/catalog corrections (W1464/W2408), the IP-takedown chain for counterfeit complaints (W3268), or the account-takeover chain where the enforcement stems from a compromised seller account (W3871) | Ecommerce Manager / Content Manager | VP Marketing | 1–4 weeks |
| 7 | **Reinstatement & verification**: selling privileges restored (appeal won, penalty points expired, or suspension lifted); verify listing integrity, search placement, order flow and settlement resumption; re-enable ad spend only after seller metrics re-cross thresholds; confirm in the event record | Ecommerce Associate / Ecommerce Manager | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | 1–3 days |
| 8 | **Post-event register & prevention**: log the event (platform, clause, appeal outcome, financial impact, days-down) in the channel-risk register feeding the platform-dependency review; add the violated policy clause to the listing-governance checklist (W1464) and the quarterly platform-rule watch (W1470) | Ecommerce Manager | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | 4 h |

### System Touchpoints
- Marketplace Seller Center/Console account-health & penalty notices (API-fed to the ecommerce operations dashboard)
- Channel-integration inventory-allocation flags (links to W2412/W2409)
- Platform settlement statement vs ERP settlement reconciliation (links to W2414)
- Listing-governance pipeline policy-clause checklist (links to W1464/W2408)
- Own-channel demand-redirection campaign tooling (links to W569)
- Channel-risk register (platform dependency, appeal outcome, days-down log)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Channel-dependency risk**: ~30% of ecommerce demand rides platforms whose enforcement is unilateral and instant, with no negotiated cure period; mitigated by the Step-3 own-channel redirection playbook and the channel-risk register's platform-dependency review
- **Appeal-deadline risk**: platforms treat an unappealed violation as confirmed, converting a recoverable event into escalation or deactivation; mitigated by the <4-hour detection SLA and Step-4 deadline tracking
- **Evidence risk**: fulfillment proof (POD, chat logs) not retained forfeits appeals even when BuildRight is right; mitigated by the W2414-class order-record retention feeding the appeal pack
- **Settlement-freeze risk**: a payout hold strands receivables while platform deductions silently net against them; mitigated by Step-5 recognition and daily reconciliation for the freeze's duration
- **Recurrence risk**: paying the penalty without fixing the metric or listing defect invites escalation to full suspension; mitigated by the Step-6 remediation gate ahead of Step-7 re-enablement

### Automation Opportunity
- Account-health API ingestion auto-opens the event ticket with severity classification and an appeal-deadline countdown (replaces manual Step-1 monitoring)
- Suspension-detected auto-pause of marketplace campaigns, listing pushes and inventory allocation (replaces manual Step-2 coordination)

### Controls
- CTL-271 (ensure controlled execution — Marketplace & Social Commerce (PA-10.3).)
- CTL-200 (ensure marketplace order & settlement reconciliation.)
- operational: no appeal is filed without the corrective-action statement attached; ad spend is not re-enabled until seller metrics re-cross platform thresholds

### Time Estimate
Per event: full-suspension class ≈ 60–100 staff-hours over the event life (Steps 1–5 ≈ 25–40 in week 1; Steps 6–8 ≈ 35–60 across weeks); listing-level events ≈ 4–8 staff-hours each × ~10–20/year ≈ 60–140 hours/year. Steady state: the channel-risk register and platform-rule watch ride the quarterly W1470 review — no incremental headcount.

### Staffing Implication
- Absorbed by the Ecommerce Manager/Associate bench with Finance settlement support and Legal on appeals; rare events riding existing channel-operations capacity. No incremental headcount.
