## Overview

Talent Acquisition (TA) Marketing, Employer Brand & Candidate Experience workflows for
BuildRight Depot Corp. — owning the **strategic attraction and candidate-side of hiring**
across a 5-entity, 205-location, ~6,911-employee group that hires ~1,200–1,600 people per year
(including turnover replacement at 15–20% annual turnover) and is growing ~10–15 stores/year.
BuildRight competes for both **corporate talent** (Davao HQ and regional offices — merchandising,
finance, supply chain, IT, marketing, legal) and **frontline retail and trade-knowledgeable
talent** (store associates across lumber, tiles, plumbing, electrical, paint, tools/hardware
departments, DC and logistics operators), in a Philippine labor market where minimum wages,
statutory benefits, and employer reputation (especially social-media and peer-driven) materially
drive attract-and-retain.

Today this discipline is **unowned as a program**: VS-19.1 executes the recruitment *transaction*
(posting a req, screening, interviewing, onboarding, W15), VS-19.1 also runs the operational
cadetship (W179), referral (W715) and internal-mobility (W682) workflows, and VS-103.2 owns the
*employee* experience/internal-communications layer — but no value stream owns the **strategic
candidate-side** discipline: employer brand and employee value proposition (EVP), career-site and
candidate-portal product, talent marketing and sourcing-channel strategy, candidate experience
and candidate-relationship management (talent community / talent CRM), campus and TESDA/vocational
feeder management, candidate NPS and offer/acceptance analytics, and TA operations/fulfillment
itself (recruiter capacity, vendor/sourcing-channel governance, offer/hire SLA). 'candidate
experience', 'career site', and 'talent community' each appear in **zero** PA files; 'employer
brand' appears only incidentally (3 PA files, none a dedicated program).

This is distinct from **VS-19.1 (Recruitment & Onboarding)** which executes the hiring
*transaction* (requisition → sourcing execution → interview → offer → hire → onboard) — this
value stream owns the *attraction, brand, channel, and candidate-relationship layer* upstream of
and around that transaction. It is distinct from **VS-102 (Compensation, Benefits & Total
Rewards)** which designs the pay/benefits *offer* (this value stream *markets* the offer and EVP to
the market). It is distinct from **VS-103.2 (Employee Experience & Internal Communications)** which
serves the *existing* employee (this value stream serves the *prospective* candidate and feeds the
front of the hiring funnel). It is distinct from **VS-123 (Skilled-Trade Apprenticeship, Vocational
Education & Capability Pipeline)** which builds trade capability through structured
apprenticeship (this value stream owns the *market attraction* of apprentices and all other talent).
Talent acquisition marketing, employer brand and candidate experience is the *attraction and
candidate-side* discipline — distinct from recruitment transaction processing, total-rewards design,
employee experience, and capability-building.

BuildRight's exposure is structural: ~1,200–1,600 hires/year against 15–20% turnover, distributed
across 205 locations and 5 entities, in a market where skilled trade-knowledgeable frontline talent
and digital/corporate talent are both scarce, and where a weak employer brand and poor candidate
experience directly inflate agency spend, time-to-fill, and offer-decline / early-attrition rates.
Without a dedicated owner, BuildRight risks over-reliance on third-party agencies, slow time-to-fill
on critical roles, a sub-scale internal talent community, and a leaky candidate funnel — risks that
no existing value stream owns end-to-end.
