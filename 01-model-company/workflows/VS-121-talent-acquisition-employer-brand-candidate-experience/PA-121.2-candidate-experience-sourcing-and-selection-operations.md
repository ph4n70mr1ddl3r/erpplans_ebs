## W3769. Candidate Journey Design & Candidate Experience Standard

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | EVP launch (W3761); experience audit; candidate feedback |
| **Frequency** | Annual + continuous improvement |
| **Volume** | Enterprise candidate-journey standard |
| **Owner** | TA Marketing / TA Operations |
| **Participants** | TA Operations, TA Marketing, Recruiting (links to VS-19.1), HR Shared Services (links to VS-103.1), IT (links to VS-27/VS-103.3), Store Ops (links to VS-07), Compliance (links to VS-22) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TA Operations/TA Marketing design the end-to-end candidate journey standard: awareness → consideration → apply → screen → interview → offer → pre-board → hire (links to VS-19.1 onboarding), with defined SLAs (apply acknowledgement, screen, interview-schedule, feedback, offer), touchpoints, and the expected candidate experience at each stage, segmented by role family (store frontline, trade, DC, corporate) | TA Operations / TA Marketing | TA Lead | Annual |
| 2 | TA Operations/IT instrument the journey in the ATS/candidate portal (links to W3762/VS-103.3), enable automated but personalized candidate communications, self-scheduling, and feedback capture, and ensure a single candidate view across all applications/entities | TA Operations / IT | TA Lead | Continuous |
| 3 | TA Operations train recruiters and hiring managers on the candidate-experience standard, monitor adherence (SLA dashboards), and drive continuous improvement from candidate feedback (links to W3776) | TA Operations / HR | TA Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- ATS/HRIS (links to VS-103.3); candidate portal (links to W3762); comms (links to VS-103.1); analytics (links to VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Inconsistent-experience risk**: Experience varies by recruiter/store/entity; mitigated by standard + SLA monitoring
- **Ghosting risk**: Candidates left without feedback damage brand; mitigated by feedback SLA and automation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-schedule of "schedule, feedback, offer" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-capture of "capture, and ensure a single candidate view across all applications/entities" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "TA Operations train recruiters and hiring managers on the" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-593 (ensure controlled execution — Candidate Experience, Sourcing & Selection Operations (PA-121.2).)
- operational: standard + SLA monitoring; operational: feedback SLA and automation

### Time Estimate
- Annual + continuous
