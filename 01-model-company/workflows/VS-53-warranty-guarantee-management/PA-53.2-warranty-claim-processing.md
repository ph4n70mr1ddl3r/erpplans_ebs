## W2126. In-Store Warranty Claim Initiation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer brings defective product with proof of purchase to store |
| **Frequency** | ~2,000–3,000 claims/month across 200 stores (one register with W1630; ~10–15 per store/month) |
| **Volume** | ~8K–10K/month; peaks Jan–Feb, Jun–Jul |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Rep |
| **Participants** | CSR, Store Manager, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CSR verifies warranty coverage on the Install Base instance (fit-gap D16); inspects product; photographs defect; checks serial number match | CSR | Store Manager | 10–15 min |
| 2 | System classifies claim: standard (auto-approve), borderline (route to Supervisor), out-of-warranty (offer paid repair) | System | CSR | Automated |
| 3 | CSR offers resolution: immediate replacement, store credit, service center repair, or DC/vendor replacement (7–14 days) | CSR | Customer | 5–10 min |
| 4 | Customer selects resolution; system processes inventory adjustment, creates claim record, notifies vendor | CSR / System | Store Manager | 5 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Oracle Install Base + Service Contracts entitlement check (fit-gap D16; links to VS-13 / W17, VS-53 / W2118) — the warranty verification and claim record
- POS/inventory (links to VS-08 / W5B, VS-05 / W92 inventory adjustment) — the inventory adjustment on replacement
- Vendor notification (links to VS-53 / W2128 vendor escalation) — the vendor-claim push

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fraudulent-claim risk**: a customer claims warranty on a non-warranty item or a non-purchased item; mitigated by the serial-number match and the purchase verification (CTL-37)
- **Resolution-delay-dissatisfaction risk**: the customer expects immediate resolution but the claim requires vendor/service-center involvement (7–14 days); mitigated by the clear timeline-setting and the loaner-option for critical items

### Automation Opportunity
- Claim auto-classified (standard/borderline/out-of-warranty); serial auto-verified; vendor-auto-notified

### Controls
- CTL-389 (ensure controlled execution — Warranty Claim Processing & Resolution (PA-53.2).)
- operational: Store Manager sign-off on borderline claims

### Time Estimate
~25 min/claim × ~9,000/month = **~3,750 hours/month CSR** (distributed across store CSR teams).

## W2127. Online Warranty Claim Submission

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer submits claim via web portal or mobile app |
| **Frequency** | ~2,000–3,000 online claims/month |
| **Volume** | ~2K–3K/month (~25% of total) |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Agent (Remote) |
| **Participants** | Customer, CSA, CS Supervisor, Receiving Clerk |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer describes defect, uploads photos/video, selects resolution and preferred store for drop-off | Customer | CSA | 5–10 min |
| 2 | CSA reviews within 24 hours; validates photos match coverage; approves or requests more info; generates return authorization QR code | CSA | CS Supervisor | 10–15 min |
| 3 | Customer drops item at store (scans QR) or schedules pickup for bulky items; store receives and triggers resolution | Customer / Receiving Clerk | CSA | 14-day window |

### System Touchpoints
- Online portal/app (links to VS-53 / W2124, VS-75 app, VS-10) — the claim-submission surface
- Install Base claim record on the instance (fit-gap D16; links to VS-13 / W17) — the claim record and return authorization
- Returns/receiving (links to VS-32 / W12, VS-04 / W3) — the item receipt at store

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Photo-insufficiency risk**: the customer's photos don't clearly show the defect, delaying the claim; mitigated by the photo-guide (what to photograph) and the CSA's request-for-more-info
- **Drop-off-friction risk**: the customer must physically bring the item to a store, which for bulky items is a burden; mitigated by the pickup-scheduling option for bulky items

### Automation Opportunity
- Claim auto-routed to CSA worklist; return-QR auto-generated on approval; pickup auto-scheduled

### Controls
- CTL-389 (ensure controlled execution — Warranty Claim Processing & Resolution (PA-53.2).)
- operational: CS Supervisor sign-off

### Time Estimate
~15 min/claim (CSA) + ~7 min (customer) × ~2,500/month ≈ ~37,500 CSA + ~17,500 customer min ≈ **~915 hours/month CSA + customer time (≈ 625 CSA + ~290 customer)**.

## W2128. Vendor Warranty Claim Escalation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Claim requires manufacturer involvement (high-value >PHP 10K, complex product) |
| **Frequency** | ~1,500–2,000 vendor escalations/month |
| **Volume** | ~1.5K–2K/month (~20% of claims) |
| **Owner** | Vendor Warranty Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Coordinator, Category Manager, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System auto-flags for vendor involvement; Coordinator packages claim with photos, purchase proof, serial number | System / Coordinator | Category Manager | 30 min |
| 2 | Coordinator submits via vendor portal or email; tracks acknowledgment within 48 hours; manages vendor resolution (approve/return/deny) | Coordinator | Vendor | 30 min |
| 3 | Coordinator tracks 14-day vendor SLA; escalates to Category Manager if breached; monthly vendor warranty scorecard | Coordinator | Category Manager | 15 min/case |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor portal (links to VS-28 / W593, VS-67 / W2458 vendor scorecard) — the vendor-claim submission and tracking
- Install Base claim record (fit-gap D16; links to VS-13 / W17) — the claim record
- Vendor scorecard (links to VS-67 / W2126) — the vendor warranty-claim performance feed

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vendor-SLA-breach risk**: the vendor doesn't resolve within 14 days, leaving the customer waiting; mitigated by the SLA-tracking and the Category Manager escalation
- **Vendor-deny-unjustified risk**: the vendor denies a valid claim to avoid cost; mitigated by the evidence-package (photos, serial, purchase proof) and the escalation to Category Manager

### Automation Opportunity
- Claim auto-flagged for vendor escalation; vendor-SLA auto-tracked with escalation; vendor-scorecard auto-fed

### Controls
- CTL-389 (ensure controlled execution — Warranty Claim Processing & Resolution (PA-53.2).)
- operational: Category Manager sign-off on vendor-SLA breaches

### Time Estimate
~45 min/case × ~1,750/month = **~1,300 hours/month Vendor Warranty Coordinator** (distributed).

## W2129. Service Center Repair Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Claim requires repair at authorized service center |
| **Frequency** | ~3,000–4,000 repair claims/month |
| **Volume** | ~3K–4K/month; 7–14 day turnaround |
| **Owner** | Service Center Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Coordinator, Service Center, CS Supervisor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System assigns to nearest authorized service center; generates work order; arranges item transfer from store | System / Coordinator | Service Center | 30 min |
| 2 | Service center diagnoses: warranty-covered repair → proceed; non-covered → quote customer; beyond repair → replacement authorized | Service Center | Coordinator | 7–14 days |
| 3 | Repair complete; item returned to store; customer notified for pickup; failure mode data logged for quality analysis | Service Center / Coordinator | CS Supervisor | 15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Service-center work-order (links to VS-12 / W440 power-tool service, VS-09 / W138 service sale) — the repair coordination
- Inventory/transfer (links to VS-05 / W22 transfers) — the item transfer store→service-center→store
- Quality/failure-mode (links to VS-31 / W110 supplier quality) — the failure-mode data for quality analysis

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Repair-delay risk**: the 7–14 day turnaround extends (parts backorder), leaving the customer without the product; mitigated by the SLA-tracking and the loaner/replacement option for extended delays
- **Non-covered-discovery risk**: the service center discovers the repair isn't warranty-covered (customer-caused damage), requiring a customer-paid quote after the item is already in the shop; mitigated by the pre-diagnosis at claim initiation (W2126) catching obvious non-covered cases early

### Automation Opportunity
- Service-center auto-assigned (nearest); work-order auto-generated; SLA auto-tracked; failure-mode auto-logged

### Controls
- CTL-389 (ensure controlled execution — Warranty Claim Processing & Resolution (PA-53.2).)
- operational: CS Supervisor repair-completion sign-off

### Time Estimate
~45 min/case (coordinator) + 7–14 day repair (service center) × ~3,500/month = **~2,600 hours/month Coordinator** (distributed).

## W2130. Warranty Replacement Fulfillment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved claim resolved via product replacement |
| **Frequency** | ~5,000–6,000 replacement orders/month |
| **Volume** | ~5K–6K/month (~60% of resolutions) |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Supervisor |
| **Participants** | CS Supervisor, CSR, DC, Receiving Clerk |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System checks replacement availability: in-store (immediate), nearby store (1–2 days), DC (3–5 days), vendor PO (7–21 days) | System | CS Supervisor | Automated |
| 2 | For in-stock: inventory reserved, customer picks up. For DC-sourced: included in next shipment, tracking SMS sent | CSR / DC | CS Supervisor | Immediate to 21 days |
| 3 | Upon pickup: scans replacement against claim; closes claim; transfers warranty registration to replacement item | Receiving Clerk | CS Supervisor | 5 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory/ATP (links to VS-05 / W105 multi-channel allocation, VS-60 / W2290 reservation) — the replacement availability
- Claim system (links to VS-53 / W2126) — the claim closure
- Warranty transfer (links to VS-53 / W2118) — the warranty registration transfer to the replacement

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Replacement-out-of-stock risk**: the replacement item is out of stock (especially for discontinued products), leaving the customer without resolution; mitigated by the substitute-option or the refund-fallback (W2131)
- **Warranty-transfer-failure risk**: the warranty registration isn't transferred to the replacement item, so the replacement has no warranty; mitigated by the atomic replacement-and-warranty-transfer transaction

### Automation Opportunity
- Replacement availability auto-checked across sources; reservation auto-made; warranty auto-transferred on pickup

### Controls
- CTL-389 (ensure controlled execution — Warranty Claim Processing & Resolution (PA-53.2).)
- operational: CS Supervisor sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 min/pickup (clerk) + availability-check automated × ~5,500/month = **~460 hours/month** distributed (CSR + Receiving + DC).

## W2131. Warranty Refund Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Claim resolved via refund (product unrepairable or customer preference) |
| **Frequency** | ~800–1,200 refund claims/month |
| **Volume** | ~800–1.2K/month (~10% of resolutions) |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Supervisor |
| **Participants** | CSR, Store Manager, Finance, CS Supervisor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CSR verifies refund eligibility; determines amount: full refund (<30 days), prorated (based on remaining period), or store credit at 110% | CSR | Store Manager | 10–15 min |
| 2 | Store Manager approves refunds >PHP 5,000; Finance processes via original payment method; system closes claim | Store Manager / Finance | CS Supervisor | 15–20 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Refund (links to VS-16 / W101 customer refund, VS-17 / W9) — the refund processing
- Claim system (links to VS-53 / W2126) — the claim closure
- Finance (links to VS-17 / W9, W70 credit/debit note) — the refund-to-original-payment

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Refund-to-wrong-method risk**: the refund goes to a different payment method than the original (fraud vector); mitigated by the refund-to-original-payment-only policy (CTL pattern)
- **Proration-dispute risk**: the customer disputes the prorated amount (expected full refund); mitigated by the clear proration-policy documented at warranty activation

### Automation Opportunity
- Refund-eligibility auto-determined; proration auto-calculated; refund auto-processed to original method

### Controls
- CTL-389 (ensure controlled execution — Warranty Claim Processing & Resolution (PA-53.2).)
- operational: Store Manager sign-off >PHP 5K

### Time Estimate
~25 min/case × ~1,000/month = **~420 hours/month** CSR + Store Manager + Finance.

## W2132. Warranty Claim Dispute Resolution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer disputes denied warranty claim |
| **Frequency** | ~300–500 disputes/month (~3–5%) |
| **Volume** | ~300–500/month; high-value items |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Director |
| **Participants** | CSR, CS Director, VP Merchandising, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer escalates; CSR logs dispute with additional evidence; CS Director reviews documentation, warranty terms, claim history | CSR / CS Director | VP Merchandising | 30–60 min |
| 2 | Director decides within 5 business days: overturn, uphold (with goodwill gesture), or escalate to Category Manager for vendor dispute | CS Director | Legal | 30 min |
| 3 | Monthly: Director reviews dispute trends; identifies systemic issues by vendor, category; implements corrective action | CS Director | VP Merchandising | 2–3 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM dispute (links to VS-13 / W41 complaint, W469 DTI escalation) — the dispute record
- Warranty terms (links to VS-53 / W2122) — the terms under dispute
- Vendor dispute (links to VS-67 / W2128, CTL-48) — the vendor-side escalation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **DTI-escalation risk**: an unresolved dispute escalates to the DTI (consumer protection), creating a regulatory complaint (W2682); mitigated by the 5-business-day resolution SLA and the goodwill-gesture option
- **Systemic-issue-neglect risk**: disputes recur on the same issue (a vendor consistently denies valid claims) without systemic action; mitigated by the monthly trend review and the corrective action

### Automation Opportunity
- Dispute auto-routed with SLA; trend auto-analyzed; systemic-issue auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-389 (ensure controlled execution — Warranty Claim Processing & Resolution (PA-53.2).)
- operational: CS Director sign-off; Legal review on high-value disputes

### Time Estimate
~45 min/dispute × ~400/month = **~300 hours/month** distributed (CSR + CS Director).

## W2133. Warranty Claim Analytics & Trends

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly analytics cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly comprehensive; weekly operational |
| **Volume** | Monthly: ~10,000 claims analyzed |
| **Owner** | Analytics Manager |
| **Participants** | Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates analytics: claim volume by category/vendor/store, claim rate by SKU, resolution time, cost, top 20 claim SKUs, repeat claim rate | System | Analytics Manager | Automated |
| 2 | Analytics Manager identifies trends: rate increase >2x triggers quality alert; vendor >5% triggers vendor review; store >2x average triggers investigation | Analytics Manager | Category Manager | 4–6 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113, VS-67 / W2458 vendor scorecard) — the claim analytics
- Quality (links to VS-31 / W110 supplier quality) — the quality-alert target
- Install Base claim dataset (fit-gap D16; links to VS-13 / W17, VS-53 / W2126) — the claim dataset

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Quality-issue-late-detection risk**: a defective-batch claim-rate spike isn't detected until month-end, allowing more defective units to sell; mitigated by the weekly operational metrics (not just monthly)
- **Vendor-scorecard-lag risk**: the vendor-claim performance feeds the scorecard monthly, but the vendor negotiation is quarterly, so a claim-rate spike isn't acted on for months; mitigated by the >5%-trigger immediate vendor review (not waiting for the quarterly cycle)

### Automation Opportunity
- Analytics auto-generated; rate-increase auto-flagged; vendor/store auto-flagged at threshold

### Controls
- CTL-389 (ensure controlled execution — Warranty Claim Processing & Resolution (PA-53.2).)
- operational: Category Manager sign-off on quality alerts

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month = **~60 hours/year** Analytics Manager.
