## W2134. Extended Warranty Product Design & Pricing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual review or new category launch |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly review; new category as needed |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 products across 6 categories |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance, VP Merchandising, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager identifies eligible categories; Finance calculates pricing based on claim rate, repair cost, target margin (40–60%) | Category Mgr / Finance | VP Merchandising | 2–3 days |
| 2 | Legal reviews contract terms for Consumer Act compliance; Manager configures in ERP with service SKU, pricing tiers, POS upsell rules | Legal / Category Mgr | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Item/warranty master (links to VS-29 / W296 service-item master, W2122 warranty config) — the extended-warranty SKU
- Pricing (links to VS-29 / W289, VS-57 / W2232) — the pricing model (claim rate + repair cost + margin)
- Legal/compliance (links to VS-22 / W22.1, VS-88) — the RA 7394 contract-terms review

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pricing-inadequacy risk**: the claim rate exceeds the pricing assumption, making the product unprofitable; mitigated by the annual re-pricing and the claim-rate monitoring (W2136)
- **RA 7394-non-compliance risk**: the contract terms don't meet the Consumer Act (e.g., unfair exclusion clauses); mitigated by the Legal review in step 2

### Automation Opportunity
- Pricing auto-suggested from the historical claim-rate + repair-cost data; RA 7394-compliance auto-checked

### Controls
- CTL-390 (ensure controlled execution — Extended Warranty & Service Contract Management (PA-53.3).)
- CTL-390: extended-warranty pricing adequacy — links to VS-157; service-contract terms conform to the Consumer Act (RA 7394) — links to VS-22; operational: VP Merchandising sign-off

### Time Estimate
~3.5 days/review × 4 + per-category = **~120 hours/year** Category Manager + Finance + Legal.

## W2135. Extended Warranty POS Upsell

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer purchases eligible item at POS |
| **Frequency** | ~42,000 upsell opportunities/month |
| **Volume** | ~42K/month; target 15–20% attach rate |
| **Owner** | Cashier |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Analytics Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | POS auto-prompts with warranty options and price; cashier presents offer using trained script | Cashier | Store Manager | 30–60 sec |
| 2 | Customer accepts: warranty added to transaction, contract printed on receipt, digital acknowledgment signed; auto-registered as a Service Contract on the Install Base instance (fit-gap D16) | Cashier / System | Cashier | 30 sec |
| 3 | Customer declines: recorded for analytics; no repeated offers to protect customer experience | POS System | Analytics Manager | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- POS / upsell (links to VS-08 / W5B, POS-013, VS-10 / W794 ecommerce upsell) — the auto-prompt and acceptance
- Oracle Service Contracts on the Install Base instance (fit-gap D16; links to VS-13 / W17, VS-53 / W2118) — the auto-registration
- Analytics (links to VS-27 / W113) — the attach-rate and decline-rate tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Customer-annoyance risk**: the upsell prompt frustrates customers who don't want it; mitigated by the no-repeated-offers rule and the brief trained script
- **Low-attach-rate risk**: the attach rate is below the 15–20% target; mitigated by the script training and the incentive (links to VS-19 / W228 sales commission)

### Automation Opportunity
- Prompt auto-displayed; acceptance auto-registered; attach-rate auto-tracked

### Controls
- CTL-390 (ensure controlled execution — Extended Warranty & Service Contract Management (PA-53.3).)
- operational: Store Manager attach-rate review

### Time Estimate
~45 sec/transaction × ~42,000/month ≈ ~1,890,000 sec ≈ **~525 hours/month Cashier** (absorbed into checkout, ~30 sec incremental).

## W2136. Extended Warranty Contract Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contract active through coverage period |
| **Frequency** | ~75,000–100,000 active contracts |
| **Volume** | ~75K–100K active contracts at any time |
| **Owner** | Warranty Administrator |
| **Participants** | Finance, Finance Manager, Warranty Admin, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System maintains contract lifecycle (active/approaching expiry/expired/cancelled); monthly liability accrual journal generated | System / Finance | Finance Manager | 4–6 hours/month |
| 2 | Quarterly: review contract portfolio (renewal rate, claim rate vs. estimate, cancellation rate, revenue vs. cost); annual re-pricing | Warranty Admin / Finance | VP Merchandising | 4–6 hours/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Warranty contract/liability (links to VS-17 / W28, VS-157 / PFRS 15, VS-54 / W2156 breakage pattern) — the deferred-revenue/liability accrual
- Analytics (links to VS-27 / W113) — the portfolio review dataset
- Pricing (links to VS-53 / W2134) — the annual re-pricing input

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Liability-accrual-inaccuracy risk**: the PFRS 15 deferred-revenue accrual doesn't match the actual revenue-recognition pattern (straight-line vs. claims-weighted); mitigated by the quarterly review and the methodology calibration
- **Portfolio-loss risk**: the claim-rate-vs-estimate divergence across the portfolio makes the program unprofitable; mitigated by the quarterly review and the re-pricing

### Automation Opportunity
- Liability accrual auto-generated monthly; portfolio-metrics auto-computed quarterly

### Controls
- CTL-390: PFRS 15 deferred-revenue accuracy — links to VS-157; CTL-44 (contract-count integrity); operational: Finance Manager monthly sign-off; VP Merchandising quarterly sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month + ~5 hours/quarter = **~80 hours/year** Warranty Administrator + Finance.

## W2137. Extended Warranty Claim Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer files claim on product covered by extended warranty |
| **Frequency** | ~1,500–2,500 extended warranty claims/month |
| **Volume** | ~1.5K–2.5K/month; higher avg value |
| **Owner** | Extended Warranty Claims Specialist |
| **Participants** | Specialist, CS Director, Finance Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System verifies active contract, coverage period, claim type; Specialist validates defect is covered (not wear/misuse/accidental) | System / Specialist | CS Director | 20–30 min |
| 2 | Specialist authorizes: repair at service center, replacement (>70% of replacement cost), or store credit; Finance tracks vs. accrual | Specialist | Finance Manager | 15–30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Warranty contract (links to VS-53 / W2136) — the active-contract verification
- Claim system (links to VS-53 / W2126–W2131) — the claim processing
- Finance/accrual (links to VS-17 / W28, VS-157) — the claim-cost-vs-accrual tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Coverage-dispute risk**: the customer believes the claim is covered but the contract excludes it (wear, accidental); mitigated by the clear coverage-scope documentation at sale and the Specialist's evidence-based validation
- **Accrual-depletion risk**: the claim cost exceeds the accrued revenue for the contract, making it a loss; mitigated by the quarterly portfolio review (W2136) and the re-pricing

### Automation Opportunity
- Contract auto-verified; coverage auto-checked against the contract terms; claim-cost auto-tracked vs. accrual

### Controls
- CTL-390 (ensure controlled execution — Extended Warranty & Service Contract Management (PA-53.3).)
- operational: CS Director sign-off; Finance Manager accrual review

### Time Estimate
~35 min/claim × ~2,000/month = **~1,170 hours/month Specialist** (distributed).

## W2138. Extended Warranty Renewal & Extension

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Contract approaching expiry (90/30/7-day alerts) |
| **Frequency** | ~6,000–8,000 renewal opportunities/month |
| **Volume** | ~6K–8K/month; target 25–30% renewal |
| **Owner** | Warranty Administrator |
| **Participants** | Warranty Admin, Customer, CSR |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System auto-sends renewal notices at 90, 30, and 7 days with pricing and easy renewal link (online/in-store/phone) | System | Warranty Admin | Automated |
| 2 | Customer renews via online payment, in-store, or phone; system extends contract, generates new certificate, schedules next expiry cycle | Customer / CSR | Warranty Admin | 10–15 min (phone) |

### System Touchpoints
- Warranty contract lifecycle (links to VS-53 / W2136) — the renewal/extension
- Customer comms (links to VS-75 app, VS-10 email) — the renewal notices
- Payment (links to VS-08 / W5B, VS-16 / W8) — the renewal payment

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Renewal-fatigue risk**: too many renewal notices annoy the customer; mitigated by the 3-touch cadence (90/30/7) and the easy-renewal-link
- **Low-renewal-rate risk**: the renewal rate is below the 25–30% target; mitigated by the pricing (renewal priced attractively) and the multi-channel ease

### Automation Opportunity
- Renewal notices auto-sent at 90/30/7; contract auto-extended on payment

### Controls
- CTL-390 (ensure controlled execution — Extended Warranty & Service Contract Management (PA-53.3).)
- operational: Warranty Admin renewal-rate review

### Time Estimate
Automated + ~12 min/phone-renewal × ~700/month = **~140 hours/month** CSR + Warranty Admin (phone subset).

## W2139. Extended Warranty Cancellation & Refund

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer requests cancellation |
| **Frequency** | ~200–400 cancellations/month (~3–5%) |
| **Volume** | ~200–400/month |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Rep |
| **Participants** | CSR, CS Supervisor, Finance, Finance Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CSR verifies contract; within 30-day cooling-off: full refund; after: prorated (remaining months / total × price minus claims) | CSR | CS Supervisor | 10–15 min |
| 2 | System processes refund to original payment method; terminates contract; adjusts warranty liability accrual; posts GL entries | System / Finance | Finance Manager | 5 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Warranty contract (links to VS-53 / W2136) — the contract termination
- Refund (links to VS-16 / W101, VS-17 / W9) — the refund processing
- Liability/accrual (links to VS-17 / W28, VS-157) — the accrual adjustment

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Proration-dispute risk**: the customer disputes the prorated refund (expected full refund after the cooling-off); mitigated by the proration-policy disclosed at sale
- **Refund-abuse risk**: a customer cancels after a large claim, getting a prorated refund despite the claim cost exceeding the premium; mitigated by the proration-minus-claims formula (the refund deducts claims paid)

### Automation Opportunity
- Proration auto-calculated; refund auto-processed; accrual auto-adjusted

### Controls
- CTL-390 (ensure controlled execution — Extended Warranty & Service Contract Management (PA-53.3).)
- operational: CS Supervisor sign-off; Finance Manager accrual-adjustment sign-off

### Time Estimate
~15 min/cancellation × ~300/month = **~75 hours/month CSR** + system.

## W2140. Performance Guarantee Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer invokes building material performance guarantee |
| **Frequency** | ~500–800 guarantee claims/month; peaks rainy season |
| **Volume** | ~500–800/month; high value (house repaint, re-tile) |
| **Owner** | Building Materials Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Technical Specialist, Category Manager, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Technical Specialist dispatched to customer site: inspects conditions, takes samples/photos, assesses product defect vs. installation error vs. environmental | Technical Specialist | Category Manager | 2–4 hours |
| 2 | Specialist submits report: product defect → full claim (material + labor); installation error → deny; mixed cause → partial coverage | Technical Specialist | Category Manager | 1–2 days |
| 3 | For approved claims: processes material replacement and coordinates re-application; vendor notified for cost recovery | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 7–14 days resolution |

### System Touchpoints
- Guarantee claim (links to VS-53 / W2126 claim system, VS-31 / W110 quality) — the claim record and quality data
- Vendor recovery (links to VS-53 / W2128 vendor escalation, VS-03 / W88 RTV) — the vendor cost-recovery
- Installation/contractor (links to VS-12 / W12 installation services, VS-172 contractor network) — the installation-error assessment

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Attribution-dispute risk**: the customer attributes the failure to the product (guarantee claim) but the root cause is installation error (not covered); mitigated by the Technical Specialist's site inspection and the evidence-based attribution
- **High-cost-claim risk**: a full-house repaint or re-tile is a high-value claim that strains the guarantee reserve; mitigated by the vendor-cost-recovery (the manufacturer funds the material) and the insurance partner (W2141)

### Automation Opportunity
- Claim auto-routed to Technical Specialist; site-inspection report auto-templated; vendor-recovery auto-initiated on product-defect finding

### Controls
- CTL-390 (ensure controlled execution — Extended Warranty & Service Contract Management (PA-53.3).)
- operational: Category Manager sign-off on claims >PHP 50K

### Time Estimate
~3 hours/site-inspection + ~1.5 days report + ~10 days resolution × ~650/month = **~2,000 hours/month** Technical Specialist + Category Manager (distributed).

## W2141. Warranty Partner & Insurer Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual contract renewal or quarterly review |
| **Frequency** | Annual renewal; quarterly reviews |
| **Volume** | 1–2 warranty insurance partners; ~PHP 15–20M annual premium |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | Finance Controller, CFO, Insurance Partner, Legal, CEO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance reviews program financials: premium vs. claims recovered, loss ratio, admin costs; determines self-insure vs. partner by category | Finance Controller | CFO | 4–6 hours/quarter |
| 2 | VP Merchandising conducts quarterly performance review with partner: claim approval rate, turnaround, customer satisfaction, loss ratio | VP Merchandising | CFO | 2–3 hours/quarter |
| 3 | Annual: contract renewal negotiation; Legal reviews terms; VP presents strategy to Executive Committee | VP Merch / Legal | CEO | 2–3 days/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance (links to VS-26 / W59 insurance policy lifecycle, W610 claims) — the warranty-insurance partner
- Finance (links to VS-17 / W26 budget, VS-158 / W85) — the program financials
- Vendor scorecard (links to VS-67 / W2458) — the partner-performance review

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Self-insure-vs-partner-misjudgment risk**: BuildRight self-insures a category that turns out to have high claim rates (more expensive than a partner premium); mitigated by the quarterly loss-ratio review and the flexibility to switch
- **Partner-approval-rate risk**: the insurance partner denies too many claims, eroding customer satisfaction; mitigated by the quarterly performance review and the approval-rate SLA

### Automation Opportunity
- Program financials auto-assembled; loss-ratio auto-computed; partner-performance auto-scored

### Controls
- CTL-390: insurance-program accuracy — links to VS-26; CTL-37 (partner-claim integrity); operational: CFO quarterly sign-off; CEO annual strategy sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/quarter + ~3 days/year ≈ 20 + 24 hours = **~45 hours/year** VP Merchandising + Finance + Legal.
