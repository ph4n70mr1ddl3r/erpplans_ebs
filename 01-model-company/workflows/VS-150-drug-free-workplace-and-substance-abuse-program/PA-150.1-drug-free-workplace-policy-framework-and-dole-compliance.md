## W4457. Drug-Free Workplace Strategy, Policy & Operating Model

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DOLE requirement; policy review; incident |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on regulatory/incident change |
| **Volume** | One enterprise drug-free workplace program across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | HR (VS-19/VS-103) / HSE (VS-24) / Compliance (VS-22) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-103), HSE (VS-24), Compliance (VS-22), Legal (VS-100), Occupational Health (VS-83), Labor Relations (VS-84), Security (VS-23) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR/HSE/Compliance set the drug-free strategy & policy — objectives (safety, legal compliance, deterrence, rehabilitation), scope (all entities/sites/roles incl. safety-sensitive), and the prevention-vs-punishment balance (DOLE rehabilitation emphasis) | HR / HSE / Compliance | CHRO / COO / Compliance Lead | Annual |
| 2 | HR/Legal draft the written policy (required by D.O. 53-03) — prohibited conduct, testing types & circumstances, consequences, rehabilitation pathway, confidentiality, due process — and align with the CBA/handbook (VS-84) | HR / Legal / Labor Relations | CHRO / VP Legal | Annual |
| 3 | HR/Compliance cascade the policy to all employees (acknowledgement), embed in onboarding/handbook (VS-19), and review on regulatory/incident change | HR / Compliance / L&D | CHRO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS/policy (VS-19/VS-103); compliance (VS-22); legal (VS-100); HSE (VS-24); labor (VS-84); records (VS-88)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Policy-absence risk**: no written policy → DOLE D.O. 53-03 non-compliance + officers' RA 9165 liability; mitigated by documented policy
- **Punitive-only risk**: pure-punishment program conflicts with DOLE rehabilitation emphasis + morale; mitigated by rehabilitation pathway (W4473)

### Automation Opportunity
- Policy library; acknowledgement-tracking; onboarding/handbook integration; change-cascade

### Controls
- CTL-129 (ensure drug-free workplace program compliance.)
- CTL-680: written DOLE-aligned policy; scope all entities/roles; rehabilitation pathway; acknowledged by all employees; reviewed annually

### Time Estimate
- ~3–4 weeks annual policy + on change

### Cross-references
- links to VS-22 (compliance), VS-100 (legal), VS-24 (HSE), VS-84 (labor/CBA), VS-19 (onboarding), W4458 (regulatory), W4473 (rehab)

## W4459. Safety-Sensitive & Driver (LTFRB/DOTr) Role Identification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Policy (W4457); org/job change |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on role change |
| **Volume** | Safety-sensitive population (operators, drivers, chemical handlers) across 5 entities |
| **Owner** | HR (VS-103) / HSE (VS-24) / Fleet (VS-06) |
| **Participants** | HR (VS-103), HSE (VS-24), Fleet (VS-06), Store Ops (VS-07), Occupational Health (VS-83), Contingent (VS-98) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR/HSE/Fleet identify & classify **safety-sensitive** roles — powered-equipment operators (forklift/reach/order-picker), delivery drivers (LTFRB/DOTr-regulated), paint/chemical/hazmat handlers, equipment operators, security with firearm, and any role where impairment risks severe harm (links VS-147) | HR / HSE / Fleet | CHRO / HSE Lead | Annual + on change |
| 2 | HR/HSE set the testing-frequency/intensity per role class — safety-sensitive: random + post-incident + reasonable-suspicion + return-to-duty; non-safety-sensitive: pre-employment + reasonable-suspicion — per policy (W4457) | HR / HSE | CHRO / HSE Lead | Annual |
| 3 | HR/Fleet/Contingent flag safety-sensitive employees in HRIS, ensure license/certification (LTFRB for drivers), and extend requirements to contingent/contractor (W4463) | HR / Fleet / Contingent | CHRO / Fleet Lead | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- HRIS/job/role (VS-19/VS-103); HSE (VS-24); fleet/driver (VS-06.2); license (VS-06); contingent (VS-98); customer safety (VS-147)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-classification risk**: safety-sensitive role not flagged → under-tested → impairment risk; mitigated by structured classification & review
- **Driver-license-gap risk**: driver without valid LTFRB license/cert → regulatory + safety risk; mitigated by license tracking (VS-06)

### Automation Opportunity
- Role-classification engine; safety-sensitive flag in HRIS; license/certification tracking; auto-inclusion of contingent

### Controls
- CTL-129 (ensure drug-free workplace program compliance.)
- CTL-680: safety-sensitive roles classified & flagged; testing-frequency per class; driver license/cert tracked; contingent included (W4463)

### Time Estimate
- ~weeks annual classification; ongoing maintenance

### Cross-references
