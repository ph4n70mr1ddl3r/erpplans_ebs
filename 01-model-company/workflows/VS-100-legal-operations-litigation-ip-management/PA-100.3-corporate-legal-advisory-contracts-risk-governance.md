## W3273. Contract Template, Clause Library & Legal Review Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New contract type; legal-review request; template refresh |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + annual template refresh |
| **Volume** | Hundreds–thousands of contracts/year across commercial/vendor/customer/real-estate |
| **Owner** | Legal Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Legal Ops Mgr, In-House Counsel, Procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34), Sales (links to VS-11), VP Legal & Compliance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal Ops Mgr/In-House Counsel maintain contract template library and clause library: standard templates by type (vendor MSA per VS-03, customer/trade per VS-11, lease as lessee per VS-42 and lessor per VS-97, NDA per W3270, employment per VS-19, IP license per W3269), approved clause alternatives (limitation of liability, indemnity, termination, governing law, dispute resolution/ADR per W3260); version and publish | Legal Ops Mgr / In-House Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual + ongoing |
| 2 | Provide legal review support for non-standard contracts: triage incoming contracts (redlines, third-party paper), assess risk (commercial, legal, compliance, data-privacy per VS-91, anti-bribery per VS-86.3), negotiate key clauses, approve or escalate per authority matrix (links to VS-22 contract review W230) | In-House Counsel / Legal Ops Mgr | VP Legal & Compliance | Per contract |
| 3 | Capture learnings into templates: incorporate recurring issues, regulatory changes (links to VS-22.3), and dispute lessons (W3264) into clause library and templates; train business users on self-service of standard templates and escalation triggers | Legal Ops Mgr / In-House Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract template/CMS (links to VS-22/VS-88); procurement (links to VS-03/VS-34); privacy (links to VS-91); anti-bribery (links to VS-86.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Non-standard-contract risk: Unmanaged redlines introduce hidden risk; mitigated by review SLA and clause standards**
- **Template-staleness risk: Outdated clauses fail to reflect law/market; mitigated by annual refresh and regulatory monitoring (links to VS-22.3)**


### Automation Opportunity
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approved clause alternatives" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review support for non-standard contracts" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-capture of "Capture learnings into templates" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-531 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Legal Advisory, Contracts & Risk Governance (PA-100.3).)
- operational: review SLA and clause standards; operational: annual refresh and regulatory monitoring (links to VS-22)

### Time Estimate
- Per contract review; annual template cycle
