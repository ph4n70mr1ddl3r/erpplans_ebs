## W3257. Legal Matter Intake, Triage & Matter Master

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Demand letter / summons received; dispute escalated by business unit; regulatory inquiry |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~50–150 new matters/year |
| **Owner** | Legal Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Legal Ops Mgr, VP Legal & Compliance, In-House Counsel, Business Owner, Records (links to VS-88) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal Ops Mgr logs the intake of the matter: capture source (summons, demand letter, business escalation, regulatory notice — links to VS-22), parties, subject matter, jurisdiction/venue, amount in controversy, key dates (answer deadline, hearing), originating business unit and documents; create unique matter ID in legal matter-management system | Legal Ops Mgr | VP Legal & Compliance | Per matter |
| 2 | VP Legal & Compliance/In-House Counsel triage: assess merits, exposure, urgency, and complexity; assign handling (in-house vs outside counsel W3259), strategy (defend/settle/counterclaim/ADR W3260), and resourcing; classify by matter type (commercial, labor per VS-84, consumer/DTI per VS-13, property per VS-97, tax per VS-79, IP per PA-100.2); set reserves (W3261) | VP Legal & Compliance / In-House Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | Per matter |
| 3 | Open matter file and notify stakeholders: matter master (parties, counsel, dates, status, exposure, reserve), conflicts check (VS-100.3/W3275 conflict-of-interest also links to VS-86.3), litigation hold if applicable (W3262), business-owner briefing and document-collection request; integrate to risk register (links to VS-21.2) | Legal Ops Mgr / In-House Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | Per matter |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal matter-management/e-billing (W3279); records (links to VS-88); risk register (links to VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Missed-deadline risk**: Failure to answer/appear → default judgment; mitigated by docket/calendar discipline and triage SLA
- **Conflicts risk**: Undetected conflict of interest with BuildRight/counterparty; mitigated by conflicts check (W3275)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "Legal Ops Mgr logs the intake of the matter" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "VP Legal & Compliance/In-House Counsel triage" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-run of "conflicts check (W3275)" against the matter register (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-529 (ensure controlled execution — Legal Matter, Case & Outside Counsel Management (PA-100.1).)
- operational: docket/calendar discipline and triage SLA; operational: conflicts check (W3275)

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 days per matter intake/triage

## W3258. Litigation Case Lifecycle & Pre-Trial Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Active litigation matter |
| **Frequency** | Per case |
| **Volume** | ~30–80 active litigation cases |
| **Owner** | Litigation Counsel |
| **Participants** | Litigation Counsel, VP Legal & Compliance, Outside Counsel (W3259), Business Owner, Records (links to VS-88) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manage answer/response and pleadings: prepare and file answer/counterclaim/third-party complaint within deadline, motions to dismiss, motions for bill of particulars; coordinate with business owner on facts and defenses; track all filings and court dates in docket | Litigation Counsel / Outside Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | Per case |
| 2 | Manage discovery: written discovery (requests for admission, interrogatories, requests for documents), depositions, pre-trial conference and pre-trial order; coordinate evidence and witnesses (W3262), e-discovery with records (links to VS-88 W2990); manage privilege review and protective orders | Litigation Counsel / Outside Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | Per case |
| 3 | Trial preparation and conduct: trial strategy, witness preparation, exhibit management, trial brief; attend hearings/trial; manage settlement opportunities throughout (W3261); update matter status, reserve, and stakeholders | Litigation Counsel / Outside Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Matter/docket management (W3279); records/e-discovery (links to VS-88); risk register (links to VS-21.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Adverse-judgment risk**: Loss at trial with damages and costs; mitigated by early merits assessment and settlement discipline (W3261)
- **Discovery-miss risk**: Failure to produce leads to sanctions/adverse inference; mitigated by hold (W3262) and records coordination (links to VS-88)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-track of "track all filings and court dates in docket" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "records" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-update of "update matter status, reserve, and stakeholders" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-529 (ensure controlled execution — Legal Matter, Case & Outside Counsel Management (PA-100.1).)
- operational: early merits assessment and settlement discipline (W3261); operational: hold (W3262) and records coordination (links to VS-88)

### Time Estimate
- Months–years per case (Philippine civil/criminal dockets)

| **Frequency** | Per engagement |
| **Volume** | ~10–30 active outside-counsel engagements |
| **Owner** | Legal Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Legal Ops Mgr, VP Legal & Compliance, Procurement (links to VS-34), Finance, Outside Counsel |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Legal & Compliance/Procurement (links to VS-34) select outside counsel: identify qualified firms (expertise, jurisdiction, track record), run conflicts check (W3275), request fee proposals; evaluate on expertise, cost, and fit; select per authority matrix; consider panel/preferred-firm strategy for recurring matter types | VP Legal & Compliance / Procurement / Legal Ops Mgr | VP Legal & Compliance | Per engagement |
| 2 | Legal/Finance negotiate engagement letter: scope, fee structure (hourly with rate caps, fixed/flat fee, contingency, blended), budget, staffing, conflict and ethical walls, document-handling/privilege, billing guidelines, termination; execute engagement letter and matter budget | Legal Ops Mgr / Finance / Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | Per engagement |
| 3 | Manage outside counsel performance and billing: review and approve invoices against engagement terms and budget (e-billing W3279), challenge excessive/unsupported entries, track spend vs budget and estimate-to-complete, manage counsel performance (responsiveness, quality); periodic review of panel | Legal Ops Mgr / Finance | VP Legal & Compliance | Per invoice/cycle |

- **Quality/fit risk**: Poor counsel performance harms outcomes; mitigated by panel management and performance review

- System auto-document of "document-handling/privilege, billing guidelines, termination" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review and approve invoices against engagement terms and budget" (replaces manual Step 3).

## W3261. Settlement, Judgment & Loss Accrual Accounting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Settlement negotiation; judgment; probable loss |
| **Frequency** | Per matter + period-end |
| **Volume** | ~20–50 settlements/judgments/year |
| **Owner** | VP Legal & Compliance |
| **Participants** | VP Legal & Compliance, Finance (links to VS-17), Litigation Counsel, Tax (links to VS-79), Treasury (links to VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish settlement authority and range: VP Legal & Compliance and Finance assess exposure, probable outcome, settlement value vs litigation cost/risk; obtain settlement authority per authority matrix (links to VS-36); negotiate within authority; for judgments, analyze appeal prospects | VP Legal & Compliance / Finance / Litigation Counsel | CEO | Per matter |
| 2 | Finance assess and accrue loss contingency per PFRS/IAS 37: probable outflow and reliable estimate → accrue and disclose; possible → disclose only; remote → no action; update reserve as facts develop; coordinate with audit (links to VS-22.2) and consolidation (links to VS-17.2) | Finance Analyst / VP Legal & Compliance | Controller | Periodic |
| 3 | Execute and account: settlement agreement and release, payment (links to VS-15/VS-18), tax treatment (deductibility, DST on settlement documents, links to VS-79), derecognize reserve and book gain/loss on settlement; close matter (W3257) and update analytics (W3264) | Finance / Legal Ops Mgr / Tax | CFO | Per settlement |

### System Touchpoints
- Matter/claim accounting (links to VS-17); treasury/payment (links to VS-18); tax (links to VS-79); audit (links to VS-22.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-accrual risk**: Inadequate reserve distorts financials and surprises audit; mitigated by PAS 37 (IAS 37) discipline and legal-finance coordination
- **Over-settlement risk**: Settling unmeritorious claims invites more claims; mitigated by merits assessment and authority discipline


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-analyze of "analyze appeal prospects" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-update of "update reserve as facts develop" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "Execute and account" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-154 (ensure legal matter & IP portfolio control.)
- operational: PAS 37 (IAS 37) discipline and legal-finance coordination; operational: merits assessment and authority discipline

### Time Estimate
- Per matter; periodic accrual review

## W3262. Litigation Hold Initiation & Evidence Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Reasonable anticipation of litigation (W3257); regulatory investigation |
| **Frequency** | Per matter |
| **Volume** | ~20–40 active holds |
| **Owner** | Litigation Counsel |
| **Participants** | Litigation Counsel, Legal Ops Mgr, Records (links to VS-88), IT (links to VS-27/VS-99), Business Owners, Custodians |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Litigation Counsel issue litigation hold notice: identify custodians and data sources (email, files, ERP records, CCTV per VS-23.2, POS/WMS data), suspend retention/deletion for hold scope (executed by VS-88 W2990 legal-hold), notify custodians of preservation obligation and consequences of spoliation | Litigation Counsel / Legal Ops Mgr | VP Legal & Compliance | Per matter |
| 2 | Coordinate evidence collection and preservation: collect and preserve relevant documents/data (chain of custody per VS-88 W2990), coordinate IT for electronic evidence (links to VS-27/VS-99), preserve CCTV/physical evidence within retention windows, manage privilege and protective orders | Litigation Counsel / Records / IT | VP Legal & Compliance | Per matter |
| 3 | Release hold at matter close: when litigation/investigation concludes, issue hold-release to records (links to VS-88) to resume normal retention/deletion; document hold lifecycle for audit (links to VS-21/VS-22.2) | Litigation Counsel / Records | VP Legal & Compliance | At close |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal hold/records (links to VS-88); e-discovery (links to VS-88 W2990); IT/evidence (links to VS-27/VS-99); CCTV (links to VS-23.2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Spoliation risk**: Destroyed/altered evidence triggers adverse inference and sanctions; mitigated by timely hold and preservation
- **Over-collection cost**: Over-broad holds inflate e-discovery cost; mitigated by scoped holds and custodian discipline


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "records, CCTV per VS-23.2, POS/WMS data" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "Coordinate evidence collection and preservation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System continuous audit of "Release hold at matter close" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-529 (ensure controlled execution — Legal Matter, Case & Outside Counsel Management (PA-100.1).)
- operational: timely hold and preservation; operational: scoped holds and custodian discipline

### Time Estimate
- Per matter; ongoing hold management

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
