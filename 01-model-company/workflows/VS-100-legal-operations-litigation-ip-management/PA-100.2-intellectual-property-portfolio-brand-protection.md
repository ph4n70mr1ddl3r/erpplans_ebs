## W5563. Brand-Impersonation Commerce-Scam Response (Fake Sellers & Fraudulent Pickup Offers)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Discovery that scammers are impersonating BuildRight to defraud consumers — fake "BuildRight sale/clearance" social-media pages and marketplace listings selling goods the scammers never hold, too-good-to-be-true offers with "pickup at [BuildRight store]", fake promo/raffle pages harvesting personal data, or scam victims arriving at a store demanding goods they "paid" online |
| **Frequency** | Recurring brand-abuse class: several confirmed impersonation waves per year across social platforms and marketplace apps (Philippine online-shopping scam prevalence); a wave can spike sharply when a scam listing goes viral |
| **Volume** | 1 wave × the takedown surface (fake pages, listings, e-wallet handles) × victim contacts arriving at ~200 stores' service desks; the blast radius is brand trust and complaint volume, not inventory — the goods never existed |
| **Owner** | IP Counsel (event owner); VP Legal & Compliance (enforcement posture) |
| **Participants** | Brand/Marketing (VS-14 advisory), Store Operations & service desks (victim-arrival scripts), VS-27.3 (phishing-domain/infrastructure coordination), platform fraud channels & marketplace IP desks, PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group (referral), VS-91 (breach assessment where personal data was harvested), Customer Service (VS-13), Talent Acquisition (W5557's careers-site advisory where the wave touches hiring) |

### Background

'Fake seller'/'scam listing' appear in zero PA files — W3271/W3272 own the monitoring and takedown machinery, W3268 the infringement action against identifiable abusers, W5557 the recruitment-fraud variant whose victims arrive at BuildRight's own door, and VS-125 watches customer-side payment fraud — nobody owned the commerce-scam event itself: the victims-arrive-at-our-stores protocol (people who paid scammers presenting at a service desk expecting goods), the same-day public advisory with a single verification source of truth, the one-simultaneous-wave takedown discipline, the e-wallet recall guidance and PNP-ACG referral, or the data-harvesting variant that triggers the W53 breach-assessment clock.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Verify & scope**: confirm the accounts/pages are not BuildRight's (cloned logos, typo-squat handles); inventory the takedown surface; preserve evidence — URLs, screenshots, payment trails, victim reports | Brand / IP | IP Counsel | Same day |
| 2 | **Victim-arrival protocol**: service-desk script live chain-wide — victims are treated as customers of a fraud committed against BuildRight's brand: never blamed, given the advisory sheet, guided to the platform report channel, the same-day e-wallet/bank recall request, and the PNP Anti-Cybercrime referral | Store Operations / VS-13 | VP Store Operations | Same day |
| 3 | **Public advisory**: same-day advisory on official channels — BuildRight never sells via personal e-wallets or chat handles; the official store list and the verification page are the single source of truth; no engagement with scammers | Brand / Marketing | VP Legal & Compliance | Same day |
| 4 | **Takedown wave**: one simultaneous multi-surface report wave riding the W3271/W3272 machinery plus platform fraud/IP channels — registrar, marketplaces, social platforms, e-wallet providers — never filed serially | IP Counsel / VS-27.3 | IP Counsel | 1–3 days |
| 5 | **Law-enforcement & regulatory referral**: evidence pack to the PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group; DTI advisory-office coordination where a promo/giveaway scam is widespread | Legal | VP Legal & Compliance | 1–2 weeks |
| 6 | **Data-harvesting variant**: where the fake page harvested personal data (fake raffle/promo forms), route to the W53 breach assessment — the NPC 72-hour clock applies from confirmation | VS-91 / DPO | Data Protection Officer | Per W53 |
| 7 | **After-action**: wave post-mortem — verification-page discoverability, service-desk script refresh, platform-relationship notes, and W3271 watch-list tuning for known scammer handles | IP Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | 1–2 weeks post-wave |

### System Touchpoints
- Brand-protection case record with evidence attachments on the W3271 watch list
- Takedown-request tracking per platform with status and impact notes
- Advisory-banner CMS on official channels and the careers site (links to W3762)
- Store-communication cascade (VS-63) carrying the victim-arrival script
- PNP-ACG/DTI referral evidence pack assembled from the case record
- Breach-assessment intake (links to VS-91/W53) for data-harvesting variants

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Victim-trust risk**: victims blamed or bounced at the service desk becomes the viral story; mitigated by Step 2's empathy-first script and advisory sheet
- **Serialization risk**: takedowns filed one platform at a time let the wave hop surfaces for weeks; mitigated by Step 4's simultaneous-wave discipline
- **Payment-recovery loss**: e-wallet remittances to scammers are rarely recoverable after day one; mitigated by Step 2's same-day recall guidance and the ACG referral
- **Brand-confusion risk**: fake pages outrank official channels in search and social; mitigated by Step 3's verification source of truth and Step 7's discoverability work
- **Recurrence risk**: the same operators re-emerge under new handles; mitigated by the W3271 watch list and Step 7's monitoring tuning

### Automation Opportunity
- Brand-monitoring alerts routed into a case queue with scam-handle clustering (replaces manual discovery)
- Takedown-request templates per platform with status tracking and escalation timers
- Advisory-banner CMS push to official channels and the store-communication cascade
- Service-desk script distribution with acknowledgment tracking across ~200 stores

### Controls
- CTL-530 (ensure controlled execution — Intellectual Property Portfolio & Brand Protection (PA-100.2).)
- operational: evidence preserved before takedown requests are filed; the advisory text approved by Legal before publication; victim-arrival scripts version-controlled and acknowledged by all service desks

### Time Estimate
Per wave ≈ 20–40 staff-hours across legal, brand and store communications (verification, advisory, takedown wave, referral pack) plus service-desk minutes per victim contact; the several-waves-per-year cadence stays absorbed within the brand-protection, store-communications and service-desk teams.

### Staffing Implication
- Absorbed by the IP/legal bench, brand team and store service desks; no incremental headcount.
