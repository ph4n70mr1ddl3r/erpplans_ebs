## W5273. Disaster Incident Staging and Physical Site Access Control

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Typhoon, fire, or earthquake incident occurrence with structural store impact (VS-69) |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc per disaster incident |
| **Volume** | ~3-5 major incident staging events annually |
| **Owner** | Loss Prevention Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, Assistant SM, Security Guard, HSE Specialist (VS-24) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Assess immediate threat levels and secure the perimeter with safety barriers. | Security Guard | Loss Prevention Manager | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Verify that all customers and personnel are accounted for per W471. | Store Manager | Loss Prevention Manager | 1 hour |
| 3 | Deploy temporary signage restricting public entry and establish secure security staging gates. | Assistant SM | Loss Prevention Manager | 2-3 hours |
| 4 | Issue temporary access badges to certified emergency assessment personnel only. | Security Guard | Loss Prevention Manager | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Physical security logs, HSE incident module (VS-24.1), access system (VS-27).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Trespass injury risk**: Public enters damaged site and gets injured, creating corporate liability; mitigated by physical perimeter barriers and 24/7 security guard staging.

### Automation Opportunity
- Geofenced access control alerts through the corporate security dashboard.

### Controls
- CTL-311: detect physical security breaches — disaster-site access control; LP Manager access approvals.

### Time Estimate
- ~1 day for site access setup

### Cross-references
- links to VS-69 (typhoon), VS-24 (HSE), VS-147 (premises liability)

## W5274. Rapid Structural Integrity and Safety Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Established physical site access control (W5273) |
| **Frequency** | Per disaster site deployment |
| **Volume** | ~3-5 structural reviews annually |
| **Owner** | Director, Facilities & Real Estate (dual-hat GM, BuildRight Property Mgmt, Inc.) |
| **Participants** | Structural Engineer, Geodetic Engineer, Store Manager, Safety Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct visual check of structural elements (roof, beams, load-bearing walls, foundations). | Structural Engineer | Director, Facilities & Real Estate, Facilities & Real Estate | 3-5 hours |
| 2 | Verify structural safety using specialized testing equipment (Schmidt rebound hammer, ultrasonic). | Structural Engineer | Director, Facilities & Real Estate, Facilities & Real Estate | 2-4 hours |
| 3 | Assess secondary hazards: electrical wire grounding, gas leaks, water main bursts. | Safety Officer | Director, Facilities & Real Estate, Facilities & Real Estate | 2-3 hours |
| 4 | Draft and sign Rapid Structural Integrity Assessment Report; tag site status (Green, Yellow, Red). | Structural Engineer | Director, Facilities & Real Estate, Facilities & Real Estate | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- Facilities Maintenance module (VS-20.3), OHS Compliance tracker.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Premature site clearance risk**: Store cleared for entry while structural elements are weak, risking collapse; mitigated by requiring certified Structural Engineer sign-off on integrity report.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-782 (ensure controlled execution — Structural Damage & Insurance Appraisal Coordination (PA-184.1).)
- operational: OHS incident prevention; facilities standards verification.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 working days per structural check

### Cross-references
- links to VS-20.3 (maintenance), VS-147 (premises liability)

## W5275. Detailed Technical Damage Quantification and Geodetic Survey

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Rapid Structural Assessment tagged Yellow or Red (W5274) |
| **Frequency** | Per yellow/red tagged site |
| **Volume** | ~2-3 detailed quantifications annually |
| **Owner** | Quantity Surveyor / Project Engineer |
| **Participants** | Structural Engineer, Geodetic Engineer, Finance Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct detailed measurement of damaged structural and architectural elements. | Quantity Surveyor / Project Engineer | Quantity Surveyor / Project Engineer | 4-6 hours |
| 2 | Perform geodetic slope and soil shift surveys to check for land subsidence. | Geodetic Engineer | Quantity Surveyor / Project Engineer | 3-5 hours |
| 3 | Compile Detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ) mapping replacement materials, steel, and concrete volumes. | Quantity Surveyor / Project Engineer | Quantity Surveyor / Project Engineer | 1-2 days |
| 4 | Verify BOQ material costs with current price matrices and generate project estimate. | Finance Analyst | Quantity Surveyor / Project Engineer | 2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Project Management module (VS-112), Cost module (VS-40).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Underestimated BOQ risk**: Rebuilding budget is insufficient due to missed structural damages, delaying completion; mitigated by double-checking BOQ against original blueprints (VS-176).

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-782: ensure controlled execution — Structural Damage & Insurance Appraisal Coordination (PA-184.1). — CAPA authorization controls; Director, Facilities & Real Estate sign-off required.

### Time Estimate
- ~3-5 working days per quantification

### Cross-references
- links to VS-112 (PMO), VS-40 (capex), VS-176 (blueprints)

## W5276. Insurance Loss Adjuster Audit Coordination and Proof of Loss Submission

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved BOQ and project cost estimate (W5275) |
| **Frequency** | Per insurance claim cycle |
| **Volume** | ~3-5 insurance audit events annually |
| **Owner** | Insurance Administrator |
| **Participants** | Loss Adjuster, Director, Facilities & Real Estate, Chief Accountant (VS-17.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compile insurance claim packet: incident reports, photos, rapid assessments, BOQ. | Insurance Administrator | Insurance Administrator | 4-6 hours |
| 2 | Coordinate site visit and inspection with designated Loss Adjuster. | Insurance Administrator | Insurance Administrator | 4 hours |
| 3 | Negotiate damage valuations and present technical engineering evidence. | Director, Facilities & Real Estate | Insurance Administrator | 3-5 hours |
| 4 | Draft and execute final Proof of Loss document and submit to insurance carrier. | Insurance Administrator | Insurance Administrator | 2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance Claim Portal (VS-26.3), GL Module.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Claim depreciation risk**: Insurance adjuster applies high depreciation rates to replacement costs, lowering payout; mitigated by presenting detailed original cost ledgers (VS-35) and repair quotes.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated claim documentation compiler parsing data directly from asset registers.

### Controls
- CTL-34: Complete tax compliance controls; CFO approval on Proof of Loss.

### Time Estimate
- ~10-20 working days for adjuster reviews and final submission

### Cross-references
- links to VS-26.3 (insurance), VS-35 (fixed assets)

## W5277. Emergency Scrap Defective Inventory and Asset Disposal Routing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Adjuster site inspection complete (W5276) |
| **Frequency** | Per insurance clearance |
| **Volume** | ~3-5 disposal runs annually |
| **Owner** | Store Manager / DC Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Stock Associate, Loss Prevention Officer, Scrap Dealer, Hazmat hauler (VS-114) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify that insurance adjusters have cleared materials for disposal. | Store Manager / DC Supervisor | Store Manager / DC Supervisor | 1 hour |
| 2 | Segregate scrap steel, lumber, and copper wire; contact scrap dealer. | Stock Associate | Store Manager / DC Supervisor | 3-5 hours |
| 3 | Identify hazardous materials (damaged paints, solvents) and route to certified hazmat disposal. | Hazmat hauler | Store Manager / DC Supervisor | 2-3 hours |
| 4 | Record physical inventory write-off in system and issue scrap sale receipt. | Store Manager / DC Supervisor | Store Manager / DC Supervisor | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- Inventory Adjustment module (VS-05.3), Tax Module.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Environmental compliance penalty risk**: Damaged chemical/paint inventory dumped in general waste, violating DENR guidelines; mitigated by utilizing licensed hazardous waste haulers (VS-114).

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile disposal checklist requiring upload of waste manifest and disposal certificate.

### Controls
- CTL-26: Asset disposal controls; DENR manifest matching requirements.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 days disposal duration

### Cross-references
- links to VS-05.3 (disposition), VS-114 (hazardous goods)

## W5278. Insurance Settlement Negotiation and Recovery Accounting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Proof of Loss submitted (W5276) and adjuster evaluation report received |
| **Frequency** | Per settlement cycle |
| **Volume** | ~3-5 settlements annually |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Insurance Administrator, CFO, Chief Accountant (VS-17.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review insurance settlement offer; compare valuation against BOQ. | Insurance Administrator | Finance Controller | 2-3 hours |
| 2 | Negotiate settlement adjustments with underwriter if discrepancies exist. | CFO | Finance Controller | 3-5 hours |
| 3 | Accept final settlement offer and monitor fund transfer clearance. | Finance Controller | Finance Controller | 1 hour |
| 4 | Post insurance recovery journal entries and offset loss accounts in GL. | Chief Accountant | Finance Controller | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- GL Module (VS-17.1), Cash Book (VS-18).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Loss misclassification risk**: Recovery funds misbooked as operational revenue instead of capital asset offset, distorting EBITDA; mitigated by separate chart of accounts for insurance recoveries.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automated bank clearance matching settlement reference codes.

### Controls
- CTL-29: accurate consolidated reporting; CFO sign-off on settlement releases.

### Time Estimate
- ~15-30 days for payment clearance

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17 (finance), VS-18 (treasury)

| **Volume** | ~3-5 reviews annually |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct post-disaster operational audit: evaluate response timeline, safety effectiveness, and damage scope. | Risk Management Specialist | Risk Management Specialist | 3-5 hours |

- Automated GIS map updates utilizing flood sensor and local weather agency data feeds.

### Controls
- CTL-30: Month-end control standards; Audit Committee board review.

### Time Estimate
- ~2 days compilation

### Cross-references
- links to VS-21 (internal audit), VS-29 (master data), VS-26 (continuity)
