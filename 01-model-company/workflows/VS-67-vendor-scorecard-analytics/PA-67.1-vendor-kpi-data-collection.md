## W2454. Vendor KPI Framework Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual KPI review or new vendor category |
| **Frequency** | Annual review; continuous monitoring |
| **Volume** | ~20 KPIs tracked for ~800–1,000 vendors |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement Mgr, Category Mgrs, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement Manager maintains the KPI framework across seven dimensions: (a) on-time delivery rate (target ≥95%), (b) order fill rate (target ≥98%), (c) quality rejection rate (target <2%), (d) invoice accuracy (target ≥99%), (e) responsiveness to RFQ/orders (target <48 hours), (f) price competitiveness vs. market index, (g) innovation contribution (new-SKU introduction, joint promotions) | Procurement Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2–3 days/year + 2–3 hours/month |
| 2 | Reviews and adjusts KPI weights by vendor category — import vs. local, strategic vs. tactical, merchandise vs. non-merchandise — so the composite reflects current business priorities (e.g., import vendors weighted harder on delivery due to lead-time risk; local vendors weighted harder on responsiveness) | Procurement Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1–2 days/year |
| 3 | Documents framework version, effective date, and rationale in the procurement policy repository; communicates changes to Category Managers and Buyers before the next scoring cycle so no mid-period surprises invalidate vendor reviews | Procurement Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2–4 hours/version |

### System Touchpoints
- Procurement/supplier-performance module — KPI-definition master (weights, targets, data sources) per vendor category; the configuration consumed by W2458's monthly calculation
- Master Data governance (links to VS-29 / W287 vendor-master) — vendor-category assignment drives which KPI set applies; CTL-51 stale-data check ensures category tags are current
- Document/policy repository (links to VS-88) — versioned framework document with approval trail

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Gaming risk**: vendors learn which KPIs are weighted and optimize narrowly (e.g., hit OTD by shipping short, padding fill rate at quality's expense); mitigated by the cross-dimensional penalty logic in W2458 (a miss in one dimension caps the composite) and the qualitative override in W2457
- **Stale-framework risk**: weights set for a pre-pandemic import mix no longer reflect a localized supply base; mitigated by the annual review trigger and a documented "weights changed" diff circulated with each version

### Automation Opportunity
- KPI-weight sensitivity simulator (VP Supply Chain models "if delivery weight 25%→35%" before committing); auto-draft of the framework-diff document from the configuration change log

### Controls
- CTL-06 (vendor-master integrity — category tag accuracy drives the right KPI set); operational: framework version approved by VP Supply Chain before the monthly calc reads it (W2458)

### Time Estimate
~2–3 days for the annual review + ~2–3 hours/month ongoing monitoring + ~1–2 days/year weight re-calibration = **~55–90 hours/year** absorbed by the Procurement Manager.

## W2455. Automated Vendor Data Collection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous transaction processing |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~1,200 POs and ~6,715 invoices/month |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 vendors with transaction data |
| **Owner** | System / Data Quality Analyst |
| **Participants** | System, Data Quality Analyst, Procurement |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System auto-collects vendor transaction data on each closed document: PO delivery date vs. promised date (on-time), PO quantity vs. GR received quantity (fill rate), GR quality flags from W110/W681 (rejection rate), 3-way-match variance from W7 (invoice accuracy), RFQ-to-response timestamp from W2 (responsiveness) | System | Data Quality Analyst | Automated |
| 2 | Data Quality Analyst monitors nightly collection job: reviews exception log (missing GR, unmatched invoice, orphaned RFQ); resolves source-system gaps or escalates to MDM (VS-29) for vendor-master fixes; confirms nightly record count within expected band before the monthly calc (W2458) reads the dataset | Data Quality Analyst | Procurement Manager | 30–60 min/day |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP transaction layer — PO (W2/W2B), GR (W3/W681), invoice match (W7/W7C), RFQ (W2) are the four source feeds; each event tags the vendor ID so aggregation is automatic
- Supplier portal (links to VS-28 / W593) — vendor-submitted ASN and invoice data captured directly, bypassing manual entry and improving invoice-accuracy KPI integrity
- Data-quality monitoring (links to VS-29 / W291) — nightly completeness check flags vendors with <minimum transaction volume for a reliable score

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Attributable-event risk**: a late delivery caused by BuildRight's own DC congestion (not the vendor) gets scored against the vendor, corrupting the OTD KPI; mitigated by a "BuildRight-caused delay" override flag at GR (links to W681 receiving exception) that excludes the event from the vendor's score
- **Thin-data risk**: a vendor with 2 POs/quarter has a statistically unstable score (one late shipment = 50% OTD); mitigated by the minimum-sample-size threshold (default ≥8 transactions/period) below which the vendor is flagged "insufficient data" rather than scored

### Automation Opportunity
- Event-streaming pipeline (POS/GR/invoice events already stream per POS-013) → vendor-scorecard fact table auto-populated; eliminates the nightly batch and the manual exception triage in step 2

### Controls
- CTL-44 (duplicate-invoice guard — prevents a double-counted invoice from inflating accuracy KPI); CTL-24 (receiving-fraud detection — fraudulent GR would corrupt fill-rate KPI)

### Time Estimate
Step 1 is automated (zero staff time). Step 2 ≈ 30–60 min/day × ~22 operating days = **~11–22 hours/month** Data Quality Analyst, absorbed into the existing data-ops role.

## W2456. Vendor Quality Data Aggregation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly quality data compilation |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; from VS-31 quality inspection results |
| **Volume** | ~2,400 quality inspections/month feeding vendor scores |
| **Owner** | Quality Manager |
| **Participants** | Quality Mgr, Procurement Mgr, DC Quality Inspectors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quality Manager aggregates per-vendor quality data from VS-31 inspections: pass rate, defect-type breakdown (cosmetic, functional, packaging, short-dated), complaint rate (from VS-13/W41), recall incidents (from VS-89/W29); feeds the quality dimension (30% weight) of the vendor scorecard | Quality Manager | VP Supply Chain | 4–6 hours/month |
| 2 | Flags vendors with declining quality trends (3-month rolling defect rate trending up, or any single-month defect spike >2× trailing average); routes flagged vendors to the W2462/W2463 corrective-action workflow | Quality Manager | Procurement Manager | 1–2 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Oracle Quality (links to VS-31 / W110 supplier-quality & CAPA, W681 DC receiving inspection — fit-gap C12) — inspection-result fact table is the source; defect-type taxonomy must match the VS-31 master
- CRM/complaint module (links to VS-13 / W41) — customer complaints attributed to a vendor via the item-master's primary-source vendor
- Product-recall register (links to VS-89 / W29) — recall events are a quality-dimension penalty with disproportionate weight

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Attribution risk**: a customer complaint logged against an item is attributed to the item's current vendor, but the defective unit may have been produced by a previous vendor (post-switch); mitigated by lot/serial traceability (W97/W296) tying the complaint to the actual producing vendor
- **Recall-distortion risk**: a single recall (rightly severe) can so dominate the quality score that a vendor's otherwise-excellent performance is masked for a year; mitigated by a recall sub-weight with a defined decay (full penalty 3 months, half 6 months, cleared 12 months post-resolution)

### Automation Opportunity
- Inspection-result → vendor-score auto-rollup (eliminates the monthly manual aggregation in step 1); defect-type Pareto auto-generated per vendor for the W2462 review

### Controls
- CTL-24: receiving-fraud / inspection integrity — falsified inspections would corrupt the quality KPI; operational: Quality Manager sign-off on the monthly quality-dimension extract before it feeds W2458

### Time Estimate
~5–8 hours/month (step 1 + step 2) Quality Manager = **~60–96 hours/year**, absorbed into the existing quality-reporting cadence.

## W2457. Vendor Survey & Qualitative Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual vendor relationship survey |
| **Frequency** | Annual; ~100 strategic vendors surveyed |
| **Volume** | ~100 vendors |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement Mgr, Category Mgrs, Buyers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement conducts the internal vendor-relationship survey: Category Managers and Buyers rate the ~100 strategic vendors on five qualitative dimensions — communication clarity, flexibility (order-change accommodation), problem-resolution speed, innovation contribution, strategic alignment with BuildRight's category plan | Category Managers / Buyers | Procurement Manager | 5–10 days/year (distributed across ~15 raters) |
| 2 | Procurement Manager normalizes scores (remove rater bias via z-score per rater), weights by the rater's transaction volume with the vendor, and produces the qualitative score that supplements the transactional KPIs in the composite (W2458) | Procurement Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2–3 days/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Survey/tooling (HR-style engagement-survey platform or a procurement module sub-form) — structured questionnaire with the five dimensions; anonymity optional (raters are internal, not the vendors)
- Supplier-performance module (links to W2458) — qualitative scores merged with transactional KPIs; the qualitative dimension carries ~10–15% of the composite
- Vendor-master (links to VS-29 / W287) — "strategic vendor" flag determines survey eligibility

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rater-bias risk**: a Buyer who had one bad interaction scores a vendor harshly across all dimensions, skewing the qualitative result; mitigated by the z-score normalization in step 2 and a minimum of 3 raters per strategic vendor
- **Recency-risk**: ratings reflect the last 30 days rather than the full year; mitigated by surveying at a fixed annual cadence with explicit "past 12 months" framing and a free-text justification that surfaces the temporal context

### Automation Opportunity
- Survey distribution + reminder automation; auto-flagging of vendors where rater-dispersion (std-dev) exceeds threshold for human review before the score is finalized

### Controls
- CTL-430 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor KPI Definition & Data Collection (PA-67.1).)
- Operational: Procurement Manager review of any vendor where qualitative and transactional scores diverge by >2 tiers (a control on gaming — a vendor beloved by Buyers but with poor OTD warrants investigation)

### Time Estimate
~5–10 days/year distributed across ~15 raters (≈ 2–4 hours/rater) + 2–3 days Procurement Manager normalization = **~80–130 person-hours/year** for the ~100 strategic vendors.

## W2458. Vendor Score Calculation & Weighting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly score calculation cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; ~800–1,000 vendors scored |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 scores/month |
| **Owner** | System / Procurement Analyst |
| **Participants** | System, Procurement Analyst, Procurement Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System calculates the composite score per vendor: weighted average of all KPI dimensions per the W2454 framework (quality 30%, delivery 25%, cost 20%, responsiveness 15%, innovation 10%); applies the cross-dimensional penalty (a bottom-quartile result in any one dimension caps the composite at "B" regardless of average); generates the monthly vendor ranking | System | Procurement Analyst | Automated |
| 2 | Procurement Analyst reviews the scores: validates data accuracy against source extracts, investigates anomalous month-over-month swings (>15 points), adjusts for exceptional circumstances (force majeure, seasonal volume spikes, documented BuildRight-caused delays from W2455); publishes the monthly vendor scorecard to Category Managers and the VP Supply Chain | Procurement Analyst | Procurement Manager | 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Supplier-performance module — the calculation engine; reads the W2454 framework master, the W2455 transactional facts, the W2456 quality facts, and the W2457 qualitative scores; outputs the composite + per-dimension drill-down
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113) — monthly scorecard dashboard, trend lines, peer-group comparison; the artifact Category Managers open in W2462 reviews
- Approval workflow (links to VS-88 document control) — versioned scorecard publication with the Analyst→Manager sign-off trail

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Black-box risk**: vendors (and internal Category Managers) distrust a composite they can't decompose; mitigated by the per-dimension drill-down published with every score and the cross-dimensional-penalty transparency (the cap is visible, not hidden in the math)
- **Override-abuse risk**: the "exceptional circumstances" override in step 2 can become a way to inflate a favored vendor's score; mitigated by the override log (every adjustment documented with reason + approver) reviewed quarterly in W2459

### Automation Opportunity
- Anomaly auto-flagging (the >15-point swing check) surfaced as a worklist rather than a manual hunt; the override log auto-generated from the approval workflow

### Controls
- CTL-48: rebate-dispute frequency is itself a vendor-scorecard input — links the commercial and performance views; operational: override log reviewed in the W2459 quarterly audit

### Time Estimate
Step 1 automated. Step 2 ≈ 4–6 hours/month Procurement Analyst = **~48–72 hours/year**, plus ~1 hour/month Procurement Manager publication sign-off.

## W2459. Vendor Scorecard Data Quality Audit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly data quality audit |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly; ~200 vendor scorecards spot-checked |
| **Volume** | ~200 spot-checks/quarter |
| **Owner** | Data Quality Analyst |
| **Participants** | Data Quality Analyst, Procurement Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audits scorecard integrity: validates data sources (W2455 transactional facts reconcile to GL/PO counts), checks for missing data periods, verifies calculation accuracy (re-computes a sample of 10 composite scores by hand), identifies vendors with anomalous override frequency from W2458 | Data Quality Analyst | Procurement Manager | 4–6 hours/quarter |
| 2 | Issues a data-quality attestation with the audit findings; any integrity failure triggers a scorecard re-publication for the affected vendors and a root-cause fix in the W2455 collection pipeline | Data Quality Analyst | Procurement Manager | 1–2 hours/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Data-quality monitoring (links to VS-29 / W291) — the same completeness/accuracy checks that govern master data apply to the scorecard fact table
- Audit log (links to VS-21 internal audit / W120 audit-planning) — the quarterly attestation is an audit-trail artifact Internal Audit can sample
- Supplier-performance module (W2458) — re-computation uses the same engine, so a hand-calc mismatch indicates a config or data-source bug, not a formula drift

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Silent-corruption risk**: a change to the W2454 framework (new weight) or a W2455 source-feed schema change silently breaks the calc and corrupts scores for months before anyone notices; mitigated by the quarterly re-computation sample and a control-total (composite-score network average must stay within a defined band month-over-month or alert)
- **Override-creep risk**: the W2458 override log accumulates adjustments that quietly invalidate the "objective" scorecard; mitigated by the override-frequency check in step 1 (a vendor with >3 overrides/quarter is flagged for Procurement Manager review)

### Automation Opportunity
- Control-total dashboard (network average composite + per-dimension averages with month-over-month drift alerts); automated reconciliation of scorecard fact count to GL vendor-invoice count

### Controls
- CTL-51: stale/incomplete master data — applies to the vendor-master tags driving the scorecard; operational: Data Quality Analyst attestation filed each quarter before the next cycle's publication

### Time Estimate
~5–8 hours/quarter (step 1 + step 2) = **~20–32 hours/year** Data Quality Analyst.

## W2460. New Vendor Baseline Performance Establishment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New vendor onboarded; first 6 months |
| **Frequency** | ~5–10 new vendors/month |
| **Volume** | ~5–10/month |
| **Owner** | Buyer |
| **Participants** | Buyer, Procurement Mgr, Quality Mgr |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For the vendor's first 6 months: enhanced monitoring on all KPIs at weekly (not monthly) cadence; establishes the baseline performance against which future months are compared; any KPI below threshold triggers an immediate corrective discussion (not waiting for the monthly cycle) | Buyer / Procurement Mgr | VP Supply Chain | 1–2 hours/week per new vendor |
| 2 | At month 6: Buyer and Quality Manager conduct a baseline-establishment review — confirm the vendor is performing within the negotiated commitments (vs. the sales-pitch promises), set the formal performance baseline, and transition the vendor from enhanced to standard monthly monitoring | Buyer / Quality Manager | Procurement Manager | 2–3 hours/vendor at month 6 |

### System Touchpoints
- Supplier-performance module (W2458) — "new vendor" flag triggers the weekly-cadence view and the 6-month baseline-capture workflow
- Vendor onboarding (links to VS-03 / W36, CTL-06) — the onboarding record carries the vendor's negotiated commitments (OTD, fill, quality targets) that the baseline is measured against
- Quality first-article inspection (links to VS-31 / W110) — the first 3 production lots receive enhanced inspection, feeding the quality baseline

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Honeymoon-corruption risk**: a vendor performs well during the monitored 6 months then degrades once on standard cadence; mitigated by the baseline being a floor (subsequent months must stay within 10% of baseline or auto-flag) rather than a one-time gate
- **Promise-vs-reality risk**: the vendor's negotiated commitments were optimistic (sales-side overpromise); the month-6 review surfaces the gap and resets the baseline to actuals, with a category-strategy implication if the vendor is structurally below what was sold; mitigated by the month-6 review sign-off by Buyer + Quality Manager

### Automation Opportunity
- Auto-transition from "new vendor" to "standard" monitoring at month 6 with the baseline locked; auto-flag any subsequent month >10% below baseline

### Controls
- CTL-06: vendor-onboarding integrity — the negotiated commitments must be documented at onboarding to be measurable at month 6; operational: month-6 review sign-off by Buyer + Quality Manager

### Time Estimate
~1–2 hours/week × 26 weeks × ~7 new vendors (mid-range) + ~2.5 hours × ~84 vendors/year at month 6 = **~250–400 hours/year**, distributed across Buyers and Quality (absorbed — part of vendor-management duties, not incremental headcount).

## W2461. Vendor KPI Benchmarking & Industry Comparison

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual benchmarking exercise |
| **Frequency** | Annual; top 50 strategic vendors |
| **Volume** | ~50 vendors benchmarked/year |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement Mgr, Category Mgrs, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Benchmarks the top 50 strategic vendors' KPIs against industry standards (retail-supplier benchmarks from a third-party service or a peer-retailer consortium) and BuildRight's own peer-group medians; identifies below-industry performers whose BuildRight score may look acceptable but is poor relative to market | Procurement Manager | VP Supply Chain | 3–5 days/year |
| 2 | Shares anonymized benchmark data with vendors during the annual W2462 performance review — frames "you are at the 40th percentile of your peer group" as a concrete improvement target rather than an abstract BuildRight expectation | Procurement Manager / Category Managers | VP Supply Chain | Absorbed into W2462 reviews |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113) — benchmark dataset loaded annually; merged with the internal scorecard for the percentile view
- Supplier-performance module (W2458) — the benchmark adds a "market percentile" column to each vendor's scorecard
- Third-party benchmark feed (external data integration) — annual refresh; data-use license terms documented in the procurement policy

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Apples-to-oranges risk**: industry benchmarks define "on-time delivery" differently than BuildRight (e.g., ship-date vs. receipt-date), making percentiles misleading; mitigated by a documented definition-mapping before any benchmark is loaded, and footnoting the percentile with the definitional caveat
- **Benchmark-staleness risk**: the third-party dataset is a year old and the market has moved; mitigated by the annual refresh trigger and a "benchmark vintage" date displayed on every percentile

### Automation Opportunity
- Benchmark-definition mapping maintained as a config (not a manual reconciliation each year); percentile auto-computed on load rather than hand-calculated

### Controls
- CTL-430 (ensure controlled execution — Vendor KPI Definition & Data Collection (PA-67.1).)
- Operational: benchmark dataset approved by Procurement Manager before it feeds the scorecard (prevents an unvetted feed from skewing vendor percentiles)

### Time Estimate
~3–5 days/year Procurement Manager for the benchmark exercise + definition mapping = **~24–40 hours/year**; step 2 absorbed into the W2462 annual review cycle.
