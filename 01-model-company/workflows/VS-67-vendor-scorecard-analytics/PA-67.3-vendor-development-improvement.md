## W2470. Vendor Capability Assessment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor development need identified |
| **Frequency** | ~20–30 assessments/year |
| **Volume** | ~20–30/year |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement Mgr, Quality Mgr, Category Mgr, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement conducts a structured capability assessment across six dimensions: production capacity (can the vendor absorb BuildRight's growth?), quality systems (ISO/process maturity), logistics capability (drop-ship, cross-dock, import handling), technology readiness (EDI/API/e-invoice maturity for W2472), financial stability (links to W2475), sustainability practices (links to W2474); identifies the highest-leverage development areas | Procurement Manager / Quality Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2–3 days per assessment |
| 2 | Documents the assessment with evidence (site-visit notes for strategic vendors, self-assessment questionnaire for others) and feeds the findings into the W2471/W2472/W2474 development plans and the W2463 quarterly review | Procurement Manager | VP Supply Chain | 0.5–1 day per assessment |

### System Touchpoints
- Supplier-performance module (W2458) — assessment findings attach to the vendor record and inform the next scorecard cycle
- Oracle Quality (links to VS-31 / W110 supplier-quality & CAPA — fit-gap C12) — quality-systems dimension draws from inspection history and CAPA closure rate
- Document repository (links to VS-88) — assessment evidence retained as audit trail

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Self-assessment-inflation risk**: vendors overstate capability on a self-assessment questionnaire to qualify for Strategic-tier benefits; mitigated by on-site verification for any vendor being considered for tier-up (W2464) and sample audits of self-assessments
- **Stale-assessment risk**: a capability assessment done 3 years ago no longer reflects a vendor that has since invested (or regressed); mitigated by the assessment-refresh trigger tied to the W2464 annual tier review

### Automation Opportunity
- Self-assessment questionnaire delivered and scored via the supplier portal (W593); capability-gap auto-identified by comparing assessment results to the Strategic-tier benchmark

### Controls
- CTL-50: vendor-master integrity — assessment outcome recorded against the vendor record as a controlled change; operational: Quality Manager co-signs the quality-systems dimension

### Time Estimate
~2.5–4 days/assessment × ~25 assessments/year = **~500–800 hours/year** (mostly Procurement Manager + Quality Manager), concentrated on the ~20–30 vendors with development potential rather than the full ~800–1,000 base.

## W2471. Vendor Training & Development Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Capability gap identified; training designed |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 vendor training events/year |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 events/year |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement Mgr, Quality Mgr, IT, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Designs and delivers targeted training to close a W2470-identified gap: BuildRight quality standards and packaging requirements, delivery-scheduling and ASN discipline, vendor-portal usage (W593), BIR-compliant invoice formatting (links to VS-79), e-invoicing onboarding; delivered on-site (for strategic vendors) or virtual (for breadth); tracks post-training adoption in the next scorecard cycle | Procurement Manager / Quality Manager | VP Supply Chain | 1–2 days per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Supplier portal (links to VS-28 / W593) — both the training-delivery channel (recorded modules) and the subject of portal-usage training
- BIR e-invoicing config (links to VS-79 / W473) — invoice-formatting training references the live BIR requirement
- OLA / training records (links to VS-19.4 learning & development, applied supplier-side) — completion tracked per vendor contact

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Training-without-adoption risk**: the vendor attends but behavior doesn't change (invoice-accuracy KPI doesn't improve post-training); mitigated by tracking the specific KPI the training targeted in the next 2 scorecard cycles and re-engaging if no movement
- **Turnover-erosion risk**: the trained vendor contact leaves and the capability walks out the door; mitigated by training vendor *teams* (not individuals) and recording training materials in the portal for new-contact onboarding

### Automation Opportunity
- Self-paced training modules on the supplier portal with completion tracking; KPI-impact dashboard auto-correlating training completion with subsequent scorecard movement

### Controls
- Operational: training completion recorded as a prerequisite for tier-up consideration (W2464); CTL-06 vendor-master carries the training-compliance flag

### Time Estimate
~1.5 days/event × ~12 events/year = **~140–180 hours/year** Procurement Manager + Quality Manager, plus vendor-side time (not BuildRight cost).

## W2472. Vendor Technology Enablement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Vendor needs system upgrade for BuildRight integration |
| **Frequency** | ~5–10 technology enablement projects/year |
| **Volume** | ~5–10/year |
| **Owner** | IT Integration Lead |
| **Participants** | IT Integration Lead, Procurement Mgr, Vendor IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT helps vendors integrate with BuildRight's systems: EDI/API for PO and order-status exchange, ASN (advance shipping notice) for inbound visibility, e-invoicing for automated AP matching (improves the W2455 invoice-accuracy KPI), vendor-portal adoption for self-service PO/ASN/invoice; provides documentation, sandbox access, and technical support through go-live | IT Integration Lead | CIO | 5–10 days per vendor |

### System Touchpoints
- Integration layer / API gateway (links to VS-27 / W257 enterprise API lifecycle) — the EDI/API endpoints vendors integrate against
- Supplier portal (links to VS-28 / W593) — the lower-effort alternative for vendors not ready for full EDI
- AP invoice automation (links to VS-15 / W7 3-way match) — the downstream beneficiary; e-invoiced vendors hit higher invoice-accuracy KPI scores

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Uneven-capability risk**: a small local vendor cannot afford EDI integration and is disadvantaged on the technology dimension relative to large import vendors; mitigated by the supplier-portal fallback (W593) that gives small vendors a no-code path to ASN/e-invoice
- **Integration-debt risk**: a vendor integrates once but doesn't maintain the connection as BuildRight's API versions evolve, causing silent data-flow failures; mitigated by API-version lifecycle notices (W257) and the W2455 nightly collection-exception monitoring that surfaces a broken feed

### Automation Opportunity
- Self-service integration onboarding kit on the supplier portal (sandbox credentials, sample payloads, test harness) reducing IT Integration Lead hands-on time per vendor

### Controls
- CTL-42 (bank-account-change guard — a vendor integrating e-invoicing is also a moment where bank details may be "updated," so the out-of-band verification control applies); CTL-44 (duplicate-invoice guard — the e-invoice integration must not bypass dedup)

### Time Estimate
~7 days/vendor × ~7 projects/year = **~280–350 hours/year** IT Integration Lead, plus Procurement Manager coordination (~2 hours/vendor).

## W2473. Joint Business Planning with Strategic Vendors

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual joint business planning cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual; top 20 strategic vendors |
| **Volume** | ~20 joint plans/year |
| **Owner** | VP Supply Chain / VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Supply Chain, VP Merch, Category Mgrs, Strategic Vendors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Joint planning session with each of the top 20 strategic vendors: review prior-year performance against last year's joint plan, align on next-year growth targets and category strategy, discuss new-product development (links to W2476 innovation), agree volume commitments and pricing (links to VS-39 rebates), set collaboration goals (sustainability, technology, joint marketing) | VP Supply Chain / VP Merchandising | CEO | 1–2 days per vendor |
| 2 | Documents the joint business plan (JBP) in a shared artifact; category Manager tracks JBP commitments quarterly against actuals and surfaces misses in the W2463 strategic-vendor review | Category Manager | VP Supply Chain | Quarterly, absorbed into W2463 |

### System Touchpoints
- Supplier-performance module (W2458) — prior-year scorecard is the JBP's starting point
- Rebate/co-op agreement master (links to VS-39 / W27, VS-14 marketing co-op) — volume commitments and pricing terms recorded in the rebate agreement
- CRM / supplier-relationship artifact (links to VS-03 / W36) — the JBP document stored against the vendor record

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Commitment-amnesia risk**: the JBP is signed in January and not referenced again until next January's session, so misses compound silently; mitigated by the quarterly JBP-vs-actual tracking in step 2 surfaced in the W2463 review
- **Asymmetric-commitment risk**: BuildRight commits to volume but the vendor commits to little in return; mitigated by the structured JBP template requiring reciprocal commitments (volume ↔ price, growth ↔ capacity, exclusivity ↔ innovation)

### Automation Opportunity
- JBP-vs-actual dashboard auto-prepared each quarter (volume committed vs. purchased, price agreed vs. invoiced, innovation milestones committed vs. delivered)

### Controls
- CTL-39 (rebate accuracy — JBP pricing terms flow into the rebate agreement and are CTL-39-validated); operational: CEO sign-off on JBPs with the top 5 vendors (materiality control)

### Time Estimate
~1.5 days/vendor × 20 strategic vendors = **~240–300 hours/year** VPs + Category Managers, concentrated in the annual planning quarter.

## W2474. Vendor Sustainability Development

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | ESG improvement need identified per VS-25 |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 vendors/year needing sustainability development |
| **Volume** | ~10–15/year |
| **Owner** | ESG Manager / Procurement Mgr |
| **Participants** | ESG Mgr, Procurement Mgr, Vendor, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ESG Manager works with the vendor on the identified gap: environmental compliance (DENR effluent/emissions, hazardous-waste per VS-24/W82), labor standards (DOLE, links to VS-19 and VS-131 human-rights due diligence), sustainable packaging (recyclable/reduced material), carbon-footprint reduction (scope-3 emissions per VS-25); sets improvement targets with dates | ESG Manager / Procurement Manager | VP Supply Chain | 2–3 days/year per vendor |
| 2 | Tracks sustainability progress quarterly via evidence (certificates, audit reports, emissions data); feeds the sustainability dimension of the W2458 scorecard and the W2619 corporate ESG rollup | ESG Manager | VP Supply Chain | Absorbed into VS-25 reporting |

### System Touchpoints
- ESG/sustainability module (links to VS-25 / W114, W2619 corporate ESG rollup) — vendor sustainability data feeds BuildRight's scope-3 reporting
- Supplier portal (links to VS-28 / W593) — vendor-submitted sustainability evidence (certificates, audit reports)
- Responsible-sourcing / human-rights (links to VS-131) — labor-standards dimension draws from VS-131 due-diligence findings

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Greenwashing risk**: vendors submit aspirational sustainability claims without evidence, inflating the sustainability scorecard dimension; mitigated by the evidence requirement (certificates, third-party audit reports) in step 2
- **Capacity-gap risk**: small vendors lack the resources to invest in sustainability improvements and are penalized on the dimension through no fault; mitigated by a maturity-model scoring (credits improvement trajectory, not just absolute level) and BuildRight co-investment for strategic small vendors

### Automation Opportunity
- Sustainability-evidence collection via the supplier portal (structured upload with expiry tracking, linking to CTL-51 stale-data checks on certificates)

### Controls
- CTL-227: sustainability-equipment evidence & data integrity; operational: ESG Manager attestation on the sustainability-dimension data feeding W2458

### Time Estimate
~2.5 days/vendor × ~12 vendors/year + quarterly tracking absorbed into VS-25 = **~240–300 hours/year** ESG Manager + Procurement Manager.

## W2475. Vendor Financial Health Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly financial review of strategic vendors |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly; top 50 strategic vendors |
| **Volume** | ~50 vendors/quarter |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | Finance Dir, Procurement Mgr, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance monitors vendor financial health for the top 50 strategic vendors: third-party credit reports, payment-behavior trends (BuildRight's own AP aging for the vendor), public news alerts for financial distress (bankruptcy filings, layoffs, ownership changes); identifies at-risk vendors for supply-continuity planning | Finance Controller | CFO | 4–6 hours/quarter |
| 2 | For at-risk vendors: recommends risk-mitigation actions — dual-sourcing for critical categories (links to VS-02 supply planning), reduced inventory exposure (consignment/VMI shift per VS-45), or accelerated W2466 delisting if the vendor is both underperforming and financially distressed | Finance Controller / Procurement Manager | CFO | 2–3 hours/at-risk vendor |

### System Touchpoints
- AP module (links to VS-15 / W7, W100 vendor statement reconciliation) — BuildRight's own payment data is a financial-health signal
- Credit-rating feed (external integration) — quarterly refresh of third-party credit scores for the top 50
- Supply-planning / risk (links to VS-02, VS-161 TPRM) — at-risk-vendor flag triggers dual-source review

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Late-signal risk**: a credit report updates quarterly but a vendor's distress can unfold in weeks; mitigated by the news-alert feed (real-time) and AP-behavior monitoring (BuildRight sees payment-pattern changes before a credit report does)
- **Over-reaction risk**: a single quarter's credit downgrade triggers premature delisting of an otherwise-recovering vendor; mitigated by the trend view (3-quarter moving average) rather than point-in-time credit score

### Automation Opportunity
- News-alert feed (vendor-name watchlist) pushed to Finance Controller and Procurement; credit-score delta auto-flagged when it crosses a threshold

### Controls
- CTL-42 (bank-account-change guard — a financially-distressed vendor may attempt to reroute payments, so the control is heightened during at-risk status); CTL-48 (rebate exposure — at-risk-vendor open rebates flagged for Finance review)

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/quarter monitoring + ~2.5 hours/at-risk vendor × ~5 at-risk/quarter = **~50–75 hours/year** Finance Controller + Procurement.

## W2476. Vendor Innovation Partnership Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual innovation challenge or ongoing partnership |
| **Frequency** | Annual; ~10–15 innovative vendors |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 vendors/year |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merch, Category Mgrs, Procurement Mgr, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Merchandising invites innovative vendors (from the W2458 innovation-dimension leaders and open submissions) to propose: new products (links to VS-01 assortment), packaging improvements (links to VS-111), display innovations, sustainability solutions (links to W2474); evaluates proposals against category strategy; pilots promising innovations in 5–10 select stores | VP Merchandising / Category Manager | CEO | 5–10 days/year |
| 2 | Scales successful pilots to chain-wide rollout; formalizes the innovation in the JBP (W2473) and the vendor's innovation-dimension score (W2458); recognizes contributors in the W2467 awards | VP Merchandising / Category Manager | VP Supply Chain | Per pilot |

### System Touchpoints
- Assortment / item master (links to VS-01 / W1, VS-29 / W252 item-master) — piloted innovations become test SKUs with a "pilot" lifecycle status
- Store-cluster / test-store master (links to VS-29 / W299 assortment & store-cluster) — pilot store selection
- Supplier-performance module (W2458) — innovation-dimension score reflects contribution

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Pilot-purgatory risk**: innovations pilot successfully but never scale (or fail to pilot at all) because no one owns the scale decision; mitigated by a defined pilot-to-scale gate with a named Category Manager decision owner and a date
- **IP-ambiguity risk**: who owns the innovation if a vendor proposed it and BuildRight scaled it (relevant for private-label per VS-41)?; mitigated by the IP-assignment clause in the innovation-partnership agreement reviewed by Legal (VS-100)

### Automation Opportunity
- Innovation-pipeline tracker (submitted → under evaluation → piloting → scaled) with auto-aging alerts so proposals don't sit unreviewed

### Controls
- CTL-37: warranty claim validity — relevant for piloted products entering the warranty pool; operational: Legal review of IP terms before scaling any vendor-proposed innovation

### Time Estimate
~7 days/year VP Merchandising evaluation + per-pilot scaling absorbed into Category Manager duties = **~60–80 hours/year** VP + Category Managers.

## W2477. Vendor Development ROI Measurement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual measurement of development program effectiveness |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | All vendor development activities |
| **Owner** | FP&A Analyst |
| **Participants** | FP&A Analyst, Procurement Mgr, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Measures the ROI of the VS-67.3 development portfolio: program cost (BuildRight labor + co-investment + training delivery) vs. vendor-performance improvement (quality-defect reduction, OTD improvement, cost savings from improved terms/JBPs, innovation-revenue lift); attributes improvement to specific development activities where traceable | FP&A Analyst | CFO | 3–5 days/year |
| 2 | Identifies the most impactful development activities (e.g., W2472 technology enablement may show the highest KPI lift per dollar); recommends next-year budget allocation across W2470–W2476 | FP&A Analyst / Procurement Manager | CFO / VP Supply Chain | 1–2 days/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Supplier-performance module (W2458) — the before/after scorecard delta is the benefit side of the ROI
- Finance / cost accounting (links to VS-17 / W85 product costing, VS-158 cost accounting) — development-program cost capture and the cost-savings measurement
- FP&A / analytics (links to VS-27 / W113, VS-17.4 FP&A) — the ROI model and the budget-allocation recommendation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Attribution risk**: a vendor's scorecard improvement may be due to a development activity, a management change at the vendor, or market conditions — and isolating the development effect is hard; mitigated by the structured before/after measurement tied to the specific activity (W2471 training → invoice-accuracy delta) rather than a general correlation
- **Short-horizon risk**: development ROI measured at 12 months misses benefits that materialize at 24–36 months (a sustainability investment, a capacity expansion); mitigated by a multi-year benefit-tracking view for capital-grade development (W2472, W2474)

### Automation Opportunity
- Development-activity → scorecard-delta correlation auto-computed from the W2458 history; ROI dashboard refreshed quarterly rather than assembled annually

### Controls
- CTL-39: rebate accuracy — cost-savings from JBPs validated against the rebate agreement; operational: CFO sign-off on the ROI methodology and budget-allocation recommendation

### Time Estimate
~4 days/year FP&A Analyst measurement + ~1.5 days recommendation = **~40–50 hours/year**, absorbed into the FP&A annual planning cycle.
