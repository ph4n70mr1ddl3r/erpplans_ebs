## W5217. Conditional Progress Waiver of Lien Issuance (upon Delivery)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Material goods delivery receipt verification (W5209); billing invoice release |
| **Frequency** | Continuous per invoice billing batch |
| **Volume** | ~1,500–2,000 conditional waivers generated monthly (the ~100–120/month are the unconditional settlement counterparts per W5218) |
| **Owner** | Project Billing Clerk / AR Specialist |
| **Participants** | Developer Attorney, General Contractor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retrieve matching project code, invoice number, and delivery receipt weights/details. | Project Billing Clerk / AR Specialist | Project Billing Clerk / AR Specialist | 2-3 min |
| 2 | Draft the Conditional Progress Waiver of Lien (specifying that BuildRight waives lien rights on delivered materials conditional upon actual payment clearance). | Project Billing Clerk / AR Specialist | Project Billing Clerk / AR Specialist | 2-3 min |
| 3 | File and print copy of the conditional waiver; attach to the Escrow Draw Request folder (W5211). | Project Billing Clerk / AR Specialist | Project Billing Clerk / AR Specialist | 2-3 min |
| 4 | Record waiver status as 'Conditional-Pending Payment' in ERP project registry. | Project Billing Clerk / AR Specialist | Project Billing Clerk / AR Specialist | 2-3 min |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP AR invoicing (VS-16), Document control database (VS-88).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unconditional release issued prematurely**: Releasing lien rights before payment clears, leaving company unsecured; mitigated by strict system validation blocking unconditional prints before payment application.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automatic system generation of conditional waivers linked to invoice output.

### Controls
- CTL-775 (ensure controlled execution — Waiver of Lien, Progress Billing Reconciliation & Account Hold Management (PA-181.3).)
- CTL-775: Document template legal-locked; waiver output verified by AR supervisor.

### Time Estimate
- ~10 mins per waiver drafting

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (O2C), VS-88 (document control)

## W5218. Unconditional Progress Waiver of Lien Issuance (upon Fund Settlement)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Escrow draw fund settlement matching (W5213) |
| **Frequency** | Continuous per payment clearance |
| **Volume** | ~100–120 waivers monthly |
| **Owner** | AR Supervisor / Credit Officer |
| **Participants** | Developer Attorney, Legal (VS-100) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify payment clearance: check cash application status in ERP showing invoices cleared (W5213). | AR Supervisor / Credit Officer | AR Supervisor / Credit Officer | 5 min |
| 2 | Draft Unconditional Progress Waiver of Lien referencing the cleared invoice IDs, payment references, and values. | AR Supervisor / Credit Officer | AR Supervisor / Credit Officer | 5 min |
| 3 | Secure signature from the B2B Credit Manager or Legal Counsel. | AR Supervisor / Credit Officer | AR Supervisor / Credit Officer | 5 min |
| 4 | Send original notarized Unconditional Waiver of Lien to the developer; update status as 'Settled-Unconditional' in ERP. | AR Supervisor / Credit Officer | AR Supervisor / Credit Officer | 5 min |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP AR cash ledger (VS-16), Legal document tracking (VS-100), notary registry.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Bounced payment clearing delay**: Bank wire reversed due to compliance holds after waiver release; mitigated by waiting 3 business days for payment confirmation before releasing unconditional notary.

### Automation Opportunity
- Automatic trigger generating draft unconditional waiver upon invoice balance matching zero.

### Controls
- CTL-775 (ensure controlled execution — Waiver of Lien, Progress Billing Reconciliation & Account Hold Management (PA-181.3).)
- CTL-775: Notarized waiver copies kept in legal archives; sign-off authorized only upon cleared cash validation.

### Time Estimate
- ~20 mins per waiver

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (O2C), VS-100 (legal)

## W5219. Project Material Return, Credit Note & Reconciliation with Escrow draws

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Developer returning unused/surplus materials post-project phase (VS-32) |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per return request |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 returns managed monthly across projects |
| **Owner** | Store Returns Clerk / B2B AR Specialist |
| **Participants** | Site Engineer, Store Manager, DC receiver (VS-04) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inspect returned materials: check for damage, grade changes, and verify matches against original delivery receipts. | Store Returns Clerk / B2B AR Specialist | Store Returns Clerk / B2B AR Specialist | 10-15 min |
| 2 | Generate Credit Note in ERP: calculate refund value at contracted prices (W5202). | Store Returns Clerk / B2B AR Specialist | Store Returns Clerk / B2B AR Specialist | 10-15 min |
| 3 | Match credit note to project escrow draws: deduct credit amount from the next scheduled escrow draw request or apply as offset to outstanding AR balance. | Store Returns Clerk / B2B AR Specialist | Store Returns Clerk / B2B AR Specialist | 10-15 min |
| 4 | Report adjustments to bank escrow agent to update trust ledger balance. | Store Returns Clerk / B2B AR Specialist | Store Returns Clerk / B2B AR Specialist | 10-15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Returns module (VS-32), POS/Receiving ledger (VS-08), B2B AR ledger.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Double counting / Rejection fraud**: Billed returns not updated at bank escrow, causing audit mismatches; mitigated by mandatory bank notifications for all project credit notes.

### Automation Opportunity
- System-generated credit matching notifications to bank agent email.

### Controls
- CTL-775 (ensure controlled execution — Waiver of Lien, Progress Billing Reconciliation & Account Hold Management (PA-181.3).)
- CTL-775: Credit notes above PHP 100K require Controller approval; physical goods receipt verified before posting.

### Time Estimate
- ~30-60 mins

### Cross-references
- links to VS-32 (returns), VS-08 (POS), VS-04 (DC ops)

## W5220. Project Escrow Delay Credit-Hold Hold-and-Release Execution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Bank draw delay exceeding credit terms (VS-16); developer account delinquent |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per delinquency |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 holds executed monthly |
| **Owner** | B2B Credit Manager / B2B VP |
| **Participants** | Category Buyer, Site Engineer, Store Managers, WMS dispatch (VS-04) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify delinquent project accounts: check ERP aging reports for payments delayed past escrow terms (e.g. over 60 days). | B2B Credit Manager / B2B VP | B2B Credit Manager / B2B VP | 5-10 min |
| 2 | Issue formal payment warning: send credit hold notice to the developer and general contractor. | B2B Credit Manager / B2B VP | B2B Credit Manager / B2B VP | 5-10 min |
| 3 | Execute ERP Credit Hold: lock the project code in ERP to prevent shipping orders or releasing reserved stocks. | B2B Credit Manager / B2B VP | B2B Credit Manager / B2B VP | 5-10 min |
| 4 | Negotiate payment release or release hold manually in ERP upon receiving bank verification of pending transfer. | B2B Credit Manager / B2B VP | B2B Credit Manager / B2B VP | 5-10 min |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Credit hold screen (VS-16), transport dispatch scheduler (VS-06), CRM notification.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Construction site blockade**: Stranding project mid-phase causing subcontractor claims; mitigated by providing 5-day warning period to developers before executing active holds.

### Automation Opportunity
- ERP automatic credit hold block upon term expiry.

### Controls
- CTL-775 (ensure controlled execution — Waiver of Lien, Progress Billing Reconciliation & Account Hold Management (PA-181.3).)
- CTL-775: Hold overrides require CFO or B2B VP approval; audit logs of credit overrides reviewed monthly.

### Time Estimate
- ~30 mins per hold execution

### Cross-references
- links to VS-16 (O2C), VS-06 (logistics), VS-04 (DC ops)

## W5224. Project Account Financial Close, Final Waiver of Lien & Release of Escrow

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Practical completion certificate issued; construction project final phase complete |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven per project close |
| **Volume** | ~65–100 project closes annually (the B2B register canon) |
| **Owner** | B2B Accounts Receivable Supervisor / Legal Operations (VS-100) |
| **Participants** | Developer Finance Director, Bank Escrow Agent, CFO, B2B Controller |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Perform final reconciliation: match all billing invoices, JQS worksheets, credit notes, and escrow draws; resolve outstanding balance to zero. | B2B Accounts Receivable Supervisor / Legal Operations (VS-100) | B2B Accounts Receivable Supervisor / Legal Operations (VS-100) | 1-2 hours |
| 2 | Prepare the Final Waiver of Lien and Release of Escrow letter. | B2B Accounts Receivable Supervisor / Legal Operations (VS-100) | B2B Accounts Receivable Supervisor / Legal Operations (VS-100) | 1-2 hours |
| 3 | Execute final waiver: secure CEO/CFO signatures, notarize the document, and deliver to bank escrow agent to release remaining escrow balance and retainage. | B2B Accounts Receivable Supervisor / Legal Operations (VS-100) | B2B Accounts Receivable Supervisor / Legal Operations (VS-100) | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Deactivate the project code and billing pricing lists in ERP. | B2B Accounts Receivable Supervisor / Legal Operations (VS-100) | B2B Accounts Receivable Supervisor / Legal Operations (VS-100) | 1-2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP GL account close (VS-17.1), Legal records database (VS-100), B2B CRM.

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unreleased escrow**: Bank holding final release due to developer dispute with other vendors; mitigated by negotiating direct bank release to BuildRight for verified material invoices.

### Automation Opportunity
- None.

### Controls
- CTL-775 (ensure controlled execution — Waiver of Lien, Progress Billing Reconciliation & Account Hold Management (PA-181.3).)
- CTL-775: Project account audited and closed by Corporate Controller; signed final releases filed in corporate archives (VS-88).

### Time Estimate
- ~5-10 working days

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.1 (GL close), VS-100 (legal), VS-88 (document control)
