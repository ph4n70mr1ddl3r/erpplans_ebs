## W4065. Benefit Case Modeling, Tracking & Realization Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project charter; benefit realization cycle |
| **Frequency** | Per project + monthly/quarterly realization review |
| **Volume** | Enterprise benefit portfolio (margin, cost, working-capital, NPS) |
| **Owner** | OpEx Finance Partner / COO |
| **Participants** | Finance (links to VS-17.4), process owners, sponsors, Audit (links to VS-21) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OpEx/Finance build the benefit case at charter (hard/soft, P&L vs balance-sheet, baseline, annualization, confidence), with Finance sign-off on the model | OpEx Finance Partner / Finance | COO / CFO | Per project |
| 2 | OpEx/Finance track realization monthly/quarterly against the case, distinguish run-rate vs one-time, and reconcile to the P&L (links to VS-17.4) | OpEx Finance Partner / Finance | COO | Periodic |
| 3 | OpEx report realized benefit to the executive/Board (links to VS-33/VS-36), and flag shortfalls for action | OpEx / Finance | COO / CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Benefit-tracking tool; GL/FP&A (links to VS-17.4); corporate performance (links to VS-33)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Overstate risk**: Soft benefits never realized; mitigated by Finance sign-off and P&L reconciliation
- **Attribution risk**: Benefit claimed that would have happened anyway; mitigated by baseline discipline and counter-factual reasoning

### Automation Opportunity
- Benefit auto-reconciliation to GL; run-rate vs one-time classification

### Controls
- CTL-162 (ensure improvement benefit realization.)
- CTL-631: benefit case approved by Finance (segregation from OpEx sponsor); realized benefit reconciled to P&L

### Time Estimate
- Per project + monthly tracking

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-33 (performance), VS-21 (audit), VS-135 (TBM value realization)

## W4066. Process Performance Monitoring & Control Tower Dashboard

| Field | Detail |

| **Frequency** | Continuous (real-time/daily) + weekly review |
| **Volume** | Enterprise process performance (~5,433 workflows across 188 value streams) |
| **Owner** | OpEx Data Lead / BI (links to VS-28) |
| **Participants** | BI (VS-28), process owners, Ops leadership |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OpEx/BI build the process-performance control tower: KPIs (W4054), process-mining health (W4058), exception/alerts, and trend vs target, by process and site | OpEx Data Lead / BI | COO | Continuous |
| 2 | OpEx/Ops leadership review exceptions weekly, trigger improvement actions or investigations, and route to the pipeline (W4052) | OpEx / Process Owners | COO | Weekly |
| 3 | OpEx tune alert thresholds, reduce false positives, and evolve the dashboard with the capability | OpEx / BI | COO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- BI platform (links to VS-28); process mining (W4058); KPI library (W4054); ERP event streams (links to VS-08/VS-15)

- Real-time exception detection; automated root-cause hypothesis on alert; action auto-routing

### Controls
- CTL-162 (ensure improvement benefit realization.)
- CTL-631: KPI definitions governed (W4054); alert changes approved

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; build ~6–12 weeks then maintained

### Cross-references
- links to VS-28 (BI), W4054 (KPIs), W4058 (mining)

| **Participants** | Process owners, Supply Chain (VS-02/VS-06/VS-04), Finance (VS-15/VS-16), ecommerce (VS-10) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

- CTL-631 (ensure controlled execution — Productivity, Benefit Realization & OpEx Analytics (PA-133.3).)

### Time Estimate
- Per flow + continuous monitoring

### Cross-references

| **Volume** | Enterprise improvement portfolio |
| **Owner** | COO |

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
