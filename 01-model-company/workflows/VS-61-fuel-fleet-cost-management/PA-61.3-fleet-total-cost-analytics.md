## W2326. Annual Fleet TCO Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual budget cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | 20 owned vehicles |
| **Owner** | Finance Controller |
| **Participants** | VP Supply Chain, CFO, DC Cost-to-Serve Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calculates TCO per vehicle: depreciation + fuel + maintenance + insurance + tolls + driver + overhead; compares vs. 3PL cost; VP recommends own vs. outsource by route | Finance Controller / VP Supply Chain | CFO | 5–10 days + 3–5 days |

### System Touchpoints
- TCO model (links to VS-158 / W85, VS-61 / W2325 dashboard, VS-35 / W39 depreciation) — the per-vehicle TCO
- 3PL benchmark (links to VS-56 / W242, VS-61 / W2331) — the own-vs-outsource comparison
- Budget (links to VS-17 / W26, VS-40 / W21 capex) — the TCO feeds the fleet budget

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Overhead-allocation-arbitrariness risk**: the overhead allocation to each vehicle is arbitrary, distorting the per-vehicle TCO; mitigated by the activity-based allocation (driver time, telematics-confirmed usage)
- **Own-vs-outsource-confound risk**: the own cost looks higher because it includes fixed overhead the 3PL rate hides; mitigated by the fully-loaded comparison (own fully-loaded vs. 3PL fully-loaded including BuildRight's management overhead)

### Automation Opportunity
- TCO auto-assembled per vehicle; own-vs-3PL auto-compared (fully-loaded)

### Controls
- CTL-414 (ensure controlled execution — Fleet Total Cost of Ownership Analytics (PA-61.3).)
- CTL-414 (TCO-accuracy — links to VS-158); operational: CFO sign-off on the TCO and the own-vs-outsource recommendation

### Time Estimate
~7.5 days/year Finance Controller + VP Supply Chain = **~60–80 hours/year**.

- Asset/fleet (links to VS-35 / W39, VS-04 / W52 lifecycle) — the replacement-criteria tracking

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Replacement-too-late risk**: a vehicle is kept past the economic replacement point, accumulating maintenance exceeding the depreciation savings; mitigated by the auto-flag at the threshold
- **Replacement-too-early risk**: a vehicle is replaced for a repairable issue, wasting residual value; mitigated by the replace-vs-maintain math (not a blanket age/km rule)

### Automation Opportunity
- Replacement-criteria auto-flagged; replace-vs-maintain auto-computed

### Controls
- CTL-414 (ensure controlled execution — Fleet Total Cost of Ownership Analytics (PA-61.3).)

### Time Estimate

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calculates: % available hours delivering, % capacity utilized per trip, idle time; targets ≥75% utilization; identifies underutilized for redeployment or disposal | Fleet Supervisor | Logistics Manager | 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Telematics (links to VS-06 / W199 fleet telematics) — the utilization data (hours, capacity, idle)
- Fleet/asset (links to VS-04 / W52, VS-35 / W39) — the redeployment/disposal decision
- Delivery scheduling (links to VS-06 / W196) — the utilization driver

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Low-utilization-keeping risk**: an underutilized vehicle is kept (sunk-cost fallacy) instead of redeployed/disposed; mitigated by the utilization-target and the redeployment/disposal decision at <threshold
- **Utilization-vs-maintenance-trade-off risk**: pushing utilization too high defers PM, causing failures; mitigated by the utilization ceiling that preserves PM time

### Automation Opportunity
- Utilization auto-computed from telematics; underutilized auto-flagged

### Controls
- CTL-414 (ensure controlled execution — Fleet Total Cost of Ownership Analytics (PA-61.3).)
- Operational: Logistics Manager utilization-review sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month = **~60 hours/year** Fleet Supervisor.

| **Frequency** | Annual; 5-year horizon |
| **Volume** | ~40 owned vehicles (the owned-fleet canon) + 3PL surge capacity |
| **Owner** | VP Supply Chain / CFO |
| **Participants** | CEO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Develops: replacement schedule, expansion for store growth (10–15/year), technology (GPS, telematics, EV charging); CEO/Board approve; feeds VS-40 Capex budget | VP Supply Chain / CFO | CEO | 5–10 days + 2–3 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Capex plan (links to VS-40 / W21 capex, VS-112 PMO) — the 5-year investment plan
- Fleet/asset (links to VS-04 / W52, VS-35 / W39, VS-61 / W2326 TCO) — the replacement/expansion inputs
- Tech/telematics (links to VS-27 / W366, VS-61 / W2315 alternative-fuel) — the technology investments

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Plan-vs-actual-divergence risk**: the 5-year plan is overtaken by store-growth changes or fuel-price shifts; mitigated by the annual refresh (not a one-time 5-year lock)
- **Over-investment risk**: the plan over-provisions fleet for a growth that doesn't materialize, leaving idle capacity; mitigated by the elastic-capacity preference (3PL surge) over permanent fleet expansion where feasible

### Automation Opportunity
- Plan auto-assembled from the replacement/expansion/tech datasets; scenario (growth low/mid/high) auto-modeled

### Controls
- CTL-414 (ensure controlled execution — Fleet Total Cost of Ownership Analytics (PA-61.3).)
- operational: capex-approval — links to VS-40; operational: CEO/Board approval of the 5-year plan

### Time Estimate
~7 days/year = **~50–60 hours/year** VP Supply Chain + CFO.
