## W2310. Fleet Fuel Card Program Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Fuel procurement for owned fleet |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~20 trucks |
| **Volume** | ~20 cards; ~PHP 2–3M/month |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manages fuel card program: issues per vehicle, limits per transaction, monitors patterns, blocks unauthorized; Finance reviews statements, matches mileage, investigates anomalies | Logistics Manager / Finance | VP Supply Chain | 2–3 hours/month + 4–6 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Fuel-card platform (links to VS-18 / W320 banking security, VS-42 / W118 rent-payment pattern applied fleet-side) — the card program
- Telematics/mileage (links to VS-06 / W199 fleet telematics) — the mileage-vs-fuel cross-check
- Finance/AP (links to VS-15 / W7C recurring invoice) — the statement reconciliation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fuel-card-fraud risk**: a driver uses the card for a personal vehicle or siphons fuel; mitigated by the per-transaction limit, the mileage-vs-fuel anomaly detection, and the card-block on unauthorized
- **Lost-card risk**: a card is lost/stolen and used before block; mitigated by the 24-hour block-SLA and the per-transaction limit capping the exposure

### Automation Opportunity
- Mileage-vs-fuel auto-reconciled; anomaly auto-flagged; block auto-on-reported-lost

### Controls
- CTL-412 (ensure controlled execution — Fuel Procurement & Consumption Management (PA-61.1).)
- operational: fuel-card-change guard — pattern from VS-42; duplicate-transaction guard; operational: Finance monthly reconciliation sign-off

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month Logistics Manager + ~5 hours/month Finance = **~120 hours/year**.

## W2311. Fuel Price Monitoring & Procurement Optimization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly review cycle — Fuel Price Monitoring |
| **Frequency** | Weekly; Philippine diesel/gas prices |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 2–3M monthly |
| **Owner** | Logistics Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Procurement, Finance Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monitors DOE weekly prices; identifies optimal stations per route; calculates delivery cost impact; Procurement negotiates fleet discounts with Petron, Shell, Caltex for volume | Logistics Finance Analyst / Procurement | Finance Manager | 2–3 hours/week + 1–2 days/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- DOE price feed (links to VS-27 / W113 external data) — the weekly fuel-price benchmark
- Route/station optimization (links to VS-06 / W196 route planning) — the per-route optimal station
- Procurement (links to VS-03 / W62 vendor contract) — the fleet-discount negotiation

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Station-detour-cost risk**: a driver detours to a cheaper station, burning more fuel than the saving; mitigated by the per-route optimal-station (not a blanket "cheapest") that accounts for detour distance
- **Discount-non-realization risk**: a negotiated volume discount isn't applied at the pump (station-level system gap); mitigated by the statement-reconciliation catching the undiscounted transactions

### Automation Opportunity
- DOE price auto-ingested; per-route optimal-station auto-suggested; discount-application auto-verified

### Controls
- CTL-181 (ensure fleet fuel-cost accuracy & fraud prevention.)
- CTL-412 (discount-realization accuracy); operational: Finance Manager quarterly sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2.5 hours/week + ~1.5 days/quarter = **~180 hours/year** Logistics Finance Analyst + Procurement.

## W2312. Vehicle Fuel Efficiency Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly vehicle review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly; ~40 owned vehicles (the 40-truck owned fleet) |
| **Volume** | ~40 reports/month (one per owned vehicle) |
| **Owner** | Fleet Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Logistics Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calculates: liters ÷ km; benchmarks vs. spec; flags >15% deviation; investigates: engine, tires, congestion, driver behavior; schedules maintenance or coaching | Fleet Supervisor | Logistics Manager | 4–6 hours/month + 2–3 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Telematics (links to VS-06 / W199 fleet telematics) — the liters/km data per vehicle
- Maintenance (links to VS-06 / W188 fleet spare parts & PM) — the maintenance scheduled on deviation
- Driver scorecard (links to VS-06 / W197 driver performance) — the driver-behavior dimension

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Efficiency-vs-driver-blame risk**: an efficiency drop is attributed to the driver when the root cause is mechanical (a failing injector); mitigated by the diagnostic-first investigation before coaching
- **Spec-benchmark-staleness risk**: the vehicle-spec benchmark is for new-condition and an aging fleet looks chronically inefficient; mitigated by the age-adjusted benchmark

### Automation Opportunity
- Liters/km auto-computed from telematics; deviation auto-flagged with diagnostic-suggestion

### Controls
- CTL-181 (ensure fleet fuel-cost accuracy & fraud prevention.)
- Operational: Fleet Supervisor monthly sign-off; Logistics Manager escalation on chronic deviation

### Time Estimate
~5 hours/month + ~2.5 hours investigation = **~90 hours/year** Fleet Supervisor.

- Cost analytics (links to VS-27 / W113, VS-158 / W85, VS-61 / W2325 TCO dashboard) — the fuel-cost dataset
- Budget (links to VS-17 / W26) — the budget-variance

### Automation Opportunity
- Fuel-report auto-assembled; price-vs-volume variance auto-split

### Controls
- CTL-181 (ensure fleet fuel-cost accuracy & fraud prevention.)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; ~2–4/year |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 partners |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Activates adjustment clause in contracts; adjusts per-delivery rates; Finance validates; communicates to partners; implements in ERP | Logistics Manager / Finance | CFO | 1–2 days + 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- 3PL contract (links to VS-56 / W62B 3PL onboarding, VS-03 / W62 contract) — the fuel-adjustment clause
- AP/rate (links to VS-15 / W7, VS-56 / W242 3PL performance) — the rate adjustment
- DOE price (links to VS-61 / W2311) — the >5%-change trigger

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Clause-ambiguity-dispute risk**: the adjustment clause is ambiguous (which price baseline, what lag), triggering a 3PL dispute; mitigated by the clear clause terms (DOE weekly price, 1-month lag, ±5% band)
- **Rate-implementation-lag risk**: the rate adjustment is agreed but not implemented in the ERP, under/over-paying the 3PL; mitigated by the Finance validation and the ERP-implementation step

### Automation Opportunity
- DOE-price-change auto-monitored; clause-trigger auto-detected; rate-adjustment auto-implemented on Finance validation

### Controls
- CTL-412 (contract-accuracy — links to VS-03); CTL-44 (rate-adjustment integrity); operational: CFO sign-off on the adjustment

### Time Estimate
~2 days/event × ~3/year = **~50 hours/year** Logistics Manager + Finance.

## W2317. Emergency Fuel Supply Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Fuel disruption (typhoon) |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; ~1–2/year |
| **Volume** | 1–2 events/year |
| **Owner** | Logistics Manager |
| **Participants** | VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tops off tanks at warning; activates backup suppliers; prioritizes essential deliveries; post-event: adjusts schedule to clear backlog; reviews emergency plan | Logistics Manager | VP Supply Chain | 4–8 hours + 1–2 days |

### System Touchpoints
- BCP (links to VS-26 / W49 disaster response, VS-69 / typhoon) — the emergency-fuel protocol
- Supplier master (links to VS-03 / W36, VS-61 / W2311) — the backup suppliers
- Delivery scheduling (links to VS-06 / W196 route planning) — the essential-delivery prioritization

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fuel-stockout risk**: a typhoon disrupts fuel supply and the fleet can't run, halting deliveries; mitigated by the top-off-at-warning and the backup-supplier network
- **Post-event-backlog risk**: the disruption creates a delivery backlog that overwhelms recovery capacity; mitigated by the post-event schedule-adjustment and the essential-delivery prioritization

### Automation Opportunity
- Typhoon-warning auto-triggers the top-off; backup-supplier auto-activated

### Controls
- CTL-412 (ensure controlled execution — Fuel Procurement & Consumption Management (PA-61.1).)
- CTL-412 (BCP/emergency-fuel — links to VS-26); operational: VP Supply Chain post-event review

### Time Estimate
~6 hours/event + ~1.5 days recovery × ~1.5/year = **~40 hours/year** Logistics Manager (concentrated during events).
