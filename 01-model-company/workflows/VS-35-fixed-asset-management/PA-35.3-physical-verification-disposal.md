## W1706. Annual Fixed Asset Physical Verification (Wall-to-Wall Count)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual internal audit plan or external audit requirement; PFRS control expectation |
| **Frequency** | Annual (typically Q4 or Q1, coordinated with external audit) |
| **Volume** | ~15,000–20,000 fixed assets across 200 stores, 4 DCs, and HQ |
| **Owner** | Internal Audit (coordinates); Store Manager / DC Supervisor (executes) |
| **Participants** | Internal Audit, Store Manager, DC Supervisor, Fixed Asset Accountant, Department Heads |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Internal Audit issues physical verification instructions: (a) each location receives a list of registered assets at their location (sorted by category and location within facility), (b) instructions: scan/verify each asset tag, record condition (good, fair, poor, missing), (c) target completion: 2 weeks for stores, 3 weeks for DCs, 1 week for HQ, (d) mobile app or handheld scanner provided for scan-and-confirm process | Internal Audit | Head of Internal Audit | 1 week prep |
| 2 | Location teams execute physical count: (a) scan each asset tag with mobile device — system confirms match to register, (b) visually inspect condition: Good (functioning, no damage), Fair (operational but needs maintenance), Poor (needs replacement), Missing (not found), (c) for assets without readable tags: record description and location for reconciliation, (d) for assets found but not in register: record as "unregistered asset found", (e) the location's custodian of record signs the count certification; Finance and Internal Audit recount a risk-based sample rather than the full estate | Store Manager / DC Supervisor / Department Head | Internal Audit | 1–2 days/location (stores); 3–5 days/DC |
| 3 | System generates reconciliation report per location: (a) matched assets (scanned and confirmed), (b) missing assets (registered but not found), (c) unregistered assets (found but not in register), (d) condition exceptions (assets in "poor" condition), (e) tag exceptions (unreadable or missing tags) | System | Internal Audit | Automated |
| 4 | Location Manager investigates discrepancies: (a) missing assets — search facility, check transfer records, confirm disposal; if truly missing: escalate to Loss Prevention and initiate write-off per W1709, (b) unregistered assets — identify source (donation, transfer not recorded, expensed item that should have been capitalized); Fixed Asset Accountant registers per W1690 if appropriate, (c) tag exceptions — replacement tags ordered per W1692 | Location Manager | Internal Audit | 1–2 weeks |
| 5 | Internal Audit compiles company-wide verification summary: (a) verification rate (% of assets physically confirmed), target ≥ 98%, (b) exception rate by category and location, (c) missing asset value and count, (d) unregistered asset value and count, (e) condition summary, (f) recommendations for register cleanup and process improvement | Internal Audit | CFO / Audit Committee | 1 week |
| 6 | Fixed Asset Accountant processes all adjustments resulting from verification: new registrations, disposal write-offs, location corrections, condition updates, tag replacements | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Asset register extract by location for verification list (W1706.1)
- Mobile scanning app with real-time match/no-match feedback (W1706.2)
- Auto-reconciliation report with exception categories (W1706.3)
- Company-wide verification summary dashboard (W1706.5)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scale challenge**: 200 stores × ~50–100 assets each = 10,000–20,000 items to verify; requiring Store Manager participation ensures accountability but requires significant coordination
- **Unscannable assets**: Assets embedded in walls (electrical, plumbing) or elevated locations (HVAC on roof) cannot be easily scanned; condition assessment by maintenance team is alternative
- **Found assets**: Assets found but not in register often indicate process failures at acquisition; root cause analysis needed


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-scan of "scan/verify each asset tag, record condition" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "record description and location for reconciliation" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-generate of "generates reconciliation report per location" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-219 (ensure fixed-asset register accuracy.)
- operational: periodic reconciliation against source documents

### Time Estimate
~1–2 days per store (Store Manager + 1 helper); ~3–5 days per DC; ~1 week for HQ. Total company-wide effort: ~400–600 person-days. Staggered schedule recommended (20–30 locations per week over 7–10 weeks).

## W1707. Spot Check & Rolling Asset Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Scheduled quarterly spot checks; triggered by specific concerns (high-value asset, anomalous location, audit finding) |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly spot checks covering ~10–15% of assets; rolling cycle ensures 100% coverage over ~2 years |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–3,000 assets per quarter |
| **Owner** | Internal Audit |
| **Participants** | Internal Audit, Store Manager, DC Supervisor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Internal Audit selects sample for spot check: (a) random sample of 5–10 stores per quarter, (b) targeted selection based on: high-value assets, locations with prior discrepancies, recently transferred assets, assets approaching end of useful life, (c) DC equipment (forklifts, racking) checked every quarter due to heavy usage | Internal Audit | Head of Internal Audit | 2 hours/quarter |
| 2 | Internal Auditor visits selected locations (or delegates to Regional Manager with photo evidence): (a) scans/verifies sampled assets, (b) assesses condition vs. register, (c) checks maintenance records for compliance with schedule per W1695, (d) interviews location staff on asset movements | Internal Auditor | Head of Internal Audit | 1–2 days/location |
| 3 | Discrepancies investigated and resolved same as W1706.4–5; patterns across locations identified (e.g., systematic failure to register new assets → process improvement needed) | Internal Audit | CFO | 1 week |
| 4 | Quarterly spot check report submitted to CFO and Audit Committee; tracking of prior findings and corrective actions | Internal Audit | Head of Internal Audit | 2 hours |


### System Touchpoints
- Fixed-asset register mobile-scan app — quarterly sample selection (random 5–10 stores plus targeted high-value / prior-discrepancy / recently-transferred assets) and tag-scan verification with photo evidence (steps 1–2)
- Register-vs-physical discrepancy report — exceptions feeding the W1706.4–5 investigation path and cross-location pattern analysis (step 3)
- Maintenance-schedule extract (links to W1695) — maintenance-record compliance check during the site visit (step 2)
- Audit finding tracker — quarterly spot-check report and prior-finding corrective-action tracking for CFO and Audit Committee (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Coverage-blindness risk**: the rolling ~2-year cycle lets a missing or untagged asset sit undetected between samples; mitigated by targeted selection layered on the random sample and audit-trigger spot checks (step 1)
- **Delegation-quality risk**: Regional-Manager-executed counts without auditor presence weaken the evidence; mitigated by mandatory photo evidence with delegated counts and Internal Audit re-performance on a sample (step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "Internal Audit selects sample for spot check" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-scan of "scans/verifies sampled assets" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-flag for investigation of "investigated and resolved same as W1706.4–5" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-219 (ensure fixed-asset register accuracy.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
Quarterly spot-check cycle ~70–135 hours (sample selection 2 hours + location visits 1–2 days × 5–10 sampled stores + discrepancy resolution ~1 week aggregate + report 2 hours) ≈ 280–540 hours/year at ~2,000–3,000 assets sampled per quarter.

## W1708. Asset Retirement & Disposal Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Asset fully depreciated and no longer usable, asset replacement, store closure, or asset upgrade |
| **Frequency** | ~100–200 retirements/month |
| **Volume** | ~1,200–2,400 retirements/year |
| **Owner** | Fixed Asset Accountant |
| **Participants** | Fixed Asset Accountant, Location Manager, Maintenance, Loss Prevention (if applicable) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Location Manager initiates asset retirement request: (a) selects asset from register, (b) specifies reason: (i) end of useful life — fully depreciated, (ii) damaged beyond repair, (iii) technologically obsolete, (iv) store closure/consolidation, (v) replacement by new asset, (c) attaches photos (if damaged), (d) recommends disposal method: (i) scrap/salvage, (ii) trade-in for new asset, (iii) sell to third party, (iv) donate, (v) return to vendor under warranty | Location Manager | Fixed Asset Accountant | 10 min/request |
| 2 | Fixed Asset Accountant reviews retirement request: (a) verifies asset identification (correct asset number, description, location), (b) reviews remaining net book value — if > PHP 0, disposal will result in gain or loss per W1711, (c) for high-value assets (≥ PHP 200K original cost): requires Accounting Manager approval, (d) checks insurance coverage — if asset is insured and damaged, initiates insurance claim per W1697, (e) confirms disposal method is appropriate | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 10 min/request |
| 3 | Approved disposal executed: (a) scrap/salvage: asset physically dismantled and removed; salvage value recorded, (b) trade-in: old asset surrendered to vendor as partial payment for new asset; trade-in value credited, (c) sale: asset listed and sold; proceeds recorded, (d) donation: asset transferred to recipient with donation documentation; no proceeds (potential tax deduction documentation) | Location Manager / Maintenance | Fixed Asset Accountant | Variable |
| 4 | Fixed Asset Accountant processes retirement in system: (a) asset status changed to "Retired," (b) gross asset value removed from register, (c) accumulated depreciation removed, (d) net book value (if any) recognized as loss on disposal (or gain if proceeds > NBV), (e) journal entry posted to GL, (f) asset removed from depreciation schedule going forward | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 5 min/asset |
| 5 | Insurance schedule updated per W1697; asset tag collected/destroyed; physical location cleared | Fixed Asset Accountant / Location Manager | — | 5 min |


### System Touchpoints
- Asset retirement request workflow — reason codes (end-of-life, damaged, obsolete, closure, upgrade) with NBV preview and gain/loss flag before approval (steps 1–2)
- Disposal execution routing — scrap/salvage weighing, trade-in credit memo, or sale invoice posted against the retired asset (step 3)
- Retirement posting engine — status change, gross-cost/accumulated-depreciation removal, NBV handoff to W1711 gain/loss recognition (step 4)
- Insurance schedule interface (links to W1697) and tag collection/destruction on completion (step 5)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Ghost-asset risk**: an asset physically gone but left on the register inflates NBV and insurance premiums; mitigated by location-initiated retirement requests and register status change gated on disposal evidence (steps 1, 4)
- **Value-recovery leakage risk**: salvage, trade-in, or sale proceeds unrecorded (or pocketed) at the location; mitigated by Fixed Asset Accountant processing all disposal documentation and Treasury posting cash proceeds (steps 3–5)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "initiates asset retirement request" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews retirement request" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "Approved disposal executed" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-348 (ensure controlled execution — Physical Verification & Disposal (PA-35.3).)
- operational: Fixed Asset Accountant review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
~30 min per retirement request (initiation 10 min + Fixed Asset review 10 min + system retirement 5 min/asset + tag/insurance closeout 5 min; physical disposal varies) × ~100–200 retirements/month ≈ 600–1,200 hours/year.

## W1709. Asset Write-Off Due to Loss, Theft or Damage

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Asset confirmed missing (physical verification per W1706/W1707), stolen (reported incident), or damaged beyond repair (natural disaster, accident) |
| **Frequency** | ~20–50 write-offs/year |
| **Volume** | Low volume but potentially high value per incident |
| **Owner** | Fixed Asset Accountant |
| **Participants** | Fixed Asset Accountant, Loss Prevention, Location Manager, CFO, Insurance & Risk Officer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Location Manager or Loss Prevention reports asset loss/theft/damage incident: (a) incident report with date, location, circumstances, (b) photos of damage (if applicable), (c) police report (for theft), (d) initial assessment of whether asset is recoverable or must be written off | Location Manager / Loss Prevention | Fixed Asset Accountant | 1–2 hours/incident |
| 2 | Loss Prevention investigates theft cases: (a) CCTV review, (b) employee investigation if internal theft suspected, (c) coordination with police; report forwarded to Fixed Asset Accountant with investigation conclusion | Loss Prevention | Head of Internal Audit | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | Fixed Asset Accountant prepares write-off recommendation: (a) asset details: description, original cost, net book value, (b) reason for write-off, (c) insurance claim status (if applicable), (d) expected recovery (insurance proceeds, police recovery), (e) net loss to be recognized | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 30 min/asset |
| 4 | Approval per tiered authority: (a) net book value < PHP 50K: Accounting Manager approval, (b) PHP 50K–500K: CFO approval, (c) > PHP 500K: CEO approval, (d) > PHP 1M: Board notification | Accounting Manager / CFO / CEO | Per tier | 1 day |
| 5 | Write-off processed in system: (a) asset derecognized from register, (b) net book value written off as loss on disposal, (c) if insurance claim pending: record receivable for expected recovery, (d) when insurance settles: cash received, receivable cleared, any difference adjusted | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 15 min/asset |

### System Touchpoints
- Incident-to-register workflow — incident report, police report for theft, and insurance-claim status attached to the write-off recommendation (steps 1–3)
- Tiered write-off approval matrix — NBV thresholds enforced in-workflow (Accounting Manager < PHP 50K; CFO PHP 50K–500K; CEO > PHP 500K; Board notification > PHP 1M) (step 4)
- Insurance claim sub-ledger — expected-recovery receivable recorded at write-off and cleared on settlement (step 5)
- Loss Prevention case link (links to VS-23) — CCTV and internal-theft investigation conclusion feeding the write-off decision (step 2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Theft of small assets**: POS peripherals, RF guns, and small tools are most susceptible to theft; individual values may be below PHP 50K but collectively material
- **Typhoon/flood damage**: Philippine typhoons can damage multiple stores simultaneously, requiring mass asset write-off processing
- **Delayed discovery**: Missing assets may not be discovered until annual physical verification; Loss Prevention spot checks help reduce discovery gap

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-report of "reports asset loss/theft/damage incident" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "Loss Prevention investigates theft cases" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "Accountant prepares write-off recommendation" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-348 (ensure controlled execution — Physical Verification & Disposal (PA-35.3).)
- operational: Fixed Asset Accountant review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
1.1–2.1 weeks elapsed per occurrence (1–2 weeks + 1 day) — at ~20–50 write-offs/year ≈ 20–50 occurrences/year.

## W1710. Asset Sale, Trade-In & Salvage Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Asset retirement with recoverable value (W1708) |
| **Frequency** | ~30–60 sales/trade-ins/year |
| **Volume** | Low volume but important for value recovery |
| **Owner** | Fixed Asset Accountant |
| **Participants** | Fixed Asset Accountant, Purchasing (for trade-in negotiation), Maintenance (for salvage), Treasury (for proceeds) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Determine disposal method based on asset type and condition: (a) vehicles — trade-in to dealer on new vehicle purchase or sell via auction, (b) IT equipment — certified data destruction then sell to e-waste recycler, (c) store fixtures — sell to used fixture dealer or scrap, (d) forklifts/DC equipment — sell to equipment dealer, (e) POS terminals — return to vendor or sell to refurbisher | Fixed Asset Accountant / Purchasing | Accounting Manager | 1–2 hours/asset |
| 2 | Obtain fair market value: (a) for high-value assets (≥ PHP 100K): minimum 2 quotes or auction listing, (b) for trade-in: vendor provides trade-in value vs. separate sale comparison, (c) for scrap: current scrap metal/e-waste rates, (d) document valuation basis for audit trail | Purchasing / Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 1–3 days |
| 3 | Execute sale/trade-in: (a) prepare sales documentation (invoice or trade-in credit memo), (b) for trade-in: credit applied to new asset purchase invoice, (c) for sale: issue sales invoice, collect payment, (d) for scrap: weigh and apply contracted rates, issue delivery receipt | Fixed Asset Accountant / Purchasing | Accounting Manager | 1–2 days |
| 4 | Treasury receives proceeds (if cash sale); Fixed Asset Accountant processes disposal per W1708 step 4 and gain/loss recognition per W1711 | Treasury / Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 15 min/asset |


### System Touchpoints
- Disposal-method decision matrix in the fixed-asset module — vehicle trade-in/auction, certified data destruction for IT, fixture resale routing (step 1)
- FMV documentation workflow — ≥2 quotes or auction listing for assets ≥ PHP 100K, trade-in vs. separate-sale comparison, contracted scrap rates (step 2)
- Sales invoice / trade-in credit memo — proceeds documentation and credit application to the new-asset purchase invoice (step 3)
- Treasury receipts interface — cash-proceeds posting with gain/loss handoff to W1711 (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Below-market-disposal risk**: assets disposed at understated value leak recovery; mitigated by the FMV documentation rule (2 quotes / auction listing) and Accounting Manager approval (steps 2–3)
- **Data-bearing-device risk**: IT equipment disposed without certified destruction exposes BuildRight to RA 10173 privacy breach; mitigated by the certified-destruction precondition in the disposal-method matrix (step 1)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "document valuation basis for audit trail" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-generate of "prepare sales documentation" (replaces manual Step 3).
- System auto-count of "Accountant processes disposal per W1708 step 4 and gain/loss recognition per W1711" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-348 (ensure controlled execution — Physical Verification & Disposal (PA-35.3).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
2–5 days elapsed per occurrence (1–3 days + 1–2 days) — at ~30–60 sales/trade-ins/year ≈ 30–60 occurrences/year.

## W1711. Gain/Loss on Asset Disposal Recognition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Asset disposal processed (W1708, W1709, W1710) |
| **Frequency** | ~100–200 disposals/month |
| **Volume** | Monthly aggregate gain/loss posted |
| **Owner** | Fixed Asset Accountant |
| **Participants** | Fixed Asset Accountant, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System auto-calculates gain/loss on each disposal: (a) gain = proceeds/recovery − net book value − disposal costs, (b) loss = net book value − proceeds/recovery + disposal costs, (c) proceeds include: cash received, trade-in credit, insurance recovery, salvage value, (d) disposal costs include: removal, transportation, environmental remediation | System | — | Automated |
| 2 | System generates disposal journal entry: (a) debit: cash/receivable (proceeds), accumulated depreciation, (b) credit: gross asset cost, (c) difference = gain (credit to other income) or loss (debit to other expense), (d) entry posted to entity-specific GL | System | Fixed Asset Accountant | Automated |
| 3 | Monthly: Fixed Asset Accountant reviews aggregate disposal gain/loss: (a) total gains and losses by entity, (b) significant individual disposals (gain/loss > PHP 100K), (c) ensures proper classification (PFRS requires gain/loss in P&L, not OCI) | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 30 min/month |
| 4 | Tax compliance: gains on disposal may be subject to capital gains tax or ordinary income tax depending on asset type and Philippine tax rules; Tax Accountant reviews material gains for proper tax treatment | Tax Accountant | CFO | Quarterly |


### System Touchpoints
- Disposal gain/loss calculator in the fixed-asset module — auto gain/loss = proceeds − NBV ∓ disposal costs, including trade-in credit and insurance recovery (step 1)
- Auto-generated disposal journal entry — cash/receivable, accumulated depreciation, and gross-cost lines with gain/loss to P&L per entity (step 2)
- Monthly disposal review report — gains/losses by entity with individual disposals > PHP 100K flagged (step 3)
- Tax review interface (links to VS-79) — capital-gains vs. ordinary-income treatment on material gains (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Classification risk**: disposal gain/loss posted to OCI or the wrong entity distorts the PFRS P&L and entity statutory books; mitigated by system posting rules and the Accountant's PFRS classification review (P&L, not OCI) (step 3)
- **Tax-treatment risk**: material gains taxed at the wrong character (capital vs. ordinary) create BIR assessment exposure; mitigated by Tax Accountant review of material gains (step 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-calculate of "calculates gain/loss on each disposal" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "generates disposal journal entry" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "reviews aggregate disposal gain/loss" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-348 (ensure controlled execution — Physical Verification & Disposal (PA-35.3).)
- operational: Fixed Asset Accountant review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
Gain/loss calculation and disposal journal entries automated; monthly aggregate gain/loss review 30 min ≈ 6 hours/year; quarterly tax-compliance review of disposal gains at realized volume.

## W1712. Asset Return to Vendor (Warranty Claim)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Capital asset fails within warranty period |
| **Frequency** | ~20–40 warranty claims/year |
| **Volume** | Low volume but important for cost recovery |
| **Owner** | Maintenance Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Maintenance Supervisor, Purchasing, Fixed Asset Accountant, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maintenance identifies asset failure within warranty period: (a) checks asset record for warranty expiry date, (b) confirms failure type is covered by warranty terms, (c) documents failure with photos and technical description | Maintenance Supervisor | — | 30 min/claim |
| 2 | Purchasing contacts vendor with warranty claim: (a) provides asset details, serial number, purchase date, PO reference, (b) vendor confirms warranty coverage and provides return authorization (RMA), (c) vendor specifies: repair, replacement, or credit | Purchasing | Maintenance Supervisor | 1–2 days |
| 3 | If repair: vendor repairs and returns; system logs downtime and tracks repair. If replacement: old asset returned, new asset received; Fixed Asset Accountant updates asset register (new serial number, reset warranty clock). If credit: asset returned, credit note received; Fixed Asset Accountant processes partial disposal and applies credit to cost center | Purchasing / Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | Variable |
| 4 | Warranty claim outcome logged in asset maintenance history per W1695; used for vendor quality evaluation and future purchasing decisions | Maintenance Supervisor | — | 10 min |


### System Touchpoints
- Asset-master warranty-expiry check — failure-vs-coverage validation before claim initiation; warranty clock reset on replacement (steps 1, 3)
- Vendor RMA workflow — claim submission with serial/PO reference and repair/replacement/credit routing (step 2)
- Asset register update on replacement — new serial number, restarted warranty, downtime logging; the credit path retires the asset via W1708 (step 3)
- Maintenance history interface (links to W1695) — claim outcome logged for vendor quality evaluation and future sourcing decisions (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Warranty-forfeiture risk**: failures reported after expiry or shipped without vendor RMA forfeit recovery; mitigated by the expiry-date check at initiation and RMA confirmation before return (steps 1–2)
- **Register-inaccuracy risk**: a replacement received without a register update (stale serial, un-reset warranty clock) breaks future claims and maintenance scheduling; mitigated by the Fixed Asset Accountant register update as a required step-3 action

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "record for warranty expiry date" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System workflow notification of "contacts vendor with warranty claim" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-capture of "center" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-348 (ensure controlled execution — Physical Verification & Disposal (PA-35.3).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
40 min per occurrence (per-step effort 10 min + 30 min/claim); elapsed timeline 1–2 days; cadence-carried steps as authored (Variable) — at ~20–40 warranty claims/year ≈ 20–40 occurrences/year ≈ 13.3–26.7 hours/year.

## W1713. Asset Register Cleanup & Archiving

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-physical verification (W1706); annual register maintenance |
| **Frequency** | Annual (post-verification) + ongoing |
| **Volume** | ~500–1,000 register cleanups/year |
| **Owner** | Fixed Asset Accountant |
| **Participants** | Fixed Asset Accountant, Accounting Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Post-verification cleanup actions: (a) fully depreciated assets still physically present → change status to "Fully Depreciated — Active" (remain in register for insurance and accountability purposes), (b) fully depreciated assets no longer present → process retirement per W1708, (c) duplicate asset records → merge with audit trail, (d) assets with incorrect location → update location per actual, (e) assets with incorrect classification → reclassify per W1691 | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 2–3 days/year |
| 2 | System archives retired/disposed assets: (a) assets moved from active register to archived register, (b) archived records retained for 10 years per BIR requirement, (c) archived records searchable but excluded from active reports and depreciation runs, (d) GL reconciliation includes both active and archived totals for audit completeness | System | Fixed Asset Accountant | Automated |
| 3 | Annual: Fixed Asset Accountant reviews archived register for records eligible for permanent deletion (> 10 years since disposal); submits deletion list to Accounting Manager for approval; deleted records logged in deletion journal for audit trail | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 1 day/year |
| 4 | Policy document update: if verification or cleanup reveals systemic issues (e.g., consistent failure to register assets, frequent tag damage), Fixed Asset Accountant updates fixed asset policy and procedures accordingly | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | As needed |


### System Touchpoints
- Register status workflow — "Fully Depreciated — Active" retention for insurance/accountability vs. retirement initiation for assets no longer present (step 1)
- Archive repository — 10-year BIR retention; searchable but excluded from active reports and the depreciation run (step 2)
- Deletion-journal workflow — approval of the >10-year deletion list with an audit-trail log of permanently deleted records (step 3)
- Policy/procedure repository — cleanup-driven policy updates where systemic tag or registration failures recur (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-deletion risk**: records destroyed before the 10-year BIR window closes break tax-audit defensibility; mitigated by Accounting Manager approval of the deletion list and the deletion journal (step 3)
- **Zombie-asset risk**: retired assets left in the active register keep accumulating insurance cover and confuse physical verification; mitigated by post-verification status cleanup and archive exclusion from the depreciation run (steps 1–2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "records → merge with audit trail" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-report of "reports and depreciation runs" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "logged in deletion journal for audit trail" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-348 (ensure controlled execution — Physical Verification & Disposal (PA-35.3).)
- operational: Fixed Asset Accountant review and approval gate; periodic reconciliation against source documents

### Time Estimate
Annual post-verification cleanup 2–3 days plus archived-register retention review 1 day ≈ 28 hours/year across ~500–1,000 cleanup actions (archival automated); policy updates as needed.
