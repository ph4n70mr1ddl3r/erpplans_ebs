## W1690. New Fixed Asset Registration & Capitalization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Purchase order receipt for capital asset or construction project completion per W40.3 |
| **Frequency** | ~200–300 new assets/month (store fixtures, IT equipment, vehicles, renovation components) |
| **Volume** | ~2,500–3,500 new fixed assets/year |
| **Owner** | Fixed Asset Accountant |
| **Participants** | Fixed Asset Accountant, Receiving Clerk, Department Head, CFO (for assets > threshold) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System auto-generates asset registration candidate from approved PO or capex project closure: (a) PO with asset category flag flows the asset candidate into the mass additions queue for Fixed Asset review, (b) capex project completion triggers CIP-to-asset conversion per W40.3, (c) manual creation for donated, leased, or transferred assets | System | Fixed Asset Accountant | Automated |
| 2 | Fixed Asset Accountant reviews and enriches asset record: (a) assigns unique asset number (format: BRD-XX-YYYY-NNNNN where XX = entity code, YYYY = year, NNNNN = sequential), (b) enters asset description, category, sub-category (POS terminal, forklift, fixture, vehicle, IT equipment, office furniture), (c) links to cost center, location (store/DC/HQ code), and responsible department, (d) records acquisition cost including non-recoverable VAT, delivery, installation, and testing costs per PFRS, (e) assigns useful life per category schedule (POS terminals: 5 years; forklifts: 7 years; store fixtures: 10 years; vehicles: 5 years; IT servers: 4 years; office furniture: 8 years), (f) assigns depreciation method (straight-line for all; declining balance for vehicles), (g) records vendor, PO reference, GR reference, and warranty expiry, (h) assigns the asset custodian of record into the Assigned-To field — the location manager, DC supervisor or department head of record for the space the asset occupies; named individual for IT equipment, tools and vehicles, role-of-record for fixtures — so custody is never blank and moves only via the W1693 release-and-accept chain | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 15 min/asset |
| 3 | System validates capitalization threshold: assets ≥ PHP 20,000 capitalized; assets < PHP 20,000 charged to expense (policy threshold reviewed annually). Threshold excludes low-value items like individual hand tools, cables, or accessories; items below the threshold but accountable by nature — power tools, measuring instruments, cameras — are flagged as tractable items, expensed yet carried on the countable minor-asset register so the W1706 count covers them | System | Fixed Asset Accountant | Automated |
| 4 | For assets requiring capitalization approval (≥ PHP 500K): Accounting Manager reviews asset record, verifies capex approval per W1811, confirms classification and useful life; approves capitalization | Accounting Manager | CFO | 10 min/asset |
| 5 | System activates asset in fixed asset register; depreciation begins from the asset's ready-for-use date (not invoice date); asset appears in entity-specific fixed asset register and consolidated group register | System | — | Automated |
| 6 | Asset tag generated and assigned per W1692; physical label applied to asset at receiving or installation | Receiving Clerk / Fixed Asset Accountant | — | 5 min/asset |

### System Touchpoints
- Auto-creation from PO receipt or capex project completion (W1690.1)
- Mass Additions queue review — the AP-to-Assets gate where candidates post as additions, charge to expense, split, merge, or reject (W1690.1)
- Assigned-To custodian field on the asset record — the custody-of-record attribute driving W1693 transfers and W1706 count accountability (W1690.2)
- Fixed asset master with full attribute set: cost, location, category, depreciation params, warranty, vendor (W1690.2)
- Capitalization threshold validation rules engine (W1690.3)
- Approval workflow for high-value assets with tiered authorization (W1690.4)
- Depreciation engine with start date = ready-for-use date (W1690.5)
- Asset tag / barcode / QR code generation module (W1690.6)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cutoff errors**: Assets received near month-end may be expensed instead of capitalized if GR is delayed; strict "ready-for-use" date tracking required
- **Threshold gaming**: Department heads may split purchases to stay below PHP 20,000 threshold; system should flag repetitive purchases to same vendor/category within 30 days
- **Custody-gap risk**: assets without a named custodian of record drift unowned between departments and locations; mitigated by mandatory Assigned-To assignment at registration (step 2) and the W1706 custodian attestation
- **Missing assets at startup**: 200 existing stores have legacy assets that may lack proper registration; migration of existing asset register is a one-time data migration challenge
- **Component vs. whole asset**: Large installations (e.g., 3 POS terminals per store = 600 total) can be registered as individual assets or as a store-level bundle; consistent policy required


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-capture of "enters asset description, category, sub-category" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-219 (ensure fixed-asset register accuracy.)
- operational: Fixed Asset Accountant review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
15 min per new asset registration; ~250/month = ~63 hours/month (~8 business days/month at 8-hour days). Bulk registration during store openings (29 assets per new store) requires dedicated session.

## W1691. Asset Classification & Category Assignment

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New asset registration (W1690) or annual classification review |
| **Frequency** | Continuous for new assets; annual review in Q4 |
| **Volume** | ~2,500–3,500 new classifications/year + ~15,000–20,000 existing assets reviewed annually |
| **Owner** | Fixed Asset Accountant |
| **Participants** | Fixed Asset Accountant, Accounting Manager, External Auditor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System maintains standardized asset category tree aligned to PFRS/IAS 16 and Philippine tax rules: (a) Land (non-depreciable), (b) Buildings & Improvements (20–40 years), (c) Leasehold Improvements (shorter of useful life or lease term), (d) Plant & Machinery (7–15 years), (e) Vehicles (5 years), (f) Furniture, Fixtures & Equipment (5–10 years), (g) IT Equipment & Software (3–5 years), (h) POS & Retail Technology (5 years), (i) Warehouse Equipment (7–10 years) | System | Accounting Manager | Pre-configured |
| 2 | Fixed Asset Accountant assigns category and sub-category during registration; system auto-populates default useful life and depreciation method; Accountant can override with justification (e.g., harsh environment may reduce useful life of DC forklifts from 7 to 5 years) | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 5 min/asset |
| 3 | Annual review (Q4): Fixed Asset Accountant reviews category assignments for material misclassifications; identifies assets with useful life that should be adjusted based on condition assessment; proposes reclassification to Accounting Manager | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 2–3 days/year |
| 4 | External auditor reviews classification and useful life assumptions during annual audit; confirms PFRS compliance; any reclassification adjustments posted before year-end close | External Auditor / Fixed Asset Accountant | CFO | During annual audit |


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "System maintains standardized asset category tree aligned to PFRS/IAS" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Accountant assigns category and sub-category during registration" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-346 (ensure controlled execution — Asset Registration & Lifecycle Tracking (PA-35.1).)
- operational: Fixed Asset Accountant review and approval gate

### System Touchpoints
- Configurable asset category tree with default useful life and depreciation method (W1691.1)
- Override capability with mandatory justification and approval workflow (W1691.2)
- Annual review report: assets approaching end of useful life, assets with remaining book value > PHP 0 at end of useful life (W1691.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Leasehold improvements**: New stores open in leased properties; lease terms vary (5–15 years); leasehold improvements must be depreciated over the shorter of useful life or lease term — tracking lease expiry dates is critical
- **Software capitalization**: PFRS distinguishes between software licenses (intangible asset) and software customization (may be part of hardware asset); classification requires judgment
- **Component accounting**: PFRS requires significant components with different useful lives to be depreciated separately (e.g., HVAC system in a store vs. the building shell)

### Time Estimate
Category assignment 5 min/asset × ~2,500–3,500 new classifications/year ≈ 210–290 hours/year; annual Q4 review of category assignments for ~15,000–20,000 existing assets 2–3 days ≈ 20 hours/year (category tree pre-configured; external-audit review at annual audit).

### System Touchpoints
- QR/barcode generation integrated with fixed asset register (W1692.1)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

- CTL-346 (ensure controlled execution — Asset Registration & Lifecycle Tracking (PA-35.1).)

## W1696. Asset Lease vs. Buy Decision & Recording

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Need for new asset (vehicle, equipment, IT hardware) identified during budgeting or operational planning |
| **Frequency** | ~20–30 lease-vs-buy decisions/year for material assets |
| **Volume** | Low volume but high value — each decision may involve PHP 1–20M |
| **Owner** | FP&A Manager |
| **Participants** | FP&A Manager, CFO, Department Head, Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Department Head identifies need for new asset; FP&A performs lease-vs-buy analysis: (a) total cost of ownership (purchase price + maintenance + disposal vs. lease payments + maintenance), (b) NPV comparison using company's incremental borrowing rate, (c) cash flow impact (upfront purchase vs. monthly lease), (d) tax implications (depreciation vs. lease expense deductibility), (e) flexibility (lease allows upgrade; purchase builds equity), (f) balance sheet impact per PFRS 16 (most leases now on-balance-sheet) | FP&A Manager | CFO | 2–4 hours/decision |
| 2 | CFO reviews analysis and decides: (a) purchase — triggers capex request per W1811, (b) finance lease — recorded as asset and liability on balance sheet per PFRS 16, (c) operating lease (short-term ≤ 12 months or low-value) — recorded as expense on straight-line basis | CFO | CEO (if > PHP 5M) | 30 min/decision |
| 3 | For leased assets: Fixed Asset Accountant records right-of-use asset and lease liability at inception: (a) right-of-use asset = present value of lease payments, (b) lease liability = same present value, (c) asset depreciated over lease term, (d) liability reduced by lease payments with interest expense on outstanding balance | Fixed Asset Accountant | Accounting Manager | 30 min/lease |
| 4 | Lease payments scheduled in treasury system; monthly: (a) lease payment processed to lessor, (b) system splits payment into principal reduction and interest expense, (c) right-of-use asset depreciated monthly | Treasury / System | Fixed Asset Accountant | Automated monthly |


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "recorded as asset and liability on balance sheet per PFRS 16" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-count of "Accountant records right-of-use asset and lease liability at inception" (replaces manual Step 3).
- System auto-schedule of "scheduled in treasury system" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-346 (ensure controlled execution — Asset Registration & Lifecycle Tracking (PA-35.1).)
- operational: FP&A Manager review and approval gate

### System Touchpoints
- Lease-vs-buy NPV calculator template (W1696.1)
- GL/FA ROU schedules (the A12 EXT): right-of-use asset and lease liability management (W1696.3)
- Lease payment scheduling with auto-split of principal and interest (W1696.4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **PFRS 16 complexity**: All leases > 12 months must be on-balance-sheet; this significantly increases reported assets and liabilities; requires careful tracking of lease terms, renewal options, and discount rates
- **Lease portfolio**: BuildRight leases all 200+ store locations; the lease portfolio represents the largest "asset" class by number and requires dedicated lease accounting functionality
- **Embedded leases**: Service contracts with embedded leases (e.g., copier contracts, IT equipment as-a-service) must be identified and evaluated per PFRS 16

### Time Estimate
~3–5 hours per lease-vs-buy decision (FP&A analysis 2–4 hours + CFO decision 30 min + right-of-use recording 30 min/lease) × ~20–30 decisions/year ≈ 60–150 hours/year on PHP 1–20M decisions; lease payments scheduled automated monthly.

- CTL-219 (ensure fixed-asset register accuracy.)

- **Underinsurance**: Rapid store expansion (10–15/year) may outpace insurance schedule updates; blanket policies help but must be reviewed for aggregate limits
- **Claims recovery lag**: When insured assets are damaged/destroyed, claim settlement can take 3–6 months; temporary assets may be needed while claim is pending
- **Policy fragmentation**: 5 entities × multiple policy types = 15–20 policies to manage; centralized tracking essential to avoid gaps

### Time Estimate
Coverage-matrix updates continuous at new-asset enrollment (auto-flagged above threshold); disposal-triggered insurance schedule updates 10 min/disposal; annual insurance renewal 2–3 days ≈ 20 hours/year; monthly asset-register-to-insurance reconciliation 2 hours ≈ 24 hours/year.
