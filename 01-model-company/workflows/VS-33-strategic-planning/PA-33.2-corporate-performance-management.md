## W1653. Monthly Executive Performance Review (Operating Review)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly calendar (first week of month for prior month results) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly (12×/year) |
| **Volume** | 1 comprehensive review covering entire enterprise |
| **Owner** | CFO |
| **Participants** | CEO, CFO, COO, CIO, CMO, CHRO, VP Supply Chain |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A prepares monthly operating review package (by Day 3 of month): (a) P&L summary: actual vs. budget vs. prior year (entity-level and consolidated), (b) revenue analysis: by store cluster, by category, by channel (in-store vs. ecommerce), (c) gross margin analysis: by category, with margin bridge (volume, mix, price, cost), (d) OpEx analysis: by department with variance commentary, (e) working capital dashboard: inventory days, receivable days, payable days, cash position, (f) store operations metrics: sales/sqm, inventory turns, shrinkage, on-time replenishment, (g) supply chain metrics: DC throughput, fill rate, on-time delivery, (h) ecommerce metrics: orders, GMV, conversion rate, AOV, BOPIS pickup rate, (i) HR metrics: turnover, open positions, training completion | FP&A | CFO | 2 days |
| 2 | CFO reviews package; identifies areas requiring executive discussion: (a) significant variances (> 5% vs. budget), (b) trend concerns (3+ months of unfavorable trend), (c) strategic initiative progress, (d) risk items | CFO | CEO | 2 hours |
| 3 | Monthly Operating Review meeting (Day 5–7): (a) CFO presents financial overview, (b) each C-Suite member reports on their area: key metrics, variances, actions, (c) CEO directs specific actions and follow-ups, (d) FP&A captures action items with owners and deadlines | CEO / C-Suite | CEO | 2–3 hours |
| 4 | Action items distributed to owners; tracked in initiative tracker per W1660; progress reviewed at next monthly meeting | FP&A | CEO | 30 min |


### System Touchpoints
- Operating review package auto-assembly (by Day 3) — P&L actual vs. budget vs. prior year, entity and consolidated, revenue/margin bridges, KPIs (step 1)
- Variance flagging engine — > 5% vs. budget or 3-month adverse-trend flags driving the agenda (step 2)
- Action-item tracker linkage — decisions routed to owners in the initiative tracker per W1660 (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Review-theater risk**: the meeting recites numbers without decisions; mitigated by the variance-flag-driven agenda and owner-tracked action items reviewed at the next monthly meeting (steps 2, 4)
- **Data-latency risk**: a late or inaccurate package erodes executive trust in the process; mitigated by the Day-3 deadline with FP&A quality checks before CFO review (steps 1–2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "FP&A prepares monthly operating review package" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-report of "reports on their area" (replaces manual Step 3).
- System auto-track of "tracked in initiative tracker per W1660" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-341 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Performance Management (PA-33.2).)
- operational: CFO review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
~21 hours per monthly cycle (operating-review package preparation 2 days + CFO review 2 hours + Monthly Operating Review meeting 2–3 hours + action-item distribution 30 min) ≈ 250 hours/year.

## W1654. Weekly Sales Flash & KPI Dashboard

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly calendar (Monday for prior week results) |
| **Frequency** | Weekly (52×/year) |
| **Volume** | Chain-wide with store-level drill-down |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, CEO, COO, CMO, VP Store Operations, Senior FP&A Analyst, CPM (Corporate Performance Management) Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System auto-generates weekly sales flash (Monday 6 AM): (a) total chain revenue: daily and weekly, vs. same week last year, vs. budget, (b) store cluster performance: Metro Manila, Luzon, Visayas, Mindanao, (c) top 10 / bottom 10 stores by revenue variance, (d) ecommerce: orders, GMV, conversion rate, (e) category performance: top 5 growing categories, top 5 declining, (f) transaction count and average transaction value, (g) key promotions performance (if active) | System | FP&A | Automated |
| 2 | FP&A adds commentary: (a) significant events affecting sales (typhoon closures, holidays, competitor promotions), (b) notable trends or anomalies, (c) promotional event results if applicable | FP&A | CFO | 30 min |
| 3 | Weekly sales flash distributed to CEO, COO, CMO, VP Store Operations by Monday 9 AM; accessible via mobile executive dashboard | FP&A | — | Automated |


### System Touchpoints
- Auto-generated weekly sales flash (Monday 6 AM) — chain and store-cluster revenue, daily/weekly, vs. same week last year and vs. budget (step 1)
- Commentary layer and mobile executive dashboard — events, anomalies, and promotional impact distributed by Monday 9 AM (steps 2–3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Noise-over-signal risk**: weekly swings trigger over-reaction (typhoon closures, holidays read as trends); mitigated by FP&A commentary separating one-off events from trend (step 2)
- **Silent-skip risk**: a failed generation or missed distribution goes unnoticed until executives ask; mitigated by scheduled auto-generation with distribution monitoring (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count and average transaction value" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-distribute of "distributed to CEO, COO, CMO, VP Store Operations by Monday 9 AM" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-230 (ensure strategy-to-budget alignment.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
30 min per occurrence (per-step effort 30 min) — at Weekly (52×/year) ≈ 52 occurrences/year ≈ 26 hours/year.

## W1655. Store-Level Monthly Performance Review & Scorecard

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly calendar (by Day 10 for prior month) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | 200 store scorecards |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, Regional Managers, Store Managers |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates monthly store scorecard per store: (a) financial KPIs: revenue vs. budget, gross margin %, controllable expense vs. budget, store contribution margin, (b) operational KPIs: sales/sqm, inventory turns, shrinkage %, on-time replenishment rate, (c) customer KPIs: customer satisfaction score, NPS (if surveyed), loyalty enrollment rate, (d) people KPIs: turnover rate, training completion, attendance compliance, (e) safety KPIs: incident count, safety audit score | System | VP Store Operations | Automated |
| 2 | Regional Manager reviews scorecards for their stores (~33 stores per Regional Manager; ~6 RMs per VS-177); identifies: (a) top performing stores (recognize and share best practices), (b) underperforming stores (coach and action plan), (c) stores with concerning trends (3+ months declining metrics) | Regional Manager | VP Store Operations | 4 hours/month |
| 3 | Regional Manager conducts monthly review call with each Store Manager: (a) discuss scorecard results, (b) identify root causes for underperformance, (c) agree on corrective actions, (d) share best practices from top-performing stores | Regional Manager | VP Store Operations | 30 min/store |
| 4 | VP Store Operations reviews aggregate store performance with COO monthly: (a) chain-wide average scorecard, (b) regional comparison, (c) bottom 10 stores with action plans, (d) top 10 stores for recognition | VP Store Operations | COO | 2 hours/month |


### System Touchpoints
- Monthly store scorecard generator — financial KPIs (revenue vs. budget, margin %, controllable expense, contribution) and operational KPIs per store (step 1)
- Regional review workflow — top/bottom identification and monthly per-store review calls with corrective actions (steps 2–3)
- Chain aggregation for COO — regional comparison and bottom-10 action plans (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric-gaming risk**: stores optimize scored metrics at the expense of unscored outcomes like customer experience; mitigated by the balanced financial + operational scorecard and root-cause reviews (steps 1, 3)
- **Bottom-decay risk**: chronic underperformers linger without consequence; mitigated by bottom-10 store action plans tracked under VP Store Operations/COO oversight (step 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count, safety audit score" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews scorecards for their stores" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-identify of "identify root causes for underperformance" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-341 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Performance Management (PA-33.2).)
- operational: VP Store Operations review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
recurring effort 4 hours/month + 2 hours/month + ~100 hours/month (200 stores × 30 min review calls) ≈ ~1,270 hours/year.

## W1656. Category Manager Monthly Performance Review & Margin Ownership

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly calendar cycle — Category Manager |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | 13 departments / ~40 product categories (per profile §6.2) |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising, Category Managers, FP&A |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A generates category-level monthly P&L: (a) revenue vs. budget by category and by store cluster, (b) gross margin % and margin bridge (cost change, price change, mix change, promo impact), (c) sell-through rate, (d) inventory turns and weeks of supply, (e) markdown rate, (f) vendor margin contribution (rebates, volume discounts) | FP&A | VP Merchandising | 1 day |
| 2 | VP Merchandising reviews category performance with each Category Manager: (a) revenue and margin vs. plan, (b) assortment effectiveness (new SKU sell-through, discontinued SKU clearance progress), (c) promotional ROI (margin given up vs. incremental revenue), (d) vendor negotiation wins/losses, (e) action items for margin improvement | VP Merchandising | — | 1 hour/category |
| 3 | Category-specific action items: (a) margin below target → vendor negotiation for better cost, price increase, or mix shift, (b) revenue below target → promotional plan, assortment adjustment, (c) inventory above target → markdown acceleration, return to vendor | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | Ongoing |


### System Touchpoints
- Category P&L generator — revenue vs. budget by category and store cluster with gross-margin bridge (cost, price, mix) (step 1)
- Assortment analytics feed — new-SKU sell-through and discontinued-item performance into the VP Merchandising review (step 2)
- Category action tracking — margin/recovery actions per category owner (step 3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-erosion-blindness risk**: mix shifts mask per-SKU margin decay; mitigated by the cost/price/mix margin bridge decomposition (step 1)
- **Action-drift risk**: the same category shortfall is reviewed monthly without vendor or price action; mitigated by category-specific action items with owner follow-through (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "FP&A generates category-level monthly P&L" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews category performance with each Category Manager" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-341 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Performance Management (PA-33.2).)
- operational: VP Merchandising review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
1 day elapsed per occurrence (1 day); cadence-carried steps as authored (Ongoing) — at Monthly ≈ 12 occurrences/year.

## W1657. Quarterly Board Performance Report & Governance Package

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly Board meeting calendar |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly (4×/year) |
| **Volume** | 1 comprehensive governance package |
| **Owner** | CFO |
| **Participants** | CFO, CEO, Corporate Secretary, Board of Directors, CPM (Corporate Performance Management) Analyst |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CFO prepares Board governance package: (a) CEO report: strategic initiative progress, market commentary, organizational highlights, (b) CFO report: quarterly financial results vs. budget and prior year, YTD performance, revised full-year forecast, cash and debt position, (c) COO report: store operations summary, new store pipeline and openings, supply chain performance, (d) CIO report: technology project status, cybersecurity posture, system performance, (e) CMO report: marketing campaign results, ecommerce growth, loyalty program metrics, (f) CHRO report: headcount, turnover, key hires, training, (g) Internal Audit report: audit findings, management action status, (h) Risk report: top risks, risk mitigation status, emerging risks | CFO / C-Suite | CEO | 1 week |
| 2 | Corporate Secretary compiles governance package; distributes to Board members 5 business days before meeting per corporate governance policy | Corporate Secretary | — | 1 day |
| 3 | Board meeting: (a) CEO and C-Suite present reports, (b) Board discusses performance, asks questions, provides direction, (c) Board approves: quarterly financial statements, dividend declaration (if applicable), major capex projects, policy changes, (d) Corporate Secretary records Board minutes and resolutions | CEO / Board | Board Chair | 3–4 hours |
| 4 | Post-meeting: (a) Board resolutions communicated to relevant officers for execution, (b) management actions tracked per W1660, (c) quarterly financial statements filed with SEC (per Philippine corporate law) | Corporate Secretary / CFO | CEO | 1 week |


### System Touchpoints
- Board governance package compiler — CEO report, CFO financials, committee reports (step 1)
- Board portal distribution 5 business days pre-meeting per governance policy (step 2)
- Resolution execution and filing tracker — minutes, officer execution, W1660 tracking, quarterly financial statement filings (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Late-distribution risk**: materials arriving days before the meeting preclude informed director review; mitigated by the 5-business-day distribution rule (step 2)
- **Resolution-execution gap**: Board approvals and directions go unactioned after the meeting; mitigated by resolution communication to responsible officers with W1660 progress tracking (step 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "technology project status, cybersecurity posture, system performance" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-compile of "compiles governance package" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "records Board minutes and resolutions" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-341 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Performance Management (PA-33.2).)
- operational: CFO review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
3–4 hours per occurrence (per-step effort 3–4 hours); elapsed timeline 1 week + 1 day + 1 week — at Quarterly (4×/year) ≈ 4 occurrences/year ≈ 12–16 hours/year.

## W1658. Annual Performance Assessment & Incentive Compensation Calculation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Year-end (January for prior year performance) |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | 6,911 employees (all headcount) |
| **Owner** | CHRO |
| **Participants** | CHRO, CEO, C-Suite, HR, Payroll, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR compiles annual performance data: (a) individual KPI achievement vs. targets (for roles with quantifiable KPIs), (b) behavioral competency assessment (for all roles), (c) store scorecard results (for Store Managers), (d) category P&L results (for Category Managers), (e) department operating plan achievement (for department heads) | HR | CHRO | 2 weeks |
| 2 | Performance calibration: (a) C-Suite reviews and calibrates performance ratings for direct reports, (b) Regional Managers calibrate Store Manager ratings, (c) distribution guidelines: top performer (15%), strong performer (35%), solid performer (35%), development needed (12%), unsatisfactory (3%) | C-Suite / Regional Managers | CEO | 1 week |
| 3 | Incentive compensation calculation: (a) **Store Managers**: bonus = base salary × performance multiplier × store achievement %; funded from store-level P&L bonus pool, (b) **Category Managers**: bonus = base salary × category margin achievement %, (c) **HQ staff**: bonus = base salary × corporate achievement multiplier (based on consolidated EBITDA vs. target), (d) **Sales Associates**: monthly commission based on individual sales performance (tracked via POS ID), (e) **13th month pay**: mandatory per PD 851; calculated separately from performance bonus | HR / Payroll / Finance | CHRO / CFO | 1 week |
| 4 | CEO approves incentive compensation for C-Suite and department heads; CHRO approves for all other employees within approved budget | CEO / CHRO | Board (C-Suite compensation) | 1 week |
| 5 | Payroll processes incentive payments; separate from regular payroll per PH tax rules; BIR withholding tax calculated on bonus income | Payroll | CHRO / CFO | 1 week |


### System Touchpoints
- Performance data compilation — individual KPI achievement and behavioral competency assessment (step 1)
- Calibration workflow — C-Suite, Regional Manager, and distribution-level rating calibration (step 2)
- Incentive calculation engine — store bonus pools (base × multiplier × store achievement), plan funding gates, and approval workflow (steps 3–4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rating-inflation risk**: calibration drift overrates performance and blows the bonus budget; mitigated by tiered calibration and CEO/CHRO approval within the approved incentive budget (steps 2, 4)
- **Calculation-error risk**: manual bonus mathematics across 6,911 employees; mitigated by system-calculated payouts with HR/Payroll verification before release (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-compile of "compiles annual performance data" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "reviews and calibrates performance ratings for direct reports" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-calculate of "calculated separately from performance bonus" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-341 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Performance Management (PA-33.2).)
- operational: CHRO review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
6 weeks elapsed per occurrence (2 weeks + 1 week + 1 week + 1 week + 1 week) — at Annual ≈ 1 occurrence/year.

## W1659. KPI Definition, Target Setting & Cascading Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual strategic planning cycle (W1645) or new KPI introduction |
| **Frequency** | Annual review; ad-hoc additions as strategy evolves |
| **Volume** | ~100–150 KPIs across all levels (corporate → department → store → individual) |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, C-Suite, all department heads |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A maintains KPI hierarchy: (a) **Tier 1 (Corporate)**: Revenue, EBITDA margin, net income, cash flow, ROIC, store count, ecommerce penetration, employee engagement — CEO and Board level, (b) **Tier 2 (Functional)**: Category margin, DC fill rate, store sales/sqm, customer NPS, inventory turns, system uptime, turnover rate — C-Suite level, (c) **Tier 3 (Operational)**: POS transaction speed, GR processing time, AP days payable outstanding, AR days sales outstanding, website conversion rate, DC picks per hour — Manager level, (d) **Tier 4 (Individual)**: Sales per associate, picking accuracy, invoice processing time, first-call resolution rate — Individual contributor level | FP&A | CFO | Ongoing |
| 2 | Annual target setting: (a) Tier 1 targets set during strategic planning (W1645), (b) Tier 2 targets decomposed from Tier 1 by C-Suite, (c) Tier 3 targets set by department heads aligned to Tier 2, (d) Tier 4 targets set by managers aligned to Tier 3 | FP&A / C-Suite / Department Heads | CEO | 2 weeks |
| 3 | KPI governance: (a) each KPI has: name, definition, calculation formula, data source, measurement frequency, target, owner, reporting level, (b) FP&A maintains KPI dictionary; no ad-hoc KPI creation without FP&A review, (c) quarterly: FP&A reviews KPI relevance; proposes additions/modifications to CFO | FP&A | CFO | 2 hours/quarter |


### System Touchpoints
- KPI dictionary — ~100–150 KPIs with definition, formula, data source, frequency, target, owner, and reporting level (step 3)
- Target cascading model — Tier 1 corporate → Tier 2 department → Tier 3 store/individual decomposition (step 2)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Metric-proliferation risk**: KPIs accrete without retirement, diluting focus; mitigated by governance requiring definition, owner, and reporting level per KPI with annual review (step 3)
- **Local-optimization risk**: cascaded targets invite gaming against enterprise goals; mitigated by alignment review of Tier 2/3 targets against the Tier 1 corporate set (step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "count, ecommerce penetration, employee engagement — CEO and Board level" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-341 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Performance Management (PA-33.2).)
- operational: FP&A review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
Annual target setting 2 weeks ≈ 80 hours/year cascading ~100–150 KPIs across corporate/department/store/individual tiers; quarterly KPI governance review 2 hours ≈ 8 hours/year; KPI hierarchy maintenance ongoing within FP&A.

## W1660. Cross-Functional Initiative Tracking & Status Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategic initiative approved in W1645 or emerging priority |
| **Frequency** | Ongoing tracking; monthly reporting to C-Suite |
| **Volume** | ~15–25 active cross-functional initiatives at any time |
| **Owner** | CEO |
| **Participants** | Initiative sponsors (C-Suite), initiative leads, FP&A |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Each strategic initiative documented in initiative tracker: (a) initiative name, strategic priority alignment, sponsor (C-Suite), lead (senior manager), (b) business case: expected outcome, financial impact, timeline, resource requirement, (c) milestones with dates, (d) status: On Track (green), At Risk (amber), Off Track (red), Complete | Initiative Lead | Sponsor | 2 hours/initiative |
| 2 | Monthly: initiative leads update status: (a) milestone completion, (b) budget spent vs. plan, (c) risks and issues, (d) decisions needed from C-Suite | Initiative Lead | Sponsor | 30 min/initiative |
| 3 | Monthly: FP&A compiles initiative status summary for CEO: (a) portfolio dashboard: all initiatives with status, (b) budget utilization: total initiative spend vs. budget, (c) at-risk initiatives with root cause and recommended actions, (d) completed initiatives with outcome vs. business case | FP&A | CEO | 2 hours |
| 4 | CEO reviews initiative portfolio with C-Suite at monthly operating review: (a) course-correct at-risk initiatives, (b) approve scope/budget changes, (c) celebrate completions, (d) archive completed initiatives | CEO | Board | 30 min |


### System Touchpoints
- Initiative tracker — sponsor, lead, business case, milestones, budget for ~15–25 active cross-functional initiatives (step 1)
- Monthly status updates — milestone completion, spend vs. plan, risks, decisions needed (step 2)
- Portfolio dashboard — consolidated status and budget utilization for the CEO (step 3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Zombie-initiative risk**: stalled efforts consume budget indefinitely; mitigated by monthly CEO/C-Suite portfolio review with course-correct or kill decisions (step 4)
- **Status-greenwashing risk**: leads report green until sudden failure; mitigated by milestone-level evidence cross-checked against budget utilization (steps 2–3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "documented in initiative tracker" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-update of "update" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-compile of "compiles initiative status summary for CEO" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-341 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Performance Management (PA-33.2).)
- operational: CEO review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
Initiative documentation 2 hours/initiative at onboarding; monthly status updates 30 min/initiative × ~15–25 active initiatives ≈ 90–150 hours/year; FP&A portfolio summary 2 hours/month ≈ 24 hours/year; CEO portfolio review 30 min within the monthly operating review.
