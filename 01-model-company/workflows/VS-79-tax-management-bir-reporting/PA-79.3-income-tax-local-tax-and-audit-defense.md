## W5545. BIR Tax-Enforcement Suspension & Closure-Order Response (Oplan Kandado)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | BIR serves a tax-enforcement suspension or closure order against a BuildRight store, branch, or registered seat — the Oplan Kandado class (preliminary 5-day closure for survey/inventory verification, or suspension of business for verified under-declaration under the RR/RMC closure framework) — or an LOA/audit escalation signals imminent enforcement closure |
| **Frequency** | Rare; realistically 0–2 enforcement events per decade chain-wide; audit-risk-weighted sites are the exposure surface |
| **Volume** | 1 order × 1+ closed site (trading halt at that site — the PHP 840K/day per-store downtime canon) × statutory reinstatement mechanics × 5-entity registration structure |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | CFO, VP Legal, BIR-accredited tax counsel, Finance Controller, Regional Manager & Store Manager (closure/reopening execution), Corporate Secretary (protests & records), Compliance (VS-22 regulatory-desk linkage), HR (site-staff continuity), IR if material (W5019) |

### Background

W2773 owns BIR audit defense and W658 the general inspection-response protocol, but neither owns the enforcement *closure event*: the order that halts trading at a site. The exposure is specific and time-boxed — every closed day costs the site's full revenue day while the reinstatement package (books, invoices, returns, settlement or protest) is assembled, and the protest windows against closure orders are short. The corpus documents Oplan Kandado nowhere ('Oplan Kandado'/'tax suspension'/'closure order' appear in zero PA files), leaving the highest-severity tax-enforcement scenario without an owner. Custody per E-25: the order dictates closure, W850 governs *how* a closure is executed and reopened, and reopening requires the BIR lifting order — never W850's trading judgment alone.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Order intake & verification**: Tax Manager receives the served order; verify authenticity and legal basis (issuing RDO, provision cited — survey closure vs under-declaration suspension, scope: which TIN/branch codes and premises, effective time and duration); confirm immediate compliance duty with counsel; notify CFO and the Regional Manager of the affected site | Tax Manager / VP Legal | CFO | < 2 h |
| 2 | **Site closure execution**: the affected site executes closure mechanics per W850 (cash securing, systems sign-out, signage per the order's terms — no voluntary scope expansion beyond what the order covers); staff deployment handled (redeployment to nearby sites or paid stand-by per HR); inventory and books at the site preserved for the survey/verification if ordered | Store Manager / Regional Manager | Tax Manager | Same day |
| 3 | **Legal assessment & protest track**: counsel assesses the cited violation against the site's documented compliance (registered invoices/CRM, VAT returns, books); file the appropriate protest/reconsideration within the statutory window; where the violation is verified, negotiate settlement/compromise per the W2773 controversy-governance framework — resolution speed is the revenue lever | Tax Counsel / VP Legal | CFO | Days–weeks |
| 4 | **Remediation & reinstatement package**: correct the underlying defect chain-wide-relevant (invoicing configuration, unreported receipts, books); assemble compliance evidence (updated returns, payment proofs, ATP/registration records); present to the issuing RDO and secure the lifting/reinstatement order in writing | Tax Manager | Tax Manager | Per case |
| 5 | **Reopening execution**: on receipt of the lifting order, the site reopens per W850 mechanics; revenue-recovery steps (promo, local outreach) per store practice; spoilage/damage and inventory variances from the closed window reconciled (W64 seasonal exposure where relevant) | Store Manager / Regional Manager | VP Store Operations | < 24 h after lifting |
| 6 | **Chain-wide prevention sweep**: audit the violated obligation across all sites and entities (same invoice/receipt configuration, same filing posture); register-level compliance checklist updated; the event feeds the W2776 tax risk register with root cause and detection gap | Tax Manager / Tax Specialists | CFO | 1–2 weeks |
| 7 | **Governance & disclosure**: penalty/settlement accrual posted and financial-statement impact assessed per W590/W2775; Audit Committee briefed; if the event is material to a listed company, IR runs the W5017/W5019 disclosure assessment | Tax Manager / Controller | CFO / Audit Committee | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- BIR RDO correspondence & order case file (evidence chain; VS-88 retention class)
- Store-closure status flag & trading-resume control (per W850)
- Tax calendar & controversy register entries (links to W2776); protest-deadline clock
- Payment/settlement postings & penalty accruals (links to W590/W2775)
- Registration/ATP records per branch (links to W1718-class entity records)
- Disclosure desk & materiality log (links to W5017/W5019)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Revenue-bleed risk**: every closed day costs the site's full revenue day while the package is assembled; mitigated by the same-day Steps 2–4 sequencing and the protest-track speed focus
- **Protest-lapse risk**: the short statutory protest windows against closure orders are missed while the site focuses on logistics; mitigated by the counsel-owned clock in Step 3
- **Scope-creep risk**: staff voluntarily closing more than the order covers (whole site when only a department/counter was ordered); mitigated by the order-scope verification in Step 1
- **Recurrence risk**: the same defect exists at other sites; mitigated by the mandatory chain-wide sweep in Step 6
- **Evidence risk**: books/invoices at the closed site are inaccessible or disordered precisely when needed; mitigated by the Step-2 preservation discipline

### Automation Opportunity
- Tax-enforcement case template auto-opens on order intake with the protest clock, site-closure checklist (W850 linkage) and evidence-request list pre-assembled (replaces manual Steps 1–3 assembly)
- Branch-level tax-compliance dashboard auto-flags the violated obligation across all sites for the Step-6 sweep (replaces manual defect replication analysis)

### Controls
- CTL-468 (ensure controlled execution — Corporate Income Tax, Local Tax, DST & BIR Audit Defense (PA-79.3).)
- operational: no site reopens without the written BIR lifting order on file — W850's trading judgment never substitutes for the regulator's order
- operational: every closure-order event closes with a chain-wide compliance sweep result recorded in the W2776 register

### Time Estimate
Per event: Steps 1–2 ≈ 8–16 staff-hours day one; Steps 3–5 ≈ 40–120 staff-hours across Tax, Legal and site operations over the enforcement window; Steps 6–7 ≈ 16–32 staff-hours. Event-driven, no annual figure claimed; the response is rehearsed through the W2776 controversy-governance cadence rather than drills.

### Staffing Implication
- Absorbed by the Tax team, VP Legal and existing site operations; external BIR-accredited counsel engaged per retainer. No incremental headcount.

## W5546. BIR eFPS & Government-Portal Outage Filing Contingency, AAB Fallback & Penalty-Relief Documentation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | eFPS, eBIRForms, or the BIR ePayment gateway (or an SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG/LGU portal on a statutory deadline) is unavailable on, or within 3 days before, a filing/payment deadline; or the accredited CAS/e-invoicing system fails to transmit sales-invoice data for more than 4 hours during the obligation window |
| **Frequency** | ~2–6 events/yr chain-wide — portal congestion near the 10th/20th/25th deadline days is a recurring Philippine reality already cited as a pain point in the W590/W2764 filing and PA-79.1 workflows, but never owned as an event |
| **Volume** | 1 event × the affected filing calendar (monthly VAT 2550M/Q, withholding 1601-EQ/FQ, remittances, annual returns) × 5 legal entities × penalty exposure per return (25% surcharge + 20% p.a. interest + compromise penalty under the RMC 7-2015 framework) |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Entity Tax Specialists, Finance Controller (AAB payments), Treasury (fund positioning per W556-class timing), Corporate Secretary (records), IT & CAS vendor (e-invoicing branch), external tax counsel (abatement), Compliance |

### Background

Four PA files name eFPS downtime as a pain point and one names the mitigation in a bullet — "manual filing with AAB as fallback" (W590-class) and "filing 3+ days early" (PA-79.1) — but no workflow owns the contingency event itself: the filing-day decision tree, the statutory fallback sequence, the evidence pack that makes penalty relief possible, and the reconciliation that prevents phantom "filed" status. The penalty asymmetry matters: failure-to-file and failure-to-pay are separate exposures, so an outage response that pays at an Authorized Agent Bank while the return is delayed is materially better protected than the reverse. This workflow is that event canon; the routine filing calendar stays with W2776 and the entity filing workflows.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Outage confirmation & clock**: confirm the outage is portal-side (BIS advisory, acquirer-of-record status page, peer confirmation) vs local connectivity; open the event log with timestamped screenshots of each filing attempt — this evidence pack is the later abatement basis; list every return/payment with a deadline inside the outage window across all 5 entities | Entity Tax Specialist | Tax Manager | 30–60 min |
| 2 | **Filing-path decision tree per return**: (a) retry eFPS through the deadline day (including off-peak windows); (b) prepare the return in eBIRForms offline and file manually at the RDO; (c) where manual filing is unavailable, file at any Authorized Agent Bank (AAB) together with payment — the AAB-received return+payment is the statutory fallback; decision rule: never let the day end with neither an electronic attempt nor a manual/AAB filing | Entity Tax Specialist | Tax Manager | Same day |
| 3 | **Payment protection**: where only payment is at risk, pay via AAB (or GCash/bank-channel instruments the BIR accepts) even if the return itself must follow — failure-to-pay carries the harsher exposure; Treasury confirms fund positioning per the payment calendar; keep deposit slips/validation as primary evidence | Entity Tax Specialist / Treasury | Finance Controller | Same day |
| 4 | **Penalty-relief documentation**: assemble the abatement/relief request per the governing BIR issuance for system-downtime relief — the timestamped attempt log, BIR advisories, screenshots, manual/AAB proofs; calendar the follow-up; where penalties are assessed despite relief, elevate per the W2773 controversy framework | Tax Manager / Tax Counsel | CFO | Per case |
| 5 | **CAS/e-invoicing outage branch**: if the accredited CAS cannot transmit sales-invoice data — notify BIR per accreditation rules, operate the approved manual/invoice-book fallback, log the outage window, and batch-transmit the backlog on recovery with BIR acknowledgment retained (links to W473-class e-invoicing operations) | IT / Entity Tax Specialist | Tax Manager | Outage window |
| 6 | **Recovery reconciliation**: once portals recover — verify each return shows as filed and each payment as applied in BIR records; chase "successful submission without acknowledgment" cases; reconcile postings against books per the W590 close linkage; confirm no duplicate filing from the retry sequence | Entity Tax Specialist | Tax Manager | 1–2 days |
| 7 | **Deadline-hygiene update**: post-event, enforce the early-filing buffer policy (≥ 3 days before deadline per the PA-79.1 mitigation) with the W2776 calendar engine flagging returns filed inside the buffer; recurrence pattern (same portal, same deadline) feeds the calendar's pre-deadline alerts | Tax Manager | Tax Manager | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- eFPS/eBIRForms submission logs & BIR acknowledgment receipts (primary evidence chain)
- AAB deposit slips & electronic-payment validation records (GCash/bank channels)
- Tax calendar engine with outage flags & pre-deadline buffer alerts (links to W2776)
- CAS/e-invoicing transmission logs & backlog queue (links to W473-class operations)
- Penalty accrual & abatement case tracking (links to W590/W2773)
- SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG portal-equivalent fallback records (links to W1527/W1306 statutory remittances)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Evidence risk**: no timestamped proof of attempts → abatement denied and penalties stick; mitigated by the Step-1 evidence pack opened at first outage confirmation, before any filing decision
- **Split-liability risk**: return filed manually but payment missed (or paid but unfiled) — the two exposures differ; mitigated by the Step-3 payment-protection rule (pay even if the return must follow)
- **Phantom-filing risk**: portal shows "successful" submission without acknowledgment and BIR records show nothing; mitigated by the Step-6 recovery reconciliation against BIR records, not portal status
- **Duplicate-filing risk**: retries after recovery create duplicate returns; mitigated by the duplicate sweep in Step 6
- **CAS-suspension risk**: unresolved e-invoicing outages jeopardize the accreditation; mitigated by the Step-5 notification-and-backlog discipline

### Automation Opportunity
- Portal-status monitor auto-opens the contingency case, pre-lists every return with a deadline inside the window across the 5 entities, and starts the attempt-logging template (replaces manual Step 1)
- Filing-status reconciliation auto-matches BIR records vs books on recovery and queues phantom/duplicate exceptions (replaces manual Step 6 sweeps)

### Controls
