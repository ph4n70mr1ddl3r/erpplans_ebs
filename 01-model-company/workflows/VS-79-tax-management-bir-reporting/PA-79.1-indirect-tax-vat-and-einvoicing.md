## W2753. VAT Output/Input Ledger Reconciliation & Monthly Form 2550M Preparation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly VAT close-and-file cycle (2550M statutory deadline: 20th of following month, or the 25th for later eFPS groups) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly per entity (5 entities) |
| **Volume** | 5 returns/month; ~PHP 5.19B monthly revenue at 12% output VAT |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, GL Accountant, AP/AR, Tax Accountant, Tax Compliance & eFPS Specialist |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GL Accountant reconciles VAT output (sales) vs. input (purchases) ledgers from ERP tax registers per entity; validates VAT treatment on every taxable line; flags mixed-tender and exempt sales | GL Accountant / Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 2–3 days/entity |
| 2 | Tax Manager computes net VAT payable/refundable; reconciles to GL VAT control accounts; investigates variances > PHP 50K; books adjusting journal entries | Tax Manager | CFO | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Tax Accountant prepares BIR Form 2550M with supporting schedules; routes for CFO sign-off; archives computation workpapers for 10-year BIR retention | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Financials tax register & VAT control accounts; BIR eFPS; e-Invoicing (EIS) log

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Mis-classified VAT-exempt sales (PWD/SC, housing, education) trigger assessment; mitigated by automated tax determination rules and monthly reconciliation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "validates VAT treatment on every taxable line" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-count of "Tax Manager computes net VAT payable/refundable" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-generate of "prepares BIR Form 2550M with supporting schedules" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-214 (ensure BIR tax compliance & filing integrity.)
- operational: automated tax determination rules and monthly reconciliation

### Time Estimate
- 3–5 days per entity per month

## W2754. Quarterly VAT Return (2550Q) & Transitional Input Tax Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly close (25 days after quarter-end) |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly per entity |
| **Volume** | 5 returns/quarter |
| **Owner** | Tax Manager |
| **Participants** | Tax Manager, Tax Accountant, External Tax Advisor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Accountant consolidates three monthly 2550M filings into quarterly summary; reconciles cumulative output/input VAT; validates against GL quarterly trial balance | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 2 days/entity |
| 2 | Tax Manager processes transitional input VAT on capital asset acquisitions (fully creditable in the acquisition month since 1 Jan 2022 per the TRAIN phase-out; residual 60-month schedules only for grandfathered pre-2022 assets per RMC 21-2022); coordinates with external advisor on contentious items | Tax Manager / External Tax Advisor | CFO | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Tax Manager files 2550Q via eFPS; settles any quarterly true-up; documents positions taken for future audit defensibility | Tax Manager | CFO | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Financials quarterly tax close workspace; the **E-Business Tax Reporting Ledger** as the extract of record behind the VAT figures (Tax Register and Tax Reconciliation reports, reporting level and context per ledger/legal entity); BIR eFPS

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reporting risk**: TRAIN capital-goods phase-out errors (post-2021 acquisitions claimed on spread instead of fully creditable, or grandfathered pre-2022 60-month balances claimed in full — per RMC 21-2022); mitigated by advisor review and rule-versioning


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "validates against GL quarterly trial balance" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-create of "Tax Manager processes transitional input VAT on capital" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "documents positions taken for future audit defensibility" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-466 (ensure controlled execution — Indirect Tax (VAT & Percentage Tax) & BIR E-Invoicing (PA-79.1).)
- operational: advisor review and rule-versioning

### Time Estimate
- 4–6 days per entity per quarter

## W2755. BIR eFPS Filing, e-Payment (PESONet/InstaPay) & Validation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Statutory deadline (per BIR filing calendar) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly/quarterly/annual — ~80+ filings/year across all taxes |
| **Volume** | 5 entities × ~16 filings/year ≈ 80 filings |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | Tax Accountant, Tax Manager, Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tax Accountant logs into BIR eFPS, selects return type and tax period, uploads/generated data; validates auto-computed fields vs. ERP workpapers before submission | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 30–60 min/filing |
| 2 | Treasury initiates e-Payment via Authorized Agent Bank (AAB) PESONet/InstaPay or revenue collection officer; captures payment confirmation/reference number | Treasury | CFO | 15–30 min |
| 3 | Tax Accountant attaches proof of filing and payment to ERP tax document repository; updates tax calendar tracker; flags any rejection for re-filing before deadline | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | 15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- BIR eFPS portal; treasury banking platform; ERP document archive; tax calendar workflow

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Operational risk**: eFPS downtime near deadlines; mitigated by filing 3+ days early and maintaining offline computation backups


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs into BIR eFPS, selects return type and tax period, uploads/generated data" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based authorization of "Authorized Agent Bank" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "Tax Accountant attaches proof of filing and payment to" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-214 (ensure BIR tax compliance & filing integrity.)
- operational: filing 3+ days early and maintaining offline computation backups

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 hours per filing cycle

## W2756. BIR EIS E-Invoicing Transmission & Daily Sales Report Sync

| **Frequency** | Daily per location |
| **Volume** | 2.8M POS transactions/month across 205 locations |
| **Owner** | Tax Technology Lead |
| **Participants** | Tax Tech Lead, IT Integration, POS Team, Tax Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Integration ensures daily Sales Data Transmission (Sales Report + e-Invoice JSON) from ERP/POS to BIR EIS via API; monitors acknowledgment receipts per location | IT Integration / Tax Tech Lead | Tax Manager | Continuous |

- System auto-review of "reviews monthly EIS transmission completeness report" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-214 (ensure BIR tax compliance & filing integrity.)
- operational: daily reconciliation and SLA monitoring

### Time Estimate

## W2758. Capital Goods Input VAT — Full-Credit Regime & Grandfathered 60-Month Amortization Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Acquisition of capital goods > PHP 1M per BIR rules (Sec. 110(A)(2) NIRC; input VAT fully creditable since 1 Jan 2022 (the 60-month spread repealed by CREATE/RA 11534 per RR 7-2021; TRAIN-era RR 13-2018 set the sunset) — spread amortization applies only to grandfathered pre-2022 assets) |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~20–40 capex assets/year qualifying |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 700M–1B annual capex (store builds alone run PHP 400–900M per W1648); ~PHP 84–120M input VAT pool |
| **Owner** | Tax Accountant |
| **Participants** | Tax Accountant, Fixed Asset Accountant, Tax Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fixed Asset Accountant flags capital goods acquisitions > PHP 1M threshold at capitalization; captures input VAT component and in-service date | Fixed Asset Accountant | Tax Manager | Per acquisition |
| 2 | Tax Accountant claims the full input VAT in the acquisition month (post-2021 regime); maintains residual 60-month amortization schedules in the ERP tax register for grandfathered pre-2022 assets, claiming the spread portion monthly on 2550M and tracking remaining balance per asset | Tax Accountant | Tax Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | Tax Manager reconciles amortization register to fixed asset subledger annually; accelerates remaining balance on asset disposal/retirement | Tax Manager | CFO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ERP Fixed Asset & input VAT amortization register; tax ledger

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reporting risk**: Mis-tracking disposal acceleration underclaims/overclaims VAT; mitigated by FA-to-tax register reconciliation


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Accountant flags capital goods acquisitions > PHP 1M threshold at capitalization" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-schedule of "ERP tax register" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconciles amortization register to fixed asset subledger annually" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-466 (ensure controlled execution — Indirect Tax (VAT & Percentage Tax) & BIR E-Invoicing (PA-79.1).)
- operational: FA-to-tax register reconciliation

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 hours per qualifying asset setup; monthly run

| **Participants** | Tax Manager, IT, Store Ops, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
