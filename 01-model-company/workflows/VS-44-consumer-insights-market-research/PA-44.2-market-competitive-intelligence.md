## W1910. Quarterly Competitive Store Visit & Price Benchmarking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly competitive intelligence cycle |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly visits to 3–5 key competitors in 4 regions |
| **Volume** | ~15–20 competitor store visits/quarter across Mindanao, Visayas, Luzon, NCR |
| **Owner** | Market Research Analyst |
| **Participants** | Market Research Analyst, Category Manager, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market Research Analyst plans competitive visits: (a) target competitors: CitiHardware (Mindanao/Visayas focus), Wilcon Depot (national), ACE Hardware (national), Handyman (Metro Manila), local independents, (b) visit frequency: top 3 competitors quarterly; secondary competitors semi-annually, (c) visit team: Market Research Analyst + Category Manager (rotated by category), (d) visit protocol: pose as regular customer; observe and document | Market Research Analyst | VP Merchandising | 1 day/quarter |
| 2 | During visit, document: (a) store layout and planogram: department organization, traffic flow, signage quality, (b) product assortment: SKUs carried that BuildRight does not (and vice versa), (c) pricing: photograph shelf prices for 50–100 benchmark SKUs (same across all competitors for comparison), (d) promotional activity: current sale events, promotional signage, discount depth, (e) staff interaction: greeting, product knowledge, upselling behavior, (f) store conditions: cleanliness, lighting, stock levels, checkout queue, (g) customer profile: observed customer types and estimated foot traffic | Market Research Analyst / Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 2–4 hours/visit |
| 3 | Analysis: (a) price comparison: BuildRight vs. competitors for benchmark SKU basket — is BuildRight cheaper or more expensive? By how much?, (b) assortment gaps: competitor products that BuildRight should consider adding, (c) competitive advantages: where does BuildRight excel? Where do competitors excel?, (d) promotional strategy comparison: frequency, depth, categories promoted, (e) recommendations for BuildRight pricing, assortment, and merchandising adjustments | Market Research Analyst | VP Merchandising | 1 day/quarter |
| 4 | VP Merchandising reviews findings: (a) adjust pricing strategy if BuildRight is significantly above/below market, (b) evaluate assortment additions based on competitor gaps, (c) adjust promotional calendar if competitors are outpromoting BuildRight, (d) share relevant findings with Category Managers for category-level action | VP Merchandising | CEO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Competitive price benchmark database with historical tracking (W1910.3a)
- Assortment gap analysis module (W1910.3b)
- Competitive visit report templates (W1910.2)
- Integration with W40 (price management), W679 (assortment optimization), W1851 (private label benchmarking)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Detection risk**: Competitor store staff in smaller stores may recognize BuildRight employees; use mystery shopping agency for visits if needed; mitigated by the Market Research Analyst review and approval gate
- **Price snapshot limitation**: Store visits capture prices at one point in time; prices may change between visits; supplement with online price monitoring (W1913)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "Market Research Analyst plans competitive visits" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "During visit, document" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "VP Merchandising reviews findings" (replaces manual Step 4).

### Controls
- CTL-374 (ensure controlled execution — Market & Competitive Intelligence (PA-44.2).)
- operational: Market Research Analyst review and approval gate

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by Market Research Analyst and rotating Category Managers
- **Travel cost**: ~PHP 50K–100K/quarter for multi-region visits

### Time Estimate
- Planning: 1 day/quarter
- Visits: ~15–20 visits × 3 hours = ~45–60 hours/quarter
- Analysis: 1 day/quarter
- Review: 1 day/quarter
- Total: ~7–13 days/quarter

## W1911. Philippine Home Improvement Market Size & Growth Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual market sizing update; quarterly trend monitoring |
| **Frequency** | Annual market sizing; quarterly trend updates |
| **Volume** | Total addressable market (TAM) for Philippine home improvement retail |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, VP Merchandising, VP Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Annual: FP&A estimates Philippine home improvement retail market: (a) total market size: ~PHP 400–600B (estimated based on PSA construction spending data, DTI retail census, and industry reports), (b) market segments: (i) big-box retail (BuildRight, Wilcon, CitiHardware, ACE): ~30–40% of market, (ii) traditional hardware (neighborhood hardware stores): ~40–50%, (iii) online/marketplace: ~5–10%, (iv) direct-from-factory/wholesale: ~10–15%, (c) market growth rate: 6–10% annually (driven by population growth, urbanization, OFW remittance-driven home construction), (d) BuildRight market share: ~PHP 62.3B ÷ ~PHP 500B = ~12.4% of total market; ~30–40% of big-box segment | FP&A | CFO | 1 week/year |
| 2 | Quarterly: FP&A updates market trend indicators: (a) PSA construction statistics (building permits issued, construction value), (b) BSP consumer spending and credit data, (c) OFW remittance trends (key driver for Philippine home construction), (d) cement and steel industry production/sales data (proxy for construction activity), (e) competitor financial reports (Wilcon Depot is publicly listed — quarterly financials available) | FP&A | CFO | 1 day/quarter |
| 3 | Annual: FP&A presents market outlook to CEO and Board: (a) market size and share, (b) growth forecast and key drivers, (c) competitive landscape changes, (d) BuildRight growth targets relative to market growth, (e) strategic implications: store expansion pace, category focus, digital investment | FP&A | CFO | 1 day/year |

### System Touchpoints
- Market size estimation model with PSA and industry data inputs (W1911.1)
- Market trend tracking dashboard with quarterly indicators (W1911.2)
- Integration with W33 (strategic planning), W26 (annual budget), W1652 (site selection — market sizing per location)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data availability**: Philippine home improvement retail market data is limited; PSA construction data and DTI retail census are the primary sources but may be delayed or incomplete


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-report of "reports" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-update of "updates market trend indicators" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-forecast of "forecast and key drivers" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-190 (protect research-participant data & insight validity.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by FP&A

### Time Estimate
- Annual market sizing: 1 week
- Quarterly updates: 1 day/quarter
- Board presentation: 1 day
- Total: ~15 days/year

## W1912. Competitive New Store Opening & Expansion Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | News report, trade intelligence, or competitor announcement of new store opening |
| **Frequency** | Continuous monitoring; monthly summary |
| **Volume** | ~10–20 competitor store openings/year in BuildRight's trade areas |
| **Owner** | Market Research Analyst |
| **Participants** | Market Research Analyst, VP Store Operations, COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market Research Analyst monitors competitor expansion: (a) Google Alerts for competitor names + "new store" + "opening", (b) social media monitoring of competitor Facebook/Instagram pages for new store announcements, (c) local news monitoring in BuildRight's 4 regions, (d) trade account feedback: contractors mentioning competitor openings, (e) LGU business permit tracking (new permits issued to competitors) | Market Research Analyst | VP Store Operations | Ongoing (1–2 hours/week) |
| 2 | Upon detecting competitor new store in BuildRight's trade area: (a) assess impact: is the new store within 5 km of an existing BuildRight store? Within 10 km?, (b) estimate competitive threat: store size, format (big-box vs. small-format), categories carried, (c) assess BuildRight store vulnerability: current store sales trend, market saturation, customer loyalty in the area, (d) develop defensive strategy recommendations: (i) increase promotional activity, (ii) enhance trade account engagement in area, (iii) improve store experience, (iv) accelerate planned improvements | Market Research Analyst | VP Store Operations | 4–8 hours |
| 3 | Monthly: Market Research Analyst distributes competitive expansion summary to VP Store Operations and COO: (a) new competitor stores opened this month (location, format, estimated size), (b) BuildRight stores potentially impacted, (c) defensive actions taken and results, (d) implications for BuildRight's own expansion planning (W1652) | Market Research Analyst | VP Store Operations | 1 day/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Competitive expansion tracker with geolocation mapping (W1912.2a)
- Impact assessment model linking competitor proximity to BuildRight store sales (W1912.2b)
- Integration with W1652 (site selection), W702 (new store opening), W67 (store performance)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Late detection**: Competitor stores in provincial areas may open before BuildRight detects them; local Store Manager and trade account network provides best early warning


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Market Research Analyst monitors competitor expansion" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-detect of "detecting competitor new store in BuildRight's trade area" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-distribute of "Monthly: Market Research Analyst distributes competitive expansion summary" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-374 (ensure controlled execution — Market & Competitive Intelligence (PA-44.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by Market Research Analyst

### Time Estimate
- Ongoing monitoring: ~2 hours/week
- Per new competitor store: ~4–8 hours
- Monthly summary: 1 day
- Total: ~80–120 hours/year

## W1914. Industry Trend & Macro-Economic Impact Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly economic review; major macro event |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly analysis; ad-hoc for major events |
| **Volume** | Philippine economic indicators relevant to home improvement retail |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, VP Merchandising, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FP&A monitors macro-economic indicators: (a) GDP growth rate (PSA) — construction sector GDP specifically, (b) inflation rate (PSA CPI) — impact on consumer purchasing power and BuildRight's cost structure, (c) interest rates (BSP policy rate) — impact on consumer financing (W38) and BuildRight's borrowing costs, (d) exchange rate (PHP/USD, PHP/CNY) — impact on import costs (W2B), (e) OFW remittance growth (BSP) — key driver for Philippine home construction spending, (f) construction permits issued (PSA) — leading indicator for home improvement demand, (g) government infrastructure spending (DPWH) — drives contractor demand for BuildRight products, (h) typhoon frequency and severity (PAGASA) — drives demand for repair and reconstruction materials | FP&A | CFO | Ongoing (2–3 hours/week) |
| 2 | Quarterly: FP&A prepares economic impact assessment: (a) which economic trends will most impact BuildRight in the next 6–12 months?, (b) impact on sales: positive (e.g., strong construction growth → higher contractor demand) or negative (e.g., inflation reducing consumer DIY spending), (c) impact on costs: FX impact on imports, wage inflation, freight cost trends, (d) recommendations: adjust buying strategy, pricing, or expansion plans based on economic outlook | FP&A | CFO | 1 day/quarter |
| 3 | Annual: CFO incorporates economic outlook into strategic planning per W33: (a) 3-year revenue forecast with economic scenarios (base, optimistic, pessimistic), (b) cost projection with FX and inflation assumptions, (c) capital allocation recommendations based on economic cycle positioning | CFO | CEO | Per W33 |

### System Touchpoints
- Economic indicator dashboard with automated data feeds (W1914.1)
- Impact assessment model linking economic indicators to BuildRight's P&L (W1914.2)
- Integration with W33 (strategic planning), W26 (annual budget), W30 (treasury)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Forecast accuracy**: Philippine economic forecasts can be unreliable (typhoon disruption, political changes); use scenario planning rather than single-point forecasts


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitors macro-economic indicators" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-generate of "prepares economic impact assessment" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-forecast of "forecast with economic scenarios" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-374 (ensure controlled execution — Market & Competitive Intelligence (PA-44.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by FP&A

### Time Estimate
- Ongoing monitoring: ~2–3 hours/week
- Quarterly assessment: 1 day/quarter
- Annual strategic input: per W33
- Total: ~25–30 days/year

## W1915. Trade Area Demographic Analysis & Store Location Market Sizing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New store site evaluation (W1652); annual store performance analysis |
| **Frequency** | Per new site evaluation (~10–15/year); annual refresh for existing stores |
| **Volume** | Per store location; 5–10 km trade area analysis |
| **Owner** | Market Research Analyst |
| **Participants** | Market Research Analyst, COO, FP&A |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For new store site: Market Research Analyst conducts trade area analysis: (a) population within 5 km and 10 km radius (PSA census data + projected growth), (b) household income distribution: % of households in middle-income bracket (primary BuildRight customer), (c) housing characteristics: % of owner-occupied homes (homeowners = BuildRight customers), average home age (older homes = more renovation spending), (d) construction activity: building permits issued in the area (past 3 years trend), (e) competitor presence: distance to nearest CitiHardware, Wilcon, ACE, or independent hardware stores, (f) accessibility: road network, public transport, visibility from main road, (g) estimated addressable market: population × average home improvement spend per household × market share target | Market Research Analyst | COO | 2–3 days/site |
| 2 | For existing stores: annual trade area refresh: (a) update population and income data (new census or PSA projections), (b) assess competitive changes: new competitor stores in trade area (W1912), (c) store sales vs. market potential: is the store capturing its fair share of the trade area?, (d) identify underperforming stores where market potential is higher than actual sales (opportunity for improvement) | Market Research Analyst | VP Store Operations | 1 day/store (rotated; ~50 stores/year) |
| 3 | COO and FP&A use trade area analysis for: (a) new store go/no-go decision per W1652, (b) store performance expectation setting, (c) store closure evaluation per W703 (is the trade area deteriorating?) | COO / FP&A | COO | Per site |

### System Touchpoints
- GIS (Geographic Information System) mapping tool with demographic data layers (W1915.1)
- Trade area market potential model (W1915.1g)
- Store sales vs. market potential comparison analytics (W1915.2c)
- Integration with W1652 (site selection), W702 (new store opening), W703 (store closure)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Data granularity**: Philippine census data may be outdated (census every 5–10 years); supplement with local government projections and field observations


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-issue of "issued in the area" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-update of "update population and income data" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-374 (ensure controlled execution — Market & Competitive Intelligence (PA-44.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by Market Research Analyst

### Time Estimate
- New site analysis: 2–3 days/site × 10–15 = ~30–45 days/year
- Existing store refresh: ~50 stores × 1 day = ~50 days/year
- Total: ~80–95 days/year

## W1916. Supplier Market Intelligence & Raw Material Price Trend Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly commodity price review; significant price movement alert |
| **Frequency** | Monthly review; ad-hoc for significant movements |
| **Volume** | Key commodities: steel, cement, copper, lumber, paint resins, plastic resins |
| **Owner** | Buyer |
| **Participants** | Buyer, Category Manager, FP&A, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buyer monitors key commodity prices: (a) steel rebar and flat steel (Philippine domestic price + China export price), (b) cement (Philippine domestic — Holcim, Republic, others), (c) copper wire (LME + import premium), (d) lumber (Philippine domestic + import), (e) paint raw materials (titanium dioxide, resins, solvents — global benchmark), (f) plastic resins (polypropylene, PVC — regional benchmark), (g) freight rates (container rates Shanghai-Manila) | Buyer | Category Manager | 1–2 hours/week |
| 2 | Monthly: Buyer and Category Manager assess commodity impact: (a) which commodity prices have moved > 5% in the past month?, (b) impact on BuildRight's COGS: which categories are most affected?, (c) impact on vendor pricing: are vendors passing through cost increases? Are there opportunities to negotiate better terms when commodity prices decline?, (d) forward outlook: are prices expected to continue rising or declining?, (e) action: (i) if prices rising: accelerate purchases to buy before further increase, negotiate price holds with vendors, evaluate retail price increase per W40, (ii) if prices declining: delay purchases, negotiate vendor price reductions, consider promotional pricing to drive volume | Buyer / Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 day/month |
| 3 | Quarterly: FP&A incorporates commodity price trends into cost forecast: (a) projected COGS impact for next quarter, (b) margin impact and pricing action needed, (c) FX + commodity combined impact on import costs | FP&A | CFO | 1 day/quarter |

### System Touchpoints
- Commodity price tracking dashboard with historical data (W1916.1)
- Cost impact model by category (W1916.2b)
- Integration with W2B (import PO), W40 (price management), W85 (product costing), W1833 (private label cost modeling)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Commodity volatility**: Steel and copper prices can move 10–20% in a quarter, significantly impacting margin; consider forward purchasing or commodity hedging for major categories


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-monitor of "monitors key commodity prices" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-import of "import costs" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-374 (ensure controlled execution — Market & Competitive Intelligence (PA-44.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Staffing Implication
- No incremental headcount — absorbed by Buyers and Category Managers

### Time Estimate
- Monitoring: ~2 hours/week
- Monthly assessment: 1 day/month
- Quarterly forecast: 1 day/quarter
- Total: ~25–30 days/year

## W1917. Competitive Intelligence Report & Strategic Decision Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly reporting cycle; strategic decision support request |
| **Frequency** | Monthly competitive intelligence report; ad-hoc for strategic decisions |
| **Volume** | Comprehensive monthly report covering all competitive intelligence |
| **Owner** | Market Research Analyst |
| **Participants** | Market Research Analyst, CMO, VP Merchandising, VP Store Operations, FP&A |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monthly: Market Research Analyst compiles competitive intelligence report: (a) competitor store openings and closings, (b) competitor promotional activity summary, (c) price benchmarking summary (key SKUs vs. competitors), (d) digital commerce competitive summary, (e) product assortment news (new products, discontinued products), (f) competitor financial performance (Wilcon Depot quarterly results — publicly listed), (g) industry news: trade association updates, regulatory changes, construction industry trends, (h) social media sentiment: BuildRight vs. competitors | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 2 days/month |
| 2 | Distribute to leadership: (a) VP Merchandising: pricing and assortment implications, (b) VP Store Operations: store experience and competitive positioning, (c) CMO: marketing and digital implications, (d) CFO/FP&A: financial impact assessment, (e) COO: expansion/contraction implications | Market Research Analyst | CMO | 1 day/month |
| 3 | Ad-hoc: Market Research Analyst supports strategic decisions with competitive intelligence: (a) new market entry: competitive landscape assessment for BuildRight expansion cities, (b) category entry: competitive analysis for new product categories BuildRight is considering, (c) pricing strategy: competitive price positioning for specific categories, (d) acquisition evaluation: if BuildRight considers acquiring smaller competitor | Market Research Analyst | VP Merchandising | Per request |

### System Touchpoints
- Competitive intelligence database with structured data from all monitoring activities (W1917.1)
- Monthly report generation from aggregated competitive data (W1917.1)
- Ad-hoc query capability for strategic decision support (W1917.3)
- Integration with W1910–W1916 (all competitive intelligence workflows), W33 (strategic planning), W1652 (site selection)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Information overload**: Monthly competitive reports can become "shelf reports" if not actionable; focus on key insights and recommendations rather than raw data


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-compile of "compiles competitive intelligence report" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-distribute of "Distribute to leadership" (replaces manual Step 2).

### Controls
- CTL-190 (protect research-participant data & insight validity.)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Staffing Implication
- **Market Research Analyst**: Competitive intelligence reporting adds ~3 days/month — absorbed by dedicated analyst role

### Time Estimate
- Monthly report: 2 days/month
- Distribution and discussion: 1 day/month
- Ad-hoc support: variable (~5–10 days/year)
- Total: ~50–60 days/year
