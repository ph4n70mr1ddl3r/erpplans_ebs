## W3017. Inbound Receiving Damage & Shortage Identification & Documentation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | DC/store receiving discrepancy vs. ASN/PO |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (~5–8% of receipts have some discrepancy) |
| **Volume** | ~72,000 inbound receipts/year |
| **Owner** | DC Receiving Supervisor |
| **Participants** | DC Receiving, QC, Inventory Control, Vendor Mgmt |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receiving Clerk inspects inbound delivery against PO/ASN; identifies visible damage, water damage, crushed packaging, or quantity shortage at goods receipt (links to VS-04) | Receiving Clerk | DC Receiving Supervisor | Per receipt |
| 2 | Receiving Clerk/QC documents damage: photos, quantities damaged vs. received, carrier/seal condition, and discrepancy code in ERP goods-receipt exception | Receiving Clerk / QC | DC Receiving Supervisor | Per discrepancy |
| 3 | Inventory Control/Vendor Mgmt route discrepancy to claim (PA-90.2) or RTV (links to VS-32); place damaged units in damage quarantine pending disposition | Inventory Control / Vendor Mgmt | DC Operations Manager | Per discrepancy |

### System Touchpoints
- Goods receipt exception (links to VS-04); damage quarantine bin; discrepancy/claim case

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Evidence risk**: Damage not documented at receipt forfeits claim rights; mitigated by mandatory photo + discrepancy capture at goods receipt


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-inspect of "inspects inbound delivery against PO/ASN" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "Receiving Clerk/QC documents damage" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-route of "route discrepancy to claim" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-499 (ensure controlled execution — Damage Identification, Documentation & Disposition (PA-90.1).)
- operational: mandatory photo + discrepancy capture at goods receipt

### Time Estimate
- 5–15 min per discrepancy

## W3018. In-Transit & DC Damage Identification, Quarantine & Classification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Damage found at DC putaway, picking, or in-transit audit |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | Across 4 DCs handling ~72,000 receipts + outbound |
| **Owner** | DC Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | DC Ops, QC, Inventory Control, LP |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DC staff identify in-house damage (putaway/pick handling, racking, contamination, pest, water) during cycle counts or pick; log to damage case | DC Staff / QC | DC Operations Manager | Per event |
| 2 | QC classifies damage cause: carrier-induced, vendor packaging, DC handling, environmental, or indeterminate; assigns liability indicator for claim routing | QC | DC Operations Manager | Per event |
| 3 | LP investigates patterns suggesting theft/concealment disguised as damage; secures evidence; escalates to VS-23 where fraud suspected | Loss Prevention | LP Manager | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Damage case; cycle count variance (links to VS-05); LP case linkage (links to VS-23)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mis-attribution risk**: DC handling damage wrongly charged to vendor/carrier; mitigated by cause classification and LP review


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "log to damage case" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-assign of "assigns liability indicator for claim routing" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-escalate of "escalates to VS-23 where fraud suspected" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-499 (ensure controlled execution — Damage Identification, Documentation & Disposition (PA-90.1).)
- operational: cause classification and LP review

### Time Estimate
- Per event

## W3019. Store-Level Damaged-on-Arrival (DOA) & In-Store Damage Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store receives damaged goods or in-store damage occurs |
| **Frequency** | Continuous across 200 stores |
| **Volume** | 200 stores; ~50,000 replenishment orders/month chain-wide (production-calibrated per data-volumes §1.1) + DSD |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Mgr, Receiving Clerk, Stock Associate, Customer Service |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receiving Clerk/Stock Associate identifies DOA at store receiving or in-store damage (customer handling, drop, spill, weather in lumber yard); logs damage case with photos | Receiving Clerk / Stock Associate | Store Manager | Per event |
| 2 | Store Mgr classifies cause (carrier transit, DC pick error, in-store handling, customer-caused); routes to claim, RTV, or write-off per disposition matrix | Store Manager | Store Manager | Per event |
| 3 | Customer Service handles customer-caused damage (broken in-aisle) per store policy; coordinates goodwill/replacement where applicable (links to W3035) | Customer Service | Store Manager | Per event |

### System Touchpoints
- Store damage case; goods receipt exception (links to VS-07); disposition matrix

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Under-reporting risk**: In-store damage written off silently without recovery attempt; mitigated by mandatory damage-case logging


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs damage case with photos" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-route of "routes to claim, RTV, or write-off per disposition matrix" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System workflow orchestration of "coordinates goodwill/replacement where applicable" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-75 (detect and recover freight & receiving damage.)
- operational: mandatory damage-case logging

### Time Estimate
- 5–10 min per event

## W3022. Damaged Inventory Quarantine, Write-Off & GL Posting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Damage dispositioned for scrap/write-off |
| **Frequency** | Continuous + month-end |
| **Volume** | Enterprise damage write-offs (target <1.5% shrinkage) |
| **Owner** | Finance Manager |
| **Participants** | Finance, Inventory Control, DC/Store Mgr, Internal Audit |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inventory Control moves dispositioned stock to write-off status; secures in quarantine pending destruction/RTV; prevents re-sale | Inventory Control | Inventory Control Manager | Per event |
| 2 | Finance posts inventory write-off to correct GL account (damage/loss vs. COGS adjustment); captures cost, liability indicator, and pending-recovery flag | Finance | Finance Manager | Per event/batch |

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "Finance posts inventory write-off to correct GL account" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System continuous audit of "Audit samples write-offs for control compliance" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-499 (ensure controlled execution — Damage Identification, Documentation & Disposition (PA-90.1).)
- operational: approval matrix and Internal Audit sampling

### Time Estimate
- Per event; batch at month-end

| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Subset of damage; hazardous categories ~8% of SKUs |
| **Owner** | HSE Manager |
| **Participants** | EHS, Quality, DC/Store Ops, Licensed Hazmat Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DC/Store staff isolate damaged hazardous goods in compliant hazmat storage; reference SDS (links to W1019); prevent spill/contamination exposure | DC / Store Staff | HSE Manager | Per event (<immediate) |

### Automation Opportunity

### Controls
