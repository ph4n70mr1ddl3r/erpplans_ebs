## Overview

Damage, Claims & Freight Recovery Management workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. — governing
the identification, documentation, disposition, and financial recovery of damaged, short-shipped,
or non-conforming goods across the inbound (vendor → DC, vendor → store DSD), inter-DC and DC →
store, and outbound (store/DC → customer and job site) legs of the supply chain. Covers ~72,000
inbound receipts/year, ~50,000 store replenishment orders/month (production-calibrated), ~42,900 ecommerce orders/month,
and ~515,000 ecommerce orders/year, against ~800–1,000 vendors, mixed owned/3PL fleet, and
delivery partners.

While **VS-04** owns DC receiving and **VS-32** owns returns/reverse logistics, and typhoon
damage is handled under **VS-69**, this value stream owns the **damage and claims management
program**: damage identification and disposition decisioning, vendor/carrier/freight claim filing
and settlement, customer damage-claim handling, subrogation/insurance recovery, and enterprise
damage cost analytics and vendor/carrier recovery scorecarding — across all five legal entities.
