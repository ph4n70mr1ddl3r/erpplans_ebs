## W3033. Customer Delivery Damage & Shortage Claim Intake

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer reports damaged/short delivery |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | ~42,900 ecommerce orders/month + B2B/trade deliveries |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Manager |
| **Participants** | Customer Service, Ecommerce, Logistics, Store Ops |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Service logs damage/shortage claim from customer (phone/chat/email/in-store): order, SKU, qty, photo, delivery date, carrier | Customer Service | Customer Service Manager | Per claim (<24 hr) |
| 2 | Customer Service/Ecommerce verify against order, delivery POD, and fulfillment source (store/DC/3PL); determines responsibility indicator | Customer Service / Ecommerce | Customer Service Manager | Per claim |
| 3 | Customer Service routes claim: goodwill replacement/refund (W3035), investigation (W3034), or denial with explanation; communicates to customer | Customer Service | Customer Service Manager | Per claim |

### System Touchpoints
- Customer claim case (links to VS-13); order/POD lookup (links to VS-10/VS-06); ecommerce messaging

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Customer-experience risk**: Slow claim handling damages loyalty; mitigated by 24-hr SLA and goodwill authority tiers


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs damage/shortage claim from customer" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "verify against order, delivery POD, and fulfillment source" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-route of "Customer Service routes claim" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-501 (ensure controlled execution — Customer Damage/Shortage Claims & Recovery Analytics (PA-90.3).)
- operational: 24-hr SLA and goodwill authority tiers

### Time Estimate
- 5–15 min per claim

## W3036. Damage Claim Refund, Credit Note & Financial Reconciliation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Claim resolved with refund/credit |
| **Frequency** | Continuous |
| **Volume** | Enterprise resolved claims |
| **Owner** | Finance Manager (AR) |
| **Participants** | Finance/AR, Customer Service, AP |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance/AR processes refund via original tender or BIR-compliant credit note (links to W101); posts to customer account or one-off refund | Finance / AR | Finance Manager | Per claim |
| 2 | Finance allocates cost to damage case with liability flag; offsets recovered amounts from vendor/carrier/insurer subrogation | Finance | Finance Manager | Per claim |
| 3 | Finance reconciles claim P&L impact (net of recovery) at month-end; reports unrecovered damage cost to management | Finance | Finance Manager | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Refund/credit note (links to W101); AR posting (links to W8); damage case cost ledger

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Net-cost risk**: Customer refunds posted without pursuing vendor/carrier recovery inflate damage cost; mitigated by liability flag and subrogation follow-through (W3038)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-count of "account or one-off refund" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-allocate of "allocates cost to damage case with liability flag" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconciles claim P&L impact" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-75 (detect and recover freight & receiving damage.)
- operational: liability flag and subrogation follow-through (W3038)

### Time Estimate
- Per claim; batch monthly

### Steps

- System auto-log of "Logistics file vendor claim" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "reports net damage cost" (replaces manual Step 3).
