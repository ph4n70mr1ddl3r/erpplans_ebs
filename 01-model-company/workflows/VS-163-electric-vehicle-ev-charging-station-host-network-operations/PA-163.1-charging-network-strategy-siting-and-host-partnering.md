## W4769. EV Charging Host-Network Strategy, Business Model & Roadmap

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program launch; annual strategy review; EV adoption inflection; RA 11697 IRR update |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on launch |
| **Volume** | 1 group strategy; ~2–6 charge points/site → ~400–1,200 ports across ~205 sites |
| **Owner** | COO / EV Charging Network Program Manager |
| **Participants** | Real Estate (VS-97), Energy (VS-108/VS-120), Finance (VS-17.4), ESG (VS-25), Marketing (VS-14) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Set the network strategy & business model: own-operate (BuildRight buys EVSE, sells energy, takes all revenue/risk) vs host-partner (a CPO installs/operates EVSE on BuildRight sites, BuildRight takes lease/host-revenue + traffic) vs hybrid; mix by site economics and EV adoption stage; phase the rollout (Metro Manila/NCR first where EV density is highest per profile store distribution, then CALABARZON, Cebu, Davao) | EV Program Manager / Finance / Real Estate | COO | Annual |
| 2 | Build the strategic case: EV adoption forecast under RA 11697 (fleet EV mandates, EV incentive scheme), dwell-time basket uplift (EV driver shops 20–45 min while charging), own-solar/storage energy arbitrage (VS-108), decarbonization/ESG contribution (VS-25), property-asset future-proofing, and the host vs own-operate unit economics; define success KPIs (ports, utilization, host-revenue, basket-uplift, tCO₂e enabled) | EV Program Manager / Finance / ESG | COO / CFO | Annual |
| 3 | Approve the multi-year roadmap, capex envelope (W4776), and the phase-gate review cadence; integrate into corporate strategy (VS-33), capex (VS-40/VS-112), and ESG plan (VS-25); sequence with the store-remodel program (VS-109) to embed charging in remodels | EV Program Manager / PMO / Strategy | COO / CFO / Board (material capex) | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- FP&A/strategy (VS-33/VS-17.4); capex (VS-40/VS-112); real-estate portfolio (VS-97); energy (VS-108/VS-120); ESG (VS-25)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Premature-build risk**: Over-building ahead of EV adoption → stranded/under-utilized capex; mitigated by phased rollout starting in highest-EV-density regions
- **Wrong-model risk**: Own-operating where host-partner is more economic (or vice-versa); mitigated by the per-site unit-economics model
- **Strategic-invisibility risk**: Program cut if basket-uplift/decarbonization not measured; mitigated by the success-KPI set

### Automation Opportunity
- EV-adoption/demand forecasting model; host-vs-own-operate decision support; basket-uplift measurement (links POS VS-08/VS-10)

### Controls
- CTL-719 (ensure controlled execution — Charging Network Strategy, Siting & Host Partnering (PA-163.1).)
- CTL-719: annual strategy + business case reviewed by CFO/COO; phased rollout gated on adoption/utilization KPIs; capex via VS-40 gate

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual strategy; ~phase-gate reviews quarterly

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-40/VS-112 (capex/PMO), VS-97 (real estate), VS-108/VS-120 (energy), VS-25 (ESG), VS-109 (remodel integration); sets inputs for W4770–W4776

| **Volume** | ~205 candidate sites; ~prioritized subset per phase |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Model EV-charging demand per site: regional EV density (NCR/CALABARZON/Cebu/Davao first), store traffic, dwell-time/basket potential, proximity to highways/destinations, competitor/host-network gaps, and BuildRight's own EV-fleet charging need (VS-06/VS-61 green-fleet transition) | Analytics / Real Estate / EV Program | EV Program Manager | Per phase |
| 2 | Select and prioritize sites: rank by demand model + basket-uplift + grid/solar feasibility (VS-108) + capex; pair each charging site with the store's basket strategy (a high-lumber-yard store charges while loading a pickup W4758; an appliance/furniture store captures longer dwell); sequence into the capex plan (W4776) | EV Program / Real Estate / Merchandising | COO | Per phase |

| 2 | Build the unit economics: host-revenue vs cost (lease foregone on the parking bay, energy, demand-charge, maintenance, insurance, capex amortization), basket-uplift attribution, and the break-even utilization; define the revenue-share/lease terms in the CPO contract (W4770) and the tariff (W4775) | Finance / EV Program | COO / CFO | Annual |

|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Own-operate model (W4769); energy-cost change; demand-charge review |
| **Frequency** | Periodic review + on energy-cost change |
| **Volume** | 1 tariff structure (own-operate sites) |
| **Owner** | EV Program Manager / Energy (VS-120) / Finance |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17), Treasury (VS-18), Energy (VS-108/VS-120), CPO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design the charging tariff: per-kWh energy price (covering utility energy + demand-charge + EVSE amortization + margin), time-of-use pricing (peak/off-peak to shape demand), idle/occupancy fee (to free bays after charge-complete), and loyalty-redemption integration (VS-13); benchmark against the CPO/host-network market | Finance / Energy / EV Program | COO / CFO | Periodic |
| 2 | Manage demand-charges and peak load: use BuildRight's own solar/storage (VS-108) for peak-shaving, smart-load-management (throttle charge-rate during site peak), scheduled charging, and buffer storage to avoid demand-charge triggers; coordinate with the RA 11285 energy program (VS-120) | Energy / EV Program / Facilities | EV Program Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | Govern energy cost recovery and margin: track utility kWh + demand-charge vs charging-revenue, manage FX on imported EVSE/spares, and re-price the tariff on energy-cost movement; feed tariff/margin performance to analytics (W4792) | Finance / Treasury / Energy | CFO | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Energy/RA 11285 (VS-120); solar/storage (VS-108); treasury/FX (VS-18); finance (VS-17); analytics (W4792); CPO settlement (VS-80)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Demand-charge-erosion risk**: Peak charging triggering demand-charges that erode margin; mitigated by peak-shaving via solar/storage (VS-108)
- **Mis-priced-tariff risk**: Tariff below cost-recovery → loss; too high → low utilization; mitigated by periodic re-pricing and benchmarking
- **Idle-bay risk**: Cars occupying bays after charge-complete → blocked revenue; mitigated by the idle/occupancy fee

### Automation Opportunity
- Smart-load-management (links VS-108 EMS); demand-charge-prediction; auto idle-fee billing; tariff-margin dashboard (W4792)

### Controls
- CTL-719 (ensure controlled execution — Charging Network Strategy, Siting & Host Partnering (PA-163.1).)
- CTL-719: tariff covers cost-recovery + margin; demand-charge managed via solar/storage; idle-fee enforced; re-priced on energy-cost change
