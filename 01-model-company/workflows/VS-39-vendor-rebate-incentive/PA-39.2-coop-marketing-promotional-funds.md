## W1795. Co-Op Advertising Fund Agreement & Budget Allocation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual vendor negotiation; new vendor onboarding |
| **Frequency** | ~50–80 co-op agreements negotiated/year |
| **Volume** | ~50–80 active co-op marketing agreements |
| **Owner** | CMO |
| **Participants** | CMO, Category Managers, VP Merchandising, Vendor Marketing |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CMO and VP Merchandising negotiate co-op advertising fund agreements with key vendors: (a) fund structure: % of purchases (typically 1–3%) or fixed annual budget, (b) eligible activities: print catalogs, social media ads, in-store displays, events, TV/radio, digital banners, (c) approval process: BuildRight proposes activity → vendor approves → execute → submit proof → vendor reimburses, (d) pre-approval: some vendors require pre-approval of all activities; others allow post-execution claim, (e) fund cap: maximum annual co-op spend per vendor, (f) carry-over: unused funds expire or roll to next year (vendor-specific) | CMO / VP Merchandising | CFO | Per agreement |
| 2 | System setup: co-op marketing fund per vendor: (a) annual budget/fund amount, (b) approved activity types, (c) pre-approval requirement flag, (d) reimbursement rate (% of cost covered, typically 50–100%), (e) documentation requirements | Marketing / System | CMO | 30 min/agreement |
| 3 | CMO allocates co-op budget across marketing calendar: (a) bi-monthly catalog: vendor co-op for product feature space (full page: PHP 50–100K; half page: PHP 25–50K), (b) social media campaigns: vendor co-op for sponsored posts, (c) in-store displays: vendor co-op for endcap and feature display materials, (d) events: vendor co-op for workshop sponsorship per VS-12.3, (e) seasonal campaigns: vendor co-op for holiday promotions | CMO | VP Merchandising | Quarterly planning |


### System Touchpoints
- Co-op fund agreement setup — fund as % of purchases, approved activity types, pre-approval rules (steps 1–2)
- Co-op budget allocation across the marketing calendar (step 3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Use-it-or-lose-it risk**: fund balances expire unspent for lack of planned activity; mitigated by calendar-based allocation at agreement setup (step 3)
- **Non-qualifying spend risk**: activities charged to the fund that the agreement disallows; mitigated by approved-activity-type setup with pre-approval rules (step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "print catalogs, social media ads, in-store displays, events, TV/radio, digital…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "documentation requirements" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-log of "CMO allocates co-op budget across marketing calendar" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-359 (ensure controlled execution — Co-Op Marketing & Promotional Fund Management (PA-39.2).)
- operational: CMO review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
Per co-op agreement: negotiation within merchandising cycles + system setup 30 min ≈ 0.5–1 day of effort × ~50–80 agreements/year ≈ 25–80 days/year; quarterly co-op budget allocation across the marketing calendar.

## W1796. Vendor-Funded Promotional Activity Planning

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Promotional calendar cycle (6 major events/year) |
| **Frequency** | ~6–10 promotional plans/year |
| **Volume** | ~30–50 vendor-funded promotional activities per cycle |
| **Owner** | CMO |
| **Participants** | CMO, Category Managers, VP Merchandising, key vendors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For each promotional event: CMO and Category Managers identify vendor-funded promotional opportunities: (a) which vendors will participate in the catalog feature, (b) which vendors will fund endcap displays, (c) which vendors will provide promotional pricing (vendor absorbs markdown cost), (d) which vendors will contribute to digital marketing campaign, (e) target: vendor funding covers 30–50% of total promotional marketing cost | CMO / Category Managers | VP Merchandising | 1–2 weeks/cycle |
| 2 | Category Managers contact vendors with promotional proposals: (a) catalog placement offer (with pricing), (b) endcap display opportunity (with expected traffic and sales lift), (c) digital advertising package (with audience reach), (d) vendor responds with commitment and budget | Category Managers | CMO | 1–2 weeks |
| 3 | CMO compiles vendor commitments into promotional plan: (a) total vendor funding committed, (b) BuildRight's own marketing spend, (c) combined promotional plan with activity schedule, (d) ROI projection: expected sales lift vs. total promotional cost | CMO | VP Merchandising | 1 week |


### System Touchpoints
- Vendor-funded promotional opportunity identification per event (step 1)
- Vendor commitment solicitation — catalog placement, endcaps, features (step 2)
- Promotional plan compilation — committed vendor funding vs. BuildRight spend (step 3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Overpromised-funding risk**: plans built on verbal vendor interest that evaporates; mitigated by documented commitments in the plan (steps 2–3)
- **Vendor-fatigue risk**: the same vendors fund every event and disengage; mitigated by rotating funding targets across the vendor base (step 1)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "For each promotional event" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System workflow notification of "contact vendors with promotional proposals" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-compile of "compiles vendor commitments into promotional plan" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-359 (ensure controlled execution — Co-Op Marketing & Promotional Fund Management (PA-39.2).)
- operational: standard operating procedures; system-enforced validation rules

### Time Estimate
2–3 weeks elapsed per occurrence (1–2 weeks + 1 week); cadence-carried steps as authored (1–2 weeks/cycle) — at ~6–10 promotional plans/year ≈ 6–10 occurrences/year.

| **Volume** | 1 proof-of-performance package per activity per vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## W1799. Vendor Co-Op Fund Reimbursement Claim & Settlement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | POP documentation submitted to vendor (W1798) |
| **Frequency** | ~50–100 claims/year |
| **Volume** | ~PHP 50–100M in annual co-op fund reimbursements |
| **Owner** | AP Manager |
| **Participants** | AP Manager, Marketing Team, Vendor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vendor reviews POP and approves/denies reimbursement: (a) typical review period: 30–60 days, (b) approval: vendor issues credit note or payment, (c) partial approval: vendor disputes portion of claim (documentation insufficient, expense not eligible, execution not per agreement), (d) denial: vendor rejects claim with reason | Vendor | — | 30–60 days |
| 2 | AP Manager processes approved reimbursement: (a) credit note: applied to vendor AP balance (reduces next payment to vendor), (b) cash payment: received in bank account; Treasury confirms and applies, (c) system records co-op fund income: debit cash/AP, credit co-op marketing income (contra-marketing expense) | AP Manager | Accounting Manager | Per settlement |
| 3 | Dispute resolution: (a) if vendor partially denies: Marketing Team provides additional documentation or accepts denial, (b) if vendor fully denies: escalate to Category Manager for vendor relationship discussion, (c) unresolved disputes > 90 days: written off; co-op fund income reduced accordingly | AP Manager / Marketing Team | CMO | As needed |
| 4 | Quarterly: AP Manager reconciles co-op fund activity per vendor: (a) annual budget, (b) claimed amount, (c) approved and received, (d) pending, (e) denied, (f) remaining fund balance available | AP Manager | CFO | 1 day/quarter |


### System Touchpoints
- Vendor reimbursement approval/denial intake (30–60 day cycle) (step 1)
- Approved reimbursement processing — credit note to AP or cash receipt (step 2)
- Dispute resolution with additional documentation (step 3)
- Quarterly per-vendor fund reconciliation — budget, claimed, approved, received (step 4)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Partial-denial drift**: short reimbursements accepted silently, eroding fund value; mitigated by dispute resolution with documentation (step 3)
- **Fund-balance-blindness risk**: remaining balances and unapproved claims lose visibility; mitigated by the quarterly per-vendor reconciliation (step 4)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "documentation insufficient, expense not eligible, execution not per agreement" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "records co-op fund income" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-escalate of "escalate to Category Manager for vendor relationship discussion" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-359 (ensure controlled execution — Co-Op Marketing & Promotional Fund Management (PA-39.2).)
- operational: AP Manager review and approval gate; periodic reconciliation against source documents

### Time Estimate
4.3–8.6 weeks elapsed per occurrence (30–60 days); cadence-carried steps as authored (Per settlement, As needed, 1 day/quarter) — at ~50–100 claims/year ≈ 50–100 occurrences/year.

## W1800. In-Store Vendor Display & Endcap Program Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly/quarterly display rotation schedule |
| **Frequency** | Monthly display rotations |
| **Volume** | ~200 stores × ~10–15 paid display locations = 2,000–3,000 vendor-funded displays active at any time |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising, Category Managers, Store Managers, Vendors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Merchandising manages paid display program: (a) premium display locations identified per store: endcaps, feature tables, entrance displays, power aisle stacks, (b) pricing per display location per month (e.g., endcap: PHP 10–20K/month; entrance feature: PHP 30–50K/month), (c) vendors pay for premium placement via co-op fund, (d) display schedule rotated monthly or per promotional cycle | Category Managers | VP Merchandising | Monthly planning |
| 2 | Store execution: (a) Store Manager receives display plan from merchandising, (b) sets up display per planogram and vendor-provided materials, (c) sends photo confirmation to Category Manager, (d) non-compliant stores flagged and corrected | Store Manager | Category Manager | 1–2 days per rotation |
| 3 | Performance tracking: Category Manager reviews display performance (sales lift for displayed items vs. baseline); high-performing displays extended; underperforming displays rotated | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | Monthly |


### System Touchpoints
- Paid display program — premium locations (endcaps, feature tables, entrances) per store (step 1)
- Store display execution per planogram with vendor-provided materials (step 2)
- Display performance tracking — sales lift vs. baseline (step 3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Paid-slot-underperformance risk**: premium slots sold to vendors without delivering sales lift, souring renewals; mitigated by performance tracking informing next rotations (step 3)
- **Execution-decay risk**: displays degrade mid-program; mitigated by store execution standards and rotation discipline (step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-schedule of "schedule rotated monthly or per promotional cycle" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-send of "sends photo confirmation to Category Manager" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "reviews display performance" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-359 (ensure controlled execution — Co-Op Marketing & Promotional Fund Management (PA-39.2).)
- operational: VP Merchandising review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
Monthly display rotation: store setup labor ~1–2 days per store across its 10–15 paid display locations ≈ 8–16 hours/store/month ≈ 1,600–3,200 hours/month chain-wide at 200 stores — standing merchandising labor on 2,000–3,000 active vendor-funded displays; program planning and monthly performance tracking ≈ 2–3 days/month.

|---|---|---|---|---|

- CTL-359 (ensure controlled execution — Co-Op Marketing & Promotional Fund Management (PA-39.2).)

### Time Estimate
~2–4 days per vendor-funded digital campaign (proposal + vendor approval + launch management + co-op claim documentation) × ~10–20 campaigns/year ≈ 20–80 days/year on ~PHP 10–20M vendor funding.

## W1802. Co-Op Marketing ROI Analysis & Vendor Reporting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Post-campaign analysis; quarterly vendor business review |
| **Frequency** | Per campaign + quarterly aggregate |
| **Volume** | ~50–100 campaign analyses/year |
| **Owner** | FP&A |
| **Participants** | FP&A, CMO, Category Managers, VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Per-campaign ROI analysis: (a) incremental sales during campaign period (vs. baseline), (b) incremental gross margin (net of promotional markdown and BuildRight's marketing spend), (c) vendor co-op funding received (or expected), (d) net cost to BuildRight = marketing spend − vendor funding, (e) ROI = incremental margin ÷ net cost, (f) campaign effectiveness metrics: reach, conversion rate, ATV lift | FP&A / Marketing Analytics | CMO | 1–2 days/campaign |
| 2 | Quarterly aggregate: (a) total vendor co-op funding received vs. marketing spend, (b) co-op as % of total marketing budget, (c) ROI by vendor and by campaign type (catalog, digital, in-store display), (d) vendor ranking: highest co-op contributors and best ROI performers | FP&A | CFO / CMO | 1 day/quarter |
| 3 | Vendor business review: Category Managers present co-op marketing results to key vendors: (a) campaign performance, (b) sales growth attributable to co-op activities, (c) proposal for next period co-op budget (increase for high-ROI activities), (d) strengthen vendor partnership based on mutual benefit | Category Managers | VP Merchandising | Quarterly per key vendor |


### System Touchpoints
- Per-campaign ROI analysis — incremental sales/margin vs. baseline (step 1)
- Quarterly aggregate — co-op funding received vs. marketing spend (step 2)
- Vendor business review presentation of co-op results (step 3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vanity-metric risk**: campaigns judged on impressions rather than incremental margin; mitigated by ROI analysis anchored on baseline lift (step 1)
- **Value-undemonstrated risk**: vendors can't see what their funding earned, weakening renewals; mitigated by quarterly vendor business reviews (step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "catalog, digital, in-store display" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "Vendor business review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-359 (ensure controlled execution — Co-Op Marketing & Promotional Fund Management (PA-39.2).)
- operational: FP&A review and approval gate

### Time Estimate
Per-campaign ROI analysis 1–2 days × ~50–100 campaign analyses/year ≈ 50–200 days/year; quarterly aggregate co-op review 1 day ≈ 4 days/year; vendor business reviews quarterly with key vendors.
