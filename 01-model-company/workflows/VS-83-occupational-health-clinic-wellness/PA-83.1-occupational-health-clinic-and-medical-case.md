## W2849. Company Clinic Operations & Occupational Health Nurse Coverage

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous clinic operations |
| **Frequency** | Daily |
| **Volume** | 200 stores + 4 DCs + HQ; ~6,925 employees |
| **Owner** | Occupational Health Nurse Manager |
| **Participants** | OH Nurse, Company Physician, HR |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OH Nurse Manager maintains nurse/doctor coverage plan: resident nurse at large DCs/HQ, rotating coverage at regional store clusters per DOLE Clinic Ratio (1 nurse per 200–300 employees) | OH Nurse Manager | CHRO | Annual |
| 2 | OH Nurse operates clinic: walk-in consultations, vital signs, dispensing, emergency first aid, referral; logs every case in occupational health record system | OH Nurse | OH Nurse Manager | Daily |
| 3 | Company Physician conducts scheduled consults and oversees clinical protocols; reviews complex cases; signs medical clearances | Company Physician | OH Nurse Manager | Weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- Occupational health record system; clinic scheduling; medical supply inventory

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: DOLE-mandated clinic coverage gaps; mitigated by ratio-based staffing plan and third-party coverage contracts


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logs every case in occupational health record system" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "reviews complex cases" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-478 (ensure controlled execution — Occupational Health Clinic & Medical Case Management (PA-83.1).)
- operational: ratio-based staffing plan and third-party coverage contracts

### Time Estimate
- Continuous clinic operations

## W2850. Workplace Injury / Illness First Aid & Medical Case Opening

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee injury or illness at work |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven; ~5–15 cases/month |
| **Volume** | All locations |
| **Owner** | OH Nurse |
| **Participants** | OH Nurse, Store/DC Manager, Employee, HSE |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OH Nurse/first-aider provides immediate first aid; stabilizes; transports to network hospital if needed; notifies manager and HSE | OH Nurse | OH Nurse Manager | Immediate |
| 2 | OH Nurse opens medical case in record system: incident description, body part, severity, witness, work-relatedness indicator | OH Nurse | OH Nurse Manager | Per case |
| 3 | HSE links medical case to OHS incident (W140 linkage); coordinates investigation; tracks recovery and case closure | HSE | HSE Manager | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- Occupational health record system; OHS incident linkage; HSE case workflow

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Care risk**: Delayed first aid worsens outcome; mitigated by trained first-aiders at every location


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "OH Nurse opens medical case in record system" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "tracks recovery and case closure" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-216 (ensure occupational-health record & claim compliance.)
- operational: trained first-aiders at every location

### Time Estimate
- 30–60 min per case opening

## W2855. Medical Record Confidentiality, Consent & DOH Recordkeeping

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous; record access or retention event |
| **Frequency** | Continuous governance |
| **Volume** | ~6,925 employee medical records |
| **Owner** | OH Nurse Manager |
| **Participants** | OH Nurse, HR, IT Security, NPC |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OH Nurse maintains medical records under strict confidentiality in secured occupational health record system; separates from general HR file | OH Nurse | OH Nurse Manager | Continuous |
| 2 | IT Security enforces role-based access (only OH staff); logs all access; obtains employee consent for any data sharing (HMO, SSS) per Data Privacy Act | IT Security | OH Nurse Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | OH Nurse Manager retains records per DOH/DOLE requirement (varies); executes secure destruction post-retention; supports NPC data subject requests | OH Nurse Manager | CHRO | Per retention cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Occupational health record system; access control/logging; consent management

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Privacy risk**: Unauthorized medical-record access violates DPA; mitigated by segregation and access logging


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "records under strict confidentiality in secured occupational health record…" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-log of "logs all access" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-post of "OH Nurse Manager retains records per DOH/DOLE requirement" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-216 (ensure occupational-health record & claim compliance.)
- operational: segregation and access logging

### Time Estimate
- Continuous governance
