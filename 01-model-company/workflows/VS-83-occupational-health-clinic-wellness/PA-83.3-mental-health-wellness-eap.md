## W2865. Employee Assistance Program (EAP) Provision & Confidential Counseling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Employee self-referral or manager referral |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~2–5% utilization/year |
| **Volume** | ~6,932 employees + dependents |

| **Participants** | EAP Mgr, EAP Provider, HR, OH Nurse |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

## W5502. Employee Financial Wellness Program — Literacy Education, Salary-Linked Lender Governance & Debt-Stress Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | (a) Program design/review cycle; (b) salary-linked lender onboarding or deduction-authorization request; (c) debt-distress indicators (EAP case mix, deduction-load spikes, post-calamity advance surges) |
| **Frequency** | Education campaigns quarterly; lender-governance review annual; counseling continuous via EAP; deduction operations continuous |
| **Volume** | 6,932 employees; Philippine salary-linked lending context (registered lending companies, digital salary-deduction apps, informal '5-6' exposure) makes deduction-load a chain-wide indicator — anonymized payroll data shows the concurrent-deduction distribution across ~200 stores/4 DCs/HQ; EAP financial-stress cases route from W2865 |
| **Owner** | EAP Manager |
| **Participants** | HR Manager, Benefits (VS-102), Payroll (VS-19.2), Legal, IT/LMS (VS-19.4 training delivery), external financial-education provider, accredited credit counselors, LP/VS-125 (aggregate risk-indicator interface only) |

### Background

'Financial wellness' appears only twice incidentally in PA files (both customer-facing financing contexts), and the catalog owns the *mechanics* but not the *program*: W76 administers company loans/advances, VS-102 designs benefits, and W2865 provides counseling — nobody owns financial literacy education, governance of third-party salary-deduction lenders, or the debt-stress support pathway. In the Philippine retail context, over-indebtedness among frontline staff is a documented driver of attrition, absenteeism, EAP utilization, and shrink/fraud risk (VS-23/VS-125), and post-typhoon periods concentrate financial shock (VS-69.3). Distinct from: W76 (payroll loan mechanic — this workflow governs the ecosystem around it), W2865 (confidential counseling channel it routes into), VS-102 (allowance/benefit design), W747/W953 (customer financing — different population).

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Program design & needs assessment: financial-wellness policy, segment needs (frontline store/DC, corporate, new hires, near-retirement) from anonymized EAP case mix, deduction-load distribution, and engagement survey items; annual plan and budget | EAP Manager / HR | CHRO | 5–8 days/year |
| 2 | Financial literacy education: quarterly modules — budgeting, debt management, saving, SSS/Pag-IBIG/PhilHealth benefits literacy, scam and predatory-lending-app awareness — via OLA with completion records (W51 pattern) plus store huddle kits for frontline reach (VS-63 delivery); BSP-aligned consumer-protection content standards | EAP Manager / L&D / External Provider | CHRO | 8–10 days/quarter (team effort) |
| 3 | Salary-linked lender governance: maintain the accredited-lender list; require SEC registration/lending-company compliance (RA 9474), Truth-in-Lending disclosures (RA 3765), fair-collection attestations, and RA 10173 data-privacy terms (VS-91); annual re-vetting; delisting process for abusive collection practices | Legal / EAP Manager | CHRO | 5–8 days/year + per lender |
| 4 | Deduction operations interface: payroll executes salary-deduction instructions only against a valid employee authorization on file with the accredited lender; process new/modified/revoked instructions with audit trail; remit collections to lenders with reconciliation; separation-time coordination with final pay (W43 pattern) so deductions stop cleanly | Payroll / HR | Payroll Manager | 30–60 min/week |
| 5 | Take-home-pay guardrail: monitor aggregate deduction load per employee; flag cases exceeding the policy guardrail (multiple concurrent lender deductions eroding net pay) for confidential outreach through EAP — never through line management; monitor formal garnishments separately per VS-19.2 statutory process | Payroll / EAP Manager | CHRO | 0.5 day/month |
| 6 | Debt-distress identification & support: referral pathways (self, manager, EAP counselor) into confidential financial-coaching sessions; navigation to W76 company loan/calamity-loan options where appropriate; referral to accredited credit counselors for debt restructuring; substance-abuse and mental-health interlocks where debt stress co-occurs (W2865/W2868/VS-150) | EAP Manager / Counselors | CHRO | Continuous; ~2–4 hrs/case |
| 7 | Crisis overlays: post-typhoon financial-first-aid sessions and calamity-loan awareness in affected regions (VS-69.3); special modules when economic shocks (fuel/food inflation) spike deduction-load indicators | EAP Manager / HR | CHRO | 2–3 days per event |
| 8 | Privacy & safeguard interlocks: wellness data confidential and never used in performance or staffing decisions; only anonymized aggregate indicators shared (e.g., with LP under VS-125/VS-23 governance) and only at group level; DPIA maintained for program data (VS-91) | EAP Manager / DPO-function / Legal | CHRO | 2–3 days/year |
| 9 | Measurement & reporting: participation, EAP financial-case mix, deduction-load trends, W76 utilization, attrition correlation; annual ROI report feeding the wellness analytics cycle (W2872) | EAP Manager / People Analytics | CHRO | 3–4 days/year |

### System Touchpoints
- OLA financial-wellness module with completion tracking (W51-pattern records)
- EAP case management with a confidential financial-stress tag (restricted access, aggregate-only reporting)
- Payroll deduction wage types with an accredited-lender master, authorization document linkage, and expiry/revocation flags
- Deduction-load monitoring report (aggregate and guardrail flags) with confidential routing to EAP
- Survey and analytics extracts feeding W2872 wellness ROI

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Privacy/stigma**: fear that financial trouble becomes an HR record suppresses participation; mitigated by EAP-held confidential data, aggregate-only reporting, and explicit no-performance-use policy
- **Predatory-lender harm**: unregistered or abusive salary-deduction lenders harming employees and dragging BuildRight's payroll into disputes; mitigated by the accreditation gate, no-authorization-no-deduction rule, and delisting process
- **Payroll deduction disputes**: incorrect amounts, deductions continuing after revocation/separation; mitigated by authorization linkage, revocation SLA, and reconciliation controls (VS-19.2)
- **Take-home-pay erosion**: stacked deductions driving attrition and shrink/fraud risk; mitigated by the guardrail monitor with confidential outreach
- **Program perceived as substitute for pay**: financial-wellness seen as an alternative to compensation adequacy; mitigated by pairing with VS-102 market-benchmarking facts in communications

### Automation Opportunity
- Lender-master validation automatically rejects deduction instructions referencing non-accredited lenders or missing authorization records (supports Step 4).
- Authorization-expiry and revocation alerts stop deductions before the next payroll cut-off (supports Step 4).
- Anonymized deduction-load dashboard auto-flags guardrail exceedances for confidential EAP outreach (supports Step 5).

### Controls
- CTL-480 (ensure controlled execution — Mental Health, Wellness & Employee Assistance Program (PA-83.3).)

### Time Estimate
- Campaign delivery: ~8–10 days/quarter team effort (provider-led sessions + huddle kits); lender governance 5–8 days/year; coaching via existing EAP contract
- Program stewardship absorbed by EAP Manager with HR/Payroll/Legal support; no incremental headcount
