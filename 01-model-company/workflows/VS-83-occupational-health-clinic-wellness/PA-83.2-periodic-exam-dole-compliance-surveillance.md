## W2857. Pre-Employment Medical Examination & Fitness-to-Work Clearance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Conditional job offer |
| **Frequency** | ~1,200–1,600 hires/year |
| **Volume** | All new hires |
| **Owner** | OH Nurse |
| **Participants** | OH Nurse, HR, Company Physician, HMO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR refers conditional hire for pre-employment medical exam (physical, chest X-ray, drug test, lab work) at accredited clinic | HR | OH Nurse Manager | Per hire |
| 2 | OH Nurse/Physician reviews results; determines fitness-to-work for specific role (lifting requirements, hazmat handling, driving) | OH Nurse / Company Physician | OH Nurse Manager | Per hire |
| 3 | HR clears hire for onboarding or rescinds offer per medical findings (subject to anti-discrimination law); archives record confidentially | HR | HR Manager | Per hire |

### System Touchpoints
- Pre-employment exam workflow; fitness-to-work determination; medical record archive

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Hiring risk**: Late or incomplete PEM delays onboarding; mitigated by accredited-clinic SLA and batched scheduling


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-test of "test, lab work" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "OH Nurse/Physician reviews results" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "HR clears hire for onboarding or rescinds offer per" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-479 (ensure controlled execution — Periodic Examination, DOLE Compliance & Disease Surveillance (PA-83.2).)
- operational: accredited-clinic SLA and batched scheduling

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 days per hire
