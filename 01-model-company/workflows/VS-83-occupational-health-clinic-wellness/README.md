# VS-83: Occupational Health, Safety Clinic & Employee Wellness

> **People** · [Value Stream Index](../value-stream-index.md)

---

## Overview

Occupational Health, Safety Clinic & Employee Wellness workflows for BuildRight Depot Corp. —
covering the company-wide occupational health program for 6,925 employees across 200 stores,
4 DCs, and HQ. This includes in-store/DC clinic operations, pre-employment and annual medical
examination (APE), DOLE-mandated OHS reporting, mental health (RA 11036) and Employee
Assistance Program (EAP), disease surveillance, ergonomics, and wellness.

While Hire-to-Retire (VS-19) owns recruitment, payroll, and benefits administration, and HSE
(VS-24) owns workplace safety incident management and inspections, this value stream owns the
**occupational health and wellness program**: clinical care delivery, medical case management,
regulatory occupational health reporting, mental health services, and population health.
