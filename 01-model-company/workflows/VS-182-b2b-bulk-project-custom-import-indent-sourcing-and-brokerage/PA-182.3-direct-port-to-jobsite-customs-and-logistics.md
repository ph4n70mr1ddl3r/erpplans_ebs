## W5241. Direct Port-to-Jobsite Transit Logistics Planning and Route Survey

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Approved drayage schedule (W5240) |
| **Frequency** | Per jobsite delivery project |
| **Volume** | ~40-50 route surveys annually |
| **Owner** | Logistics Fleet Supervisor |
| **Participants** | Freight Coordinator, Drayage Carrier Dispatcher, Customer Site Engineer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conduct physical route survey to identify height clearances, bridge weight limits, and truck bans. | Logistics Fleet Supervisor | Logistics Fleet Supervisor | 3-5 hours |
| 2 | Secure local LGU special permits for oversized cargo or route exemptions. | Logistics Fleet Supervisor | Logistics Fleet Supervisor | 2-3 hours |
| 3 | Establish transit schedule avoiding municipal truck ban hours (typically peak commute windows). | Freight Coordinator | Logistics Fleet Supervisor | 1 hour |
| 4 | Brief transport crew on route restrictions and emergency contact protocols. | Logistics Fleet Supervisor | Logistics Fleet Supervisor | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- Fleet Operations module (VS-06), LGU Permit Tracker (VS-76).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Truck ban infraction risk**: Drayage trucks seized by municipal traffic authorities for violating local truck ban ordinances; mitigated by obtaining official LGU exemption permits (VS-76).

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile logistics application displaying approved route restrictions and digital permits.

### Controls
- CTL-25: In-transit cargo safety controls; Fleet Manager route sign-off.

### Time Estimate
- ~1-2 days per project survey

### Cross-references
- links to VS-06 (logistics), VS-76 (LGU compliance)

## W5243. Direct Delivery Container Devanning and Joint Quantity Survey at Jobsite

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Container arrival at jobsite |
| **Frequency** | Per container delivery |
| **Volume** | ~100-150 containers devanned annually |
| **Owner** | B2B Sourcing Quality Inspector |
| **Participants** | Customer Site Engineer, Drayage Driver, Quantity Surveyor |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify container seal number matches BOC documentation before breaking seal. | B2B Sourcing Quality Inspector | B2B Sourcing Quality Inspector | 30 min |
| 2 | Unload materials and perform joint physical count (BuildRight inspector + customer surveyor). | Quantity Surveyor | B2B Sourcing Quality Inspector | 2-4 hours |
| 3 | Inspect materials for visual transit damage, warping, or water ingress. | B2B Sourcing Quality Inspector | B2B Sourcing Quality Inspector | 1-2 hours |
| 4 | Draft Joint Quantity Survey (JQS) statement; sign off by inspector and client. | B2B Sourcing Quality Inspector | B2B Sourcing Quality Inspector | 1 hour |

### System Touchpoints
- B2B Site Delivery module, Quality System (VS-31).

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Receiving discrepancy dispute risk**: Customer claims shortages after container leaves site; mitigated by signing the joint (BuildRight–customer) JQS statement at the time of seal breaking.

### Automation Opportunity
- Mobile tablet application with barcode scanning and instant photo upload to JQS database.

### Controls
- CTL-21: Inventory accuracy controls; QA Director verification of JQS uploads.

### Time Estimate
- ~4.5–7.5 hours per container devanning (per the step durations)

### Cross-references
- links to VS-31 (quality management), VS-171 (loading zone)
