## W4505. CSR Strategy, Pillars & Social-License Framework

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Corporate strategy (VS-33); board review; community/stakeholder signal |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on strategy change |
| **Volume** | One enterprise CSR strategy across 200 store communities |
| **Owner** | Foundation Executive Director / Sustainability/ESG Manager |
| **Participants** | Strategy (VS-33), ESG (VS-25), Marketing (VS-14.3), Govt Affairs (VS-104), Board (VS-36) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation ED/Sustainability Lead set the CSR strategy and pillars (e.g., shelter/housing, livelihood, education, disaster resilience, environment), aligned to BuildRight's "Home Building Partner" purpose and corporate strategy (VS-33) | Foundation ED / Sustainability Lead | CEO / Board CSR Committee | Annual |
| 2 | Foundation ED/Stakeholders map social-license priorities per region/community (Mindanao HQ, Visayas, Luzon) using community/stakeholder input (links to VS-104/VS-44), and sequence the portfolio | Foundation ED / Stakeholders | CEO / Board CSR Committee | Annual |
| 3 | Foundation ED/Sustainability Lead document the strategy, obtain board endorsement (VS-36), and cascade to program design (W4514), budget (W4508), and reporting (W4525) | Foundation ED / Sustainability Lead | CEO / Board CSR Committee | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy/CPM (VS-33); ESG (VS-25); board governance (VS-36); marketing/brand (VS-14.3); govt affairs (VS-104)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Strategic-disconnect risk**: CSR detached from corporate purpose → wasted/low-impact spend; mitigated by strategy alignment & board endorsement
- **Social-license-blind risk**: Programs miss actual community priorities; mitigated by stakeholder mapping & community input

### Automation Opportunity
- Community-priority/stakeholder analytics; strategy-to-program linkage tracking; social-license dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-165 (ensure CSR / foundation governance & safeguarding.)
- CTL-686: CSR strategy board-endorsed (VS-36); aligned to corporate purpose (VS-33); community-priority-informed

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual strategy cycle

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33 (strategy), VS-25 (ESG), VS-36 (board), VS-14.3 (brand), VS-104 (govt affairs), W4508 (budget), W4514 (programs)

## W4506. Foundation Legal Setup, SEC Registration & BIR Qualified-Donee Status

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Foundation formation; regulatory change; BIR re-accreditation |
| **Frequency** | Per lifecycle (formation) + periodic renewal |
| **Volume** | One BuildRight Foundation legal entity + donee status |
| **Owner** | Legal (VS-100.3) / Foundation ED / Tax (VS-79) |
| **Participants** | Compliance (VS-22), Tax (VS-79), Finance (VS-17), Governance (VS-36) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal/Foundation ED register the Foundation with SEC (Articles, Bylaws, purpose clause), secure LGU/permits, and establish the legal/operating framework distinct from operating entities (profile §2) | Legal / Foundation ED | VP Legal / Board | Per formation |
| 2 | Tax/Legal secure and maintain BIR qualified-donee status (donations deductible, tax-deductible receipts) and the PCNC accreditation (W4507), with annual revalidation | Tax / Legal / Foundation ED | CFO / VP Legal | Annual |
| 3 | Compliance/Legal maintain the regulatory calendar (SEC filings, BIR donee re-accreditation, PCNC renewal, LGU permits) and manage audit response (VS-22/VS-21) | Compliance / Legal | VP Legal | Continuous |

### System Touchpoints
- Legal (VS-100.3); tax/BIR (VS-79); compliance (VS-22); finance/GL (VS-17); governance (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Lapsed-accreditation risk**: BIR donee/PCNC status lapses → donations non-deductible, donor flight; mitigated by regulatory calendar & renewal SLA
- **Purpose-drift risk**: Foundation activity outside its legal purpose clause → regulatory exposure; mitigated by purpose-clause discipline

### Automation Opportunity
- Regulatory-calendar tracker with renewal alerts; donor-receipt automation; accreditation document vault

### Controls
- CTL-165 (ensure CSR / foundation governance & safeguarding.)
- CTL-686: Foundation SEC-registered & purpose-bounded; BIR donee status maintained; PCNC accredited (W4507)

### Time Estimate
- Formation ~2–4 months; renewals annual

### Cross-references
- links to VS-100.3 (legal), VS-79 (tax/BIR), VS-22 (compliance), VS-17 (finance), VS-36 (governance), W4507 (PCNC)

## W4507. Foundation Governance, Board & PCNC Accreditation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Board cycle; PCNC cycle; governance review |
| **Frequency** | Board quarterly + PCNC periodic |
| **Volume** | Foundation Board + committees + PCNC accreditation |
| **Owner** | Foundation Board / Corporate Secretary (VS-36) |
| **Participants** | Foundation ED, Audit (VS-21), Legal (VS-100.3), Finance (VS-17) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Board/Corp Secretary run Foundation governance (board composition, independence, conflict-of-interest policy, committees — audit/finance/programs), per VS-36 governance standards | Board / Corp Secretary | Board Chair | Continuous |
| 2 | Foundation ED/Board pursue and maintain PCNC (Philippine Council for NGO Certification) accreditation — the standard NGO-credibility seal — with the documentary/financial evidence cycle | Foundation ED / Board / Audit | Board Chair | Per cycle |
| 3 | Audit/Finance ensure Foundation financial oversight (separate books, annual external audit, fund-use discipline) links to VS-21/VS-17, and report to the board | Audit / Finance / Foundation ED | Board Audit Committee | Quarterly/Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Governance/board (VS-36); audit (VS-21); finance/GL (VS-17); legal (VS-100.3)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Governance-weakness risk**: Weak board/oversight → misuse/perception of misuse; mitigated by independence, COI policy, separate audit
- **Credibility-loss risk**: PCNC lapse → partner/donor confidence loss; mitigated by accreditation maintenance

### Automation Opportunity
- Board/COI/governance management (VS-36); PCNC evidence-pack automation; fund-use dashboards

### Controls
- CTL-165 (ensure CSR / foundation governance & safeguarding.)
- CTL-686: Foundation board independent w/ COI policy; separately audited (VS-21); PCNC accredited

### Time Estimate
- Board ~quarterly; PCNC ~per 3-year cycle with annual upkeep

### Cross-references
- links to VS-36 (governance), VS-21 (audit), VS-17 (finance), VS-100.3 (legal), W4506 (donee status)

| Field | Detail |

| **Frequency** | Annual + per program/donation |

| **Owner** | Foundation ED / Finance (VS-17.4) / Sustainability/ESG Manager |
| **Participants** | Strategy (VS-33), Treasury (VS-18), Programs, Board Finance Committee |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation ED/Finance build the annual CSR budget (corporate allocation + Foundation endowment draw + donations), aligned to the strategy pillars (W4505) and corporate budget (VS-33/VS-17.4) | Foundation ED / Finance | CFO / Board Finance Committee | Annual |
| 2 | Board Finance/Sustainability/ESG Manager allocate funds across programs/geographies with an approval matrix, governed disbursement, and fund-use restrictions (purpose-bounded per W4506) | Board Finance / Sustainability/ESG Manager / Foundation ED | Board Finance Committee | Per program |
| 3 | Finance/Treasury manage the endowment/investment policy (links to VS-18), track CSR spend vs budget, and report utilization to the board (VS-36) | Finance / Treasury | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Corporate budget/FP&A (VS-33/VS-17.4); treasury/endowment (VS-18); board governance (VS-36); GL (VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks

### Automation Opportunity
- Strategy-to-budget linkage; fund-use/destination tracking; spend-vs-impact dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-165 (ensure CSR / foundation governance & safeguarding.)
- CTL-686: CSR budget strategy-aligned; disbursement approval-matrix-governed; endowment policy-governed; board-reported (VS-36)

### Time Estimate
- ~4 weeks annual budget; per-program approvals ongoing

### Cross-references
- links to VS-33/VS-17.4 (budget/FP&A), VS-18 (treasury/endowment), VS-36 (board), W4505 (strategy), W4514 (programs)

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

| 2 | Procurement/Programs apply partner due diligence (NGO/LGU/cooperative vetting, financial integrity, no terrorist/sanctioned-entity links) before funding, integrated to VS-34/VS-03 vendor due diligence | Procurement / Programs / Compliance | VP Legal | Per partner |
