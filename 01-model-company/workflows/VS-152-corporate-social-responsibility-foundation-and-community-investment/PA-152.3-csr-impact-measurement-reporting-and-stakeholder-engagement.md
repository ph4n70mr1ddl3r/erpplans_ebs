## W4521. CSR Impact Measurement, Logic Model & Outcome Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Program design; measurement cycle (W4514–W4517); reporting |
| **Frequency** | Per program + periodic measurement |
| **Volume** | Outcome tracking across all programs/beneficiaries |
| **Owner** | Foundation ED / M&E Lead / Data (VS-28) |
| **Participants** | Programs, Analytics (VS-28), Partners (W4519), Insights (VS-44) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | M&E/Foundation ED build the logic model / theory of change per program (inputs → activities → outputs → outcomes → impact) and define indicators (IRIS+/SMART) before launch | M&E / Foundation ED / Programs | Foundation Board Programs Committee | Per program |
| 2 | M&E/Partners collect output/outcome data (beneficiaries reached, houses built, income gained, enterprises surviving) on the CSR data platform (W4512), with data quality | M&E / Partners / Data | Foundation ED | Continuous |
| 3 | M&E/Foundation ED analyze outcomes vs targets, capture beneficiary feedback (VS-44/W4525), and report to the board (VS-36) and into SROI (W4522) | M&E / Foundation ED | Foundation Board | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- CSR data/impact model (W4512); analytics (VS-28); insights/feedback (VS-44); board governance (VS-36)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Vanity-metric risk**: Tracking outputs (houses built) not outcomes (lives improved); mitigated by outcome-focused logic model
- **Attribution risk**: Claiming impact not caused by the program; mitigated by counterfactual/baseline design

### Automation Opportunity
- Logic-model/indicator tracker; beneficiary-outcome capture app; outcome-vs-target dashboards

### Controls
- CTL-165 (ensure CSR / foundation governance & safeguarding.)
- CTL-688: programs have logic model & outcome indicators pre-launch; data quality managed; outcomes (not vanity metrics) reported

### Time Estimate
- ~per-program design + periodic measurement cycles

### Cross-references
- links to W4512 (data), VS-28 (analytics), VS-44 (insights), VS-36 (board), W4514–W4517 (programs), W4522 (SROI), W4525 (feedback)

## W4522. Social Return on Investment (SROI) & Benefit Valuation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Portfolio review; investment decision; reporting |
| **Frequency** | Periodic + per major program |
| **Volume** | SROI/benefit valuation across the CSR portfolio |
| **Owner** | Foundation ED / Finance (VS-17.4) / M&E |
| **Participants** | Finance (VS-17.4), Strategy (VS-33), Analytics (VS-28), Programs |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finance/M&E/Foundation ED value social outcomes monetarily (SROI methodology — housing value, income gain, avoided disaster cost) using outcome data (W4521) and standard proxies | Finance / M&E / Foundation ED | CFO / Foundation Board | Periodic |
| 2 | Foundation ED/Strategy compare SROI across programs/geographies, informing portfolio allocation (W4508) and investment decisions | Foundation ED / Strategy / Finance | Foundation Board | Periodic |
| 3 | Finance/Foundation ED report SROI to the board (VS-36) and integrate into CSR reporting (W4523), with methodology transparency (avoid over-claiming) | Finance / Foundation ED | CFO / Foundation Board | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Finance/FP&A (VS-17.4); strategy (VS-33); analytics (VS-28); board (VS-36); CSR data (W4512)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-valuation risk**: Inflated SROI → greenwashing/credibility loss; mitigated by conservative proxies & methodology disclosure
- **Decision-misuse risk**: SROI as sole allocator → misses non-monetizable value; mitigated by multi-criteria allocation

### Automation Opportunity
- SROI calculation tooling; outcome-to-value proxy library; portfolio SROI dashboard

### Controls
- CTL-688 (ensure controlled execution — CSR Impact Measurement, Reporting & Stakeholder Engagement (PA-152.3).)
- CTL-688: SROI conservatively valued w/ methodology disclosed; multi-criteria allocation; board-reported (VS-36)

### Time Estimate
- ~per valuation cycle weeks; periodic

### Cross-references
- links to VS-17.4 (FP&A), VS-33 (strategy), VS-28 (analytics), VS-36 (board), W4521 (outcomes), W4508 (budget)

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
