## W4513. Community-Needs Assessment & Partnership Identification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategy (W4505); community signal; periodic cycle |
| **Frequency** | Annual + on opportunity |
| **Volume** | Needs assessment across 200 store communities |
| **Owner** | Foundation ED / Programs / Consumer Insights (VS-44) |
| **Participants** | Store Ops (VS-07), Govt Affairs (VS-104), Insights (VS-44), Research |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Programs/Foundation ED conduct community-needs assessment (shelter, livelihood, education, disaster-resilience gaps) leveraging store-community network (VS-07) and insights (VS-44) | Programs / Insights / Stores | Foundation ED | Annual |
| 2 | Programs/Partnerships identify credible partners (NGOs, LGUs, cooperatives, faith groups) per need, with due diligence (W4509) and alignment to strategy pillars | Programs / Partnerships | Foundation ED | Per opportunity |
| 3 | Programs/Foundation ED prioritize & sequence interventions by need/impact/fit, and feed to program design (W4514–W4517) and budget (W4508) | Programs / Foundation ED | Foundation Board Programs Committee | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Insights/research (VS-44); store network (VS-07); govt affairs (VS-104); CSR data (W4512)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Misread-need risk**: Programs address assumed-not-real needs; mitigated by community-driven assessment
- **Partner-mismatch risk**: Wrong partner for the need; mitigated by needs-first then partner-fit selection

### Automation Opportunity
- Community-needs data integration; partner-directory/fit matching; prioritization scoring

### Controls
- CTL-687 (ensure controlled execution — Community Investment, Disaster Response & Volunteer Programs (PA-152.2).)
- CTL-687: needs community-driven (VS-44/VS-07); partners due-diligenced (W4509); priorities strategy-aligned (W4505)

### Time Estimate
- ~4–6 weeks annual cycle; per-opportunity ongoing

### Cross-references
- links to VS-44 (insights), VS-07 (stores), VS-104 (govt affairs), W4505 (strategy), W4509 (DD), W4514–W4517 (programs)
