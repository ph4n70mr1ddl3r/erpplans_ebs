## W2654. In-Store Kiosk Management & Content Updates

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Kiosk content refresh or system update |
| **Frequency** | Monthly updates; daily monitoring |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × 1–2 kiosks = ~300 kiosks |
| **Owner** | Store Operations IT |
| **Participants** | Store Ops IT, Marketing, Merchandising, Digital Product Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store Ops IT manages kiosk fleet: monitors uptime (99%), remote content management, OS updates, hardware maintenance; replaces faulty units within 48 hours | Store Ops IT | CIO | Daily monitoring |
| 2 | Marketing updates content: promotional videos, product spotlights, store map, loyalty info, DIY videos, seasonal campaigns; refreshes monthly with weekly promo updates | Marketing / Merchandising | CMO | Monthly/weekly |
| 3 | Digital Product Manager analyzes usage: interaction count, most-viewed content, session duration, conversion to sale; optimizes content placement | Digital Product Manager | CMO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Kiosk-downtime risk**: a faulty unit beyond the 48-hour replacement window dead-ends the in-store digital journey it anchors; mitigated by the 99% uptime monitor with the 48-hour replacement commitment (Step 1)
- **Kiosk-content-staleness risk**: kiosk screens promoting last month's promo mislead customers at the shelf; mitigated by the monthly refresh with weekly promo updates (Step 2) and the usage-analytics placement optimization (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Process steps auto-sequenced with system controls; data-entry auto-populated from master data; exceptions auto-flagged for human review

### Controls
- CTL-455 (ensure controlled execution — In-Store Digital Experience & Self-Service (PA-75.2).)
- operational: Store Operations IT sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: 200 stores × 1–2 kiosks = ~300 kiosks
- Frequency: Monthly updates; daily monitoring
- Absorbed into the Store Operations IT role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2656. Self-Checkout Pilot & Operations

| **Frequency** | Continuous at pilot stores |
| **Volume** | ~20–30 pilot stores × 2 terminals |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | Store Manager, LP Officer, IT, VP Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer uses self-checkout: scans items, places on weighted verification platform, pays via card/GCash/Maya (no cash); system verifies weight matches expected | Customer / System | Store Manager | 5–10 min per transaction |
| 2 | LP Officer monitors: reviews exception alerts (weight mismatch, barcode override), assists customers, responds to intervention alerts; staff available for age-restricted authorization | LP Officer / Staff | Store Manager | Continuous |
| 3 | VP Store Operations evaluates: throughput vs. staffed checkout, satisfaction, shrinkage rate, maintenance cost; determines expansion, modification, or discontinuation | VP Store Operations | COO | Quarterly review |

- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

- **Intervention-latency risk**: customers queued behind unworked intervention alerts abandon the lane; mitigated by the staffed assist requirement with age-restricted authorization available (Step 2)

### Controls

| **Volume** | ~50K–100K activations/month |
| **Owner** | Digital Product Manager |
| **Participants** | Digital Product Mgr, Store Ops, Marketing, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System detects entry (GPS geofence + Bluetooth beacon): activates in-store mode showing map, promotions, aisle locations, stock check, scan-to-shop, associate assistance | System / Digital Product Manager | CIO | Automated |

## W2661. Digital Price Tag & Electronic Shelf Label Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Price change cycle |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; per change |
| **Volume** | 200 stores × ~15,000 ESLs = ~3M ESLs |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Operations |
| **Participants** | Merch Ops, IT, Store Ops, Pricing Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing Manager triggers change in ERP: pushes to ESL system via store network; ESLs update within 5 minutes; supports dynamic and time-based promotional pricing | Pricing Manager / System | VP Merchandising | Per price change |
| 2 | Store Ops verifies daily: spot-checks 20–30 ESLs vs. POS price; reports sync failures; ESL system alerts for low-battery labels | Store Ops / Merch Ops | VP Store Operations | Daily |
| 3 | Merch Ops tracks performance: price sync accuracy (99.9%), battery life management, hardware failure rate; ROI: eliminates ~18M paper labels/year | Merch Ops / Finance | VP Merchandising | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Price-sync failure risk**: an ESL showing yesterday's price at the shelf against a different POS price is the classic overcharge complaint; mitigated by the daily 20–30-label spot-check vs. POS (Step 2) against the 99.9% sync-accuracy target (Step 3)
- **Battery-death risk**: labels dying silently on the shelf revert to frozen prices; mitigated by the low-battery alerts (Step 2) and battery-life management (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
