## W2662. Digital Channel Attribution & Customer Journey Analytics

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly analytics cycle |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~300K app users + ~5M web visits/month |
| **Owner** | Digital Analytics Manager |
| **Participants** | Digital Analytics Manager, Marketing, CMO, CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Digital Analytics Manager maps customer journey: web → app → in-store → BOPIS; tracks cross-channel attribution; builds unified customer view | Digital Analytics Manager | CMO | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | Manager analyzes attribution: app-driven in-store sales, web-to-store conversion, digital promo redemption, notification influence on purchase | Digital Analytics Manager | CMO | 1 week |
| 3 | CFO reviews digital ROI: acquisition cost per channel, lifetime value per digital vs. non-digital customer, marketing spend effectiveness; adjusts investment allocation | CFO / Digital Analytics Manager | CEO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
- CRM (links to VS-13 / W17 loyalty, W156 CDP, W65 CSAT)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113 BI platform, W231 QBR dashboard)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Attribution-myth risk**: last-click attribution crediting the final touch starves the app's assist channels of credit; mitigated by the cross-channel journey mapping (Step 1) with the CFO's ROI review adjusting investment (Step 3)
- **Journey-fragment risk**: identities unresolved across web, app and store build three customers out of one; mitigated by the unified customer view build (Step 1)

### Automation Opportunity
- Dashboard auto-refreshed from source systems; anomalies auto-flagged; report auto-distributed to stakeholders

### Controls
- CTL-456 (ensure controlled execution — Digital Engagement Analytics & Optimization (PA-75.3).)
- CTL-456 (data-privacy — links to VS-91 / W53, RA 10173); Operational: Digital Analytics Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 weeks per occurrence
- Volume: ~300K app users + ~5M web visits/month
- Frequency: Monthly
- Absorbed into the Digital Analytics Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2663. App User Acquisition & Retention Funnel Analysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly analytics |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~300K MAU; ~20K–30K downloads/month |
| **Owner** | Digital Product Manager |
| **Participants** | Digital Product Mgr, Marketing, Digital Analytics Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Product Manager analyzes acquisition funnel: impressions → downloads → registrations → first purchase → repeat; calculates CPI and CPA per channel | Digital Product Manager | CIO / CMO | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Manager analyzes retention: Day 1/7/30 rates; compares by source, segment, feature usage; identifies churn signals | Digital Product Manager / Digital Analytics Manager | CMO | 1–2 days |
| 3 | Marketing implements retention campaigns: re-engagement push, personalized recommendations, app-only offers, points acceleration; Product Manager measures impact | Marketing / Digital Product Manager | CMO | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints
- Mobile app (links to VS-75 / digital engagement app, VS-10 / ecommerce)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **CPI-illusion risk**: acquisition cost read without retention quality buys downloads that churn within a week; mitigated by the Day 1/7/30 retention comparison by source (Step 2) weighting the per-channel CPI/CPA (Step 1)
- **Churn-signal-ignore risk**: churn signals identified but never wired into campaigns; mitigated by the retention campaigns (Step 3: re-engagement push, personalized recommendations, points acceleration) with measured impact

### Automation Opportunity
- Self-service workflows (scan-and-go, product lookup, self-checkout) reducing staff intervention; feature-adoption auto-tracked from app telemetry

### Controls
- CTL-456 (ensure controlled execution — Digital Engagement Analytics & Optimization (PA-75.3).)
- operational: Digital Product Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~300K MAU; ~20K–30K downloads/month
- Frequency: Monthly
- Absorbed into the Digital Product Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

| **Participants** | Ecommerce Mgr, Digital Product Manager, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ecommerce Manager analyzes checkout funnel: view → cart → checkout → payment → complete; identifies drop-off points; calculates abandonment rate | Ecommerce Manager | CMO | 1–2 days |

- **Novelty-decay risk**: one-time optimization wins assumed permanent as traffic mix shifts; mitigated by the monthly impact tracking (Step 3) on conversion, abandonment, AOV and payment mix

| **Owner** | Customer Experience Analyst |
| **Participants** | CX Analyst, Digital Product Manager, CMO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CX Analyst deploys in-app NPS monthly (random 10%): satisfaction, reliability, features, ease of purchase, recommendation likelihood; captures verbatim feedback | CX Analyst | COO | Automated |

## W2668. Omnichannel Digital Integration Health Monitoring

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous monitoring |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; monthly review |
| **Volume** | All digital-physical integrations |
| **Owner** | IT Integration Manager |
| **Participants** | IT Integration Mgr, Digital Product Manager, Ecommerce Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Integration Manager monitors: POS-to-app inventory sync, price propagation, BOPIS transmission, fulfillment updates, loyalty sync; alerts for failures | IT Integration Manager | CIO | Continuous |
| 2 | Manager investigates incidents: root cause analysis, impact assessment; implements preventive measures (retry logic, circuit breakers) | IT Integration Manager | CIO | Per incident |
| 3 | Digital Product Manager reviews health monthly: uptime (99.5%), data freshness, error rates; identifies integrations needing improvement | Digital Product Manager / IT Integration Manager | CIO | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Ecommerce/marketplace platform (links to VS-10 / W11 BOPIS, W180 marketplace integration)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cross-domain incident risk**: an incident spanning several integrations (POS, BOPIS, loyalty) resolved inside one owner's queue leaves the others unaware; mitigated by the Digital Product Manager's monthly cross-integration review (Step 3) identifying the integrations needing improvement
- **Silent-degradation risk**: an integration failing below alert thresholds (stale prices, delayed BOPIS transmission) erodes trust before it pages anyone; mitigated by the failure alerting (Step 1) with the monthly uptime (99.5%), freshness and error-rate review (Step 3)
- **Repeat-incident risk**: incidents patched without preventive measures recur; mitigated by the root-cause analysis with retry-logic and circuit-breaker implementation (Step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- API-driven real-time sync (catalog, inventory, pricing, orders) eliminating manual data entry; auto-reconciliation of marketplace settlement vs. BuildRight records

### Controls
- CTL-456 (ensure controlled execution — Digital Engagement Analytics & Optimization (PA-75.3).)
- operational: IT Integration Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: All digital-physical integrations
- Frequency: Continuous; monthly review
- Absorbed into the IT Integration Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
