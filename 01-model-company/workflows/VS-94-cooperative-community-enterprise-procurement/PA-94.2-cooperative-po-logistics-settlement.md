## W3121. Cooperative Demand Aggregation & PO Placement

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Demand plan (links to VS-02); coop production cycle; new SKU introduction |
| **Frequency** | Per cycle (often monthly/seasonal) |
| **Volume** | ~10–40 coop POs/month |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Sourcing Manager |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, Buyer (links to VS-03), Inventory Planning, Coop Representatives |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inventory Planning/Sustainability Sourcing Mgr aggregate demand across stores for coop-sourced SKUs (links to VS-02); respect coop production capacity, minimum viable batch, and lead time; smooth demand to fit coop labor | Inventory Planner / Sustainability Sourcing Mgr | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Per cycle |
| 2 | Buyer/Sustainability Sourcing Mgr place PO (links to VS-03) with contract pricing (W3116), delivery window aligned to coop cycle (W3123), and clear spec reference (W3118); PO communicated in coop's preferred language/channel and acknowledged | Buyer / Sustainability Sourcing Mgr | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Per PO |
| 3 | System reserves commitment against capacity-building / advance-financing facility if applicable (links to W3127); flag PO as coop-sourced for impact tracking (links to W3129) | System | Sustainability Sourcing Manager | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- Demand planning (links to VS-02); PO placement (links to VS-03); impact tagging (links to W3129)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Demand-volatility risk**: Sudden de-stocking strands coop inventory; mitigated by minimum-offtake commitment (W3116) and demand smoothing


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-communication of "communicated in coop's preferred language/channel and acknowledged" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "System reserves commitment against capacity-building / advance-financing facility if" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-512 (ensure controlled execution — Cooperative Purchase Order, Logistics & Livelihood Settlement (PA-94.2).)
- operational: minimum-offtake commitment (W3116) and demand smoothing

### Time Estimate
- Per cycle; ~1–2 days

## W3122. Cooperative Production Monitoring & Progress Tracking

| **Volume** | All open coop POs |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Sourcing Manager |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, Coop Representatives, Quality, Logistics |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |

- System auto-inspect of "inspection if applicable" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-68 (ensure cooperative vendor qualification & settlement integrity.)

| **Frequency** | Per PO settlement |
| **Volume** | Per coop PO |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Sourcing Manager |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr, AP (links to VS-15), Finance, Treasury (links to VS-18), Coop Representatives |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coop submits invoice per accepted quantity at contract price (W3116); AP performs match: PO → GR (W3125) → Invoice, with adjustment for any partial-reject/short-ship; validate fair-trade price compliance | AP Clerk | Finance Manager | Per invoice |
| 2 | Sustainability Sourcing Mgr/Finance execute direct-to-coop-account settlement: payment to coop's CDA-registered bank account; for indigenous/artisanal work, consider direct-to-beneficiary split per contract; favor faster terms than mainstream (e.g., net 15 vs. net 30) | Finance Analyst / Treasury | CFO | Per settlement |

- System auto-count of "Sustainability Sourcing Mgr/Finance execute direct-to-coop-account settlement" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record in AP subledger and impact tracker" (replaces manual Step 3).
