## W3137. Marketplace Platform Strategy, Business Model & Catalog Coverage

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategic planning cycle (links to VS-33); assortment gap analysis; competitive move |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | Enterprise-wide; target ~1,000–5,000 3P sellers and ~10K–50K 3P SKUs at maturity |
| **Owner** | Marketplace Director |
| **Participants** | Marketplace Director, GM, Digital Commerce Inc. (links to VS-10), Category (links to VS-01), Finance, Legal, IT |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketplace Director/Category (links to VS-01) identify assortment gaps that 3P fills: niche/specialty, regional brands, vendor direct-ship, refurbished (links to VS-09 trade-in), long-tail SKUs; size opportunity (incremental GMV, customer acquisition, take-rate revenue) | Marketplace Director / Category | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | 3–4 weeks |
| 2 | Define business model: open marketplace vs. curated; commission % by category; fulfillment options (seller-fulfilled vs. BuildRight fulfillment / Fulfilled by BuildRight — links to VS-93); buyer protection fund; advertising integration (links to VS-48) | Marketplace Director / Finance | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. | 2–3 weeks |
| 3 | Steering committee approves strategy, capex (links to VS-40), 24-month P&L, and launch phasing; integrate to VS-33 strategic plan and VS-30 innovation pipeline | GM, Digital Commerce Inc. / CFO | CEO | 2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy/portfolio (links to VS-33); capex (links to VS-40); innovation (links to VS-30)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Cannibalization risk**: 3P offers undercut 1P; mitigated by category-level 1P/3P rules and buy-box logic (W3146)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-identify of "identify assortment gaps that 3P fills" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves strategy, capex" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-514 (ensure controlled execution — Marketplace Platform Strategy & Seller Onboarding (PA-95.1).)
- operational: category-level 1P/3P rules and buy-box logic (W3146)

### Time Estimate
- 6–8 weeks per strategic cycle

- CTL-514 (ensure controlled execution — Marketplace Platform Strategy & Seller Onboarding (PA-95.1).)

### Steps

## W3144. Seller Offboarding, Exit & Data Portability

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Seller request; performance termination (links to W3156); seller bankruptcy; contract expiry |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Per seller |
| **Owner** | Marketplace Manager |
| **Participants** | Marketplace Mgr, Legal, Finance, IT, Seller Operations, CS (links to VS-13) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Marketplace Mgr documents offboarding rationale (voluntary exit, performance, policy violation, bankruptcy); for performance/policy, follows termination process per contract (cure period, written notice) | Marketplace Mgr / Legal | Marketplace Manager | Per exit |
| 2 | IT/Seller Operations execute offboarding: delist SKUs (preserve active orders for fulfillment), wind down open POs/payouts, settle final payout net of fees/returns/claims (W3153), return seller data per data-portability right (links to VS-91) | IT / Seller Operations / Finance | Marketplace Manager | Per exit |
| 3 | CS (links to VS-13) manage customer impact: notify buyers of any unfulfilled orders, process refunds (links to VS-80), alternative-product suggestions; preserve records for warranty/returns (links to VS-53/VS-32) and audit (links to VS-88) | CS / Finance | Marketplace Manager | Per exit |

### Pain Points / Risks

### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-document of "documents offboarding rationale" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-record of "records for warranty/returns" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-514 (ensure controlled execution — Marketplace Platform Strategy & Seller Onboarding (PA-95.1).)
- operational: Fulfill-by-BuildRight backstop and buyer-protection fund (W3158)

### Time Estimate
- 2–6 weeks per exit
