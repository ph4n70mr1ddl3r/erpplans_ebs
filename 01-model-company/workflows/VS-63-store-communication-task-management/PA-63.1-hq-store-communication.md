## W2358. Corporate Announcement Broadcasting

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Corporate announcement from HQ |
| **Frequency** | ~10–15 announcements/month |
| **Volume** | ~10–15 broadcasts to 200 stores/month |
| **Owner** | VP Corporate Communications |
| **Participants** | VP Corp Comms, COO, Regional Ops Manager, Field Communications Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Corp Comms drafts announcement in store communication platform: targets (all stores, regional, specific stores), priority (info/action/urgent), category (policy, promo, safety, HR); attaches documents | VP Corp Comms | COO | 30–60 min |
| 2 | System pushes to store manager app with notification; tracks read receipts per store; auto-escalates unread urgent messages after 4 hours; Regional Ops follows up with non-responsive stores | System / Regional Ops Mgr | VP Corp Comms | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-comms platform (links to VS-07 / W571 — the canonical HQ→store system) — the broadcast, targeting, and read-receipt tracking
- Store Manager app (links to VS-75 / digital engagement) — the receiving device
- Notification/escalation (links to VS-27 / W380 alert & event management) — the auto-escalation on unread-urgent

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Announcement-fatigue risk**: too many low-priority announcements desensitize Store Managers to the urgent-priority ones; mitigated by the priority-discipline (info/action/urgent strictly enforced) and the W2378 information-overload assessment
- **Unread-urgent risk**: an urgent announcement goes unread (Store Manager off-shift, app-notification muted) and the action isn't taken; mitigated by the 4-hour auto-escalation and the Regional Ops phone-follow-up for urgent-unread

### Automation Opportunity
- Broadcast auto-targeted per the priority/region/category; read-receipt auto-tracked with escalation

### Controls
- CTL-418 (ensure controlled execution — HQ-to-Store Communication & Broadcast (PA-63.1).)
- Operational: COO sign-off on urgent-priority broadcasts (prevents priority-creep); VP Corp Comms read-rate review

### Time Estimate
~45 min/broadcast × ~12/month = **~9–11 hours/month** VP Corp Comms (Regional Ops follow-up absorbed).

## W2359. Merchandising Directive Communication

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Planogram change or merchandising directive |
| **Frequency** | ~20–30 directives/month |
| **Volume** | ~20–30/month to 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Merchandising Planner, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinator creates merchandising directive: action required, affected zones, product details, photos of expected result, deadline, compliance photo requirement; distributes to stores | Merch Coordinator | Merchandising Planner | 1–2 hours |
| 2 | Store Manager acknowledges receipt; assigns to stock team; executes by deadline; submits compliance photos; Coordinator verifies within 48 hours | Store Manager / Merch Coordinator | Merchandising Planner | 1–2 hours + execution time |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-comms platform (links to VS-07 / W571, VS-55 / W2181 planogram-change implementation) — the directive distribution and compliance-photo verification
- Planogram (links to VS-55 / W314, W2170) — the directive's subject matter
- Store Manager app (links to VS-75) — the acknowledgment and photo-submission channel

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Directive-vs-planogram-drift risk**: the directive references a planogram version that's been superseded, confusing the store; mitigated by the directive auto-linked to the current planogram version (W2170)
- **Compliance-photo-fraud risk**: a store submits a compliance photo from a prior cycle or another store; mitigated by the timestamp/geo-tag on the photo (the W2175 pattern)

### Automation Opportunity
- Directive auto-assembled from the planogram change; compliance-photo auto-scored against the expected result

### Controls
- CTL-418 (ensure controlled execution — HQ-to-Store Communication & Broadcast (PA-63.1).)
- operational: planogram-version integrity; operational: Merchandising Planner verification sign-off

### Time Estimate
~2 hours/directive × ~25/month + ~2–2.5 hours/store execution × 200 stores = **~450–550 hours/month** Coordinator + Store teams (execution absorbed into store-ops).

## W2360. Emergency Communication Protocol

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Emergency event (typhoon, safety incident, system outage) |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; ~5–10 emergencies/year |
| **Volume** | ~5–10 emergency broadcasts/year to 200 stores |
| **Owner** | COO |
| **Participants** | CEO, Regional Ops Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | COO or designated executive sends emergency broadcast: mandatory read, audio alert on store devices, clear instructions (close store, evacuate, shelter in place, system workaround) | COO | CEO | 15–30 min |
| 2 | System tracks real-time acknowledgment; non-responding stores contacted by phone; Regional Ops confirms physical safety; follow-up communications as situation evolves | System / Regional Ops Mgr | COO | Continuous during emergency |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-comms platform / emergency broadcast (links to VS-07 / W571, VS-69 / typhoon response, VS-26 / BCP) — the emergency broadcast and acknowledgment tracking
- Store devices (links to VS-07 / W5 store ops, VS-27 / W366) — the audio-alert surface
- BCP/DR (links to VS-26 / W49 disaster response, W55 IT DR) — the emergency-communication as a BCP element

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Channel-failure-during-emergency risk**: the emergency knocks out the very communication channel (power/network outage at the store) needed to deliver the alert; mitigated by the multi-channel fallback (app, SMS, phone-tree) and the VS-26 BCP communication protocol
- **Ambiguous-instruction risk**: the emergency instruction is ambiguous (evacuate vs. shelter-in-place), causing inconsistent action; mitigated by the pre-defined emergency-instruction templates per emergency type

### Automation Opportunity
- Emergency broadcast auto-multi-channel (app + SMS + phone-tree); acknowledgment dashboard real-time

### Controls
- CTL-199 (ensure store-communication task closure.)
- CTL-418 (BCP communication — links to VS-26 / W49); operational: CEO sign-off on emergency broadcasts; post-emergency review of acknowledgment rates

### Time Estimate
~20 min/broadcast + continuous Regional Ops follow-up during the emergency × ~7/year = **~50–80 hours/year** (concentrated during emergencies).

## W2361. Weekly Store Manager Newsletter

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly editorial schedule |
| **Frequency** | Weekly; 200 Store Managers |
| **Volume** | ~200 recipients/week |
| **Owner** | VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations, COO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Store Operations curates weekly newsletter: top-performing stores, new procedures, upcoming promos, compliance scores, safety tips, HR updates; system distributes every Monday morning | VP Store Operations | COO | 2–3 hours/week |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-comms platform (links to VS-07 / W571) — the newsletter distribution and read-tracking
- Performance/compliance data (links to VS-07 / W67 store performance, VS-55 / W2174 planogram compliance) — the newsletter content source
- Store Manager app (links to VS-75) — the delivery channel

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Newsletter-skim risk**: the newsletter is skimmed/not read because it's routine; mitigated by the high-signal curation (top-performers recognition, upcoming-deadline action-items foregrounded)

### Automation Opportunity
- Newsletter content auto-assembled from the performance/compliance/promo datasets; read-rate auto-tracked

### Controls
- CTL-418 (ensure controlled execution — HQ-to-Store Communication & Broadcast (PA-63.1).)
- Operational: COO weekly review; read-rate monitored (W2374)

### Time Estimate
~2.5 hours/week = **~130 hours/year** VP Store Operations.

## W2362. Two-Way Store Feedback Channel

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Store Manager submits feedback or question |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~50–100 feedback items/month |
| **Volume** | ~50–100/month |
| **Owner** | Store Manager |
| **Participants** | VP Store Operations |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Store Manager submits feedback/question via the platform (category, priority, attachments); system routes to the responsible HQ function; HQ responds within SLA (48 hours standard, 24 hours urgent); feedback closed-loop on resolution | Store Manager / HQ Function | VP Store Operations | Per item |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-comms platform / feedback (links to VS-07 / W571, VS-13 / W41 complaint pattern) — the feedback submission and routing
- Routing/SLA (links to VS-27 / W380 alert, W379 service request) — the responsible-function routing and SLA tracking
- Store Manager app (links to VS-75) — the submission channel

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Response-SLA-miss risk**: HQ functions don't respond within the SLA, eroding Store Manager trust in the channel; mitigated by the SLA-tracking dashboard and the escalation on SLA-miss
- **Feedback-black-hole risk**: feedback is submitted but never acted on (no closure), so Store Managers stop submitting; mitigated by the closed-loop requirement (every feedback gets a resolution, even if "noted, no action")

### Automation Opportunity
- Feedback auto-routed by category; SLA auto-tracked with escalation; closed-loop auto-enforced

### Controls
- CTL-418 (ensure controlled execution — HQ-to-Store Communication & Broadcast (PA-63.1).)
- Operational: VP Store Operations SLA-compliance review; closed-loop-rate monitored

### Time Estimate
~15 min/item × ~75/month ≈ ~1,125 min ≈ **~19 hours/month** (Store Manager + HQ) distributed.

## W2363. Policy & Procedure Update Distribution

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Policy or procedure change |
| **Frequency** | ~5–10 policy updates/month |
| **Volume** | ~5–10/month to 200 stores |
| **Owner** | VP Legal / VP Store Operations |
| **Participants** | VP Legal & Compliance, VP Store Operations, COO, Store Manager, Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | VP Legal / VP Store Operations distributes the policy/procedure update: the changed policy, effective date, the "what changed" summary, the required store action (acknowledge, retrain, post); read-and-acknowledge required | VP Legal / VP Store Operations | COO | Per update |
| 2 | Store Manager acknowledges; posts/communicates per the required action; Coordinator verifies acknowledgment across all 200 stores | Store Manager / Coordinator | VP Store Operations | Per update |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-comms platform (links to VS-07 / W571, VS-88 / W255 document control) — the policy distribution and acknowledgment
- Policy repository (links to VS-88 / W255, VS-22 / W22.1 regulatory) — the versioned policy source
- Store Manager app (links to VS-75) — the acknowledgment channel

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Policy-non-compliance-due-to-ignorance risk**: a store doesn't implement the policy because they didn't read/understand the update; mitigated by the acknowledge-required distribution and the W2174 compliance-audit catching non-implementation
- **Version-confusion risk**: multiple policy versions circulate (old and new), causing inconsistent practice; mitigated by the single-canonical-source policy repository (VS-88) and the "what changed" summary

### Automation Opportunity
- Policy update auto-distributed from the VS-88 repository; acknowledgment auto-tracked

### Controls
- CTL-418 (ensure controlled execution — HQ-to-Store Communication & Broadcast (PA-63.1).)
- operational: policy-version integrity — links to VS-88; operational: VP Store Operations acknowledgment-completion sign-off

### Time Estimate
~1.5 hours/update × ~7/month + Store Manager acknowledgment ~10 min × 200 stores × ~7 updates = **~245 hours/month** distributed.

## W2364. Seasonal Preparation Communication Package

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Pre-season communication (6 seasons/year) |
| **Frequency** | 6/year; 200 stores |
| **Volume** | ~1,200 seasonal packages/year |
| **Owner** | Merchandising Planner |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandising Planner assembles the seasonal-prep package: seasonal assortment, planogram changes, pricing/promo, training materials, display guide, timeline; distributes as a bundled package 3 weeks before season | Merchandising Planner | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days/season |

### System Touchpoints
- Store-comms platform (links to VS-07 / W571, VS-64 / W2382 seasonal assortment) — the bundled package distribution
- Planogram/promo/training (links to VS-55 / W2166, VS-14 / W262, VS-19.4 / W51) — the package content sources
- Store Manager app (links to VS-75) — the delivery channel

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Package-overload risk**: the seasonal package is so large that Store Managers can't absorb it; mitigated by the prioritized action-list (the must-do-first items foregrounded) and the staged rollout
- **Mis-timed-distribution risk**: the package lands too late for the store to prep before the season start; mitigated by the 3-week-pre-season distribution trigger

### Automation Opportunity
- Package auto-assembled from the seasonal planogram/promo/training datasets; action-list auto-prioritized

### Controls
- CTL-418 (ensure controlled execution — HQ-to-Store Communication & Broadcast (PA-63.1).)
- Operational: VP Merchandising package sign-off; VP Store Operations readiness-check before season start

### Time Estimate
~1.5 days/season × 6 + Store Manager absorption ~2 hours/store × 200 stores × 6 seasons = **~2,500 hours/year** Planner + Store Managers.

| **Frequency** | ~200 Store Managers trained; ad-hoc feature training |
| **Volume** | 200 users; ~10–15 new users/year (turnover) |
| **Owner** | IT Training Specialist |
| **Participants** | CIO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT Training Specialist trains Store Managers on the platform: new features, best practices, troubleshooting; delivers via e-learning + live sessions for new hires (turnover onboarding); maintains a help resource | IT Training Specialist | CIO | Per feature/new-hire |

### System Touchpoints
- OLA (links to VS-19.4 / W51 training) — the e-learning and completion tracking
- Store-comms platform (links to VS-07 / W571) — the system under training
- IT helpdesk (links to VS-27 / W48) — the support channel

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Feature-under-adoption risk**: a new feature ships but Store Managers don't use it (no training); mitigated by the feature-rollout-with-training discipline

### Automation Opportunity
- Feature-training auto-assigned on rollout; onboarding-training auto-triggered by the HRIS new-hire event

### Controls
- CTL-418 (ensure controlled execution — HQ-to-Store Communication & Broadcast (PA-63.1).)
- Operational: IT Training Specialist completion-rate sign-off; CIO feature-adoption review

### Time Estimate
~4 hours/feature × ~6 features + ~2 hours/new-hire × ~12 = **~50–70 hours/year** IT Training.
