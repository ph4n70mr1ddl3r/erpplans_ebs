## W3281. Annual Merchandise Financial Plan & Sales/Margin Budget Development

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual corporate planning cycle (links to VS-33.1) |
| **Frequency** | Annual, with quarterly refresh |
| **Volume** | 1 annual plan, 13 departments / ~40 categories |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising, Merchandise Planners, Category Managers, FP&A (links to VS-17.4), CFO |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandise Planners receive the corporate revenue and gross-margin target from FP&A (links to VS-33.1 annual business plan) and decompose it into a top-down merchandise financial plan: total sales, gross-margin %, GM$ , beginning/ending inventory, and receipt budget by month and by department | Merchandise Planners | VP Merchandising | Per cycle |
| 2 | Category Managers build the bottom-up plan: prior-year actuals, known events (new stores per VS-37, store closures per VS-59, promo calendar per VS-14.1, seasonal calendar per profile §13.2), price/mix changes, and private-label growth (links to VS-41); reconcile top-down to bottom-up and resolve gaps | Category Managers / Merchandise Planners | VP Merchandising | Per cycle |
| 3 | FP&A/CFO approve the annual merchandise financial plan, lock the IMU/markdown assumptions (W3286), set the OTB envelope (W3283), and feed the plan into the merchandising and finance systems; establish monthly P&A review cadence (W3288) and variance thresholds | FP&A / CFO | CFO | Per cycle |

### System Touchpoints
- Merchandise financial planning / OTB module; FP&A/budgeting (links to VS-17.4); BI planning (links to VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Top-down/bottom-up mismatch risk**: Unrealistic corporate targets force overbuy or margin sacrifice; mitigated by structured reconciliation and scenario modeling (W3296)
- **Assumption-staleness risk**: Locked annual assumptions drift from reality; mitigated by in-season reforecast (W3288)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-file of "Category Managers build the bottom-up plan" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "FP&A/CFO approve the annual merchandise financial plan, lock the" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-532 (ensure controlled execution — Seasonal Merchandise Financial Planning & Open-to-Buy (PA-101.1).)
- operational: OTB change-authority matrix enforced in-system with SLA-tracked approvals; in-season reforecast per W3288

### Time Estimate
- 4–6 weeks annual cycle

## W3282. Seasonal Merchandise Plan & Department/Category Financial Plan

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Season boundary (per profile §13.2: summer/dry, rainy-season/flood-control, ber-months/Christmas) |
| **Frequency** | Seasonal (≈6 major seasons/year) |
| **Volume** | 13 departments, ~40 categories per season |
| **Owner** | Merchandise Planner |
| **Participants** | Merchandise Planner, Category Managers, Buyers (links to VS-03), Allocation (links to VS-55) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandise Planner develop the seasonal merchandise plan by department/category: seasonal sales, GM%, planned receipts, BOM/EOM stock, weeks-of-supply (W3289), and markdown budget (W3285); incorporate seasonal-calendar events and prior-season actuals (links to VS-64 seasonal transition) | Merchandise Planner / Category Managers | VP Merchandising | Per season |
| 2 | Category Managers translate the seasonal financial plan into a category plan: key items (links to VS-01.1 assortment), depth/width, launch/clearance timing, promo tie-ins (links to VS-14.1/VS-58), and import-vs-domestic mix (links to VS-02.2/VS-87); commit the buy against OTB (W3283/W3284) | Category Managers / Buyers | VP Merchandising | Per season |

## W3283. Open-to-Buy (OTB) Calculation, Maintenance & Weekly Recalculation

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Plan setup; weekly merchandise review |
| **Frequency** | Weekly recalculation |
| **Volume** | 13 departments / ~40 categories weekly |
| **Owner** | Merchandise Planner |
| **Participants** | Merchandise Planner, Buyers (links to VS-03), VP Merchandising, FP&A (links to VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandise Planner calculate OTB per department/category: planned EOM stock + planned sales + planned markdowns − stock-on-hand − on-order (open POs per VS-03.2 and in-transit import per VS-02.2); the residual is the available-to-buy dollars for the period | Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Weekly |
| 2 | Buyers consume OTB by raising POs/blanket releases (links to VS-03.2) within available OTB; system blocks or warns on over-buy; Merchandise Planner release additional OTB only with VP Merchandising approval and documented rationale | Buyers / Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Weekly |
| 3 | Merchandise Planner recalculate OTB weekly using latest actuals (sales, receipts, markdowns) and reforecast (W3288); adjust forward commitments, cancel/defer open orders where indicated, and report OTB utilization to VP Merchandising and FP&A | Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- OTB / merchandise planning module; PO/open-order (links to VS-03.2); in-transit import (links to VS-02.2); markdown (links to VS-64)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-buy risk**: Buying ahead of OTB inflates inventory and erodes turn (W3290); mitigated by hard OTB controls and weekly recalculation
- **Under-buy risk**: Over-tight OTB causes lost sales on trending items; mitigated by reforecast-driven OTB release (W3288)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-calculate of "calculate OTB per department/category" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "Buyers consume OTB by raising POs/blanket releases" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-report of "report OTB utilization to VP Merchandising and FP&A" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-69 (ensure merchandise financial plan & OTB discipline.)
- operational: hard OTB controls and weekly recalculation; operational: reforecast-driven OTB release (W3288)

### Time Estimate
- Weekly

## W3285. Markdown & Clearance Budget Allocation & Spend Authorization

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual/seasonal plan; in-season clearance need |
| **Frequency** | Seasonal allocation + in-season as needed |
| **Volume** | ~6 seasonal clearance events/year + ongoing slow-mover markdowns |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising, Category Managers, Merchandise Planners, Pricing (links to VS-01.2/VS-57), Finance (links to VS-17.1) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandise Planner/VP Merchandising allocate the markdown budget (markdown $ and %) across departments/categories from the annual plan (W3281); set seasonal clearance envelopes (links to VS-64) and slow-mover/reserve policy (W3294) | Merchandise Planner / VP Merchandising | VP Merchandising | Seasonal |
| 2 | Category Managers request markdowns (permanent price reductions, promotional markdowns, clearance) within budget; system validates against remaining markdown budget; VP Merchandising authorizes per authority matrix (links to VS-36); price execution via VS-01.2/VS-08 | Category Managers / Pricing | VP Merchandising | As needed |
| 3 | Merchandise Planner/Finance track markdown spend against budget, compute realized margin impact (W3286), and report markdown variance to FP&A; coordinate clearance execution (links to VS-64.2) and disposition (links to VS-05.3/VS-32.3) | Merchandise Planner / Finance | VP Merchandising | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Markdown management; price management (links to VS-01.2); clearance (links to VS-64); GL margin posting (links to VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Over-markdown risk**: Excess markdown spend erodes planned margin; mitigated by budget controls and optimization analytics (W3299)
- **Under-markdown risk**: Withholding needed markdowns leaves aged inventory (W3294) and ties up working capital; mitigated by reserve policy and clearance discipline

- System auto-validate of "validates against remaining markdown budget" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "track markdown spend against budget, compute realized margin impact" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-69 (ensure merchandise financial plan & OTB discipline.)
- operational: budget controls and optimization analytics (W3299); operational: reserve policy and clearance discipline

### Time Estimate
- Seasonal + in-season

## W3286. Gross-Margin Plan, Initial Markup (IMU) & Markdown-Margin Modeling

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual plan; pricing/cost change; markdown decision |
| **Frequency** | Annual + periodic |
| **Volume** | ~40 categories, ~35,000 SKUs |
| **Owner** | Merchandise Planner |
| **Participants** | Merchandise Planner, Category Managers, Pricing (links to VS-01.2/VS-57), FP&A (links to VS-17.4), Rebate (links to VS-39) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandise Planner set the IMU plan (gross margin before markdown) by category using cost (incl. landed cost per VS-02.2/VS-87), retail, vendor rebates (links to VS-39), and mix; model the maintained margin = IMU − markdown % − shrink (links to VS-23) to hit the 28–32% gross-margin target | Merchandise Planner / Pricing | VP Merchandising | Periodic |
| 2 | Category Managers model price/cost/mix scenarios: vendor cost increase (links to VS-03.3), SRP change (links to VS-01.2), bundle pricing (links to VS-92), promo elasticity (links to VS-58), and import FX impact (links to VS-18.3); select the mix that preserves maintained margin | Category Managers / Pricing | VP Merchandising | Periodic |
| 3 | Merchandise Planner/FP&A reconcile planned vs actual maintained margin, isolate drivers (IMU, markdown, shrink, mix, FX), and feed variance to weekly P&A (W3288) and monthly finance close (links to VS-17.1) | Merchandise Planner / FP&A | CFO | Periodic |

### System Touchpoints
- Margin/IMU planning; price management (links to VS-01.2); landed cost (links to VS-02.2/VS-87); rebate (links to VS-39); GL (links to VS-17.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-erosion risk**: Cost inflation not passed through erodes IMU; mitigated by pricing analytics (links to VS-57) and margin modeling
- **Model error risk**: Wrong elasticity/shrink assumptions distort the plan; mitigated by P&A variance tracking (W3288)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-import of "import FX impact" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile planned vs actual maintained margin, isolate drivers" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-532 (ensure controlled execution — Seasonal Merchandise Financial Planning & Open-to-Buy (PA-101.1).)
- operational: pricing analytics (links to VS-57) and margin modeling; operational: P&A variance tracking (W3288)

### Time Estimate
- Periodic (monthly/quarterly)

## W3288. Merchandise Plan-vs-Actual Review, In-Season Forecast & Reforecast

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Weekly merchandise review; in-season |
| **Frequency** | Weekly |
| **Volume** | All departments/categories in-season |
| **Owner** | VP Merchandising |
| **Participants** | VP Merchandising, Merchandise Planners, Category Managers, Buyers, Supply Planning (links to VS-02.1), FP&A (links to VS-17.4) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Merchandise Planner compile weekly P&A: actual vs plan for sales, GM%, receipts, markdowns, EOM stock, sell-through, and weeks-of-supply by department/category/key-item; flag variances beyond threshold | Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Weekly |
| 2 | Category Managers/Merchandise Planner reforecast in-season: update season-end projection using actuals + remaining weeks; decide reorder/cancel/markdown actions against OTB (W3283/W3284) and markdown budget (W3285); coordinate supply response (links to VS-02.1) | Category Managers / Merchandise Planner | VP Merchandising | Weekly |
| 3 | VP Merchandising conduct weekly P&A review with Buyers, Supply Planning, and FP&A; approve actions (chase, cancel, markdown, transfer per VS-05.2), update OTB and markdown budget, and report material variance to CFO/COO (links to VS-33.2) | VP Merchandising | CEO | Weekly |

### System Touchpoints
- P&A / reforecast module; demand planning (links to VS-02.1); OTB (W3283); BI/dashboard (links to VS-28.1)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reforecast-latency risk**: Late reaction to in-season trend causes stockouts or overstock; mitigated by weekly cadence and key-item decisioning (W3298)
- **Decision-deferral risk**: Slow markdown/chase decisions lock in margin loss; mitigated by clear authority and SLA


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-compile of "compile weekly P&A" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-update of "update season-end projection using actuals + remaining weeks" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "review with Buyers, Supply Planning, and FP&A" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-69 (ensure merchandise financial plan & OTB discipline.)
- operational: weekly cadence and key-item decisioning (W3298); operational: clear authority and SLA

### Time Estimate
- Weekly
