## Overview

Merchandise Financial Planning, Open-to-Buy (OTB) & Margin Management workflows for BuildRight Depot
Corp. — governing the **merchandising-finance discipline** that translates the corporate revenue and
gross-margin budget (per VS-33.1 annual business planning) into a sellable, buyable, and
inventory-funded merchandise plan across 200 stores, 4 DCs, and 35,000 active SKUs. This value
stream owns the seasonal merchandise financial plan, department/category financial plans,
open-to-buy (receipt budget) management, markdown/clearance budgeting, initial markup (IMU) and
gross-margin modeling, inventory investment and turn/productivity planning (weeks-of-supply,
GMROI, stock-to-sales), in-season plan-vs-actual reforecast, and merchandise performance analytics
(sell-through, margin, key-item decisioning, promotion post-mortem, vendor margin contribution).

This is distinct from **VS-01 (Merchandise Strategy)** which decides *which* products to range
(assortment, product lifecycle, pricing & promotions, product information); from **VS-02 (Supply
Planning)** which executes *operational* demand forecasting, S&OP, import/customs, and replenishment
orchestration; from **VS-33.1 (Annual Business Planning)** which sets the *corporate* revenue, OPEx
and capex budget; and from **VS-17.4 (FP&A & Reporting)** which performs *finance* consolidation and
group financial reporting. This value stream owns the *merchandise financial* layer that sits
between corporate budgeting and operational buying — the financial control of the open-to-buy,
receipt commitments, markdown spend, and gross-margin outcome that a PHP 42–45B COGS / ~28–32%
gross-margin retailer must plan and govern.

BuildRight's merchandise-financial exposure is large: ~PHP 62.3B annual revenue, ~PHP 42–45B annual
COGS, a curated 35,000-SKU assortment with a strong import component (~40% of COGS, 45–90-day import
lead times per VS-02.2/VS-87), bi-monthly catalog promotions (per VS-14.1), seasonal merchandise
calendar (per profile §13.2), and ~PHP 150M/month ecommerce GMV growing to 5–7% of revenue. Without
an OTB and markdown-margin discipline, inventory overbuy and margin erosion at this scale directly
degrade EBITDA (target 12–14%) and inventory turns (target 6–8x).
