## W3169. Lease Booking, Asset Capitalization & Start-Day Setup

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Origination complete (W3168) |
| **Frequency** | Per lease deal |
| **Volume** | Per lease deal |
| **Owner** | Lease Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Operations Mgr, Finance, IT, Treasury (links to VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Operations Mgr book lease in Lease & Finance Management (OKL): contract terms, payment schedule, asset record (serial, location, customer), residual, end-of-term option, lessor (W3165), funding source; generate lease ID | Lease Operations Mgr / IT | Lease Operations Manager | Per deal |
| 2 | Finance capitalize asset / record lease receivable per lease type (operating vs. finance lease per PFRS 16, which superseded PAS 17 — links to VS-17); post advance rental receipt and initial direct cost; reconcile to GL | Finance Analyst | Controller | Per deal |
| 3 | Treasury (links to VS-18) confirm funding draw (if lessor-funded) or BuildRight warehouse line; disburse to vendor; activate billing schedule (links to W3170); confirm start day and first billing date | Treasury Analyst | CFO | Per deal |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease & Finance Management (OKL) lease booking & contracts; GL/FA (links to VS-17/VS-35); treasury (links to VS-18); billing schedules → AR (links to W3170)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Booking-error risk**: Wrong terms / schedule cause chronic billing issues; mitigated by 4-eyes booking review


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "Lease Operations Mgr book lease in Lease & Finance Management (OKL)" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile to GL" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-schedule of "schedule" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-518 (ensure controlled execution — Lease Booking, Billing & Asset Lifecycle Management (PA-96.2).)
- operational: 4-eyes booking review

### Time Estimate
- Per deal; ~1–2 days

## W3170. Lease Billing Cycle, Auto-Debit & AR Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Billing cycle (monthly) |
| **Frequency** | Monthly per active lease |
| **Volume** | All active leases (~hundreds to low-thousands) |
| **Owner** | Lease Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Operations Mgr, AR (links to VS-16), Finance, Treasury (links to VS-18) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates monthly rental invoice per lease: rental amount, VAT (W3183), other charges (insurance, maintenance if bundled); deliver to customer per preference (email, portal, e-invoice — links to VS-79) | System / AR | Lease Operations Manager | Monthly |
| 2 | AR (links to VS-16) process collection: auto-debit (ADA) via customer-bank mandate, online transfer, PDC (post-dated cheques) on file; apply cash; manage exceptions (failed debit, NSF cheque) | AR Clerk | Finance Manager | Monthly |
| 3 | AR monitor aging; flag past-due to delinquency workflow (W3174); reconcile customer ledger; report portfolio billing metrics (on-time collection %, average days-past-due) | AR Clerk / Finance | Lease Operations Manager | Monthly |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease billing schedules (OKL) → AR; cash application (links to VS-16); auto-debit (links to VS-18); e-invoicing (links to VS-79)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Collection-failure risk**: Auto-debit fails disrupt cash flow; mitigated by PDC back-up and proactive outreach


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-generate of "generates monthly rental invoice per lease" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-file of "file" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-monitor of "AR monitor aging" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-518 (ensure controlled execution — Lease Booking, Billing & Asset Lifecycle Management (PA-96.2).)
- operational: PDC back-up and proactive outreach

### Time Estimate
- Monthly cycle; ~1–3 days

| **Frequency** | Per schedule + event-driven |
| **Volume** | All leased assets |
| **Owner** | Lease Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Operations Mgr, Field Service (links to VS-12), Quality, Customer, Insurance (links to VS-26) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Operations Mgr maintain asset record: location, condition, hours/usage (where telematics available), maintenance history, serial; track moves between sites with customer notification | Lease Operations Mgr / IT | Lease Operations Manager | Continuous |

| 3 | Lease Operations Mgr conduct periodic inspection (annual or per contract): asset condition, misuse, unauthorized modification, insurance compliance; document with photos; flag issues for remediation (W3172) or default path (W3174) | Lease Operations Mgr / Inspector | Lease Operations Manager | Annual per lease |

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Asset-abuse risk**: Customer misuse accelerates depreciation; mitigated by inspection and maintenance contract


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-record of "Lease Operations Mgr maintain asset record" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-schedule of "scheduled maintenance per contract" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "document with photos" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-105 (prevent unauthorized lease credit extension.)
- operational: inspection and maintenance contract

### Time Estimate
- Per lease; ~annual inspection

## W3172. Lease Mid-Term Modification, Upgrade & Add-On

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer request; technology refresh; contract amendment |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Subset of active leases |
| **Owner** | Lease Origination Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Origination Mgr, Lease Operations Mgr, Finance, Legal, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Origination Mgr assess modification request: upgrade (new asset swap), add-on (additional asset under same contract), term extension, re-pricing; validate credit impact (links to W3163) and residual impact (links to W3162) | Lease Origination Mgr | Lease Origination Manager | Per request |
| 2 | Finance/Lease Origination re-price: new payment schedule, residual adjustment, fees, taxes; Lease Credit Mgr re-approve if exposure increase; Legal execute amendment | Finance / Legal / Credit | CFO | Per request |
| 3 | Lease Operations Mgr book modification: update schedule, asset record (swap-in/out — links to W3175 for returned asset); communicate new terms; archive amendment | Lease Operations Mgr / IT | Lease Operations Manager | Per request |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease & Finance Management (OKL) contract amendment; pricing (links to W3162); contract amendment workflow (links to VS-22/VS-30)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Mod-error risk**: Mis-booked modification causes downstream issues; mitigated by 4-eyes review and audit trail


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "validate credit impact" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-schedule of "schedule, residual adjustment, fees, taxes" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "Lease Operations Mgr book modification" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-518 (ensure controlled execution — Lease Booking, Billing & Asset Lifecycle Management (PA-96.2).)
- operational: 4-eyes review and audit trail

### Time Estimate
- 1–3 weeks per request

## W3173. Lease Insurance, Loss & Damage Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Asset damage, theft, total loss |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven |
| **Volume** | Subset of leases |
| **Owner** | Lease Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Operations Mgr, Insurance (links to VS-26), Customer, Legal, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer reports damage/theft; Lease Operations Mgr capture incident with evidence (photos, police report for theft); confirm coverage per insurance policy (W3168) | Lease Operations Mgr / Customer | Lease Operations Manager | Per incident (<48 hr) |
| 2 | Insurance Coordinator (links to VS-26) file claim with insurer; coordinate adjuster inspection; obtain repair/replacement authorization; manage deductible (customer or BuildRight per contract) | Insurance Coordinator | CFO | Per incident |
| 3 | On settlement: Finance reconcile insurer payout, customer liability (deductible, uninsured loss), and lease balance; repair or replace asset (links to W3172) or settle lease (links to W3176); update asset/insurance records | Finance / Lease Operations Mgr | Controller | Per incident |

### System Touchpoints
- Insurance (links to VS-26); claim module; lease accounting (links to W3169/VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Uninsured-loss risk**: Coverage gap leaves BuildRight exposed; mitigated by mandatory insurance verification (W3168) and annual check (W3171)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-report of "Customer reports damage/theft" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-inspect of "inspection" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "records" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-518 (ensure controlled execution — Lease Booking, Billing & Asset Lifecycle Management (PA-96.2).)
- operational: mandatory insurance verification (W3168) and annual check (W3171)

### Time Estimate
- 2–8 weeks per incident

## W3174. Lease Delinquency, Restructure & Cure Period

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Past-due payment |
| **Frequency** | Per delinquency |
| **Volume** | Subset of leases |
| **Owner** | Lease Credit Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Credit Mgr, AR (links to VS-16), Legal, Customer Service, Field Recovery |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AR (links to VS-16) flag past-due; escalate per timeline (1–3 days: reminder; 7–15: notice; 30+: demand letter); Lease Credit Mgr outreach to customer, understand hardship, agree cure plan | AR Clerk / Lease Credit Mgr | Lease Credit Manager | Per delinquency |
| 2 | For viable customers: Lease Credit Mgr offer restructure (extended term, lower payment, deferment); re-underwrite (links to W3163); Legal document amendment (links to W3172) | Lease Credit Mgr / Legal | CFO | Per case |
| 3 | For non-curable default: Legal/Field Recovery execute remedies per contract — repossession of asset, demand full balance, file collection suit, report to credit bureau (where applicable); recover asset (links to W3175) and remarket (W3179) | Legal / Field Recovery | VP Legal & Compliance | Per case |

### System Touchpoints
- AR/collections (links to VS-16); legal/case management; asset recovery

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Repossession difficulty risk**: Self-help repossession constrained by Philippine law; mitigated by voluntary-surrender negotiation and legal process


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-escalate of "escalate per timeline" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "For viable customers" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-file of "file collection suit, report to credit bureau" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-518 (ensure controlled execution — Lease Booking, Billing & Asset Lifecycle Management (PA-96.2).)
- operational: voluntary-surrender negotiation and legal process

### Time Estimate
- 30–180 days per case

## W3175. Lease Maturity, Return Inspection & Disposition

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Lease end-of-term with return option exercised |
| **Frequency** | Per maturing lease |
| **Volume** | Per lease |
| **Owner** | Lease Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Operations Mgr, Inspector, Quality, Customer, Inventory (links to VS-05) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-maturity (90 days): Lease Operations Mgr contact customer re: end-of-term options (return/renew/buy-out — W3176); provide return logistics guidance if returning | Lease Operations Mgr | Lease Operations Manager | 90 days pre-maturity |
| 2 | On return: Inspector assess asset vs. end-of-lease condition standard (links to W3162 residual); identify excess wear/damage; compute customer charge for excess (per contract); document with photos | Inspector / Quality | Lease Operations Manager | Per return |
| 3 | Disposition returned asset: remarket via used-equipment channel (W3179), refurbish (links to VS-09), redeploy to new lease, or scrap (links to VS-73/VS-35); reconcile to residual and post gain/loss (links to VS-17) | Lease Operations Mgr / Inventory / Finance | Lease Operations Manager | Per return |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease & Finance Management (OKL) asset/end-of-term records; inspection module; inventory/disposition (links to VS-05/VS-35); GL (links to VS-17)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Residual-realization risk**: Returned asset value below residual → loss; mitigated by conservative residual (W3162) and strong remarketing (W3179)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-log of "logistics guidance if returning" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-document of "document with photos" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-reconcile of "reconcile to residual and post gain/loss" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-518 (ensure controlled execution — Lease Booking, Billing & Asset Lifecycle Management (PA-96.2).)
- operational: conservative residual (W3162) and strong remarketing (W3179)

### Time Estimate
- 2–6 weeks per return

## W3176. Lease Buyout, Early Termination & Renewal

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer elects buy-out, early termination, or renewal |
| **Frequency** | Per lease at customer election |
| **Volume** | Subset of leases |
| **Owner** | Lease Operations Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Operations Mgr, Finance, Legal, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Operations Mgr compute early-termination / buy-out quote per contract formula (typically: PV of remaining rentals + residual − unearned income − early-termination fee); communicate to customer with Truth-in-Lending-style disclosure | Lease Operations Mgr / Finance | Lease Operations Manager | Per request |
| 2 | On customer acceptance: Finance process buy-out payment; transfer asset title to customer (release the chattel-mortgage registration / RA 11057 notation — W3168); book lease termination and gain/loss (links to VS-17) | Finance / Legal | Controller | Per deal |
| 3 | For renewal: Lease Origination Mgr originate new/renewed lease per current rates (W3162) and credit (W3163); book new lease (W3169) and close prior lease; or convert to month-to-month per contract | Lease Origination Mgr / Lease Operations Mgr | CFO | Per deal |

### System Touchpoints
- Lease accounting (links to VS-17); title/security release (links to VS-22/VS-35); new booking (links to W3169)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Buy-out-discount risk**: Below-market buy-out formula transfers value to customer; mitigated by actuarially-sound formula and periodic recalibration


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-communication of "communicate to customer with Truth-in-Lending-style disclosure" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-renew of "renewal" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-105 (prevent unauthorized lease credit extension.)
- operational: actuarially-sound formula and periodic recalibration

### Time Estimate
- 1–4 weeks per deal
