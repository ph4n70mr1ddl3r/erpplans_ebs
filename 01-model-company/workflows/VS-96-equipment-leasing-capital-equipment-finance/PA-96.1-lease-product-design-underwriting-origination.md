## W3161. Lease Product Strategy & Portfolio Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Strategic cycle (links to VS-33); B2B customer demand; partner-bank opportunity |
| **Frequency** | Annual + event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~3–6 lease products; addressable ~5,400 B2B accounts |
| **Owner** | Leasing Product Manager |
| **Participants** | Leasing Product Mgr, CFO, B2B Sales (links to VS-11), Category (links to VS-01), Legal, Risk |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leasing Product Mgr/B2B Sales (links to VS-11) assess demand: which customer segments (trade/corporate/government), which asset categories (generators, HVAC, solar — links to VS-70, material handling, scaffolding), typical ticket size (PHP 100K–5M), desired tenor (12–60 months); size addressable portfolio | Leasing Product Mgr | CFO | 2–3 weeks |
| 2 | Define product menu: operating lease (off-balance-sheet for lessee), finance lease (capital lease / lease-to-own), sale-and-leaseback; tenor, advance rental, residual %, end-of-term options (return/renew/buy-out); align to lessor-partner appetite (W3165) | Leasing Product Mgr / Risk | CFO | 2–3 weeks |
| 3 | Steering committee approves strategy, capex/funding envelope (links to VS-40/VS-18), risk appetite, 24-month P&L; integrate to VS-33 strategic plan | CFO / Finance Controller | CEO | 1–2 weeks |

### System Touchpoints
- Strategy/portfolio (links to VS-33); capex (links to VS-40); treasury/funding (links to VS-18)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Credit-cycle risk**: Lease portfolio vulnerable to downturn; mitigated by conservative LTV, residual discipline, and portfolio diversification


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-renew of "Define product menu" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System rule-based auto-approval of "approves strategy, capex/funding envelope" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-517 (ensure controlled execution — Lease Product Design, Credit Underwriting & Origination (PA-96.1).)
- operational: conservative LTV, residual discipline, and portfolio diversification

### Time Estimate
- 5–8 weeks per strategic cycle

| **Owner** | Leasing Product Manager |
| **Participants** | Leasing Product Mgr, Finance/Treasury (links to VS-18), Risk, Category (links to VS-01) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Treasury (links to VS-18) provide cost-of-funds (funding spread over benchmark); Finance build lease-pricing model: monthly rental from asset cost, tenor, advance rental, residual value, cost-of-funds, operating cost, target margin, tax (VAT/EWT — W3183) | Finance Analyst / Treasury | Leasing Product Manager | Per rate review |

## W3163. Customer Credit Underwriting & Lease Approval

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Lease application from B2B customer |
| **Frequency** | Per application |
| **Volume** | ~50–200 applications/year |
| **Owner** | Lease Credit Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Credit Mgr, Credit Analyst, B2B Sales (links to VS-11), Trade Credit (links to VS-68), Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | B2B Sales (links to VS-11) submits lease application: customer KYB (links to VS-86), financial statements, trade references, existing exposure (links to VS-68 credit), asset & tenor requested, intended use | B2B Sales / Credit Analyst | Lease Credit Manager | Per application |
| 2 | Credit Analyst underwrite: credit-scoring model (financial ratios, payment history with BuildRight — links to VS-16 AR, industry risk, project/contract backing), exposure aggregation vs. limit, sanctions/PEP screening (links to VS-86); assign internal rating | Credit Analyst | Lease Credit Manager | Per application (1–3 days) |
| 3 | Lease Credit Manager approves per authority matrix (small-ticket automated; large-ticket committee with CFO/Risk); communicate decision to Sales; on decline, provide reasons and remediation path; record decision and conditions | Lease Credit Manager / Credit Committee | CFO | Per application |

### System Touchpoints
- Credit scoring; KYB/sanctions (links to VS-86); AR/credit exposure (links to VS-16/VS-68)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Credit-mis-pricing risk**: Under-pricing risk leads to losses; mitigated by risk-based pricing (margin tier by internal rating)


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-submit of "submits lease application" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-assign of "assign internal rating" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record decision and conditions" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-105 (prevent unauthorized lease credit extension.)
- operational: risk-based pricing (margin tier by internal rating)

### Time Estimate
- 1–5 days per application

| **Volume** | Per B2B customer opportunity |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lease Origination Mgr provide decision tool to Sales (links to VS-11): compare lease vs. buy vs. rent (links to VS-12) on total-cost-of-ownership, cash-flow, tax treatment (deductibility of rentals vs. depreciation — W3183), balance-sheet impact, technology refresh | Lease Origination Mgr / Finance | Head of Trade / Account Management | Per tool/cycle |

## W3168. Lease Origination Documentation, Insurance & Perfection

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Asset delivered; contract signed |
| **Frequency** | Per lease deal |
| **Volume** | Per lease deal |
| **Owner** | Lease Origination Manager |
| **Participants** | Lease Origination Mgr, Legal, Insurance (links to VS-26), Finance, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal perfect lease: contract execution; security agreement / chattel mortgage registration with the Registry of Deeds (Act 1508) / notation under the RA 11057 personal-property security registry, where applicable; lien on asset | Legal Counsel | VP Legal & Compliance | Per deal |
| 2 | Insurance (links to VS-26) bind required coverage: property damage, theft, casualty, third-party liability; confirm lessor as loss-payee; verify customer-maintained coverage where applicable | Insurance Coordinator | CFO | Per deal |
| 3 | Lease Origination Mgr complete booking package: signed contract, perfect security interest, insurance certificate, asset record, customer acceptance, funding confirmation; book lease (links to W3169) and disburse to vendor | Lease Origination Mgr / Finance | CFO | Per deal |

### System Touchpoints
- Contract/security filing (links to VS-22/VS-36); insurance (links to VS-26); lease booking (links to W3169)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Unperfected-security risk**: Loss of priority in customer insolvency; mitigated by mandatory perfection checklist


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-validate of "verify customer-maintained coverage where applicable" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-record of "record, customer acceptance, funding confirmation" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-517 (ensure controlled execution — Lease Product Design, Credit Underwriting & Origination (PA-96.1).)
- operational: mandatory perfection checklist

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks per deal
