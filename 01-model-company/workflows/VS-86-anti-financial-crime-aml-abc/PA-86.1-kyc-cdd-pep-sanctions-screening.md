## W2921. Customer KYC Tiering, Risk Scoring & Due Diligence Level Determination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New customer onboarding (trade/corporate) or periodic review |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven + periodic |
| **Volume** | ~5,200 AR trade/corporate accounts; ~50,000 MSME (VS-82) |
| **Owner** | Compliance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Compliance, AR, Trade Sales |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliance risk-scores each customer: geography, business type, transaction profile, ownership; assigns low/medium/high tier | Compliance Analyst | Compliance Officer | Per onboarding |
| 2 | Compliance determines due-diligence level: Simplified (low), Standard (medium), Enhanced (high) per AMLA risk-based approach | Compliance | Compliance Officer | Per onboarding |
| 3 | AR configures KYC tier in customer master; applies transaction monitoring rules; flags for periodic review cadence | AR | AR Manager | Per onboarding |

### System Touchpoints
- KYC risk-scoring engine; customer master; monitoring-rule configuration

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Under-tiered customer escapes enhanced controls; mitigated by documented risk methodology


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-file of "Compliance risk-scores each customer" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-review of "AR configures KYC tier in customer master" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-237 (ensure AML/KYC & sanctions compliance.)
- operational: documented risk methodology

### Time Estimate
- 30–60 min per onboarding

## W2922. Vendor/Supplier Due Diligence, UBO Identification & Beneficial Ownership

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New vendor onboarding or periodic review |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven + annual |
| **Volume** | ~800–1,000 active vendors; import vendors higher-risk |
| **Owner** | Procurement Compliance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Procurement, Compliance, Legal |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement collects vendor KYC: SEC/DTI registration, financials, ownership structure; identifies Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) ≥ 25% | Procurement | Procurement Manager | Per onboarding |
| 2 | Compliance validates UBO integrity, screens against PEP/sanctions/adverse media; assigns vendor risk tier | Compliance | Compliance Officer | Per onboarding |
| 3 | Procurement configures vendor master with KYC tier and review cadence; flags high-risk vendors for enhanced controls | Procurement | Procurement Manager | Per onboarding |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor onboarding workflow; UBO registry; screening integration

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Fraud risk**: Shell/related-party vendor enables procurement fraud; mitigated by UBO identification and screening


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-collect of "collects vendor KYC" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-validate of "validates UBO integrity, screens against PEP/sanctions/adverse media" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-review of "Procurement configures vendor master with KYC tier and review" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-487 (ensure controlled execution — KYC, Customer Due Diligence & PEP/Sanctions Screening (PA-86.1).)
- operational: UBO identification and screening

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 hours per vendor

## W2923. PEP (Politically Exposed Person) Screening & Enhanced Due Diligence

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Onboarding or periodic screening |
| **Frequency** | Event-driven + continuous re-screening |
| **Volume** | All customers/vendors/UBOs |
| **Owner** | Compliance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Compliance, Senior Management (approval) |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliance screens customer/vendor/UBO against PEP database (domestic and foreign PEPs, family, close associates) | Compliance Analyst | Compliance Officer | Per screening |
| 2 | On PEP match, Compliance conducts Enhanced Due Diligence: source of wealth/funds, business rationale, senior-management approval | Compliance | Compliance Officer | Per match |
| 3 | Compliance sets enhanced monitoring (lower thresholds, more frequent review); documents approval and rationale | Compliance | Compliance Officer | Per match |

### System Touchpoints
- PEP database; EDD case file; enhanced monitoring configuration

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Reputational risk**: Onboarding PEP without EDD invites scrutiny; mitigated by mandatory screening and senior approval


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-match of "match, Compliance conducts Enhanced Due Diligence" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-document of "documents approval and rationale" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-487 (ensure controlled execution — KYC, Customer Due Diligence & PEP/Sanctions Screening (PA-86.1).)
- operational: mandatory screening and senior approval

### Time Estimate
- 2–4 hours per EDD

## W2924. Sanctions & Watchlist Screening (UN/OFAC/EU/AMLC/INTERPOL)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Onboarding + continuous re-screening against updated lists |
| **Frequency** | Continuous (list updates) + daily batch |
| **Volume** | All customers/vendors + payment parties |
| **Owner** | Compliance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Compliance, IT, Treasury |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IT maintains sanctions screening engine with UN, OFAC, EU, AMLC, and other watchlists; runs daily batch and real-time screening | IT | Compliance Officer | Continuous |
| 2 | Compliance reviews potential matches (fuzzy-match triage); confirms true positive vs. false positive; escalates true positive | Compliance Analyst | Compliance Officer | Per match |
| 3 | On confirmed match, Compliance blocks transaction/relationship; files STR with AMLC; freezes assets per sanctions requirement | Compliance | Compliance Officer | Per match |

### System Touchpoints
- Sanctions screening engine; match-triage workflow; AMLC STR portal

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Legal risk**: Transacting with sanctioned party violates AMLA and exposes to penalty; mitigated by real-time screening and blocking


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-review of "reviews potential matches" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-file of "files STR with AMLC" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-237 (ensure AML/KYC & sanctions compliance.)
- operational: real-time screening and blocking

### Time Estimate
- Continuous; 1–4 hours per confirmed match
