## W2937. ABC Policy Maintenance, Risk Assessment & Training

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual cycle or regulatory update |
| **Frequency** | Annual |
| **Volume** | ~6,911 employees; high-risk roles |
| **Owner** | Compliance Officer |
| **Participants** | Compliance, Legal, HR, Training |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Compliance maintains ABC policy aligned with RA 6713 (Code of Conduct), RA 3019 (Anti-Graft), and ISO 37001; updates for regulatory change | Compliance Officer | VP Legal & Compliance | Annual |
| 2 | Compliance conducts enterprise ABC risk assessment: geographies, government interaction, procurement, licensing, intermediaries | Compliance | Compliance Officer | Annual |
| 3 | HR/Training delivers ABC training to all employees; certifies high-risk roles (procurement, licensing, government-facing); tracks completion | HR / Training | CHRO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Policy repository; risk-assessment tracker; OLA certification

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Compliance risk**: Outdated policy / untrained staff; mitigated by annual cycle


### Automation Opportunity
- System auto-update of "updates for regulatory change" (replaces manual Step 1).
- System auto-capture of "enterprise ABC risk assessment" (replaces manual Step 2).
- System auto-track of "HR/Training delivers ABC training to all employees" (replaces manual Step 3).

### Controls
- CTL-489 (ensure controlled execution — Anti-Bribery, Gifts & Conflict of Interest Management (PA-86.3).)
- operational: annual cycle

### Time Estimate
- 1–2 weeks annual setup
