## W2446. Project Dashboard & Status Tracking

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Continuous project monitoring |
| **Frequency** | Continuous; ~300–450 active projects at the B2C/trade mini-project scope (in-store design orders; distinct from the 80–120 active B2B construction register) |
| **Volume** | ~300–450 active projects/month |
| **Owner** | Project Consultant |
| **Participants** | Project Consultant, Store Manager, Head of Trade / Account Management |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System maintains project dashboard: status (design/procurement/delivery/installation/completion), milestones, payment status, open issues; Consultant updates daily | System / Project Consultant | Head of Trade / Account Management | 30 min/day |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113 BI platform, W231 QBR dashboard)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stale-status risk**: a dashboard showing yesterday's milestone state dispatches decisions on dead data; mitigated by the Consultant's daily update ownership (Step 1) across status, milestones, payments and open issues
- **Issue-burial risk**: open issues listed without ownership age until they stall the project; mitigated by the open-issues panel (Step 1) reviewed inside the daily cadence

### Automation Opportunity
- Dashboard auto-refreshed from source systems; anomalies auto-flagged; report auto-distributed to stakeholders

### Controls
- CTL-429 (ensure controlled execution — Project Tracking & Completion (PA-66.3).)
- operational: Project Consultant sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~30 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~300–450 active projects/month
- Frequency: Continuous; ~300–450 active projects
- Absorbed into the Project Consultant role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2447. Project Timeline Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project execution in progress |
| **Frequency** | Per project; avg 2–8 weeks |
| **Volume** | ~300–450 projects in execution |
| **Owner** | Project Consultant |
| **Participants** | Project Consultant, Contractor, Logistics, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project Consultant tracks timeline vs. plan: material delivery dates, installation start/end, milestone completions; identifies delays; adjusts schedule; communicates to customer | Project Consultant | Customer | ~30 min/day per consultant (rolling across the assigned 20–30-project portfolio) |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Delay-communication risk**: slippage discovered but not communicated until the milestone date converts a recoverable delay into a customer complaint; mitigated by the delay identification and customer communication (Step 1) at tracking time
- **Critical-path blindness risk**: adjusting one milestone date without its dependencies re-plans a schedule that cannot hold; mitigated by the timeline-vs-plan tracking across material dates, installation and milestones (Step 1)

### Automation Opportunity
- Project milestone status auto-tracked from installer check-ins and PO/payment events; 30-day post-completion satisfaction survey auto-sent; warranty cases auto-created from survey flags; aging of open projects auto-flagged to the coordinator queue

### Controls
- CTL-429 (ensure controlled execution — Project Tracking & Completion (PA-66.3).)
- operational: Project Consultant sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~30 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~300–450 projects in execution
- Frequency: Per project; avg 2–8 weeks
- Absorbed into the Project Consultant role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2449. Project Completion & Punch List

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Installation complete; final walkthrough |
| **Frequency** | ~300–450 completions/month |
| **Volume** | ~300–450/month |
| **Owner** | Project Consultant |
| **Participants** | Project Consultant, Contractor, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Consultant conducts final walkthrough with customer: verifies all work per design, identifies punch list items (minor fixes), documents completion with photos; customer signs acceptance | Project Consultant | Customer | 1–2 hours |
| 2 | Punch list items resolved within 7 days; contractor returns for fixes; Consultant verifies; final payment collected; project closed in system | Contractor / Project Consultant | Head of Trade / Account Management | 7 days |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Acceptance-without-punch risk**: customers signing acceptance before the punch list is agreed lose their leverage on the final fixes; mitigated by the punch-list documentation inside the walkthrough (Step 1) ahead of the acceptance signature
- **Punch-list-aging risk**: minor fixes promised at completion aging past the 7-day window close the project with open items; mitigated by the 7-day resolution gate with contractor return and Consultant verification before final payment (Step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- Project milestone status auto-tracked from installer check-ins and PO/payment events; 30-day post-completion satisfaction survey auto-sent; warranty cases auto-created from survey flags; aging of open projects auto-flagged to the coordinator queue

### Controls
- CTL-429 (ensure controlled execution — Project Tracking & Completion (PA-66.3).)
- operational: Project Consultant sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~300–450/month
- Frequency: ~300–450 completions/month
- Absorbed into the Project Consultant role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scorecard: projects completed, revenue generated, margin achieved, customer satisfaction score, change order rate, on-time completion rate; used for performance review and incentive calculation | Head of Trade / Account Management | COO | 2–3 hours/month |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113 BI platform, W231 QBR dashboard)

- **Metric-gaming risk**: consultants chasing completion count over margin and satisfaction win incentives while the program loses; mitigated by the balanced scorecard (Step 1: revenue, margin, satisfaction, change-order rate, on-time completion) used for review and incentives together
