## W2438. Project Sales Order Processing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer approves quotation |
| **Frequency** | ~300–450 project orders/month |
| **Volume** | ~300–450/month; avg project value PHP 200K–1M |
| **Owner** | Project Consultant |
| **Participants** | Project Consultant, Store Manager, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project Consultant converts approved quotation to sales order: links all materials to SKUs, confirms pricing, sets delivery schedule, allocates inventory from store/DC; deposit verified | Project Consultant | Store Manager | 1–2 hours |
| 2 | Finance validates deposit payment; system reserves inventory; generates pick lists for store/DC; project entered into project tracking system with milestones | Finance / System | Project Consultant | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Deposit-validation risk**: order processing on an unverified deposit reserves inventory against an unfunded project; mitigated by the Finance deposit validation (Step 2) gating the inventory reservation and the pick lists
- **Order-quotation divergence risk**: SKU links or prices drifted between quotation and sales order bill a different project than the customer approved; mitigated by the SKU linking and pricing confirmation (Step 1) against the approved quotation

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls
- CTL-428 (ensure controlled execution — Project Quotation & Material Management (PA-66.2).)
- operational: Project Consultant sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~300–450/month; avg project value PHP 200K–1M
- Frequency: ~300–450 project orders/month
- Absorbed into the Project Consultant role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2440. Project Delivery Coordination

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Materials ready; schedule delivery to customer site |
| **Frequency** | ~300–450 deliveries/month |
| **Volume** | ~300–450/month; often multi-drop |
| **Owner** | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Logistics Coord, 3PL Partner, Project Consultant, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coordinator schedules delivery: coordinates with 3PL per VS-56 for bulky materials (tiles, lumber, cement); ensures customer or representative on-site for receipt; confirms delivery window | Logistics Coordinator | Project Consultant | 1–2 days |
| 2 | 3PL delivers; customer verifies quantity and condition; system updates goods delivered status; discrepancies reported and resolved within 48 hours | 3PL / Customer / System | Logistics Coordinator | 1 day delivery |

### System Touchpoints
- Order management (links to VS-08 / W536 unified order management, VS-60 / W2286 routing)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Failed-receipt risk**: bulky deliveries arriving to no on-site customer are refused or left exposed, re-running a full truck for one signature; mitigated by the on-site receipt confirmation before dispatch (Step 1) and the 48-hour discrepancy resolution (Step 2)
- **Transit-damage risk**: tile, lumber or cement damage signed for at the curb surfaces only when the installer opens the bundle; mitigated by the customer's quantity-and-condition verification at delivery (Step 2) before the goods-delivered status updates

### Automation Opportunity
- Delivery scheduling auto-optimized from the route/dock/traffic data; real-time tracking auto-pushed to customer and store; POD auto-captured digitally

### Controls
- CTL-428 (ensure controlled execution — Project Quotation & Material Management (PA-66.2).)
- operational: Logistics Coordinator sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~300–450/month; often multi-drop
- Frequency: ~300–450 deliveries/month
- Absorbed into the Logistics Coordinator role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2443. Project Payment Collection & Milestone Billing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Project milestone completed; payment due |
| **Frequency** | ~600–900 milestone payments/month (2–3 per project) |
| **Volume** | ~600–900/month |
| **Owner** | Finance Analyst |
| **Participants** | Finance Analyst, Project Consultant, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System generates milestone invoice upon milestone completion sign-off; Project Consultant collects payment; Finance tracks project payment status: deposit, progress payments, final payment | System / Project Consultant / Finance Analyst | Finance Manager | 30 min per milestone |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Milestone-billing lag risk**: invoices generated late against completed milestones convert finished work into aged receivable; mitigated by the sign-off-triggered invoice generation (Step 1) with Finance tracking deposit, progress and final payment status
- **Final-payment leakage risk**: projects closing without the final payment collection write off the last milestone; mitigated by the payment-status tracking (Step 1) tied to the VS-17 financial close
- **Quote-accuracy risk**: estimated quantities diverge from billed actuals (takeoff error, site-condition change), eroding margin and customer trust; mitigated by the post-project estimate-vs-actual reconciliation feeding takeoff-factor updates

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls
- CTL-185: duplicate-invoice/billing guard on milestone billing; Operational: Finance Analyst sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~30 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~600–900/month
- Frequency: ~600–900 milestone payments/month (2–3 per project)
- Absorbed into the Finance Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2445. Project Material Returns & Surplus Handling

| **Frequency** | ~200–300 surplus return requests/month |
| **Volume** | ~200–300/month |
| **Owner** | Store Receiving Clerk |
| **Participants** | Receiving Clerk, Project Consultant, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer returns unused materials in saleable condition; Receiving Clerk inspects and processes return; system credits customer account or issues refund; material restocked | Receiving Clerk / System | Store Manager | 15–30 min per return |
| 2 | Finance adjusts project final cost for returned materials; damaged/unsaleable returns processed per vendor return policy | Finance | Finance Manager | Automated |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)

- **Return-condition risk**: used or damaged materials accepted as saleable returns restock defects for the next customer; mitigated by the Receiving Clerk inspection (Step 1) with damaged returns routed per the vendor return policy (Step 2)
- **Final-cost reconciliation risk**: returned materials credited to the customer but not to the project's final cost overstate the project's loss; mitigated by the Finance final-cost adjustment (Step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls
- CTL-428 (ensure controlled execution — Project Quotation & Material Management (PA-66.2).)

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~200–300/month
