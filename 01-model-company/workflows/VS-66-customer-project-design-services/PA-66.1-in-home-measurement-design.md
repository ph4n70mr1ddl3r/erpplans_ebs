## W2430. Customer Project Inquiry & Qualification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer inquires about project services in-store or online |
| **Frequency** | ~2,000–3,000 project inquiries/month |
| **Volume** | ~2K–3K/month; ~30% qualify for design consultation |
| **Owner** | Sales Associate / Project Consultant |
| **Participants** | Sales Associate, Project Consultant, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sales Associate qualifies inquiry: project type (kitchen, bathroom, whole house), budget range, timeline, property location; if project >PHP 50K, refers to Project Consultant for design consultation | Sales Associate | Project Consultant | 15–30 min |
| 2 | Project Consultant schedules in-home measurement visit: confirms date, prepares measurement tools and product samples; provides customer with project intake form | Project Consultant | Customer | 10–15 min |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM (links to VS-13 / W17 loyalty, W156 CDP, W65 CSAT)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Qualification-mismatch risk**: inquiries accepted outside the program's scope (below the PHP 50K referral threshold, or outside kitchen/bath/whole-house) consume consultant visits without converting; mitigated by the project-type/budget/timeline qualification gate (Step 1) routing sub-threshold projects back to store channels
- **Visit-no-show risk**: in-home visits scheduled on unqualified demand strand consultant hours; mitigated by the confirmed date and the project intake form prepared before the visit (Step 2)

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls
- CTL-427 (ensure controlled execution — In-Home Measurement & Design Consultation (PA-66.1).)
- CTL-427 (data-privacy — links to VS-91 / W53, RA 10173); Operational: Sales Associate / Project Consultant sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~2K–3K/month; ~30% qualify for design consultation
- Frequency: ~2,000–3,000 project inquiries/month
- Absorbed into the Sales Associate / Project Consultant role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2431. In-Home Measurement Visit

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Scheduled measurement appointment |
| **Frequency** | ~600–900 visits/month |
| **Volume** | ~600–900/month; avg 2–3 hours per visit |
| **Owner** | Project Consultant |
| **Participants** | Project Consultant, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project Consultant visits customer's home: measures rooms, documents existing layout, photographs space, discusses customer's vision and requirements, presents material samples | Project Consultant | Customer | 2–3 hours |
| 2 | Consultant creates rough sketch with dimensions; identifies potential challenges (plumbing relocation, structural issues); discusses budget alignment; promises design proposal within 5 business days | Project Consultant | Customer | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Measurement-error risk**: a mis-measured room propagates into the design, the material take-off and the quotation, surfacing as site rework; mitigated by the photographed space and the dimensioned sketch (Step 2) giving the designer a verifiable record
- **Concealed-condition risk**: plumbing relocation or structural issues invisible at the walkthrough emerge mid-installation as change orders; mitigated by the challenge identification at the visit (Step 2) and the 5-business-day proposal promise leaving review time before the design commitment

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls
- CTL-427 (ensure controlled execution — In-Home Measurement & Design Consultation (PA-66.1).)
- operational: Project Consultant sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~2–3 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~600–900/month; avg 2–3 hours per visit
- Frequency: ~600–900 visits/month
- Absorbed into the Project Consultant role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

| **Owner** | Design Consultant |
| **Participants** | Design Consultant, Project Consultant, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|

- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Rendering-expectation risk**: a 3D rendering that outshines the specified materials sets expectations the installation cannot meet; mitigated by the physical design boards and material samples presented alongside the rendering (Step 2)
- **Approval-limbo risk**: proposals neither approved, modified nor declined stall in the pipeline and expire unseen; mitigated by the explicit approve/modify/decline branch (Step 2) feeding W2435's revision loop

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls

## W2433. Material Estimation & Specification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Design approved; detailed material take-off |
| **Frequency** | ~400–600 material estimates/month |
| **Volume** | ~400–600/month |
| **Owner** | Project Consultant |
| **Participants** | Project Consultant, Category Mgr, Merch Planner |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project Consultant performs detailed material take-off from approved design: tile sqm, paint liters, lumber board feet, pipe meters, wire meters, fixture counts, accessory quantities | Project Consultant | Design Consultant | 2–4 hours |
| 2 | System validates quantities against standard waste factors; checks inventory availability at nearest store/DC; identifies lead-time items (special order, import); generates material specification sheet | System / Project Consultant | Category Manager | 1–2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Take-off error risk**: a missed tile-sqm or fixture count in the take-off shorts the delivery and stalls installation mid-project; mitigated by the system validation against standard waste factors (Step 2) over the consultant's line-item take-off (Step 1)
- **Long-lead surprise risk**: special-order and import items discovered after quotation push the timeline past the customer's patience; mitigated by the lead-time item identification (Step 2) feeding W2439's procurement coordination

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-demand auto-forecast from the project schedule; delivery-phasing auto-sequenced; site-reconciliation auto-tracked from the delivery-vs-installed delta

### Controls
- CTL-427 (ensure controlled execution — In-Home Measurement & Design Consultation (PA-66.1).)
- operational: Project Consultant sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~400–600/month
- Frequency: ~400–600 material estimates/month
- Absorbed into the Project Consultant role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2434. Project Quotation & Pricing

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Material specification complete; pricing required |
| **Frequency** | ~400–600 quotes/month |
| **Volume** | ~400–600/month |
| **Owner** | Project Consultant |
| **Participants** | Project Consultant, Pricing Analyst, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project Consultant prices all materials at trade/project price per VS-11; includes delivery, installation (if BuildRight-managed), waste allowance; generates comprehensive project quotation | Project Consultant | Category Manager | 2–4 hours |
| 2 | Finance reviews project margin: material cost vs. project price; ensures minimum margin threshold met; approves or requests pricing adjustment; quotation valid for 30 days | Finance | Project Consultant | 1–2 hours |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Margin-threshold risk**: project pricing that wins on trade-price competitiveness can land below the minimum margin; mitigated by the Finance margin review (Step 2: material cost vs. project price) requiring approval or adjustment before the quotation issues
- **Quotation-expiry risk**: a 30-day validity against volatile material prices quotes a project the purchase order cannot honor late; mitigated by the explicit validity window (Step 2) forcing re-quote and fresh approval on expiry

### Automation Opportunity
- Pricing auto-computed from the cost + margin target; settlement auto-reconciled with discrepancy-flagging; invoice auto-generated from the POD/delivery record

### Controls
- CTL-201 (ensure design-service quotation & change-order control.)
- CTL-427 (price-change authorization — links to VS-08 / W40); Operational: Project Consultant sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~400–600/month
- Frequency: ~400–600 quotes/month
- Absorbed into the Project Consultant role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2435. Design Revision & Customer Approval

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer requests design or quotation changes |
| **Frequency** | ~200–300 revisions/month (~50% of proposals) |
| **Volume** | ~200–300/month |
| **Owner** | Design Consultant / Project Consultant |
| **Participants** | Design Consultant, Project Consultant, Customer |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer requests changes: material substitution, layout adjustment, budget reduction; Design Consultant revises design; Project Consultant updates material list and quotation | Design Consultant / Project Consultant | Customer | 2–4 hours per revision |
| 2 | Customer approves final design and quotation; generates sales order; deposit collected (30–50% of project value); project enters execution phase | Project Consultant / Cashier | Customer | 30 min |

### System Touchpoints
- CRM (links to VS-13 / W17 loyalty, W156 CDP, W65 CSAT)
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Mobile app (links to VS-75 / digital engagement app, VS-10 / ecommerce)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Scope-approval gap risk**: verbal change requests proceed to procurement before the revised quotation and deposit are approved; mitigated by the approval-gated sales order and the 30–50% deposit collection (Step 2) preceding the execution phase
- **Revision-cost drift risk**: successive small revisions re-price the project piecemeal and erode the approved margin; mitigated by the material-list and quotation update on every change (Step 1) under the W2434 Finance threshold

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls
- CTL-201 (ensure design-service quotation & change-order control.)
- CTL-427 (data-privacy — links to VS-91 / W53, RA 10173); Operational: Design Consultant / Project Consultant sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~2–4 hours per occurrence
- Volume: ~200–300/month
- Frequency: ~200–300 revisions/month (~50% of proposals)
- Absorbed into the Design Consultant / Project Consultant role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
