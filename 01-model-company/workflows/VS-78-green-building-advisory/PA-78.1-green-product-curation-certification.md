## W2718. Green Product Identification & Certification Verification

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual assortment review or new green product launch |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly; 50–100 new products/year |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–3,000 green products |
| **Owner** | Category Manager |
| **Participants** | Category Mgr, Procurement, Quality, Sustainability Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category Manager identifies candidates: energy-efficient appliances (DOE label), water-saving fixtures, low-VOC paints, FSC lumber, recycled materials, LED, solar; develops criteria per category | Category Manager / Sustainability Coordinator | VP Merchandising | 1–2 weeks/quarter |
| 2 | Procurement verifies certifications: DOE Energy Label, FSC/PEFC, Green Seal/EcoLogo, LEED-contributing, BERDE-compliant; confirms validity with issuing body | Procurement / Quality | VP Merchandising | 1 week |
| 3 | Category Manager creates 'BuildRight Green Choice' designation in ERP: certification type, environmental benefit, savings data, BERDE/LEED contribution; enables filtering on ecommerce; targets 8–10% of assortment | Category Manager / IT | VP Merchandising | 1 week |

### System Touchpoints
- ESG/sustainability (links to VS-25 / W114 ESG reporting, W2619 corporate ESG rollup)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Certificate-forge risk**: a supplier's lapsed or fabricated certification riding on the Green Choice designation; mitigated by the validity confirmation with the issuing body (Step 2) before the ERP designation (Step 3)
- **Criteria-drift risk**: green criteria widening without the standards' updates dilute what the designation promises; mitigated by the documented criteria (Step 1) behind the filterable 'BuildRight Green Choice' designation (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Certification-expiry auto-tracked with renewal-alerts; ESG-metrics auto-computed from the product/supplier data; compliance-evidence auto-collected from the vendor portal

### Controls
- CTL-463 (ensure controlled execution — Green Product Curation & Certification (PA-78.1).)
- operational: Category Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 weeks per occurrence
- Volume: ~2,000–3,000 green products
- Frequency: Quarterly; 50–100 new products/year
- Absorbed into the Category Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2719. Green Product Supplier Evaluation & Development

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | New supplier or annual review |
| **Frequency** | Annual per supplier; event-driven |
| **Volume** | ~200–300 green product suppliers |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement Mgr, Sustainability Coordinator, Quality |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Procurement evaluates suppliers: ISO 14001, sustainable sourcing, carbon disclosure, waste reduction, ethical labor; assigns sustainability score alongside quality/price/delivery | Procurement / Sustainability Coordinator | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days per supplier |
| 2 | Sustainability Coordinator works with suppliers to improve: encourages certification, supports carbon measurement, provides green building guidance | Sustainability Coordinator / Procurement | COO | Ongoing |
| 3 | Procurement incorporates sustainability in selection: minimum threshold for Green Choice products, preference for higher scores in reviews; reports supplier distribution | Procurement | VP Merchandising | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor/partner management (links to VS-03 / W36, VS-56 / W62B 3PL onboarding)
- ESG/sustainability (links to VS-25 / W114 ESG reporting, W2619 corporate ESG rollup)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Score-washing risk**: sustainability scores assigned without supplier engagement become unimprovable labels; mitigated by the development work with suppliers (Step 2: certification support, carbon measurement) alongside the scored evaluation (Step 1)
- **Threshold-erosion risk**: the Green Choice minimum slipping to keep a favored supplier; mitigated by the minimum threshold applied in selection (Step 3) with the reported supplier distribution

### Automation Opportunity
- Certification-expiry auto-tracked with renewal-alerts; ESG-metrics auto-computed from the product/supplier data; compliance-evidence auto-collected from the vendor portal

### Controls
- CTL-06: vendor/partner-master onboarding; Operational: Procurement Manager sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~200–300 green product suppliers
- Frequency: Annual per supplier; event-driven
- Absorbed into the Procurement Manager role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2721. Green Product Pricing & Margin Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly pricing review |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–3,000 green products |
| **Owner** | Pricing Analyst |
| **Participants** | Pricing Analyst, Category Manager, Finance |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pricing Analyst reviews pricing: green products carry 5–15% premium; validates premium vs. certification cost and customer value (energy savings); checks competitiveness | Pricing Analyst | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Category Manager monitors sell-through: conversion (green vs. conventional), price sensitivity, margin vs. category average; adjusts to drive adoption while maintaining margin (30–35%) | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 day |
| 3 | Finance tracks green revenue: sales as % of total (target: 5–8% within 3 years), margin contribution, customer segment analysis; reports quarterly to CFO | Finance | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Pricing/financials (links to VS-17 / W9 financial close, VS-29 / W289 pricing master)
- ESG/sustainability (links to VS-25 / W114 ESG reporting, W2619 corporate ESG rollup)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Premium-erosion risk**: green premiums discounted toward conventional prices without recovering certification cost; mitigated by the premium-vs-cost validation (Step 1) with the 30–35% margin guard (Step 2)
- **Adoption-margin tension risk**: driving adoption through price cuts that gut the program's own economics; mitigated by the sell-through and price-sensitivity monitoring (Step 2) with Finance tracking green revenue share (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Pricing auto-computed from the cost + margin target; settlement auto-reconciled with discrepancy-flagging; invoice auto-generated from the POD/delivery record

### Controls
- CTL-463 (ensure controlled execution — Green Product Curation & Certification (PA-78.1).)
- CTL-463 (price-change authorization — links to VS-08 / W40); Operational: Pricing Analyst sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~2,000–3,000 green products
- Frequency: Monthly
- Absorbed into the Pricing Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2724. BERDE/LEED Project Material Documentation Support

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer requests documentation for certified project |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc; 10–20 requests/month |
| **Volume** | ~10–20/month |
| **Owner** | Trade Sales Representative |
| **Participants** | Trade Sales Rep, Sustainability Coordinator, Category Manager, Quality |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trade Sales Rep receives request: product certifications, MSDS, environmental product declarations, recycled content certs, chain-of-custody for wood, energy performance data | Trade Sales Rep | Trade Sales Manager | 15–30 min |
| 2 | Sustainability Coordinator compiles package: contacts vendors, verifies currency, matches to BERDE/LEED credit requirements, formats per submission standards | Sustainability Coordinator / Quality | COO | 1–3 days |
| 3 | Trade Sales Rep delivers package with product-credit mapping summary; facilitates project team Q&A; positions BuildRight as preferred green supplier; follows up on order | Trade Sales Rep | Trade Sales Manager | 1 day |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stale-document risk**: certifications or declarations compiled past their currency fail the project's submission review; mitigated by the currency verification (Step 2) against the BERDE/LEED credit requirements
- **Credit-mapping error risk**: products mapped to the wrong credits shift the project's scorecard and its certification target; mitigated by the product-credit mapping summary delivered with the package (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls
- CTL-463 (ensure controlled execution — Green Product Curation & Certification (PA-78.1).)
- operational: Trade Sales Representative sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~15–30 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~10–20/month
- Frequency: Ad-hoc; 10–20 requests/month
- Absorbed into the Trade Sales Representative role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
