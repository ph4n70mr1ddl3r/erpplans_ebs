## W2734. Green Product Sales Analytics & Market Intelligence

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Monthly reporting cycle — Green Product Sales |
| **Frequency** | Monthly |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–3,000 green products |
| **Owner** | BI Analyst |
| **Participants** | BI Analyst, Category Manager, FP&A, Sustainability Coordinator |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BI Analyst produces analytics: revenue by green category (energy, water, materials, solar), growth vs. conventional, penetration by store, AOV, segment analysis | BI Analyst | VP Merchandising | 1–2 days |
| 2 | Category Manager analyzes trends: Philippine green building growth (15–20% CAGR), competitor offerings, regulatory drivers, consumer awareness | Category Manager | VP Merchandising | 1 day |
| 3 | FP&A forecasts: 3-year projection, market share targets, investment required, ROI; presents to CFO and CEO for strategic planning | FP&A | CFO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- ESG/sustainability (links to VS-25 / W114 ESG reporting, W2619 corporate ESG rollup)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113 BI platform, W231 QBR dashboard)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Growth-attribution risk**: green growth credited to the program when the 15–20% market CAGR drives it; mitigated by the market-growth and competitor context (Step 2) around the category analytics (Step 1)
- **Forecast-theater risk**: 3-year projections built to the investment answer already chosen; mitigated by the CFO/CEO strategic-planning review (Step 3) on the stated assumptions

### Automation Opportunity
- Dashboard auto-refreshed from source systems; anomalies auto-flagged; report auto-distributed to stakeholders

### Controls
- CTL-465 (ensure controlled execution — Sustainability Compliance & Analytics (PA-78.3).)
- operational: BI Analyst sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1–2 days per occurrence
- Volume: ~2,000–3,000 green products
- Frequency: Monthly
- Absorbed into the BI Analyst role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.
## W2735. Environmental Impact Quantification of Green Product Sales

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Quarterly ESG reporting |
| **Frequency** | Quarterly |
| **Volume** | All green product sales |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Coord, Category Manager, FP&A |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Coordinator calculates impact: energy savings (MWh, tons CO2), water savings (liters), trees preserved (FSC wood), VOC reduction, solar capacity installed (kW) | Sustainability Coordinator | COO | 1 week |
| 2 | Coordinator validates: uses DOE baselines, water utility data, FSC records, product specs; ensures defensible methodology; documents calculations | Sustainability Coordinator | COO | 3–5 days |
| 3 | Coordinator prepares summary for ESG report: total CO2 avoided, energy/water saved, trees preserved, renewable installed; compares prior year; includes in sustainability report | Sustainability Coordinator | COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ESG/sustainability (links to VS-25 / W114 ESG reporting, W2619 corporate ESG rollup)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Methodology-fragility risk**: impact numbers built on assumptions collapse under ESG-report scrutiny; mitigated by the validated, documented methodology (Step 2: DOE baselines, utility data, FSC records) before the ESG summary (Step 3)
- **Unit-confusion risk**: energy, water, carbon and VOC metrics mixed without per-category discipline; mitigated by the per-benefit-category calculation (Step 1)

### Automation Opportunity
- Certification-expiry auto-tracked with renewal-alerts; ESG-metrics auto-computed from the product/supplier data; compliance-evidence auto-collected from the vendor portal

### Controls
- CTL-465 (ensure controlled execution — Sustainability Compliance & Analytics (PA-78.3).)
- operational: Sustainability Coordinator sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~1 week per occurrence
- Volume: All green product sales
- Frequency: Quarterly
- Absorbed into the Sustainability Coordinator role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legal Compliance Officer monitors: Green Building Code updates, LGU green building ordinances (Metro Manila requiring BERDE for new buildings), DOE energy labels, DENR EIA requirements | Legal Compliance Officer | VP Legal | Ongoing |

### System Touchpoints

- ESG/sustainability (links to VS-25 / W114 ESG reporting, W2619 corporate ESG rollup)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Ordinance-lag risk**: LGU green ordinances adopted before the assortment and training respond; mitigated by the impact assessment with the accelerated-assortment and staff-training response (Step 3)
- **Compliance-myopia risk**: monitoring the code but missing the customer-advisory opportunity it creates; mitigated by the customer-advisory assessment (Step 2) alongside own-construction compliance

## W2737. Green Product Lifecycle Assessment Data Management

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Product introduction or annual review |
| **Frequency** | Per product; annual batch |
| **Volume** | ~2,000–3,000 green products |
| **Owner** | Sustainability Coordinator |
| **Participants** | Sustainability Coord, Category Manager, Vendors |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Coordinator collects LCA data: raw material sourcing, manufacturing energy, transportation carbon, durability, end-of-life recyclability; stores in sustainability database | Sustainability Coordinator / Vendors | COO | Ongoing |
| 2 | Coordinator validates: cross-checks claims, identifies gaps, works with vendors to improve; for key categories engages third-party LCA firm for independent verification | Sustainability Coordinator | COO | Annual |
| 3 | Coordinator uses for product selection: identifies best lifecycle performance, replaces lower-performing, provides LCA to customers; contributes to Scope 3 emissions accounting | Sustainability Coordinator | COO | Annual |

### System Touchpoints
- ESG/sustainability (links to VS-25 / W114 ESG reporting, W2619 corporate ESG rollup)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Claim-verification risk**: vendor-supplied lifecycle data taken at face value into customer-facing claims; mitigated by the cross-checks with third-party LCA verification for key categories (Step 2)
- **Scope-3 leakage risk**: LCA data gathered but not fed to the emissions accounting it exists for; mitigated by the Scope 3 contribution (Step 3) from the sustainability database (Step 1)

### Automation Opportunity
- Certification-expiry auto-tracked with renewal alerts; LCA data auto-validated against vendor claims; Scope 3 contribution auto-computed

### Controls
- CTL-465 (ensure controlled execution — Sustainability Compliance & Analytics (PA-78.3).)
- CTL-465 (data-privacy — links to VS-91 / W53, RA 10173); Operational: Sustainability Coordinator sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: ~2,000–3,000 green products
- Frequency: Per product; annual batch
- Absorbed into the Sustainability Coordinator role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Coordinator compiles: total green revenue, % of total (target: 5–8%), growth rate, breakdown by benefit category (energy, water, materials, renewable) | Sustainability Coordinator / FP&A | COO | 1 week |

- **Category-mixing risk**: revenue classified green by loose attribution inflates the 5–8% target metric; mitigated by the benefit-category breakdown (Step 1) under the approved definitions
