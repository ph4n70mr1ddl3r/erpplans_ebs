## W2726. Green Building Project Material Consultation Service

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer inquires about green building materials |
| **Frequency** | ~50–100 consultations/month |
| **Volume** | ~50–100/month |
| **Owner** | Green-Certified Sales Associate |
| **Participants** | Green-Certified Sales Associate, Sustainability Coordinator, Trade Sales Rep |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Green-Certified Sales Associate conducts consultation: project type, green target (BERDE/LEED or general sustainability), budget, timeline; identifies contributing BuildRight products | Green-Certified Sales Associate | Department Supervisor | 30–45 min |
| 2 | Associate develops recommendation: lower-impact alternatives, energy/water savings calculations, payback periods, BERDE/LEED credit mapping for recommended products | Green-Certified Sales Associate / Sustainability Coordinator | Department Supervisor | 15–30 min |
| 3 | Associate creates quotation with green line-up, environmental benefit summary (energy saved, water saved, carbon reduced), BERDE/LEED scorecard; escalates to Coordinator for advanced support if needed | Green-Certified Sales Associate | Department Supervisor | 15–20 min |

### System Touchpoints
- Project/design (links to VS-11 / W58 corporate account, VS-66 / W211 design)
- ESG/sustainability (links to VS-25 / W114 ESG reporting, W2619 corporate ESG rollup)
- Logistics/DC (links to VS-04 / W3 receiving, VS-04 / W52 fleet, VS-77 / material staging)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Overselling risk**: consultation recommendations drifting past the project's stated green target into premium upselling; mitigated by the target/budget-based recommendation (Steps 1–2) with the escalated advanced-support path (Step 3)
- **Savings-defensibility risk**: energy/water savings quoted without a calculation basis collapse under the customer's bill comparison; mitigated by the savings calculations with payback periods (Step 2) in the quotation's benefit summary (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Material-takeoff auto-computed from the project dimensions; quotation auto-generated from the takeoff × pricing; project-status auto-tracked from milestone completion

### Controls
- CTL-464 (ensure controlled execution — Green Building Project Consultation (PA-78.2).)
- operational: Green-Certified Sales Associate sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~30–45 min per occurrence
- Volume: ~50–100/month
- Frequency: ~50–100 consultations/month
- Absorbed into the Green-Certified Sales Associate role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2730. Sustainable Product Return & Recycling Program

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Customer returns green product or recycling event |
| **Frequency** | Ad-hoc returns; quarterly events |
| **Volume** | 200 stores |
| **Owner** | Customer Service Rep |
| **Participants** | Customer Service Rep, Sustainability Coordinator, Store Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Service processes returns per policy; additionally assesses recyclability: LED → recycling partner, paint → hazwaste vendor, fixtures → manufacturer take-back | Customer Service Rep | Store Manager | Per return |
| 2 | Sustainability Coordinator organizes quarterly events: collection of old fixtures, lighting, electronics for recycling; LGU partnership; loyalty points incentive | Sustainability Coordinator / Store Manager | COO | Quarterly |
| 3 | Coordinator reports end-of-life metrics: return rate (green vs. conventional), recycling rate, event participation; identifies product design and take-back improvements | Sustainability Coordinator | COO | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Stream-contamination risk**: LED, paint and fixtures mixed into the wrong disposal stream create the liability the program avoids; mitigated by the recyclability assessment with per-stream routing (Step 1: recycling partner, hazwaste vendor, manufacturer take-back)
- **Event-dependency risk**: customer recycling waiting on quarterly events accumulates in garages and erodes trust; mitigated by the quarterly collection events with LGU partnership and loyalty incentives (Step 2) and the end-of-life metrics (Step 3)

### Automation Opportunity
- Process steps auto-sequenced with system controls; data-entry auto-populated from master data; exceptions auto-flagged for human review

### Controls
- CTL-464 (ensure controlled execution — Green Building Project Consultation (PA-78.2).)
- operational: Customer Service Rep sign-off on the workflow output

### Time Estimate
- ~varies per occurrence
- Volume: 200 stores
- Frequency: Ad-hoc returns; quarterly events
- Absorbed into the Customer Service Rep role; not incremental headcount unless volume scales significantly.

## W2732. Green Product Vendor Sustainability Scorecard

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Annual vendor review |
| **Frequency** | Annual; quarterly monitoring |
| **Volume** | ~200–300 green vendors |
| **Owner** | Procurement Manager |
| **Participants** | Procurement Mgr, Sustainability Coordinator, Category Manager |

### Steps

| # | Activity | Role (R) | Role (A) | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sustainability Coordinator compiles scorecard: certifications, carbon disclosure, sustainable sourcing, packaging waste, ethical labor, end-of-life program; 100-point scale | Sustainability Coordinator | COO | 1–2 weeks |
| 2 | Procurement incorporates: minimum 70/100 for Green Choice suppliers, preferred status for top scorers, development plans for below-threshold; includes in vendor reviews | Procurement Manager | VP Merchandising | Per review cycle |
| 3 | Category Manager identifies opportunities: high sustainability + poor availability (increase), low score + unique product (improve or replace), excellent sustainability story (co-marketing) | Category Manager / Procurement | VP Merchandising | Quarterly |

### System Touchpoints
- Vendor/partner management (links to VS-03 / W36, VS-56 / W62B 3PL onboarding)
- ESG/sustainability (links to VS-25 / W114 ESG reporting, W2619 corporate ESG rollup)
- Analytics/BI (links to VS-27 / W113)

### Pain Points / Risks
- **Score-gaming risk**: suppliers polishing disclosures without operational change to hold the 70/100 threshold; mitigated by the multi-dimension scorecard (Step 1: certifications, carbon, sourcing, packaging, labor, end-of-life) with development plans below threshold (Step 2)
- **Opportunity-paralysis risk**: excellent sustainability stories left unco-marketed; mitigated by the opportunity identification (Step 3: increase, improve-or-replace, co-market)
